# Liu Heifeng — Tactics & Action Economy

---

## The core loop

**Round 1 (setup):**
`Exploit Vulnerability` ◆ → `Overwhelming Combination` ◆ → third action (Strike / Battle Cry already fired free / reposition)

**Round 2+ (sustained):**
`Intensify Vulnerability` ◆ → `Overwhelming Combination` ◆ → **flex** ◆

The flex action is the whole point of Overwhelming Combination — two Strikes for one action buys you a Parry, a scroll draw, a Demoralize, or a third Strike.

### Why Intensify every round is worth an action
The **+2 status to attack** applies to *every* Strike vs the target — falcata, fist, **and your Implement's Interruption reaction** (it lasts until the start of your next turn). You're converting your lowest-value action into +10% hit and +10% crit on everything else. The alternative (a third Strike at MAP −10) is worth far less.

**Swap to tome Intensify when:** DeeJay has Heroism on you (both are *status* bonuses to attack — they don't stack). Bank a d20; if you bank a **17+**, cash it with **Mythic Strike** (mythic proficiency +10) for a near-guaranteed fatal-d12 crit. That combo is your boss-deleter.

### MAP inside Overwhelming Combination
- Falcata (S1): MAP 0
- Fist (S2, agile): **−4**
- Third Strike, falcata (non-agile): **−10** → often better spent on Parry, a scroll, or Demoralize

### When *not* to use OC
Only when the target has a big mortal weakness (15+), **no** resistance, **and** you have nothing worth doing with the freed action. Rare. Default to OC.

## Key interactions

**Cutting Heaven, Crushing Earth = a private off-guard engine.**
Falcata hit → target is off-guard to your fist. Fist hit → off-guard to your falcata. **No flanking partner required** — works on reach bosses nobody can flank. Inside a single OC, if the falcata connects, the fist swings at −2 target AC.

**Kudu's Slam Down is your reaction generator.**
He trips → enemy is prone → it **Stands (a move action)** → that provokes **both** his Reactive Strike **and** your Implement's Interruption. Your Interruption Strike ignores MAP entirely (+25 vs a still-prone, off-guard target), and the weapon adept benefit means **even a miss deals 1 damage → triggers mortal weakness** (8–15 free damage).

**Implement's Interruption triggers constantly.**
Trigger is *concentrate, manipulate, or move* by your Exploit target. Every spell is concentrate + manipulate — and this AP is full of casters. A **crit disrupts the spell.** The only common dead case: a melee brute standing still and swinging at you.

**Flowing Palm Deflection.**
Your fist has *parry* (Spirit Warrior) and this makes it **+2 circumstance AC**. Defensive round = Intensify + OC + **Parry** = **AC 36** while still swinging twice. Circumstance bonus, so it stacks with everything.

## Reaction priority

You have **one** reaction until Esoteric Reflexes at 14, contested between:

| Reaction | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Implement's Interruption** | Your Exploit target casts/moves — a free no-MAP Strike, and crits disrupt spells |
| **Retributive Strike** (Justice) | An enemy damages Tinzen or Kudu in your aura — ally gets **resistance 14** and you counter-Strike |

**Nimble Reprisal** lets you **Step** to reach an enemy just outside reach before the Retributive Strike — covers enemies that repositioned onto your allies.

Rule of thumb: **striker mode** vs a caster boss (hold Interruption), **bodyguard mode** in a melee scrum (hold Retributive Strike). At 14, Esoteric Reflexes gives a bonus implement-only reaction and you stop choosing.

## Your job in this party (you are not the tank or the debuffer)

**Unique to you:**
1. **Intel.** Exploit Vulnerability reveals the highest weakness (crit = *all* weaknesses, resistances, immunities). **Call it out loud on round 1** — it tells the Oscillating Wave psychic whether to bring fire or cold, and stops them wasting a Fireball on something fire-immune.
2. **Weakness amplification.** **Share Weakness → Kudu.** His 3d12 Spirit Thresher hits each carry your +8 mortal weakness — worth roughly **+12 damage per turn to him**. Sympathetic Vulnerabilities spreads it to same-kind adds.
3. **Arcane/primal scrolls.** Party covers divine ×2 and occult ×1. **Arcane is uncovered.** Scroll Thaumaturgy fires any tradition at **class DC 31** — Teleport, Wall of Stone, Earthbind, Fly.
4. **Recall Knowledge.** Sage's Calling (spend a Mythic Point → mythic proficiency ~+27) + Diverse Lore (any topic) + Know-It-All (successes pay out like crits) + tome adept (free RK every turn). Nobody else has invested here.

**⚠️ Coordinate with Tinzen — he has Battle Cry too.** Frightened does **not** stack (take the highest value). If you both auto-Demoralize the same enemy at initiative, one is wasted. **Agree in advance: he takes the boss, you take a second target** (or skip it and Strike).

## Openers

**Boss fight:** Battle Cry (free, at initiative — *different target from Tinzen*) → Exploit Vulnerability → Overwhelming Combination. Call the weakness to the table immediately.

**Trash/adds:** skip Exploit if the fight will be over in 2 rounds; just OC and let Sympathetic Vulnerabilities carry the weakness across same-kind enemies.

**When the casters are threatened:** hold your reaction for Retributive Strike, stand between the threat and the backline, use the Champion's Aura radius deliberately.

## Survivability stack (lean into it)

You are extremely hard to kill, and **Galan is a healing machine** (Medic Dedication, Battle Medicine, Continual Recovery, Ward Medic, Assurance (Medicine), Restorative Channel, Fast Recovery, Caretaker's Calling). This party does not need another cautious frontliner — **play aggressively**.

Your death-defiance is layered and fires in sequence:
1. **Mortal Herald** — free-action rank-6 Heal *before* you'd fall unconscious. You never hit 0.
2. Ally heals you if you do go down.
3. **Fiery Rebirth** — free action while unconscious: stand up (no action spent, no Reactive Strike provoked), +12 HP, and **24 fire damage to adjacent enemies** (Reflex vs class DC 31).

Plus AC 34 (36 with Parry), ~163 HP, Champion's Aura resistance for allies, and Aura of Courage for party-wide fear defense.

## Mythic Point economy (3/session)

| Spend on | Why |
|---|---|
| **Mythic Strike** | Mythic proficiency (+10) — pair with a banked tome d20 of 17+ for a fatal-d12 crit |
| **Correct the Story** | Force a reroll of an enemy's crit. Boss-fight insurance (Tinzen has this too — coordinate) |
| **Sage's Calling RK** | Mythic-proficiency Recall Knowledge (~+27) on anything |
| **We've Met Before** | After a minute of conversation: a mythic-proficiency Lore about that creature for 24 hours — gold in Influence encounters |
| **Summon Mythic Power** | 1/day, regain a point |
| **Fiery Rebirth** | The revive |

**Regain:** Sage's Calling refunds a point the first time each day you **crit a Recall Knowledge against an enemy** — and your tome adept gives you a *free* RK at the start of every combat turn. Against lower-level adds that crits often. Your recharge is effectively passive.
