# Liu Heifeng — Progression Plan

Starting point: **level 12 mythic**. Campaign runs 12 → 20.

---

## Confirmed plan

### Level 13
- **Weapon Mastery** → martial/unarmed **master**. Falcata and fist go to **+25 / +27** vs Exploit target.
  - This is the level where you match a *fighter's* accuracy against your exploited target (master +6 & +2 Intensify = +8, same as legendary +8).
- **Keen rune** becomes available (item 13, 3,000 gp) — see "Gear targets" below.
- Ancestry feat slot.

### Level 14 — the big one
- **FA 14 → Mortal Ascension** (Mortal Herald archetype, prereq: Mortal Herald Dedication ✓)
  - *"You gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed"* → **fly 35, or 45 with the Tailwind wand.**
  - Strictly better than the Soaring Form (flat 20 ft) traded away with Skyborn Tengu.
  - ⚠️ **One-line GM confirm**: Mortal Ascension is a *non-mythic* archetype feat (`archetype` trait only), so RAW it legally fills a free-archetype slot. Some tables assume "destiny archetype = mythic slots only." It isn't. Clear it before 14 to avoid a surprise.
  - Opportunity cost: this is *not* a deeper Spirit Warrior feat (God's Palm / spirit damage line). Flight wins in a vertical mythic AP.
- **Class feat 14 → Esoteric Reflexes**
  - *"At the start of your turn, you gain an additional reaction, usable only for reactions granted by your implements."*
  - **Solves the reaction congestion**: Implement's Interruption fires from the bonus pool, Retributive Strike from your real one. You get both, every round. This is when the Champion investment fully blooms.
- Skill feat, mythic feat 14.

### Level 15
- **Greater Weapon Specialization**, **Third Implement**, **Second Adept**.
- **Third implement pick:** community consensus favors **Amulet** — it grants champion-level resistance and fills the thaumaturge's famously thin reaction economy. **Regalia** (party-wide +3 status damage, mental-save aura) and **Lantern** (auto-reveals invisible/ethereal in a 30-ft aura — strong during Pride/Xanderghul illusion content) are the alternatives.
- Decide at 15 based on what the campaign has actually been throwing at you. Implements must be *held*, so you can rotate per scene: tome for a library day, lantern for a haunted vault, regalia for an audience with Belimarius.

### Levels 16–20 (sketch)
- 17: **Thaumaturgic Mastery** (class DC master), **ability apex** — put it on **Strength** (Cha reaches 20 on boosts alone; apex takes Str 20 → 23 = **+6**).
- 19: Major striking via ARP (4 damage dice).
- Mythic destiny feats from Mortal Herald continue to be available in FA/class slots.

## Retrain candidates (if the table shifts)

- **Pilgrim's Token** (skill 4) — winning initiative *ties* is marginal; everyone in this party has huge initiative already. Softest feat on the sheet.
- **Intimidating Glare** — largely redundant with **Intimidating Prowess** (Prowess already ignores the language penalty). Glare only adds value vs deafened/silenced foes. Not wrong, just insurance.
- **Correct the Story** (mythic 6) — Tinzen has it too. Two crit-negations across the party is fine insurance, but **Unrivaled Retort** is the level-6 mythic nobody has taken if you want uniqueness.
- **Summon Mythic Power** (mythic 8) — keep if Mythic Points feel tight; **Unbelievable Interception** is the alternative (though Kudu already has it).
