## Session Summary

Liu Heifeng caught up with the queen's other honored guests in the worst possible way — mid-brawl, on the threshold of a ruined Dawnsilver workshop, where a blue-skinned snow oni was refusing them entry by beating them senseless. He stepped into a fight already two rounds gone and, within his first turn, did the thing that would define his evening: named the creature, read out its whole defensive profile, and handed the party its weak point.

The fight ended in a surrender rather than a kill, and it was Liu who closed it. Guar pushed Gaston into the oni's reach to re-establish flanking and ate the reaction for it; Liu answered unprompted with a **Retributive Strike** that cut the oni from 81 HP to 10 in a single counter-blow. The oni threw up his hands: *"This isn't worth any amount of gold."* He was a hired doorman with a family, and once the pressure was off he talked. A cult called the **Risen of the Triad** had taken the workshop's back rooms — a dozen or more, all spellcasters — led by a haughty man whose name he could only half-remember. Liu spent a Recall Knowledge chasing that half-name and came back with something worse than useless: there was a **Runelord of Envy called Gimel**, seven thousand years dead. Kudu, playing an absent friend's character, proposed patching the beaten man up; Galan did the actual healing; Liu waved him off down the street.

What followed was a long, ugly push inward. Four **greedspawn** — glaive-wielding aberrations whose bite clawed at the mind — spent five rounds trying specifically to kill Liu, landing three natural 20s on him. He survived on Galan's healing, a **Delay Consequence** that postponed an entire bite, and a Mythic Point that wasn't his: Guar burned **Correct the Story** to erase the crit that would have dropped him. All four died — one to Guar's Blazing Bolt, one to a single 98-damage crit from Kudu, two to Liu, the last to a 101-damage combination.

Beyond that room, an undetected **haunt** boiled the eastern wall into lava and hurled phantasmal molten Dawnsilver at everyone standing in it. It could not be fought — the party did zero damage to it all fight — only talked down, and only by Religion or Intimidation. Galan, who had been safely outside, was told plainly that there was no running: *"You gotta go face those that need to be exorcised."* He walked in, ate 31 damage for it, and exorcised the thing in three successes with Liu feeding him aid.

Then the noise brought the cult. The party opened the last door onto eight robed initiates already dug in behind overturned tables, and Guar — down to his last sixth-rank slot and out of Mythic Points entirely — dropped a **Fireball** into the middle of them: eight targets caught, seven damaged, **340 total**. The session ended there, mid-round, with one initiate dead, seven alive, and the party untouched.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Doorman (Encounter 1)
- The party had been fighting for two rounds when Liu arrived. The foe: an **elite snow oni** (Foundry: *Haruto*) — **level 14 against the party's 12**, a PL+2 solo — invisible, standing in a self-cast **freezing cloud** that concealed everything and hurt anyone who lingered.
- **Liu's Recall Knowledge** established creature type and the full saving-throw profile — **Will is lowest** — and **Know-It-All** bought a second answer revealing **two** reactions: **Reactive Strike** and **Icy Deflection**. He also confirmed its 10-foot reach before committing.
- **Liu's Exploit Vulnerability** found **spirit weakness 15** — which his *astral* falcata rune already existed to exploit.
- **Galan's Dispel Magic** became a live lesson in counteract ranks (a rank-2 dispel cannot touch a rank-7 effect). The DM **rewound the action**, and Galan cast **Bless** instead — a party-wide +1 to attacks that ran for the rest of the fight.
- **Guar** spent a Mythic Point on **Correct the Story**, rewriting a critical hit on Kudu into a normal hit for 37. Minutes later **Liu** spent his own on a crit against himself — and the reroll came up a crit *again* for 36. Liu finished the night **0-for-1** on rewriting history.
- **Liu's fist crit was erased by the DC 5 concealment flat check**, prompting the DM's ruling of the night: *"You can't Mythic a flat check."*
- **Guar** dismounted, had Gaston **tail swipe** for 13, then burned a Mythic Point on **Dispel Magic** to strip the oni's **7th-rank invisibility** — success. He then lined up a sixth-rank **Fireball**, measured the template, found Galan inside it, and **cancelled**.
- The oni stepped into his own cloud and **flurried Kudu for 78**, then 31 more.
- **Kudu's Mythic Strike crit for 87**, and the oni **critically failed its Reflex save — knocked prone**.
- **The finish**: Guar moved Gaston into the oni's reach to put it off-guard again — *"Blind Dragon can handle it"* — and accepted the reaction. It hit the dragonet for 35. **Liu called Retributive Strike on his own**: Champion's Resistance cut the damage to **11**, and his counter-Strike landed for **71**, taking Haruto from **81 to 10 HP**.
- **Top of round 5**: *"I give up. This isn't worth any amount of gold."*

### Act I (cont.) — The Interrogation
- Liu, dryly, on the sudden collapse: *"But at first, he talked so much. For someone giving up so quickly."*
- The oni doused the freezing cloud to save his own life and explained himself: he doesn't even like the cultists, he needed the work, he has **70 kids to feed back home**.
- **Galan asked the question that opened him up** — *"Do you have any idea how many might be in there?"* — and got the number: *"a dozen, maybe more."*
- **What he gave up**: the cultists are in the room next door, they never let him inside, **they're all spellcasters**, and they believe they are **bringing back runelords**. Their leader is a burly, haughty man with a mustache and goatee who paid him personally. The name, half-remembered: *"Limmel, Gimmel, Gabby, something."*
- **Liu chased it with a Recall Knowledge** — and, on his first night at a strange table, **argued a −2 penalty onto his own roll** that the DM had skipped. He succeeded anyway. The answer: there was once a **Runelord of Envy named Gimel**, seven thousand years ago — *"but it couldn't possibly be him."* Liu relayed it to the party immediately: *"Maybe a fan? A wannabe?"*
- **Kudu, playing Tinzen, proposed healing the man who had just surrendered** — *"because I think he would."* **Galan performed it**, his Treat Wounds critical success bringing the oni from 10 back to 35. **Liu** sealed it: *"There was no one drop today, so we can end it peacefully."* The oni left; Liu waved him off.
- **Galan then spent roughly 40 in-game minutes** running the party back to full on Treat Wounds — rolling each one, with **Ward Medic** letting him treat two patients at a time and **Continual Recovery** removing the usual hour-long lockout.
- Exploration: doors unlocked and untrapped. Voices behind the two eastern doors, something *moving* behind the southern one, silence to the north. **Guar's Thoughtsense counted four creatures beyond the door before it was opened.** The party chose the sound.

### Act II — Greedspawn (Encounter 2)
- Four aberrations with polearms in a room half-buried under an ancient **wall of hardened lava**. They hissed and attacked without a word. Not a uniform group — **two at 200 HP, two at 180**, from two distinct stat variants.
- **Liu opened on initiative with Battle Cry** — a free-action Demoralize that **critically succeeded by +11**: **frightened 2** before anyone had a turn.
- **Liu's Exploit Vulnerability critically succeeded by +10**, revealing the full defensive profile — **no weaknesses, resistance 10 mental** — so he took **personal antithesis** instead, and **Sympathetic Vulnerabilities** spread it across all four. It shows in the log as a flat **+8 on every hit Liu landed for the rest of the fight**.
- **Kudu** opened with **Slam Down** into **Crashing Slam**, using his flail's d12 instead of a d6 for the fall damage — a feat he'd picked up specifically for that interaction right before the session.
- One greedspawn **taunted the party in Aklo** — *"You wish you were this pretty"* — which **Liu could read** and **Galan read aloud**. Half a minute later Galan answered it, casting **Stasis** with the line *"you have a face only a mother could love."* The target froze out of time entirely: untargetable, unable to act, and cut off from Liu's shock-rune chain — it lost its round-2 turn to it.
- **The greedspawn focused Liu almost exclusively**, and the pack rolled **four natural 20s in a row**. Three landed on Liu (63, 40, 54). **The fourth hit Guar for 45** — an enemy Reactive Strike provoked by Gaston — and **Liu's Retributive Strike cut it to 29**. A fifth crit, 49 on Galan, came off a nat 18.
- Liu survived on **Galan's 80-point two-action heal**, a **Delay Consequence** reaction that postponed an entire bite — damage *and* its mind-clawing rider — by a full round, his own Retributive Strikes (a second of which protected Galan), and finally **Guar's Correct the Story**, which turned the 54-damage crit into a miss. Liu's real floor was **68 of 165**.
- **Guar** worked the back line all fight: **Blazing Bolt** at fourth, fifth and sixth rank, **Psi Burst**, an amped **Frostbite** at DC 37, and a **Lose the Path** reaction that made a charging greedspawn treat every square as difficult terrain.
- **Sinful Bite** left Liu **sickened**; he spent an action retching it off, and Guar cast **Prestidigitation to pat him on the back** while he did.
- **Kill ledger**: Liu → **Guar** (sixth-rank Blazing Bolt, 46 fire) → **Kudu** (a single natural-20 crit for **98**) → **Liu**, closing it with **Intensify Vulnerability → Overwhelming Combination**: falcata crit into fist, **101 damage** rolled.

### Act III — The Haunted Forge (Encounter 3)
- Nobody detected it. The eastern wall transformed into molten lava and **dozens of shrieking human spirits made of fire** flew out to scorch the room.
- **The opening blast was not universal.** Four targets rolled a DC 34 Reflex save: **Kudu and Liu succeeded** (7 and 2 damage). **Guar and Gaston failed** — 32 each, plus **persistent fire** and **frightened 1**. **Galan was outside the room and took nothing.**
- **Guar** realized a beat too late that his **Sixth Sense** grants a secret check to detect haunts while exploring, and flagged it himself rather than letting it slide. The DM added it as a permanent reminder on his sheet, along with **Hydra's Bond**.
- The haunt attacked with **Molten Dawnsilver** at **+29** — **four randomly determined targets per round**, from out of reach, applying **slowed 1**.
- **An unnoticed +4 AC carried the whole party**: the old lava flow granted **greater cover**, auto-applied by the DM's cover module. It turned roughly six hits per ten attacks into four. Nobody at the table remarked on it.
- **It could not be fought.** The party dealt **zero damage to it all fight** — Kudu missed five times against AC 36, three of them critical misses, and his weapon simply bounced off the masonry.
- **Disabling it required three successes** at **Master Religion** (exorcise) or **Intimidation** (quell), two actions each.
- **Guar had neither skill.** Rather than force a bad roll, he cast **Thermal Stasis** twice for party-wide **fire resistance 12** — and made the single best positional play of the fight, stepping **one square** to remove himself and Gaston from the random target pool permanently, dropping the haunt from four attacks a round to three.
- **Galan was safe, and the DM made him walk into the fire**: *"No running. You gotta go face those that need to be exorcised."* He entered and took 31 for it.
- **The three successes**: Galan's Religion (a 36 lifted to 40 by Liu's Aid — *"I wouldn't have made it otherwise"*), then **Liu's Intimidation**, then Galan's Religion again, a 37 that was one short until a **Mythic Point reroll** turned it into 47.
- **Liu failed two Intimidation attempts** (by 3 and by 1), spending a Mythic Point rerolling each, and **all three of his Aid checks critically succeeded**. The night's only natural 1 landed on one of those Aids.
- **Liu's Retributive Strike** in round 3 cut Kudu's incoming 29 down to 15.
- **Liu's Recall Knowledge found the haunt's cold weakness** — which the party never got to exploit; Guar twice said he'd throw ice at it and never cast an attack spell before it went down.
- Throughout, the party could see **cultists in the next room** flipping tables into cover and casting protective spells on themselves.

### Act IV — The Risen of the Triad (Encounter 4 — **unfinished**)
- Eight **Trinity Initiates**, 150 HP each, already concealed by their own spells and **already dug in** — every one of them had **Mystic Armor +1** plus **Cover +2**, three of them **Greater Cover +4**, by the time the party's first attack resolved.
- **Guar rode Gaston to the doorway and let go his last sixth-rank slot** — a straight **Fireball**, no Mythic Point left to spend on it. **Eight caught in the burst, seven damaged: 340 total.** Two critical failures took 80 apiece (on a natural 1 and a natural 2), four failures took 40, one success took 20 — **and one initiate rolled a natural 20 and walked out of it untouched.**
- **Kudu** advanced to the doorway and **Lunged**, deliberately not committing his token deeper into the room under the DM's vision rules, and hit for **66** — taking one initiate from 90 down to 24.
- **Liu** moved up, **Exploited Vulnerability** (no weaknesses — they're merely humanoid, so **personal antithesis** again for +8), and finished Kudu's target with **Overwhelming Combination**: 84 rolled, **92 applied**.
- The cult's answer was **Vision of Death** (8d6 mental), cast three times, and it did nothing. **Kudu critically succeeded twice** on natural 19s; **Liu critically succeeded** on a natural 6 — a 28 against DC 26, upgraded by **Disciplined Mind**. One initiate screamed *"You killed Janet!"* before throwing the spell that bounced.
- **The party took zero damage this encounter.** **Galan never got a turn.**
- **Only one initiate died. Seven are still standing**, none below roughly half health — and one of them is **statistically distinct from the rest** (Perception +17 / Reflex +18 against +15/+16 on the other seven), very likely a leader.
- **The session ended mid-round after roughly a round and a half.** The fight picks up next week.

## Character Spotlight

### Liu Heifeng · the Recording Blade
- **He arrived and immediately became the party's eyes.** In his first turns he had the oni's creature type, its full save profile, **both** of its reactions, its reach, *and* spirit weakness 15. He did the same to the greedspawn and to the haunt. Guar's read on him — *"we're fighting for our lives and he's off to the side re-writing a book"* — landed, and Liu owned it: *"Gotta record everything."*
- **He also ended the first fight.** Guar baited the oni's reaction with Gaston; Liu's **Retributive Strike** cut the hit from 35 to 11 and answered for **71**, dropping Haruto from 81 HP to 10 and straight into surrender. He accounted for **183 of the 300 damage the party dealt after he arrived** — 61% of it — in a fight he joined two rounds late.
- **His defining moment was mercy, not damage.** When the oni gave up, Liu's first instinct was suspicion, his second was to interrogate properly, and his third was to let the man walk: *"There was no one drop today, so we can end it peacefully."* A justice-cause Champion playing his edicts straight — and it bought more than the fight did.
- **He argued a penalty onto his own roll.** Chasing the leader's half-remembered name, he stopped the DM to point out he should be taking a −2 for the repeat Recall Knowledge, on his first night at a table full of strangers. Then he made it anyway, and surfaced **Gimel**.
- **He was the crit sponge and stayed useful.** Three natural 20s landed on him in the greedspawn fight while he continued to Recall Knowledge, Retributive Strike on his allies' behalf — once for Guar, once for Galan — and set up his own kills.
- **He closed two of the three finished fights**: 101 damage on the last greedspawn, and 92 on the first cultist to fall.

### Kudu · the One Who Stood in Front
- **He absorbed the boss.** The oni picked him and stayed on him — a 78-damage flurry in a single turn, then 31 more — and Kudu kept swinging through it. He was the body between a PL+2 solo and the rest of the party.
- **His build is deliberate, not incidental.** He picked up improved **Slam Down** immediately before the session specifically so **Crashing Slam** would use his flail's d12 instead of a d6, and he opened the greedspawn fight with exactly that combination.
- **When he connects, the fight changes.** His **Mythic Strike** crit for 87 and knocked the snow oni prone; his single natural-20 crit for **98** killed a greedspawn outright. His own summary is the honest one: *"When I do hit, man, it hurts."*
- **He played Tinzen with real generosity.** Handed an absent friend's sheet he'd never read, he ran the grapples and Needle Darts competently — then made the call that gave the encounter its ending: *"before he goes, I'm gonna treat his wounds, because I think he would."* Playing someone else's character *in character*, on their behalf, is a quietly classy thing to do.
- **His comic timing is the table's metronome** — calling his shot on the Mythic Strike, diagnosing his own luck as *"feast or famine around this motherfucker,"* and refusing to accept that a building could dodge him.
- He ran **Scout** in exploration and was the one who spotted the cultists rousing in the far room and relayed it.

### Guar · the Right Tool at the Right Time
- **He rebuilt his character to fit a party he'd never played with.** He arrived with a tanking Commander, looked at three bodies already in melee, and swapped to an Oscillating Wave Psychic on the spot — then took **Drake Rider** on top of it.
- **He spent his own Mythic Points on other people's problems.** **Correct the Story** to rewrite the crit on Kudu in the first fight, and again in the greedspawn room to erase the 54-damage critical hit that would have dropped Liu. Neither was his turn, neither was his damage.
- **The Fireball he didn't cast came first.** In the first fight he lined one up, measured the template, found Galan inside it, and cancelled. He spent the rest of the session hunting a clean shot — and got one on eight cultists behind a table, with his last sixth-rank slot and no Mythic Points left: **340 damage.** His own verdict: *"Didn't feel too powerful until I freaking threw that Fireball."*
- **He found a lane when his kit had nothing.** *"Religion and intimidation are two of the only skills that I do not have"* — so rather than force a bad roll at the haunt he handed the party **fire resistance 12** twice, and worked out that a single square of movement would take him and Gaston out of its random target pool for good.
- **He filled the utility gaps**: a mythic **Dispel Magic** that stripped the oni's 7th-rank invisibility, **Thoughtsense** counting four hostiles beyond a closed door, and **Detect Magic** as his exploration activity when nobody else had it. He also volunteered his own missed **Sixth Sense** haunt check to the DM rather than letting it pass.
- **He is generous with what he knows** — mentoring Pax through first-session overwhelm (*"they're all just tools in a toolbox. You just need to remember to use the right thing at the right time"*) and catching Galan's mythic action economy mid-turn.

### Galan Magani · the Power of Milani
- **He is the reason Liu is alive.** Three natural 20s landed on the thaumaturge and Galan answered every one: an 80-point two-action heal, a **Delay Consequence** that deferred an entire bite — damage *and* rider — by a round, and a second Retributive-Strike setup by staying in reach.
- **He walked into the fire.** He was outside the haunt's room and took nothing from its opening blast. The DM told him there was no exorcising from safety — *"You gotta go face those that need to be exorcised"* — and he went in, took 31, and rolled the checks. **Two of the three successes that killed the haunt were his**, the last one rescued by a Mythic Point reroll from a 37 that was one short.
- **His comedy is a comeback, not a non sequitur.** A greedspawn taunted the party in Aklo — *"you wish you were this pretty"* — Galan read it out, and half a minute later answered it while freezing the thing out of time: *"you have a face only a mother could love."*
- **He asked the question that cracked the interrogation.** *"Do you have any idea how many might be in there?"* is what produced *"a dozen, maybe more"* — the party's only estimate of what they're walking into.
- **He thinks past the current fight.** After the haunt he pointed out what everyone had missed: *"I feel like we should have just came into this room to the left and made them come in here with the haunt. Because they probably would have walked through here."* Using a hazard as a weapon against the *next* encounter is a level above what the fight asked for.
- **He builds for his role deliberately.** He took **Elf Step** for exactly one reason — *"as a healer, I need to be able to get away from people"* — and runs **Ward Medic** and **Continual Recovery** so he can treat two patients at a time without the hour lockout. He also caught and fixed a sheet error between sessions where every ability increase had silently failed to apply: *"I realized after we played last time that none of my ability points were right."*

### What Liu is thinking about

- **The tome is working, and it's free.** The adept benefit's Recall Knowledge happens automatically at the start of each turn — no action, no cost. Take it every round regardless of the escalating DC: the real prize is the **+1 circumstance bonus to the next attack** on a success, and a failure costs nothing. The genuine action competition is between **Exploit Vulnerability, Intensify Vulnerability and Overwhelming Combination**, which all want the same three actions.
- **Dubious Knowledge is a trap on a failed RK.** One truth and one lie, with no way to tell them apart, is worse than no information. Correctly declined this session.

AC 34, Parry, Champion's Resistance and a dedicated healer mean Liu can hold attention that would kill Guar outright. But it consumed Galan's biggest heal, a reaction spell, and one of *Guar's* Mythic Points.
