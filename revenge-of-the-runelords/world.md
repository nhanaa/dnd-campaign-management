# Revenge of the Runelords — World & Storyline

Pathfinder 2e Remastered | Adventure Path by James Jacobs | **Levels 12 → 20, mythic**
Setting: the **Saga Lands** (Varisia), beginning in **Xin-Eurythnia**, New Thassilon.
Start date in-world: **late 4725 AR**.

*Source: official Player's Guide. Campaign-specific developments get appended after each session.*

---

## The premise

The final chapter of the runelord saga that began with *Rise of the Runelords*, continued through *Shattered Star*, *Return of the Runelords*, and *Seven Dooms for Sandpoint*. It is the **first AP to require mythic characters**.

**Queen Sorshen** — the reformed Runelord of Lust, now ruler of eastern New Thassilon — has invited heroes from across the world to the unveiling of the **Circle of Open Hands**, a portal hub in her capital that will link Xin-Eurythnia to **Absalom**, and eventually to the wider world. She wants trade, legitimacy, and proof that the old Runelord Sorshen is dead.

The PCs each received a **Sihedron medallion** from Sorshen as a badge of honored-guest status (bolster your flesh: 10 temporary HP once/day for 8 hours; also preserves corpses).

**Core theme:** a desperate stand against an overwhelming power bent on destroying everything. Edicts/anathema that push toward destruction or upending civilization don't fit.

## Xin-Eurythnia

Once **Xin-Shalast**, capital of Runelord Karzoug's Shalast, hidden for millennia in the eastern **Kodar Mountains**. Sorshen relocated here after *Return of the Runelords*, restored its wards against the high altitude, and renamed it after her own lost capital (the original Eurythnia lies buried under modern Korvosa). With the **church of Nocticula**, she has positioned the city as a haven for exiles and creators.

### Districts
- **Artisan District** (south) — shops, inns, eateries; the closest thing to a market district.
- **Residential District** (east) — once a prison-slum, then destroyed by Earthfall volcanism; now restored and home to most newcomers.
- **Jotunburg** (center) — where the city's **giants** live, alongside lamias and other "monstrous" residents. Contains **Shahlaria**, now Sorshen's **Blood Moon Palace**.
- **Entertainment District** (north) — museums, arenas, galleries, temples, schools. Its **Temple Plaza** is overlooked by **Midnight's Gallery**, the cathedral of Nocticula, and is where the Circle of Open Hands will be unveiled.
- **Rising District** (upper slopes of **Mhar Massif**, Avistan's tallest peak) — Karzoug's old noble quarter, running up to the **Pinnacles of Avarice** and the colossal carved visage of Karzoug at the summit. Largely unoccupied; beyond the wards, in the mountain's death zone.

## The Runelords

Seven rulers of ancient **Thassilon**, each a powerful wizard tied to one of the seven sins and a school of rune magic. All were cruel; the title functioned like "king." A **runewell** is an artifact inside a personal demiplane (an "Eye"), linked to Golarion by an **anima focus**. Only Sorshen, Xanderghul, Alaznist, Karzoug, and Belimarius ever made one (Belimarius's is faulty).

| Runelord | Sin / Magic | Polearm | Nation | Fate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Xanderghul** | Pride — illusion | bec de corbin | Cyrusian (N. Varisia) | Assassinated by Alaznist before *Return of the Runelords* |
| **Sorshen** | Lust — mind/mental | guisarme | Eurythnia (S. Varisia) | **Alive** — reformed, rules eastern New Thassilon |
| **Alaznist** | Wrath — destruction/elemental | ranseur | Bakrakhan (W. Varisia) | Defeated in *Return of the Runelords* |
| **Karzoug** | Greed — transmutation | glaive | Shalast (C. Varisia) | Defeated in *Rise of the Runelords* |
| **Zutha** | Gluttony — void/undeath (lich) | scythe | Gastash (E. Varisia) | Gluttonous Tome destroyed; can't return |
| **Belimarius** | Envy — defensive/dispelling | halberd | Edasseril (NW Varisia) | **Alive** — rules western New Thassilon, unrepentant |
| **Krune** | Sloth — conjuration | longspear | Haruka | Died in Earthfall; briefly resurrected, then laid to rest |

**Note:** "runelord" also now refers to a *modern* school of arcane spellcasters who adopted the title (the **runelord archetype**, *Lost Omens Rival Academies*). They are not the ancient tyrants.

### The two living runelords
- **Sorshen** — patron and quest-giver. Genuinely reformed, but a mythic spellcaster whose longest feud was with Xanderghul.
- **Belimarius** — rules western New Thassilon, clings to pre-Earthfall Thassilonian methods, sees herself as the sole worthy heir. Sorshen has given up on her reforming.

## Campaign mechanics to know

### Mythic rules
- **3 Mythic Points per session.** Mythic proficiency = **+10**, a tier beyond legendary.
- **Calling** → first access to mythic power + a daily way to regain a point.
- **Mythic feats** at every even level.
- **Destiny** at 12 — the AP adds four beyond *War of Immortals*' nine: **avenging runelord, heroic scion, timewracked, warshard warrior**.
- GMs are told to hand out Mythic Points generously; ask if an action followed your calling.

### Subsystems in play (GM Core)
**Victory Points** underpins the rest. Also: **Chases**, **Infiltration**, **Influence**, **Research**.

### Skills that matter most
- **Research/knowledge:** Arcana, Occultism, Religion (then Nature, Society)
- **Physical:** Athletics, then Thievery
- **Social:** Diplomacy > Intimidation > Deception
- **Lores by frequency:** Warfare, Academia, Library, Art; then Architecture, Mercantile, Mountain
- **Languages: Thassilonian is the single most important.** Others of note: Aklo, Azlanti, Chthonian, Draconic, Fey, Jotun, Protean, Requian, Talican, Thalassic.

### Gear at creation
Lump sum **4,500 gp** (common items ≤ level 11), or the pick-your-items list. *This table uses **Automatic Rune Progression (modified)**, so fundamental runes are automatic and gold goes to property runes, wands, scrolls, and worn items.*

### Session-zero downtime activities (DC 30, one magic item reward max)
| Activity | Skill | Crit reward |
|---|---|---|
| Spread Word of Your Homeland | Diplomacy | 5 consumables (≤ lvl 12) |
| Revelations from the Time Before | Occultism | held magic item (≤ lvl 12) |
| Feats of Strength and Bravado | Athletics | magic weapon (≤ lvl 12 / ≤2,000 gp) |
| Admire Artistry | Religion | worn magic item (≤ lvl 12) |
| A Lively Debate | Arcana | staff or wand (≤ lvl 12) |
| Seek Clues for Eventual Vengeance | Nature | magic armor (≤ lvl 12 / ≤2,000 gp) |

*Liu's best odds: **Admire Artistry** (Religion master) or **Feats of Strength** (Athletics master).*

## Faiths in the region

Most of the core 20 work. Also commonly worshipped in Varisia: **Ashava, Black Butterfly, Kazutal, Milani, Shizuru, Tsukiyo, Ylimancha**. **Nocticula's faith plays a larger role in this AP** — her cathedral, Midnight's Gallery, overlooks the Circle of Open Hands.

Deities of world-ending or mass destruction (Rovagug, Groetus) are poor fits.

## Campaign-specific developments

### The Risen of the Triad
A cult operating out of a ruined **Dawnsilver workshop** in Xin-Eurythnia, three days before the Circle of Open Hands ceremony. Per the hired doorman: **a dozen or more, all spellcasters**, and they believe they are **bringing back runelords**. Their leader is a burly, haughty man with a mustache and goatee who handles payments personally — not yet seen by the party.

Their rank and file are **Trinity Initiates** (150 HP, Mystic Armor, self-cast concealment, *Vision of Death*, Force Barrage, Fly). **All eight are now dead.** The initiate flagged in Session 1 as "measurably sharper" turned out to be nothing — he died mid-fight to a rank-3 Fireball. The last survivor was simply the luckiest creature in the room.

The workshop also held **greedspawn soldiers** (180 HP, AC 30, glaive with 10-ft reach + jaws with **Sinful Bite**, Reactive Strike, +1 status to saves vs magic) — a heavier variant than Session 1's greedspawn.

⚠️ **The initiates were all carrying *+1 striking* bec de corbins.** That is **Xanderghul's** polearm — the Runelord of **Pride**, and the inventor of the *raise runelord* ritual. The greedspawn carried **glaives** (Karzoug, **Greed**). *Nobody at the table remarked on this; recorded as an observation, not a conclusion.*

**The cult's objective is confirmed.** The recovered intelligence briefing is, in the DM's words, *"overwhelming evidence that they clearly are planning to do something during the portal ceremony."*

### Risen Runelord Gimmel — RESOLVED as GREED

The cult's on-site leader was **Risen Runelord Gimmel** — **rare**, with the **dream** and **mythic** traits. **Killed by Liu on 2026-07-28.**

**195 HP · AC 30 · Perception +22 · spell attack +22 / DC 30.** Enlarge, Telekinetic Projectile, Disintegrate, **Wall of Stone**. On death he detonated — **Vanishing Death**, DC 27 Will or stupefied 1 for 24 hours (2 on a critical failure).

**He was Greed, not Envy.** Session 1's Recall Knowledge result is now understood to have been a wrong lead:
- Liu's and Galan's identical reads off his gear and polearm, before initiative: *"he is a runelord of greed."*
- His mooks were **greedspawn**.
- His correspondence is signed **"the Rune of Greed."**

**Belimarius is not implicated by anything the party has actually seen.** Treat the Session-1 "Envy" answer as a mis-association, not a clue.

### What a risen runelord is

*(From the lore entry Liu unlocked with his Know-It-All follow-up. **The DM revealed it silently as a Foundry journal — it was not read aloud at the table.** Audio-established facts are only the name, the dream and mythic traits, the "runelord of greed" read, the Rune of Greed signature, and Vanishing Death.)*

- Created by a ritual called **raise runelord**, **invented by Runelord Xanderghul** (Pride).
- **All risen runelords are at least level 11.** Once created they **cannot become more powerful** — they are manifestations of a runelord's legacy frozen at a specific point in time.
- They have a modicum of free will, but are **bound to their creator's desires for as long as that creator lives.** Ordered to do something obviously self-destructive, one might resist — and could potentially break free entirely.
- The ritual draws on a runelord's legacy **as it exists within the Dreamlands**, so **no risen runelord can ever sleep or dream** (hence the dream trait). All are **mythic** and **immune to sleep effects**, regardless of level.
- **Xanderghul can use any risen runelord — including ones he did not create — as a spare body.** If he is slain, his soul instantly jumps to the nearest risen runelord and continues the fight.
- **Lust and Pride** were each only ever held by a single runelord, and both still live — so **no risen runelords of lust or pride can exist** at present.
- **A risen runelord's polearm is part of them and vanishes when they die.** Other items they carry are left behind.

### Locations
- **The Dawnsilver workshop — now fully cleared.** A ruined smithing hall, partially buried by an ancient lava flow. Rooms: the entry hall (freezing cloud); the greedspawn room (a **+1 greater striking dawnsilver greatpick** recovered from the lava); the **forge chamber**, which housed a complex haunt of burning spirits hurling molten dawnsilver; the **common room**, where the Trinity Initiates dug in behind overturned tables under a 15-foot balcony; **two storage rooms**, stripped to dust (a **Greater Skeleton Key** and Dawnsilver oddments); a hallway trapped with an **Endless Reality** mythic hazard; and the **office** — four tables of scrolls, star charts and annotated maps, an incense burner, everlight crystals in the ceiling.

- **The Endless Reality** — a **rank-7 complex magical illusion mythic hazard**, DC 39, covering an entire hallway. Disable options were legendary **Thievery**, legendary **Arcana**, or a **7th-rank Dispel Magic**. No level-12 character has a legendary skill, so it was passable only by spending mythic power. Guar cleared it with a **rank-6 Dispel at mythic proficiency**. Galan's read on what that implies: *"someone who was mythic placed the trap there in the first place."* The DM confirmed it was a good guess.

### The table
**DM: Tom Brister** — professional GM, runs eight Pathfinder campaigns a week, and has run *Revenge of the Runelords* **three times through**. Runs rules-as-written with minimal house rules, high player agency, and a couple of house-adjusted mythic rules. He gives out generous Recall Knowledge results (full trait and save profiles, often the whole stat block on a success).

**Mythic house rules confirmed in play (S2):**
- Clearing a **mythic hazard refills every PC's mythic pool**.
- Returning from the mid-session break grants **+1 Mythic Point**, hard cap **3**.
- **Mythic hazards are designed to require mythic power** — the DM said so explicitly: no level-12 character has a legendary skill, so you auto-fail the skill attempt and must spend a mythic reroll (or mythic-cast the dispel).

**Encounter-mode ruling on pre-buffing (S2):** an encounter begins when **both parties are aware of each other**. *"You're casting in a long, loud, strong voice — so just as you can hear them on the other side of the door, they can hear you."* Exploration rounds are **10 minutes**, so most buffs burn out before a door opens.

**Economy:** treasure sells at **100%**, everything else at **50%**. **Xin-Eurythnia is an 18th-level city** — any common item up to level 18; uncommon and rare negotiable. The DM allows shopping to continue between sessions.

**He offers take-backs on decision-time information.** Twice in S2 he warned a player that an action carried the **manipulate** trait inside a reach threat and offered to rewind it.
