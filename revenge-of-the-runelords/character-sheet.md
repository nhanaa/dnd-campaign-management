### ⚠️ Rune optimization
**Cutting Heaven, Crushing Earth shares the handwraps' runes to the falcata.** Duplicating the same rune on both is wasted gold. Carry **two different types**:
- Falcata → **astral**
- Handwraps → **thundering** (shared to falcata via CHCE)

**Why sonic:** bestiary scan of 7,588 creatures — at level 10+, resistance/immunity rates are **fire 11.7% · cold 9.0% · acid 5.7% · electricity 5.1% · sonic 2.0% · spirit 0.3%**. Shock is a defensible alternative (its crit rider chains to 2 foes with no save), but sonic is resisted ~2.5× less often. You don't need the rune to match enemy weaknesses — **Exploit Vulnerability already converts the enemy's highest weakness into damage on every Strike.**

**ARP note:** fundamental runes (potency/striking/resilient) are automatic. Gold goes to **property runes and items only**.

## The Fist Ruling (the build's load-bearing interaction)

Three rules stack to let you Strike twice, hands full, with full damage:

1. **Implement's Empowerment** — you don't lose it while holding *"a single one-handed weapon, other implements, or esoterica."* Falcata = a one-handed weapon that *is* your implement; tome = another implement. **Both hands legal.**
2. **Fist with hands full** — unarmed attacks let you *"Strike with your fist **or another body part**"* (knee, elbow, headbutt). A fist doesn't occupy a hand and has no free-hand requirement. **Overwhelming Combination works with both hands full.**
3. **Empowerment applies to the unarmed Strike** — it keys off *"When you **Strike** … empower **the Strike**,"* not "weapon Strikes." *"Weapon damage die"* is just the counting mechanism and includes unarmed dice. **Confirmed by community consensus and the Foundry pf2e implementation** (the bug where it *didn't* apply to unarmed was fixed).

Standard optimization guides warn against fist thaumaturges because they *"require a free hand."* **Overwhelming Combination is the recognized exception** — it's a kick/elbow, so the warning doesn't apply. It leans on the *uncommon* Tian Xia archetype, so it's GM-permission territory (granted at this table).

**Overwhelming Combination does NOT double weakness.** It *"combines their damage for the purposes of resistances and weaknesses"* — so mortal weakness *and* personal antithesis each trigger **once** per OC. It's action compression, not a weakness multiplier. (It also means a resistance only bites once — a bonus vs constructs.)
