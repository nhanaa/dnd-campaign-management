## Character notes

**Tinzen** — heavy-armor frontline *caster*, not a pure melee body. Titan Wrestler, Binds That Tie, Flying Tackle, Punishing Shove, Reactive Shield, Shield Block, Guarded Advance. Also has **Fly on Shadowed Wings**. Debuff kit: **Bon Mot, Intimidating Glare, Battle Cry, Terrified Retreat**.

**Kudu** — **+2 Greater Striking Astral Thundering Spirit Thresher** (1d12 advanced flail, sweep, versatile S). Slam Down, Vicious Swing, Lunge, Crashing Slam, **Reactive Strike**, Dragon's Rage Breath/Wings, Terrifying Howl, Unbelievable Interception. Roughly **38 damage per hit** — a peer striker to Liu, not behind him.

**Galan** — a healing engine: Battle Medicine, Continual Recovery, Ward Medic, Treat Condition, Assurance (Medicine), Restorative Channel, Healing Hands, Magic Hands, Fast Recovery. Plus **Wall of Roses** for control. Also runs Fiery Rebirth (same as Liu).

**Psychic** — Oscillating Wave granted spells alternate elements at nearly every rank: fire (Breathe Fire, Blazing Bolt, Fireball, Volcanic Eruption) and cold (Ice Storm, Howling Blizzard, Frozen Fog, Arctic Rift). Covers the party's AoE and ranged needs.

## Synergies

### 1. The stand-up punish (best combo in the party)
Kudu **Slam Downs** → enemy prone → it **Stands** (a move action) → provokes **both** Kudu's **Reactive Strike** and Liu's **Implement's Interruption**. A prone enemy standing up eats two premium reaction Strikes. Happens every time he trips something.

### 2. Share Weakness → Kudu
Liu's mortal weakness rides every one of Kudu's 3d12 Spirit Thresher hits — roughly **+12 damage per turn** to the party's other big striker. Highest-leverage thing Liu does for someone else's sheet.

### 3. Exploit Vulnerability aims the psychic
Oscillating Wave chooses between fire and cold constantly. Liu's round-1 EV reveals the enemy's highest weakness (crit = the full resistance/immunity list). **"Cold 10, not fire"** is worth an entire spell slot to that player — and stops a wasted Fireball on a fire-immune target.

### 4. Fear lowers the grapplers' DCs
Liu's Battle Cry frightened 1 drops the target's Fort/Reflex DC — exactly what Kudu's Slam Down trip and Tinzen's grapples roll against.

### 5. Dense melee line = reliable Retributive Strike
With Tinzen and Kudu both in melee beside Liu, Justice's Retributive Strike **always** has a valid trigger and a reachable target (Nimble Reprisal's Step covers repositioning). Ally gets resistance 14, Liu counter-Strikes.

### 6. Natural flanking
Three melee bodies means Liu and Kudu flank without spending anything — on top of Liu's self-contained CHCE off-guard chain.

## ⚠️ Overlaps to manage

**Battle Cry duplicated (Liu + Tinzen).** Frightened does **not** stack — you take the highest value. If both auto-Demoralize the *same* enemy at initiative, one is wasted.
→ **Fix: agree on a target split before session 1.** Tinzen takes the boss, Liu takes a second threat (or skips and Strikes).

**Intimidating Glare duplicated** (Liu + Tinzen). Harmless.

**Liu is not the party's debuffer.** Tinzen owns that lane more completely — he has Bon Mot *and* Terrified Retreat, which Liu doesn't. Liu's unique lanes are **intel, weakness amplification, arcane scrolls, and reaction punishment**.

**Correct the Story duplicated** (Liu + Tinzen). Fine — two crit-negations per fight is real insurance. **Unrivaled Retort** is the alternative if Liu wants uniqueness.

**Grapple lane is crowded** — Kudu and Tinzen both have Titan Wrestler + Binds That Tie. This is why Liu dropped Sword of Sealing.

**Fire overlap** — Liu's runes should avoid fire; the psychic already owns that element, and fire is the most-resisted damage type (11.7% at level 10+). Astral + thundering keeps Liu's damage in rare types.

## Party gaps

| Gap | Who covers it |
|---|---|
| **Arcane tradition** (party is divine ×2 + occult ×1) | **Liu only** — Scroll Thaumaturgy at class DC 31 |
| **Recall Knowledge / research** | **Liu only** — Sage's Calling + Diverse Lore + Know-It-All + tome |
| Ranged / AoE | Psychic (Oscillating Wave), Tinzen's spells, Kudu's Dragon's Rage Breath |
| Anti-air | Psychic + Tinzen at range; Kudu gets wings at 12, Liu at 14. Earthbind scroll as a nice-to-have |
| Healing | Galan, overwhelmingly |
| Tanking | Tinzen (shield wall) + Liu (aura, AC 34) |

**Net:** the party is a fortress with strong control and healing. Liu's contribution is **not** more tanking or more debuffs — it's the intel that aims everyone else, the weakness multiplier on Kudu, arcane utility nobody else has, and reaction punishment on a dense melee line.
