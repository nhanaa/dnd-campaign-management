# Sevek

**Human (Dragonblood — Sky Dragon) · Monk 13 · Local Savior (Roderic's Cove) · Worshipper of Ashava**

> He was the fist that collected. Now he's the thing standing in front of the people he used to collect from.

---

## Roderic's Cove

A small town on the **Varisian Gulf**, at the mouth of the **Chavali River**. Founded around **4638 AR** by emigrants out of Magnimar under the privateer **Sir Roderic** — so it's under a century old, which in Varisia makes it practically new.

It exists for one reason: it's the port merchants use when they're **too nervous to dock at Riddleport**. That's the town's entire economic proposition, and it's also its entire problem. Riddleport's crime lords don't love a competitor eighty miles up the coast, and a town that small can't stop them reaching into it. So they reach.

The town's principal building is the **Temple of the Stars and Roads**, raised right after the founding. It's primarily **Desna's**, but it holds **a half-dozen side shrines to empyreal lords**, and it's where the town's musicians end up after work — holidays there are music, not sermons.

One of those side shrines is **Ashava's**. That's where Sevek ended up.

---

## What he was first

**Hired muscle.**

Not a criminal exactly — that's how everyone tells it afterward, and it's generous. He was big, he had a knack for standing where he needed to stand, and there was steady work in a port town for someone willing to be leaned on other people's behalf. Collections. Standing behind a man while he explained terms. Making sure a shipment went where the person paying said it went.

Some of the people paying were local. Some of them were **Riddleport's**, one or two hands removed. He knew. Everybody knew. That's how it works in a town whose whole business is being the polite alternative to a pirate harbour.

That's where the sheet comes from:

| | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| **Athletics** (master) | The work. Hauling, holding, and putting people on the ground without killing them. |
| **Warfare Lore** (master) | Years around organized violence — who's armed, who's bluffing, how a crew moves. |
| **Acrobatics** and **Religion** (master) | After. The shrine. |
| **Art Lore** | Growing up in the temple that doubles as the town's music hall. |

The **scales** down his forearms are just ancestry — a sky dragon somewhere back up the line, thinned to almost nothing. In Roderic's Cove it mostly meant people remembered him.

---

## The turn

There wasn't a tragedy. That's the part he can't dress up.

Nobody killed anyone he loved. He didn't get betrayed. He just did the job long enough to watch what it did — a town of maybe a few hundred people slowly learning to be afraid of its own harbour — and one day he was standing behind the wrong man saying the wrong thing to someone who couldn't pay, and he **stopped**.

Then he had to keep stopping, every week, for years, because the work didn't go away and neither did the people doing it.

**That's what "local savior" means for him.** Not a destiny. Not a birthright. A man with a useful body who kept **volunteering**, over and over, in a town where nobody else was going to. The guide's phrasing is exact: *entirely self-made*, and *perhaps failures* along the way.

He is not proud of the first part and he does not hide it. Ask him and he'll tell you plainly, once, without performing the guilt.

## Ashava

> **The True Spark.** Moonlight, dancing, lonely spirits.
> **Edicts:** dance even when there is no music · cast light in places of darkness · lead the lost
> **Anathema:** intentionally mislead someone · desecrate graves · abandon a creature in darkness

He didn't convert. He started sitting at the side shrine because it was quiet and the main hall wasn't, and the woman who kept it didn't ask him what he'd been doing.

**"Lead the lost" is not a metaphor in a port town.** People go into Riddleport and don't come back — debts, crews, girls who took a berth on the wrong ship. Somebody has to go and get them, and for a long time the only person willing was the man who used to know where they'd been taken.

Eventually the town started saying he'd been given something for it. He had. **Guidance**, at will — a small thing, and he uses it constantly, on other people, for nothing.

Three places the faith and the sheet are the same object:

- **"Cast light in places of darkness"** — the Ascended Celestial nimbus is a literal thirty-foot light. Play it as **moonlight**: silver and cool, never gold. Her colours are midnight blue and silver; her symbol is a dancing woman and a moon.
- **"Dance even when there is no music"** — he learned to move in a temple full of musicians. The martial art *is* the dance, and he'd say so without irony.
- **"Intentionally mislead someone" is anathema.** He spent years being the quiet threat behind someone else's lie. **He does not do that anymore, at all.** He never feints, never bluffs, never sets anyone up. Taunt is not a trick — it's an invitation, delivered loudly, and he means every word of it.

---

## Why he stands in front

Same skills, opposite direction. That's the whole character.

- **Taunt** — *look at me instead.* Auditory and visual, not mental, so it lands on things immune to fear.
- **Bodyguard** — he stands over **Mithtirith**. AC 30 holding the party together is not a stable arrangement.
- **Guardian's Intercept** and **Unbelievable Interception** — take the hit that was going somewhere else.
- **Trip, not kill.** Old habit from the collections, now doctrine. He puts things on the ground. Paya finishes them.
- **Guidance**, every round he has the action spare — a stranger's roll goes +1 and he doesn't mention it.

**"Abandon a creature in darkness"** as anathema is a tank's commandment, and he takes it literally as well as figuratively.

## Where he is right now

Sorshen invited him **by name**. Her letter complimented his early work in Roderic's Cove and said she'd like him to help **inspire the people of Xin-Eurythnia** to do the same for their own city — which is either genuine or extremely good flattery, and he hasn't decided.

He came. He'd been in the city several days for the festival, same as every other invited hero, and he hadn't met these four.

Then the ground opened, the queen who invited him went into a fifty-foot pit, and enormous golden-eyed birds came out of it and started eating the people he'd been asked to inspire.

**He was never going to be somewhere else during that.** No introduction needed — the party looks up and there's a man with a staff standing between the birds and the crowd, and afterward he's still there.

**Threads already in play that touch him:**
- **Riddleport** is a name he knows in his bones, and the campaign is set in a world where its reach is a live thing.
- **Ayavah** and the church of **Nocticula** — Nocticula is the patron of exiles and the redeemed. A man who used to work for the wrong people has a very short conversation to have with that faith.
- **Liralarue**, the shared dream, and the mythic destinies all happened before he arrived.

## Playing him

**Register:** plain, level, unhurried. Not a mystic — a **working man** who found a discipline late. He reads a room the way a doorman does: where the exits are, who's armed, who's about to do something stupid.

- Doesn't perform guilt and doesn't hide his history. Answers honestly, briefly, then moves on.
- **Will not lie.** Not a flourish — an anathema. If he can't say something he'll say he can't say it.
- Deflects praise, but not out of modesty. He has a specific and accurate sense of what he's owed.
- Uses **Guidance** on people constantly and never points it out.

**With the party:**
- **Mithtirith** (Cleric of Nethys) — his charge, decided unilaterally, and he'd find being thanked for it strange.
- **Paya** (Leshy Fighter, Medic) — the other body in the front rank. Sevek trips, Paya kills. A work partnership, and they'll both like it that way.
- **Aureys** (Dragonet Rogue) — the only other one with dragon in them, which Sevek finds funny given his own version amounted to scales and good balance.

**Anathema in play:** never mislead anyone · never desecrate a grave · never abandon a creature in darkness. The middle one will bite in a campaign made of Thassilonian tombs. Let it.
