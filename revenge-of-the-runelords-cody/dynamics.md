## Combat Lanes

- **Sevek — frontline controller/tank.** The Trip lock (Flurry of Maneuvers → Stand Still on the stand), Taunt at 120 ft, highest AC (37/39), permanent flight. **Bodyguard is assigned to Mithtirith** — the enemy magnet. Kill pressure is real (Krenlith, Warleader A, Gavergash) but the job is making enemies waste actions: Gavergash spent 2 of his 3 Mythic Points standing up from Sevek's Trips. Side lanes: master **Religion** (the party's actual Religion source — Mithtirith is only trained), at-will Guidance, and daily-prep logistics (party-wide water breathing, Lucky Number, Life Connection). **S7 additions**: the **grapple-and-Taunt-the-caster turn** (Tumble Through, Constricting Whip Tail grab, Strike, Taunt — Maddenmist put Polar Ray + jaws into Sevek instead of Paya; the DC 5 flat check to cast while grabbed is the weak link, he passed it twice); **Taunt + Prevailing Position** as the actual tank (Baolen after Taunt: ten Strikes, two hits, none on Sevek); Trip works underwater at this table; **Feet to Fins** (wand, rank 6) for water days. Level 15 picks: **Third Path to Perfection on Will** (legendary), Canny Acumen (Reflex). Staff is now **+3** (attack +29, Trip +32). Every one of Baolen's three mythic points answered a Sevek action. **S8 corrections, and they matter:** (1) **Taunt only works on creatures** — Group Taunt did nothing to the Rune Sentry hazard, and range was never the issue. (2) **Taunt is not an aggro lock against a boss willing to pay for it** — with Taunt live, Czaresia strode *past* the frontline to Wide Swing Kreebnock and Mithtirith for 55 and 45, eating the off-guard (Cody, out loud: *"this'll just keep going at the two spellcasters here"*). What Taunt actually buys is the −1 and the off-guard, and **the off-guard is what feeds the flanking math**. (3) **Prevailing Position is the reaction that pays** — it erased a devotee crit worth ~39 and downgraded Czaresia's crit to a normal hit. **The mitigation is in the reactions, not the aggro.** S8 also confirmed two critical Trips off **Rippling Spin** (DC 41 by 11 and by 10) and a plain Trip on a rune giant that cost her a mythic point to stand.
- **Paya — the executioner.** Vicious Swing crit machine and designated kill-securer: both natbakhs, three troll warleaders *in one turn*, plus S4's mukradi/Siktempora B/Krune. Legendary weapon proficiency at 14. Also the **secondary healer** — four leveling Kineticist channels (Dash of Herbs, Ocean's Balm, Fresh Produce, Deflecting Wave as mitigation) plus Medic: "Apparently I'm only going to play this AP as a healer." **S7**: five Vicious Swing crits over 100 (108 / 110 / 106 / 120 / 110), the Maddenmist kill, three Reactive Strikes into spellcasting (never disrupted — *"One of these days I will interrupt. Not apparently today"*), and, with Mithtirith out, the whole healing lane: Treat Wounds, Fresh Produce every break, Unusual Treatment (natural 1, mythic reroll, natural 1 — then a DC-40 crit one fight later). Warshard rune set to **Greater Flaming** this session. Bought **21 lesser Sea Touch Elixirs**.
- **Aureys — and her reaction is the real weapon.** S6 receipts: **Opportune Backstab landed four kills in one session** (cave worm, two pridespawn sentinels, Thybidos), and in the finale she went **four-for-four on it, all four critical hits — 340 of her 383 damage came off a reaction.** Her fan's persistent bleed + fire riders killed a fifth sentinel outright and dropped Angothane the first time. Practical consequence: **whoever swings before her should expect to be setting up a crit**, and her damage ceiling is gated by how often allies connect, not by her own turns.
- **Aureys — debuff engine and skill monkey.** Feint (Distracting), Demoralize, Majestic Presence + Frightening Power (two separate fear auras — Dragonblood and Draconic Acolyte), Enfeebled-on-hit debilitations; fan/claw Overwhelming Combinations hit like a striker (109/117/122 in S5, 145 record in S4). ## Known Enemy Pattern

**Everything hunts the healer.** S4: every fight converged on Mithtirith. S5: the natbakhs' screeches caught him twice, the troll warleaders strode past the frontline for him ("No, they're high-level trolls. They know to get the healer" — Paya), Chain Lightning arced to him first. **S6: unchanged and getting worse** — a pridespawn sentinel took two Strides *around* Paya's reach on round 1 to crit him for 61, a second flanked him for a 69 crit, and he took 389 in the finale alone, more than anyone all session.

Party counters, now three-deep: Sevek's **Bodyguard + Taunt + Stand Still** interception; **Life Connection** (a wand-prepped reaction that lets Sevek eat a hit aimed at Mithtirith — it absorbed 74 in S6 and is worth prepping every morning); and Mithtirith's own best answer, which is **leaving the ground** — he flew 20 ft straight up out of a sentinel's reach and was untouchable there. **Sevek's read after S6: Bodyguard and Taunt only work if he's standing inside the cone; positioning is the real fix, and Mithtirith's vertical solution is better than his.**

**S7, with no healer to hunt: the body out front takes it.** Paya took ~630 across six fights — the whale's jaws crit (72) into a Restrained grapple, Maddenmist's 70 crit off a Shared Feast bite, Frigid Flurry 61, two of Baolen's three landed crits (54, 80). Sevek's Taunt redirected the rest: the kraken callers put twelve tridents into AC 38–40 across two fights and landed one; Baolen after Taunt went two for ten. **Sevek spent Prevailing Position four times** — it is the second half of the tank.

**Cody warns before he punishes.** Mid-finale, unprompted: *"They've got Mythic Ferocity… I try to warn folks about boss tunnel vision. I've had a lot of PCs nearly die because the minions are liquidating them while they're attacking the boss with full health."* The party switched to the minions and it was correct. **Take his tactical asides at face value — they are not flavor.**

## Mythic Economy (table-observed)

- MP refunds: killing blow +2 / others +1 on mythic enemies; hazard completion refunds similarly — and a **mythic hazard refunds everyone to full** (S7, Zutha's Legacy).
- Enemy MP are spent on the party's control: Gavergash burned 2 of 3 standing from Trips (S5); **Baolen burned all 3 on Sevek** — two to stand, one to survive an Electric Arc (S7).
- Everyone leans on mythic-proficiency rerolls for out-of-lane skill checks — it's the only way untrained checks function at DC 40+.
- Cody rules generously on player-side mythic creativity (S4's daily Summon Mythic Power reset; S5's Draconic Salvation on a plain fail) but enemies get the same toys: Mythic Ferocity on all Risen, Mythic Resilience on the natbakhs, Remove Condition/Recharge on Gavergash.
- **S8 bits.** **Kreebnock's** first night, and he arrived with a voice: *"You can call me Kreebnock, or Kree, if you prefer. I've been called both"*; *"Oh, no retirement. Just wandering. My kind of retirement"*; *"Cool. I want one of those"* (a spectral peacock the size of the dais); *"Well, it all started with a dream"* (to a rune giant demanding to know who they were); and he **narrates his magic in fiction constantly** — *"I will speak a little poem," "I've got multicolored energy flowing all around me now," "I'm going to come up and form a comically large hammer and swing down at it," "he just suddenly appears forty feet away."* **Sevek** shortened him to **"Kree"** on the spot (*"because I feel like that makes us closer… the real reason is I just have trouble pronouncing longer names"*) and Paya adopted it a minute later. ⚠️ **Whisper renders "Kreebnock" as "Creep knock"** — do not let a future recap canonize "Creep" as a nickname; it was never said.
- **S8, Aureys**: the parley (*"my companions and I are heroes of these lands. You may have heard of me"* → *"I am a dragon after all"*), and the line that won it — *"there is probably wonderful, delicious stuff, and maybe a few undead treats inside. Snacks, if you will."* Grovel continues every fight: *"you awesome, silent, whatever you are, creepy green asshole — I am so afraid"*; *"but I'm so weak and insignificant"*; and the best one, *"You know, you're so awesome! But not as good as my pineapple."* On Kandirvek policing the armies: *"if you have to kill a few to keep the rest in line, yeah, you do what you gotta do, guy."*
- **S8, Paya**: *"Trying to stop Doomsday by starting a war seems counterproductive"* (his call, and he proposed the white flag); the pineapple-knight reveal played straight; *"Thank your local bard, folks"* → *"to be fair, it was a poem"* → *"it was an oratory, but it's still a performance"*; and the warmest line of the session, healing Aureys — ***"Well, you're still my favorite dragon, don't tell Kandirvek."***
