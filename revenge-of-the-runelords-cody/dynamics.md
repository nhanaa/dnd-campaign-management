Every one of Baolen's three mythic points answered a Sevek action. **S8 corrections, and they matter:** (1) **Taunt only works on creatures** — Group Taunt did nothing to the Rune Sentry hazard, and range was never the issue. (2) **Taunt is not an aggro lock against a boss willing to pay for it** — with Taunt live, Czaresia strode *past* the frontline to Wide Swing Kreebnock and Mithtirith for 55 and 45, eating the off-guard (Cody, out loud: *"this'll just keep going at the two spellcasters here"*). What Taunt actually buys is the −1 and the off-guard, and **the off-guard is what feeds the flanking math**. (3) **Prevailing Position is the reaction that pays** — it erased a devotee crit worth ~39 and downgraded Czaresia's crit to a normal hit. **The mitigation is in the reactions, not the aggro.** S8 also confirmed two critical Trips off **Rippling Spin** (DC 41 by 11 and by 10) and a plain Trip on a rune giant that cost her a mythic point to stand.

  **S9 — the clearest data yet on what Taunt is actually for.** Three fights, and the damage-taken numbers do not support the aggro-sink model: **288 in the Warsworn fight (most of anyone), 236 against the Tolokands (third, behind Mithtirith's 388 and Paya's 341), and zero against Haphrama.** Nine party-wide **Desiccates** ignored Taunt entirely — only five hand Strikes all fight were even Taunt-eligible. **What Taunt buys is the −1 and the off-guard, and the off-guard is the product**: it fed four Paya crits and three Aureys crits, and Kreebnock's round-one Murder of Crows crit landed by *exactly* the margin Taunt's off-guard provided. Against a caster group, **Taunt is a to-hit debuff and a flanking enabler, not a damage sink**; the mitigation lane there is Prevailing Position and spacing.

Cody caught it live once (*"that is going to crit because taunt penalty does not apply to you"*). **Get the automation fixed — the log currently understates every enemy roll against Sevek by 1 and makes Prevailing Position look weaker than it is.**

## Known Enemy Pattern

**Everything hunts the healer.** S4: every fight converged on Mithtirith. S5: the natbakhs' screeches caught him twice, the troll warleaders strode past the frontline for him ("No, they're high-level trolls. They know to get the healer" — Paya), Chain Lightning arced to him first. **S6: unchanged and getting worse** — a pridespawn sentinel took two Strides *around* Paya's reach on round 1 to crit him for 61, a second flanked him for a 69 crit, and he took 389 in the finale alone, more than anyone all session.

Party counters, now three-deep: Sevek's **Bodyguard + Taunt + Stand Still** interception; **Life Connection** (a wand-prepped reaction that lets Sevek eat a hit aimed at Mithtirith — it absorbed 74 in S6 and is worth prepping every morning); and Mithtirith's own best answer, which is **leaving the ground** — he flew 20 ft straight up out of a sentinel's reach and was untouchable there. **Sevek's read after S6: Bodyguard and Taunt only work if he's standing inside the cone; positioning is the real fix, and Mithtirith's vertical solution is better than his.**

**S7, with no healer to hunt: the body out front takes it.** Paya took ~630 across six fights — the whale's jaws crit (72) into a Restrained grapple, Maddenmist's 70 crit off a Shared Feast bite, Frigid Flurry 61, two of Baolen's three landed crits (54, 80). Sevek's Taunt redirected the rest: the kraken callers put twelve tridents into AC 38–40 across two fights and landed one; Baolen after Taunt went two for ten. **Sevek spent Prevailing Position four times** — it is the second half of the tank.

**Cody warns before he punishes.** Mid-finale, unprompted: *"They've got Mythic Ferocity… I try to warn folks about boss tunnel vision. I've had a lot of PCs nearly die because the minions are liquidating them while they're attacking the boss with full health."* The party switched to the minions and it was correct. **Take his tactical asides at face value — they are not flavor.**
