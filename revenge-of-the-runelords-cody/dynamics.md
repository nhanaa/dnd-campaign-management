## Combat Lanes

- **Sevek — frontline controller/tank.** The Trip lock (Flurry of Maneuvers → Stand Still on the stand), Taunt at 120 ft, highest AC (37/39), permanent flight. **Bodyguard is assigned to Mithtirith** — the enemy magnet. Kill pressure is real (Krenlith, Warleader A, Gavergash) but the job is making enemies waste actions: Gavergash spent 2 of his 3 Mythic Points standing up from Sevek's Trips. Side lanes: master **Religion** (the party's actual Religion source — Mithtirith is only trained), at-will Guidance, and daily-prep logistics (party-wide water breathing, Lucky Number, Life Connection).
- **Paya — the executioner.** Vicious Swing crit machine and designated kill-securer: both natbakhs, three troll warleaders *in one turn*, plus S4's mukradi/Siktempora B/Krune. Legendary weapon proficiency at 14. Also the **secondary healer** — four leveling Kineticist channels (Dash of Herbs, Ocean's Balm, Fresh Produce, Deflecting Wave as mitigation) plus Medic: "Apparently I'm only going to play this AP as a healer."
- **Aureys — debuff engine and skill monkey.** Feint (Distracting), Demoralize, Majestic Presence + Frightening Power (two separate fear auras — Dragonblood and Draconic Acolyte), Enfeebled-on-hit debilitations; fan/claw Overwhelming Combinations hit like a striker (109/117/122 in S5, 145 record in S4). Master Thievery/Deception/Intimidation/Performance; rolls **Performance for initiative** (Battle Cry). New Living Nexus kit: Absorb Magic reaction economy + Aether Beam + the Black Pearl Aeon Stone reflect.
- **Mithtirith — heal battery, knowledge lane, and now the arcane answer.** 99–108 point two-action Heals, Fast Channel (3-action Heal in two), Scholarly Recollection on every Recall Knowledge; master Arcana/Nature/Medicine/Crafting + Sandpoint Lore. Buff suite: Mystic Armor, Energy Aegis, Tailwind, Conductive Weapon, Protection. The S4 "Runelord campaign with no arcane caster" gap is becoming his job: **Hedge Mage dedication + Wand of Wall of Force + Wand of Howling Blizzard + the retrieval belt.**

## Known Enemy Pattern

**Everything hunts the healer.** S4: every fight converged on Mithtirith. S5: the natbakhs' screeches caught him twice, the troll warleaders strode past the frontline for him ("No, they're high-level trolls. They know to get the healer" — Paya), Chain Lightning arced to him first. Party counters: Sevek's Bodyguard + Taunt + Stand Still interception; Mithtirith positioning at max Heal range.

## Mythic Economy (table-observed)

- MP refunds: killing blow +2 / others +1 on mythic enemies; hazard completion refunds similarly.
- Everyone leans on mythic-proficiency rerolls for out-of-lane skill checks — it's the only way untrained checks function at DC 40+.
- Cody rules generously on player-side mythic creativity (S4's daily Summon Mythic Power reset; S5's Draconic Salvation on a plain fail) but enemies get the same toys: Mythic Ferocity on all Risen, Mythic Resilience on the natbakhs, Remove Condition/Recharge on Gavergash.
