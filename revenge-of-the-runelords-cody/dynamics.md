## Combat Lanes

- **Sevek — frontline controller/tank.** The Trip lock (Flurry of Maneuvers → Stand Still on the stand), Taunt at 120 ft, highest AC (37/39), permanent flight. **Bodyguard is assigned to Mithtirith** — the enemy magnet. Kill pressure is real (Krenlith, Warleader A, Gavergash) but the job is making enemies waste actions: Gavergash spent 2 of his 3 Mythic Points standing up from Sevek's Trips. Side lanes: master **Religion** (the party's actual Religion source — Mithtirith is only trained), at-will Guidance, and daily-prep logistics (party-wide water breathing, Lucky Number, Life Connection).
- **Paya — the executioner.** Vicious Swing crit machine and designated kill-securer: both natbakhs, three troll warleaders *in one turn*, plus S4's mukradi/Siktempora B/Krune. Legendary weapon proficiency at 14. Also the **secondary healer** — four leveling Kineticist channels (Dash of Herbs, Ocean's Balm, Fresh Produce, Deflecting Wave as mitigation) plus Medic: "Apparently I'm only going to play this AP as a healer."
- **Aureys — and her reaction is the real weapon.** S6 receipts: **Opportune Backstab landed four kills in one session** (cave worm, two pridespawn sentinels, Thybidos), and in the finale she went **four-for-four on it, all four critical hits — 340 of her 383 damage came off a reaction.** Her fan's persistent bleed + fire riders killed a fifth sentinel outright and dropped Angothane the first time. Practical consequence: **whoever swings before her should expect to be setting up a crit**, and her damage ceiling is gated by how often allies connect, not by her own turns.
- **Aureys — debuff engine and skill monkey.** Feint (Distracting), Demoralize, Majestic Presence + Frightening Power (two separate fear auras — Dragonblood and Draconic Acolyte), Enfeebled-on-hit debilitations; fan/claw Overwhelming Combinations hit like a striker (109/117/122 in S5, 145 record in S4). Master Thievery/Deception/Intimidation/Performance; rolls **Performance for initiative** (Battle Cry). New Living Nexus kit: Absorb Magic reaction economy + Aether Beam + the Black Pearl Aeon Stone reflect.
- **Mithtirith — heal battery, knowledge lane, and now the arcane answer.** 99–108 point two-action Heals, Fast Channel (3-action Heal in two), Scholarly Recollection on every Recall Knowledge; master Arcana/Nature/Medicine/Crafting + Sandpoint Lore. Buff suite: Mystic Armor, Energy Aegis, Tailwind, Conductive Weapon, Protection. The S4 "Runelord campaign with no arcane caster" gap is becoming his job: **Hedge Mage dedication + Wand of Wall of Force + Wand of Howling Blizzard + the retrieval belt.**

## Known Enemy Pattern

**Everything hunts the healer.** S4: every fight converged on Mithtirith. S5: the natbakhs' screeches caught him twice, the troll warleaders strode past the frontline for him ("No, they're high-level trolls. They know to get the healer" — Paya), Chain Lightning arced to him first. **S6: unchanged and getting worse** — a pridespawn sentinel took two Strides *around* Paya's reach on round 1 to crit him for 61, a second flanked him for a 69 crit, and he took 389 in the finale alone, more than anyone all session.

Party counters, now three-deep: Sevek's **Bodyguard + Taunt + Stand Still** interception; **Life Connection** (a wand-prepped reaction that lets Sevek eat a hit aimed at Mithtirith — it absorbed 74 in S6 and is worth prepping every morning); and Mithtirith's own best answer, which is **leaving the ground** — he flew 20 ft straight up out of a sentinel's reach and was untouchable there. **Sevek's read after S6: Bodyguard and Taunt only work if he's standing inside the cone; positioning is the real fix, and Mithtirith's vertical solution is better than his.**

**Cody warns before he punishes.** Mid-finale, unprompted: *"They've got Mythic Ferocity… I try to warn folks about boss tunnel vision. I've had a lot of PCs nearly die because the minions are liquidating them while they're attacking the boss with full health."* The party switched to the minions and it was correct. **Take his tactical asides at face value — they are not flavor.**

## Relationships & Running Bits

- **Aureys ↔ Kandirvek**: "We're like a buddy team, you and I. Two dragons." / "Fighting against the world." / "Makes the dream work." She hailed him as "Brother"/"kinsmen" in S4, begged his most expensive portable treasure out of him in S5, and had already retrained into Draconic Acolyte *before learning what kind of dragon he was*.
- **Aureys and property**: certified stuff acquirer (S4), eats monster bones, searches every corpse. The S5 arc: claimed the Troll King's throne mid-combat ("I'm on your fucking throne, man"), danced on Gavergash's body, and took his mythic **Hollow Robes** on etymological grounds — "I am technically Aureys Hollow." Paya notarizes her claims: "They were named for her. He was just holding on to them for her. Much like the chair."
- **Aureys' one serious register**: the refugees. Asked if they made it: "They are my brothers." **S6 repeat**: she boasts to four hostile omen dragons ("I am Aureys Hollow, greatest of the dragons of this world") and then, when asked what became of their kin, **refuses to lie** — *"I could tell you a lie, but you would know that it was a lie."* That honesty is what got the party past them. She closed the scene straight, no bit: *"We of course seek no harm, but we feel we must proceed."*
- **Aureys ↔ Mithtirith**: after he shut down the Shattered Runewell, she turned to him and said the kindest line of S6 — *"I think that made sense to me, given your god."* (Nethys.)
- **Paya's bits**: pineapple jokes he did not start but fully owns ("I am so plant in this moment"; "crispy pineapple"; "Is Paya trying to call himself a snack?"), "my lobster's too buttery" (recurring, unexplained — S6: *"my lobster remains perfectly buttery"*), seasoning monster bones so Aureys gets the cooking buff, and a stated aversion to "strange geological phenomenons called pits" (Sandpoint flashbacks). S6 additions: *"a pineapple can dream"*, *"like a pineapple with my head cut off"* (a triple-Stride turn), *"it looks like Godzilla, but it's legally distinct"* (Howling Blizzard's template), and *"Love from Sandpoint, friend"* called after a Runelord who burned his last mythic point on an escape and went nowhere.

- **The Nightmare rune is a strategic weapon, not a damage rider.** S6: Paya's reactive-strike crit stapled **Stupefied** on Thybidos in round 2. It disrupted his casting three separate times, and **every disruption cost Cody a mythic point to fix** — so when Thybidos hit zero he had nothing left for Mythic Ferocity and stayed dead. Angothane, never stupefied, spent all three points surviving a death. **Landing Stupefied on a mythic caster early is worth more than the damage.**

- **Mithtirith's register**: driest deadpan at the table ("Can I be selfish for a round?"; "That was their worst save too, guys"; "You know she's going to sit there for an entire day to become the king, right?"). S6 additions: *"I come from a long line of people thwarting Runelord plans, so welcome"* (to two Risen Runelords, one second after Sevek's "You talk too much"); *"Was Mithtirith stunned three by the mirror, or by the fact that Paya missed twice?"*; and, after Angothane Disintegrated his Wall of Force by Recognizing the Spell, *"I just had to see if they actually bothered to get that skill feat in."* Mask collector (S4). ## Mythic Economy (table-observed)

- MP refunds: killing blow +2 / others +1 on mythic enemies; hazard completion refunds similarly.
- Everyone leans on mythic-proficiency rerolls for out-of-lane skill checks — it's the only way untrained checks function at DC 40+.
- Cody rules generously on player-side mythic creativity (S4's daily Summon Mythic Power reset; S5's Draconic Salvation on a plain fail) but enemies get the same toys: Mythic Ferocity on all Risen, Mythic Resilience on the natbakhs, Remove Condition/Recharge on Gavergash.
