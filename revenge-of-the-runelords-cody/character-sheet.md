## How it plays

**The engine is Trip + crit blind, and S4–S7 priced it.** At **+35** Trip, crits land on anything within 15 of the DC — and every crit now carries a **Fort DC 39** blind (Celestial Armaments) on top of prone (staff crit spec). Paya's two-hander can't maneuver, Aureys converts everyone's debuffs into crits — prone is the debuff that feeds *all* melee, and it works on the mental-immune.

- **Flurry of Maneuvers** — two Trips for one action at MAP 0 / −5
- **Bullying Staff** — Trip or Shove up to Gargantuan
- **Rippling Spin** — reaction: hit in melee → Step + free Trip or Disarm
- **Stand Still** — reaction: movement in reach eats a Strike; **crit disrupts** (disrupted the mukradi's Stand in S4, and two Stand Still crits on trolls in S5)

> **The lock:** Trip → they must Stand (or fight prone at −2) → Stand Still fires → crit puts them back down. Tripping a flier knocks it out of the sky. At 16 a crit Trip also threatens **blind**, which is off-guard to everyone + DC 11 flat check to target anything.

**Taunt converts AC 42 into party-wide mitigation, from 120 feet.** S7 receipts: after Taunt went up on Baolen, the kraken landed two of ten Strikes (five at Sevek, none hit). Auditory/visual, not mental — works on the fear-immune.

**Reach 10 + Elf Step + fly 70 + Flyby** covers positioning that used to cost whole turns.
