# Revenge of the Runelords (Cody) — World & Storyline

> Table canon only — built from what happened at the table (sessions 4–7; sessions 1–3 predate Pax joining and are summarized from in-game references). No AP-book knowledge. Updated after each session.
> **Name correction (S7)**: what S6 recorded as "Emperor Jinn" is **First King Xin** — Thassilon's founder; the DM said "First King Xin" plainly in S7 and Whisper had been mangling it. Same root for the **Isle of Xin**.

**Frame**: Saga Lands / Kodar Mountains, one year after the other three PCs' adventures in Sandpoint. The party carries **mythic power** — "that spark of the war god Gorum that crashed down on Golarion one year ago" (Kandirvek) — tied to the fall of the **Trinity Star**.

---

## The Story So Far

### Before Pax joined (sessions 1–3, reconstructed from table references)
- A year ago, Paya, Aureys, and Mithtirith **delved the pit at Sandpoint** — where they met and defeated someone who *claimed* to be a Runelord ("left her at the bottom of the pit, gladder for it, once the real one appeared in our dreams" — Paya). The Sandpoint Devil "was a thing."
- The **Trinity Star** fell from the sky into the Kodars around the same time, carrying the **Warshard of Gorum** — the source of the party's mythic sparks. Paya has a **Warshard rune** infused into his halberd.
- The party traveled to **Xin-Eurythnia**, Queen **Sorshen's** high-Kodar city, invited by **Ayavah** for the unveiling of the **Circle of Open Hands** portal. Sevek was invited by Sorshen's own letter.
- They fought and destroyed the Risen Runelord **Gimmel** (per Krune: "I expect you're the reason that waste of skin Gimmel hasn't reported back"). Aureys claims to have eaten part of him.

### Session 4 — The Flight of the Eurythnians (2026-08-01)
- The portal unveiling ended in catastrophe: a magical shockwave, earthquakes, a **sinkhole that swallowed the portal and Sorshen herself**. Xin-Eurythnia (pop. ~12,500, ~1/5 dead immediately) could not be saved.
- Seven days of evacuation prep (interrupted by a **pyroclastic mukradi** from the sinkhole), then a ten-day trek south with ~10,000 refugees: an avalanche, the naiad **Svevenka** turned from hazard to gift-giver, Risen saboteurs, a night ambush by four **misery siktemporas**, stone giant bandits (the Kodar Kings), and **hundred-foot watchers** with red eyes motionless on the mountaintops.
- At the trek's end, two **Risen Runelords of Sloth — Krune and Krenlith —** attacked on mirage dragon mounts. Both burned Mythic Ferocity; both died anyway (Paya ran Krune through; Sevek's bo staff finished Krenlith). **Kandirvek**, an omen dragon, killed the third runelord and its mount off-screen and joined the fight: "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?"

### Session 5 — The Hollow Throne (2026-08-08)
- The refugees reached **Vekr's Crossing** with **98/100 Survival Points** — Xin-Eurythnia's culture will survive; talk of eventual return is popular. Ayavah stays with them as a standing ally.
- Krune's **retrieval belt** yielded a mythic **Wand of Never Mind**, a **crystal ball**, and an engraved **star chart** of the Trinity Star's flight path — which shows where it landed. Whoever engraved it could follow it too.
- At his lair, Kandirvek showed the party **four prophetic visions** on his wings (see Mysteries). The fourth was cut off by a **regal man in peacock-feather robes** who *looked back through the vision* and psychically spiked the dragon.
- Kandirvek flew the party three days to the **Throne of the Troll King** (fighting two mythic **natbakhs** en route). The party killed the throne's mythic occupant **Gavergash** and his four warleaders.
- The throne proved to be an **illusion capping an 800-foot pit**. The descent triggered **A Falling Star's Memories** — the Trinity Star's voyage through space, relived. At the bottom: **Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul**, thought lost at Earthfall — with a fresh 250-foot crater of iron shards and **glowing footprints walking east out of the wreckage**.
### Session 6 — The Garden of Pride (2026-08-15) — **BOOK ONE COMPLETE**
- Following the footprints east, the party met **four omen dragons** studying the ruins — **Shephodian**, **Ithariel**, **Nobukis**, **Yvinsbrel** ⚠️. They were kin to the mirage dragons the party killed in S4. Aureys admitted it outright rather than lie; Shephodian: *"I see in your eyes you don't deceive."* They stood down and gave up two facts: **the Warshard of Gorum is no longer in this city**, and **a woman came weeks earlier** (human, tall, black robes, staff, blonde) who went east and never re-emerged, seeming to vanish into thin air.
- A **cave worm** and three **shulns** ambushed the party at her abandoned campsite. Her journals identify her as **Aethusa** — a modern wizard who *took* the name of a Runelord of Gluttony, believing dreams from a **cloaked, smoke-like figure** (the same being from the party's own apocalypse dream) were memories of that past life. She tracked the Trinity Star exactly as the party did; **the guardians expected her and the trolls did not stand in her way.** Her last entry: a dream of meeting Xanderghul and being promised secret knowledge, waking too early. Then nothing.
- Past five **pridespawn sentinels** in peacock robes stood a quicksilver portal guarded by the **Mirror of Glory**, a mythic hazard projecting Xanderghul's contempt. Disabled after six successes; yielded the **Paradigm Cube**.
- **The four visions inside the portal** (each narrated by a PC's mythic future self) explain the whole book — see Mysteries.
- Beyond the portal: **the Garden of Pride** and the **Shattered Runewell of Pride** — Kandirvek's fourth vision, arrived at last. Two **Risen Runelords of Wrath, Angothane and Thybidos**, died there (Aureys took Thybidos, Paya took Angothane), and the Runewell was shut down by Mithtirith's third success.
- **Book One complete. Level 15.** The garden's gemstone throne is unlooted; next session opens there.

### Session 7 — The Eater of History (2026-08-22) — **BOOK TWO BEGINS** *(Mithtirith absent)*
- The peacock throne is a **memory engine**. Sitting in it replays Xanderghul's last year: Paya saw him lost in **the heart of an immense eclipse** before the Warshard tore him free and hurled him into Xin-Cyrusian; Aureys saw his frustration at wearing Aethusa and his plan to forge a new body; Sevek saw the **Risen-making ritual** and the decision to relocate it to the **Emerald Chambers**; Aureys (again) saw the binding — **Xanderghul's body and the Risen's are magically tied: slain, he transfers into one of them.** *"So long as more Runelords are around, Xanderghul cannot be killed."* The throne is also **paired with the Throne of the Troll King** — sit in one and envision the other to teleport.
- Back topside, **Kandirvek** pointed them at the **Isle of Xin** (west of Varisia) and its sunken temple, which may hold the plans to the Chambers, then used an ally's **teleportation circle to Absalom** for a day of selling and shopping (**8,514 gp each**). On the flight west he said plainly that he is level 15, the party has passed him, and *"in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon"* — research and contacts from here.
- The islet's altar was **Zutha's Legacy**, a mythic haunt wearing the Runelord of Gluttony's face (disabled by Performance, mostly Aureys'). Beneath it, the flooded temple: a **clockwork whale**, a workshop of **sedacthy** (fish-folk) chiseling tablets under their crimson caster **Maddenmist**, an **elemental tsunami**, a clam-grotto vault with the mutant octopus **Quoroc** and two more sedacthy, and in a 200-ft octagon beneath a missing colossus, the mythic kraken **Baolen, Eater of History** — *"And so I found the ones who slaughtered my loyal subjects… Your history comes to an end here."* All dead; Aureys killed six of nine. **Level 16.** Baolen's chamber is unsearched and holds "info about where you're going next."

### Session 8 — The Trial of Runes (2026-08-29) — **the Emerald Chambers found and entered** *(Kreebnock joins)*
- Baolen's chamber held **nine mural alcoves**, a gallery of Runelord landmarks: the **Black Squares of Ungevik** (Zutha's necromantic engine, still beneath the sea off Varisia), **Sekhnavali** the Hanging Monastery, the **Storval Stairs** (Karzoug), the **Emerald Chambers** themselves, the obelisk forest of **Mundatei**, the **Shining Queen** (today the **Sunken Queen** — Sorshen's pyramid in the eastern Mushfens, whose carving resembles Xin-Eurythnia), **Rimeskull** (which once bore First King Xin's face), the **Dome Hive**, and **Azaradne's Gardens** (third Runelord of Sloth). In the Emerald Chambers alcove Aureys found the **Emerald Eye** — a live blueprint of the Chambers that grants Sense Direction inside them. **Without it the Chambers are unnavigable.**
- Absalom: the Sunken Temple sell-off (**4,788 gp each**), and Kandirvek introduced **Kreebnock**, a mythic gnome ally. Studying the Eye overnight gave up the entrance: the **Velashu Uplands**.
- At the dais, **three armies in a standoff**: Velashu Horselords under **Nood Helarsin**, a Shoanti tribe under **Roaring Storm**, and — the new development — an army of **Runelord Belimarius** under **General Lograsi**. Paya proposed the white flag; Aureys ran the parley and won a truce. Lograsi: *"My lady Belimarius has no care for the Runelord of Pride… I say we allow them the chance to at least deal with those heads. All the better for us."* Kandirvek expects the truce to collapse the moment the party is underground.
- **The Trial of Runes.** Seven eight-foot carved Runelord heads circling the dais. Mithtirith presented the party's mythic power as proof of a right to rule; then six heads asked one question each. The party's frame, Mithtirith's: **play to each Runelord's sin.** They went **five for six** — Kaliphesta (Gluttony), Envy, Greed, **Xirie** (Sloth), and **Sorshen** (Lust) all satisfied; **Alderpash** (Wrath) rejected them. Sevek raised the objection Cody later ruled on (*"it says exclusively to enemies"*) and the table answered past it. The seventh head, **Xanderghul's**, refused to ask anything: *"Nothing you say will suffice, intruders. The only answer I'll accept is your death."*
- **The answers were the encounter's difficulty dial.** Xanderghul's head split into a **peacock phoenix** (his Peacock Spirit alter ego); the five satisfied heads crashed to the ground and only **Alderpash's** stayed up as an active **Rune Sentry**. The hazard gets one action per surviving head — the party faced **one Lightning Arc a round instead of six.** Cody, mid-fight: *"Imagine this fight if you had gotten them all wrong."*
- The phoenix died to Aureys (188 of its 400 HP were hers). The sentry was an **object** — hardness 28, immune to crits — and was **disabled**, not killed, by Mithtirith's third legendary Arcana check at DC 45, with 10 HP left on it. A staircase then manifested beneath the dais.
- **Into the Emerald Chambers**: infinite branching emerald corridors. Five **Silent Devotees** ambushed the party a day in; all five died. A further day's travel reached **a magnificent emerald door inscribed with the seven-sided Star of Thassilon**, and through it the **Crypt of Runes**.
- **Czaresia**, a rune giant covered in the rune of pride and serving as the crypt's guardian, is **dead** (Aureys). **Six of the crypt's seven doors stand ajar; the seventh, directly north, is sealed in glowing runes.** Party remains **level 16**; no XP was announced.

### Session 9 — Seven Doors, Seven Sins (2026-09-05) — **the Crypt of Pride opened**
- **The lock.** The north door carries **all seven Thassilonian runes overlaid and circulating**, each a barrier; nothing teleports or bypasses it. Mithtirith's legendary Arcana found the exploit: the runes hold each other up, so **the more you remove, the more unstable the enchantment becomes — you don't need all seven.** The number was unknown going in. **Four sufficed.** Compass: **N pride, NE envy, E gluttony, SE greed, SW sloth, W lust, NW wrath**; order irrelevant. The central statue and the southern mural **both depict Xin**, and the mural holds nothing else (S8 thread closed).
- **Wrath (NW).** Five looted mausoleums — Alaznist, Alderpash, Angathane, Ziren, Thibodos. The spectral upper torso of **Runelord Alaznist** screamed the party onto **the Field of Ruin**: a bloody battlefield under a lightning-shot sunset, five pristine white mausoleums on a distant hill, and three **Warsworn** rising from the dead. After the fight Alaznist's ghost screamed one word — ***"Xanderghul!"*** — then *"You will pay for this,"* and shattered into fire with the rest of the Runelords of Wrath. **Rune removed.**
- **Sloth (SW).** A daunting staircase spiralling hundreds of feet into darkness that **goes nowhere** — it dumps climbers back on the floor; people at this table have fallen for it before. Aureys refused to climb it *because stairs are not a slothful thing to build*, and a thorough search found a **false wall** running south. Behind it, seven looted mausoleums and the **seven Runelords of Sloth** — ⚠️ *Ziriae, Illithrius, Azaradne, Zalilat, Krenleth, Iravina, Kroon* — floating overhead, too bored to fight. **Zalilat**: *"Can you not see? You've been beaten to the theft. That fiend Xanderghul's already made off with everything."* **Mithtirith won the crypt with one sentence** — *"But wouldn't you want someone to do the work for you to get your revenge?"* — and Paya closed it. They removed their own rune and dismissed the party. **No combat, no resources spent.**
- **Lust (W).** A pillared hall of carnal murals. From the western mural stepped an ethereal **Queen Sorshen**, haggard, *"as though she's been through some kind of ordeal"* — and she **recognised them**: ***"At last I've reached you. Look, as you already know, Xanderghul has returned, and I believe he intends to destroy Golarion, starting with the Saga Lands. Find me in Jandelay, and I can—"*** Gray smoke cut her off, showed **a ruined cityscape** of crumbling buildings and broken boulevards, and disgorged three **Jandelay Tolokands**. Afterward the party noted **there are no mausoleums in the Lust crypt — Sorshen never died.** **Rune removed.**
- **Envy (NE) — scouted and refused.** Ten statues of the Runelords of Envy (⚠️ *Napht, Tannaris, Ivamura, Jura, Chasarda, Esedria, Zar, Dissimilia, Faradni*), with **Belimarius** standing apart at the east end holding an ornate halberd. The whole corridor is a **9th-rank magic-item-stealing hazard**; disabling it needs Arcana, Athletics, Thievery or Dispel Magic **on all ten statues**, any failure triggering it. Sevek argued the party out of it on resources and the table followed. **The hazard is mapped and still untriggered.**
- **Greed (SE).** Seven mausoleums spilling coins and gems — **all of it illusory**; Aureys crit-saved a Fortitude check and got a vision of herself as *"a somewhat tacky obsidian statue,"* one save from being turned into treasure. A brass plaque in **Azlanti** (Mithtirith is the only reader) says ***Abundance Everlasting*** and lists the Runelords of Greed: ⚠️ *Kaldurne, Fethrir, Gimel, Lignia, Mazerana, Aethusa,* and **Haphrama**. Behind a hidden door Sevek found: **Karzoug's tomb**, and a negotiation instead of a fight (see NPCs). Price paid: the **Paradigm Cube**. **Rune removed.**
- **The Crypt of Pride.** After a full 8-hour rest at the foot of Xin's statue, the party opened the north door onto a hall 80 ft wide and nearly twice as long, 50-ft ceiling, twin marble colonnades, **two stairwells down on the west wall and a passage east**, and a giant-scale fresco on the north wall. Waiting: three **Pridespawn Cultists** and **Risen Runelord Haphrama**. *"However you got down here, it seems I'm to make sure you leave in pieces."* Two cultists died; **Haphrama Translocated out at 94% HP having dealt zero damage**, and the last cultist left with him. **Both are warning the others.** Party remains **level 16**; no XP announced.

### Session 10 — The Cult's House (2026-09-12) — **the Crypt of Pride is Xanderghul's headquarters**
- **The Crypt of Pride is not a tomb — it is where the cult lives.** Off the great hall: a **study** (maps, notes, bookshelves, a lectern), an **octagonal statue chamber**, a **balcony/pentagonal chamber**, **six identical bedrooms** with made beds, a **workbench heaped with treasure**, an **alchemical lab**, and a **vat room**. The party cleared all of it in one push, no rest taken.
- **The study.** A tall **lectern** whose gemstone inlay forms **a blazing comet with three tails** *(undiscussed at the table — the Trinity Star had three tails)*. It held an **Aeon Stone (Amplifying)** and a **folio containing a planar displacement formula**. The bookshelves held arcane theory, a collection of treatises, and **"Thrice Born Souls"** — a wizard's spellbook belonging to **Aethusa**, with a heavily enchanted dagger as a bookmark. Neither the book nor the dagger has been read or identified.
- **Vogorond, and two Risen Runelords.** A cracked statue of a **feminine figure with feathered wings in place of arms** and **a crown of flowers**, on a plinth with an altar slab at her feet, animated as a **Vogorond**. Two rounds in, **Risen Runelords Tannaris and Ivamura** appeared on a balcony thirty feet up. Tannaris: *"Well, if it isn't the roaches who have been making a mess of the crypts."* All three died. ⚠️ **Cody named Tannaris and Ivamura Runelords of *Sloth* on-mic** — but the S9 Envy-hall statue list (audio-only, flagged) also contained "Tannaris" and "Ivamura". Foundry confirms the spellings; the *sin* is now in conflict. **The statue's identity was never established** and the chamber was empty afterward.
- **The hidden northeast wall.** Aureys found it, Sevek assisting: **"illusion on illusion on enchantments,"** expertly hidden, *"the work of not just a master, but a legend among this kind of magic."* It **cannot be brute-forced or disabled** — it requires **a specific formula**. Mithtirith's Arcana and Kreebnock's Occultism both failed. **Kreebnock connected the planar displacement folio to it**; the party never went back to test it.
- **The research — Xanderghul's objective, stated plainly for the first time.** Ten minutes with the cult's papers: Xanderghul and his cultists have **adopted the Crypt of Pride as their new headquarters**; by robbing the other Runelords' crypts he gathered the objects needed to create **many of** the Risen; **most of the cult is currently elsewhere**; and his goal is **to destroy Golarion and use the energy released in its destruction to become a god.** Cody: there is *"much more to learn."* The party was interrupted and has not returned.
- **Haphrama came back and died.** He returned with **Risen Runelord Xirie** and three Pridespawn while the party was mid-research. He raised a **Prismatic Wall** to split them; **Mithtirith dispelled it** with mythic casting (three actions, one Mythic Point, 41+4=45). **Xirie then Translocated out and abandoned him mid-fight** — *"Xirie, you wretch!"* — having taken 10 damage all fight. **Aureys killed him.** He never spent a point on Mythic Ferocity.
- **The portal window.** In a chamber above the stairs, a **window 30 feet in diameter** onto an expanse of wispy multicolored mist, out of which **gaunt sunken faces with glowing eyes and mouths** watch *"as if daring you to enter."* An unidentifiable dimension — **Kreebnock and Mithtirith both failed to identify it.** Not entered.
- **The alchemical lab.** An **orichalcum cauldron nearly 10 feet across** (immensely valuable, nearly immovable) boiled over into **two Chromatic Oozes**. Both died.
- **The vat room — and the S6 vision.** Behind the last door: **four steel vats** burbling with foul liquid, work tables of surgical tools, anatomical drawings, and jars of eyeballs and strips of flesh. **This is the room from Aureys' Session 6 portal-vision** — Aethusa unconscious amid magical apparatuses and four immense steel vats. **Nobody at the table made the connection out loud.** Waiting there: **Xirie**, two Pridespawn, and **Risen Runelord Kaliphesta** of Gluttony, who snapped her fingers and boiled the vats over into *"twisted amalgamations of flesh and bone."* ⚠️ **Both Xirie and Kaliphesta were Trial of Runes heads the party *satisfied* in S8** — the ones they talked past are the ones now showing up in person.
- **Session ended mid-scene, before initiative.** Next session opens on that fight, **at level 17**; Cody expects to finish the Crypt of Runes in it. No XP announced. The campaign is **past its halfway point**.

### Session 11 — The Emerald Prison (2026-09-19) — **THE CRYPT OF RUNES IS COMPLETE · party reaches level 17**
- **The vat-room fight ran 105 minutes.** Kaliphesta, Xirie, two Pridespawn Cultists and four **Unrisen** (300 HP, AC 33) that climbed out of the vats. The Unrisen have **Rise Again** — killed by anything but fire, they return at **100 HP** on their own turn; two of them did. **Kaliphesta spent Mythic Ferocity and came back at 200 HP**, so she had to be killed twice (581 damage against a 400-point bar). **Xirie never used hers and took exactly 400.** Paya killed Kaliphesta; **Sevek killed Xirie** five minutes later. Both Risen were **Trial of Runes heads the party had satisfied in S8**.
- ⚠️ **Kaliphesta's answer to a guardian was to delete him.** She cast **Quandary** on Sevek and removed him from the battlefield for five minutes of real time. He got **one escape check per turn, needing two successes**, passed the first, and never got another turn inside — the spell only broke when Paya killed her. **Against a Runelord, the counter to a frontline tank is removal, not damage.**
- **The library, unguarded — the single biggest lore dump of the campaign.** With the floor finally clear, Aureys called for the party to go back up and research the hidden wall. Cody required **no checks at all**. What they learned:
  - **Xanderghul no longer possesses Aethusa.** He has **created a new body for himself and is mortal once again** — but **his soul can inhabit Aethusa or any of the Risen Runelords if he dies.**
  - **Aethusa is alive and imprisoned** in a specially created prison elsewhere in the Chambers, guarded by creatures called **Shining Children** bound to the runes, plus *"other security which is not mentioned."*
  - **The Doomsday Doors connect to Jandelay**, and **one has already been opened beneath Xin-Eurythnia.** See Mysteries for the full location list.
  - **The Ashen Man** orchestrated Xanderghul's return — see Threats.
  - **Lirallaru is imprisoned and findable** — see Mysteries.
  - **The incantation to undo the illusions on the hidden northeast door.**
  - **Xanderghul's deployment plans for opening the doors** — enough that the party *"might be able to intercept many of the Risen of the Trinity Star before they have the chance to open those doors,"* but only by **calling upon several allies** to cover the regions in time.
- **The party rested a full night**, undisturbed, then opened the emerald door: **Mithtirith** read the incantation on **Arcana at mythic proficiency** (natural 1, his own reroll, 44) with **Kreebnock aiding on Occultism**. Magical backlash, **no save** — 33 mental to Mithtirith, 25 to Kreebnock.
- **Behind it: a chamber of pure emerald**, a 30-foot ceiling, a central inner sanctum under an **iridescent sphere** — and **Karzoug**, *"the last Runelord of greed."* ⚠️ **Whether this is the same Karzoug who cut the party their 10% deal in S9 was never established on-mic.** He summoned **three Shining Children** and immediately put **195 points of his own Cataclysms into them** across the fight.
- **Karzoug escaped rather than died.** Sevek grappled and Tripped him (costing a Mythic Point to break), **Stand Still critically hit for 82 on the Stand**, and Sevek's Flurry took him to **33 HP (6%)**. **Mithtirith's Needle Darts crit on a natural 20** landed next and Cody answered it: *"This has been most entertaining, and thank you all for relieving me of that monotony. But I've places to be."* Where every other Risen **vanishes into light**, Karzoug **melted into prismatic ink**. ⚠️ **His true exit HP is unrecorded** — the crit's damage was never applied in Foundry.
- **Aethusa fell out of the shattering sphere.** Recovered alive. See NPCs.
- **Absalom.** The party teleported out (carrying the gargantuan mace and the orichalcum cauldron), ran **the first sell-off since S7**, and distributed **20,344 gp each**. **Level 17.**

### Session 12 — A Dream of Cyrusian (2026-09-26) — **into the Dreamlands, and on to Leng**
- **Three days of research in Absalom** with Aethusa (lucid now, still unfit to fight), on *Xanderghul's Immortality*. What the party learned:
  - **Aethusa and the Risen Runelords are Xanderghul's soul cages.** As long as one exists, he can make more with ease.
  - Aethusa **no longer sleeps**, and neither do the Risen: the link runs through the **Dreamlands** (a dimension formed from the dreams of the Sleepers). The Risen were woven from energy siphoned from the Dimension of Dreams. Aethusa is still a living person, so to use her as a cage **Xanderghul made a dream simulacrum of her**. Destroying it would sever his link to her and *"perhaps show you all how to prevent the Runelord from creating more duplicates."*
  - **Aethusa's physical body can be used as a focus** for Interplanar Teleport or planar displacement, landing the caster where her simulacrum is.
  - Capping the research turned up a **scroll of Interplanar Teleport** from the Crypt of Runes haul. It may also give *"bonuses when dealing with ethereal or dreamlike creatures in the Dreamlands."*
- **Doomsday Doors, via allies** (Kandirvek, Ayavah and the Church of Nocticula, others across the Saga Lands and Varisia):
  - A door was **closed in old Xin-Edasseril**. The Risen tried to open it on **Belimarius'** turf and *"she didn't take too kindly to it."* She is still not an ally; she intends to claim much of the Saga Lands.
  - Doors at **Windsong Abbey** and **Sekhnavali** are **secured**.
  - Per Cody, the doors are *"at least most of them, not an immediate concern"*; the primary concern is stopping Xanderghul jumping bodies.
- **Cyrusian.** Mithtirith teleported the party to a Dreamlands village near the simulacrum. The old Lord Mayor, **Simon Cradle**, is a librarian from Boston on Earth who dreams his way there. The new "Lord Mayor" was **Dream Aethusa**, riding an **ancient Mirage Dragon** and collecting tribute at sunset. Aureys stole Simon's typewriter.
- **Dream Aethusa and the dragon died** (both to Paya). Dream Aethusa's body became **the Trinity Star**, a three-tailed comet hazard, which the party disabled by pulling its tails.
- **Lirallaru called to them**: *"Find me. I'm here. Leng. The Amaranthine Oubliette. Come find me, please. It's Lirallaru. I can help you."*
- **Simon's method**: because the party is in the Dreamlands **physically**, they can **dream themselves to a person they know**. They fall asleep focused on that person and place, all focusing on the same thing, and only one of them needs to succeed; a failure can be retried. Sevek's Religion check was the only success.
- **Leng, and the Amaranthine Oubliette.** Inside the first chamber, three **Elder Shantaks** died. Then a **horse-headed keeper** stepped out: *"I know who you've come to seek."* Session ended there. **Level 17**, no XP announced.

## House Rules & Table Facts

- **Objects and hardness (S8)**: hardness applies to the **whole damage packet**, not per damage type — so elemental riders (flaming, shock, Jolt Coil) buy nothing against a hard object. Objects are **immune to critical hits and precision damage**. ⚠️ Cody applied this **inconsistently in one fight**: he doubled a natural-20 Blinding Foam against the crit-immune Rune Sentry at 14:48 (the only reason 16 damage got through), then correctly enforced crit immunity on Sevek's natural 20 seven minutes later. Worth pinning down.
- **Taunt requires a creature (S8)** — it does nothing to a hazard, no matter the range. Group Taunt on the Rune Sentry was stopped before an action was spent.
- **Pall of Silence went unenforced (S8)** — both Sevek and Kreebnock cast spells inside the Silent Devotees' aura. Kreebnock caught his own, after the fact. Cody let both stand: *"I didn't catch it, you guys didn't catch it, it's how it is."* **Unsettled going forward.**
- PF2e Remastered + **Mythic (War of Immortals)**, Free Archetype, Ancestry Paragon, **no ARP, no GAB** (see character-sheet.md).
- **Mythic Point economy**: killing blow on a mythic enemy = +2 MP to the killer, +1 to everyone else; completed hazards refund similarly (best contributor +2). Mythic-proficiency rerolls can rescue *untrained* skill checks — the party's emergency key.
- Cody opened **Impossible Magic** content table-wide at level 14 (rebuilds allowed with notice: Mithtirith → Hedge Mage; Aureys → Living Nexus + Draconic Acolyte).
- Item access for the level-14 shopping trip: items up to party level. **Absalom (S7) is a level-20 settlement** — anything up to 20; retroactive purchases allowed until the next session.
- **Mythic hazards refund all mythic points on completion** (S7, Zutha's Legacy) — not just +2/+1.
- **Trip works underwater at this table** (*"Vanilla rules, no. But my rules, you can"*) with the size extension (Titan Wrestler / Bullying Staff). A creature that *is* water (the elemental tsunami) can't be knocked prone.
- **The Phantasmal Doorknob's blind is not automatic** — Cody rules a **Fortitude save against Class DC**, with no Foundry automation; the player has to ask for it every time.
- **Research subsystem (S12)**: one check per PC per day in a fixed order, from a list of skills with Perception always allowed at the highest DC; points unlock thresholds, and a critical success is worth extra. Mythic-proficiency rerolls apply. **Research critical successes refunded a Mythic Point to Mithtirith and Kreebnock** as table rulings (their callings).
- **Interplanar Teleport needs no planar key at this table (S12)**, per Cody. Uncommon spells are fine to learn; **rare needs a check-in**. **Uncommon or rare items count as higher level** for settlement access: the rare Bracers of Hammers counts as level 27 in level-20 Absalom, so they can be tracked down with time but not auto-bought.
- **Dream travel (S12)**: physical visitors to the Dreamlands can travel by falling asleep focused on a person they know. It's a group check (a magical-tradition skill or Perception) where one success carries everyone, and a failure can be retried.
