# Revenge of the Runelords (Cody) — World & Storyline

> Table canon only — built from what happened at the table (sessions 4–7; sessions 1–3 predate Pax joining and are summarized from in-game references). No AP-book knowledge. Updated after each session.
> **Name correction (S7)**: what S6 recorded as "Emperor Jinn" is **First King Xin** — Thassilon's founder; the DM said "First King Xin" plainly in S7 and Whisper had been mangling it. Same root for the **Isle of Xin**.

**Frame**: Saga Lands / Kodar Mountains, one year after the other three PCs' adventures in Sandpoint. The party carries **mythic power** — "that spark of the war god Gorum that crashed down on Golarion one year ago" (Kandirvek) — tied to the fall of the **Trinity Star**.

## The Story So Far

### Before Pax joined (sessions 1–3, reconstructed from table references)
- A year ago, Paya, Aureys, and Mithtirith **delved the pit at Sandpoint** — where they met and defeated someone who *claimed* to be a Runelord ("left her at the bottom of the pit, gladder for it, once the real one appeared in our dreams" — Paya). The Sandpoint Devil "was a thing."
- The **Trinity Star** fell from the sky into the Kodars around the same time, carrying the **Warshard of Gorum** — the source of the party's mythic sparks. Paya has a **Warshard rune** infused into his halberd.
- The party traveled to **Xin-Eurythnia**, Queen **Sorshen's** high-Kodar city, invited by **Ayavah** for the unveiling of the **Circle of Open Hands** portal. Sevek was invited by Sorshen's own letter.
- They fought and destroyed the Risen Runelord **Gimmel** (per Krune: "I expect you're the reason that waste of skin Gimmel hasn't reported back"). Aureys claims to have eaten part of him.

### Session 4 — The Flight of the Eurythnians (2026-08-01)
- The portal unveiling ended in catastrophe: a magical shockwave, earthquakes, a **sinkhole that swallowed the portal and Sorshen herself**. Xin-Eurythnia (pop. ~12,500, ~1/5 dead immediately) could not be saved.
- Seven days of evacuation prep (interrupted by a **pyroclastic mukradi** from the sinkhole), then a ten-day trek south with ~10,000 refugees: an avalanche, the naiad **Svevenka** turned from hazard to gift-giver, Risen saboteurs, a night ambush by four **misery siktemporas**, stone giant bandits (the Kodar Kings), and **hundred-foot watchers** with red eyes motionless on the mountaintops.
- At the trek's end, two **Risen Runelords of Sloth — Krune and Krenlith —** attacked on mirage dragon mounts. Both burned Mythic Ferocity; both died anyway (Paya ran Krune through; Sevek's bo staff finished Krenlith). **Kandirvek**, an omen dragon, killed the third runelord and its mount off-screen and joined the fight: "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?"

### Session 5 — The Hollow Throne (2026-08-08)
- The refugees reached **Vekr's Crossing** with **98/100 Survival Points** — Xin-Eurythnia's culture will survive; talk of eventual return is popular. Ayavah stays with them as a standing ally.
- Krune's **retrieval belt** yielded a mythic **Wand of Never Mind**, a **crystal ball**, and an engraved **star chart** of the Trinity Star's flight path — which shows where it landed. Whoever engraved it could follow it too.
- At his lair, Kandirvek showed the party **four prophetic visions** on his wings (see Mysteries). The fourth was cut off by a **regal man in peacock-feather robes** who *looked back through the vision* and psychically spiked the dragon.
- Kandirvek flew the party three days to the **Throne of the Troll King** (fighting two mythic **natbakhs** en route). The party killed the throne's mythic occupant **Gavergash** and his four warleaders.
- The throne proved to be an **illusion capping an 800-foot pit**. The descent triggered **A Falling Star's Memories** — the Trinity Star's voyage through space, relived. At the bottom: **Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul**, thought lost at Earthfall — with a fresh 250-foot crater of iron shards and **glowing footprints walking east out of the wreckage**.
### Session 6 — The Garden of Pride (2026-08-15) — **BOOK ONE COMPLETE**
- Following the footprints east, the party met **four omen dragons** studying the ruins — **Shephodian**, **Ithariel**, **Nobukis**, **Yvinsbrel** ⚠️. They were kin to the mirage dragons the party killed in S4. Aureys admitted it outright rather than lie; Shephodian: *"I see in your eyes you don't deceive."* They stood down and gave up two facts: **the Warshard of Gorum is no longer in this city**, and **a woman came weeks earlier** (human, tall, black robes, staff, blonde) who went east and never re-emerged, seeming to vanish into thin air.
- A **cave worm** and three **shulns** ambushed the party at her abandoned campsite. Her journals identify her as **Aethusa** — a modern wizard who *took* the name of a Runelord of Gluttony, believing dreams from a **cloaked, smoke-like figure** (the same being from the party's own apocalypse dream) were memories of that past life. She tracked the Trinity Star exactly as the party did; **the guardians expected her and the trolls did not stand in her way.** Her last entry: a dream of meeting Xanderghul and being promised secret knowledge, waking too early. Then nothing.
- Past five **pridespawn sentinels** in peacock robes stood a quicksilver portal guarded by the **Mirror of Glory**, a mythic hazard projecting Xanderghul's contempt. Disabled after six successes; yielded the **Paradigm Cube**.
- **The four visions inside the portal** (each narrated by a PC's mythic future self) explain the whole book — see Mysteries.
- Beyond the portal: **the Garden of Pride** and the **Shattered Runewell of Pride** — Kandirvek's fourth vision, arrived at last. Two **Risen Runelords of Wrath, Angothane and Thybidos**, died there (Aureys took Thybidos, Paya took Angothane), and the Runewell was shut down by Mithtirith's third success.
- **Book One complete. Level 15.** The garden's gemstone throne is unlooted; next session opens there.

### Session 7 — The Eater of History (2026-08-22) — **BOOK TWO BEGINS** *(Mithtirith absent)*
- The peacock throne is a **memory engine**. Sitting in it replays Xanderghul's last year: Paya saw him lost in **the heart of an immense eclipse** before the Warshard tore him free and hurled him into Xin-Cyrusian; Aureys saw his frustration at wearing Aethusa and his plan to forge a new body; Sevek saw the **Risen-making ritual** and the decision to relocate it to the **Emerald Chambers**; Aureys (again) saw the binding — **Xanderghul's body and the Risen's are magically tied: slain, he transfers into one of them.** *"So long as more Runelords are around, Xanderghul cannot be killed."* The throne is also **paired with the Throne of the Troll King** — sit in one and envision the other to teleport.
- Back topside, **Kandirvek** pointed them at the **Isle of Xin** (west of Varisia) and its sunken temple, which may hold the plans to the Chambers, then used an ally's **teleportation circle to Absalom** for a day of selling and shopping (**8,514 gp each**). On the flight west he said plainly that he is level 15, the party has passed him, and *"in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon"* — research and contacts from here.
- The islet's altar was **Zutha's Legacy**, a mythic haunt wearing the Runelord of Gluttony's face (disabled by Performance, mostly Aureys'). Beneath it, the flooded temple: a **clockwork whale**, a workshop of **sedacthy** (fish-folk) chiseling tablets under their crimson caster **Maddenmist**, an **elemental tsunami**, a clam-grotto vault with the mutant octopus **Quoroc** and two more sedacthy, and in a 200-ft octagon beneath a missing colossus, the mythic kraken **Baolen, Eater of History** — *"And so I found the ones who slaughtered my loyal subjects… Your history comes to an end here."* All dead; Aureys killed six of nine. **Level 16.** Baolen's chamber is unsearched and holds "info about where you're going next."

### Session 8 — The Trial of Runes (2026-08-29) — **the Emerald Chambers found and entered** *(Kreebnock joins)*
- Baolen's chamber held **nine mural alcoves**, a gallery of Runelord landmarks: the **Black Squares of Ungevik** (Zutha's necromantic engine, still beneath the sea off Varisia), **Sekhnavali** the Hanging Monastery, the **Storval Stairs** (Karzoug), the **Emerald Chambers** themselves, the obelisk forest of **Mundatei**, the **Shining Queen** (today the **Sunken Queen** — Sorshen's pyramid in the eastern Mushfens, whose carving resembles Xin-Eurythnia), **Rimeskull** (which once bore First King Xin's face), the **Dome Hive**, and **Azaradne's Gardens** (third Runelord of Sloth). In the Emerald Chambers alcove Aureys found the **Emerald Eye** — a live blueprint of the Chambers that grants Sense Direction inside them. **Without it the Chambers are unnavigable.**
- Absalom: the Sunken Temple sell-off (**4,788 gp each**), and Kandirvek introduced **Kreebnock**, a mythic gnome ally. Studying the Eye overnight gave up the entrance: the **Velashu Uplands**.
- At the dais, **three armies in a standoff**: Velashu Horselords under **Nood Helarsin**, a Shoanti tribe under **Roaring Storm**, and — the new development — an army of **Runelord Belimarius** under **General Lograsi**. Paya proposed the white flag; Aureys ran the parley and won a truce. Lograsi: *"My lady Belimarius has no care for the Runelord of Pride… I say we allow them the chance to at least deal with those heads. All the better for us."* Kandirvek expects the truce to collapse the moment the party is underground.
- **The Trial of Runes.** Seven eight-foot carved Runelord heads circling the dais. Mithtirith presented the party's mythic power as proof of a right to rule; then six heads asked one question each. The party's frame, Mithtirith's: **play to each Runelord's sin.** They went **five for six** — Kaliphesta (Gluttony), Envy, Greed, **Xirie** (Sloth), and **Sorshen** (Lust) all satisfied; **Alderpash** (Wrath) rejected them. Sevek raised the objection Cody later ruled on (*"it says exclusively to enemies"*) and the table answered past it. The seventh head, **Xanderghul's**, refused to ask anything: *"Nothing you say will suffice, intruders. The only answer I'll accept is your death."*
- **The answers were the encounter's difficulty dial.** Xanderghul's head split into a **peacock phoenix** (his Peacock Spirit alter ego); the five satisfied heads crashed to the ground and only **Alderpash's** stayed up as an active **Rune Sentry**. The hazard gets one action per surviving head — the party faced **one Lightning Arc a round instead of six.** Cody, mid-fight: *"Imagine this fight if you had gotten them all wrong."*
- The phoenix died to Aureys (188 of its 400 HP were hers). The sentry was an **object** — hardness 28, immune to crits — and was **disabled**, not killed, by Mithtirith's third legendary Arcana check at DC 45, with 10 HP left on it. A staircase then manifested beneath the dais.
- **Into the Emerald Chambers**: infinite branching emerald corridors. Five **Silent Devotees** ambushed the party a day in; all five died. A further day's travel reached **a magnificent emerald door inscribed with the seven-sided Star of Thassilon**, and through it the **Crypt of Runes**.
- **Czaresia**, a rune giant covered in the rune of pride and serving as the crypt's guardian, is **dead** (Aureys). **Six of the crypt's seven doors stand ajar; the seventh, directly north, is sealed in glowing runes.** Party remains **level 16**; no XP was announced.

## NPCs

- **Nood Helarsin** (S8) — war-leader of the **Velashu Horselords**; mid-thirties, battle-scarred face, auburn hair, several weapons at his side, rides in on horseback. Agreed to the truce with visible reluctance and nodded to the party rather than to Lograsi: *"If you've come for that area, by all means. But know, we will be watching."*
- **Roaring Storm** (S8) — **Shoanti** war-leader; a woman with a deep scowl and bird tattoos across her neck and the sides of her head. The first to connect the party's arrival to the heads (*"I don't suppose this has anything to do with the heads that have suddenly appeared in the sky"*), and she promised Lograsi an arrow in the throat before he ever proposed the truce. **She never audibly agreed to it.**
- **General Lograsi** (S8) — commands **Runelord Belimarius'** army in the Velashu Uplands. **Azlanti**, not Thassilonian: well-groomed beard, striking black leather armor, almost-glowing violet eyes. Sensed the party's mythic power on sight. Proposed the truce purely to let the party clear the heads for him — *"I say we allow them the chance to at least deal with those heads, yes? All the better for us."* Smiling, patient, and obviously using everyone in the room.
- **Czaresia** (S8) — a female **rune giant**, guardian of the **Crypt of Runes** and self-declared *"servant of Runelord Xanderghul."* Burning red eyes, a mace *"so large it may as well be a scepter, even by her standards,"* and the **rune of pride inscribed over and over** on every inch of exposed skin. Met Paya's honesty with *"your honesty would be charming if it wasn't so foolish."* **Dead** (S8, Aureys). Cody ran her at **600 HP — 1.6× the book.**
- **Kandirvek** — **omen dragon** (corrected from S4's "blue"), scholar, centuries in the Kodars, Cody's favorite NPC. Dreams potential futures (dragonkind lost perfect foresight when Aroden died). Dreamt of the party repeatedly; allied with them against the Risen. Gave Aureys the Black Pearl Aeon Stone. "Two dragons. Fighting against the world." **S7: level 15, and he knows it** — *"I fear in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon. But I'll aid in what ways I can, whether it be through research or contacts."* He has an ally with a teleportation circle to Absalom, flew the party to the Isle of Xin, and is patrolling above it so nothing follows them down.
- **Baolen, "Eater of History"** — a **mythic kraken** (level 18) lairing in the sunken temple under the Isle of Xin; the sedacthy were "my loyal subjects." Knew the party was coming (*"just as the Eater of History foretold"*) and claimed to sense *"the destinies within you, the history, the paths to come."* **Dead** (S7, Aureys) after burning Undying Myth. What he hoarded and what he "ate" are unknown — his chamber is unsearched.
- **Maddenmist** — crimson-scaled sedacthy speaker with emerald eyes, 8th-rank arcane caster (Desiccate, Polar Ray, Frigid Flurry, Disintegrate). Ran a chisel-workshop of broken Thassilonian tablets in the temple. **Dead** (S7, Paya). Dropped a Greater Staff of Arcane Might and Bands of Force.
- **Quoroc** — a giant mutant octopus (level 15) in the clam vault, venom that escalates to paralysis. **Dead** (S7, Aureys).
- **Ayavah** — High Priestess of Nocticula; invited the party to Xin-Eurythnia and feels responsible. Now running the refugee effort at Vekr's Crossing. Standing offer: "If you ever have need of an ally in the Saga Lands, the Kodars, or just anywhere, you can call on me."
- **Sorshen** — Queen of Xin-Eurythnia, dragged into the sinkhole at the festival. **No word since.** Knew who Sevek was before he arrived (invited him by name).
- **Svevenka** — naiad of the Fen of the Ice Mists (S4); turned ally after Paya's critical parley; gifted the tidal crossbow (since sold) and Wand of Howling Blizzard (Mithtirith). Warned: "Something is wrong out in the Kodars."
- **Aethusa** — a modern wizard who took the name of a **Runelord of Gluttony**, believing dreams sent by the cloaked smoke-figure were memories of that past life. Tracked the Trinity Star to Xin-Cyrusian and camped there. **Xanderghul's ghost passed through her own wards and possessed her**; she raised the Risen from looted Runelord bones on his behalf; he then abandoned her for a fresh body and had her carried away unconscious past **four immense steel vats**. Her fate is unknown and nobody has said she is dead. *Not to be confused with the historical Runelord whose name she took.*
- **Shephodian** ⚠️, with **Ithariel**, **Nobukis**, and **Yvinsbrel** ⚠️ — four **omen dragons** who have studied the Xin-Cyrusian ruins for several months. Kin to the mirage dragons the party destroyed in S4; they let the party pass specifically because Aureys refused to lie about it. Not allies, but not enemies: *"Though I hold no love of you and your friends for what has happened to our kin, we also understand that is the nature of the business."*

## Threats

- **Runelord Belimarius** — **now an active player, not a footnote (S8).** Besides Sorshen, the **only surviving true Runelord** prior to the Risen. She has never gotten along with Sorshen, is undisputedly the weaker of the two, and **covets Sorshen's domain** — and with Sorshen missing, Cody's read is that she *"might have some confidence."* She has **an army in the field in the Velashu Uplands** under General Lograsi, camped around the entrance to the Emerald Chambers, professing no interest in the Runelord of Pride. Kandirvek expects the three-way truce to collapse into open war the moment the party is underground.
- **The Trial of Runes** (S8) — the entrance test to the Emerald Chambers. Seven eight-foot carved Runelord heads circle the dais; they demand *"proof of your right to rule through your command of rune magic,"* then ask **one philosophical question per head**. The correct frame is to **play to each Runelord's sin**. ⚠️ **The answers are the encounter's difficulty dial** — every head satisfied by a correct answer crashes to the ground; every head left unsatisfied stays airborne as an active **Rune Sentry**, and the resulting complex hazard gets **one action per surviving head** (Lightning Arc, 3d12+19, DC 42 basic Reflex, random target), with unused arcs healing the peacock phoenix 20 each. The party went **5 of 6** and faced one arc a round instead of six. Cody: *"Imagine this fight if you had gotten them all wrong."* The seventh head, **Xanderghul's**, refuses to ask a question at all and attacks regardless.
- **The peacock phoenix** — an enormous spectral peacock, *"a being of pure magic,"* manifesting from Xanderghul's cracked-open head. It is his **alter ego as the Peacock Spirit**, mythic and incorporeal. Cody ran it at 400 HP with **no Rebirth** (the book's second-phase resurrection). **Destroyed** (S8, Aureys).
- **Silent Devotees** — an order of martial spellcasters founded when the Runelords first built the Emerald Chambers, to guard **Xin's tomb**. They **cut out their own eyes and tongues** so knowledge of the Chambers could never spread beyond the order. Ice-pale skin, pits of emerald green for eyes, barefoot, leaving no sound or mark on the tile. *Transdimensional Step* (30-ft teleport that never provokes), *Pall of Silence* (10-ft aura suppressing auditory actions and all non-subtle spellcasting), *Cruel Sneak Attack* (+3d6 against anyone off-guard **or merely taking persistent bleed**), and **+2 greater striking wounding frost longswords** that apply bleed on every hit — a self-sustaining loop. Five killed (S8); Cody ran them at PL+0, 325 HP each.
- **The Risen of the Trinity Star** — **now explained (S6): they are manufactured.** Aethusa raised them by ritual from **the bones and burial raiments of real Runelords**, looted out of the tomb of **First King Xin**, Xanderghul's "beloved friend." The party watched **Gimmel** rise from a pile of dusty bones in a vision. Destroyed so far: **Gimmel**, **Krune**, **Krenlith**, one unnamed (eaten mid-air), and the Runelords of Wrath **Angothane** and **Thybidos** (S6). All have **Mythic Ferocity** (they die twice) and **Vanishing Death** (body and polearm vanish on death). **S7, from the throne**: they are *"not the real Runelords — far too weak for that,"* but carry their memories and personalities; they are Xanderghul's own ritual, fueled by the belongings and magic items of the Runelords they imitate; they are unfalteringly loyal; and **Xanderghul's body is magically tied to theirs — if he is slain he transfers into one.** With the Emerald Chambers' materials he can make many, some stronger than Angothane and Thybidos. **Kill order: the Risen first.**
- **Zutha's Legacy** — a mythic haunt (level 18) wearing the face of **Zutha, last Runelord of Gluttony**, bound to the altar on the Isle of Xin's islet: rubble-masks vomiting spectral maggots, a bone scythe of void, a poison belch. Disabled S7 (Performance/Occultism). The local packs say Zutha himself was defeated on *"the risen isle of Xin"* years ago — this was what he left behind.
- **Sedacthy** — amphibious fish-like humanoids who press sea creatures into service and hunt air-breathers; Baolen's "loyal subjects." Kraken Callers (tridents, Shared Feast bites) and their speaker Maddenmist; five dead in the temple (S7).
- **Runelord Xanderghul** — **no longer a hypothesis. He is the campaign's prime mover.** First and last Runelord of Pride, greatest among the Runelords. He is the regal man in peacock robes from S5's vision. Thybidos, S6: *"when our Lord Xanderghul cast his judgment on Sorshen and her pathetic stolen city"* — **he destroyed Xin-Eurythnia.** He possessed Aethusa, discarded her for a fresh body, manufactured the Risen, and intends to **open a doomsday door, ride the Oliphaunt of Jandelay out of it, and ascend to godhood.** **S7, from the throne: he cannot be killed while any Risen exist — slain, he transfers into one — and he has moved Risen production to the Emerald Chambers.** Before the Trinity Star he was somewhere inside *"the heart of an immense eclipse,"* being consumed.
- **Pridespawn** — sinspawn of Pride in green peacock robes with enchanted bec de corbins. Resistant to mental damage with large bonuses on saves against mental effects; **Hallucinatory Backlash** reaction (mental damage to anyone who hits them). Seven killed in S6.
- ~~**The man in peacock-feather robes**~~ — **RESOLVED S6: it is Xanderghul.** He stepped out of Kandirvek's prophetic vision, waved it shut, and psychically spiked the dragon. He casts magic powerful enough to reach backward through prophecy.
- ~~**The Trinity Star's passenger**~~ — **RESOLVED S6: it was Aethusa's trail, not the passenger's.** The glowing footprints east out of the crater are hers. What rode the Trinity Star down was the **Warshard of Gorum**, and it is **no longer in the city**.
- **The Oliphaunt of Jandelay** — an enormous creature Xanderghul releases from the doomsday door. In the vision it **killed the Risen in attendance** the instant it emerged, and he then climbed onto its back. It is not on his side by default. *(Transcribed as "Elephant"/"Oliphant"; the canon name is the **Oliphaunt of Jandelay**, subject of the rank-10 mythic spell **Summon Oliphaunt of Jandelay** — which is presumably what the doomsday door actually is.)*
