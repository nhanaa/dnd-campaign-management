# Revenge of the Runelords (Cody) — World & Storyline

> Table canon only — built from what happened at the table (sessions 4–6; sessions 1–3 predate Pax joining and are summarized from in-game references). No AP-book knowledge. Updated after each session.

**Frame**: Saga Lands / Kodar Mountains, one year after the other three PCs' adventures in Sandpoint. The party carries **mythic power** — "that spark of the war god Gorum that crashed down on Golarion one year ago" (Kandirvek) — tied to the fall of the **Trinity Star**.

---

## The Story So Far

### Before Pax joined (sessions 1–3, reconstructed from table references)
- A year ago, Paya, Aureys, and Mithtirith **delved the pit at Sandpoint** — where they met and defeated someone who *claimed* to be a Runelord ("left her at the bottom of the pit, gladder for it, once the real one appeared in our dreams" — Paya). The Sandpoint Devil "was a thing."
- The **Trinity Star** fell from the sky into the Kodars around the same time, carrying the **Warshard of Gorum** — the source of the party's mythic sparks. Paya has a **Warshard rune** infused into his halberd.
- The party traveled to **Xin-Eurythnia**, Queen **Sorshen's** high-Kodar city, invited by **Ayavah** for the unveiling of the **Circle of Open Hands** portal. Sevek was invited by Sorshen's own letter.
- They fought and destroyed the Risen Runelord **Gimmel** (per Krune: "I expect you're the reason that waste of skin Gimmel hasn't reported back"). Aureys claims to have eaten part of him.

### Session 4 — The Flight of the Eurythnians (2026-08-01)
- The portal unveiling ended in catastrophe: a magical shockwave, earthquakes, a **sinkhole that swallowed the portal and Sorshen herself**. Xin-Eurythnia (pop. ~12,500, ~1/5 dead immediately) could not be saved.
- Seven days of evacuation prep (interrupted by a **pyroclastic mukradi** from the sinkhole), then a ten-day trek south with ~10,000 refugees: an avalanche, the naiad **Svevenka** turned from hazard to gift-giver, Risen saboteurs, a night ambush by four **misery siktemporas**, stone giant bandits (the Kodar Kings), and **hundred-foot watchers** with red eyes motionless on the mountaintops.
- At the trek's end, two **Risen Runelords of Sloth — Krune and Krenlith —** attacked on mirage dragon mounts. Both burned Mythic Ferocity; both died anyway (Paya ran Krune through; Sevek's bo staff finished Krenlith). **Kandirvek**, an omen dragon, killed the third runelord and its mount off-screen and joined the fight: "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?"

### Session 5 — The Hollow Throne (2026-08-08)
- The refugees reached **Vekr's Crossing** with **98/100 Survival Points** — Xin-Eurythnia's culture will survive; talk of eventual return is popular. Ayavah stays with them as a standing ally.
- Krune's **retrieval belt** yielded a mythic **Wand of Never Mind**, a **crystal ball**, and an engraved **star chart** of the Trinity Star's flight path — which shows where it landed. Whoever engraved it could follow it too.
- At his lair, Kandirvek showed the party **four prophetic visions** on his wings (see Mysteries). The fourth was cut off by a **regal man in peacock-feather robes** who *looked back through the vision* and psychically spiked the dragon.
- Kandirvek flew the party three days to the **Throne of the Troll King** (fighting two mythic **natbakhs** en route). The party killed the throne's mythic occupant **Gavergash** and his four warleaders.
- The throne proved to be an **illusion capping an 800-foot pit**. The descent triggered **A Falling Star's Memories** — the Trinity Star's voyage through space, relived. At the bottom: **Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul**, thought lost at Earthfall — with a fresh 250-foot crater of iron shards and **glowing footprints walking east out of the wreckage**.
### Session 6 — The Garden of Pride (2026-08-15) — **BOOK ONE COMPLETE**
- Following the footprints east, the party met **four omen dragons** studying the ruins — **Shephodian**, **Ithariel**, **Nobukis**, **Yvinsbrel** ⚠️. They were kin to the mirage dragons the party killed in S4. Aureys admitted it outright rather than lie; Shephodian: *"I see in your eyes you don't deceive."* They stood down and gave up two facts: **the Warshard of Gorum is no longer in this city**, and **a woman came weeks earlier** (human, tall, black robes, staff, blonde) who went east and never re-emerged, seeming to vanish into thin air.
- A **cave worm** and three **shulns** ambushed the party at her abandoned campsite. Her journals identify her as **Aethusa** — a modern wizard who *took* the name of a Runelord of Gluttony, believing dreams from a **cloaked, smoke-like figure** (the same being from the party's own apocalypse dream) were memories of that past life. She tracked the Trinity Star exactly as the party did; **the guardians expected her and the trolls did not stand in her way.** Her last entry: a dream of meeting Xanderghul and being promised secret knowledge, waking too early. Then nothing.
- Past five **pridespawn sentinels** in peacock robes stood a quicksilver portal guarded by the **Mirror of Glory**, a mythic hazard projecting Xanderghul's contempt. Disabled after six successes; yielded the **Paradigm Cube**.
- **The four visions inside the portal** (each narrated by a PC's mythic future self) explain the whole book — see Mysteries.
- Beyond the portal: **the Garden of Pride** and the **Shattered Runewell of Pride** — Kandirvek's fourth vision, arrived at last. Two **Risen Runelords of Wrath, Angothane and Thybidos**, died there (Aureys took Thybidos, Paya took Angothane), and the Runewell was shut down by Mithtirith's third success.
- **Book One complete. Level 15.** The garden's gemstone throne is unlooted; next session opens there.

## NPCs

- **Kandirvek** — **omen dragon** (corrected from S4's "blue"), scholar, centuries in the Kodars, Cody's favorite NPC. Dreams potential futures (dragonkind lost perfect foresight when Aroden died). Dreamt of the party repeatedly; allied with them against the Risen. Currently **topside at the Troll King's hill**, holding off the troll nation while the party is below. Gave Aureys the Black Pearl Aeon Stone. "Two dragons. Fighting against the world."
- **Ayavah** — High Priestess of Nocticula; invited the party to Xin-Eurythnia and feels responsible. Now running the refugee effort at Vekr's Crossing. Standing offer: "If you ever have need of an ally in the Saga Lands, the Kodars, or just anywhere, you can call on me."
- **Sorshen** — Queen of Xin-Eurythnia, dragged into the sinkhole at the festival. **No word since.** Knew who Sevek was before he arrived (invited him by name).
- **Svevenka** — naiad of the Fen of the Ice Mists (S4); turned ally after Paya's critical parley; gifted the tidal crossbow (since sold) and Wand of Howling Blizzard (Mithtirith). Warned: "Something is wrong out in the Kodars."
- **Aethusa** — a modern wizard who took the name of a **Runelord of Gluttony**, believing dreams sent by the cloaked smoke-figure were memories of that past life. Tracked the Trinity Star to Xin-Cyrusian and camped there. **Xanderghul's ghost passed through her own wards and possessed her**; she raised the Risen from looted Runelord bones on his behalf; he then abandoned her for a fresh body and had her carried away unconscious past **four immense steel vats**. Her fate is unknown and nobody has said she is dead. *Not to be confused with the historical Runelord whose name she took.*
- **Shephodian** ⚠️, with **Ithariel**, **Nobukis**, and **Yvinsbrel** ⚠️ — four **omen dragons** who have studied the Xin-Cyrusian ruins for several months. Kin to the mirage dragons the party destroyed in S4; they let the party pass specifically because Aureys refused to lie about it. Not allies, but not enemies: *"Though I hold no love of you and your friends for what has happened to our kin, we also understand that is the nature of the business."*

> ⚠️ **The four omen dragon names are unconfirmed** — audio-only, no Foundry token, and they do not appear anywhere in the local PF2e packs, so they are likely Cody's own NPCs. Confirm spellings with him. *(**Aethusa** and the **Oliphaunt of Jandelay** were flagged here too and are now **confirmed** against the local packs — see Threats.)*

## Threats

- **The Risen of the Trinity Star** — **now explained (S6): they are manufactured.** Aethusa raised them by ritual from **the bones and burial raiments of real Runelords**, looted out of the tomb of **Emperor Jinn**, Xanderghul's "beloved friend." The party watched **Gimmel** rise from a pile of dusty bones in a vision. Destroyed so far: **Gimmel**, **Krune**, **Krenlith**, one unnamed (eaten mid-air), and the Runelords of Wrath **Angothane** and **Thybidos** (S6). All have **Mythic Ferocity** (they die twice) and **Vanishing Death** (body and polearm vanish on death).
- **Runelord Xanderghul** — **no longer a hypothesis. He is the campaign's prime mover.** First and last Runelord of Pride, greatest among the Runelords. He is the regal man in peacock robes from S5's vision. Thybidos, S6: *"when our Lord Xanderghul cast his judgment on Sorshen and her pathetic stolen city"* — **he destroyed Xin-Eurythnia.** He possessed Aethusa, discarded her for a fresh body, manufactured the Risen, and intends to **open a doomsday door, ride the Oliphaunt of Jandelay out of it, and ascend to godhood.**
- **Pridespawn** — sinspawn of Pride in green peacock robes with enchanted bec de corbins. Resistant to mental damage with large bonuses on saves against mental effects; **Hallucinatory Backlash** reaction (mental damage to anyone who hits them). Seven killed in S6.
- ~~**The man in peacock-feather robes**~~ — **RESOLVED S6: it is Xanderghul.** He stepped out of Kandirvek's prophetic vision, waved it shut, and psychically spiked the dragon. He casts magic powerful enough to reach backward through prophecy.
- ~~**The Trinity Star's passenger**~~ — **RESOLVED S6: it was Aethusa's trail, not the passenger's.** The glowing footprints east out of the crater are hers. What rode the Trinity Star down was the **Warshard of Gorum**, and it is **no longer in the city**.
- **The Oliphaunt of Jandelay** — an enormous creature Xanderghul releases from the doomsday door. In the vision it **killed the Risen in attendance** the instant it emerged, and he then climbed onto its back. It is not on his side by default. *(Transcribed as "Elephant"/"Oliphant"; the canon name is the **Oliphaunt of Jandelay**, subject of the rank-10 mythic spell **Summon Oliphaunt of Jandelay** — which is presumably what the doomsday door actually is.)*
- **The watchers** (S4) — hundred-foot spider-limbed figures, red eyes, motionless on the Kodar mountaintops. Never moved. Never explained.
- **The Kodar trolls** — their mythic king Gavergash is dead and the throne legend is live; a whole hostile civilization surrounds the pit the party descended.

## Mysteries & Lore

- **The Trinity Star**: made entirely of iron; fell a year ago carrying the Warshard of Gorum; its charted path ends at the Troll King's hill — directly above Xin-Cyrusian. The crater's blast came from **what it struck**, not the impact. What was the true target?
- **The four wing-visions** (S5): ① riding Kandirvek over the Kodars consulting the star chart (*came true*); ② fighting powerful trolls (*came true*); ③ descending into a ruined cavern city in **Narvoth** (*came true*); ④ **prying open a portal above the shattered Rune Well of Pride** (***came true, S6*** — all four visions are now spent).
- **The four portal-visions** (S6) — narrated by each PC's mythic future self on the walk through the quicksilver:
  - **(Paya) The possession.** Aethusa flies into the ruined city, examines the crater, takes notes, camps, erects magical defenses, and sleeps. Xanderghul's ghost descends, **passes through her wards as if they weren't there**, and flows into her. She wakes laughing, eyes shimmering.
  - **(Sevek) Where the Risen come from.** Xanderghul claims the tomb of **Emperor Jinn**. Aethusa walks the crypt collecting the **bones and burial raiments of long-dead Runelords**, then rituals with the Risen over a pile of bones — and **Gimmel rises out of them**.
  - **(Aureys) The second body.** The Risen carry a cloth-wrapped human figure into a room where Aethusa lies unconscious amid magical apparatuses and **four immense steel vats**. Xanderghul's ghost leaves her and enters the wrapped body; it tears free; the Risen clothe and adorn him. **Aethusa is carried away, still unconscious.** He blesses each attendant with a touch to the brow.
  - **(Mithtirith) The doomsday door.** Xanderghul raises his arms before an enormous door to attain godhood and lay waste to the Saga Lands. The earth shakes, the **Oliphaunt of Jandelay** surges out and **slays the Risen in attendance**, and he climbs onto its back. The cavern opens above and sunlight and rubble bury the door — and in the last instant, **Xanderghul and Queen Sorshen are locked in battle**, whisked through the doors alongside the elephant the moment before they slam shut.
- **The four steel vats** — Aethusa was carried out past them, unconscious. Never explained.
- **Where is the Warshard of Gorum?** It came down inside the Trinity Star, it is **not in Xin-Cyrusian**, and four dragons who have studied the ruins for months cannot say where it went.
- **The trolls let Aethusa through.** Her journals: the guardians expected her arrival and the Kodar trolls did not stand in her way. The party had to kill Gavergash and four warleaders to reach the same shaft. Somebody told the trolls she was coming.
- **The Eighth Runelord** — Mithtirith is writing a thesis on "the one who almost was a Runelord," the **Runelord of Vainglory** (name rendered ~"Lirallaru"; spelling unconfirmed). Even Kandirvek had never heard of them. In a campaign now pointed at the city of *pride*, increasingly relevant.
- **The star chart's maker** — someone engraved the Trinity Star's path. They can find the landing site exactly as the party did.
- **Sorshen's fate** — ~~and what force actually hit Xin-Eurythnia at the unveiling~~. **Half-resolved S6**: Thybidos confirms **Xanderghul** cast judgment on Sorshen and her city, and the last portal-vision shows **her alive and fighting him**, swept through the doomsday doors an instant before they were buried. The visions show what *has* come to pass, not her state now. Where those doors lead, and whether they open from this side, is unknown.
- **The cloaked, smoke-like figure** — the being from the party's own apocalypse dream that granted their mythic power **also sent Aethusa the dreams that led her here**, posing as memories of her past life. It has been recruiting on both sides. Its goal is unstated.
- **The Wand of Never Mind** — a mythic wand of "a mean Xanderghul spell," found on a Runelord of *Sloth*. Why did Krune carry Xanderghul's magic and a map to Xanderghul's city?

## House Rules & Table Facts

- PF2e Remastered + **Mythic (War of Immortals)**, Free Archetype, Ancestry Paragon, **no ARP, no GAB** (see character-sheet.md).
- **Mythic Point economy**: killing blow on a mythic enemy = +2 MP to the killer, +1 to everyone else; completed hazards refund similarly (best contributor +2). Mythic-proficiency rerolls can rescue *untrained* skill checks — the party's emergency key.
- Cody opened **Impossible Magic** content table-wide at level 14 (rebuilds allowed with notice: Mithtirith → Hedge Mage; Aureys → Living Nexus + Draconic Acolyte).
- Item access for the level-14 shopping trip: items up to party level.
