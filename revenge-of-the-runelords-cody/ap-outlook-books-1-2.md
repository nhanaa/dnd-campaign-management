> Built from a full read of the first two AP volumes. **Spoiler policy: no plot, no proper names, no scene descriptions for anything ahead of the party** — encounters are described by shape and numbers only. Creatures you've already fought are named. All numbers are **printed book values**. *(Corrected 2026-08-07: an earlier claim that "S4 proved Cody runs hotter than print" was an artifact of comparing against base-bestiary creatures — the books print custom pre-buffed variants, and S4's measured numbers match those variants closely. ## Headline findings

1. **These are Will-save, mental-damage books, and your Will is the best in the party.** Save-targeting census of every enemy/hazard AoE and forced save: **Book 1 ≈ Will 19 / Reflex 9 / Fort 6** (hazards add Will 6 / Reflex 4 / Fort 1); **Book 2 ≈ Will 22 / Reflex 17 / Fort 15**. Mental is the single most common non-physical damage type in both volumes — including a recurring boss chassis whose *weapon* deals 3d12+ mental. Master Will +25 with success→crit and the 1/hr reroll is the meta answer to the entire AP. The Wyrm's Wingspan tattoo's mental-leaning resistance 10 keeps paying every book.
2. **The flight investment is massively vindicated.** Book 1's remaining chapter has an aerial fight at 200 ft altitude (flightless PCs fight from mounts and spend an action per round just staying on), a 200-ft-ceiling cavern with four flying dragons, an 800-ft vertical shaft, and bosses that fight from the ceiling. Book 2: **roughly half the roster flies or teleports**, several enemies are fly-*only*, there's a 400-ft climb (DC 40 — or just fly), a vertical prison spire, and one hazard whose escape routes include "DC 47 Acrobatics while flying." One Book 1 trap and one Book 2 trap are **explicitly ineffective against fliers**. Ascend at 14 may be the best-timed feat pick available to you.
3. **Spirit damage is the golden ticket; holy is nearly dead.** There are essentially **no undead and almost no unholy enemies** in either book (one unholy boss in Book 2; one undead wave-spawner in an escape set piece). The recurring boss chassis is a *dream* creature, not undead. **But** Book 2 fields ~6 incorporeal enemies plus resist-all-15/10 profiles whose printed exceptions are **force, ghost touch, and spirit** — Herald's Weapon (all-spirit staff) and Celestial Armaments (+1d8 spirit every hit) let you hit those at full value while most of the party blanks. Herald's Weapon re-rates: the *sanctified/holy* half is flavor, the *all-spirit conversion* is the payload.
4. **The Reflex patch plan lands exactly on time.** Remaining Book 1 Reflex threats: one blaster-heavy finale (chain lightning / fireball / cold AoEs) and breath weapons. Book 2 is where Reflex DCs spike to 38–43 across hazards — arriving at **levels 16–18, right as Canny Acumen flips Reflex to master at 17**. And the **only printed effect in either book that steals Mythic Points keys off a crit-failed Reflex save**. Same for Grab-an-Edge saves (constant in Book 2, DC 36–43): Reflex again — though a flier mostly opts out of falling entirely.
5. **The Trip/lockdown engine gets partially degraded — two printed mechanics do it, and one earlier claim here was wrong.** *(Corrected 2026-08: an earlier draft said fliers "can't be tripped" — rules-checked and false. Trip has no ground requirement, and the Prone condition says a flier that would be knocked prone **falls**. The escape valve is Arrest a Fall — a **reaction**, requires a fly Speed, Acrobatics/Reflex vs DC ~15. So vs fliers a landed Trip = burn their reaction or slam them down for fall damage + prone + re-fly actions — and you're the one PC who can deliver it at altitude.)* What genuinely degrades the engine: (a) one boss line is flat-out **immobilization-immune** — and since Grabbed/Restrained work through the immobilized condition, that kills the *grapple* lane against them (prone still applies); (b) **every boss-tier enemy carries "Remove a Condition" for 1 of its 3 Mythic Points** — prone won't stick, it becomes an MP/action tax instead of a lock (though a tripped flier already paid the fall damage and altitude before stripping it); (c) **every boss caster has Steady Spellcasting** (DC 15 flat to keep the spell), which blunts about a third of Stand Still's disruption value. Trip stays excellent against the ground-bound elite-mob fights, of which several are coming.
6. **One chapter is actively bad for this build: Book 2 opens with a mandatory underwater dungeon at level 15** — including a Severe boss fight in a 200-ft flooded hall. No fly, no land speed, flaming rune dead (fire doesn't function underwater), and under the aquatic-combat rules bludgeoning melee takes a penalty and creatures without a swim Speed fight compromised (verify the exact sidebar with Cody). Prep list below. It's one chapter; the rest of the AP goes back to being your terrain.

## Book 2 — chapter shapes

- **Ch 1 (Lv 15): the underwater dungeon.** Multiple submerged fights ending in a Severe boss (Gargantuan, reach 40–60, Grab + constrict that steals your held breath, a Will-save ink curse that permanently taxes Recall Knowledge). **Printed kill-lever: the boss's mythic save-upgrade shuts off — and it takes 6d10 mental per round — while confused.** Party prep before this chapter: water breathing for everyone (scroll-tier), the breath-suppressing elixir line the book sells, and temper expectations for your own numbers — this is the party-carry chapter, not yours.
- **Ch 2 (15→17): the crypt gauntlet.** An **Extreme** set-piece fight vs an incorporeal Gargantuan flier + a multi-part hazard (**explicitly tunable down by pre-fight roleplay Q&A — engage it**); then a dungeon of six themed wings that are mostly hazard/social (the "favor" you earn there weakens later fights) and a combat wing with **four separate boss-caster fights on the chassis you know** (fly + wall spells + pin spells; Fort is their weak save; one pre-casts a resist-11-everything buff; two ambush resting parties invisibly) ending in a second **Extreme**: an 18th-level caster + 4 blinding-aura minions + a self-repairing hazard. Also in this chapter: a corridor that **temporarily eats your magic items' power**, a party-splitting floor trap (**fliers immune**), and a 550-HP ooze with a 90-ft sicken aura. |
- **Ch 3 (17→18): vertical + planar.** A timed region-wide chase subsystem (deploy allies, beat DCs 36–41 across many skills), a **Severe 17 open-sky fight vs an airborne illusionist + an ancient dragon** — followed *immediately* by a **5-round-timer mythic hazard, so hold resources through the fight** — then a floating prison: 400-ft approach climb (or fly), a boss **trio** of teleporting hag-casters (fight them separately — together they're rated beyond Extreme; **cold iron weakness 15**; mirror-light powers you can disable with one Interact each) escorted by incorporeal fly-only nightmares (**your spirit damage hits them at full value through resist-all-10**), and the AP's biggest skill-wall hazard (DC 47–52, legendary everything).

## What this changes in the plan (to discuss, not yet locked)

1. **Level-15 skill increase → Athletics legendary.** The books assume it; nobody else in the party will have it.
2. **The "how much aerial combat?" question for Cody is now answered by the text: constantly.** The flight stack and the Ascended Celestial pick are validated on paper — no destiny relitigation needed.
3. **Rune roadmap tilt**: spirit/astral damage is worth more than elemental diversity for Book 2 (resist-all-except-spirit profiles); the flaming rune's quiet job is **turning off regeneration** in a big Book 1 fight — keep it through Book 1, reassess before Book 2 (underwater + no enemy fire weakness).
4. **Trip expectations**: vs ground bosses, count a landed Trip as "cost them 1 MP or 1 action," not "they're locked down." **Vs fliers, count it as "burn your reaction (Arrest a Fall, DC ~15) or eat the pavement"** — best delivered when the target's reaction is already spent, and the fall damage + altitude loss happen even if they later MP-strip the prone. Vs the two elite-mob walls, trip everything that moves.
5. **New Cody questions when relevant**: aquatic-combat rules he'll use (Ch 1 of Book 2); whether Taunt works on mindless constructs; loot-as-written for the armor drop.
