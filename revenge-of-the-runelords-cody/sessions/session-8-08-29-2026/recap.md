## Session Summary

Book Two's road finally has a map on it. The party finishes picking over Baolen's chamber and finds that the nine alcoves are a gallery of Runelord landmarks — the Black Squares of Ungevik, Sekhnavali, the Storval Stairs, Rimeskull, the Dome Hive, the Sunken Queen — and, in the fourth alcove, a flawless green gem that is not a gem. The **Emerald Eye** is a living blueprint of the Emerald Chambers, and studying it that night in Absalom gives up the one thing nobody had: **where the entrance is.** The Velashu Uplands, a sprawl of grassland in the Saga Lands, three days out on Kandirvek's back.

Between the temple and the road, two pieces of housekeeping. Paya runs the sell-off — the kukri, the tridents, the shield, the Staff of Arcane Might, the Bands of Force, the Potency Crystal all go out the door for **4,788 gp each** — and Sevek finally claims the greater flaming runestone that has been sitting in the stash since Quoroc's vault. And Kandirvek makes an introduction: an old ally of his, a mythic one, a gnome who looks like a kindly grandfather until you notice the blood-red left eye. **Kreebnock** floats a few feet off the ground, bows without landing, and says he's looking forward to ending this threat so he can get back to wandering.

The Uplands are not empty. Three armies are camped around the dais where seven enormous carved Runelord heads circle in the air: Velashu Horselords under **Nood Helarsin**, a Shoanti tribe under **Roaring Storm**, and — the reason everyone is nervous — an army flying the flag of **Runelord Belimarius**, led by an Azlanti man with violet eyes calling himself **General Lograsi**. Paya calls the approach (*"trying to stop Doomsday by starting a war seems counterproductive"*) and suggests the white flag; Aureys runs the parley, and over an honest accounting of what they've come for, wins a truce. Lograsi is delighted: the heroes can deal with the heads, and *"all the better for us."*

The heads are the **Trial of Runes**. Mithtirith presents the party's mythic power as proof of a right to rule, and then six of the seven Runelords ask a question each — Kaliphesta on the nature of the dead, Envy on loyalty, Greed on truth versus lies, Xirie on the shortest path to greatness, Alderpash on how to handle two enemies, Sorshen on which of her works will endure. The party answers by playing to each sin, workshopping every one out loud, and lands **five of six**. Only Alderpash rejects them: the table's answer was to ally with the weaker enemy and betray them later, and Cody ruled the question had already named both as enemies. Then the seventh head — Xanderghul's — takes a long pause and refuses the game entirely. *"Nothing you say will suffice, intruders. The only answer I'll accept is your death."* His head splits like an eggshell and a **peacock phoenix** unfolds out of it; the five satisfied heads crash to the ground, and only Alderpash stays airborne, eyes lighting with runic fire.

That five-out-of-six is the whole fight. The Rune Sentries are a level-17 complex hazard that gets **one action per surviving head** — six heads would have meant six Lightning Arcs a round, roughly 190 points of electricity the party never had to eat. They faced one. It is still an extreme-threat fight: the phoenix opens with a 102-damage Reactive Strike crit that puts Paya on 69 before round one ends, then drops Phantasmagoria on everyone and **Confuses Sevek and Mithtirith** — Sevek puts 38 into Paya's back, and Mithtirith swings a staff in melee for the first time in fifteen levels and rolls a natural 1. Aureys does 188 of the phoenix's 400 herself, moves her astral rune onto her fan mid-fight to beat its incorporeal resistance, and finishes it with a keen claw crit. **She takes zero damage all fight.** The sentry itself is an *object* — hardness 28, AC 43, immune to crits and precision — and simply cannot be killed fast; Mithtirith disables it on his third legendary Arcana attempt, at DC 45, with **10 HP left on the thing.** A staircase unfolds beneath the dais.

Below, the Emerald Chambers: infinite branching emerald corridors that swallow people for a lifetime, navigable only by the Eye. A day in, five **Silent Devotees** come around a corner at a sprint and make no sound at all — pale, eyeless things with emerald pits for sockets, wearing a ten-foot aura that suppresses hearing and speech, teleporting thirty feet a turn without provoking, and dealing sneak-attack damage to anyone who is merely *bleeding.* Cody ran five PL+0 bodies at 325 HP each: **200 XP, the exact Extreme threshold for a five-player table**, and forty-eight minutes of real time. The party grinds it out on flanks, Feints, two critical Trips off Sevek's Rippling Spin, and Kreebnock's first real showing — anthems up every round, amped Dazes, and a hit he described as forming *"a comically large hammer"* over a prone devotee. Five +2 greater striking wounding frost longswords come off the bodies.

Another day's walk, a full rest, and then an emerald door bearing the seven-pointed Star of Thassilon. Through it, in a flash of prismatic light: the **Crypt of Runes** itself — an eighty-foot circular chamber, seven doors around a seven-pointed star, six ajar and the seventh sealed in glowing runes, a statue on a plinth facing a mural. And one occupant. **Czaresia**, a rune giant with the rune of pride carved over and over into her skin and a mace the size of a person, servant of Xanderghul and guardian of the crypt. Paya answers her "who are you" honestly and gets it thrown back: *"Your honesty would be charming if it wasn't so foolish."*

Six hundred hit points — 1.6× the book — a twenty-foot reach, a Wide Swing that rolls once and applies to two people, an 8d12 electricity cone she recharges with mythic points, and two Reactive Strikes a turn. Her opening turn crits Aureys for 92, critically fails Mithtirith for 61, and still connects twice more at MAP −10. Sevek Trips her (she spends a mythic point standing up), Taunts her twice, and takes 188 — but the Taunt does not keep her off the casters: in round three she walks past everyone to Wide Swing Kreebnock and Mithtirith for 55 and 45, eating the off-guard to do it. Paya answers with two natural 20s. And in round four, with the giant at 27 hit points, Aureys puts both halves of an Earth to Heavens Strike through her: *"the dragonet carves a giant hole through her torso, and there is a torrent of blood that cascades down onto the stones as she falls dead."*

Six doors stand open. The seventh is still sealed.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Nine Alcoves (exploration / lore)
- The party finishes searching Baolen's chamber under the Isle of Xin. The pocket alcoves are murals, each depicting a Runelord landmark; Cody waives the checks — *"there's no reason you wouldn't be able to identify these."*
- Going counterclockwise from the south: the **Black Squares of Ungevik** (Zutha's necromantic engine, still under the sea off Varisia); **Sekhnavali**, the Hanging Monastery, greatest temple to the peacock spirits; the **Storval Stairs** and the colossi flanking them (Karzoug); and the **Emerald Chambers** themselves — built as a memorial for **First King Xin** and a tomb for the Runelords who followed.
- Aureys' search of the Emerald Chambers alcove turns up a single flawless green gem: the **Emerald Eye**. Peering into it shows shifting passageways — a live map. Aureys: *"It will be a centerpiece of my hoard."* Paya: *"Yes, but first it'll be very useful in our travels."* Sevek: *"It can be both."*
- Mithtirith insists on seeing the rest — *"I'll indulge my obsession with the Runelords"* — and gets five more: the obelisk forest of **Mundatei** in the Mindspin foothills; the **Shining Queen** (known today as the **Sunken Queen**), Sorshen's pyramid in the eastern Mushfens, whose carving Cody notes bears a striking resemblance to **Xin-Eurythnia**; **Rimeskull**, whose skull-carved mountainside once bore First King Xin's face; the **Dome Hive** and its legions of bureaucrats; and **Azaradne's Gardens**, grown by the third Runelord of Sloth from seeds that never needed tending.
- Up and out. Kandirvek is circling above the isle. Aureys holds up the gem. *"Plus, I like flying on your back."*

### Act II — Absalom, and a New Ally (downtime / RP)
- Kandirvek: *"I took the liberty of contacting an old ally of mine… They've mythic power like yourselves."* Aureys: *"They're more than welcome to join our little band."* Paya: *"Doom-driven heroes should work together."*
- **The sell-off**, Paya running it as loot master: the +2 greater striking keen kukri, three +2 greater striking shock tridents, the Major Sturdy Shield, the **Greater Staff of Arcane Might** (nobody can invest a second staff — Mithtirith already has the Staff of Healing), the second Greater Bands of Force, and the Major Potency Crystal (*"unless you have an Investigator, they're generally just very sad"*). Kept: the Scroll of Teleport (*"that's a group decision to use teleport"*), the scroll case, the countering charm — and, obviously, the Emerald Eye. **4,788 gp each**, with Kreebnock given an equivalent starting share.
- **The greater flaming runestone finally lands.** Sevek: *"I will take the greater flaming, though, if you guys don't mind."* The S7 stash argument is over. The **Runic Skullcap** goes to Mithtirith; Paya keeps the **Paradigm Cube** — *"if you spend a mythic point on it, you just get to choose, and I do like that."*
- **Kreebnock's introduction.** An elderly gnomish man, orange and green hair, kindly-grandfather face ruined by a **scarlet blood-red left eye** (the right is bright amber). He is floating the entire time and never touches the ground — he bows *"while standing a few feet in the air."* Name negotiation follows immediately: *"You can call me Kreebnock, or Kree, if you prefer. I've been called both."* Aureys, after he mentions his mother's preference: *"Alright, let's not offend the mama."* Sevek takes the short one: *"I call you Kree, because I feel like that makes us closer."* Paya, a minute later: *"I think I'm gonna go with Kree as well — I struggle with long names too."*
- Aureys introduces herself as a very small dragon in slightly oversized robes: *"I am Aureys. Hello. You may have heard of me."* Kreebnock has: *"Kandirvek did mention you and your companion."* Paya removes his helmet to reveal a pineapple head, explains he's a fruit leshy, and shakes hands: *"Hi, I'm Paya. Nice to meet you."*
- Asked whether he's coming out of retirement, Kreebnock: *"Oh, no retirement. Just wandering. My kind of retirement."*
- Mithtirith's Staff of Healing won't cast above 3rd rank. A few minutes of Cody-and-Paya debugging finds it: the staff was set to three charges instead of eight. *"Oh, that's an easy fix."*
- Studying the Emerald Eye overnight gives up the entrance: the **Velashu Uplands**, a hundreds-of-miles sprawl of rolling grassland home to **Shoanti** tribes and **Velashu Horselords**. Three days on dragonback. Cody warns them to prepare — *"once you're in the Crypt of Runes, it may take some time before you emerge again."* Paya buys bottled sunlight.

### Act III — Three Armies (social)
- The destination is a stone dais on the highest hill for miles, with **seven enormous carved heads circling in the air above it** — Xirie, Xanderghul, and others, each a perfect and eerily lifelike likeness. Around the hill: three full-sized armies at a standoff.
- Velashu Horselords; a Shoanti tribe; and an army in steel and enchanted gear flying the flag of **Runelord Belimarius** — *"prior to the Risen of the Trinity Star and Queen Sorshen herself, the only surviving Runelord."* Mithtirith asks about Belimarius' history with Sorshen and gets the shape of it: they have never gotten along, Sorshen was the more powerful by a wide margin, and with Sorshen out of the picture Belimarius may be feeling confident.
- Mithtirith also asks the sharpest question of the scene, about the heads themselves: *"the description of the carved Runelord heads makes it sound like they're iron stones revolving around something. Is that the vibe it's giving off?"* Cody: *"It's not dissimilar magic, but they're too large to be iron stones."*
- Paya says the obvious thing out loud — *"Trying to stop Doomsday by starting a war seems counterproductive"* — and it's Paya who proposes the white flag and the neutral ground. Aureys agrees and takes the talking.
- One rider from each army. **Nood Helarsin** of the Horselords, mid-thirties, battle-scarred, auburn. **Roaring Storm** of the Shoanti, deep scowl, bird tattoos across her neck and scalp. And from Belimarius' army, an **Azlanti** man with a well-groomed beard, black leather armor, and almost-glowing violet eyes: **General Lograsi**.
- Introductions all round. Aureys leads with *"my companions and I are heroes of these lands. You may have heard of me."* Paya: *"Hi, I'm Paya. We're here to stop Doom."* Sevek: *"I'm just a guy that's trying to do good."* Kreebnock bows without landing. Mithtirith: *"I am Mithtirith, Nethys's humble servant."*
- Roaring Storm connects the dots immediately — *"I don't suppose this has anything to do with the heads that have suddenly appeared in the sky."* Lograsi senses the mythic power on them (Cody's phrasing came through the recording as *"the Rorschach blessing"* — ⚠️ almost certainly a mishear of the Godsrain; confirm) and presses the real question twice: **why are you here?**
- Aureys gives the honest answer and then the dragon answer: Xanderghul is working out of the Emerald Chambers to craft his return — *"and speaking only for myself, I'm also here because there is probably wonderful, delicious stuff, and maybe a few undead treats inside. Snacks, if you will."* Nood: *"A dragon, no matter the dragon, is still a dragon, I suppose."*
- Lograsi turns it into a truce: *"My lady Belimarius has no care for the Runelord of Pride… I say we allow them the chance to at least deal with those heads, yes? All the better for us."* Roaring Storm had already promised him an arrow in the throat and never audibly agrees to anything. Nood nods — to the party, not to Lograsi: *"If you've come for that area, by all means. But know, we will be watching."*
- Aureys closes it: *"And if we do not return, you may well have to deal with this yourselves."* Lograsi: *"Confident, aren't we?"* Aureys: *"I am a dragon after all."*
- Kandirvek stays topside. Thassilonian magic makes his own unreliable down there, he has contacts to work, and he'll make sure nobody follows them down. His parting read: *"I expect once you descend down into the Emerald Chambers, they're primed to start killing one another."* Aureys, unbothered: *"if you have to kill a few to keep the rest in line, yeah, you do what you gotta do, guy."*

### Act IV — The Trial of Runes (puzzle / RP)
- The seven heads descend as the party sets foot on the dais — each eight feet tall and nearly as wide — and speak in one merged voice: *"You seek entrance to the Emerald Chambers, the final resting place of First King Xin. To begin the trial, show us, the Lords of Thassilon, proof of your right to rule through your command of rune magic."*
- Mithtirith's Recall Knowledge reads the assignment: cast a spell of rune magic, or present an item inscribed with it. Cody rules it narrative, not mechanical. Mithtirith presents his own mythic power and his ties to Thassilon; the sentries' eyes flash and the heads begin to circle.
- Then six questions, one per head. The party's working theory, Mithtirith's: **play to each Runelord's sin.** Every answer was argued out loud first.
  - **Kaliphesta, Runelord of Gluttony** — *"What separates the living, the mindless dead, the dead that think, and that which moves without spirit?"* Mithtirith burns **Scholarly Recollection** on a Recall Knowledge and gets the frame (philosophical answers count; there is no single right answer). → **Aureys**: *"For what they hunger."* ✅
  - **Runelord of Envy** — *"How does a ruler ensure the loyalty of those who serve?"* → **Kreebnock**, cold, with no table discussion at all: *"By serving those beneath him."* ✅
  - **Runelord of Greed** — *"What fills your coffers more quickly, and why: truth or lies?"* The table splits — Mithtirith: *"I feel like this is a philosophical difference in the party"* — and Paya notes Paya wouldn't lie himself but has watched Aureys do very well at it. → **Aureys**: *"Lies. Because through schemes and deception, you are able to fill your coffer as quickly as possible."* ✅
  - **Xirie, Runelord of Sloth** — *"What is the shortest path to greatness?"* Aureys offers *"letting it come to you"*; Paya offers *"sacrifice"*; **Sevek** synthesises and delivers it: *"Step on the path that others have laid for yourself, so you don't have to build a path yourself."* ✅
  - **Alderpash, Runelord of Wrath** — *"You have two enemies. One camps at your borders but could be crushed easily. The other poses no immediate threat but is more formidable. How do you proceed?"* ❌ **The one wrong answer, and it was the whole table's.** Aureys opened with *"destroy them both"*; **Kreebnock** proposed the mechanism — *"play one against the other, and then strike when they're both weakened"*; **Sevek** shaped it into *"ally with the weaker one to take out the stronger threat"* — and then **raised the exact objection Cody would later rule on**: *"I think in this question it says exclusively to enemies."* **Paya** and **Mithtirith** both said they liked the plan; **Aureys** added the betrayal; Sevek gave the final version anyway. Cody, after: *"The question specified two enemies. The word 'enemies' dictates they're already both against you. Can't just say you're one."* **The party had the right read in the room and answered past it.**
  - **Sorshen, Runelord of Lust** — *"Which of my works will be best remembered?"* Aureys names the city and Paya supplies the name — *"Xin-Eurythnia"* — and Aureys delivers it: *"Your mighty city of Xin-Eurythnia. As you are building a mighty country, a mighty land."* ✅ (Cody corrects her out of character mid-answer: the city had a different name before Sorshen claimed it. ⚠️ Whisper rendered the older name unintelligibly; confirm it with Cody.)
- **Five of six.** It's **Paya**, not Sevek, who names the miss afterward — *"Sevek said it in everything"* — and then immediately lets it go: *"I will take one out of six. That's pretty good."*
- The seventh head is **Xanderghul's**, and it does not ask a question. A long, uncomfortable pause, and then: *"Nothing you say will suffice, intruders. The only answer I'll accept is your death."*
- His head cracks open like an eggshell; an enormous spectral **peacock phoenix** unfolds out of the iridescent light. The five satisfied heads crash to the ground. **Alderpash's remains airborne**, glowing with runic energy. Kreebnock, on the phoenix: *"Cool. I want one of those."*

### Act V — The Peacock Phoenix and Rune Sentry Alderpash (combat)
- See Combat Encounters. The short version: **the trial answers were the fight's difficulty dial**, and the party had turned it almost all the way down without knowing it. Cody, mid-fight: *"Imagine this fight if you had gotten them all wrong."* Paya: *"Maybe if you had gotten three wrong even, it would be very hard."*
- After the sentry falls, a staircase manifests in a glyph of runes beneath the dais. Aureys spends her Sihedron Medallion's Bolster Flesh and a Summon Mythic Power to top off before the descent, and reminds the table to do the same. She goes down first, looking for traps.

### Act VI — Into the Emerald Chambers (exploration + combat)
- *"Tall, sprawling hallways that seem to go on forever. A maze of passageways that spiral out into infinity."* Magnificent locked doors, catacombs and tombs that could hold riches, all of it passed by. Cody's line for the place: *"It is not an uncommon story to hear of one who somehow finds the Emerald Chambers, dying of old age well before they reach their target plunder."* Only the Emerald Eye makes it navigable.
- Not far in, the first guardians. *"You don't hear them. You never hear them."* Five **Silent Devotees** — skin ice pale, eyes pits of emerald green, barefoot, leaving no sound or mark on the tile — sprinting at the party in total silence. Mithtirith, on the art: *"Looks like overcompensation to me."*
- The fight (see Combat Encounters) runs forty-eight minutes of real time. Loot: **five +2 greater striking wounding frost longswords.** Sevek, watching the party total go up: *"I still need some time to get accustomed to the big numbers."*
- A day's travel and a full rest inside the Chambers. Then the destination: **a magnificent emerald door inscribed with the seven-sided Star of Thassilon.** Aureys pre-buffs at the threshold — Bolster Flesh, then Hollow Robes for invisibility.
- Through the door, a flash of prismatic light, and the party is somewhere entirely new: the **Crypt of Runes.** An 80-foot circular chamber, ceiling twice that. A seven-pointed star set into the floor, its tips aimed at seven doors. **Six stand ajar; the seventh, directly north, glows with runes.** A statue of a long-haired man holding a staff stands on a plinth at the center, facing a mural on the southern wall.

### Act VII — Czaresia, Guardian of the Crypt (mythic boss)
- One occupant: a towering female **rune giant** with burning red eyes and a mace *"so large it may as well be a scepter, even by her standards."* On every inch of exposed skin, the **rune of pride, inscribed over and over.**
- *"What is this? Who are you? And how have you all come here?"* Kreebnock, deadpan: *"Well, it all started with a dream."* Paya, flatly honest: *"Heroes of Golarion, here to stop Xanderghul."*
- *"Is that so? Your honesty would be charming if it wasn't so foolish. I am **Czaresia**, servant of Runelord Xanderghul and guardian of the Crypt of Runes. I don't know how you all found your way here, but in doing so, you've forfeited your lives."*
- Four rounds. See Combat Encounters. Aureys kills her with an Earth to Heavens Strike — both halves connecting — and Cody calls the session on the description: *"the dragonet carves a giant hole through her torso, and there is a torrent of blood that cascades down onto the stones as she falls dead."*
- Cody switches the backdrop to Book Two's art and immediately notes he can't use it as a landing page: *"it's a spoiler, you know."*

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Peacock Phoenix and Rune Sentry Alderpash
**Level 17 mythic incorporeal spirit + level 17 complex hazard · 6 rounds · 14:10–15:02 · extreme threat**

**How Cody built it.** The book phoenix is AC 39, 240 HP, spell DC 42, Radiant Beam 10d12/DC 38, Radiant Wing +32 for 4d10+15 — and it has **Rebirth**, which brings it back at full HP, corporeal, the round after it dies. Cody ran it at **AC 43, 400 HP**, spell DC 44, Radiant Beam **12d12/DC 42**, Radiant Wing **+38 for 4d10+23**, plus a **Reactive Strike it doesn't have on paper** — and **no Rebirth**. He traded a two-phase fight for one long bar. Its defenses were the book's: **immune to precision and void, resistance 15 to everything except force, ghost touch, and spirit.** Every melee weapon in the party is astral and ghost touch, which is why only Kreebnock's spells ever ate the 15 and only Aureys' Sneak Attack ever ate the immunity. **The Rune Sentries hazard ran exactly to book**: AC 43, hardness 28, 110 HP, immune to critical hits and precision, disable **DC 45 Arcana (legendary)** or Dispel Magic at 9th rank.

**Initiative**: Alderpash 65 · **Aureys 51** · Phoenix 49 · Paya 47 · Sevek 38 · Kreebnock 33 · Mithtirith 29 — but **Sevek Delayed on turn 1 to take Haste first**, so the live order all fight was Alderpash · Aureys · Phoenix · Paya · **Kreebnock · Sevek** · Mithtirith.

**The setup that decided it.** The hazard gets **one action per surviving head**, each spent on Lightning Arc (3d12+19, DC 42 basic Reflex, random target). Five heads had crashed on correct answers, so the party faced **one arc a round instead of six** — roughly 190 points of electricity a round they never had to eat. The healing clause is smaller than it sounds and still real: unused arcs restore 20 HP each to the phoenix, and with six sentries against five PCs exactly one would have gone begging — **20 HP a round.**

**Round 1.** Alderpash zaps Mithtirith (12). Aureys critically fails a Recall Knowledge and Cody gives her the disable method anyway, then she Hides in the open on Legendary Sneak. The phoenix flies down and opens **Radiant Beam** — 77 force — on Sevek and Paya; Sevek spends a mythic point to save at mythic proficiency (33 through), Paya eats all 77. Paya leaps in on Needle in the Gods' Eyes, makes her Radiant Panoply save by exactly zero, and takes a **Reactive Strike crit for 102 mental** on the way in — **69 of 248 before round one is over.** One halberd hit lands for 42. Kreebnock, one round into his first session, drops **Rallying Anthem and Haste on all five.** Sevek fuses Reflective Ripple and Whirlwind, Flurries two Trips into the phoenix's **Reflex DC 46** and misses by 4 and by 1, and Taunts instead. Mithtirith puts **116 into Paya.**

**Round 2 — Phantasmagoria.** Alderpash finds Mithtirith again (29). Aureys stops to **move her astral rune from her handwraps onto her fan** — the direct answer to the incorporeal read — then Feints for exactly the DC and lands fan-and-claw for **63**, leaving the phoenix **enfeebled 1**. The phoenix's Radiant Wing misses Sevek by 1 *because of Taunt*; Sevek points out that Taunt's penalty shouldn't apply when he's the target, and takes the **40** anyway. Then **Phantasmagoria**: 16d6, 63, everyone. Kreebnock and Aureys critically succeed for nothing. Paya saves for 31. **Sevek burns his Ascended Celestial Will reroll and still fails; Mithtirith spends a mythic point for mythic proficiency and still fails.** Both take 63 and both are **Confused.** Paya auto-crit-Trips the phoenix prone off Slam Down. Kreebnock's first Unleash Psyche fires an amped Daze — 46 rolled, halved, 8 through resistance. Then the confused turns: **Sevek's d4s come up Paya, phoenix, Aureys** — 38 into Paya's back, 45 into the phoenix, a miss on Aureys by 1. **Mithtirith Frostbites Kreebnock for 12 and then swings a staff in melee for the first time in fifteen levels — natural 1.** Both shake it at end of turn.

**Round 3.** Alderpash goes for Mithtirith a third straight time and rolls a **natural 20** into a critical success — nothing. Aureys lands **Majestic Presence** (frightened 1) and then the fight's biggest single turn: fan hit plus **keen claw crit, 130 rolled, 97 through precision immunity.** The phoenix spends a **mythic point on Remove a Condition** to stand for free and Radiant Beams again — Sevek burns **Prevailing Position**, gives up the stance for +4, fails anyway, and drops to **53 of 248 (21%)**; Paya spends a mythic point and critically succeeds. Paya's Vicious Swing puts in 54. Kreebnock's Murder of Crows lands 26 and **Razzle-Dazzle** stretches the dazzle to two rounds. Sevek's Inner Upheaval Flurry misses three times, the last a natural 1, and he moves to **Group Taunt the phoenix and the sentry together** — Cody stops him before any action is spent: *"You can't taunt the sentry because the sentry is not a creature. It is a hazard."* (Cody also floats a 30-foot range limit; Long-Distance Taunt already covers 120, and Sevek says so.) He Taunts the phoenix instead. Mithtirith three-action Heals for 42 across four PCs.

**Round 4 — the kill, on turn two.** Alderpash finally picks someone else (Paya, 30). Aureys steps to line up an **Aether Beam** across both the phoenix and the sentry — force, chosen specifically to beat incorporeal resistance — and it does nothing to either: the phoenix critically succeeds, and the sentry's halved 26 dies against hardness 28. Then the fan: **natural 19 on a keen rune, critical hit, 90 rolled, 70 through — and the phoenix only had 28 left.** *"It explodes into iridescent light and is gone."* **+2 mythic points to Aureys, +1 to everyone else.** The claw that followed critically missed. **400 damage exactly: Aureys 188, Paya 133, Sevek 45 (all of it thrown while Confused), Kreebnock 34.**
The rest of the round is the party discovering what an object is. Kreebnock's **Blinding Foam rolls a natural 20** — the hazard is immune to critical hits, but Cody doubles it anyway, and that ruling is the only reason **16** gets past hardness. Sevek's **Electric Arc does literally nothing**, and the Jolt Coil plan behind it dies with it: *"it's not really going to help against object hardness, because it's against all damage."* Mithtirith's first Arcana: **43 vs DC 45.**

**Rounds 5–6 — the object.** For two rounds almost nothing clears hardness 28. Aureys hits it once for 21, absorbed. Sevek hits it once for 33, absorbed. Both of them stop swinging and start **Aiding**, and that turns out to be the correct read — Sevek's +3 is the entire margin on Paya's round-6 hit (44 vs AC 43). What actually lands: **Paya's two Vicious Swings** (22, 18) and **Kreebnock's amped Imaginary Weapon** — *"a comically large hammer"* — for **43 through hardness**, taking it from 54 to 11. Sevek rolls a **natural 20** and gets nothing: *"it's also immune to crits because it is an object… it only takes the persistent fire at the end of that"* — and the persistent fire couldn't beat hardness either. Kreebnock's persistent acid never got a point through; both ticks read *"takes no damage."* The arc finds Aureys (natural 20, nothing) and then Sevek (**48**, through a second Prevailing Position).
**Resolution: not a kill, a disable.** Aureys tries first — *"this is my job, and I don't care if I have to lie to do it"* — Trained Arcana, mythic reroll, **42**. Mithtirith goes three times at legendary +29, with mythic rerolls on the last two: **43, 43, 46.** *"Ironically, when it had 10 HP left, that is when you get the success."* The magic leaves it and it crashes to the floor.

**Damage taken: 637** — 468 from the phoenix, 119 from six Lightning Arcs, and **50 from the party's own confused PCs.** Paya bottomed at 69/248 in round 1, Sevek at 53/248 in round 3. **Aureys took nothing at all.**

### Encounter 2 — Five Silent Devotees
**Level 14 rare humanoids run at level 16 — AC 39, HP 325 each, five of them**
*Six rounds, 15:24–16:11 (48 minutes — the longest fight of the session)*

**Difficulty.** Cody scaled every number by a uniform +3/+4: AC 36→39, attack +29→+33, Perception +26→+30, all saves +3, longsword 2d8+13→3d8+19, Cruel Sneak 2d6→3d6, HP 255→325. That turns five PL−2 bodies into five PL+0 bodies — **100 XP (Moderate) as written, 200 XP (Extreme) as run**, the exact Extreme threshold for a five-PC party. Foundry tagged it `threat extreme` until the third devotee dropped.

**Initiative**: Paya 51 · **Aureys 48** · Devotee C 46 · Devotee A 46 · E 41 · B 40 · D 37 · Kreebnock 37 (mythic **Prescience**, +6) · Sevek 32 · Mithtirith 32.

**What they do.** *Transdimensional Step* — a 30-foot teleport with no move, concentrate, or manipulate traits, so it never provokes (Cody, on air: *"no concentrate or manipulate or move traits, so no provocation"*). *Pall of Silence* — a 10-foot aura blocking auditory actions and any spell without the subtle trait. *Cruel Sneak Attack* — +3d6 against anyone off-guard **or taking persistent bleed**, and their wounding longswords apply bleed on every hit. The loop is self-sustaining: one landed hit makes every later hit a sneak attack, and it ran on Paya, Sevek, and Kreebnock all fight.

**Rounds 1–2 — the backline gets teleported into, and B dies.** Paya opens with Needle in the Gods' Eyes (47 into B). Aureys **critically Feints** B and puts **126** into it on a natural-20 claw. The devotees answer by ignoring the front line entirely: C strides to Mithtirith — **Sevek's Stand Still catches it on the way in and misses** — A teleports behind Paya (Deflecting Wave cuts 39 to 17), E strides to Sevek, B teleports into a flank and lands the fight's first Cruel Sneak Attack, and D teleports onto Aureys for an **80-point crit.** Mithtirith goes **invisible**; Cody rules the devotees can't see through it, which hands the next two rounds of attention to Kreebnock. Round 2, Paya spends **two mythic points** on back-to-back **Divide and Conquer**: 44 into A, an 84 crit into B, then a natural 20 for 82. *"This one falls. Even as they hit the floor, they make no sound."* — first kill, Paya. E answers with an **80-point crit on Kreebnock**, and his gnome **Unexpected Shift** lands the DC 11 flat check for resistance 16.

**Rounds 3–4 — Sevek turns into a speed bump, and A dies.** E hits him and **Rippling Spin fires: a critical Trip, Reflex DC 41 by 11.** Cody makes him honor the reach clause — *"you do need to take that damage, and step and end it within the attacker's reach"* — and E never gets up again, fighting prone for the rest of its life. Sevek **Group Taunts E, C, and A** onto himself and eats it: C crits him and **Prevailing Position downgrades it to a plain hit**, ~39 damage erased on one reaction. Aureys stacks Majestic Presence and Frightening Power and grovels through a Feint every round; A becomes the frightened, sickened, burning, bleeding center of the fight until Paya's Needle in the Gods' Eyes takes it apart. *"And with that, A is down."*

**Round 5 — two at once.** Aureys Feints D and lands **Earth to Heavens Strike with both halves critting for 158 — D dies where it stands.** Sevek's **second critical Trip** (DC 41 by 10) puts C on the floor. Kreebnock finishes E with the comically large hammer, a 52-point Imaginary Weapon; E's Reactive Strike on the manipulate trait misses by one first. *"You're faster as you bring the hammer down, and they are dead — because he was prone, see, he was lying at your feet."*

**Round 6 — the last one.** C teleports out, crits Aureys for 82 on the way (her floor, 67 of 200), then whiffs twice. Paya crits it for 68, Sevek grinds 37 through, and **Aureys finishes it on an Opportune Backstab off Sevek's hit** — *"I will stab down at him when he lands."* *"And with that, the last of them is down."*

**Damage**: Aureys ~584 · Paya ~544 · Sevek ~283 · Kreebnock ~214 — 1,625 total, exactly five full health bars. Mithtirith healed 378 across four Heals, mostly into Sevek. Mythic points: 3 (Paya 2, Kreebnock 1). Party floors: Kreebnock 22%, Aureys 33%, Sevek 35%, Paya 44%.

**Rules notes**: both Sevek (Electric Arc) and Kreebnock cast spells inside a Pall of Silence. Kreebnock caught it himself, after the fact. Cody let both stand — *"I didn't catch it, you guys didn't catch it, it's how it is."* Separately, the devotees' own stat block carries constant **truesight** — Cody's ruling that they couldn't see the invisible Mithtirith went the party's way.

**Loot**: 5× **+2 greater striking wounding frost longsword.**

### Encounter 3 — Czaresia, Guardian of the Crypt of Runes

**Initiative**: **Aureys 48** · Czaresia 40 · Mithtirith 39 · Paya 39 · Sevek 36 · Kreebnock 28. Sevek spent a mythic point to reroll at mythic proficiency and went *down*, 37 to 36. **Her kit.** 20-foot reach mace, permanently quickened. **Wide Swing** — one attack roll compared against the AC of *two* targets in reach; one action, counts as two attacks for MAP, no flourish trait, so she repeats it. **Invoke Rune** — a 30-foot 8d12 electricity cone that also stacks a rune onto her mace (she took Rune of Flames, +3d6 fire), rechargeable with a mythic point. **Flashing Runes** — a free Fortitude save inside 10 feet whenever she uses an arcane ability. **Two Reactive Strikes a turn.** Immune to fire; best save Fortitude. **She spent three mythic points**: two recharges and one Remove a Condition.

**Round 2 — the Trip.** Aureys re-applies frightened with Majestic Presence and lands 37. The rune giant goes down.** She burns a **second mythic point on Remove a Condition** to stand without using the move trait, denying the party the reactions a Stand would have provoked. Mithtirith heals Aureys 92. Kreebnock's Blinding Foam critically misses. Paya adds 38. Sevek fires **Herald's Weapon**, critically misses twice, then connects at MAP −10 for 28, and Taunts again.

**Round 3 — the round she went at the casters anyway.** Aureys' Frightening Power gets a critical success and does nothing. Czaresia strides, and **three party reactions land on one movement**: Paya's Reactive Strike for 39, Aureys' **Opportune Backstab for 45**, and Sevek's **Stand Still missing by exactly 1.** She arrives and **Wide Swings Kreebnock (55) and Mithtirith (45)** — the Taunt is live and she simply eats the off-guard rather than stay on the frontline. Mithtirith self-heals. **Kreebnock's Canticle of Everlasting Grief is critically saved and does nothing.** Paya provokes with another leap, eats a **124-damage crit (78 after Deflecting Wave)**, and answers with a **second natural 20 — Needle in the Gods' Eyes for 86.** Sevek Elf Steps, fires the **one-action Mortal Herald Heal for 94**, strides into her second Reactive Strike — a critical that resolves as a normal hit for 37 — and puts both halves of a Flurry through her for **69. Twenty-seven hit points left.**

**Round 4 — one turn.** Aureys whiffs a Feint, steps in, and puts both halves of an **Earth to Heavens Strike** into her; the second connects by 8 and it is over before Czaresia's turn comes back around. **She never got a fourth turn.** *"The dragonet carves a giant hole through her torso, as there is a torrent of blood that cascades down onto the stones as she falls dead."*

**Damage taken**: Paya **234**, Sevek **188**, Aureys **121**, Mithtirith **106**, Kreebnock **55**. **Nobody dropped**; the low-water mark was Aureys at 54% after round one.

The table liked the plan and it went in regardless. **Alderpash's head stayed in the air because of that answer, and that head was the entire second half of the fight.** Paya named the miss afterward, not Sevek.
- **Introduced himself to three generals as "just a guy that's trying to do good."** Aureys had just claimed the room as a hero of the lands. Sevek did not correct her and did not compete.

What he did instead: **six Taunts, four Prevailing Positions, two critical Trips via Rippling Spin, one Trip on a rune giant, one Stand Still (missed by 1), and Aid actions that supplied the exact winning margin on Paya's hits.** Delayed on turn one of the phoenix fight to take Haste first.

### Things Sevek knows that the party does not

- **The trial answers were the encounter's difficulty dial, and nobody at the table knew that while they were answering.** The Rune Sentries hazard gets one action per surviving head. Five right answers meant one Lightning Arc a round instead of six — roughly 190 points of electricity a round the party never had to eat — plus denying the phoenix 20 HP a round from arcs with no target. Cody said the quiet part: *"Imagine this fight if you had gotten them all wrong."* **Expect more encounters built this way in this book.**
- **Flaming is dead weight twice over right now.** Hardness applies to the whole damage packet, not per type, so Electric Arc did zero to the sentry and the flaming rider did nothing either. Then Czaresia was **immune to fire** and ate the rest of it. The staff has been a stick with a +3 on it for two of three fights.
- **Taunt does not work on hazards.** The Rune Sentry was an object; Group Taunt was stopped before an action was spent. Long-Distance Taunt's 120 feet was never the problem, and Cody's separate range objection was wrong. In a dungeon built out of Thassilonian complex hazards, that limitation will come up again.
- **Taunt did not keep Czaresia off the casters.** This is the correction worth writing down. With Taunt live in round three, she strode past the frontline and **Wide Swung Kreebnock and Mithtirith for 55 and 45**, accepting the −1 and the off-guard to do it. Cody said it out loud: *"This'll just keep going at the two spellcasters here."* What Taunt actually bought was the −1 and the off-guard, and the off-guard is what fed the flanking math — Aureys' 45 Opportune Backstab and two hit-by-exactly-zero strikes.
- **Czaresia has two Reactive Strikes a turn**, and Sevek confirmed it the expensive way. Assume every rune giant down here does.

### What Sevek is thinking about

- **The new gnome is the softest body the party has ever fielded, and Bodyguard is still assigned to Mithtirith.** Kreebnock has 184 HP and AC 37/38, floated the whole session, and still bottomed at 22% — the lowest anyone reached. Mithtirith solved his own exposure problem last book by leaving the ground; Kreebnock is *already* off the ground and it didn't help. **Reassign Bodyguard, or accept that the Taunt lane no longer covers the backline at all.**
- **The tank math needs a harder look than "it held."** Six Taunts across three fights bought a phoenix that missed and a rune giant that walked past him anyway. Prevailing Position was the reaction that actually paid — it erased a devotee crit worth ~39 and downgraded a giant's crit to a normal hit. **The mitigation is in the reactions, not the aggro.**
- **The Trial was a skill check the character couldn't roll.** Int 10, untrained Arcana, no Thassilon Lore. He answered two questions anyway because this table workshops out loud, and got one wrong. Playing to the sin was the right frame; reading a question's literal terms is the part he missed. **The Chambers are a Thassilonian test, and this book is built out of them.**
- **Kreebnock is a control-and-buff engine, not a striker, and that raises Sevek's floor.** Haste on five bodies, two anthems up every round, amped Dazes, and the single best hit anyone landed on the Rune Sentry. Courageous Anthem was live on several of the few attacks Sevek connected with. The party's action economy just went up by a third.
- **Paya and Aureys are carrying the damage and it is not close** — roughly 2,000 of the session's ~2,700. That's fine, but it prices the next class choices toward more control and more mitigation, not toward chasing damage the dice aren't giving.
