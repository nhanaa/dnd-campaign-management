Session Summary

Two fights, and everything that matters happens in the gap between them.

The first is the one Cody stopped the clock on last week: the vat room, with Kaliphesta and Xirie — two Risen Runelords who asked the party questions at the Trial of Runes three sessions ago — plus two Pridespawn Cultists and four Unrisen that climb out of the vats. It runs an hour and three-quarters of table time and it is a grind in the honest sense. Two of the Unrisen come back from the dead. Kaliphesta comes back too — Mythic Ferocity, at 200 HP — so Paya has to kill her twice. Paya goes down and gets picked up. Aureys gets critically hit for 68, spends Correct the Story to reroll it, and then Sevek catches Foundry still resolving that rerolled attack against her unconscious AC; both applications end up reverted and she takes zero damage from the turn. Late on, Kaliphesta casts Quandary on Sevek and simply removes him from the battlefield for five minutes of real time. He passes his one escape check, needs a second, never gets another turn inside, and settles for announcing that he is "going to sit here and contemplate the meaning of life." Paya kills Kaliphesta, the spell breaks, and Sevek reappears mid-sentence: "Well, I guess meditation worked after all."

Then the party does something it has been unable to do for two sessions: it reads in peace. With the floor clear, Cody hands over the single biggest lore dump of the campaign and asks for no checks at all. Xanderghul no longer possesses Aethusa — he has built himself a new mortal body, and his soul can jump to Aethusa or to any Risen Runelord if that body dies. Aethusa is alive, imprisoned behind the hidden northeast wall, guarded by Shining Children bound to the runes. The Doomsday Doors connect to Jandelay — a demiplane assembled from the wreckage of destroyed worlds — and one is already open beneath Xin-Eurythnia. The party learns five more locations, learns that Xanderghul's return was arranged by the Ashen Man (a being from the Dimension of Time whose motives Xanderghul himself cannot work out), and learns that the Bittersweet Sisters have imprisoned the dream spirit Lirallaru — the Eighth Runelord of Mithtirith's thesis — in the Oubliette Plains in the realm of Leng. And they get the incantation for the door.

They rest, and Mithtirith reads the incantation on Arcana at mythic proficiency — natural 1, his own reroll, 44 — with Kreebnock aiding on Occultism. The wall opens onto pure emerald and hits them both with backlash, no save. Waiting inside, under a sphere of iridescent light, is Karzoug. Mithtirith greets a legend with the flattest possible insult, Cody misattributes it to Kreebnock, Kreebnock declines the credit, and Karzoug's expression sours at the right player. Three Shining Children manifest — and Karzoug promptly puts 195 points of his own Cataclysms into them. Aureys kills the first on an Opportune Backstab off Paya's crit, Sevek kills the second, Aureys kills the third. Kreebnock spends a Mythic Point to survive Cataclysm #2 and has nothing left for #3, which drops him; Mithtirith's Heal picks him up. The fight's hinge is a Trip. Sevek critically Grapples Karzoug (a natural 2 rerolled at mythic proficiency) for Restrained and Immobilized, then Trips him prone; Karzoug spends a Mythic Point purely to escape the grab, and the Stand provokes two reactions — Sevek's Stand Still crits on a natural 20 for 82 with Clumsy 2 and Enfeebled 2 attached, and Paya's Reactive Strike answers the same Stand. Karzoug's next Strike then misses by exactly the Enfeebled, and his Plasma Whirl eats Paya's second Reactive Strike for 48, landing by exactly the Clumsy. Sevek's Flurry takes him to 33 HP, Mithtirith's natural-20 Needle Darts lands on top, and rather than die he melts into prismatic ink, says "I've places to be," and leaves.

The sphere above him shatters like stained glass, and a woman falls out of it. Blonde, black dress, no possessions, unconscious — and they have all seen her face in visions under Xin-Cyrusian. Aethusa. She is not wounded; she is drained, enfeebled, stupefied and clumsy from a very long time without rest, and nothing the party has will fix it. Aureys proposes cutting her throat and Paya talks the table out of it with one sentence. Aureys then runs the interrogation anyway, with a threat and a swear — but it is Kreebnock's Request that turns her, and specifically his refusal to oversell: asked whether they dealt with Karzoug, he says "He fled from us."

The chapter ends there. Level 17. Then, with forty minutes left, the party teleports to Absalom and finally runs the sell-off it has been deferring since Session 7, walking away with 20,344 gp each.

The biggest lore dump of the campaign, and no checks required

  • Aureys makes the call that drives the rest of the session: "we still have to figure out what's on the other side of that secret door.   • Cody requires no checks at all — the dungeon is empty and the party has unlimited time.
  • Xanderghul no longer possesses Aethusa. He has created a new body and is mortal again — but his soul can inhabit Aethusa or any of the Risen Runelords if he dies. Killing him is a two-part problem.

  • The Ashen Man orchestrated Xanderghul's return — "a being from the Dimension of Time" with "an association with Jandelay," whose motivations are "a mystery even to Xanderghul."
  • Lirallaru is findable. The Thassilonian dream spirit of Mithtirith's thesis is imprisoned by the Bittersweet Sisters in the Oubliette Plains, in the realm of Leng. Xanderghul dismisses her as "a long-dead echo of a failure barely worth remembering."

Paya stops it with one sentence — "But that's true of all the Risen Runelords though, right?" — and Kreebnock adds the other side: "maybe they have something to help us against him." Tabled.

  • Mithtirith's Medicine check: no wounds, no disease. Drained, enfeebled, stupefied and clumsy — "the kind of condition one might be in if they were unable to gain any kind of restful sleep for a very long time… like muscular atrophy, almost." Cody is explicit it cannot be fixed at the table.
