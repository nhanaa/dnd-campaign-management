## Session Summary

Two fights, and everything that matters happens in the gap between them.

The first is the one Cody stopped the clock on last week: the vat room, with **Kaliphesta** and **Xirie** — two Risen Runelords who asked the party questions at the Trial of Runes three sessions ago — plus two Pridespawn Cultists and four **Unrisen** that climb out of the vats. It runs **an hour and three-quarters of table time** and it is a grind in the honest sense. The Unrisen have **Rise Again**: kill one with anything but fire and it stands back up at 100 HP on its own turn, which two of them do. Paya goes down and gets picked up. Aureys goes down and *un*-goes-down, spending **Correct the Story** to force a reroll of the critical hit that dropped her — and then Sevek catches that the follow-up attack was still being resolved against her unconscious AC, which turns a hit into a miss. Kaliphesta spends the back half of the fight throwing Eclipse Bursts into her own cultist, who survives every one of them; Paya, watching: *"This cultist just does not quit, huh? He's ride or die right now."* Late in the fight she casts **Quandary** on Sevek and simply removes him from the battlefield for five minutes of real time. He passes his one escape check, needs a second, never gets another turn inside, and settles for announcing that he is *"going to sit here and contemplate the meaning of life."* Paya kills Kaliphesta — **for the second time**, after she spent Mythic Ferocity to come back at 200 HP — the spell breaks, and Sevek reappears mid-sentence: *"Well, I guess meditation worked after all."* He kills Xirie five minutes later, and the fight ends.

Then the party does something it has been unable to do for two sessions: it reads in peace. With the floor finally clear, Cody hands over **the single biggest lore dump of the campaign** and asks for no checks at all. Xanderghul no longer possesses Aethusa — he has built himself a **new mortal body**, and his soul can jump to Aethusa or to any Risen Runelord if that body dies. Aethusa herself is alive, imprisoned somewhere in the Chambers behind the hidden northeast wall, guarded by creatures called **Shining Children** bound to the runes. The **Doomsday Doors** connect to **Jandelay** — a demiplane assembled from the wreckage of destroyed worlds — and one is already open beneath Xin-Eurythnia. The party learns the locations of five more. They learn that Xanderghul's return was arranged by the **Ashen Man**, a being from the Dimension of Time whose motives Xanderghul himself does not understand, and that the **Bittersweet Sisters** have imprisoned the dream spirit **Lirallaru** — the Eighth Runelord of Mithtirith's thesis — in the Oubliette Plains in the realm of Leng. And they get the incantation for the door.

They rest, and Mithtirith reads the incantation on Arcana at mythic proficiency — rolling a natural 1, rerolling it himself, and landing a 44 — with Kreebnock aiding on Occultism. The wall opens onto pure emerald and hits them both with mental backlash, no save. Waiting inside, under a sphere of iridescent light, is **Karzoug** — and the working assumption is that this is not the Runelord of Greed who cut them a deal three sessions ago, but one of Xanderghul's Risen wearing the name. ⚠️ **Nobody said that at the table.** Cody introduced him flatly as *"the last Runelord of greed, Karzoug,"* and the distinction has never been confirmed on-mic. Mithtirith greets a legend with the flattest possible insult — *"Karzoug has gone from being a Runelord and ruling an empire to being somebody else's lackey"* — Cody misattributes the line to Kreebnock, Kreebnock declines the credit (*"I'm being polite"*), and Karzoug's expression sours at the right player. Three Shining Children manifest — and Karzoug promptly puts **195 points of his own Cataclysms into them**, nearly a third of the first one's health bar. Aureys kills that one with an **Opportune Backstab** off Paya's crit inside two minutes, Sevek kills the second, Aureys kills the third. Kreebnock spends a Mythic Point to survive the second Cataclysm and has nothing left for the third, which drops him; Mithtirith's three-action Heal puts him back on his feet. The fight's hinge is a reaction: Karzoug pays a Mythic Point to escape Sevek's grapple, stands, and **Sevek's Stand Still crits on a natural 20 for 82**, disrupting the Stand and stamping Clumsy 2 and Enfeebled 2 on him — after which Karzoug's next Strike misses by exactly the Enfeebled and Paya's next hit lands by exactly the Clumsy. Sevek's Flurry takes him to **33 HP**, **Mithtirith's Needle Darts crit on a natural 20** lands on top of it, and rather than die he melts into prismatic ink, says *"I've places to be,"* and leaves.

The sphere above him shatters like stained glass, and a woman falls out of it. Blonde, black dress, no possessions, unconscious — and they have all seen her face in visions under Xin-Cyrusian. **Aethusa.** She is not wounded; she is drained, enfeebled, stupefied and clumsy from a very long time without rest, and nothing the party has will fix that. Aureys proposes cutting her throat, is talked out of it, and then handles the interrogation anyway with a threat and a swear (*"Because we're here trying to save the fucking world"*); it is **Kreebnock's Request** (Diplomacy 41) — *"We seek the same thing as you, which is to no longer have Xanderghul in this world"* — that actually turns her. She agrees to talk, on the condition they get her out of the room first.

The chapter ends there. **Level 17.** Then, with forty minutes left, the party teleports to Absalom and finally runs the sell-off it has been deferring since S7 — ten bec de corbins, five longswords, eleven sets of raiment leather, the gargantuan mace, an Aeon Stone, the Beloved's Bracelets — and walks away with **20,344 gp each.**

## Key Events

### Act I — The Vat Room (00:00–1:47)
- Combat opens already in progress from last session's cliffhanger. **Aureys Demoralizes a Pridespawn Cultist into fleeing** on the first action of the session; it runs 90 feet and spends most of the fight out of the picture before coming back.
- **Four Unrisen** clamber out of the steel vats. Mithtirith's Recall Knowledge on them is a **natural 1** — his own deadpan: *"Why am I not surprised?"*
- **Kaliphesta opens with Desiccate**, then an **Incarnate Tempest of Shades**, which Cody has to recast twice when Foundry's automation refuses to cooperate.
- **Xirie casts Sure Strike + a 9th-rank Disintegrate at Aureys.** Aureys converts a critical failure into a success with **Draconic Salvation**, which her build upgrades again — the save that keeps her alive. Cody, cheerfully: *"That's one fun thing about GMs in Mythic — death spells are fair game."*
- **Aureys drops to a Bec de Corbin crit for 68** and immediately spends a Mythic Point on **Correct the Story**, forcing the crit to be rerolled. It comes back a normal hit and she stays up. **Sevek then catches that the second attack was still being resolved against her unconscious AC** — *"Wait, hold on. That shouldn't hit, actually."* Cody checks, agrees, and it becomes a miss.
- **Paya goes down** under sustained Pridespawn and Unrisen pressure; **Mithtirith's 8th-rank Heal for 106** brings him back.
- The Unrisen's **Rise Again** turns the fight into a slog — killed by anything but fire, they return at 100 HP on their own turn. It fires three times. The party adapts by routing fire damage into every kill.
- **Sevek's Group Taunt** catches Unrisen D, Unrisen B and Pridespawn B in one action.
- **The Foundry Taunt bug is fixed, live, by Pax.** He works out that the effect has to be dragged from *his* card so the origin resolves correctly, tells Cody how, and it holds — **Cody, confirming on-mic mid-fight**: *"it seems like it works, it doesn't have the Taunt on it."*
- **Kaliphesta casts Quandary on Sevek** and removes him from the fight entirely. **He passes his one escape check** (Perception 42 vs DC 40 — *"on the right path. Normal success. That's one of two"*), needs a second success, never gets another turn inside, and declines to spend his remaining actions.
- **Paya kills Kaliphesta** with a MAP-10 halberd strike for 44. The Quandary collapses and **Sevek reappears**. Paya, underselling it: *"I didn't expect that to actually work. Usually I just hit my problems and nothing happens."*
- **Sevek kills Xirie** with an Inner Upheaval Flurry seven minutes later. Out of combat. Both Risen die within six minutes of each other after 105 minutes of fighting.

### Act II — The Library, Unguarded (2:02–2:11)
- The party loots two more bec de corbins and two sets of raiment leather off the Pridespawn, and Aureys searches the tables for reagents and coin.
- **Aureys makes the call that drives the rest of the session**: *"we still have to figure out what's on the other side of that secret door. So now that we've seemingly cleared out this place, we should probably go back up to that library and have some people research it."*
- Paya, on the difference from last session: *"This time we won't have to post a guard."* Cody requires **no checks** — the dungeon is empty and the party has unlimited time.
- **The research dump** (see Open Threads for the full list): Xanderghul's new mortal body and soul-jumping; Aethusa's imprisonment and the Shining Children; the Doomsday Doors and Jandelay; the **Ashen Man**; **Lirallaru** imprisoned by the **Bittersweet Sisters** in the Oubliette Plains, realm of Leng; the incantation for the hidden wall; and deployment plans that would let the party **intercept the Risen before the doors open** — if they can call in allies.
- **Aureys draws the strategic conclusion**: *"it sounds like we should definitely kill Aethusa, because while that wizard lives, we cannot fully kill [Xanderghul]."* Paya points out that's true of every Risen; Aureys' counter is that Aethusa is *"not just a prisoner, they're a potential vessel."* Kreebnock argues the other way — *"maybe they have something to help us against him"* — and the party tables it.
- **The party takes a full night's rest**, undisturbed, in the defensible southern rooms with the secret door blocked.

### Act III — Opening the Emerald Door (2:16–2:22)
- Aureys eats **Unrisen leftovers for breakfast** (*"it tastes like refried beans"* / *"Like a breakfast burrito"*), which sets off Sevek's running backwater-town bit — *"Burrito? What's that? I've never heard of it."* / *"Is that a food from where you're from?… I would love to try one of that."*
- **Mithtirith reads the incantation on Arcana at mythic proficiency**, with **Kreebnock aiding on Occultism** for +2. He rolls a **natural 1**, **rerolls it himself**, and lands a **44**. (Aureys suggested the reroll; the resource was his.)
- The wall opens onto a chamber of **pure emerald**. Magical backlash hits both of them with no save — **33 mental to Mithtirith, 25 to Kreebnock**, whose Energy Aegis absorbed the difference.
- A voice from inside, before anyone rounds the corner: *"I'm surprised anyone's made it in here. And you're not who should be allowed in here… I'd like to at least look at the eyes of those I'm about to destroy."* Paya and Sevek walk straight around the corner. Paya: *"Because we're good at following instructions, I guess."*

### Act IV — Karzoug (2:22–3:13)
- Cody's reveal: *"Before you stands the last Runelord of greed, Karzoug"* — the name the modern age knows from twenty years ago.
- **Mithtirith's opener**: *"Wow, how the mighty have fallen. Karzoug has gone from being a Runelord and ruling an empire to being somebody else's lackey."* Cody attributes it to Kreebnock; Kreebnock corrects him (*"I'm being polite"*); Cody re-aims the reaction at the right player.
- **Three Shining Children** manifest — the same faces the party saw watching from inside the portal window last session.
- **Karzoug opens with Cataclysm**, catching one of his own Shining Children. Aureys converts her save to a critical success with Draconic Salvation. Cody, in character: *"You're all still in one piece. Impressive. We'll see how many more of those you can take."*
- **Aureys kills Shining Child B** with an **Opportune Backstab** reaction off Paya's double crit — the first kill of the fight, inside two minutes.
- **Karzoug counterspells Mithtirith's Heal with Nullify**, taking 5d8 to zap it out of the air.
- **Sevek kills Shining Child C** with an Electric Arc into a Flurry of Blows.
- **Kreebnock survives Cataclysm #2 at 26 HP** by spending a Mythic Point on **Divert Destiny** — which leaves him nothing when **Cataclysm #3 drops him.** Cody, unprompted: *"You were not the main target of that ire, but your dice do not like you."* **Mithtirith's three-action Heal picks him up**, and Kreebnock narrates his own revival — *"The twisted remains of Kreebnock spring back to life"* — then tops off with an Aeon Stone and a Potion of Greater Healing, retreats out of line of sight, and keeps casting.
- **Aureys kills Shining Child A**, and Karzoug gains **quickened**: *"Now that we've gotten the rabble out of the way."*
- **Sevek Grapples Karzoug on a critical success** — natural 2, rerolled at mythic proficiency for a 19 → **Restrained and Immobilized** — and then **Trips him prone.**
- **Karzoug burns a Mythic Point purely to escape the grab**, then has to Stand — **which provokes both Sevek's Stand Still (natural 20, 82, Clumsy 2 + Enfeebled 2) and Paya's Reactive Strike.** His answering Strike then misses by exactly the Enfeebled, and his Plasma Whirl eats **Paya's second Reactive Strike** for 48, landing by exactly the Clumsy.
- **Mithtirith asks for a two-action spell that reaches 35 feet**, the table answers *"Needle Darts,"* and he rolls a **natural 20** — a critical hit for 28 plus bleed that puts Karzoug at **33 HP (6%)**.
- **Karzoug does not die.** *"This has been most entertaining, and thank you all for relieving me of that monotony. But I've places to be."* Where the other Risen vanish into light, he **melts into prismatic ink** and is gone. Mithtirith takes 2 Mythic Points, everyone else 1.

### Act V — Aethusa (3:13–3:24)
- The iridescent sphere overhead **shatters like stained glass**, and a woman falls out of it: blonde, black dress, no possessions, unconscious. The face from the Xin-Cyrusian visions. **Aethusa.**
- **Mithtirith's Medicine check**: no wounds, no disease. **Drained, enfeebled, stupefied and clumsy** — the state of someone who has gone a very long time without restful sleep, plus muscular atrophy. Cody is explicit that this is not curable at the table: *"it's not about curing conditions right now. It's about whether you want to wake her."*
- **Aureys**: *"Or we could slit her throat, just saying."* **Mithtirith**: *"That seems problematic, even for you."*
- The party heals up first, then wakes her. She comes to like a bad hangover: *"Oh, who in the hells are you all? You're not Runelords, are —"*
- **Aureys handles it with a threat**: *"We are their slayers. We've come to free you, or kill you, whichever you prefer. That'll be determined by how cooperative you are."* Aethusa calls the bluff — *"Do you have any idea what I've been through down here?"*
- **Kreebnock closes it** with a **Request** (Diplomacy 41): *"We seek the same thing as you, which is to no longer have Xanderghul in this world."* Asked whether they dealt with Karzoug, he answers honestly — *"He fled from us"* — and it is the honesty that lands: *"Well, then I suppose you can't be completely full of it. Then maybe we can work together."*
- Her one condition: **get her out of this place first.** The party leaves the Crypt of Runes for the surface.
- **Chapter complete. The party reaches level 17.**

### Act VI — Absalom (3:24–3:34)
- Paya runs the **sell-off the party has deferred since S7**, offering every item round the table before selling it.
- **20,344 gp distributed to each PC.**
- Level 17 picks land at the table: **Aureys** to legendary Thievery and **Agile Mind** (a successful Will save becomes a critical success); **Sevek** to **legendary Acrobatics** (*"I'm no longer off-guard while I'm prone"*) with **master Reflex** arriving automatically off Canny Acumen; **Paya** to master AC; **Kreebnock** to legendary Will. **Sevek raises Apex items first** (*"Are we at the level we can get Apex items?"*) and Mithtirith asks the follow-up about pushing an attribute past 20.

- Cody closes with table news: the prospective sixth player **no-showed their first session** and is out.
