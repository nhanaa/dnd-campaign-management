Session Summary

The plan from last week held. At the base of Spindlehorn, an enormous needle-like tower that "almost seems to sway in the breeze" ("You'll be climbing up there later"), Kreebnock read the party's Umbral Journey scroll and the world went grey. In the Netherworld the Storval Deep was a graveyard of ash and bone, and an iron gate swung in perfect silence over a bottomless pit. Down a staircase thousands of feet deep, three Urveth (gargantuan flying undead worms with human arms) came up out of the dark. One caught Aureys in its jaws on a natural 20 and held her there through a failed Escape and a Mythic Point reroll that came up a natural 1, until Sevek Shoved it off her. Paya killed all three, each on a Timeflaying Blade crit.

At the bottom the party stepped back into the Material Plane, inside the buried Prosperity Vault, and the room's guardians manifested: eight wolf-headed Adamantine Sentinels. It was a six-round brawl. Kreebnock hasted the party, Aureys and Sevek both opened with Majestic Presence, and Paya's hasted Whirlwind Strike landed four crits in one turn, then took the most punishment at the table and went down before Kreebnock's Soothe brought him back. Aureys killed three, Paya two, and Sevek, Kreebnock and Mithtirith one each; Cody: "You overkilled 90% of them."

After the break, a stairwell down, and three people waiting: Risen Runelord Aethusa of Gluttony, Jurah of Envy, and Alaznist of Wrath with her fiery ranseur ("No further. Your little dungeon delve ends here."). Aureys answered for the party: "Well, I am Aureys Hollow, greatest dragon of Golarion. You could surrender if you like." Sevek's Celestial Armaments crit blinded Aethusa for four rounds, she spent her Undying Myth on a Paya crit, and Aureys finished her. Paya's Reactive Strike crit Jurah mid-Haste, and the spell never took hold; then Alaznist killed her own ally with an Arctic Rift he critically failed (Kreebnock: "Your lack of faith disturbs her"). Kreebnock Nullified her Eclipse Burst, Aureys forced her Undying Myth and survived her answer at 23 HP, and Paya, sent in by Mithtirith ("Let me send in someone much better than me at killing people") and a Kreebnock Message, struck the blow that made her "vanish into light."

Deeper in, the vault itself: black statues of a gold-eyed man, claw-gouged tunnels, and a plug of solid gold blocking the way north (Mithtirith: "That seems inflationary"). Four Goldgorgers, elementals of whirling gold, burst out of the walls. Sevek's Glory on High blinded all three in range, and not one of their melee hits got through the flat checks after that. Paya killed two, Kreebnock's Daze a third, and Aureys's Opportune Backstab the last. They fell to piles of gold shards, and Aureys started picking them up. No game next week; the penultimate session is November 14.
