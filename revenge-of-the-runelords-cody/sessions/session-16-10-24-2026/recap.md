## Session Summary

Belimarius' **spellbook** was mostly a cipher that would take months to break, but the notes at the back were plain. She had been hunting for a way to reach Sorshen, and she believed **Sorshen knew how to reach the Apocalypse Archive**, Jandelay. The answer was in **Sorshen's archives**, deep under the Blood Moon Palace. After a sale (10,642 gp each) and a shopping run in Absalom, where Aureys turned into a huge dragon and strutted down the street with Paya on her shoulder, the party went down.

The archives were Sorshen's private wing: closets, guest rooms, baths, a kitchen staffed by polite constructs, and a great deal of very expensive furniture. **Aureys robbed it**, cheerfully and in character: the clothes, the chandeliers, Sorshen's desk, an art vault and a vault of antiques worth about 30,000 gp. The table went along with the chandeliers and pushed back at the desk and the vaults, and Cody warned her twice (*"there is no situation in which Aureys gets away with this"*). Her search also turned up **Sorshen's Scintillating Garment**, the Runelord's own apex robes, behind a false wall, and **Kreebnock** "borrowed" them. Two fights interrupted the looting. Six **Abysium Sentinels** came off the hallway statues and were destroyed in six rounds; **Sevek took three of them**, Paya two, Aureys one. Then a **Malfunctioning Perfumery**, a complex mechanical hazard whose disable checks turned out to be **DC 47–50**, beat the party up for seven rounds. It confused Paya into swinging at Sevek ten times, missing every one, and it took Mithtirith to 8 hit points. Nobody disabled it; they broke it, and **Sevek landed the last hit**.

Deeper in, they met **Astelaur**, Sorshen's four-eyed cat, who had been waiting weeks for *"mother"* to come back, and who was not at all frightened of Belimarius but very worried about Xanderghul. Then came a week of research in the library. **Gimmel**, the third Runelord of Greed, once summoned the **Oliphaunt of Jandelay** to Golarion at **Spindlehorn**. When it left, it planted an **apocalypse seed** in the ground, most likely deep under the tower in the sealed **Prosperity Vault**, if it exists at all. The way down is through the **Netherworld**, where Spindlehorn's mirror is a pit; Umbral Journey or Interplanar Teleport gets you there, and Sorshen's collection had scrolls of both. With the seed and Lissala's runes, the party could repeat Gimmel's ritual. Next week: the Prosperity Vault. Cody thinks three sessions remain.

### Act VII — A Week of Research (3:24–3:38)
- Cody: the books, the office and **the relics Aureys had hoarded** were all relevant. Aureys: ***"See? There was a reason I was collecting everything."*** The better part of a week in luxury. Kreebnock: *"I hang out with a cat half the time."* Astelaur: ***"You know Mother's going to be very, very angry at the little dragon."*** Aureys: *"We haven't sold anything yet."* Cody: *"If you do, God help you."* In Foundry chat, Kreebnock put on 80s music for the research montage.
- **What they learned** (in order of research points):
  - **Gimmel, the third Runelord of Greed, conjured the Oliphaunt of Jandelay** during a war between Thassilon and Azlant, **−6301 AR**. It had never been drawn to a world that wasn't facing imminent destruction. His method was lost.
  - **Jandelay** is a demiplane at the centre of the Maelstrom, *"the metaphorical and literal center of the Great Beyond,"* built of fragments of every city, country, planet or plane an apocalypse destroys. The Oliphaunt is its most legendary inhabitant; the rare 10th-rank mythic **Summon Oliphaunt of Jandelay** calls a brief manifestation. **No planar keys for Interplanar Teleport are known to exist for it.**
  - **The Watchers**: gargantuan one-eyed beings who revere the Oliphaunt, follow it across the planes, and collect the lost places. **The party has seen them**: clinging to the Kodar peaks like massive spiders as the people of Xin-Eurythnia were led down the mountains (the "watchers" of S4). Sevek: *"Yeah, we saw them before. Creepy."*
  - **Gimmel's ritual site was Spindlehorn**, and Sorshen believes it split Golarion's fate into **multiple timelines**: in this one, the Oliphaunt drove back Azlant and returned to Jandelay; in another, **Alaznist** pulled it forward into modern **Korvosa** and it ended Varisia; in a third, it destroyed Thassilon early.
  - **Spindlehorn** was built by Gimmel in **−6313 AR**, ostensibly to look the Oliphaunt in the eye, really because it was the only place such a conjuration could be performed. ***The Epic of Jandelay*** is inscribed at its base.
  - It stands on the **Prosperity Vault**, an arcane ritual chamber built by the first Runelords of Greed after **Shalast** was founded, to turn rock and mud into gold. Gimmel raised Spindlehorn out of it, sealing every entrance and turning its magic outward into an amplifier for *"world-piercing rituals."*
  - ⭐ **The apocalypse seed**: when the Oliphaunt left, it planted *"something terrible behind… as a promise of future devastation."* If it can leave something behind, it can take something back: a theoretical way into Jandelay. *"No one has ever recovered this apocalypse seed, if indeed it even exists"*; it is most likely buried under Spindlehorn's roots.
  - ⭐ **The route**: Spindlehorn's mirror in the **Netherworld** is a pit. With **Umbral Journey** or **Interplanar Teleport**, a traveller can enter the Netherworld, descend the pit, and come out on Golarion **2,000 feet underground, inside the Prosperity Vault**, where the seed most likely is.
  - **Ancient sources believed it was Lissala**, the Thassilonian rune goddess, who led Gimmel to Jandelay. The party learned **her secret runes**, which with an object of the Oliphaunt's (like the seed) could **replicate Gimmel's conjuration**.
  - **Sorshen's own research**: what happens if she meets an alternate version of herself from another timeline. She doubted two could coexist, and feared she'd have to kill the other or both would perish. (Cody: *"There are some spoilers in there, ignore that."*)
- **The plan**, as Aureys summed it up: get under Spindlehorn through the Netherworld, find the apocalypse seed, and repeat Gimmel's ritual; Sorshen's collection had **scrolls of both Umbral Journey and Interplanar Teleport**. Mithtirith wanted to be sure of the way back; Paya: *"With interplanar teleport, you can always go to the plane you were just on."*
