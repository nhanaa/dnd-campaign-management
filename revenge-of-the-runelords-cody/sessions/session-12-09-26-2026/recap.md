## Session Summary

The session opens with homework. Aethusa — more lucid now, still in no shape to fight — piles a table in the party's rented Absalom quarters with books and scrolls, and Cody runs a three-day **research subsystem** on *Xanderghul's Immortality*. It is the party's weakest arena and everyone says so: Mithtirith (*"Oh, Mithtirith is so excited, which means the rolls will be low"*) burns three Mythic Points on it, Sevek declares himself *"definitely not book smart"* and brute-forces it on Perception — his day-one 50 is what crosses the first threshold — and after one failed reroll Cody observes that the table has *"found the party's weakness. Books."* They cap it out anyway. What they learn: **Aethusa and the Risen Runelords are Xanderghul's soul cages**, and because Aethusa is a living person rather than something woven from dream-stuff, Xanderghul must have made **a duplicate of her in the Dreamlands** to use her as a cage. Kill the duplicate and his link to her is cut. Her real body can be the **focus** for a planar teleport straight to where the copy is hiding, and the last of the research turns up a **scroll of Interplanar Teleport**. Meanwhile the allies the party called in last session are paying off: a Doomsday Door was shut in old **Xin-Edasseril** (the Risen tried to open it on **Belimarius'** turf and *"she didn't take too kindly to it"*), and the doors at **Windsong Abbey** and **Sekhnavali** are secured. The real Aethusa's send-off: *"Burn her for all I care. Just don't take too much pleasure in it."*

Mithtirith casts the teleport, and the party lands on the edge of **Cyrusian** — a tidy village of a few dozen houses in a forest clearing, ringed by a wall of black trees, where every inhabitant looks human and looks at the party like they have never seen a pineapple or a dragon before. **Aureys runs the investigation from the front**: the barkeep **Thomas** explains that the village's *"Lord Mayor"* — a blonde woman — flies in every sunset to collect tribute of mushrooms and produce, and in exchange the nightmares in the forest stay away. Kreebnock sketches her and Thomas confirms it. The **old** Lord Mayor turns out to be **Simon Cradle**, a bow-tied bookkeeper from the **Boston Library** who dreams his way to Cyrusian between shifts to work on a typewriter — and who figures out, unprompted, that the party is here physically rather than dreaming. He mentions that the new Lord Mayor rides *"a big dragon… all green and purple,"* and Sevek calls it before anyone else: *"Sounds exactly like a Mirage Dragon."* Aureys' Dragon Lore confirms it's an **ancient** one. Aureys asks Simon to get the villagers into their basements, and then — as soon as he leaves — steals his typewriter (*"Gonna be a centerpiece of my hoard"*).

At sunset **Dream Aethusa** arrives on the dragon's back with a rune-engraved bec de corbin. Aureys tries to talk; Dream Aethusa calls it *"sickening optimism"* and draws steel. The fight runs **an hour and a quarter of table time** with a break in the middle. She opens with **Cataclysm and a quickened Eclipse Burst** in the same turn, and the dragon's Hallucinatory Breath confuses Paya and Aureys — Aureys spends her confused turn hitting Sevek (*"I'm just taking a beating"*) until Mithtirith's Restorative Channel counteracts Paya's before the fighter can do the same to him. The turn of the fight is **Kreebnock's Falling Sky**, which grounds the dragon; after the break, Aureys' Earth to Heavens Strike stupefies it, Paya's Nightmare rune stacks Frightened on top, and **Paya kills it with back-to-back crits**. Kreebnock's **Weight of the World** stuns Aethusa, on the heels of a Bon Mot. Sevek lays into her with **Herald's Weapon** (87 in two hits), **Bear Hugs** her into a grab, and when she swings at him, **Rippling Spin trips her** — and she burns a Mythic Point to shake off prone. Paya finishes her.

She doesn't die cleanly. She tears apart into tricolored light and becomes the **Trinity Star** — a comet with three tails, a hazard that blinds on a bad Fortitude save and dives through the party for 10d6 to 20d6 force. You can't hurt it (Paya's Vicious Swing doesn't get through its hardness); you have to **pull its tails off**. **Sevek** yanks the first with Athletics, **Mithtirith** takes the second on Arcana (with Sevek's Guidance lifting a 44 to the 45 he needed), and **Paya** siphons the last away with a Nature check of **50**.

In the quiet afterward, a woman's voice: *"Find me. I'm here. Leng. The Amaranthine Oubliette. Come find me, please. It's **Lirallaru**. I can help you."* Cody confirms at the table that Lirallaru is *"the person you met at the beginning of the campaign"* — met *"in our dreams"* — and Paya adds, uncontradicted, *"She gave us our mythic power."* Simon returns, forgives the typewriter (*"it was my old one anyway"*), trades Aureys stories and a gold coin for paper-money trivia, and explains the trick: because the party is in the Dreamlands physically, they can **dream their way to someone they know** — fall asleep focusing on them. They pitch the **Dream Hunter's Lodge** they took off Dream Aethusa, sleep, and roll for it. Only one of five succeeds: **Sevek**, on Religion. They wake on the purple-gray plains of **Leng**, under an iron spindle-prison hanging hundreds of feet above an inverted-pyramid pit, and Lirallaru's voice again: *"I am at the top. Watch out for the Bittersweet Sisters."*

The gate opens for them as they fly up — Aureys: *"I think I should be worried that they're inviting us in"* — and inside, three **Elder Shantaks** uncoil. **That fight belongs to Sevek.** He opens by critically Tripping one and Taunting all three; when that one stands, **Stand Still crits on a natural 20 for 82 and blinds it**, and the blind makes its follow-up critical hit on Paya miss its flat check outright. Rippling Spin crit-trips another. Then, with Cody having just announced that *"Paya's not the main attack roll culprit. It's Sevek this game"*: Inner Upheaval, Flurry, **four attacks, four hits** — **269 damage in one turn**, taking the last Shantak from 303 to 34. Paya, watching: *"Didn't move, hit with all four attacks from a monk. That's crazy."* Aureys kills two of them, Paya finishes the third.

A door creaks in the chamber's southern outbuilding, and a very tall hooded figure with **the head of a horse**, glowing yellow eyes, and **a cage over its face** steps out: *"Come here. We have much to talk about. I know who you've come to seek. And I can tell you what's waiting for you."*

End of session.

### Act II — Cyrusian (0:33–1:12)
- **Mithtirith casts the teleport**. The party arrives outside **Cyrusian**: a few dozen neat wooden houses in an enormous clearing, a perfectly circular wall of 50-foot black trees, and darkness beyond. Every villager is human and stares in quiet bewilderment. Cody: *"Welcome to the Dreamlands, everybody."*
- Paya, on the prospect of a dream tavern: *"I wonder what a dream drink tastes like. Tastes like nostalgia."* To villagers who have never seen a talking pineapple: *"I'm well aware that we're delicious, as a rule."*
- **Aureys leads the investigation.** *"All good adventures start at a tavern."* She orders *"some nostalgia, if you've got that on tap,"* then works the barkeep **Thomas**: the **Lord Mayor** is a blonde woman who flies in every sunset for tribute and flies off over the trees; in exchange the forest's monsters stay away. **Kreebnock sketches her** from Thomas' description (Cody: *"You're an artist, Kreebnock"*) and Thomas confirms the likeness. Kreebnock asks about the dark forest — and, later, where the Lord Mayor lands (the town center): *"A lot of nightmares roam that way… Step into that forest, you're likely to get turned around or eaten."*
- **Simon Cradle**, the old Lord Mayor: late twenties, sandy blond, white shirt, black vest, bow tie, clacking away at a black box. On meeting Paya: *"A person with a pineapple for a head?"* — Paya: *"Common mistake. My whole personage is a pineapple. My head is just the fronds."* Simon is a **bookkeeper at the Boston Library** who comes here between shifts to write; Aethusa took the ceremonial title from him and he didn't argue. Aureys: *"Is that on Golarion?"* — *"I can't say I'm familiar with Golarion."* Mithtirith and Kreebnock both roll Recall Knowledge on "Boston, Massachusetts" and come up empty; Sevek: *"Maybe is this some kind of ancient place that's no longer in any books?"*
- **Simon clocks the party**: *"You all, you're not asleep right now, are you? You're here here."* Sevek: *"What made you say that?"* Most visitors only come when they dream; you have to be very powerful to arrive in body.
- **The dragon**: *"a big dragon… all green and purple."* Aureys: *"Bigger than me?… Really, that's not possible."* **Sevek calls it first**: *"Green and purple. Sounds like a Mirage Dragon. Sounds exactly like a Mirage Dragon."* Aureys' **Dragon Lore** confirms: an **ancient** Mirage Dragon, Huge. Paya: *"Last time we saw one of them, it ended on amicable terms."*

Well, you have to answer for that later."* Kreebnock: *"You know how hard it is to find a typewriter in the Dreamlands?"* Cody adds **Simon's Typewriter** (2 Bulk, 1,500 gp, *"an artifact from another world"*). Aureys: *"Gonna be a centerpiece of my hoard."*

### Act IV — The Trinity Star (2:33–2:46)
- Aethusa's body becomes **the Trinity Star** — a comet with three blazing tails. **Starform** on initiative: DC 40 Fortitude or dazzled (blinded permanently on a crit fail). Mithtirith fails and is dazzled; everyone else saves. Mithtirith: *"It looks like she's doing the hula hoop."*
- **Routine**: it flies 60 feet through as many creatures as it can — Reflex DC 44, 10d6 force on a success, 20d6 and prone on a failure. **Kreebnock's Scintillating Safeguard** takes 10 off for the allies in range.
- **You can't hit it**: Paya's Recall Knowledge is a natural 4, and his Vicious Swing doesn't get through the hazard's hardness. **Aureys' Recall Knowledge** finds the method — break it, or **remove its tails**, using the magical-tradition skills or Athletics. Her own Arcana misses; Kreebnock's Occultism rolls a 2.
- **Sevek takes the first tail** — frees a hand, then Athletics **51**. **Mithtirith takes the second** — Arcana 44, one short, and Cody points out he hadn't used **the Guidance Sevek had just cast on him**; with it, 45. **Paya takes the third** — Nature **50** — *"this thing bursts into a prismatic spray of light and vanishes."* Paya +2 Mythic Points, everyone else +1.
