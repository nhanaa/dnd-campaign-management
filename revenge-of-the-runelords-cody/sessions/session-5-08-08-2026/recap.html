Session Summary

The evacuation ends where it began: with Ayavah, gratitude, and an unanswered question about Sorshen. Ten thousand Eurythnians reach Vekr's Crossing with 98 of 100 Survival Points intact — enough that their culture will survive, with talk of one day returning home. Krune's retrieval belt yields three prizes that shape the whole session: a mythic Wand of Never Mind, a crystal ball, and an engraved star chart that Mithtirith identifies as the flight path of the Trinity Star — a map to where it landed. And, as Mithtirith is first to note: whoever engraved the chart could have followed it too.

The party flies to Kandirvek's waterfall-hidden lair, a collector's hoard that has Aureys physically knitting her fingers together to keep from stealing. The omen dragon unfurls his wings and shows them visions of their own possible futures — riding his back over the mountains, fighting green-skinned giants, descending into a ruined city in the Darklands realm of Narvoth, prying open a portal above the Rune Well of Pride — until a regal man in peacock-feather robes steps out of that last vision, sneers, waves a hand, and shuts it down, spiking a psychic migraine through a centuries-old dragon. Something looked back. Kandirvek names the site of the first two visions — the Throne of the Troll King, a legendary Kodar location a few hundred miles away — and the star chart confirms the Trinity Star landed right beside it. Two reasons to go. He flies them there himself, pausing mid-journey for the party to carve through two mythic, dream-eating natbakhs while he handles a third.

At the throne — a 40-foot stone chair on a heat-blasted hill, ringed by outward-facing bone totems — waits Gavergash, a mythic troll spellcaster with an 8th-rank arcane arsenal, flanked by four rune-axed troll warleaders. The parley is brief ("I see no dragon here, just a puffed up lizard" — "Ah, those are fighting words, buddy"), the fight long and genuinely dangerous: Eclipse Burst, Desiccate, Chain Lightning, and Frigid Flurry rain down at DC 39 while the warleaders hunt the healer. Paya eats a 100-point Chain Lightning crit-fail and later kills three warleaders in a single turn; Sevek trips the troll king twice, remembers his Mortal Herald self-Heal at last, and drops Gavergash with a Herald's-Weapon-wreathed, Inner-Upheaval flurry he'd saved the entire fight — without spending a single Mythic Point.

Then the twist: the throne Aureys has been loudly claiming all fight is hollow — an illusion concealing an 800-foot pit. The descent plunges the party into A Falling Star's Memories, a complex hazard that hurls their minds through the Trinity Star's voyage through space. Stupefied values climb; Mithtirith hits Stupefied 5 and is ejected through a vision of the crash itself for 81 damage; Aureys and Sevek spend every Mythic Point they have on mythic-proficiency Thievery checks — Sevek's from a base of untrained — to cut the illusion apart. At the bottom waits a buried city lit by ghost-light, a fresh 250-foot crater of pure iron shards, and a single line of glowing footprints walking away from the wreckage. Paya and Aureys name the ruin: Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul, thought lost at Earthfall. Something rode the Trinity Star down. Next session: the Book 1 finale.

Timeline

Act I · Roleplay · Farewells at Vekr's Crossing

98 of 100 — a civilization saved

  • Krune's retrieval belt survives his death: inside are a mythic Wand of Never Mind ("a mean Xanderghul spell, if I've ever seen one" — Paya), a crystal ball, and an engraved star chart.
  • Mithtirith identifies the star chart: it plots the Trinity Star's path across the sky one year ago — and can be used to find where it landed. His immediate follow-up: whoever made the chart could have done the same.
  • The refugees arrive: 98/100 Survival Points. Xin-Eurythnia's society will hold together; talk of reclaiming the city is popular, and the culture survives thanks to the party.
  • Ayavah thanks them — and confirms there has been no word from Sorshen since the sinkhole. Sevek: "That's a problem."
  • Ayavah stays to manage the refugees and offers herself as a standing ally: "If you ever have need of an ally in the Saga Lands, the Kodars, or just anywhere, you can call on me." Paya: "We appreciate it, and we will not forget it."
  • Mithtirith claims the Wand of Wall of Force and the retrieval belt — the party's S4 "no arcane caster" problem is quietly becoming his job.
Act II · Visions · The Omen Dragon's Lair

Four futures on a dragon's wings — and something looking back

  • Behind a waterfall: a collector's hoard of astrolabes, gemstones, and knickknacks. Aureys visibly knits her little fingers together to keep from nicking things; asked whether the refugees made it, she gives the session's one dead-serious answer: "They are my brothers."
  • Kandirvek reintroduces himself: an omen dragon, a scholar centuries in the Kodars. Since Aroden's death dragonkind lost perfect foresight, but he dreams potential futures — and he has dreamt of the party, over and over, fighting Thassilon's legacy.
  • The wing visions (4 Perception successes): ① the party riding Kandirvek, consulting the star chart; ② battle with green-skinned giants — Mithtirith IDs powerful trolls; ③ descent into a ruined city in a vast cavern — the Darklands realm of Narvoth; ④ magic prying open a portal above a shattered stone well — the Rune Well of Pride.
  • The fourth vision is interrupted: a regal man in peacock-feather robes steps out of it, sneers, and waves it shut — striking Kandirvek with a psychic spike mid-trance. "Something looked back through the vision." Whoever it was casts magic powerful enough to reach backward through prophecy.
  • Mithtirith checks the wincing dragon over (Medicine): an extreme mental spike, painful but no lasting harm — and no known anti-scrying ward works like that.
  • Kandirvek names the location: the Throne of the Troll King — legend says any troll who sits it one full night undisturbed rules all Kodar trolls for a year. The star chart puts the Trinity Star's landing site right beside it. Two reasons to visit.
  • Mithtirith, accepting the flight: "I think fate has said we've already accepted your offer and flown there for your visions."
  • Shopping via teleportation circle (level-14 access; 1,865 gp each after loot-master Kelsie's great sell-off). Aureys talks the dragon out of his most expensive portable "bobble" — the Black Pearl Aeon Stone. Sevek buys his long-planned Greater Resilient rune. Rebuilds: Mithtirith → Hedge Mage; Aureys → Living Nexus + Draconic Acolyte (picked before learning what kind of dragon Kandirvek was).
Act III · Journey · Three Days on Dragonback

Wyverns below, natbakhs above, a thesis by firelight

  • Day one is uneventful; that evening Kandirvek shares what his visions told him of the party's past — several of them delved the pit at Sandpoint a year ago.
  • Paya recounts the false Runelord to the dragon: "We met one who claimed to be a Runelord. Left her at the bottom of the pit, gladder for it, once the real one appeared in our dreams."
  • Mithtirith offers his thesis-in-progress on the Eighth Runelord — "the one who almost was a Runelord," the Runelord of Vainglory — a name even a centuries-old omen dragon has never heard.
  • Midday two: three bat-like horrors bigger than Kandirvek inbound — natbakhs, mythic dream-eaters not native to the Material Plane. He takes one solo and drops the party on a cliff for the other two. (See Combat.)
  • The natbakhs' bone-strewn lair yields 1,700 gp, a Greater Sturdy Shield, and Greater Boots of Bounding (Sevek: "I'm planning to zoom around").
  • Paya cooks dinner over the bone pile — "As weird as this party has made me, I season the bones so she gets the healing" — while Aureys eats them. Kandirvek's verdict: "You all fight well. I can see how you've given the Risen so much trouble."
Act IV · Combat · The Throne of the Troll King

"Who dares approach the throne of Gavergash?"

  • Day three: a flat-topped hill crowned by a 40-foot stone throne, ringed by bone totems that face outward, on a slab blasted to glass-sheen — but the throne itself pristine.
  • Atop it: Gavergash, mythic troll caster in emerald robes wielding a sparking bec de corbin, four rune-axed warleaders below. The party's mythic sense pings: he has mythic power too.
  • Kandirvek plays "bad cop," drawing the region's trolls away so the fight stays clean; Aureys appoints herself good cop: "We're like a buddy team, you and I. Two dragons." — "Fighting against the world." — "Makes the dream work."
  • The parley, in its entirety: "You've never met a dragon like Aureys Hollow, have you?" — "I see no dragon here, just a puffed up lizard." — "Ah, those are fighting words, buddy."
  • Sevek's post-mortem, three seconds into initiative: "I guess there's no talking then." Paya: "I mean, we wanted his chair. He was never going to be amenable to it." (See Combat.)
Act V · Hazard · The Hollow Throne

An 800-foot pit wearing a chair as a lid

  • Aureys, finally sitting her prize, notices the throne feels hollow. Dispelling the illusion reveals the truth: the throne is a lid over an 800-foot sheer pit.
  • Mithtirith, carefully: "This looks like a pit and not that well we were trying to open, right?" — correct. Very distinct from the vision.
  • The descent (Mithtirith carried by Sevek and Paya) plunges everyone into supernatural darkness — limbs pinned, hurtling at impossible speed through a void full of stars.
  • A Falling Star's Memories: the Trinity Star's own centuries-long voyage, relived from inside. Stupefied stacks; the illusion must be picked apart from within. (See Combat.)
  • Aureys, mid-clarity: this is the path the Trinity Star took through the cosmos before it crashed into Golarion.
Act VI · Reveal · Xin-Cyrusian

The city of pride, and the footprints that walked away

  • The pit bottoms out in an immense cavern: ceiling lights like stars 200 feet up, ruins glowing with ghostly radiance, a city crushed into a chamber not quite big enough for it.
  • At its heart, a fresh 250-foot crater heaped with iron shards — the Trinity Star was iron, all of it. Whatever it hit was obliterated almost completely.
  • Aureys' deduction from the rubble: the crater's blast came from what the star struck, not the impact itself.
  • A 50-foot path of melted stone runs east, marked by glowing footprints — as if someone stepped out of the wreckage and simply walked away. Paya: "Somebody was in the Trinity Star?"
  • Paya and Aureys (with a Mythic Point) place the ruin from legend: Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul, thought destroyed at Earthfall.
  • Almost no real time passed inside the illusion — but the thing that left those footprints has had a year's head start.

Combat Encounters

Encounter 1 · Flight Day 2 · 4 rounds

Natbakhs ×2

Enemies2× natbakh — mythic dream-eaters, AC 33–34, Nightmare Screech 10d8 mental, jaws +29 w/ 2d6 bleed, Drain Dreams, Mythic Resilience
KillsPaya ×2 — Vicious Swing crits of 110 and 102
Low pointMithtirith — 93 mental across two failed screeches
Mythic pointsSevek 1 (Become Destiny) · Aureys 1 (counteract reroll)

Round 1 — three screeches incoming

  • Kandirvek peels the third natbakh off solo. Aureys' Battle Cry Demoralize lands on A (Frightened 1) and she wins initiative at 40 — Feint whiffs, but her fan-and-claw Overwhelming Combination opens the fight for 74 (Enfeebled 1 rider).
  • Mithtirith Scholarly-Recollects: best save Reflex, worst Will, immune to cold — and drops Protection on the melee.
  • A's Nightmare Screech (51 mental): Paya and Sevek crit-save, Mithtirith fails and eats all 51 (Sickened 2); its Jaws tag Aureys for 42 plus bleed. Paya's Vicious Swing crit-misses.
  • B's Screech (33): Sevek burns his Ascended Celestial roll-twice and still fails — 33 mental and Sickened 2, answered by Become Destiny (1 MP, free action) to shed the condition. He closes and puts an Inner Upheaval flurry into A: hit + crit for 110 with persistent fire; A's Fortitude shrugs off the Phantasmal Doorknob blind. Aureys' reaction fan strike adds 43.

Round 2 — "right through the face"

  • Aureys Feints A off-guard and fires Aether Beam (12d6 force line at both natbakhs) — both zero it out via Mythic Resilience. Mithtirith's Fear targets the Will weakness he'd found; both save anyway ("That was their worst save too, guys").
  • A bites Sevek (35 + bleed) and latches on with Drain Dreams — Sevek crit-saves the stupefy — then whiffs its tongue at Aureys.
  • Paya's Vicious Swing crits A for 110 — "you just run this thing right through the face and it dies" (+2 Mythic Points, +1 to everyone else), followed by the greatest action declaration of the session: "Stride and turn feed cat."
  • The survivor turns on the killer: B's Jaws and Tentacle Tongue maul Paya for 30 + 23. Sevek answers with a flurry into B (26) and Taunts it onto himself.

Rounds 3–4 — the stone that screamed back

  • Aureys calls her spectral dragon and tries Majestic Presence — B crit-saves on a nat 20. Mithtirith heals Paya 66 (+5 Communal to Sevek). Paya lands 46 + a MAP−10 Spear Dancer crit for 70, declining the forced movement to keep B inside Sevek's reach.
  • B steps back for a third Nightmare Screech (42) — Mithtirith fails again (42), everyone else crit-saves, and Aureys' Black Pearl Aeon Stone reflects a copy of the screech back at B on a Mythic-Point counteract reroll (B crit-saves its own scream, but style points are eternal — "I'm like a magic user. I steal stuff"; the stone's DC 5 shatter check comes up a 10, and it lives).
  • Sevek recovers a Focus Point with Sash of Prowess, lands another Inner Upheaval hit (38), and keeps the Taunt up. Aureys debuts mythic Earth to Heavens Strike (41, Stupefied 1); Mithtirith self-heals 79 (+5 to Aureys).
  • Paya's Vicious Swing crits for 102 — kill, his second of the fight (+2 Mythic Points again; Sevek, at cap: "I'm already full").
  • Aftermath: Kandirvek returns "banged up, but whole," and flags the creatures as extraplanar and mythic — the Risen aren't the only powerful things in the Kodars.
Encounter 2 · Flight Day 3 · 8 rounds · The Throne

Gavergash & the Troll Warleaders

EnemiesGavergash — mythic troll king, 8th-rank arcane (DC 38–39), +3 bec de corbin +33, regen 25; spent 3 Mythic Points · 4× warleader (axes +26, Primordial Roar, regen 20)
KillsSevek — Warleader A + Gavergash · Paya — C, D, B in one turn
Low pointPaya — 100-damage Chain Lightning crit-fail + 76 reactive-strike crit
Sevek's ledgerKilling blow on Gavergash · zero Mythic Points spent

Round 1 — trip, blind, and burn the regeneration

  • Sevek opens by raising Whirlwind Stance, closing in, and spending his third action on Flurry of Maneuvers — Trip A, then a crit while it's down (62), Phantasmal Doorknob blinding it before it ever acts.
  • Gavergash answers with Eclipse Burst (69 cold/void, DC 39): Paya and Aureys nat-20 out; Sevek and Mithtirith take all 69.
  • Warleader D opens Paya's account (22); Mithtirith gives Paya Conductive Weapon (+1 shock, d12s vs metal) and confirms via Recall Knowledge: weakness to fire and electricity — Cody turns regeneration off for the whole warleader roster on the spot.
  • Blind A stands into Sevek's Stand Still (crit, 56); B whiffs both swings into Whirlwind AC 39. Paya's Vicious Swing crits D for 100 and blinds it via the Brilliant rune; C answers with a crit through Deflecting Wave (36 net).
  • Aureys closes the round in style: Majestic Presence, a Feint, and a fan/claw combination for 109 into the blinded troll Sevek had tripped.

Round 2 — hunting the healer

  • Sevek's flurry finishes A (96) and re-Taunts B. Gavergash escalates: Desiccate (52 void, DC 39) — Aureys and Sevek crit-save; Paya's Mythic reroll lands a crit that his plant anatomy downgrades to a plain success ("I am so plant in this moment") for 26; Mithtirith saves for 26.
  • D's Primordial Roar bounces off three crit-saves but sticks Frightened 2 on Aureys.
  • Mithtirith debuts Fast Channel — a 3-action-radius Heal in two actions (46 to the frontline) — and the trolls answer by hunting him: B's stride at the healer eats Sevek's second Stand Still crit (68) before its axe connects for 25.
  • Paya crits D again (102) and carves C for 66 + 51; Aureys' new Frightening Power crit-fails C to Frightened 4, then her crit-Feint combination puts 117 more into it.

Round 3 — the triple kill

  • Gavergash goes for the kill: 8th-rank Chain Lightning, arcing healer-first — Mithtirith, then Paya, then Aureys. Mithtirith saves (25), Aureys crit-saves and ends the chain — but Paya crit-fails for 100 ("smells like crispy pineapple").
  • Sevek's flurry hammers B (81), and Mithtirith runs the 99-point Heal into Paya through B's reactive strike (26) to deliver it.
  • Then the answer: Paya kills three warleaders in one turn — C (118, flanked and dragging Frightened 4 on its save), D (102), and B via a MAP−10 crit for 96 ("that's the most crit I've ever seen in my life" — Pax, from the cheap seats).
  • "And for the first time, you see Gavergash look worried."

Rounds 4–7 — the duel

  • Aureys Demoralizes the troll king ("Hey. You suck. ... And I'm a dragon") and perches on his throne mid-fight: "I'm on your fucking throne, man."
  • Sevek burns Ascend — 130 feet of fly — to storm the throne itself, eating a reactive strike (33) on the way up; he tail-grapples twice (fail, then crit-fail: knocked prone, Kip Up erases it) and switches to what works: Trip.
  • Gavergash spends his entire fight economy answering it — two Mythic Points to stand from Sevek's Trips without provoking (casting Haste on himself with the turn the first one bought), one more to Recharge an 8th-rank slot — while cycling Phantasmal Calamity (43 mental; Aureys' Draconic Salvation buys her save up a degree), Frigid Flurry (60 cold/slashing down the party's line as he surfs away on it — Mithtirith's last Mythic Point goes to that save; the escape buys him nothing, because Ascend's speed lets Sevek run him down the same round, re-enter his stance, and put him right back on the ground), and Blood Vendetta (6d6 persistent bleed) on Paya.
  • Sevek casts his Mortal Herald Heal mid-duel — one action, 81 HP, trading a reactive strike (39) to get it off — and blanks a bec de corbin hit with Prevailing Position (+4 AC, AC 41).
  • Paya's Sudden Charge eats a 76-point reactive-strike crit but answers with a 96 crit that sticks Stupefied 1 on the caster; Mithtirith's 102- and 108-point Heals keep him standing ("Your Paya is fully operational again").

Final round — the saved flurry

  • Aureys Majestic-Presences Frightened 1 onto Gavergash, then her mythic Earth to Heavens Strike crits for 122 — Clumsy 1, Stupefied, Enfeebled 1, "debuffed him as much as one can be debuffed."
  • Sevek: Herald's Weapon (all-spirit, sanctified, +2d4), the Inner Upheaval he saved the whole fight, Flurry of Blows — crit + hit, 110, Gavergash falls. "I think that's overkill, but yeah." (+2 Mythic Points for the kill; zero spent.)
  • Aureys does a little dance on the body — "And now I'll take your throne" — and claims his mythic Hollow Robes by etymological right: "Especially because I am technically Aureys Hollow." Paya, notarizing: "They were named for her. He was just holding on to them for her. Much like the chair."
  • Aftermath: Paya tops the party off with Fresh Produce (50) and Ocean's Balm (36) — "Here is some food for y'all." / "Is Paya trying to call himself a snack?" / "Uh, you said it, not me."
Encounter 3 · The Pit · Complex Hazard · 4 routine cycles

A Falling Star's Memories

HazardIllusion of the Trinity Star's voyage — cannot be attacked · Will DC 40/round stacks Stupefied · Stupefied 5 = ejected via 20d6 crash
Disable3 successes — Thievery DC 43 / Arcana / Dispel · Aureys 2, Sevek 1
CasualtyMithtirith — Stupefied 5, ejected for 81 bludgeoning
Mythic pointsEveryone's last point spent; refunded +2 Aureys / +1 others

The plunge

  • Supernatural darkness, limbs pinned, hurtling at impossible speed. Opening Will save (DC 40): Sevek crit-saves ("Alright, come on. There you go. I'm good at this."), Aureys and Mithtirith fail (Stupefied 1), Paya crit-fails (Stupefied 2).
  • Round 1: Paya's Recall Knowledge finds the exit — Arcana, Thievery, or Dispel, three successes needed. Sevek, untrained in both, tries mythic-rerolled Thievery (fail) and pivots to support: Guidance on Mithtirith.
  • The routine bites: Sevek's second Ascended Celestial reroll of the day fails again (Stupefied 1); Mithtirith and Paya crit-fail toward Stupefied 4 — "you feel the ground approaching."

The ejection

  • Aureys goes all-in: Eyes That See Eternity (Mythic Point, truesight) then Hands That Unweave Disaster (Mythic Point, mythic-proficiency Thievery) — 51, success one of three.
  • Paya and Sevek both burn Mythic rerolls into DC 43 and both land on 41 — short. The routine claims its victim: Mithtirith hits Stupefied 5 and rides the Trinity Star into Golarion — 81 bludgeoning, dumped at the rim, out of the hazard.
  • Paya, at Stupefied 4 with the ground approaching, stays in only by spending a Mythic Point on the Will save — nat 19, 45. Aureys pours her last Mythic Point into a second mythic Thievery — 41, agonizingly short — and converts her final action into a critical Aid (a 44, +3) for Sevek.

Cutting the star loose

  • The party turns the ejection into a tool — Mithtirith's voice from outside drops the save DC by 5 (the Thievery DC stays 43). Paya crit-fails his last mythic reroll.
  • Sevek stacks See the Unseen's illusion-piercing +2 and Aureys' critical Aid, burns his last Mythic Point: 48 — success two of three ("Come on! — There you go!"), then spends his final action on Bless Ally so Aureys keeps saving.
  • Paya self-rescues on a second mythic-point Will save; Aureys slips to Stupefied 3, rolls Thievery — 42, "one shy" — and Aids Paya.
  • Then Aureys does the accounting: "I didn't include the plus two from the truesight — that would have actually been a forty-four." The 42 was a 44 all along. Third success, retroactive. The starfield tears away mid-fall, the Stupefied conditions lift, and the hazard refunds the war chest: Aureys +2 Mythic Points, everyone else +1.

Character Spotlight

Sevek · the Patient Blade

Pax — a slow burn cashed in at the perfect moment, and the S4 lesson paid off in full.

  • Played the whole session on a slow burn and cashed it in at the perfect moment: Herald's Weapon + the Inner Upheaval he'd "been saving this whole fight" + Flurry of Blows dropped Gavergash — the killing blow on a mythic troll king, followed by the quietest flex at the table: "Just note that I didn't use any mythic point."

Also debuted Prevailing Position (dropping stance for +4 AC to blank a bec de corbin strike) and Ascend's 1/day burst: 130 feet of flight to storm a troll king on his own 20-foot throne — and to run down his Frigid Flurry escape when he tried to surf away.
  • The Trip engine keeps printing: a Flurry-of-Maneuvers Trip + crit opened the troll fight, he killed Warleader A outright, and he put Gavergash on the ground twice — forcing the troll to burn two Mythic Points just to stand up without provoking. Stand Still crit twice — once on blind A trying to rise, once for 68 on Warleader B mid-stride toward the healer, the fight's cleanest answer to "they know to get the healer." The one failed experiment: tail-grappling a Huge troll (crit fail, knocked prone — Kip Up, free action, like it never happened).
  • His voice all session was the measured diplomat of Roderic's Cove: "I don't know what would have happened if he was not an ally, but a foe" about Kandirvek; the honest shrug before the troll fight — "I guess there's no talking then...
