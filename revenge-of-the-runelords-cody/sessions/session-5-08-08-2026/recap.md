## Session Summary

The evacuation ends where it began: with Ayavah, gratitude, and an unanswered question about Sorshen. Ten thousand Eurythnians reach Vekr's Crossing with 98 of 100 Survival Points intact — enough that their culture will survive, with talk of one day returning home. Krune's retrieval belt yields three prizes that shape the whole session: a mythic **Wand of Never Mind**, a **crystal ball**, and an engraved **star chart** that Mithtirith identifies as the flight path of the **Trinity Star** — a map to where it landed. And, as Mithtirith is first to note: whoever engraved the chart could have followed it too.

The party flies to **Kandirvek's** waterfall-hidden lair, a collector's hoard of astrolabes and curios that has Aureys physically knitting her fingers together to keep from stealing. The omen dragon unfurls his wings and shows them visions of their own possible futures: riding his back over the mountains, fighting green-skinned giants, descending into a ruined city in the Darklands realm of **Narvoth**, and prying open a portal above the **Rune Well of Pride** — until a regal man in peacock-feather robes steps out of that last vision, sneers, waves a hand, and shuts it down, spiking a psychic migraine through a centuries-old dragon. *Something looked back.* Kandirvek names the site of the first two visions — the **Throne of the Troll King**, a legendary Kodar location a few hundred miles away — and the star chart confirms the Trinity Star landed right beside it. Two reasons to go. He flies them there himself, pausing mid-journey for the party to carve through two mythic, dream-eating **natbakhs** while he handles a third.

At the throne — a 40-foot stone chair on a heat-blasted hill, ringed by outward-facing bone totems — waits **Gavergash**, a mythic troll spellcaster in emerald robes with an 8th-rank arcane arsenal, flanked by four rune-axed troll warleaders. The parley is brief ("I see no dragon here, just a puffed up lizard" — "Ah, those are fighting words, buddy"), the fight long and genuinely dangerous: Eclipse Burst, Desiccate, Chain Lightning, and Frigid Flurry rain down at DC 39 while the warleaders hunt the healer. Paya eats a 100-point Chain Lightning crit-fail and later kills **three warleaders in a single turn**; Sevek trips the troll king twice, remembers his Mortal Herald self-Heal at last, and drops Gavergash with a Herald's-Weapon-wreathed, Inner-Upheaval flurry he'd saved the entire fight — without spending a single Mythic Point.

Then the twist: the throne Aureys has been loudly claiming all fight is **hollow** — an illusion concealing an 800-foot pit. The descent plunges the party into **A Falling Star's Memories**, a complex hazard that hurls their minds through the Trinity Star's centuries-long voyage through space. Stupefied values climb; Mithtirith hits Stupefied 5 and is ejected through a vision of the crash itself for 81 damage; Aureys and Sevek spend every Mythic Point they have on mythic-proficiency Thievery checks — Sevek's from a base of *untrained* — to cut the illusion apart. At the bottom waits a buried city lit by ghost-light, a fresh 250-foot crater of pure iron shards, and a single line of glowing footprints walking away from the wreckage. Paya and Aureys name the ruin: **Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul**, thought lost at Earthfall. Something rode the Trinity Star down. Next session: the Book 1 finale.

## Key Events

### Act I — Farewells at Vekr's Crossing (RP)
- **Krune's retrieval belt** survives his death: inside are a mythic **Wand of Never Mind** ("a mean Xanderghul spell, if I've ever seen one" — Paya), a **crystal ball**, and an engraved **star chart**.
- **Mithtirith identifies the star chart**: it plots the Trinity Star's path across the sky one year ago — and can be used to find where it landed. His immediate follow-up: *whoever made the chart could have done the same.*
- The refugees arrive: **98/100 Survival Points**. Xin-Eurythnia's society will hold together; talk of reclaiming the city is popular. Ayavah thanks the party — and confirms there has been **no word from Sorshen** since the sinkhole. Sevek: "That's a problem."
- Ayavah stays to manage the refugees and offers herself as a standing ally. The party sets out for Kandirvek's lair. Mithtirith claims the **Wand of Wall of Force** and the **retrieval belt** — the party's S4 "no arcane caster" problem is quietly becoming his job.

### Act II — The Omen Dragon's Lair (RP)
- Behind a waterfall: a collector's hoard. Aureys visibly restrains her kleptomania; asked whether the refugees made it, she gives the session's one dead-serious answer: **"They are my brothers."**
- Kandirvek reintroduces himself: an **omen dragon**, a scholar centuries in the Kodars. Since Aroden's death dragonkind lost perfect foresight, but he dreams potential futures — and he has dreamt of the party, over and over, fighting Thassilon's legacy. He offers his aid.
- **The wing visions** (4 Perception successes): ① the party riding Kandirvek, consulting the star chart; ② battle with green-skinned giants — Mithtirith IDs powerful **trolls**; ③ descent into a ruined city in a vast cavern — the Darklands realm of **Narvoth**; ④ magic prying open a portal above a shattered stone well — the **Rune Well of Pride**.
- The fourth vision is **interrupted**: a regal man in peacock-feather robes steps out of it, sneers, and waves it shut — striking Kandirvek with a psychic spike mid-trance. "Something looked back through the vision." Whoever it was casts magic powerful enough to reach *backward through prophecy*.
- Kandirvek names the location: the **Throne of the Troll King** — legend says any troll who sits it one full night undisturbed rules all Kodar trolls for a year. The star chart puts the **Trinity Star's landing site right beside it**. He offers to fly them there (three days) and produces a teleportation circle for a shopping run first (level-14 item access; ~1,865 gp each distributed after Kelsie's loot-master selling spree).
- Aureys talks the dragon out of a "bobble": the **Black Pearl Aeon Stone**, his most expensive portable item, which she orbits around her head. Sevek buys his long-planned **Greater Resilient rune**. Rebuild notes: Mithtirith → **Hedge Mage**; Aureys → **Living Nexus + Draconic Acolyte** (she picked draconic *before* learning what kind of dragon Kandirvek was, and is delighted).

### Act III — Three Days on Dragonback (RP → combat)
- Evening one, Kandirvek shares what his visions told him of the party's past: several of them delved the pit at **Sandpoint** a year ago. Mithtirith offers his thesis-in-progress on **the Eighth Runelord — "the one who almost was a Runelord," the Runelord of Vainglory** — a name even Kandirvek has never heard.
- Midday two: three bat-like horrors **bigger than Kandirvek** inbound — **natbakhs**, mythic dream-eaters not native to the Material Plane. He takes one solo and drops the party on a cliff to handle the other two. (See Combat Encounters.)
- The natbakhs' bone-strewn lair yields loot; Paya cooks dinner over it ("I season the bones... so she gets the healing"), and Kandirvek's verdict lands: "You all fight well. I can see how you've given the Risen so much trouble."

### Act IV — The Throne of the Troll King (RP → combat)
- Day three: a flat-topped hill crowned by a **40-foot stone throne**, ringed by bone totems that face *outward*, on a slab blasted to glass-sheen — but the throne itself pristine. Atop it: **Gavergash**, mythic troll caster in emerald robes wielding a sparking bec de corbin, with four warleaders below.
- Kandirvek plays "bad cop," drawing the region's trolls away so the fight stays clean; Aureys appoints herself good cop. Gavergash: "Who dares approach the throne of Gavergash?" Aureys, chest puffed: "You've never met a dragon like Aureys Hollow, have you?" — and the fight is on. (See Combat Encounters.)

### Act V — The Hollow Throne (exploration → hazard)
- Aureys, finally sitting her prize, notices the throne feels **hollow**. Dispelling the illusion reveals the truth: the throne is a lid over an **800-foot sheer pit**. Mithtirith confirms it is *not* the rune well from the vision.
- The descent (Mithtirith carried by Sevek and Paya) plunges everyone into supernatural darkness and the **A Falling Star's Memories** hazard — the Trinity Star's own voyage through space, experienced from inside. (See Combat Encounters.)

### Act VI — Xin-Cyrusian (reveal)
- The pit bottoms out in an immense cavern: ceiling lights like stars, ruins glowing with ghostly radiance, a city crushed into a chamber not quite big enough for it — and a fresh **250-foot crater** heaped with **iron shards**. The Trinity Star was iron, all of it. The crater's blast, Aureys realizes, came from **what it struck**, not the impact itself.
- A 50-foot path of melted stone runs east out of the crater, marked by **glowing footprints** — as if someone stepped out of the wreckage and simply walked away. Paya: "Somebody was in the Trinity Star?"
- Paya and Aureys (with a Mythic Point) place the ruin from legend: **Xin-Cyrusian, city of pride, throne of Xanderghul**, thought destroyed at Earthfall. Next session finishes Book 1; level 15 after. Sevek: "I'm ready to become legendary."

### Encounter 3 — A Falling Star's Memories (complex hazard, the pit)
**Hazard**: an illusion woven into the 800-foot descent — the Trinity Star's voyage through space, relived from inside. Cannot be attacked. Routine **"Voyage Through the Infinite"**: Will save DC 40 each round, failures stack Stupefied; **Stupefied 5 ejects the victim through the crash itself** (20d6 bludgeoning, Reflex DC 40). Disable: 3 successes at Thievery DC 43 / Arcana / Dispel Magic.

- **The plunge**: supernatural darkness, limbs pinned, hurtling at impossible speed. Opening Will save: **Sevek crit-saves** ("Alright, come on. There you go. I'm good at this."), Aureys and Mithtirith fail (Stupefied 1), **Paya crit-fails** (Stupefied 2).
- **Round 1**: Paya's Recall Knowledge finds the exit — Arcana, Thievery, or Dispel, three successes. Sevek, untrained in both, tries mythic-rerolled Thievery (fail) and pivots to support: Guidance on Mithtirith. The routine bites: **Sevek's second Ascended Celestial reroll of the day fails again** (Stupefied 1); Mithtirith and Paya crit-fail toward Stupefied 4 — "you feel the ground approaching."
- **Round 2**: Aureys goes all-in: **Eyes That See Eternity** (Mythic Point, truesight, +2 circumstance) then **Hands That Unweave Disaster** (Mythic Point, mythic-proficiency Thievery) — **51, success one of three**. Paya and Sevek both burn Mythic rerolls into DC 43 and both land on 41 — short. The routine claims its victim: **Mithtirith hits Stupefied 5** and rides the Trinity Star into Golarion — **81 bludgeoning**, dumped at the rim, out of the hazard. **Paya, at Stupefied 4 and feeling the ground approach, stays in only by spending a Mythic Point on the Will save — nat 19, 45.** Aureys pours her **last Mythic Point** into a second mythic Thievery — 41, agonizingly short — and converts her final action into a **critical Aid (a 44, +3) for Sevek**.
- **Round 3**: The party turns the ejection into a tool — Mithtirith's voice from outside **drops the save DC by 5** (the Thievery DC stays 43). Paya crit-fails his last mythic reroll. **Sevek stacks See the Unseen's illusion-piercing +2 and Aureys' critical Aid, burns his last Mythic Point: 48 — success two of three**, then spends his final action on **Bless Ally** so Aureys keeps saving. Paya self-rescues on a second mythic-point Will save; Aureys slips to Stupefied 3, rolls Thievery — **42, "one shy"** — and Aids Paya.
- **Round 4**: Paya, out of Mythic Points and expecting to be tossed out, starts his turn — and Aureys does the accounting: **"I didn't include the plus two from the truesight — that would have actually been a forty-four."** The 42 was a 44 all along. Third success, retroactive. The starfield tears away mid-fall, the Stupefied conditions lift, and the hazard refunds the war chest: **Aureys +2 Mythic Points, everyone else +1.**
