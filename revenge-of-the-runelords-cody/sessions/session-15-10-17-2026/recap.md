## Session Summary

The session opened where S14 stopped, at the edge of the Temple Plaza sinkhole. The party went down and, between them, **closed the Doomsday Door** beneath Xin-Eurythnia. Closing it gave them a vision. The door was the link **Xanderghul** used to reach across from **Jandelay** ("the Apocalypse Archive", a demiplane of ruined worlds) and pull Sorshen through. The **Oliphaunt of Jandelay** was once summoned to Golarion at **Spindlehorn**, a spiral tower in the Wyvern Mountains said to be the Runelord **Gimmel**'s work, and it left something of itself behind there. Prisoners and intelligence put **Belimarius** in the atrium of Sorshen's **Blood Moon Palace**. The party rested, sold off the rune giants' gear (13,292 gp each), and kept the Ancient Ones' **Timeflaying Blade**.

Kandirvek flew them to the palace and stepped aside: *"The battles you'll be facing are beyond him by this point."* In the atrium, **General Lograsi** (last seen in S8) was waiting with two other generals and a glass of wine. He asked the party to convince him. Aureys took him apart: he did not want freedom, he wanted pleasures, and he wanted to know what the party would give him for betraying Belimarius. *"Now you're catching on."* His price was the party's word that they would ask **Sorshen to pardon** him and his generals once all of this was over. Aureys gave it. Lograsi, his generals and their armies left the Blood Moon Palace, and he pointed the party at the fountain: the entrance to Sorshen's **Runewell of Lust**, where Belimarius was using the warshard to take control of it.

The fountain's defences rose first as three **Phantoms of Desire**. They died in five rounds (two to Aureys, one to Mithtirith's Frostbite), the highlight being **Kreebnock's Daze for 155 on a natural 1**. Then the party anointed themselves and sank into the **Eye of Desire**, a seaside grotto with a pearl-shell throne, where **Belimarius, last Runelord of Envy**, stood with two Knights of Malice. The fight ran eleven rounds. **Sevek's Glory on High blinded both knights on two natural 1s**, Aureys and Sevek killed one each, and then the party spent nine rounds wearing down a 600-HP runelord who forced rerolls with Foresight, countered spells with Spell Riposte and Nullify, and teleported away from the melee. **Mithtirith went down to dying 3** (his Contingency could not carry the Heal he had prepared) and **Aureys went down** taking hits meant for him. Kreebnock picked up both of them. **Sevek took both of Belimarius' lives**: a natural-20 crit forced her **Undying Myth**, and the last Strike of the fight finished her. The party reached **level 19**.

## Key Events

### Act I — Closing the Door (0:00–0:10)
- **The Timeflaying Blade** was left behind by the Ancient Ones: a mythic orichalcum bastard sword (Cody read out +3, major striking and greater corrosive; the item card also carries keen and quickstrike) with **Flay Time** (once a minute, a Mythic Point: a creature within 120 feet attempts a DC 43 Will save against being cut out of time; incapacitation). Nobody wields a longsword, but Cody pointed out that *"even if you don't use a longsword, it doesn't mean you can't use its effect."* The party kept it.
- **The Doomsday Door** under the sinkhole can't be used as a portal to Jandelay; it is a conduit for Jandelay's energies. It took *"the combined physical and magical forces"* of all of them, but they **re-closed it**, and the planar link Xanderghul built through it is severed.
- **What they learned in closing it**: every Doomsday Door leads to **Jandelay**, *"an ancient demi-plane hidden within the folds of the Maelstrom"* that holds the remains of ruined worlds. **Xanderghul** was the spellcaster on the other side who reached through this one and pulled Sorshen into Jandelay. Its herald, **the Oliphaunt of Jandelay**, appears when a world's destruction is imminent. It was summoned to Golarion long ago at **Spindlehorn**, an enormous spiral tower hidden in the **Wyvern Mountains** of the Saga Lands, *"said to be a project by Runelord of Greed, Gimmel, thousands of years ago,"* for a purpose since lost, and it **left something of itself behind there**.
- Who might know more: **Sorshen**, and the **Blood Moon Palace**.
- From rescued locals, captives and intelligence, the party learned that **Belimarius is hiding in the palace's atrium**. *"One last thread to tie up before your battle with Xanderghul."*

### Act II — Selling Up (0:10–0:16)
- Long rest at the war camp, then a sale. The rune giants' armor and weapons went, except a few runes: **Aureys** took the **+3 potency** off a greater resilient chain shirt and the **acid resistance** rune from the plate; **Sevek** took the **fire resistance** rune. About **60,000 gp** in all, **13,292 gp each**.
- Cody, again: *"Mythic runes, they will come into play in this game, but you will not be paying for them. Don't save money in anticipation."* Sevek bought **Major Resilient** for his explorer's clothing during the break.

### Act III — Lograsi (0:16–0:31)
- Kandirvek flew them to the **Blood Moon Palace**, once built as a training academy for giants, still standing, crawling with giants and sinspawn none of whom posed a threat. **Kandirvek stepped aside**; the fights ahead are beyond him now. Aureys: *"Who's the best dragon here?"* — Cody: *"Was that ever in contest?"*
- **Sorshen's atrium**: wrecked gardens around a fountain no damage has touched, three pitchers pouring endlessly into it, red energy flickering around it. Three figures watched it: a sinspawn Knight of Malice with a green-flamed halberd (**General Nonomanalia** ⚠️), a rune giant in heavy armor with crackling hands (**General Asvorag** ⚠️), and **General Lograsi**, leaning on a gazebo pillar, pouring himself wine. *"Well met, well met. After I heard of Yrasthura's fall, I anticipated it would only be a short time before you came here."*
- **Aureys answered in kind**: *"Indeed, you are odd… quite well-mannered and very diplomatic in your approach. No need to be uncivilized."* Then the terms: *"Well, then you stand in our way, so you can either step aside and let us pass, or we can get down to business."* — *"There is wisdom to your words, little dragon."* — *"I am the greatest dragon here, the greatest dragon you'll ever meet."*
- **The ask**: *"Convince me… why we should let you stand aside… And please, don't just say you'll kill us or make threats of violence. It's also mundane."* Belimarius has the warshard and *"a source of mythic power"*, and means to deal with Sorshen and Xanderghul personally.
- **Aureys ran the parley.** *"And so you believe that trading one master for another is true freedom, as opposed to being your own master?"* He said rulership was not one of his pleasures. She pushed again (*"To be one's own master is to choose one's destiny"*), then named it: ***"I think you seek pleasure. You do not seek responsibility of any sort, and you wish to know what pleasures we could offer you in exchange for you betraying Belimarius."*** — *"Now you're catching on."* He conceded that Belimarius had let her army crumble while she worked on the Runewell: *"A bit irresponsible, if you ask me."*
- **Kreebnock** tried fate: *"Surely you can see you're on the wrong side of fate here… These attempts are not going to end well for Belimarius, Xanderghul, yourself. You're destined to lose."* Lograsi: *"How easy to preach of control of one's destiny when you're able to bend destiny to your will."* **Sevek** offered to keep him fed and indulged, and added that history *"has always been unpleasant to the ones trying to destroy the world."* Lograsi: *"That's only because those who have succeeded left no one alive to speak of their own conquests."*
- **The deal**: Aureys asked what he wanted. When the party has dealt with Belimarius and found Sorshen, *"you will have her pardon us."* And, should she be willing, he and his would *"more than eagerly enter the service of one with a more rational head on their shoulders."* *"She'll have need of powerful allies when all of this is over. Someone will need to rebuild."* Sevek: *"Sounds sensible to me."* Aureys: ***"We can speak to her on your behalf. We give you our word."***
- Lograsi drained the goblet into an extradimensional space and told them the fountain is the entrance to the **Runewell of Lust**, that Belimarius is using the warshard to take control of it, and that her efforts had activated its defences, and *"I doubt it can differentiate between friend or foe."* **Lograsi, Nonomanalia, Asvorag and their armies left the Blood Moon Palace.**

### Act IV — The Phantoms of Desire (0:38–1:12)
- At the fountain, ruby-red liquid poured from the three jugs and rose into three mirage-shapes of **Queen Sorshen**: **Phantoms of Desire**.
- **Mithtirith's critical Recall Knowledge**: illusions made manifest, the Runewell's defences tripped into lashing out at anything nearby; **weak to mental, immune to spirit, vitality and void**. That ruled out most of his own damage spells; Kreebnock's mental cantrips became the fight's weapon.
- **Sevek** opened with **Channel Divine Spark** (first use) and a natural-20 crit that blinded Phantom C. **Kreebnock's Daze on Phantom A: natural 1, 155 damage, stunned.** Cody: *"That might be the most intense daze I've ever seen."*
- **Aureys killed C** (Opportune Backstab, natural 20) **and B** (a quickened fan Strike). **Mithtirith's Frostbite** finished A on a natural-1 Fortitude save: *"a million shards of ice."*
- After: Mithtirith did **Treat Wounds** himself for the first time in a long while (*"Do we all remember Mithtirith's early experiments with treat wounds?"* — Aureys: *"It was really terrible"*) and crit it. Aureys searched; nothing, they were illusions. Kreebnock: *"First I carve my initials into this statue."* Sevek said *"one last prayer to my deity before going on."*

### Act V — The Eye of Desire: Belimarius (1:32–3:11)
- Anointed from each jug, the party sank through warm, breathable water for minutes and surfaced in **a sunlit sea grotto**: a pool of seawater, a **giant pearlized seashell throne**, a hot-spring bath, a half-toppled statue of a beautiful woman, stepping stones, and **thousands of red runes** across the walls, each one shimmering *"as if something in the area were trying and failing to erase them."* Two **Knights of Malice** flanked the throne. **Belimarius** stood on it.
- Belimarius: *"If it isn't the ones who have been laying siege to my forces these past few days. Come to witness history, have we?"* — Aureys: *"Or to make it."* — *"You face a real runelord here, children."* — Aureys: *"And you face the greatest dragon you'll ever know or even imagine in your nightmares."* — *"I have known and killed more dragons than you will ever meet, little one."* — Aureys: *"I will take your power and destroy you with it."* Sevek, to Aureys: *"You gotta avenge your fellow dragonkins for what she just said."* — *"Exactly."* 
- Eleven rounds; see Combat Encounters. **Glory on High** blinded both knights; **Aureys** killed Knight B and **Sevek** Knight A; then Belimarius. Mithtirith dropped to **dying 3**, Aureys to **dying 2**, and Kreebnock brought both back. **Sevek's natural 20 forced Undying Myth**, and **Sevek's Jolt Coil Strike** ended it: *"And with that, Belimarius, last runelord of envy, was no more."*
- Aureys tore out Belimarius' eyeball and ate it: ***"I said I would feast on your bones. This is what I have to do for an appetizer."*** Kreebnock: *"Like a crow?"* Sevek: *"Sevek will look away."*
- **Cody's reveal**: *"This is the one fucking fight in which you never grab anything? I had contingency ready to go."* Belimarius had a **Contingency: Translocate if grabbed**. Sevek: *"I did try, yeah."* (He did, once; the Grapple failed.)
- Mithtirith: *"Wait, does this make Sevek now the new Runelord? I feel like it does."* — Sevek: ***"I'm just a humble man. I'm no Runelord."*** — Aureys: *"I am not a humble dragon."*
- **Loot** off Belimarius (see Loot). **Mithtirith took the Robes of Xin-Edasseril** after Kreebnock offered to defer. **Level 19.** Sevek: *"I can't roll below a 10 anymore for my first attack. Let's go."*
