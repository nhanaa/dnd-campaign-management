## Session Summary

The Crypt of Runes is a lock, and the party spends the session picking it. Seven doors ring a circular hall with a seven-pointed star set into the floor; six stand ajar, and the seventh — north, to the Crypt of Pride, where the Risen are — is sealed under all seven Runelord sigils stacked on top of one another. Mithtirith reads the enchantment and finds the exploit: the runes hold each other up, so the party doesn't have to strip all seven. Just enough to make the whole thing collapse. Nobody knows what "enough" means until they try.

What follows is four crypts and three very different answers. **Wrath** answers with violence — Alaznist's screaming spectral torso yanks the party onto a phantom battlefield where three mountains of welded corpses are already closing in. **Sloth** answers with a joke: a daunting staircase spiraling hundreds of feet into darkness that goes precisely nowhere, a false wall Aureys finds by refusing to believe Sloth would build stairs, and seven Runelords who cannot be bothered to fight and take their own door-rune down when Mithtirith points out that letting the party do the work *is* the lazy option. **Lust** answers with Queen Sorshen herself, haggard and reaching through from somewhere else with a warning she doesn't get to finish — *"Find me in Jandelay, and I can—"* — before gray smoke swallows her and three burning, elephant-masked things step out of it. And **Greed** answers with a negotiation, because Karzoug is a businessman: he'll pull his rune off the door, but he wants paying, and words don't count.

The party skips **Envy**. Aureys scouts the hall of ten statues and finds it rigged to strip magic items off anyone who walks through — disabling it means ten separate checks with a hazard waiting on any failure. Sevek does the math out loud — *"we can do another room, we don't have to go down this"* — and the table follows him. It's the correct call and it costs nothing, which is a sentence that has rarely applied to this dungeon.

Four runes down, the north door dims and dies. The party sleeps at the foot of Xin's statue, walks into the Crypt of Pride, and finds a hall eighty feet wide with three Pridespawn cultists in it and a man in gold and green robes admiring the frescoes, Aeon stones set into his forehead. **Risen Runelord Haphrama** — Greed — turns around, decides the *how* doesn't matter, and orders them killed. It goes badly for his cultists and fine for him: two die, Paya goes down under a polearm, and Haphrama — who lands nothing all fight, both of his big spells critically saved by every target — simply casts Translocate and leaves. The last cultist goes with him, to warn the rest. Whatever is behind the next door now knows they're coming.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Lock (13:05–13:27)
- Cleanup from Czaresia: a **+2 Greater Resilient Explorer's clothing** and a **+3 Greater Striking Flaming Frost Shock mace** — the mace *gargantuan*, the clothing rune-giant-sized and needing its rune transferred — plus a mythic pair of **Beloved's Bracelets**. Aureys' immediate regret: *"if only Aureys had taken as her 16th level feat Dragon's Hoard, where she could do that, apparently."*
- Cody re-describes the hall: 80 feet across, ceiling twice that, a seven-pointed star in the floor pointing at seven doors, and a statue of a long-haired man with a staff facing a mural on the south wall. Both turn out to be **Xin** — and the mural holds nothing else. Aureys searches the statue for a hidden stairwell; there isn't one.
- **Mithtirith identifies the north door.** He tries Thassilonian Lore first and Cody steers him to **Arcana**: all seven Thassilonian runes overlaid and circulating, each a barrier, and no teleport or bypass will beat it. His key finding — the session's operative fact — is that *the more runes you remove, the more unstable the enchantment gets*, so the party doesn't need all seven, just enough. Nobody knows the number.
- Cody maps the compass: **N pride, NE envy, E gluttony, SE greed, SW sloth, W lust, NW wrath.** Order doesn't matter. Paya votes clockwise "because it's appealing to my brain."
- **Sevek and Aureys frame the choice as a damage-type question** — she'd rather take "fiery boom spells" because she can dodge them; he says flatly he'd rather not face anything that hits his mind. Four minutes of circling later, **Kreebnock ends it**: *"Not knowing what they hold, I think any door is as good as another until we try one and then go from there. We can start with Wrath if you want."* They start with **Wrath**.

### Act II — Wrath, and the Field of Ruin (13:27–14:06)
- Aureys scouts ahead and finds five mausoleums — **Alaznist, Alderpash, Angathane, Ziren, Thibodos** — doors shattered, contents gone, runes drained. But a spiritual presence is still in the room, and she flies back to warn the party rather than push: *"I'm going to fly back stealthily to my friendly neighborhood cleric."*
- The party moves in anyway. Alaznist's undead upper torso fills the chamber screaming, other faces rise with her, and the scream carries everyone somewhere else: **a bloody battlefield under a lightning-shot sunset**, a ring of five pristine white mausoleums on a distant hill, and three titanic masses of congealed corpses and armor rising out of the ground.
- **Combat 1: three Warsworn.** (See below.)
- Back in the crypt, Alaznist's ghost screams one word — ***"Xanderghul!"*** — then *"You will pay for this,"* and shatters into fire along with the rest of the Runelords of Wrath.
- The party searches the room. Every grave is already empty. Mithtirith: *"Does this officially make us grave robbers?"* Aureys: *"I've always been a grave robber, you know."* Paya dates it precisely: *"I imagine at some point during Rusthenge at level 2, we became grave robbers."*
- **The Rune of Wrath is gone from the north door.**

### Act III — Sloth, and the Stairs That Go Nowhere (14:06–14:22)
- The Sloth crypt opens on *"a daunting flight of stairs spiral[ing] upward hundreds of feet before disappearing into the darkness."* Aureys: *"Hope you got your cardio in, boys."* Sevek: *"Yeah, don't worry. Sevek has a lot of stamina."*
- Mithtirith: *"They're magicians and they couldn't put it in an elevator?"* — which sets off the running gag, because Sevek doesn't know what one is. *"An elevator? What is that?"* … *"Yeah, I'm from backwater town, so I'm not really familiar with all those technologies."*
- **Aureys refuses to climb.** Her reasoning is character, not caution: *"stairs just doesn't seem like a slothful thing to do."* A thorough search turns up a **false wall** running south. Out of character, the table confirms the staircase is a pure troll — it climbs to nothing and dumps you back on the floor — and that people at this table have fallen for it before.
- Behind the wall: seven more looted mausoleums and the seven Runelords of Sloth — **Ziriae, Illithrius, Azaradne, Zalilat, Krenleth, Iravina, and Kroon** — floating lazily overhead, too bored to attack. Zalilat, bemoaning: *"Can you not see? You've been beaten to the theft. That fiend Xanderghul's already made off with everything."*
- **Mithtirith wins the crypt with one sentence**: *"But wouldn't you want someone to do the work for you to get your revenge?"* Paya closes it — *"You already have an axe to grind with Xanderghul. We could take a hit on him for you."* The Runelords agree, remove the Sloth rune themselves, and dismiss the party: *"Now off with you."*
- **The Rune of Sloth is gone. No fight, no resources spent.**

### Act IV — Lust, and Sorshen's Warning (14:22)
- A long pillared hall, murals of carnal pleasures on both walls. Aureys: *"so it's just porn."* Cody: *"Look, she's the runelord of lust, not the runelord of chastity."* Sevek announces he *"is going to ignore all these worldly desires. He's a monk."* Kreebnock, one beat later, *"takes notes as he goes along."*
- From the western mural, pieces detach and assemble into **Queen Sorshen** — ethereal, haggard, *"as though she's been through some kind of ordeal."* Recognition dawns when she sees them:
  > *"At last I've reached you. Look, as you already know, Xanderghul has returned, and I believe he intends to destroy Golarion, starting with the Saga Lands. Find me in Jandelay, and I can—"*
- Gray smoke cuts her off. Through it: **a ruined cityscape**, crumbling buildings and broken boulevards. And out of the smoke and ash step three humanoid figures dripping oil, flames licking their sides, each wearing **a mask shaped like an elephant's skull, broken tusks and all**.
- **Combat 2: three Jandelay Tolokands.** (See below.)
- Afterward, **Mithtirith brackets the deduction** — he asks the question (*"do you think that means that Sorshen's dead?"*), Paya notes she looked tired, Aureys points at the destination (*"He said to find her in Jandelay, so… she's probably trapped somewhere"*), and Mithtirith closes it: *"there's no mausoleums here, so… **So she never died.**"*
- **The Rune of Lust is gone.** Three down; Cody says the enchantment looks like it's *"really struggling to hold it together"* and one more should do it.

### Act V — Envy, Scouted and Refused (15:26–15:52)
- The Envy hall: nine statues of Runelords in finery holding magical implements, five north and four south, with a tenth — **Belimarius**, holding an ornate halberd — standing apart at the east end. The full lineage on the plaques: *Napht, Tannaris, Ivamura, Jura, Chasarda, Esedria, Zar, Dissimilia, Faradni,* and Belimarius.
- Aureys walks the hall and her trap check comes back bad: **the whole corridor is a magic-item-stealing hazard**, and it triggers the moment anyone goes further. Disabling it means Arcana, Athletics, Thievery, or a Dispel Magic against a **9th-rank** effect — *on all ten statues*, with any failure setting the hazard off.
- Aureys, delighted anyway: *"These are thieves, so they're kind of my kind of people."*
- **Sevek calls it off**: *"I say we need to do that ten times. We can do another room. We don't have to go down this."* Mithtirith pushes back honestly — it's checks, not something attacking us — and Paya lands it: *"Just 10 chances of failure is just not the best."* **Kreebnock buries it**: *"then the fight at the end of the statues is probably not going to go well."* Sevek closes: *"you don't even know what's going to happen after that, and we're already low on resource as we're near the end."*
- Mithtirith cannot let it go and circles back thirty seconds later, after the party has already chosen another door: *"I'm, I'm, I'm upset. Hold on a second. I'm obsessing on this. So the trap steals your magic items, right?… **What if you don't walk in with any magic items?**"* Sevek points out their weapons are magic items.
- They go to **Greed** instead. The hazard is still there, untriggered, if they ever need it.

### Act VI — Greed, and the Karzoug Negotiation (15:52–16:00)
- Seven mausoleums, doors open, coins and gems and jewelry spilling out across the floor. A brass plaque in Azlanti — **Mithtirith is the only one who reads it** — says ***Abundance Everlasting***, and lists the Runelords of Greed: *Kaldurne, Fethrir, Gimel, Lignia, Mazerana, Aethusa,* and **Haphrama**. Mithtirith, reading the hoard: *"I'm not sure I believe this."*
- Aureys reaches for a coin and has to make a **Fortitude save**. She succeeds, and her build upgrades it to a **critical success** — which is the only reason she gets a flash of herself *"as a somewhat tacky obsidian statue"* instead of becoming one. The hoard is an illusion; none of it is real gold. Kreebnock, immediately: ***"Don't touch the treasure!"*** Aureys: *"It's all fake crap anyway, so don't touch it."*
- Mithtirith, dryly: *"I just shake my head and look at Paya and go, whatever happened to greed is good."*
- **Paya works out where to look** — the east side is *"the non-treasure area"* — and three PCs search it. **Sevek finds the hidden door** behind the wall plaque, beating two better-trained searchers on the die. Cody: *"Sevek, you're the one who ends up finding it."* Behind it, more looted mausoleums and one circular tomb more opulent than the rest, inscribed **Karzoug**.
- Spectral Runelords boil out screaming *"Thieves!"* and *"The wealth! Gone! All gone!"* — and are cut off by a new voice. **Karzoug** forms among them: *"That is quite enough. These troublesome heroes aren't worthy of our ire. They are not the thieves. This we all know."*
- He reads the situation instantly — common enemy in Xanderghul, and they're here for the rune — and names his terms: ***"Then what price will you pay in return for our help?"***
- **The negotiation, in three beats:**
  1. **Kreebnock opens it.** A Diplomacy Request under his **Cloak of Repute (Greater)**. Cody: the offer is *considered*, but *"words ring hollow where wealth is concerned"* — it **lowered the asking price** without closing the deal. Nothing after this happens without it.
  2. **Aureys holds it open.** She offers **10% of everything the party recovers from the despoiled tombs**, and rolls well. It gets them thinking — until **Gimel** steps forward: *"A promising start. And yet, for all promises you have made, no material has been produced."* Aureys tries the illusory coin. Karzoug: *"You mean the bauble? Surely a dragon such as yourself has an eye for what is valuable."* Her answer is the best line of the scene: ***"I do indeed, which is why I, like you, do not bargain away my wealth happily. But I'm sure we have something that perhaps we can part with."***
  3. **Paya closes it.** He produces the **Paradigm Cube** from his stash — a mythic item he'd claimed the session before — without a beat of hesitation. Cody, out of character: it wouldn't be worth enough, *but because it's mythic, its monetary value is doubled for the negotiation.* Karzoug plucks it out of the air, turns it over twice: ***"This will suffice."***
- **The Rune of Greed is gone.** The prismatic light on the north door fluctuates and dies. Aureys checks it for traps anyway (*"You can never be too careful"*), then, on the way out: ***"Never make a deal with a dragon. I don't know if you ever heard that one."***

### Act VII — The Crypt of Pride (16:00–16:44)
- **Kreebnock calls the rest** — *"So maybe the rest is in order"* — after Mithtirith had flagged he was *"out of my healing font spells… I think I have about one more fight in me"* and Kreebnock had volunteered his own Soothes to cover the gap. The party takes a **full 8-hour rest** at the center of the Crypt of Runes, at the foot of Xin's statue. Paya: *"Bedrolls and candlelight."*
- Beyond the door: a hall 80 feet wide and nearly twice as long, 50-foot ceiling, twin rows of marble columns, two stairwells down on the west wall and a passage east, and a giant-scale fresco on the north wall.
- Three **Pridespawn cultists** with bec de corbins — *"easily the most powerful among their number you've encountered thus far"* — and, at the back, a man in opulent gold and green robes admiring the frescoes, **Aeon stones embedded in his forehead**. **Risen Runelord Haphrama**, of Greed.
  > *"How— No, how doesn't matter. However you got down here, it seems I'm to make sure you leave in pieces. Get them, now! Leave none alive."*
- **Combat 3: three Pridespawn Cultists + Risen Runelord Haphrama.** (See below.)
- **Haphrama escapes.** After a polearm strike at Sevek that comes up a natural 1, he declines to reposition and simply casts **Translocate**. Cody: *"He has gone from sight. And he is out of here."* Aureys: *"No one said that, you know, Runelords fell off the turnip truck yesterday. There's some smart cookies."*
- Two cultists are dead; the last one leaves with the Runelord — Cody, on the survivor's decision to run: *"He left with the Runelord."* Mithtirith gets the closing line of the fight: ***"The Runelord has his pride."***
- **Aureys initiates the looting** after Cody has already moved to wrap-up — which is the only reason the party got the Pridespawn gear at all.
- Session ends. Cody notes this chapter is **the longest in the entire AP**, and there is much more of the Crypt of Runes to come.

## Character Spotlight

### Sevek · the one who says stop
- **He ran the party's resource argument and won it.** At the mouth of the Envy hall — ten statues, ten checks, a hazard on any failure — he made the case on resources and unknown payoff rather than fear: *"I say we need to do that ten times. We can do another room. We don't have to go down this."* Then, when Mithtirith pushed back: *"you don't even know what's going to happen after that, and we're already low on resource as we're near the end."* It is the only time all session the party changed course because one person insisted.
- **He found the door that led to Karzoug.** Paya worked out which wall to search; three PCs searched it; Sevek — carrying the *worst* Perception proficiency of the three — rolled a 44 and found the hidden slide-away panel. Cody: *"Sevek, you're the one who ends up finding it."*
- **He knows exactly what he can't take.** Asked at the seven doors which sin he'd rather face, he doesn't posture: he'd rather eat fire than anything that touches his mind, and says so plainly.
- **The tank job, honestly accounted.** He led damage taken in the Warsworn fight (288) — but was only third against the Tolokands, and took **zero** in the Haphrama fight, where everything aimed at him missed, twice critically. Two Prevailing Positions in the Warsworn fight, **both erasing a critical hit** — worth roughly 40 extra damage each. His own read on the job, mid-fight: *"How many crit am I gonna eat today? I dunno."* Then, without waiting for an answer — *"Better me than everyone else, so."*
- **Five kills, and only one of them was really his own doing.** Warsworn C and A, Jandelay Tolokand C, Pridespawn Cultist C and A. Four were criticals — but on natural 17, 16, and 14, against ACs that Aureys' Feints and frightened stacks had already dragged down by four to six points. The Tolokand kill was a MAP −10 gamble that hit by exactly zero. He is not the party's damage lane; he is the one standing where the debuffs have already landed.
- **The backwater-town gag has legs.** *"An elevator? What is that?"* → *"Yeah, I'm from backwater town, so I'm not really familiar with all those technologies."* And on finding a fourth looted crypt: *"We're late again."* On the last unopened door: *"Gluttony sounds like we're going to get eaten."* He also wrote Paya's best joke of the night for him, setting up *"He's living on a dream"* for Paya to finish.

### Paya · the man who had the cube
- **He ended the Karzoug negotiation.** Kreebnock opened it and Aureys kept it alive, but it was Paya who produced the **Paradigm Cube** from his stash — a mythic item he had claimed one session earlier — and handed it to a Runelord of Greed without hesitating. The full courtly bit came with it: *"Milady. Milady Miss Hollow."*
- **He is the party's navigator and archivist.** The clockwise vote that routed the whole session, the notes that tracked which door was which sin, and the deduction in the Greed crypt that the east side was *"the non-treasure area"* — which is what pointed Sevek at the hidden door.
- **He taught a new player a resource that saved his life.** Kreebnock announced he had no Mythic Point left and was going down; Paya remembered **Liralarue's Glimpse** — a free once-weekly reroll everyone has access to — was still unspent, and talked him through it. *"It's just a free reroll once a week, as long as you don't overspend it."* Kreebnock rolled an 18 and stayed up.
- **He spent a Mythic Point to take a killing blow meant for someone else.** Kreebnock was on 3 HP with a visibly empty health bar and a Warsworn winding up. Paya used **Unbelievable Interception** — Strode twice across the battlefield, became the target using the enemy's own roll against AC 38, and ate it. *"That's what we were hoping for. And it got me to move across the battlefield too."*
- **Four kills across two fights**, plus the healing lane entire: Ocean's Balm, Fresh Produce, Dash of Herbs, Battle Medicine mid-fight (including a **DC 40 critical success for 87** on himself under fire), Doctor's Visitation, Treat Wounds every break, Ward Medic to cover two at once. Aureys has a name for it: *"Tasty, tasty fruit salad."*
- **His Nightmare rune killed an Earthquake.** A Reactive-Strike crit stapled Stupefied 1 on a Tolokand, which then failed its DC 6 flat check to cast, burned a Mythic Point to reroll, and rolled a natural 1. Paya, calling it: *"Not today. Nightmares of Sandpoint save us in this case."*
- **The comic timing is deliberate and it lands.** *"Then it's just me. As fighters intended."* On the first of nine Desiccates: *"It's a bad day to be a leshy, friends."* On the tenth thing that could have been one: *"Fully expected another desiccate just because of the rules of comedic timing."*

### Aureys Hollow · never make a deal with a dragon
- **She cracked the Sloth crypt by refusing to walk into the joke.** A staircase spiraling hundreds of feet up into darkness, and her reasoning was fiction, not mechanics: *"stairs just doesn't seem like a slothful thing to do."* A thorough search found the false wall. Her summary, walking through it: *"Always remember which runelord you're dealing with."*
- **She matched a Runelord of Greed on his own ground.** Karzoug tested her — *"surely a dragon such as yourself has an eye for what is valuable"* — and her answer kept the scene a negotiation instead of a fight: ***"I do indeed, which is why I, like you, do not bargain away my wealth happily. But I'm sure we have something that perhaps we can part with."*** She had already put 10% of everything recoverable on the table. Exit line, rune in hand: *"Never make a deal with a dragon. I don't know if you ever heard that one."*
- **She scouted every single door in this dungeon** — traps, stealth, trap finder, the statue, the mausoleums, the Envy hall that she then talked the party out of — including the north door *after* it had already been disenchanted: *"just to be on the safe side… You can never be too careful."* She also started the end-of-session looting after Cody had moved on, which is the only reason the party owns the Pridespawn gear.
- **She was the top damage dealer of the Warsworn fight** (436, ahead of Paya's 342) and the hardest to hit all night — **55 damage across the entire Tolokand fight**, which she described as *"dipping and dodging over here. Not doing much else, but dipping and dodging."*
- **The Grovel bit is the engine and the character at once**, and it escalates all session: *"oh, you look like a wonderful buffet. How will I ever fill my tummy with you?"* → *"hey, big bag of snacks. You're so awesome."* → ***"Say, you're so awesome, Mr. Elephant."*** → *"ah, you're so beautiful. Your smile brings a glow to my heart."* Every one is a Feint or a Demoralize with a Distracting Feint riding on it, and the off-guard it hands out is what most of the party's crits are built on.
- She also came one Fortitude save from being turned into treasure, and got a vision of herself as *"a somewhat tacky obsidian statue"* for her trouble.

### Mithtirith · two crypts, two sentences
- **He talked seven Runelords of Sloth into disarming their own crypt.** They were floating overhead, bored, telling the party they'd been beaten to the theft. Mithtirith found the only argument that could possibly work on the personification of sloth: ***"But wouldn't you want someone to do the work for you to get your revenge?"*** Paya sealed it, the Runelords agreed, and the party left without rolling a single attack.
- **He read the lock on the whole dungeon.** He tried Thassilonian Lore, was steered to Arcana, and produced the session's operative fact: the seven runes hold each other up, so *the more you remove, the more unstable the enchantment gets*. Every decision after that follows from his call.
- **He is the only one who reads Azlanti**, which is how anyone knew the Greed plaque said *Abundance Everlasting*. He is also the party's continuity memory — the one who asks whether the murals matched what they saw at the Sunken Temple.
- **He bracketed the Sorshen deduction**, opening it (*"do you think that means that Sorshen's dead?"*) and closing it (*"there's no mausoleums here, so… so she never died"*).
- **He would not let the Envy hall go.** The party had already chosen another door when he came back to it: *"I'm, I'm, I'm upset. Hold on a second. I'm obsessing on this. So the trap steals your magic items, right?… **What if you don't walk in with any magic items?**"* It doesn't work — everyone's weapons are magic items — but it is the sharpest lateral thinking anyone did all night.
- **He went to zero and caught himself.** His Contingency was pre-set to fire a Heal the instant he hit 0 HP, and it did exactly that in the Tolokand fight — Foundry rolled it at rank 7 and Cody corrected it *up* to rank 8. He was unconscious for seconds. He also took the single biggest hit anyone ate all session: **93, on a critical failure that was a natural 1 on a mythic-proficiency reroll.**
- **The driest voice at the table, still undefeated.** Watching an enemy shrug off his mythic reaction with a natural 20: *"And I have a silly question. When you roll with Mythic Proficiency, how much higher is that than Expert?… Would a 48 have been a critical success?… Asking for a friend. I'm good. Thanks."* Also *"Heal the healer. Always a good option."*, *"whatever happened to greed is good"*, *"You act like they haven't been doing that for like 16 levels"*, and the last line of the session's last fight: ***"The Runelord has his pride."*** The pineapple bit is his and he keeps it warm: *"I think I'm going to go with Paya because he's my favorite pineapple."*

### Kreebnock · once more into the breach
- **He made four calls that shaped the session.** After the party had circled the seven doors for four minutes, **he picked one**: *"Not knowing what they hold, I think any door is as good as another until we try one and then go from there. We can start with Wrath if you want."* He **buried the Envy plan** with the one consequence nobody had said out loud (*"then the fight at the end of the statues is probably not going to go well"*). He **called the long rest** before the Crypt of Pride. And earlier he had **volunteered the second healing pool** — *"I do have a couple 4th rank soothes and a 6th rank soothe I can use also for healing"* — which is the only reason pushing forward was on the table at all.
- **He opened the Karzoug negotiation.** A Diplomacy Request under his Cloak of Repute (Greater). It didn't close the deal, but it **moved the price** — and everything Aureys and Paya did afterward was bidding against a number he had already lowered.
- **He delivered on the two-healer promise in the worst moment.** Paya was dying, out of Mythic Points, and Mithtirith was out of position. Kreebnock Unleashed his Psyche, dropped a **Soothe for 64**, then **Restore the Mind** for another 34 — floor to standing in one turn. Sevek: *"Hey, we've got two healers. Let's go."*
- **His buffs bought more than his damage did, and the receipts are specific.** Courageous Anthem essentially every round of every fight; Haste on the party in two of three; **Scintillating Safeguard** twice during the Desiccate barrage, which is precisely what kept Mithtirith and Paya standing through two of the nine (Paya, out loud: *"I take one degree less"*). **With Friends like These** cost a Tolokand its second Mythic Point. ### What Sevek is thinking about

- **Taunt did not absorb the Tolokand fight, and that's the real lesson.** Nine party-wide Desiccates and 467 rolled void damage do not care about aggro; only five hand Strikes were even Taunt-eligible. What Taunt actually bought was the **−1 and the off-guard** — and the off-guard is what let Paya crit four times and Aureys crit three. **Against a caster group, Taunt is a to-hit debuff and a flanking enabler, not a damage sink.** The mitigation lane there is Prevailing Position and standing further apart.
- **The reach-100 Animated Weapon is the counter-case.** In round one of the Warsworn fight, a taunted Warsworn reached across the entire battlefield to crit Mithtirith for 78 anyway. Taunt's penalty applied; the distance did not matter. Positioning cannot solve a weapon that ignores it.
