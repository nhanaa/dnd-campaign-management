Session Summary

Liralarue wakes, and the first twenty minutes are hers. She takes the party through a green portal that smells of wildflowers to the meadow where she first spoke to them, the place where they gained their mythic destinies. She is a dream now, part of the plane. Her gift and her curse is that she sees every possible future and can never know which are true. In many of them the party stands against Xanderghul, "and in all of them, you oppose him." When Mithtirith asks whether she was the one who showed them the worst of those futures, she says no: that was the Ashen Man, one of the Ancient Ones, beings from the dimension of time who claim to predate the universe. He represents the end of time for individual worlds, is tied to the energies of Jandelay, and has lent Xanderghul his aid because he wants Golarion "returned to Jandelay." Sorshen is not a prisoner there. She and Xanderghul are fighting, right now, in a pocket where time runs slow. The battle will last some weeks, and in every future Liralarue has seen, Sorshen falls. "With your help, the tide might turn."

A mirage of Ayavah interrupts: Runelord Belimarius has invaded the ruins of Xin-Eurythnia and brought a warshard with her. Liralarue sends the party off with three things. The first is a lead: no planar key to Jandelay is known to exist, but Sorshen's archive in the Blood Moon Palace might point the way. The second is a fact that changes the campaign: Xanderghul's ability to jump into the bodies of the Risen runs through the Dreamlands, and so long as she is awake, he can be killed. The third is a field of treasure collected over millennia, 200,000 gp of it. Foundry briefly reads it as four million. Aureys, who has just learned Hidden Hoard, dives in like Scrooge McDuck.

After a rest and a shopping trip to Absalom (41,850 gp each, and Cody opens rare apex items to the table), the party teleports to Vekr's Crossing, where a small war camp has grown beside the refugee tents. Ayavah, Kandirvek and an enormous rime dragon, Galasodia, Keeper of Records (name as heard), lay out the war. Belimarius' rune giants escorted a fragment of Gorum out of the Red Mountains. Ayavah's visions show her on a scallop-shell throne beside three pitchers pouring into a pool, which Mithtirith's critical Arcana identifies as the Eye of Desire, Sorshen's own Runewell of Lust. The allied forces can hold the rank and file. The party is asked to hit three strike points in one day: the trapped locals, the rune giants at the city gates, and the Temple Plaza, where a rune-giant commander guards the sinkhole over the open Doomsday Door. Aureys argues for the people first, Kreebnock backs her, and everyone agrees.

It is four fights. Three Shoggoths ooze out of the residential ruins: crit-immune, reach 30, and they grab. Aureys is snatched out of the air and Paya is restrained and slammed, but Kreebnock, Paya and Aureys each put one down. The grateful locals hand over gear, including +3 Greater Winged half plate that Paya puts on behind some rubble ("Now you're a flying pineapple"). At the City Gates, two rune giants and two runic warmaws breathe fire and lightning down a too-narrow street. With no cleric the party comes out of it visibly hurt, and Cody remarks on it. Persistent bleed and void finish the giants, and Aureys and Sevek take a warmaw each. At the Temple Plaza, Commander Yrasthura is waiting with a larger warmaw and two winged envyspawn, the Knights of Malice. Sevek opens with his new Glory on High and blinds a knight for the whole fight. Then Yrasthura's Wide Swing crits both Sevek and Paya for over a hundred each, and her sword is vorpal. Sevek's Fortitude save fails by one. He burns a Mythic Point, rerolls, and keeps his head. Two turns later Aureys crits Yrasthura twice in a row and the commander collapses. Aureys scares one knight into flying away for good, Paya cuts down the warmaw, and Mithtirith's Tempest Surge finishes the blind knight.

The party gets one round. Sevek finally spends Heroic Presence. Then two robed figures with faces of shadow and starlight rise out of the sinkhole, and a familiar voice arrives in everyone's head: "And here you stand again." Ancient Ones, each teleporting as a free action on Mythic Points. Paya rolls three natural 20s in one round, the third on a reaction Kreebnock hands him, and the first Ancient One goes down, spends Undying Myth, and answers with a 10th-rank Spirit Song that stuns most of the party. Sevek kills it for good on a natural 20. The second one keeps slithering to Mithtirith, who answers with a Spirit Blast it critically fails for 118, and Sevek's last natural 20 of the night turns it to mist. That is where the session stops.
