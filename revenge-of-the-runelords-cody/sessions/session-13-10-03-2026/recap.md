## Session Summary

The session picks up where the last one ended. The **horse-headed figure** steps out of the outbuilding and turns out to be **Javownir**, a **dramofir** (Mithtirith recognises the type: dream collectors who weave dreams into their robes, and a prideful bunch). He was **the Oubliette's jailer** until the **Bittersweet Sisters** took the tower, locked a magic-suppressing cage over his face, and began letting the imprisoned nightmares out of their bubble-cells. **Liralarue** is at the very top, wearing the same kind of mask. The tower has no stairs. Its floors rotate like a puzzle, and Javownir hands over the **Spindle Key** that turns them. **Kreebnock takes the key**, since it runs on Occultism and he is the only one legendary in it (*"I have no intelligence. Even though I'm legendary in occultism"*). He solves it on every floor of the night, with Aureys' aid crits and Sevek's Guidance. The jailer's own mask needs a Thievery check at **DC 45** with two successes, and one slip releases a gas that could kill the room. **Aureys disables it on 48 and 46** (*"I am the greatest thief alive. And by thief, I mean acquirer of goods that are left behind locked devices that are too simple"*). Mithtirith stamps the pieces to dust: *"What a heresy."*

From there it is **four floors and four fights**, each a step up in security. On **minimum security**, two nightmare **axolotls** swell out of their bubbles. They are incorporeal, which Aureys learns when her claws pass through. Sevek takes the first, on a reaction Strike Kreebnock set up for him, and Aureys' Overwhelming Combination finishes the second. **Low security** has a skeletal **famine bear** being hand-fed a prisoner by **Aceta, youngest of the Bittersweet Sisters**. *"Seems that idiot Aethusa bungled the whole thing."* Aureys double-crits the bear into nothing, then takes Aceta apart, and Aceta's last three claws all miss. **Sevek ends it with an Electric Arc**, and Aureys flexes: *"Does anyone doubt I'm a dragon now?"* — *"There was never any doubt, Aureys."* **High security** is a bubble maze with two **plague wolves** and **Raanka, the middle sister** (*"Puppies, it's dinner time"*). It is the longest fight of the night at forty minutes. Raanka's Phantasmagoria confuses Sevek, and Mithtirith for the second fight running, and the confused Mithtirith hits Kreebnock again (*"Starting to think it's personal, you know?"*). Sevek has Kreebnock zap him to snap out of it, kills the second wolf, and Taunts Raanka. **Kreebnock's natural-20 imaginary hammer lands for 152.** When Raanka's Rend is about to land on Kreebnock, **Sevek's Life Connection takes the 48 for him**. Aureys kills Raanka.

After the break, the top security floor holds **Spleen, the eldest sister**, in a chamber lit by four moon-mirrors that keep her **quickened and harder to hit**. She claws open a 30-foot bubble and lets out the **Lurking Cataclysm**. Sevek opens with **Bear Hug and a Trip**, and **Spleen burns a Mythic Point to break the grab, then Kip Ups**, the first enemy Kip Up since Cody's S7 comment. Aureys stacks clumsy, enfeebled and stupefied on the Cataclysm. **Sevek kills it in one turn**: a natural-20 crit for 153 into an Inner Upheaval Flurry, then a hasted strike to finish. Spleen answers with **Wails of the Damned** (Sevek drained 2), Spirit Song, Phantasmal Calamity and **Ride the Moonbeams**, teleporting 60 feet a turn. After a **Herald's Weapon turn** from Sevek for 136 (with a That's My Number reroll), his next hit brings her down and she spends **Undying Myth** to come back at half HP with the elite adjustments while the mirrors crack. She drops Mithtirith to zero, and his **pre-set Contingency** heal catches him (*"Hey, guys. Could you kill this thing before she kills me?"*). **Aureys' Majestic Presence draws a natural 1, frightened 4.** Spleen's last turn takes Aureys to 18 HP. Then **the bleed from Kreebnock's Weight of the World** finishes her: *"The wounds catch up to her and she collapses."*

There is **no loot anywhere in the Oubliette**. Cody calls it an oversight and promises *"a lot of it"* next session. The last climb is 500 feet through a **gray-black void of black webbing**, where the party watches **the Saga Lands — Sandpoint and much more — dragged screaming into the abyss** by fire, ice, lightning and shadow. At the top is a quiet bedroom with books, a writing desk and a couch. On the couch **Liralarue** is deeply asleep, with a cage over her face. Mithtirith gives Aureys Guidance (*"I have faith in you"*), and Aureys takes the mask apart in two clean successes: *"This will be what my legend is written from this day."* Liralarue opens her eyes, looks around, finds the party, and smiles: ***"Good morning."***

### Act I — The Jailer (0:05–0:25)
- **Javownir** steps out: about seven feet tall, horse-headed, glowing yellow eyes, a cage over his face. *"I'm not sure who you are, but I think I know who you've come for. The one at the top of this tower, yes? Liralarue."* **Mithtirith's Recall Knowledge** identifies a **dramofir**: wanderers of the Dreamlands who incorporate dreams into their robes, and *"a pretty prideful bunch."*

- **What he tells them.** The tower is *"a prison for dangerous nightmares, too dangerous even to set free on the plains of Leng."* He was its jailer *"until the sisters showed up."* The **Bittersweet Sisters** control it now and have let the nightmares run rampant. **Liralarue is at the very top in a mask like his, one meant to suppress magic.** Sevek asks the practical question first: *"Is there any way to disable the device?"* Yes, with very careful handiwork, because one mistouch releases a gas that could kill the wearer and everyone in the room.
- **The Spindle Key.** The tower has no stairs. Each floor has holes in its floor and ceiling, and the floors rotate *"like pieces on a puzzle."* Javownir hands over a key that aligns them. It's an Occultism check, and **Kreebnock takes it**.
- **Kreebnock interviews the jailer.** Are there last-resort defences they could trigger?
