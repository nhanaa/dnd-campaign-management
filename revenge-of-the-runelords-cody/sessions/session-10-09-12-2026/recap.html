Session Summary

Haphrama ran last session, and the party spends this one finding out what he ran to. The answer is that the Crypt of Pride is not a tomb any more — it is a headquarters. Bedrooms with made beds. A library with cross-referenced research. A workbench heaped with magic items. An alchemical lab. Xanderghul's cult lives here, and the party spends three and a half hours walking through their house, killing everything in it, and reading their notes.

Four fights, twelve kills, and nobody drops. A cracked statue of a winged woman peels off its plinth as Vogorond — a creature that shrugs off physical damage and whose debris aura grows every time somebody crits it — and two rounds in, two Risen Runelords of Sloth step onto a balcony thirty feet up to watch. Tannaris and Ivamura fight the whole battle in the air and from behind walls: Gravity Wave, Implosion, Weight of the World, Disintegrate, and finally a Wall of Stone that seals Sevek, Paya and Aureys into a stone box with a lid on it. Sevek punches a hole in the wall and flies out through it. Both Risen die in the air, and both of them have to spend Mythic Points to get that far.

Then the house fights back twice. Four Pridespawn Cultists ambush the party the instant Aureys cracks a secret door — and the fight's best moment is a reaction: Sevek's Stand Still crits a cultist and the Celestial Armaments save blinds it for two rounds, after which it rolls two natural 20s at Aureys and misses both on the flat check. She finishes that fight at full health. Later, while Mithtirith and Aureys read the cult's research and Sevek stands guard outside the door, Haphrama comes back — with Xirie, a Runelord of Sloth whose stone face asked the party a question at the Trial of Runes two sessions ago. Aureys deletes a third of the enemy line with a free-action Battle Cry that sends a cultist fleeing off the map and never brings it back. Haphrama answers with a Prismatic Wall across the corridor. Mithtirith spends his entire turn and a Mythic Point on a mythic-proficiency counteract check and takes it down. Xirie Translocates out and abandons Haphrama where he stands — "Xirie, you wretch!" — and Aureys, who had delayed her whole turn betting the wall would fall, kills him. The Risen who escaped at 94% HP last session does not escape twice.

The last fight is two Chromatic Oozes — 550 hit points each, thirty feet of reach, five Strikes a round, and a reaction that swaps their element every time somebody finds the one they're weak to. They are also mindless, which quietly switches off Aureys' entire debuff engine: no Demoralize, no Feint, no Stupefied, no Frightened. So the job of dragging their AC down falls to Sevek, and he does it with two of his own tools — Greater Crushing's Clumsy on every crit, and his own Taunt for the off-guard — walking them from AC 28 to 24. The party converts that into nine critical hits, and Sevek kills both. In between, the party reads the cult's research and gets the plainest statement of the campaign's stakes yet — Xanderghul intends to destroy Golarion and use the energy of its destruction to become a god — and Aureys finds a wall in the northeast that is "illusion on illusion on enchantments," the work of "not just a master, but a legend," which cannot be brute-forced and needs a formula nobody has yet.

They open one more door with four minutes left on the clock. Behind it: four steel vats burbling with foul liquid, work tables of surgical tools, jars of eyeballs and strips of flesh. Nobody at the table says it out loud, but this is the room from Aureys' portal-vision in Session 6 — the one where Aethusa was carried out unconscious. Xirie is there. So is a Risen Runelord of Gluttony, Kaliphesta, who snaps her fingers and boils the vats over. Cody looks at the clock and stops.

Timeline

Act I · Exploration · The Study

A comet with three tails, and Aethusa's spellbook

  • Cold open immediately after S9's entrance fight. Paya runs the heal queue — Fresh Produce, Ocean's Balm, Dash of Herbs, Torrent in the Blood — and has to talk Mithtirith through dropping his own Invisibility to be healed. Mithtirith, still invisible: "I will grab his hand and guide it to me."
  • Aureys scouts east into an L-shaped study: tables of maps and scribbled notes to the south, bookshelves to the north, and where the arms meet, cushioned benches around a dais holding a tall lectern. A gemstone inlay on its face forms a blazing comet with three tails.
  • Not trapped, but very valuable. Aureys pries the gems off and takes an Aeon Stone (Amplifying); Cody volunteers that the lectern also holds a folio containing a planar displacement formula, which Mithtirith asks about, Aureys reads out, and Paya logs into the stash.
  • The aeon stone gets negotiated in twenty seconds. Mithtirith: "I'm trying not to drool." Aureys: "Well, you're our main spellcaster, so drool away." Sevek: "You and Kreebnock can do rock, paper, scissors." Kreebnock: "I've already got two stones that help me, so I'm good." Mithtirith: "Well, if you're good, I'll be greedy." Paya, notarizing: "It's a perfect 50-50 split. One of you is good and one of you is great." It goes to Mithtirith.
  • Mithtirith sweeps the shelves with Detect Magic, which is how the party learns which books are enchanted before anyone starts reading.
  • The bookshelves hold history, philosophy, religion and a great deal of arcane theory. Two things stand out: a collection of treatises, and a book titled "Thrice Born Souls" with a heavily enchanted dagger shoved in as a bookmark. It is a wizard's spellbook, and it belongs to Aethusa.
  • Sevek sets the route before anyone opens a door: "Do we want to check the room down there? … I think big rooms maybe before all the little rooms?"
Act II · Combat · Vogorond, and the Runelords of Sloth

A statue with wings for arms, and an hour in the air

  • An archway east opens on an octagonal chamber 70 feet across. At the center, an enormous statue of cracked stone: a feminine figure with feathered wings in place of arms, her face crumbled away, the carvings in her hair suggesting she once wore a crown of flowers. An altar slab lies at her feet.
  • It springs to life as Aureys approaches, debris rising around it in a swirling aura. Sevek: "Classic."
  • Mithtirith burns Scholarly Recollection for two Recall Knowledge rolls and gets nothing — "you're actually not certain what this being is." Rare creature. Mithtirith: "That's extra scary." … "Though there's lots of things in life I haven't encountered."
  • The creature's shape emerges the hard way: heavy physical resistance turns Paya's 45 into 22, and when Paya's Reactive Strike crits it, Cody names the mechanic — "because that crits, the aura goes from 10 to 20 feet." It gets stronger the harder you hit it.
  • Vogorond's second Launch Rubble is centred on itself to catch more of the party, eating 25 of its own damage. It never lands an attack all fight.
  • Round 3 — two humanoid figures appear thirty feet up. "Runelord Tannaris and Runelord Ivamura, Runelords of Sloth." Tannaris: "Well, if it isn't the roaches who have been making a mess of the crypts." Ivamura: "Let's get on with it, shall we?"
  • Paya kills Vogorond on the same beat they arrive — a Vicious Swing crit, 92 rolled and 60 after resistance. Cody: "Well, just in time, as this thing will shatter."
  • The fight goes vertical and stays there. Sevek flies up and critically Grapples Ivamura; she spends a Mythic Point to break it. Paya Godspeeds and jumps 35 feet straight up. Aureys flies 60.
  • The Wall of Stone. Tannaris quickens it and boxes Aureys, Paya and Sevek into a sealed cube, ceiling included. Kreebnock: "Do your Kool-Aid Man impression." Aureys: "Mix me a hole." Sevek Flurries a section apart for 76 and flies out through the gap.
  • Mithtirith kills Ivamura with Tempest Surge — a critical failure on a natural 1, 98 into her last 10 hit points. Sevek kills Tannaris a round later on the first swing of a Flurry.
Act III · Exploration · The Wall That Can't Be Forced

Illusion on illusion on enchantment

  • Aureys searches the balcony and the chamber beyond with Sevek assisting, and finds it: the northeast wall is not mundane. "Illusion on illusion on enchantments," expertly hidden, concealing something.
  • Cody is explicit that there is no brute-force answer: "the work of not just a master, but a legend among this kind of magic." It needs a specific formula.
  • Kreebnock makes the connection nobody else does: "Perhaps we can search in the library? That planar displacement formula?" — tying the folio Aureys pocketed an hour earlier to the wall they can't open. Paya, immediately: "Oh, that's true."
  • Mithtirith's Arcana and Kreebnock's Occultism both come up short. Sevek hunts for a bypass instead — "Is there any teleportation? … like fifth rank Translocate, if it's a magical gate" — and gets nowhere.
  • Sevek calls the session's ending five seconds before Paya seconds it: "Come back. I say that and then we come back and everything's gone." Paya: "Surely we'll be able to come back and nothing bad will happen."
  • After the break, south: a sitting area and six identical bedrooms, nothing valuable. Sevek: "Maybe there's treasure under this bed." Cody: "Maybe the treasure is the bed, you don't know." Kreebnock: "It's the rest you get along the way."
  • At the end of a side hallway Aureys finds a secret door, and the wall slides open on a great wide chamber — which is exactly when doors to the south start swinging open.
Act IV · Combat · Four Pridespawn Cultists

The blind that blanked two natural 20s

  • "With Bec de Corbin brandished, they'll attack." Sevek rolls a natural 1 for initiative and goes last all fight — everything he contributes before his own turn is a reaction.
  • Paya calls the shape of the fight before anyone gets punished for it: "These guys are reactive strikers from book one." Nobody eats a Reactive Strike they hadn't chosen to.
  • The reaction that decided the fight. Cultist B strides through Sevek's reach; Stand Still fires, natural 20, and the Celestial Armaments Fortitude save comes up a natural 1 — blinded, two rounds.
  • And the blind pays out completely. The blinded cultist rolls two natural 20s at Aureys in the same turn and misses both on the DC 11 flat check. Cody: "the blind saved you from a crit." Aureys finishes the entire fight without taking a single point of damage.
  • Aureys runs the Grovel/Feint engine all fight, and after a cultist puts two hits into Paya: "I don't like people who hit my pineapple, but you are beautiful, I will admit. So pretty." → "Don't hit my pineapple." Cody: "One hell of a euphemism."
  • ⚠️ The Fortification rune bug surfaced again. Cody: "go ahead and make the fortification rune." Sevek: "No, there's no fortification." Cody: "Where does it keep saying that from?"
  • Sevek closes the fight in a single turn: Elf Step, Inner Upheaval, then a Flurry with one attack on each remaining cultist — both critical hits, 98 apiece, killing Cultist A and Cultist C back to back.
  • Searching afterward, Sevek sees the cult's treasure workbench first: "Oh, I see a lot of gold. And by gold, I mean gold color." … "This is like the dragon's dream right here." Aureys: "A treasure hoard! Aureys is like, yes!"
  • The research pays off in ten minutes. Xanderghul has adopted the Crypt of Pride as his headquarters; by robbing the other Runelords' crypts he gathered the objects to create many of the Risen; most of the cult is elsewhere; and his objective is stated plainly for the first time — destroy Golarion and use the energy of its destruction to become a god. Aureys: "That tracks."
  • Mithtirith, three minutes before it matters: "I feel like the people that ran away didn't just run away, you know what I mean?"
Act V · Combat · Haphrama Returns, with Xirie

A wall of seven colours, and one turn spent well

  • From his post at the door, Sevek sees them coming. More Pridespawn, Runelord Haphrama, and a figure the party recognises — "it was her face who asked you a question." Runelord of Sloth, Xirie.
  • Xirie, to Haphrama: "So these were the ones who gave you such a fright." Haphrama: "Silence. Just deal with them."
  • Aureys deletes a third of the enemy line with a free action. Battle Cry on the initiative roll: a natural 20 Demoralize against a Pridespawn Cultist, Frightened 2 and Fleeing. It runs and never comes back — nobody in the party ever touches it.
  • Sevek and Kreebnock both spend Mythic Points on initiative. Kreebnock's 51 is the highest in the fight, which is why he opens it with a Telekinetic Bombardment that throws two cultists prone.
  • Xirie's Phantasmagoria lands worst on exactly the wrong person: Kreebnock is Confused. Cody: "How did the psychic do the worst on the will save?" He rolls a random target, gets Aureys, and hits her for 28. Aureys: "What did I do to you, little man?"
  • ⚠️ Kreebnock had prepared for this. He carries Still Earth as an anti-Confused reaction, checked for it the moment it landed, and found it on cooldown — he'd spent it in the morning's first fight.
  • Paya kills Cultist A on a natural 20, and his next strike sets up Aureys' Opportune Backstab, a crit for 95 that kills Cultist B.
  • Haphrama raises a Prismatic Wall — 30 feet high, seven layers, cutting the corridor. Sevek is on the far side of it with no answer of his own, so he names Dispel Magic to Mithtirith and tells Aureys to hold her turn for it. Aureys delays her entire turn on the chance it works.
  • Mithtirith takes it down, and it costs him his whole turn — Mythic Casting plus a rank-8 Dispel Magic, three actions and a Mythic Point, rolling 41 plus 4 for mythic = 45. Kreebnock, mourning: "It was so pretty though."
  • Xirie Translocates out and abandons Haphrama — "Xirie, you wretch!" Aureys: "They really are quite cruel to one another." She had taken 10 damage all fight.
  • Aureys kills Haphrama with a critical Earth to Heavens Strike. The Risen who Translocated out at 94% HP last session does not get to do it twice.
Act VI · Combat · The Portal, and the Chromatic Oozes

Two mindless things, and the AC that walked down

  • Up the stairs, set into the north wall, a window 30 feet in diameter onto an expanse of wispy multicolored mist — and out of it "bright glowing eyes and mouths stare back at you. Gaunt, sunken faces that are the only source of real light out there." They watch "as if daring you to enter."
  • Aureys defers to the casters; Kreebnock and Mithtirith both fail to identify it. Kreebnock: "Yeah, that sounds terrible. Let's not go there." Paya: "Definitely not. The door definitely isn't staring back, right, Cody?"
  • The east door smells of sulfur. Aureys: "Unfortunately, it's not undead. I mean, because I'm kind of getting hungry." Kreebnock: "And breach."
  • Beyond, an alchemical lab with an iron cauldron nearly 10 feet across full of liquid "that rapidly shifts between all colors of the spectrum." Aureys flies in and the substance smells her back. Two Chromatic Oozes.
  • They are mindless, and it costs Aureys her whole kit — Demoralize ruled out, round-1 Feint failed on the die, round-2 Feint succeeded and was then ruled out. She commits to the bit anyway: "Oh, aren't you so oozealicious?"
  • With her debuff engine off, the AC job falls to Sevek — Greater Crushing's Clumsy on every crit (28 → 26) and his own Taunt for the off-guard (26 → 24). Nine of the encounter's seventeen party crits came off that drop, only five of them his.
  • He reads the Chromatic Shift correctly: "A is now weak to fire and resistant to electricity." Forcing a second shift converts the old resistance into weakness 20, and the party plays both oozes back and forth across it for +20 twice.
  • Paya spends Unbelievable Interception on a hit aimed at Mithtirith and Deflecting Wave on an 80-point crit — and finishes as the fight's top damage dealer on seven crits from eight rolls.
  • Sevek kills both oozes. Loot: bombs, Elixirs of Life, and a cauldron Kreebnock identifies as orichalcum — extremely valuable, nearly immovable. Paya: "If right before we teleport, I transform into a Tyrannosaurus and then pick it up, would that be okay?"
Act VII · Mystery · The Vats

The room from Aureys' vision

  • One more door, four minutes on the clock. Cody makes the party walk into the room rather than get caught in the hallway.
  • Four steel vats burbling with foul liquid fill the southern leg of an L-shaped room. The northern half is work tables "cluttered with surgical tools, as well as anatomical drawings and jars in which eyeballs, strips of flesh, and other body parts are suspended."
  • This is the room from Aureys' Session 6 portal-vision — Aethusa unconscious among magical apparatuses and four immense steel vats, Xanderghul's ghost leaving her for a fresh body. Nobody at the table says it out loud.
  • Xirie is here, two Pridespawn Cody suggests "might well be the two that have ran away before" — and one more Risen, known "not from personal memory, but if only from historical texts." One of the Runelords of Gluttony: Kaliphesta.
  • She snaps her fingers, the cauldrons boil over, and "twisted amalgamations of flesh and bone tumble forth."
  • Aureys: "Oh, tasty." Paya: "I'm glad one of us is appetized." Mithtirith: "Fancy."
  • Cody stops there. Next session opens on this fight, at level 17, and he expects to finish the Crypt of Runes in it.

Character Spotlight

Sevek · the One Standing in Front

  • Five kills — more than anyone at the table — 1,372 damage rolled, and the most damage taken in the party at 621. He was in the front rank of all four fights and never dropped.
  • He is the party's answer to walls. Tannaris sealed three PCs into a stone cube with a ceiling on it, and Sevek's response was to punch through — Flurry of Blows on the wall for 76, destroy it, fly out the hole, keep fighting.
  • Two crit-blinds in two fights, both on natural-1 saves, and the first one was worth more than the kill. A cultist walked through his reach, Stand Still crit it, and the save failed — blinded two rounds, during which it rolled two natural 20s at Aureys and missed both on the flat check. She took zero damage in that entire fight, and that's why.
  • He ended two fights personally. The Pridespawn ambush died to one turn — Inner Upheaval, then a Flurry with a crit on each of the two survivors, 98 apiece. Both oozes died to his staff as well.
  • The ooze fight is the one where the debuff job was genuinely his. Mindlessness switched off Aureys' entire Feint/Demoralize engine — the reverse of the usual arrangement — so Sevek supplied it himself: Greater Crushing's Clumsy on every crit, his own Taunt for the off-guard, AC 28 down to 24. Nine of the party's seventeen crits that fight came off that drop, and only five were his. He built the condition; everyone spent it. Paya spent it best.
  • He read the Chromatic Shift correctly and said so plainly: "A is now weak to fire and resistant to electricity." Forcing a second shift turns the old resistance into weakness 20, and the party played both oozes across it for +20 twice.
  • Off the battlefield he took the unglamorous jobs and they paid. He assisted Aureys' search on the hidden wall. He hunted for a teleport bypass. And he volunteered to stand watch so the casters could research — "I'll do my old job. I used to be a bodyguard" — which is the only reason the party got warning that Haphrama was coming back.
  • He also called the session's ending before it happened. Debating whether to research now or later: "Come back. I say that and then we come back and everything's gone." Paya seconded it five seconds later, and the recap of that argument is the whole of Act V.
  • When the party dithered after Haphrama died, he ended it: "They're just going to keep coming. Might as well go find them." And on spotting the cult's treasure hoard first and immediately handing the moment to Aureys: "This is like the dragon's dream right here."

Aureys Hollow · the Compliment Before the Knife

  • Three kills, and she killed Haphrama — the Risen who got away last session at 94% HP without dealing a single point of damage. Earth to Heavens Strike, the claw a critical hit for 86, and he vanished into light.
  • She deleted an enemy with a free action. Battle Cry on the initiative roll: a natural 20 Demoralize, critical success, Frightened 2 and Fleeing. The cultist ran off the map and never came back, and nobody in the party ever had to touch it. That is a third of the enemy line removed before round one.
  • She found everything worth finding this session. The lectern's gems and the Aeon Stone. The planar displacement folio. The secret door. And the hidden northeast wall — "the work of a legend" — which she then argued the party into spending real time on. Mithtirith diagnosed it exactly: "This is your greed talking, isn't it?" / "It is." She'd set that up ninety minutes earlier: "I'll put the rest of the stones in what I like to think of as my hoard — but it's the party stash."
  • The flattery patter is a mechanic and a bit at the same time, and it ran in all four fights — including the one where it couldn't possibly work. To a Runelord of Sloth: "oh, you're so energetic, my lovely sloth lord" … "You're just lovely and slow." To his colleague: "Oh, you're so awesome, Ivamura! Why did I think that other dude was great?" To a mindless ooze that has no mind to flatter: "Oh, aren't you so oozealicious?"
  • The best line of the session is a defense of Paya. After a cultist put two hits into him: "I don't like people who hit my pineapple, but you are beautiful, I will admit. So pretty." Then, flatly: "Don't hit my pineapple."

Draconic Salvation turned a failed Implosion save into a success, which her build upgraded to a critical — the only save conversion anyone managed all session. She then cleared both of Xirie's Phantasmagorias on plain successes and kept her reaction where Paya lost his, which is why the Opportune Backstab that killed a cultist was available at all.

Paya · the Man Who Reads the Fight First

  • Top damage in the party by a wide margin, three kills, and the best dice night at the table — including seven critical hits from eight attack rolls in the ooze fight, where he out-damaged everyone.
  • He killed Vogorond on the exact beat the reinforcements arrived — a Vicious Swing crit as two Runelords stepped onto the balcony. Cody: "Well, just in time, as this thing will shatter."

  • His own reaction economy is a weapon. Tactical Reflexes gives him two Reactive Strikes a round and he used them: a crit for 82 punishing a Stride, a crit for 64 that set up Tannaris' death by persistent damage, and the strike that fed Aureys' Opportune Backstab kill.

Mithtirith · the Answer to the Wall

  • He killed Ivamura and he took down the Prismatic Wall — two of the session's most consequential acts, both from one of the quietest seats at the table.
  • The Prismatic Wall dispel is the play of the session, and it cost him everything he had that turn. Haphrama split the party with a 30-foot, seven-layer wall nobody could safely cross. Mithtirith worked out mid-turn that a Mythic Point could push a counteract check to mythic proficiency, confirmed mythic casting with Cody, and spent all three actions and the point on it.
