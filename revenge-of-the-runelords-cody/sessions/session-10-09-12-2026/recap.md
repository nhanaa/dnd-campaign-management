## Session Summary

Haphrama ran last session, and the party spends this one finding out what he ran *to*. The answer is that the Crypt of Pride is not a tomb any more — it is a **headquarters**. Bedrooms with made beds. A library with cross-referenced research. A workbench heaped with magic items. An alchemical lab. Xanderghul's cult lives here, and the party spends three and a half hours walking through their house, killing everything in it, and reading their notes.

Four fights, twelve kills, and nobody drops. A cracked statue of a winged woman peels off its plinth as **Vogorond** — a creature that shrugs off physical damage and whose debris aura *grows* every time somebody crits it — and two rounds in, two **Risen Runelords of Sloth** step onto a balcony thirty feet up to watch. Tannaris and Ivamura fight the whole battle in the air and from behind walls: Gravity Wave, Implosion, Weight of the World, Disintegrate, and finally a **Wall of Stone** that seals Sevek, Paya and Aureys into a stone box with a lid on it. Sevek punches a hole in the wall and flies out through it. Both Risen die in the air, and both of them have to spend Mythic Points to get that far.

Then the house fights back twice. Four **Pridespawn Cultists** ambush the party the instant Aureys cracks a secret door — and the fight's best moment is a reaction: Sevek's Stand Still crits a cultist and the Celestial Armaments save **blinds it for two rounds**, after which it rolls two natural 20s at Aureys and misses both on the flat check. She finishes that fight at full health. Later, while Mithtirith and Aureys read the cult's research and Sevek stands guard outside the door, **Haphrama comes back** — with **Xirie**, a Runelord of Sloth whose stone face asked the party a question at the Trial of Runes two sessions ago. Aureys deletes a third of the enemy line with a **free-action Battle Cry** that sends a cultist fleeing off the map and never brings it back. Haphrama answers with a **Prismatic Wall** across the corridor. Mithtirith spends his entire turn and a Mythic Point on a mythic-proficiency counteract check and **takes it down**. Xirie Translocates out and abandons Haphrama where he stands — *"Xirie, you wretch!"* — and Aureys, who had delayed her whole turn betting the wall would fall, kills him. The Risen who escaped at 94% HP last session does not escape twice.

The last fight is two **Chromatic Oozes** — 550 hit points each, thirty feet of reach, five Strikes a round, and a reaction that swaps their element every time somebody finds the one they're weak to. They are also **mindless**, which quietly switches off Aureys' entire debuff engine: no Demoralize, no Feint, no Stupefied, no Frightened. So the job of dragging their AC down falls to Sevek, and he does it with two of his own tools — Greater Crushing's Clumsy on every crit, and his own Taunt for the off-guard — walking them from AC 28 to 24. The party converts that into **nine critical hits**, and Sevek kills both. In between, the party reads the cult's research and gets the plainest statement of the campaign's stakes yet — Xanderghul intends to **destroy Golarion and use the energy of its destruction to become a god** — and Aureys finds a wall in the northeast that is *"illusion on illusion on enchantments,"* the work of *"not just a master, but a legend,"* which cannot be brute-forced and needs a formula nobody has yet.

They open one more door with four minutes left on the clock. Behind it: **four steel vats** burbling with foul liquid, work tables of surgical tools, jars of eyeballs and strips of flesh. Nobody at the table says it out loud, but this is the room from Aureys' portal-vision in Session 6 — the one where Aethusa was carried out unconscious. Xirie is there. So is a **Risen Runelord of Gluttony, Kaliphesta**, who snaps her fingers and boils the vats over. Cody looks at the clock and stops.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Study (00:00–13:00)
- Cold open immediately after S9's entrance fight. **Paya runs the heal queue** — Fresh Produce, Ocean's Balm, Dash of Herbs, Torrent in the Blood — and has to talk Mithtirith through dropping his own Invisibility to be healed. Mithtirith, still invisible: *"I will grab his hand and guide it to me."*
- Aureys scouts east into an **L-shaped study**: tables of maps and scribbled notes to the south, bookshelves of tomes to the north, and where the arms meet, cushioned benches around a dais holding a tall **lectern**. A gemstone inlay on its face forms **a blazing comet with three tails**.
- Not trapped, but very valuable. Aureys pries the gems off and takes an **Aeon Stone (Amplifying)**; Cody volunteers that the lectern also holds a **folio containing a planar displacement formula**, which Mithtirith asks about, Aureys reads out, and Paya logs into the stash.
- The aeon stone gets negotiated in about twenty seconds. Mithtirith: *"I'm trying not to drool."* Aureys: *"Well, you're our main spellcaster, so drool away."* Sevek: *"You and Kreebnock can do rock, paper, scissors."* Kreebnock: *"I've already got two stones that help me, so I'm good."* Mithtirith: *"Well, if you're good, I'll be greedy."* Paya, notarizing: *"It's a perfect 50-50 split. One of you is good and one of you is great."* It goes to **Mithtirith**.
- **Mithtirith sweeps the shelves with Detect Magic**, which is how the party learns which books are enchanted before anyone starts reading.
- The bookshelves: history, philosophy, religion, and a great deal of arcane theory. Two things stand out — a collection of **treatises**, and a book titled **"Thrice Born Souls"** with a **heavily enchanted dagger** shoved in as a bookmark. It is a wizard's spellbook, and it belongs to **Aethusa**.
- Sevek sets the route before anyone opens a door: *"Do we want to check the room down there? … I think big rooms maybe before all the little rooms?"*

### Act II — Vogorond, and the Runelords of Sloth (13:25–74:30)
- An archway east opens on an octagonal chamber 70 feet across. At the center, an **enormous statue** of cracked stone at odds with the pristine marble everywhere else: a **feminine figure with feathered wings in place of arms**, her face crumbled away, the carvings in her hair suggesting she once wore **a crown of flowers**. A slab at her feet serves as an altar.
- It springs to life as Aureys approaches. Marble chips and debris rise around it in a **swirling aura**. Sevek: *"Classic."* Initiative against the **Vogorond**.
- Mithtirith burns **Scholarly Recollection** for two Recall Knowledge rolls and gets nothing — *"you're actually not certain what this being is. It's not of any kind you've encountered before."* Rare creature, very high DC. Mithtirith: *"That's extra scary."* … *"Though there's lots of things in life I haven't encountered."*
- The creature's shape becomes clear the hard way. **Heavy physical resistance** turns Paya's 45 into 22 and Aureys' 42 into 19. A two-action **Launch Rubble** (20-foot burst) **consumes its debris aura**, which it then reapplies at 10 feet. And when Paya's Reactive Strike crits it, Cody says the quiet part out loud: *"because that crits, the aura goes from 10 to 20 feet."* **The thing gets stronger the harder you hit it.**
- Sevek fuses Reflective Ripple and Whirlwind, **lands a Trip**, and misses the follow-up — but the Trip costs Vogorond an action to stand in round 2 and sets up Paya's crit.
- The second Launch Rubble catches only two PCs (Paya critically saves), and **Vogorond centres the burst on itself to catch more of the party, eating 25 of its own damage.**
- **Round 3, and the room gets bigger.** Two humanoid figures appear on a platform 30 feet up. *"Those who have made a study of ancient Thassilon recognize Runelord Tannaris and Runelord Ivamura, Runelords of Sloth."* Tannaris: *"Well, if it isn't the roaches who have been making a mess of the crypts."* Ivamura: *"Let's get on with it, shall we?"*
- **Paya kills Vogorond on the same beat they arrive** — a Vicious Swing crit, 92 rolled and 60 after resistance, into 31 remaining hit points. Cody: *"Well, just in time, as this thing will shatter."*
- The fight goes vertical immediately and never comes down. **Sevek flies thirty feet up and critically Grapples Ivamura**, Restraining her — and **she spends a Mythic Point on Remove a Condition** to get free, then Gravity Waves him prone. Aureys flies sixty feet to join them.
- Tannaris throws **Implosion** at Aureys; she converts the failed save to a success with **Draconic Salvation**, and her build upgrades it to a **critical** success.
- Ivamura's two-action **Force Rain** catches the ground party. Mithtirith answers with **Vindictive Thoughts** — a Mythic Point to save at mythic proficiency and throw the damage back — mis-clicks a Will save and **crit-fails it on a natural 1**, then **spends a second Mythic Point to reroll** and lands a 19 for half. Ivamura critically succeeds against the mental damage, so **two Mythic Points buy exactly one halved save.**
- Mithtirith asks a rules question mid-fight and gets an answer he clearly files away: *"Can you cast two spells in a round if you have the action economy for it?"* → yes, if one is single-action. *"That's what I wanted to hear."*
- Paya spends **Godspeed** and jumps 35 feet straight up for a 98-damage crit, then crits again on a Reactive Strike for 64 — which leaves Tannaris on **2 hit points**, and **the persistent fire and void off Paya's Greater Flaming and Decaying runes tick him to zero.** He burns **Mythic Ferocity** to return at 200, and Cody catches his own automation mid-sentence: *"It should be all of his Mythic Points to do this, not just one."*
- **The Wall of Stone.** On the way down Tannaris quickens a **Wall of Stone** and boxes in Aureys, Paya and Sevek — floor and ceiling both — leaving Mithtirith outside. Kreebnock: *"Do your Kool-Aid Man impression."* Aureys: *"Mix me a hole."* **Sevek Flurries a section of the wall to rubble for 76 and flies out through the gap.** Mithtirith, alone on the far side, uses the same wall for cover and Hides.
- Ivamura wants Implosion on Mithtirith and can't reach — *"They have made so many spells, only 30 feet range"* — and drops **Weight of the World** on him instead: stunned 2, dazzled. Tannaris tries the same spell on Sevek a round later and **Sevek critically succeeds the Fortitude save for nothing at all.**
- Ivamura tries to fly clear and eats both reactions on the way — **Sevek's Stand Still for 37 and Paya's Reactive Strike for 48**, which would have killed her — so she burns her own **Mythic Ferocity**. Her 7th-rank **Disintegrate at Kreebnock then misses on a natural 3**; Kreebnock takes nothing at all in the entire fight. Paya, watching: *"Some days you spend all your Mythic points leading up to the big spell, and then the big spell misses."*
- **Mithtirith kills Ivamura with Tempest Surge** — she critically fails the Reflex save on a **natural 1**, 98 damage into 10 remaining hit points. Two Mythic Points to him, one to everyone else.
- **Sevek kills Tannaris** one round later: Electric Arc for 9 (Tannaris saves), then the first swing of a Flurry for 43 into 12 remaining hit points. The second swing was never needed. *"And with that, he too vanishes into light. Sevek gets two mythic points back."*
- Vogorond, for the record, **never landed an attack.** Its entire offence was two Launch Rubbles, one of which it dropped on its own head.
- Post-fight, Sevek: *"I think that's the longest time I've been in the air."* Aureys: *"Me too. You should try it more, it's awesome."* Kreebnock: *"Kreebnock just floats there even though he didn't rise in the air the whole battle."*

### Act III — The wall that can't be forced (74:30–98:00)
- Aureys searches the balcony and the pentagonal chamber beyond with **Sevek assisting**, and she finds it: the **northeast wall is not mundane.** The faintest trace of magic, *"expertly hidden"* — *"illusion on illusion on enchantments,"* a very complex series of incantations concealing something. Cody is explicit that this one has no brute-force answer: *"the work of not just a master, but a legend among this kind of magic."* It needs **a specific formula**.
- **Kreebnock makes the connection nobody else does**: *"Perhaps we can search in the library? That planar displacement formula?"* — tying the folio Aureys pocketed in Act I to the wall they can't open. Paya, immediately: *"Oh, that's true."*
- Mithtirith's Arcana and Kreebnock's Occultism both come up short on undoing it. Aureys, on where to look, in a line the transcript only half-preserves: someone will have left a diary with the password written in it.
- Sevek hunts for a bypass instead — *"Is there any teleportation? … like fifth rank Translocate, something like that, if it's a magical gate"* — and gets nowhere.
- The party debates researching now vs. clearing first, and **Sevek calls the ending five seconds before Paya seconds it**: *"Come back. I say that and then we come back and everything's gone."* Paya: *"Surely we'll be able to come back and nothing bad will happen."* Break called.
- After the break, south: a sitting area and **six identical bedrooms**, well-made furniture, nothing valuable. Sevek: *"Maybe there's treasure under this bed."* Cody: *"Maybe the treasure is the bed, you don't know."* Kreebnock: *"It's the rest you get along the way."*
- At the end of a side hallway Aureys finds a **secret door**, and the wall slides open on a great wide chamber — and that is exactly when doors to the south start swinging open.

### Act IV — Four Pridespawn Cultists (97:37–131:11)
- *"With Bec de Corbin brandished, they'll attack."* Sevek rolls a **natural 1 for initiative** and goes last all fight — everything he contributes before his own turn is a reaction.
- Paya calls the shape of the fight before anyone gets punished for it: **"These guys are reactive strikers from book one."**
- Cultist D outruns its own allies to Feint Paya and open on him. Cody: *"it didn't quite realize how much further it was going ahead than the rest of its buddies."*
- Paya spends the fight's only Mythic Point on **Divide and Conquer** — two mythic-proficiency strikes, **Sickened 2 on both targets** — then enters **Lunging Stance** for 15-foot Reactive Strikes. Kreebnock's rank-8 Daze finds the encounter's real defence and does **zero damage**: resist mental 10 on top of a +4 status bonus to mental saves.
- **The reaction that decided the fight.** Cultist B strides through Sevek's reach; **Stand Still** fires, **natural 20**, 76 damage, and Greater Crushing stamps Clumsy 2 and Enfeebled 2. The **Celestial Armaments Fortitude save (DC 34) comes up a natural 1 — blinded, two rounds.**
- **And the blind pays out completely.** The blinded cultist rolls **two natural 20s at Aureys in the same turn and misses both on the DC 11 flat check.** Cody: *"the blind saved you from a crit."* His own follow-up a beat later: *"It didn't hit."* **Aureys finishes the entire fight without taking a single point of damage.**
- Cultist A flanks Sevek for a **Cruel Sneak Attack critical, 68 damage** — the worst hit he takes in this fight.
- ⚠️ **The Fortification rune bug surfaced again.** Cody: *"go ahead and make the fortification rune."* Sevek: *"No, there's no fortification."* Cody: *"Where does it keep saying that from?"* The prompt is embedded in the *attacker's* crit line, which is why it keeps ambushing everyone.
- Aureys runs the Grovel/Feint engine all fight: *"Aren't you so awesome? I know you are."* … *"You're so awesome, you know? So pretty and sinful."* … and, after a cultist puts two hits into Paya, **"I don't like people who hit my pineapple, but you are beautiful, I will admit. So pretty."** → *"Don't hit my pineapple."* Cody: *"One hell of a euphemism."*
- Mithtirith casts **Humble** to strip the manipulate trait off his next spell, denying a Reactive Strike Cody had already announced out loud.
- **Paya's Whirlwind Strike kills Cultist D.** Cody then rolls his **third natural 20 in a row** — on a Feint, not an attack.
- Paya's exit provokes from **both** surviving cultists: B's Reactive Strike lands for 33, and **C's misses by exactly one — the margin Taunt was buying.**
- **Aureys kills Cultist B** with a fan strike off her own successful Feint.
- Round 4: Sevek's second Stand Still **critically misses on a natural 1**, but **Paya's Lunging-Stance Reactive Strike crits for 82.**
- **Sevek closes the fight in a single turn**: Elf Step, Inner Upheaval, then a Flurry of Blows with one attack on each remaining cultist — **both critical hits, 98 damage apiece**, the second a natural 20, **killing Cultist A and Cultist C back to back.** Cody: *"And with that, C is down as well, and we are out of combat."*
- Loot off the bodies: each cultist carried a **+2 greater striking shock bec de corbin** and a set of **+2 greater resilient raiment leather armor**.
- Searching the southern room, **Sevek sees it first**: a workbench *"just filled with treasure and magical items."* Sevek: *"Oh, I see a lot of gold. And by gold, I mean gold color."* … **"This is like the dragon's dream right here."** Aureys: *"A treasure hoard! Aureys is like, yes!"*
- Among it: a **Wand of Widening Fireball (7th-rank)** and an **aeon stone**. Kreebnock can't cast Fireball and Sevek would have to Trick Magic Item it — but **Mithtirith can, and does**: *"Because I can, if so."* Paya: *"Fireball from our side sounds great."* The wand goes to Mithtirith. Cody's *"Need to teach some of you guys Trick Magic Item"* is an aside about everyone else.
- Aureys pushes to spend the time researching the hidden wall. Mithtirith: *"This is your greed talking, isn't it?"* Aureys: *"It is."*
- **Sevek volunteers for the door**: *"I'll do my old job. I used to be a bodyguard."*
- **The research pays off in ten minutes.** Xanderghul and his cultists have adopted the **Crypt of Pride as their new headquarters**. By robbing the crypts of the other Runelords he gathered the objects he needed to create **many of** the Risen Runelords who now serve him. **Most of the cult is currently elsewhere.** And his objective is stated plainly for the first time: **to destroy Golarion and use the energy released in its destruction to become a god.** Aureys: *"That tracks."* Cody, closing: there is *"much more to learn."*
- Mithtirith, three minutes before it matters: *"I feel like the people that ran away didn't just run away, you know what I mean?"*

### Act V — Haphrama returns, with Xirie (138:38–168:41)
- **From his post at the door, Sevek sees them coming**: more Pridespawn, **Runelord Haphrama**, and another figure — *"You recognize this from one of the statues outside, as it was her face who asked you a question."* **Runelord of Sloth, Xirie.**
- Xirie, to Haphrama: *"So these were the ones who gave you such a fright."* Haphrama: *"Silence. Just deal with them."*
- Sevek alerts the party. **Aureys deletes a third of the enemy line with a free action**: **Battle Cry** on the initiative roll, a **natural 20** Demoralize against **Pridespawn Cultist C** — critical success by 10, **Frightened 2 and Fleeing.** C runs on its turn and **never comes back.** No one lays a finger on it all fight.
- Both **Sevek and Kreebnock spend Mythic Points on initiative** to roll at mythic proficiency. Kreebnock's 51 is the highest in the fight, which is why he opens it.
- Kreebnock leads with **Telekinetic Bombardment** into all three cultists — A and B thrown — then Courageous Anthem.
- Sevek's round-1 Flurry crits **Cultist A**, and the Celestial Armaments save comes up a **natural 1** again: **blinded two rounds**, with Greater Crushing's Enfeebled 2 on top. A's two follow-up swings produce a miss-by-1 and a critical miss against AC 41.
- Xirie's opener is **Phantasmagoria**, and it catches everyone except Mithtirith, who is out of her line of sight and takes **zero damage all fight**. **Kreebnock fails and is Confused.** Paya succeeds and loses his reactions. **Aureys simply succeeds** — and, unlike Paya, keeps her reaction, which is what makes the Opportune Backstab possible next round.
- Confused, Kreebnock rolls a d6 for a random target, gets **Aureys**, and puts a **Telekinetic Projectile into her for 28**. Aureys, taking it: **"What did I do to you, little man?"** Forced to close, he then eats **Cultist B's Reactive Strike crit for 80** and drops to a third of his health before clearing the Confused on the flat check.
- ⚠️ Worth noting: **Kreebnock had prepared for exactly this.** He carries **Still Earth** off an aeon stone as an anti-Confused answer, checked for it the moment it landed, and found it on cooldown — he'd spent it in the first fight of the day.
- **Paya kills Cultist A on his own turn** — first Strike of Needle in the Gods' Eyes, **natural 20** — after eating A's Reactive Strike for 33 on the Leap. His second Strike into Cultist B sets up **Aureys' Opportune Backstab, a crit for 95 that kills it.**
- Sevek strides to flank with Paya, Flurries, and **Group Taunts all three remaining enemies**.
- Xirie spends a Mythic Point to **Recharge** and casts **Phantasmagoria a second time**; **Kreebnock spends his last Mythic Point** and this time critically succeeds.
- **Haphrama's play: a Prismatic Wall**, 30 feet high, seven layers, cutting the corridor in half. Paya Reactive Strikes the cast and rolls a natural 2. **Sevek is on the far side of it and can do nothing about it himself** — so he puts **Dispel Magic** in front of Mithtirith and tells Aureys to hold her turn for it.
- **Mithtirith takes it down, and it costs him his whole turn.** He works out mid-turn that a Mythic Point buys mythic proficiency on a counteract check, confirms **mythic casting** with Cody, and spends all three actions on it — the 1-action spellshape plus a 2-action Dispel Magic. He rolls **41, plus 4 for mythic proficiency = 45.** *"That would succeed and dispel the wall."* Kreebnock, mourning: *"It was so pretty though."*
- **Aureys had delayed her entire turn betting on exactly that.** Sevek: *"So, Aureys, it feels good that you waited."*
- She flies through and goes to work on Haphrama: *"oh, you're so bald, so purple-eyed and beautiful, and I love the gems in your head"* … **"Anyway, I love the gems that I'm going to take out of your head when you're dead."**
- Paya lands all three strikes on Haphrama, the middle one a crit for 90 — Sevek: *"That's the reason it was a crit."* Paya: *"Thank you for flanking, no matter where I am."*
- **Xirie leaves.** She casts Translocate and abandons Haphrama mid-fight. Haphrama, watching her go: *"Xirie, you wretch!"* Aureys: *"They really are quite cruel to one another."* She had taken **10 damage** all fight, every point of it from Sevek's Electric Arc.
- Haphrama's last acts are a **Plasma Whirl** and a strike at Aureys. Aureys: *"I thought we had something going here, Guy."* Sevek: *"I don't think he agrees."*
- **Aureys kills Haphrama** — Earth to Heavens Strike, the claw a critical hit for 86. *"Haphrama vanishes into light. Aureys gets two mythic points back, everyone else gets one back."* He **never spent a point on Mythic Ferocity**, which — going by the S6 pattern — means he had none left. The Risen who Translocated out at 94% HP last session does not get to do it twice.
- Aureys loots the dead cultists during the healing: more becs de corbin, more raiment armor.

### Act VI — The portal, and the Chromatic Oozes (170:58–204:06)
- Up the stairs: a narrow chamber with steps down west and a wider stairwell up east. Set into the north wall is a **window 30 feet in diameter**, and through it an expanse of **wispy multicolored mist**. Out of the mist, *"these bright glowing eyes and mouths stare back at you. Gaunt, sunken faces that are the only source of real light out there."* They watch *"as if daring you to enter."*
- Aureys defers to the casters — *"I look over at the more learned among us and I'm like, what's that?"* — and **Kreebnock and Mithtirith both roll to identify it.** Neither can: a portal into a dimension nobody recognises.
- Kreebnock: *"Yeah, that sounds terrible. Let's not go there."* Sevek: *"Yep, maybe not. Or at least, not for now."* Paya: *"Definitely not. The door definitely isn't staring back, right, Cody?"*
- The east door isn't locked, but something behind it smells — *"just this overwhelming sulfurous chemical smell."* Aureys: *"Unfortunately, it's not undead. I mean, because I'm kind of getting hungry."* Kreebnock: *"And breach."*
- Beyond: an **alchemical lab**. Shelves and marble work tables in three corners, and in the center an **enormous iron cauldron nearly 10 feet across**, full of glowing liquid *"that rapidly shifts between all colors of the spectrum, casting rainbow dappled light throughout the room."*
- Aureys flies in, and the substance smells her back. The pot boils over. **Two Chromatic Oozes.**
- They are **mindless**, and it costs Aureys her whole kit. Her pre-combat Demoralize is ruled out on the spot. Her round-1 Feint fails on the die. Her round-2 Feint **succeeds** and is *then* ruled out for mindlessness — the sharper loss of the two. Her Stupefied riders are inert and her Frightened never reaches the AC math. She commits to the bit anyway: *"Oh, aren't you so oozealicious?"*
- Their kit: **Pseudopod Eruption** (two actions, four Strikes at **30 feet of reach**) plus a third-action Strike at MAP −10 — **five Strikes a round, most of the party every round** — and **Chromatic Shift**, a reaction that changes their element and resistances in response to the damage type that just hit them. They are also **immune to critical hits**, which Cody enforces by halving every crit by hand: *"his oozes are already automated to not take the double."*
- **With Aureys' debuff engine off, the AC job falls to Sevek, and he does it with two tools.** His crits stamp **Clumsy 2** from the Greater Crushing rune (AC 28 → 26), and his **Taunt** supplies the off-guard on top (26 → 24). Five of his six critical hits this fight would have been ordinary hits at base AC. The party cashes it: **nine of the seventeen crits in this encounter came off the reduced AC** — five Sevek, two Paya, two Aureys.
- **He also reads the Chromatic Shift correctly**, out loud: *"A is now weak to fire and resistant to electricity."* Forcing a second shift converts a creature's *previous* resistance into **weakness 20**, and the party works both oozes back and forth across it. It pays twice, +20 each time — including a 22-point Electric Arc that landed for 42.
- Paya spends **Unbelievable Interception** to take a hit aimed at Mithtirith, and **Deflecting Wave** on an 80-point crit.
- Mithtirith's Recall Knowledge fails on these too — also rare — and he uses **Fast Channel** to two-action a three-action Heal.
- Aureys **swaps her fan's rune back to fire mid-combat** — the fourth time this has happened, and still an unsettled table question.
- **Sevek kills both oozes**, Ooze A at the end of round 2 and Ooze B with an Inner Upheaval crit for 96 in round 3. But **Paya was the fight's top damage dealer** — roughly 355 applied to Sevek's 303, on **seven critical hits from eight attack rolls.**
- Loot: bombs, **three major and four moderate Elixirs of Life**, and the cauldron itself, which **Kreebnock identifies as orichalcum** — extremely valuable and nearly immovable. Paya: *"I have no way on this planet to carry it."* … *"If right before we teleport, I transform into a Tyrannosaurus and then pick it up, would that be okay?"* Kreebnock: *"I can transform into a dragon and help you carry it."*
- Kreebnock breaks out a **Healing Well** (four charges) he'd never used: *"Gotta justify my purchase."*
