Session Summary

Sevek's first session opens in the immediate aftermath of catastrophe: the unveiling of the Circle of Open Hands portal ended with an enormous magical shockwave hitting Xin-Eurythnia — earthquakes, landslides, a sinkhole that swallowed both the portal and Queen Sorshen herself, and giant flightless birds boiling up out of the pit. Ayavah, High Priestess of Nocticula, gathers the battered party at Midnight's Gallery and introduces the newcomer she's been working with: a plain-spoken monk from Roderic's Cove who was invited to the festival by Sorshen's own letter. Introductions are made — Sevek gives a pineapple in full plate a very long look — and the situation crystallizes fast. The climate-control towers that keep a city this high in the Kodars livable are dead, things are crawling out of the rifts, and Xin-Eurythnia is finished. Some 10,000 survivors must be evacuated south to Vekr's Crossing.

What follows is a session built almost entirely around that exodus. Seven days of preparation (Fortify Shelters, Comfort and Protect, Oppose the Risen, Scavenge Supplies — a daily-activity minigame with Supply Points on the line) are interrupted on day four by a pyroclastic mukradi, a mythic-empowered, three-headed Gargantuan horror that puts Sevek on the floor dying before Paya impales it. Then the Flight of the Eurythnians proper: a ten-day infiltration-style trek through the High Kodars — hundred-foot-tall watchers clinging to the mountaintops, an avalanche Sevek clears with a nat 20, a parley bought with a critical success from Paya that turns the naiad Svevenka from a hazard into a gift-giver, Risen saboteurs sniffed out by Aureys, a night ambush by four misery siktemporas, stone giant bandits flattened by Paya alone, and finally two Risen Runelords of Sloth — Krune and Krenlith — on mirage dragon mounts, come to finish what Gimmel could not.

The runelord fight is the session's crescendo: mounted enemies bombing the party from 50 feet up, Sevek unfurling actual dragon wings from his tattoo, Aureys dealing the most damage she's ever done in this game and then getting dropped by a teleporting runelord, and the sky-shaking arrival of Kandirvek, a blue dragon who evaporates the third runelord's mount off-screen, snaps the rider out of the air, and lands with "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?" Both runelords burn Mythic Ferocity to survive their first deaths; both die anyway — Krune run through by Paya, Krenlith beaten down by Sevek's bo staff. Ten thousand people, minus one hundred souls lost in the mountains, arrive within sight of Vekr's Crossing, and the party hits level 14.

Timeline

Act I · Roleplay · Ashes of the Festival

Midnight's Gallery — introductions in the rubble

  • Cody's cold open recaps: the mythic dreams, Ayavah and Sorshen, the Risen of the Trinity Star, the Warshard of Gorum that fell into the Kodars last year, the festival disaster, Sorshen swallowed by the sinkhole.
  • Ayavah, haggard from her own fight, collects the party and brings them to Midnight's Gallery, now packed with refugees.
  • Sevek is introduced — ex-muscle turned local hero of Roderic's Cove, invited to the festival personally. Paya opens his helm to show full palm fronds: "Well met, friend. I'm Paya, and you will see a pineapple in full plate." Sevek: "I haven't seen one like you before."
  • Aureys flits over munching a dried eyeball "like an apple" and — eyeing Sevek's dragon scales — makes clear that loot is shared equally, but the hoard itself is not up for negotiation. (And, to Ayavah: "We do get to keep the loot, right?")
  • Ayavah lays it out: no Sorshen, no infrastructure, monsters from below, cold closing in. The city cannot be saved. Evacuation to Vekr's Crossing — a small town a week's march down the Kodars — is the only option. Roughly one-fifth of the city's 12,500 people are already dead.
Act II · Skill Challenge · Seven Days of Preparation

Supply Points, stuff acquisition, and a nat 20 wasted on chores

  • Daily activities, one per PC per day: Fortify Shelters (Survival Points bleed if neglected), Comfort and Protect (Supply Points), Oppose the Risen (consumables + prevents casualties), Scavenge Supplies (Supply Points).
  • The party settles into lanes: Mithtirith alternates comfort and shelters, Aureys scavenges every single day ("certified stuff acquirer"), Sevek opposes the Risen with Religion — the Trinity Star remnant is disorganized without Gimmel, and the activity has Sevek publicly dismantling their mischief day after day — and Paya floats between comfort, shelters, and Earn Income (a crit for 28 gp — "I've not had income do that much").
  • Aureys discovers Summon Mythic Power resets daily and cheerfully abuses it for rerolls all week ("plays by the rules, especially if she can bend them"). Cody allows it, noting the designers clearly never considered it.
  • Sevek's Oppose the Risen streak (including a nat 20 he'd rather have spent in combat) plus one weapon-strike crit from Paya nets 4,200 gp of common consumables; Paya buys potion patches and potions of quickness, the rest left to pick retroactively.
  • Preparation ends at 17 Supply Points. The wall-of-force wand prompts the discovery that a party in a Runelords campaign has no arcane caster. Cody: "Runelord game with no arcane. Interesting."
Act III · Combat · Day 4 of Preparations

The Pyroclastic Mukradi

  • At dusk on day four, a mythic-empowered three-headed monstrosity — flame, shock, and smoke maws — erupts from the sinkhole and turns its burning eyes on the party.
  • Full round-by-round in Combat Encounters below. Headlines: Correct the Story un-writes a crit on Sevek, Sevek crit-Trips a Gargantuan, a Stand Still crit triggers Spitting Rage and 124 poison damage, Sevek drops to dying, and Paya lands the kill.
  • The fight cost Sevek 262 damage on his fourth in-game day with the party — Mithtirith: "It was your fourth day on the job." Sevek: "If you count the days where you didn't almost die."
  • After the fight, grateful townsfolk send thanks: a shockwave warhammer, the eye of fortune (Aureys' new eyepatch), and a wand of wall of force. Mechanically, Cody waives post-fight healing worries — it's the only combat of that day.
Act IV · The Trek · Flight of the Eurythnians, Days 1–5

10,000 people into the High Kodars

  • The trek runs as a 10-day infiltration: obstacles need 2–3 successes; failures accrue Awareness Points; thresholds trigger complications. Survival Points track the refugees — 100 points, each worth ~100 lives. Supplies tick down one per day.
  • Day 1 — Leaving Xin-Eurythnia: Will saves to hold the column together. Some citizens refuse to leave and are left to their fate. On the horizon: hundred-foot creatures clinging to the mountaintops like spiders, red eyes "like giant bloody sores," watching. They don't move. They don't pursue.
  • Day 2 — The avalanche: Sevek rolls a nat 20 on Athletics ("Finally, something I can do") and clears the path; buried in the snow, a destroyed merchant caravan yields +2 Supply Points.
  • Day 3 — Fen of the Ice Mists: the naiad Svevenka harries the column with blizzards. Paya's critical Nature check (working with Mithtirith) earns a face-to-face parley; Aureys steps up as spokes-dragon and lays out Sorshen's abduction with real gravity. Svevenka trades warnings — something is wrong out in the Kodars — and gifts: a tidal crossbow and a wand of howling blizzard.
  • Day 4 — Risen saboteurs: hidden cultists among the refugees ruin gear and food. Aureys thinks like a thief, crits, and roots them out instantly — no losses, +1 Supply Point. "It takes a hoarder to know a hoarder."
  • Day 5 — Nightmare rumors: the party's worst skill spread. Failures stack 3 Awareness Points → Disaster complication: phantom creatures lash out at panicking refugees. Sevek's Religion 43 vs DC 35 clutch save prevents any deaths. Aureys then crit-dances the column calm — earning everyone mythic-proficiency initiative for the fight that night.
Act V · Combat · Night of Day 5

Misery in the Tent City

  • On evening patrol of the tent city, the paranoia the party spent all day soothing comes for them: four misery siktemporas — eyeless, spider-limbed entities of the dimension of time, embodying misery itself — rise up behind the tents.
  • Full breakdown below. Headlines: regeneration 40 cracked by Paya's Recall Knowledge, a triple-reaction ambush of the ambushers, and Sevek Taunt-tanking to literally zero damage taken.
  • Kill spread: Paya (B), Sevek (A), Aureys (C and D — the last one shredded while she stood on its chest "like a menacing little pest").
  • No loot on the bodies — "Stuff of dreams, but not my dreams, because I dream of stuff" (Aureys). The refugees sleep safe; the party heals up with the morning.
Act VI · The Trek · Days 6–10

Storms, stragglers, giants, and dragon sightings

  • Day 6 — The Harrowing Storm: whispers in the blizzard try to stampede refugees into the wilds; Paya's and Mithtirith's Nature checks hold the line.
  • Day 7 — The Missing Eurythnians: dozens wander off. Aureys and Sevek take to the air; Paya's Nature check fails — a hundred people lost to the snow — and Mithtirith's follow-up fails too, his heart no longer in it after the table wrote the wanderers off ("my heart wasn't in it"). Then Aureys' crit recovers a hundred more, found freezing in a cave. Aureys: "Can't solve for stupid" → after the crit → "I guess I can solve for stupid."
  • Day 8 — The Kodar Kings: organized stone giant bandits eye 10,000 walking treasure troves. Sevek's strike whiffs on a 2 ("I have given you false confidence. I apologize," offers Paya) — and the failure bites: another hundred lives and a Supply Point gone. Then Paya walks up alone and clears the entire obstacle with one crit strike — "just a blank stare and then just a bloodbath." Mithtirith: "Paya just looks at him with that stare he has."
  • Day 9 — Frayed Nerves: Mithtirith leads the faithful in prayer ("Nethys cares more about magic than dogma. I get you."); Aureys' chicken dance crit-fails, gets mythic-rerolled, and works anyway.
  • Day 10 — Dragon sightings: Paya's Arcana failure tips the party to the 6-Awareness threshold → the sightings are real. Three adult mirage dragons with riders inbound — until a blue dragon slams one out of the sky mid-approach.
Act VII · Combat · Day 10

The Runelords of Sloth

  • Krune and Krenlith, former Runelords of Sloth, descend on mirage dragon mounts. Krune, eyeing the party's silver mythic auras: "I expect you're the reason that waste of skin Gimmel hasn't reported back in… I hope you've all made your peace."
  • Paya: "Long made my peace with your deaths. Let any runelords come." Aureys goes darker still — see Memorable Quotes.
  • Mid-fight, the blue dragon — Kandirvek — finishes the third runelord off-screen and joins the party's side: "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?" Full breakdown below.
Act VIII · Roleplay · Kinsmen

A very large ally, a very small handshake

  • Kandirvek — Cody's self-declared favorite NPC — introduces himself: "Though I wish it were under different circumstances, it's a pleasure to meet you all. I think we've much to talk about."
  • Sevek bows: "Oh no, we needed your help." Aureys lands on the big dragon's shoulder: "Hello, kinsmen."
  • Vekr's Crossing is less than a day out. The party reaches level 14.

The Pyroclastic Mukradi

Opening — first blood and first fire

  • Aureys' Battle Cry Demoralize lands (Frightened 1) — then the mukradi wins initiative and its Flame Maw breath (16d6 = 65) catches all four PCs: Sevek 60, Paya 65, Mithtirith 65; Aureys crit-saves.
  • Round 1: Aureys draws first blood with a claw (23). Sevek enters Reflective Ripple Stance and whiffs two Trips ("That's a bad start"). Mithtirith Scholarly-Recollects the immunities ("Pass that on to my friends") and heals Paya 69. Paya's Sudden Charge misses twice.
  • The mukradi Thrashes — two misses, then a Smoke Maw crit on Sevek → Pax burns a mythic point on Correct the Story and the reroll comes up a critical miss. Paya: "The story was incorrect." The mukradi burns a mythic point of its own to Recharge its breath. Cody: "You guys couldn't hit it. It couldn't hit you. It was a slap fight that round."

Round 2 — the Gargantuan goes down (literally)

  • Aureys swaps her flaming rune for holy mid-fight (fire immunity) and Distracting-Feints it. Sevek crit-Trips the Gargantuan monster (47 vs DC 33) — Bullying Staff earning its keep — Taunts, and settles into Whirlwind Stance (AC 38).
  • Paya's Vicious Swing whiffs but his MAP −10 follow-up sneaks in for 19, and Aureys' Opportune Backstab (27) applies Enfeebled 1.
  • Second breath (electricity line at the back rank): Paya crit-saves on a mythic reroll, Mithtirith takes 27.

Round 3 — Spitting Rage and the floor

  • Sevek's Inner Upheaval flurry connects (37); Aureys backstabs again (21) and her Majestic Presence sticks Frightened 1; Mithtirith's Divine Wrath is crit-saved ("Fortitude's crazy good on this guy"); Paya Vicious Swings for 41.
  • The prone mukradi tries to stand — double reaction: Paya's Reactive Strike (33) and Sevek's Stand Still CRIT (34), disrupting the action and keeping it prone — but the crit triggers Spitting Rage: instant poison breath. Sevek crit-fails on a nat 3 and takes 124, down to 6 HP — "That's still up. I'll be fine. I'll take it."
  • The Thrash that follows hits Aureys 44 and Paya 34, then crits Sevek (vs AC 38!) for 78 — the Fortification flat check even succeeds (19), but mid-roll Cody rules the rune can't legally sit on explorer's clothing, so the crit stands and Sevek drops to dying, his Mortal Herald self-Heal forgotten in the chaos. Cody, moving the token: "Don't worry, as Mythic, it's much harder to kill you." The crit tail lash then catches Mithtirith for 72 from 30-foot reach.

Round 4 — one desperate thrust

  • Aureys' Breath of the Dragon (void, 26) lands; Mithtirith closes distance ("I might as well go big") and puts a 100-point Heal into the dying Sevek.
  • Paya Spear Dances (35), Aureys' third Opportune Backstab of the fight (47) leaves it "in single digits," and Paya's second Spear Dance strike (30) impales it — "With one desperate thrust, you just impale this thing as a burst of red light emits from it." Sevek's kip-up comes a beat after the red light fades.
  • Aftermath: Paya +2 mythic points, everyone else +1. Sevek took 262 damage — on his fourth in-game day with the party. Mithtirith, deadpan: "It was your fourth day on the job." Sevek: "If you count the days where you didn't almost die."

Round 1 — the ambushers get ambushed

  • Aureys' day-5 crit means everyone rolls initiative at mythic proficiency (+6) — the whole party acts before the ambushers. Her Battle Cry Demoralize bounces — immune to emotion. "We learned something."
  • Paya Sudden Charges B (36); Aureys — swapping her fearsome rune for holy on the spot — combos B twice (34+34); Sevek enters Whirlwind Stance, Taunts B, and belatedly assigns Bodyguard to Mithtirith ("It's a daily preparation thing I just forgot to do"); Mithtirith casts Sanctuary "because I feel vulnerable."
  • A pounces Paya — Paya's Reactive Strike crits for 88 — and Inflict Misery leaves Paya Clumsy 2 / Enfeebled 2 / Stupefied 2; its Aklo Demoralize crit-fails ("nightmare gibberish").
  • B regenerates 40, pounces Sevek — and eats three reactions on its own turn: Paya's second Reactive Strike (Tactical Reflexes) crits for 62, Sevek's Stand Still lands 26, Aureys' Opportune Backstab adds 32 — then all three of its claws bounce off Sevek's Whirlwind-Stance AC. Its only win: a Demoralize in Aklo, which Sevek speaks — "Punished for speaking languages."
  • D's target proves less lucky: a nat 20 shrugs off Sanctuary — and a second nat 20 crits Mithtirith for 74 ("There's my double nat 20s"). C claws Paya.

Round 2 — cracking the regeneration

  • Paya's Recall Knowledge — squeaking past the DC with Stupefied 2 dragging the check — reveals the kill condition: mental or vitality damage shuts off regeneration, and his Nightmare rune already does mental. A's and B's regeneration switches off retroactively. Paya's Spear Dance drops B for good.
  • Aureys crits A for 99; Sevek's vitality-infused Inner Upheaval flurry kills A and shuts off C's regeneration with the follow-through ("Oh, it's down"), then re-Taunts onto D. Mithtirith Vampiric Feasts C (23 + 11 temp HP).
  • D ignores the Taunt to pound Paya (27+27 — down 108 at his low point); C claws Mithtirith (23).

Rounds 3–4 — a menacing little pest

  • Paya crits C (84); Aureys' fan combination finishes C ("You had exactly 31 HP left"). Sevek flows back into Reflective Ripple Stance and Trips D at exactly the DC — the stance's +1 made it — then strikes it prone (31), triggering Aureys' Opportune Backstab crit for 75 with bleed and persistent fire. Mithtirith's Heal restores 101 to Paya.
  • D regenerates 40 and flails at Aureys from the ground, landing one (29). Paya reapplies mental (41) to hold the regeneration off, and Aureys, standing on D's chest "like a menacing little pest," cuts the last of them to pieces.
  • Aftermath: Sevek took literally zero damage — "Interesting. I didn't take any damage." No loot ("Stuff of dreams, but not my dreams, because I dream of stuff" — Aureys).
Encounter 3 · Trek Day 10 · 5 rounds · Ally joins round 3

Risen Runelords Krune & Krenlith + Mirage Dragons

Enemies2× Risen Runelord (Sloth; mythic, Mythic Ferocity) · 2× mirage dragon mounts (fly 150 ft)
AllyKandirvek, adult blue dragon — 6-for-6 on attacks

Round 1 — fifty feet of altitude

  • The runelords open mounted at 50 feet — Krune on Dragon B, Krenlith on Dragon A. Rider and mount share one effective space; riders spend an action each turn to ride.
  • Sevek activates Dragon Wings from the Wyrm's Wingspan tattoo and climbs. Krune sneers "Back on the ground where you belong" — Telekinetic Bombardment at the two fliers (Sevek mythic-rerolls to a save and takes 21 — his only damage of the fight; Aureys: "Not this dragon, buddy").
  • Mithtirith identifies the dragons and casts Divine Immolation — 20 fire plus persistent into Dragon A, 10 into Krenlith. Aureys breathes void on the same pair, then sneak-hides in Sevek's square on a crit Stealth.
  • Krenlith's Telekinetic Bombardment knocks Mithtirith prone; his fumbling for the never-before-used Runelord's Response reaction ends when it turns out to cost a mythic point he doesn't have. Dragon B's Hallucinatory Breath is double-crit-saved by Paya and Mithtirith; Paya takes to the air on winged sandals; Dragon A dives and breathes on the hidden pair — Sevek and Aureys both crit-save (Aureys on a mythic-reroll nat 20).

Round 2 — the record-setter

  • Sevek chases, whiffs two Trips on Dragon B. Krune quickened-Slows Sevek ("seeing as you seem to want to move around a lot") and shields. Mithtirith debuts the Wand of Howling Blizzard — Dragon B eats all 38 cold; Krune nat-20 crit-saves.
  • Aureys Feints Krune and unloads a double-crit for 145 — Frightened 2, Enfeebled 1, bleeding, burning — "the most damage I've ever done in this game."
  • Krenlith's Arrow Salvo hammers the ground pair (Mithtirith 37, Paya 18). Dragon B's Frenzy answers with a nat-20 claw on Aureys for 70 ("Payback is a bitch") — but when it tries to fly clear, Sevek's Stand Still crits, disrupting the move, his Phantasmal Doorknob rider blinds it (class-DC save, failed by 1), and Aureys' Opportune Backstab rides Sevek's hit for 29 more.
  • Paya Vicious Swings Krune (114 crit). Dragon A's full Frenzy mauls the lone grounded Mithtirith (24+24) — "there's never a line of defenders when you need them."

Round 3 — the sky tears open

  • Sevek's Inner Upheaval flurry drops Krune — who burns every mythic point on Mythic Ferocity and returns at half HP.
  • Then Kandirvek: the blue dragon evaporates the third mirage dragon off-screen, snaps its runelord out of the air mid-dive, lands, and joins initiative — "Let's even the odds a bit, shall we?" Aureys, spotting a kindred silhouette, cries out: "Ah! Brother!"
  • Krune casts Arrow Salvo from dragonback — Paya's Reactive Strike crits for 84 and disrupts the spell outright. Mithtirith Heals himself 98.
  • Aureys' Majestic Presence crit-fails Dragon B on a nat 1 — Frightened 4 — and her follow-up combination (129 double-crit) kills it outright, dumping Krune 30 feet to the ground — 15 damage, prone — with Aureys landing on top of him.
  • Krenlith Blink Charges the little dragon: two polearm hits drop Aureys unconscious. Kandirvek's Frenzy crits Dragon A twice (82 + 88) and "snaps this one's neck." Paya flies down and runs Krune through — "he too vanishes into light" (mythic points refresh) — then immediately releases a hand and Ocean's Balms the dying Aureys (41).

Rounds 4–5 — dead twice each

  • Sevek, Slowed, whiffs on a nat 1 + nat 2 ("Oh, God, I roll so terrible"). Mithtirith tops Aureys back up with a 108 Heal; she kips up, fan-dances into Krenlith's square, Feints, Frightens him again, and lands a 111 crit combination.
  • Krenlith tries to retreat — his Step is blocked, and the Stride eats Sevek's Stand Still (26) plus Aureys' Opportune Backstab (31) — then casts Cast into Time at them both: two more crit-saves (Cody: "It's like all crits this combat").
  • Kandirvek's Frenzy brings Krenlith down — he burns all his mythic points on Mythic Ferocity — and the wing strike still lands; Kandirvek finishes six-for-six on attacks. Paya crits for 70.
  • Sevek's Guidance-boosted Flurry of Blows lands both hits: "And again, a Risen Runelord vanishes into light." (Cody never applied the last damage in Foundry — the strikes killed him.)
  • Aftermath: Kandirvek: "Though I dare say you probably didn't need my help there." Sevek, bowing: "Oh no, we needed your help." Level 14.

Character Spotlight

Sevek · the Man Who Stands in Front

Pax's debut — a plain-spoken monk whose faith did work all session without ever being named.

  • Introduced himself the way he plays: plainly. Ex-hired-muscle from Roderic's Cove, no embellishment — and gave the pineapple knight a long, honest stare: "I haven't seen one like you before."
  • "Lead the lost" is Ashava's edict, and he spent seventeen days leading ten thousand of them out of darkness — opposing Risen cultists with Religion checks by day (six days running, one nat 20, 3,600 gp of the party's 4,200 in consumables recovered), and making the clutch DC 35 Religion save that kept phantom-maddened refugees alive during the Disaster complication.
  • The dice war: his d20s were legendarily bad (Trip whiffs, a nat 2 that cost dearly on day 8, back-to-back nat 1–nat 2 in the finale — "Oh, God, I roll so terrible") and he took it with total grace: "a one's always going to be a one," and, to Cody's consolation math, "Very glass half full of you, Cody. I appreciate it."
  • The moments that mattered were all his: Correct the Story rewrote the mukradi's crit into a whiff; his Stand Still crit pinned the mukradi prone; his Taunt ate three straight siktempora claws against Whirlwind-Stance AC 38 (he ended that fight untouched); his vitality-infused Inner Upheaval flurry killed Siktempora A and shut off C's regeneration in one action; his flurry forced Krune to burn every mythic point on Mythic Ferocity; and his bo staff landed the killing blow on Risen Runelord Krenlith.
  • First-session bonds: designated Mithtirith as his Bodyguard charge mid-fight, unfurled the Wyrm's Wingspan dragon wings to fight runelords in mid-air, and closed the day bowing to Kandirvek: "Oh no, we needed your help." The table's verdict, unprompted: "That was nice getting a new player. You did a great job."

Paya · the Stare in Full Plate

A pineapple leshy in full plate who opens his helm to show palm fronds and shakes hands.

  • Utterly self-aware about his job: "What about Paya would make you think he's going to sudden charge in?" — asked while Mithtirith pre-healed him for exactly that.
  • The damage ledger is absurd: crits of 88, 84, 62 on the siktemporas, 114 and 84 on Krune, 70 on Krenlith — and the killing blows on the mukradi, Siktempora B, and Runelord Krune. Then the humility: "I don't want to be the fighter who does 200 damage and then complains. Based on a true story."
  • His non-combat day was just as strong: the critical Nature check that turned Svevenka from hazard to ally, and the day-8 obstacle he cleared alone — one crit strike into the Kodar Kings and the stone giants' banditry ended in "kneecaps everywhere."
  • Quiet team-first play throughout: Ocean's Balm on the downed Aureys mid-fight, tactical delay calls, mythic points spent on evacuation chores without complaint, and the party's rules brain whenever Foundry or flight rules got fuzzy.

Aureys Hollow · the Second Coolest Dragon Here

Tiny dragon, teeny tiny eyepatch, record-breaking damage, zero shame about any of it.

  • Ran the session's best long con of a bit: official party "certified stuff acquirer," scavenging every single day of the prep week, capped by claiming the eye of fortune as an eyepatch — "It'll make me look dashing." Cody: "Legend will be told about how the city of survivors was led away by a teeny tiny dragon with an eyepatch."
  • Discovered the daily mythic-point reset and monetized it shamelessly — "plays by the rules, especially if she can bend them."
  • When it counted, she was the party's voice: the Svevenka parley ("Our queen, Sorshen, has been abducted…") delivered with real weight, and the single darkest line of the session, hissed at two runelords — see Memorable Quotes.
  • Combat arc of the night: a miserable 2-2-2-2-2 start ("Its little arms are waving. We're like flat, flailing") that flipped completely — a 99 crit combination, a 75 crit, kill credit on Siktemporas C and D, then a personal-record 145 damage into Krune, a 129 double-crit that killed Mirage Dragon B out from under him, riding a dragon's neck "ripping and tearing," getting dropped by Krenlith's Blink Charge, and getting back up to Feint him into Sevek's kill.
  • Ended the session as she began it — on a dragon's shoulder, this time one several sizes her senior, whom she'd already hailed mid-battle with "Ah! Brother!": "Hello, kinsmen."

Mithtirith · the Mask Collector of Sandpoint

The engine that kept four people alive, with the driest one-liners at the table.

  • Two-action Heals of 100, 101, 98, and 108, Communal Healing riders, heroic recovery buffs handed out like candy — capped with the weary, affectionate "I'm healing you in every campaign, I think."
  • Ate the worst of every fight: a 65 breath, a 72 crit tail lash from 30-foot reach, a 74 crit claw ("Mithtirith is surprised he survived that round"), then three dragon attacks in the finale purely because he was the only one left on the ground ("there's never a line of defenders when you need them").
  • Sharp and funny in his own register: cast Sanctuary "on myself because I feel vulnerable" — and was proven immediately, horribly right when a siktempora rolled double nat 20s, one to break the spell and one to crit him for 74; sized up the dead siktemporas with "Their face looks like a good model for my next mask"; deadpanned "Don't worry, I'll crit fail for you" right before rolling a success.
  • The party's knowledge lane: Scholarly Recollection on the mukradi (fire immunity, reflex weakness — "Pass that on to my friends") and the mirage dragon identification mid-fight — a Sandpoint-campaign veteran with the master Lore to prove it.
  • His cleric-of-Nethys voice got a whole beat on day 9, leading terrified refugees in prayer: "Nethys cares more about magic than dogma. I get you."

Pax's Notes — Sevek's Journal

Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.

What Sevek did this session

  • Met the party at Midnight's Gallery through Ayavah. Three of them: the pineapple in plate (Paya), the tiny dragon (Aureys), the cleric of Nethys (Mithtirith). Good people. Strange people.
  • Worked six days of Oppose the Risen — Religion checks, public work, the way it should be done. Kept the cultists off the refugees. Recovered ~3,600 gp of the party's 4,200 gp in consumables.
  • Fought the three-headed thing out of the pit. Took 262 damage and dropped to dying when the Fortification rune turned out to be illegal on explorer's clothing. Correct the Story spent one mythic point to unwrite an earlier crit. Worth it. Trip works on Gargantuan — Bullying Staff earns its keep.
  • Cleared the avalanche on day 2 (nat 20). Made the DC 35 Religion save during the nightmare panic — no one died that night. Whiffed the day-8 strike against the Kodar Kings on a 2 — a hundred refugees and a supply point paid for it. Own it; Paya cleaned it up.
  • Taunt-tanked the siktemporas to zero damage taken. The kit works: Taunt + Whirlwind Stance + Bodyguard on Mithtirith.
  • Killed Siktempora A and Risen Runelord Krenlith. Forced Krune to burn his Mythic Ferocity.
  • First flight in combat — Dragon Wings (tattoo), one activation covering the whole fight. Flight economy is real: one action per round to stay up unless hovering.

Things Sevek knows that the party does not

  • His history with Riddleport's people — told plainly if asked, but no one has asked yet.
  • Sorshen's letter invited him by name and praised his work in Roderic's Cove. Whether that was flattery or intelligence-gathering, the queen knew who he was before he arrived.
  • He will not lie to these people. They should learn that sooner rather than later — Aureys especially deals in misdirection.

What Sevek is thinking about

  • The watchers. Hundred feet tall, red eyes, motionless on the mountaintops. Things that size don't need to chase. They were counting us.
  • Mithtirith stands where the line breaks and the enemy knows it — every fight, they went for him. Bodyguard stays on him. Be on the ground when he is.
  • Krune and Krenlith needed killing twice each (Mythic Ferocity). Assume every Risen has it. Budget damage for the second death.
  • The party has no arcane caster in a campaign about arcane god-kings. Think about what covers that gap.
  • Aureys hailed Kandirvek as "Brother" the moment he appeared. A dragon that hunts runelords is an ally worth keeping — and worth understanding first.
  • Went to 0 HP with Mortal Herald's 1/day free-action self-Heal unspent — it triggers exactly there. First session, new kit, chaos at the table; next time it fires.
  • Slowed 1 for a full minute off a quickened Slow. Casters at this level spend actions better than we do. Close distance faster.
