## The short version

Cody had a bad-dice night on Foundry (he said so himself — "after the 20th nat 2"), got progressively grumpier through six fights the party won cleanly, and at the climax — right after Sevek critically Tripped the mythic kraken for the second time — told Pax to his face that **"most things are going to start having Kip Up"** and that Trip **"can't be an auto-win every time."** That is a DM announcing, mid-fight, that he intends to design around one player's build because it worked. He walked it back within four minutes, apologized five separate times by the cleanup agent's count ([192:55], [193:49]–[194:10], [194:47]–[195:05], [196:08], [198:12]), and blamed Foundry and Paizo. Pax's only ask in response was reasonable and quiet: *if that's the direction, can we retrain?*

Nothing Pax did all session was unreasonable. He took a natural-20 crit for 60 without the Fortification rune he isn't entitled to, self-policed it out loud, volunteered "I'm going to take that damage," and rolled with every ruling. The frustration was Cody's, and he said as much.

## What the numbers say

- **Cody's dice really were bad.** Baolen crit-missed four of its last eight Strikes; the kraken callers whiffed 9 of 12 trident attacks across two fights; three enemy nat-1 Will saves. His frustration was real and the cause was real.
- **The party's dice were not unusual.** Aureys crit on Feint-set-up attacks at 19–20 because she has Keen and stacks off-guard + Frightened; that's build, not luck. Sevek's Trip is +32 against DC 35–36 — it succeeds on a 4. None of that is "insane."
- **Trip was not an auto-win.** Baolen stood up twice (two mythic points), Jetted 280 ft away once, and went **Double Attack ×3 from prone** on the final round and still hit Paya for 42. The elemental was ruled immune. The whale took one Trip out of two attempts.
- **Pax was the one eating the punishment.** Sevek took the 56-point belch, the 91-void scythe roll, a nat-20 jaws for 60 with no Fortification, Desiccate 67, Polar Ray 66 + jaws 36 + bleed in one turn, Drained 2 for the last three fights (Paya rolled two consecutive natural 1s trying to clear it), and tanked 14 of Baolen's Strikes by Taunt. He spent his Mortal Herald heal, Prevailing Position four times, two mythic rerolls on saves, and never once complained beyond *"I'm in a bit of a pickle."*

## What to watch for

- Whether "one or two more things" with Kip Up / stand-without-provoking actually stays at one or two, or becomes the default on anything Pax can reach. He said retraining will be available — hold him to that if it's needed.
- Whether the "you're too strong / Paizo didn't design for you" frame keeps escalating the buffs (Maddenmist took **434 damage** against a book HP of 251; the tsunami took 418 against 195). If the buff-then-resent loop continues, it's the loop, not the dice, that's the problem.
- Whether in-character lines get re-labeled as player misconduct again ("racist stuff"). Once is a bad joke. Twice is a pattern.

**Bottom line:** one bad night, fully acknowledged, with repeated apologies. As a reasonable adult, Pax understands and does not fault him for it. As a player who had real emotions and wanted to have fun, the bad feelings couldn't be reversed. **A strike regardless. If it recurs, he will leave. No matter how good Cody is as a DM.**

**The Kip Up test is specific:** it's about archetypes. Kip Up is a master Acrobatics feat. If a brute, a giant, a bulky monster that has no business being acrobatic somehow has it — that's Cody designing against Trip, not designing a creature. Watch for that. "One or two more things" with ways around Trip is fine *if they make sense for what the creature is.* A sedacthy caster with Escape or a kraken with Jet — those are real creature abilities. A brute with master Acrobatics is a tell.
