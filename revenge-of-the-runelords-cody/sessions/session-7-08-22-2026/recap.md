## Session Summary

Book Two opens on the throne nobody had sat in yet. The gemstone peacock feathers turn out to be glass — *"the stash behind it is where all the good stuff is"* — but the throne itself is a memory engine, and one by one the party sits in it and relives Xanderghul's last year from the inside: the heart of an immense eclipse, the possession of Aethusa, the research into raising Risen Runelords, and the decision to relocate that work to the **Emerald Chambers**, the lost Thassilonian crypt of First King Xin and the Runelords who followed him. The last vision is the one that matters. The Risen are bound to him — **if Xanderghul is slain, he simply moves into one of them**, and as long as he has the Chambers he can make as many as he wants. Aureys, who has by now sat on the throne twice because she is "certainly got a lot of pride," says the quiet part for everyone: *"we probably have to kill them first."* The throne also turns out to be the exit — it's paired with the Throne of the Troll King, and she's through it before anyone else has stood up.

Kandirvek is waiting on the far side, a little perplexed, and has a lead: the **Isle of Xin**, off Varisia's west coast, where Thassilon designed its inventions — including the Chambers — and where a sunken temple might hold the plans. First, Absalom. He knows someone with a teleportation circle; the party sells a stash the size of a small armory at a level-20 settlement, banks **8,514 gp each**, and Aureys goes looking for the sewer dragons who will become her kobolds. On the flight west Kandirvek admits what the level-15 party has quietly noticed: *"I fear in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon."*

The Isle of Xin is six fights in three hours. The altar on the islet is a mythic haunt wearing **Zutha's** face, vomiting spectral maggots and swinging a bone scythe that has to be *performed* at to be disabled — Aureys lands three of the four successes and takes no damage; Sevek, with a +0 in every one of those skills' key abilities, eats the belch, the maggots, and a 91-void scythe roll that a mythic reroll turns into 26. Below the islet, underwater: a **clockwork whale** that crits Paya into its jaws and is dead by the end of round two; a trio of **sedacthy** — fish-people who greet the party as "a feast… just as the Eater of History foretold" — whose crimson-scaled caster **Maddenmist** puts 102 points into Sevek in a single turn and is grappled by a tail for his trouble; a living **tsunami** that can't be tripped because it's water; a giant mutant octopus, **Quoroc**, and two more sedacthy, whom Aureys kills by groveling at them until they hold still; and, in a 200-foot octagon beneath a missing colossus, the Eater of History himself — **Baolen**, a mythic kraken with 60-foot tentacles, who declares *"Your history comes to an end here, my unfortunate explorers,"* spends every mythic point he has answering Sevek's Trips and Electric Arc, and is cut to pieces by Aureys' fan.

The session ends at **level 16**, with Baolen's hoard unsearched and Cody apologizing. The dice had been ugly on his side of the screen all afternoon; when Sevek's second critical Trip landed on the kraken, he said, mid-fight, that *"most things are going to start having Kip Up"* — and then took it back, twice, before the goodbyes. (Pax's notes on that are in a separate file.)

## Key Events

### Act I — The Peacock Throne (lore)
- Cody opens with no combat for the day; the garden is empty except the shattered Runewell and the throne. Aureys: *"I'm going to go be a dragon and look for valuables everywhere."* The gems on the throne are not gems. The **stash behind it** is real — Bands of Force (Greater), a +2 greater striking greater vitalizing mace, more. - **Paya's vision**: being lost inside the heart of an **immense eclipse** and slowly consumed, before an immense warshard rips you free and sends you hurtling to crash into Xin-Cyrusian — Xanderghul's own memory of riding the Trinity Star. The eclipse is new; Paya's Religion check (26) doesn't place it.
- **Aureys' vision** (*"I'm certainly got a lot of pride"* — Cody: *"You certainly do"*): joy at having a body, frustration born of pride at having to borrow Aethusa's, and **plans to forge a new body** in his old likeness — which the mirror-visions already showed he has done.
- **Sevek's vision** (*"I'm not very prideful, but I shall try it"*): research into the ritual that creates Risen Runelords, and a decision to **relocate to the Emerald Chambers** for access to the raw materials. The Risen *"are not the real Runelords. They're far too weak for that"* — they are Xanderghul's own ritual, fueled by the belongings and magic items of the Runelords they imitate, and with the Chambers he can make *a lot* of them, some far stronger than the ones just killed. Sevek: *"That's not good."*
- **Aureys' second vision**: binding the Risen to himself; they are unfalteringly loyal. And the key fact: **Xanderghul's body and the Risen's bodies are magically tied. If he is slain, he transfers into one of them.** *"So long as more Runelords are around, Xanderghul cannot be killed."* Aureys: *"we probably have to go find this Emerald Chamber… if we kill Xanderghul, he'll just arise again and take over one of the other Risen Runelords, so we probably have to kill them first."*
- One more thing in the chair: **the peacock throne is linked to the Throne of the Troll King.** Sit and envision the other, and you teleport. Aureys: *"Oh, and I've figured out the whole magic thing… Boop."* Paya follows on trust: *"At least if Aureys doesn't come back through and say, Wait! That was bad! Don't do it! Then I trust it."*

### Act VI — Baolen, Eater of History (mythic boss)
- The corridor opens into an **octagonal chamber nearly 200 feet across**, entirely submerged: seven archways into collapsed side rooms, harsh yellow light on a plinth holding **a pair of titanic stone feet** — the rest of the colossus long gone — tree-sized columns holding up a ceiling 100 feet up, and a hallway north.

Baolen's chamber is unsearched — *"you'll find a bunch of items and you will also learn some info about where you're going next."* Aureys: *"Aureys definitely feels like she's a powerful little dragon with her little horde of sewer dragons."* She'll tell Jesse to level up and shop; *"there'll probably be some stuff in the stash that he would definitely want to look at."*
