Session Summary

Book Two opens on the throne nobody had sat in yet. The gemstone peacock feathers turn out to be glass — "the stash behind it is where all the good stuff is" — but the throne itself is a memory engine, and one by one the party sits in it and relives Xanderghul's last year from the inside: the heart of an immense eclipse, the possession of Aethusa, the research into raising Risen Runelords, and the decision to relocate that work to the Emerald Chambers, the lost Thassilonian crypt of First King Xin and the Runelords who followed him. The last vision is the one that matters. The Risen are bound to him — if Xanderghul is slain, he simply moves into one of them, and as long as he has the Chambers he can make as many as he wants. Aureys, who has by now sat on the throne twice because she is "certainly got a lot of pride," says the quiet part for everyone: "we probably have to kill them first." The throne also turns out to be the exit — it's paired with the Throne of the Troll King, and she's through it before anyone else has stood up.

Kandirvek is waiting on the far side, a little perplexed, and has a lead: the Isle of Xin, off Varisia's west coast, where Thassilon designed its inventions — including the Chambers — and where a sunken temple might hold the plans. First, Absalom. He knows someone with a teleportation circle; the party sells a stash the size of a small armory at a level-20 settlement, banks 8,514 gp each, and Aureys goes looking for the sewer dragons who will become her kobolds. On the flight west Kandirvek admits what the level-15 party has quietly noticed: "I fear in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon."

The Isle of Xin is six fights in three hours. The altar on the islet is a mythic haunt wearing Zutha's face, vomiting spectral maggots and swinging a bone scythe that has to be performed at to be disabled — Aureys lands three of the four successes and takes no damage; Sevek, with a +0 in every one of those skills' key abilities, eats the belch, the maggots, and a 91-void scythe roll that a mythic reroll turns into 26. Below the islet, underwater: a clockwork whale that crits Paya into its jaws and is dead by the end of round two; a trio of sedacthy — fish-people who greet the party as "a feast… just as the Eater of History foretold" — whose crimson-scaled caster Maddenmist puts 102 points into Sevek in a single turn and is grappled by a tail for his trouble; a living tsunami that can't be tripped because it's water; a giant mutant octopus, Quoroc, and two more sedacthy, whom Aureys kills by groveling at them until they hold still; and, in a 200-foot octagon beneath a missing colossus, the Eater of History himself — Baolen, a mythic kraken with 60-foot tentacles, who declares "Your history comes to an end here, my unfortunate explorers," spends every mythic point he has answering Sevek's Trips and Electric Arc, and is cut to pieces by Aureys' fan.

The session ends at level 16, with Baolen's hoard unsearched and Cody apologizing. The dice had been ugly on his side of the screen all afternoon; when Sevek's second critical Trip landed on the kraken, he said, mid-fight, that "most things are going to start having Kip Up" — and then took it back, twice, before the goodbyes. (Pax's notes on that are in a separate file.)

The Session

Act I · Lore · The Peacock Throne

Five memories in a chair, and the one that matters

  • Cody opens with no combat for the day; the garden is empty except the shattered Runewell and the throne. Aureys: "I'm going to go be a dragon and look for valuables everywhere." The gems on the throne are not gems. The stash behind it is real — Bands of Force (Greater), a +2 greater striking greater vitalizing mace, more.
  • Nobody has Detect Magic without Mithtirith; the statues are inert. Sevek: "Want to try sitting on it? Anyone want to try sitting on it?" — "Go ahead, Mr. Pineapple." Paya: "I am the prideful pineapple… With nothing else making itself known to me, I will sit on the throne."
  • Paya's vision: being lost inside the heart of an immense eclipse and slowly consumed, before an immense warshard rips you free and sends you hurtling to crash into Xin-Cyrusian — Xanderghul's own memory of riding the Trinity Star. The eclipse is new; Paya's Religion check (26) doesn't place it.
  • Aureys' vision ("I'm certainly got a lot of pride" — Cody: "You certainly do"): joy at having a body, frustration born of pride at having to borrow Aethusa's, and plans to forge a new body in his old likeness — which the mirror-visions already showed he has done.
  • Sevek's vision ("I'm not very prideful, but I shall try it" — and to Paya, "You said you were prideful, okay? Don't take it back now."): research into the ritual that creates Risen Runelords, and a decision to relocate to the Emerald Chambers for access to the raw materials. The Chambers are a Thassilonian crypt — First King Xin's, and many Runelords after him — hidden somewhere in the Saga Lands; the location was secret even in Thassilon's day. The Risen "are not the real Runelords. They're far too weak for that" — they are Xanderghul's own ritual, fueled by the belongings and magic items of the Runelords they imitate, and with the Chambers he can make a lot of them, some far stronger than the ones just killed. Sevek: "That's not good."
  • Aureys' second vision (Paya nominated her: "the most prideful, the most majestic, if you will"): binding the Risen to himself; they are unfalteringly loyal. And the key fact: Xanderghul's body and the Risen's bodies are magically tied. If he is slain, he transfers into one of them. "So long as more Runelords are around, Xanderghul cannot be killed." Aureys: "we probably have to go find this Emerald Chamber… if we kill Xanderghul, he'll just arise again and take over one of the other Risen Runelords, so we probably have to kill them first."
  • One more thing in the chair: the peacock throne is linked to the Throne of the Troll King. Sit and envision the other, and you teleport. Aureys: "Oh, and I've figured out the whole magic thing… Boop." Paya follows on trust: "At least if Aureys doesn't come back through and say, Wait! That was bad! Don't do it! Then I trust it."
Act II · Roleplay · Kandirvek and the Isle of Xin

A perplexed dragon, a lead, and an admission

  • The Kodar cold hits exposed skin and Kandirvek is there, "a bit perplexed" — he didn't expect them back this way. The trolls are fighting each other over Gavergash's empty throne ("might makes right"), so he's had little trouble. Paya says the plain thing first: "Yes, we went to Xin-Cyrusian. We have grim tidings, but we should definitely get off this chair."
  • Told about the Emerald Chambers, he ponders: "Many have tried, and if my own research is to be believed, a few have succeeded. But finding the Chambers is only half the battle. Most who go there don't come back." The Chambers are a maze — "many people die of old age exploring the chambers before reaching their destination."
  • His lead: the Isle of Xin, west of the Saga Lands, where many Thassilonian inventions — including the Chambers — were dreamt up, and a sunken temple beneath the isle that may hold information. "Even for me, the Isle of Xin is dangerous."
  • Kandirvek can smell the magic coming off them. Nowhere in the Saga Lands can buy that much; he knows an ally with a teleportation circle to Absalom. Aureys: "I've never been to the big city." Then: "Aureys goes to Absalom to find her dragons who will become our kobolds. The sewer dragons. I shall take over them. The sewer kings." Cody: "when a level 15 little dragonette shows up, the kobolds will follow."
  • On the flight west, the admission: "you've grown in power quickly… I feel your mythic power may be accelerating your growth. As such, and as the creatures we deal with become more dangerous, I fear in combat I soon will be more hindrance than boon. But I'll aid in what ways I can, whether it be through research or contacts." Sevek: "Thank you… We'll bring you back with news." Cody, out of character: Kandirvek is level 15. They've caught up.

Sevek keeps the Antiplague: "I'm drinking this. This is actually kind of useful."
  • 8,514 gp each. Cody: "I imagine most of you forgot just like one or two expensive items you want." Sevek spends most of his on the +3 potency rune for the staff ("That's actually most of the money… I'm broke"). Aureys takes the stash's +3 rune on her handwraps for 80 gp transfer, buys a Keen rune and an Elemental Wayfinder (Water) for the Black Pearl Aeon Stone. He drops them on a small islet to the southwest: toppled columns and slabs of black marble, the upper halves of titanic statues breaking the waterline, a triumphal arch missing its top "as if swatted by one of the submerged statue's giant hands," and before the arch, a block of marble serving as an altar.
  • Sevek has Water Breathing (8 hours) on everyone already; he casts Feet to Fins from a wand at rank 6 — a 70-foot swim speed until tomorrow's preparations.
Act IV · Mythic Hazard · Zutha's Legacy

A Runelord's ghost, and the three skills nobody brought

  • Aureys goes looking for traps and "stuff." "Stuff indeed, and stuff can be found, but mayhap not the stuff you want." The altar shakes; overwhelming hunger and nausea — "like you're surrounded by food that's spoiled, even by your standards" — and it explodes in void energy. In its place hovers a heavyset spectral figure with a scythe of bone: Zutha, last Runelord of Gluttony. Screaming mouths manifest on the broken pillars and spit streams of spectral maggots.
  • Paya's Recall Knowledge: different magic entirely from the Risen — a very, very angry haunt, disabled by Occultism, Intimidation, or Performance, "and a good one. Definitely expect to burn some Mythic Points." Sevek: "Wow, that's great, because I suck at all of them… I suck as in I have no modifier at all."
  • Four successes later the pillar is gone and beneath it is a single entrance leading down. As a mythic hazard, everyone's mythic points refill. Aureys, who took zero damage: "I was made for this." Sevek: "Yes, you are. While I'm suffering over here." (See Combat Encounters.)
  • Loot scattered around the islet, "some of them very valuable": a key, a scroll of water breathing, a divine scroll of Sunburst (Sevek: "I can cast it, but I don't have hands, I guess. I'm going to save them"), and more.
  • Pre-dive: Aureys Bolsters Flesh (16 temp HP) and activates the wayfinder; Paya drinks the first of twenty-one elixirs.

  • Down the stairwell into a chamber whose floor has collapsed into a rubble-choked pool; salt deposits say it connects to the sea. Aureys, hidden, flies across — the way on is an underwater passage east. Sevek: "I do have a lot of water inside my body, so I guess—" Paya: "You just are water, you are not a water human." Nothing on it is salvageable afterward.
  • The next chamber: a smaller pool, steps rising to a pillared landing, chisels striking stone from an archway beyond. Paya: "let the trap finder go first. It has helped us innumerably in Sandpoint." Sevek: "Are we in a dungeon? Like, officially in a dungeon?" Cody: "Yes, we are."

They grin: "Ah, a feast has come to us today, friends. It's just as the Eater of History foretold." Sevek: "You're right, I haven't had fish in a while." Cody: "If you say shit like that, they're just gonna straight up attack." They do. Afterward, Aureys gnaws on "a few bits of sushi." Aureys: "I mean, that's awesome." Afterward Paya's Unusual Treatment to scrub Sevek's Drained 2 rolls a natural 1, and the mythic reroll rolls another natural 1 — "Hate to let you know, I rolled another one."
  • Doubling back west (Sevek: "since it's in the opposite direction, we might as well do that before heading deeper in"): a collapsed vault with giant-clam grottos draped in seaweed, more kraken callers, and an enormous octopus — not any normal octopus — looking at you all hungrily.   • Their hoard: "They've been hoarding some powerful stuff" — a talisman, a major striking rune, a greater flaming runestone (Sevek: "I have Flaming as well, which I would love to upgrade" — banked to the stash), and a magic skullcap heard but not identified.
Act VI · Mythic Boss · Baolen, Eater of History

"Your history comes to an end here"

  • The corridor opens into an octagonal chamber nearly 200 feet across, entirely submerged: seven archways into collapsed side rooms, harsh yellow light on a plinth holding a pair of titanic stone feet — the rest of the colossus long gone — tree-sized columns holding up a ceiling 100 feet up, and a hallway north.
  • A very large, dark shape squeezes through the north passage, filling it, and expands as it leaves. An enormous kraken takes the center of the room: "And so I found the ones who slaughtered my loyal subjects. I sense the destinies within you, the history, the paths to come — but none of it shall be. Your history comes to an end here, my unfortunate explorers." — Baolen, Eater of History. Aureys: "That's a cool name. We think we heard something about him before."
  • Sevek Trips him critically twice and Rippling-Spins a third Trip off the kraken's own arm; every one of Baolen's three mythic points goes to standing up or surviving Sevek's Electric Arc; Paya crits him for 110 off the Taunt's off-guard; and Aureys takes the kill — "you cut this enormous kraken to pieces. As those pieces float upward and away, we are out of combat." +2 mythic points to her, +1 to the others. (See Combat Encounters.)
  • Cody calls it there: level 16. Baolen's chamber is unsearched — "you'll find a bunch of items and you will also learn some info about where you're going next." Aureys: "Aureys definitely feels like she's a powerful little dragon with her little horde of sewer dragons." She'll tell Jesse to level up and shop; "there'll probably be some stuff in the stash that he would definitely want to look at."
  • And the apology. After the Kip Up remark mid-fight, Cody walks it back — "that was something in the moment I should not have said… that's on me" — and again at the goodbyes: "thank you all for putting up with me. I'm sorry for the grumpiness." Sevek asked one question: whether retraining would be available. "There will."
