## Session Summary

Morning broke over the bound, sleeping quasit in the blighted forest east of Iliora. The party woke Skizgrit, tried to interrogate him, and ran face-first into the limits of a captive's compliance — until **Jaethal's Charm Person** turned the demon into a temporary friend. Skizgrit, now cordial, told them what the giant claw marks belonged to (a **Barlgura**, invisible, immune to half the elements). The party — flagged by Avon's prior Augury of *Woe* — chose not to seek the creature out. Jaethal sketched a portrait of herself and Skizgrit. Akasha offered him a cupcake. Then they followed the fairy dragon's scouting north-west to the sound of shouting.

What they found was Akasha's childhood grove — twisted into a corrupted scene of black roots and dead vegetation, with **Equatia** (a forest spirit Akasha had spoken with in her youth) controlling the vines that were strangling Deepbloom and Liriel. Mist looked to Avon for cleric-cleanse options; none prepared. Akasha made the appeal in-character — *"It's me. Guardian of the forest. I have protected it"* — rolling Persuasion 15 with advantage. Equatia's hands stilled. She begged *"help me"*, then snapped back to the dark voice. A Nature 18 (with Avon's Guidance) reminded Akasha that Equatia loved the music of elven minstrels; Jaethal launched into a Sylvan song on the hurdy-gurdy (Performance 23), and the spirit's head bowed. The vines went slack. Akasha yanked a black root from Deepbloom's belly; Avon Cure Wounds-ed the hole shut. Liriel — Akasha's childhood best friend — embraced her.

Back in the cleared toad-clearing, the party debated Skizgrit's fate. **Mist made the kill gesture** wordlessly — pragmatic, the demon was a known threat the moment the charm dropped. **Akasha softened the room** — *"I kind of like him, but if you want, I just don't want to watch"* / *"I think he's so cute. I'm just gonna walk away."* **Jaethal cut the debate short by dropping the Charm**; Skizgrit went feral, started slashing through the rope. **Mist, dry**: *"I kind of miss Silas now."* — a callback to the departed rogue from S5 who'd have handled this swiftly. **Avon javelined Skizgrit through the mouth** on the demon's fourth swing — *"Boop."* The DM clarified after the fact that killing demons just sends them back to Hell; Avon, dryly: *"You could have shared this information before."* They then chased the fleeing Deepbloom through the forest (group athletics check, three rounds — they caught him), tripped him to the dirt, and Avon used **Detect Thoughts** to probe. Mist tried to spend an Unsettling Words die on the WIS save; the DM ruled Deepbloom failed it clean, no debuff needed. Avon dug deeper and found the buried thought: *he doesn't feel right*. The party ran a shortcut to Iliora (Akasha leading, Avon carrying Jaethal on his shoulder) and beat Deepbloom to the city. **Calith** (Akasha's brother) ran the quarantine play; guards apprehended Deepbloom at the gate, isolated him in a study-like room, and accepted his hand-written list of equipment to fetch from his Skybreeze shop. The list, Akasha later discovered, was about half real equipment and half snacks and ales.

The session closed with the party at Akasha's family home — a small tree-grown house — having dinner with her mother **Pelletheria** and sister **Shonda**. Constitution checks on the vegetable stew: Mist genuinely enjoyed it, Jaethal loved it, Avon found it *okay*, Talya struggled through, and Akasha's gag was caught immediately by her mother (Performance 4). Avon — with advantage — pulled off a 16 and faked enjoying the meal convincingly. Jaethal's fairy dragon, set on her lap, gagged so hard the family laughed. Long rest. End session.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Charmed Demon
- The party woke the bound quasit (later self-named **Skizgrit**) in the clearing east of Iliora. **Mist flew over and slapped him awake.** *"Master? I don't have a master. I do what I want."*
- Mist drove the interrogation verbally: *"I guess you're just dumb in the head"*, *"Like a deal, right? We can make a deal"*, *"Can't make trouble if you tie it up here."* Skizgrit refused: *"You're thinking of my cousins, the devils. I don't make deals."*
- **Jaethal cast Charm Person** (10:20). DM ruled quasit's true form ambiguously humanoid; allowed the spell. Skizgrit failed his DC 15 WIS save (+0 WIS). *"You know what? I actually kind of like you. I don't know why."*
- Charmed Skizgrit named the big claw-mark creature: a **Barlgura**. Invisible, no useful elemental weaknesses (cold/fire/lightning/poison all bad ideas). Akasha's History 18 (with Guidance) corroborated.
- Avon, referencing his prior Augury: *"We were pretty much going to fail if we fought it."* Party agreed not to pursue.
- Jaethal asked to draw a portrait of herself and Skizgrit (*"It's a pleasure to meet you, Skizgrit. Can I make a portrait of us?"*). Skizgrit agreed.
- Mist, dry: *"That's just how friends are, you know? You're good friends if you're compelled to, you know, be nice to each other."*

### Act II — Aerial Reconnaissance
- Jaethal sent her fairy dragon up through the canopy. Perception 17.
- Fairy dragon reported: probable hag activity to the **northeast**; **shouting** (a man and a woman) to the **northwest**.
- Party headed northwest toward the shouting.

### Act III — Equatia's Grove
- Akasha recognized the area as her childhood favorite grove — once a peaceful stream-fed clearing, now black ooze on the trees and rot in the vegetation.
- **Deepbloom and Liriel** were trapped in dark roots that were trying to choke them. Pindle was still with the party, quiet and bleeding from earlier.
- A spectral forest spirit floated 20 ft above the clearing — Akasha recognized her as **Equatia**, corrupted. *"What are you doing here? Leave this place. These are my toys."*
- Akasha (Druid instinct) felt a strong pull not to harm her. Mist asked Avon for cleric-cleanse options; Avon had nothing prepared. Jaethal offered: *"Maybe we could just knock her out and fix her after."*
- A vine stabbed Deepbloom's belly. The clock was ticking.
- **Akasha made the in-character appeal** (Avon coaching her on phrasing): *"It's me — Akasha, guardian of the forest. I have protected it. There's something evil going on. Please. Don't be a part of the evil."* Persuasion 15 with advantage + Guidance.
- Equatia faltered. *"It's hard to fight it. Help me."* Then snapped back. *"I will not let your friends go. The forest is mine. I must protect it."*
- Akasha's Nature 18 (with Guidance) recalled that Equatia loved the music of elven minstrels.
- **Jaethal launched a Sylvan song** on the hurdy-gurdy (Performance 23 with advantage — Akasha humming along to guide). The spirit's hands lowered. Her head bowed.
- Akasha pulled the vine out of Deepbloom's belly (Medicine check — the wound widened); **Avon cast Cure Wounds**, blue starlight, the wound closed.
- Liriel ran to Akasha, weeping. *"I don't know where you came from, but thank you."*
- Jaethal kept playing until the party retreated. The spirit's head shot up at the last moment before the treeline obscured her.

### Act IV — The Skizgrit Question
- Back in the toad-clearing, the party debated bringing Skizgrit into Iliora. Decision: no.
- Avon: *"I mean, I can make him my familiar."* DM patiently explained that demons don't qualify. Avon: *"Oh, so then I can't make him my familiar."*
- The math: once Charm Person ended, Skizgrit would know he'd been charmed and turn feral. Jaethal had cast the spell; only she and Mist had Charm Person on their lists.
- **Mist's stance was pragmatic**: he made a quiet kill-gesture — *"I'm just going to make a gesture. Why don't we just, you know..."* The demon was a known threat; the charm was finite; the math was simple.
- **Akasha pushed back softly** — *"He's a devil anyway… I mean, I kind of like him, but if you want, I just don't want to watch."* The against-killing argument that followed (*"I can't kill him, he hasn't done anything to me personally"* / *"Yeah, but that was before — before we used a spell to make him"*) was Akasha's voice. Mist's counter: *"He was fighting you and trying to eat you."*
- Akasha continued: *"I think he's so cute. I don't want to watch it either. I'm just gonna walk away."* Walked away physically; left the rest to handle it.
- **Jaethal cut the debate by dropping the Charm.** *"I can make this easier for us."* Skizgrit: *"What the fuck did you do to me?"* — and started slashing.
- Mist, watching the demon thrash against his bonds: *"I kind of miss Silas now."* The callback to the departed rogue from S5 — who would have ended this without the debate.
- Skizgrit's three bite attempts missed. On his fourth swing, **Avon's javelin went through his mouth**. *"Boop."*
- DM clarified post-execution: killed demons just go back to Hell. Avon, dryly: *"You could have shared this information before. We could have had a little execution."*
- Jaethal: *"But it's more fun. It's more fun to have deep conversations about whether killing demons is ethical."*
- Pindle, still quiet, still bleeding from earlier — **Avon cast Healing Word** on him (2d4+3, full heal). Pindle: *"That was a little painful. I guess being an adventurer, you have to expect a degree of pain, huh? You guys probably put up with a lot more than that every day. But I can take it."*

### Act V — The Deepbloom Chase
- Liriel reminded the party Deepbloom was getting away. They pursued — group athletics rolls across three rounds. Round 1: caught up. Round 2: lost ground. Round 3: closed the gap (Talya's roll dropped to 14 but Akasha's Guidance pushed her over).
- **Talya tripped Deepbloom** (DC unstated; Deepbloom rolled DEX 3, fell on his face). His sample jars and notes scattered.
- Deepbloom refused to talk. *"You would all just hold me back."* Mist tried to convince him to share information; cold rebuff.
- Mist asked aloud if Deepbloom seemed corrupted or stricken. DM called for Insight; **Avon hardcast Detect Magic** in parallel — Deepbloom's bag, samples, and the dirt on his face all glow with carried Blight, all corrupted.
- **Mist's Insight 22** found the rest: the desperation to research a cure is sincere, but there's a buried piece he's *not* telling them. The Eloquence Bard finally landed a roll that mattered.
- Mist suggested Detect Thoughts. Avon: *"I'm just wondering if that counts as hostile."* DM allowed it (not as invasive as Command).
- Avon read surface thoughts — *"I have to find a cure. I must get home. I don't know what that was. It was so big."*
- For the deep probe (sense thoughts, WIS save), **Mist offered Unsettling Words on Deepbloom's save** — *"If you don't tell us, you will meet a very dark end, just like all of us."*
- **DM, immediately**: *"You don't even need to unsettle him. He failed."* The Bardic Inspiration die spent on the rider was rendered moot.
- Avon's deeper probe found the buried thought: Deepbloom *doesn't feel right physically*. He's afraid of something he saw. There's a piece of the experience he's trying to hide even from himself.
- Deepbloom, grabbing his head: *"Stay out of my head!"* He kept walking.

### Act VI — Quarantine
- Liriel described the creature she and Deepbloom saw: a large hole in the ground, a deep cavern beneath, something massive that howled like she'd never heard. *"It scared us both, but I think he was a little more ill-prepared than I for such a sight."*
- Party decided Deepbloom couldn't be allowed to teleport to Skybreeze in his state. **Akasha knew a shortcut** to Iliora (Survival, with Guidance, beat 14).
- Avon carried Jaethal on his shoulder so her short wings wouldn't slow them down. Group athletics across two rounds, succeeded.
- They beat Deepbloom to Iliora and intercepted at the gate. **Akasha tracked down Calith** (her brother, still in his post from earlier) and asked him to call the guards: *"Hey bro, you remember that guy that we went looking for?"*
- **Avon ran the medical pitch**: *"Yeah — Deepbloom — we think he might be contaminated, so we need to keep him quarantined so he doesn't spread it to other cities."* Calith pulled in his superior and two more guards. Quarantine room arranged.
- Akasha to her brother: *"I knew I could count on you, bro."* Calith, hand on Akasha's shoulder: *"Because I got your back, sis."* The first time Akasha's family-of-origin landed on-screen in the campaign.
- Deepbloom arrived a few minutes later, spotted the party, sighed, surrendered. Guards led him to a quarantine room (more library/study than cell).
- Akasha and Jaethal pressed him for the equipment list to ferry from his Skybreeze shop. He wrote across multiple of Jaethal's spare pages. Final note: *"Watch out for those Vale Tempest idiots — I'm sure they'll be around my shop."*
- Akasha later read the list: about half real reagents and instruments; the second half snacks and ales. *"Man's got needs."*

### Act VII — Dinner with the Family
- Evening. Akasha brought the party to her family home — a smaller tree carved into a branch of Idranthusin. Inside: her mother **Pelletheria** and sister **Shonda**, cooking dinner.
- Pelletheria received the party warmly. *"If you're all looking for a cure, then you're all heroes amongst my people."*
- Jaethal poofed her fairy dragon into existence on her lap to share the meal. Pelletheria clapped, delighted.
- Pelletheria checked Avon's intelligence score in a private aside to Akasha — *"That's reasonable. That's a very eclectic bunch."*
- Vegetable stew with potato. CON checks across the table:
  - **Mist** (genuine enjoy)
  - **Jaethal** (absolute love)
  - **Avon** (okay, like a bad traveler's meal)
  - **Talya** (not into it)
  - **Akasha** (rolled 3 — gagging)
  - **Fairy dragon** (rolled 7 — visible gag, the family laughed)
- Performance checks to feign enjoyment: Avon 16 (with advantage), faked it convincingly. Akasha's mom *immediately* caught her. *"Akasha, I guess your tastes have changed."* Akasha pivoted to *"oh I'll have to bring you some of the things that I've come across."*
- Avon, to Akasha re: Shonda: *"If she cooked it, that explains why I didn't enjoy it."*
- Pelletheria, to Akasha privately: *"I worry so much about your brother all the time, being out there on the border. I managed to get him pulled off of patrols because those patrols have gone missing."*
- The party long-rested at the family home. End session.
