Session Summary

Morning broke over the bound, sleeping quasit in the blighted forest east of Iliora. Mist flew over and slapped him awake; the demon — later self-named Skizgrit — refused to deal until Jaethal’s Charm Person turned him into a temporary friend. Skizgrit gave them the threat: a Barlgura (invisible, no useful elemental weaknesses) is making the claw marks. Jaethal sketched a portrait of herself and the charmed demon. Akasha offered him a cupcake. The party agreed not to seek the Barlgura out — Avon’s prior Augury was Woe — and followed the fairy dragon’s scouting toward distant shouting.

They found Akasha’s childhood grove, twisted into corruption. Equatia, a forest spirit Akasha had spoken to in her youth, was controlling dark roots that were strangling Deepbloom and Liriel. Akasha made the appeal in-character — Persuasion 15 with advantage and Guidance. Equatia faltered (“Help me”), snapped back. A Nature 18 recalled the spirit’s love of elven music. Jaethal’s hurdy-gurdy Sylvan song (Performance 23) bowed the spirit’s head; Akasha extracted a root from Deepbloom’s belly; Avon closed the wound with Cure Wounds. Liriel embraced Akasha — her childhood best friend, finally back.

Back at the toad-clearing, the party debated Skizgrit’s fate. Mist made the kill gesture wordlessly; Akasha held the soft moral-dissent line (“I think he’s so cute. I’m just gonna walk away”) and physically stepped back. Jaethal cut the debate by dropping the Charm. Skizgrit went feral; Mist, dryly, “I kind of miss Silas now” — a callback to the departed rogue from S5. Avon javelined the demon through the mouth on his fourth swing. “Boop.” They chased Deepbloom through the forest, tripped him, used Mist’s Insight 22 and Avon’s Detect Magic + Detect Thoughts to find the buried fear (he doesn’t feel right), ran a shortcut to Iliora and beat him to the city. Calith arranged a quarantine. Avon ran the medical pitch; Akasha and her brother shared a quiet brother-sister moment (“I knew I could count on you, bro” / “Because I got your back, sis”). The session closed at Pelletheria’s home, with vegetable stew, a fairy dragon gagging on its first taste of root vegetables, and Akasha’s mother catching her instantly faking enjoyment of the meal.

Timeline

03:00 – 25:00
Act I: The Charmed Demon
Roleplay
  • The party wakes in the clearing east of Iliora with the bound quasit at their feet. Mist flies over and slaps him awake. He blinks at his dead companions: “What do you want with me? Master? I don’t have a master. I do what I want.”
  • Mist drives the verbal pressure — jabs (“I guess you’re just dumb in the head”), deal offers, threats. The demon stonewalls: “You’re thinking of my cousins, the devils. I don’t make deals.”
  • Jaethal casts Charm Person. DM rules quasit’s humanoid form qualifies; DC 15 vs Skizgrit’s +0 WIS — fails on a 13. “You know what? I actually kind of like you. I don’t know why.”
  • Charmed Skizgrit names the big-claw-marks creature: a Barlgura. Invisible. No useful elemental weaknesses (cold, fire, lightning, poison all bad). Akasha’s History 18 (with Guidance) corroborates — massively strong, extremely feared
  • Avon, referencing his prior Augury: “We were pretty much going to fail if we fought it.” Party agrees not to pursue
  • Jaethal asks to draw a portrait of herself and Skizgrit. Mist, dryly: “That’s just how friends are, you know? You’re good friends if you’re compelled to, you know, be nice to each other.”
  • Akasha offers Skizgrit her regenerating Witchlight cupcake; he declines, prefers “the food that’s got the squishy squishy and the spurty spurty”
  • Talya bonds wordlessly — holds up her little portable stein in solidarity
25:00 – 32:00
Act II: Aerial Reconnaissance
Exploration
  • Jaethal sends her fairy dragon up through the canopy. The trees are too dense to see open sky, but the dragon can get above some of the canopy
  • Fairy dragon Perception 17 with advantage. Reports back via the familiar link
  • Northeast: probable hag activity — corrupted area, broken trees, no movement
  • Northwest: shouting — a man and a woman, distant, urgent
  • Party agrees: head northwest toward the shouting. The decision puts them on Deepbloom’s and Liriel’s trail without realizing
32:00 – 65:00
Act III: Equatia’s Grove
Roleplay · Climax
  • Akasha recognizes the area as her childhood favorite grove — once a stream-fed peaceful clearing, now black ooze on the trees, dead vegetation, the forest spirit floating 20ft up
  • Deepbloom and Liriel are entangled in dark roots that are trying to choke them. Pindle is still with the party, bleeding quietly from earlier wounds
  • The spirit (Akasha names her Equatia) is corrupted — dark voice, hands gesturing the roots. “What are you doing here? Leave this place. These are my toys.”
  • Akasha (Druid instinct) feels a strong pull not to harm her. Mist asks Avon for cleric-cleanse options — none prepared. Jaethal: “Maybe we could just knock her out and fix her after.”
  • A vine stabs Deepbloom’s belly. Clock ticking. First Nature 18 (with Guidance) tells Akasha that Equatia can be appealed to as a child-of-the-forest rather than dispelled
  • Akasha makes the appeal in-character: “Akasha, it’s me — guardian of the forest. I have protected it. There’s something evil going on. Please. Don’t be a part of the evil.” Avon coaching her on phrasing. Persuasion 15 with advantage + Guidance
  • Equatia falters. “It’s hard to fight it. Help me.” Then snaps back to the dark voice. “I will not let your friends go. The forest is mine.”
  • Second Nature 18 (with Guidance) recalls that Equatia loved the music of elven minstrels
  • Jaethal plays a Sylvan song on hurdy-gurdy — Performance 23 with advantage (Akasha humming along to guide). The spirit’s hands lower. Her head bows
  • Jaethal holds the melody through a second Performance check (19) and a third improvising-extension during the retreat (auto-succeed). Three rolls deep, the spirit stays still
  • Akasha yanks the vine from Deepbloom’s belly (the wound widens); Avon casts Cure Wounds — blue starlight, wound closes
  • Liriel runs to Akasha, weeping. “I don’t know where you came from, but thank you.” Avon, dry to Akasha: “I would hate to have to break in a new friend.”
  • Party retreats. The spirit’s head shoots up at the last moment before the treeline closes
70:00 – 77:00
Act IV: The Skizgrit Question
Roleplay · Moral
  • Back in the toad-clearing, the party debates bringing Skizgrit into Iliora. Decision: no. Once the Charm ends, he turns feral — he knows he was charmed
  • Avon: “I mean, I can make him my familiar.” DM patiently explains demons don’t qualify. Avon: “Oh, so then I can’t make him my familiar.”
  • Mist’s stance is pragmatic: makes a quiet kill-gesture, “Why don’t we just, you know...” Counters Akasha’s reluctance: “He was fighting you and trying to eat you.”
  • Akasha holds the moral-dissent line: “I kind of like him, but if you want, I just don’t want to watch… I think he’s so cute. I’m just gonna walk away.” Walked away physically; left the rest to handle it
  • Jaethal cuts the debate by dropping the Charm. “I can make this easier for us.” Skizgrit: “What the fuck did you do to me?” — and starts slashing through the rope
  • Mist, watching the demon thrash: “I kind of miss Silas now.” The callback to the departed rogue from S5 — who’d have ended this without the debate
  • Skizgrit’s three bite attempts miss. On his fourth swing (rolled 18), Avon’s javelin goes through his mouth. “Boop.”
  • DM clarifies post-execution: killed demons just go back to Hell. Avon, dryly: “You could have shared this information before.” Jaethal’s thesis line: “It’s more fun to have deep conversations about whether killing demons is ethical.”
  • Avon casts Healing Word on Pindle (2d4+3, full heal). Pindle: “That was a little painful. But I can take it.”
77:00 – 95:00
Act V: The Deepbloom Chase
Skill Challenge
  • Liriel reminds the party Deepbloom is getting away. Group athletics chase, three rounds. R1 close ground, R2 lose ground (Akasha bonks a branch), R3 close the gap — Akasha’s Guidance pushes Talya’s roll over
  • Talya trips Deepbloom. DEX save 3, hard fail. Faceplant; his sample jars and notes scatter
  • Deepbloom refuses to talk. “You would all just hold me back.” Mist tries to convince him to share information — cold rebuff
  • Mist asks aloud if Deepbloom seems corrupted or stricken. Avon hardcasts Detect Magic in parallel — Deepbloom’s bag, samples, and the dirt on his face all glow with carried Blight, all corrupted
  • Mist’s Insight 22 finds the rest: Deepbloom’s desperation to research a cure is sincere, but there’s a buried piece he’s not telling them. The Eloquence Bard’s expertise stack landed exactly where it should
  • Mist suggests Detect Thoughts. Avon casts Detect Thoughts (Knowledge-domain expanded list). Reads surface: “I have to find a cure. It was so big. I have to get home.”
  • For the deep probe (sense thoughts, WIS save), Mist offers Unsettling Words: “If you don’t tell us, you will meet a very dark end, just like all of us.”
  • DM immediately: “You don’t even need to unsettle him. He failed.” The Bardic Inspiration die spent on the rider is rendered moot
  • Avon’s deeper probe finds the buried thought: Deepbloom doesn’t feel right physically. He’s afraid of something he saw. There’s a piece of the experience he’s trying to hide even from himself
  • Deepbloom grabs his head: “Stay out of my head!” He keeps walking
95:00 – 120:00
Act VI: The Quarantine
Roleplay · Family
  • Liriel describes the creature: a large hole in the ground, deep cavern beneath, something massive that howled like she’d never heard. “Deepbloom was a little more ill-prepared than I for such a sight.”
  • Party decides Deepbloom can’t teleport to Skybreeze in his state. Akasha’s Survival (with Guidance) recalls a shortcut. Avon carries Jaethal on his shoulder so her short wings don’t slow them down
  • Group athletics across two rounds, succeed via Guidance. Party beats Deepbloom to Iliora
  • Akasha tracks down Calith at his post. “Hey bro, you remember that guy that we went looking for?”
  • Avon runs the medical pitch: “We think he might be contaminated, so we need to keep him quarantined so he doesn’t spread it to other cities.” Calith pulls in his superior and two more guards
  • Akasha to her brother: “I knew I could count on you, bro.” Calith, hand on Akasha’s shoulder: “Because I got your back, sis.” First time Akasha’s family-of-origin lands on-screen
  • Deepbloom arrives a few minutes later, spots the party, sighs, surrenders. Guards lead him to a quarantine room (more library/study than cell)
  • Akasha and Jaethal press him for the equipment list to ferry from his Skybreeze shop. He writes across multiple of Jaethal’s spare pages. Final note: “Watch out for those Vale Tempest idiots — I’m sure they’ll be around my shop.”
  • Akasha later reads the list: about half real reagents and instruments; second half snacks and ales. “Man’s got needs.”
120:00 – 145:00
Act VII: Dinner at Pelletheria’s
Roleplay · Family
  • Evening in Iliora. Akasha brings the party to her family home — a smaller tree carved into a branch of Idranthusin. Inside: her mother Pelletheria and sister Shonda, cooking dinner
  • Pelletheria receives the party warmly. “If you’re all looking for a cure, then you’re all heroes amongst my people.”
  • Jaethal poofs her fairy dragon into existence on her lap to share the meal. Pelletheria claps, delighted
  • Pelletheria checks Avon’s intelligence score in a private aside to Akasha. “That’s reasonable. That’s a very eclectic bunch.”
  • Vegetable stew with potato. CON checks: Mist genuinely enjoys it; Jaethal absolutely loves it; Avon finds it okay; Talya isn’t into it; Akasha rolls 3 — gagging. Fairy dragon rolls 7, visible gag, the family laughs
  • Performance check to feign enjoyment: Avon 16 (forgot to roll the second advantage die), faked it convincingly. Akasha’s mother catches her instantly. “Akasha, I guess your tastes have changed.”
  • Avon to Akasha re: Shonda: “If she cooked it, that explains why I didn’t enjoy it.” Shonda overhears: “Oh, she’s too good for our cooking now.”
  • Pelletheria, privately, to Akasha: “I worry so much about your brother all the time, being out there on the border. I managed to get him pulled off of patrols because those patrols have gone missing.”
  • Party long-rests at the family home. End session
