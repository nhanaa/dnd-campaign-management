# World — Hellbreakers (Isger)

## Campaign Overview

- **AP**: Hellbreakers (levels 1-10) → Hell's Destiny (levels 11-20)
- **System**: Pathfinder 2e Remastered
- **Setting**: Isger, Inner Sea region, Golarion
- **Variant Rules**: Free Archetype, Gradual Ability Boost, Ancestry Paragon
- **Theme**: Revolution against infernal tyranny. Gothic horror meets high fantasy. 50/50 combat and roleplay (flexible based on party decisions).
- **Availability**: All Common and Uncommon options available. Rare options require DM approval — story-driven or fun reasons are usually accepted.
- **GM**: Kaazz (StartPlaying)
- **Schedule**: Weekly, Saturday 6:00 PM CDT, 3 hours
- **Platform**: Foundry VTT (The Forge) + Discord

## The Conflict

The Inner Sea War has begun. Cheliax, an empire powered by infernal pacts, is tightening its grip on Isger, its vassal state. The Hellbreakers League, a rebel organization, is fighting to free Isger from Chelaxian oppression. Player characters are low-ranking Hellbreakers carrying out missions against Chelaxian forces.

As of the **8th of Lamson, 4726**, Steward **Stuart Hedvind VII** has escalated the crackdown with a decree offering gold rewards to any citizen willing to report suspected Hellbreakers or anti-Chelaxian activity. The result is a rapidly spreading climate of neighbor-against-neighbor denunciation, forcing the Hellbreakers to operate openly only in safehouses and to travel incognito in public. Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias are currently in Eladir attempting to blunt its effects.

## Key Factions

### The Hellbreakers League
- **Type**: Rebel organization, national scope (Isger)
- **Goal**: Free all nations from Chelaxian rule
- **HQ**: Saringallow, Isger
- **Leaders**: Alceo Demetrias (cleric of Milani), Phoebe Demetrias
- **Allies**: Eagle Knights (Andoran)
- **Enemies**: Isgeri government, Cheliax, Hell
- **Values**: Fight oppressors, reject tyranny, protect the innocent
- **Anathema**: Condoning oppression, serving tyrants, harming civilians, seeking power for self-gain

### Cheliax
- Empire ruled by infernal pacts. Her Infernal Majestrix Abrogail Thrune rules through devil-binding nobility and Hellknight enforcers. Treats Isger as a resource to exploit.
- **Stuart Hedvind VII** — Isgeri steward operating under Chelaxian authority. Author of the bounty decree of Lamson 4726.

### The Hellknights
- Lawful military orders that enforce order through fear and brutality. The Order of the Rack was dispatched to Isger to root out suspected Hellbreakers. They occupied Breachill and conducted public executions.

### Andoran / Eagle Knights
- Democratic nation opposed to Cheliax. Eagle Knights of the Steel Falcons were "advised" to help Isger. Their intervention triggered the formal declaration of war.

## Key Locations

- **Isger**: Small, resource-poor vassal state of Cheliax. The battleground.
- **Breachill**: Small town at the foot of the Five Kings Mountains. Where the war's first sparks occurred. Hellknights occupied it, terrorized the population.
- **Cadence Keg** (Breachill): Tavern run by **Brynn Tate**, named for Cayden Cailean ("Caden Kaelin"). Site of the party's first meeting with Ethran Howell in Session 1.
- **Draymoor**: Small, muddy farming village west-southwest of Breachill along the Saringallow road. Tense under the Hedvind decree — several drunk locals wear homemade Chelaxian badges.
- **North Kansira River**: Ferry crossing on the road to Saringallow, run by **Otto** the ferryman. Recent undead activity on the riverbank.
- **Chitterwood**: Large forest in southern Isger, mostly occupied by goblins. Partially razed during the Goblinblood Wars.
- **Citadel Altaerein**: Abandoned Hellknight headquarters, retaken by Andoren forces.
- **Saringallow**: Hellbreakers League headquarters. HQ is in a nondescript mansion on the east side of town, hidden behind a false fireplace. Passphrase to enter: *"friends of the Rose."*
- **Everbloom Imports Warehouse** (Saringallow docks): Hellbreaker cover operation. Isolated from the other warehouses by design. Currently compromised — site of an ambush in progress.
- **Eladir**: City where Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias are currently operating to counter the Hedvind decree.
- **Traveler's Square** (Saringallow): Shopping center with forges and blacksmiths. Level 5 town — common items level 5 or below available. Home of Hagrath the Hammer.
- **North Kansira River Ambush Point**: Rocky outcropping at a river bend, two days' travel upriver from Saringallow. Deep channel on one side forces ships close to the cliff. Hellbreaker-scouted ambush site. Site of the Night's Prize boarding (Session 2).
- **Lake Encarthan**: A lich is rumored to dwell here and command the restless dead flooding Isger (per ferryman Otto's cousin).
- **Waterspout Inn**: Riverside tavern with lodging, halfway between Saringallow and Logos. Two-story building, once painted bright blue, now faded gray. Currently occupied by Chelaxian forces — tents, a two-masted warship, and a Chelaxian banner. Site of Rhokrag's capture and the party's ongoing rescue mission (Session 3).
- **Logos**: Town further along the road from Saringallow. Rhokrag's original destination before capture.
- **Wolf Point**: Hilltop fortress at the southern edge of the Chitterwood. Nightmare source was a **Drude** (Feywild nightmare dog) — killed by Ren in S5. No birds, no mice, no life while the Drude was active; life returned after its death. Steel Circle moved back into the keep after the deal was fulfilled.
- **Wolf Point Bandit Camp**: Abandoned farmstead ~1 mile southeast of the Wolf Point tower. Steel Circle band led by **Runa Bloodhammer**. Deal fulfilled in S5 — Steel Circle moved back to the keep and will target only Chelaxians.
- **Fernsby**: Quaint Chitterwood village — thatched cottages, large oak in the village green. Goblin-blamed desecration was actually caused by Hissrath (goblin-snake hybrids) from a nearby ravine. Party solved the mystery in S4-S5; Fernsby rewarded the party with a +1 weapon potency rune. One Goblin-speaking man lives in town.
- **The Goblin-Snake Ravine** (near Fernsby): Stone-outcropping shaded ravine, 20-ft walls, brackish pool at the back. Hissrath lair. Multi-headed boss hybrid killed in S4. Battle plan found in S5 revealed the Hissrath were framing Stumpfield's goblins for Fernsby attacks.
- **Stumpfield**: Goblin-human art commune in the Chitterwood. Semi-permanent tents and wagons, traveling-festival atmosphere. Cooperative sculpture tradition. Residents include **Eric** (emotional goblin, main contact) and **Jeb** (talented human sculptor). Pro-Hellbreaker — grateful the party solved the Hissrath frame-up. Visited S5.
- **Vordia**: Chelaxian-loyal town southeast of the Chitterwood. Flags of Cheliax and Asmodeus on every building. Has a constable office, county records. Residents generally content under Chelaxian rule. The **Bright Embers Orphanage** is half a day east.
- **Bright Embers Orphanage** (est. 4701 AR): Abandoned Asmodean orphanage east of Vordia. Run by the **Sisters of the Golden Erinyes**. Two-story building with murals of friendly-looking devils, nuns of Asmodeus holding children's hands, red pentagrams on bedroom walls. Haunts cleansed S5 (Attic Whisperer killed). Fully looted S6 — found unholy text (~50 gp value), red sister's robe (Rama claimed), draft letter to "a sister in the Lintel." **Sister Sylvie will return to reopen it** as a children's home post-S6.
- **The Lucent Mission** (S6): Golden Erinyes hidden mission half a day south of Vordia, up a switchbacked trail in the foothills of the **Aspodel Mountains**. Two wooden buildings behind a stone-walled garden, pentagram above the double doors. Interior: temple (pulpit + three pews, pentagram-burned wall), dining hall (devil scrollwork in ceiling), kitchen (steel basin + iron stove), training room (two dummies, two weapon racks), Sister Endreah's archive-study (ebony desk, bookshelves), and bedrooms. Party raided S6: 2 sisters killed, 3 manacled, 1 summoned devil (Velavi) one-shot. Three sisters left unconscious and tied on-site as of S6 end.
- **Aspodel Mountains** (mentioned S6): Foothill mountain range south of Vordia. The Lucent mission sits in its foothills.
- **The Lintel** (unknown — mentioned S6): Referenced in a draft letter recovered from the Bright Embers Orphanage to "another sister in the Lintel." Another mission? Another order? A sister network? Significance unclear.
- **Umok**: Fire-free gnome village on the western edge of the Chitterwood (final hearts-and-minds stop — **won, S7**). Forty years ago the Goblinblood Wars burned the area to the ground; ever since, the gnomes allow **no open flame within 500 yards** — they cook, light, and live entirely by magic, with colored light-orbs on every corner and Isgeri boarhounds the locals can "talk" to. Signs at every approach: *"now entering the Umok fire-free zone, please extinguish all flames."* Captain of the guard **Ion Marbleberg**; watering hole the **Rubber Ducky** (a doorless tavern entered via a magical teleport sigil). The party cleared a Hell Hound infestation that was being blamed on goblins, earning the bracers of missile deflection and clearing the burned goblin **Grizzo** to stay.
- **Thorngrip**: Hidden goblin town deep in the Chitterwood (introduced S7). Concealed behind a curving **wall of living thorns** that only the goblins know how to part, centered on a colossal **sequoia** with thatch-roofed huts built up the trunk — rooftops kept below the thorn line so the town can't be spotted from outside. Ruled by the ancestor-speaker **Bracknap**; scouted by **Zagnub**'s look-see party. Wary of humans (the Goblinblood Wars), but allied with the Hellbreakers after the party agreed to drive off the Iskari Slayers.
- **The Practice-Ritual Stump** (Chitterwood, S7): A petrified stump ringed with ritual candles and crystal shards, churned with boot prints, a crack leading to an underground hollow. The site of an **amateur "first-try" devil-summoning ritual** — Viro Ahala's practice run, not his true working — that spawned a clutch of **Orts**. Held a stashed 250-gp shield and ritual supplies.
- **Stagfall** (caves, deep Chitterwood, S8): A series of caves hidden behind a grove whose trees, at the right angle, look like a **stag's antlers** over black, gnarled brush. Named for **Bracknap's grandmother**, who at the end of the Goblinblood Wars used the power of the woods to transform into a **mighty stag** and led human hunters away from the tribe — she died, but the survivors escaped, found the great tree, and raised the thorn wall of Thorngrip. Was **Viro Ahala's ritual lair** — **fully cleared S9**. Entry chamber guarded by 3 **Insidiators** (killed S8); the inner four-tunnel cavern held a **basilisk** (the S8 cliffhanger "snake," killed S9). Deeper: a glowworm-column chamber (2 Orts + a Hellknight), a waterfall guard-post (2 Hellknight Armigers + an Insidiator), and the ritual chamber where Viro was killed. A rubble-blocked tunnel (≈a day to clear) and a couple of passages remain unexplored.
- **The Three Sacred Sites** (Chitterwood, near Thorngrip, S8): "Sites of subtle power" tied to the forest's essence, each **corrupted by Viro's ritual** (the Nine Hells reaching tendrils through planar cracks), each a complex trap the party cleansed to charge Bracknap's Crystal Pendant:
   - **Little Tree** (~30 min SE of Thorngrip): an ageless sapling fed by magic, never growing or dying. Corruption made it flux big-and-small (Will-save shrink/slow trap) and woke a guardian **grizzly bear**. Cleansed by prying the hellfire grip off its roots; "thanked" the party with a spacious pouch + rare mushrooms.
   - **Moss Pond** (<1 hr W): a rippling patch of moss tied to the **Plane of Wood**. Corruption made it a spear-trap (piercing + poison) and spawned **2 moss-sloths**. Yielded a smuggler's padlock.
   - **The Hungry Hole** (E side): an old **goblin wizard's** botched spacious-pouch that tore open a bottomless extradimensional mouth. Corruption made it **inhale and drag** anyone near the edge (Fort saves). No creatures. Yielded a low-grade talisman + 20 gp.
- **Swiftrun** (town, east of the Chitterwood, S8): Where the two new members (Zanthar & Sammy) were dispatched and learned of Viro's Hell-Knight-gathering ritual. Their lead matched the party's.

## Relevant Faiths

- **Milani** — Goddess of revolution, hope, devotion. Most popular among Hellbreakers.
- **Iomedae** — Goddess of honor, justice, valor
- **Cayden Cailean** — God of freedom, bravery, ale
- **Arazni** — Goddess of the abused, undead, and the wronged
- **Asmodeus** — God of tyranny, contracts, Hell. Worshipped by Chelaxian upper class. The enemy's patron.
- **Ina** — Sun goddess. Has caught **Moob's** eye — she inspired his star-grenade formula mid-craft. Her priests are said to emit "solar radiation." Moob wants nothing to do with her clergy but won't douse fires with water either.

## Session Log

### Session 1 — Friends of the Rose (11 Apr 2026 · 7 Lamson 4726 AR)
The party met at the Cadence Keg in Breachill the morning after the rout of the Hellknights of the Rack. After a tavern storytelling contest (won by Fang on a Wing-powered wheelie run), they were briefed by wounded Eagle Knight Ethran Howell. Overnight, Stuart Hedvind VII posted a bounty decree on all suspected Hellbreakers, forcing the party incognito. They traveled west-southwest, defused a confrontation with Chelaxian sympathizers in Draymoor, then killed two undead "dread bats" at the North Kansira ferry crossing before crossing to Saringallow. At the Hellbreaker HQ (hidden behind a fireplace in a nondescript mansion — passphrase *"friends of the Rose"*) they met Rhokrag and Zara, received Ethran's +1 Eagle Knight greatsword as a gift, and were sent to check on Gertraud Middenhelm at the Everbloom Imports warehouse. The session ended on a cliffhanger: Bai Jian tripped a hairline tripcord, took 14 damage, and the party rounded the corner to find four figures waiting — a burly woman in the back greeting them with *"like rats in a trap."*

### Session 2 — Night's Prize (18 Apr 2026 · 8 Lamson 4726 AR)
The party resolved the Everbloom warehouse cliffhanger in two rounds — Bai Jian crit-killed two loyalists, the third surrendered. After interrogating and releasing the survivor with a warning, they rescued Gertraud Middenhelm and escorted her back to HQ. Rhokrag briefed them on a supply-raid mission: intercept a Chelaxian cargo ship on the North Kansira River, minimize killing. The party hatched a fake-prisoner ruse — Moob and Cyrathul (disguised via masquerade scarf) presented "captured Hellbreaker" Bai Jian to board the Night's Prize. The deception worked until Wing knocked a boulder off the cliff. An Extreme-difficulty ship combat followed: Captain Julenus Aedin and his animated armor bodyguard (all-physical-damage resistance) proved devastating. Fang went down twice; Moob was downed once. Cyrathul killed the captain via Exploit Vulnerability + bite combo after Moob's tenderizer grenade; Bai Jian grappled the animated armor and shoved it overboard, triggering a failsafe explosion that set the ship ablaze. The party fought fires with water bombs, rescued trapped crew, secured supplies, and barely escaped the sinking ship — both goblins nearly drowned, with Moob sacrificing HP to save Fang. 286 XP earned (88 warehouse + 128 Extreme encounter + 70 bonus for crew rescue and supplies).

### Session 3 — The Waterspout Inn (25 Apr 2026 · ~10 Lamson 4726 AR)
Picking up on shore after the Night's Prize sinking, the party dealt with the grateful crew (Moob tipped sailors to spread Hellbreaker goodwill), fought two river snakes from the disturbed wreck (Moob crit-killed one, Cyrathul Mirror-killed the other), salvaged Wing, and distributed loot. After a two-day return to Saringallow, Ethran and Marie reported Rhokrag missing — he'd gone to investigate Hellknight regrouping at the Waterspout Inn and never returned. The party traveled two more days to find the inn occupied by Chelaxian forces. After nightfall, diplomacy failed and combat erupted: three camp soldiers dispatched in Round 1, then Marines emerged from the docked warship. A flying devil webbed Bai Jian and Cyrathul; a Chelaxian navy captain appeared. Fang set the ship's sails on fire via Explode innovation. Bai Jian escaped webbing, leaped from the rigging, and killed the devil with a hero-pointed critical Snagging Strike — the session's defining moment. Session ended on cliffhanger: ship captain and crow's nest Marine still standing, sails burning, Rhokrag visible but still bound.

### Session 4 — Plans Ruined (9 May 2026 · ~13 Lamson 4726 AR)
The Waterspout cliffhanger resolved in one more round — Wing's mast-explosion finished the crow's-nest Marine; Bai Jian crit-Snagging-Struck the captain open and Panacea hero-pointed Needle Darts into her throat. Rhokrag rescued and freed. Two days of road travel followed; on the second night, ~5 Chelaxian survivors ambushed the camp. Combat closed cleanly, but Panacea's post-combat Battle Medicine crit-failed and killed Moob — Bai Jian saved him with Moob's own Quick-Alchemy Elixir of Life from his hand and a regular elixir from his pouch. Rhokrag named **Hellknight Alaya** as the captain's Hellbreaker-bounty employer. Level 2 reached. At Saringallow HQ: Cyrathul committed to Draconic Ravager, Panacea took Medic Dedication + Assurance, Bai Jian +CON and gained the Beastmaster boarhound **Ren** (anonymous donor). Hellbreakers' first reputation point banked from the Night's Prize sailors. New mandate from Eladir: ease back on raids, win hearts and minds in the Chitterwood — Wolf Point, Verodia, Fernsby, Stumpfield, Umach. The two goblin players left the table; Moob and Fang stayed at HQ. The Wolf Point territory turned out a Steel Circle bandit camp (offered a tower-clearing trial deal), a Chelaxian-sympathizer farmstead, friendly halfling/orchard farmsteads, and Fernsby — where Cyrathul's Nature check identified snake tracks (not goblins) as the source of the village green's desecration. Two-stage goblin-snake combat at the ravine: a single-round skirmish, then a boss fight with a multi-headed hybrid (yammering Will-save aura). DM rolled four nat 20s in a row — Bai Jian and Ren both crit-downed and revived (Battle Medicine on Bai Jian, Treat Wounds on Ren). Cyrathul mopped the boss with Exploit Vulnerability. Cliffhanger: more goblin voices echoing further into the ravine.

### Session 6 — The Lucent Mission (30 May 2026 · ~19 Lamson 4726 AR)
Session opened in the cleansed orphanage. **Rama** (older blue-skinned Jaunmari-coded monk, white hair, reds-and-golds robes; player: crackrockcowboy) introduced as a Hellbreakers contact dispatched by Rhokrag — trapped behind a stuck door during the haunt cleansing. Bai Jian vouched once Rhokrag's name landed. Orphanage looted (unholy text, red robe, draft letter to "the Lintel"). On the road back to Vordia, the follower from S5 stepped out — **Sister Sylvie** of the Golden Erinyes, missing her ring finger, here to expose **Viro Ahala** as an imposter Hell Knight running a devil-summoning ritual through her sisters. Sylvie drew a map to the Lucent mission half a day south of Vordia; asked the party not to kill her sisters. Party rode out at nightfall. At the mission gate, two gardener sisters attacked on sight — Panacea crit-killed one (Needle Darts through the carotid artery); Bai Jian Sudden-Charged the other for 20 non-lethal damage (captured). Inside: a kitchen sister with poison fists (sicken-on-hit, missing-ring-finger venom) plus two mindless **Orts** (devil-creatures); Rama got hit with the poison and went unconscious from persistent ticks post-combat; Panacea's second Battle Medicine saved him. Rama's Electric Arc confirmed (training-room cast chained both sisters). Two more sisters in a training room — Bai Jian crit-Struck one (eviscerated, lethal); Rama swift-kicked the other unconscious. The last captive's eyes flashed to the southern door. The final room was **Sister Endreah**'s archive-study; she snapped her fingers and summoned **Velavi**, a librarian-shaped devil with bird legs and pointed horns. Bai Jian asked the DM to confirm it was a fiend (Ren's foe-scent rider), Potency-Crystal-activated and Sudden-Charged for **a 37-damage crit one-shot kill on Velavi** before it acted. Cyrathul crit-Demoralized Endreah for Frightened 2 (no bite that round). Bai Jian Reactive-Struck Endreah at the door when she tried to flee. Party then committed to non-lethal — Cyrathul tagged Rama in as "the non-lethal expert" after his own bites whiffed; Rama's swift kick to the dome put Endreah out. **She surrendered.** Interrogation (Cyrathul Make Impression in Exploration with Bai Jian aiding, Cyrathul hero-pointed the reroll): **Viro is in the Chitterwood preparing the ritual; it targets Isger's goblin populations starting in the east; takes weeks; he left several days ago.** Party looted devil-skin lore book (+1 RK on devils), Crimson Thorn (+1 asp coil), bow staff, bag of gold. Left three sisters manacled at the mission, descended the mountain, camped. Next steps: Vordia for resupply, then Umok + Chitterwood for Viro. **Hellknight Alaya confirmed NOT the same person as Viro Ahala.** Bai Jian's Potency Crystal consumed.

### Session 7 — Fire-Free Zone (6 Jun 2026 · ~22 Lamson 4726 AR)
Cleanup-and-pursuit session. Back in **Vordia**, the party reported the orphanage cleansed (20 gp), sold the Lucent loot (Robe of the Erinyes, unholy text, Asmodeus statue, trinkets), and saw reputation hit **17** — Chelaxian flags coming down (20 → 10), open street whispers about the League. Travel to **Umok**, the last hearts-and-minds stop: a **fire-free gnome village** (no open flame within 500 yards since the Goblinblood Wars burned the area) that talks to its boarhounds and blames goblins for a string of woodland fires. A badly-burned goblin, **Grizzo**, collapsed in the square (*"Fire dogs chased me"*); the party kept her alive and talked captain **Ion Marbleberg** into sheltering her. They tracked the fires to a raging forest blaze (victory-point skill challenge, **60 XP**) and then **two Hell Hounds** in a circle of charred stumps. Combat went badly: Bai Jian Sudden-Charge-crit-killed the first (cut in half), but the second's breath crit-failed **Rama** (DM-controlled after crackrockcowboy's departure) and **also caught Cyrathul**; with heals spent keeping the badly-burnt Cyrathul up, **Rama died of his burns and was left in the forest.** Cyrathul Exploited the hound's cold weakness and decapitated it. Umok rewarded the party with **Bracers of Missile Deflection** and a tip: a figure in black armor had stalked out the western gate. Tracking the boot prints into the **Chitterwood**, the party found a **petrified stump** ringed with ritual gear — a **practice ritual** (not Viro's true working) that spawned **four Orts** (one Elite; weak holy/silver, immune fire/mental). Bai Jian opened with holy water and called the silver weakness; Panacea's silver Needle Darts and Cyrathul's Exploit-crits cleared them. **Party reached level 3**; loot: a 250-gp shield + ritual supplies. Then ~20 red goblin eyes: **Zagnub** of the **Thorngrip Goblins** brought them to a hidden town behind a thorn wall around a giant sequoia, ruled by the ancestor-speaker **Bracknap** (spectral goblins at his back). Bracknap's ancestors will lead them to the man in black **after** the party drives off the **Iskari Slayers** — human goblin-hunters out to finish the Goblinblood Wars. Reward: an **oil of potency**. At the Slayer camp, the first three were routed by Cyrathul's Intimidation (50 XP, camp looted); two more returned with crossbows and a war-wolf, and combat followed — Bai Jian Fleet-crit-killed one, Cyrathul crit-bit the wolf, and the last Slayer surrendered. Bai Jian swung anyway over the *"slaving"* talk and **missed** (reputation preserved), then manacled the survivor and handed him to the goblins. Session ends with Zagnub leading them back to Thorngrip. **Two new players join next session.**

### Session 8 — The Ancestor's Crystal (13 Jun 2026 · ~25 Lamson 4726 AR)
Two new members joined on the goblin trails: **Zanthar Melodune III** (Gnome generalist Wizard / Blessed One — *"a rebel without a clue"*) and **Sammy Shadowfoot** (Halfling Polymath Bard — lute and frying pan; her player battled Wi-Fi drops all night and was often DM-run). Both had been sent east to **Swiftrun** and brought intel confirming Viro Ahala is gathering Hell Knights for a ritual in the Chitterwood. Back at **Thorngrip**, **Bracknap** named the whole party **friends of Thorngrip** (gate opened for good) and gave them a quest: carry a cloudy **Crystal Pendant** to three corrupted **sites of subtle power** so the ancestors can pierce the veil hiding Viro. Before setting out, **Bai Jian traded his longtime jian to Gug the goblin smith for a falcata** (*"Please take care of that sword. It meant a lot to me."*), transferring his +1 potency rune. The three sites, each a complex trap corrupted by the Nine Hells: the **Little Tree** (flux-shrink trap + a guardian **grizzly bear** — Bai Jian tanked and killed it with two crits, then stamped out the ember corrupting the roots); **Moss Pond** (Plane-of-Wood spear-trap + **two moss-sloths** weak to fire — Cyrathul read and exploited them, Sammy danced the trap quiet with an occultism check + lute, Zanthar crit with Hand of the Apprentice; a moss spear crit Bai Jian for 18); and the **Hungry Hole** (an old goblin wizard's botched spacious-pouch turned inhaling mouth — Cyrathul and Zanthar peeled off the hellfire grip with occultism). At each site the crystal drank a tendril of magic and glowed greener. Back at Thorngrip, Bracknap read the charged crystal — eyes flashing red — *"The caves! The Hell-Human Vero is in the caves!"* The caves are **Stagfall**, named for his grandmother's stag-sacrifice, now defiled by Viro. The elders gifted a **rune of striking** (Gug applied it to the falcata). The party tracked to Stagfall, fought **three Insidiators** in the dark (Sammy cast Light; Cyrathul was webbed; Panacea's silver Needle Darts and Cyrathul's chomp/claw each killed one; **Bai Jian's falcata crit decapitated the last for 60**), cleared a rubble wall, and stopped at a four-tunnel cavern holding a shattered armored statue and a **slithering snake-like creature** — the cliffhanger, on the threshold of Viro's lair. Loot: spacious pouch, rare mushrooms, bear pelt, smuggler's padlock, a low-grade talisman + 20 gp, the rune of striking, and the still-charged Crystal Pendant. Party at level 3.

### Session 9 — The False Hell Knight (20 Jun 2026 · ~26 Lamson 4726 AR)
The party cleared all of **Stagfall** and ended Viro Ahala. The S8 cliffhanger "snake" was a **basilisk**: its petrifying gaze nearly turned **Bai Jian** permanently to stone (saved by a hero point) before **Cyrathul** killed it on a double natural 20. The DM kept initiative running and **three Hellknight Armigers** ambushed from the dark — the party fought half-blind until **Sammy** cast Light, then cut them down (Bai Jian a 48-damage Sudden Charge crit-decapitation; Cyrathul slinking up a wall to slit the last one's throat). That combined brawl was **Extreme, 128 XP**. A methodical crawl followed: a **glowworm-column chamber** with 2 **Orts** + a Hellknight (Bai Jian crit-beheaded an Ort; **Zanthar** phase-bolted the Hellknight through her shield) — **Low, 48 XP**; then the **waterfall guard-post**, the hard fight — 2 Hellknight Armigers behind log barricades + a chameleon **Insidiator** webbing the party, where **Cyrathul dropped to dying** (first since Rama; stabilized on a recovery check, revived by Panacea's Heal + Life Boost), **Sammy** crit-killed the Insidiator, Zanthar's Blazing-Bolt combo cleared an Armiger, and Bai Jian swept the last Novice's legs — **Moderate, 72 XP**. The ritual chamber held **Viro Ahala** in full Hell Knight plate over an ivory flame-bowl: *"You're too late. I've already completed the ritual."* He crit Bai Jian round one (saved by Dueling Parry) while the **Ritual Backlash** hazard healed him and pulsed Will-save hellfire (the frontline shrugged it off). The party stacked debuffs — Sammy's hero-pointed **Biting Words**, Bai Jian's trip, Cyrathul's Demoralize — and **Zanthar's cold cone froze Viro to death** before the **greater-gate ritual** (a greater devil for the Chitterwood) could resolve. **Severe, 88 XP.** They never learned **why** Viro was a "fake" Hell Knight (killed first) and bagged his body + Hell Knight plate to identify at HQ. **+2 reputation → 20 (max tier)**; back at **Thorngrip** they got a hero's welcome, goblin mead, and Bracknap reforged the spent crystal into a **Pendant of the Occult** (*"This is for you now."*) — plus **+40 mission + 40 reputation-milestone XP**. Total ~416 XP; still level 3, closing on 4.

### Session 5 — Bright Embers (23 May 2026 · ~18 Lamson 4726 AR)
Ravine aftermath: party found a Hissrath battle plan in Goblin revealing the hybrids were framing Stumpfield's goblins for Fernsby's attacks. Fernsby rewarded the party with a +1 weapon potency rune. Wolf Point: no life on the hilltop. Bai Jian and Cyrathul had nightmares (Bai Jian: brother kidnapped repeatedly; Cyrathul: hoard stolen). Panacea heard unnatural sounds and a voice — *"You're all gonna die here."* Two Mitflits on a rooftop and a **Drude** (Feywild nightmare dog) were the source. Bai Jian long-jumped onto the roof; Ren killed the Drude (throat rip while flanking — Ren's first kill, redemption after S4). Steel Circle deal fulfilled: **Runa Bloodhammer** moved the band back into the keep, will target only Chelaxians. *"Ride wild, my friends."* Hearts-and-minds tour through the Chitterwood: halfling brewery, welcoming farmsteads, 10 reputation points reached (Hellbreakers now **favorable**). **Stumpfield**: goblin-human art commune. Eric the goblin read the Hissrath plan, cried, hugged Bai Jian. Party contributed to a cooperative sculpture. Eric pointed to **Vordia** for Hellknight intel. Someone was following the party — Bai Jian spotted a figure ~2 miles back. Shook them via Survival + Ren decoy. **Vordia**: Chelaxian-loyal town. Three sources pointed to the **Bright Embers Orphanage** (abandoned Asmodean orphanage, Sisters of the Golden Erinyes). Downstairs: playroom haunt (Cyrathul exorcised via Esoteric Lore — *"Playtime is over"*) and dining room haunt (ghost children throwing silverware; Panacea hero-pointed Occultism to banish). Upstairs: **Attic Whisperer** (undead) stole Bai Jian's voice, enfeebled him, bit for heavy damage. Simultaneous darkness haunt (*"Lights out!"*). Bai Jian used Harpy's Talon (consumed — bleed wasted on undead) and Ren grappled. Cyrathul exorcised the darkness and killed the Attic Whisperer (skull crushed in jaws). All haunts collapsed. Session ended in the orphanage, not fully explored.
