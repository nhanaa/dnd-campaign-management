## Session Summary

Last week ended with the party one floor below their people and agreeing out loud that they were the bad guys. This week they spent three hours trying, mostly, not to be — and the dice, the prison, and a dragon on horseback kept deciding otherwise.

The third floor of the Gyve opened on two faces the party had every reason to remember. **Rencelos Azri**, the legionnaire darts champion they framed for buying stolen talismans in Session 16, was in the first cell, armed, and knew exactly who had put him there — *"You! You're the reason I'm in here! You tricked us!"* His nephew **Moccoviz** — the noble brat who had thrown silver at them after the Daeodon — came out of the next cell feinting. What followed was the hardest *Moderate* encounter the table has had in weeks, fought by a party actively trying not to kill two people it had already wronged: Panacea opened by telling the kid *"we want to let you out of here"* and casting Fear on his uncle — who crit-saved it and laughed in her face: *"you die here"* — Bai Jian put Moccoviz to bed with the flat of a Sure-Strike falcata (*"Well kid, let's go to bed"*), Rencelos put a dagger into Panacea and a longsword into Cyrathul and Bai Jian, and Cyrathul — after the party had just agreed to keep him alive — rolled 19 of a possible 20 on the dice and cut the man's jugular. He stayed on his feet anyway (*Battle Scarred*, the GM explained: *they don't call it Last Stand anymore*), crit Cyrathul for **38** (*"I told you I wasn't done"*), and went down non-lethal under Bai Jian's blade before the round was out. Both Azris are alive. Bai Jian: *"Just for the record, I didn't kill them."* The GM: *"He might not know that."*

Then the cells. **Roxy. Oramos** (*"I was debating my choice whether or not I should have joined you, but you saved us"*). **Hexroot**, the party's own former leshy, grinning — *"I knew you guys wouldn't leave us behind."* **Father Kindness** (*"This is impossible"*), the Thieves' Guild kid with his curt nod, the costume-maker, a red-eyed prisoner with the shakes whom the GM called *"the alligator"* — almost certainly **Zarok**, the Aleigator — and **Otton Nettlebrush** — three days a prisoner, bamboo shoots still splintered under every fingernail, sobbing that he had told them everything. On the warden's desk, the notes confirmed it: *the authorities learned about the Hellbreakers hideout after they captured Otton and tortured him over several days.* The S17 question — how did the army know about the safehouse meeting — is answered, and it isn't Jenverick and it isn't Sarevial. It's a kind manservant who broke.

The warden herself, **Irkazer**, six foot five with the biggest flail anyone had seen, charged Zanthar the moment the party came up the stairs and took him from 62 to **10** in one round (*"I'm still alive!"*). The fight that followed was the party at its most coordinated: Bai Jian hero-pointed a Slam Down into a **36 crit** and tripped her, Cyrathul hero-pointed a nat-1 Twisting Tail into a **nat-20** that kept her on the floor (*"make up for my entire turn of nothing happening"*), Panacea kept Zanthar breathing with Battle Medicine and a Trade Death for Life, Bai Jian cast Guidance on himself and crit her again, and Cyrathul opened her throat with what he called *"the tried and true jugular slash."* Her last words were *"Who are you?"* Panacea: *"You'll never know."*

Then the escape, run as a Pathfinder chase — five obstacles in fifteen-minute rounds — and the party was very, very bad at it. They bluffed the front gate with the day's passphrase (*"pineapple juice"*, off the warden's desk) and were asked where their uniforms were; Bai Jian walked up to ask what was going on and was told, by a jailer, that there was a prison break (*"It's a prison break?"* — *"my job is just not good. He is not good at this, guys"*); three Deception rolls failed and Cyrathul simply conjured a staircase over the wall. A whiteout blizzard hit. Panacea's memory of the city and Cyrathul's asking around found the stables, a familiar voice met them there with the horses intact, Bai Jian tore the tack shed open and calmed a stable of spooked warhorses (*"I have experience taming an animal right here. Surely these horses can't be harder than this guy"*), and the party rode out with every rescued Hellbreaker behind them. A gawking crowd cost them a round; a hundred imps overhead cost them two; the watch mages twisted the road back toward the prison for three more. In between, two street fights: the party killed three of the first patrol (Cyrathul bit two heads clean off from horseback — *"I don't mean to. I just keep killing people"*) and then, chastened, took the entire second patrol alive, including a pistol-drawing *unsanctioned sheriff* who opened with a **nat-20 for 39** into Bai Jian's shoulder. Panacea's second Arcana unravelled the mages' labyrinth *"like a bow on a Christmas present"*, the **Druma Road Gate** rose fifty feet in front of them — and a Hellknight cavalry brigade, a line of infantry and a standard bearer were waiting under it. *"Kill the Hellbreakers!"* Pin. **280 XP.**

## Key Events

### Act I — The Third Floor *(the Azri cell — Encounter 1)*
- The GM opened with the key ring: the surrendered guard's keys open *almost every door in the place*. A small goblin voice from the doorway — *"You guys okay? I hear lots of commotion"* — and the sound of the rest of the prison emptying itself. **Leoz** volunteers to stay in the basement and guard Sarevial and Osney; that is his exit.
- Panacea Treat-Wounded Ren, Cyrathul and herself before going up (28 / 26 / 30). Zanthar, catching up on the loot: *"I have three spell books."*
- Third floor: solid wooden cell doors with head-height panels, moans and sad laughter, *"the stench of misery."* The first door Bai Jian opened held **Rencelos Azri** — armed — who recognised them on sight. Bai Jian's honest first reaction: *"Remind me again who he was?"* → *"Oh, he's the one we blamed for everything."* → to Rencelos: *"Why would you blame us? We don't do anything wrong."* (Someone, off-mic: *"We're just innocently wandering around the third floor of a prison."*)
- **Moccoviz Azri** — Rencelos's nephew, the S11 street brat who had thrown silver at them — stepped out of the next cell, furious: *"You're the reason we're in this cell!"* The GM confirmed to Panacea that the party had framed **both** of them.
- Panacea's opening move was a plea, not a spell: *"Listen kid, we want to let you out of here. We don't want to have to hurt you."* Then Fear on Rencelos, framed in her own words as a way *"to make it clear that I was trying not to kill him."* Rencelos laughed in her face: *"Did you really think that was going to work? You die here."*
- Bai Jian asked the table the question directly — *"Are we killing this young kid or not?"* — and Panacea answered for everyone: *"Probably shouldn't. I think that we've killed enough."* Cyrathul: *"I don't think they'd care if we killed him because he's a prisoner, but yeah."*
- Mid-fight, with Rencelos bloodied and cornered, Bai Jian offered terms — *"You're cornered. Surrender."* Rencelos: *"You ruined my reputation. You kill my nephew and you think we can negotiate now?"* Bai Jian, for the record: *"I didn't kill them."*
- The other argument of the night, in two lines. Bai Jian: *"He's like an innocent."* Cyrathul: *"Innocent is a strong word for this man… We might have framed him, but he's still, at the very least, essentially a Chelaxian noble. Almost guaranteed deals in slavery."* Panacea's verdict: *"We're the good guys. War is hell, though."* Bai Jian: *"I think that ship has already sailed last session, but I'm trying to keep it alive right now. Keep it not sinking."*
- Panacea drew her **+1 striking dancer's spear** in anger for the first time — *"This is my first time with my weapon… Okay, I'm excited now"* — and missed twice (*"I'm just not so good with the spear yet"*). Bai Jian used the downtime between her turns to teach her the **Aid** action.
- Rencelos survived a cut jugular on **Battle Scarred** and dropped non-lethally to Bai Jian before the round ended: *"You'll regret this, Hellbreakers."* Both Azris alive, unconscious, left in their cell. *"It was a moderate encounter,"* the GM said. Bai Jian: *"That was harder than it should have been… We gave them a chance."* And: *"Bai Jian feels conflicted because his mercy may have almost downed Cy also."* See Combat Encounters.

### Act II — Opening the Cells
- Loot off the Azris: **high-fashion fine clothing** (Cyrathul: *"I'm gonna steal his clothing"*), a **rapier**, **loaded dice**, a **+1 longsword**, **half plate**, and Rencelos's **signet ring**.
- Twenty minutes of healing and refocusing: Panacea's Heal and Treat Wounds, Zanthar's Lay on Hands ×2 for 18 apiece (*"I feel useful."* — Panacea: *"You are useful. You're always useful."*), Life Boost sustained for another 12. Bai Jian wanted a second ten minutes; Zanthar: *"You might as well take it because I'm going to have to refocus anyway."*
- Bai Jian: *"We should free them all."* Every door on the floor, in order: **Roxy** (*"You guys came for us?"*); **Oramos** (*"I was debating my choice… but you saved us"*); **Hexroot**, *"super happy"* — *"I knew you guys wouldn't leave us behind"* (Bai Jian: *"We wouldn't leave comrades behind"*); *"the alligator"* — red-eyed, with the DTs, *"hasn't had a drink in a while"* — which can only be the Aleigator, **Zarok** (the GM never said the name); the **Thieves' Guild kid** (a curt nod, nothing said); **Father Kindness** (*"You guys are breaking us out of the Gyve? This is impossible"*); **Otton Nettlebrush**; the **costume-maker**, whose cell someone described as *"high fashion."* Beans, Ievis and Padisco were on the GM's roster too.
- **Otton** was the worst of it. *"No! I don't even deserve this! It's my fault!… They captured me three days ago… They tortured me. I told them everything. I'm so sorry."* Bamboo shoots under almost every fingernail on both hands, chewed at the ends where he had tried to work them out with his teeth. Bai Jian: *"You didn't have a choice."*

### Act III — The Warden *(Encounter 2)*
- Upstairs, past the cell block: *"Intruders. I thought I heard something outside."* **Warden Irkazer** stood up from behind her desk — six foot five — hefted a flail *"the biggest-looking flail you've ever seen"* and charged straight at Zanthar: *"Just stop! In the name of the law!"*
- Her Demoralize was a natural 1 (*"Zanthar is unshakeable"*); her flail was not — **34**, a crit, before he had acted. Zanthar: *"It's not unkillable. I'm going to cast this right in your face"* — Dizzying Colors, which she crit-saved and *"spit a rainbow at you."* He ran, and her reactive strike took **18** more. *"I'm still alive!"*
- The turn that decided it: Bai Jian's Slam Down (hero point: nat 2 → 9, +10) for a **36 crit** and a Trip; then Cyrathul's Twisting Tail (hero point: nat 1 → nat 20) for **32** as she tried to stand — *"So she's still prone."* She never got upright with a full set of actions again.
- *"Who are you? What are you doing in my prison?"* Two rounds, dead on Cyrathul's claw. Panacea, to her last words: *"You'll never know."* See Combat Encounters.
- Loot off the Warden: **+1 striking flail, +1 composite longbow + 20 arrows, +1 gauntlet, half plate**, and a chest in the back corner Cyrathul found holding **five silver ingots** (Bai Jian: *"Anyone want a silver weapon?"*).
- **The desk.** Two things on it. First, the answer to Session 17's open question: *the authorities learned about the Hellbreakers hideout after they captured Otton and had tortured him over the period of several days.* Second, a note that **all the Hellbreakers were on the third floor except two in the basement**, and the guards' **daily passphrase: "pineapple juice."** (Somebody: *"Would have been helpful knowledge at the beginning of this."*)

### Act IV — The Front Gate *(chase obstacle 1)*
- Back down through the dark to the front gate, the cannon on the far tower firing every six seconds and *hundreds* of prisoners crushing toward the hole in the wall. The GM announced the **chase subsystem**: an obstacle per stage, fifteen-minute rounds, a set number of successes per obstacle, and the option to spend a round healing or refocusing instead. Nobody at the table had done one (*"First edition, that's about it"*). Break.
- After the break, **everyone got a second hero point** and Panacea had the first good idea of the chase: *"We have the keys. We could just go out the front entrance."* She unlocked the gate and Zanthar opened the door — straight into two jailers and a watch officer who turned around expecting reinforcements.
- Cyrathul's Diverse Lore recalled the passphrase. He got within shouting distance and **yelled "pineapple juice."** Deception 23 — suspicious, but *"that is the passphrase."* The next question: *"Where's your uniforms?"* (The S18 disguise kit had long since worn off; the GM let it stand.) Cyrathul: *"We had to make an emergency change. We got torn up in the chaos."*
- Bai Jian's turn: *"I'll let the dog come first."* He walked up and asked what was going on — and a jailer told him: *"Turn around! Those prisoners are escaping! There's a prison break!"* Bai Jian: *"It's a prison break?"* Then, to the table, in the third person: *"Okay, my job is just not good. He is not good at this, guys."* Cyrathul (*"You're needed at the breach"*), Panacea (*"We're relieving you of your post"*) and Bai Jian (*"We're tired. We couldn't handle them"*) all rolled Deception; all three failed, and nobody spent the new hero point (*"I'm not going to bother"*). The jailer: *"If you continue coming through here, we'll be forced to kill you."*
- Bai Jian: *"Are we forcing it, guys?… I'll take the guards."* Panacea: *"It's either these three guards or the whole crowd. How tall is the wall?"* Cyrathul remembered his **three scrolls of Helpful Steps** (a 40-foot conjured staircase). Bai Jian **closed the gate on the guards** as his second action; Cyrathul dropped a staircase up the 30-foot wall and another down the far side, and the whole party — plus every freed prisoner — went over it while a **whiteout blizzard** started.

### Act V — The Stables *(chase obstacle 2)*
- *"It is snowing so hard… hard to tell left from right."* **Panacea's Society (28)** remembered the city layout; **Zanthar's failed (17)** and he had to be *"lifted up out of the snow bank"* by the others; **Cyrathul asked the crowd** (Diplomacy 29 vs DC 27) and got them there — the GM credited *"the two of you putting your heads together."*
- The military stables were nearly unoccupied, and from around the corner *"a familiar voice"*: *"Oh, I'm so glad you guys made it. There's horses here, intact. Is anybody good with horses?"* The GM never said the name; by the plan, it is **Jenverick**. Bombing from the prison towers continued in the background — *"they're bombing the people still that are trying to escape out the other side."*
- **Bai Jian forced the tack shed** (Athletics 33 vs DC 28, on a reroll). Panacea tacked half the horses with a hero-pointed **Crafting (31)**. Zanthar's **Skeleton Key** cast Knock on the second locked cupboard — *"Seemed worth it, for sure. I need to get better at that skill if I'm the thief in the party."* Cyrathul asked the rescued Hellbreakers for anyone who could handle horses (Diplomacy 24): *"Oh no, no, I'll be lucky if I can get on one."*
- **Bai Jian calmed the warhorses** (Nature 27): *"I have experience taming an animal right here"* — pointing at Ren — *"Surely these horses can't be harder than this guy."* GM: *"Trained warhorses are nothing compared to an unruly Isgeri hound."* Everyone mounted; the Hellbreakers followed as a single token.

### Act VI — The Gawking Crowd *(chase obstacle 3)* and the First Patrol *(Encounter 3)*
- Shocked residents stood in the streets in the blizzard, *"deer caught in the headlights."* **Panacea's Acrobatics (26)** stayed in the saddle; **Zanthar's Intimidation** failed even on a hero-pointed reroll (25 vs 27), *"standing on top of the saddle, trying to scream out at them"* — the crowd's answer: *"Hey buddy, I don't mean to be in your way"*; **Cyrathul's roar (18)** did no better; **Bai Jian's Nature (24)** pushed the horses through. *"Unfortunately, the delay has cost you guys."*
- Bai Jian initially refused to mount — *"my speed is the same as the horse"* — and asked whether the horses acted on their own or cost an action to command. The GM asked for a Fortitude save (19) for running in a whiteout (*"Oh boy, how am I regretting my decision now?"*), then clarified the horses got a free move each turn, and he rode.
- A stretch of Foundry refusing to let anyone own a horse (*"from the land of make-believe"* — *"Imagine you're riding horses"*), then two watch officers and two infantry: *"Stop! In the name of the law!"* See Combat Encounters. **Three dead, one left alive.**
- Bai Jian, after killing the first officer: *"Not that I don't know if it matters too much at this point. I think we're kind of forsaken."* Cyrathul, after the second decapitation and Bai Jian's *"this dragon is a little violent"*: *"I don't mean to. I just keep killing people."*

### Act VII — The Imps *(chase obstacle 4)* and the Sheriff *(Encounter 4)*
- *"About 100 imps have been released and are on the lookout for you guys"* — a swarm like ravens over the whole city. Options: Religion to ward, Stealth, Survival to mask the passage.
- Round one went 1-for-4: Ren's Survival failed by one (Bai Jian routed the roll through the dog — *"you have a pretty strong bond with Ren"*), Cyrathul's Religion was a natural 1 (*"I'm out of hero points, guys. I'm sorry"*), Panacea's Religion couldn't reach her patron, and **Zanthar's Shared Invisibility** — *"on a person and a horse and a person and a horse for the two worst people at sneaking"* — was an automatic success.
- Round two: Ren again — *"Ren, come on. I didn't spend all that food on you just for this, man. You're fired"* — natural 1. Cyrathul failed again. **Panacea's Religion (23)** finally connected — Serrafina *"paying attention a little bit more"* — and half the imps banked off toward the other side of Elidir. Zanthar's Stealth reroll landed on **26 vs DC 27**. The imps screeched, pointed, and a group of sheriffs knew exactly where to stand.
- A *"cowboy looking guy"* in the middle of the street: *"Stop in the name of the law."* Bai Jian: *"Hit me. I can take it."* The **Unsanctioned Sheriff** drew *"just like Jesse James"* and rolled a **natural 20 — 39 damage** through Bai Jian's shoulder. Bai Jian: *"Well, he didn't just try, he actually shot me in the face."*
- The party took **all three alive**. Cyrathul double-crit the sheriff (*"Oh my gosh. Oh my gosh. Oh my gosh… This poor sheriff. Don't kill him"*) and pulled his third attack; Bai Jian burned a second Retrieval Prism and his last Sure Strike scroll for a **52 non-lethal crit** that dropped him; Zanthar's Blazing Bolts and Cyrathul's claws and Bai Jian's falcata all went non-lethal on the infantry. Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability crit on a plain guard produced the night's best nonsense — *"What's his favorite sandwich? Bologna and peanut butter"* (GM: *"It's an Elidiran favorite"*) — and, sparing him: *"Well, you will live to eat another bologna and peanut butter sandwich."* See Combat Encounters.
- **Retrieval Prism ruling**: the GM first said one talisman a day, then corrected himself — one talisman *affixed at a time*, and affixing takes ten minutes, which the party had spent on the healing stops. So the second prism stood. *"Okay, I'll keep that in mind then."* Panacea's natural-20 Needle Darts on the sheriff, by contrast, never landed at all — Foundry rolled it un-doubled and dropped it on Zanthar's token, and nobody noticed. Zanthar wanted the sheriff's badge; there was no time to loot.

### Act VIII — The Labyrinth *(chase obstacle 5)* and the Druma Road Gate
- Past the sheriff, the road began to twist: the **watch mages** working together to *"twist the road beneath your feet and try to lead you back towards the prison,"* a Labyrinth effect. Counter it with magic, or let Ren guide.
- Three rounds. Ren failed three times (a 2, a 4, and a 12 that missed by one). Cyrathul's Religion failed twice more — a natural 1 (*"I do not know how to do this. I don't know why I keep trying. Evidently I have no idea what I'm doing"*; the GM had him finger through his esoterica *"like Constantine does"* and drop it) — but his **Occultism (26)** landed in between. Zanthar's Arcana failed twice, once by one. **Panacea's Arcana succeeded twice** — the first on a hero-point reroll (*"we got to get out of here"*), the second a clean 31: *"like a bow on a Christmas present, it just unravels, and the road straightens out."* In between she spent a round bandaging Bai Jian (25). Bai Jian: *"We're not cut out for this subsystem."*
- **The Druma Road Gate**: a massive wooden door fifty feet above the city walls. A battle cry from beneath it — a **Hellknight Cavalry Brigade**, a line of **Line Infantry**, and a **Standard Bearer** in formation across the road. Initiative rolled, party still mounted. Bai Jian's last words of the session: *"We don't have to do this. Stop getting in the way."* The answer: ***"Kill the Hellbreakers!"***
- GM's sign-off: *"Lots of bad rolls tonight. But it was still fun. You guys are alive. You have all the Hellbreakers with you. You're mounted up. Just one obstacle left."*

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the Flat of the Blade
- Asked the question every fight this session hinged on, out loud and early: *"Are we killing this young kid or not?"* — and then held the line he was given. Both Azris, the sheriff and an infantryman went down under the flat of his blade; the one man he killed — a watch officer, on two swings he never called non-lethal — he rolled the GM's d20 for. His own verdict on the Azri fight: *"We gave them a chance."*
- Not soft about it, either. To the Warden, mid-crit: *"I don't think we're going to keep you alive."* After Rencelos's 38 on Cyrathul: *"Okay, this is just rude. Do we even keep him alive at this point?"* — and then, because the man still hadn't dropped, took him down non-lethal anyway. When the second patrol appeared: *"I dread that more, actually."*
- Offered terms to a man he had framed (*"You're cornered. Surrender"*), was refused, and put his own name on the record anyway: *"Just for the record, I didn't kill them."* The GM's answer — *"He might not know that"* — is the whole moral position of the party in six words, and Bai Jian was the one who forced it into the open.
- Owned the worst bluff of the night and narrated it in the third person: walked up to a jailer to ask what was going on, was informed there was a prison break, said *"It's a prison break?"*, and turned to the table — *"Okay, my job is just not good. He is not good at this, guys."* Then fixed it in the next action by **closing the gate** on them.
- The party's animal man, twice over: tore the tack shed open, then calmed a stable of spooked warhorses on his own Nature by pointing at Ren — *"Surely these horses can't be harder than this guy."*
- Taught Panacea the **Aid** action mid-fight, unprompted, when she said she was stuck with nothing to do. Argued for a second ten minutes of healing when everyone wanted to move. Delayed his turn behind Cyrathul in the sheriff fight. Made a point of letting Ren *"participate"* — *"I'll come in, Ren, so that he can get a love bite in."*
- Combat: five crits — **48** non-lethal on Moccoviz (Sure Strike), **36** on the Warden (hero point, Slam Down + Trip), **28** Snagging Strike on the Warden off his own Guidance, **41** on an infantryman (Slam Down + crit Trip), **52** non-lethal on the sheriff (second Retrieval Prism + last Sure Strike scroll). Ate a **39** nat-20 pistol shot for the team (*"Hit me. I can take it"*). Spent both hero points; ended the night on 81/92.

### Cyrathul · the Dragon on a Horse
- Set the party's ethical floor in the Azri cell with the sharpest line of the argument: *"Innocent is a strong word for this man… essentially a Chelaxian noble. Almost guaranteed deals in slavery"* — and then, having said it, agreed to keep the man alive. Rolled 19 of 20 on the dice and cut his throat anyway. The GM: *"Tell me how you accidentally kill this man."*
- Killed the Warden (*"the tried and true jugular slash"*) and two of the first patrol, both by biting their heads off at a gallop — the GM's *"drive-by beheading… like a cherry just pops."* His own review: *"It's just a dragon riding through town biting people's heads off. I don't know why a dragon on a horse is so effective. You might want to look into that."*
- And then, unprompted, stopped: after the sheriff fight opened with two crits, *"This poor sheriff. Don't kill him"* — third attack pulled non-lethal, and the finishing blow on the next guard too. *"I'll not make the mistake again."* His answer to *"this dragon is a little violent"* — *"I don't mean to. I just keep killing people"* — is a joke and a confession at once.
- Carried the party out of the prison: remembered the second hero point at the break (*"I technically get another hero point"*) and declined to spend it on a bluff he didn't believe in; Diverse Lore recalled *"pineapple juice"*, his Deception carried the bluff as far as it went (*"We had to make an emergency change. We got torn up in the chaos"*), and when the bluff failed he remembered his **three Helpful Steps scrolls** and built a staircase over a 30-foot wall for the whole party and every prisoner behind them. In the blizzard it was his Diplomacy (29) asking the crowd that found the stables.
- The hero point of the night: nat 1 on a Twisting Tail as the Warden stood up, rerolled to a **natural 20**, crit for 32, and *disrupted the move* — she stayed prone. *"Make up for my entire turn of nothing happening."*
- His Esoteric Lore was the comic engine of the back half: a crit Exploit Vulnerability on a plain guard revealed his favourite sandwich (bologna and peanut butter — *"an Elidiran favorite"*), and four Religion failures against the imps and the mages had him narrating his own incompetence with real grace: *"Evidently I have no idea what I'm doing."*

### Panacea · the One Who Tried Words First
- Opened the Azri fight with a speech instead of a spell — *"Listen kid, we want to let you out of here. We don't want to have to hurt you"* — then cast Fear on Rencelos specifically *"to make it clear that I was trying not to kill him."* He crit-saved it and laughed: *"You die here."* Her answer: *"Well, I guess we'll see."* When Bai Jian asked whether to kill Moccoviz, she was the one who answered: *"I think that we've killed enough."* Her line for the session, delivered flat: *"We're the good guys. War is hell, though."*
- Got the Warden's last question — *"Who are you?"* — and answered it: *"You'll never know."*
- Had the single best idea of the escape and said it as if it were obvious: *"We have the keys. We could just go out the front entrance."* Then, when the bluff collapsed, the second best: *"It's either these three guards or the whole crowd. How tall is the wall?"* — the prompt that reminded Cyrathul he owned a staircase.
- Drew her spear for the first time — *"This is my first time with my weapon… Okay, I'm excited now"* — and told the man she was stabbing *"we're trying to keep you alive here."* Missed twice, learned Aid from Bai Jian, and took the lesson the way she takes everything: *"That was a lot."*
