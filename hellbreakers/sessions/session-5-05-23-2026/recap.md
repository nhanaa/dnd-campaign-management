## Session Summary

The session opened in the aftermath of the ravine boss fight from Session 4. The party searched the hybrid lair and found a battle plan written in Goblin — the Hissrath had been framing Stumpfield's goblins for attacks on Fernsby to trigger a village-on-village war. A Goblin-speaking man in Fernsby translated the note and rewarded the party with a +1 weapon potency rune. With the Fernsby mystery solved, the party headed for Wolf Point to fulfill the Steel Circle deal.

Wolf Point was wrong from the first moment. No birds, no mice, no life of any kind on the hilltop. Bai Jian and Cyrathul fell asleep and were immediately wracked by nightmares — Bai Jian chasing his brother endlessly as he was ripped away, Cyrathul watching adventurers cart off his entire hoard. Panacea heard unnatural squawking from the north, then from the south, then a voice: *"You're all gonna die here."* The party tracked the sounds to a pair of Mitflits perched on a rooftop, shooting darts from the dark, and a Drude — a wolf-like Feywild creature with human eyes that was the source of the nightmares. Bai Jian long-jumped onto the roof and nearly cleaved a Mitflit in half on the first hit; Cyrathul finished it like sliced salami. Panacea's Needle Darts tore through the second. The Drude went down to Ren's jaws — commanded to flank, the boarhound leapt on the nightmare beast and ripped its throat out. Life returned to the hilltop by morning.

The party rode down to honor the Steel Circle deal. Runa Bloodhammer inspected the Drude's head, went wide-eyed, and upheld her word: the Steel Circle would move back into the Wolf Point keep and restrict their banditry to Chelaxian targets only. *"Ride wild, my friends."* The hearts-and-minds tour continued through farmsteads and breweries toward Stumpfield — a goblin-human art commune where a cooperative sculpture drew the party into adding their own pieces. Eric the goblin read the Hissrath battle plan, burst into tears, and hugged Bai Jian around the waist. The party hit 10 reputation points — the Hellbreakers were now viewed favorably across the Chitterwood. Bai Jian also picked up intel: Hellknight activity pointed toward Vordia, the Chelaxian-loyal town.

Someone was following them. Bai Jian's perception caught a figure two miles back on the trail. Bai Jian rolled Survival and used Ren to lead a merry chase through the forest, shaking the pursuit. They arrived at Vordia — flags of Cheliax and Asmodeus on every building — and blended in to gather intelligence. Three separate sources pointed to the same place: the Bright Embers Orphanage, run by the Sisters of the Golden Erinyes. Teens had been scared off by noises on the second floor. The constable didn't care.

The orphanage was an Asmodean ruin. Downstairs, a possessed toy box and a dining room haunt threw broken toys and ectoplasmic silverware at the party. Cyrathul exorcised the playroom with Esoteric Lore — *"Playtime is over"* — and the party fought through the dining room's ghost children until Panacea hero-pointed an Occultism check that made all twelve ghosts visible for a half-second, shout *"Boo!"*, and vanish. Upstairs was worse. An Attic Whisperer — a skeletal child-thing with a canine skull — reached out to Bai Jian, drained his voice, enfeebled him, and bit deep. A simultaneous haunt plunged the room into darkness with a nun's voice screaming *"Lights out!"* Bai Jian commanded Ren to grapple the creature, then struck with the Harpy's Talon for the first time, activating its bleed (though the undead had no blood to give). Cyrathul exorcised the darkness haunt with Esoteric Lore, then crushed the Attic Whisperer's skull in his jaws. Every door in the building unlocked at once. The spiritual energy exhaled.

## Key Events

### Act I — Ravine Aftermath & Fernsby (00:00–17:00)
- Party searched the goblin-snake lair. Found a tonic and a note in Goblin on a makeshift desk — drawings of a town on fire and goblin-snake hybrids advancing on another settlement.
- No one speaks Goblin anymore (*"We lost our people that did"* — Cyrathul on Moob and Fang's departure).
- Returned to Fernsby. Lily fetched a Goblin-speaking man who translated the note: the Hissrath was planning to set up Stumpfield's goblins as scapegoats for Fernsby's attacks.
- **Reward**: +1 weapon potency rune (crystal). Cyrathul needs a Handwraps of Mighty Blows to use it — not available in Fernsby.
- Bai Jian encouraged Lily and the villagers to build trust with the goblins: *"Maybe it will be better. It's always better to have peace among the people."*
- **+1 reputation point** for solving Fernsby's problem.
- Party rested the night. Fed and sheltered by the village.

### Act II — Hearts and Minds Road (17:00–27:00)
- Two days' travel toward Wolf Point, stopping at farmsteads for diplomacy checks.
- **Day 1**: Bai Jian rolled Diplomacy — crit success. Farm family enthusiastically pro-Hellbreaker; offered their barn and whiskey. Comfortable night, no watch needed.
- **Day 2**: Snowgeen rolled — average result. Cold-shoulder farmer, too busy with the final harvest to talk.
- Perception checks on overnight watch. Bai Jian and Ren both rolled low but caught some rustling — investigated, found nothing. Woke Cyrathul for second watch, no further incident.
- **Day 3**: Arrived at Wolf Point as the sun went down.

### Act III — Wolf Point: The Nightmare Tower (27:00–48:00)
- Wolf Point was devoid of all life. No birds on the rooftops, no mice in the buildings, no evening birdsong. Birds audible only far in the distance.
- Party set up camp with a fire. Panacea took first watch.
- **Nightmares hit immediately**: Bai Jian dreamed of his brother being kidnapped and ripped away — chasing endlessly, never catching up. Cyrathul dreamed of adventurers stripping his secret hoard bare.
- **Panacea's first perception** (rolled 18): heard squawking cries and moans from the north — *"like the Lost Boys doing a really bad interpretation of a crow."* Party investigated, found nothing. Sounds stopped when they stood up.
- Went back to sleep. **Nightmares resumed, harder to fall asleep**.
- **Panacea's second perception**: sounds now from the south. This time, a voice: *"You're all gonna die here."* Panacea woke everyone.
- Panacea cast Light on Cyrathul. Initiative rolled.

### Act IV — Combat: Mitflits and the Drude (48:00–80:00)
- Two Mitflits on a rooftop, shooting darts from the dark. A Drude (nightmare Fey dog with human eyes) lurking behind a building.
- Bai Jian and Cyrathul delayed waiting for light. Panacea cast Light on Cyrathul but the brightness blinded them to anything outside the 30-foot radius.
- Darts started hitting — 1 damage each, two hit Bai Jian.
- **Bai Jian**: long-jumped onto the roof (Athletics success, 2 actions), struck the Mitflit — 8 damage, *"almost cleaved it in half."*
- **Cyrathul**: climbed up, struck the same Mitflit — killed it. *"Sliced salami on top of the house."*
- **Panacea**: Needle Darts at the second Mitflit — 10 damage, tore right through it. Both Mitflits dead.
- **The Drude appeared** — wolf-like creature with glowing human eyes, Cheshire-cat blinks. Pax: *"Oh my God."*
- Drude: psychic pressure aura targeted Panacea (Will save) — **succeeded** (patron experience helped resist).
- **Bai Jian**: Sudden Charge at the Drude — missed (the psychic headache made it *"not feel like it's really there"*).
- **Cyrathul**: Exploit Vulnerability (failure but still gets Personal Antithesis), stride, strike — 16 damage (13 base + 3 PA).
- **Panacea**: Divine Lance — missed (rolled 14, needed higher). Trade Death for Life on the Drude — full 4 damage (failed Fort save), fast healing rider on Bai Jian. Stupefied 1 on the Drude via Familiar of Paired Perplexity.
- **Drude**: bit Cyrathul (9 damage), then psychic eye-stare (Will save) — Cyrathul **failed**, 2 damage + frightened.
- **Bai Jian**: commanded Ren to stride + bite. Ren flanked the Drude — **killing blow**. *"Ren just kind of rips the throat out of it."* Dog on dog.
- **93 XP** (Severe encounter).
- Post-combat: recall knowledge — Panacea's patron hinted Feywild origin. Cyrathul (Esoteric Lore): some sort of nightmare dog, new to him. Party searched the Mitflits' rooftop stash: short sword, Lord of Milani talisman (spirit damage), and a bomb (Bai Jian took it for range).
- **Overnight**: Cyrathul's second watch — peaceful. Animals returning: rats squeaking, normal sounds. *"Life has started to return."*

### Act V — Steel Circle Fulfilled (90:00–95:00)
- Morning. Rode down the hill to the Steel Circle camp. Runa Bloodhammer's sentries whistled — bird-calls, distinctly different from the Drude's cawing.
- Cyrathul reported success and threw the Drude's head at Runa's feet: *"I'll just throw the head over."*
- Runa stepped back, eyes wide: *"I didn't know you guys were playing with Feywild up there."*
- **Deal honored**: *"We'll stop robbing countryside people. We'll stick to just those flying the Chelaxian flag."*
- Runa: *"I trust you won't be telling the authorities where we are."* Cyrathul: *"No, no, we're not fans of the authorities ourselves."* Runa: *"Ride wild then, my friends, ride wild!"*
- **+1 reputation point, +30 XP.**

### Act VI — The Chitterwood Tour: Stumpfield (95:00–120:00)
- Multi-day travel toward Stumpfield. Hearts-and-minds checks each day.
- **Halfling brewery stop**: three halfling women running a cozy tavern-brewery. All for the Hellbreakers. Fed the party early supper and all the mead they could drink. Sugar hangover next morning.
- **Welcoming farmstead**: *"Damn Chelaxians, we can't wait to get out from under their rule."*
- **10 reputation points reached** — Hellbreakers now viewed as **favorable** in the Chitterwood. +10 XP bonus.
- **Three-family village**: painted a Hellbreaker flag and hung it behind their fireplace. Bai Jian cautioned them: *"Thank you for the enthusiasm, but maybe try not to be too noticeable."*
- **Stumpfield**: a goblin-human art commune. Semi-permanent tents, wagons-as-homes, a traveling-festival atmosphere. The whole town was building a cooperative sculpture — a four-legged wicker animal decorated with clay, paint, and scarves.
  - Bai Jian painted a Tian spirit symbol on it. Panacea added pine sprigs as whiskers (*"to make it cat-like"*). Cyrathul embedded a silver coin in a clay lump.
  - **Eric the goblin** read the Hissrath battle plan — eyes went huge. *"See, I told you something weird was going on. That other town was trying to blame us for things."* Burst into tears and hugged Bai Jian around the waist. Gave the party a healing potion. Wine and fire-juggling celebration.
  - Bai Jian asked about Hellknight activity. Eric pointed to **Vordia** — the Chelaxian-loyal town.
  - **Jeb's sculpture**: actually good. A woman holding a crystal, staring at the sky — worth several gold. Contrast with Eric's terrible stick-figure paintings.
- **+1 automatic reputation** for solving the goblin-snake problem before even arriving.

### Act VII — The Follower and Vordia (120:00–140:00)
- Multi-day travel toward Vordia. More hearts-and-minds checks — mixed results.
- **Bai Jian's perception caught a figure** following ~2 miles behind on the trail. When the party stopped to look, the figure vanished. Same as the rustling at the Wolf Point camp. Bai Jian: *"I think it must be that same thing. Someone definitely is keeping track of us."*
- Party debated ambush vs. evasion. Decided to lose the tail — Ren led a merry chase through the forest (Survival check — success). Party confident they shook the pursuer.
- Bai Jian: *"I wonder if that is the Hellknight."*
- A scared dwarf couple near Vordia took down their Chelaxian flag while the party visited. *"The only reason we had it is safety."*
- **Vordia**: bustling Chelaxian-loyal town. Flags of Cheliax and Asmodeus on every building. Residents looked happy. Party went undercover.
- **Three intel sources**:
  1. A sour-faced man mentioned the nuns at the **Bright Embers Orphanage** doing *"unsavory things with the kids"* before storming off.
  2. **Panacea** (crit success Diplomacy/Society): overhead gossip — teens snuck into the abandoned orphanage two nights ago, scared off by noises from the second floor.
  3. **Cyrathul**: a woman told him the **Sisters of the Golden Erinyes** had been *"doing weird stuff with the kids"* — wished the constable would act.
- Party visited the constable. He confirmed the teens' story, deflected responsibility. Gave directions — half a day's travel east of town.

### Act VIII — Bright Embers Orphanage: Haunts (140:00–165:00)
- Arrival: walled yard, rectangular two-story building hunched in on itself. Curtains drawn, front door ajar banging in the breeze. Stunted bare trees, dying grass, a faded red seesaw. *"Bright Embers Orphanage, est. 4701 AR."*
- Entered. Scuffed floors, crooked doors, a wide staircase. Footsteps of children running around — but no children visible.
- **Playroom haunt** (Trivial, 10 XP): Toy box burst open, expelled broken toys. Cyrathul struck it — barely chipped. Then used **Esoteric Lore** (great roll) to exorcise it: *"Playtime is over."* Box slammed shut, crying inside.
- **Dining room haunt**: Plates and silverware floated, ectoplasmic food appeared, ghost children flung food. Multiple rounds of exorcism attempts:
  - Panacea: Occultism — partial success.
  - Bai Jian: Intimidation — puffed up, one ghost child looked at him, dropped its food, and disappeared.
  - Cyrathul: Occultism — failed. Ghost children stuck out tongues and threw food anyway.
  - Panacea: Occultism — hero-pointed a 7 into a success. All twelve ghost children became visible for a half-second, shouted *"Boo!"*, and vanished.
- Panacea took 12 damage, Cyrathul took 14 damage from thrown plates/forks.
- Party healed up with potions (Bai Jian gave his healing potion to Cyrathul, Panacea drank one). Bai Jian also used Treat Wounds.
- Panacea: *"Well, at least I didn't kill him — like I did Moob. That is true."*

### Act IX — Bright Embers Orphanage: The Attic Whisperer (165:00–192:00)
- Second floor. Sparse rooms with wooden cots, padded beams, red pentagrams painted on walls above each beam.
- Entered a communal bedroom — two rows of decrepit beds, tattered blankets. A canine skull sat on one bed next to a ratty stuffed bear.
- **The skull stood up.** Bony hand reached out. Initiative — **it went first.**
- **Round 1**:
  - **Attic Whisperer**: touched Bai Jian — drained life (10 damage), **stole his voice** (cannot speak). Bit Bai Jian — 10 more damage, Fortitude save succeeded (avoided worse). Third attack — critical miss.
  - **Haunt simultaneously**: nun's voice screamed *"Lights out!"* — Cyrathul failed Will save, **blinded** by supernatural darkness.
  - **Bai Jian**: enfeebled 1 still active (drops end of turn). Stupefied 1 from the creature's sobbing aura (failed Will save). Commanded Ren — stride + grapple (success, creature grabbed). Then struck with **Harpy's Talon** — hit, 18 damage. Activated Harpy's Talon (consumable talisman) for persistent bleed — wasted on undead. Used **Dueling Parry** for +2 AC. Enfeebled wore off at end of turn.
  - **Cyrathul**: blinded — used **Esoteric Lore** to exorcise the darkness haunt. Success — darkness lifted immediately. Stride + bite — hit, 12 damage. Entered the creature's sobbing aura — failed Will save, **stupefied 1**.
  - **Panacea**: **3-action Heal** (emanation) — 1d8 = 4 HP to all allies (Bai Jian, Cyrathul healed; also damages undead — Attic Whisperer takes 4 vitality damage).
- **Round 2**:
  - **Attic Whisperer**: grabbed, reached at Bai Jian — hit (5 damage). Second and third attacks — missed (Ren grapple + Dueling Parry AC).
  - **Haunt fired again**: *"Lights out!"* — Bai Jian rolled Will save, **succeeded** this time. Darkness did not take hold.
  - **Bai Jian**: commanded Ren — two bites (6 damage). Then **hero-pointed an Athletics trip** attempt. Struck with Harpy's Talon — hit, 6 damage.
  - **Cyrathul**: Exploit Vulnerability + Personal Antithesis. Bite — hit, 8 damage. Second bite — **killed it**. *"I'll just clamp down on it until the skull shatters."*
- **Post-combat**: all doors in the building unlocked simultaneously. Silverware crashed downstairs. The spiritual energy exhaled — the orphanage was cleansed.
- **106 XP** (Severe encounter).
- Panacea rolled Occultism: 12 orphans slept communally here. The occult energy had prevented anyone from sleeping in these rooms for years. The Attic Whisperer was likely the anchor keeping the haunts active.
- Session ended with the party still in the orphanage, planning to explore the rest next time.
- Bai Jian: *"I'm not down this time."*
