Session Summary

The session opened in the aftermath of the ravine boss fight. The party found a Hissrath battle plan written in Goblin — the goblin-snake hybrids had been framing Stumpfield's goblins for attacks on Fernsby to trigger a village-on-village war. A Goblin-speaking man in Fernsby translated the note and rewarded the party with a +1 weapon potency rune. With the Fernsby mystery solved, the party headed for Wolf Point to fulfill the Steel Circle deal.

Wolf Point was wrong from the first moment. No birds, no mice, no life of any kind on the hilltop. Bai Jian and Cyrathul fell asleep and were immediately wracked by nightmares — Bai Jian chasing his brother endlessly as he was ripped away, Cyrathul watching adventurers cart off his entire hoard. Panacea heard unnatural squawking, then a voice: “You're all gonna die here.” The party tracked the sounds to a pair of Mitflits on a rooftop and a Drude — a wolf-like Feywild creature with human eyes that was the source of the nightmares. Bai Jian long-jumped onto the roof and nearly cleaved a Mitflit in half; Cyrathul finished it like sliced salami. Ren killed the Drude — flanking throat rip, dog on dog. Life returned to the hilltop by morning.

The party rode down to honor the Steel Circle deal. Runa Bloodhammer inspected the Drude's head, went wide-eyed, and upheld her word: the Steel Circle would restrict their banditry to Chelaxian targets only. “Ride wild, my friends.” The hearts-and-minds tour continued through farmsteads and breweries toward Stumpfield — a goblin-human art commune where a cooperative sculpture drew the party in. Eric the goblin read the Hissrath battle plan, burst into tears, and hugged Bai Jian around the waist. The party hit 10 reputation points — the Hellbreakers were now viewed favorably across the Chitterwood.

Someone was following them. Bai Jian's perception caught a figure two miles back on the trail. Bai Jian rolled Survival and used Ren to lead a merry chase through the forest, shaking the pursuit. They arrived at Vordia — flags of Cheliax and Asmodeus on every building — and blended in to gather intelligence. Three separate sources pointed to the same place: the Bright Embers Orphanage, run by the Sisters of the Golden Erinyes. The orphanage was an Asmodean ruin. Downstairs, haunted toys and ectoplasmic silverware. Upstairs, an Attic Whisperer — a skeletal child-thing that stole Bai Jian's voice, enfeebled him, and bit deep. Cyrathul crushed its skull in his jaws. Every door in the building unlocked at once. The orphanage was cleansed.

Act I · Faction · Ravine Aftermath & Fernsby

The Hissrath Battle Plan

  • Party searched the goblin-snake lair. Found a tonic and a note in Goblin on a makeshift desk — drawings of a town on fire and goblin-snake hybrids advancing on another settlement.
  • Cyrathul: “We lost our people that did” — on no one speaking Goblin after Moob and Fang's departure.
  • Returned to Fernsby. Lily fetched a Goblin-speaking man who translated: the Hissrath was framing Stumpfield's goblins as scapegoats for Fernsby's attacks.
  • Reward: +1 weapon potency rune (crystal). Cyrathul needs Handwraps of Mighty Blows to use it — not available in Fernsby.
  • Bai Jian encouraged reconciliation: “It's always better to have peace among the people.”
  • +1 reputation point for solving Fernsby's problem. Party rested the night, fed and sheltered.
Act II · Exploration · The Road to Wolf Point

Hearts and Minds

  • Two days' travel toward Wolf Point with Diplomacy checks at each farmstead.
  • Day 1: Bai Jian rolled Diplomacy — crit success. Farm family enthusiastically pro-Hellbreaker. Offered their barn and whiskey. No watch needed.
  • Day 2: Cyrathul rolled — average. Cold-shoulder farmer, too busy with the final harvest to talk.
  • Overnight watch — Bai Jian and Ren caught rustling in the trees. Investigated, found nothing. Woke Cyrathul for second watch; no further incident.
  • Day 3: Arrived at Wolf Point as the sun went down. Devoid of all life — no birds on rooftops, no mice in buildings, no evening birdsong.
Act III · Mystery · The Nightmare Tower

Wolf Point After Dark

  • Party set up camp. Panacea took first watch. Bai Jian and Cyrathul fell asleep.
  • Nightmares hit immediately: Bai Jian dreamed of his brother being kidnapped and ripped away — chasing endlessly, never catching up. Cyrathul dreamed of adventurers stripping his secret hoard bare.
  • Panacea's first perception (18): squawking cries and moans from the north. “Like the Lost Boys doing a really bad interpretation of a crow.” Party investigated, found nothing. Sounds stopped when they stood up.
  • Went back to sleep. Nightmares resumed, harder to fall back asleep.
  • Panacea's second perception: sounds now from the south. A voice: “You're all gonna die here.” Panacea woke everyone.
  • Panacea cast Light on Cyrathul. Initiative rolled — nothing visible yet.
Act IV · Combat · Mitflits & the Drude

Nightmare Dog

  • Two Mitflits on a rooftop shot darts from the dark — 2 hit Bai Jian for 1 damage each.
  • Bai Jian: long-jumped onto the roof (Athletics success), struck a Mitflit — 8 damage, “almost cleaved it in half.”
  • Cyrathul: moved up to the rooftop, struck the same Mitflit — killed it. “Sliced salami on top of the house.”
  • Panacea: Needle Darts at the second Mitflit — 10 damage, killed it. Both Mitflits dead in one round.
  • The Drude appeared — wolf-like Feywild creature with glowing human eyes, Cheshire-cat blinks. Psychic pressure aura targeted Panacea — she succeeded (patron experience).
  • Bai Jian: Sudden Charge at the Drude — missed (psychic headache interference).
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability (PA active), stride + strike — 16 damage.
  • Panacea: Divine Lance missed. Trade Death for Life — 4 damage on failed Fort save. Fast healing on Bai Jian. Stupefied 1 on the Drude via familiar.
  • Drude: bit Cyrathul (9 damage), psychic eye-stare — Cyrathul failed Will save, 2 damage + frightened.
  • Bai Jian: commanded Ren to stride + bite. Ren flanked the Drude — killing blow. “Ren rips the throat out of it. Dog on dog.”
  • 93 XP (Severe). Post-combat: Panacea's patron hinted Feywild origin. Mitflit stash: short sword, Lord of Milani talisman, bomb. Overnight — life returned to the hilltop.
Act V · Faction · Steel Circle Fulfilled

Ride Wild

  • Morning. Rode to the Steel Circle camp. Cyrathul threw the Drude's head at Runa Bloodhammer's feet.
  • Runa's eyes went wide: “I didn't know you guys were playing with Feywild up there.”
  • Deal honored: Steel Circle will stop robbing countryside people, stick to those flying the Chelaxian flag.
  • Runa: “I trust you won't be telling the authorities where we are.” Cyrathul: “No, no, we're not fans of the authorities ourselves.” Runa: “Ride wild then, my friends, ride wild!”
  • +1 reputation point, +30 XP.
Act VI · Faction · Stumpfield

Art, Wine, and Vindication

  • Multi-day travel. Halfling brewery: three women, all for the Hellbreakers. All the mead they could drink. Sugar hangover.
  • Welcoming farmsteads. 10 reputation points reached — Hellbreakers now viewed as favorable in the Chitterwood. +10 XP.
  • Three-family village painted a Hellbreaker flag and hung it behind the fireplace. Bai Jian cautioned: “Try not to be too noticeable.”
  • Stumpfield: goblin-human art commune. Wagons-as-homes, traveling-festival atmosphere. Cooperative sculpture of a four-legged wicker animal.
  • Bai Jian painted a Tian spirit symbol. Panacea added pine-sprig whiskers (“to make it cat-like”). Cyrathul embedded a silver coin in a clay lump.
  • Eric the goblin read the Hissrath battle plan. Eyes went huge. Tears. Hugged Bai Jian around the waist. Gave the party a healing potion. Wine and fire-juggling.
  • Jeb's sculpture: a woman holding a crystal, staring at the sky. Actually exquisite — worth several gold. Eric's stick-figure paintings: not exquisite. Cyrathul: “Inspirational.”
  • Bai Jian asked about Hellknight activity. Eric pointed to Vordia — the Chelaxian-loyal town.
  • +1 automatic reputation for solving the goblin-snake problem.
Act VII · Exploration · The Follower & Vordia

Undercover in Chelaxian Territory

  • Multi-day travel toward Vordia. More hearts-and-minds checks — mixed results.
  • Bai Jian's perception caught a figure following ~2 miles back. Same as the rustling at Wolf Point. “Someone definitely is keeping track of us.”
  • Bai Jian rolled Survival; Ren led a merry chase through the forest. Pursuer lost.
  • Scared dwarf couple near Vordia took down their Chelaxian flag: “The only reason we had it is safety.”
  • Vordia: bustling Chelaxian-loyal town. Flags of Cheliax and Asmodeus everywhere. Party went undercover.
  • Three intel sources all pointed to the Bright Embers Orphanage: a sour-faced man, Panacea's crit-success gossip, and Cyrathul chatting up a concerned local woman. The Sisters of the Golden Erinyes had been doing “weird stuff with the kids.”
  • Constable confirmed the teens' story. Gave directions — half a day's travel east.
Act VIII · Mystery · Bright Embers Orphanage — Haunts

Playroom and Dining Room

  • Arrival: walled yard, two-story building hunched in on itself. Curtains drawn, door ajar. Faded seesaw, stunted trees, no sign of life. “Bright Embers Orphanage, est. 4701 AR.”
  • Entered. Scuffed floors, crooked doors. Footsteps of invisible children running around.
  • Playroom haunt: toy box burst open, expelled broken toys. Cyrathul struck it — barely chipped. Then Esoteric Lore — success. “Playtime is over.” Box slammed shut, crying inside. 10 XP.
  • Dining room haunt: plates and silverware floated, ectoplasmic ghost children flung food.
  • Panacea: Occultism — partial success. Bai Jian: Intimidation — ghost child dropped its food and disappeared. Cyrathul: Occultism — failed; ghost children stuck out their tongues.
  • Panacea: Occultism — rolled 7, hero-pointed into success. All twelve ghosts became visible, shouted “Boo!”, and vanished.
  • Damage: Panacea 12 (fork + plate), Cyrathul 14 (plates). Party healed with potions and Treat Wounds.
  • Panacea: “Well, at least I didn't kill him — like I did Moob. That is true.”
Act IX · Combat · The Attic Whisperer

Lights Out

  • Second floor: sparse rooms, wooden cots, red pentagrams on walls. Communal bedroom — canine skull on a bed next to a stuffed bear.
  • The skull stood up. Bony hand reached out. Initiative — it went first.
  • Attic Whisperer: touched Bai Jian — 10 damage, stole his voice. Bit — 10 more damage, Fort save succeeded. Third attack — crit miss.
  • Haunt: nun's voice screamed “Lights out!” — Cyrathul blinded by supernatural darkness.
  • Bai Jian: enfeebled and stupefied. Commanded Ren → grapple (success). Struck with Harpy's Talon — hit, 18 damage (talisman consumed, bleed wasted on undead). Dueling Parry for +2 AC.
  • Cyrathul: Esoteric Lore exorcised the darkness (blindness removed). Stride + bite — 12 damage. Entered the aura — failed Will save, stupefied 1.
  • Panacea: 3-action Heal (emanation) — 4 HP to all allies, 4 vitality damage to the undead.
  • Round 2: Attic Whisperer hit Bai Jian (5 damage), missed twice (grapple + Dueling Parry). Haunt fired again — Bai Jian succeeded Will save.
  • Bai Jian: Ren bites (6 damage). Hero-pointed a trip attempt. Jian strike — 6 damage.
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability + bite — 8 damage. Second bite — killed it. Skull shattered in his jaws.
  • Every door unlocked. Silverware crashed downstairs. Spiritual energy exhaled. Orphanage cleansed.
  • 106 XP (Severe). Session ended in the orphanage. Bai Jian: “I'm not down this time.”
