## Session Summary

The Hellbreakers left Thorngrip at ten in the morning on the 4th of Calistril with a forty-mile walk south ahead of them and a goblin elder riding a dog at the back of the column. Zanthar spoke the night's win into the Wand of Dream Message — *we found it, we won the bet* — and while he was still talking, a goblin guard came running: someone had walked up to a village nobody can find. She let herself be seen, gave up her stealth entirely, and bowed. **Elaine Stormfrost**, sent by Alceo and Phoebe, with master's stealth, a silver whip and a flat refusal to be anywhere near the front. Bai Jian took one look at the party's record and welcomed her on exactly that basis: *"We're a bit loud. We are not sneaky at all."*

Two fights later they came out of the Chitterwood onto farmland and found **two Hell Knights of the Order of the Pike** fighting a pair of skeletal giants. The party killed the giants in a single round — neither giant ever took a turn — and then did something the Hellbreakers have never once managed with a Hell Knight: they talked. **Zanthar called the parlay** instead of the fight. **Hestlebeth and Hecatinia** are Isgerian twins who were *ordered to ignore the undead* and disobeyed, lost their whole contingent to an ambush, and were down to one scroll and each other. They named the officer who gave the order — **Vulaunex** — and nobody at the table reacted to the name at all. The twins handed over a rare **Divine Keystone** ritual scroll and its components, the party burned their dead for them, and between Elaine's Make an Impression, Zanthar's cover story and Panacea's hero-pointed recruit roll, two Hell Knights became a **Hell Knight Cavalry Brigade** on the Hellbreakers' side.

The town itself was Gillamoor: empty, cursed, and holding one survivor — **Sister Endreah**, the Lucent Mission's head sister, whom this party manacled in her own monastery in Session 6 and who is now dying on a cot with the curse pulling the soul out of her body. Up the hill, in a graveyard where every headstone had been dug up from the inside, the source was a single grave marked *forever in the memory of the 27th contingent of the Mercenary League*, bones roiling with void energy at the bottom of it. Brakknap sat down in the lotus position, his eyes lit blue, his ancestors stacked up behind him to the sky, and he began a ritual the goblins said would take minutes instead of the scroll's four days. The graves answered. Four **Deathless Acolytes of Urgathoa** came out of the ground and a **Godclaw Hellcrown** rose over the graveyard on a column of smoke and asked, *"You dare counteract the will of Urgathoa?"* Then it threw a rock at a ten-hit-point goblin and killed him instantly — the only attack it made in the entire fight — and Brakknap's soul stood up off his own corpse and kept chanting, louder. Panacea: *"Oh, so he's stronger dead."*

They finished a **Severe** encounter in two rounds. The curse broke, the graveyard became a tree, and Brakknap's ancestors chained their arms together and carried him up into a sky that went blue-green and then went quiet. The party healed the woman who still thinks they destroyed her monastery, and she gave them an hourglass charm and nowhere to go. In the dead surveyor's backpack at the bottom of the grave was a diary that names the next stop — **Finder's Gulch**, rumoured hideout of the **Wight Mother of Isger**, a child of Urgathoa said to be destined to conquer Isger with an army of wights. It is a hundred and forty miles *north*. They carried Brakknap's body back to Thorngrip and gave it to Neemi, who already knew.

## Key Events

### Act I — Leaving Thorngrip (4 Calistril, 10 AM)
- **Zanthar's Dream Message to Alceo**: the curse is located at Gillamoor, the party is going south, and Rhokrag has lost the bet. **Panacea was the one who remembered to send it** — she flagged it in her own session recap — and Bai Jian pushed to include the undead witch as well: *"We also should tell them about the witch, no?"*
- A goblin guard interrupted mid-message: someone was outside the thorn wall. They'd only noticed because she'd nearly been stepped on.
- **Elaine Stormfrost introduced herself by choice** — dropped stealth on being spotted and went into a performative bow. Sent by **Alceo and Phoebe** as support, already a Hellbreaker, *"I usually try to stay away from things as much as possible."*
- Bai Jian welcomed her on the party's actual weakness: *"I think we need that because we're part of the group. We're a bit loud. We are not sneaky at all."*
- **Brakknap said goodbye to Neemi.** She embraced him and looked worried anyway; he told her *"I have seen this, and this is the best course of action."* He rode out on a goblin dog with his two guards. Nobody at the table registered it as foreshadowing.
- **The party left the horses at Thorngrip.** Bai Jian opened the question (*"Do we even need horses?"*) and argued the upkeep — *"We don't have enough resources to keep these horses"*; **Panacea supplied the terrain argument** — *"If we're going through the forest, they're not gonna help."* Between them it decided the last twenty minutes of the session.

### Act II — The Wights on the Trail (Combat 1)
- Midday halt. **Panacea smelled them first** — the rotting-corpse smell she now knows on sight. Bai Jian missed it entirely; he was feeding Ren boar off his own plate.
- **Four wights** came shuffling through the trees. Two rounds, four kills, **16 damage taken across the party**.
- **Panacea's Trade Death for Life did nothing at all** — void damage into a void-healing creature. The wight *failed* its save and still took zero, and because it took no damage the hex's fast-healing rider never fired for Cyrathul either. A whole turn negated on both ends. She filed it immediately: *"Should have used my other one."* Bai Jian said the lesson out loud for the table.

### Act III — South Through the Forest
- Camp, watches, and a quiet night: a doe and two fawns, owls, and three goblins snoring in a pile *"like a Donald Duck cartoon."* **Elaine took first watch** — as she would again the following night.
- **Bai Jian flagged that he had the party's best Perception and then gave the watch away anyway** — he could take it "mechanically," he said, but he was not going to; someone else took second watch. On the way back north he took second watch himself, and couldn't concentrate through the noise of a forest that had just come back to life.
- Morning. **Panacea led the party back the way they'd come**; **Ren overruled her**, wagged his tail, and found an **abandoned caravan road** running dead south — a route the Scourge closed years ago.
- An hour later, an old village on a hillock, and the sound of a blade on armour. **Panacea, Zanthar and Elaine heard it; Bai Jian didn't** (Elaine 27, Zanthar 30, Panacea 31 — Bai Jian 21 on a natural 6, with Master Perception). **Panacea drew the conclusion**, crediting Elaine for the thought: *"Yeah, you're right, Elaine. The undead wouldn't be crying out in pain."*

### Act IV — The Order of the Pike (Combat 2 → Parlay → Recruit)
- Two armoured figures fighting **two skeletal giants** on the hillside, both still at full health. The one in front: *"Please, I beseech you — help us."*
- The party killed both giants inside one round; **neither giant nor either Hell Knight ever took a turn.**
- **Bai Jian nearly Sudden-Charged the Hell Knights by mistake and caught it himself** off a one-word prompt from the GM — *"Oh, wait. Are they on our side? Shit, I forgot"* — then rerouted around a 10-foot cliff to reach an actual giant.
- **Bai Jian also remembered that Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability carries a free Recall Knowledge rider**, asked for it on the party's behalf, and spent it on the giants' weakest save: **Will**. Cyrathul being GM-run, that was Pax's play, not Snowgeen's.
- **Zanthar called the parlay** rather than the fight. Both knights sheathed immediately.
- **The reveal**: Hestlebeth and Hecatinia, twins, Order of the Pike, both born Isgerian. Their commander **ordered them to ignore the undead**. They disobeyed, researched it themselves, traced the corruption to Gillamoor, and were ambushed on arrival — four piles of bones and several dead Hell Knights are still lying around the hill. Hecatinia named the officer: **Vulaunex**.
- **Zanthar held the cover story together** on a Deception 23 — the party were *"here from some of the villages around the area"* — and talked his way through the twins' follow-up when they pointed out that no village on this side of the Chitterwood is still active.
- **Elaine's Glad-Hand Make an Impression** opened them up: she cozied up to them and asked outright to be shown what their research had turned up, so the party could stop the curse. **Master Society, natural 5 → hero point → reroll of 10 → the table's +10 house rule → 36.**
- They revealed the **graveyard** as the suspected source and handed over their answer to it: a rare scroll of **Divine Keystone** (rank-4 consecration ritual) plus the full component set — holy water, powdered silver, purified chalk, 200 gp worth — because as Hell Knights they could not cast it themselves.
- **The recruit**: the party argued the twins out of going home, on the reasoning that deserters who lost their company have nothing good waiting at the fort. **Panacea rolled it on a hero point** and treated Hecatinia's limp while the negotiation ran. The twins went to *"talk to Auntie"* instead — and the Hellbreakers gained a **Hell Knight Cavalry Brigade** and **+2 reputation**.
- The party **burned the fallen Hell Knights** so nothing could raise them. Hecatinia cried. The twins would not let them strip the armour — *"I can't allow you to take any Hell Knights armor without passing the tests first."*

### Act V — Sister Endreah
- **Elaine spotted a figure through a window** of one of the hovels. Soft groaning inside. She declined to open the door — *"I'll just chill over here if someone wants to open this door"* — and **Bai Jian opened it.**
- Inside: **Sister Endreah**, head sister of the Lucent Mission, last seen manacled by this party in **Session 6**. Pale, dying. *"After you destroyed the monastery."* Bai Jian, immediately: *"We didn't destroy the monastery. We just knocked a lot of people out."*
- The undead drove her from the mission; she came to Gillamoor looking for answers and the curse got into her. **Brakknap diagnosed it**: she is dying and *will rise again* unless the curse is removed. *"Her ways and her beliefs have allowed whatever evil is in the ground to infect her soul."*
- Brakknap offered the ancestors' route instead of the four-day ritual — *"We will impart the spirit of the forest back into this desecrated land if you will allow us to."*
- The party **locked Endreah in the house** and went up the hill.

### Act VI — The Graveyard (Combat 3 — Severe)
- The path up was clear of snow because **skeletal feet had marched it flat.** A ten-foot wrought iron fence, a dying tree, and graves dug open **from the inside.**
- **Elaine found the source**: a hole pouring off void energy so strongly it registered as a physical thing, headstone reading *"forever in the memory of the 27th contingent of the Mercenary League."* Bones at the bottom with black tar boiling off them.
- **Brakknap performed the ritual.** Lotus position, blue fire in his eyes, a voice that was not his, the ancestor-goblins stacked behind him. *"It feels like it's trying to almost fight with something not natural."*
- The graveyard answered: **four Deathless Acolytes of Urgathoa** punched out of the ground, and a **Godclaw Hellcrown** — smoke with a burning face — rose above the fence and addressed Brakknap directly: **"You dare counteract the will of Urgathoa?"**
- **The acolytes kept standing back up.** Round 2, **Elaine's critical Recall Knowledge** explained why: each acolyte projects a **20-foot void-healing aura**, and *as long as one is standing they are all being healed.* Bai Jian drew the conclusion — *"But we gotta take them all down, then"* — and the party switched to clearing the ground before touching the boss.
- **The Hellcrown killed Brakknap** — a telekinetic rock, a critical hit, 26 damage into a 10 HP goblin, dead where he sat. It was the **only attack it made all fight**.
- **His soul stood up off his own body and kept chanting**, brighter than before, his ancestors stacked in a line behind him that ran all the way up toward the heavens.
- **Bai Jian flew** — Dragon's Flight to get airborne, then closed and hit the hovering Hellcrown, and took the fall damage on the way down rather than not reach it at all.
- **Zanthar ended it**: Lightning Bolt through the centre of the thing, then Hand of the Apprentice — his own maul, hurled — natural 20, straight through its face.

### Act VII — The Tree
- Brakknap's soul brought the ritual to a crescendo. Green light poured into the grave, the void energy washed out, and **a tree grew out of the graveyard.**
- Brakknap appeared on a branch ten feet up, **looking younger than the party had ever seen him**, hanging by his knees: *"Fear not, my friends. This was what was meant to happen. You have all done well and I have done my part."*
- His ancestors chained their arms together, took hold of his feet, and **lifted him into the sky.** It went bright blue-green, warbled, and dissipated. Snow started falling on the new tree.
- His two guards were crying and smiling at once — *"as if they knew that this might happen."* Panacea: *"So did the other, the one that was leading him out. She knew, too. She was sad before we left."*

### Act VIII — The Diary and the Charm
- **Sister Endreah survived.** *"What did you do? I don't feel like my insides are being ripped to the outside."* Panacea treated her wounds and the colour came back.
- She has no home and nowhere to go. She gave the party **an hourglass-shaped charm** off her own neck — *"I don't have much. Let me offer you something."* Bai Jian and Panacea sent her to **Saringallow**, and Bai Jian apologised for the monastery.
- A **backpack at the bottom of the grave**, pushed up by the tree's growth: the buried surveyor's **diary**, **a disgusting skull**, and the coin he was carrying.
- **The diary**: he was surveying caravan routes through Isger. His survey took him near **Finder's Gulch**, *"rumoured to be the hideout of the Wight Mother of Isger."* He recorded feeling sickly afterward; his last entry, months later, complains of **dysentery** and rejoices that he'll reach Gillamoor in the morning. He never left it. *(His own name is garbled in the recording — ask Kaazz.)*
- **Cyrathul's lore crit** on the name: *"The legendary Wight Mother of Isger is supposedly a child of Urgathoa and is said to be destined to conquer Isger with an army of wights."* **+5 reputation.**
- **Zanthar drew the practical conclusion** — the surveyor died of the thing he caught out there — and started pricing **anti-plague** before they set off. Bai Jian backed him: *"We can find a town somewhere and stop by."* The moderate ones are level 6 and 35 gp, so Thorngrip couldn't supply them; it waits for Logos.
- **Elaine's Recall Knowledge placed it**: **Finder's Gulch is 140 miles *north*, past Logos** — the fact that reversed the party's entire direction of travel. Their horses are at Thorngrip. They turned around.

### Act IX — Back to Thorngrip (6 Calistril)
- The forest was **loud** on the way back — deer herds, foxes, night birds, chipmunks. Bai Jian stood a watch he couldn't concentrate through; the GM's framing was that the forest had been almost dead before and was now full of life, and that he half-feared he wouldn't hear an attack through it.
- **Rhokrag's dream-rant reached Zanthar in his sleep**, around shift change: *"I can't believe you fuckers actually found out where it was. And you already lifted the curse. My coin purse is going to be so light."*
- **Neemi met them at the gate** and unstrapped Brakknap's body from the dog herself, sobbing, and waved off the guard who tried to help. *"I knew he was planning that this was going to happen, but it never does prepare you."* He will be planted under the sequoia.
- Panacea: *"His death brought life back to the land here and where it was cursed."*
- **Alceo's overnight message closes the session**: Rhokrag concedes the bet. And **the Hellclad Goblins have retaken Logos** — they decided Logos needed saving from an undead encroachment, stole weapons and armour off a band of Hell Knights, and took the city. The League is in peace treaties with them. **All Hellbreaker recruits now report to Logos, not Saringallow.**

## Combat Encounters

### Combat 1 — Wights on the Trail (`threat low` · 2 rounds · 60 XP)
**Enemies**: 4× **Wight** (lvl 3, 40 HP, AC 18, Fort +11) — *fueled by spite*, **void healing**, immune to death effects/disease/paralysis/poison/unconscious/bleed. **Final Spite**: on being reduced to 0 HP, a wight gets one last Strike before it is destroyed. **Corrupting Spite** on their attacks (DC 17 Fort).
**Allies**: Goblin Commando, Goblin Pyro, Brakknap.
**Initiative**: Elaine 38 (Stealth) · Cyrathul 38 (Esoteric Lore, nat 20) · Bai Jian 33 · Wight 30 · Wight 28 · Goblin Commando 24 · Zanthar 21 · Wight 17 · Wight 17 · Panacea 15 · Goblin Pyro 6 · Brakknap 4

**Round 1**
- **Elaine** — Stride, **Thorn Whip natural 20 → critical hit by +21** vs the wight's off-guard AC 16 (she was undetected off her Stealth initiative; nobody had acted yet, so this was not flanking). **34 damage**, and the whip's critical specialization **Slowed it 1**. She stepped straight back out — *"we'll pop on back because I don't want to get smacked."* GM: *"you guys see this elf fly almost out from you, whack this thing, and then come and almost stand behind you like a tornado."*

Double 12."*

A live wight then threw **three dagger Strikes** at **Bai Jian's AC 26** and landed none — miss by 9, **critical miss on a natural 1**, miss by 6.
