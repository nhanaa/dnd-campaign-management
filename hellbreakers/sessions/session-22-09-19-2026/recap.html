Session Summary

The Hellbreakers left Thorngrip at ten in the morning on the 4th of Calistril with a forty-mile walk south ahead of them and a goblin elder riding a dog at the back of the column. Zanthar spoke the night’s win into the Wand of Dream Message — we found it, we won the bet — and while he was still talking, a goblin guard came running: someone had walked up to a village nobody can find. She let herself be seen, gave up her stealth entirely, and bowed. Elaine Stormfrost, sent by Alceo and Phoebe, with master’s stealth, a silver whip and a flat refusal to be anywhere near the front. Bai Jian took one look at the party’s record and welcomed her on exactly that basis: “We’re a bit loud. We are not sneaky at all.”

Two fights later they came out of the Chitterwood onto farmland and found two Hell Knights of the Order of the Pike fighting a pair of skeletal giants. The party killed the giants in a single round — neither giant ever took a turn — and then did something the Hellbreakers have never once managed with a Hell Knight: they talked. Zanthar called the parlay instead of the fight. Hestlebeth and Hecatinia are Isgerian twins who were ordered to ignore the undead and disobeyed, lost their whole contingent to an ambush, and were down to one scroll and each other. They named the officer who gave the order — Vulaunex — and nobody at the table reacted to the name at all. The twins handed over a rare Divine Keystone ritual scroll and its components, the party burned their dead for them, and between Elaine’s Make an Impression, Zanthar’s cover story and Panacea’s hero-pointed recruit roll, two Hell Knights became a Hell Knight Cavalry Brigade on the Hellbreakers’ side.

The town itself was Gillamoor: empty, cursed, and holding one survivor — Sister Endreah, the Lucent Mission’s head sister, whom this party manacled in her own monastery in Session 6 and who is now dying on a cot with the curse pulling the soul out of her body. Up the hill, in a graveyard where every headstone had been dug up from the inside, the source was a single grave marked forever in the memory of the 27th contingent of the Mercenary League, bones roiling with void energy at the bottom of it. Brakknap sat down in the lotus position, his eyes lit blue, his ancestors stacked up behind him to the sky, and he began a ritual the goblins said would take minutes instead of the scroll’s four days. The graves answered. Four Deathless Acolytes of Urgathoa came out of the ground and a Godclaw Hellcrown rose over the graveyard on a column of smoke and asked, “You dare counteract the will of Urgathoa?” Then it threw a rock at a ten-hit-point goblin and killed him instantly — the only attack it made in the entire fight — and Brakknap’s soul stood up off his own corpse and kept chanting, louder. Panacea: “Oh, so he’s stronger dead.”

They finished a Severe encounter in two rounds. The curse broke, the graveyard became a tree, and Brakknap’s ancestors chained their arms together and carried him up into a sky that went blue-green and then went quiet. The party healed the woman who still thinks they destroyed her monastery, and she gave them an hourglass charm and nowhere to go. In the dead surveyor’s backpack at the bottom of the grave was a diary that names the next stop — Finder’s Gulch, rumoured hideout of the Wight Mother of Isger, a child of Urgathoa said to be destined to conquer Isger with an army of wights. It is a hundred and forty miles north. They carried Brakknap’s body back to Thorngrip and gave it to Neemi, who already knew.

Act I · Social · Leaving Thorngrip

The Message, and the Stranger at the Wall

  • Zanthar’s Dream Message to Alceo: the curse is at Gillamoor, the party is going south, Rhokrag has lost the bet. Panacea was the one who remembered to send it — she flagged it inside her own session recap — and Bai Jian pushed to include the undead witch: “We also should tell them about the witch, no?”
  • A goblin guard interrupted mid-message: someone was outside the thorn wall. They had only noticed because she had nearly been stepped on.
  • Elaine Stormfrost introduced herself by choice — dropped stealth on being spotted and went into a performative bow. Sent by Alceo and Phoebe as support, already a Hellbreaker: “I usually try to stay away from things as much as possible.”
  • Bai Jian welcomed her on the party’s actual weakness: “I think we need that because we’re part of the group. We’re a bit loud. We are not sneaky at all.”
  • Brakknap said goodbye to Neemi. She embraced him and looked worried anyway; he told her “I have seen this, and this is the best course of action.” Nobody at the table registered it as foreshadowing.
  • The horses stayed at Thorngrip. Bai Jian opened the question and argued the upkeep; Panacea supplied the terrain case — “If we’re going through the forest, they’re not gonna help.” Between them it decided the last twenty minutes of the session.
Act II · Combat · The Wights on the Trail

Four Wights, Two Rounds, Sixteen Damage

  • Midday halt. Panacea smelled them first — the rotting-corpse smell she now knows on sight. Bai Jian missed it entirely; he was feeding Ren boar off his own plate.
  • Elaine opened with a natural 20 against the wight’s off-guard AC — she had initiative on Stealth and was undetected — for 34, then stepped straight back out of reach.
  • Cyrathul, Bai Jian, Zanthar and Elaine took one wight each. Zanthar killed his from 500 feet with Hand of the Apprentice, which meant the wight’s Final Spite death-throe found nobody to hit.
  • Panacea’s Trade Death for Life did nothing at all — void damage into a void-healing creature. The wight failed its save and still took zero, and because it took no damage the hex’s fast-healing rider never fired for Cyrathul either. A whole turn negated on both ends.
  • Panacea, immediately: “Should have used my other one.” Bai Jian said the lesson out loud for the table — void damage is not very good against undead.
Act III · Exploration · South Through the Forest

A Caravan Road the Scourge Closed

  • Camp, watches, and a quiet night: a doe and two fawns, owls, and three goblins snoring in a pile “like a Donald Duck cartoon.” Elaine took first watch — as she would again the following night.
  • Bai Jian flagged that he had the party’s best Perception and then gave the watch away anyway — he could take it “mechanically,” he said, but he was not going to; someone else took second watch. On the way back north he took second watch himself, and couldn’t concentrate through the noise.
  • Morning. Panacea led the party back the way they had come; Ren overruled her, wagged his tail, and found an abandoned caravan road running dead south — a route the Scourge closed years ago.
  • An hour later, an old village on a hillock and the sound of a blade on armour. Panacea, Zanthar and Elaine heard it; Bai Jian didn’t — Elaine 27, Zanthar 30, Panacea 31, Bai Jian 21 on a natural 6 with Master Perception.
  • Panacea drew the conclusion, crediting Elaine for the thought: “Yeah, you’re right, Elaine. The undead wouldn’t be crying out in pain.”
Act IV · Combat → Parlay · The Order of the Pike

Two Hell Knights Who Disobeyed

  • Two armoured figures fighting two skeletal giants on the hillside, both still at full health. The one in front: “Please, I beseech you — help us.”
  • The party killed both giants inside one round; neither giant nor either Hell Knight ever took a turn.
  • Bai Jian nearly Sudden-Charged the Hell Knights by mistake and caught it himself — “Oh, wait. Are they on our side? Shit, I forgot” — then rerouted around a 10-foot cliff to reach an actual giant.
  • Bai Jian also remembered Cyrathul’s Exploit Vulnerability carries a free Recall Knowledge rider, asked for it, and spent it on the giants’ weakest save: Will. Cyrathul being GM-run, that was Pax’s play.
  • Zanthar called the parlay rather than the fight. Both knights sheathed immediately.
  • The reveal: twins, Order of the Pike, both born Isgerian. Their commander ordered them to ignore the undead. They disobeyed, researched it themselves, traced the corruption to Gillamoor, and were ambushed on arrival — four piles of bones and several dead Hell Knights still lie around the hill. Hecatinia named the officer: Vulaunex.
  • Zanthar held the cover story together on a Deception 23 when the twins pointed out that no village on this side of the Chitterwood is still inhabited.
  • Elaine’s Glad-Hand Make an Impression opened them up — Master Society, natural 5, hero point, reroll of 10, the table’s +10 house rule, final 36.
  • They gave up the graveyard as the suspected source and handed over a rare scroll of Divine Keystone plus its full 200 gp component set — as Hell Knights they could not cast it themselves.
  • The recruit: the party argued them out of going home, on the reasoning that deserters who lost their company have nothing good waiting at the fort. Panacea rolled it on a hero point and treated Hecatinia’s limp while the negotiation ran. Hell Knight Cavalry Brigade recruited · +2 reputation.
  • The party burned the fallen Hell Knights so nothing could raise them. Hecatinia cried. The twins would not let them strip the armour — “not without passing the tests first.”
Act V · Roleplay · Sister Endreah

Sixteen Sessions Later, Dying on a Cot

  • Elaine spotted a figure through a window nobody else was looking at. Soft groaning inside. She declined to open the door — “I’ll just chill over here if someone wants to open this door” — and Bai Jian opened it with no idea whether the dying woman was the witch they had come for.
  • Inside: Sister Endreah, head sister of the Lucent Mission, last seen manacled by this party in Session 6. Pale, dying. “After you destroyed the monastery.”
  • Bai Jian, immediately: “We didn’t destroy the monastery. We just knocked a lot of people out.”
  • The undead drove her from the mission; she came to Gillamoor for answers and the curse got into her. Brakknap diagnosed it — she is dying and will rise again unless the curse is removed. “Her ways and her beliefs have allowed whatever evil is in the ground to infect her soul.”
  • Brakknap offered the ancestors’ route instead of the scroll’s four days: “We will impart the spirit of the forest back into this desecrated land if you will allow us to.”
  • The party locked Endreah in the house and went up the hill.
Act VI · Combat · The Graveyard (Severe)

“You Dare Counteract the Will of Urgathoa?”

  • The path up was clear of snow because skeletal feet had marched it flat. A ten-foot wrought iron fence, a dying tree, and graves dug open from the inside.
  • Elaine found the source — a hole pouring off void energy, headstone reading “forever in the memory of the 27th contingent of the Mercenary League.” Bones at the bottom with black tar boiling off them.
  • Brakknap performed the ritual. Lotus position, blue fire in his eyes, a voice that was not his, the ancestor-goblins stacked behind him. “It feels like it’s trying to almost fight with something not natural.”
  • The graveyard answered: four Deathless Acolytes of Urgathoa punched out of the ground, and a Godclaw Hellcrown — smoke with a burning face — rose above the fence and addressed Brakknap directly.
  • The acolytes kept standing back up. Round 2, Elaine’s critical Recall Knowledge explained why: each projects a 20-foot void-healing aura, live as long as any one of them still stands. Bai Jian drew the conclusion — “But we gotta take them all down, then” — and the party cleared the ground before touching the boss.
  • The Hellcrown killed Brakknap — a telekinetic rock, a critical hit, 26 damage into a 10 HP goblin. It was the only attack it made all fight.
  • His soul stood up off his own body and kept chanting, brighter than before, his ancestors stacked in a line behind him that ran all the way up toward the heavens.
  • Bai Jian flew — Dragon’s Flight to get airborne, closed, hit the hovering Hellcrown for 21, then took the fall damage on the way down rather than not reach it at all.
  • Zanthar ended it: Lightning Bolt through the centre of the thing, then Hand of the Apprentice — his own maul, hurled — natural 20, straight through its face.
Act VII · Mystery · The Tree

A Release Throughout the Entirety of the Forest

  • Brakknap’s soul brought the ritual to a crescendo. Green light poured into the grave, the void energy washed out, and a tree grew out of the graveyard.
  • Brakknap appeared on a branch ten feet up, looking younger than the party had ever seen him, hanging by his knees: “Fear not, my friends. This was what was meant to happen.”
  • His ancestors chained their arms together, took hold of his feet, and lifted him into the sky. It went bright blue-green, warbled, and dissipated. Snow started falling on the new tree.
  • His two guards were crying and smiling at once — “as if they knew that this might happen.” Panacea: “So did the other, the one that was leading him out. She knew, too. She was sad before we left.”
Act VIII · Mystery · The Diary and the Charm

The Wight Mother of Isger

  • Sister Endreah survived. “What did you do? I don’t feel like my insides are being ripped to the outside.” Panacea treated her wounds and the colour came back.
  • She has no home and nowhere to go. She gave the party an hourglass-shaped charm off her own neck. Bai Jian and Panacea sent her to Saringallow, and Bai Jian apologised for the monastery.
  • A backpack at the bottom of the grave, pushed up by the tree’s growth: the surveyor’s diary, a disgusting skull, and the coin he was carrying.
  • The diary: he surveyed caravan routes through Isger. His survey took him near Finder’s Gulch, “rumoured to be the hideout of the Wight Mother of Isger.” He felt sickly afterward; his last entry, months later, complains of dysentery and rejoices that he will reach Gillamoor in the morning. He never left it.
  • Cyrathul’s lore crit: the Wight Mother is “a child of Urgathoa… destined to conquer Isger with an army of wights.” +5 reputation.
  • Zanthar drew the practical conclusion — the surveyor died of the thing he caught out there — and started pricing anti-plague. Bai Jian backed him. The moderate ones are level 6 and 35 gp, so Thorngrip can’t supply them; it waits for Logos.
  • Elaine’s Recall Knowledge placed it: Finder’s Gulch is 140 miles north, past Logos. Their horses are at Thorngrip. They turned around.
Act IX · Roleplay · Back to Thorngrip

Neemi at the Gate

  • The forest was loud on the way back — deer herds, foxes, night birds, chipmunks. Bai Jian stood a watch he couldn’t concentrate through; the forest had been almost dead before and was now full of life, and he half-feared he wouldn’t hear an attack through it.
  • Rhokrag’s dream-rant reached Zanthar in his sleep: “I can’t believe you fuckers actually found out where it was. And you already lifted the curse. My coin purse is going to be so light.”
  • Neemi met them at the gate and unstrapped Brakknap’s body from the dog herself, sobbing, waving off the guard who tried to help. “I knew he was planning that this was going to happen, but it never does prepare you.” He will be planted under the sequoia.
  • Panacea: “His death brought life back to the land here and where it was cursed.”
  • Alceo’s overnight message closes the session: Rhokrag concedes the bet, and the Hellclad Goblins have retaken Logos — they decided Logos needed saving from an undead encroachment, stole weapons and armour off a band of Hell Knights, and took the city. The League is in peace treaties with them. All recruits now report to Logos, not Saringallow.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Open Door

  • Welcomed the new player before anyone else did, and did it by naming the party’s real flaw. Elaine explained she avoids trouble; Bai Jian turned that into the reason she belonged: “I think we need that because we’re part of the group. We’re a bit loud. We are not sneaky at all.” He then made sure she had a last chance to shop before they left.
  • He was the one who opened Sister Endreah’s door. Elaine found the figure through the window and explicitly declined to go in. Bai Jian walked up and opened it — “Police here” — with no idea whether the dying woman was the witch they had come for.
  • He corrected the record and then apologised for it anyway. Endreah opened with “after you destroyed the monastery”; Bai Jian pushed back immediately, and twenty minutes later, once she had been healed and had nowhere left to go, closed with “And sorry about the monastery.”
  • He ran an absent player’s character better than the sheet did. Twice — on the skeletal giants and again on the acolytes — he remembered that Exploit Vulnerability carries a free Recall Knowledge rider, asked the GM for it, and chose the question. That produced the giants’ weakest save (Will) and the confirmation that the acolytes were not mindless.
  • He taught the lesson that cost the party a turn. When Panacea’s Trade Death for Life did nothing to a wight, he called it in plain language — void damage is not very good against undead — the same diagnostic instinct that named the troop problem in S20 and the resistance problem in S21.
  • He drew five of the six wight attacks and lost eight hit points doing it. Three dagger Strikes came at his AC 26 in Round 1 alone; all three missed, one on a natural 1. When a dying wight’s Final Spite claw found him anyway: “Ah, why me? I didn’t even kill you.”
  • He flew at the boss. The Hellcrown parked out of reach where nothing on the ground could touch it. Bai Jian burned Dragon’s Flight, closed, connected for 21 — then took 9 falling out of the sky and landed prone with no action left to stand, because the alternative was not reaching it at all.
  • The horses. He opened the question and argued the upkeep; Panacea supplied the terrain case. It was right, and it is also why the session ends with a 140-mile walk in the wrong direction.

Panacea · the One Who Kept Reading the Room

  • She ran the whole social half of the Hell Knight encounter and proposed the recruit before anyone else saw it. While the table was still deciding whether the twins were enemies, she had already jumped to “Maybe we can recruit them after?” — then asked the question that actually opened them (“So you disobeyed your orders?”) and answered their story with the line that bought their trust. She rolled the recruit itself, on a hero point, and treated Hecatinia’s limp while the negotiation was running.
  • She is the party’s early-warning system. She smelled the wights through a boar-and-berry lunch while Bai Jian was feeding his dog, and rolled the Seek that found them. She also volunteered the session recap unprompted, gave a clean and complete one, and it was inside that recap that she reminded Zanthar to send the Dream Message at all.
  • “Oh, so he’s stronger dead.” A rock had just killed Brakknap outright and his soul had stood up off his own corpse still chanting. Panacea named it. Best line of the night, delivered flat.
  • She owned the grief. She is the one who insisted on going back through Thorngrip at all — “Pay our respects anyway, because, you know, he sacrificed himself” — and the one who found the words for Neemi at the gate. She had also already clocked that Neemi knew: “She was sad before we left.”
