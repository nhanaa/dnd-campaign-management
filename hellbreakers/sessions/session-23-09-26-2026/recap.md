## Session Summary

Brakknap went into the ground at the foot of the sequoia on the morning of the 7th. Neemi sang him down — a song for her betrothed — and every goblin in Thorngrip took a turn with the shovel and a line of thanks. Panacea thanked him for showing her *"that in death sometimes we can be stronger"*; Bai Jian bowed and promised to remember; Cyrathul, back at the table after missing the last session, said nothing and put his handful of earth on the grave. The roots closed over the cairn and glowed the same blue that used to burn in Brakknap's eyes. Neemi told the party Thorngrip would keep every promise he'd made them.

They collected the horses and rode north to **Logos**, which the goblins had just freed from an undead siege, and found a war camp outside the gates flying Hellbreaker colours: goblins in stolen Hell Knight plate, hobgoblin battalions, the Wolf Point rogues, and the twins' **Hell Knight cavalry**, still visibly unsure what side of the camp they belong on. One of them said the thing out loud — the undead are *"more important to a free Isger than it is to whatever **Caeto** wanted us to do."* **Alceo and Phoebe** are marching the whole army on **Finder's Gulch in three days**, and asked the party to go ahead and scout it. The party sold off the stash, bought anti-plague, and left that afternoon.

The road north was a night-time swarm of crow skulls that wasn't undead, a guard post of two fungus-eaten **redcaps** who wanted snacks and served a *"Mistress,"* and then the ruins of Finder's Gulch itself: a hundred-year-dead ghost town where a **Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger** announced *"the living will feed me"* and Bai Jian ended it with a warhammer — the answer he went looking for after S21. A second swarm spoke inside Cyrathul's head. And at a cathedral with no god's markings on it, three **Erinyes sisters** — succumbed to the same blight that nearly killed Sister Endreah — were chanting over a **sulfur zombie**. Cyrathul went down; Ren tore one of the sisters off her feet by the ankle; Bai Jian's Sure Strike crit exploded the zombie in a thirty-foot fireball. Bai Jian pushed the cathedral door open, and from inside came voices speaking **Aklo**.

## Key Events

### Act I — Brakknap's Funeral (Thorngrip, morning)
- **Panacea opened the session with the S22 recap** — the funeral, Endreah, and the switch of the muster from Saringallow to Logos.
- The whole village gathered at the sequoia. **Neemi sang in Goblin — a song for her betrothed going to his final reward** — and she and the blacksmith lowered Brakknap into a small, deep grave. Every goblin took a scoop of earth and said something: *"thank you for teaching me to fish"*; *"thank you for teaching me how to do that magic trick to catch his brother on fire."*
- Neemi invited the party to help lay him to rest.
  - **Panacea**: *"Thank you for showing me that in death sometimes we can be stronger, and you're a hero."*
  - **Bai Jian** walked up and bowed: *"You did a lot. I will remember your sacrifice. I hope you find peace."*
  - **Cyrathul** walked over silently and put some dirt on the grave. Neemi smiled at him and stayed silent too.
- The sequoia's roots grew over the cairn and glowed with the blue light that had always been in Brakknap's eyes.
- **Neemi**: Thorngrip will **still send the promised battalions to Logos and honor its agreement.** *"He told me this would be the most likely outcome for him. He was happy to cure the land with his final sacrifice."* The S22 worry that the autonomy terms might not survive Brakknap is answered.

### Act II — North to Logos
- Survival out of the Chitterwood: Ren caught the scent of a forest hare and wandered east until **Panacea** redirected him (Panacea 28, Ren 18).
- Camp at the forest's edge rather than push on with exhaustion. **Bai Jian took first watch** and found goblin tracks behind the camp around midnight — escort or watchers, unclear; Cyrathul took second. The forest is audibly alive again.
- **Panacea kept the shopping list alive** — she asked the table what the rogue had wanted last session (Kaazz: anti-plague), because the dead surveyor had dysentery.
- **Logos, 2 p.m.** — the former capital of Isger, still patched from the Goblinblood Wars. A war camp outside the gates: the **Hellclad Goblins in ill-fitting Hell Knight plate**, and a Hellbreaker-flagged **Hell Knight camp** with the blonde twins in it.
- **Panacea greeted the twins first**: *"So you kept your word and came along, didn't you?"* — and asked how they were mending. They're healing, out of place, shifting on their feet. *"It's very strange to stand on this side, but the undead menace is more important to a free Isger than it is to whatever **Caeto** wanted us to do."* Panacea: *"I hope that you start to feel a little bit more at home soon."*
- **Logos is a level-8 city.** On Zanthar's and Cyrathul's suggestion the party sold the **Staff of Phantasms**, the half plate and loose gemstones — **82 gp to each PC** — bought **anti-plague** (one extra for Elaine), and **Bai Jian** reminded everyone without an armor potency rune to buy one.
- **Alceo and Phoebe** in the commander's tent. The army — goblin rebels, hobgoblin battalions, the Wolf Point rogues, the Hell Knight cavalry — **marches on Finder's Gulch in three days**; the party is asked to go first and scope it out. Phoebe could not believe Hell Knights had ridden in under a white flag saying the party recruited them. **Panacea** explained, and suggested the twins might help organize the army. **Cyrathul** asked after **Rhokrag**: arriving tomorrow, *"both pleased and scorned"* by the lost bet, and *"a hobgoblin of honor."*
- Finder's Gulch is **just over 50 miles** — about a day and a half. They left at 3:20 that afternoon.

### Act III — Combat 1: The Crow-Skull Swarm (night camp)
- An hour and a half into the night, **Panacea smelled undead again** and woke the camp, then warned them about rustling in the trees: *"we need to make sure that we don't have another one of those flying guys."*
- A swarm of crow skulls. Zanthar asked the question out loud — *is it undead?* — Cyrathul put it to Kaazz, and the answer was **no**. Cyrathul's Demoralize did nothing to it, and in Round 2 Panacea's Vitality Lash didn't either — Bai Jian reminded the table as she cast: *"I don't think they're undead, though."*
- **Zanthar debuted Floating Flame** — the swarm critically failed and took **44**, more than a quarter of its 155 HP. In Round 2 he sustained it and added a Fireball.
- Round 2 the swarm came apart into falling heads and bit all three frontliners — **Cyrathul crit for 38**, Ren 24, Bai Jian 19.
- Mid-turn, checking whether Ren's support benefit applied to a non-undead, Kaazz let slip a hint — *"without giving all the cards away, it may be affected by that"* — Ren's bonus damage keys to fiends or undead.
- **Bai Jian finished it**: Guidance on himself, Ren's support benefit, flank, **critical hit for 54**. *"Bai Jian just somehow does a very quick maneuver that just killed multiple of the crows dead at the same time, after most of them have been burnt off by Zanthar."*
- Bai Jian's read after the fight: *"I think having an AoE person is important."*
- **Cyrathul's Seek**: the swarm had been hopping tree to tree *towards* them — coming south, from the direction they're headed.

### Act IV — The Redcap Guard Post (Combat 2)
- Next day the trail died into a deer path; the surveyor's hundred-year-old map no longer matched the land. Around 4 p.m. an old caravan route reappeared.
- The party heard voices ahead **in Fae** (Zanthar understood them): two guards complaining about sitting out here, and *"don't let her hear us."* Everyone's Perception was low and **Bai Jian's full plate crunched through the refrozen ice.**
- **Zanthar answered them in Fae** — *"We are. Come and talk to us."* They wanted snacks. *"No living around here. Knows we don'ts."*
- **Panacea's Medicine** read them: gnome-like creatures covered in **fungal growth**. *"What are you infected with?"* — *"What makes you think I'm infected?"* — *"I see some growth upon you."* It was a guard post; they keep everyone out for **"Mistress."**
- **Cyrathul tried the bluff**: *"This is just a big misunderstanding. We actually work for her. She'll be expecting us if you'll lead us to her."* Deception rolled a 4, hero-pointed — **and the reroll was a natural 1.** Kaazz returned the hero point.
- *"The mistress wouldn't have any living beings coming up here."* Initiative. **Bai Jian**: *"Well, fuck your mistress. I take no order. I shall do whatever I want."*
- **Bai Jian's first swing, a natural 20 for 56, nearly cleaved the first one in half** — and before the second swing he stopped and asked the table: *"Do we kill or do we not kill, guys?"* Panacea: they work for the witch. He swung again — natural 19, dead. Zanthar: *"That was kind of sad."* Bai Jian, of the second one: *"Maybe he will. I don't know how loyal he is to the witch."* Panacea offered it surrender; it didn't take it.
- **Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability** found cold iron and **divine revulsion** — the redcap had seen Panacea cast a divine spell and was **fleeing** (Foundry showed Frightened 6, summing Fear with the revulsion; by the rules only the higher frightened value applies). As Cyrathul's jaws closed, it looked him in the eyes: ***"Burn my body. Don't let her raise me."***
- **Panacea called for honouring the request** — *"Well, I guess we better burn them and do their final request"* — and Bai Jian backed her: *"Finish the wish."* Cyrathul, looking at what was left: *"I think that's overkill. These poor guys."* They burned both bodies.

### Act V — Finder's Gulch: the Bonesinger (Combat 3)
- Nightfall. **Cyrathul** led by darkvision and his Shining Symbol; Bai Jian took Zanthar's **Everlight Crystal**.
- Slow, heavy footsteps. A tall figure with a warhammer: ***"The living will feed me."*** Bai Jian: *"Oh my god, not a fucking skeleton again."*
- A **Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger** — the same kind of creature that crit Bai Jian twice in S21 and resisted the falcata down to 4 damage. It went first and summoned a **Skeletal Giant** out of the ground, which hit Cyrathul for 13.
- **Bai Jian had planned for this one.** He crit the giant with the falcata (Snagging Strike, 30, vitalizing rune applied), then **swapped to the +1 striking warhammer**. Next round he asked whether the giant's persistent vitality had actually ticked — it hadn't (summons have no initiative slot) — rolled it: **6, and the giant was destroyed.** After three S22 fights without ever ticking, the vitalizing rune finally killed something.
- **Cyrathul's crit Exploit Vulnerability** named everything: weak holy and vitality, resists cold/electricity/fire/piercing/slashing 10. Mortal Weakness, tail for 31, twice.
- **Bai Jian's warhammer, natural 20: 26, not resisted.** *"It's not resisted, at least."*
- Panacea's Vitality Lash worked the boss down (and her Doctor's Visitation put 29 back into Cyrathul) — and then the Bonesinger **crit Cyrathul for 42**. *"There goes all your healing."* Panacea hissed at it.
- It tried to cast. **Bai Jian's Reactive Strike with the warhammer: 19, destroyed.** *"He was saying his incantation and I'm like, ah, the audacity of this guy — and then just bash him in the head."*
- A second **+1 striking warhammer** and a set of **Hellknight plate** into the stash. The town is a ghost town — a city centre from a hundred years ago.

### Act VI — The Second Swarm (Combat 4)
- Creeping through the ghost town, **Cyrathul** smelled the smell from the night before and heard the chattering of beaks.
- A voice spoke **inside Cyrathul's head**: *"These guys are real creative. They like death."*
- **Cyrathul, as initiative went up: *"Well, at least we now know it's a fiend."*** His Exploit Vulnerability later in the round found it weak to holy and splash — *"which I did not find out last time."*
- Foundry had lost the swarm's attacks; Kaazz rebuilt them mid-fight and cleared the chat log.
- **Panacea's hero-pointed Moonlight Ray** — holy, into a holy weakness — did **54**, the single biggest hit of the fight. **Cyrathul's Frenzy tail swipe finished it**: *"I'll just do a little spin around and flail my tail everywhere."*
- Bai Jian burned a hero point on a Will save that came up a natural 1 — reroll, **natural 20**.

### Act VII — The Cathedral (Combat 5)
- A garden gone rank and poisonous, a fallen wall, and a **large cathedral** with no markings of any god the party knows — better built than anything else in town.
- Chanting. **Three Erinyes sisters** from the Lucent Mission, **turned by the same blight that nearly took Sister Endreah**, chanting over something in the centre. *"It's those that desecrated our…"*
- **Cyrathul**: *"Looks like someone with a severe smoking problem from what I can see. Maybe they were trying to summon lung cancer."* It was a **sulfur zombie**.
- **Bai Jian** killed the first sister on his opening swing (crit, 44) and parried.
- The zombie and a sister's flurry took **Cyrathul from 55 to 2**, with persistent fire and Drained. When Cyrathul and Kaazz were confused why his failed Blinding Sulfur save showed as a success, **Bai Jian explained it: the effect is incapacitation**, which upgrades the save — Foundry had already applied it, and it saved him from being blinded.
- **Ren killed a sister** — Bai Jian whistled, and *"Ren immediately understands and just kind of runs out to the back and just bites the ankle and takes her down."* GM: *"Ren really loves undead flesh."*
- **Cyrathul went down** to the zombie's fist. **Zanthar brought him back with a 24-point Lay on Hands**, extended with Reach Spell after **Bai Jian** checked the feat text for him.
- **Bai Jian: Sure Strike scroll, critical hit — "Fatal 12 is crazy" — 83 damage.** The zombie exploded in a 30-foot burst of fire; Cyrathul crit-failed the save. Zanthar was clear of it: *"I'm 35 feet away."*
- After the fight Cyrathul was on fire again (from the zombie's last fist); **Panacea's sustained Cloak of Light** healed him while he rolled recovery checks with **Zanthar assisting**, until it went out and he was back to full.
- The zombie left behind **Dust of Corpse Animation**. Zanthar: *"Cool, we can make our own zombies!"* Bai Jian: *"Yeah, I don't know about that one… Oh, you guys magical people will do that. I'm just gonna stick to my own thing."*

### Act VIII — The Door
- Cyrathul: *"I have a sneaking suspicion the witch will be in this cathedral."*
- **Bai Jian pushed the door open.** From farther inside came voices speaking **Aklo**. Nobody at the table speaks it.
