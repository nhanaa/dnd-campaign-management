Session Summary

Brakknap went into the ground at the foot of the sequoia on the morning of the 7th. Neemi sang him down — a song for her betrothed — and every goblin in Thorngrip took a turn with the shovel and a line of thanks. Panacea thanked him for showing her “that in death sometimes we can be stronger”; Bai Jian bowed and promised to remember; Cyrathul, back at the table after missing the last session, said nothing and put his handful of earth on the grave. The roots closed over the cairn and glowed the same blue that used to burn in Brakknap’s eyes. Neemi told the party Thorngrip would keep every promise he’d made them.

They collected the horses and rode north to Logos, which the goblins had just freed from an undead siege, and found a war camp outside the gates flying Hellbreaker colours: goblins in stolen Hell Knight plate, hobgoblin battalions, the Wolf Point rogues, and the twins’ Hell Knight cavalry, still visibly unsure what side of the camp they belong on. One of them said the thing out loud — the undead are “more important to a free Isger than it is to whatever Caeto wanted us to do.” Alceo and Phoebe are marching the whole army on Finder’s Gulch in three days, and asked the party to go ahead and scout it. The party sold off the stash, bought anti-plague, and left that afternoon.

The road north was a night-time swarm of crow skulls that wasn’t undead, a guard post of two fungus-eaten redcaps who wanted snacks and served a “Mistress,” and then the ruins of Finder’s Gulch itself: a hundred-year-dead ghost town where a Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger announced “the living will feed me” and Bai Jian ended it with a warhammer — the answer he went looking for after S21. A second swarm spoke inside Cyrathul’s head. And at a cathedral with no god’s markings on it, three Erinyes sisters — succumbed to the same blight that nearly killed Sister Endreah — were chanting over a sulfur zombie. Cyrathul went down; Ren tore one of the sisters off her feet by the ankle; Bai Jian’s Sure Strike crit exploded the zombie in a thirty-foot fireball. Bai Jian pushed the cathedral door open, and from inside came voices speaking Aklo.

Act I · Roleplay · Thorngrip, Morning

Brakknap’s Funeral

  • Panacea opened the session with the S22 recap — the funeral, Endreah, and the switch of the muster from Saringallow to Logos.
  • The whole village gathered at the sequoia. Neemi sang in Goblin — a song for her betrothed going to his final reward — and she and the blacksmith lowered Brakknap into a small, deep grave. Every goblin took a scoop of earth and a line of thanks: “thank you for teaching me to fish”; “thank you for teaching me how to do that magic trick to catch his brother on fire.”
  • Panacea: “Thank you for showing me that in death sometimes we can be stronger, and you’re a hero.” Neemi squeezed her shoulder.
  • Bai Jian walked up and bowed: “You did a lot. I will remember your sacrifice. I hope you find peace.”
  • Cyrathul walked over silently and put some dirt on the grave. Neemi smiled at him and stayed silent too.
  • The sequoia’s roots grew over the cairn and glowed with the blue light that had always been in Brakknap’s eyes.
  • Neemi: Thorngrip will still send the promised battalions to Logos and honor its agreement. “He told me this would be the most likely outcome for him. He was happy to cure the land with his final sacrifice.” The S22 worry that the terms wouldn’t survive him is answered.
Act II · Social · North to Logos

An Army Under a Hellbreaker Flag

  • Out of the Chitterwood: Ren chased the scent of a forest hare east until Panacea redirected him. Camp at the forest’s edge; Bai Jian took first watch and found goblin tracks behind the camp — escort or watchers, unclear.
  • Panacea kept the shopping list alive — she asked the table what the rogue had wanted last session (anti-plague), because the dead surveyor had dysentery.
  • Logos, 2 p.m. — the former capital, still patched from the Goblinblood Wars. Outside the gates: the Hellclad Goblins in ill-fitting Hell Knight plate, and a Hellbreaker-flagged Hell Knight camp with the blonde twins in it.
  • Panacea greeted the twins first: “So you kept your word and came along, didn’t you?” One of them: “It’s very strange to stand on this side, but the undead menace is more important to a free Isger than it is to whatever Caeto wanted us to do.” Panacea: “I hope that you start to feel a little bit more at home soon.”
  • Logos is a level-8 city. On Zanthar’s and Cyrathul’s suggestion the party sold the Staff of Phantasms, the half plate and loose gemstones — 82 gp to each PC — and bought anti-plague. Bai Jian reminded everyone without an armor potency rune to buy one.
  • Alceo and Phoebe: the army — goblin rebels, hobgoblin battalions, the Wolf Point rogues, the Hell Knight cavalry — marches on Finder’s Gulch in three days; the party goes ahead to scout. Panacea explained the Hell Knights to a disbelieving Phoebe and suggested they might help organize the army.
  • Cyrathul asked after Rhokrag: arriving tomorrow, “both pleased and scorned” by the lost bet, and “a hobgoblin of honor.” Finder’s Gulch is just over 50 miles. They left at 3:20 that afternoon.
Act III · Combat · The Night Camp

A Swarm of Crow Skulls That Wasn’t Undead

  • An hour and a half into the night, Panacea smelled undead again, woke the camp, and warned them to watch the trees: “we need to make sure that we don’t have another one of those flying guys.”
  • Zanthar asked out loud whether it was undead; Cyrathul put it to Kaazz. It is not. Demoralize did nothing, and Panacea’s Vitality Lash did nothing — Bai Jian reminded her as she cast: “I don’t think they’re undead, though.”
  • Zanthar debuted Floating Flame — a natural-1 critical failure for 44 — then sustained it and added a Fireball. 81 from him, the most anyone put in.
  • Round 2 the swarm came apart into falling heads and bit all three frontliners — Cyrathul crit for 38, Ren 24, Bai Jian 19.
  • Kaazz let slip a hint while Bai Jian checked Ren’s support benefit: “without giving all the cards away, it may be affected by that” — Ren’s bonus damage keys to fiends or undead.
  • Bai Jian finished it: Guidance, Ren’s support, flank, critical hit for 54. “Bai Jian just somehow does a very quick maneuver that just killed multiple of the crows dead at the same time, after most of them have been burnt off by Zanthar.”
  • Bai Jian’s read afterward: “I think having an AoE person is important.” Cyrathul’s Seek: the swarm had been hopping tree to tree toward them, from the direction they’re headed.
Act IV · Combat · The Guard Post

“Burn My Body. Don’t Let Her Raise Me.”

  • The trail died into a deer path and the surveyor’s hundred-year-old map stopped matching. Around 4 p.m. an old caravan route reappeared. Zanthar, on the approach: “Yeah, we’re good. We’re sneaky people.” Then Bai Jian’s full plate crunched through the refrozen ice.
  • Voices ahead in Fae. Zanthar understood and answered: “We are. Come and talk to us.” They wanted snacks.
  • Panacea’s Medicine read them: gnome-like creatures under fungal growth. “What are you infected with?” — “What makes you think I’m infected?” — “I see some growth upon you.” They guard the road for “Mistress.”
  • Cyrathul’s bluff: “This is just a big misunderstanding. We actually work for her.” A 4, hero-pointed — natural 1. Kaazz refunded the point.
  • Bai Jian: “Well, fuck your mistress. I take no order. I shall do whatever I want.” A natural 20 for 56 nearly cleaved the first one in half — then he stopped: “Do we kill or do we not kill, guys?” Panacea: they work for the witch. Natural 19, dead. Zanthar: “That was kind of sad.”
  • Cyrathul’s Exploit Vulnerability found cold iron and divine revulsion — the second redcap had seen Panacea cast and was fleeing. As his jaws closed: “Burn my body. Don’t let her raise me.”
  • Panacea: “Well, I guess we better burn them and do their final request.” Bai Jian: “Finish the wish.” Cyrathul: “I think that’s overkill. These poor guys.” They burned both bodies.
Act V · Combat · Finder’s Gulch

The Warhammer Answer

  • Nightfall. Cyrathul led on darkvision and his Shining Symbol and called for stealth; Bai Jian took Zanthar’s Everlight Crystal.
  • Slow, heavy footsteps. “The living will feed me.” Bai Jian: “Oh my god, not a fucking skeleton again.” A Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger — the same kind of creature that resisted his falcata down to 4 damage in S21 — summoned a Skeletal Giant.
  • Bai Jian had planned for it. Falcata crit on the giant (30, vitalizing rune applied), then swapped to the +1 striking warhammer.
  • Next round he noticed the giant’s persistent vitality hadn’t ticked — summons have no initiative slot — rolled it: 6, and the giant was destroyed. After three fights without ticking, the rune finally killed something.
  • Cyrathul’s crit Exploit Vulnerability named the whole resistance line; Mortal Weakness, tail for 31, twice. Bai Jian’s warhammer, natural 20: 26, not resisted.
  • The Bonesinger crit Cyrathul for 42, undoing Panacea’s 29-point Doctor’s Visitation. “There goes all your healing.” Panacea hissed at it.
  • It tried to cast. Bai Jian’s Reactive Strike with the warhammer: 19, destroyed. “He was saying his incantation and I’m like, ah, the audacity of this guy — and then just bash him in the head.”
Act VI · Combat · The Ghost Town

A Voice in Cyrathul’s Head

  • Creeping through the ghost town, Cyrathul smelled the smell from the night before and heard the chattering of beaks.
  • A voice spoke inside Cyrathul’s head: “These guys are real creative. They like death.”
  • Cyrathul, as initiative went up: “Well, at least we now know it’s a fiend.” His Exploit Vulnerability later found it weak to holy and splash — “which I did not find out last time.”
  • Foundry had lost the swarm’s attacks; Kaazz rebuilt them mid-fight, then cleared the chat log.
  • Bai Jian hero-pointed a natural-1 Will save and rerolled a natural 20.
  • Panacea’s hero-pointed Moonlight Ray — holy into a holy weakness — hit on the +10 house rule for 54. Cyrathul finished it: “I’ll just do a little spin around and flail my tail everywhere.”
Act VII · Combat · The Cathedral

Three Sisters and a Sulfur Zombie

  • A garden gone rank and poisonous, and a large cathedral with no markings of any god the party knows. Cyrathul: “Sitting in a town full of undead, chanting slowly in the night.”
  • Three Erinyes sisters of the Lucent Mission, turned by the blight that nearly took Sister Endreah: “It’s those that desecrated our…” Cyrathul, on the thing in the centre: “Looks like someone with a severe smoking problem… Maybe they were trying to summon lung cancer.” A sulfur zombie.
  • Bai Jian killed the first sister on his opening swing. The zombie and a sister’s flurry took Cyrathul from 55 to 2; Bai Jian explained why his failed Blinding Sulfur save still counted — it’s incapacitation.
  • Ren killed a sister: “Ren immediately understands and just kind of runs out to the back and just bites the ankle and takes her down.” GM: “Ren really loves undead flesh.”
  • Cyrathul went down. Zanthar brought him back with a 24-point Lay on Hands through Reach Spell, after Bai Jian read him the feat text.
  • Bai Jian: Sure Strike scroll, critical — 83. “Fatal 12 is crazy.” The zombie exploded in a 30-foot burst of fire; Cyrathul crit-failed the save. Zanthar: “I’m 35 feet away.”
  • Cyrathul burning again afterward; Panacea’s Cloak of Light and Zanthar’s assisted recovery got him back to full. The zombie left Dust of Corpse Animation. Zanthar: “Cool, we can make our own zombies!” Bai Jian: “Oh, you guys magical people will do that. I’m just gonna stick to my own thing.”
Act VIII · Mystery · The Door

Voices in Aklo

  • Cyrathul: “I have a sneaking suspicion the witch will be in this cathedral.”
  • Bai Jian wondered aloud what the sisters had been doing out there; Panacea guessed a graveyard.
  • Bai Jian pushed the door open. “Bonk.”
  • From farther inside came voices speaking Aklo. Nobody at the table speaks it. End of session.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Answer to Last Time

  • He came back to the Bonesinger with a plan. In S21 the same kind of monster resisted his falcata down to 4 damage and crit him twice. Tonight he opened on the summoned giant with the falcata, swapped to the warhammer before the boss ever reached him, and killed it twice over — a natural-20 crit for 26 the resistance never touched, then a Reactive Strike when it tried to cast. “He was saying his incantation and I’m like, ah, the audacity of this guy.”
  • He made the vitalizing rune pay. It had not ticked once in three fights since he installed it. On the Skeletal Giant he noticed the persistent damage hadn’t fired — summons have no initiative slot — asked Kaazz, rolled the 2d6, and the 6 destroyed it.
  • At the funeral he bowed and said the right amount: “You did a lot. I will remember your sacrifice. I hope you find peace.”
  • He asked the question mid-swing. His first crit had nearly cut a redcap in half; before the second he stopped: “Do we kill or do we not kill, guys?” — the question he’s been asking since the party’s lethality flipped in S18. Panacea answered that they work for the witch, and he swung again.
  • “Well, fuck your mistress. I take no order. I shall do whatever I want.” — the redcaps’ refusal to let the living pass, answered.
  • He kept the table’s rules straight — explained why Cyrathul’s failed Blinding Sulfur save counted (it’s incapacitation), read out the Reach Spell text so Zanthar’s Lay on Hands could reach a dying Cyrathul, and diagnosed the swarm on the spot: “I think having an AoE person is important.”
  • He killed the first swarm, a redcap, the Skeletal Giant, the Bonesinger, two sisters and the sulfur zombie — seven of the night’s ten enemies. The zombie was an 83-damage Sure Strike crit. Ren got the other sister.
  • He opened the door. “I’m pushing the door open. Bonk.”

Panacea · the Nose and the Hands

  • She spoke first at the grave — “thank you for showing me that in death sometimes we can be stronger, and you’re a hero” — last week’s “he’s stronger dead,” said to his face this time.
  • She’s the party’s early warning, again. She smelled the swarm at the night camp and woke everyone, then warned them to watch the trees for “another one of those flying guys.”
  • She ran the Logos social scene. She greeted the twins first, asked how they were mending, and told them she hoped they’d feel at home; she explained their recruitment to Phoebe and suggested their discipline might help organize the army.

  • She kept the party’s memory. She opened the session with the S22 recap and was the one who asked what the rogue had wanted bought — the anti-plague.
  • She kept Cyrathul alive through two fights — Treat Wounds, Doctor’s Visitation for 29, Life Boost four times, and a sustained Cloak of Light that carried him back to full after the fight while he was still burning. Serrafina handed her the healing staff mid-fight.
  • Her hero-pointed Moonlight Ray did 54 to the second swarm — holy into a holy weakness, the biggest hit of that fight.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Fire Behind the Blade

  • He talked to the redcaps in their own language. He was the only one who understood the Fae, relayed it to the party quietly, and answered them in it: “We are. Come and talk to us.”
  • Floating Flame’s debut was the first swarm’s biggest spell hit — a critical-failure save for 44 — and his 81 total was the most anyone put into it; Bai Jian’s killing blow was narrated straight off it. He reused it on the second swarm for 29.
  • He brought Cyrathul back up. With the dragon down and on fire, he spent Lay on Hands through Reach Spell for 24, then assisted his recovery checks until the fire went out.
