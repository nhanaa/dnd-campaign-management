## Session Summary

The party came into this session having spent all of Session 17 refusing to kill anybody. They end it standing in a corridor full of dead prison guards, agreeing out loud that they are the bad guys now.

The night opened quietly. Bai Jian, standing watch outside the Soldier's Aegis, heard a single set of boots in the snow — Jenverick, who had been told to stay put and hadn't — and then a second man who made no sound at all. **Judge Leoz Balake** stepped out of the shadows: once a genuinely well-loved magistrate, high integrity, tough on crime, never took a bribe, until somebody threw acid in his face. He has been a vigilante ever since, and that is how the Hellbreakers heard of him. The party looted fifteen feet of jagged pit for eternal eruptions and frozen lava, argued cheerfully about who got to carry the bomb (Zanthar won, and put it up his sleeve "so it's kind of protected from going boom on accident"), and asked the new man whether he could tell them where to plant charges. Leoz answered by drawing his pistol: ***"I've got a detonator."*** Panacea rolled a natural 20 on Society and picked the placements herself. They took a room at the White Stag, slept through the sound of boots marching past the window all night, and in the morning Jenverick pulled a spare Masquerade Scarf out of her back pocket for the one man at the table who wasn't wanted yet.

Then they blew a ten-foot hole in the wall of the Gyve, and the tone of the campaign changed inside of one round. A sniper on the guard tower opened with an arquebus crit for **36** into Bai Jian's chest before anyone had taken a turn. Zanthar — the gnome who introduced himself an hour earlier as "a do-gooder" — looked at the tower and said, *"I think this is a we-don't-care-if-they-die situation."* Leoz put a bullet clean through the sniper for **42**, twirled the pistol, told him *"you're guilty,"* and reloaded John Wick-style. Zanthar hero-pointed a Blazing Bolt down the same hole the bullet had made. Panacea killed both artillerists — one with a Moonlight Ray that went *"right over the dead guy,"* one with a hex she narrated as Serrafina's ray bouncing off his skull and taking his head off. Nobody on the tower survived, and nobody at the table hesitated. Leoz's read on the party's famous non-lethal doctrine, delivered at the break: *"Yeah, that whole non-lethal thing you guys apparently tried last time. I figured as much."*

Inside, it got worse before it got better. The basement was a torture chamber, and the two things working in it were an interrogator with a long slender razor and a bruiser with a greatclub. The interrogator hit Bai Jian with a **27-damage crit**, filled the room with an aura of blood and fear, and then hit him again for **25** — extra mental damage, because by then Bai Jian was frightened of it. Cyrathul's Draconic Frenzy finally put it down (*"boss, no!"* from the back of the room), and Bai Jian vaulted off a desk to open the bodyguard's chest while Ren tore at his leg. Chained to the wall were **Sarevial**, the League's own courier, sobbing that he had told them everything — and **Osney**, who looked up and said *"me say nothing. They can get nothing from me."* Upstairs, Zanthar picked lock after lock with the skeleton key, and the first door he opened released a stampede: a mass cell of prisoners so tightly packed they had nowhere to lie down, all of them trying to get past the party at once. It hit like a hazard, because it was one — a Reflex DC 25 crush that knocked **21 damage** into half the party. Panacea dropped a Calm into the middle of the mob, and Cyrathul found a use for a skill feat he'd evidently never had a use for (*"I can't think of another situation which could use it"*), roaring the corridor into silence. The party did not just take their own people. **They decided to free everyone in the building.**

The last fight was a corridor lined with arrow slits and nothing to hit. Ren pointed a paw at a blank wall — a gesture his handler couldn't actually see — Bai Jian burned a hero point on the dog's Perception and then found the half-moon of scratch marks himself anyway (*"I shouldn't have used the hero point"*), and a hidden door swung open onto three elite archer sentries and a hired mage. Cyrathul mirrored straight through an arrow slit to give them "a nice toothy dragon smile." Zanthar burst a man's head "like a watermelon" and announced *"I'm a killer machine."* Bai Jian crit-succeeded a Grease save on Panacea's Guidance and then one-shot the mage with a **40-damage crit**. And in the middle of it the argument the whole session had been building to finally happened out loud — Zanthar noting they were just killing everybody now, Bai Jian first defending the guards (*"they're just doing their job"*), Panacea refusing to let anyone pretend (*"they **knew** they were torturing people in the basement"*), and Bai Jian then rebutting himself (*"they're accomplices. Silence is compliance"*) — before circling back three minutes later, still unsatisfied: *"I don't think at this point we're just killing innocent guards."* The last guard standing dropped his sword, fell to his knees, and said he only makes two gold a week. **Judge Leoz flipped a coin for his life.** It came up in the guard's favour, and the man handed over his keyring and offered to lead them to the third floor, where the Hellbreakers are being held in high security. Bai Jian, watching him go: *"I guess we're the bad guys now."* Cyrathul: *"We're the bad guys."*

## Key Events

### Act I — The Night Judge *(session zero + the new member)*
- The GM ran a short **session zero** for the new player: lines and veils, fade-to-black on romance, and an explicit warning that this is a grim AP — Cheliax as Isger's mother country, slavery abolished in name and running as indentured servitude in fact.
- Each returning player described their character. **Panacea**: a catfolk witch-medic from a cult of catfolk who keep *human* familiars, who was playing in the jungle as an adolescent when she and Serrafina fell *into* a portal and came out the other side near Breachill — and who noted, still, that *"this is my first time ever playing a role-playing game."* **Bai Jian**: from Tian Xia, five years on the trail of a missing family member, joined the League voluntarily for a good cause and because it might help him find them — *"hasn't paid out so far, but it has been a journey."* **Zanthar**: *"a gnome that's particularly small,"* a wizard with healing, *"a do-gooder."* Cyrathul, absent, was described by the GM: a **Lunar Dragon** Thaumaturge who underwent the **Lux Aeterna** ritual to come down and help throw off Cheliax's chains, and who collects esoteric relics.
- **Panacea took the recap hero point**, delivering a tight, accurate account of Session 17 unprompted (*"I'll do it if nobody else wants to"*). House economy confirmed: one hero point at start, one more at the break, one for the recap.
- Outside the Aegis, Bai Jian's Perception caught **one** set of boots in the snow — Jenverick, disobeying orders to stay put. He turned his back to the building and warned the others. The **second** arrival made no sound at all.
- **Judge Leoz Balake** introduced himself: *"some in certain circles refer to him as the Night Judge."* A well-loved, high-integrity, tough-on-crime magistrate who never took a bribe — until he was hit in the face with acid, and turned to vigilantism. He was supposed to be at the meeting; he arrived late, which is the only reason he isn't in the Gyve with everyone else.
- Bai Jian tried to bench Jenverick (*"You, though. I don't know. I think you should stay back"*). She pulled the thinnest needle of a dagger anyone had ever seen out of her belt loop, looked scared and determined, and took a lookout post instead — blending into the corner of the building so well she nearly vanished.

### Act II — The Pit, the Plan, and the White Stag
- The munitions pit was ~15 feet deep and jagged. Contents: enough unused **frozen lava** and **eternal eruptions** to blow the prison wall, stage several diversionary explosions across town, *and* leave the party a couple spare. Everything went into Cyrathul's **spacious pouch**.
- Zanthar claimed the eternal eruption on the grounds that *"that sounds like fun"* and *"that looks like a fun toy to me,"* then stowed it in his sleeve *"so it's kind of protected from going boom on accident."* Bai Jian: *"Don't have too much fun, though."*
- **Leoz tied his first mechanical contribution to his backstory** — asking whether his knowledge of prisons, "from being who he is," could place the charges. He had Underworld Lore, not Warfare Lore, so the check went elsewhere. When Zanthar worried they had bombs but no way to set them off remotely, Leoz produced the answer: ***"I've got a detonator," he says as he pulls out his pistol.***
- **Panacea rolled a natural 20 on Society (32)** and personally chose where the diversionary charges went; Zanthar's Crafting (13) and Leoz's untrained Society (8) added nothing. The GM's narration gave her the credit outright — *"Panacea really seems like she knows what she's doing… almost like she's done this before."*
- Planning ran long and messy and Panacea kept dragging it back to the part nobody wanted to answer: *"And while we're planning this, are we also planning on what we're going to do with the people that we free?"* → *"Yeah, this is why we should be discussing it. We need a good plan."*
- **The boat is gone as an escape route.** Quoster will sail as soon as he's loaded. The replacement: the **military stables next to the Gyve**. Jenverick took the job of securing the horses, and after debate settled on clearing the stables *after* the wall goes up, since the blast will pull the stable guards toward the breach.
- **GM warning, delivered plainly**: *"Once we move to the next map, there'll probably be no long rest until you guys are done."* The party took the hint. Jenverick got them a room at the **White Stag** (she knows the owners; *"gold speaks more than anything,"* and the lockdown has the inn near-empty), reached under Zanthar's last **Shared Invisibility**.
- One cramped room, one bed, everyone on the floor. **Bai Jian took first watch** and **Zanthar second**; both were too distracted to notice anything, and both heard the same thing anyway — booted feet marching in formation up and down the street outside all night. No door got kicked in. The party took a long rest and re-prepped.
- In the morning **Jenverick handed Leoz a Masquerade Scarf** out of her back pocket — *"I know that you aren't actually wanted yet, but maybe you could use this as well"* — and put one on herself.
- Recon over breakfast under the scarves: **Panacea's Society (24)** turned up a **torture chamber in the basement**; **Bai Jian's Gather Information Diplomacy (26)** established that prisoners are held on the **second and third floors** unless they're out in the yard on forced labour. Jenverick added the tactical read: blow the wall and the stable guards come running.
- Bai Jian's standing answer to being a man in full plate with a falcata and a dog: **Follow the Expert**, routed through the party's sneaks. *"You look at Bai Jian, heavy armor, big ass sword, and a dog. Yeah, no. You need someone to guide him."* Leoz, introducing his own lane: *"I'm very, very sneaky… I have a lot of, like, shadow and illusion and tricky tricks."*

### Act III — The Wall Breach *(Encounter 1)*
- The party set charges against an outer wall — any of the four would do, per Panacea's earlier work — and blew a **10-foot section** open. Shouting from the ramparts: *"Wall breach! To arms!"* Then a loud crack of a firearm from the top of a tower. See Combat Encounters.
- **The courtyard was empty.** Normally it would be full of prisoners forced to make arms for the army; there was no labour detail running. Panacea shouted the League's recognition phrase into it anyway — *"If you are friends of the rose, come over here to the prisoners"* — and nobody heard her.
- **Doctrine flipped in one line.** Zanthar, looking up at the tower: *"I think this is a we-don't-care-if-they-die situation."* Nobody argued. Panacea, a round later: *"apparently we're going all the way with these guys, huh?… We are going to kill them."* Leoz: *"That's the only way that Leoz starts, unfortunately."*
- Post-fight, the party stripped the tower: two dueling pistols, an arquebus, **four silencers**, two siege-weaponry artisan's toolkits, repair toolkits, and forty-odd rounds. **Leoz claimed the silencers and a spare dueling pistol.** Cyrathul, reading the item text aloud: *"Four completely silenced shots."*
- **Snowgeen arrived at the break** and took Cyrathul back from the GM. Caught up on the situation, his first line was *"seems to be working pretty well."* Leoz's welcome to the campaign's ethics: *"Yeah, that whole non-lethal thing you guys apparently tried last time. I figured as much."*

### Act IV — The Torture Chamber *(Encounter 2)*
- Down the stairs into a round subterranean chamber: black stone, dripping water amplified into echoes, rusty shackles on the ceiling and walls, an iron smell of blood under a grave-scent of earth, a rack under a pulley system, and cruel pokers and blades hung on the back wall.
- **Leoz, under his breath, before anyone rolled anything: *"This is injustice."***
- Bai Jian's boots gave the party away. Two figures turned: one sharpening a long slender razor, one with a nasty gauntlet and a huge club. And from the wall, a voice — *"no, no more."*
- Bai Jian's answer to *"coming to join the party?"*: ***"Yeah, no, we're here to disrupt the party. You gotta stop this madness."***
- Leoz crit-failed initiative and went last, and made a joke of it in character: *"He's slow. He's new. He's got to get with the rhythm."*
- The prisoners on the wall were **Sarevial**, the Hellbreaker courier who has hosted the party at two different safehouses, and **Osney**, the goblin burglar who taught them how to stage a robbery. Sarevial fell to his knees sobbing when Cyrathul unlocked his second hand — *"I couldn't help myself… I was telling them everything. He was so wicked."* Osney had given up nothing: *"me say nothing. They can get nothing from me."*
- **Bai Jian: *"Yeah, we won't abandon our comrades."*** Sarevial confirmed the S17 raid hit within a minute of the party leaving the safehouse, that everyone was there, and that Jenverick went out a window with a Hellknight after her. The rest are **upstairs**, brought down to the basement one at a time.
- Panacea, on whether Sarevial needed healing: *"They look perfectly fine. His brain's just broken, I guess, is the problem."* Cyrathul: *"Yes, it'll be all right. We have a handle on things now. You can come with us."*
- **Leoz**, as the party turned for the stairs: ***"No time to waste. Justice waits for no one."*** **Bai Jian**, approving it out loud: *"I like the sound of that."* The Night Judge set the tone and the fighter signed off on it.

### Act V — The Prison Break *(Encounter 3 — hazard)*
- Zanthar took the **Skeleton Key** (which doubles as a bonused thieves' toolkit — a fact Bai Jian had to point out to him after failing to Force Open the door at DC 27) and picked the first cell lock in two successes.
- The door opened on a mass cell — stale sweat, mounds of straw for beds, people packed shoulder to shoulder with nowhere to lie down — and every one of them tried to push out past the party at once. **It was a hazard, not a fight.** Bai Jian, deadpan on seeing initiative rolled: *"Oh yeah, it's a hazard. Of course."*
- The crowd's rush ran as one improvised **d20+19 Athletics** disarm against everyone in the hall (it came up 23 and beat four Reflex DCs, but **nobody was actually disarmed** — Bai Jian corrected the rules read mid-resolution), then a **basic Reflex DC 25** for **21 damage**. Bai Jian, Zanthar and Cyrathul all failed; Leoz, Panacea and Ren saved. **Only Panacea and Cyrathul ever got a turn** — the whole thing resolved in one round.
- **Panacea cast Calm into the middle of the mob** — not to stop them, but *"so they can get out orderly like"* — and scored the first success toward disabling it.
- **Cyrathul closed it out with No Cause for Alarm**, a skill feat he had evidently been carrying without a use for it (*"I can't think of another situation which could use it"*), hero-pointed for safety. He roared the corridor down; the mob froze *"like deer caught in headlights,"* then began politely asking to squeeze past. *"Excuse me, can I squeeze through right here?"*
- **The party banked an Uprising point** for handling it.
- **The scope decision.** These weren't Hellbreakers — the party recognised nobody. Zanthar: *"some criminals maybe."* **Cyrathul: *"Yeah, but it's what the current Chelaxian regime views as criminals, so I don't know if I trust their judgment on this matter."*** He turned to the second cell and told them outright: ***"Yes, we're freeing everyone in this prison. So if you want to get out of here, now's your chance."*** Panacea gave them the route and one instruction — *"there's a hole downstairs in the wall… but don't go all the way downstairs into the torture chamber."*
- A dozen prisoners of various ages and ancestries pointed the way: **the Hellbreakers are in high security on the third floor.** Panacea, trying and failing to list her own recruits by name: *"Have you guys seen a little dwarf veteran and a… oh gosh, I can't remember all of the people. The little gnomes that were locked up as Hellbreakers?"*
- Rooms cleared on the way: a tool room (Bai Jian took a **crowbar**), and an **evidence locker** of confiscated prisoner property — **Leoz: *"This seems to be the evidence locker. Evidence of injustice."*** Cyrathul talked the party into looting it anyway: *"it seems none of our escapees got their stuff back, so I think it's fair game… it's just a lost and found. It's been there too long."* Bai Jian took **bracers granting a 20-foot climb speed**.

### Act VI — The Arrow-Slit Corridor *(Encounter 4)*
- A winding corridor hung with manacles, its entire inner wall lined with **arrow slits** — bows firing from a room the party could not reach or even see into. Zanthar, on the shooters' terrible accuracy: *"That seems like a personal problem."*
- **Finding the way in took three of them.** **Leoz** worked the geometry first (*"I wonder if we go upstairs and then go back downstairs into it… could be around the other side"*). **Bai Jian** sent **Ren** forward on scent; the hound rolled a natural 1, Bai Jian **spent a hero point** on the reroll (10 → +10 house rule → **32**), and Ren held out a paw at a blank stretch of wall — a gesture **Bai Jian couldn't see** (*"you just hear him barking"*). Bai Jian then made the same check himself on a plain **26** and spotted a **half-moon of scratch marks** where a hidden door swings — *"I shouldn't have used the hero point."*
- He forced the hidden door open onto three **elite archer sentries** and a **mage for hire**. Cyrathul **mirrored through an arrow slit** to flank, *"I'll give like a nice toothy dragon smile,"* passed on Exploit Vulnerability (*"the odds of them having a mortal weakness is zero"* — the GM confirmed they were all human), and opened with a crit.
- **The argument.** It had a prologue: four minutes earlier Bai Jian had already conceded, *"I think at this point we should just accept it. Just kill people."* Then Zanthar: *"Guess we're just killing everybody."* Panacea: *"These aren't good people."* **Bai Jian argued the other way anyway** — *"I mean, they're prison guards. They're just doing their job. Come on, guys."* Panacea shut that down: *"No. They're torturing people in the basement. **They knew** they were torturing people in the basement. Don't act like they didn't."* And Bai Jian rebutted **himself**: *"They're working for the oppressors… they're accomplices. **Silence is compliance.**"* Three minutes later, still chewing on it: *"I don't think at this point we're just killing innocent guards."*
- **Two sentries died; the third dropped his shortsword**, fell to his knees, and surrendered: *"I only make two gold a week."* Bai Jian, immediately: *"Now I feel that."*
- **Judge Leoz flipped a coin for him.** The table made him actually roll it. It came up in the guard's favour. Bai Jian: *"Well, it's your lucky day. You get to join your friends."*
- Panacea took the surrender and turned it into an asset — *"I want you to lead the way, please, to show us the way to get there"* — and Bai Jian took his **ring of keys**. The stairs to the third floor are just outside, visible through the arrow slits.
- The room behind the guards is an **on-duty armoury**: any common weapon, free for the taking.
- Zanthar stripped the dead mage and finally got what he'd been complaining about needing all night: a **thieves' toolkit and a seven-spell spellbook**.
- Session ends with the party holding the keys, a guide, and a building they have emptied — one floor below the people they came for.

## Character Spotlight

### Judge Leoz Balake · the Night Judge
- **The debut landed.** Not "a gunslinger joins the party" but a fully-formed person: a well-loved, high-integrity magistrate, tough on crime, who never took a bribe — until somebody threw **acid in his face**. He's been a vigilante since, and that's how the League found him. He was supposed to be at the safehouse meeting; **arriving late is the only reason he isn't in the Gyve with everyone else.**
- **He speaks about himself in third person and ties his mechanics to his fiction.** His first act was asking whether Leoz's knowledge of prisons, *"from being who he is,"* could place the charges. His second was the best line of the night: ***"I've got a detonator," he says as he pulls out his pistol.***
- **He hits like a truck.** First round of play, ever: a **critical hit for 42** through the tower sniper — 2d10 + Slinger's Precision + fatal d10 — narrated as the bullet going in one side and out the other. He closed the fight with a full **Raconteur's Reload** flourish: the pistol twirl, the steely glare, *"you're guilty,"* and a John Wick reload. The Demoralize itself failed. Nobody cared.
- **He is the party's conscience in a way none of the others are.** In the torture chamber, before a single die was rolled, he muttered *"this is injustice."* In the evidence locker: *"This seems to be the evidence locker. **Evidence of injustice.**"* And turning the party toward the stairs: ***"No time to waste. Justice waits for no one."*** The Night Judge names what he's looking at — and Bai Jian, hearing that last one, said *"I like the sound of that."* One session in, the new man is supplying the party's watchwords.
- **And he owns the moral weight of the ending.** When the last guard surrendered and said he only makes two gold a week, Leoz didn't argue for mercy or execution — **he flipped a coin.** The table made him roll it for real. It came up in the guard's favour. That is a *judge* who has stopped believing in his own judgment, and it is a hell of a thing to establish in one session.
- **He is already contributing tactically, not just atmospherically.** He offered to take point with a **silenced shot** loaded; he flagged that a barracks room could be locked from outside before the party walked past it; he was the first to work the geometry of the arrow-slit corridor (*"I wonder if we go upstairs and then go back downstairs into it… could be around the other side"*) before Bai Jian sent the dog; and he checked the penalty for shooting into melee rather than assuming it.
- Also, gamely, a new player learning Foundry in public — targeting, macros, the party stash, "do I just drag and drop it?" — and cracking jokes about it. On critically failing initiative: *"He's slow. He's new. He's got to get with the rhythm."* On the scatter-shot feat he decided against: *"your party members maybe don't like you as much."*

### Panacea · the One Who Asks the Hard Question
- **She ran the session's planning and she ran its conscience.** While everyone else was arguing about who got to hold the bomb, Panacea was the one who said: ***"And while we're planning this, are we also planning on what we're going to do with the people that we free?"*** — and when the table drifted again, *"Yeah, this is why we should be discussing it. We need a good plan."*
- **She took the recap hero point unprompted** (*"I'll do it if nobody else wants to"*) and delivered a genuinely accurate three-minute account of Session 17 from memory — the staged robbery, the pickle barrels, Jenverick arriving bloodied, the mass arrest.
- **Natural 20 on Society** to place the diversionary charges across the city, and the GM handed her the credit in narration: *"Panacea really seems like she knows what she's doing… almost like she's done this before."* Her Society check also turned up the torture chamber before they ever went down there.
- **She is still, at Session 18, the table's first-time roleplayer** — she says so out loud at session zero — and she is running the party's logistics, its healing, and its ethics.
- **The moral spine of the ending is hers.** When Bai Jian started making the case that the prison guards were just doing their job, Panacea didn't hedge: ***"No. They're torturing people in the basement. They knew they were torturing people in the basement. Don't act like they didn't."*** She had just come up from that basement.
- **And she is kind in the small places.** She shouted Milani's recognition phrase — *"if you are friends of the rose"* — into a courtyard that turned out to be empty, on the off-chance someone was listening. She checked whether the freed torture victim needed healing. She told the released prisoners the way out *and* warned them off the basement so they wouldn't walk into the torture chamber. She asked the surrendering guard to lead them, with a *"please."* And when she couldn't remember her own recruits' names — *"a little dwarf veteran and a… oh gosh, I can't remember all of the people"* — it played as a woman rattled by what she'd just seen, hunting for people she'd personally signed up.
- Two kills in the wall breach, both narrated with relish: a Moonlight Ray that went *"right over the dead guy,"* and Serrafina's ray bouncing off a skull and beheading its owner.

### Zanthar Melodune III · the Do-Gooder Who Kept Score
- **He has the session's clearest arc, and it is not a flattering one — it's a great one.** At session zero he described himself as *"a gnome that's particularly small… he's just looking for, trying to be better, trying to do good things for the world. **He's a do-gooder.**"*
- Then he looked up at the guard tower and said ***"I think this is a we-don't-care-if-they-die situation"*** — and that one sentence became the party's rules of engagement for the whole night, replacing six sessions of scrupulous non-lethal doctrine. Nobody voted. Nobody argued.
- Then he **hero-pointed a Blazing Bolt** to finish the sniper, sending it down the hole Leoz's bullet had already made, *"Robin Hood style."*
- Then, two acts later in the torture chamber, he swung his maul at the interrogator **deliberately non-lethally** and was disappointed it wasn't enough: *"I was hoping I'd knock him out."* The do-gooder is still in there.
- Then, walking into the last corridor, ***"We're gonna kill them all!"*** — the peak of it. Then he burst a sentry's head *"like a watermelon"* on a second consecutive critical Blazing Bolt and announced ***"That's it. I'm a killer machine."***
- And then, minutes later, he was the one who said it out loud: *"Guess we're just killing everybody."* He opened the door on this and he's the first to notice where it went.
- **He was also the party's key that opened the prison.** Zanthar picked lock after lock with the Skeleton Key all night — the first cell in two successes, later ones fighting him (*"this lock seems to be tricky"*) — while wryly noting the obvious gap in his kit: *"I really need to get a thief's toolkit, because I've got the skill, but not the…"* **The session's last act paid it off**: he stripped the dead Mage for Hire and walked away with a **thieves' toolkit and a seven-spell spellbook**, the two things he'd complained about needing all night.
- Comedy throughout: claiming the biggest bomb in the pit because *"that sounds like fun,"* stowing it **in his sleeve** *"so it's kind of protected from going boom on accident"*; refusing to be first through any door (*"I'm kind of a easy… I go down real quick"*); and when Foundry disconnected him mid-turn and his token ran back upstairs, ***"I'm so embarrassed I ran away."***

### Cyrathul · the Dragon Who Set the Terms
- **Half his session was played by the GM** — Snowgeen arrived at the break — and the GM's version of him still charged a guard tower alone, because Bai Jian vouched for it: *"He's brave. He runs in all the time."* Mirror Cloak straight to the top of the tower, jaws, critical hit, 30. His first words on taking the character back: *"Seems to be working pretty well."*
- **He supplied the principle that made the prison break stick.** Freeing prisoners for cover was already in Jenverick's plan from S17 — Zanthar's addition to it. But the plan assumed *Hellbreakers*. When the second cell turned out to hold a dozen strangers nobody recognised and Zanthar shrugged that they were *"some criminals maybe,"* Cyrathul answered the actual question: ***"Yeah, but it's what the current Chelaxian regime views as criminals, so I don't know if I trust their judgment on this matter."*** Then, to the cell: ***"Yes, we're freeing everyone in this prison. So if you want to get out of here, now's your chance."*** A tactical diversion became a moral position, and it was his.
