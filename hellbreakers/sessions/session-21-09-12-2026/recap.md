## Session Summary

The southern arc opened with the GM reading out three new subsystems at once, and saying so: *"nothing like throwing three new game mechanics in all at the same time, but that's where we are."* **Assist a Settlement** (a day's work fortifying a town), **Recruit for the Cause** (an hour's pitch for a troop), and **Locate the Curse** (a day's investigation toward a 10-point triangulation of the undead source). The table spent the first twenty minutes pinning down how they worked — Bai Jian pressing the per-person-or-per-party and per-day-or-per-month details — and the party rode south along the frozen river toward Wolf Point with Zanthar's Arcana already scoring the first point.

They smelled it before they saw it. A **Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger** — a Hellknight's armor with only bones inside it, knees knocking together, dragging one foot — shook a bone out of its own ribcage and grew a **Skeletal Giant** from it. It was a fight built against the party's front line: both skeletons **resisted slashing and piercing**, which took the teeth out of Bai Jian's falcata and Ren's bite at once. (Cyrathul's jaws bit through at full value — Exploit Vulnerability's weakness damage cancelled the resistance.) Bai Jian smashed the giant with a flanking Snagging Strike crit, and his next hit on the Bonesinger rolled 14 and did 4. What Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability found instead was **weakness 10 to vitality** — *"which I will yell out to everyone"* — and Panacea had prepared exactly the spell for it. Her **Vitality Lash** caught a natural 1 on the save for **52 damage** (*"half its ribcage just turns bright white and disappears from existence"*), and her second one, cast right after she Battle-Medicined Bai Jian off 13 hit points, finished it. In between, the Bonesinger crit Bai Jian on a natural 20 and a 19 — and with the giant's glaive and the grasping skeletons, **Bai Jian took 100 of the party's 122 damage in four rounds**. His read afterward: *"I can't do anything. I need to find my solution."*

The rest of the session was the new subsystems, and the party ran them well. At **Wolf Point**, Runa Bloodhammer's Steel Circle met them with crossbows on the rooftops and then, on Cyrathul's pitch and diplomat's badge, agreed to send fighters to Saringallow; Bai Jian's Athletics crit rebuilt their wall. At **Fernsby**, Lily reported the undead getting *"bigger and badder"* and trade routes avoiding the far south; Cyrathul's Diplomacy logged a second curse point, Panacea disguised the village's children as Chelaxian orphans in Asmodean robes to smuggle them north, and a recruitment roll brought in **war saboteurs from Stumpfield**.

**Thorngrip** was the centerpiece. Bracknap had convened the Council of Elders under the sequoia, and it ran as an influence encounter. Panacea's discovery found the council's weakness — **the elders love rhymes** — and she delivered three original limericks, each one dropping every DC for the round (*"Our cause is good. Please join our hood"*). Cyrathul worked Goblin and Theater Lore through Diverse Lore until Bracknap noticed he knew *"much about goblins. It is almost like you have been around longer than you appear to be."* Zanthar closed with Diplomacy three times. The goblins joined the Hellbreakers on one condition — **autonomy for the Chitterwood** — sent **goblin rebels and a Hobgoblin Battalion**, and Bracknap drew **Bounty's Light**, a living relic staff, down out of the sequoia's branches and gave it to Zanthar. Then Bracknap joined the party himself.

That afternoon Gug took Bai Jian's falcata for four hours to add a **vitalizing rune** — stolen off a Hellknight outside town. And that evening, Bracknap's eyes blazed brighter than they ever have and fixed on Bai Jian: *"There is something or someone you are looking for. I believe at the end of this path, the catalyst may be that thing."* The next morning, with the triangulation one point short, Bai Jian read the forest's animals on a Nature crit and remembered the name: **Gillimore**, an abandoned human town at the far southern edge of the Chitterwood that every trader avoids. The curse is located. Rhokrag owes them a hundred gold a head.

## Key Events

### Act I — Three Subsystems and a Frozen River
- The GM opens the southern arc with **Assist a Settlement**, **Recruit for the Cause** and **Locate the Curse**, plus the Hellbreakers' top-tier reputation giving **+3 to recruitment**. Panacea immediately names the stakes: *"we want to do that because we have a bet going that's pretty high dollar."*
- **The table works out the rules together**; Bai Jian presses the details — whether each activity is per person or per party, and whether a settlement's once-a-month assist is per PC. The table's ruling: each PC picks Assist *or* Locate once per day; everyone can Recruit; a successful assist gives the whole party the recruitment bonus.
- **Zanthar scores the first Locate the Curse point** on Arcana (31). Panacea's Arcana fails (18) and she declines to hero-point it.
- The route: Saringallow → Wolf Point (24 miles) → Fernsby → the Chitterwood, pacing the horses to avoid exhaustion.
- **The Forge goes down.** The GM's Foundry freezes repeatedly, he cancels the paid session listing on the spot so nobody gets charged, and moves to Edge. A player recommends the dedicated Foundry client; Bai Jian confirms he runs it.
- On the road, a Perception check: they smell undeath before they see it. Bai Jian: *"I think we saw the vampire before."*

### Act II — The Bonesinger *(Encounter — Moderate, 80 XP)*
- A skeletal Hellknight in hollow plate steps into the road. The party dismounts. Cyrathul remembers he should have Tailwind up from morning prep and the GM lets him add it.
- **Round 1.** The Bonesinger rattles a bone out of its ribcage and summons a **Skeletal Giant**. Panacea's Divine Lance hits it for 14 (*"Oh my god, I am rolling so bad today. Oh, it hit though"*). Bai Jian **Sudden Charges** the giant — 16 damage, 11 through its resistance — and raises Dueling Parry. Cyrathul crits Exploit Vulnerability on the giant (resists cold, electricity, fire, piercing, slashing; no weakness) and bites on a natural 1. Panacea: *"You didn't want the taste of a dead in your mouth anyway."* Zanthar triple-moves.
- **Round 2.** The giant's Broad Swipe hits Bai Jian for 9 and misses Cyrathul; the Bonesinger's **Rouse Skeletons** erupts under Zanthar (5). Panacea lances the giant again for 12. **Bai Jian's first Snagging Strike is a natural 1; his second crits from the flank for 37 and smashes the giant into a pile of bones.** Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability on the Bonesinger finds **weakness 10 to vitality** — he announces it — and bites for 20 through its off-guard. Zanthar casts **Slow**; the Bonesinger saves, Slowed 1 for a round.
- **Round 3.** The Bonesinger's Rouse Skeletons clips Bai Jian and Cyrathul (3 each) and catches itself, harmlessly. Bai Jian catches the Slow's lost action for the GM (*"It's slow."* — *"Oh, it's slow. You're right. Thank you."*). Then a **natural 20 warhammer crit on Bai Jian for 32**: *"Well, better me than anyone else, so."* **Panacea's Vitality Lash — the Bonesinger rolls a natural 1 — 52 damage.** Bai Jian commands Ren for the support benefit, pulls a **scroll of Sure Strike** off his Retrieval Prism, casts it and Snagging-Strikes for 14 — **which the slashing resistance cuts to 4**; Ren's bonus bite is resisted entirely. Cyrathul's Frenzy lands a jaws hit for 23, two claws miss on a 1 and a 4, and he spends his last action on a Demoralize he's *"like 90% sure won't work… I'll give him a boo"* — it works (Frightened 1). Zanthar's six-shard **Force Barrage** rolls five 1s and still does 14, with Bai Jian correcting the dart count mid-roll.
- **Round 4.** Rouse Skeletons again — Cyrathul (7) and Ren (7) fail, Bai Jian saves (3). A second **warhammer crit on Bai Jian for 44** (*"Well, I'm still up"*), then **Invoke Reckoning** — he fails the Fortitude save, *"I might be done. Have fun,"* and it racks his bones for 9 more. **Bai Jian is at 13 of 113.**
- **Panacea ends it.** Battle Medicine on Bai Jian for 24, then a second Vitality Lash for 29: *"After I bandage up my comrade, I just look glaringly at him and wiggle my fingers… and unleash the vitality, and it wraps around the rest of whatever bones he has and explodes them."* The GM: *"You guys are all showered in some disgusting former Hellknight bones."*
- Aftermath. Someone deadpans *"That was the source of the curse. We did it."* Bai Jian: *"I took a beating there… I can't do anything. I need to find my solution."* The party heals to full in a few ten-minute rests. **Loot: Hellknight plate and a +1 striking warhammer.**

### Act III — Wolf Point
- Around 1 p.m. the party climbs the hill. A crossbow on the rooftop — *"Stop where you are"* — and an answering whistle brings two more.
- **Cyrathul opens**: *"We're here to recruit for the cause… We're in need of fighters."* The lookout recognizes them as the ones who cleared the place. **Runa Bloodhammer** comes round the corner with two guards, defensive: *"We've only been attacking those that are flying the Chelaxian flag."*
- **Cyrathul**: *"We're not interested in cracking down on you. We'd rather recruit you for a more important cause."* Runa has seen **a battalion of Hellknights headed south that never came back.**
- Cyrathul primes his **diplomat's badge** for Runa's people with a Diverse Lore check (30), then rolls Diplomacy — **29 against DC 28**, the badge the margin. Runa conferred with her lieutenant: *"You did us right by clearing this place out and leaving us alone when we kept our word."* **The Steel Circle sends fighters to Saringallow.**
- **Assist a Settlement**: Runa's people are trying to re-stand a collapsed wall. **Bai Jian Guidances himself and crits Athletics (36)** — *"with especially Bai Jian in the front there,"* the wall is set as strong as the day it was built. Runa's thanks come with **messenger missives** and a **+2 circumstance to recruitment**.

### Act IV — Fernsby
- Dusk, tired horses. **Lily** is sitting in the rebuilt village green. *"It's the Hellbreakers."* The undead started as a few zombies and have been *"getting bigger and badder."*
- Panacea asks where it's coming from, and Bai Jian presses when they come. Lily: rumours of a vengeful ghost from the old children's tales who would *"turn us all into the undead"* — *"that's just children's tales, right?"* — except someone killed a zombie just last week. **Trade routes avoid certain places farther south.**
- **Cyrathul's Diplomacy with Lily (26) is the second curse point** — the undead are coming up from the south.
- Lily feeds them stew and hard biscuits and lends them an empty cottage.
- **Morning.** Lily asks for help getting children out before the undead — and passing Hellknights — close in. **Panacea disguises the children as Chelaxian orphans in the robes of Asmodeus's disciples** on Religion, hero-pointing a 4 into a 25 against DC 25. Zanthar's Arcana (28) is the **third curse point** — south, slightly west.
- **Cyrathul recruits (33, with Bai Jian's Guidance)**. Fernsby has no one to send, so they send word to **Stumpfield**, now at truce with Fernsby: **war saboteurs** join the cause.
- Break.

### Act V — The Council of Elders at Thorngrip
- On the ride in, Bai Jian takes the **+1 striking warhammer** from the stash — *"I feel like I might become useless if I don't switch out my weapon"* — while noting it's a −2 outside his weapon group and none of his feats work with it. He heads for Thorngrip's blacksmith instead.
- Five hours into the Chitterwood to the thorn wall. The goblins recognize the Longshanks. Bracknap is kneeling under the sequoia and turns before they arrive: *"You come in difficult times, yes? To make some difficult requests?"* The town proper hasn't had the undead problem, but he knows of it — *"this took place quite often… 10 generations ago. I wonder why it stopped."* He has already called the **Council of Elders**.
- Panacea passes Alceo's check-in; Bracknap: *"You can tell your cleric that we are safe inside this town."*
- The council introduces itself: the **master of communal mud**, **Two**, **Three**, **Grandma Neemi** (Bracknap's betrothed), **Four** (who also gives the name Wind), and **Five**. One group influence target, six ten-minute rounds.
- **Round 1 — discovery.** **Bai Jian** reads them: the lowest influence skill is **Goblin or Chitterwood Lore**. **Cyrathul** — *"Diverse Lore for the win"* — lands the first influence point on it; Five states the terms: *"We want the Chitterwood to rule itself autonomously… part of a greater Isger, but outside of your jurisdiction."* **Panacea's** Society discovery finds the weakness: **the elders love rhymes**, and any real limerick lowers every DC by 2 for the round. **Zanthar's** Society finds the second skill: **Diplomacy**.
- **Round 2.** Bai Jian's second discovery turns up nothing. **Cyrathul switches to Theater Lore** — *"I'll spice it up"* — and succeeds; the council proposes a **reconciliation committee** and a **holiday of reconciliation**. **Panacea's first limerick**: *"We come to ask for help from our lips, help from the goblins, from Thorngrip. Your hospitality has been great, but the undead has added such weight. Our cause is good. Please join our hood."* The table applauds; the council: *"Your tongue is as pretty as your fur."* She curtsies and twirls. **Zanthar's Diplomacy** lands exactly on the DC with the limerick bonus. Three asks that Isger help **restore the woods burned in the Goblinblood War**.
- **Round 3.** Bai Jian — who had told the others *"You guys got this"* after his own round-2 discovery came up empty — Guidances himself and rolls Diplomacy: a natural 1, hero-pointed into a 13, still short: *"I'm only trained. I'm not great."* The elders insist on the restoration and add that the holiday will involve **an exchange of Bounty's Light**. **Cyrathul's Goblin Lore crits** — two influence points — and Bracknap lifts a finger: *"You know much about goblins. It is almost like you have been around longer than you appear to be."* **Two goblin rebels** join, +2 reputation. **Panacea's second limerick** (*"The undead are knocking on your door. Please listen to our deep implore…"*) sets Two dancing and clapping. Bracknap, warming: *"I think of a beautiful friendship once again blossoming between the humans and the goblins. Even though I think only one of you are human."* **Zanthar hero-points a 3 into a 32** on Diplomacy — a **Hobgoblin Battalion** joins, +2 reputation. Bracknap to Zanthar: *"You really seem to know what would be best for Thorngrip."*
- **Round 4.** Bai Jian stops trying to talk and turns to support: **Guidance on Cyrathul** and an **Aid on Zanthar's Diplomacy that crits on a natural 20** — though the bonus never made it onto Zanthar's roll. Bai Jian works out that the limerick's DC reduction stacks with Aid — *"So that is plus four, basically."* **Cyrathul's Lore succeeds again**; one of Bracknap's ancestor-spirits seems to look at him *"to try to see who you really are, who you really were,"* and Bracknap: *"I would be curious to speak with you at some point."* **Panacea's third limerick**: *"Join us. We are Sisters of Rose. With your help, our ranks will only grow. Fight the evil that is Cheliax. For this fight, it's time to fight. It's not time to relax."* **Zanthar closes** on Diplomacy — the +4 forgotten on the roll, *"It will not be a crit, but tell me the last thing you're going to say"* — with a final pitch about bringing all the goblins in.
- **The council stands as one.** Bracknap: *"We have no further demands. We only insist on our autonomy… We will join your cause with all of our might."* He reaches into the sequoia; a branch bends into his hand and releases a staff of summer flowers radiating light — **Bounty's Light**: *"It will help you, and it will help us defeat what is coming next."*
- Panacea offers it to Bai Jian as a joke; Bai Jian: *"If you guys gave it to me, I would just use it to bomb people."* Cyrathul could Trick Magic Item it but wouldn't use it; Zanthar points out no one else can charge the staff, the GM and Panacea agree it's his, and he takes it: *"Better than the last unique weapon we got that no one could use."* **80 XP.**

### Act VI — Gug's Forge and Bracknap's Vision
- **Gug**: *"That sword I see still on hip."* Bai Jian asks for something against undead. *"You in luck. Me stole from Hellknight outside town. Have one."* A **vitalizing rune** goes on the falcata over four hours; the **crushing rune** comes out and goes back to Bai Jian for later.

I need to go reflect on myself… I can't be this useless. I'm going to use a hero point"* — rerolls another 4, saved by the +10 house rule, then casts Guidance on him. **Cyrathul's Diplomacy: a natural 20, 43.** Bracknap: the humans had a rumour of *"a witch that lived in this part. I believe at the end of this path, the catalyst may be that thing."* The light dims; Bracknap seems not to know what he just said. Bai Jian, after a silence: *"All I can say is I hope what you said was true."*
- Night in the cottage, with Gug and his helpers hammering armor for the goblin armies until dawn — *"the gears of war have started here in the heart of the Chitterwood."* Bai Jian buys **doubling rings** before they leave. Zanthar and the GM set up Bounty's Light's spellcasting in Foundry.
