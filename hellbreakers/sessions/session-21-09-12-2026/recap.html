Session Summary

The southern arc opened with the GM reading out three new subsystems at once, and saying so: "nothing like throwing three new game mechanics in all at the same time, but that's where we are." Assist a Settlement (a day's work fortifying a town), Recruit for the Cause (an hour's pitch for a troop), and Locate the Curse (a day's investigation toward a 10-point triangulation of the undead source). The table spent the first twenty minutes pinning down how they worked — Bai Jian pressing the per-person-or-per-party and per-day-or-per-month details — and the party rode south along the frozen river toward Wolf Point with Zanthar's Arcana already scoring the first point.

They smelled it before they saw it. A Skeletal Hellknight Bonesinger — a Hellknight's armor with only bones inside it, knees knocking together, dragging one foot — shook a bone out of its own ribcage and grew a Skeletal Giant from it. It was a fight built against the party's front line: both skeletons resisted slashing and piercing, which took the teeth out of Bai Jian's falcata and Ren's bite at once. (Cyrathul's jaws bit through at full value — Exploit Vulnerability's weakness damage cancelled the resistance.) Bai Jian smashed the giant with a flanking Snagging Strike crit, and his next hit on the Bonesinger rolled 14 and did 4. What Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability found instead was weakness 10 to vitality — "which I will yell out to everyone" — and Panacea had prepared exactly the spell for it. Her Vitality Lash caught a natural 1 on the save for 52 damage, and her second one, cast right after she Battle-Medicined Bai Jian off 13 hit points, finished it. In between, the Bonesinger crit Bai Jian on a natural 20 and a 19 — and with the giant's glaive and the grasping skeletons, Bai Jian took 100 of the party's 122 damage in four rounds. His read afterward: "I can't do anything. I need to find my solution."

The rest of the session was the new subsystems, and the party ran them well. At Wolf Point, Runa Bloodhammer's Steel Circle met them with crossbows on the rooftops and then, on Cyrathul's pitch and diplomat's badge, agreed to send fighters to Saringallow; Bai Jian's Athletics crit rebuilt their wall. At Fernsby, Lily reported the undead getting "bigger and badder" and trade routes avoiding the far south; Cyrathul's Diplomacy logged a second curse point, Panacea disguised the village's children as Chelaxian orphans in Asmodean robes to smuggle them north, and a recruitment roll brought in war saboteurs from Stumpfield.

Thorngrip was the centerpiece. Bracknap had convened the Council of Elders under the sequoia, and it ran as an influence encounter. Panacea's discovery found the council's weakness — the elders love rhymes — and she delivered three original limericks, each one dropping every DC for the round ("Our cause is good. Please join our hood"). Cyrathul worked Goblin and Theater Lore through Diverse Lore until Bracknap noticed he knew "much about goblins. It is almost like you have been around longer than you appear to be." Zanthar closed with Diplomacy three times. The goblins joined the Hellbreakers on one condition — autonomy for the Chitterwood — sent goblin rebels and a Hobgoblin Battalion, and Bracknap drew Bounty's Light, a living relic staff, down out of the sequoia's branches and gave it to Zanthar. Then Bracknap joined the party himself.

That afternoon Gug took Bai Jian's falcata for four hours to add a vitalizing rune — stolen off a Hellknight outside town. And that evening, Bracknap's eyes blazed brighter than they ever have and fixed on Bai Jian: "There is something or someone you are looking for. I believe at the end of this path, the catalyst may be that thing." The next morning, with the triangulation one point short, Bai Jian read the forest's animals on a Nature crit and remembered the name: Gillimore, an abandoned human town at the far southern edge of the Chitterwood that every trader avoids. The curse is located. Rhokrag owes them a hundred gold a head.

The Road South, Act by Act

Act I · Travel · Three Subsystems and a Frozen River

Nothing like throwing three new game mechanics in at once

  • The GM opens the southern arc with Assist a Settlement, Recruit for the Cause and Locate the Curse, plus the Hellbreakers' top-tier reputation giving +3 to recruitment. Panacea names the stakes: "we have a bet going that's pretty high dollar."
  • The table works out the rules together; Bai Jian presses the details — per person or per party, and whether a settlement's once-a-month assist is per PC. Ruling: each PC picks Assist or Locate once per day; everyone can Recruit; a successful assist gives the whole party the bonus.
  • Zanthar scores the first Locate the Curse point on Arcana (31). Panacea's Arcana fails (18) and she declines to hero-point it.
  • The route: Saringallow → Wolf Point (24 miles) → Fernsby → the Chitterwood, pacing the horses to avoid exhaustion.
  • The Forge goes down. The GM's Foundry freezes repeatedly; he cancels the paid session listing on the spot so nobody gets charged, and moves to Edge. A player recommends the dedicated Foundry client; Bai Jian confirms he runs it.
  • On the road, they smell undeath before they see it. Bai Jian: "I think we saw the vampire before."
Act II · Combat · The Bonesinger

A skeleton that resisted almost everything

  • A skeletal Hellknight in hollow plate steps into the road, rattles a bone out of its ribcage, and grows a Skeletal Giant from it. The party dismounts.
  • Bai Jian Sudden Charges the giant — 16 rolled, 11 through its resistance. Cyrathul bites it on a natural 1. Panacea: "You didn't want the taste of a dead in your mouth anyway."
  • Bai Jian's first Snagging Strike is a natural 1; his second crits from the flank for 37 and smashes the giant into a pile of bones.
  • Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability finds the Bonesinger's weakness: "He is very weak to vitality. Weakness 10, which I will yell out to everyone." Zanthar Slows it for a round.
  • A natural-20 warhammer crit on Bai Jian for 32: "Well, better me than anyone else, so." Then Panacea's Vitality Lash — the Bonesinger rolls a natural 1 — 52 damage. "Half of its ribcage just turns bright white and disappears from existence."
  • Bai Jian's Sure Strike–powered Snagging Strike hits for 14 — the slashing resistance cuts it to 4. Ren's bonus bite does nothing at all.
  • Cyrathul's Demoralize — "I'm like 90% sure it won't work, but I'll… I'll give him a boo" — works. Zanthar's Force Barrage rolls five 1s and still does 14.
  • A second crit on Bai Jian for 44 and Invoke Reckoning — "I might be done. Have fun." — to 13 of 113. Panacea Battle-Medicines him for 24 and kills it with a second Vitality Lash: "it wraps around the rest of whatever bones he has and explodes them."
  • Afterward, Bai Jian: "I took a beating there… I can't do anything. I need to find my solution." Loot: Hellknight plate and a +1 striking warhammer.
Act III · Social · Wolf Point

The bandits who only rob Chelaxians

  • Around 1 p.m. the party climbs the hill. A crossbow on the rooftop — "Stop where you are" — and an answering whistle brings two more.
  • Cyrathul opens: "We're here to recruit for the cause… We're in need of fighters." Runa Bloodhammer arrives defensive: "We've only been attacking those that are flying the Chelaxian flag."
  • Cyrathul: "We're not interested in cracking down on you. We'd rather recruit you for a more important cause." Runa has seen a battalion of Hellknights headed south that never came back.
  • Cyrathul primes his diplomat's badge with Diverse Lore (30), then Diplomacy — 29 against DC 28. "You did us right by clearing this place out and leaving us alone when we kept our word." The Steel Circle sends fighters to Saringallow.
  • Assist a Settlement: Runa's people are re-standing a collapsed wall. Bai Jian Guidances himself and crits Athletics (36) — the wall set as strong as the day it was built. Runa's thanks: messenger missives and +2 to recruitment.
Act IV · Roleplay · Fernsby

Children in Asmodean robes

  • Dusk, tired horses. Lily is sitting in the rebuilt village green: "Oh, it's the Hellbreakers." The undead started as a few zombies and have been "getting bigger and badder."
  • Panacea asks where it's coming from and Bai Jian presses when they come. Lily: a vengeful ghost from old children's tales who would "turn us all into the undead" — "that's just children's tales, right?" — except someone killed a zombie just last week. Trade routes avoid certain places farther south.
  • Cyrathul's Diplomacy with Lily (26) is the second curse point. Stew, hard biscuits, and a borrowed cottage.
  • Morning. Lily asks for help getting the children out. Panacea disguises them as Chelaxian orphans in the robes of Asmodeus's disciples, hero-pointing a 4 into a 25 against DC 25. Zanthar's Arcana (28) is the third curse point — south, slightly west.
  • Cyrathul recruits (33, with Bai Jian's Guidance). Fernsby sends word to Stumpfield, now at truce with them: war saboteurs join the cause.
Act V · Council · The Council of Elders at Thorngrip

The elders love rhymes

  • On the ride in, Bai Jian takes the +1 striking warhammer — "I feel like I might become useless if I don't switch out my weapon" — noting it's a −2 outside his group with no feats that work with it.
  • Bracknap turns before they arrive: "You come in difficult times, yes? To make some difficult requests?" The undead "took place quite often… 10 generations ago. I wonder why it stopped." He has already called the Council of Elders: the master of communal mud, Two, Three, Grandma Neemi (his betrothed), Four, and Five.
  • Round 1 — discovery. Bai Jian reads the lowest skill: Goblin or Chitterwood Lore. Cyrathul — "Diverse Lore for the win" — lands the first point; Five wants "the Chitterwood to rule itself autonomously." Panacea finds the weakness: rhymes. Zanthar finds the second skill: Diplomacy.
  • Round 2. Cyrathul switches to Theater Lore — "I'll spice it up" — and the council proposes a holiday of reconciliation. Panacea's first limerick: "Our cause is good. Please join our hood." The council: "Your tongue is as pretty as your fur." She curtsies and twirls. Zanthar's Diplomacy lands exactly on the DC.
  • Round 3. Bai Jian's Diplomacy fails even on a hero point: "I'm only trained. I'm not great." Cyrathul's Goblin Lore crits — "It is almost like you have been around longer than you appear to be" — two goblin rebels. Panacea's second limerick sets Two dancing; Bracknap: "Even though I think only one of you are human." Zanthar hero-points a 3 into a 32 — a Hobgoblin Battalion.
  • Round 4. Bai Jian turns to support: Guidance on Cyrathul and a natural-20 Aid on Zanthar (the bonus was forgotten on the roll). Cyrathul succeeds again and one of the ancestor-spirits tries "to see who you really were." Panacea's third limerick: "It's not time to relax." Zanthar closes.
  • The council stands as one. "We only insist on our autonomy… We will join your cause with all of our might." A sequoia branch bends into Bracknap's hand and releases Bounty's Light. Bai Jian: "If you guys gave it to me, I would just use it to bomb people." Zanthar, the only one who can charge it, takes it: "Better than the last unique weapon we got that no one could use."
Act VI · Mystery · Gug's Forge and Bracknap's Vision

Something or someone you are looking for

  • Gug: "That sword I see still on hip." Bai Jian asks for something against undead. "You in luck. Me stole from Hellknight outside town. Have one." A vitalizing rune goes on the falcata; the crushing rune comes back to him for later.
  • Bracknap joins the party, with two goblin guards — one with a scar through a pure-white eye, one short and scrappy.
  • Zanthar's Dream Message to Alceo: Thorngrip is safe, more goblins coming. "I don't do it in rhyming."
  • Evening curse check. Bai Jian blows an Aid — "I'm so useless in talking. I need to go reflect on myself… I can't be this useless. I'm going to use a hero point" — and the +10 reroll saves it. Cyrathul: natural 20, 43. Bracknap: the humans had a rumour of "a witch… A horrid, dead thing."
  • Bracknap's eyes flare bluer and brighter than ever, and every ghost behind him turns to Bai Jian: "There is something or someone you are looking for. I believe at the end of this path, the catalyst may be that thing." The light dims; he seems not to know what he said.
  • Bai Jian, after a silence: "All I can say is I hope what you said was true."
  • All night, Gug's anvil: "the gears of war have started here in the heart of the Chitterwood." Bai Jian buys doubling rings.
Act VII · Exploration · Gillimore

The curse is located

  • Zanthar crits Arcana (36), turning down Bai Jian's Guidance: "Why don't you Guidance someone else?" — "I'll go sulk over there. Because I got rejected." Seven of ten.
  • Cyrathul asks around again. Bai Jian's Aid crit-fails — "I have harmed you. I will atone with the Guidance on you" — the Guidance cancels it, and Cyrathul crits. Bracknap half-remembers an old human town, like "the taste on the tip of your tongue."
  • Alceo's reply: good job at Thorngrip — and something is happening toward Logos; he and Phoebe are headed there.
  • One more check. Bai Jian: "I don't trust my luck." Panacea: "You got this." He argues for Nature — "I will look at the animals and use them as traces, because animals do exhibit certain behaviors" — and crits (31).
  • He remembers Gillimore: an abandoned human town at the far end of the Chitterwood, avoided by every trader "like the plague, because something isn't right there."
  • "He owes us, what, 400 gold?" — "That's why you always double down."

Round 1 — the summoning

  • Bonesinger: summons the Skeletal Giant.
  • Panacea: Divine Lance on the giant, hit, 14.
  • Bai Jian: Sudden Charge, hit, 16 (11 through resistance); Dueling Parry.
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability on the giant, crit (33 vs DC 18) — resists everything, no weakness; Draconic Advance jaws, natural 1, miss.
  • Zanthar: triple-moves.

Round 2 — the giant falls, the weakness found

  • Skeletal Giant: Broad Swipe — Bai Jian hit for 9 through Parry (AC 28), Cyrathul missed.
  • Bonesinger: Rouse Skeletons under Zanthar, save success, 5.
  • Panacea: Divine Lance, 12.
  • Bai Jian: Snagging Strike natural 1, miss; second Snagging Strike from a flank crits (26 vs AC 15) for 37 — giant destroyed.
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability on the Bonesinger (33 vs DC 24) — vitality weakness 10; jaws hit through off-guard, 20.
  • Zanthar: Slow — Bonesinger saves (31 vs DC 26), Slowed 1 for a round.

Round 3 — the 52

  • Bonesinger (slowed): Rouse Skeletons — Bai Jian 3, Cyrathul 3, itself unharmed. Warhammer natural 20 crit on Bai Jian, 32.
  • Panacea: Vitality Lash — Bonesinger natural 1, critical failure — 52.
  • Bai Jian: commands Ren (support +1), Retrieval Prism → scroll of Sure Strike, casts it, Snagging Strike hit (33) for 14 → 4 after resistance; Ren's bonus damage resisted.
  • Cyrathul: Draconic Frenzy — jaws hit 23, claws miss on a 1 and a 4; Demoralize 33 → Frightened 1.
  • Zanthar: all three actions on a six-shard Force Barrage, 14.

Round 4 — 13 HP, and Panacea ends it

  • Bonesinger: Rouse Skeletons — Cyrathul 7, Ren 7, Bai Jian saves (3). Warhammer crit on Bai Jian, 44 (38 vs AC 26). Invoke Reckoning, Fortitude failed, 9 — Bai Jian at 13.
  • Panacea: Battle Medicine (Assurance) +24, then Vitality Lash for 29 — Bonesinger destroyed.
One table miss, and two quiet contributions. The first Vitality Lash's critical failure should also have left the Bonesinger Enfeebled 1 — its Round 4 crit would have been 42, not 44. Zanthar's Slow took a Round 3 action away from it, and Cyrathul's Frightened turned its final save against Panacea from a success into a failure.

80 XP (Moderate). "You guys are all showered in some disgusting former Hellknight bones as it crumples to the ground."
