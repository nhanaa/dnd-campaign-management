## Session Summary

A detective session, and the party was good at it. They began the night with one murder and a missing corpse. They ended it with four bodies, a suspect under surveillance, and a working theory that explains the whole building.

The first break came off a cipher — twelve pieces of vertebra strung together, pulled from the costume of the woman they'd captured, decoded by Panacea into a sentence that named a god's servant and a name nobody recognized: *Illcayna will reward you when Isger is hers.* From there the manor started producing corpses faster than the party could sweep it. Merpelia Carafo in the art gallery, drained white with two neat punctures in her neck. A manor guard on the guest landing, throat opened by claws with the fang marks added afterward, as an afterthought. Ambassador Frigg in the conservatory, the same way. Zanthar called it barely twenty minutes in and never wavered: *"That is someone trying to make it look like a vampire."* Bai Jian tested it the direct way and ran a corpse through the heart with a silver blade — and nothing came out. No blood at all. Someone had taken it.

What the party built instead of a confession was a pattern. Zanthar noticed the water first and kept checking: a small puddle under an unlocked window at every scene, in a manor where every latch is on the inside. Cyrathul identified the black-sand hourglass under the game-room table as a talisman that hurries or halts decomposition — which is why the bodies went cold too fast. Panacea reached back into a conversation from a *previous session* and remembered that Ezio Daeta had hurled away a plate because of the garlic in it, then caught him contradicting himself about where Merpelia had gone and started keeping a tally out loud: *"Lie number one."* By three in the morning they had a tail on Ezio, a garlic trap being assembled from a magic platter, and a Hellknight Paralictor telling them that circumstantial wasn't going to be enough.

Twice the manor fought back. A room filled floor to ceiling with rats, spiders and centipedes pouring through every crack — a hazard Panacea's own Recall Knowledge identified as something a vampire can summon and command telepathically, *but only within a hundred yards*, which told them the killer was still in the building. And when the party finally followed bootprints out into the blizzard toward the stewards' mausoleum, the ornamental hedges stood up. Bai Jian ended the tiger in a single turn with two consecutive falcata crits. Then Cyrathul recalled what those things actually were — the manor's own defense system, keyed to attack anything *living* that comes near without clearance — and Zanthar took it the last step: which means the thing that has been walking in and out of this house all night isn't.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Cipher
*The first ~15 minutes of play happened before the recording started; those bullets are reconstructed from the Foundry log alone and carry no quotes. The decode itself and everything after it is on tape.*

- The party worked the aftermath of the gala attack with **Fessy Finwinkle** on hand offering healing. **Panacea's** Medicine check on a patient came up just short (14 vs DC 15).
- Attention turned to **Lefranek Meyal's** discarded devil costume. The party went to Identify Magic on its contents — **Cyrathul crit-failed** the Nature attempt (9), **Panacea** missed the Arcana by 3, **Zanthar** matched her exactly.
- **Panacea** carried the decode itself: a **Society check against DC 25, made by 4 (29)**, on a wheel cipher assembled from **twelve pieces of vertebra** — an instrument used by the agents of **Urgathoa**. Cyrathul rolled a **natural 19** on the same check and still failed it by 6, because he is untrained in Society and Panacea is not.
- The message: **"Bring the guards to a servant cloaked in our most sacred color. Illcayna will reward you when Isger is hers."**
- Also in the costume: a **Platter of Putrid Dreams** and a **Skeleton Key**, both moved to the party stash by **Zanthar**. **110 XP.**

### Act II — Two Inferences and a Scream
- **Cyrathul** took two inferences off one sentence inside a minute: the sacred color is **red** — which points straight back to the man in the red cloak — and **"Illcayna"** reads as an alias. *"Would that be a name that's recognizable? Or maybe a pseudonym? Something related to, like, the one goddess?"*
- He also read the **Platter of Putrid Dreams** correctly — it conjures a dish only its intended target can stomach — and asked the question that makes it evidence rather than loot: ***"Who is this compelled to?"***
- Upstairs in the steward's bedroom: maroon velvet, a folded-silver glaive over the fireplace, a canopy bed with nothing on it but an indent in the quilt. **A drift of snow and a melting puddle under the open window.**
- A woman screamed directly below them. **Cyrathul** floated leaving **Ren** behind as an alarm; **Bai Jian** took the bait — *"He knows, like, 101. Never split the party"* — briefly considered instructing the hound, then argued the whole thing down, since a Hellknight was already posted on that landing. Then a guard came up the stairs: another murder.
- **Merpelia Carafo** lay dead in the art gallery. The body was already cold to the touch. **Panacea's** medical exam returned **exsanguination** and two puncture marks. *"Fantastic, we have vampires in the house."*
- **Zanthar's** Society check placed the loss: Merpelia helped run the Isgeri government. *"It would be like the Secretary of State being murdered."* **30 XP.**
- **Cyrathul's Recall Knowledge crit success** ruled out the obvious suspect: Hellknights are lawful evil and entirely by the book. They would march in the front door. **Cheliax already owns Isger** — there is nothing here worth assassinating for.
- **Zanthar** made the call the whole session runs on: *"That is someone trying to make it look like a vampire."*
- The party tested it. **Bai Jian** put his silver falcata through Merpelia's heart and out the back of her chest cavity — *"there's almost zero fluid that comes out."* The corpse did not react. **Cyrathul**: *"Red cloak. Fake vampire."* **Panacea**, totting it up: *"Fake Hellknights, fake vampires. Okay. And a cipher."*
- **Zanthar** swept the room and found the first puddle: water under a **closed but unlocked** window.

### Act III — Working the Room
- **Cyrathul interviewed the guards over the body**, and this is where the entire Ezio thread starts. *"How'd y'all find the body?"* — and on a **Diplomacy 22** the guard gave it up: Merpelia had not been in the gallery alone. *"I believe she was arguing with the fat man… Mr. Daeta, before she came in here."*
- **Panacea** then ran **Ezio Daeta** — the gossip she'd cultivated at the gala — and turned him into an unwitting asset: *"Why don't you go work the room on people and see what you can find out from them and let me know."* He scurried off and immediately told a cluster of nobles *"I thought she was down in the art gallery"* — after telling Panacea to her face that he had no idea where Merpelia had gone.
- **Panacea**, dry: *"So now we've caught him in a lie. **Lie number one.**"*
- **Panacea** also flagged the conflict of interest nobody else raised: **Caeto** was upstairs, turned up covered in blood, and is the man directing the investigation.
- **Bai Jian** pushed the tempo: *"Let's go just check everywhere to make sure. Because the more we just laying around, the more time we lose in finding."* The party swept the ground floor room by room.
- **Cyrathul** had the guards herd the surviving guests into one room so the killer would run out of isolated targets — gesturing a claw at the corpse in the corner by way of argument.
- The **kitchen**: **Sasatoli Bronc** with a turkey leg in one hand and a tankard in the other. **Zanthar** tried to intimidate him and got a mug of mead for his trouble — *"You are one funny little guy. I really like you."* **Cyrathul** stepped in behind him and got a straight answer: *"I'll play ball."* The exhausted kitchen servant alibi'd Sasatoli completely.
- The **conservatory** (a glass-walled greenhouse growing the manor's food), the **servants' quarters** (where **Zanthar** stole a card game's hands to give an absent servant a better draw), the **dining room**, and the **library** — where **Zanthar** asked the one useful question of a room full of drunk nobles: *are any of you new faces?* That surfaced **Ambassador Frigg**, in Isger for the first time, all the way from **Oprak**.
- **Bai Jian**, on discovering the privy has two doors: *"Hmm, a toilet with two doors. Intriguing. The people over here is quite eccentric, I see."*

### Act IV — The Second Victim
- On the guest landing: a manor guard in chainmail, throat torn out, a frozen expression of shock. This was the second victim **Caeto** had mentioned in S13 — the one killed by *"someone or something."*
- **Panacea's Medicine check crit-succeeded** (hero point, 28) and produced the session's most important forensic detail: the throat was opened by **claws**, there were **no defensive wounds**, there was far less blood than there should have been — and the **fang marks were added moments *after* he was already dead.**
- **Zanthar's** perception found the coin purse still on the belt, the longsword still sheathed, and — under the window — **more water on the floor.** Cyrathul: *"Seems to be a common factor between them."* **150 XP**, and a break.

### Encounter 1 — Creatures of the Night *(complex hazard)*
*(see Combat Encounters)*

- Post-fight, the party's read hardened. **Panacea's** Recall Knowledge had established the leash — **a hundred yards**. Cyrathul: *"So it's still here. It's nearby. And we know it's a vampire now for sure."*
- **Cyrathul** put a Seek out the window to see whether anything was watching from outside (29) and found nothing but snow and a magical construct sentry patrolling the grounds below.

### Act V — The Game Room
- Hedvind's actual murder site. A door off its hinges, taxidermied monster heads, a smoldering fire, an overturned chair speckled with blood.
- **Two hands of cards on the table** — one face up with three aces, one face down with nothing. **Cyrathul** raised the question and offered Games Lore; the payoff came off **Panacea's Society check (26)**: Hedvind was mid-hand when he died, which means **he knew his killer and wasn't alarmed by them.**
- **A key on the table**, with the door broken down from outside: the room was **locked from the inside.** No signs of struggle at all.
- Under the table, knocked over: an **hourglass filled with black sand.** **Cyrathul** identified it as a talisman that **preserves or accelerates decomposition on a body** — and that it had **been used**. Also under the table: an **onyx idol of Urgathoa**, recovered by **Zanthar**.
- **Zanthar** checked the **Staff of the Dead** from last session and confirmed it had **not** been used today.
- **Zanthar**, again: *"Are any of the windows open or have water near them?"* There was.

### Act VI — The Conservatory
- A man screamed. **Ambassador Frigg**, dead among the tomato vines. Two punctures in the neck. A puddle under a closed window whose latch was on the inside.
- **Zanthar's** Society check: an alliance with **Oprak** would have benefited Isger enormously, and now it is impossible.
- **Cyrathul** built a testimony chain rather than trusting one witness — Frigg had to pass two guards outside the library — and the guards delivered: **Frigg and Ezio Daeta had been arguing**, specifically about whether Oprak would benefit Isger, and Ezio *"was staring pretty intently at him."*
- **Panacea** landed the session's sharpest line: *"We **did** tell him to talk to everybody — but it just happens to be that he's talking to everybody that's been dead."*
- Then she produced the leap of the night, reaching back into a **previous session's** influence scene: Ezio had refused a dish in a tantrum, and she thought she knew why. *"I think he threw the plate that had garlic in it, honestly… if I remember right."* She went to check her own notes and came back with *"Yes, it was bad cheese and garlic sauce"* — then flagged its limits herself: *"Or that was his claim when I asked him about it."* **The DM never confirmed the detail.** **Zanthar** went straight to the kitchen for garlic anyway, and the party began assembling a sting using the **Platter of Putrid Dreams**.
- **Panacea** covered the whole operation to Ezio's face while it was being planned — *"we're just trying to figure out how to keep everybody well fed so that they don't get too cranky with us"* — with a small Cheshire grin.
- **Zanthar** put a **tail on Ezio**: *"Not formally guard him. Just kind of make sure where he is and what he's doing."*
- A servant reported that **Fessy Finwinkle** had become badly frightened and **locked herself in the private dining room upstairs.** Bai Jian: *"That's definitely a recipe for success right there."* Nobody followed up.

### Act VII — Reporting to Caeto
- **Panacea** led: they have a suspect, not proof. Caeto's bar: *"We're going to need hard proof."*
- **Cyrathul** pressed him on where that bar actually sits — *"How much circumstantial evidence would you consider undoubtable?"* — then spelled the cipher name aloud: ***"It's I-L-L-C-A-Y-N-A."*** Caeto had never heard it.
- **Panacea** asked the operational question: the grounds are ringed with magical construct sentries, so how is anyone getting in and out? Caeto admitted he is **untrained in the arcane arts** and has no idea how the wards identify anything — then opened a desk drawer and showed them the **control box of glowing crystals** that arms and disarms the manor's entire defense system.
- **Cyrathul** briefed him on the false flag and the magically edited memories. **Bai Jian** supplied the framing: *"I mean, if you want to just cause some havoc like this, what's better than using the name Hellbreakers?"*
- Caeto, satisfied: *"Seems like you're on the right path. I'm glad I entrusted this to you."*
- **Panacea** got an Arcana read on a second crystal he pulled out — a **speaking crystal**, paired to another across great distances, and expensive.

### Act VIII — The Bedroom, and Outside
- **Bai Jian**: *"Should we check outside?"* The party finished the interior first.
- Back in the steward's bedroom, **Cyrathul crit-succeeded** his Perception (nat 20): **handfuls of dirt scattered across the bed**, more dirt tracked onto the bearskin rug, scratches on the floorboards by the western wall — and a **secret door**. *"Guys, I found something."*
- Inside the hidden compartment: **ledgers tracking bribes and corruption in and outside the city** (400 gp of leverage), recovered by **Cyrathul**.
- Outside the secret door, **tracks**. **Panacea** and **Bai Jian** both made the Survival check and followed them out into the blizzard — where they led, exactly as Bai Jian had said they would. *"I feel like we should have checked outside."*
- Through heavy snow with thirty feet of visibility: a **stone mausoleum lit by everlight crystals**, the historic resting place of Isger's stewards, ringed by trimmed hedges and topiaries.
- **Bai Jian** stopped the party before they closed: *"Hold on, before we come closer. We have two of our members still pretty low. You guys want to drink potions?"* They healed up. Then the hedges moved.

### Encounter 2 — The Topiaries
*(see Combat Encounters)*

- **Cyrathul's** Esoteric Lore (31) closed the session's loop: the topiaries are **part of the manor's own defense system**, activated to attack **anything living** that approaches without proper clearance.
- **Zanthar** took it the rest of the way: *"It only goes after living things. So undead things might not be able to go."* **Panacea**: *"A dead, but can go right by it. That's probably how he's getting in and out."*
- **Bai Jian**: *"There must be someone that planted these, right?"* Someone armed the grounds.
- Final sweep: **Zanthar** found the mausoleum doors **locked**, snow and dust undisturbed — nobody has been inside recently. And **Panacea** found, right beside it, a **small freshly dug hole in the soil** and **a torn-off imp tail from one of the manor servants' costumes.**

> **Session ends there. Somebody in a servant's costume was out here in the snow, digging.**

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the One Who Wanted to Go Outside
- **He was right about the exits, and he said so before it mattered.** With the ground floor swept and the party circling, Bai Jian asked *"Should we check outside?"* The party finished the interior instead. Five minutes later a secret door in the steward's bedroom opened onto bootprints leading into the snow, and he said it twice, without heat: *"Should have checked outside, huh?"* … *"I feel like we should have checked outside."* The tracks led to the mausoleum, and the mausoleum was the session.
- **He set the party's investigative tempo early.** *"Let's go just check everywhere to make sure. Because the more we just laying around, the more time we lose in finding."* That's the sentence the entire room-by-room sweep runs on. He also split the labour at the third crime scene without being asked — *"You guys can check the body. I'll go check around the room"* — so the autopsy and the room search happened at once.
- **He ran the vampire test himself, with the only silver in the party.** The theory was that Merpelia might rise. Bai Jian put the falcata through her heart and out the back of her chest cavity. Nothing came out. It was a genuinely decisive experiment, and it belonged to him because he's the one carrying silver.
- **He reframed the false flag in one line.** Cyrathul was briefing Caeto on the impostors and the edited memories; Bai Jian supplied the reason it works: *"I mean, if you want to just cause some havoc like this, what's better than using the name Hellbreakers?"* The League's reputation is now a weapon anyone can pick up, and he's the one who said it out loud to a Hellknight.
- **He is out of the knowledge lane, and he has made peace with exactly which lane is his.** He tried untrained Religion on the vampire question and abandoned it mid-sentence. But in the vermin swarm his **Expert Nature was the party's best answer in the room**, and he spent a hero point to make sure of it — a success toward the disable, narrated as him drawing his sword and hammering it around until the swarm broke apart. Later, sorting loot: *"I'll be the nature guy. You guys can deal with religion as well as magic."*
- **He taught the table a rule mid-hazard rather than take the spotlight.** With one action left he asked *"Does anyone else want to use any skill that I might be able to aid?"* Then, when Cyrathul tried to Aid on Panacea's turn, he corrected it: *"No, no — you have to **prepare** to aid. So on your turn, you have to prepare by rolling the DC 15…"* (Zanthar finished the sentence for him: *"Use it as a reaction."*) He also redirected Panacea to Recall Knowledge instead — which is the check that produced the hundred-yard leash.
- **He stopped the party from walking into the ambush at low HP.** Before closing on the mausoleum: *"Hold on, before we come closer. We have two of our members still pretty low. You guys want to drink potions?"* They topped up, and then three topiaries stood up out of the hedges.
- **Combat:** he ended the Tiger Topiary in a single turn. Ren's Support benefit fed **+1** into the opening swing, which **crit for 46**; he made the follow-up a **Snagging Strike**, and the fatal-d12 falcata **crit again through the off-guard for 44**. **90 damage in one turn**, the whole of it against the fight's only real threat. The DM: *"the whole tiger just kind of seems to fall in half, and then it's just a lifeless bush on the ground."* He also hero-pointed his Will save against the hound's Warning Howl into a **critical success** — Bravery and Dragonblood turning a bare success into an auto-crit, as designed.
- **Deadpan of the session**, on being asked what people were doing in the bedrooms during a party: *"Don't ask questions you don't want answers to."*

### Cyrathul · the Dragon Who Reads the Room

- **He treats magic items as forensic evidence, not loot.** Handed a Platter of Putrid Dreams, his first question was ***"Who is this compelled to?"*** — because the item names its intended victim. Later he asked whether it had been used in the past week, and whether the Staff of the Dead had been used today. Both are the questions of someone building a timeline.
- **His Recall Knowledge crit eliminated the obvious suspect.** Hellknights are lawful evil and by the book; they'd march in the front door; and Cheliax already owns Isger outright, so there's nothing to assassinate for. That crit is what turned the investigation away from the occupying power and toward something hiding inside the house.
- **He identified the mechanism.** The black-sand hourglass under the game table is a talisman that preserves or accelerates decomposition — which is precisely why Merpelia was stone cold minutes after dying. He also worked the two hands of cards into the night's most human deduction: Hedvind was mid-game when he died, so **he knew the person across the table and wasn't afraid of them.**
- **He found the secret door on a natural 20.** Dirt scattered across the steward's bed, more of it tracked onto the rug, scratches along the western wall — and a hidden compartment holding ledgers of every bribe in the city. *"Guys, I found something."*
- **He started the Ezio thread, and nobody has credited him for it since.** Standing over Merpelia's body he turned to the guards — *"How'd y'all find the body?"* — and a **Diplomacy 22** produced the thing the entire back half of the session runs on: she had not been in that gallery alone. *"I believe she was arguing with the fat man… Mr. Daeta, before she came in here."* Every lie Panacea catches Ezio in afterward is downstream of that question.
- **He is the party's closer in a room, and he knows when to walk in behind someone.** Zanthar's intimidation of Sasatoli Bronc landed as comedy and got him a beer. Cyrathul stepped in behind the man mid-laugh: *"It is in your best interest to cooperate with us. This isn't really a laughing matter."* Sasatoli sat down his turkey leg and said *"I'll play ball."* He pressed Caeto the same way, on the only question that mattered: *"How much circumstantial evidence would you consider undoubtable?"*

He found the **weakness 5 to fire** with Exploit Vulnerability — *"which is not good, because I now do fire damage"* — and turned it into **+5 on every Strike he landed**, putting 37 into one hound before Zanthar's bolt finished it. Then he killed the last one with a two-step of his own making: **Demoralize crit → Frightened 2 → its AC drops to 16 → hero-pointed jaws crit for 43.** He built the opening and then walked through it. His kill narration: *"I'll just open my mouth and there'll be a flame coming out of my teeth, and as I'm biting I'll move through him as it's burning the rest of him."* In the vermin swarm he also spent the hero point that **ended the encounter** — and notably he got there by *changing skills*, not by repeating himself: his Intimidation roar had failed a round earlier, so he came back with **Religion (29)** and the DM re-narrated it as the same roar, finally landing.

### Zanthar Melodune III · the One Who Was Right First
- **He called the central twist twenty minutes into the session and never moved off it.** Everyone else was working out how to find a vampire. Zanthar looked at the same body and said *"That is someone trying to make it look like a vampire."* Panacea's autopsy on the guard — claws first, fangs afterward — confirmed him forty minutes later.
- **He assembled the pattern that connects every crime scene.** The puddles themselves came from all over — Cyrathul's Seek turned up the first in the gallery, Zanthar asked for the second in the game room, Panacea asked for the third in the conservatory. Zanthar is the one who put them together into a method. The DM confirmed the latches were all on the inside; Zanthar took it from there: *"That means someone's coming around and then jumping out the window to escape."* Three unrelated details became one signature on his turn.
- **And he's the one who solved how.** The session's last real deduction is his: the topiaries are keyed to attack the living, *"so undead things might not be able to go"* past them. The manor's own defenses have been letting the killer through all night because the killer doesn't register as alive.
- **He drove the investigation by asking better questions than anyone else.** To a library full of drunk nobles: *are any of you new faces?* — which is how Ambassador Frigg surfaced as the outsider from Oprak, hours before Frigg turned up dead. To the party at large: *"Is there anyone in particular that would benefit from the death of all these people?"* — motive-first suspect narrowing. And he established the blizzard as the evidence clock early: *"If it happened recently, they'd show up in the snow."*
- **The garlic plan is his, end to end.** Panacea remembered the plate; Zanthar went to the kitchen, got the garlic, and came back with it. He also put the tail on Ezio in exactly the right register — *"Not formally guard him. Just kind of make sure where he is and what he's doing."*
- **His comedy is a running bit about tampering with evidence.** He rearranged a card game in the servants' quarters to give an absent servant a better hand — *"I'm going to give someone a really good hand"* — and then, at the actual murder scene, went to do it again: *"I'm going to switch the cards up again so that the other one's got four twos."* He also floated *"We can make up clues"* and a plan to stab everyone at the party with a stake and see who bursts into flame.

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Creatures of the Night *(complex hazard)*

**Initiative:** Creatures of the Night (36) → Panacea (29) → Cyrathul (23) → Zanthar (21) → Bai Jian (12)

Rats, spiders, centipedes and bats poured in through every crack, vent and chimney flue in the room as the party examined the guard's body. The door slammed shut. This was **a complex hazard, not a creature** — there was nothing to kill, only a disable to work, and the party recognised it immediately. The DM: *"You guys are used to complex hazards now. How would you like to try to disable this?"*

**On entry — Infest, DC 24 Fortitude**
- **Bai Jian** critical success (33) — unaffected
- **Cyrathul** success (25)
- **Panacea** failure (22) — **Sickened 1**
- **Zanthar** failure (15) — **Sickened 1**

**Round 1**
- **The hazard** went first and hit the whole room: **basic Reflex DC 24**, 2d8+3 = **11 damage**. Bai Jian saved (26) for 5; Zanthar saved (24) for 5; **Cyrathul spent a hero point** to turn an 18 into a 27 and took 5 instead of 11; **Panacea failed** (16), took the full **11**, and came out of it with **persistent bleed 3** on top.
- **Panacea** opened with **Recall Knowledge (Medicine)**, then went for the disable with **Religion** — **hero point** — and still came up **2 short** (22 vs DC 24). Her persistent bleed ticked for 3, and she **critically failed the recovery check on a natural 1.**
- **Cyrathul** templated a **dragon's roar** at the swarm as an Intimidation attempt and failed — *"you roar and a bunch of them just flood into your mouth instead, which isn't probably the worst thing you've had in your mouth"* — and his follow-up **Pest Lore** (Diverse Lore, −2) also missed — *"it's on the tip of your tongue, but it's just evading you."*
- **Zanthar** rolled a **natural 20** on Recall Knowledge (Arcana 32) — which returned the same hint Panacea had surfaced a turn earlier. **The DM had specified Nature or Religion for the disable, and Zanthar is untrained in both**, so the party's knowledge specialist had no legal way to contribute to the actual objective. He considered **Dizzying Colors**, discarded it, and burned a rank-2 slot on **Chilling Spray** instead — a 15-foot cone for **8 cold** into a hazard with no hit points. *"You see quite a few of the spiders and stuff curl up and die. And as soon as they do, just more keep flooding in down the chimney."*
- **Bai Jian** went to his **Expert Nature** — the party's best skill in the room — **spent a hero point**, and made it by 5 (**29**). *"You take your sword out, and you start banging it around, and you see that they kind of spread out a little bit — that will be a success towards disabling this."* **First success.** With his last action he offered to Aid anyone else's attempt, then took **Dueling Parry**.

**Round 2**
- **The hazard** hit again: **basic Reflex DC 24**, 2d8+3 = **17 damage**. Zanthar saved (27) for 8. Panacea (16) and Cyrathul (15) failed for **17** each. **Bai Jian critically failed** (13) and took **34** — the single biggest hit anyone took all session.
- **Panacea** missed the Religion disable again, this time **by 1** (23 vs 24), then converted her turn into the intelligence that mattered: a **Recall Knowledge success (26)** establishing that **some vampires can summon creatures like this and command them telepathically — but only within about a hundred yards.** Her bleed ticked for another 3 and she failed the recovery a second time.
- **Bai Jian and Zanthar never got a Round 2 turn** — Cyrathul ended it before initiative came back around. And **nobody healed anyone for the entire encounter**: Panacea asked, and the disable cost two actions, so it never happened until the fight was over.
- **Cyrathul** went back to the roar, **spent a hero point**, and hit it (**29**). *"This time you dig deep into that roar, and you bellow it out, and all of the pests start racing back outside, up through the chimney, out through the vent holes, out through the crack in the window. They uncover the door. And they all disappear."* **Encounter over.**

**Damage taken:** Bai Jian **39** · Panacea **34** · Cyrathul **22** · Zanthar **13**
**Hero points:** 4 spent — Cyrathul ×2 (a Reflex save, and the disable that ended it), Panacea ×1 (failed), Bai Jian ×1 (the first success).
**Disable:** two successes — **Bai Jian (Nature)** and **Cyrathul (Religion)**.

**Aftermath:** with initiative dropped, Panacea ran the whole party back up — Treat Wounds on Bai Jian (13), Zanthar (5) and Cyrathul (5), plus Life Boost — while **Zanthar healed himself for 18 with Lay on Hands** and Bai Jian drank a Soothing Tonic.

**What it bought the party:** the hazard dealt 108 points of damage across the party and gave up no XP award of its own, but Panacea's Recall Knowledge turned it into the session's tightest constraint — **the killer was within a hundred yards of that room, and had been the whole time.**

### Encounter 2 — The Topiaries

**Initiative:** Hound Topiary (28) → Hound Topiary (26) → Panacea (21) → Cyrathul (19) → Tiger Topiary (18) → Bai Jian (18) → Zanthar (17)
*(Panacea's initiative went in late — Foundry logged a 29, but the DM couldn't get it to register and manually re-slotted her at **21**, behind both hounds. Ren acts on Bai Jian's turn.)*

Three ornamental hedges — a **Tiger Topiary** (AC 21, attack +13) and two **Hound Topiaries** (AC 18, attack +10, Pack Attack +1d6, Warning Howl DC 17 Will) — came alive around the party as they approached the stewards' mausoleum through the blizzard. All three carried **weakness 5 to fire**.

**Round 1**
- **Hound A** opened on **Cyrathul**: claw **hit for 7**, then a **Warning Howl** (DC 17 Will) that the entire party shrugged off — **Cyrathul** critically succeeded (29), **Bai Jian** hero-pointed a 15 into a 23 and **critically succeeded** via Bravery/Dragonblood, Zanthar (20) and Panacea (17) both held — then jaws at MAP for **8**.
- **Hound B** flanked in on Cyrathul, making him **off-guard**: jaws with **Pack Attack for 11**, then a claw for **13**. **Cyrathul had taken 39 in the first round and was the only PC either hound would look at all fight.**
- **Panacea** dropped a two-action **Heal for 15** into Cyrathul, then cast **Trade Death for Life** on a hound — it **failed its Fortitude save** and took **10 void damage**, handing Cyrathul **fast healing 3** on top.
- **Cyrathul** spent an action on **Exploit Vulnerability**, took the **mortal weakness**, and found fire — *"which is not good, because I now do fire damage"* — which added **+5 to his own Strikes**: **chomp for 17**, **claw for 20**, **37 into one hound.** *"Your claws just burn a big arc right to the center of this thing."*
- **The Tiger Topiary** went for **Ren**: a claw **hit the boarhound for 18**, then missed twice, both **critical misses**.
- **Ren** used his action for the **Support benefit**, feeding **+1** into Bai Jian's next Strike.
- **Bai Jian** strode in and **critically hit the Tiger for 46**. He made the second swing a **Snagging Strike** — and **critically hit again through the off-guard for 44.** *"You just tear through the center of this thing. Slice once across half of it. It kind of tumbles to the ground. Slice again, and the whole tiger just kind of seems to fall in half, and then it's just a lifeless bush on the ground."* **90 damage in one turn. Tiger dead.**
- **Zanthar** **Drained his Bonded Item** to recover **Blazing Bolt** and fired two rays — which must strike *different* targets, so one went to each hound. Both rode the hounds' own **fire weakness 5** (nothing to do with Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability, which only buffs his own Strikes). **Hero point on the first ray** turned a 16 into a 27 — **22 damage**, *"a scorching hole right through the center of this thing,"* and it survived. The second **hit the hound Cyrathul had already opened up for 37** and **finished it at 17**: *"the bolt just sears through, and it's such a big gaping hole the head of this thing just falls off."*

**Round 2**
- The **last hound** kept working Cyrathul: jaws missed, then a **natural 20 on the claw critically hit for 22**, and the second jaws missed.
- **Panacea** sustained **Trade Death for Life**, cast **Life Boost**, and used **Doctor's Visitation + Battle Medicine for 7** — Cyrathul recovering **16 in that round alone** between the heal and two sources of fast healing.
- **Cyrathul** **critically succeeded a Demoralize** (28 vs DC 16) for **Frightened 2** — and that is precisely what killed the hound. Frightened 2 dropped its AC from 18 to **16**; he **spent a hero point** on the follow-up and rerolled into a **26**, beating 16 by exactly ten. The DM: *"Barely crit."* **43 damage.** Without his own Demoralize the swing is an ordinary hit and the fight goes another round. *"I'll just open my mouth and there'll be a flame coming out of my teeth, and as I'm biting I'll move through him as it's burning the rest of him. And I'll just chop him in half."* **Encounter over in 2 rounds**; Bai Jian and Zanthar never took a Round 2 turn.

**Kill credit:** Tiger Topiary — **Bai Jian** (90 in one turn, unassisted). Hound #1 — **Zanthar's** Blazing Bolt decapitation, finishing a hound **Cyrathul had already put 37 into**. Hound #2 — **Cyrathul's** crit jaws, off his own Demoralize.
*(The two hounds land at roughly 64–65 HP each on the arithmetic, which is the only split consistent with one dying to a 17-point ray while the other survived 22 and needed a 43 to drop.)*
**Damage dealt:** Bai Jian **90** · Cyrathul **80** · Zanthar **39** · Panacea **10** · Ren 0 *(Support only)*
**Damage taken:** Cyrathul **61** · Ren **18** · Bai Jian, Panacea, Zanthar **0**
**Healing:** Panacea put **34 into Cyrathul during the fight** (Heal 15, Battle Medicine 7, fast healing 12) and another **26–36 afterward**, plus 8 on Ren.

**The tuning.** A textbook Moderate that funnelled entirely onto one PC. The two hounds ignored everyone else for two rounds, and Cyrathul absorbed **61 of the 79 damage the party took**. The counterweight was that Bai Jian removed the only genuinely dangerous creature — the Tiger, hitting at +13 for 2d6+7 — from the board before it took a second turn.
