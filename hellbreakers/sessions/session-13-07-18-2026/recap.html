Session Summary

The party spent the back half of the New Year's gala at Reeves Rest Manor doing what they came to do: working the room. Four more rounds of influence and discovery, and a great deal of champagne. Bai Jian scouted rather than pushed — he found that Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex was born near Saringallow and handed the intel off rather than spend it himself, and Cyrathul converted it into a hero-pointed crit that won Caeto outright. Panacea gossiped her way into Ezio Daeta's confidence over cognac and pork-belly tarts, and her own hero-pointed crit early on handed all four of them a bonus to every discovery check for the rest of the night. Zanthar quietly ran the entire Quoster Thistlewait arc — both discoveries and the close — after everyone else had bounced off the halfling.

Twice the evening broke. The first time was the Widow Queen — a Mammon priestess five centuries old — whose velstrac bodyguard lifted a servant off the floor by the throat over a splash of spilled wine. Bai Jian stepped in unarmed. Three imps dropped their trays, and the party fought a combat in ballgowns under a constraint they'd set themselves: nobody was supposed to die at this party, because a corpse would be remembered. The Widow Queen got bored, dropped the servant, and walked out. The crowd applauded.

The second break was not survivable by politeness. A masked woman raised a flag none of them recognized — a flaming sword through Asmodeus' pentagram — declared herself a Hellbreaker, and ordered the room killed. The party knew immediately these were impostors, and knew just as immediately what a massacre committed under their own banner would cost them. So they fought a second battle inside a stampeding crowd, deliberately taking the leader alive. Then Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex appeared at the top of the stairs, bloodied, and announced that Steward Hedvind VI had been murdered. The manor sealed. Everyone under arrest. Caeto — having watched the party defend a room full of Chelaxian nobles — deputized them to solve it. The prisoner's memories turned out to have been surgically edited, leaving only a man in a red cloak and a name: the Bountiful Lady of Harvests. Urgathoa. And when the party finally reached the bedroom where the steward died, the bed was empty.

Act I · Social · The Gala, Rounds 3–6

Working the Room

  • Panacea opened the session by delivering the party's entire accumulated intel picture from her own notes — every mark's weakness and resistance, the dart match, who bet what on whom. She is the table's record-keeper.
  • Bai Jian worked Colonel Rithert on the "you belong in the field" angle — a success. Ambassador Frigg, standing beside him, frowned; he has actually seen battle, and Rithert's posturing grates.
  • Bai Jian then took the scouting lane rather than crowd anyone's mark, and ran a Perception Discovery on Caeto Vulaunex — the highest-value find of the encounter: Caeto was born near Saringallow and is fond of it. He passed it straight to the party: "My job here is to get the intel to the other members of the party. Because I already talked to him once, and I don't want to repeat my humiliation."
  • Cyrathul converted that intel immediately, strolling up to the Hellknight as a fellow local asking after restaurants, volunteering his own diverse-lore penalty before the DM waived it, and spending a hero point on a Saringallow Lore check — a crit success, about roast pork. It won Caeto outright.
  • Panacea hero-pointed a Society check on Ezio Daeta into a crit success, unlocking a party-wide +1 circumstance bonus to all discovery checks at the gala — mechanically the biggest single unlock of the night.
  • Zanthar intimidated a waiter into hauling an entire tray over to Sasatoli — using a hostile skill as a gift-delivery mechanism — and ran a Society discovery that found Quoster's lowest influence skills: mercantile lore and diplomacy.
  • Cyrathul tried the mercantile-lore angle on Quoster and missed. Bai Jian tried a cold approach on Quoster's fashion and also missed — Quoster isn't actually knowledgeable about clothes and read the flattery as insincere. Cyrathul passed him on the way out and quietly patted him on the shoulder.
  • Bai Jian noticed a halfling servant in an ill-fitting imp costume scrubbing a wall after Ezio hurled a tray at it, and was simply kind to him: "Why are you saying sorry to me? Well, just be careful." (This pays off in Act VI.)
  • Panacea — having heard about Ezio's tantrum — brought him a fresh pork-belly tart to soothe it. A success that unlocked a checkpoint: crit failures against Ezio now downgrade to ordinary failures.
  • Zanthar ran a second discovery and found Quoster's real weakness: anti-Cheliax framing — the slavery history, and Cheliax's grip on the Carnecia River trade routes — then used it himself with Diplomacy and closed him. +1 Hellbreakers League reputation, and a special badge promised after the gala. Cyrathul had failed three attempts on the halfling; Zanthar landed it.
  • Quoster then asked the question that ended the evening: "Where is Hedvind? He was supposed to come down and make an announcement."
Act II · Roleplay · The Widow Queen

"You dare?"

  • A cry cut off in a gurgle: a velstrac held a human servant off the floor by the throat. Its mistress — an imperial woman in a gown worth "more than your life" — watched, grinning.
  • Nobody could place her. Zanthar's Society crit success turned up nothing ("none of you recognize who she is"), and Bai Jian declined outright: "Bai Jian doesn't know shit about magic."
  • It was Panacea's Religion check that named the magic pouring off her: a high priestess of the arch-devil Mammon, rumored over 500 years old by devil-pact.
  • Bai Jian asked Cyrathul's permission, got a nod, and stepped between them anyway. Panacea offered Guidance and he refused it — casting magic at a Chelaxian gala was not worth the risk.
  • Cyrathul moved in to Aid and critically failed, actively making the check worse by 1 — for the first time in his life, per the DM, he stumbled over his words. Bai Jian's Diplomacy then failed on top of it.
  • "You dare? You dare. I don't think you know who you are talking to." Three tray-carrying imps dropped everything.
Act III · Combat · Three Iron Rose Imps

A Fight in Ballgowns

  • The crowd stepped back into a battle circle. The velstrac never joined — it spent the whole encounter holding the servant off the ground.
  • Bai Jian set the doctrine: "Let's try not to kill them." When Cyrathul objected that nobody would miss a random imp, his counter was tactical, not squeamish: "Yeah, but it draws attention."
  • Cyrathul argued the other way throughout and bit lethally the entire fight. Panacea had no non-lethal option and sat out offense entirely. All three imps lived because the finishing blow in each case happened to be non-lethal.
  • Imps are Tiny — Bai Jian rules-lawyered it for the DM mid-combat: they can't flank, and have to share a space to attack at all.
  • Zanthar knocked two of them cold in one turn with a non-lethal Blazing Bolt, both rays connecting.
  • Bai Jian finished the last one with a 44-damage non-lethal crit. The DM: "it's amazing that it's not dead."
  • The Widow Queen lost interest — "What a waste of my time. We're leaving." — dropped the servant, who began breathing immediately, and swept out with her velstrac. The ballroom applauded the party.
  • A servant collected the unconscious imps on a tray and carried them back to the kitchen.
Act IV · Social · Cognac and the Toast That Never Came

Aftermath

  • The guests filled in the gap: she is the Widow Queen, of Iron Rose Manor in the Menador Mountains. She is also the silent, magic-radiating woman Zanthar clocked at the ball last session — the DM carried his S12 Society crit forward rather than re-rolling it, and even that crit turned up nothing. The party only learned who she was from crowd gossip after she'd walked out.
  • Fessy Finwinkle, a shy halfling priestess of Abadar in white-and-gold, introduced herself and offered healing. Panacea recognized her from the hospital where she'd worked during downtime.
  • Caeto Vulaunex handed over a diplomat's badge — awarded to Cyrathul, explicitly for his crit success. (The DM first narrated it to Panacea for her mid-combat healing, then retracted and re-issued it twice; Panacea rolled her whole turn back without complaint.)
  • Cognac replaced champagne. Renacilos Aziri — the legionnaire ringer who won the darts contest — and Quoster settled into a private, escalating drinking contest that ran the rest of the night.
Act V · Mystery · The False Flag

"In the name of the Hellbreakers League"

  • The music stopped. A masked woman: "For far too long, you and Hedvind have been Cheliax' lapdogs… It is time for everyone here to die in the name of the Hellbreakers League."
  • She raised a flag depicting a flaming sword piercing Asmodeus' red pentagram — a banner none of the party had ever seen. Masked compatriots attacked.
  • Bai Jian, immediately: "Fucking fake Hellbreakers."
  • Zanthar was the only one to make the secret Perception check — every guard in the room had quietly vanished in the preceding minutes. +1 initiative to the party.
  • Zanthar then drove the verification that made the frame-up legible: there is no second Hellbreaker cell in the city. "There may be Hellbreakers and another group of Hellbreakers that have their own group. We're the only ones."
  • Doctrine was set before initiative — Cyrathul: they could not be seen to have massacred a party full of sympathizers. Zanthar: "We need to have some of them alive, though, so that they can be questioned."
Act VI · Combat · Lefranek Meyal and the False Hellbreakers

Three Rounds in a Stampede

  • Crossbow archers on the balcony and stairs, and the gala crowd itself as a complex environmental hazard — the entire floor difficult terrain, rolling a d12 each round for either Crushing Press or Wild Dismay.
  • Lefranek's False Flag aura imposed a −1 circumstance penalty to saves and DCs within 60 feet, with no save. She re-applied it every round.
  • Two guests were killed by stray crossbow bolts in Round 1 — the party could not protect the room and fight at the same time.
  • Panacea was the only PC to attack the hazard, casting and sustaining Calm for 3 of the 4 successes needed while everyone else fought.
  • Zanthar dropped Lefranek with a non-lethal Blazing Bolt — unconscious instead of dead, and the entire basis of the rest of the session.
  • Ren took an archer's femoral artery out on the stairs; Bai Jian charged up after him and crit the last one for 33 in one swing.
  • Three hero points spent across the fight; Zanthar finished it confused, the only PC to fail that save all encounter.
Act VII · Mystery · Arrest, Commission, Interrogation

"Hedvind VI has been murdered"

  • Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex appeared at the top of the stairs with battered Hellknights and announced the steward's murder. The manor's magical defenses had activated; everyone was under arrest and forbidden to leave.
  • 600 XP for the social encounter (15 points × 40 XP) — the party hit Level 5, 480 XP into the next. Per the AP, brand-new daily abilities and spell slots were available immediately without a rest.
  • Caeto cleared the party and deputized them to investigate, with free movement of the manor but no leaving. One of his own guards had been killed — "Someone or something killed one of my guards. I'm convinced there's more going on here." He handed over a scroll of Talking Corpse.
  • Zanthar converted the commission into a plan before anyone else spoke: "Do you need help interrogating the guy that we captured alive?"
  • Lefranek Meyal was hog-tied with the three dead. She was not afraid to die, which gutted Intimidation; Zanthar and Cyrathul both clocked it on a Perception check. Cyrathul pivoted to a threat that fit her actual fear — telling her own people she'd betrayed them.
  • She gave up the cause: "the first step in freeing Isger from the tyranny of Cheliax and delivering it to the Bountiful Lady of Harvests." Cyrathul's Religion check placed it — Urgathoa, goddess of undeath, whose statue stands at the capital's entrance because the original Isgeri tribes read her as a harvest goddess.
  • Her handler was "the man in the red cloak." Beyond that, nothing — Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore crit success confirmed her memory had been magically altered.
  • Cyrathul read the Talking Corpse scroll aloud and ruled the party out of using it — it recalls latent memories, precisely the thing that was tampered with. Panacea had read out its activation rules to get there, and closed it: "Don't want to waste it."
  • Cyrathul asked why the red cloak survived an edit that erased everything else — "is it a red herring?" Bai Jian offered the other reading: "Maybe it's kind of a way to identify." A planted signature, not an oversight.
  • The halfling servant Bai Jian had been kind to — Otton Nettlebrush — came back by name: "do you remember you were kind to me earlier?" He reported that a woman he could not name had come out of a back supply closet. Inside were two items: a Staff of the Dead and an intricately carved onyx skull. Cyrathul moved both to the party stash; neither has been identified.
  • Zanthar asked the Hellknights the question nobody had put to them: how did these people get past you? The answer — some of the guards turned, and were killed in the fighting.

The False Hellbreakers were level 1 (AC 15–16, attack +7), and their saps were agile and nonlethal: for all the "everyone here will die," the melee attackers were swinging to subdue. Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Blade That Bows First

  • Started the first fight by refusing to watch a servant die. With a Mammon priestess's velstrac strangling a man in front of a ballroom, he asked Cyrathul whether to intervene, got a nod, and stepped in with open hands: "Let's just enjoy the party. There's no need for violence here." The Aid crit-failed and his own check failed after it. He did it anyway, and the servant lived.
  • Then immediately enforced restraint on everyone else. "Let's try not to kill them." When Cyrathul pointed out nobody would miss a random imp, his counter was tactical, not squeamish: "Yeah, but it draws attention."
  • Turned down a buff to protect the operation. Panacea offered Guidance mid-scene; he refused — "I don't know if casting magic here at the party would be a good idea" — and asked for a trained ally to Aid him with words instead.
  • Found the night's most valuable intel and gave it away. His Perception Discovery on Caeto uncovered the Saringallow angle that won the Hellknight over. He handed it to the party rather than spend it, and said why: "I already talked to him once, and I don't want to repeat my humiliation." He went 2-for-4 on the night and spent the misses scouting other people's marks.
  • Ran a genuinely clever social con. Rather than out-talk Colonel Rithert, he played smaller than he is: "I don't think I'm very charismatic, you see… I'll just follow the lead of someone like you." A man who fights better than anyone in the room, performing incompetence to flatter a desk officer. His own read: "I said lie to his face successfully."
  • Was kind to a servant for no reason, and it paid off hours later. He checked on a halfling scrubbing wine off a wall. In Act VII, that same halfling walked into a room full of Hellknights specifically to find Bai Jian and handed the party its only physical lead. He also rules-lawyered for the DM mid-combat on imp sizing.
  • Combat: a 44-damage non-lethal crit to end Encounter 1. In Encounter 2, a Sudden Charge through a stampede, a Snagging Strike that made Lefranek off-guard to the entire party, Ren sent up the stairs into a femoral artery, and a 33-damage crit to close the fight.

Plus Weapon Mastery (Sword): master with the falcata. His justification: "Let's just say Bai Jian has some best dwarf friends as he grown up and learned a thing or two."
