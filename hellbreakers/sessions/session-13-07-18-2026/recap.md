## Session Summary

The party spent the back half of the New Year's gala at Reeves Rest Manor doing what they came to do: working the room. Four more rounds of influence and discovery, and a great deal of champagne. Bai Jian scouted rather than pushed — he found that Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex was born near Saringallow and handed the intel off rather than spend it himself, and Cyrathul converted it into a hero-pointed crit that won Caeto outright. Panacea gossiped her way into Ezio Daeta's confidence over cognac and pork-belly tarts, and her own hero-pointed crit early on handed all four of them a bonus to every discovery check for the rest of the night. Zanthar quietly ran the entire Quoster Thistlewait arc — both discoveries and the close — after everyone else had bounced off the halfling.

Twice the evening broke. The first time was the Widow Queen — a Mammon priestess five centuries old — whose velstrac bodyguard lifted a servant off the floor by the throat over a splash of spilled wine. Bai Jian stepped in unarmed. Three imps dropped their trays, and the party fought a combat in ballgowns under a constraint they'd set themselves: nobody was supposed to die at this party, because a corpse would be remembered. The Widow Queen got bored, dropped the servant, and walked out. The crowd applauded.

The second break was not survivable by politeness. A masked woman raised a flag none of them recognized — a flaming sword through Asmodeus' pentagram — declared herself a Hellbreaker, and ordered the room killed. The party knew immediately these were impostors, and knew just as immediately what a massacre committed under their own banner would cost them. So they fought a second battle inside a stampeding crowd, deliberately taking the leader alive. Then Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex appeared at the top of the stairs, bloodied, and announced that Steward Hedvind VI had been murdered. The manor sealed. Everyone under arrest. Caeto — having watched the party defend a room full of Chelaxian nobles — deputized them to solve it. The prisoner's memories turned out to have been surgically edited, leaving only a man in a red cloak and a name: the Bountiful Lady of Harvests. Urgathoa. And when the party finally reached the bedroom where the steward died, the bed was empty.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Gala, Rounds 3–6
- The party returned indoors after the dart tournament. **Panacea** recapped the night's intel from her own notes: Rithert is jealous of combat veterans but melts at flattery; Ezio Daeta is weak to Society and Diplomacy; Sasatoli Bronc folds to Intimidation.
- **Bai Jian** worked **Colonel Rithert** on the "you belong in the field" angle — a success. **Ambassador Frigg**, standing beside him, frowned; he has actually seen battle, and Rithert's posturing grates.
- **Bai Jian** then took the scouting lane rather than crowd anyone's mark, and ran a **Perception Discovery on Caeto Vulaunex** — the single highest-value find of the encounter: Caeto was **born near Saringallow** and is fond of it. He passed it straight to the party: *"My job here is to get the intel to the other members of the party. Because I already talked to him once, and I don't want to repeat my humiliation."*
- **Cyrathul** converted that intel immediately, strolling up to the Hellknight as a fellow local asking after restaurants, then **spending a hero point** on a **Saringallow Lore** check — a **crit success**, about roast pork. It won Caeto outright.
- **Panacea** hero-pointed a Society check on **Ezio Daeta** into a **crit success**, unlocking a party-wide **+1 circumstance bonus to all discovery checks** at the gala — mechanically the biggest single unlock of the night.
- **Zanthar** intimidated a waiter into hauling an entire tray over to Sasatoli — using a hostile skill as a gift-delivery mechanism — and ran a Society discovery that found **Quoster's lowest influence skills: mercantile lore and diplomacy.**
- **Cyrathul** tried the mercantile-lore angle on Quoster and **missed**. **Bai Jian** tried a cold approach on Quoster's fashion and **also missed** — Quoster isn't actually knowledgeable about clothes and read the flattery as insincere. Cyrathul passed him on the way out and quietly patted him on the shoulder.
- **Bai Jian** noticed a halfling servant in an ill-fitting imp costume scrubbing a wall after Ezio hurled a tray at it, and was simply kind to him: *"Why are you saying sorry to me? Well, just be careful."* *(This pays off in Act III.)*
- **Bai Jian** closed **Rithert** with a deliberately self-effacing gambit — *"I don't think I'm very charismatic, you see."*
- **Panacea** — having heard about Ezio's tantrum — brought him a fresh pork-belly tart to soothe it. A success that unlocked a **checkpoint: crit failures against Ezio now downgrade to ordinary failures.**
- **Zanthar** ran a second discovery on Quoster and found his real **weakness: anti-Cheliax framing** (the slavery history, and Cheliax's grip on the Carnecia River trade routes) — then used it himself with Diplomacy and **closed him**. **+1 Hellbreakers League reputation**, and Quoster promised a special badge after the gala. Cyrathul had failed three separate attempts on the halfling; Zanthar landed it.
- Quoster then asked the question that ended the evening: *"Where is Hedvind? He was supposed to come down and make an announcement."*

### The Widow Queen
- A cry cut off in a gurgle: a **velstrac** held a human servant off the floor by the throat. Its mistress — an imperial woman in a gown worth "more than your life" — watched, grinning.
- Nobody could place her. Zanthar's Society crit success turned up nothing (*"none of you recognize who she is"*), and Bai Jian declined outright: *"Bai Jian doesn't know shit about magic."* It was **Panacea's Religion check** that named the magic pouring off her: a **high priestess of the arch-devil Mammon**, rumored **over 500 years old** by devil-pact.
- **Bai Jian** asked Cyrathul's permission, got a nod, and stepped between them anyway. **Panacea** offered Guidance and he refused it — casting magic at a Chelaxian gala was not worth the risk.
- Cyrathul moved in to **Aid** and **critically failed**, actively making the check *worse* by 1 — for the first time in his life, per the DM, he stumbled over his words. Bai Jian's Diplomacy then failed on top of it.
- *"You dare? You dare. I don't think you know who you are talking to."* Three tray-carrying imps dropped everything.

### Act II — Aftermath and the Toast That Never Came
- The Widow Queen tired of the fight, dropped the servant (who revived), and swept out with her velstrac. **The crowd applauded the party.**
- The guests filled in the gap: she is the **Widow Queen**, of **Iron Rose Manor** in the **Menador Mountains**. **She is also the silent, magic-radiating woman Zanthar clocked at the ball last session** — the DM carried his S12 Society crit forward rather than re-rolling it, and even that crit turned up nothing. The party only learned who she was from crowd gossip *after* she'd walked out.
- **Fessy Finwinkle**, a shy halfling priestess of Abadar in white-and-gold, introduced herself and offered healing. **Panacea recognized her from the hospital** where she'd worked during downtime.
- **Caeto Vulaunex** handed over a **diplomat's badge** — awarded to **Cyrathul**, explicitly for his crit success. *(The DM first narrated it to Panacea for her mid-combat healing, then retracted and re-issued it twice.)*
- Cognac replaced champagne. **Renacilos Aziri** — the legionnaire ringer who won the darts contest — and **Quoster** settled into a private, escalating drinking contest that ran the rest of the night.

### The False Flag
- The music stopped. A masked woman: *"For far too long, you and Hedvind have been Cheliax' lapdogs... It is time for everyone here to die in the name of the Hellbreakers League."*
- She raised a flag depicting **a flaming sword piercing Asmodeus' red pentagram** — a banner none of the party had ever seen. Masked compatriots attacked.
- **Bai Jian**, immediately: *"Fucking fake Hellbreakers."*
- **Zanthar** was the only one to make the secret Perception check — **every guard in the room had quietly vanished** in the preceding minutes. **+1 initiative to the party.**
- **Zanthar** then drove the verification that made the frame-up legible: there is no second Hellbreaker cell in the city. *"There may be Hellbreakers and another group of Hellbreakers that have their own group. We're the only ones."*
- Doctrine was set before initiative: **Cyrathul** — they could not be seen to have massacred a party full of sympathizers; **Zanthar** — *"We need to have some of them alive, though, so that they can be questioned."*

### Act III — Arrest, Commission, Interrogation
- **Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex** appeared at the top of the stairs with battered Hellknights: **"Hedvind VI, steward of all Isger, has been murdered."** The manor's magical defenses had activated; everyone was under arrest and forbidden to leave.
- **600 XP** awarded for the social encounter (15 points at 40 XP each) — **the party hit Level 5**, 480 XP into the next. Per the AP, brand-new daily abilities and spell slots were available immediately without a rest.
- Caeto summoned the party to a private study. One of *his own guards* had been killed — *"Someone or something killed one of my guards. I'm convinced there's more going on here."* He cleared them of the murder and **deputized them to investigate**, with free movement of the manor and grounds but no leaving. He handed over a **scroll of Talking Corpse**. **Zanthar** was the one who converted the commission into a plan: *"Do you need help interrogating the guy that we captured alive?"*
- The captured leader — **Lefranek Meyal** — was hog-tied in a holding room with the three dead. She was not afraid to die, which made Intimidation nearly useless; Zanthar and Cyrathul both clocked it on a Perception check. Cyrathul pivoted to a threat that fit her actual fear — telling her own people she'd betrayed them.
- She gave up the cause: *"the first step in freeing Isger from the tyranny of Cheliax and delivering it to the Bountiful Lady of Harvests."* **Cyrathul's** Religion check placed it: **Urgathoa**, goddess of undeath — whose statue stands at the capital's entrance, plaque and all, because the original Isgeri tribes read her as a harvest goddess.
- Her handler was **"the man in the red cloak."** Beyond that: nothing. **Cyrathul's** Esoteric Lore **crit success** confirmed why — **her memory has been magically altered.**
- Cyrathul turned that on her: if they edited the meeting, what else did they edit? She refused to follow the thought.
- **Cyrathul** read the Talking Corpse scroll aloud and **ruled the party out of using it** — it recalls *latent memories*, which is precisely what was tampered with. The scroll was saved. **Panacea** had read out its activation rules to get there, and closed it: *"Don't want to waste it."*
- **Bai Jian** supplied the session's sharpest deduction. Cyrathul asked why the red cloak survived an edit that erased everything else — *"is it a red herring?"* — and Bai Jian offered the other reading: *"Maybe it's kind of a way to identify."* A planted signature, not an oversight.
- The halfling servant Bai Jian had been kind to — **Otton Nettlebrush** — came back on his own, by name: *"do you remember you were kind to me earlier?"* He reported that a woman he could not name had come out of a back supply closet earlier. Inside were **two items: a Staff of the Dead**, and an **intricately carved onyx skull**. **Cyrathul** moved both to the party stash. Neither has been identified.
- **Zanthar** asked the Hellknights the question nobody had put to them: *how did these people get past you?* The answer — **some of the guards turned**, and were killed in the fighting.
- The party went upstairs to the **Steward's apartments**. Past the boudoir, through the door to the murder room —

> **"There's no body laying on the bed. The body of Hedvind VI seems to be missing."**

## Character Spotlight

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Three Iron Rose Imps (and one velstrac that never joined)

**Initiative:** Iron Rose Imp → Iron Rose Imp → Cyrathul → Bai Jian → Iron Rose Imp → Panacea → Zanthar

**The constraint that defined the fight:** the party was in costume at a Chelaxian gala, undercover, surrounded by people they had spent all night cultivating. **Bai Jian set the doctrine — *"Let's try not to kill them"* — and he and Zanthar were the ones who actually executed it.** Cyrathul argued the other way and bit lethally throughout; Panacea had no non-lethal option and chose to sit out offense entirely. All three imps ended up unconscious rather than dead because the *finishing* blow in each case happened to be non-lethal. The velstrac never entered the fight — it spent the encounter holding the servant off the ground.

**Round 1**
- **Imp 1** flew at **Bai Jian** (he had spoken to the Widow Queen first) and stung for **2** plus poison — Bai Jian made the Fortitude save (**24**) and shrugged it off. Its second sting missed.
- **Imp 2** went for a flank on **Cyrathul** and ran into the Tiny-creature rule: with no reach, it had to share a space to attack at all. It stung **Cyrathul for 5**; he saved, and its follow-up missed.
- **Cyrathul** spent an action on **Exploit Vulnerability** and hit **mortal weakness**. His chomp missed — the imp fluttered clear — but the agile **claw** landed for **13**, bumped to **16** by the weakness.
- **Bai Jian** repositioned to keep the imp in his space, took **Ren's Support benefit** as the hound's own action, and struck **non-lethally for 9**. Realizing mid-fight that imps are fiends — Ren's designated foe — he went back and added the Support rider's **1d6 piercing (6)**, for **15**. His second Strike missed.
- **Imp 3** dove in on **Cyrathul** (the DM first called it toward Serrafina, who was sharing his space), hit for **3**, and this time the poison took: Cyrathul failed the save (**14**), ate **8 poison damage**, and picked up **Clumsy 1**. Its second attack was a critical miss.
- **Panacea** declined to attack rather than reveal herself as a caster. She used **Doctor's Visitation** to stride and **Battle Medicine** Cyrathul for **6** in a single action, strode back to Serrafina, and cast **Guidance** on Cyrathul — then made sure Ezio Daeta overheard her say she can't stand to see people hurt.
- **Zanthar** spent an action on the **Nonlethal Spell** spellshape and fired a two-action **Blazing Bolt**, both rays. Ray one hit for **11** and dropped Cyrathul's imp; ray two hit for **17** and dropped Bai Jian's. **Two imps unconscious.**

**Round 2**
- Both downed imps were skipped — **no enemy acted this round.**
- **Cyrathul**, still Clumsy, chomped the last imp for **19** — Panacea's **Guidance** was what carried the roll to a hit — then missed with both claws. At the end of his turn he **crit-succeeded** the lingering poison save (**29**) and shed **Clumsy** and the poison outright.
- **Bai Jian** closed and **crit non-lethally for 44** on a fatal-d12 falcata. The DM's ruling: *"it's amazing that it's not dead."* Knocked cold, not killed. The third imp never got a Round-2 turn.

**Damage ledger:** the party took **18 total** (Bai Jian 2; Cyrathul 16, with 6 healed back). **KOs:** Zanthar 2, Bai Jian 1 (after Cyrathul softened it for 19). **No hero points spent.** **Damage dealt: Bai Jian 53 · Cyrathul 35 · Zanthar 28 · Ren 6.**

**The imps:** **AC 19, attack +11, Imp Venom DC 18** (1d6+2 poison plus Clumsy 1) — level 2 creatures, three of them. **Foundry priced the encounter at 60 XP — "Low."** The party was never in danger; the constraint was entirely social.

**Resolution:** the Widow Queen lost interest — *"What a waste of my time. We're leaving."* — dropped the servant, who began breathing immediately, and left with her velstrac. The ballroom applauded. A servant collected the unconscious imps on a tray and carried them back to the kitchen.

### Encounter 2 — Lefranek Meyal and the False Hellbreakers

**Initiative:** Cyrathul → Lefranek Meyal → False Hellbreaker → Bai Jian → False Hellbreaker → False Hellbreaker → Zanthar → Panacea
*(Zanthar's secret Perception on the vanished guards gave the party +1 initiative.)*

**The battlefield:** crossbow archers on the balcony and stairs, and **the gala crowd itself as a complex environmental hazard** — the entire floor difficult terrain, rolling a d12 each round for either **Crushing Press** (2d8 bludgeoning, basic Fortitude) or **Wild Dismay** (Will save or **confused**). It required **4 successes** to disperse. **Lefranek's False Flag** aura imposed a **−1 circumstance penalty to saves and DCs** within 60 feet, with no save.

**Round 1**
- **Cyrathul** tried to Exploit Vulnerability and found nothing, so he took **Personal Antithesis** instead — 4 extra damage on every hit he landed. He deployed the **Mirror Cloak** and stepped out of it *behind* Lefranek: jaws for **21**. Two Cyrathuls on the board.
- **Lefranek** denounced the room (**False Flag**), then drove her lance into the **real** Cyrathul for **7**; her second attack missed. *(The DM spent a solid minute fighting her lance-and-sickle stat block before ruling "I'm just going to give her the lance.")*
- A **False Hellbreaker** with a billy club swung three times at the Cyrathul who had just materialized beside him and hit nothing but scales.
- **The crowd** rolled **Crushing Press**. **Bai Jian saved and took 6**; Cyrathul and Panacea took **12** each.
- **Bai Jian** **Sudden Charged** through the stampede to reach Lefranek — **9** on the charge, **6** on the follow-up, flanking established. Ren could only wade partway through the crush. He forgot Snagging Strike entirely, and said so.
- **Archers**: one bolt skidded off Bai Jian's full plate; the next missed and **killed a noblewoman** behind him. A third passed Cyrathul's mirror and **killed a second guest**.
- **Zanthar** moved, drew, and threw his hammer with **Hand of the Apprentice** — into the balcony rail. He spent a **hero point**; the reroll hit the rail too. The DM gave the point back.

**Round 2**
- **Cyrathul** **Demoralized** Lefranek to **Frightened 1**, then missed with both chomp and claw. He spent no action refreshing the **Mirror Cloak**, and it lapsed — from here he fought as one dragon.
- **Lefranek** denounced again and connected with her lance on Bai Jian for **6**: *"Die, Chelaxian slave!"*

- **The crowd** rolled **Wild Dismay**. **Panacea** failed and **spent a hero point** to reroll into a success; everyone else shook it off.
- **Bai Jian** opened Lefranek up for **16**, commanded **Ren** in for the **Support benefit**, then made the follow-up a **Snagging Strike** for **8** — leaving her **off-guard to the entire party** until his next turn — and spent his third action on **Dueling Parry**. Cyrathul's Frightened was still riding on her, so that Snagging Strike landed against an AC of **18** rather than 21. The DM: *"she's still standing, but barely — a huge gushing wound on the front of her chest."*
- An archer **dropped his crossbow**, came down the stairs, and missed the back of Cyrathul's head; another put a bolt into Cyrathul's side beneath his wing for **5**. A third shot dropped a noble lady in the crowd — wounded, not killed.
- **Zanthar** cast **non-lethal Blazing Bolt**, two rays, and put the first into the enemy nearest death — **Lefranek** — for **18**, straight into the back of her skull. She went down **unconscious instead of dead**. The second ray hit a masked attacker for **15**. *"I wanted to take the leader guy alive."*

**Round 3**
- **Cyrathul** bit the wounded False Hellbreaker and **tore his arm completely off** — **19**. He **Demoralized** the next one and opened his chest with a claw for **14**.
- **The crowd** rolled **Wild Dismay** again. Bai Jian and Panacea held; **Cyrathul** failed and burned a **hero point** to reroll into a success; **Zanthar** failed outright and spent the rest of the fight **confused**.
- **Bai Jian** sent **Ren** up the stairs. The boarhound latched onto an archer's groin and took out the femoral artery — **8** damage and a spray of blood, **dead**. Bai Jian charged up after him and **crit** the last archer with a **Snagging Strike**, the falcata's fatal d12 turning it into **33 damage in one swing**: *"I crit this guy. Goodbye."*

**Resolution:** over in **3 rounds**. Lefranek absorbed **78 damage** across two rounds and lived only because the last hit was deliberately non-lethal. **Zanthar** went looking for someone to **stabilize** and found nobody — the other three were already dead. **Lefranek survived** as the party's prisoner, and the entire basis of Act III. With the fight over, **Panacea's Calm** finished dispersing what was left of the crowd.

**Hero points:** three spends — Zanthar (Round 1, refunded), Panacea (Round 2), Cyrathul (Round 3).
**Damage dealt:** Bai Jian 72 · Cyrathul 54 · Zanthar 33 · Ren 8 · Panacea 0 (by choice).
**Damage taken:** Cyrathul 28 · Bai Jian 12 · Panacea 12 · Zanthar 0. **Civilian dead:** 2, both in Round 1.

**The tuning.** **Lefranek: AC 21, attack +14, Perception +11**, lance (deadly d8, jousting d6, 10-ft reach) — a level-4 creature built High across every defensive axis, but hitting for only 1d8+4. A wall with a megaphone. The **False Hellbreakers were level 1** (AC 15–16, attack +7), and notably their saps were **agile and *nonlethal*** — for all the "everyone here will die," the melee attackers were swinging to subdue.

**Foundry priced it at 85 XP — "Severe."** The creatures alone decompose to Moderate (`40 + 15×3 = 85`); the gap is the **Gala Crowd**, which rolled its own initiative and threw DC 21 saves every round but contributed no XP. Counted as the level-4 complex hazard it was, the encounter lands at ~125 — genuinely Severe. And the numbers bear that out: **the crowd dealt 30 of the 52 damage the party took — more than all four enemies combined (22).** Panacea was the only one fighting it.

*(One break in the party's favor: Panacea **critically failed** her Crushing Press save in Round 1, which should have doubled the damage to 24. The DM applied the standard 12.)*
