## Session Summary

The crossbows at the top of the orphanage stairs turned out to be the easy part. The Children's Charity — the thieves' guild fronting as the Old House of the Found Family — got its surprise round and put two arrows into **Zanthar** the instant he peeked around the corner, but the fight was over almost before it started: **Bai Jian** opened with a **63-damage non-lethal crit** that folded one rogue in a single flat-of-the-blade Slam Down, **Zanthar** climbed a bunk bed and dropped a **Dizzying Colors** cone that stunned and blinded the room, and when **"the father" himself** — **Father Kindness** — stepped out shouting *"My children!"*, Bai Jian answered with a **second non-lethal crit (59)** and put him down too. Every blow was pulled. Nobody died.

And then the session turned, because this party doesn't win fights so much as it wins *people*. With Father Kindness manacled and his adopted son crit-Intimidated into dropping his sword, the party healed the old thief awake and — instead of interrogating him — **recruited him**. He steals only from Chelaxians; the Hellbreakers *are* the uprising; the math did itself. Father Kindness disarmed his own bedroom trap, opened his chest, and handed over not just the stolen **Lazy Bones pendant** but a pile of **artwork** "as a contribution to the cause," and a standing offer of intel on which noble estates are easy pickings. Two uprising points, a thieves' guild turned asset, and not a single body. The party is, as they kept saying all night, *really good at this rebellion thing*.

The back half was preparation and payoff. A day of shopping and downtime in Elidir (scrolls, sleeves of storage, a banned book fenced with a straight face), a **goblin pickpocket** Bai Jian covered for and quietly paid off under a guard's nose, and a costume-shop confrontation where a preening noble named **Frederick Consum** tried to strong-arm the party out of their gala outfits — until his own bodyguard eyeballed Bai Jian's sword and refused to fight, and Frederick slunk off berating his men. Then the main event: the **New Year's Eve ball** at the Regent's manor, a Chelaxian celebration of the conquest of Isger, where the party — in devil costumes, Lazy Bones pendant around Cyrathul's neck — went to close the deal the founders couldn't. The gala is a slow social duel (PF2e's influence subsystem), and the party opened strong: **Cyrathul** worked the merchant **Quoster** with a razor-sharp bit of social sabotage, **Panacea** gossiped her way into a magic-loving noble's good graces, **Zanthar** bullied a lower-class hanger-on into liking him, and **Bai Jian** shot his way to second place in a darts contest against a ringer legionnaire while a humiliated colonel stormed off. Session pauses mid-gala — three more influence rounds to go, and the deal not yet closed.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Guild on the Stairs (06:00–37:00) — *Combat, non-lethal*

- **The surprise round.** Picking up on the S11 cliffhanger, the party crept up the orphanage stairs and **Zanthar popped his head around the corner** — straight into a purpose-built defensive nook (a bunk bed with an arrow slit). The Children's Charity Housebreakers got a **surprise round** (a rogue ability leaving the party off-guard the whole first round) and put **two arrows into Zanthar (16 + 9 = 25 damage)**.
- **Bai Jian's opener.** He moved **Ren** up (the hound tumbled through toward the rogues — a dagger flashed over his head, missing), then **tumbled through behind the enemies** himself and led with a **Slam Down**. It crit. Asked to make it non-lethal, Bai Jian rerolled straight damage: **63**. The DM: *"that's going to zero them."* Flat of the blade, *tink* — a *"thin little waif of a girl"* crumpled instantly. No trip needed; she was out.
- **"My children!"** A deep voice boomed and **Father Kindness** — the guild's leader — stepped out from behind the next bunk. He **Feinted** Bai Jian (off-guard) and stabbed, his dagger sliding along the backside of the armor — **a miss**.
- **Panacea** moved in, **Battle-Medicined** Bai Jian's wounds, then cast **Trade Death for Life** — void damage into Father Kindness (a **failed** basic Fort save, 16 vs DC 20; the 1d4 barely fazed him, *"doesn't seem to take hold"*) that routed **fast healing to the arrow-riddled Zanthar**. Her **Familiar of Paired Perplexity Stupefied him regardless** — casting the hex is enough.
- **Zanthar's control play.** *"I am small — maybe I can stand on top of the bed."* He scurried up onto the bunk (Athletics) and, from the high ground, dropped a **Dizzying Colors** cone onto two enemies: the remaining Housebreaker was **Stunned 1, Blinded (2 rounds), and Dazzled**. Bai Jian, watching: *"Stunned and blind. And dazzled afterward."*
- **Cyrathul drops in.** Snowgeen loaded in mid-round — *"What happened?" "We're in combat, it's about to be your turn."* He crossed the room, **Exploited Vulnerability on Father Kindness** (*"nothing to speak of of note"* — these rogues have no weakness, same as the kitchen pair in S11), and **non-lethally chomped** him (eating the –2).
- **The finisher.** Round 2, Bai Jian **Commanded Ren** for the **Support** rider (+1 to hit, foe-scent bonus damage) and swung — **another non-lethal falcata crit: 59** (`2*(2d12+4)+1d12`). *"Oh my lord."* Father Kindness went down, alive.
- **The son surrenders.** Blinded and stunned, the last Housebreaker flailed at the party by guesswork (attacks vs a flat DC — all missed), panicking — *"father, father, are you there?"* Watching the party beat unconscious a house of orphans over a necklace, **Bai Jian** said the quiet part out loud: *"I'm feeling very guilty right now."* **Cyrathul crit-Intimidated the son** (Intimidating Glare, critical success): he **dropped his sword immediately**, hands up — *"Did you kill my dad?"* Cyrathul talked him to his knees and manacled him.
- **Encounter ends.** Three enemies subdued, zero dead. The party tied up Father Kindness and healed him conscious to talk.

### Act II — A Contribution to the Cause (37:00–48:00) — *The win-over*

- **Panacea opened honestly**: they came for the stolen **Lazy Bones pendant** (a blue ribbon left in its place), were attacked on sight, and had answered non-lethally throughout. Father Kindness, unimpressed: *"Would you not attack someone that broke into your home?"* — then, on the pendant: *"We steal from all Chelaxians."*
- **The pivot.** Bai Jian: *"You heard of the Hellbreakers?"* Father Kindness lit up — *"Uprisers, yes… at some point there'll be a chapter here."* **Cyrathul** closed it: *"We happen to be the perpetrators of such activities. And we are looking to expand the scope."* A **Diplomacy check** (Panacea's **Guidance** stacked on) won him fully over.
- **The donation.** *"Untie me, and I will undo the trap in my room, and you are more than welcome to it."* Father Kindness disarmed his own bedroom trap, opened his chest, and handed over **the Lazy Bones pendant** *and* **valuable artwork** — *"as a contribution to the cause"* — plus a standing offer: keep him in mind for *"easy pickings in any of the Chelaxian nobles' estates."* His parting grace: *"had you not… I would congratulate my own children on such a successful theft."*
- **Reward.** Recruiting the guild banked **+2 uprising points**, pushing the party to **5** — unlocking a second one-time capital bonus (**+1 to an attack roll or saving throw after the roll**, on top of the existing +1 to a skill/perception check). Father Kindness went to check on his "children," healing potions in hand.

### Act III — Downtime in Elidir (48:00–83:00) — *Shopping, Earn Income, a small mercy*

- **Briefing.** Back at the safehouse, the party caught **Cyrathul** up and got the full picture from the DM: **Quoster Thistleweight**, the wealthy halfling merchant the founders had been courting — *"believes in charity and hates Cheliax,"* wants a free Isger — was on the cusp of funding the League for post-liberation trade rights when Hedvind outed the founders. He got cold feet; the deal now has to close at the **New Year's Eve ball** at the Regent's palace, five days out — the Lazy Bones pendant is the recognition token so Quoster knows who to trust.
- **Shopping.** Elidir is a level-8 city. The party sold the artwork and fenced a **banned book** — the *Abergale the First* script, contraband — which **Cyrathul deception-sold** for **150 gold** on a cover story Bai Jian floated (*"I just found it in my hoard"*; the shopkeep: *"I don't believe you, but I want this book"*). **Zanthar** bought **four Sure Strike scrolls** and **sleeves of storage** (*"I need to get to level five — that's a lot of things to buy"*); **Cyrathul** grabbed Sure Strike scrolls to flick out with his implement. The DM redistributed **Sammy's leftover gold** to the party.
- **The goblin pickpocket.** Earning income and gathering info in the streets, a **hungry goblin girl** lifted the gold from Bai Jian's pocket — and the **town guard** saw it. Bai Jian covered for her instantly: *"I was just about to give her this,"* palming her a **gold coin**, and waved the captain off — *"do you think I would leave someone alone who tried to steal from me?"*, a hand drifting to his sword. The guard bought it and moved on. The goblin, near tears, tried to **bite the coin in half to give some back** (*"I only need a little bit"*), then vanished into the crowd — *"I would do anything for you."* **+1 uprising point.**
- **Earn Income & Gather Info.** **Panacea** worked shifts at the **hospital helping sick kids**; Bai Jian earned income and gathered intel (a celebrity bard, **Lazenia**, is the talk of the town; the ball is at **Reeves Rest Manor**, the Regent's state house, two stories, multiple entrances). **Zanthar** tried to help at a scroll station and **spilled ink over the day's work** — no pay.

### Act IV — Dress to Impress (92:00–108:00) — *De-escalation*

- **Costume pickup, interrupted.** At **Brightgate's** shop the party walked into a shouting match: **Frederick Consum**, a self-important noble with two bodyguards, was trying to **seize the party's finished costumes** for himself — *"Do you know how much weight I have in all of Isgard?… or I will have my two gentlemen beat you to a bloody pulp."*
- **Cyrathul, dry as ever:** *"I mean, if you're that heavy, maybe you should cut off the carbs."* The party refused to budge (*"Those are ours. We paid for those."*).
- **The bluff breaks.** Bai Jian and Cyrathul overheard a **bodyguard whisper**: *"Boss, did you see that guy's sword? I don't want to tangle with him."* Frederick folded — **shoulder-checked Bai Jian** (*"like shoulder-checking a brick wall"*), got nowhere, and stormed out berating his men (*"what did I hire you for?"*).
- **Reward.** The grateful tailor pressed a **potency rune** and a **quick-change (raiment) rune** on the party for free — Bai Jian took both (potency on his armor, the raiment to disguise it). Costumes finalized: Bai Jian as an **Armengur Hell Knight**, Cyrathul as a **chain devil** (over his real wings), Panacea in a **fur-matched blue devil dress** with ear-sparing horns, Zanthar as a **tiny Angeval devil**, and **Ren as a hellhound**. **XP and the two free runes** for handling it without blood — but Frederick will be at the ball tonight.

### Act V — The New Year's Eve Ball (115:00–end) — *Influence encounter (in progress)*

- **The send-off.** The Demetriases prepped the party and left town loudly on the opposite end — a decoy exit (their departed **bard friend** already started a diversionary *"kerfuffle"*) to make it look like the League fled, buying the party cover to *become* the League's presence in the capital. **Objective:** find **Quoster**, close the funding deal, and seed a Hellbreakers chapter in Elidir. **Cyrathul wore the Lazy Bones pendant** as the recognition token (Zanthar volunteered too — *"I have been known to talk"*).
- **The room.** Reeves Rest Manor, floating red-and-white lights, a string quartet, guests in Hell-themed finery, guarded stairs to a forbidden upper landing. The ball is a **six-round influence duel** over six hours; five NPCs can be influenced (**Quoster, Iso Data, Colonel Rithert, Cato Volnax, Sasatoli Bronc**), plus un-influenceable minor guests.
- **Round zero — meeting the room.**
  - **Cyrathul** was steered straight to **Quoster** — a giggling halfling fop with a **levitating ruby cane** ("worth a small country") — and traded undead-silk small talk.
  - **Panacea** met **Iso Data**, a big ruddy noble whose **magical cape conjures an iron throne** behind him wherever he goes (an order from **Tilaax**).
  - **Zanthar** got cornered by **Mirapila Carafo**, a bored, casually-cruel noble (*"they should just know their lot in life"*), and clocked a **silent, imperial woman radiating immense magic** he couldn't identify.
  - **Bai Jian** flattered his way through drake-hunting shop talk and an audience with **Lazenia**, the self-worshipping celebrity bard.
- **Round 1 — first moves.**
  - **Cyrathul influenced Quoster** with a scalpel: praising Cheliax's *"great institutions… like slavery — sorry, I mean debt,"* then **glaring at Quoster** to isolate the halfling from the pro-slavery nobles around him. Hero point spent → **success, +1 influence.** The DM: *"the glare does have an effect… he hates it here, but he's smiling and pretending he doesn't."*
  - **Panacea** landed a **crit Discover** on Iso Data (Society) — his two lowest influence skills (Diplomacy, Society).
  - **Zanthar** befriended **Sasatoli Bronc**, a lower-class dock-worker dragooned in as "representation," by eating and drinking alongside him — and **Discovered** his lowest influence skill (Intimidation, of all things).
  - **Bai Jian Discovered Colonel Rithert** — and hit a wall: the colonel is *"jealous of those who have seen combat,"* so any warrior who talks battle takes **–2**. (Bai Jian, a career warrior: *"good thing, I think I can still spin it around."*)
- **Round 2 — pressing.**
  - **Cyrathul influenced Quoster again** (hero point) → **success**, and the win **earned the Hellbreakers League a reputation point**. *"I got it from a mutual friend,"* he said of the pendant.
  - **Panacea influenced Iso Data** with **gossip** — a *"gossipy look like she's enjoying the tea,"* +2 circumstance, hero point — trading dirt about Cato just returning from Cheliax. **Success → influence point on Iso.**
  - **Zanthar intimidated Sasatoli Bronc** into liking him (*"better be careful how much food you're eating — these rich people, they come after you"*). *"You're a chip off the old block,"* Bronc laughed.
  - **Bai Jian** tried to butter up the Hell Knight **Cato Volnax** — bored him, no dice.
- **Manticore (the darts contest).** A garden game: three straw targets (halfling 20 ft, goblin 40 ft, human 60 ft; AC 18; crits and double-throws double points). Contestants: **Ambassador Frigg**, **Colonel Rithert**, **Renacilos Aziri**, and **Bai Jian** (invited in).
  - **Colonel Rithert** whiffed **all six darts**, storming off humiliated. Bai Jian, flat: *"Your money's gone."*
  - **Renacilos Aziri** — a legionnaire ringer — hit everything for the win (Cyrathul's suspicious Perception check found no cheating, just skill).
  - **Bai Jian** shot **16 points for second place** despite darts being off-stat for him (*"I'm actually not that good"*), earning **Frigg's lucky silver dart**. **Zanthar** then took a turn and **tied Bai Jian for second**, pocketing a second silver dart.
  - Because Bai Jian competed, the party got a **bonus influence round on Rithert**: Bai Jian **Discovered his weakness** (flattery — being urged back into the field, +2), and **Zanthar influenced him** on it (Diplomacy +2 → **success, influence point on Rithert**).
  - **Cyrathul lost 20 gold** betting on Bai Jian (and **Zanthar lost 3** on Rithert); Cyrathul's last-ditch **Deception** to argue his way out of paying (*"isn't he the Hell Knight?"* pointing at the ringer) flopped.
- **Pause.** The guests drift back inside as snow falls again. The influence duel is only partway through — **the Quoster deal is not yet closed**, and more rounds run next session.

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the Blade That Never Draws Blood

Pax's fighter is now defined by the hits he *doesn't* let land. He opened the session by rolling a **63-damage crit** and immediately asking to make it non-lethal — folding a rogue with the flat of the blade instead of splitting her skull — and closed the fight with a **59-damage non-lethal crit** on Father Kindness himself. Two of the biggest swings in the campaign, both pulled on purpose, in a city where one loud death means lockdown. That's the whole character: overwhelming force, held in check by principle.

But the fight was the short part of his night. The through-line of Bai Jian's session was **mercy that reads as strength**. Mid-fight, watching the party beat a house of orphans unconscious over a necklace, he'd already said the quiet part aloud — *"I'm feeling very guilty right now."* And when the guild leader woke in manacles, Bai Jian didn't interrogate — he asked *"You heard of the Hellbreakers?"* and turned a burglary into a recruitment. Ribbed by the DM that the party had *"just broke into your house and murdered your dad,"* he owned it flat: *"Yeah, kind of."* When a starving **goblin girl** picked his pocket in full view of the town guard, he covered for her without a beat — *"I was just about to give her this"* — palmed her a gold coin, and stared the captain down with a hand near his sword: *"do you think I would leave someone alone who tried to steal from me?"* He gave a thief his own gold and made the guard thank him for it. At the gala he played the everyman-in-costume beautifully — trading **real monster-hunting expertise** in drake-hunting shop talk (aim for the eyes and mouth; thick hide resists spear and arrow alike — his family hunts things like this), flattering a celebrity bard, taking **second place in a darts contest** on a stat he doesn't even build for, and roasting a self-important colonel off the field (*"Your money's gone"*). He still hands the big conversations to Cyrathul, but the *plans* and the quiet decency are his, every time.

### Cyrathul · the Dragon Who Reads the Room

Snowgeen was back, and Cyrathul immediately reminded the table why the party can't run a social scene without him. He dropped into the fight a round late and still did real work — **Exploit Vulnerability**, a non-lethal chomp, and the **crit Intimidating Glare** that ended the combat outright (the last rogue dropped his sword on the spot). But his real weapon is his tongue. The **Quoster influence** was the most sophisticated single play of the night: rather than flatter the merchant, Cyrathul praised Cheliax's *"great institutions — like slavery, sorry, I mean debt"* and then **glared at Quoster** to socially isolate the halfling (whose people *are* Cheliax's slaves) from the pro-slavery nobles crowding him — a read of the target's psychology that turned a toast into a recruiting pitch. Twice over, hero points spent, +2 influence and a League reputation point banked.

He's also the party's dry wit. He deflated **Frederick Consum** with *"if you're that heavy, maybe you should cut off the carbs,"* fenced a banned book with a straight face (*"I found it in my hoard"*), and made undead-silk gala chatter sound effortless. Even his losses were in character — betting 20 gold on Bai Jian at the darts, losing, and trying to *deception* his way out of paying up. He is the mouth, the mirror, and the closer.

### Panacea · the Cat Who Keeps Everyone Standing

Bog Witch's healer had a quietly essential session, and a genuinely charming social one. In the fight she was the reason the party could afford to peek around corners into arrow slits — **Battle Medicine** on Bai Jian, **Trade Death for Life** routing fast healing to the arrow-riddled Zanthar while chipping and Stupefying Father Kindness, and a follow-up **Heal** to patch the wizard back up. She's still learning the VTT (a running thread of good-natured *"operator error"*), but she never stops doing her job, and she's the one who keeps insisting the party's fights stay survivable and its captives stay alive. She isn't all softness, either: she led the verbal pushback when a noble tried to strong-arm the party out of their costumes (*"Those are ours. We paid for those."*), and she's genre-savvy enough to call *"we're in a rogue's house — watch for traps"* before anyone stepped wrong.

Her social game is a delight precisely because it's disarming. Her whole gala approach is **"I'm just a cat"** — she opened on the iron-throne noble **Iso Data** with *"I'm just a cat, but I'm drawn to your cape,"* introduced herself as *"just a cat, my name is Panacea"* (earning the DM's *"what a very inclusive name"*), and then **gossiped** her way into his confidence with *"a gossipy look like she's enjoying the tea."* A crit Discover and a hero-pointed influence later, Iso was hers. Off the clock she's the party's conscience made literal — she spent her downtime **at the hospital helping sick children** — and she feeds her two-headed familiar **Serrafina** scrolls to learn new spells (*"she has two heads, so she was pretty hungry"*). The healer who solves the fight and then goes to volunteer.

### Zanthar Melodune III · the Gnome Who Takes the High Ground

Scott's wizard once more rounded the corner first and paid for it — **two arrows, 25 damage**, the instant he peeked into the guild's ambush nook, continuing his S11 habit of catching the volley so no one else has to. And once more he answered with the single best mechanical play of the fight: *"I am small — maybe I can stand on top of the bed,"* scrambling onto a bunk to line up a **Dizzying Colors** cone that **stunned, blinded, and dazzled the room** and effectively ended the enemies' turns. One spell, perfectly placed.

His downtime and gala work fleshed him out past the artillery role. He's a compulsive **shopper and planner** (four Sure Strike scrolls, sleeves of storage, *"I need to get to level five"*), a **comic fail waiting to happen** (he spilled ink across a full day's Earn Income), and — when it mattered — the party's surprising **influence utility**. His two social wins both came sideways: he **bonded with a lower-class hanger-on** by drinking and eating beside him, then *intimidated* him into liking it (*"careful how much food you're eating… these rich people, they come after you"* — *"you're a chip off the old block"*), and he **influenced Colonel Rithert** on the flattery angle Bai Jian dug up. He even stepped up to the darts on a lark and **tied Bai Jian for second**, pocketing a silver dart. *"I have been known to talk,"* he'd said, volunteering for the pendant. Turns out he can do more than that.
