Session Summary

The crossbows at the top of the orphanage stairs turned out to be the easy part. The Children's Charity — the thieves' guild fronting as the Old House of the Found Family — got its surprise round and put two arrows into Zanthar the instant he peeked around the corner, but the fight was over almost before it started: Bai Jian opened with a 63-damage non-lethal crit that folded one rogue in a single flat-of-the-blade Slam Down, Zanthar climbed a bunk bed and dropped a Dizzying Colors cone that stunned and blinded the room, and when "the father" himself — Father Kindness — stepped out shouting "My children!", Bai Jian answered with a second non-lethal crit (59) and put him down too. Every blow was pulled. Nobody died.

And then the session turned, because this party doesn't win fights so much as it wins people. With Father Kindness manacled and his adopted son crit-Intimidated into dropping his sword, the party healed the old thief awake and — instead of interrogating him — recruited him. He steals only from Chelaxians; the Hellbreakers are the uprising; the math did itself. Father Kindness disarmed his own bedroom trap, opened his chest, and handed over not just the stolen Lazy Bones pendant but a pile of artwork "as a contribution to the cause," and a standing offer of intel on which noble estates are easy pickings. Two uprising points, a thieves' guild turned asset, and not a single body.

The back half was preparation and payoff. A day of shopping and downtime in Elidir (scrolls, sleeves of storage, a banned book fenced with a straight face), a goblin pickpocket Bai Jian covered for and quietly paid off under a guard's nose, and a costume-shop confrontation where a preening noble named Frederick Consum tried to strong-arm the party out of their gala outfits — until his own bodyguard eyeballed Bai Jian's sword and refused to fight. Then the main event: the New Year's Eve ball at the Regent's manor, a Chelaxian celebration of the conquest of Isger, where the party — in devil costumes, Lazy Bones pendant around Cyrathul's neck — went to close the deal the founders couldn't. The gala is a slow social duel (PF2e's influence subsystem), and the party opened strong: Cyrathul worked the merchant Quoster with a razor-sharp bit of social sabotage, Panacea gossiped her way into a magic-loving noble's good graces, Zanthar bullied a lower-class hanger-on into liking him, and Bai Jian shot his way to second place in a darts contest while a humiliated colonel stormed off. Session pauses mid-gala — the deal not yet closed.

Timeline

Act I · Combat · The Guild on the Stairs

Intruders, Answered (non-lethal)

  • The surprise round. Resolving the S11 cliffhanger, the party crept up the orphanage stairs and Zanthar peeked around the corner — into a purpose-built defensive nook (a bunk bed with an arrow slit). The Housebreakers got a surprise round (a rogue ability leaving the party off-guard the whole first round) and put two arrows into Zanthar (16 + 9 = 25).
  • Bai Jian's opener. He moved Ren up (the hound tumbled through toward the rogues, a dagger flashing over his head), then tumbled through behind them and led with Slam Down. It crit; asked to make it non-lethal, he rerolled straight damage: 63. Flat of the blade, tink — a "thin little waif of a girl" crumpled instantly.
  • "My children!" Father Kindness — the guild's leader — stepped out from behind the next bunk, Feinted Bai Jian off-guard, and stabbed — his dagger sliding along the backside of the armor, a miss.
  • Panacea moved in, Battle-Medicined Bai Jian, then cast Trade Death for Life — void damage into Father Kindness (a failed Fort save, 16 vs DC 20; 1d4 barely fazed him) routing fast healing to the arrow-riddled Zanthar, and her Familiar of Paired Perplexity Stupefied him on the cast.
  • Zanthar's control play. "I am small — maybe I can stand on top of the bed." He scurried onto the bunk (Athletics) and, from the high ground, dropped a Dizzying Colors cone: the remaining Housebreaker was Stunned, Blinded, and Dazzled. Bai Jian: "Stunned and blind. And dazzled afterward."
  • Cyrathul drops in. Snowgeen loaded in mid-round ("What happened?" "We're in combat."), crossed the room, Exploited Vulnerability on Father Kindness ("nothing to speak of of note" — no weakness), and non-lethally chomped him.
  • The finisher. Round 2, Bai Jian Commanded Ren for the Support rider and swung — another non-lethal crit: 59. "Oh my lord." Father Kindness went down, alive.
  • The son surrenders. Blinded, the last Housebreaker flailed by guesswork (all missed), panicking — "father, are you there?" Watching the party beat a house of orphans unconscious over a necklace, Bai Jian muttered the quiet part aloud: "I'm feeling very guilty right now." Cyrathul crit-Intimidated the son (Intimidating Glare, critical success): he dropped his sword immediately — "Did you kill my dad?" — and was manacled. Three enemies subdued, zero dead.
Act II · Faction / Roleplay · A Contribution to the Cause

Recruiting the Thieves' Guild

  • Panacea opened honestly: they came for the stolen Lazy Bones pendant (a blue ribbon left in its place), were attacked on sight, and answered non-lethally throughout. Father Kindness, unimpressed — "Would you not attack someone that broke into your home?" — then, on the pendant: "We steal from all Chelaxians."
  • The pivot. Bai Jian: "You heard of the Hellbreakers?" Father Kindness lit up ("Uprisers, yes… at some point there'll be a chapter here"). Cyrathul closed it: "We happen to be the perpetrators of such activities. And we are looking to expand the scope." A Diplomacy check (Panacea's Guidance stacked on) won him fully over.
  • The donation. "Untie me, and I will undo the trap in my room, and you are more than welcome to it." Father Kindness disarmed his own bedroom trap and handed over the Lazy Bones pendant and valuable artwork — "as a contribution to the cause" — plus a standing offer: keep him in mind for "easy pickings in any of the Chelaxian nobles' estates."
  • His parting grace: "had you found it yourselves, I would congratulate my own children on such a successful theft." Recruiting the guild banked +2 uprising points, pushing the party to 5 — unlocking a second one-time capital bonus (+1 to an attack roll or saving throw after the roll).
Act III · Downtime · A Day in Elidir

Shopping, Earn Income, and a Small Mercy

  • Briefing. Back at the safehouse, the party caught Cyrathul up: Quoster Thistlewait, the wealthy halfling merchant who "believes in charity and hates Cheliax" and wants a free Isger, was on the cusp of funding the League — the deal now has to close at the New Year's Eve ball, and the Lazy Bones pendant is the recognition token.
  • Shopping. The party sold the artwork and fenced a banned book — the Abergale the First script, contraband — which Cyrathul deception-sold for 150 gold on a cover story Bai Jian floated (the shopkeep: "I don't believe you, but I want this book"). Zanthar bought four Sure Strike scrolls and sleeves of storage; Cyrathul grabbed Sure Strike scrolls for his implement. The DM redistributed Sammy's leftover gold.
  • The goblin pickpocket. A hungry goblin girl lifted the gold from Bai Jian's pocket — and the town guard saw it. Bai Jian covered instantly ("I was just about to give her this," palming her a gold coin) and waved off the captain — "do you think I would leave someone alone who tried to steal from me?", hand drifting to his sword. She tried to bite the coin in half to give some back, then vanished into the crowd. +1 uprising point.
  • Earn Income & Gather Info. Panacea worked shifts at the hospital helping sick kids; Bai Jian earned income and learned about the ball (a celebrity bard, Lazenia; Reeves Rest Manor, the Regent's two-story state house). Zanthar tried to help at a scroll station and spilled ink over the day's work — no pay.
Act IV · Roleplay / Social · Dress to Impress

The Costume Thief

  • Costume pickup, interrupted. At Brightgate's shop the party walked into a shouting match: Frederick Consum, a self-important noble with two bodyguards, was trying to seize the party's finished costumes — "or I will have my two gentlemen beat you to a bloody pulp."
  • Cyrathul, dry as ever: "I mean, if you're that heavy, maybe you should cut off the carbs." The party refused to budge ("Those are ours. We paid for those.") — Panacea leading the pushback.
  • The bluff breaks. Bai Jian and Cyrathul overheard a bodyguard whisper: "Boss, did you see that guy's sword? I don't want to tangle with him." Frederick folded — shoulder-checked Bai Jian ("like shoulder-checking a brick wall"), got nowhere, and stormed out berating his men.
  • Reward. The grateful tailor pressed a potency rune and a quick-change (raiment) rune on the party for free — Bai Jian took both. Costumes finalized: Bai Jian as an Armengur Hell Knight, Cyrathul as a chain devil, Panacea in a fur-matched blue devil dress, Zanthar as a tiny Angeval devil, and Ren as a hellhound. But Frederick will be at the ball tonight.
Act V · Faction / Social · The New Year's Eve Ball

The Influence Duel (in progress)

  • The send-off. The Demetriases prepped the party and left town loudly on the opposite end — a decoy exit (their departed bard friend already started a diversionary "kerfuffle") to make it look like the League fled. Objective: find Quoster, close the funding deal, seed a Hellbreakers chapter in Elidir. Cyrathul wore the Lazy Bones pendant as the recognition token.
  • The room. Reeves Rest Manor — floating red-and-white lights, a string quartet, guests in Hell-themed finery, guarded stairs to a forbidden upper landing. A six-round influence duel over six hours; five NPCs can be influenced (Quoster, Ezio Daeta, Colonel Rithert, Caeto Vulaunex, Sasatoli Bronc).
  • Round zero. Cyrathul was steered to Quoster (a giggling fop with a levitating ruby cane); Panacea met Ezio Daeta (iron-throne cape, an order from Tilaax); Zanthar got cornered by the casually-cruel Mirapila Carafo and clocked a silent woman radiating immense magic; Bai Jian flattered the celebrity bard Lazenia and traded real monster-hunting expertise in drake-hunting shop talk.
  • Cyrathul influenced Quoster with a scalpel — praising Cheliax's "great institutions… like slavery — sorry, I mean debt," then glaring at Quoster to isolate the halfling from the pro-slavery nobles (hero point → success, +1 influence). A second round (hero point) landed another point and earned the League a reputation point.
  • Panacea crit-Discovered Ezio Daeta (Society), then influenced him with gossip ("a gossipy look like she's enjoying the tea," +2, hero point). Zanthar befriended lower-class Sasatoli Bronc and intimidated him into liking it ("you're a chip off the old block"). Bai Jian Discovered Colonel Rithert's resistance (jealous of combat vets, −2).
  • Manticore (the darts contest). Three targets, AC 18, crits & double-throws double points. Colonel Rithert whiffed all six darts and stormed off (Bai Jian: "Your money's gone"); ringer Renacilos Aziri won; Bai Jian took second (16 pts) and earned Ambassador Frigg's lucky silver dart; Zanthar then tied him for second.
  • Bonus round. Because Bai Jian competed, the party got an extra Rithert round: Bai Jian Discovered his weakness (flattery, +2), and Zanthar influenced him on it (Diplomacy → success). Cyrathul lost 20 gold on Bai Jian (and Zanthar lost 3 on Rithert); a last-ditch Deception to weasel out of paying flopped. Session pauses mid-gala.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Blade That Never Draws Blood

  • Pax's fighter is now defined by the hits he doesn't let land. He opened by rolling a 63-damage crit and immediately asking to make it non-lethal — folding a rogue with the flat of the blade — and closed the fight with a 59-damage non-lethal crit on Father Kindness. Two of the biggest swings in the campaign, both pulled on purpose, in a city where one loud death means lockdown.
  • The through-line was mercy that reads as strength. When the guild leader woke in manacles, Bai Jian didn't interrogate — he asked "You heard of the Hellbreakers?" and turned a burglary into a recruitment. Ribbed by the DM about having "just broke into your house and murdered your dad," he owned it flat: "Yeah, kind of."
  • When a starving goblin girl picked his pocket in full view of the town guard, he covered for her without a beat — "I was just about to give her this" — palmed her a gold coin, and stared the captain down with a hand near his sword. He gave a thief his own gold and made the guard thank him for it.
  • At the gala he played the everyman-in-costume beautifully — trading real monster-hunting expertise in drake-hunting shop talk, flattering a celebrity bard, taking second place in a darts contest on a stat he doesn't even build for, and roasting a self-important colonel off the field ("Your money's gone"). He still hands the big conversations to Cyrathul, but the plans and the quiet decency are his.

Cyrathul · the Dragon Who Reads the Room

  • Snowgeen was back, and Cyrathul reminded the table why the party can't run a social scene without him. He dropped into the fight a round late and still did real work — Exploit Vulnerability, a non-lethal chomp, and the crit Intimidating Glare that ended the combat outright (the last rogue dropped his sword on the spot).
  • His real weapon is his tongue. The Quoster influence was the most sophisticated play of the night: rather than flatter the merchant, Cyrathul praised Cheliax's "great institutions — like slavery, sorry, I mean debt" and then glared at Quoster to socially isolate the halfling (whose people are Cheliax's slaves) from the pro-slavery nobles crowding him. Twice over, +2 influence and a League reputation point banked.
  • He's also the party's dry wit — he deflated Frederick Consum with "if you're that heavy, maybe you should cut off the carbs," fenced a banned book with a straight face ("I found it in my hoard"), and made undead-silk gala chatter sound effortless.
  • Even his losses were in character: he bet 20 gold on Bai Jian at the darts, lost, and tried to deception his way out of paying up. He is the mouth, the mirror, and the closer.

Panacea · the Cat Who Keeps Everyone Standing

  • In the fight she was the reason the party could afford to peek around corners into arrow slits — Battle Medicine on Bai Jian, Trade Death for Life routing fast healing to the arrow-riddled Zanthar while chipping and Stupefying Father Kindness, and a follow-up Heal to patch the wizard back up. She never stops doing her job, and she keeps the party's fights survivable and its captives alive.
  • She isn't all softness, either: she led the verbal pushback when Frederick tried to strong-arm the party out of their costumes ("Those are ours. We paid for those."), and she's genre-savvy enough to call "we're in a rogue's house — watch for traps" before anyone stepped wrong.
  • Her social game is a delight because it's disarming. Her whole gala approach is "I'm just a cat" — she opened on the iron-throne noble Ezio Daeta with "I'm just a cat, but I'm drawn to your cape," introduced herself as "just a cat, my name is Panacea" (DM: "what a very inclusive name"), then gossiped her way into his confidence. A crit Discover and a hero-pointed influence later, Ezio was hers.
  • Off the clock she's the party's conscience made literal — she spent her downtime at the hospital helping sick children — and she feeds her two-headed familiar Serrafina scrolls to learn new spells ("she has two heads, so she was pretty hungry"). The healer who solves the fight and then goes to volunteer.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Gnome Who Takes the High Ground

  • Scott's wizard once more rounded the corner first and paid for it — two arrows, 25 damage, the instant he peeked into the guild's ambush nook. And once more he answered with the single best mechanical play of the fight: "I am small — maybe I can stand on top of the bed," scrambling onto a bunk to line up a Dizzying Colors cone that stunned, blinded, and dazzled the room. One spell, perfectly placed.
  • His downtime fleshed him out past the artillery role. He's a compulsive shopper and planner (four Sure Strike scrolls, sleeves of storage, "I need to get to level five") and a comic fail waiting to happen (he spilled ink across a full day's Earn Income).
  • When it mattered, he was the party's surprising influence utility. His two social wins both came sideways: he bonded with a lower-class hanger-on by drinking and eating beside him, then intimidated him into liking it ("careful how much food you're eating… these rich people, they come after you" — "you're a chip off the old block").
  • He also influenced Colonel Rithert on the flattery angle Bai Jian dug up — and on a lark stepped up to the darts and tied Bai Jian for second, pocketing a silver dart. "I have been known to talk," he'd said, volunteering for the pendant. Turns out he can do more than that.

Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.

What Bai Jian did this session

  • Finished the guild fight the way we started it: non-lethal, no bodies. Opened on a Housebreaker with a 63 crit (flat of the blade) and dropped Father Kindness with a 59 crit off Ren's Support. Two of my biggest hits ever, both pulled.
  • Turned the win into a recruitment. Asked the old thief if he'd heard of the Hellbreakers — he had. The Children's Charity is ours now. He gave us the Lazy Bones pendant and artwork for free and offered to point us at Chelaxian estates worth robbing.
  • Covered for a goblin pickpocket in front of the guard — gave her my gold and made the captain thank me for it. Another uprising point. This city pays you back for being decent.
  • Faced down Frederick Consum at the tailor's — his own bodyguard wouldn't fight me once he saw the sword. Got a potency rune and a quick-change rune out of it, free.
  • At the ball: bluffed through the nobles, took second in the darts (Aziri's a ringer), and dug up Colonel Rithert's weakness so Zanthar could work him. We're two influence up on Quoster and climbing.

Things Bai Jian knows that the party does not

  • We keep winning by not killing. The guild, the goblin, the costume ass — none of it needed a body. Non-lethal isn't a handicap in this city; it's the whole strategy, and it's stacking uprising points faster than any fight would.
  • Father Kindness is a weapon we haven't fired yet. A whole guild that robs only Chelaxians and owes us a favor. When we need something stolen from a noble's estate, we don't have to do it ourselves.
  • That woman at the ball is wrong. Zanthar felt it too — an imperial-looking woman in undead silk, radiating more magic than anyone in the room, saying nothing. Nobles don't glow like that. Watch her.
  • Ezio's cape came from Tilaax. That's the infernal-contracts faction — the same one behind the torture devil at the Waterspout. There's a devil-contract noble in this ballroom. He might be a mark, or he might be the reason this whole party exists.
  • Rithert can't stand being reminded he's not in the field. Combat vets make him jealous (−2 if you talk battle), but flattery about "getting back out there" opens him right up. Careful which lever I pull.

What Bai Jian is thinking about

  • Darts are Dex, and I'm not a Dex fighter. Second place off raw luck. Fine for a party game — but a reminder that my whole kit is melee and reach. Don't get talked into a ranged contest that matters.
  • I'm the muscle and the moral center, not the mouth. I keep handing the talking to Cyrathul and it keeps working. My job is the plan, the sword on display, and knowing when not to swing. Own it.
  • Ren earns his slot every fight. The Support rider turned my finisher into a crit on Father Kindness. Command, block, scent — keep leaning on him.
  • The founders are gone. It's just us now. No cavalry at that ball. If Frederick makes us, or that magic woman turns on us, we handle it quiet — one loud fight and the whole city locks down.

  • Does Frederick recognize us under the costumes? He's here and he hates us.
