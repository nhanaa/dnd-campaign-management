Session Summary

The session opened where the last one ended: in initiative, fifty feet from the Druma Road Gate, with a Hellknight cavalry brigade between the party and the rest of their lives. It nearly killed all of them. The GM said so out loud afterward — "boy, I thought we were having a TPK. You guys scared me there for a minute. I thought, oh no, this wasn't supposed to be that hard." Foundry flagged every single round of the six as threat extreme. Zanthar bottomed out at 1 hit point, Cyrathul at 9, Bai Jian at 15, and Ren spent most of the fight unconscious and dying on the ground while Bai Jian narrated his own dog's obituary in real time.

What made it that hard was structural, and Bai Jian diagnosed it at the table before the GM ruled on it. When Cyrathul lined up a Demoralize, Bai Jian warned him off — "If you're doing it on the troop, don't do it" — and he was right: PF2e troops are immune to single-target non-damaging effects, so Demoralize, Snagging Strike's off-guard and sword crit specialization all did nothing. Cyrathul asked the right question back, though — "Would I know this?" — the table agreed he wouldn't, Bai Jian waved him on, and he spent the action in character. It rolled a 28 against a Will DC of 27 and the GM ruled it off regardless. Against a level-8 troop with a DC 26 lance charge and a once-per-round area attack, backed by a level-6 infantry troop with its own, half the party's control kit was dead on arrival. What was left was damage, saves, and Panacea.

They won it on four things. Zanthar opened with the biggest single action of the night — a Frozen Lava bead into the centre of the formation for 76 damage across three targets — after working out aloud that a troop's formation is also its weakness. Bai Jian held the line, crit twice for 36 and 20, and cast Bless from a scroll in Round 1 that stayed up the entire fight — the export flags it as the margin on two separate kills, including Cyrathul's Brigade-killer, which landed at exactly Hit by +0. Cyrathul killed all three enemies. And underneath all of it, Panacea healed 88 of the party's 111 hit points restored while running visibly dry.

Then the session exhaled for two hours. They rode into a blizzard, and after Ren led them in a circle back toward Elidir, Bai Jian took the Survival check himself and found the road. They stumbled into the crossroads inn at sunrise to find Fang, Moob and Wing failing loudly to raise the mast on the sailing ship the party left them. They sold the prison's loot, reached Saringallow, and were met by Maire Phelan, Quoster, Jenverick swinging Quoster in a circle, and Rhokrag — "You broke into the frigging prison in the capital of Isger, and you made it out alive. I never doubted you for a minute." Nine openly ceremonial XP later, the party hit level 7.

Rhokrag's surprise was a keep. Two covered wagons, two days into the mountains, and an abandoned hobgoblin lair with a newly rebuilt bridge. Six days of work turned it into a garrison with a kitchen, an arsenal and beds for everyone they had just broken out of the Gyve — each PC contributing through the skill they actually own. Then Grizzo knocked: the burned goblin the party kept alive in Umok after the Hell Hounds, who had walked to a mountain keep in winter to hand-deliver a letter from Ion Marbleberg. Her co-signatory was Fessimeth Finwinkle — the priestess the party left locked in a terrified room back in Session 14 and never went back for. She is alive.

The session ended at Rhokrag's dinner table, and the dinner was not celebratory for long. Phoebe read out the reply Caeto Vulaunex — the acting steward who cleared and deputized the party in S15 — sent to the city's request for aid: "Isger has greater problems than a few undead. The Hellbreakers are a pest that must be nipped in the bud." Alceo's answer was to take the whole crisis on themselves. Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore surfaced a children's story about an undead witch; Rhokrag, goaded by a Deception check Zanthar had quietly Aided up to a 37, let slip the name: a Pallid Princess. Panacea asked the only question that mattered — "if the story is that she's in the south, how are you going to find her in the north?" — and got "triangulation" for an answer.

The Night, Act by Act

Act I · Combat · The Druma Road Gate

Severe, and the GM's own near-TPK

  • The session resumes mid-initiative. The magic in the stolen tack dissipates on arrival — no mounted combat, and no forward progress on the map. Oramos volunteers to hold the horses: "I'm better with a pen than a sword anyway."
  • Zanthar reasons his way to the opening move, working out that a troop is a single unit with an area weakness, then hurling Frozen Lava into the centre. The Line Infantry critically fails for 41; the Brigade fails for 26; the Standard Bearer succeeds and still takes 9. 76 damage in one action, before anyone swung a weapon.
  • Bai Jian casts Bless from a scroll — never sustained, up all fight, and by the export's own flags the reason two later kills connect.
  • The brigade answers with a three-action Lance Charge (DC 26). Bai Jian and Ren take 29 each. Both he and Zanthar burn hero points on the save; Bai Jian's still fails. "This DC is really high."
  • The troop rules eat half the party's kit — and Bai Jian calls it first. His Snagging Strike off-guards nothing; the GM confirms troops are immune to single-target non-damaging effects. "This is when we need blaster, guys."
  • Two turns later, the table plays the knowledge gap straight. Cyrathul lines up a Demoralize; Bai Jian warns him off out of character — "It doesn't work. If you're doing it on the troop, don't do it." Cyrathul's answer is "Would I know this?", Zanthar's is "He wouldn't", and Bai Jian immediately waves him on: "So actually, yeah, go." Cyrathul spends the action in character, rolls a 28 against Will DC 27 — and the GM rules it off anyway.
  • The Standard Bearer's "Stay in the Fight!" hands out 10 temporary hit points three separate times — 40 points of absorbed chip damage, including all of Panacea's offensive output.
  • Ren goes down in Round 2 and never really recovers. Bai Jian shouts Panacea's Heal off himself and onto the dog mid-turn: "Heal Ren! Get him off! No, heal Ren, please."
  • Bai Jian's Round 2 nat 20 — a Snagging Strike crit for 36, leaving the segment clumsy, enfeebled and off-guard.
  • Cyrathul's Round 3 nat 20 — a claw crit for 32. "You take five of them out with just a single claw segment." His Demoralize the same round rolls 28 vs DC 27 and is ruled ineffective anyway.
  • Zanthar bottoms out at 1 hit point in Round 4. Panacea's second Battle Medicine brings him back 29.
  • Cyrathul kills the Brigade (R4), the Standard Bearer (R5) and the last infantry (R6). 112 XP, Severe. Bai Jian: "I was ready to say goodbye to Ren, honestly."
Act II · Travel · The Blizzard and the Crossroads

A whiteout, a circle, and a boat that won't stand up

  • The party rides into a whiteout "the kind where you can't really see 10 feet in front of you." Bai Jian routes the Survival check through Ren, who leads them in a circle back toward Elidir — then takes it himself and finds the road. "Alright, Ren. I know you're tired, but you can do this." → "That shit. I'll do it myself."
  • They travel all night, healing on the move via sustained Life Boost and Trade Death for Life. Bai Jian and Zanthar work out between them that Continual Recovery lets the party keep healing on ten-minute intervals.
  • The crossroads inn at sunrise: one room upstairs, one queen bed, a common-room floor for a dozen freed prisoners. Cyrathul pays the silver.
  • Hammering out back is Fang, Moob and Wing repairing the party's sailing ship, badly — Moob screaming at Fang about which way to push the mast, which falls down again the moment they turn to look at the party. Four or five days from seaworthy; they can't take the refugees.
  • Panacea tells the goblins to lie low. Moob produces a vial from his waistband: "I got something for any Chelaxian dogs that would come my way."
  • Otton — three days tortured a week ago — comes around the corner leading sixteen horses, groomed, fed and bedded down. He had spent the whole night on them.
Act III · Social · Saringallow, and Nine Ceremonial XP

The town they left is exactly the town they left

  • A sleepy guard, no increased Chelaxian presence — "the sleepy town that you guys left just several weeks ago."
  • A long, funny loot liquidation, run mostly by Bai Jian. They keep the Lifebloom Glaive, the siege toolkit, the repair kit and one of five silver ingots; they sell the rest, including the high-fashion clothing off Rencelos's back — "I'm not going to any more parties. Bai Jian had enough parties for his life after that one."
  • Bai Jian argues the party's only Invisibility Potion onto Panacea's sheet rather than his own, because she can go invisible without taking hostile actions.
  • 195 gp 1 sp each, then another 28 gp 1 sp each.
  • At the safehouse: Maire Phelan — "We thought you were all dead." Quoster — "You actually succeeded." Jenverick picks Quoster up and swings him in a circle while he says "put me down."
  • Rhokrag awards 9 XP the GM cheerfully admits isn't in the book. Level 7. Zanthar had predicted the exact number minutes earlier.
  • Phoebe's first reaction to a dozen new Hellbreakers: "Where are we going to put everybody?" Rhokrag: "I've got an idea" — and runs out the door. Rabbit and Cole tap a keg nobody knew was there.
Act IV · Exploration · The Fort

A home base, for the first time in the campaign

  • Two covered wagons, two days of travel, a single-file mountain trail, and a keep across a freshly rebuilt bridge — "I was here just last week getting this done." An old hobgoblin lair: holes in the roof, snow coming through, rats.
  • The wagons are full of roofing material, building stone, beds and a whole kitchen array. "I figure this is a nice new Hellbreakers headquarters."
  • Six days, four skills, each PC in their own lane. Panacea and Zanthar craft (29 and 32); Cyrathul, who says outright he cannot craft at all, rolls Diplomacy to direct the other Hellbreakers (30); Bai Jian rolls Athletics to lift the heavy beams (27).
  • Result: a working kitchen, a full garrison, an arsenal, and beds for everyone. Jenverick kicks the door open, sends half-eaten bones flying, and jumps up and down: "I've never had— I mean, I've had a place of my own, but it was just a small apartment."
  • Grizzo knocks — the burned goblin who collapsed in Umok fleeing the Hell Hounds in S7, now living under the gnomes' protection. She has walked to a mountain keep in winter to hand-deliver a sealed letter. Cyrathul invites her in out of the storm.
  • The letter, from Ion Marbleberg and Fessimeth Finwinkle: "The undead are becoming an issue here in the Southlands. We have written to the capital and it seems to have fallen on deaf ears." Fessy's signature quietly closes a thread open since Session 14.
  • Cyrathul, aloud: "Hopefully that won't come up later."
Act V · Council · Rhokrag's Table, and the Pallid Princess

The bad news, over dinner

  • Four downtime days: Panacea works the hospital (two Medicine crits), Bai Jian hauls freight, Zanthar crafts and learns spells, Cyrathul busks — and Lezinia, the singer from the Elidir gala, walks past and recognises him: "I didn't know you busked too." She independently confirms the southern undead problem and that Caeto is sending no troops.
  • Guests at the house include Ellery "Gram" Smalltoes, halfling editor of the Saringallow Gazetteer, who hands over today's copy and waits, beaming. Bai Jian: "I don't really follow the news."
  • Caeto's letter, read aloud by Phoebe as "the reply Vulaunex sent to Logos" — the same Paralictor who deputized them in S15: "Isger has greater problems than a few undead. The Hellbreakers are a pest that must be nipped in the bud before their dissidence spreads like a plague. Though their self-righteous act may still be of some use, your concerns are noted."
  • Alceo's answer: "If the so-called leaders of Isger won't protect its people from the undead, I suppose we'll have to deal with it ourselves."
  • Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore (31) surfaces the rumour of an undead witch south of the Chitterwood — a location he explicitly doubts. Alceo heard the source was a curse; Phoebe suggests triangulating it from which southern caravan routes have gone unsafe.
  • Rhokrag bets a hundred gold a head. Cyrathul tries to raise it — "Why not 200?" — and Rhokrag counters "Why not 100 gold from each of you?"
  • Cyrathul goads him with Deception: 35 on the roll, 37 with the +2 Zanthar quietly Aided in. Rhokrag lets slip the name — "some sort of Pallid Princess somewhere that's causing all this trouble, and I'm going to be the first one to find her."
  • Assignments. The party takes the south: Thorngrip within three days, then Swiftrun, Stumpfield, Umok, Vordia — and Wolf Point, which Rhokrag raises with a straight face, asking whether they know anything about bandits who only rob Chelaxians. (They do: Runa Bloodhammer's Steel Circle, from S5.)
  • A Wand of Dream Message goes to Zanthar. Rescued civilians go to Saringallow. Reconvene on the 9th.

Round 1 — the opener and the charge

  • Zanthar activates and hurls Frozen Lava: Line Infantry crit-fails (41), Brigade fails (26), Standard Bearer succeeds (9). 76 total.
  • Bai Jian casts Bless from a scroll, moves up with Ren.
  • Brigade: three-action Lance Charge, DC 26. Ren and Bai Jian take 29; Zanthar 14. Zanthar hero-points into a success; Bai Jian hero-points a crit-fail up to a plain failure.
  • Line Infantry: Bolt Salvo, 6 apiece.
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability fails (21 vs DC 24); jaws miss.
  • Standard Bearer: "Stay in the Fight, lads!" — 10 temp HP to two troops.
  • Panacea: Spiritual Armament, hero-pointing a nat 4 into a 19 to hit, for 5; then Trade Death for Life onto Bai Jian.

Round 2 — the first crit

  • Zanthar: Chilling Spray, 13.
  • Bai Jian: nat 20, Snagging Strike crit for 36 — clumsy, enfeebled, off-guard on the segment.

  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability fails twice more; Frenzy opener misses.
  • Panacea: the three-action Heal is proposed, then abandoned when Bai Jian points out it would heal the enemy too — goes off as the two-action version redirected to Ren for 13. Ren is at dying 1 when it lands.

Round 3 — the low point

  • Zanthar: Hand of the Apprentice hits for 19.
  • Bai Jian: drinks an Elixir of Life (14), stands Ren up, and crits for 20 — "you swing and then backswing and you take out several of the mounted Hellknights." Brigade to two segments.

  • Cyrathul: rolls a 28 Demoralize against Will DC 27 and it is ruled ineffective against the formation, then crits a claw for 32.
  • Panacea: Battle Medicine on Cyrathul for 25, on Assurance, and sustains.

Round 4 — the Brigade falls

  • Zanthar: Electric Arc, crit-saved by the Brigade.
  • Ren, back up, gives the Isgeri Boarhound support benefit (+1); Bai Jian hits for 10.
  • Brigade: Stab from the Saddle hits four targets; Cyrathul takes 23. A bolt salvo takes Zanthar to 1; Panacea's second Battle Medicine returns 29.
  • Cyrathul kills the Hellknight Cavalry Brigade — "I'll just jump up and then rip them off their horse." The jaws Strike landed at Hit by +0: without Bai Jian's Bless it misses.
  • His follow-up Twisting Tail reaction strikes the wrong target and misses.

Round 5 — the Standard Bearer falls

  • Zanthar: Illusory Creature as a non-lethal guard dog, to flank and chip.
  • Bai Jian: Snagging-Strikes the Standard Bearer for 16 while flanking — and because the Bearer is a normal creature rather than a troop, the off-guard actually sticks.
  • Line Infantry: Clash of Steel, 10 to Cyrathul.
  • Cyrathul: heals himself 9 from a scroll, then crits the Standard Bearer with jaws for 40 and kills him — the off-guard from Bai Jian's flank was live but not load-bearing here, since a 32 crits AC 20 as well as AC 18 — and carries the Frenzy into the infantry for 15.

Round 6 — the last troop

  • Zanthar: sustains the illusion (6 mental); Hand of the Apprentice misses.
  • Bai Jian: hits twice for 18 and 12 — "you slice through and you see three men fall to the ground" — breaking the troop to two segments.
  • Line Infantry: Clash of Steel for 8 and 17.
  • Cyrathul finishes the encounter with a Frenzy jaws hit for 20. "You tear through the last crowd of these men."
Two reroll systems, told apart. Nine two-d20 lines appear in the log. Two carry a + 10 — the table's hero-point house rule firing on a reroll of 10 or lower. The rest split cleanly: Panacea and Cyrathul say "hero point" aloud, and Bai Jian settles his own Round 1 case later when the GM says he's used both Elidir rerolls and he corrects him — "OK, I only used one." Four of the five Elidir rerolls came up 10 or lower and none took the +10, which is the tell.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Rules Memory

  • He was the table's rules engine all night, almost never for his own benefit. Bless doesn't need sustaining; Panacea's Heal is 30 feet and the three-action version would heal the enemy; Ren's wounded and dying thresholds; Twisting Tail against troop movement; Cyrathul striking the wrong target; Draconic Frenzy isn't single-target; Trick Magic Item is retryable; Continual Recovery for the ten-minute heal loop. Three of the four biggest tactical corrections of the night were his, and none were about his own character — and when Cyrathul asked whether Cyrathul would know a Demoralize was pointless, he dropped it in one line rather than pushing.
  • He diagnosed the troop problem before the GM ruled on it. "It doesn't work. If you're doing it on the troop, don't do it." He'd already worked it out on his own Snagging Strike: "This is when we need blaster, guys."
  • The highest-leverage thing he did was a one-action scroll. The Round 1 Bless is the margin on Cyrathul's Brigade kill (Hit by +0) and on his own Round 3 crit — Bless flipped two rolls that mattered: his own Round 3 hit into the 20-damage crit, and Cyrathul's Brigade-killing jaws from a miss into a hit. Both are provable against the log.
  • 105 effective damage, the highest in the party, on two crits (36 and 20) and four hits — while taking 91, second-most, and bottoming at 15.
  • Ren nearly died and he narrated all of it. "Heal Ren! Get him off! No, heal Ren, please." → "I mean, we can forsake him." → "I think I might actually lose him." Then, in the blizzard: "Alright, Ren. I know you're tired, but you can do this."
  • Quartermaster with a generous streak — ran the loot sale, kept the Glaive and the healing consumables, and argued the party's only Invisibility Potion onto Panacea's sheet rather than his own.
  • Dry to the last: "I'm not going to any more parties." And, four seconds after Cyrathul said things were looking up: "They might be just raiding us tomorrow and then we're none the wiser."

Cyrathul · the Closer

  • He killed all three enemies — the Cavalry Brigade in Round 4, the Standard Bearer in Round 5, the last Line Infantry in Round 6 — and narrated every one, from ripping riders off horses to cutting a man's Achilles tendon while he faced Bai Jian.
  • 121 damage applied, the most in the party, on a nat-20 claw crit for 32 and a jaws crit for 40 — while also taking the most damage of anyone (93).
  • His Exploit Vulnerability had a genuinely bad night — four failures against DC 24, and the one Demoralize that beat the DC was ruled off by the formation. He spent most of the fight's action economy on tools that didn't fire, and kept swinging anyway.
  • He is the party's mouth, and he knows exactly what he's not. "All right, well, I can't craft at all" — and then spent the six-day fort rebuild running Diplomacy to direct everyone else's hands, which the GM narrated as him taking "a superficial role."
  • The warmest beat of the night was his. Grizzo — shy, burned, hopping foot to foot on the threshold after walking to a mountain keep in winter — got "Do you want to stay for a little bit? Maybe get a break from the storm?"
  • He got the Pallid Princess out of Rhokrag with a deliberate goad: talk him up, let him boast, let the name fall out. A 37 Deception with Zanthar's Aid.
  • And he read the room correctly twice, without knowing it: "Hopefully that won't come up later" about the southern undead, and "Things are looking up, allegedly."

Panacea · the Reason Everyone Lived

  • 88 of the party's 111 hit points restored. Battle Medicine 25 to Cyrathul and 29 to Zanthar — both automatic on Assurance — a Heal redirected to Ren, and the fast-healing ticks that kept Bai Jian and the dog upright. Zanthar hit 1 HP and Bai Jian 15; both finished the fight standing solely because she got to them.
  • She fought the whole encounter on empty and said so. "And then for my last action — oh my gosh, I am running out of stuff, guys." Every offensive point she dealt was absorbed by temp HP, so the log credits her with zero damage; the log is a bad witness here.
  • She adapted her turn three times under pressure — abandoned the three-action Heal when told it would heal the enemy, redirected it to the dying dog, and dropped Spiritual Armament rather than sustain it because "everybody needed heals."
  • The only person at the crossroads thinking about operational security: telling Wing and Moob to be "on the DL about being Hellbreakers" and to watch for Chelaxians asking questions.
  • She punctured Rhokrag's whole plan in one sentence — "if the story is that she's in the south, how are you going to find her in the north?" — and got "triangulation" for an answer, which is not an answer.
  • She also asked the practical question nobody else did: when the party saves people in the south, where do they actually send them?
  • Level 7 gave her master Medicine and armor proficiency. "Can you wear armor?" / "I can now."

Zanthar Melodune III · the Opening Move

  • He worked out the fight's whole solution out loud in Round 1 — that a troop is one unit, that you only need to hit one of them, and that the formation is therefore the weakness — and then threw Frozen Lava into the middle of it for 76 damage across three targets, including a crit-failed troop that ate 41 at once. Biggest single action of the night.
  • 103 effective damage on 35% of the party's output, mostly from consumables and cantrips, while being the squishiest body on the field.
  • He also nearly died for it — dropped to 15, then to 1, twice pulled back by Panacea. He spent the fight standing exactly where the area attacks landed because that's where the spells reached.
  • The illusory guard dog was the session's quiet joke and a real tactical piece: a second dog to flank with, summoned while the party's actual dog lay unconscious twenty feet away — and he specified its Strikes were non-lethal unprompted.
  • He is the other half of the party's rules desk, correcting the Bless ruling mid-Cyrathul's-turn and calling Wounded 3 as the death threshold when Bai Jian was tracking Ren.
  • He quietly Aided Cyrathul's Deception with his own roll — the +2 that turned a 35 into the 37 that got the Pallid Princess out of Rhokrag — and ended the night holding the Wand of Dream Message as the party's designated contact.
  • Called his shot on the XP award ("we might get nine experience somehow") and delivered the night's driest line about the adventure's broken map: "But Wikipedia is great."
