## Session Summary

The session opened where the last one ended: in initiative, fifty feet from the Druma Road Gate, with a Hellknight cavalry brigade between the party and the rest of their lives. It nearly killed all of them. The GM said so out loud afterward — *"boy, I thought we were having a TPK. You guys scared me there for a minute. I thought, oh no, this wasn't supposed to be that hard."* Foundry flagged **every single round** of the six as `threat extreme`. Zanthar finished the fight having bottomed out at **1 hit point**, Cyrathul at **9**, Bai Jian at **15**, and Ren spent most of the fight unconscious and dying on the ground while Bai Jian narrated his own dog's obituary in real time.

What made it that hard was structural, and **Bai Jian diagnosed it at the table before the GM ruled on it**. When Cyrathul lined up a Demoralize, Bai Jian warned him off — *"If you're doing it on the troop, don't do it"* — and he was right: PF2e troops are immune to single-target non-damaging effects, so Demoralize, Snagging Strike's off-guard and sword crit specialization all did nothing. Cyrathul asked the right question back, though — *"Would I know this?"* — the table agreed he wouldn't, Bai Jian waved him on, and he spent the action in character. It rolled a 28 against a Will DC of 27 and the GM ruled it off regardless: *"it just doesn't seem like it's as intimidating when there's 20 Hellknights lined up together and one of you."* Against a level-8 troop with a DC 26 lance charge and a once-per-round area attack, backed by a level-6 infantry troop with its own, half the party's control kit was dead on arrival. What was left was damage, saves, and Panacea.

They won it on four things. **Zanthar opened with the biggest single action of the night** — a Frozen Lava bead into the centre of the formation for **76 damage across three targets**, including a crit-failed Line Infantry that ate 41 at once — after working out aloud that a troop's formation is also its weakness. **Bai Jian held the line, crit twice** for 36 and 20, and **cast Bless from a scroll in Round 1 that stayed up the entire fight** — the export flags it as the margin on two separate kills, including Cyrathul's Brigade-killer, which landed at exactly `Hit by +0`. **Cyrathul killed all three enemies.** And underneath all of it, **Panacea healed 88 of the party's 111 hit points restored** while running visibly dry: *"And then for my last action — oh my gosh, I am running out of stuff, guys."*

Then the session exhaled for two hours. They rode into a blizzard, and after Ren led them in a circle back toward Elidir, Bai Jian took the Survival check himself and found the road. They stumbled into the crossroads inn at sunrise to discover **Fang, Moob and Wing** in the back yard, failing loudly to raise the mast on the sailing ship the party left them — Moob screaming at Fang about which way to push while the mast fell down again behind him. They sold the prison's loot in a long, funny liquidation scene, reached **Saringallow**, and were met by Maire Phelan, Quoster, Jenverick swinging Quoster in a circle, and **Rhokrag** — *"You broke into the frigging prison in the capital of Isger, and you made it out alive. I never doubted you for a minute."* Nine openly ceremonial XP later, the party hit **level 7**.

Rhokrag's surprise was a keep. Two covered wagons, two days into the mountains, and an abandoned hobgoblin lair with holes in the roof, a rat infestation and a newly rebuilt bridge — *"I figure this is a nice new Hellbreakers headquarters."* Six days of work turned it into a garrison with a kitchen, an arsenal and beds for everyone they had just broken out of the Gyve, with each PC contributing through the skill they actually own: Panacea and Zanthar crafted, Bai Jian hauled beams on Athletics, and Cyrathul — who says outright that he cannot craft at all — used Diplomacy to direct other people's hands. Then **Grizzo** knocked: the badly-burned goblin the party kept alive in Umok after the Hell Hounds, now living openly under the gnomes' protection, who had walked to a mountain keep in winter to hand-deliver a letter from **Ion Marbleberg** — *the undead are becoming an issue here in the Southlands, and we have written to the capital and it seems to have fallen on deaf ears.* Cyrathul invited her in out of the storm. Her co-signatory was **Fessimeth Finwinkle** — the Abadaran priestess the party left locked in a terrified room back in Session 14 and never went back for. She is alive, and in Umok.

The session ended at Rhokrag's dinner table on the first of Calistril, and the dinner was not celebratory for long. Phoebe read out the reply **Vulaunex** sent to the city's request for aid — that is **Caeto Vulaunex**, the acting steward who cleared and deputized the party in S15: *"Isger has greater problems than a few undead. The Hellbreakers are a pest that must be nipped in the bud before their dissidence spreads like a plague."* Alceo's answer was to take the whole crisis on themselves. Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore surfaced a children's story about **an undead witch** south of the Chitterwood — a location he explicitly doesn't buy. Rhokrag bet a hundred gold a head that he and Maire would find her first from the north, and then, goaded by a Deception check Zanthar had quietly Aided up to a 37, let slip the name: **a Pallid Princess**. Panacea immediately asked the only question that mattered — *"if the story is that she's in the south, how are you going to find her in the north?"* — and got *"triangulation"* for an answer. The party leaves with a wand of Dream Message, a three-day deadline on Thorngrip, and a map pointing south.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Druma Road Gate *(Encounter 1 — Severe, and the GM's own near-TPK)*
- The session resumes mid-initiative. The magic in the stolen tack **dissipates on arrival** — no mounted combat, everyone dismounts as a free action, and **no forward progress on the map**: the army is between them and the gate. Oramos volunteers to hold the horses: *"I'm better with a pen than a sword anyway."*
- **Zanthar reasons his way to the opening move.** Before throwing anything he works out that a troop is a single unit with an area weakness — *"I can hit all of them… they're actually just one unit, you just have to hit one of them"* — then activates and hurls **Frozen Lava** into the centre. The Line Infantry **critically fails** and takes **41**; the Hellknight Cavalry Brigade fails for **26**; the Standard Bearer succeeds and still takes **9**. **76 damage in one action**, before anyone had swung a weapon. It cost two actions rather than one: the GM ruled activate-then-throw after the fact.
- **Bai Jian casts Bless from a scroll** — a 15-foot emanation he never has to sustain, running the entire fight and appearing on nearly every party attack roll afterward. It is, by the export's own flags, the reason two later kills connect.
- The brigade answers with a three-action **Lance Charge**, DC 26 basic Reflex. Bai Jian and Ren take **29** each; Zanthar takes 14. Zanthar and Bai Jian both spend hero points on the save; Bai Jian's still fails: *"This DC is really high."*
- **The troop rules eat half the party's kit — and Bai Jian calls it first.** His Snagging Strike lands and off-guards *nothing*: the GM confirms a troop is *"immune to non-damaging effects that target a single creature, such as charm or Demoralize"* and that an ability must be able to target five creatures to affect a segment. Bai Jian's read: *"it's a single target… This is when we need blaster, guys."*
- **Two turns later, the table plays the knowledge gap straight.** Cyrathul lines up a Demoralize; Bai Jian warns him off out of character — *"It doesn't work. If you're doing it on the troop, don't do it."* Cyrathul's answer is *"Would I know this?"*, Zanthar's is *"He wouldn't"*, and Bai Jian immediately waves him on: *"So actually, yeah, go."* Cyrathul spends the action in character, rolls a **28 against Will DC 27** — and the GM rules it off anyway.
- The **Standard Bearer's "Stay in the Fight!"** hands 10 temporary hit points to a troop, three separate times across the fight, absorbing 40 points of chip damage — all of Panacea's offensive output among it.
- **Ren goes down in Round 2** and never really recovers. Bai Jian tracks his dying and wounded conditions out loud all night — *"I think I might actually lose him. He might actually die"* — and shouts Panacea's Heal off himself and onto the dog mid-turn.
- **Bai Jian's Round 2 nat 20**: a Snagging Strike crit for **36** with the crushing rune, leaving the segment clumsy, enfeebled and off-guard.
- **Round 3 is the low point.** Stab from the Saddle catches three PCs; a bolt salvo follows; **Zanthar falls to 15**. Panacea spends the round on Battle Medicine (25 to Cyrathul) with Assurance making it automatic.
- **Cyrathul's Round 3 nat 20** — a claw crit for **32** through Implement's Empowerment. The GM: *"You take five of them out with just a single claw segment."* His Demoralize the same round rolls a 28 against Will DC 27 and is ruled ineffective anyway; the Brigade's saves show no Frightened penalty before or after.
- **Bai Jian's Round 3 crit for 20** drops the brigade to two segments — *"You swing and then backswing and you take out several of the mounted Hellknights in a single attack."*
- The GM reminds the party mid-fight that their **Elidir city bonuses expire when they leave the city** — a +1 after the roll, a reroll that must be kept, and a roll-twice-take-better. Five get spent in the back half, on top of the four hero points already gone.
- **Zanthar bottoms out at 1 hit point** in Round 4 after Stab from the Saddle and a bolt salvo in the same round. Panacea's second Battle Medicine brings him back 29.
- **Cyrathul kills the Hellknight Cavalry Brigade** in Round 4 — *"I'll just jump up and then rip them off their horse."* The jaws Strike that did it landed at exactly `Hit by +0`: **without Bai Jian's Bless it misses.**
- **Zanthar summons an illusory guard dog** with Illusory Creature to flank and chip — the party's second dog, and the one that survives the fight intact. He specifies its Strikes are non-lethal.
- **Cyrathul kills the Standard Bearer** in Round 5 with a jaws crit for 40, whipping around to cut the man's Achilles tendon while he faces Bai Jian. Bai Jian's flanking Snagging Strike had already made him off-guard (AC 20→18), though the crit cleared the un-lowered AC too — it beat the reduced AC by 14, and 20 by 12.
- **Round 6, the last infantry.** Bai Jian hits twice for **18 and 12** — *"you slice through and you see three men fall to the ground"* — and Cyrathul finishes the troop with a Frenzy jaws Strike. Oramos is waiting on the other side with the horses.
- **112 XP (Severe).** The GM afterward: *"That was awesome. I mean, skin to teeth."* Bai Jian: *"Yeah, I was ready to say goodbye to Ren, honestly."*

### Act II — The Blizzard and the Crossroads
- The party rides into a whiteout — *"the kind where you can't really see 10 feet in front of you."* Bai Jian routes the **Survival** check through Ren, who leads them in a circle back toward Elidir, and then **takes it himself and finds the road**. *"Alright, Ren. I know you're tired, but you can do this."* → *"That shit. I'll do it myself."*
- They travel all night, healing on the move via sustained Life Boost and Trade Death for Life, and reach **the crossroads inn** at sunrise — the same one from the hobgoblin rescue. One room upstairs, one queen bed, and a common room floor for a dozen freed prisoners. Cyrathul pays the silver. Bai Jian and Zanthar work out between them that **Continual Recovery** lets the party keep healing on ten-minute intervals.
- Hammering in the back yard turns out to be **Fang, Moob and Wing** repairing the party's sailing ship, badly. Moob is screaming at Fang about which way to push the mast; the mast falls down again the moment they turn to look at the party. They are four or five days from seaworthy and cannot take the refugees.
- **Panacea tells the goblins to lie low** — *"be on the DL about being Hellbreakers right now, and be careful of Chelaxian people coming through looking for us."* Moob produces a vial from his waistband: *"I got something for any Chelaxian dogs that would come my way."*
- **Otton** — three days tortured a week ago — comes around the corner leading **sixteen horses**, groomed, fed and bedded down. He had spent the whole night on them.

### Act III — Saringallow, and Nine Ceremonial XP
- The town is untouched: a sleepy guard, no increased Chelaxian presence, *"the sleepy town that you guys left just several weeks ago."*
- A long, genuinely funny **loot-liquidation scene**, run mostly by Bai Jian. They keep the Lifebloom Glaive (*"I don't know if you want to get rid of a unique weapon like that"*), the siege toolkit and the repair kit, and **one of five silver ingots** on Cyrathul's argument that they should be able to make something silver if they need to. They sell the rest — including the high-fashion clothing off Rencelos's back, on Bai Jian's flat refusal: *"I'm not going to any more parties. Bai Jian had enough parties for his life after that one."*
- **Bai Jian argues the party's one Invisibility Potion should go to Panacea rather than himself** — *"a second-rank invisibility, it's pretty useful for Panacea since she can get away with no hostile actions"* — and it does.
- **195 gp 1 sp each**, plus a second distribution of **28 gp 1 sp each**.
- At the safehouse: Maire Phelan opens the door — *"We thought you were all dead."* Quoster: *"You actually succeeded in blowing through the guy and getting everyone out of that prison."* Jenverick picks Quoster up and swings him in a circle while he says *"put me down."*
- **Rhokrag**: *"You broke into the frigging prison in the capital of Isger, and you made it out alive. I never doubted you for a minute."* The GM awards **9 XP** he cheerfully admits is not in the book — *"there's not actually a bonus of experience here, but I'm going to go ahead and award you guys 9 experience anyway."* **Level 7.** Zanthar had predicted the exact number minutes earlier.
- Phoebe's first reaction to a dozen new Hellbreakers is *"Where are we going to put everybody?"* Rhokrag: *"I've got an idea"* — and runs out the door.
- Rabbit and Cole tap a keg nobody knew was there. *"Are you telling me that we've had beer here the whole time? You dirty rascals."*

### Act IV — The Fort
- Two covered wagons, two days of travel, a winding single-file mountain trail, and a keep across a freshly rebuilt bridge — *"I was here just last week getting this done."* An old hobgoblin lair: holes in the roof, snow coming through, rats.
- Rhokrag's reveal: the wagons are full of **roofing material, building stone, beds and a whole kitchen array**. *"I figure this is a nice new Hellbreakers headquarters, at least away from Saringallow."*
- **Six days of work, four skills, each PC in their own lane.** Panacea and Zanthar craft (a 29 and a 32); **Cyrathul, who states outright that he cannot craft at all, rolls Diplomacy instead to direct the other Hellbreakers** (a 30); Bai Jian rolls Athletics to lift the heavy beams (a 27). The GM narrates each of them doing exactly the thing their sheet is good at — including Cyrathul *"taking a superficial role and directing other Hellbreakers on where to place things."*
- Result: a working kitchen, a full garrison, an arsenal on either side, and beds for everyone. **The Hellbreakers have a home base for the first time in the campaign.** Jenverick kicks the door open, sends half-eaten bones flying, and jumps up and down: *"I've never had— I mean, I've had a place of my own, but it was just a small apartment."*
- **Grizzo** knocks — the badly-burned goblin who collapsed in **Umok** fleeing the Hell Hounds in Session 7, whom Panacea kept alive and Ion Marbleberg was talked into sheltering. She is living openly under the gnomes' protection now, still shy, hopping foot to foot on the threshold, and she has walked to a mountain keep in winter to hand-deliver a sealed letter. **Cyrathul invites her in out of the storm.** She comes in, stays a few minutes, and visibly relaxes the moment she's back outside.
- **The letter, from Ion Marbleberg and Fessimeth Finwinkle**: *"The undead are becoming an issue here in the Southlands. We have written to the capital and it seems to have fallen on deaf ears… If at some point you find yourself in the South, we really could use some assistance."* **Fessy's signature quietly closes a thread open since Session 14**, when a servant reported her locked in a terrified room and the party never went back. She is alive and working in Umok.
- The party writes back that they'll come if it worsens. Cyrathul, aloud: *"Hopefully that won't come up later."* Bai Jian, four seconds after Cyrathul says things are looking up: *"They might be just raiding us tomorrow and then we're none the wiser."*

### Act V — Rhokrag's Table, and the Pallid Princess
- Four downtime days in Saringallow. **Panacea works the hospital** (two Medicine crits), **Bai Jian hauls freight** (Athletics, 4 gp), **Zanthar crafts and learns spells**, **Cyrathul busks and tells stories** (Diplomacy) — and while he's performing, **Lezinia**, the singer from the Elidir gala, walks past and recognises him: *"I didn't know you busked too."* She independently confirms the southern undead problem and that **Caeto is sending no troops**, then invites him to the Fiend's Funeral — on the one night he's already committed.
- Shopping talk: **the first armor potency rune is confirmed available** in a level-5 town and Bai Jian pushes everyone toward it, but the GM defers all actual purchasing to the week between sessions, so nothing is bought on screen. Panacea gained armor proficiency at level 7 and intends to buy some — *"Can you wear armor?"* / *"I can now."*
- **Rhokrag's house, evening of 1 Calistril.** Bai Jian's entrance is to check the window first and then walk in through the kitchen. Guests include **Ellery "Gram" Smalltoes**, halfling editor and publisher of the *Saringallow Gazetteer*, who hands over today's copy and waits, beaming, to hear whether anyone reads it. Bai Jian: *"I don't really follow the news."*
- Rhokrag's toast — *"When Phoebe and Alceo first came to me… I never thought it would get to this point"* — and then Phoebe stands up with the bad news.
- **Caeto's letter**, read aloud by Phoebe as *"the reply Vulaunex sent to Logos"* — the same Paralictor who deputized them in S15: *"Isger has greater problems than a few undead. The Hellbreakers are a pest that must be nipped in the bud before their dissidence spreads like a plague. Though their self-righteous act may still be of some use, your concerns are noted."*
- **Alceo's answer**: *"If the so-called leaders of Isger won't protect its people from the undead, I suppose we'll have to deal with it ourselves."*
- **Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore** (a 31) surfaces the rumour: an **undead witch**, believed by a lot of Isgeri, supposedly **south of the Chitterwood** — though he doesn't buy the location. Alceo adds that he heard **a curse** was the source; Phoebe adds that **caravan merchants know which southern routes are safe**, and that asking the right questions could **triangulate the origin**.
- **Rhokrag bets a hundred gold a head** that he and Maire find the source first from the north. Cyrathul tries to raise it — *"Why not 200?"* — and Rhokrag counters *"Why not 100 gold from each of you?"* Deal.
- **Cyrathul goads him** with a Deception check: a 35 on the roll, **37 with the +2 Zanthar quietly Aided in** on his own Deception roll a few seconds earlier. Rhokrag: *"I see what you're trying to do. Let's just say — I believe the rumours that there's probably some sort of **Pallid Princess** somewhere that's causing all this trouble, and I'm going to be the first one to find her."*
- **Panacea punctures it immediately**: *"But if the story is that she's in the south, that she's south of the Chitterwood, how are you going to find her in the north?"* Rhokrag: *"Triangulation."*
- **Assignments.** The party takes the south — **Thorngrip first, within three days**, at Alceo's request, to learn whether the goblins will stand with the Hellbreakers. Then Swiftrun, Stumpfield, Umok and Vordia, where the party already has standing. **Rhokrag** adds **Wolf Point** to the list, having *"heard you might want to add"* it — *"there may be some redeemable bandits hiding there that somebody said isn't attacking anyone other than Chelaxian scum anymore. You wouldn't happen to know anything about that, would you?"* Those are **Runa Bloodhammer's Steel Circle**, whose deal the party made in Session 5. Jenverick recruits around the capital. Phoebe and Alceo go to Logos. Gram holds Saringallow.
- **A Wand of Dream Message** goes to Zanthar as the party's designated contact — one minute of speech a night, delivered instantly or on the recipient's next sleep. Panacea sorts out that she has Message, not Dream Message; Zanthar doesn't have it either but it's arcane, so the wand is his. Rescued civilians go to Saringallow until told otherwise. Reconvene on the 9th.
- **Bai Jian's parting tactical note**: they're going to be fighting undead, so the kept silver ingot should be spent silvering weapons — his own falcata is already silvered, so he pushes it toward Zanthar's maul and Panacea's spear.

## Combat Encounters

### The Druma Road Gate — Severe (112 XP)

**Enemies**: Hellknight Cavalry Brigade (level 8 troop, 135 HP, AC 27, weak to area damage 8, resist mental 5 / slashing 5, immune to visual) · **one** Line Infantry troop (level 6, 96 HP, AC 24, weak to area damage 5) · Standard Bearer (level 4, 60 HP, AC 20, off-guard 18) · five war horses. *(A second Line Infantry token sits in the scene roster but never rolled a save, took a point of damage, or held an initiative slot — the rest of the line was cleared by NPC Hellbreakers in narration.)*

**Structure**: 6 rounds, seven initiative slots — Zanthar → Bai Jian → brigade → line infantry → Cyrathul → standard bearer → Panacea. **Foundry tagged all six rounds `threat extreme`.**

**Damage contribution** — two ways of counting, and they disagree, which is itself the story:

| Character | Damage applied | Effective (HP actually removed) |
|---|---|---|
| Cyrathul | **121** | 83 |
| Zanthar | **119** | 103 |
| Bai Jian | **112** | 105 |
| Panacea | 12 | **0** |

*Applied* is what the party rolled onto targets; *effective* strips out overkill and the 40 temporary hit points the Standard Bearer handed out. The three damage dealers were within nine points of each other on output — **this was not carried by one person.** Panacea's entire offensive contribution was absorbed by temp HP, which is a scheduling accident rather than a comment on her: she spent the fight healing. *(The effective column sums to 291, which is exactly the three enemies' combined HP — that's arithmetic, not a coincidence worth reading into: every enemy died, so effective damage must equal their total HP.)*

**Damage taken**: Cyrathul 93 · Bai Jian 91 · Ren 76 · Zanthar 49.
**Healing**: **Panacea 88** — Battle Medicine 25 (Cyrathul, R3) and 29 (Zanthar, R4) on Assurance, a two-action Heal for 13 redirected to Ren, and the Trade Death for Life / Life Boost fast-healing ticks, including two 3-point ticks to Ren the GM called aloud that never hit the log. Plus Bai Jian 14 self (Elixir of Life) and Cyrathul 9 self (scroll of Heal). **111 total.**

**Low-water marks**: Zanthar **1/62** · Cyrathul **9/68** · Bai Jian **15/92** · Ren unconscious and dying from Round 2, revived to 3 HP twice by fast healing. Zanthar finished the fight on 30 and Bai Jian on 19, both purely because Panacea got to them.

**Round 1** — Zanthar activates and hurls **Frozen Lava**: Line Infantry crit-fails (**41**), the brigade fails (**26**), the Standard Bearer succeeds (**9**). Bai Jian casts **Bless** from a scroll and moves up with Ren. The brigade answers with a three-action **Lance Charge** (DC 26): Ren and Bai Jian take **29** each, Zanthar 14 — Zanthar hero-points his save into a success, Bai Jian hero-points a crit-fail up into a plain failure. Line infantry **Bolt Salvo** for 6 apiece. Cyrathul's **Exploit Vulnerability** fails (21 vs DC 24) and his jaws miss. The Standard Bearer's **Stay in the Fight!** hands 10 temp HP to two troops. Panacea opens **Spiritual Armament** — hero-pointing a nat 4 into a 19 to hit — for 5, then **Trade Death for Life** onto Bai Jian.

**Round 2** — Zanthar's **Chilling Spray** for 13. Bai Jian **nat 20** with the falcata: **36 damage**, clumsy, enfeebled and off-guard on the segment. **Stab from the Saddle** (DC 23) catches Bai Jian for 30, Ren for 25, Cyrathul for 15 — Cyrathul hero-points a nat 1 into a success. Another Bolt Salvo. Cyrathul fails Exploit Vulnerability twice more and misses his Frenzy opener. Panacea's Heal — proposed as the three-action version, then abandoned when Bai Jian points out it would heal the enemy too — goes off as the **two-action version redirected to Ren** on his shout, for 13. Ren is at dying 1 when it lands.

**Round 3** — Zanthar's **Hand of the Apprentice** hits for 19. Bai Jian drinks an **Elixir of Life** (14), stands Ren up, and **crits for 20**, dropping the brigade to two segments — *"you swing and then backswing and you take out several of the mounted Hellknights."* Stab from the Saddle again: Ren to 13 and wounded 1, Cyrathul and Zanthar for 15 each; a bolt salvo follows. Cyrathul rolls a **28 Demoralize against Will DC 27 and it is ruled ineffective against the formation**, then **crits a claw for 32**. Panacea Battle-Medicines Cyrathul for 25 and sustains.

**Round 4** — Zanthar's Electric Arc is crit-saved by the brigade. Ren, back up, gives the **Isgeri Boarhound support benefit** (+1) and Bai Jian hits for 10. Stab from the Saddle hits four targets; Cyrathul takes 23. A bolt salvo takes **Zanthar to 1**; Panacea's second Battle Medicine returns 29. **Cyrathul kills the Hellknight Cavalry Brigade** with a jaws Strike that connected at `Hit by +0` — Bai Jian's Bless is the entire margin. His follow-up Twisting Tail reaction strikes the wrong target and misses.

**Round 5** — Zanthar casts **Illusory Creature** as a non-lethal guard dog to flank. Bai Jian **Snagging-Strikes the Standard Bearer** for 16 while flanking — and because the Bearer is a normal creature rather than a troop, the off-guard **actually sticks**. Line infantry **Clash of Steel** for 10 to Cyrathul. Cyrathul heals himself 9 from a scroll, then **crits the Standard Bearer with jaws for 40 and kills him** — the off-guard from Bai Jian's flank was live but not load-bearing here, since a 32 crits AC 20 as well as AC 18 — and carries the Frenzy into the infantry for 15 more.

**Round 6** — Zanthar sustains the illusion (6 mental) and misses a Hand of the Apprentice. Bai Jian hits the last infantry twice for **18 and 12**, breaking it to two segments. Clash of Steel for 8 and 17. **Cyrathul finishes the encounter** with a Frenzy jaws hit for 20. *"You tear through the last crowd of these men."*

**Resource spend**: **4 hero points** — Zanthar and Bai Jian on the Round 1 Lance Charge, Panacea on her Round 1 spell attack (*"I'm going to hero point that"*), Cyrathul on the Round 2 Stab from the Saddle — and **5 Elidir city-bonus fortune rerolls** in the back half, of which Bai Jian used two. Everyone got a hero point back at the level-up.

> **Two reroll systems, told apart.** Nine two-d20 lines appear in the log. Two carry a `+ 10` — the table's hero-point house rule firing on a reroll of 10 or lower. The rest split cleanly: Panacea and Cyrathul say "hero point" aloud, and Bai Jian settles his own Round 1 case later in the fight when the GM says he's used both Elidir rerolls and he corrects him — *"OK, I only used one."* Four of the five Elidir rerolls came up 10 or lower and **none** took the +10, which is the tell.
