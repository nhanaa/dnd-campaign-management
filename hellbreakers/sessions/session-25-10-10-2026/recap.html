Session Summary

Ten days on the clock, and the party spent nine of them. The morning started at an old encampment near the centre of Finder’s Gulch with a haunt — a young priestess crying tears of joy and an old one screaming, roped to pillars, both bleeding from long cuts — that tore 46 out of Cyrathul in its first pass. His lore couldn’t crack it. Bai Jian could: a natural 20 on Recall Knowledge told him it could be pushed back into its own grave, and he did it twice — “Go away. Back to your grave.” — while Cyrathul walked out of its reach and cheered him on. Three rounds of research on the site and the spirits themselves filled in the rest: how to use Varlario’s magnet (an Interact, adjacent to Illcayna), the Whispering Way’s counter-ritual, the White Queen’s Thaw, and a warning — the haunt will turn on Illcayna if she is ever cut by her own Reaper’s Scythe, the one melted into her flesh. At noon Alceo and Phoebe marched the army in, Alceo read the notes and said he could perform the Thaw himself, and Rhokrag paid out the bet: 100 gold each.

What nobody had was a way to get the scythe off her. Alceo’s answer was the library at Elidir — the city the party broke out of a few weeks ago. Bai Jian: “Risk it for the biscuit.” Zanthar’s Shared Invisibility walked them through the lockdown without a roll; Cyrathul had them hide in the stacks while a doddering old gnome pinched out the candles and locked up (“There better not be rats in here again”); Panacea hero-pointed a natural 1 into a natural 20 on the first shelf and Bai Jian’s Perception found the right one; and in the morning the same gnome, now their eager helper, pulled an old tome from behind the row Bai Jian had been searching. The scythe belonged to Jaidi, the Azlanti goddess of agriculture and Erastil’s wife, worshipped by the old Isgeri tribes and replaced by Urgathoa after Earthfall — with a shrine in Finder’s Gulch. Invoke her name with the right prayer, one action, and she may reclaim her scythe. Panacea wrote it down. 80 XP.

The road back was a recruiting drive. Wolf Point shrugged off the party’s help and then signed on — again — on Cyrathul’s natural-20 Diplomacy. Ren led them through the dark to Vordia, where Zanthar showed the townsfolk how to brace a wall and Bai Jian lifted it into place “like Thor” — and a blacksmith took them down a cellar door to a whole clan of dwarves the town doesn’t know it has. Swiftrun’s mages gave up an Apprentice Magician Clique to Panacea’s hero-pointed Society. Back in a fortified Finder’s Gulch, Kaazz read out a message from Storm — Elaine isn’t coming back — handed each PC a troop, and on the afternoon of the 19th the dead came up the canyon with their leader — Illcayna, by every sign — driving them from the rear: “Go, pretties. You take back my town.” Two rounds in, one Wight Battalion is destroyed — by Bai Jian’s Bandit Gang — Cyrathul’s Hellknights are nearly gone, and the session stopped at the top of Round 3.

Act I · Combat · Finder’s Gulch, ~5 a.m. 9 Calistril

The Priestess Haunt

  • The party crossed the ice one at a time. Elaine told Bai Jian she was going back to research at the grotto and vanished — Bai Jian: “She disappeared right in front of my face.”
  • The party came ashore behind the old mercantile district, found it already picked over, and headed for the centre of town. At an encampment there, something burst out of the ground, swirling and screeching: a ghost in a cultist’s robe. Initiative. See Combat Encounters.
  • Bai Jian, first up, asked the right question — “Is it haunt?” — then delayed for Cyrathul’s Recall Knowledge.
  • A rules aside: can you retry Recall Knowledge after a failure? Bai Jian looked it up mid-turn and corrected himself — the lockout is for additional knowledge after a success, or on a critical failure.
  • Disabled, the haunt showed what it was: a young priestess crying tears of joy, arms out to accept her fate, pushed back into an elderly priestess crying out in pain, arms roped to pillars, both bleeding from long cuts. Kaazz tied it to “the pallid priestess.”
Act II · Research · morning → 4 p.m.

Research in the Ruins

  • Round 1 (four hours, at the haunt site): Cyrathul’s Esoteric Lore crit (35), Bai Jian’s Occultism (22) and Zanthar’s (26) succeeded; Panacea “kept looking behind her” for the spirit. Playing the magnet against the haunt taught them how it works: an Interact action, adjacent to Illcayna, wielding the magnet. Panacea: “So it sounds like one of the fighters need to be holding the magnet.” Cyrathul: “Need to blitz her.”
  • Panacea’s Treat Wounds put Cyrathul (29) and Ren (20) back together; Zanthar’s Stupefied from the grotto water wore off.
  • The garden: Zanthar’s Perception (30) turned up poisons nestled in the roots — Tangle Root Toxin and Nettleweed Residue.
  • Round 2: Zanthar and Panacea on Occultism; Bai Jian went back to the grave to talk to the spirits (Diplomacy 29). The spirits are “really agitated” with Illcayna and filled in what Varlario’s notes only started: the Whispering Way’s counter-ritual, the White Queen’s Thaw (as heard — possibly Wight Queen’s) — “an anti-wight ritual” that locks Illcayna down and slows her.
  • Noon: the army arrived — goblins chattering, Hellknights calling flanking orders, the Stumpfield saboteurs, Alceo and Phoebe at the front. Panacea briefed them and showed Alceo the notes. Alceo: “I believe I do [know the ritual] with these notes. I will perform this ritual when she arrives to ensure that you can take the fight to her.”
  • The army dug in; Finder’s Gulch now counts as a level 3 settlement. Rhokrag paid the bet — “There’s 100 each in there for you.”
  • Round 3 (to ~4 p.m.): Panacea crit (33); Bai Jian (23, exactly the DC) and Cyrathul (30) talked to the spirits again. The haunt can be turned against Illcayna if she is damaged by Reaper’s Scythe — the scythe she carries, melted into her flesh. The spirit: “She has found the descendant of the one who turned against her over a thousand years ago.” It must happen before the final sacrifice.
  • How do you take a scythe off someone when it’s part of her? Not Disarm. The answer was “an old text” in one of the places she has been — and it was Panacea who said “maybe we should look for a library.” Zanthar asked how far. Alceo named the nearest — Elidir, 74 miles off. Logos’s burned in the Goblinblood Wars; Gillamoor was too far. Cyrathul did the arithmetic and made the call, Bai Jian seconded it — “Risk it for the biscuit” — and the party slept first.
Act III · Infiltration · 10–12 Calistril

The Library at Elidir

  • A night at the party’s mountain keep (Kaazz: “High Fort”). Sarevial: undead reports are down to “a third or even a quarter.” Panacea: “Have you seen a witch come through here with a shard?” — “I think you’re the only witch I’ve seen recently.”
  • Elidir is still locked down — sentries in rowboats checking every vessel in and out. Zanthar’s Shared Invisibility got the party in; Kaazz: “I won’t even make you guys roll.”
  • First research round: Panacea rolled a natural 1, hero-pointed, and rolled a natural 20 (34, crit); Bai Jian’s Occultism (26) added another success.
  • Closing time. Cyrathul: “We could hide in the library after they lock up and be invisible while they’re locking it up and then just stay in the library.” Zanthar: “We can make a fire. We can have all kinds of fun.” A doddering old gnome pinched out the candles with his bare fingers. Group Stealth — Bai Jian on Follow the Expert rolled a 13, and the gnome stopped right where Zanthar was holding him quiet: “Is there somebody there? … There better not be rats in here again, either.” The lock clicked.
  • Second round, near midnight: everyone tired and failing — except Bai Jian, who switched to Perception (33) and “narrow[ed] in on some books on a shelf that look promising.” Zanthar took first watch, Cyrathul second; the streets outside were eerily quiet between heavy patrols.
  • Morning. Panacea raised the problem — reappear out of thin air in a locked library? — and the answer was to step outside and come back in. Cyrathul supplied the timing — wait for the key in the lock, then go invisible and slip out as he comes in. The gnome dropped his keys in the cold while the invisibility ran down, lit the candles with a snap of his fingers, and asked if he could help them find anything.
  • Asked about a fused Reaper’s Scythe, he asked what they knew about the Azlanti, made tea, and helped (+2). Third round: Panacea crit again (33), Zanthar (31) and Cyrathul (27) succeeded. He pulled a very old tome from behind the shelf Bai Jian had been searching:
    • Jaidi, “the Blessing and Bounty” — Azlanti goddess of agriculture, hard work and self-sufficiency; spouse of Erastil; worshipped by the old Isgeri tribes; replaced by Urgathoa after Earthfall. Pictured as a tall woman on a warhorse with a huge scythe.
    • The Reaper’s Scythe was hers. There was a shrine to Jaidi in Finder’s Gulch.
    • “If you evoke Jaidi’s name and this prayer, it would purify her scythe.” In play: one action to implore Jaidi to reclaim her scythe. Panacea wrote the prayer down.
  • 80 XP. Out through the gate, invisible, alongside a departing caravan.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · Back to Your Grave

  • He solved the haunt. He asked the right question first (“Is it haunt?”), stepped aside for Cyrathul’s lore, and when that came up empty rolled a natural 20 himself: it could be shoved back into its grave. Two Athletics checks, each over the DC by one — “Go away. Back to your grave.” … “Please, leave us alone.” … “Get off of us.” — and it was done. Both successes were his. In between he hero-pointed a natural 2 on the haunt’s Fortitude save into a success.
  • He went back and talked to it. In the first research round he rolled Occultism anyway — “I’m not good at it at all. But I’ll try anyway, because I’m trying” — and it landed. After that he went to the source: “I’ll go back after pushing them down and go ask them.” Diplomacy, two for two (29, then 23 on the nose). His were among the successes in the two rounds that produced the Thaw and the Reaper’s Scythe warning. “Hopefully they don’t hit me.”
  • He put his weight behind Elidir. Cyrathul made the case; Bai Jian settled it in four words — “Risk it for the biscuit.” — and agreed with the others to rest first and leave in the morning.
  • He found the shelf. The party’s worst Stealth roll nearly got them caught by a gnome with a candle — and by midnight, with everyone else exhausted and failing, he switched to Perception, rolled a 33, and narrowed in on the books. The tome with Jaidi in it was sitting behind that row.
  • He lifted the wall. Athletics crit at Vordia, the wall set in place “like Thor.” “Bai Jian turned into the Super Saiyan.” At Swiftrun his Survival cleared the ground for the wards.
  • He trusted the dog, again. “Come on. You have a good nose.” Ren, 31, straight down the trail in the dark. And when Zanthar’s Tailwind talk turned to the horses: “Maybe you can teach the horse to cast that.” (Panacea: “You’re a beast master, aren’t you?”)
  • He looked after the party’s kit. It was Bai Jian who asked whether Cyrathul had his armor potency rune yet — “I can’t get that.” “You can.” — and who asked whether they wanted an Earthbind. For himself: nothing — “I’m just saving for level eight.”
  • He commands the Bandit Gang, and complained about it — “Damn, I picked the worst troop, guys. I’m sad” — right up until it destroyed the first Wight Battalion. He Sudden Charged with the whole troop behind him, kept his hero point when the dying wights’ Final Grudge got through, took their curse (Drained 1), and closed the round with a Sure Strike scroll off his Retrieval Prism and three hits in three swings on the Skeleton Mob.

Panacea · the Party’s Voice

  • She was the party’s best researcher by a distance: three critical successes in the session — on the haunt’s site, in the Elidir stacks (a natural 1, a hero point, a natural 20), and with the librarian. When the gnome read out Jaidi’s prayer, she was the one who said “Panacea is going to write down the prayer.”
  • She spoke for the party. She gave the opening recap, briefed Alceo — “She has the shard… of the God of War fused to her body, and she’s after doing this big ritual, and we need to stop her” — handed him the notes, and told him which buildings were safe to use and which one had a restless spirit in it.
  • She asked the questions that moved the plan: is there a spell to disarm something that’s part of her? “Maybe we should look for a library” — the idea that sent them to Elidir. Is there one closer? Could someone the city wouldn’t recognize go in for them — “if we had our rogue with us, we could have sent her in.”
  • She kept everyone standing. A Heal on Cyrathul at 11 HP (“Never mind. I got you. Don’t worry”), Treat Wounds on him and Ren after, and Life Boost on him again in the battle.
  • She was funny with the NPCs. To Sarevial: “Have you seen a witch come through here with a shard?” — and took the answer in stride. To the dwarves: “It’s always good to have dwarves on your side,” then, “Do you have anything with catnip in it?” Offered halfling weed instead: “We’ll see what it does.”
  • She kept asking where the fight would be. From the first edge of town — “So we have high ground for potentially setting our battleground?” — to the army’s arrival: “do we need to start thinking about where we’re going to stage the fight?”
  • She recruited Swiftrun. A rough night on the dice, by her own account, and she still stepped up for the Society check she knew she was good at, hero-pointed it, and brought home the Apprentice Magician Clique.
  • She gave the real speech: “Just keep listening to us and keep with us and we’ll get it down. We’ll get that shard out and we’ll get that scythe out.” — and, of the cheer that followed: “That was my troop getting excited.”
  • Her Gnome Cannon Corps fired the first shot of the battle — an Arcane Explosion from 70 feet out — with a Spiritual Armament behind it.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Way In

  • Elidir happened because of his spellbook. When Panacea said library, he asked how far the nearest one was — “We’ve got some time” — and then cast Shared Invisibility again and again: through the lockdown, out the library door, back out the city gate. Kaazz didn’t make the party roll to get in.

They’re weak to area damage.” His brigade also took the brunt: two segments lost and nearly destroyed by the end of Round 2.
