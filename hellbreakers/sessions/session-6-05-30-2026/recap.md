## Session Summary

The session opened in the cleansed orphanage. Doors clicked open down the hallway and the party heard something stirring in one of them — Rama, an older blue-skinned monk in reds and golds, white shock of hair, sent by Rhokrag and finding himself trapped behind doors that wouldn't open. Bai Jian vouched for him, the party finished looting the orphanage (an unholy Asmodean text, a red sister's robe Rama claimed, a draft letter to a "sister in the Lintel"), and headed back toward Vordia. On the trail, the follower from the past three days finally stepped out — a young woman in red robes with a missing ring finger.

**Sister Sylvie** of the Golden Erinyes was the party's tail. She confessed: a man calling himself a Hell Knight, *Viro Ahala*, has been impersonating the Order, has deceived her sisters at a hidden mission south of Vordia, and is working a ritual to summon a powerful devil to serve his ambitions. Sister Sylvie can't return to the mission — her superior, **Sister Endreah**, has lost trust in her. She drew the party a map to the Lucent mission and asked them, in the same breath, not to kill her sisters. They are misguided, not evil. Cyrathul Sense Motive'd her: sincere, slightly afraid. The party rode out the same hour, arriving at the mission at nightfall.

The night that followed was a five-sister grind that the party kept trying to fight with the wrong end of the sword. At the gate, two gardener sisters attacked on sight — Panacea's Needle Darts crit-killed the first through the carotid artery before anyone could call for restraint; Bai Jian Sudden-Charged the second for 20 non-lethal damage and put her down. Inside, a kitchen sister with poison fists (sicken-on-hit) and two mindless **Orts** ate Cyrathul's jaws and Rama's Electric Arc; Rama himself went unconscious from the poison ticks after combat. A training-room ambush put two more sisters down — Bai Jian crit-killed one, Rama swift-kicked the other unconscious. The last live captive's eyes flashed to the southern door, and the party kicked it open.

The study was tall, thin **Sister Endreah** with a book in her hand and ebony walls of archives. She snapped her fingers, and *something* appeared in a poof of smoke — a librarian-shaped devil, **Velavi**, with bird legs and two pointed horns. Bai Jian activated his Potency Crystal, Sudden-Charged through the door, and one-shotted the devil for 37 damage before it took a single action. Sister Endreah's eyes went wide. Cyrathul crit-Demoralized her for Frightened 2; Bai Jian Reactive-Struck her at the door when she tried to flee; the party then committed hard to non-lethal — Bai Jian taking the -2, Cyrathul tagging in Rama as *"the non-lethal expert,"* and Rama finally landing a swift kick to the dome that put her down. She surrendered. Under interrogation she confirmed: Viro Ahala has gone into the Chitterwood to find a safe place for his ritual, which targets "Isger's goblin problem, starting in the east." The ritual takes weeks to prepare. The party left the unconscious sisters tied up, took an asp coil (Crimson Thorn, +1), a devil-skin lore book, a bow staff, and a bag of gold, then made camp at the foot of the mountain. Next move: back through Vordia, then either Umok or straight into the Chitterwood after Viro Ahala.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Cleansed Orphanage & Rama (00:00–17:00)

- Session-zero safety talk: dark themes acknowledged, party comfortable with the campaign's tone.

- Rama introduced himself in-character: a representative of *Jaunmari*, come to help the people of Cheliax. Out of game, the player explained the Hindu-coded inspiration. Bai Jian recognized that Rhokrag had sent Rama from the Saringallow HQ — vouched for.

  - Bai Jian: Tian human, draconic scale on his face, breastplate and sword on his back.
  - Panacea: catfolk witch, bipedal female, blue/green/purple fur, black leather coat, third eye, two-headed familiar. *"This is my very first time playing, so I get kind of quiet sometimes and just learning."*

### Act II — Sister Sylvie & The Lucent Mission Mandate (17:00–32:00)

- On the trail back, perception checks: the follower presence again. This time the figure didn't hide — a young woman in red robes with hands raised.
- **Sister Sylvie** of the Golden Erinyes, missing her ring finger. *"From her posture she seems like a tense coil, ready to spring — but her face is devoid of any emotion."*
- The reveal: **Viro Ahala**, a man falsely claiming to be a Hell Knight, has deceived Sylvie's sisters. He used the order's resources to find a ritual that will summon a powerful devil. *"I do not wish to see any more orphans created as a result of this."* He's already left to enact it; her superior **Sister Endreah** trusts him completely and may know where the ritual will happen.
- Sylvie can't return to the mission — Endreah no longer trusts her.
- Cyrathul Sense Motive: sincere, slightly afraid. Panacea concurred.
- Sylvie drew the party a map to the Lucent mission, half a day south of Vordia. *"I would prefer that you not kill anyone. They are my sisters and they really do have good in their heart."* Will the party promise? *"We'll do our best,"* said Rama.
- The party agreed to head straight there. Treat Wounds rolls; Rama volunteered Battle Medicine as backup if Panacea couldn't keep up. *"If she can't heal or goes down, I can also be a little backup a little bit."*

### Act III — Arrival at the Lucent Mission (32:00–38:30)

- Half a day's travel south of Vordia. Up the foothills of the Aspodel Mountains. Sun already down, nightfall.
- A rough dirt path through hills, boulders flanking the trail. The building visible above on a steep incline. Pentagram emblazoned in wood above double doors.
- Cyrathul proposed a deception: claim Viro Ahala had gone rogue and they came to warn the sisters. Panacea didn't want to lie outright — *"we can do half-truth."*
- Bai Jian walked up first: *"I'm the most normal out of here."* The dragon would emerge second.
- A low stone fence, a garden of grapes and autumn flowers, two women pruning.

### Act VII — Kitchen Fight: Sister + Two Orts (66:30–101:00)

- A formal dining hall — six chairs, "hundreds of cavorting devils" in the ceiling scrollwork. Doors north and east.
- Through the north door, Rama heard a crockery shatter. Diabolic muttering on the other side — Panacea, the only one who spoke it: *"Motherfucker, not again."*
- The kitchen: a sister in the same garb as Rama, plus **two strange, mindless devil-creatures — Orts.** *"As soon as the door opens, her eyes dart to you guys, and she says, get them."*
- **Initiative**: Sister · Rama · Cyrathul · Panacea · Bai Jian · Ort · Ort

**Round 1:**
- **Sister**: Fury-of-blowed in the doorway — hit on first, crit-miss on second, miss on third. Bai Jian: *"She's a maniac."*
- **Rama**: Stance up. Missed first attack — *"I'm going to hero point that. I'm tired of this shit."* (House rule 10+ kicked in: rerolled, hit.) Second attack also hit. Third: miss.
- **Cyrathul**: Recall'd weakness — *"She's weak to holy. Four weakness."* Mirror-positioned and bit — missed. *"She just moves like Neo in the Matrix."*
- **Panacea**: Battle-healed Rama for 12. Trade Life for Death on the sister (failed Fort, took damage), familiar's Paired Perplexity gave her Stupefied 1, and the fast-healing rider went on Bai Jian.
- **Bai Jian**: Pushed Ren through the doorway scrum (tumble through — success). Ren bit the sister, hit thanks to off-guard, 7 damage. Bai Jian moved up — strike missed. Trip with MAP — *"Trip, trip, trip, trip"* — failed; *"I regret my action."*
- **Ort + Ort**: Two slimy claws, both missed. *"Ren doing all kinds of acrobatic dance around, just dodging all the attacks."*

**Round 2:**
- **Sister**: First strike on Rama hit hard — *crit damage*. Forced a DC 18 Fortitude save. *"It instantly makes you feel sick to your stomach. This must be some sort of poison or something in her... it looks weird, she's also missing a finger her ring finger and whatever it is seems to be coming from that."* **Rama failed — sickened 1, persistent poison.** Second strike missed.
- **Rama**: Stepped back ten with stance speed. *"Get out of everybody's way."* No attack.
- **Cyrathul**: *"Fuck this."* — chomped the sister, lethal this time. Eviscerated her arm at the shoulder. Sister down. Then Exploit Vulnerability + Demoralize on the closest Ort — *"It's mindless. Immune to all mental."* Wasted.

They are."* Bai Jian: *"It looks like they're mindless."* Used Needle of Vengeance — mental damage, immune. *"So you might want to use your other attack for life."* Switched to Trade Life for Death, hit, familiar stupefied an Ort.
- **Bai Jian**: Commanded Ren up and asked the DM to confirm — *"Is it a fiend by any chance?"* Yes. Ren's foe-scent kicked in. Bai Jian advanced over the corpse, Snagging Strike — hit, 5 damage, off-guard applied. Boarhound rider: +1d6 piercing.
- **Orts**: Slow and stupid. One swiped Bai Jian — claw scrape, contact. *"This thing is slow like a sloth."* The other missed.

**Round 3:**

- **Panacea**: Moved to line of sight. **Divine Lance — crit.** Asked how she wanted to describe it: *"The Divine Lance takes out all the darkness out of it as it explodes into shatters of light."* Encounter over.
- Rama: *"What?"* DM: *"Very cool. Very cool."*

**Post-combat poison:**
- Rama still had persistent poison damage ticking. Six rolls. He went **unconscious** mid-loot. *"You see, you guys look over and in the matter of like 30 seconds, Rama is laying on the floor."*
- Panacea: second Battle Medicine (Medic Dedication allows two per long rest) — kept Rama alive. *"Help the guy, he's not okay."* Trade Life for Death kept up for a minute gave Rama six bonus HP.
- Loot: a fine **whip** (d4, reach, non-lethal) — passed; an **asp coil** (Crimson Thorn — d6, reach, deep red hue with pitch-black pommel) — party stash; 1d5 darts.
- Panacea processed: *"It doesn't seem like any of our diplomacy is working here, and our whole goal to come here was to try to get them to change their minds, right? But they're just attacking us."*

Cyrathul rolled Make Impression (in Exploration, not Diplomacy), failed, then **hero-pointed** the reroll — landed: *"Now it looks like there's skepticism in her face, but not because she doesn't believe you, but she maybe doesn't believe what he told her. Who told you such a thing?"*

- Cyrathul: *"Agree to disagree. You did operate an orphanage. I imagine you saw quite a few orphans that would probably beg to differ."* Endreah, defensive: *"It's haunted."* Cyrathul: *"It was. - Watch: Panacea first (quiet), Bai Jian second (quiet), Ren beside him. Long rest.
