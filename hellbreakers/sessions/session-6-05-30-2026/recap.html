Session Summary

The session opened in the cleansed orphanage. Doors clicked open down the hallway and the party heard something stirring — Rama, an older blue-skinned monk in reds and golds, white shock of hair, sent by Rhokrag and trapped behind doors that wouldn't open. Bai Jian vouched for him, the party finished looting the orphanage (an unholy Asmodean text, a red sister's robe Rama claimed, a draft letter to a "sister in the Lintel"), and headed back toward Vordia. On the trail, the follower from the past three days finally stepped out — a young woman in red robes with a missing ring finger.

Sister Sylvie of the Golden Erinyes was the party's tail. She confessed: a man calling himself a Hell Knight, Viro Ahala, has been impersonating the Order and deceived her sisters at a hidden mission south of Vordia. He's working a ritual to summon a powerful devil to serve his ambitions. Sylvie can't return — her superior, Sister Endreah, has lost trust in her. She drew the party a map to the Lucent mission and asked them, in the same breath, not to kill her sisters. They are misguided, not evil. The party rode out the same hour, arriving at the mission at nightfall.

The night that followed was a five-sister grind that the party kept trying to fight with the wrong end of the sword. At the gate, two gardener sisters attacked on sight — Panacea's Needle Darts crit-killed the first through the carotid artery before anyone could call for restraint; Bai Jian Sudden-Charged the second for 20 non-lethal damage. Inside, a kitchen sister with poison fists (sicken-on-hit) and two mindless Orts ate Cyrathul's jaws and Rama's Electric Arc; Rama himself went unconscious from the poison ticks after combat. A training-room ambush put two more sisters down — Bai Jian eviscerated one with a crit, Rama swift-kicked the other unconscious. The last live captive's eyes flashed to the southern door, and the party kicked it open.

The study was tall, thin Sister Endreah with a book in her hand. She snapped her fingers, and a librarian-shaped devil — Velavi, bird legs and two pointed horns — appeared in a poof of smoke. Bai Jian activated his Potency Crystal (jian becomes +1 striking, 2 weapon damage dice), Sudden-Charged through the door, and crit-one-shotted the devil for 37 damage before it took an action. Sister Endreah's eyes went wide. Cyrathul crit-Demoralized her for Frightened 2; Bai Jian Reactive-Struck her at the door when she tried to flee; the party then committed hard to non-lethal — Cyrathul tagging in Rama as "the non-lethal expert," and Rama finally landing a swift kick to the dome. She surrendered. Under interrogation: Viro Ahala has gone into the Chitterwood to find a safe place for his ritual, which targets "Isger's goblin problem, starting in the east." The ritual takes weeks. The party left, descended the mountain, and camped. Next move: back through Vordia, then either Umok or straight into the Chitterwood after Viro.
