Session Summary

The party’s part of the battle ended in one round. Cyrathul’s Hellknights died to a Wight Battalion’s daggers in the first exchange. A battalion of dwarves from Vordia came up behind him and asked if he needed help, and he took command of them on a 34 Diplomacy. Zanthar’s Lightning Bolt finished a Skeleton Mob and gutted the battalion, and his apprentices’ wands finished it (Kaazz: “That was an awesome turn”). Bai Jian’s bandits slung the last Skeleton Mob down to a few dozen bones, and he spun through what was left of it on a crit. More undead were already coming up the canyon. Across the field Alceo finished his ritual. Illcayna screamed, dropped to one knee, then shot four or five hundred feet into the air trailing a sack in a magical harness and flew for the ritual site. Alceo stayed with the troops to hold the line — “You have to stop her before she finishes that ritual.” The party levelled to 8 on the spot, healed for ten minutes, and went up through the Urgathoan temple to the pool.

She was waiting forty feet above the water. Beside her a man with long dark hair hung in pillars of light and darkness, shackled, head on his chest. She looked straight at Bai Jian: “I see you have come to rescue him now, have you? … I have been waiting for this. You look almost identical.” What followed was eight rounds against her and four Warshard Wights she had made in her own image, scythes drilled into their arms, plus a Bog Mummy she summoned. Her Sudden Blight did 49 to Cyrathul and Zanthar in the first round, and hero points went down all over the table. Panacea dropped in Round 3 and Cyrathul fed her a potion to get her up. Bai Jian and Ren killed three of the wights between them, a Blazing Bolt crit from Zanthar killed another, and Cyrathul tore the mummy apart. Then Zanthar cast Fly on Bai Jian and he went up after her. “Two sacrifices for the price of one. Most excellent.” She took him apart in one turn: a 65-damage crit from the scythe and the shard-tipped entrail out of her chest. He fell forty feet, dying.

Cyrathul said Jaidi’s prayer — “I am an expert farmer” — and Kaazz turned to Bai Jian (“this scene is familiar to you”): a tall Azlanti woman on a black stallion rode out of the side of the cave, her face peeled back to a skull, and she cut through Illcayna’s arm — the Reaper’s Scythe went tumbling to the ground. Illcayna came down screaming “What have you done to me?” and put Vampiric Feast into Cyrathul. A round later, after one last Sure Strike and a Reactive Strike that couldn’t quite finish her, she put it into Bai Jian. Both were dead, and Panacea’s Vitality Lash pulled her out of the air: “This can’t be.” Panacea pulled the war shard out of the corpse with the magnet, and the man above the pool came slowly down. He looked like Bai Jian. “No, my brother’s dead. They told me my brother’s dead.” Panacea: “Well, he is now.” Alceo and Phoebe arrived with the army — half the undead had crumbled, the rest retreated — and Alceo asked Phoebe if she still had “those gems.” The last line of the night: “We must immediately start the ritual of resurrection.”
