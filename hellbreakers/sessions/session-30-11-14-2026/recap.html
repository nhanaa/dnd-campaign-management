Session Summary

Pax came back to a party on the roof of the Sereni Manor after dark. While he was away they had claimed Sephone Haugini’s bounty, seen the dig in the garden break through and Sephone go down it, and made a pact with the Spring’s Crown, a small, caterpillar-like Fae queen holed up in the manor’s ruins who had knighted Cyrathul. Their side of it was to deal with Rustflesh. They went up to ambush the pukwudgie. A nat 1 on Stealth meant he was ready for them, with two redcaps and an invisible elananx, a cat made of mist that burst into a cloud of cinders and left the whole roof fighting half-blind. Cyrathul lit the dark with his Shining Symbol and read Rustflesh’s weakness (cold iron), Bai Feng turned the Bleeding Heart of Urgathoa into a Divine Wrath that left Rustflesh sickened and slowed, Zanthar put a Lightning Bolt through him, and Panacea’s cold-iron Needle Darts went straight through him. Then Cyrathul shouted into the smoke: “Rustflesh is dead. Stop in the name of your queen.” The redcaps didn’t know which queen. He had to rack his brain twice for her name before the fight could stop. It ended with a truce and a handshake, the redcap’s whole hand closed around the tip of one claw.

The rest of the night was quiet work. Cyrathul tried to pry up the floor to reach the Spring’s Crown and tore up a board with a shriek of rusty nails. When the night watch officer came in, Zanthar’s Shared Invisibility hid them, with the officer’s torch so close to Bai Feng’s face he nearly touched him. The queen gave the League a redcap infantry unit and advised picking the soldiers off one at a time. A pixie took the party’s side, promised to make men vanish from the camp at night and turned pink at the news of Rustflesh. On the Fae’s suggestion, Panacea and Cyrathul rigged the library’s shelves to fall. The party also took twenty elixirs of life from the mess hall. A patrol stopped for coffee and mentioned that Sephone had “found what she was looking for” in the hole. That night Alceo’s dream message reached Zanthar: clear the whole manor; the League’s armies would reach High Fort in three days.

At five in the morning, with fifty tents of soldiers outside and three hours before the shift change, they chose the manor over the camp. One runner from the camp would undo everything. Cyrathul talked the mess-hall watch officer out of his own weapon and killed him. The guards decided for themselves that the Fae got him, one flipped Zanthar a coin (“I ain’t going to tell nobody if you don’t”), and the night shift went home early. Upstairs, Zanthar picked the lock on Sephone’s office. Inside were her spellbook (they took it), a greater emerald grasshopper, a jade bauble, and an appointment book that reads “Research” for today, Monday the 15th, and “Saringallow” for tomorrow. Then the day shift opened their door. In the narrow hallway Bai Feng crit their commander, and when the commander swung at Cyrathul, Bai Feng’s Retributive Strike crit him for 77 and his head “explodes all over the back wall.” Zanthar’s Telekinetic Projectile put the troop down with their own swords. The researchers’ door had an alarm spell on it. Bai Feng went through it first with a double-crit Flowing Spirit Strike that cut the Demonologist into three pieces before he could move, 135 damage in two swings. Cyrathul killed the Watchmage, Panacea and Cyrathul each killed a Zealot of Asmodeus, the Kylix lay down under Zanthar’s hammer and gave up, and Bai Feng took the last zealot’s head off from behind with a fire-wreathed blade. The inside of the manor belongs to the party. The morning watch arrives within the next hour and a half.

Act I · Combat · The roof, after dark, 14 Pharast

Character Spotlight

Bai Feng · Accept Your Fate

  • He didn’t like the dirty work and did it anyway. When Cyrathul talked the mess-hall officer out of his weapon so they could kill him: “I don’t usually use this kind of underhanded tactics, but I guess that’s what we have to do.” It’s the same hunter-not-killer line he drew last session, and he’s still drawing it. He tried to give his first opponent a way out: “I advise you to stand down and accept your fate. Maybe that’ll hurt less.” When the Kylix surrendered, he was the one who said it plainly: “I’m not used to enemies giving up. I’m used to monsters trying to kill me,” and then “So we don’t want to kill him, right?” When Cyrathul offered to knock the prisoner out: “We don’t need to knock him out, do we?… I’ll leave him there.” Then he stepped around him to take the zealot who was still fighting. On the roof, offered a truce with the redcaps: “A truce sounds preferable.”
  • He was ready to go. At five in the morning, when the party weighed hitting the soldiers outside: “Combat doesn’t take that long. As long as we don’t die… Let’s do it.” When the plan changed to the manor: “Let’s do it. Gotta start somewhere.” Then: “Operation Ambushing.”
  • He’s still learning the Exemplar, and it showed what it can do. “Oh my god, I have to remember how to play this fucking character.” On the roof he cast the Bleeding Heart of Urgathoa’s Divine Wrath (“The problem is I don’t have spell”) and Rustflesh’s Sickened 2 is why he failed Zanthar’s Lightning Bolt save. In the hallway his Retributive Strike on behalf of Cyrathul blew the commander’s head across the back wall: 77 damage, and “I’m not even playing a fighter anymore. What am I creating?” Kaazz: “I don’t think even Bai Jian hit a 74, dude.” In the researchers’ room he went first, caught the −2 Foundry had wrongly put on his first Strike, and cut the Demonologist into three pieces with two crits before he moved: “Jing Jing.”
  • He holds a line, and he’s the party’s rules reference. “My will is too strong for you,” to a Demoralize that missed by 9. He sorted out concealed and hidden for the elananx, the flat check for targeted spells in the smoke, and how a troop’s space works. When Zanthar took a channel smite he spent his reaction to cut it by 10. The Radiant healed Cy twice and Zanthar once. He passed on Mirrored Aegis’s difficult terrain because “that’s just going to do more harm for Cy.”
