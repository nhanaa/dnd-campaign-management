Session Summary

Behind the cathedral door in Finder’s Gulch, voices were talking in Aklo and a buzzing Necril — Illcayna would not be back for eleven days; “she will perform the ceremony on the 19th as it was written”; she had gone to fetch “the last piece for her puzzle.” Then, to the door: “You may come inside.” Inside was a gutted temple of Pharasma daubed with the signs of Tar-Baphon, two huge undead bats, a lone beaked fiend, and a tall, pale woman in black leather robes — Varlario Demicci, a diplomat, she said, for “those that whisper along the way,” lodged there as Illcayna’s guest. She offered wine. The party called it poison; she was delighted to be offended. Cyrathul asked her, more than once and very politely, to take a vacation. She was on assignment. She stepped into the party’s huddle to share “a secret too” — in Aklo — and the party fanned out.

It was an Extreme fight and it was over in four rounds. Bai Jian read a Bless scroll and opened with a 57-damage crit on the first bat; the fiend’s own Malodorous Shriek then blew that bat apart. Elaine’s whip crit slowed the fiend, Cyrathul’s critical Demoralize and Draconic Frenzy tore its head off, and Zanthar’s Slow and a Sure Strike crit, Bai Jian’s hero-pointed 44 and Elaine’s whip put Varlario on her knees: “I’ll give you anything. Just spare my life.” The party took the surrender on its own terms — no walking away, manacles on — and Bai Jian spent his turn getting her to call the last bat down where he could reach it. It answered that it would eat him. Panacea’s Divine Lance crit left it at 4 HP, and Bai Jian ran up Cyrathul’s conjured spiral staircase and leapt off it to kill the thing, taking 13 falling damage “for a style point.”

The interrogation was the session’s real prize. Varlario is from the Whispering Way, allied with Tar-Baphon, sent to spy on the Pallid Princess — and the Wight Mother has fused a war shard, a fragment of the dead god Gorum, into herself. Varlario traded her research — an occult magnet that will tear the shard out once the right ritual is performed, and notes that set Illcayna’s final sacrifice for dusk on 19 Calistril, through the new Urgathoan temple across the frozen river — for her freedom, and left as a bat. Then a frozen river that dropped Bai Jian and Zanthar through the ice, a riverbed corpse that gave Elaine an aeon stone and a Devilwing Badge, a hidden road Ren sniffed out, and a sealed grotto of seasonal murals that Zanthar’s Bounty’s Light lit a path into. Cyrathul’s Lifebloom Glaive woke the spirits of the old Isgeri tribes; the summer mural put everyone but Bai Jian to sleep for a full night’s rest; and eight hours of research turned up a Pallid Plague vaccine, a save bonus in Bounty’s Light against Illcayna’s spells, and a way to make wights flee from the Lifebloom Glaive. Bai Jian, napping under the summer mural, dreamed of a woman in white ash who bleached into a wight in front of him while her horse rotted to bone.

Act I · Social · The Cathedral, Evening of the 8th

A Diplomat in Pharasma’s House

  • Overheard through the door: Illcayna won’t be back for at least 11 days; “she will perform the ceremony on the 19th as it was written”; she went “to get the last piece for her puzzle.” Elaine relayed what she understood. Then: “You may come inside.”
  • Inside: shattered, boarded windows, two huge undead bats (Skavelings), a lone beaked fiend (a Sarcovalt), and a tall, very pale woman in black leather robes. “You must be the Hellbreakers that have been causing such a ruckus in the south.” Zanthar: “We’re famous.”
  • Cyrathul opened with diplomacy and the attacks on the road; she called them the Witch Queen’s servants: “We’re here on a diplomatic mission” — from “those that whisper along the way.” Illcayna had “graced us with this wonderful abode.”
  • Elaine’s Religion (28): a temple of Pharasma, desecrated with the signs of Tar-Baphon. “Pharasma hates the dead.” Cyrathul: “Or undead. That’s what the whispering was about.”
  • In the huddle, Elaine: “This being here seems like an abomination to this temple” — but try diplomacy first. Varlario offered wine; called poison, she was theatrically offended: “I can’t believe you would think so lowly of me.”
  • Cyrathul: “We seem to be at a bit of an impasse here.” — “Leave my home?” — “It doesn’t seem very homely. I’m sure you could take a vacation.” She was on assignment.
  • She joined the huddle to share “a secret too” — in Aklo. The party “fanned out, exploring the place.” Initiative.
Act II · Combat · Varlario’s Court

Four Rounds, One Surrender

  • Round 1: the bat’s Bone-Chilling Screech; Bai Jian’s Bless scroll and a 57 crit; Elaine’s Faux Bulwark and a crit that slowed the fiend; Zanthar’s Slow on Varlario for a minute; Panacea’s Divine Lance crit; Varlario’s Confusion hero-pointed into a Cyrathul crit success; the fiend’s Malodorous Shriek blew its own bat apart.
  • Round 2: Bai Jian’s natural 1 → hero point → 44 crit on Varlario (“The duality of man”); a bat mauled Ren for 35 (“Leave my dog alone”); Cyrathul’s crit Demoralize and Frenzy took the fiend’s head off; Zanthar’s Sure Strike Blazing Bolt crit Varlario for 34.
  • Varlario surrendered at ~15 of 110 HP: “I said I was an emissary.” Cyrathul: “You could have been on a vacation.” “I’ll give you anything. Just spare my life.”
  • Round 3: surrender accepted, terms not — “Simply leaving is not enough anymore.” Bai Jian spent his turn getting her to call the bat down “so that we can jump it”: “I’ll be benevolent… Bai Jian has always been a kind person at heart.” The bat: “He says he will eat you.” Cyrathul: “Unfortunately, that violates our treaty.”
  • Cyrathul’s Helpful Steps — a 40-ft spiral staircase; Zanthar’s thrown maul (“Thank you, Maul”); Panacea’s 38 crit.
  • Round 4: Bai Jian ran up the staircase, killed the last bat and jumped off — 13 falling damage, for fun.
Act III · Social · Terms & Interrogation

The Whispering Way and the War Shard

  • Bai Jian’s vitalizing rune kept burning her — she has void healing. “We’re not really geared to trying to kill someone to heal them” (Cyrathul); Assisted Recovery stopped it at 9 HP. Manacles on. “I thought we had an accord.” — “We said we’d tie you up. I think manacles are similar. Probably more comfortable.”
  • Panacea Treated Wounds on Bai Jian (25) while the questions ran.
  • Cyrathul’s Esoteric Lore (29): the Whispering Way — a secret cult that holds undeath to be the perfect state, in league with Tar-Baphon. Elaine’s Society had it on the tip of her tongue. Cyrathul tied them to Ezio, “the fat man from the ball.”
  • Varlario: “To spy on our good friend the pallid princess… The Wight Mother has a war shard that she’s fused with herself. How are you so ignorant of all these things?” — “We’ve been caught up in politics.” Panacea knew: a shard of the dead god of war, Gorum.
  • Elaine: “Out with it, please. You’re not in the best situation to keep secrets.” — “The Way doesn’t really believe that the Urgathoans are very controllable.” Manacles off? “Not a chance. Definitely not… We know you’re a caster.”
  • The deal: an occult magnet and her notes, for her freedom. Bai Jian, as she cast to open her cache: “We have several reactive strikes. I’ll stand here. Go ahead.” Cyrathul: “You do anything funny, we’ll kill you.” “Very hard to research how to kill the lion while sitting in the lion’s den.”
  • Zanthar: “You need to leave and not come back… We treated you well.” Zanthar asked her name — “Varlario.” She became a bat and flew into the night.
  • The notes (Elaine read the Necril aloud): Illcayna’s final sacrifice — dusk, 19 Calistril — finalizes the curse, somewhere reached through the new Urgathoan temple across the frozen stream. It is the evening of the 8th.
Act IV · Exploration · The Town & the River

A Wight Factory and Thin Ice

  • Elaine dragged the undead out of Pharasma’s temple; on Zanthar’s call the party burned them behind the building, out of sight of the door.
  • Elaine and Panacea searched the houses south of the cathedral: a black onyx from Create Undead rituals, and correspondence from Illcayna’s servants — the town mass-produced wights and skeletons, “slitting throats and then immediately moving to raise those same people back up.”
  • On the frozen river everyone but Panacea went prone; the ice cracked and Bai Jian and Zanthar went through — “I still fail even with the hero point.” Underwater, Zanthar spotted something shiny.
  • Panacea threw the rope and hauled them out — “Panacea is just strong” — and: “You guys are probably getting hypothermia, so probably need to make a fire.” Cyrathul: “I would help, but unfortunately I’m not that kind of dragon.”
  • Elaine — cold-resistant, roped, on Zanthar’s Air Bubble — brought up an aeon stone and a Devilwing Badge (Greater) off a corpse on the riverbed. Panacea holds the badge for now.
  • Crossing one at a time — Zanthar first (“This place looks like a Pokemon region”), Ren and Bai Jian last. Everyone missed the Perception check; Bai Jian rolled for Ren, and Ren found a hidden bank and a road. “Good boy. He’s finally useful, guys.” Kaazz: “He’s been useful a lot.”
Act V · Exploration · The Desecrated Shrine

Chains of Phalanges and a Hidden Door

  • The road ran into a cave shrine, desecrated with Urgathoan hedonism and draped in chains of phalanges.
  • Zanthar’s Seek found a locked box in an old stash behind the altar.
  • Elaine picked a “really hard lock” over four successes, a hero point to save her only set of tools: “God dang, this thing does not want to be opened.”
  • Inside: a greater staff of healing (Elaine holds it; Panacea already has one) and a potion of flying — “That doesn’t bode well.” Panacea: “Honestly, I think we need some Earthbind at this level.”
  • Panacea touched a wall and a hidden door slid open.
Act VI · Mystery · The Grotto of Seasons

The Murals and the Spirits

  • Bounty’s Light sprang to life in the passage, lighting glowing hand- and footprints into a cavern open to the stars, with a warm, clear pool and four murals.
  • Winter (“This doesn’t sound like Urgathoa”): Panacea named Abadar and Shelyn; the third should be Zon-Kuthon, and isn’t.
  • Spring: Cyrathul touched the mural with the Lifebloom Glaive and the old Isgeri spirits rose. Zanthar and Cyrathul drank from the pool to understand them. The last “preacher” here was undead with “a scythe for an arm” — “I’m almost positive she is kin.” “I cannot believe that Lifebloom Glaive has returned to us.”
  • Bai Jian and Elaine recognized the spring animals as Desna’s and Pharasma’s; Cyrathul read the wind as Gozreh.
  • Summer: a Will save, and everyone fell asleep except Bai Jian and Ren — a full long rest, once every seven days. Bai Jian slapped “the sleeping dragon”: “He got rewarded for failing?… Well, whatever. I’m martial. I don’t care.” His Survival: Sarenrae, Erastil, Gorum.
  • Autumn: Cyrathul read the serpent as Asmodeus, the jackals Rovagug, the scorpions Lamashtu — “as if the Isgeri predicted Cheliax’s infernal future” (Kaazz). A woman on horseback with a black scythe and stripes of white ash — not Urgathoa; no flies.
Act VII · Mystery · Research Overnight

The Dream and Eight Hours of Answers

  • Bai Jian napped under the summer mural. A field after the final harvest; a tall woman in white ash rides up, bleaches into a wight, her horse rotting to bone: “This is what the Isgeri chose to defend their land… evoke the true goddess of the harvest to cleanse the scythe.” “What the hell was that?”
  • Panacea, on her last hero point, crit: the Pallid Plague recipe — a temporary vaccine.
  • Cyrathul crit: anyone in Bounty’s Light’s bright light gains a bonus to saves against Illcayna’s spells.
  • Around 1 a.m.: Cyrathul’s Diplomacy with the spirits crit; Zanthar’s second drink cost him a headache; Elaine worked the mural puzzles — the Lifebloom Glaive can be brandished (1 action) to make wights flee.
  • The spirit hinted there’s more to find in town. ~5 a.m., 9 Calistril.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · For a Style Point

  • He played the surrender for position. With Varlario on her knees and the bat out of reach overhead, he spent his turn getting her to call it down — “so that we can jump it” — and sold it with a grin: “I’ll be benevolent. I’ll be a trusting person. You know, Bai Jian has always been a kind person at heart.” The bat answered that it would eat him. When she healed herself next round, he shrugged: “Whatever, it is what it is… I’m going to get this bat down.”
  • Then he made the leap. Up Cyrathul’s spiral staircase, a Strike that killed the last bat, and a deliberate jump off the top: “You see Bai Jian just literally run up the stairs and then jump out and then hit it in the head… I just wanted to do it for fun.” Thirteen falling damage, prone, worth it.
  • He opened the fight. He’d held the Bless scroll through the parley — giving up his parry for it — read it, sent Ren to flank, and crit the first bat for 57. On Varlario: a natural 1, a hero point, an 18, a 44 crit. “The duality of man.”
  • He stood guard while she cast. “We have several reactive strikes. I’ll stand here. Go ahead.”
  • He trusted the dog. When the whole party missed the Perception check on the ice, he rolled for Ren anyway — and Ren found the road to the shrine. “Good boy. He’s finally useful, guys.” And earlier, when a bat put 35 into him: “Leave my dog alone.”
  • He stayed awake. The summer mural put everyone down but Bai Jian and Ren; he slapped “the sleeping dragon” and missed the free long rest for passing the save — “He got rewarded for failing?… Well, whatever. I’m martial. I don’t care.” His Survival read the summer mural (Sarenrae, Erastil, Gorum) — and he rolled Ren’s too: “He doesn’t have to tell me.”
  • He had the dream. The woman in white ash bleaching into a wight: “evoke the true goddess of the harvest to cleanse the scythe.” “What the hell was that?”
  • He’s frank about his lane: “My job is not where it looks smart” on research; “Bai Jian’s just gonna be looking around and enjoy things because he doesn’t know anything” at the murals.

Panacea · the Steady Hand

  • She knew what a war shard was when nobody else in the party did — a fragment of Gorum, rained down on Golarion when the god of war died.
  • She was the one who stayed on her feet. The only PC to make the Acrobatics check on the ice, she threw the rope and hauled Bai Jian and Zanthar out — “Panacea is just strong” — then: “You guys are probably getting hypothermia, so probably need to make a fire.”
  • She found the grotto. Cruising the shrine while Elaine picked the lock, she touched a wall and the hidden door slid open. At the winter mural she named Abadar and Shelyn, and spotted that the third figure wasn’t who it should be.
  • Her research crit is the party’s plague answer — the Pallid Plague recipe, on her last hero point, and a vaccine they can brew.
  • She fought through a bad start. Foundry wouldn’t stop targeting Serrafina (“Man, I am doing so bad today”); she reset, delayed, and put 72 into the two bats with Divine Lance — including the 38 that left the last one at 4 HP: “I am going to stick to what I know works.” She hero-pointed the shriek to half and kept Life Boost on a mauled Ren.
  • She healed after. Treat Wounds on Bai Jian (25) while the interrogation ran, and a practical read of the loot: “Honestly, I think we need some Earthbind at this level.”

Zanthar Melodune III · the Light in the Grotto

  • His staff opened the way. Bounty’s Light lit the glowing hand- and footprints into the grotto — and the research says that light now protects anyone in it from Illcayna’s spells.
  • He was the one who saw it. Head under the frozen river, he spotted the shine; his Air Bubble scroll put Elaine down there to fetch it. His Seek found the locked box behind the altar.
  • He drank first. When the spectre mimed drinking from the pool, he and Cyrathul went — and he went back for a second drink that cost him a headache.
  • His Slow landed for the full minute on Varlario; his Sure Strike Blazing Bolt crit her for 34; and he threw his maul at the hovering bat — “Thank you, Maul” — then “an Electric Arc type guy.”
  • He set the terms of the release, and asked her name on the way out: “You need to leave and not come back… We treated you well, so remember that if we come across you later.” — and after: “Or come back to bite us.” He argued the shard was worth taking for the cause, and called for burning the dead where no one coming in would see.
  • He was the table’s comic undercut: “We’re famous”; “Put Life Boost on her to kill her easier,” about a void-healing prisoner; “This place looks like a Pokemon region”; “Is Aklo the language of demons?”

Cyrathul · the Diplomat with Teeth

  • He ran the parley. Diplomacy first, the attacks on the road named, and three different requests to leave: “It doesn’t seem very homely. I’m sure you could take a vacation.” When she surrendered: “You could have been on a vacation.”
  • He wrote the surrender terms. “We’ll accept it, but she can’t leave.” On the bat: “Unfortunately, that violates our treaty.” On the manacles: “We said we’d tie you up. I think manacles are similar. Probably more comfortable.”
  • He played the interrogation in character. His Esoteric Lore named the Whispering Way and Tar-Baphon; he linked them to Ezio; when his lore missed the war shard, his player kept what he knew out of it (“Alas, what is this war shard you’re talking about?”); and he asked whether the Wight Mother could be beaten without extracting it. When she asked to cast: “You do anything funny, we’ll kill you.”
  • He killed the fiend. Critical Demoralize, then a jaws crit for 48 that the fear turned from a hit into a crit, then the claw that took its head off — “Yeah, this is why I demoralize.” His Confusion save was a hero-pointed crit success.
  • He built the staircase — Helpful Steps, 40 feet of spiral stairs, the ones Bai Jian ran up for the kill.
  • He woke the spirits. He touched the spring mural with the Lifebloom Glaive, drank, translated, told the spirit about “a necromancer of sorts… who has a war shard infused in her,” read the autumn mural as Asmodeus, Rovagug and Lamashtu, and crit the research that makes Bounty’s Light a shield. Also: “I would help, but unfortunately I’m not that kind of dragon.”

Elaine Stormfrost · the Rogue Who Reads the Room

  • Back at the table, she read the room first. She relayed the voices through the door, her Religion identified Pharasma’s desecrated temple — “Pharasma hates the dead” — and in the huddle: “This being here seems like an abomination to this temple.” She wanted to try diplomacy first.

She dragged the undead out of Pharasma’s temple, wanted an ambush set for the 19th, and worked out why the grotto was never defiled: “it wasn’t open… because she had to touch a wall.”
  • She went into the river. Cold-resistant, roped, on Air Bubble, she brought up the aeon stone and the Devilwing Badge.
  • She picked the shrine’s lock — four successes and a hero point to save her only tools. “God dang, this thing does not want to be opened.” On the potion of flying: “That doesn’t bode well.”
