## Session Summary

Behind the cathedral door in Finder's Gulch, voices were talking in Aklo and a buzzing Necril — **Illcayna** would not be back for eleven days; *"she will perform the ceremony on the 19th as it was written"*; she had gone to fetch *"the last piece for her puzzle."* Then, to the door: *"You don't have to just listen. You may come inside."* Inside was a gutted temple of **Pharasma** daubed with the signs of **Tar-Baphon**, two huge undead bats, a lone beaked fiend, and a tall, pale woman in black leather robes — **Varlario Demicci**, a diplomat, she said, for *"those that whisper along the way,"* lodged there as Illcayna's guest. She offered wine. The party called it poison; she was delighted to be offended. **Cyrathul** asked her, more than once and very politely, to take a vacation. She was on assignment. She stepped into the party's huddle to share *"a secret too"* — in Aklo — and the party fanned out.

It was an Extreme fight and it was over in four rounds. **Bai Jian** read a Bless scroll and opened with a 57-damage crit on the first bat; the fiend's own **Malodorous Shriek** then blew that bat apart. **Elaine**'s whip crit slowed the fiend, **Cyrathul**'s critical Demoralize and Draconic Frenzy tore its head off, and **Zanthar**'s Slow and a Sure Strike crit, Bai Jian's hero-pointed 44 and Elaine's whip put Varlario on her knees: *"I'll give you anything. Just spare my life."* The party took the surrender on its own terms — no walking away, manacles on — and **Bai Jian spent his turn getting her to call the last bat down** where he could reach it. It answered that it would eat him. **Panacea**'s Divine Lance crit left it at 4 HP, and Bai Jian ran up **Cyrathul's conjured spiral staircase** and leapt off it to kill the thing, taking 13 falling damage *"for a style point."*

The interrogation was the session's real prize. Varlario is from the **Whispering Way**, allied with Tar-Baphon, sent to spy on the Pallid Princess — and the **Wight Mother has fused a war shard, a fragment of the dead god Gorum, into herself**. The Way doesn't trust the Urgathoans to stay controllable. Varlario traded her research — **an occult magnet that will tear the shard out once the right ritual is performed**, and notes that set **Illcayna's final sacrifice for dusk on 19 Calistril**, somewhere reachable through the new Urgathoan temple across the frozen river — for her freedom, and left as a bat. The night after was exploration: a frozen river that dropped Bai Jian and Zanthar through the ice, a riverbed corpse that gave Elaine an aeon stone and a **Devilwing Badge**, a hidden road **Ren** sniffed out, and a sealed grotto of **seasonal murals** that Zanthar's **Bounty's Light** lit a path into. Cyrathul's **Lifebloom Glaive** woke the spirits of the old Isgeri tribes there. Drinking from their pool let him and Zanthar understand them, the summer mural put everyone but Bai Jian to sleep for a full night's rest, and eight hours of research turned up a **Pallid Plague vaccine**, a **save bonus in Bounty's Light against Illcayna's spells**, and a way to make **wights flee from the Lifebloom Glaive**. Bai Jian, napping under the summer mural, dreamed of a woman in white ash who bleached into a wight in front of him while her horse rotted to bone.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Cathedral (Finder's Gulch, evening of 8 Calistril)
- Overheard through the door, in Aklo and then Necril: **Illcayna** won't be back for **at least 11 days**; *"she will perform the ceremony on the 19th as it was written"*; she went *"to get the last piece for her puzzle"*; she spends her time *"in that temple across the river."* **Elaine relayed what she understood to the party.** Then the voice called out: *"You may come inside."*
- Inside: windows shattered and boarded, **two huge bat-like undead (Skavelings)**, a single beaked flying fiend like the swarms outside (**a Sarcovalt**), and a tall, very pale elf-like woman in black leather robes.
- In Common: *"Oh, you must be the Hellbreakers that have been causing such a ruckus in the south."* **Zanthar**: *"We're famous."* She told a bat that no, it did not get to eat them.
- **Cyrathul** opened: *"I'm sure we can talk this out diplomatically, but… I'm assuming it's your undead minions, or maybe someone you knew, attacked us on the way here."* She called them the **Witch Queen's** servants, not hers: *"We're here on a diplomatic mission"* — from *"those that whisper along the way."* **Illcayna** had *"graced us with this wonderful abode."*
- **Elaine's Religion check (28)**: it was a **temple of Pharasma**, its stained glass smashed, desecrated with the signs of **Tar-Baphon**. Elaine: *"Pharasma hates the dead."* Cyrathul: *"Or undead. That's what the whispering was about."*
- The Witch Queen — *"the Pallid Princess"* — was away fetching something; Varlario wouldn't say what.
- In a whispered huddle **Elaine** made the case: *"This being here seems like an abomination to this temple"* — but try it diplomatically first. Varlario brought out wine; the party called it poison, and she looked theatrically offended — *"I can't believe you would think so lowly of me."*
- **Cyrathul**: *"We seem to be at a bit of an impasse here. Is there any chance you could leave this building?"* — *"Leave my home? I've been granted this place."* — *"It doesn't seem very homely. I'm sure you could take a vacation."* She was on assignment. He gave her one last chance.
- She joined the huddle to share *"a secret too"* — in **Aklo** (Kaazz: *"Dark Fae"*). The party *"fanned out, exploring the place."* Initiative.

### Act II — Combat: Varlario's Court (see Combat Encounters)
- **Round 1**: the bat's **Bone-Chilling Screech**; **Bai Jian**'s Bless scroll and a **57-damage crit**; **Elaine**'s Faux Bulwark and a crit that slowed the fiend; **Zanthar**'s **Slow** landed on Varlario for a minute; **Panacea**'s Divine Lance crit; Varlario's **Confusion** at Cyrathul, hero-pointed into a crit success; the fiend's **Malodorous Shriek blew its own bat apart**.
- **Round 2**: Bai Jian's hero-point reroll — a natural 1 into an 18 and a **44 crit** on Varlario (*"The duality of man"*); the other bat mauled **Ren** for 35 (*"Leave my dog alone"*); **Cyrathul's crit Demoralize and Frenzy took the fiend's head off** (*"this is why I demoralize"*); Zanthar's Sure Strike Blazing Bolt crit Varlario for 34.
- **Varlario surrendered** at ~15 HP of 110: *"I'm not sure why you decided to come into my house and attack me. I said I was an emissary."* Cyrathul: ***"You could have been on a vacation."***
- **Round 3**: the party accepted the surrender but not the terms — *"Simply leaving is not enough anymore"* — manacles, not freedom. **Bai Jian spent his turn getting her to call the bat down** — the plan, he said, was to bring it within reach *"so that we can jump it"* — with a wry *"I'll be benevolent… Bai Jian has always been a kind person at heart."* The bat: *"He says he will eat you."* Cyrathul: *"Unfortunately, that violates our treaty."* Then **Helpful Steps** — a 40-ft spiral staircase — Zanthar's thrown maul, and Panacea's crit Divine Lance.
- **Round 4**: Varlario healed herself with Harm while Bai Jian let her; Bai Jian **ran up the staircase, jumped off and killed the last bat** — and fell 15 feet for fun.

### Act III — Surrender Terms
- Varlario was still burning from Bai Jian's **vitalizing rune** — she has **void healing**, so vitality hurts her (the table read her as undead; Elaine's Recall Knowledge on her failed, so it was never confirmed). The party worked out **Assisted Recovery** to stop it — *"We're not really geared to trying to kill someone to heal them"* (Cyrathul) — and got there after three more ticks, at 9 HP.
- Manacles on. *"I don't understand. I thought we had an accord."* Cyrathul: *"We said we'd tie you up. I think manacles are similar. Probably more comfortable."*
- **Panacea Treated Wounds on Bai Jian** (25) — *"That's why I'm moving towards you, Bai Jian"* — while the others talked.

### Act IV — The Interrogation
- **Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore (Diverse Lore, 29)**: the **Whispering Way** — an ancient, secretive cult that promotes undeath as the perfect state; **in league with Tar-Baphon**. Elaine's Society (23) had it on the tip of her tongue.
- Cyrathul tied it to **Ezio** — *"the fat man from the ball"* — and the Urgathoans the party has fought before.
- Varlario's real assignment: *"To spy on our good friend the pallid princess, to make sure that she doesn't go against our wishes. We really want what she has… the **war shard**, of course. **The Wight Mother has a war shard that she's fused with herself.** How are you so ignorant of all these things?"* Cyrathul: *"We've been caught up in politics."*
- Cyrathul's lore missed; his player knew what a war shard was and kept it out of the character's mouth (*"I know what it is, but I'm not going to play the game"*): *"Alas, what is this war shard you're talking about?"* **Panacea knew**: a shard of the destroyed **god of war (Gorum)**, rained down on Golarion when he died — a shard of divinity.
- **Elaine** pressed: *"Go on. Yeah, out with it, please. You're not in the best situation to keep secrets."* — *"The Way doesn't really believe that the Urgathoans are very controllable. And so I've been devising a way to remove the shard from her."*
- She asked for the manacles off. **Elaine**: *"Not a chance. Definitely not. We will just carry you where we need to go. We know you're a caster."*
- **The deal**: her notes and the device, for her freedom. **Cyrathul** asked whether the Wight Mother could be beaten *without* extracting the shard — *"I wouldn't try it"* — *"No offense, [you're not] the best gauge of strength."* Zanthar argued the shard was worth taking for the cause.
- Unshackled, she cast to reveal a hidden cache — **Bai Jian**: *"We have several reactive strikes. I'll stand here. Go ahead."* A floor tile opened: a **powerful occult magnet** and her **notes**. *"As long as someone is able to perform the correct ritual… the magnet should just tear the shard from her body."* She hadn't found the ritual: *"Very hard to research how to kill the lion while sitting in the lion's den."* Cyrathul, before she left: *"You do anything funny, we'll kill you."*
- Zanthar: *"You need to leave and not come back… We treated you well, so remember that if we come across you later."* Zanthar asked her name: **"Varlario."** She walked out, became a bat, and flew into the night.
- **The notes** (Necril — Elaine read them aloud): **Illcayna's final ritual is the evening of 19 Calistril — as dusk gives way to night, a final sacrifice finalizes the curse**, at a location in Finder's Gulch Varlario was never shown. She believed it was reached **through the new Urgathoan temple across the frozen stream**. It is the evening of the 8th: **~11 days**.
- Kaazz introduced the **research subsystem** — four hours per check. **Bai Jian**: *"You guys do it. My job is not where it looks smart."*

### Act V — Clearing the Town (after the break)
- **Elaine** dragged the undead out of the temple — *"so they're not desecrating"* — and on **Zanthar**'s call the party burned them behind the building, *"just so that they won't see [them] when they come in."* Elaine also wanted to set up an ambush for the 19th.
- **Elaine and Panacea** searched a cluster of old houses south of the cathedral: a **black onyx** left over from **Create Undead** rituals, the ritual etchings beside it, and correspondence from **Illcayna's servants** — Elaine read it: the town was a **wight and skeleton factory**. *"They were slitting throats and then immediately moving to raise those same people back up."*

### Act VI — The Frozen River
- The river is a flooded street frozen over. On the ice: Acrobatics — everyone but **Panacea** (*"the cat who made the acrobatics check"*) went prone. A loud crack. Reflex saves: **Bai Jian and Zanthar went through** — *"I still fail even with the hero point."* (Elaine's hero point hadn't registered in Foundry; Kaazz gave her the success.)
- Underwater, **Zanthar** spotted something shiny in the light coming off Bai Jian.
- **Panacea threw the rope** (*"Help me pull, guys"*) — Kaazz: *"Panacea is just strong, she's able to pull you guys right out."* Then: *"You guys are probably getting hypothermia, so probably need to make a fire."* Cyrathul: *"I would help, but unfortunately I'm not that kind of dragon."*
- **Elaine** — cold-resistant, roped, on Zanthar's **Air Bubble** scroll — swam down to **a corpse on the riverbed** and brought up an **aeon stone** (a dying-related effect; *"pretty niche"*) and a **Devilwing Badge (Greater)** spellheart. She asked for the badge as a ranged option; the table worked out it needs the Cast a Spell activity. **Panacea is holding it for now**, likely to sell.
- They crossed one at a time — **Zanthar** first (*"I'm nice and small"*; *"This place looks like a Pokemon region"*), then Cyrathul, Elaine and Panacea, with **Ren and Bai Jian** last.

### Act VII — Ren's Road and the Shrine
- Kaazz called for Perception checks halfway across; everyone missed (*"You guys are killing me"*). **Bai Jian rolled for Ren too** — *"Let me roll for Ren. Let's see if he's spotted anything"* — and Ren sniffed the air and stepped back out onto the ice toward a **second bank** and a **road** nobody had spotted. *"Good boy. He's finally useful, guys."* Kaazz: *"He's been useful a lot."*
- The road ran into a cave: an older shrine, **desecrated with Urgathoan hedonism**, draped in **chains of phalanges**.
- **Zanthar's Seek** found an old stash behind the altar — **a locked box** — and **Elaine** worked a *"really hard lock"* through four successes (a Guidance from the party; a hero point to protect her only set of tools): *"God dang, this thing does not want to be opened."* Inside: a **greater staff of healing** and a **potion of flying** (*"That doesn't bode well."*). Panacea already has a greater healing staff; **Elaine took it** for her once-a-day Faith Tattoo heal. Cyrathul and Panacea both concluded the party needs **Earthbind** at this level.
- **Panacea** touched a wall and **a hidden door slid open**.

### Act VIII — The Grotto of Seasons
- **Zanthar's Bounty's Light sprang to life** in the passage — fiery tendrils, and glowing hand- and footprints guiding them in like UV paint. Bai Jian tried the Religion check untrained anyway (*"I know nothing about religion"*).
- A cavern open to the stars 150 feet up, a shallow, warm, crystal-clear pool, and **four murals**. **Bai Jian**, untrained in Religion: *"Bai Jian's just gonna be looking around and enjoy things because he doesn't know anything."*
- **Winter**: three figures at a hearth (Zanthar: *"This doesn't sound like Urgathoa"*) — **Panacea recognized Abadar and Shelyn**; the third should be **Zon-Kuthon** and looks nothing like him.
- **Spring**: the twelve tribes around a warrior raising the **Lifebloom Glaive**. **Cyrathul drew the Glaive from his spacious pouch and touched the mural** — and spectres of the old Isgeri tribes rose around them. A woman mimed drinking from the pool; **Zanthar and Cyrathul drank** (hot as spicy sauce) and could understand her. Cyrathul translated.
  - She spoke of the last *"preacher"* to come here: undead, *"a scythe for an arm."* **Cyrathul**: *"I think this is the woman with the war shard in her."* The spirit: *"I believe she was actually one of my people a long, long time ago… I'm almost positive she is kin."* The spirits can't act against her unless she moves against them first.
  - **Bai Jian and Elaine** recognized the animals as **Desna's and Pharasma's**; Cyrathul read the wind as **Gozreh**. *"I cannot believe that Lifebloom Glaive has returned to us."*
- **Summer**: Kellids and goblinoids exchanging **Bounty's Light**, a sun-crowned dove, a stag, a rhinoceros. Touched — a Will save — and **everyone crumpled asleep except Bai Jian and Ren**, spirits included. Bai Jian slapped *"the sleeping dragon"* and sent Ren to Panacea; after sixty seconds they woke with **the benefits of a long rest** (usable once every seven days). Bai Jian: *"Wait. He got rewarded for failing?… Well, whatever. I'm martial. I don't care."*
  - Bai Jian's Survival (*"Can Ren do it as well?… He doesn't have to tell me"*): the dove is **Sarenrae**, the stag **Erastil**, the rhinoceros the dead **Gorum**.
- **Autumn**: a wheat field at harvest, with scorpions and jackals leering. Cyrathul's Religion: a serpent carved around the whole grotto is **Asmodeus** (the leap month of an ancient calendar), the jackals **Rovagug**, the scorpions **Lamashtu** — Kaazz: *"It's as if the Isgeri predicted Cheliax's infernal future."* A woman on horseback holds a **black scythe**: not Urgathoa — no flies. And Kaazz told one of the summer-mural sleepers that **she had visited them in that dream** — wearing **stripes of white ash**, not Kellid dress.

### Act IX — Research Under the Murals (overnight)
- **Bai Jian** took watch, then a nap under the summer mural. **The dream**: a field after the final harvest, the road out of town at the start of the adventure. A tall woman in **stripes of white ash** rides up; she **bleaches into a wight** and her horse rots to a skeleton. Speaking for a whole people: *"This is what the Isgeri chose to defend their land… evoke the true goddess of the harvest to cleanse the scythe."* Her hair blows back over bone, a huge black scythe in her hands — and he woke. *"What the hell was that?"*
- **Panacea** spent her last hero point and **crit**: the recipe for the **Pallid Plague**, the disease all children of Urgathoa carry — the party can **brew a temporary vaccine**.
- **Cyrathul crit**: **anyone in the bright light of Bounty's Light gains a bonus to saves against Illcayna's spells.**
- Second round, around 1 a.m.: Cyrathul drank again and his Diplomacy with the spirits crit; Zanthar's second drink gave him a pounding headache; **Elaine worked the murals' puzzles**. Together: **the Lifebloom Glaive can be brandished like a religious symbol (one action) to make wights that see it flee.**
- The spirit suggested there's more to find in town. **~5 a.m., 9 Calistril.** Next session: more of Finder's Gulch, more research.
