## Session Summary

The party spent the morning disguised as ranch hands at the **Vulaunex Ranch**, delivering calves for an old cowboy, until the cart they had escaped Saringallow in came up the road with League scouting news. Two groups of new recruits were camped a few miles south and were due at the **Sereni Manor** in the morning: a disciplined contingent of Isgeri soldiers from Elidir, and a band of mercenaries. Replace one of them and the party walks in the front door. The rancher's parting advice was to stay off the south road. The League used to treat with the Fae there, but they have been at war with them since the Chelaxians took over.

They picked the mercenaries, some of them wearing the insignia of the broken **Azri** house, and walked in by the road with a story about missing the rendezvous. **Cyrathul** carried it. Asked *"Are you a freaking dragon?"* he said *"Yes, I get that a lot,"* and when the camp took them for Fae he cut his own palm on the sergeant's iron dagger to prove otherwise. His missed-rendezvous story landed. Then he rolled three 3s in a row, the Drill Sergeant went to check the story with **Nixa Volsetti**, the Elidir commander, and Cyrathul stepped into her path: *"I can't let you do that."* Two rounds later the sergeant and the whole mercenary band were dead, the last of them cut down by **Zanthar's** Lightning Bolt as they ran screaming for the other camp. Nixa came marching over to find bodies in the road. Cyrathul told her there had been a mole among them, claimed **Colonel Rithert** had sent the party, and offered to take the dead men's posts: *"evidently we're a little better at guarding than these people were."* She handed them shovels.

It didn't hold. Before long Nixa had worked out that Rithert *"was not at liberty to send out anyone."* **Bai Feng**, keeping watch, overheard her soldiers cursing Caeto for leaving the south to the undead and turning the army on Saringallow, and **Panacea** saw the opening: *"We want to recruit these guys."* Nixa already knew who they were. A dragon walking into camp *"really fit the parameters of those we were told had defeated Illcayna."* Panacea asked her straight (*"Is your loyalty with Caeto or with Isger?"*), Cyrathul's nat-20 lore turned up her history (rescued from the **demon bile** incident as a child by **Caeto** himself), and his Diplomacy 39 found her answer. *"He's doing exactly what he said he wouldn't do. He's becoming one of them."* She sealed a letter saying her contingent had been called away and these mercenaries sent in its place, marched her troops to **High Fort**, gave the League **two heavy cavalry**, and left the party a lesser reinforcing rune, two greater smoke balls and a Swift Block Cabochon. 80 XP, and the first three edge points of the infiltration.

At the Sereni Manor a watchmage and a **Draconic Fumecrux** guard the door, and the banner over the bell tower is **Sephone Haugini's**. The captain of the guard toured the new hires through a house lit to banish every shadow. They learned the shift rotation and the guard posts, and that the second floor is off limits. A research team of **Kylixes and Zealots of Asmodeus** is in the library pulling books on teleportation and hellish summoning for *"the ritual for the mistress,"* and indentured servants and conjured demons are digging in the garden for an old basement. The Fae took the manor after Illcayna fell, *"calling it like an overdue payment"*. Sephone drove them out, but they still come at night, and the **redcaps** are said to be under the thumb of a pukwudgie named **Rustflesh**. Sephone has offered climbing armbands to anyone who will clear the **breathless thicket** off the roof. The party took the bounty. Cyrathul built a Helpful Steps staircase. One thicket grabbed Cyrathul, and Bai Feng's nat-20 crit killed it. The other two grabbed Bai Feng and tried to suffocate him, and Zanthar burned them both off the roof. Among the ash: a pair of Boots of Bounding, and written on the wall in rust-coloured letters, *"Rustflesh's house. Stay out."* The session ended at five in the afternoon on the roof of a diabolist's manor, the party waiting for dark.

## Key Events

### Act I — Ranch Hands (morning, ~12 Pharast)
- The party was disguised as **ranch hands** at the Vulaunex Ranch, delivering calves with a helpful old cowboy. Ethran pitched in one-armed. Zanthar: *"We're doing ranch hand activities."* Cyrathul: *"Why are we hiding as ranch hands? Get in the manor."*
- The cart they had escaped Saringallow in arrived with a League contact and the scouting report: **two groups of new recruits**, due at the Sereni Manor the next morning — a well-trained **contingent from Elidir** and a band of **mercenaries**. *"Infiltrate one or both of them, maybe intercept or replace one of the groups."* Asked how they were fitting in, Zanthar: *"Well, no one's died yet."*
- **The south road belongs to the Fae.** The area *"used to belong to the Fae. We used to treat with them a long time ago,"* but since the Chelaxians took over the two have been at war, and there are supposedly Fae in the manor itself too. Cyrathul wanted to treat with them (*"They're fighting the Cheliax, you said, right?"*). The rancher spat on the ground: *"I don't know if I'd trust in a Fae."*
- Zanthar: *"We never followed instructions."* Cyrathul: *"Yeah, we're real bad at that."*

### Act II — Plan A (the two camps)
- **Stealth.** Bai Feng followed the expert on Cyrathul, whose regalia makes it work even though he's only trained. With the bonus he rolled the party's best Stealth, a **29**.
- Two camps: the northern one loud and boisterous, the southern one quiet, mismatched and disorganized, its shields embossed with odd crests. A few of the southerners wore **Azri** family insignia. **Bai Feng** was the only one who could make out their whispers (Perception 31): the loud northerners would *"attract the Fae"*, and the redcaps would *"come out and try to gobble them up."*
- **The plan.** Cyrathul proposed passing as late hires who'd missed the rendezvous, with the masquerade scarves from the gala as a fallback (*"see, I have a piece of the insignia here"*). For a name to drop, he put Recall Knowledge (Esoteric Lore 31) into the gala's guest list. Kaazz supplied Caeto, the **Azris** and **Colonel Rithert**, and **Panacea**, digging through her notes (*"Man, that's so far back in my notes"*), added **Sasatoli** and **Lezinia**. The Azri house has broken up since **Rencelos**, which is likely why its men are selling their swords now. Cyrathul built the cover story from that.
- **Walking in.** A lone merc: *"Are you a freaking dragon?"* — **Cyrathul**: *"Yes, I get that a lot. A wizard cursed me after I…"* — *"Look alive, boys. The Fae are here."* — **Panacea**: *"We are not the Fae."* — **Bai Feng**: *"You ever seen the Fae looking like a dragon?"* Cyrathul asked for iron to prove it, and the **Drill Sergeant** handed him a dagger hilt-first. He drew a claw across it, let it bleed, and handed it back.
- The mercenaries sneered at non-humans. Bai Feng: *"I'm a human here."* **Cyrathul**: *"I'll vouch for them… normally it's my kind looking down on you."*
- The rendezvous story itself landed (Deception 33). Then the questions kept coming. *"This is the rendezvous."* Cyrathul rolled a 3, then put a hero point into the reroll, another 3 that took the +10 and came in at 30. *"Who hired you?"* — *"Yes, I'm afraid that's confidential with our clients."* Another 3 (*"That was three times in a row I got a three"*). *"Where are you from?"* — *"The moon."* Bai Feng: *"I am from a land far away."* Zanthar: *"We're here for gold."* — *"Yeah, ain't we all?"* Then a 25 that still wasn't enough. Bai Feng: *"The more we lie, the more in trouble we are."*
- The sergeant went to verify the story with **Nixa** in the northern camp. Every hand in the mercenary camp went to a sword. Cyrathul stepped into her path: *"I can't let you do that. I'm sorry."* Bai Feng: *"We're activating Plan B."*

### Act III — Plan B (combat — see below)
- Two rounds. **The Drill Sergeant and the whole Mercenary Band dead.** Zanthar took both kills: a Blazing Bolt *"straight through the side of their ear"* and a Lightning Bolt through the routing band. Cyrathul's Draconic Frenzy did the heaviest damage.
- Bai Feng's first fight as an Exemplar: *"I don't usually hit people, but I guess we gotta do what we gotta do."* Out of character: *"This character is a lot. Oh my god."* He entered his Marshal Stance with *"Alright, guys. Follow my lead,"* and Kaazz: *"See, Bai Feng, look how regal on the battlefield."*
- **80 XP** (Moderate).

### Act IV — Nixa Volsetti (noon)
- *"What the fuck is going on over there? I knew those mercenaries gonna be up to no good."* **Nixa** marched out to find dead men in the road. Bai Feng deferred to Cyrathul: *"I'm not used to deal with these situations."* Cyrathul's first Fae story missed (23). The second landed (34): *"There was a mole among them and when we accused them, they attacked us."*
- *"Who exactly sent you?"* Cyrathul went through the gala guest list out loud, ruled out Caeto, and said **Colonel Rithert**. *"The dandy?"* Then he offered the dead men's jobs: *"We did just finish our contract"* (gesturing at the bodies) *"and evidently we're a little better at guarding than these people were"* (Diplomacy 31). Bai Feng offered to Aid and then thought better of it, since Bai Feng wouldn't know any of this. She re-sheathed her sword, tossed down shovels and walked off without a word.
- **The bodies.** Panacea: *"Got to start digging."* Zanthar **crit-identified** the sergeant's *"unusual"* sword (Arcana 36) as a +1 striking longsword, and **Cyrathul** found the sergeant's tent and **600 gp** of pay in it, 150 each. While they searched the camp, Bai Feng: *"I'm supposed to be a hunter, not a killer."* Cyrathul: *"War is hell. We tried to do this the peaceful way."*
- **The second interview went worse.** *"Who might you all be?"* The party realized they had no group name, and no names they could use. **Cyrathul**: *"You may call me Rem"* (nat 1 on the Deception). *"It's not personal. It's more safety-wise."* Cyrathul: *"We all have alliances."* Bai Feng, backing him: *"We all have alliances. You know, in this line of work."* Cyrathul: *"We'd rather go by traveling names… like a stage name of sorts."* Then Nixa: *"Colonel Rithert was not at liberty to send out anyone."* That Deception failed too.
- The retreat. **Panacea**, setting up her own joke: *"Should we go to bed? It's time for bed, isn't it? Don't we have an early morning?"* … *"It's noon."* Bai Feng: *"We're tired after drinking all those grapes."* **Cyrathul**: *"I am a moon dragon. We're a bit nocturnal."*

### Act V — Turning the Contingent (afternoon)
- **Bai Feng and Zanthar** (Perception 34 and 32) overheard Nixa as she walked away: *"I was really hoping they were here to alleviate the problem in Saringallow, but I wonder what they're doing."* Zanthar proposed ambushing them asleep (*"Maybe we need to ambush them when they're asleep"*), and Bai Feng was for it (*"We can wait until they get all drunk and ambush them"*). Then **Bai Feng** caught the soldiers on the next listen: *"It's not like Caeto sent anybody down to stop the undead uprising… This is supposed to be Isger, not at the whim of some turncoat Chelaxian dog."*
- **Panacea** saw it first: *"Oh, maybe we want to turn them. We want to recruit these guys."* Cyrathul: *"Yeah, the one lady was ostracized too."* Bai Feng turned the party around: *"Well, it's time to go talk."* Then **Panacea's** Perception (30) caught Nixa herself, to her men: *"Time and time again the Isger army is turned against its own people… when the southern towns asked for help against the undead, we sent no one. No one!"*
- They walked back in and were handed mead. **Panacea's Make an Impression** came up 6, and she spent a hero point on it. She rerolled a 10, and the +10 made it 30. Nixa: *"I was to understand that the Hellbreakers were the ones that defeated the undead, supposedly finding a war shard in the process."* Bai Feng: *"You have a reliable source of information."* **Panacea**: *"But we also are here to befriend you… Saringallow is a travesty."* Her Diplomacy came up 3. A second hero point brought it to 23, still short. *"We need Sai to be doing these diplomacy checks."* Bai Feng pushed Sai forward.
- Nixa: *"A large dragon walk into the camp… really fit the parameters of those we were told had defeated Illcayna. So, tell me what you want from us."* **Cyrathul**: *"Why don't you join us? … I think the administration has proven time and time again that they cannot rule justly or effectively."* His Diplomacy came up 24, two short, and the table broke for ten minutes.
- **After the break.** *"I want to help. I do… I'm a good soldier… Whether I like it or not, I do owe Caeto."* **Cyrathul**: *"Don't you owe your country more than him?"* **Panacea**: *"Is your loyalty with Caeto or with Isger?"*
- **Recall Knowledge.** **Cyrathul's nat 20 (Esoteric Lore 37)**: Nixa was caught up in the **demon bile incident** as a child, **rescued by Caeto** and left at an orphanage, and she is *"Isgeri through and through."* Zanthar's Society (32) succeeded as well. **Bai Feng rolled an untrained nat 20**, and Kaazz: *"Somehow Bai Feng, who's never been in Isger in his entire life until he woke up here just a week ago."* Pax had an answer ready: he'd heard about it from someone he talked to after he woke up.
- **Cyrathul**: *"You know it isn't right what's going on."* **Diplomacy 39, a crit.** A single tear rolled off the tip of Nixa's nose, and she wiped it away the way a proud person does. *"He's doing exactly what he said he wouldn't do. He's becoming one of them. He is one of them now… I will not arrive tomorrow with you… I want to free Isger."*
- **The letter.** She wrote it on her standard bearer's back, then sealed and stamped it: her contingent has been *"called away on an important mission"* and has sent these mercenaries in its place (with a wink). *"Don't break the seal."* Her troops are going to **High Fort**. Panacea said Logos first and Kaazz pointed them to High Fort instead. The party sent a message ahead to High Fort the night before (retconned in at the table), so **Jenverick** would expect them. She warned them: *"You know that it's Diabolist there, right? … very wicked?"* **Panacea**: *"We've met a lot of wicked people on our travels, so we will do what we can."*
- **+2 Heavy Cavalry** for the League army. **Edge points** (Kaazz): spend one to turn any failed skill check outside combat into a success. Nixa's reward: a **lesser reinforcing rune**, **two greater smoke balls** and a **Swift Block Cabochon**. **80 XP** for handling Nixa without a fight, and **3 edge points** by morning, with no awareness gained.

### Act VI — The Sereni Manor (morning → afternoon, 13 Pharast)
- Thirty minutes through the woods: a stone wall, a huge manor with part of its roof caved in and braced up, and a lot of activity. At the door, a robed **watchmage** and *"a massive demon looking thing"*, the **Draconic Fumecrux**. The mage wouldn't touch the letter (*"I'm sure that's not for me"*) and sent the demon in, in Diabolic, which only Panacea understood. It came back: *"the mistress is sleeping."* Then, in Common: *"But away from me. You look like you stink."*
- The **captain of the guard** looked down the road for the soldiers who should have been with them, read the letter, and handed out shoddy uniforms bearing a seal none of them had seen before. The same flag flies over the bell tower. Zanthar: *"Do you have anything of my size?"* — *"Of course. We have many halflings here."* — *"Calling me a halfling is an insult."*
- **The tour** ran as victory-point rounds. What they learned:
  - The house is **lit everywhere**: candles, lanterns and magical light *"to keep shadows out of certain areas."* The doors are in poor shape.
  - **Shifts** run 8 a.m.–4 p.m., 4 p.m.–midnight and midnight–8 a.m., one per guard. Two meals a day. **Cyrathul** heard that slacking is common and guards often miss their posts.
  - **Panacea** got the guard posts. Each shift there is one **line infantry** and one **elite watch officer** covering the entrance and the downstairs hall, the same again in the **second-floor corridor**, and the **Draconic Fumecrux and a watchmage** out front and in the garden at all times.
  - The east wing is collapsed rubble. *"Don't try to climb it."* **No one is allowed on the second floor.**
  - *"You must, must, must stay out of Sephone's way."* Her **private research team** of **two Kylixes and three Zealots of Asmodeus**, *"the Lord's chain around their neck"*, works mostly in the library. *"I just know that there's something here that they're looking for. I know they're digging in the garden."*
  - The Fae *"took over the entire manor"* right after the heroes killed the wight who wanted to be a god, *"calling it like an overdue payment or something."* Sephone drove them off, but they hide upstairs and come out at night.
  - A guard told **Cyrathul** about the old Kellid tribes who set out treats and trinkets for the Fae, who turned angry when the tributes stopped. There are pixies and draxies about, and some trinkets would probably keep them friendly. The **redcaps** kill on sight. *"You ever seen a redcap before?"* — *"Oh, yeah. Nasty buggers."* He'd heard the redcaps are oppressed by a pukwudgie named **Rustflesh**, and that if Rustflesh were gone they might turn friendly. He's been leaving trinkets out for the pixies himself, though he'd never admit it.
  - In the garden, demons and indentured humanoids are digging a huge hole. Zanthar: *"I'm glad we're not digging."* Sephone has offered *"special armbands"* that make climbing easier to whoever clears the **breathless thicket** on the roof. Zanthar: *"How much is the bounty?"*
- **Asking around (4 p.m., off until the 8 a.m. shift).** Zanthar: *"Why don't we find out what they're looking for?"* A malnourished servant in the pit said it's *"some sort of old basement,"* and that's all he's been told.
- **The library.** Two humans and two tailed humanoids were pulling books and transcribing. **Zanthar** read the spines: **teleportation, conjuration and summoning, especially hellish summoning.** Panacea: *"Probably in this basement that we're digging for."* **Cyrathul**: *"What are you guys transcribing?"* — *"Oh, just the ritual for the mistress, you know, the usual."* The researcher put a hand to his temple to shut Cyrathul out, and Cyrathul slapped him on the back, hard, on the way out. **Bai Feng** noticed the ancient shelves could easily be rigged to collapse on the researchers. *"I don't know if I want to… do anything with that information."*

### Act VII — The Roof (combat — see below)
- The guards turned them back at the stairs (*"No one's allowed upstairs"*) and at the rubble. Bai Feng: *"We have Helpful Steps, surely."* **Cyrathul** cast it outside, a staircase to the roof, and the watchmage let them go once Zanthar told him it was for the bounty. Up top was a ten-foot patch of dead shrubs with wilted flowers: **three breathless thickets**.
- Barely two rounds. **Bai Feng's nat-20 crit killed the first**, and **Zanthar's two-ray Blazing Bolt** burned the other two off the roof.
- Tangled in the burnt vines: **Boots of Bounding**, which went to Cyrathul (*"Extra speed never hurts"*). On the wall, in rust-coloured letters that might be blood: ***"Rustflesh's house. Stay out."*** Panacea read it (she knows Fey): *"That's the guy that has the oppression spell on the redcaps."*
- Almost 5 p.m. and the sun setting. The Fae only come out after dark, and the party stayed on the roof to wait. Bai Feng: *"Our Halloween is destroying these plants."* **End of session.**

He shifted immanence to the Mirrored Aegis.

- **Drill Sergeant** rallied her men (*"come on boys, you got this"*) and swung twice at Cyrathul. Both missed (23, 18).
- **Zanthar** cast a two-ray **Blazing Bolt**. The first ray hit the sergeant (31), and the bolt went *"straight through the side of their ear and blows out the other side of their head."* **Killed.** The second ray crit-missed the band on a nat 1.

**Round 2**
- **Panacea**: **Divine Lance** on the band (28) for 18, then **Trade Death for Life** (band failed, 9 void). The fast healing went to Bai Feng (+4).
- **Bai Feng** sparked **Raise the Walls** on himself and Cyrathul (+1 status to AC and Reflex). **The Radiant** rolled 18 of healing on Cyrathul, and 8 of it landed because that was all he was down. He entered **Inspiring Marshal Stance** (Diplomacy by Assurance 22 vs DC 22) for a +1 status bonus to the party's attacks: *"Alright, guys. Follow my lead."* Then he struck the band (28) for 24.
- **Cyrathul**: **Exploit Vulnerability crit (37)**, exploiting the band's area-damage weakness (+10 on each of his hits). He asked for the weakest save (Will) and the band's favorite cupcake flavor. Then a **Draconic Frenzy**: jaws hit (33 taken), claw hit (34), claw crit-miss. *"We got this, boys!"* Bai Feng: *"I'm not too sure about that."* Half the band was down and bleeding.
- **Mercenary Band** broke and ran around the party for the northern camp, screaming for help. Bai Feng's **Reactive Strike** missed (23) and Cyrathul's **Twisting Tail** hit (27 taken).
- **Zanthar**: **Lightning Bolt** through the fleeing troop (failed save, 33). **Killed.** *"You actually just really hate these guys."*

**Damage dealt** (after the troop's weakness): Cyrathul 163 · Bai Feng 69 · Zanthar 60, both finishing blows, of which 21 was needed · Panacea 27. Party damage taken: 16, all from one Let 'em Have It. **Kills: Zanthar 2.**

### Encounter 2 — The Roof (breathless thickets)
**Enemies**: **3 × Breathless Thicket** (58 HP, AC 21, Sinewy Leaves +20 reach 10 with a grab, *Suffocate* DC 26 Fort; shrugged off most electricity and physical damage; **weakness fire 15**)
**Threat**: Low · **1 round plus Zanthar's opening turn of round 2** · XP not yet awarded
**Initiative**: Zanthar 32 · Cyrathul 31 · Bai Feng 26 · Thickets 19 / 17 / 16 · Panacea 15
**Opening**: Bai Feng went in on Defend and started the fight with his Sapling Shield raised.

**Round 1**
- **Zanthar**: **Lightning Bolt** (rank 4, 35) through two thickets. Both failed but only took **20 each**: *"it doesn't work as well as you would think."*
- **Cyrathul**: **Exploit Vulnerability** (31) turned up **fire 15**, which his esoterica triggers on every hit. **Draconic Frenzy**: the nearest thicket lashed out first with a reaction (26, exactly his AC) and **grabbed** him. Jaws, claw and claw all hit. The physical damage was mostly soaked, but the fire weakness landed every time. *"Thaumaturge for the win."* *"This plant looks shredded."*
- **Bai Feng** sparked **Raise the Walls** on himself and Cyrathul, shifted to the Gleaming Blade, and strode past the first thicket. It had already used its reaction. He struck: **nat 20, 38, crit for 43** (his strike carries fire). **Killed**, and Cyrathul was free. Cyrathul had already done 51 of its 58 HP, most of it fire weakness that Kaazz added by hand. *"And you know what's funny? I do deal fire damage."*
- **Thickets.** As Bai Feng moved into reach, one grab missed (25) and the next **grabbed** him (36). Kaazz resolved both grab reactions after Bai Feng's turn. **Suffocate**: he made the Fort save (35; Foundry raised it a step) for no damage, and Kaazz let him Escape right away (35, a success). The other survivor grabbed him again (37) and Suffocated. His 20 was raised a step to a success, so he took half of 17, **8 bludgeoning**, dizzy and stupefied. A duplicated token briefly *"came back alive"* until Kaazz deleted it.
- **Panacea**: Divine Lance hit, but spirit damage did nothing to it. She cast **Life Boost** on Bai Feng (fast healing).

**Round 2**
- **Zanthar**: two-ray **Blazing Bolt**, hits on 30 and 24, for **25 and 33** plus the weakness. **Both remaining thickets killed.** *"You guys set the rooftop on fire."*

**Kills**: **Zanthar 2 · Bai Feng 1.** Bai Feng took 8 and Life Boost healed it.

**Hero points this session**: Cyrathul ×1 (Deception, 3 → 3+10 = 30) · Panacea ×2 (Make an Impression 6 → 10+10 = 30; Diplomacy 3 → 13 = 23) · Bai Feng ×1 (Reflex, 1 → 11 = 25).
