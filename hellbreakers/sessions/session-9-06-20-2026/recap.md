## Session Summary

The party finished what they started at Stagfall. The slithering shape on the rocks from last week's cliffhanger turned out to be a **basilisk** — and its petrifying gaze nearly ended Bai Jian's campaign in the opening minutes, freezing him toward permanent stone before he dug deep and burned a hero point to shrug it off. Cyrathul ended the monster with a freak **double natural 20**, biting into its neck mid-gaze and tearing its head off. But the fight wasn't over: out of the dark behind them came a squad of **Hellknight Armigers** with crossbows and halberds, and the party — fighting half-blind until Sammy's Light lit the cavern — cut them down, Bai Jian decapitating one with a 48-damage Sudden Charge crit and Cyrathul slinking up a wall to slit the throat of the last one as it fled. That whole running brawl scored **128 XP (Extreme)**.

From there it was a methodical, bloody crawl deeper into the caves, one chamber at a time. A glowworm-lit column room held a pair of **Orts** (hairy, eyed devil-blobs) and a Hellknight commander — Bai Jian crit-beheaded an Ort, Zanthar phase-bolted the Hellknight through her own shield, and the Orts went down to teeth and goo. The waterfall chamber was the real meat-grinder: two more Armigers dug in behind log barricades plus a chameleon-skinned **Insidiator** spitting immobilizing web. Cyrathul took a Morningstar to the neck and **dropped to dying** — the first party member to go down since Rama — and Bai Jian whistled Ren onto the dragon's body to anchor a vengeance strike (*"Avenge Sai"*) while Panacea poured healing in. Sammy crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts, Zanthar's Blazing Bolt combo cleared an Armiger, and Bai Jian swept the scared young Novice's legs out for the kill.

Then the last chamber: brimstone, candlelight, and **Viro Ahala** himself — the false Hell Knight — standing over an ivory bowl of flame, burning a blood-inked parchment. *"You're too late. I've already completed the ritual."* He alpha-struck Bai Jian with a round-one crit (saved only by Dueling Parry) while a **Ritual Backlash** hazard fed him healing and pulsed Will-save waves of hellfire at the frontline. But the party was rolling hot this time — Sammy hero-pointed **Biting Words** to terrify him, Bai Jian tripped him prone, Cyrathul and Panacea stacked debuffs, and Zanthar's cold cone **froze Viro to death** with that same grin frozen on his face. The flame in the bowl flared and snuffed out; the ritual — which would have torn open a greater gate for a powerful devil — died with him. They stuffed his body in the spacious pouch to identify later (they never did learn *why* he was a "fake" Hell Knight), looted his rubies and Hell Knight plate, and walked back to a hero's welcome at Thorngrip: goblin mead, Bracknap's undying thanks, the spent crystal reforged into a **Pendant of the Occult**, and the Hellbreaker reputation finally maxed at **20** — *"talk of Isgeri independence on everyone's lips."*

## Key Events

### Act I — The Basilisk & the Ambush (00:00–46:00)

- Quick recap of S8 (Panacea narrated: the three corrupted sites, the crystal, Bracknap's quest, into the caves). Everyone topped up to one hero point; Panacea got two.
- The S8 cliffhanger creature resolved: a **basilisk** coiled on the rocks. Its **petrifying gaze** hit Bai Jian repeatedly on Fortitude saves — first **Slowed**, then a failed save that would have made him **permanently petrified, like a Medusa victim**. Bai Jian **hero-pointed** the save and pushed the petrification off (*"Oh wait, can I hero point that?"*).
- **Cyrathul** moved behind it, Exploited Vulnerability (no luck reading it), and ended it with a **double nat 20** — *"that's double 20… nat 20 twice. Let's go."* — biting its neck mid-gaze and clawing its head clean off. Bai Jian's verdict: *"the superior reptile."*
- The DM kept initiative running — and **three Hellknight Armigers** opened up with crossbows **out of the darkness behind the party**. Bai Jian, who'd run forward into open ground, had to do the **"Walk of Shame"** back into the room.
- **Sammy** cast **Light** across the cave (and **Courageous Anthem** — whose over-the-top Foundry animation was lagging everyone's computers so badly the DM had to disable the module mid-session; *"a little too dramatic"*).
- **Zanthar** opened with **Blazing Bolt at all three** Armigers. **Panacea**'s Divine Lance + Trade Life for Death killed one outright — *"their eyes just pop right out of their head."* **Bai Jian** crit-decapitated a Novice with a **48-damage Sudden Charge** (massive-damage instakill). **Cyrathul** chased the last one down a tunnel, slinking up the wall to **slit its throat**.
- **128 XP (Extreme).** Loot from the bones in the basilisk room: an **angel statue**, a **jeweled helmet**, and a **healing-looking pendant** Bai Jian pocketed.

### Act II — The Glowworm Column (47:00–65:00)

- The party pressed through an encampment (bedrolls, a hooded lantern — the Armigers' camp) into a chamber lit by a **glowing column full of glowworms** (Panacea ID'd them with Arcana; Zanthar coaxed them brighter by tracing arcane symbols on the stone).
- A Hellknight commander and a pair of **Orts** (hairy, eyed devil-blobs — she yelled *"Intruders! Get them!"*) ambushed them.
- **Bai Jian** crit the first Ort — *"No mercy. 33 damage."* — splitting it into hair-goo, then **avenged** down the line, beheading the second. **Zanthar** **phase-bolted** the Hellknight: *"It goes right through her shield and hits her in the face."* (*"Did I steal the kill? Nice."*) **Cyrathul** Exploited and chomped; **Panacea** and **Sammy** chipped with Needle Darts and Guidance; **Ren** dodged an Ort and tore it down.
- **48 XP (Low).** Materialism watch: Bai Jian eyeing the loot — *"As we always say, we need money for this operation"* — with Pax narrating his own character's arc: *"Bai Jian has started to slowly become more and more materialistic."*

### Act III — The Waterfall (66:00–127:00) — *the hard fight*

- A 20-foot waterfall, a pool, **log barricades** forming a makeshift guard post. Two **Hellknight Armigers** dug in behind cover (they'd been expecting someone else) plus a **chameleon-skinned Insidiator** lurking invisible above.
- Sammy's connection dropped here (the table took its break to wait for her); she got back in and stayed active the rest of the night.
- **The Insidiator** spat **immobilizing web**, sticking **Zanthar** to the floor; the Armigers used **Pinning Strikes** (immobilize) and Morningstars. **Cyrathul** Exploited and chomped — then took a **Morningstar to the neck and dropped to dying** (*"Fizz, you're dead"*). First party member down since Rama.
- **Bai Jian** whistled **Ren** to stand on Cyrathul's body for the Support benefit and struck the Armiger — *"Avenge Sai."* Cyrathul **stabilized on his own recovery check**, and **Panacea**'s ranged **Heal** + **Life Boost** (fast healing) brought him back up the next round.
- **Zanthar** **Dizzying Colors'd** the Insidiator (*"Brazzle Dazzle"*), then Drain-Bonded-Item → restored **Blazing Bolt** → killed an Armiger. **Sammy** **crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts** (the DM narrated the kill: *"a thousand needle darts… it turns to ash"*) and whacked the young Novice with her **frying pan**.
- The last Novice (*"fights to the death,"* shield up) fell to **Bai Jian**: Ren bit the leg as a distraction, and Bai Jian **swept the leg and cut him down**.
- **72 XP (Moderate).** Loot: a **bag of gold** and a **chunk of silver** (for silvering weapons). The party calls it their **first real boss-grade fight**.

### Act IV — Viro Ahala & the Ritual (128:00–162:00) — *the boss*

- The ritual chamber: brimstone, candlelight, scattered ritual materials, an **ivory bowl with a flame**, and **Viro Ahala** in full Hell Knight plate burning a blood-inked parchment, an Armiger Novice at his side.
- *"You're too late. I've already completed the ritual."* He refused to explain it (*"You think I'm a fool?"*), then charged Bai Jian — round-one shield-bash to off-guard, then a **longsword crit** (saved only by **Dueling Parry**: *"Thank god I had the parry"*). Bai Jian, meanwhile, had rolled a **nat 1 initiative**.
- The **Ritual Backlash** hazard (the burning bowl) activated: a tendril fed **healing** to Viro, and a **Will-save pulse** of hellfire hit Cyrathul and Bai Jian — **both shrugged it off**.
- The party stacked him: **Cyrathul** Exploited (his Hell Knight plate resisted physical), chomped, and **Demoralized** (*"which I realize I should have done first"*). **Panacea** threw Divine Lance and Needle Darts (hero-pointed). **Bai Jian** struck for 16 and **tripped him prone** (hero-pointed). **Sammy** landed **Biting Words** (hero-pointed) and visibly terrified him.
- **Zanthar** finished it: a **cold cone** (Chilling Spray) that, on top of everyone's stacked damage, **froze Viro to death** — the DM: *"he just dies with that same grin on his face."* The flame in the bowl flared up and snuffed out. The ritual was unmade.
- The grieving Novice went berserk; Bai Jian deadpanned (*"What, he's your father or something?"*) and the party put him down — **Sammy sustaining Biting Words to insult him to death**: *"Just take him to filth for his wardrobe."*
- **Boss encounter: 88 XP (Severe).** A Recall Knowledge (Bai Jian & Cyrathul) revealed the ritual was a **greater summoning** — it would have opened a **greater gate** for a powerful devil. They stopped it in time.
- On returning to Thorngrip: **+40 XP for completing the whole mission**, and **+40 XP for hitting 20 reputation (max tier)**.

### Act V — Spoils & the Hero's Return (162:00–184:00)

- They never learned **why** Viro was a "fake" Hell Knight — they killed him before they could ask. They stuffed his **body and Hell Knight plate into the spacious pouch** to identify back at HQ (and to hunt for a possible "real" one).
- Looting the cave: Viro's **rubies** and extra ritual materials; from a long-dead climber's skeleton, his **climbing bolts** (ironically what would have saved his fall — *"Safety third"*) and fancy gear worth ~**35 gp**; and a **shining ritual symbol** granting a bonus to Religion (Bai Jian, an atheist: *"Looks like cash to me"*).
- **+2 reputation** for defeating Viro → the tracker hit **20, the maximum tier**: *"Talk of Isgeri independence is on everyone's lips. The Hellbreakers League are considered the best chance for the future of Isger."*
- Back to **Thorngrip** at sundown to a **hero's welcome** — goblins in rows, children cheering, Bracknap's blue glow. *"You are heroes here. You are welcome here forever. We are in your debt."* Bracknap took the spent crystal, whispered his grandmother's essence into it, and gave it back transformed into a **Pendant of the Occult** — *"This is for you now."* Then three kegs of goblin mead and a party. Bai Jian: *"I need that level four."*

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the Blade That Would Not Break

Pax's session: the tank who took the worst the caves could throw and kept standing. In the first two minutes the **basilisk's gaze nearly turned him permanently to stone** — a campaign-ending failed save — and he dug in and **hero-pointed his way out of it**, the single clutchest roll of the night. From there he was the closer at every fight: a **48-damage Sudden Charge crit** that decapitated a Novice Armiger in Act I, the *"No mercy, 33 damage"* crit-beheading of an Ort in Act II, and the leg-sweep kill that ended the waterfall meat-grinder. When **Cyrathul dropped to dying**, Bai Jian didn't panic — he **whistled Ren onto the dragon's fallen body** to anchor a Support strike and went for blood: *"Avenge Sai."* In the boss fight he ate Viro's round-one crit on his Dueling Parry, then **tripped the false Hell Knight prone** to set up the kill.

The session's comedy was all his: the **"Walk of Shame"** back into the room after running out to meet enemies that came from behind, and the **escalating materialism** he played up himself — *"We can sell this helmet for more money. As we always say, we need money for this operation"* — to the point Pax narrated his own character's arc out loud (*"Bai Jian has started to slowly become more and more materialistic"*). His atheist's-eye appraisal of a holy relic — *"Looks like cash to me"* — was peak. Underneath it, a quieter conscience: over the corpse of the Novice who'd charged them, *"poor this guy… not knowing what was waiting for him"*; he insisted on hauling **Viro's body back** to identify him, and admitted he *"actually kind of feel[s] a bit bad about the kid"* who watched his idol die.

### Cyrathul · the Superior Reptile

Snowgeen's Moon Dragon opened and nearly closed the session at both extremes. He **ended the basilisk** with a freak **double natural 20** — *"that's double 20… nat 20 twice. Let's go."* — narrating the kill with relish: a bite into the neck *as it was still trying to petrify Bai Jian*, then a claw to rip the head off, earning Bai Jian's salute (*"the superior reptile"*). His Exploit Vulnerability was the party's enemy-reader all night, and his signature move — **slinking into a dark tunnel, maneuvering up the wall, and slitting a fleeing Armiger's throat** — ended the whole opening brawl in style. The deadpan stayed dry throughout: a Demoralize delivered as *"a good boo,"* and his go-to attack announced as *"the good old classic chomp."*

He also took the session's hardest hit: a **Morningstar that crushed his neck and dropped him to dying** in the waterfall room, the first party member down since Rama died. He bounced back (a clutch recovery check stabilized him; Panacea's Heal and Life Boost revived him the next round) and was right back in the boss's face — Exploiting, chomping through Hell Knight plate, and self-critiquing his own tactics with a dragon's dry honesty: *"I'll Demoralize him — which I realize I should have done first."* By the end he was flying up moss-covered cave walls on his wings just because he could.

### Panacea · the Prepared Hand

Bog Witch's healer came in **loaded for bear** — and said so: *"I prepared for a big one this time. I thought we were going into something scary."* She had the heals to back it up, which mattered when Cyrathul went down: a clutch ranged **Heal** plus **Life Boost** fast-healing kept the dragon in the fight, and she had reserves to spare (*"I have two more heals if I need it"*). On offense she was the party's **divine-damage and debuff engine** — Divine Lance, Trade Life for Death (with her familiar's free Stupefy rider), and the **eyes-pop-out kill** on a basilisk-room Armiger.

Her tactical reads were sharp: she **called the Insidiator's silver weakness** (*"I think it was silver… I'm going to do silver Needle Darts"*) and quietly reminded the table when Sammy needed the same metal. And she carried the session's wry-economist throughline opposite Bai Jian's greed — *"It's an expensive world we're living in here"* — while gamely fighting Foundry and her own dice all night (*"I'm having bad rolls again"*). A first-time TTRPG player running the party's entire healing economy through a hard, five-fight dungeon, and holding it together.

### Zanthar Melodune III · the Closer

Scott's gnome wizard is funnier and more self-effacing than his kill count lets on — and the kill count is real. He's the party's artillery (**Blazing Bolt at all three** is his happy place), but what makes him Zanthar is the sheepish grin after it works: a **Phase Bolt through a Hellknight's own shield and into her face**, then — *"Did I steal the kill? Nice."* He runs control, too: a **Dizzying Colors** ("Brazzle Dazzle") that dazzled the Insidiator, even while he himself was glued to the floor in devil-web, gamely asking *"Can I try and break out of the goo?"* And he's honest about still learning his own kit (puzzling through Blazing Bolt's targeting rules mid-cast, working out his Drain Bonded Item economy out loud).

When it counted he closed the session: with Viro stacked in debuffs and bleeding from the whole party, Zanthar's **cold cone (Chilling Spray) was the tick that froze the false Hell Knight to death** — crystalline ice wrapping a dead man still grinning. From "rebel without a clue" to the one who put the boss in the ground.

### Sammy Shadowfoot · the Frying-Pan Bard

fallernon's halfling was **far more present this session** — connection drops and all, she played her own turns and stacked up real contributions. She was the party's **light source** (casting Light so everyone could fight the dark cavern), buff engine (**Courageous Anthem**, whose absurd animation she cheerfully owned was *"a little too dramatic"* once it crashed everyone's Foundry), and a genuine threat: she **crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts** (the DM narrating the kill — *"a thousand needle darts… it turns to ash"*) and, when in doubt, **brained a Hellknight Novice with her frying pan**.

Her best beats were at the boss. She hero-pointed **Biting Words** to terrify Viro Ahala at the exact right moment — then, with him dead and his Novice raging in grief, she **sustained Biting Words to insult the man to death**, the DM handing her the kill: *"tell me how you sing or insult this man to death."* Her answer, deadpan: *"Just take him to filth for his wardrobe."* And over the corpse of a climber who'd died because he forgot his climbing gear, she delivered the session's driest line — *"Safety third."* She's curious and still learning (asking how she'd know an enemy's weakness), but she's all the way in the fight now.
