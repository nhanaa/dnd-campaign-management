Session Summary

The party finished what they started at Stagfall. The slithering shape on the rocks from last week's cliffhanger turned out to be a basilisk — and its petrifying gaze nearly ended Bai Jian's campaign in the opening minutes, freezing him toward permanent stone before he dug deep and burned a hero point to shrug it off. Cyrathul ended the monster with a freak double natural 20, biting into its neck mid-gaze and tearing its head off. But the fight wasn't over: out of the dark behind them came a squad of Hellknight Armigers with crossbows and halberds, and the party — fighting half-blind until Sammy's Light lit the cavern — cut them down, Bai Jian decapitating one with a 48-damage Sudden Charge crit and Cyrathul slinking up a wall to slit the throat of the last one as it fled. That whole running brawl scored 128 XP (Extreme).

From there it was a methodical, bloody crawl deeper into the caves, one chamber at a time. A glowworm-lit column room held a pair of Orts (hairy, eyed devil-blobs) and a Hellknight commander — Bai Jian crit-beheaded an Ort, Zanthar phase-bolted the Hellknight through her own shield, and they went down to teeth and goo. The waterfall chamber was the real meat-grinder: two more Armigers dug in behind log barricades plus a chameleon-skinned Insidiator spitting immobilizing web. Cyrathul took a Morningstar to the neck and dropped to dying — the first party member down since Rama — and Bai Jian whistled Ren onto the dragon's body to anchor a vengeance strike ("Avenge Sai") while Panacea poured healing in. Sammy crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts, Zanthar's Blazing Bolt cleared an Armiger, and Bai Jian swept the scared young Novice's legs out for the kill.

Then the last chamber: brimstone, candlelight, and Viro Ahala himself — the false Hell Knight — standing over an ivory bowl of flame, burning a blood-inked parchment. "You're too late. I've already completed the ritual." He alpha-struck Bai Jian with a round-one crit (saved only by Dueling Parry) while a Ritual Backlash hazard fed him healing and pulsed Will-save waves of hellfire at the frontline. But the party was rolling hot this time — Sammy hero-pointed Biting Words to terrify him, Bai Jian tripped him prone, Cyrathul and Panacea stacked debuffs, and Zanthar's cold cone froze Viro to death with that same grin frozen on his face. The flame in the bowl flared and snuffed out; the ritual — which would have torn open a greater gate for a powerful devil — died with him. They stuffed his body in the spacious pouch to identify later (they never did learn why he was a "fake" Hell Knight), looted his rubies and Hell Knight plate, and walked back to a hero's welcome at Thorngrip: goblin mead, Bracknap's undying thanks, the spent crystal reforged into a Pendant of the Occult, and the Hellbreaker reputation finally maxed at 20 — "talk of Isgeri independence on everyone's lips."

Timeline

Act I · Combat · The Basilisk & the Ambush

Stone, Crossbows, and a Double 20

  • Quick recap of S8 (Panacea narrated the three corrupted sites, the crystal, Bracknap's quest, into the caves). Everyone topped to one hero point; Panacea got two.
  • The S8 cliffhanger creature resolved: a basilisk. Its petrifying gaze hit Bai Jian on repeated Fortitude saves — first Slowed, then a failed save that would have made him permanently petrified, like a Medusa victim. Bai Jian hero-pointed it and pushed the stone off ("Oh wait, can I hero point that?").
  • Cyrathul moved behind it, Exploited (no readable weakness), and ended it on a double natural 20 — "that's double 20… nat 20 twice. Let's go." — biting its neck mid-gaze and clawing the head clean off. Bai Jian's salute: "the superior reptile."
  • The DM kept initiative running — three Hellknight Armigers opened with crossbows from the dark behind the party. Bai Jian, who'd charged into open ground, had to do the "Walk of Shame" back into the room.
  • Sammy cast Light across the cave (and Courageous Anthem — whose over-the-top Foundry animation lagged everyone's computers so badly the DM disabled the module mid-session; "a little too dramatic").
  • Zanthar opened with Blazing Bolt at all three. Panacea's Divine Lance + Trade Life for Death killed one ("their eyes just pop right out of their head"). Bai Jian crit-decapitated a Novice with a 48-damage Sudden Charge (massive-damage instakill). Cyrathul chased the last one down a tunnel, slinking up the wall to slit its throat.
  • 128 XP (Extreme). Loot from the bones in the basilisk room: an angel statue, a jeweled helmet, and a healing-looking pendant Bai Jian pocketed.
Act II · Combat / Exploration · The Glowworm Column

Orts in the Glowworm Light

  • Through the Armigers' camp (bedrolls, a hooded lantern) into a chamber lit by a glowing column full of glowworms (Panacea ID'd them with Arcana; Zanthar coaxed them brighter by tracing arcane symbols on the stone).
  • A Hellknight commander and a pair of Orts (hairy, eyed devil-blobs) ambushed them — "Intruders! Get them!"
  • Bai Jian crit the first Ort — "No mercy. 33 damage." — splitting it into hair-goo, then beheaded the second. Zanthar phase-bolted the Hellknight: "It goes right through her shield and hits her in the face." ("Did I steal the kill? Nice.")
  • Cyrathul tumbled into flank, Exploited, chomped; Panacea and Sammy chipped with Needle Darts and Guidance; Ren dodged the Ort's swings and tore it down.
  • 48 XP (Low). Materialism watch: "As we always say, we need money for this operation" — Pax narrating his own character's arc: "Bai Jian has started to slowly become more and more materialistic."
Act III · Combat · The Waterfall — the hard fight

The Dragon Goes Down

  • A 20-foot waterfall, a pool, log barricades as a makeshift guard post. Two Hellknight Armigers dug in behind cover (expecting someone else) plus a chameleon-skinned Insidiator lurking invisible above.
  • Sammy's connection dropped here (the table broke to wait); she got back in and stayed active the rest of the night.
  • The Insidiator spat immobilizing web, gluing Zanthar to the floor; the Armigers used Pinning Strikes and Morningstars. Cyrathul Exploited and chomped — then took a Morningstar to the neck and dropped to dying. First party member down since Rama.
  • Bai Jian whistled Ren onto Cyrathul's body for the Support benefit and struck back — "Avenge Sai." Cyrathul stabilized on his own recovery check; Panacea's ranged Heal + Life Boost revived him the next round.
  • Zanthar Dizzying Colors'd the Insidiator ("Brazzle Dazzle"), then Drain-Bonded-Item → restored Blazing Bolt → killed an Armiger. Sammy crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts (the DM narrated the kill: "a thousand needle darts… it turns to ash") and whacked the Novice with her frying pan.
  • The last Novice ("fights to the death," shield up) fell to Bai Jian: Ren bit the leg as a distraction, and Bai Jian swept the leg and cut him down.
  • 72 XP (Moderate). Loot: a bag of gold and a chunk of silver. The party calls it their first real boss-grade fight.
Act IV · Combat · Viro Ahala & the Ritual

The False Hell Knight

  • The ritual chamber: brimstone, candlelight, scattered ritual materials, an ivory bowl with a flame, and Viro Ahala in full Hell Knight plate burning a blood-inked parchment, an Armiger Novice at his side.
  • "You're too late. I've already completed the ritual." He refused to explain it ("You think I'm a fool?"), then charged Bai Jian — shield-bash to off-guard, then a longsword crit saved only by Dueling Parry ("Thank god I had the parry"). Bai Jian had rolled a nat 1 initiative.
  • The Ritual Backlash hazard (the burning bowl) activated: a tendril healed Viro, and a Will-save pulse of hellfire hit Cyrathul and Bai Jian — both shrugged it off.
  • The party stacked him: Cyrathul Exploited (Hell Knight plate resisted physical), chomped, and Demoralized ("which I realize I should have done first"). Panacea threw Divine Lance and hero-pointed Needle Darts. Bai Jian struck for 16 and tripped him prone (hero-pointed). Sammy landed Biting Words (hero-pointed) — Viro terrified.
  • Zanthar finished it: a cold cone (Chilling Spray) that, on top of everyone's stacked damage, froze Viro to death — "he just dies with that same grin on his face." The flame flared up and snuffed out. The ritual was unmade.
  • The grieving Novice went berserk; Bai Jian deadpanned ("What, he's your father or something?") and the party put him down — Sammy sustaining Biting Words to insult him to death: "Just take him to filth for his wardrobe."
  • Boss: 88 XP (Severe). A Recall Knowledge (Bai Jian & Cyrathul) revealed the ritual was a greater summoning — it would have opened a greater gate for a powerful devil. They stopped it in time.
Act V · Faction / Loot · Spoils & the Hero's Return

Heroes of Thorngrip

  • They never learned why Viro was a "fake" Hell Knight — they killed him before they could ask. They stuffed his body and Hell Knight plate into the spacious pouch to identify back at HQ (and to hunt for a possible "real" one).
  • Looting: Viro's rubies and ritual materials; from a long-dead climber's skeleton, his climbing bolts (ironically what would've saved his fall — "Safety third") and gear worth ~35 gp; and a shining ritual symbol giving a bonus to Religion (Bai Jian, an atheist: "Looks like cash to me").
  • +2 reputation for defeating Viro → the tracker hit 20, the maximum tier: "Talk of Isgeri independence is on everyone's lips. The Hellbreakers League are considered the best chance for the future of Isger."
  • Back to Thorngrip at sundown to a hero's welcome — goblins in rows, children cheering, Bracknap's blue glow. "You are heroes here. You are welcome here forever. We are in your debt."
  • Bracknap took the spent crystal, whispered his grandmother's essence into it, and gave it back transformed into a Pendant of the Occult — "This is for you now." Then three kegs of goblin mead and a party. Bai Jian: "I need that level four."

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Blade That Would Not Break

  • In the first two minutes the basilisk's gaze nearly turned him permanently to stone — a campaign-ending failed save — and he dug in and hero-pointed his way out of it, the clutchest roll of the night.
  • The closer at every fight: a 48-damage Sudden Charge crit that decapitated a Novice Armiger, the "No mercy, 33 damage" crit-beheading of an Ort, and the leg-sweep kill that ended the waterfall meat-grinder.
  • When Cyrathul dropped to dying, he didn't panic — he whistled Ren onto the dragon's fallen body to anchor a Support strike and went for blood: "Avenge Sai." In the boss fight he ate Viro's round-one crit on his Dueling Parry, then tripped the false Hell Knight prone to set up the kill.
  • The session's comedy was his: the "Walk of Shame" back into the room after charging out to meet enemies who came from behind, and the escalating materialism he played up himself — "we need money for this operation" — to the point Pax narrated his own arc out loud ("Bai Jian has started to slowly become more and more materialistic"). His atheist's appraisal of a holy relic: "Looks like cash to me."
  • Underneath it, a quieter conscience: over the Novice who'd charged them, "poor this guy… not knowing what was waiting for him," and he insisted on hauling Viro's body back to identify him — admitting he "actually kind of feel[s] a bit bad about the kid" who watched his idol die.

Cyrathul · the Superior Reptile

  • Opened and nearly closed the session at both extremes. He ended the basilisk on a freak double natural 20 — "that's double 20… nat 20 twice. Let's go." — biting the neck as it was still trying to petrify Bai Jian, then clawing the head off (earning Bai Jian's salute, "the superior reptile").
  • His Exploit Vulnerability was the party's enemy-reader all night, and his signature kill — slinking into a dark tunnel, maneuvering up the wall, and slitting a fleeing Armiger's throat — ended the opening brawl in style.
  • The deadpan stayed dry: a Demoralize delivered as "a good boo," his go-to attack announced as "the good old classic chomp."
  • He took the session's hardest hit — a Morningstar that crushed his neck and dropped him to dying, the first party member down since Rama. He bounced back (a clutch recovery check stabilized him; Panacea's Heal + Life Boost revived him next round) and was right back in the boss's face.
  • Self-critiquing with a dragon's dry honesty — "I'll Demoralize him — which I realize I should have done first" — and by the end he was flying up moss-covered cave walls on his wings just because he could.

Panacea · the Prepared Hand

  • Came in loaded for bear — and said so: "I prepared for a big one this time. I thought we were going into something scary." Which is exactly what saved Cyrathul.
  • When the dragon went down, a clutch ranged Heal plus Life Boost fast-healing brought him back, with reserves to spare ("I have two more heals if I need it").
  • The party's divine-damage and debuff engine — Divine Lance, Trade Life for Death (familiar Stupefy rider), and the eyes-pop-out kill on a basilisk-room Armiger.
  • Sharp reads: she called the Insidiator's silver weakness ("I think it was silver") and quietly reminded the table to use the right metal.
  • Carried the session's wry-economist throughline opposite Bai Jian's greed ("It's an expensive world we're living in here") while gamely fighting Foundry and her own dice ("I'm having bad rolls again") — a first-time player running the whole healing economy through a five-fight dungeon.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Closer

  • Funnier and more self-effacing than his kill count lets on — and the kill count is real. The party's artillery (Blazing Bolt at all three is his happy place), but what makes him Zanthar is the sheepish grin after it works: a Phase Bolt through a Hellknight's own shield and into her face, then — "Did I steal the kill? Nice."
  • Runs control, too: a Dizzying Colors ("Brazzle Dazzle") that dazzled the Insidiator — even while glued to the floor in devil-web, gamely asking "Can I try and break out of the goo?"
  • Honest about still learning his kit (puzzling through Blazing Bolt's targeting mid-cast, working out his Drain Bonded Item economy out loud).
  • When it counted he closed the session: with Viro stacked in debuffs and bleeding from the whole party, Zanthar's cold cone (Chilling Spray) was the tick that froze the false Hell Knight to death — crystalline ice wrapping a dead man still grinning. From "rebel without a clue" to the one who put the boss in the ground.

Sammy Shadowfoot · the Frying-Pan Bard

  • Far more present this session — connection drops and all, she played her own turns and stacked real contributions.
  • The party's light source (casting Light so everyone could fight the dark cavern), buff engine (Courageous Anthem, whose absurd animation she cheerfully owned was "a little too dramatic" once it crashed everyone's Foundry), and a genuine threat: she crit-killed the Insidiator with Needle Darts and, when in doubt, brained a Hellknight Novice with her frying pan.
  • Her best beats were at the boss: she hero-pointed Biting Words to terrify Viro at the right moment — then, with him dead and his Novice raging in grief, she sustained Biting Words to insult the man to death. The DM handed her the kill ("tell me how you sing or insult this man to death"); her answer, deadpan: "Just take him to filth for his wardrobe."
  • Over the corpse of a climber who died because he forgot his climbing gear, she delivered the session's driest line — "Safety third."
  • Curious and still learning (asking how she'd know an enemy's weakness), but she's all the way in the fight now.

Pax's Notes — Bai Jian's Journal

Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.

What Bai Jian did this session

  • Survived the basilisk's petrifying gaze — failed the save, would have been permanently turned to stone, spent a hero point to push it off. Too close.
  • Decapitated a Novice Hellknight Armiger with a 48-damage Sudden Charge crit (massive damage). Crit an Ort for 33 ("No mercy") and slew Armigers across the dungeon.
  • When Cyrathul dropped to dying, whistled Ren onto his body for Support and struck back — kept the line.
  • Tanked Viro Ahala's round-one longsword crit on Dueling Parry; tripped Viro prone to set up the kill.
  • Took Viro's body and Hell Knight plate in the spacious pouch to identify at HQ.
  • Pocketed a healing-pendant, eyed a shining religion-symbol to sell, and pushed the party to sell everything not nailed down.

Things Bai Jian knows that the party does not

  • The crystal is now a Pendant of the Occult — Bracknap's grandmother's essence is in it. That's not just loot; it's a piece of Thorngrip's dead. Treat it accordingly.
  • Viro called himself a fake Hell Knight and we never found out why. If there's a real one wearing that mask — and Alaya is still out there — this isn't closed. His body might tell HQ something we can't read.
  • The ritual was a greater gate — a hair from loosing a greater devil on the Chitterwood goblins. We were early by minutes, not days.
  • I felt the Ritual Backlash wave wash off me on a Will save. A ritualist's hellfire — next time the bowl might not miss. Disable the hazard, don't just race the boss.

What Bai Jian is thinking about

  • Petrification is a hard counter to me. "Decent saves" almost cost the whole campaign in two minutes. I need a way to not be the only one staring down the gaze — or a backup save boost.
