Session Summary

The party turned for home after the Lucent mission and spent the first hour cashing out. Back in Vordia they reported the orphanage cleansed, pocketed 20 gold from a grateful (and unsettlingly cheerful) Asmodean townsperson, and sold off the haul — the Robe of the Erinyes, the unholy text, the ebony statue of Asmodeus, a fistful of donated trinkets. Reputation ticked to 17 — "highly regarded everywhere" — and the Chelaxian flags over Vordia were quietly coming down, twenty become ten. Then it was on toward Umok, the last hearts-and-minds stop, with one farmstead feeding them sweetbreads and another chasing Panacea off with pitchforks ("damn catfolk, always stealing").

Umok turned out to be a fire-free gnome village — magic lights, no open flame anywhere, boarhounds the locals literally talk to — still gun-shy forty years after the Goblinblood Wars burned it flat. The gnomes were blaming goblins for a string of woodland fires when a badly-burned goblin, Grizzo, staggered into the square and collapsed. Panacea kept her breathing; Bai Jian read her trail back into the trees. Her story: "Fire dogs. Huge fire dogs chased me." The party talked the captain of the guard out of throwing her to the mob and went to see for themselves — a raging forest fire fought to a standstill, and then, in a hundred-foot circle of charred stumps, the fire dogs themselves: two Hell Hounds.

It went badly fast. Rama charged in alone and, when the second hound's breath caught both him and Cyrathul, crit-failed his save and went up like he'd been doused in gasoline. Bai Jian had already cut the first hound clean in half on a Sudden Charge crit; Cyrathul — left at death's door by that same breath — Exploited the second's cold weakness and tore its head off, but with Panacea's heals spent keeping him upright, no one reached Rama in time. He died of his burns, and the party left a body in the woods. They carried a hound's head back to Umok as proof the goblins were innocent, collected Bracers of Missile Deflection, and got a tip: a figure in all-black armor had been stalking west, deeper into the Chitterwood.

They followed the boot prints in. At a petrified stump ringed with ritual candles and crystal shards, four devils — Orts — boiled out of a hollow. Cyrathul read them cold (weak to holy, resistant to everything physical except silver), Bai Jian opened with holy water and called the silver weakness to Panacea, who turned her Needle Darts silver and detonated the things one by one. The stump was an amateur's practice ritual — not Viro Ahala's real working, but his trail. The fight pushed the party to level 3. Then twenty pairs of red eyes opened in the trees: Zagnub of the Thorngrip Goblins, all teeth and good cheer, inviting his new "Longshanks" friends home.

Thorngrip is a hidden goblin town behind a wall of living thorns, built up the trunk of an enormous sequoia, ruled by an ancient ancestor-speaker named Bracknap with a crowd of spectral goblins at his back. His ancestors confirmed the party hunts the man in black — and Bracknap will point the way, after a favor: a band of human bigots calling themselves the Iskari Slayers has come to finish the Goblinblood Wars. The party found their camp, intimidated the first three drunks into bolting, and then had to fight when two more and a war-wolf came back. Bai Jian tore one in half, Cyrathul snacked the wolf, and the last man surrendered on his knees — Bai Jian very nearly executing him over the word "slaving" before a missed swing made the mercy decision for him. They manacled him, handed him to a delighted Zagnub, and the goblins led them back toward Thorngrip. Next week: the man in black, and two new faces at the table.

Act II · Roleplay / Exploration · The Road to Umok

The Fire-Free Zone

  • Hearts-and-minds at farmsteads en route. One family, swayed by the party's reputation, put them up and packed sweetbreads for the road. A second crit-failed introduction — "damn catfolk, always stealing from us" — ended in pitchforks.
  • Umok: a quaint gnome village ringed with "now entering the Umok fire-free zone, please extinguish all flames" signs. Forty years after the Goblinblood Wars burned the region down, the gnomes cook, light, and live entirely by magic — no open flame within 500 yards.
  • A gnome conversed with a passing boarhound, then crouched and "talked" to Ren the same way — the hound's eyes lit up, tail wagging, like two old friends catching up.
  • Bai Jian's Diplomacy to make a good impression landed at 19 after a false start ("the problem is I got zero… wait, wasn't I trained in Diplomacy?"). The gnomes blamed recent woodland fires on goblins and pointed toward Ion Marbleberg, captain of the guard.
  • Grizzo, a badly-burned goblin, stumbled into the square and collapsed ("Firebringers! I knew it!"). Panacea Treated her Wounds; Bai Jian read her trail back into the woods.
  • Calming Grizzo (Cyrathul Diplomacy, Bai Jian aiding): "Fire dogs. Huge fire dogs chased me." The party convinced Ion to shelter Grizzo under guard rather than expel her, and rode out to investigate the fires themselves.
Act III · Combat · The Forest Fire & The Hell Hounds

Where the Fire Dogs Live

  • The trail led half a mile north to a raging forest fire — run as a victory-point skill challenge. The party scrambled a patchwork of checks: Cyrathul's lore, Panacea buffing with Guidance and shooting down dry branches with Needle Darts, Bai Jian and Ren digging a firebreak. Comic beat: Ren's paws were too small to move enough dirt, so Bai Jian dug while the hound "supervised." Contained — 60 XP and a reputation point.
  • Charred dog footprints led to a hundred-foot circle of burned stumps. A failed group Perception meant the party walked in distracted — "you don't see them before they see you." Two Hell Hounds.
  • Bai Jian Sudden-Charged into a flank and crit-killed the first Hell Hound — sliced it clean in half, the body exploding into smoke.
  • The second hound's breath caught Cyrathul and Rama. Rama crit-failed, took double, "burned up like someone poured gasoline over him," and dropped to dying. Cyrathul took the full hit too — "death's door, still standing."
  • With heals spent keeping the badly-burnt Cyrathul up, Rama was never revived. Cyrathul Exploited the second hound's cold weakness, bit its neck and tore its head off with a claw.
  • Rama died of his burns — the party left his body in the forest. His player had departed; the DM-run guest body exits the campaign here.
  • Back in Umok, a hound's head as proof. Captain Ion handed over Bracers of Missile Deflection and 10 gp, cleared Grizzo to stay, and pointed them to the Rubber Ducky — a tavern with no outside door, where a firewatch gnome described a figure in all-black armor stalking out the western gate, far past the patrol line. Long rest.
Act IV · Combat / Mystery · Into the Chitterwood

The Practice Ritual

  • Resupply: Panacea swapped in Rousing Splash; Bai Jian bought two holy water flasks from the temple and kept one in hand, ready to throw.
  • Into the Chitterwood — old-growth pine and maple, no roads, only game trails. Bai Jian's Survival picked up the black-armored figure's heavy boot prints and tracked them deep.
  • They found a petrified stump ringed with ritual candles and crystal shards, boot prints churned all around, a crack leading to an underground hollow. Four devils — Orts — poured out.
  • Cyrathul crit his Exploit Vulnerability and read the full block; Bai Jian flagged the silver weakness; Panacea's silver Needle Darts started blowing the Orts apart while Bai Jian, Ren, and Cyrathul cleaned up. Party reached level 3.
  • The stump read as an amateur practice ritual — "the first kid's try" at summoning devils — that birthed the Orts. Not Viro's true working, but his trail.
  • The hollow held a gleaming shield (~250 gp, level-6 item) and a stash of valuable ritual supplies. Bai Jian pocketed the shield for the party stash and briefly mused about going sword-and-board.
Act V · Roleplay / Mystery · Thorngrip & Bracknap

The Ancestors' Welcome

  • Panacea spotted ~20 red eyes in the trees. Zagnub of the Thorngrip Goblins stepped out — tall, grinning, deliberately unarmed: "Relax, Longshanks. If we wanted to kill you, we would have done so." Devils are no friends of his either. He invited the party home.
  • Half a day through trackless brush, swinging vine-to-vine after the goblins, to Thorngrip: a hidden town behind an impenetrable wall of living thorns, built up the trunk of a colossal sequoia, rooftops kept below the wall line so it can't be spotted from outside.
  • Bracknap, an ancient goblin ancestor-speaker, met them at a shrine — a flash of blue light in his eyes and the barest glimpse of a crowd of spectral goblins standing behind him. Wary of humans since he was a child in the Goblin War.
  • The ancestors already knew the party hunts the man in black. Cyrathul made the impression (hero-pointed; Bai Jian aided). Reward: an oil of potency.
  • Bracknap's favor: drive off the Iskari Slayers, a human band that has come to "finish what was started during the Goblinblood Wars" — a threat to every goblin in the Chitterwood, Thorngrip included.
Act VI · Combat / Roleplay · The Iskari Slayers

War Never Ends

  • Zagnub guided them toward the Slayer camp — five tents, whiskey bottles jammed neck-first in the dirt, the men drunk.
  • The first three Slayers were openly genocidal: "goblins are filth… we are going to clear the Chitterwood out of them"; on whether goblins could be useful, "maybe as slaves." Cyrathul's Intimidation (hero-pointed, 23) carried the whole rout solo — Bai Jian's aid whiffed — and over a few rounds all three fled. 50 XP. The camp gave up 2 lesser healing potions and a pouch of stolen trinkets.
  • Two more Slayers returned with crossbows and a war-wolf. Cyrathul's Deception didn't hold, and combat broke.
  • Bai Jian used Fleet to close 30 ft, struck, then struck again at MAP — hero-pointed into a crit and tore the man in half. "You should have left when you had the chance."
  • Cyrathul Exploited the wolf (no weaknesses — "he's a wolf") and crit-bit it for 19; Panacea Battle-Medicined Cyrathul and hexed the wolf, routing the healing back to him.
  • Bai Jian + Ren dropped the last Slayer to his knees: "Mercy." Bai Jian swung again over the "slaving" talk and missed — which preserved the party's clean reputation. The survivor was manacled and handed to Zagnub ("we won't hurt him too much").
