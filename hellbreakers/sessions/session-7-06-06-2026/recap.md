## Session Summary

The party turned for home after the Lucent mission and spent the first hour cashing out. Back in Vordia they reported the orphanage cleansed, pocketed 20 gold from a grateful (and unsettlingly cheerful) Asmodean townsperson, and sold off the haul — the Robe of the Erinyes, the unholy text, the ebony statue of Asmodeus, a fistful of donated trinkets. Reputation ticked to **17** — *"highly regarded everywhere"* — and the Chelaxian flags over Vordia were quietly coming down, twenty become ten. Then it was on toward **Umok**, the last hearts-and-minds stop, with a friendly farmstead feeding them sweetbreads and a less friendly one chasing Panacea off with pitchforks (*"damn catfolk, always stealing"*).

Umok turned out to be a fire-free gnome village — magic lights, no open flame anywhere, boarhounds the locals literally talk to — still gun-shy forty years after the Goblinblood Wars burned it flat. The gnomes were blaming goblins for a string of woodland fires when a badly-burned goblin, **Grizzo**, staggered into the square and collapsed. Panacea kept her breathing; Bai Jian read her trail back into the trees. Her story: *"Fire dogs. Huge fire dogs chased me."* The party talked the captain of the guard out of throwing her to the mob and went to see for themselves. They found a raging forest fire first — fought to a standstill as a victory-point skill challenge, Ren gamely trying to dig a firebreak with paws too small for the job — and then, in a hundred-foot circle of charred stumps, the fire dogs themselves: **two Hell Hounds**.

It went badly fast. Rama charged in alone, ate a faceful of fire breath, and when the second hound's breath caught both him and Cyrathul, Rama crit-failed the save, burned like he'd been doused in gasoline, and dropped to dying. Bai Jian had already cut the first hound clean in half on a Sudden Charge crit; Cyrathul — himself left at death's door by that same breath — Exploited the second's cold weakness and tore its head off, but with Panacea's heals spent keeping *him* on his feet, **no one reached Rama in time.** He never got back up. The party carried his head-count of the dead lighter by one and left a burnt body in the woods. They brought a hound's head back to Umok as proof the goblins were innocent, collected **Bracers of Missile Deflection** and a tip from a firewatch gnome: a figure in all-black armor had been stalking west, deeper into the Chitterwood.

They followed the boot prints in. At a petrified stump ringed with ritual candles and crystal shards, four devils — **Orts** — boiled out of an underground hollow. Cyrathul read them cold (weak to holy, resistant to everything physical *except silver*), Bai Jian opened with holy water and called the silver weakness to Panacea, who turned her Needle Darts silver and started detonating the things one by one while Bai Jian and Ren cleaned up and Cyrathul Exploit-crit his way through the middle. The stump was an amateur's practice ritual — *"first kid's try"* — not Viro Ahala's real working, but it was his trail. The fight pushed the party to **level 3**, and the hollow gave up a 250-gp shield and a cache of stashed ritual supplies. Then twenty pairs of red eyes opened in the trees: **Zagnub** of the Thorngrip Goblins, all teeth and good cheer, inviting his new *"Longshanks"* friends home.

Thorngrip is a hidden goblin town behind a wall of living thorns, built up the trunk of an enormous sequoia, ruled by an ancient ancestor-speaker named **Bracknap** with a crowd of spectral goblins at his back. His ancestors confirmed the party hunts the man in black — and Bracknap will point the way, *after* a favor: a band of human bigots calling themselves the **Iskari Slayers** has come to finish the Goblinblood Wars, and he wants them gone. The party found their camp, intimidated the first three drunks into bolting (Cyrathul leaning hard on being a literal dragon), looted the tents, and then had to fight when two more Slayers and a war-wolf came back and wouldn't take the hint. Bai Jian tore one in half, Cyrathul snacked the wolf, and the last man surrendered on his knees — Bai Jian very nearly executing him over the word *"slaving"* before a missed swing made the mercy decision for him. They manacled him and handed him to a delighted Zagnub (*"we won't hurt him too much"*), and the goblins led them back toward Thorngrip. Next week: the man in black, and two new faces at the table.

## Key Events

### Act I — Cashing Out in Vordia (00:00–15:00)

- Recap of S6. The DM confirmed the table's read: **Viro Ahala is an impersonator** — not a true Hell Knight, *"not a real full-blood"* of the Order. He stole the armor and has been fooling people.
- Hero-point reset: everyone starts at 1 (Rama allotted 2 as the newer character) — the post-break bonus point becomes relevant later.
- Three uneventful hours back to **Vordia**. The party reported the Bright Embers haunt cleansed; the cheerfully Asmodean local (*"Hail Asmodeus, wonderful"*) handed over **20 gp**.
- **Loot sale** at the general store. Debated the optics of selling the sisters' **Robe of the Erinyes** (55 gp) before agreeing it came from an abandoned orphanage and belonged to no one. Sold the unholy text, the ebony Asmodeus statue, and the donated trinkets. Kept a spare short sword and the darts.
- Striking runes discussed and shelved — level-4 items, out of reach until they level.
- **Reputation → 17.** *"Highly regarded everywhere."* Vordia's twenty Chelaxian flags were down to ten; townsfolk whispering, *"Have you heard about the Hellbreakers League? I heard they're gonna free us."*

### Act II — The Road to Umok (15:00–42:00)

- Hearts-and-minds checks at farmsteads en route. One family, swayed by the party's reputation, put them up and packed **sweetbreads** for the road. A second crit-failed introduction — *"damn catfolk, always stealing from us"* — and chased the party off with pitchforks.
- **Umok**: a quaint gnome village ringed with *"now entering the Umok fire-free zone, please extinguish all flames"* signs. Forty years after the Goblinblood Wars burned the region down, the gnomes cook, light, and live entirely by magic — no open flame within 500 yards.
- A gnome conversed with a passing boarhound, then crouched and "talked" to **Ren** the same way — the hound's eyes lit up, tail wagging, like two old friends catching up.
- Bai Jian's Diplomacy to make a good impression landed at **19** after a false start (*"the problem is I got zero… wait, wasn't I trained in Diplomacy?"*). The gnomes blamed recent woodland fires on goblins and pointed toward **Ion Marbleberg**, captain of the guard.
- **Grizzo**, a badly-burned goblin, stumbled into the square and collapsed. Cries of *"Firebringers! I knew it!"* Panacea Treated her Wounds; Bai Jian rolled Survival to read her trail back into the woods.
- Calming Grizzo (Cyrathul Diplomacy, Bai Jian aiding): *"Fire dogs. Huge fire dogs chased me."* The party convinced Ion to let Grizzo stay under guard rather than expel her, and rode out to investigate the fires themselves.

### Act III — The Forest Fire & The Hell Hounds (42:00–95:00)

- The trail led half a mile north to a **raging forest fire** — run as a **victory-point skill challenge** (start 5 VP, lose 1 at the end of each phase). The party scrambled a patchwork of checks: Cyrathul's Esoteric Lore (after a rocky start), Panacea buffing with Guidance and shooting down dry branches with Needle Darts, Bai Jian and Ren digging a firebreak. Comic beat: Ren's paws were too small to move enough dirt, so Bai Jian dug while the hound "supervised." They contained the blaze — **60 XP and a reputation point.**
- Charred dog footprints led north to a hundred-foot circle of burned stumps. A failed group Perception meant the party walked in distracted — *"you don't see them before they see you."* **Two Hell Hounds.**
- **Combat — Hell Hounds** (init: Rama, Hell Hound, Panacea, Cyrathul, Bai Jian, Hell Hound):
  - **Rama** (DM-run, stance improvised on an unfamiliar sheet) charged in alone, took **11** fire-breath damage and a bite.
  - **Bai Jian** Sudden-Charged into a flank and **crit-killed the first Hell Hound — sliced it clean in half**, the body exploding into smoke without shrapnel.
  - The second hound's breath weapon caught both **Cyrathul** and Rama. **Rama crit-failed**, took double damage, *"burned up into flames like someone poured gasoline over him,"* and collapsed to dying. Cyrathul took the full hit too and ended *"at death's door — still standing."*
  - With heals committed to keeping the badly-burnt Cyrathul up, **Rama was never revived.**
  - **Cyrathul** Exploited the hound's **cold weakness (5)**, bit into its neck and **tore its head off** with a claw (12 with the weakness), killing the second hound.
  - **Rama died of his burns** — the party left his body in the forest. (His player had departed; the DM had run him as a guest body, now exited in-fiction.)
- Recall Knowledge confirmed Hell Hounds (immune to fire and visual effects). Panacea topped Cyrathul off with her **Life Boost** focus spell.
- Back in Umok, the party presented a hound's head. Captain Ion gave over **Bracers of Missile Deflection** (family heirlooms) and 10 gp, cleared Grizzo to stay, and pointed them to the **Rubber Ducky** tavern.
- The Rubber Ducky has no outside door — you step on a glowing sigil and teleport in. A firewatch gnome described **a figure in all-black armor** stalking the woods, pushing out the **western gate** far past the patrol line. Long rest in the visitors' house.

### Act IV — Into the Chitterwood & The Orts (102:00–135:00)

- Resupply: Panacea swapped in **Rousing Splash**; Bai Jian bought **two holy water** flasks from the temple and kept one in hand, ready to throw.
- Into the **Chitterwood** — old-growth pine and maple, no roads, only game trails. Bai Jian's Survival picked up the black-armored figure's heavy boot prints and tracked them deep.
- They found a **petrified stump** ringed with ritual candles and discarded crystal shards, boot prints churned all around it, a crack leading to a small underground hollow. **Four devils — Orts — poured out.**
- **Combat — The Orts** (init: Bai Jian, Cyrathul, Panacea, Ort, Elite Ort, Ort, Ort):
  - **Bai Jian** stepped up and **threw holy water at the Elite Ort, spending a hero point to land it** — it screamed as the water seared it. Followed with Dueling Parry.
  - **Cyrathul** crit his Exploit Vulnerability and read the whole stat line: **weak to holy 3, resistant to all physical except silver, resistant to poison, immune to fire and mental.** Bit (hero-pointing the reroll to connect) — *"like chewing on sandpaper."*
  - **Bai Jian** flagged the silver weakness to Panacea: *"you can make your Needle Darts silver to trigger its weakness."*
  - **Panacea** switched to **silver Needle Darts** and blew the Elite Ort apart, then chipped a second with her cantrip and healed Bai Jian; her Trade-Life-for-Death hex fizzled on a successful save (her first run-in with *"save-or-suck"*).
  - The Orts are slow (20 ft) and clumsy — they whiffed repeatedly, including a crit-miss on Bai Jian.
  - **Cyrathul** Exploit-crit a second Ort, bit (**13**) and clawed it apart.
  - **Bai Jian + Ren** combined — Ren's Support benefit (+1 attack, +1d6) feeding Bai Jian's Strikes — to finish the last two, the final one ended with a leaping spin and the jian driven through its skull.
  - **Party reached level 3.**
- The stump read as an **amateur practice ritual** — *"the first kid's try"* at summoning devils — that birthed the Orts. **Not** Viro's true ritual, but his trail. The hollow held a **gleaming shield (~250 gp, level-6 item)** and a stash of valuable **ritual supplies**. Bai Jian pocketed the shield for the party stash and briefly mused about going sword-and-board.

### Act V — Thorngrip & Bracknap (135:00–162:00)

- Panacea spotted **~20 red eyes** in the trees. **Zagnub** of the Thorngrip Goblins stepped out — tall, grinning, deliberately unarmed: *"Relax, Longshanks. If we wanted to kill you, we would have done so."* Devils are no friends of his either. He invited the party home.
- Half a day through trackless brush, swinging vine-to-vine after the goblins, to **Thorngrip**: a hidden town behind an impenetrable **wall of living thorns**, built up the trunk of a colossal **sequoia**, rooftops kept below the wall line so it can't be spotted from outside.
- **Bracknap**, an ancient goblin ancestor-speaker, met them at a shrine — a flash of blue light in his eyes and the barest glimpse of **a crowd of spectral goblins standing behind him**. Wary of humans since he was a child in the Goblin War.
- The ancestors already knew the party hunts the man in black. Bracknap's terms: prove themselves first. Cyrathul made the impression (hero-pointed, plus Bai Jian's aid). Reward: an **oil of potency**.
- Bracknap's favor: drive off the **Iskari Slayers**, a human band that has come to *"finish what was started during the Goblinblood Wars"* — a threat to every goblin in the Chitterwood, Thorngrip included.

### Act VI — The Iskari Slayers (162:00–191:00)

- Zagnub guided them toward the Slayer camp — five tents, whiskey bottles jammed neck-first in the dirt, the men drunk.
- The first three Slayers were openly genocidal: *"goblins are filth… we are going to clear the Chitterwood out of them"*; on whether goblins could be useful, *"maybe as slaves."* The party leaned on them hard — **Cyrathul Intimidation (hero-pointed, 23)** carried it solo (Bai Jian's aid — hand on his sword, a flat stare — whiffed, *"three fours in a row"*) — and over a few rounds all three fled. **50 XP.** The abandoned camp gave up **2 lesser healing potions** and a pouch of **stolen trinkets**.
- Two more Slayers returned with crossbows and a **war-wolf**. Cyrathul's Deception didn't hold. **Combat:**
  - **Cyrathul** opened on the lead Slayer (Esoteric Lore: utterly unremarkable), strode in and bit.
  - The Slayer drew a short sword, missed, then cut Cyrathul across the chest.
  - **Bai Jian** used **Fleet** to close 30 ft, struck, then struck again at MAP — **hero-pointed into a crit and tore the man in half.** *"You should have left when you had the chance."*
  - The **wolf** bit Cyrathul; **Gar**, the second Slayer, dropped his bow for a short sword and missed into Bai Jian's **Dueling Parry**.
  - **Panacea** Battle-Medicined Cyrathul and dropped a Trade-Life-for-Death hex on the wolf, routing the healing back to Cyrathul.
  - **Cyrathul** Exploited the wolf (no weaknesses — *"he's a wolf"*), crit-bit it for **19**, and moved to flank.
  - **Bai Jian + Ren** finished it — blood gushing, the last Slayer dropping to his knees, weapons down: *"Mercy."* Bai Jian, furious at the slaver talk, swung anyway — and **missed**, which preserved the party's clean reputation.
- The party manacled the survivor and handed him to **Zagnub**, who promised *"we won't hurt him too much."* No reputation lost for sparing a man who surrendered. Zagnub led them back toward Thorngrip. **Pick up next week.**
