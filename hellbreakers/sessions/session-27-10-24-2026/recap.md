## Session Summary

The night after Illcayna fell, Alceo performed the ritual of resurrection twice in the cave by the pool. **Bai Feng** woke at 1 hit point, severely drained, unsure where he was. Alceo's Heal and Panacea's Treat Wounds got him on his feet, and he crawled to his brother's body and put Lay on Hands into it. It did nothing. **Panacea** and **Zanthar** assisted the ritual. Cyrathul went first, *"because he's big."* He drifted down the River Styx into Pharasma's hands (*"very peculiar… for your kind to be so selfless"*), heard Panacea calling him back, pondered for a moment, and followed her voice. Zanthar touched the Grotto's summer mural with Bounty's Light, the party took a full long rest in one minute, and Alceo started again. Pharasma barely glanced at Bai Jian. She said one word, *"Redeemed,"* and he was sitting on a cliff's edge in Elysium in springtime, cherry blossoms below and his horse snorting behind him. **Bai Feng** called to him: *"I'm back. I'm alive. I'm here. Please come back to me."* Bai Jian heard it as a whisper through the ether. He didn't know it was his brother, and he believed the brother he had been chasing was already dead. He chose to stay: *"I feel peaceful here. I think I'll let my friends carry on their own mission and maybe join my brother on the other side."* Alceo: *"If the spirit is unwilling, we cannot force them to come back. Otherwise, we are no better than Illcayna."*

The party took Bai Jian back for a hero's burial. Phoebe laid a rose on his body (*"He really was a friend of the rose"*) and sealed the war shard in a lead box, and the ritual site was collapsed behind them. Bai Feng took his brother's sword and armour: *"I will carry them all."* That night on the road, **Ren** barked twice and ran into the woods. In Elysium, when Bai Jian turned to mount his horse, the dog was waiting for him. Panacea identified the **Bleeding Heart of Urgathoa** by the campfire, and Bai Feng attuned it. Zanthar identified the scythe in Logos: the **Reaper's Sigh**, a +1 wounding striking relic, kept. Then four days north to Saringallow. At headquarters they were told they'd be remembered as the **Wight Mother's Bane**, and Maire sent them up the river to check on **Moob and Fang** at the Waterspout Inn. The goblins had rebuilt the **Witchfire** with armour plating, a ram and a push-button mast. The goblins didn't believe Bai Jian had a brother, so Bai Feng played along: *"Yeah, I totally did all that."* There were butter rums and an old grudge between Moob and Panacea, and the party sailed home for the festival on the 5th of Pharast.

There was no festival. The Hellknights had taken Saringallow. Rabbit and Cole met them at the dock (*"They've taken everyone"*), and the party ran across the city with mounted Hellknights, hellhounds, Erinyes and battle mages behind them. They saved Maire and Alceo. **Rhokrag** was carried off into the sky by Erinyes even after Cyrathul's Earthbind pulled the first one down, and **Phoebe**, Rabbit and Cole stayed behind to hold the mages. Bai Feng lifted the gate on his shoulders and **Quoster**'s cart got them up the trail to **High Fort**. Ethran Howell arrived days later dressed as an unwashed druid. Saringallow is under curfew, Rhokrag and the others are alive in Caeto's hands, and **Caeto** sails in two days to fetch a Chelaxian interrogator. Ethran's plan is to take the infamous **Sereni Manor**, now held by a diabolist, as a command post. They floated two days down the clover stream to a sympathetic rancher, who greeted them: *"I hear you're here to kill my brother."* He is **Theodore Vulaunex**.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Morning After (night of 19 Calistril)
- **Panacea's recap** opened the session and earned the extra hero point.
- **Alceo** healed Zanthar, then turned to the man beside Bai Jian's body and took him for Bai Jian (*"Bai Jian, you don't look very good"*) before he stopped, confused: *"You… Oh. Who are you?"* **Bai Feng** was conscious on 1 HP and severely drained (Drained 6): *"Where am I? What happened?"* Zanthar: *"Just do risky surgery."* **Panacea's Treat Wounds (23)** and **Alceo's Heal (49)** stood him up. *"I heard something about my brother."*
- **Panacea**: *"He fell trying to save you. He's over here. Follow me."* Bai Feng stood, fell, and kept going. When he reached the body he cast **Lay on Hands** on his brother, and nothing happened. Pax narrated the rest: *"he's in denial right now… trying to cope with the reality as well as has a bunch of questions."* Panacea put her little paw on him.
- **Panacea** volunteered for the ritual's Medicine: *"I'm not good enough to have saved them this time, but I've saved many others."* **Zanthar** took Society (*"I don't know anything about medicine, though"*). Bai Feng heard *resurrection* and was on his feet: *"You can save my brother. You can bring him back."* Asked what he could offer: *"We were just hunters."*

### Act II — Two Resurrections
- **Cyrathul first.** Eight hours of chanting to Pharasma. In death he drifted down the **River Styx** to a figure *"looking in two directions at once,"* and psychopomps lifted him into her hands: *"You may not yet be done… very peculiar for your time, for your kind to be so selfless."*
  - **Panacea** called him back: *"Sai, if you can hear us, we need you on this side. We need you over here. You're not done yet."* **Cyrathul**: *"I'll ponder for a moment, and then I'll head towards the voice."* (Alceo's Religion 34, Panacea's Medicine 20, Zanthar's Society 31.)
  - He tried to get up and collapsed — *"How long have I been on the ground?"* — *"Almost too long."* — *"Is everyone okay?"*
- **The summer mural.** Alceo needed rest before a second ritual. Panacea and Cyrathul remembered the Grotto of Seasons' summer mural (*"a magic place that we can do long rests in short amount of time"*), Zanthar touched it with **Bounty's Light**, and the whole party took a full long rest in a minute.
- **Bai Jian.** Alceo chanted through the night again (Religion 26). Pharasma's word to Bai Jian was *"Redeemed,"* and he woke on a cliff in Elysium, his horse behind him.
  - Kaazz asked who would call him, *"even if it's his brother."* **Bai Feng**: *"I'm back. I'm alive. I'm here. Please come back to me."*
  - Pax, as Bai Jian, worked it through out loud: the voice was faint, he never saw Illcayna die, and if anyone was calling him back the party must have won. He didn't know the voice was his brother's, and he thought the ritual had taken too long and his brother was already dead. *"I think my mission is completed. I feel peaceful here. I think I'll let my friends carry on their own mission and maybe join my brother on the other side."*
  - The doorway closed. **Alceo**: *"If the spirit is unwilling, we cannot force them to come back. Otherwise, we are no better than Illcayna."* **Bai Feng**: *"But I called out to him. Didn't he hear it?"* — *"He did."*

### Act III — Mourning and Loot
- **Bai Feng**: *"At least he died like a true hero, like he always dreamt of when we were young."* And: *"He went and looked for me, and then… Well, I guess it fits his personality."* Then: *"Well, if it is his will, then I'll accept it."*
- **Phoebe** laid a rose on Bai Jian (*"He really was a friend of the rose"*). Panacea gave her the **war shard**, and Phoebe locked it in a rune-marked lead box to be hidden at HQ: *"This is very dangerous to, for anyone to have."*
- **Bai Feng will go with them.** *"Now that he's not here with me, I guess there's no use of me going back. It was just only the two of us back home."* **Panacea**: *"We can be your family now."* — *"Whoa, whoa, whoa. Let's not move too fast here."* — *"Sometimes you got to choose family."* — *"I appreciate the thought… I would like to know his comrades."* Alceo handed him Bai Jian's sword and armour. *"I will carry them all."*
- **The ritual site was collapsed** by Alceo's alchemists, along with most of the Urgathoan temple.
- **Illcayna's gear** (see Loot). The scythe and an *"unusual object"* resisted everyone. Panacea and Zanthar identified the **Bands of Force**. Zanthar drank a potion from the loot that held a memory: *"a chilling and unending winter,"* a scythe-arm dragging behind, a figure trapped in ice before a frozen doorway. Recall Knowledge placed it *"maybe at the top of the world, but you're not real sure."* Cyrathul: *"Something's going on in the north."*

### Act IV — North With the Army (20–21 Calistril)
- A day's march with the army to Logos. At the campfire **Panacea crit-identified the Bleeding Heart of Urgathoa** (a greater symbol of conflict, holy or unholy only). **Bai Feng** attuned it: *"Like Divine Wrath might… Bless is kind of redundant with my build, actually."*
- **Ren.** Head hung all day. That night he barked twice and darted into the woods. **In Elysium, Bai Jian turned to mount his horse and Ren was standing there instead**, ready to run beside him for the rest of eternity. Panacea: *"But so sad."* **Bai Feng**: *"Sad ending without a tragic ending."*
- **Logos, noon, 21 Calistril.** **Zanthar identified the scythe** on the third day: the **Reaper's Sigh**, +1 wounding striking. Kept; nobody can sell a relic. The party sold the surplus for 25 pp each. **Bai Feng sold some of Bai Jian's gear**: *"This is bad. Sorry, brother. It is what it is."* He talked Cyrathul into the Bands of Force on natural armour, and suggested bolas for ranged trips.
- **Panacea**: *"So where do we go from here?"* **Bai Feng**: the undead are gone; *"all the other towns can be happy and enjoy their undead-free life."*

### Act V — Four Days to Saringallow (21–26 Calistril)
- More traffic on the road than the party had ever seen. The undead had been the real problem, and trade was moving again.
- **Watches.** Cyrathul heard wolves that never came close. Zanthar was startled by a hay cart. At **Wolf Point**, empty now, Panacea jumped at the old haunt all night. Bai Feng, on the ledges at second watch, saw a fox take a rabbit and an owl cross the full moon. Kaazz: *"So maybe there is some things that are familiar to home."*
- **Saringallow HQ.** **Maire** and **Zara** greeted them. *"Did you really defeat the Wight Mother?"* Panacea: *"We did. At our price."* Asked about Bai Jian: *"He's no longer with us, but you can meet Bai Feng here."* Zara ran at him and squeezed him. **Bai Feng**: *"I at least should go visit the people that have taken care of him while he was here. It looks like he was in good company and I'm glad for him."* Maire: *"You guys are gonna be remembered as the Wight Mother's Bane from here on out."*
- Maire asked a favour: the two goblins sent to repair the ship at the **Waterspout Inn** hadn't reported in. *"Mostly I wanted to make sure they didn't burn the whole town down."*
- **Alceo's Dream Message**: a festival on **5 Pharast**. If they missed it, *"return for the Day of Bones."*

### Act VI — The Witchfire (28 Calistril → 5 Pharast)
- **Moob and Fang** were arguing on deck (*"I'm telling you, it's going to sink the boat"*). The **Witchfire** now had armour plating, a ram on the prow and a collapsible metal mast. Fang: *"You tell Maire we'll be done when we're done."*
- **The mast.** It weighed 2,000 pounds and Wing's tires were smoking. The first lift failed. On the second the rope slipped through Cyrathul's hands, and **Bai Feng got it around his shoulder (Athletics 29)**. With Panacea (27) and Zanthar pulling too, the party got it up the four inches it needed. Moob pushed the button and the mast shot up 25 feet.
- **Butter rums at the inn.** The innkeeper begged for *"no fires anywhere but the hearth tonight."* Zanthar learned a spell from a scroll (Arcana 29).
  - **The goblins didn't know Bai Jian had a brother**, and as far as they were concerned this was Bai Jian: *"We came here, and then we sunk a boat, and then we knocked this boat down with him… and killed that demon with one swipe of his sword."* **Bai Feng** played along (*"Bai Feng is a bit different from Bai Jian. He's a bit more like jester"*): *"That was a great battle. Okay, so I did all that, huh?… Yeah, I totally did all that. See, I told you."*
  - **Moob and Panacea.** *"Remember I killed you?"* — *"That's why we don't travel with you anymore."* Moob showed the scar (from S4's crit-failed Battle Medicine). **Panacea** flexed her claws and smiled: *"It was an accident, and I was a young medic. I have kept my nails much shorter these days."* She bought him another butter rum to make up for it, and herded them back to the boat (*"I mean, I'm not his mom"*). Zanthar: *"I trust them. They're goblins."* Cyrathul: *"I would not trust them."* Later Cyrathul, quite drunk: *"I can't find the door handle."*
- Two days down the river, with Wing's paddle wheels turning. The goblins started drinking again the moment the anchor dropped (*"Party pooper"* when Panacea declined). On Cyrathul's watch: *"almost sounds like a lot of horses are moving somewhere."*

### Act VII — The Fall of Saringallow (5 Pharast · chase, victory points)
- **Bai Feng spotted them first** (Perception 31): **Rabbit and Cole** running at the dock in a dead panic. *"They're looking for you. They've taken everyone. The Hellknights, all of the Hellknights are here."* A chase. See below for every obstacle.
- **Streets** — a cart rolled into the Hellknights' path.
- **The candle workshop** — hellhounds and smoke. **Bai Feng kicked down the door** and Cyrathul threw a leg of ham to the hounds.
- **Merchant Square** — Erinyes diving on **Maire and Rhokrag**. **Bai Feng's Religion crit (36, on a hero point)**: radiant light poured off him and the fiends hissed and fled. The party could reach only one of the two, and chose Maire. An Erinyes rose with Rhokrag. **Cyrathul's Earthbind** crashed it to the ground and it lost its grip, but *"another one just swoops in and grabs him and tears off with him."* *"Oh, I tried."*
- **Quoster** was waiting with a cart and three horses. When Zanthar offered a Fireball for the crowd, Bai Feng shoved people aside while **Cyrathul roared them out of the way (Intimidation 39)**.
- **The battle mages** — fifty of them on **Alceo and Phoebe**. Phoebe: *"You take him with you. He's more important."* **Zanthar and Panacea** raised a magical barricade. Bai Feng's Intimidation didn't land (*"Beware of me. Well, that didn't work"*). Kaazz gave Cyrathul the choice of Alceo or Phoebe. **Cyrathul**: *"Who resurrected me?"* He took Alceo. **Rabbit and Cole jumped out of the cart to stand with Phoebe.** Alceo screamed for her from the back of the cart.
- **The gate** — **Bai Feng got under it and lifted it on his shoulder (37)** while Panacea worked the crank. **Alceo**: *"I can't believe we had to leave so many behind, including Phoebe."*
- **The trail** — **Bai Feng's Nature (33)** and **Panacea's Survival** guided Quoster up to **High Fort**, doubling back to lose the hellhounds. **Alceo is now Elite.** **110 XP** at High Fort the next morning (80, plus 30 for saving Alceo).

### Act VIII — High Fort (~7–12 Pharast)
- **Jenverick** at the keep: *"What are you guys doing here?"* **Panacea**: *"We were attacked. Saringallow was attacked. There is no celebration."* Ethran had sent the festival purse ahead, so **100 gp each**. *"This was supposed to be a surprise for the party."* Kaazz: *"Ren's in Elysium."*
- **Ethran Howell** came through the locked door on 10 Pharast dressed as a filthy druid (everyone but Bai Feng recognised the one-armed Eagle Knight). His news:
  - **Saringallow is seized** and under strict curfew. Most of the forces are Caeto's, though some Isgeri soldiers resent his inaction during the undead crisis.
  - **Rhokrag and the other Hellbreakers are alive.** Caeto will interrogate them for High Fort's location and *"an item of very significant value"* the party hid (the war shard). *"If they bring the mind worms in, no one is safe."*
  - **Caeto sails in two days** on a commandeered armoured ship to fetch a **Chelaxian interrogator** from **Miserias or Senera**. He'll be gone at least a week.
- **Panacea**: find Caeto's critics and *"help take back Saringallow."* **Cyrathul**: *"Better we capture him while he's split off."*
- **Ethran's plan**: the **Sereni Manor**, an hour from the city, as the army's command post. **Panacea's Society (29)** brought up the history: house Sereni sacrificed its own servants' children to fiends, the locals lynched the family, and the surviving servants founded Saringallow. A **demon bile** source was there ten years ago, supposedly cleared. The diabolist **Sifon Hagouni** *(as heard)* now holds it *"for private research."* Ethran, to Bai Feng: *"Your brother helped us very much."*
- Zanthar clicked Lightning Bolt in Foundry by accident while picking his spells. Kaazz: *"Zanthar immediately cast lightning bolt and killed him."* On the raft Zanthar asked Ethran why he won't bathe: *"I just figure if I stink enough, people won't come close to me."*
- **Two days down the clover stream** to the **Vulaunex Ranch**. A grizzled old rancher greeted them: *"It's good to meet you. I hear you're here to kill my brother."* **End of session.**
