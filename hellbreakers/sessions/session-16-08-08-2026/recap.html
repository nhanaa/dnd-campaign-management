Session Summary

The morning after the vampire hunt, the party woke up level 6 in Sarevial's safehouse — and found out the frame-up stuck anyway. Quoster Thistlewait walked in with a WANTED leaflet: the Hellbreakers League are murderers of Hedvend VI. Bai Jian's reaction was the whole campaign in five words: "Damn it, those bastards succeeded." Quoster's offer: he'll smuggle the party out of Elidir in his trade wares — weeks of arranging under the lockdown — and he pays in advance: a case of Masquerade Scarves, a Staff of Phantasms, a ring off his own finger. The price is personal. His friend Siadrius has been arrested on a rival's false accusation, and the army sweep is dumping people into the Gyve, the black prison tower over the Moyle. "He won't survive long in there."

The rescue became the session's one real fight — and one of the campaign's most Hellbreakers fights ever. A riot crowd already at the gates chanted "Free Siadrius!" while guards held the man strapped Ewok-style to a pole. The GM debuted a Control the Crowd action, and the fight turned into an argument between violence and reputation: Bai Jian nearly killed the Captain of the Guard with a falcata crit, failed the lethality flat check — +1 Suppression — and spent a hero point to undo it: "I'm going to keep our reputation clean." From there the party fought like a rebellion instead of a wrecking crew: Cyrathul crit-commanded the crowd into stoning the guards, Zanthar's Fear crit-broke a weeping guard who "didn't sign up for this," Hexroot — Tim's brand-new leshy — soaked three Reactive Strikes untying Siadrius mid-melee, and every takedown landed non-lethal. Then, with the crowd chanting "Hellbreakers! Isger for Isger!" and the army coming, they talked their own riot into going home before it became a massacre. +2 Uprising, +1 reputation, nobody dead.

The back half belonged to a new NPC who may be the most consequential recruit the Elidir chapter ever makes. Siadrius led the party to Quoster's own safehouse — the reveal landing when he sprinted into Quoster's arms, kissing his cheeks; "Siadrius means more to me than you'll ever know" — and put them in touch with his fixer: Jenverick, a tall, blue-skinned, horned hellspawn woman in high heels, terminal enthusiasm made flesh. She fenced the party's entire hoard (bribe ledgers at full price), delivered a shopping list of runes and scrolls, realized who her clients were, and demanded to found the Elidir chapter on the spot: "The time is now… Let me join. Let me help. I have so many ideas. Please, please, please." Her ideas, executed across twelve in-game days: loot the abandoned Reeves Rest Manor (Caeto fired the staff and moved out — Otton Nettlebrush confirmed it, got recruited, and the party robbed the steward's house for the city's needy, walking out with an ancient relic glaive nobody can wield), and frame the Aziri family — gala contest winners and the army's chief suppliers — exactly the way the League was framed. Fake talismans, forged army orders slipped to Colonel Rithert on Bai Jian's gala friendship, and a staged sting that ended with the Aziris marched into the Gyve while the party watched from the rooftops. Zero suppression. The Uprising tracker hit its final stage: Revolution.

Two quieter beats carried weight. Ren started coughing — Panacea diagnosed choking death, the vampire plague from Ezio's death-burst, and a rank-3 Cleanse Affliction scroll made the hound cough up a clawed embryo-thing that got stomped flat. But the roll log holds a detail nobody chased: Cyrathul failed the same Cloud of Plague save that morning, and only Ren was ever treated. The session closed with a haggard Quoster needing leverage to get his goods — and the party — out of the city, and Jenverick supplying the answer: stage a robbery at the docks. Sasatoli Bronc agreed to leave empty crates and a single guard out tomorrow night — "anything to get under Caeto's skin." The fake heist is next week's cold open.

Act II · Combat · Free Siadrius! (Encounter 1)
  • The Gyve: a black-stone prison tower over the Moyle labor camp. An angry crowd had poured through the outer gates chanting "Free Siadrius!" — the prisoner strapped wrist-and-ankle to a 10-foot pole, "Luke Skywalker in Return of the Jedi with the Ewoks."

Act III · Faction · Send the Riot Home (victory-point challenge)
  • The freed crowd erupted — "Hellbreakers! Hellbreakers! Send the devils back to Cheliax!" — and the GM laid out the stakes: leave now, and the army slaughters these people.
  • Cyrathul led — "You guys need to scatter before the army gets here. No use in dying" — closing on a critical-success Diplomacy 32.
  • Panacea worked Society (25); Zanthar cast Fear on the crowd itself via Drain Bonded Item ("Let's have some fun" — counted a success); Bai Jian took the Aid lane ("I'm not confident I can make it").

  • +2 Uprising, +1 Hellbreakers reputation. Nobody died in front of the Gyve.
Act IV · Roleplay · Quoster's Heart, Quoster's House
  • Siadrius led the party through alleys to a nondescript house in a nice neighborhood — Quoster's own safehouse, hidden key and all.
  • The reveal: Siadrius sprinted into Quoster's arms, kissing his cheeks — "It was so awful. I thought I was going to die." Quoster, softly: "Siadrius means more to me than you'll ever know. This only proves I'm making the right choice by supporting the Hellbreakers League."
  • The house is the party's new HQ — Sarevial already relocated to its kitchen ("your other safe house will no longer be safe").
  • Siadrius, calmed by whatever Sarevial put in his tea, donned a Masquerade Scarf of his own — a foot taller, human-shaped, "Joe Common Man" — and offered his contact: "Jenverick. She's… well, you'll see. She's very excitable."

Kept: skeleton key, face paint, climbing bolt.

("Hopefully not."); Hexroot a +1 armor potency rune; Bai Jian scrolls and a wand for the new occult kit. Everyone +1 hero point.
  • Then she put the pieces together and detonated: "Are you guys really the Hellbreakers?… The time is now. Elidir is ready for its own chapter. We need leaders. Let me join. Let me help. I have so many ideas. Please, please, please." Bai Jian: "Your boy Bai Jian's getting dizzy."
Act VI · Mystery · Ren's Cough
  • Mid-shopping, another Fortitude save. Ren was coughing. Panacea's Medicine named it: choking death — the disease from Ezio's death-burst.
  • Her Cleanse Affliction was only rank 2 — too low to counteract — so Bai Jian bought a rank-3 scroll (justified by an earlier Nature check that spotted the sickness pre-shopping) and Panacea cast it.
  • Ren coughed up "a nasty blood-phlegm ball… almost like an embryo… growing claws out of it." Panacea: "I think we're going to have to fight this thing." They got it in time. Stomped.
  • ⚠ Cyrathul failed the same save that morning and it was never addressed on-screen — the DM's half-finished aside ("I think two of them actually—") was the only brush with it.
Act VII · Heist · The Reeves Rest Job
  • Jenverick returned with Otton Nettlebrush — Bai Jian's servant contact from the gala. The intel: Caeto fired most of the staff and moved to the Chelaxian quarter; Reeves Rest sits nearly empty with art, rare books, and an ancient glaive kept "since they defeated the original Iskari." The pitch: rob it for the city's needy.
  • Fast theater-of-the-mind heist: Panacea cat-flipped the gate; Zanthar tumbled over; Bai Jian failed his climb in full plate — saved because Hexroot had already crafted "the sleepiest potion in the world" and the guards were unconscious before he crashed down.
  • Hexroot's Arcana (after a familiar-modifier mixup) mapped the magical window-traps; Cyrathul picked the one clean window; Zanthar's Thievery worked the locks. "You guys get in like professional thieves… nobody even knows you were there."
  • Take: fenced for the needy — plus the Lifebloom Glaive (a relic: +1 vitalizing striking with locked minor/major/grand gifts) and the Token of Salt and Earth (sold). Bai Jian flirted with a full rebuild for it and declined; kept "just for fun."
  • Uprising hit its FINAL stage: Revolution — banked +1s, a one-time full reroll, and roll-twice-take-better once per check while in Elidir. Otton Nettlebrush recruited as a Hellbreaker.
Act VIII · Infiltration · The Aziri Frame-Job
  • Jenverick's masterstroke: "They're accusing us of killing Hedvend… What if we turned the tables and framed some of them?" Target: the Aziri family — gala darts winners, "fewer scruples than a devil from the hells," and the army's primary suppliers. The AP's Infiltration Points system debuted.
  • The rumor: a missing Druman talisman shipment — Hexroot seeded it through Father Kindness's guild (Underworld Lore 29), Zanthar spread it in the streets (Deception 28); Cyrathul's version didn't land (22).
  • The props: a day and a half crafting "really, really good" fake talismans — Hexroot (Crafting 31), Zanthar (23), Panacea (Arcana 31). Zanthar's brief: "We don't want ones that actually work. We want fake ones."
  • The paperwork: forged army orders with a stamped seal — Panacea (Society 31), Zanthar (25) — delivered to Colonel Rithert on Bai Jian's friendship, Cyrathul's hero-pointed Deception crit 32 carrying the handoff. Rithert passed them to his lieutenant without a glance.
  • The hunt: Bai Jian alerted Rithert personally — "Do you still have that silver dart I gave you?" "Yes, I do." — Diplomacy 25, then rerolled a second Diplomacy into 27, the DC exactly; Panacea picked locks (28) and ran rooftops (30 — "your resident cat did that"); Zanthar hero-pointed Thievery (26); Hexroot muscled a wall (25). Every failed die was covered by a teammate.
  • The trap closed perfectly: Aziris buying "stolen" talismans, investigators arriving opposite, the party gone. The Aziris were handcuffed and marched into the Gyve. Zero suppression points.

Round 2 — "I'm Going to Keep Our Reputation Clean"

  • Bai Jian: second crit on the Captain — 39… with non-lethal never declared. DC 10 lethality flat check: rolled 3 — the Captain dying, +1 Suppression. Hero point — automatic success under the +10 rule — "I'm going to keep our reputation clean." The Captain lives. Dueling Parry up.
  • Guards: fist and halberd missed Bai Jian; three swings missed Cyrathul.
  • Cyrathul: Demoralize crit-failed, Draconic Frenzy — jaws hit (16), claws missed. Took a 17-damage halberd in reply.
  • Hexroot untied Siadrius in the open: the manipulate triggered all three guards' Reactive Strikes — hit (10), miss, crit (26) — and Zanthar caught that the crit disrupts the attempt. At 36 damage taken, Hexroot spent his next action doing it again. Siadrius freed.
  • Panacea: 2-action Heal on Bai Jian (17), Assurance Battle Medicine on Hexroot (8). Zanthar: Fear, rank 3, three targets — two saves and one critical failure: a guard at Frightened 3 and Fleeing, knees shaking, tears down his face — "He didn't sign up for this when he was a guard."

Round 3 — "Larry, Open the Gate!"

  • Ren (commanded): bite, 13 (Foundry briefly bit his own owner — "don't bite your owner").
  • Bai Jian: cast Guidance on himself — the Hedge Mage debut — then a non-lethal strike (10) and Dueling Parry. The frightened guard's halberds landed at exactly 26 vs Parry-boosted AC 27, twice — "Parry that ching. Parry ching again. Double Parry."
  • The Frightened-3 guard fled — Bai Jian's Reactive Strike missed by 1; Cyrathul hero-pointed Twisting Tail (reroll 12 → 27) for 20, leaving him "near death" hammering the gate: "Larry, open! Open the gate!"
  • Cyrathul chased — eating a Reactive Strike upgraded to a crit on corrected math — then non-lethally clawed the runner (17) into the gate: unconscious. Guard 1 down.
  • Hexroot: yanked his familiar close, Enfeeble — failed save, Enfeebled 2. Panacea: Life Boost on Bai Jian (fast healing 6), then controlled the crowd — success — "But I can see you, Cy. I'm thinking about you."
  • Zanthar: "non-lethally lethal spell them" — Nonlethal Electric Arc: one guard failed (10) and crumpled unconscious; the other crit-saved. Guard 2 down.

Round 4 — The Flat of the Blade

  • Life Boost ticked +6. The last guard (Enfeebled 2) missed Ren; Ren bit back for 15 (Nature's Precision).
  • Bai Jian: non-lethal crit — 44 ("a non-lethal crit, but it's a crit nonetheless"): flat of the falcata, face-down, concussed. Guard 3 down — all four enemies alive.
  • The crowd erupted — "Hellbreakers! Hellbreakers! Send the devils back to Cheliax! Isger for Isger!" — rolling straight into the dispersal challenge.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the Blade That Stays Sheathed Enough

  • His hero point was the session's defining choice: a forgotten non-lethal call, a failed flat check, a dying Captain of the Guard, +1 Suppression — bought back on the spot. "I'm going to keep our reputation clean." The whole fight fought non-lethal after that.
  • Opened the rescue at full commitment — Sudden Charge into a 35-damage crit, ate the nat-20 riposte ("Well, isn't that the consequence of my own actions?"), and stared down authority itself: "This is the city guard! You can't do this!" — "Right. Like I care about that."
  • Ran the Parry Master clinic: two separate turns where Dueling Parry's +2 was the exact margin on incoming halberds — "Parry that ching. Parry ching again… That's why you play smart." Finished the fight with a non-lethal 44 crit and narrated the mercy.
  • Debuted Hedge Mage with maximum comedy: "You see, out of nowhere, Bai Jian, for some reason, casts Guidance on himself."
  • His S12 social investment paid the scheme's biggest dividend: the forged-orders delivery rode his friendship with Colonel Rithert, and he made the follow-up visit himself — "Do you still have that silver dart I gave you?" He did; he'd kept it since the gala. His summary of why the Colonel trusts him: "I even licked his boot."
  • Dog dad and rules captain: spotted Ren's sickness first, solved the cure (rank-3 scroll, correct counteract math), caught the missing XP award, and talked the DM out of a phantom Reactive Strike on the reach rules — then stress-tested a Lifebloom rebuild and kept the falcata system: "I guess we'll just keep it. Just for fun."
  • Kept his instincts about convenient plans, even ones he joins: "Sounds like a plan, but I have bad feelings about that. No matter. We can try."

Cyrathul · the Dragon Who Commands Crowds

  • First PC to touch the new mechanic and he crit it: Diplomacy 35 turned the riot into a weapon — 9 crowd damage to every guard — then Draconic Advance into a crit bite (30) on the Captain. In disguise the whole time: "What are you talking about? We're upstanding citizens."
  • The conscience of the optics war — vetoed Zanthar's lightning bolt through the crowd in four words: "I don't know about the optics on that."
  • Paid for aggression like a frontliner: chased the fleeing guard through an upgraded Reactive Strike crit ("Maybe I should not have stride." "Then I can't do my fun new ability."), hero-pointed Twisting Tail into a 20-damage interception, and non-lethally ended the runner against the gate he was begging Larry to open.
  • Led the dispersal that saved the crowd from the army — "You guys need to scatter before the army gets here. No use in dying" — grinding through treacherous d20s before the closing crit Diplomacy 32.
  • Commissioned a bane rune from a hellspawn fixer and answered her "you're not going to use that on me, are you?" with a perfectly dragon "Hopefully not."
  • Gave Jenverick the campaign recap in six words: "No, no, that was a vampire."
  • Quietly closed two schemes' final doors: spotted the one un-trapped window that let the manor heist finish clean, and rolled the Diplomacy (29) that turned Sasatoli's amusement into a signed-on co-conspirator.

Panacea · the Cat Who Runs the Rooftops

The frame-job ran on her skill list.

  • Debuted Living Nexus mid-fight, inhaling Zanthar's spell with Absorb Magic ("I got pretty excited about it") — and built the kit for it: a +1 striking dancer's spear and a ceremonial dagger.
  • Diagnosed choking death on Ren and cast the scroll that made him cough up the clawed embryo — then hit the table's horror button: "I think we're going to have to fight this thing."
  • Owned the deadpan lane on Jenverick — "I was expecting you to be a little bit more excitable" on arrival, "You're just a plan person, aren't you?" by the end — and the gala self-own: "You did? I made friends with the vampire."
  • And Jenverick exists in the party's orbit because of her: she asked Quoster about trustworthy merchants, then gently worked the still-shaking Siadrius over his tea until he offered his contact. The session's defining NPC arrived through Panacea's patience.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Wizard Who Weaponizes Everything (Including Fear, Twice)
