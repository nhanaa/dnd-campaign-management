## Session Summary

The morning after the vampire hunt, the party woke up level 6 in Sarevial's safehouse — and found out the frame-up stuck anyway. Quoster Thistlewait walked in with a WANTED leaflet: *the Hellbreakers League are murderers of Hedvend VI.* Bai Jian's reaction was the whole campaign in five words: *"Damn it, those bastards succeeded."* Quoster's offer: he'll smuggle the party out of Elidir in his trade wares — it will take weeks under the lockdown — and in the meantime he pays in advance: a case of Masquerade Scarves, a Staff of Phantasms, a ring off his own finger. The price is personal. His friend **Siadrius** has been arrested on a rival's false accusation of making Quoster's actual Hellbreaker deal, and the army sweep is dumping people into **the Gyve**, the black prison tower over the Moyle. *"He won't survive long in there."*

The rescue turned into the session's one real fight — and one of the campaign's most Hellbreakers fights ever. A riot crowd was already at the prison gates chanting *"Free Siadrius!"* while guards held the man strapped Ewok-style to a pole. The GM debuted a **Control the Crowd** action (Diplomacy/Intimidation to part the mob — a crit turns the crowd itself into a weapon), and the fight became a five-way argument between violence and reputation: Bai Jian nearly killed the Captain of the Guard with a falcata crit, failed the lethality flat check — **+1 Suppression** — and spent a hero point to undo it: *"I'm going to keep our reputation clean."* From there the party fought like a rebellion instead of a wrecking crew: Cyrathul crit-commanded the crowd into stoning the guards, Zanthar's Fear crit-broke a weeping guard who *"didn't sign up for this,"* Hexroot — Tim's brand-new leshy — ate three reactive strikes untying Siadrius in the middle of the melee, and every takedown landed non-lethal. Then, with the crowd chanting *"Hellbreakers! Isger for Isger!"* and the army coming, they talked their own riot into going home before it became a massacre. **+2 Uprising, +1 reputation, nobody dead.**

The back half belonged to a new NPC who may be the most consequential recruit the Elidir chapter will ever make. Siadrius led the party to Quoster's own safehouse — the reveal landing when he sprinted into Quoster's arms and kissed him all over the cheek; *"Siadrius means more to me than you'll ever know"* — and put them in touch with his fixer: **Jenverick**, a tall, blue-skinned, horned hellspawn woman in high heels, terminal enthusiasm made flesh. She fenced the party's entire hoard (including the bribe ledgers at full price), delivered a shopping list of runes and scrolls, realized who her clients were, and demanded to found the Elidir chapter on the spot: *"The time is now… Let me join. Let me help. I have so many ideas. Please, please, please."* Her ideas, executed across twelve in-game days: **loot the abandoned Reeves Rest Manor** (Caeto fired the staff and moved out — Otton Nettlebrush confirmed it, got recruited, and the party robbed the steward's house blind for the city's needy, walking out with an ancient relic glaive nobody can wield — Bai Jian flirted with rebuilding his whole fighter for it and kept his falcata system instead); and **frame the Aziri family** — the gala contest winners and the army's chief suppliers — exactly the way the League was framed. Fake Druman talismans crafted by Hexroot, Zanthar, and Panacea, forged army orders slipped to Colonel Rithert on the strength of Bai Jian's gala friendship (Cyrathul's hero-pointed Deception crit doing the rolling), Bai Jian returning in person to set the army hunting — *"Do you still have that silver dart I gave you?" "Yes, I do."* — and a staged sting that ended with the Aziris marched into the Gyve while the party watched from the rooftops. **Zero suppression. The Uprising tracker hit its final stage: Revolution.**

Two quieter beats carried weight. Ren started coughing — Panacea diagnosed **choking death**, the vampire plague from Ezio's death-burst, and burned a rank-3 Cleanse Affliction scroll to counteract it; the hound coughed up a clawed embryo-thing that got stomped flat. And the roll log holds a detail nobody chased: **Cyrathul failed the same Cloud of Plague save that morning**, and only Ren's case was ever treated. The session closed with a haggard Quoster needing leverage to get his goods — and the party — out of the city, and Jenverick supplying the answer: stage a robbery at the docks. Sasatoli Bronc, Zanthar's gala buddy, agreed to leave empty crates out and just a single dock guard on duty tomorrow night — *"anything to get under Caeto's skin."* The fake heist is next week's cold open.

## Key Events

### Act I — Wanted: The Hellbreakers League
- Long rest at Sarevial's safehouse, level-6 bookkeeping. Zanthar discovered Blessed One *does* have a level-6 feat and took **Mercy**; Bai Jian debuted **Hedge Mage (occult)**; Panacea debuted **Living Nexus**. Bai Jian is still **Drained 1** from Ezio's fangs.
- Morning **Cloud of Plague saves** (the choking-death follow-up from Ezio's death-burst): Bai Jian succeeded — **Cyrathul and Ren both failed** (roll log, 18:12). Only Ren's case would surface later.
- Tim's new PC: **Hexroot, a little leshy** occult witch with a club and a hissing familiar. Zarok Brewhide is retired.
- **Quoster Thistlewait** arrived with the WANTED leaflet — the League is officially blamed for Hedvend VI's murder. Bai Jian: *"Damn it, those bastards succeeded. We tried."*
- Quoster's deal: he smuggles the party out of Elidir in his wares (weeks of arranging), pays up front — **Masquerade Scarves for everyone, the Staff of Phantasms (Zanthar), the Ring of Fair Assessment** — and asks one thing: rescue **Siadrius**, falsely accused by a rival, from the Gyve *"before they put the nails to him."*

### Act II — Free Siadrius! *(Encounter 1)*
- The Gyve: a black-stone prison tower over the Moyle labor camp. An angry crowd had poured through the outer gates chanting **"Free Siadrius!"** — the prisoner strapped wrist-and-ankle to a 10-foot pole between two guards, *"Luke Skywalker in Return of the Jedi with the Ewoks."*
- New mechanic: **Control the Crowd** (Diplomacy/Intimidation) — success parts the mob, crit sends it *at the enemy* for 2d8.
- The fight (see Combat Encounters) ran four rounds and ended with all four guards alive: the Captain hero-pointed back from a lethal crit, one guard KO'd against the gate mid-flight, one dropped by non-lethal Electric Arc, one knocked out cold with the flat of Bai Jian's falcata.
- Hexroot's debut play: walked into the middle of the melee and spent two actions untying Siadrius — a manipulate action that **triggered all three guards' Reactive Strikes, 36 damage** — the first attempt disrupted by a crit, so he simply did it again. The DM confirmed the guards had burned every reaction on him, *"so that you could get in there."*

### Act III — Send the Riot Home *(victory-point challenge)*
- The freed crowd erupted — *"Hellbreakers! Hellbreakers! Send the devils back to Cheliax!"* — and the GM laid out the stakes: leave now and the army slaughters these people.
- The party talked their own riot down: Cyrathul led (*"You guys need to scatter before the army gets here. No use in dying"*, closing on a **critical-success Diplomacy 32**), Panacea worked Society, Zanthar cast **Fear on the crowd itself** via Drain Bonded Item (*"Let's have some fun"* — counted as a success), Bai Jian took the Aid lane (*"I'm not confident I can make it"*), and Hexroot — after a crit-fail Intimidation that riled them worse — pulled **Underworld Lore** (learning of Father Kindness and the **Queen of Kanersha**, the Mysterious River Pirate) and bluffed: *"The queen and the father of all kindnesses has decided to run — you should run away."* It worked.
- **+2 Uprising, +1 Hellbreakers reputation.** Nobody died in front of the Gyve.

### Act IV — Quoster's Heart, Quoster's House
- Siadrius led them through alleys to a nondescript house in a nice neighborhood — **Quoster's own safehouse**, keys and all. The reveal: Siadrius sprinted into Quoster's arms, kissing his cheeks — *"It was so awful. I thought I was going to die."* Quoster, softly: *"Siadrius means more to me than you'll ever know. This only proves I'm making the right choice by supporting the Hellbreakers League."*
- The house is the party's now — Sarevial was already relocated to its kitchen (*"your other safe house will no longer be safe"*). Siadrius, calmed by whatever Sarevial put in his tea, donned a Masquerade Scarf of his own (a foot taller, human-shaped, *"Joe Common Man"*) and offered his contact for discreet shopping: *"Jenverick. She's… well, you'll see. She's very excitable."*

### Act V — Jenverick, Fixer of Elidir
- Enter **Jenverick**: tall, blue-skinned, horned hellspawn, high heels announcing her down every hallway. Panacea, deadpan: *"I was expecting you to be a little bit more excitable."*
- The great fencing operation: sold the Staff of the Dead, the carved onyx skull, Viro's Hellknight plate, the imposing shield, the Dream Crusher, the marvelous miniature horse, the platter, the whispering wire, Lezinia's jewelry, the padlock, and the Ring of Fair Assessment — and the **bribe ledgers at the full 400 gp** (*"They won't even try to talk me down"* — wink). Kept: the skeleton key, the face paint, the climbing bolt.
- Purchases: Panacea a **+1 striking dancer's spear** (her Living Nexus martial weapon) and a ceremonial dagger; Zanthar **scrolls of Blindness, Invisibility, and Water Breathing** — all three learned into his spellbook (Blindness on a crit at half cost, Invisibility rescued with a hero point); Cyrathul commissioned a **bane rune for his handwraps** — Jenverick, a hellspawn, taking the order: *"You're not going to use that on me, are you?"* (*"Hopefully not."*); Hexroot a **+1 armor potency rune**; Bai Jian scrolls and a wand for his new occult kit. Everyone +1 hero point.
- Then she watched Panacea cast a spell, put the pieces together, and detonated: *"Are you guys really the Hellbreakers? … The time is now. Elidir is ready for its own chapter. That protest at the Moyle just proves it. We need leaders. Let me join. Let me help. I have so many ideas. Please, please, please."* Bai Jian: *"Your boy Bai Jian's getting dizzy."*

### Act VI — Ren's Cough
- Mid-shopping, the GM called for another Fortitude save. Ren was coughing. Panacea's Medicine check named it: **choking death** — the disease from Ezio's death-burst.
- Her own Cleanse Affliction was only rank 2 — too low to counteract — so Bai Jian retroactively bought a **rank-3 scroll of Cleanse Affliction** (justified by an earlier Nature check that spotted the sickness before the shopping trip) and had Panacea cast it: Ren coughed up *"a nasty blood-phlegm ball… almost like an embryo… growing claws out of it."* Panacea: *"I think we're going to have to fight this thing."* They got it in time. Stomped.
- ⚠️ **Cyrathul failed the same save that morning**, and it was never addressed on-screen — the DM's half-finished aside (*"I think two of them actually—"*) was the only brush with it. See Open Threads.

### Act VII — The Reeves Rest Job
- Jenverick returned with **Otton Nettlebrush** — Bai Jian's servant contact from the gala. The intel: Caeto fired most of the manor staff and moved to the Chelaxian quarter; Reeves Rest sits nearly empty with its art, rare books, and an **ancient glaive the old servants say has hung there since the original Iskari were defeated**. Her pitch: rob it blind and give the money to the city's needy.
- The heist ran as a fast theater-of-the-mind victory-point challenge: Panacea cat-flipped the gate (*"her cat-like reflexes,"* after first floating a guard-impersonation plan); Zanthar tumbled over on Acrobatics; **Bai Jian failed his climb in full plate** — saved from consequence because **Hexroot had already crafted "the sleepiest potion in the world"** and the guards were unconscious before he crashed down; Hexroot's Arcana (after a familiar-modifier mixup) mapped the magical window-traps; **Cyrathul picked the one window they knew was clean** and the party slipped in through it; Zanthar's Thievery worked the locks. *"You guys get in like professional thieves… nobody even knows you were there."*
- Take: fenced for the needy — plus the **Lifebloom Glaive** (a **relic**: +1 vitalizing striking, with locked minor/major/grand gifts) and the Token of Salt and Earth (sold). Nobody in the party can use a Strength polearm; Bai Jian flirted with a full rebuild and declined — the falcata kit is a system, not a pile of feats. Kept *"just for fun."*
- **Uprising hit its FINAL stage: Revolution.** Each PC now holds: the two banked one-time +1s, a one-time full reroll (fortune), and — while in Elidir — a once-per-check roll-twice-take-better. **Otton Nettlebrush was recruited as a Hellbreaker.**

### Act VIII — The Aziri Frame-Job *(infiltration point system)*
- Jenverick's masterstroke, delivered with a Cheshire-cat grin: *"They're accusing us of killing Hedvend… What if we turned the tables and framed some of them?"* Target: the **Aziri family** — gala darts-contest winners, *"fewer scruples than a devil from the hells,"* and the Isgeri army's primary weapons suppliers.
- The GM debuted the AP's **Infiltration Points** system (success = infiltration point; failure = awareness/complication). The steps, across days:
  - **The rumor**: a shipment of Druman talismans for the army, gone missing — Hexroot seeded it through Father Kindness's guild (Underworld Lore 29), Zanthar spread it in the streets (Deception 28); Cyrathul's version didn't land (22).
  - **The props**: a day and a half crafting *"really, really good"* **fake talismans** — Hexroot (Crafting 31), Zanthar (23), Panacea (Arcana 31). Zanthar's design brief: *"We don't want ones that actually work. We want fake ones."*
  - **The paperwork**: forged army orders **complete with a stamped seal** — Panacea (Society 31), Zanthar (25) — hand-delivered to **Colonel Rithert**, who remembered his gala friends and passed them to his lieutenant without a second glance (Cyrathul's hero-pointed Deception **crit 32**). Bai Jian on why Rithert trusts them: *"I even licked his boot."*
  - **The hunt**: Bai Jian alerted Rithert to the "theft" personally — *"Do you still have that silver dart I gave you?" "Yes, I do."* — Diplomacy 25, then rerolled a stumbling second Diplomacy against the army investigators into **27, the DC exactly**; Panacea picked the meet's locks (Thievery 28) and ran the rooftops (Acrobatics 30 — *"your resident cat did that"*); Zanthar hero-pointed his Thievery (26); Hexroot muscled a wall (Athletics 25). Not every die cooperated — Cyrathul's follow-up Diplomacy, Zanthar's tumble, and Bai Jian's armored wall-climb all failed — but a teammate covered every miss, and the awareness track stayed clean. Bai Jian, mid-scheme, mostly retrained Occultism and cheered: *"I'm just going to sit here being moral support. Let's go, guys."*
- The trap closed perfectly: the Aziris arrived to buy cheap "stolen" talismans, the army investigators arrived from the other side, and the party ghosted. **The Aziris were handcuffed and marched into the Gyve — watched from the rooftops. Zero suppression points.**

### Act IX — The Dock Robbery (to be continued)

- High heels in the hallway: *"Did somebody say you need leverage? I've got another idea."* Jenverick's plan: **stage a robbery at the docks**, deliberately get spotted, so Quoster can argue his goods aren't safe in Elidir. Panacea: *"You're just a plan person, aren't you?"*

Zanthar made the pitch, Cyrathul's Diplomacy (29) sealed it, and the dock boss laughed his way in: *"Anything to get under Caeto's skin."* Tomorrow night: empty crates left out (Hexroot's touch: crates that *"didn't really have anything in them in the first place"*), a single dock guard on duty. *"The next mead's on you guys."*

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Free Siadrius! (the Gyve gates) · XP awarded in Foundry (amount not stated on-mic)

The party fought disguised in their brand-new Masquerade Scarves — and after round 2, entirely non-lethal.*

**Initiative**: Bai Jian 30 · Gate Guard A 25 · Gate Guard C 23 · Cyrathul 22 · Captain 20 · Gate Guard B 16 · Hexroot 13 · Panacea 12 · Zanthar 11 · Siadrius 6

**Round 1**
- **Bai Jian**: Stride + Sudden Charge through the crowd — **crit 35** on the Captain (fatal d12): off-guard (sword crit spec) plus Clumsy 1/Enfeebled 1 (crushing rune). When a guard re-gripped his halberd (manipulate), Bai Jian's **Reactive Strike** hit for 15. The guards' answer: a **nat-20 crit for 20** (*"the consequence of my own actions"*), then whiffs.
