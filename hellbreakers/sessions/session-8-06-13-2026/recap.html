Session Summary

Two new faces fell in with the party on the goblin trails this session — Zanthar Melodune III, a small, white-bearded gnome wizard with a hammer too big for him and no real plan ("a rebel without a clue"), and Sammy Shadowfoot, a halfling bard with a lute, a trusty frying pan, and a grudge against the Chelaxian Guard. Both had been sent east to Swiftrun, stumbled onto the same lead the party was chasing — a false Hell Knight named Vero Ahala gathering devils for a ritual in the Chitterwood — and decided the cause was worth a detour. Back at Thorngrip the ancestor-speaker Bracknap welcomed the whole group as true friends of the town, opened the gate to them for good, and laid out the job: Vero's ritual has clouded him from the ancestors' sight, but the woods themselves can find him. He handed over a cloudy Crystal Pendant and a map to three sacred "sites of subtle power" — each one corrupted by the ritual, each one now dangerous — and asked the party to carry the crystal to all three so it could read the disturbance and point the way.

Before they left, Bai Jian made a quiet trade at the goblin smithy: he gave up his jian — the blade he'd carried for years — for a falcata better suited to the cause, asking only that the smith take care of the old sword. Then it was out into the forest. The Little Tree site woke a corrupted complex trap and an enraged grizzly bear; Bai Jian tanked and killed the bear while the party pried hellfire fingers off the tree's roots. The Moss Pond rippled open into a planar trap with two regenerating moss-sloths from the Plane of Wood; Cyrathul read their fire weakness, Sammy danced the trap quiet with her lute, and the party ground the sloths back into moss. The Hungry Hole — an old goblin wizard's botched spacious-pouch turned into a hungry extradimensional mouth — tried to inhale Bai Jian and Cyrathul off the edge, but a few clean occultism checks peeled the corruption away. At each site the crystal drank in a thread of magic and glowed warmer and greener.

They brought it back to Bracknap, who held it, went pale, and saw it: "The caves! The Hell-Human Vero is in the caves!" The caves are Stagfall, named for the grandmother whose stag-shaped sacrifice once led human hunters away from his tribe — and now defiled by Vero's ritual. Bracknap marked the hidden cave on the map, and the tribe's elders gifted Bai Jian a rune of striking for his new blade. The party found the cave behind a grove that looked like a stag's antlers, stepped into the dark, and hit three web-spitting devils — Insidiators. Fighting half-blind until Sammy cast Light, they cut the devils down one by one, Bai Jian capping it with a 60-damage falcata crit that took the last devil's head clean off and sent it rolling out the cave mouth like a bowling ball. Past a hastily-piled rubble wall, a circular cavern opened with four tunnels, a shattered statue of an armored figure, and — coiled on a pile of rocks — a slithering snake-like creature. The session ended there, on the threshold of Vero's lair.

Timeline

Act I · Roleplay · Two New Longshanks

Friends of the Rose

  • Session zero for the two newcomers. Kaazz (DM) introduced himself; the three returning players re-introduced; Bai Jian recapped Session 7 (Umok, the Hell Hounds and Rama's death, the Orts, the Iskari Slayers, Thorngrip).
  • On the trail back to Thorngrip the party met Zanthar Melodune III (gnome wizard) and Sammy Shadowfoot (halfling bard) traveling together — introduced organically in-fiction.
  • Sammy hailed them with the Hellbreaker passphrase: "Are you friends of Rose?" Zanthar, bewildered at the armed strangers in the deep woods: "Why are we in the woods?"
  • The two had been dispatched east to Swiftrun, ran into trouble, and learned Vero Ahala is gathering Hell Knights for a ritual in the Chitterwood. Shared goal → the party folded them in, Panacea vouching for the link: "The trust goes up now for me. We're looking for the same person."
Act II · Roleplay / Faction · Thorngrip, the Smithy, and the Crystal

The Ancestor-Speaker's Errand

  • Back through the thorn wall into Thorngrip. Bracknap, ghostly ancestors flickering at his back, named the party friends of Thorngrip — "Our home is your home" — and Zagnub showed them the hidden lever so they could come and go freely.
  • The jian trade. Bai Jian visited Gug, the gruff goblin smith, and traded in his longtime jian for a falcata, asking to transfer his +1 potency rune — a genuine farewell: "Please take care of that sword. It meant a lot to me."
  • Bracknap's quest. Vero's ritual has "obscured him from our ancestors' sight," but three nearby sites of subtle power — disturbed by the ritual — can reveal him. He handed over the Crystal Pendant: carry it to all three and he can see Vero's location.
  • The three sites: the Hungry Hole (a bottomless pit), Little Tree (a sapling that never grows or dies, fed by magic), and Moss Pond (a rippling patch of moss tied to the Chitterwood's essence).
  • Panacea reasoned a fighter should carry it ("if they place it and something comes out, they'll be right there"); the party agreed Bai Jian — most HP, "least chance of immediate death" — would be the bearer.
  • Overnight rest while Gug worked the rune; the falcata waited at the door in the morning. Quiet downtime shopping (Ren's barding, rations).
Act III · Combat · The Little Tree & The Grizzly Bear

Hellfire in the Roots

  • Half an hour southeast to a small, vibrant sapling radiating peace — but the peace was wrong. The tree pulsed big-and-small in a magical flux, washing nearby PCs with Will saves (Slowed / Off-Guard, a shrinking effect).
  • It was a complex trap: hellfire "fingers" had reached through a planar crack and gripped the tree's roots, draining its magic. The flux woke a giant grizzly bear, which locked onto Bai Jian and charged.
  • The party split focus — disarming the trap (Nature/Occultism) while fighting the bear. Sammy, brand new and partly fighting through a laggy connection, dropped a Courageous Anthem to buff the team. The tree even shrank Sammy down to pixie size for a round.
  • Bai Jian tanked the bear, Dueling Parry up, and cut it down with two crits (25, then a 36-damage kill). He then disarmed the final trap component himself — spotting a smoldering ember-coal at the tree's base and stamping it out.
  • With the corruption gone, the tree radiated peace and "thanked" the party psychically. Bai Jian held out the crystal: an invisible tendril of magic drifted into it, and it glowed warm. The tree spat up a spacious pouch and rare mushrooms. Bai Jian skinned the bear for a pelt.
  • +30 XP for earning Bracknap's trust, plus the site XP.
Act IV · Combat / Exploration · The Moss Pond

A Crack to the Plane of Wood

  • An hour-and-a-half west to a large patch of undulating moss "floating" with no water beneath it. Bai Jian, learning from the tree, scouted ahead alone with the crystal ("let me approach it, because I have decent save").
  • The moss speared spikes up at Bai Jian and Ren the moment they reached the center (piercing + poison; both saved, one via hero point). A planar shimmer — the Plane of Wood converging — and two moss-sloths appeared. The moss itself was a corrupted complex trap.
  • Cyrathul read the sloths: from the Plane of Wood, weak to fire, regenerate each turn unless they take fire damage. His Exploit Vulnerability triggered the fire weakness on his bite/claw.
  • Sammy — by now DM-operated, her Wi-Fi having dropped — disabled the moss trap with an occultism check; the DM narrated her turn (a trance, a few bars on the lute, a spin and a dance, the field going calm) and her sung Courageous Anthem: "You can do it!"
  • Bai Jian Snagging-Struck the sloths off-guard ("Off-guard, baby") and ground them down with the falcata — normal hits of 12, 16, and 11 (lifted by the exploited weakness; no crit of his own). A moss spear later crit him on a nat 20 for 18 ("I need some healing, guys"). On the off-guard target, Zanthar crit with Hand of the Apprentice, hurling his oversized hammer. Panacea kept Bai Jian topped (ranged Heal, Life Boost fast healing); the sloths "melted back into moss."
  • Crystal out again: it drank the pond's magic and turned bright vibrant green. Loot from the dissolving sloths: a smuggler's padlock and consumables.
Act V · Mystery / Exploration · The Hungry Hole

The Mouth That Cannot Be Filled

  • Two hours east to a perfect 40-foot circle of barren dirt around a plain hole — no grass, no encroaching branches, an eerie inhaling sound. As Bai Jian and Cyrathul approached, an unseen force tried to drag them toward the edge (Fortitude saves). No creatures — just the hazard.
  • Cyrathul Recall-Knowledge'd it: long ago a goblin wizard tried to make his own spacious pouch out here and instead tore open a hole to an extradimensional space — now warped by Vero's ritual into a hungry mouth.
  • The party disarmed it cleanly with occultism — Cyrathul slapped a hellfire hand away; Zanthar (guidance up, hero-pointed) peeled the last fingers off and ended the encounter.
  • Bai Jian, dragged to the lip, stepped back rather than test the legend he'd joked about back in Thorngrip — that a goblin who falls through comes out the other side of the world ("maybe if I fall into the hole, I can just come back home," he'd grinned, needling his own faraway Tian Xia origin).
  • The hole belched up a low-grade talisman and 20 gold. Crystal out one last time: it went crystal-clear and vibrant green, glowing with warm radiant energy. All three sites done.
Act VI · Mystery / Faction · Bracknap's Warning & Stagfall

The Stag's Sacrifice

  • Back to Thorngrip. Bracknap held the charged crystal, his eyes flashing from calm blue to alarmed red: "The caves! The Hell-Human Vero is in the caves!"
  • The Stagfall lore. Before Thorngrip existed, his tribe was hunted by humans at the end of the Goblinblood Wars. His grandmother used the power of the woods to transform into a mighty stag and led the hunters away, dying so the rest could escape. The survivors raised the thorn wall and named the caves Stagfall in her honor. Now Vero defiles them.
  • Bracknap, "trembling with anger," sent the party to stop him — marking the hidden cave on the map (behind a grove of stag-antler trees) and warning the crystal still has power left, "it will be very useful."
  • The tribe's elders gifted a rune of striking, which Gug applied to Bai Jian's falcata (6 gp 5 sp). Overnight rest and a goblin grog send-off.
Act VII · Combat · Into the Caves: The Insidiators

Three Devils in the Dark

  • Three hours to a rocky hill whose trees, at the right angle, look like a stag's antlers over black, gnarled brush. A small cave hid behind a tall tree.
  • The party crept into the dark chamber — Bai Jian has no darkvision — and three devils (Insidiators) were waiting, hidden in the corner.
  • Cyrathul opened with a crit Exploit Vulnerability and read the block: resist all physical except silver, resist poison, immune to fire, weak to holy — the read that defined the fight. An Insidiator cast Bane (−1 attacks) and webbed Cyrathul (immobilized); a second web at Bai Jian missed.
  • Panacea cast Light so the party could finally see, then blew an Insidiator apart with silver Needle Darts ("foo, foo, foo, all come out the back"). Zanthar Electric-Arced the cluster. Cyrathul Mirror'd in, off-guarded a second devil, and tore it apart with chomp + claw.
  • Sammy cast Light so the party could finally see. Bai Jian closed and critted the last Insidiator — base dice maxed ("Double 12") for 63, 60 after resistance — a massive-damage instant kill. "I just do a spin and just cut his head clean off." The head rolled out the cave mouth past the newcomers.
  • Three devils, all turned to ash. Past a hastily-piled rubble wall (Ren "showed them how to dig"), a circular cavern with four tunnels, a moss alcove, a shattered statue of an armored figure — and on a pile of rocks, a slithering snake-like creature.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the White Blade, Reforged

  • Volunteered to carry and place the Crystal Pendant at every site and made himself the point man at each hazard ("let me approach it, I have decent save") — eating the bear's charge, the moss spikes, and the Hungry Hole's pull so the squishier members didn't have to.
  • Solo-killed the grizzly bear (Dueling Parry, two crits — 25 then 36 — self-Battle-Medicine afterward "because I never get to use it") and personally disarmed the Little Tree's last trap component by stamping out the corrupting ember at its roots.
  • The session's quiet heart: the jian trade. After years carrying the "gentleman of weapons," he gave it to Gug for a falcata — a one-handed fatal blade that fits his free-hand Dueling build — asking only that the smith "take care of that sword. It meant a lot to me."
  • The trade paid off at once: Bracknap's elders gifted a rune of striking, and in Stagfall the new +1 striking falcata crit with its base dice maxed ("Double 12") for 63 — 60 after resistance — a massive-damage instant decapitation of the last Insidiator. "I just do a spin and just cut his head clean off."
  • Spent real table-time mentoring the two newcomers through their first turns.

Cyrathul · the Dragon's Eye

  • The party's knowledge engine at every site: read the moss-sloths (Plane of Wood, weak fire, regenerating), the Hungry Hole (a goblin wizard's spacious-pouch gone wrong), and — most decisively — the Insidiators (resist physical except silver, resist poison, immune fire, weak holy), the single read that shaped the whole cave fight.
  • His Exploit Vulnerability and Mirror Cloak chomp-and-claw closed out a moss-sloth and a cave devil.
  • Before any fighting, flagged the party's one real fragility — invoking Rama's death from a failed treatment check to make sure someone could heal after a battle: "that could be a real problem when you go out after a battle and no one knows how to heal the wounds."
  • Trademark deadpan all night: offered to scout the Hungry Hole because he "can arrest fall as well," and when there was nothing useful left to do at the Little Tree, simply announced he would "stand there menacingly."
  • His Exploit on the grizzly turned up the session's first comedic letdown: "He has no weaknesses."

Panacea · the Steady Hand

  • The party's sole healer, holding three fights together without backup — kept Bai Jian topped across the bear, the sloths, and the cave (ranged Heal, Life Boost fast-healing, downtime Battle Medicine).
  • In the pitch-black Stagfall cave — once Sammy's Light let everyone see — she waded in and shredded an Insidiator with silver Needle Darts (10, a clean kill).
  • It was her tactical read that put the crystal in the tank's hands in the first place ("if they place it and something comes out, they'll be right there").
  • Navigated the Foundry learning curve as a first-time player with patience and a dry wit — sorting out her witch's trained skills, her detect-magic limits, and her familiar mid-fight — ribbing the tank even as she healed him: "Never too much healing, especially with you. You keep running into stuff."
  • And she warmed visibly to the team's animals: "Ren's growing on me."

Zanthar Melodune III · the Generalist (debut)

  • Wrote his own introduction: a small, white-bearded gnome mage with a hammer that "doesn't look like it makes much sense," "a rebel without a clue, looking for some cause to fight for" who's latched onto the Hellbreakers — a jack-of-everything ("a generalist in my spellcasting... a little bit of fire and a little bit of everything," plus a Blessed One's "little lay on hands").
  • His best newcomer beat was pure bewilderment, walking up on a band of armed strangers in the deep woods: "Why are we in the woods?"
  • Pulled his weight fast — a Hand of the Apprentice crit on a moss-sloth (hurling that oversized hammer, after Bai Jian's Snagging Strike set the target off-guard), a guidance-buffed occultism check that helped close the Hungry Hole, and an Electric Arc into the Insidiator cluster.
  • Refreshingly modest about it, too: "I wouldn't call myself well versed yet — still just starting out, like the rest of y'all."

Sammy Shadowfoot · the Frying-Pan Bard (debut)

  • Introduced herself with the detail that stuck — a musical instrument always on hand and "her trusty frying pan" — and a backstory of "run-ins with the Chelaxian Guard" that pushed her toward the Hellbreakers.
  • She hailed the party first, testing them with the Hellbreaker passphrase: "Are you friends of Rose?" Her biggest hard contribution came in the Stagfall cave — the party was fighting blind until Sammy cast Light and let everyone see the devils.
  • At the Moss Pond, Sammy disabled the corrupted trap with an occultism check — though by then her Wi-Fi had cut out, so the vivid version (a trance, the lute, a spin and a dance, a sung Courageous Anthem — "You can do it!") was the DM narrating her turn in her absence.
  • An honest note: her connection dropped repeatedly all night and the DM ran much of her combat; her player genuinely spoke only a handful of lines. But the halfling with the lute and the frying pan still made a first impression, and the table clearly wanted her in it.
