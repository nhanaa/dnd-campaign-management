## Session Summary

Two new faces fell in with the party on the goblin trails this session — **Zanthar Melodune III**, a small, white-bearded gnome wizard with a hammer too big for him and no real plan ("a rebel without a clue"), and **Sammy Shadowfoot**, a halfling bard with a lute, a trusty frying pan, and a grudge against the Chelaxian Guard. Both had been sent east to Swiftrun, stumbled onto the same lead the party was chasing — a false Hell Knight named **Vero Ahala** gathering devils for a ritual in the Chitterwood — and decided the cause was worth a detour. Back at Thorngrip the ancestor-speaker **Bracknap** welcomed the whole group as true friends of the town, opened the gate to them for good, and laid out the job: Vero's ritual has clouded him from the ancestors' sight, but the woods themselves can find him. He handed over a cloudy **Crystal Pendant** and a map to three sacred "sites of subtle power" — each one corrupted by Vero's working, each one now dangerous — and asked the party to carry the crystal to all three so it could read the disturbance and point the way.

Before they left, Bai Jian made a quiet trade at the goblin smithy: he gave up his **jian** — the blade he'd carried for years — for a **falcata** better suited to the cause, asking only that the smith take care of the old sword. Then it was out into the forest. The **Little Tree** site woke a corrupted complex trap and an enraged grizzly bear; Bai Jian tanked and killed the bear while the party pried hellfire fingers off the tree's roots. The **Moss Pond** rippled open into a planar trap with two regenerating moss-sloths from the Plane of Wood; Cyrathul read their fire weakness, Sammy danced the trap quiet with her lute, and the party ground the sloths back into moss. The **Hungry Hole** — an old goblin wizard's botched spacious-pouch turned into a hungry extradimensional mouth — tried to inhale Bai Jian and Cyrathul off the edge, but a few clean occultism checks peeled the corruption away. At each site the crystal drank in a thread of magic and glowed warmer and greener.

They brought it back to Bracknap, who held it, went pale, and saw it: **"The caves! The Hell-Human Vero is in the caves!"** The caves are **Stagfall**, named for the grandmother whose stag-shaped sacrifice once led human hunters away from his tribe — and now defiled by Vero's ritual. Bracknap marked the hidden cave on the map, and the tribe's elders gifted Bai Jian a **rune of striking** for his new blade. The party found the cave behind a grove that looked like a stag's antlers, stepped into the dark, and hit three web-spitting devils — **Insidiators**. Fighting half-blind until Sammy cast Light, they cut the devils down one by one, Bai Jian capping it with a 60-damage falcata crit that took the last devil's head clean off and sent it rolling out the cave mouth like a bowling ball. Past a hastily-piled rubble wall, a circular cavern opened with four tunnels, a shattered statue of an armored figure, and — coiled on a pile of rocks — a slithering snake-like creature. The session ended there, on the threshold of Vero's lair.

## Key Events

### Act I — Two New Longshanks (00:00–18:00)

- Table session zero for the two newcomers. **Kaazz** (DM) introduced himself; the three returning players re-introduced; Bai Jian recapped Session 7 (Umok, the Hell Hounds and Rama's death, the Orts, the Iskari Slayers, Thorngrip).
- On the trail back to Thorngrip the party met **Zanthar Melodune III** (gnome wizard) and **Sammy Shadowfoot** (halfling bard) traveling together — introduced organically in-fiction. Sammy hailed them with the Hellbreaker passphrase: *"Are you friends of Rose?"*
- The two had been dispatched **east to Swiftrun**, ran into trouble, and learned that **Vero Ahala** is gathering Hell Knights for a ritual somewhere in the Chitterwood. Shared goal → the party folded them in, **Panacea** vouching for the link: *"The trust goes up now for me. We're looking for the same person."*

### Act II — Thorngrip, the Smithy, and the Crystal (18:00–43:00)

- Back through the thorn wall into **Thorngrip**. Bracknap, ghostly ancestors flickering at his back, named the party **friends of Thorngrip** — *"Our home is your home"* — and Zagnub showed them the hidden lever so they could come and go freely. The goblins now look on the party with open admiration.
- **The jian trade.** Bai Jian visited **Gug**, the gruff goblin smith, and traded in his longtime **jian** for a **falcata**, asking to transfer his +1 potency rune. A genuine farewell beat: *"Please take care of that sword. It meant a lot to me."* (Thorngrip stocks up to level-3 gear but trades in goods, not gold.)
- **Bracknap's quest.** The ancestor-speaker explained Vero's ritual has *"obscured him from our ancestors' sight,"* but three nearby **sites of subtle power** — disturbed by the ritual — can reveal him. He handed over the **Crystal Pendant**: carry it to all three sites and it will let him see Vero's location. The three sites: the **Hungry Hole** (a bottomless pit), **Little Tree** (a sapling that never grows or dies, fed by magic), and **Moss Pond** (a rippling patch of moss tied to the Chitterwood's essence).
- **Panacea** reasoned a fighter should carry it — *"if they place it and something comes out of whatever it is, they'll be right there"* — and the party agreed **Bai Jian** (most HP, *"least chance of immediate death"*) would be the bearer. Overnight rest while Gug worked the rune; the falcata was waiting at the door in the morning. Quiet downtime shopping (Ren's barding, rations).

### Act III — The Little Tree & the Grizzly Bear (43:00–86:00)

- Half an hour southeast to a small, vibrant **sapling** radiating peace — but the peace was wrong. The tree pulsed **big-and-small** in a magical flux, washing nearby PCs with Will saves (Slowed / Off-Guard, a shrinking effect). It was a **complex trap**: hellfire "fingers" had reached through a planar crack and gripped the tree's roots, draining its magic.
- The flux **woke a giant grizzly bear** behind the tree — enraged, it locked onto Bai Jian and charged.
- The party split focus between disarming the trap (Nature/Occultism checks) and fighting the bear. **Sammy**, brand new and partly fighting through a laggy connection, dropped a **Courageous Anthem** to buff the team. The tree even shrank Sammy down to pixie size for a round (*"Honey, I shrunk the kids"*).
- **Bai Jian** tanked the bear, **Dueling Parry** up, and cut it down with two crits (see Combat). He then **disarmed the final trap component himself** — spotting a smoldering ember-coal at the tree's base and stamping it out (*"that will be a success — the tree goes back to its normal little shape"*).
- With the corruption gone, the tree radiated peace again and "thanked" the party psychically. Bai Jian held out the crystal: an invisible tendril of magic drifted from the tree into it, and it **glowed warm**. The tree spat up a **spacious pouch** and **rare mushrooms** in gratitude. Bai Jian skinned the bear for a pelt. **+30 XP** for earning Bracknap's trust, plus the site XP.

### Act IV — The Moss Pond (86:00–127:00)

- An hour-and-a-half west to a **large patch of undulating moss** "floating" with no water beneath it. Bai Jian, learning from last time, scouted ahead alone with the crystal (*"let me approach it, because I have decent save"*) — and the moss **speared spikes up** at him and Ren the moment they reached the center (piercing + poison; both made their saves with a hero point).
- A planar shimmer — the **Plane of Wood** converging — and **two moss-sloths** sculpted of living moss appeared. Like the tree, the moss itself was a **corrupted complex trap**, the Nine Hells reaching tendrils into a place tied to the plane of wood.
- **Cyrathul** read the sloths with Recall Knowledge / Exploit Vulnerability: **from the Plane of Wood, weak to fire, and they regenerate each turn unless they take fire damage.** His Exploit triggered the fire weakness on his bite/claw.
- **Sammy** — by this point DM-operated, her Wi-Fi having dropped — **disabled the moss trap** with an occultism check; the DM narrated her turn (a trance, a few bars on the lute, a spin and a dance, and the whole field going calm), then voiced her Courageous Anthem as she sang the party up: *"You can do it!"*
- **Bai Jian** Snagging-Struck the sloths off-guard (*"Off-guard, baby"*) and ground them down with his falcata — normal hits of **12, 16, and 11** (each lifted by the weakness Cyrathul had exploited; no crit of his own this fight). A moss spear later **crit *him*** on a nat 20 for **18**, which is what had him calling *"I need some healing, guys."* On the off-guard target, **Zanthar** crit with **Hand of the Apprentice**, hurling his oversized hammer. **Panacea** kept Bai Jian topped (ranged Heal, **Life Boost** fast healing) and chipped in with Needle Darts. The sloths "melted back into moss."
- Crystal out again: it drank the moss pond's magic and turned **bright vibrant green**. Loot from the dissolving sloths: a **smuggler's padlock** and consumables.

### Act V — The Hungry Hole (127:00–151:00)

- Two hours east to a perfect **40-foot circle of barren dirt** around a plain hole — no grass, no encroaching branches, an eerie inhaling sound. As Bai Jian and Cyrathul approached, an unseen force tried to **drag them toward the edge** (Fortitude saves). Another corrupted site — this one with **no creatures**, just the hazard.
- **Cyrathul** Recall-Knowledge'd it: long ago a **goblin wizard** tried to make his own spacious pouch out here and instead tore open a **hole to an extradimensional space** — now warped by Vero's ritual into a hungry mouth.
- The party disarmed it cleanly with occultism — **Cyrathul** slapped a hellfire hand away, **Zanthar** (guidance up, hero-pointed) peeled the last fingers off and ended the encounter. Bai Jian, dragged right to the lip, stepped back rather than test the legend he'd joked about back in Thorngrip — that a goblin who falls through comes out the other side of the world (*"maybe if I fall into the hole, I can just come back home,"* he'd grinned at Bracknap, needling his own faraway Tian Xia origin).
- The hole belched up a **low-grade talisman** and **20 gold**. Crystal out one last time: it went **crystal-clear and vibrant green**, glowing with warm radiant energy. **All three sites done.**

### Act VI — Bracknap's Warning & Stagfall (151:00–164:00)

- Back to Thorngrip. Bracknap held the charged crystal, his eyes flashing from calm blue to **alarmed red**: *"The caves! The Hell-Human Vero is in the caves!"*
- **The Stagfall lore.** Before Thorngrip existed, his tribe was hunted by humans at the end of the Goblinblood Wars. They hid in a series of caves — but his **grandmother used the power of the woods to transform into a mighty stag** and led the hunters away, dying so the rest could escape. The survivors found the great tree, raised the thorn wall, and named the caves **Stagfall** in her honor. Now Vero defiles them. Bracknap, *"trembling with anger,"* sent the party to stop him.
- He marked the hidden cave on the map (behind a grove of stag-antler trees) and told them the crystal **still has power left** — *"it will be very useful."* The tribe's **elders gifted a rune of striking**, which **Gug applied to Bai Jian's falcata** (6 gp 5 sp). Overnight rest and a goblin grog send-off.

### Act VII — Into the Caves: The Insidiators (164:00–190:00)

- Three hours to a rocky hill whose trees, at the right angle, look like a **stag's antlers** over black, gnarled brush. A small cave hid behind a tall tree.
- The party crept in. The chamber was **dark** — Bai Jian has no darkvision — and **three devils (Insidiators)** were waiting in the corner, hidden. (Initiative: Cyrathul · Bai Jian · Insidiator · Insidiator · Sammy · Zanthar · Insidiator · Panacea.)
- **Cyrathul** opened with a crit Exploit Vulnerability and read the block: **resist all physical except silver, resist poison, immune to fire, weak to holy** — the read that defined the fight. He bit through the weakness for 10.
- An Insidiator cast **Bane** on the party (−1 to attacks — it's why Bai Jian's later crit shows +12 instead of his usual +13) and spat **immobilizing web at Cyrathul**, sticking the dragon to the floor; a second web spat at Bai Jian **missed** ("goes clear over your head"). Fighting blind, Bai Jian swung in the dark and found nothing.
- **Sammy** cast **Light** so the party could finally see. **Panacea** then moved in and blew an Insidiator apart with **silver Needle Darts** (10 dmg — *"foo, foo, foo, all come out the back"*), a kill. **Zanthar** Electric-Arced the cluster (both saved → 3 each). **Cyrathul** Mirror'd, off-guarded a second devil, and tore it apart with chomp + claw (10 + 7 = 17 — *"crumples like paper"*), a kill.
- **Bai Jian** closed and **critted the last Insidiator**: his falcata's base dice came up max (*"Double 12"*) for **63**, leaving **60** after the devils' physical resistance — enough to trigger the **massive-damage** rule for an instant kill. *"I just do a spin and just cut his head clean off."* The head rolled out the cave mouth past the newcomers' feet.
- The Bane-bearer was the devil Bai Jian obliterated, so Bane dropped. Three devils, all turned to ash.
- Past a **hastily-piled rubble wall** (Ren "showed them how to dig"; ~25 minutes), a roughly circular cavern with **four tunnels**, a **moss alcove**, a **shattered stone statue of an armored figure**, neatly-stacked rocks — and on a pile of rocks, a **slithering snake-like creature**. **Cliffhanger.**

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the White Blade, Reforged

Pax's session — the tank, the crystal-bearer, and the closer. Bai Jian volunteered to carry and place the **Crystal Pendant** at every site ("least chance of immediate death," most HP) and made himself the point man at each hazard — *"let me approach it, because I have decent save"* — eating the bear's charge, the moss spikes, and the Hungry Hole's pull so the squishier members didn't have to. He **solo-killed the grizzly bear** (Dueling Parry, two crits, self-Battle-Medicine afterward "because I never get to use it") and personally **disarmed the Little Tree's last trap component** by stamping out the corrupting ember at its roots.

But the session's quiet heart was the **jian trade**. After years carrying the "gentleman of weapons," Bai Jian gave it to Gug for a **falcata** — a one-handed fatal blade that fits his free-hand Dueling build — and asked only that the smith *"take care of that sword. It meant a lot to me."* The trade paid off immediately: Bracknap's elders gifted a **rune of striking**, and in the Stagfall caves the new +1 striking falcata crit on the last Insidiator with its base dice maxed (*"Double 12"*) for **63 — 60 after the devils' resistance** — enough to trigger the massive-damage rule for an instant decapitation, the head rolling out the cave like a bowling ball. *"I just do a spin and just cut his head clean off."* He also spent real table-time **mentoring the two newcomers** through their first turns.

### Cyrathul · the Dragon's Eye

The party's knowledge engine, and he carried it at every site. Cyrathul did the lore lifting that solved each puzzle: he read the **moss-sloths** (Plane of Wood, weak to fire, regenerating), the **Hungry Hole** (an old goblin wizard's spacious-pouch gone wrong), and — most decisively — the **Insidiators** (resist all physical except silver, resist poison, immune to fire, weak to holy), the single read that shaped the whole cave fight. His Exploit Vulnerability and Mirror Cloak chomp-and-claw closed out a moss-sloth and a cave devil. Earlier, before any fighting, he'd flagged the party's one real fragility — invoking Rama's death from a failed treatment check to make sure someone could heal after a battle: *"that could be a real problem when you go out after a battle and no one knows how to heal the wounds."*

He supplied his trademark deadpan throughout: offered to scout the Hungry Hole because he *"can arrest fall as well,"* and when there was nothing useful left to do at the Little Tree, simply announced he would *"stand there menacingly."* His Exploit Vulnerability on the grizzly turned up the session's first comedic letdown: *"He has no weaknesses."*

### Panacea · the Steady Hand

The party's sole healer, holding three fights together without a backup. Panacea kept **Bai Jian topped** across the bear, the sloths, and the cave (ranged Heal, **Life Boost** fast-healing, downtime Battle Medicine), ribbing the tank even as she patched him: *"Never too much healing, especially with you. You keep running into stuff."* In the pitch-black Stagfall cave — once Sammy's Light let everyone see — she waded in and **shredded an Insidiator with silver Needle Darts** (10 damage, a clean kill). It was also Panacea's tactical read that put the crystal in the tank's hands in the first place (*"if they place it and something comes out, they'll be right there"*). She navigated the Foundry learning curve as a first-time player with patience and a dry wit — sorting out her witch's actual trained skills, her detect-magic limits, and her familiar mid-fight — and warmed visibly to the team's animals along the way: *"Ren's growing on me."*

### Zanthar Melodune III · the Generalist (debut)

Scott Smith's gnome wizard wrote his own introduction: a small, white-bearded mage with a hammer that *"doesn't look like it makes much sense,"* *"a rebel without a clue, looking for some cause to fight for"* who's latched onto the Hellbreakers. He calls himself a jack-of-everything — *"a generalist in my spellcasting... a little bit of fire and a little bit of everything,"* even a dash of healing (a Blessed One's *"little lay on hands"*). His best newcomer beat was pure bewilderment, walking up on a group of armed strangers in the deep woods: *"Why are we in the woods?"* Mechanically he pulled his weight fast — a **Hand of the Apprentice crit** on a moss-sloth (hurling that oversized hammer, after Bai Jian's Snagging Strike set the target off-guard), a guidance-buffed occultism check that helped close the Hungry Hole, and an **Electric Arc** into the Insidiator cluster. Refreshingly modest about it, too: *"I wouldn't call myself well versed yet — still just starting out, like the rest of y'all."*

### Sammy Shadowfoot · the Frying-Pan Bard (debut)

fallernon's halfling bard introduced herself with the detail that stuck — a musical instrument always on hand and *"her trusty frying pan"* — and a backstory of *"run-ins with the Chelaxian Guard"* that pushed her toward the Hellbreakers. She hailed the party first, testing them with the Hellbreaker passphrase: *"Are you friends of Rose?"* Her biggest hard contribution came in the Stagfall cave, where the party was fighting blind in the dark until Sammy cast **Light** and let everyone finally see the devils. And at the **Moss Pond**, Sammy **disabled the corrupted trap** with an occultism check — though by then her Wi-Fi had cut out, so the vivid version (a trance, a few bars on the lute, a spin and a dance, and a sung Courageous Anthem, *"You can do it!"*) was the **DM narrating her turn** in her absence. An honest note: her connection dropped repeatedly all night and the DM ran much of her combat; her player genuinely spoke only a handful of lines. But the halfling with the lute and the trusty frying pan still made a first impression, and the table clearly wanted her in it.
