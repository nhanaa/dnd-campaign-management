## Session Summary

No combat for the first two hours — and nobody minded. The party woke inside the Elidir safehouse they'd reached at midnight, and met the people they'd crossed a locked-down capital to find: **Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias**, the Hellbreakers' co-founders, now burned, identified, and handing the whole Elidir chapter off to the PCs. The job comes down to one meeting: a Druman merchant named **Quoster** wants proof the League is worth backing, and he'll take that meeting at the New Year's Ball in a week. Get in, close the deal. First, though — three errands: forged **invitations** (from a red-skinned tax clerk who can't be seen helping them), gala **costumes** (the ball is devil-themed, of course), and a **Lazy Bones pendant** that's the recognition token for Quoster.

So the session became a day of walking a festival-day capital and doing the small, decent things that build an uprising. They scooped a legless dwarf out of the path of a runaway carriage. They talked a devilishly enthusiastic four-foot tailor into disguises — Bai Jian as a Chelaxian Hellknight paralictor, Panacea in a blood-dripping devil gown, Zanthar as a little Asmodeus, Cyrathul as a literal chain devil (with wings that matched his own), and **Ren in devil warhound barding**. They conned a nervous Nephilim clerk into a private office and walked out with the invitations. They calmed a runaway **Daeodon** a cruel handler had loosed at a pack of noble brats. Everywhere they went the city was a little kinder than it should be — *"I hear there's an uprising going on"* — and the Uprising points piled up.

The pendant was the hitch. At the **Love Sting Printing House** — a risqué-novel press whose owner writes Phoebe's favorite books — a fire-throwing goblin and a smoke-shrouded caster were beating the elf owner and torching the shop over "Hellbreaker material." The party put both down non-lethally and bucketed out the fire with barrels of printer's ink, but the pendant was already gone: stolen mid-fight, a **blue ribbon** left in its place. Zanthar recognized the calling card — the **nascent thieves' guild** that fronts as an orphanage, the "Old House of the Found Family," run by a figure called **the father**. A night of recon, a morning report to the Demetriases, and the party went in the back door to get the pendant back. The two "orphans" in the kitchen turned out to be genuinely dangerous rogues — no weaknesses, hard to pin, and *terrified*, screaming that their home was under siege. The party took them alive anyway, but one spat that *"the father will have your throat"* and wouldn't talk. Heading upstairs to find the father, they tripped a spring-loaded net and heard the twang of crossbows. **Session ends** at the top of the stairs, staring down a surprise volley.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Safehouse & the Founders (00:00–24:00)

- **Sarevial** got the party the last ten blocks to a nondescript mansion, ushered them inside near midnight, and went to wake the house. The party healed up and waited.
- They were led into a chapel to meet **Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias**, the Hellbreakers' co-founders — barefoot, half-dressed, roused from bed. *"Your exploits have reigned throughout the South."*
- **The situation**: Alceo and Phoebe spent a month stirring discontent against Cheliax's puppet elites, but **Hedvind** cracked down on the protests, used his army against the people to frame the League as rioters, and **identified the founders** — their faces are circulated. They're handing Elidir operations to the party.
- **The mission**: they were about to close a deal with a Druman merchant, **Quoster**, who'll supply the League in exchange for post-liberation trade rights — but Hedvind's exposure spooked him. Quoster now wants *proof*, and will take the meeting at the **New Year's Ball** (a week out). The party negotiates in the founders' place.
- **Three errands** to be ball-ready: (1) **forged invitations** from **Oramos**, a Hellspawn Nephilim clerk at the city tax office (approach subtly — he can't be seen helping); (2) **costumes** from **Rasumili Brightgate** at *Dress to Impress* (Phoebe already arranged it); (3) the **Lazy Bones pendant** at the **Love Sting Printing House** — the recognition token so Quoster can ID them at the ball without anyone saying "Hellbreaker" aloud.
- Alceo laid **four keys** on the table — full run of the safehouse — and warned that the city is *one bad fight from full lockdown*: keep the bloodshed and noise down. Taking stock of last session's haul, the party realized what they were actually carrying: a **dead Toady's body in the spacious pouch** — while the constable **Adiutor**, only knocked out, had been *left behind at Isger's Circle to wake up* (a loose witness, not a prisoner). Food, wine, and the first real beds in weeks.

### Act II — A Player Departs (21:00–24:00)

- Over breakfast, Sarevial quietly pulled **Sammy** aside; she thanked the group and left on "a different mission." *(Out of character: Sammy's player stepped away from the campaign so as not to keep taking a seat while missing sessions.)* Bai Jian: *"Bye, Sammy… see you again."*
- The DM recapped the new party composition — wizard, fighter, witch-medic, and "the dragon fighter" (Cyrathul) — *"We'll do pretty good."*

### Act III — Small Kindnesses on Fire Day (24:00–48:00)

- The streets were packed for **solstice ("fire day")** — coffee, fresh pastries, last-minute wine and cheese, the tower of **Isger's Fist** (the military fortress) looming over a changing of the guard.
- **The runaway carriage**: a **legless dwarf** on two knee-pigs slipped on the ice, one prosthetic leg popping off, right in the path of a speeding carriage. **Bai Jian sprinted and scooped him clear** (Athletics); **Panacea retrieved and returned his fallen leg**. The dwarf, near tears: *"I hear there's an uprising going on… everyone is being so much kinder."* **+30 XP and the party's first Uprising point of the day.**
- **Dress to Impress**: **Rasumili Brightgate**, a four-foot tailor bursting with enthusiasm, fitted the whole party for the devil-themed ball. He pitched **Bai Jian as a Chelaxian Hellknight paralictor** (his sword worn openly as part of the costume — solving the "how do I wear full plate to a ball" problem), **Panacea** in a deep-red satin gown with ragged, blood-dripping papier-mâché wings, **Zanthar** as a little **Asmodeus** in a silk tuxedo, and **Cyrathul** as a **chain devil** whose red wingset matched his real dragon wings. **Ren** was fitted in **devil warhound barding** (a horned dog-mask was tried first and rejected). Come back in **six days** (the 30th) for the finished costumes.

### Act IV — The Invitations (48:00–61:00)

- Across the bridge to the **Trade House** at the docks — barrels crashing, taxmen collecting, a merchant loudly refusing a tax hike (*"fine, this is the last time I'm bringing cargo to Elidir"*) and storming out.
- At the front desk sat **Oramos** — a tall, very-red-skinned man. **Bai Jian architected the cover** — pose as anxious newcomers with private tax questions so Oramos could step away from the public hall without looking like he was helping anyone — then handed the actual pitch to **Cyrathul** (Diplomacy) and **spent a hero point** to land it. Oramos "graciously" walked them to a private office.
- Behind the closed door, Panacea gave the sign — *"We were sent by Phoebe"* — and Oramos laid out **four forged invitations**. *"It's wonderful to be a friend of the Rose… and I assume you'll never mention this to anyone."* **+40 XP.** Panacea, on getting through a whole scene without a fight: *"It's just weird to not be fighting."*

### Act V — The Daeodon (61:00–83:00) — *De-escalation encounter*

- Past a **Gorumite veterans' hospital**, a pack of finely-dressed **noble teens** were pelting a laborer's enormous **Daeodon** (a giant boar hauling a cart) with rocks and dung — and the **handler deliberately loosed the beast at the kids** when it enraged, cackling.
- **Bai Jian** shouted at the handler to stop (Intimidation, +0 — *"that's not going to happen"*), then ran to put himself between the boar and the children. The boar raked its tusks up his leg.
- **Panacea appealed to the handler's holiday good nature** (Diplomacy — no luck: *"there's no talking to this guy"*), then **cast Calm** over the pair. **Calm carries the incapacitation trait**, and the boar *succeeded* its Will save — upgraded to a critical success, so it was **unaffected**: it stayed enraged and **gored Bai Jian's leg with its tusks**. The spell pacified only the **handler**, who went glassy and near-comatose. (The DM initially narrated the boar calming too, then re-read the roll and corrected it.)
- **Cyrathul talked the dazed handler down** (Diplomacy — deliberately *not* Intimidate, which would have snapped the Calm), and the man grabbed the reins; the boar recognized its master and finally settled. The teens scattered and fled. The handler tossed **four silver at the party's feet** — *"now we're even"* — and slunk off. XP awarded; Panacea Life-Boosted Bai Jian's tusk wound back to full over the break.

### Act VI — The Love Sting Printing House (92:00–128:00) — *Combat 1*

- Around the corner: a **bruised elf** crumpled in the snow outside a shop leaking smoke, pleading — *"They're just harmless entertainment!"* Inside, a **goblin was juggling fire and torching the place**, ranting that *"Hellbreaker material won't be tolerated"* and *"Elidir is a state of Cheliax."* A second, smoke-shrouded **caster (Odvolos)** lurked deeper in.
- **Panacea tried to defuse it first** — moving to Calm the goblin — but the visible spellcasting blew it: *"We got company!"* he shouted inside, and the fight was on. (That's why she then delayed her initiative behind Bai Jian.)
- Mindful of the lockdown, the party fought to **subdue**. **Zanthar opened by casting Fear on the goblin** (it made its save → Frightened 1); **Bai Jian's Slam Down** struck it (14) and **tripped it prone**, ending the fire-throwing; and **Cyrathul finished it with a non-lethal tail-whip** — *"a somersault flop… this huge tail comes around and knocks the goblin out."*
- **Odvolos** — the smoke-shrouded caster — was just out of Ignition range, so he closed and **Demoralized** Bai Jian instead (Frightened 1). **Bai Jian crit him with a Snagging Strike for 51** (falcata, fatal d12), leaving him **off-guard** — then deliberately *held back* the killing follow-up, taking the –2 non-lethal or waving it off rather than kill. (Odvolos proved surprisingly tanky — the 51 didn't drop him.)
- **Zanthar** hit Odvolos through the wall with **Phase Bolt** (missed, hero-point reroll, still missed), then **KO'd him with a non-lethal Hand of the Apprentice** hurled through the smoke.
- **The fire was the second enemy.** The party stayed in initiative and bucketed **printer's ink** from the shop's barrels onto the spreading flames — Bai Jian, Zanthar, and Panacea (via **Rousing Splash**) each dousing five-foot increments until it was out.
- The grateful elf handed over **two disguise face-paints**, a **rare gold-laced book**, and **romance novels for Phoebe** — and announced the next title would be **"Breaking Hell for Her,"** starring the party. Bai Jian: *"I would respectfully decline that offer."* **+2 Uprising points.** Then the catch: **the pendant was already stolen**, a **blue ribbon** pinned where it had been.

### Act VII — The Blue Ribbon (128:00–148:00) — *Investigation*

- **Zanthar recognized the calling card** (a crit Elidir/Society check): the **nascent thieves' guild** styling itself the **"Children's Charity,"** fronting as the **"Old House of the Found Family" orphanage** — and he knew exactly where it was. *"I had a criminal background. I wouldn't mind knowing personally."* Bai Jian: *"I'll pretend I didn't hear that."*
- Zanthar dumped **buckets of ink on the two knocked-out attackers** for the guard to collect — *"they're going to be blue for a really long time."*
- **Recon**: the party staked out the orphanage into the night. Panacea and Bai Jian couldn't get an angle in the exposed, run-down district, but **Zanthar** (in his element) clocked the **comings and goings** and confirmed a **back entrance** — people leaving by the front, returning around the back.
- Next morning, over Sarevial's meat pies, they briefed the **Demetriases**: invitations and costumes handled, but the pendant's with the guild. Alceo's read: the "orphans" are **adults now — highly trained thieves the father adopted**; and since they *left the ribbon on purpose*, they may well **sell the pendant back**. No secret words to offer. *"We can't even leave the house."*

### Act VIII — The Old House of the Found Family (148:00–187:48) — *Combat 2 (cliffhanger)*

- The party crossed town and went for the **unlocked back door** (the front was shuttered and no one answered a knock, though Bai Jian heard a floorboard squeak inside). Bai Jian pulled **Cyrathul up front** — *"we need our talker."*
- The kitchen held **two rogues** mid-snack; they dropped their food, locked eyes, and screamed **"Intruders!"** — playing the party, accurately, as home invaders. Bai Jian: *"We'll knock them out, and then we'll see what happens."*
- These were **no pushovers**: **Mobility**, **Deny Advantage** (immune to off-guard), and **no exploitable weaknesses** — **Cyrathul's Exploit Mortal Weakness came up empty** (*"quite some rogues"*). One loosed a single arrow at Bai Jian (5) as he came through the door, then both drew short swords and **cut Cyrathul up in melee** (~34 across the fight); **Panacea healed him 22**.
- **Zanthar opened with Blazing Bolt** — two rays, **15 then a crit 34** — *"the whole backside of him bursts into flames."* **Bai Jian** Snagging-Struck (15), **grappled** the rogue, and dropped them with a **hero-pointed, non-lethal Reactive Strike for 43** when they tried to draw a weapon in his reach.
- The second rogue fell to **Zanthar's non-lethal Hand of the Apprentice** (18, *"shatters into their collarbone"*) and **Bai Jian's finishing non-lethal strike** — Ren's **Support** benefit stacked with **Panacea's Guidance** for +15 to hit; flat-of-the-blade KO (13).
- The party **manacled and revived one** to question — but the moment his mouth was uncovered he screamed for his "family," then: *"It doesn't matter what you do to me. **The father will have your throat** for this intrusion."* Zanthar clubbed him back out — *"He's got a brain trauma. Seems to already have it."*
- Heading for the stairs, **Zanthar triggered a spring-loaded net trap** by a training dummy (**Reflex save — dodged, "flies over my head"**). Cautious now — *"let me and my dog go first"* — they crept up, and **heard crossbow clicks**: the guild upstairs gets a **surprise round**. **Session ends** at the top of the stairs. Panacea: *"We are far from stealthy, this group."*

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the Blade That Pulls Its Punches

Pax spent the session proving the party's heaviest hitter is also its most careful. In a city one loud fight from lockdown, Bai Jian ran the whole session on **non-lethal discipline** — Slam-Down-tripping the arsonist goblin so someone else could put it out gently, crit-Snagging Odvolos for a monstrous **51** and then *deliberately holding the follow-up* rather than kill, and closing the orphanage fight with a **hero-pointed non-lethal Reactive Strike (43)** and a flat-of-the-blade finisher. *"We'll knock them out, and then we'll see what happens"* was the through-line. He's a tank who leads with restraint.

He's also the party's designated everyman, and he *knows* it. Handed the talking at the tailor's, he immediately punted — *"Actually, why am I doing the talking? You guys do the talking. I gave up"* — and did it again at the tax office and the orphanage, pulling **Cyrathul** up as "our talker" every time a scene needed a mouth. But the *plan* was often his: the tax-office ruse to peel **Oramos** away from the public hall was Bai Jian's design, handed to Cyrathul to deliver. His agency shows in the doing, not the deals: he **scooped a legless dwarf out from under a carriage**, put himself between the Daeodon and a pack of kids and ate a tusk to the leg for it, and ran the whole action-economy engine — commanding **Ren** to block doorways and feed him Support bonuses, tracking his own frightened condition, reading Mobility and Deny Advantage off the rogues in real time. And he still lands the character button: as the crossbows came up on the stairs, he ribbed Zanthar for ending up on point — *"You were saying I should be the one going forward, and now you're the one…"* — while the gnome took the volley.

### Cyrathul · Our Talker (with a Tail)

Snowgeen was out, so the DM ran Cyrathul — third-person, and mostly quiet — but the dragon still owned two lanes the party can't fill without him. He's **the face**: the party literally routed every hard conversation through him, and he delivered, hero-pointing the **Diplomacy** that peeled **Oramos** away for the invitations and talking the dazed Daeodon handler down (pointedly choosing Diplomacy over his stronger Intimidate so he wouldn't shatter Panacea's Calm). *"We need our talker,"* Bai Jian said at the orphanage door, and meant it.

And he's the party's **non-lethal closer**. When the goblin arsonist was prone, Cyrathul strode in and *"somersault-flopped,"* whipping his huge tail around to knock it out cold — exactly the kind of subdue this lockdown mission wants. He read the orphanage rogues with **Exploit Mortal Weakness** (and honestly reported they had none — *"quite some rogues"*), took the brunt of their short-sword melee so squishier party members didn't have to, and got patched up by Panacea and bandaged by Zanthar after. His costume beat was pure Cyrathul, too: fitted as a **chain devil** whose false red wings lined up perfectly over his real dragon wings — a monster wearing a costume of itself.

### Panacea · The Healer, Not the Shit Starter

Bog Witch had the session's best line and its clearest conscience. Told to open the printing-house fight, she balked in the most in-character way possible: *"I don't like being the first one. **I'm the healer, not the shit starter.**"* — and delayed her initiative to sit *behind* Bai Jian so she could do her actual job. That's the whole character in two sentences: she is the party's moral center and its safety net, and she knows exactly which one she is.

Her agency is de-escalation. She's the reason the Daeodon scene didn't end in a slaughtered animal or trampled kids — first **appealing to the handler's holiday decency** (*"his boar is going toward some kids and it's going to end badly for both of them"*), then **Calming** the beast when talk failed, then wishing the sheepish handler a *"blessed solstice"* on his way out. She **returned the legless dwarf's fallen leg** without being asked, drove the "keep them alive so we can question them" logic through both fights (*"I don't want to kill him — that's my whole point"*), and kept steering the party back to the Demetriases for intel and next moves — she's the quiet planner as much as the healer. There's a dry streak under the conscience, too: told how readily Zanthar knew the thieves'-guild calling card, she just shrugged — *"Aren't we all criminals?"* And she closed the session with the group's honest self-assessment: *"We are far from stealthy, this group."* Mechanically she was the anchor — Bless, Guidance, a clutch **22-point Heal** on a bleeding Cyrathul, Rousing-Splash fire suppression, and Life Boost cleanup — but the spotlight is that she plays a healer who'd rather solve the fight than start it.

### Zanthar Melodune III · The Rebel With a Rap Sheet

Scott's gnome quietly ran the utility belt again — and let a little backstory slip. He's the party's **artillery and its brain**: a **Blazing Bolt crit (34)** that set a rogue's back on fire, **Phase Bolt** through a solid wall, and two clean **non-lethal Hand of the Apprentice** KOs (hurling his hammer to shatter a collarbone or drop a fleeing caster — *"a non-lethal spell,"* every time, mission-discipline intact). He's also the one who **cracked the mystery**: a crit knowledge check IDed the blue ribbon as the nascent thieves' guild *and* pinpointed the orphanage, and he ran the successful **recon** that found the back door.

The character beat was the reveal. Recognizing a thieves'-guild calling card a little *too* readily, he shrugged: *"I had a criminal background. I wouldn't mind knowing personally"* — earning Bai Jian's deadpan *"I'll pretend I didn't hear that."* He clubbed the uncooperative captive back into unconsciousness and diagnosed it with a shrug — *"He's got a brain trauma. Seems to already have it."* — dumped ink on the downed arsonists so they'd be marked blue for the guard, and, right after Panacea admitted the party was hopeless at stealth, offered the perfect two-word rebuttal: *"I can be stealthy."* Then he rounded the orphanage's upstairs corner first, straight into the guild's crossbows — *"I'm drawing fire for y'all."* Utility, comedy, a criminal past he's only half-hiding, and the guy who takes the first volley.
