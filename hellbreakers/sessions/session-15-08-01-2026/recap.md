## Session Summary

The murder night ended tonight, and the party solved it the way good detectives do — they stopped chasing testimony and built a test. Cyrathul had the insight early, staring at fingertip marks in windowsill snow: *undead take damage from healing magic.* Gather every living soul in the manor into one room, cast a three-action Heal over the crowd, and whoever flinches is the killer. It took an hour of shepherding terrified nobles, a berserk steam-bull, and a false confession to pull off — and when Panacea's Heal washed over the assembled guests and every last one of them just felt better, the elimination was complete. Every guest in the room was clean, and the one guest who'd slipped away before the test was the big man. **Ezio Gaeta.** The gossip, the influence target, the "prime suspect too convenient to trust" — he was exactly what he looked like. The garlic was real all along.

The misdirection ran deep before it broke. A tailless servant, cracked by Zanthar's blunt "why'd you lose your tail?", confessed that a tall figure in a red cloak with burning eyes made her dig mausoleum dirt and scatter it on Hedvend's bed — and then her memory wiped itself mid-interrogation, just like Lefranek's. Lezinia the bard diva suddenly screamed a confession to the whole ballroom — *he promised to make me queen of Isger* — and bolted out the front door, straight into the manor's own construct sentry, which chased her back inside and trampled half the party. Zanthar's Lay on Hands proved she was living; a red cloak planted in her travel bag proved someone was framing her; and Cyrathul called the whole shape of it: *"I think that the red cloak is a red herring."* Even Fessy Finwinkle's locked door turned out to be a booby trap of sorts — Lezinia had handed her a summoning scroll "for protection," and the terrified priestess had accidentally summoned a light-ray-spitting guardian of holy books that the party had to put down before it put Cyrathul down.

Then Cyrathul opened the storage closet, and Hedvend VI — the murdered steward whose body had been missing all night — was standing inside it with fangs. The fight that followed was the real thing: Hedvend paralyzed Cyrathul and drank from his neck while Ezio stalked in from the far end of the hall — *"You just couldn't help yourselves"* — and clamped Bai Jian in a vice-grip claw. Bai Jian, locked in Ezio's grip, declined to escape — *"I don't even gonna bother to escape, so I'm just gonna swing"* — and put his silver falcata through Hedvend twice, ending the steward for good. Ezio made him pay for it a round later, fangs in his neck, draining him to Drained 2. The party ground Ezio down through fast healing and physical resistance until Panacea, the healer he'd gossiped with all gala, raised her hand: *"As I throw this — I thought we were friends. I thought we were friends, darling."* The Moonlight Ray took him apart in silver-cold holy light. He burst into blood, goo, and flies — and per the roll log, that death-burst was a **Cloud of Plague**, and **Ren failed the save**. That detail matters enormously (see Open Threads).

Paralictor Caeto Vulaunex poked the remains with a letter opener — *"The dandy? The disgusting fat blob was a vampire?"* — lifted the lockdown, declared himself **acting steward of Isger**, and armed the party with a sword and shield off his office wall. The manor emptied like a concert letting out. The party walked back to the safehouse through the blizzard at **Level 6** — having killed two vampires, cleared the Hellbreakers of a regicide, and put a Hellknight in charge of the country.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Aleigator in the Bushes
- Panacea delivered the last-session recap (bonus hero point); the GM ran lines-and-veils for the table's **new player**.
- Outside by the mausoleum, Panacea and Bai Jian spotted someone hiding behind a bush in the blizzard: **a very tall lizardfolk in priestly gear, drinking, no weapons** — the new PC introducing himself. Bai Jian, deadpan: *"Look, we found the murderer."*
- **Zarok Brewhide** explained himself: he was delivering ale to "the breakers" for a sender whose *"unique name"* he couldn't remember — *"I don't know which ones they are, but they broke something"* — and it was **Alceo and Phoebe** who put him in a devil costume and pointed him at the manor. His read on the org chart: *"They broke hell and now we gotta fix it."* — *"My name is Zarok Brewhide, but everybody just calls me the Aleigator."*
- Bai Jian welcomed him to the team; the party walked their new cleric back through the whiteout toward the manor, lit up "like a Christmas tree."

### Act II — The Tailless Servant
- Cyrathul set the objective — find the servant whose imp-costume tail matched the torn-off tail by the mausoleum: *"that's likely our vampire."* Zarok, immediately invested in tail matters: *"How'd he lose his tail? Did it just fall off?"*
- Cyrathul's Gather Information (success, 21) turned her up fast — a servant came down from upstairs **missing her costume tail**. Cyrathul's opening move was pure genre-awareness: *"They look like they've been hurt. You should heal them"* — a covert heal-test, gesturing at Panacea.
- Zanthar skipped the subtlety: *"Why'd you lose your tail?"* The servant checked her own costume, blurted that she was glad Hedvend was dead — *"Can we pretend I didn't say that?"* — and under Cyrathul's **critical Gather Information (31)** she broke: **a tall figure in a red cloak with burning eyes** told her to **dig dirt from the mausoleum and scatter it on Hedvend's body** where it lay in state. She wept that it felt like desecrating the dead.
- Cyrathul asked the question that mattered — *"How did you get past the defenses?"* — and got the mechanism: a **locket with the house stamp**. *"These let us go anywhere. The mechanicals just ignore us."* The servants can walk past the manor's kill-anything-living wards.
- When Zanthar pressed for the red-cloak's description, her face went quizzical — reaching for memories no longer there. Panacea: *"Oh no, it's wiping its memory. Just like the other guy we interrogated."* Zanthar's crit Recall Knowledge: this is **Rewrite Memory**-grade magic — and, on his own follow-up question, **vampires have domination abilities that can do exactly this**.

### Act III — The Fifth Victim, and the Plan
- A scream from the solarium: **another manor guard dead** — punctures, exsanguinated, sword still sheathed. Zarok's Medicine (33) ran his first forensics for the table; Zanthar found the window **unlocked from the inside** again; the perception sweep found **wet boot prints walking in from the window, over the body, and back out**.
- Bai Jian pushed the examination outward — check the sill, check below the window — and found **two fingertip indentations in the snow on the windowsill**, still filling in with fresh snowfall. The killer had braced a hand there minutes ago.
- Then Cyrathul assembled the session's central idea from first principles: *"We could, in theory, heal people, because if they take positive healing, they'll take it as damage… We can AoE heal. If people use three-action heal, that could hit the entire ballroom at once."* Bai Jian added the operational discipline: *"We also have to make sure that no one knows our intention. We need a valid excuse."*
- The party had the guards herd every guest into the ballroom — **Cyrathul's Diplomacy, boosted by Bai Jian's critical-success Aid (+2)**, carried it, with the guard relaying the stakes to the room: people are dying.

### Act IV — The False Confession *(and Encounter 1)*
- As the guards announced the round-up, **Lezinia** — the celebrity bard — cracked in front of the whole room: *"Fine. You caught me. I did it. I killed that miserable old man. He promised he would try to marry me and make me queen of Isger… I killed him, and I don't know what happened afterward."* She charged out the front door — and charged straight back in with a **berserk steam-powered automaton bull** on her heels. The manor's own outdoor sentry, doing exactly what the wards do to living things outside.
- Cyrathul, before initiative was even done: *"I have a theory that the memory that she killed him is planted, or she was dominated to do it."*
- The bull fight (see Combat Encounters) ended with the thing detonating in a 30-foot fireball that dropped Panacea dying — **Zarok's first three-action Heal got her back up**, with Zanthar's Lay on Hands topping her off — and the same Heal washed over the wounded bystanders. **60 XP.**
- Mid-triage, Cyrathul kept one eye on the room and caught it thinning: *"Hold on. Wait a second. Someone did slip out… the big boy."* **Ezio had left during the chaos** — noted a full hour before the sting confirmed what it meant.
- Upstairs in the boudoir, Lezinia repeated her confession word for word — then couldn't say *how* she'd killed him. Her eyes crossed grasping for the memory (Cyrathul's and Zanthar's Sense Motive both flagged that she wasn't acting normally). She improvised, badly: *"I had to drain the blood out of the bodies. I must be a vampire."* Zanthar tested her with **Lay on Hands — the healing healed her.** Living. Zarok's Detect Magic found **enchantment magic wafting off her**.
- Zanthar spotted red fabric in her travel bag: **a red cloak**, wrapped around a wire, a jar of oil, and her own bracelet — planted evidence she'd unknowingly bundled with her valuables. She let the party keep all of it.
- Cyrathul's Recall Knowledge (Diverse Lore) landed the thesis: Urgathoa's sacred colors are **red *and green*** — and this cloak had never been seen on anyone at the party. *"I think that the red cloak is a red herring… trying to frame someone very poorly. So more a distraction than a framing."* Calming her down and proving her innocent earned **30 XP**.

### Act V — The Sting
- After the break, the guards packed the ballroom. Zanthar told the grumbling nobles exactly why: *"You're picking off guests one at a time, so I want you all together."*
- Cyrathul clocked the gap before the test even ran: *"I think we're missing the big men."* Bai Jian's call: run it anyway — *"if everyone here is good, then maybe it's that guy, the guy that's not here."*
- Bai Jian staged the cover story — *"We have something to announce"* — and winked at Panacea and Zarok. **Panacea cast the three-action Heal over the entire crowd.** Scrapes sealed. Bruises faded. **Nobody flinched.**
- Everyone in that room was alive — so the elimination closed around the guest who wasn't in it. Cyrathul: *"So it's likely the big man is once again suspect number one."* **Ezio Gaeta.**

### Act VI — Fessy's Guardian *(Encounter 2)*
- Sweeping for Ezio, Cyrathul **hero-pointed a Seek** and spotted a window **cracked open again** — the killer was still moving through the house. Then the party remembered the door nobody had opened: **Fessy Finwinkle**, locked in the private dining room since mid-panic last session. Cyrathul peeked through the keyhole (Perception 20) — Fessy lying on the floor behind a table — and **Mirror Cloak'd through the locked door**. Frightened spellcasting answered him.
- The fight (see Combat Encounters) was against a **summoned guardian of holy books** — two spiraling rings of tomes with angel wings, spitting light rays — not against Fessy, who spent the whole fight curled in a ball apologizing. Zarok announced himself to the party in the most direct way possible: he **roundhouse-kicked the locked door off its hinges**.
- The source: **Lezinia had given Fessy a summoning scroll** and told her to hide upstairs and lock the door. The terrified priestess had read it when the door opened. She healed Cyrathul to full on the spot, apologized roughly thirty times, and gave the party her savings — a lesser Elixir of Life and a **Marvelous Miniature horse** (*"I've been saving this pony for a while, but you guys can have it"*).
- Zanthar lay-on-hands-tested Fessy too — living. The scroll: a summoning scroll. Another planted thread through Lezinia, the night's designated patsy.

### Act VII — The Steward in the Closet *(Encounter 3)*
- One room left unswept: the back storage closet. Cyrathul opened it — and **Hedvend VI, the murdered steward of all Isger, was standing inside**, fangs bared, an open window behind him. Panacea: *"There's the missing body!"* Cyrathul: *"I might have been a bit overzealous."*
- Mid-fight, **Ezio Gaeta appeared at the far end of the hall**: *"You just couldn't stay to yourselves. You just had to butt your noses right in, didn't you?"* Two vampires, one hallway (see Combat Encounters).
- **Bai Jian destroyed Hedvend** — locked in Ezio's grip, he skipped escaping and swung: a 39-damage silver falcata crit and a follow-up ended the steward permanently. **Panacea destroyed Ezio** — Moonlight Ray, holy and silver-cold, delivered with *"I thought we were friends, darling."* He exploded in blood, goo, and flies. **96 XP (Severe)** — plus a **120 XP bonus** for identifying the killer early and saving lives by gathering the guests.
- The table's post-mortem was half triumph, half indignation. Zanthar: *"And we influenced him. That's a waste. What was I wasting all my time on him for?"* Panacea: *"But that's how we knew, though, with the garlic."* The S12 influence work — Ezio refusing the garlic dish — was the clue that cracked the case; her hedged recollection from two sessions ago was right.

### Act VIII — The Hellknight Regent
- The party shook both vampires' remains out of the spacious pouch in front of **Caeto Vulaunex** — who objected, *"Oh, disgusting. Not on the desk"* — and poked the goo with a letter opener: *"The dandy? The… disgusting fat blob was a vampire?"*
- On Ezio's remains: an **encryption ring** whose runes match the vertebra wheel-cipher. Ezio wasn't a lone monster — he was a *correspondent* of whoever "Illcayna" is.
- Caeto flagged the piece that doesn't fit: vampires take **days** to rise, and Hedvend rose in hours. *"Some weird magic must have been at work here… it was no ordinary vampire."* The mausoleum dirt, the used decomposition talisman, and four bodies' worth of missing blood are presumably that magic — the ritual was never explained.
- Then the political earthquake, delivered casually: *"I will begrudgingly step up and be the temporary steward"* — pending word from *"Mother Cheliax"* on a permanent replacement. **The Hellknight Paralictor who deputized the party is now acting ruler of Isger**, promising *"a much better country… I look forward to a better Isger after tonight."*
- Rewards: a **sword and shield** off his office wall (unidentified). Bai Jian asked about coin; the coffers are "a bit light." Zanthar, genre-savvy to the end, made Caeto promise to **burn the drained victims** so nothing else rises. Caeto keyed the ward control box from red to green — **lockdown lifted**.
- The manor emptied "like a concert letting out." The party walked back to the safehouse through the blizzard. **Level 6.**

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Steam Bull (Aurocan) · 60 XP
*The manor's outdoor construct sentry, drawn inside when Lezinia bolted out the front door — "There's nobody driving it. It's a berserk robot." Immune to mental; weakness 10 to orichalcum — which Cyrathul's dragon jaws happen to be — and, as Zanthar's cantrips revealed, weakness 10 to electricity.*

**Initiative**: Aurocan 33 · Zarok 29 · Panacea 23 · Bai Jian 15 · Zanthar 15 (tie, GM ran Bai Jian first) · Cyrathul 14 · Lezinia 3

**Round 1**
- **Aurocan** charged through the front door and **Trampled four of the five PCs** (DC 24 Reflex — Zanthar was out of its line, "the only one that's safe"): Panacea, Cyrathul, and Zarok failed (17 damage each); Bai Jian saved via Bulwark (8). Lezinia spent the fight cowering behind Zarok: *"Please don't let that thing get me."*
- **Zarok** (first action of his career): **Warrior's Regret** — the bull saved; a 5-damage tick was applied and then retracted once the timing was read (the bull damaged nobody on its next turn, so the curse dealt 0).
- **Panacea**: Battle Medicine on Cyrathul (Assurance, 4 HP), then **Trade Death for Life** — the bull's Fortitude failed but its construct chassis shrugged the void damage; the GM let the hex's riders through anyway (fast healing 3 to Cyrathul, Serrafina's Stupefied 1 on the bull).
- **Bai Jian**: **Slam Down** hit (19 rolled, 14 taken through resistance) and the **Trip landed — huge bull, prone** (Titan Wrestler). Dueling Parry up. (The attempted Ren flank turned out to be geometrically invalid — discovered a round later, after it had quietly cost him two would-be crits.)
- **Zanthar**: Electric Arc (14 rolled; the construct took 17 — electricity weakness) — *"like Johnny Five getting zapped"* — then bounced a Demoralize off it and discovered the hard way that it's **immune to mental**: *"I learned something."*
- **Cyrathul**: Exploit Vulnerability (success) — **weakness 10, orichalcum** — then bit it for 13, which its frame took as **23**.

**Round 2**
- **Aurocan** stood (eating Bai Jian's Reactive Strike — 12 rolled, 7 taken), activated **Blistering Touch** (heating its frame, +1d6 fire on melee), and missed Bai Jian's horn-swipe by 4.
- **Zarok**: cast **Bless**, first declaring targets 5e-style — *"Are you thinking of 5E Blast?" "Yes. Yes, I am. Sorry."*
- **Panacea**: Needle Darts missed; Battle Medicine on herself (7).
- **Bai Jian**: second **Slam Down** (14 rolled/9 taken) and a **hero-pointed Trip reroll** (nat 1 → reroll 5, +10 house rule → 29, success) — **prone again**. From the trample onward, the bull never took a turn on its feet.
- **Zanthar**: Electric Arc again (12 rolled/16 taken).
- **Cyrathul**: **Scroll of Sure Strike → critical bite** (36 rolled, **46 taken** with weakness) — the bull died mid-chomp and **detonated**: **Explosive End**, 30-foot emanation, 36 damage, DC 21 basic Reflex. Bai Jian, Cyrathul, and Zarok saved (17 taken each); **Zanthar and Ren failed (36 each)** — and **Panacea dropped dying in the blast**. (Bookkeeping note from the log: her save was actually a **success by +4**, but the full 36 was applied instead of 17 — a correct 17 would have left her standing. Nobody caught it at the table.)

**Aftermath**: **Zarok's first three-action Heal (8) got Panacea up from dying** and washed over party and wounded bystanders alike; Zanthar's Lay on Hands (18) topped her off; treat-wounds triage followed. Kill credit: **Cyrathul** (who also ate the point-blank explosion for his trouble). The bull absorbed 132 damage across its two rounds.

### Encounter 2 — The Tome Guardian (Gnokesh) · 40 XP (Low)
*A summoned guardian — two spiraling rings of holy books on angel wings — accidentally called by the terrified Fessy Finwinkle from a scroll Lezinia gave her. Holy, divine; weak to unholy (5), which Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability turned his Strikes into.*

**Initiative**: Bai Jian 24 · Gnokesh 20 · Zarok 19 · Zanthar 18 · Cyrathul 18 · Panacea 11

**Round 1**
- **Bai Jian** delayed (couldn't see through the door).
- **Gnokesh** fired three **Light Rays** at Cyrathul: one hit (9 fire/spirit), two grazed his wings.
- **Zarok** **roundhouse-kicked the locked door open** (Athletics 23, two actions) and walked in.
- **Zanthar** cast **Fear** — crit success, unmoved.
- **Cyrathul** talked Fessy down (*"We're not here to hurt you"* — *"I swear I'm not sustaining it!"*), Exploited Vulnerability (weak to **unholy**), and bit it for 17 (**22 taken**).
- **Bai Jian** returned: Snagging Strike missed, second connected (13).
