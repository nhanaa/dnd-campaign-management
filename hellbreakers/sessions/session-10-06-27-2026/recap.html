Session Summary

The hero's hangover wore off fast. The party woke at Thorngrip the morning after the Stagfall victory, re-geared with Gug (Bai Jian splurging on a brand-new suit of full plate), and set off through the Chitterwood toward Saringallow as the first snow of the year began to fall. Three days of bad survival rolls and quiet, too-quiet forest later, the quiet broke: undead came crunching through the snow at the third night's camp — ghouls, a life-draining "white" wight, and a pair of slow zombie shamblers. Bai Jian, the only one who'd slept in his armor, met them in the dark while Panacea threw up Light; what followed was a one-sided slaughter (the DM "only rolling crits for the players"), with Bai Jian cleaving corpses in half, Ren ripping a zombie's leg clean out of the socket, Cyrathul biting a ghoul's head off, and Zanthar — who'd logged on mid-fight, half-asleep, and immediately fumbled his own hammer — closing it out with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice decapitation. The party hit level 4 (Ren matured right after).

Out of the woods (Ren finally allowed to lead the way by scent), two crit-success diplomacy stops swelled the Hellbreaker ranks, and they reached Saringallow to report in. The HQ scene paid off a season of work: Cyrathul tipped Viro Ahala's week-old corpse out of the spacious pouch at Rhokrag's feet (Marie ran for a barrel), and Rhokrag confirmed the kill — Viro was the apprentice-thief he'd been chasing when he was captured, the man gathering Isger's scattered Hell Knights, undone with a stolen suit of plate. Moob and Fang incinerated the body; the goblins flatly refused to ever leave HQ again. Then a new mission: Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias are pinned down in the capital, Elidir — too hot, too identified. Rhokrag is sending the party in undercover for Hedvind's gala in ten days. Stay incognito. No spouting off about the League. A long shopping spree and the brand-new Uprising / Suppression tracker later, they marched north.

Elidir's gate was a Disneyland-line of refugees and inspectors, and getting in meant splitting up to blend with the crowd: Bai Jian muscled in as a green northern recruit (after fumbling his own heavy sword and flatly refusing to leash Ren), Panacea crit-tumbled through with a troupe of acrobats who turned out to be working thieves, and Zanthar talked a nervous Andoran clockmaker — and himself — past the guard. Then to the meeting point: Isger's Circle, an ancient, shunned monument to Urgothoa, goddess of excess and the first undead. They waited hours in the snow. Their courier finally arrived — "friends of the rose" — and so did the trap: "Halt, criminals!" A devil-masked constable named Adiutor and his thugs had been watching the circle. The fight was fast and deliberately non-lethal: Bai Jian one-shot a thug, Sammy's calm-song placated another, and Cyrathul clawed the masked constable bloody before a hero-pointed non-lethal strike to the skull put him down alive. They stuffed Adiutor into the spacious pouch — no body, no evidence — and their courier Sarevial hustled them through the dark to a safehouse. Session ends inside, undercover, in the capital, with a captive in the bag.

Timeline

Act I · Exploration · Thorngrip Morning & the Road North

Hangover, Snow, and a New Suit of Plate

  • The party woke at Thorngrip the morning after Stagfall, hungover on goblin mead ("Wake up, hangover. It's been a while since I drank this much."). Everyone banked an extra hero point and took a long rest.
  • Bai Jian shopped Gug one more time — spending his own gold on a full suit of plate armor (the smith measuring the too-tall human with two notched sticks taped together) — and the party picked Sammy a halberd out of the stash to replace her frying pan.
  • They set off through the Chitterwood toward Saringallow — ~3½ days, no real paths, very bad survival rolls ("you guys kind of wish you had brought a ranger along").
  • The first snow of the year fell on the second night; winter was arriving (the 13th of Kuthona).
  • The forest went unnervingly quiet — "a little off-putting." Bai Jian and Ren both clocked it on watch but chalked it up to the snow. On the third night, crunching footsteps in the now-crusted snow woke them: visitors, from the east.
Act II · Combat · The Snowbound Undead

Crits in the Cold

  • Bai Jian woke the camp and, as the only one who'd slept in his armor, ran up to meet the threat; Panacea cast Light on him so the no-darkvision members (her, Bai Jian, Sammy) could see.
  • The enemies were undead: ghouls with a paralytic bite, slow grabbing zombie shamblers, and a life-draining "white" wight. They came in two waves — the second revealed when Zanthar caught movement to the south ("I'll scream like a little girl").
  • Zanthar arrived mid-fight, half-asleep — logged on, whiffed his first swing (the DM narrating the hammer slipping from his sleepy grip), and loved it: "Oh, I'm going to cry. I love that." (Both Zanthar and Cyrathul had been DM-run until their players logged in mid-fight — Bai Jian greeting Snowgeen's arrival: "You're right on time for our first combat.")
  • Bai Jian carved through them: an opening 40-damage crit destroyed a wight, then a second crit (54) split a zombie shambler clean in half — "I'm overkilling these guys. Holy." — the DM narrating it "like an anime." He commanded Ren (still young) to finish a near-dead zombie, narrated as the hound ripping its leg out of the socket.
  • The undead bit back: the white wight's life-draining dagger punched through Bai Jian's brand-new plate ("I need to get a refund" — he made every save, hero-pointing the last). Its drain caught Zanthar twice; he shrugged both off (incapacitation, higher level).
  • Panacea anchored the back — Vitality Lash, Divine Lance, and a clutch three-action burst Heal that topped the party while searing every undead in range (the white wight crit-failed). Cyrathul read the weaknesses and bit a ghoul's head off. Sammy (DM-run) kept up Courageous Anthem and crit-swung her halberd. Zanthar ended it with a Hand of the Apprentice crit — "the head of the hammer just flies sideways… and takes his head off."
  • Party levels to 4. Ren leveled to a Mature companion (now acts independently); Bai Jian retrained his level-1 ancestry feat tankier still ("How tanky do you want to be?" "Really tanky.").
Act III · Faction / Roleplay · Hearts, Minds & the Hero's Report

"He's in Here"

  • Next morning the forest's sounds returned — and Ren scent-tracked the party out of the woods by an elk trail (Bai Jian: "You wonder why you haven't been listening to him this whole time"), popping out southwest of Fernsby.
  • Two crit-success diplomacy stops: a farmstead already on-board offered to march to Saringallow (+2 reputation), and Sammy sang a Hellbreaker ditty at a second — "you can almost swear there's magic behind her singing" — winning another (+1 reputation).
  • At Saringallow HQ, Marie welcomed them and led them to Rhokrag. Asked where the false Hell Knight was, Cyrathul gestured to the spacious pouch "under his wing" and tapped it with a claw — "He's in here." Marie recoiled at the week-old smell and ran to vomit into a barrel.
  • Rhokrag identified the body: Viro Ahala. The man he'd been chasing — an apprentice himself who'd stolen Hell Knight plate after the Battle of Breachill to gather Isger's scattered, fleeing Hell Knights. This was the trail that got Rhokrag captured. Moob and Fang torched the corpse — and flatly refused to ever go into the field again ("We die when we leave"), eyeing Panacea, who reassured them: "I've got everybody covered."
  • The new mission: Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias have stirred up too much trouble in Elidir, the capital; the authorities have made them. Get in incognito, go to Isger's Circle, and wait for a courier. Hedvind's gala is in ~10 days; the party will be on the guest list. Rhokrag handed over a goblin-made magic item — a Lifting Belt — for Bai Jian.
Act IV · Downtime · Spoils & the Long Shop

"Rebellion Is Profitable"

  • Zanthar scribed two spells into his book — Scroll of Grease and Scroll of Force Barrage (Arcana checks, hero-pointed).
  • The party liquidated the dungeon: seven Hellknight breastplates, weapons, rare mushrooms, rubies, ritual supplies, 35 bolts. "Rebellion is profitable." / "We're rich!"
  • Kept for the mission: Viro's Hell Knight plate (a future infiltration disguise), the devil journal, a climbing bolt, and a silver chunk — which Bai Jian used to silver his falcata.
  • Gear: Cyrathul claimed the Pendant of the Occult and the Shining Symbol ("I'm sparkly") and upgraded his handwraps to striking. Panacea bought a Staff of Healing. Zanthar stocked Helpful Step and second-level Heal scrolls. Bai Jian added a crushing rune.
  • On the road north, the DM introduced the Uprising / Suppression tracker: Uprising points build Hellbreaker support; Suppression points accrue from noise or killing (a flat check each time). Travel passed the very spot where they'd sunk the Chelaxian ship in Session 2.
Act V · Exploration / Social · The Gate of Elidir

Three Disguises and a Refusal

  • Elidir — a walled capital of ~20,000, the Carnecia River running through it — greeted them with a refugee line "as long as the Matterhorn at Disneyland," exhausted and gouged by water-and-ale vendors. Several groups offered cover.
  • Bai Jian tried to pass as one of the green northern recruits (Athletics). It went badly first: showing off, he dropped his own heavy falcata in the snow, and when the guard demanded he leash Ren, he refused outright — portcullis down, denied.
  • Panacea joined the Menagerie of Fae Fantastico acrobats — a hero-pointed crit-success triple-backflip — and realized mid-tumble the troupe were working thieves pickpocketing the line. "I'll blend right in. Keep a low profile, smile and nod."
  • Zanthar disguised as a plain human and worked the nervous Andoran clockmaker dwarf (Crafting, crit success) — deducing the dwarf feared rejection over the Andor–Cheliax war, then sidestepping the guard with a cover story and walking him through. The grateful dwarf gifted him a little hand-carved clock.
  • Bai Jian hero-point-retconned his attempt and got in clean: vouching for the green recruits ("They're new, but they have the spirit") and still refusing to leash Ren — the guard, eyeing his gleaming plate and huge sword, waved them through. The party drew a little suspicion (a Suppression point), but everyone made it inside.
Act VI · Combat · Isger's Circle & the Trap

Halt, Criminals

  • Inside the capital, the party immediately forgot why they'd come — "Let's go bar hopping" — until the DM reminded them about the meeting ("Not a single note-taker in the group").
  • They found Isger's Circle by asking around (a snobby half-elf: "There'll be no one else there. Why would you want to go there? Disgusting."), crossing Elidir's great bridge past Moyle (the former slave market, slavery only just outlawed by the Emancipation Edict — now a debtor's munitions workhouse), the docks, and a derelict menagerie.
  • The Circle was an ancient, worn standing-stone monument to Urgothoa — goddess of excess, disease, and the undead, the first being to refuse death at the River Styx. Centuries of attempts to remove it had failed (workers fell ill). Everyone avoids it. Cyrathul, unbothered: "Are we going to sacrifice someone on the altar now?"
  • They waited hours in the snow. A courier walked up — "Friends of the rose? Wonderful, we've been expecting you" — and then: "HALT, CRIMINALS!" A devil-masked constable, Adiutor, with two thug "Toadies," had been watching the circle.
  • The fight (deliberately restrained, the Suppression rules looming): Zanthar opened with Blazing Bolt; Bai Jian dropped a Toady with a single 42-damage strike; Adiutor fumbled his unloaded musket ("I guess he's not very good at his job"), then fired and shot Bai Jian. Sammy's calm-song placated the surviving Toady; Adiutor shrugged it off. Panacea's improvised attempt to channel Urgothoa was shut down by her own patron. Cyrathul Exploited, Demoralized, and clawed Adiutor open — then hero-pointed a non-lethal claw (narrated as a spinning tail-strike to the head), dropping the constable alive.
  • No body, no evidence: they stuffed Adiutor into the spacious pouch, the calmed Toady fled, and they made a clean getaway with zero exposure. Their courier, Sarevial, led them through the dark to a safehouse.

Round by Round

  • Setup: Bai Jian (only one armored) woke the camp and engaged; Panacea cast Light on him so the no-darkvision half could see. His opening falcata crit (40) destroyed the first wight — whose dying-reaction claw clipped him for 10 (Fort save, success).
  • Bai Jian — 4 kills: the 40-crit wight; a second crit (49 rolled → 54 with weakness) that split a zombie shambler in half; a Sudden Charge (14) that finished the white wight; and a MAP strike (13) that beheaded a ghoul. Commanded Ren (still Young) to bite a near-dead zombie for the kill. Made every paralysis/drain Fort save — spending a hero point to reroll the white wight's final draining dagger ("I need to get a refund").
  • Cyrathul: Exploit Vulnerability flagged the undead's weaknesses (vitality/slashing), then chomped a ghoul's head off (30 → 33). Whiffed both attacks on the last ghoul late.
  • Panacea: Vitality Lash (target saved, weakness still bit two undead for 3/8), Divine Lance, and a three-action burst Heal — healing the party 7 each while searing all three remaining undead; the white wight crit-failed for 14.
  • Zanthar: arrived half-asleep, whiffed his first hammer Strike (narrated as a dropped hammer), chipped a zombie with Phase Bolt (7), Dizzying Colors dazzled two, shrugged off two life-draining wight bites (incapacitation), and ended the fight with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice crit (30) decapitation.
  • Sammy (DM-run): Courageous Anthem + Runic Weapon on herself; crit-swung her halberd into the white wight (14), then MAP-hit it again (9). Soaked the wave-2/3 bites (~22 dmg) and passed every paralysis save.
  • Ren (matured to Lv 4 after this fight): landed the finishing zombie bite; next morning, scent-led the party out of the forest.

Outcome: all undead destroyed, no party drops. Party levels to 4.

Character Spotlight

Bai Jian · the White Blade in Winter

  • The quiet competence the party leans on. He slept in his armor, woke the camp, and ran toward the dark to buy everyone a turn — then once Panacea lit him, was a butcher: a crit that slid an undead in half "like an anime" ("I'm overkilling these guys"), a ghoul beheaded, and a single 42-damage strike that one-shot a thug in the capital.
  • He ate a wight's life-draining dagger through his brand-new plate with the session's best deadpan — "I need to get a refund" — and made every save it threw at him (hero-pointing the last).
  • The spotlight beat was Ren. With the boarhound newly matured, Bai Jian stopped babysitting him and started trusting him — handing Ren the survival check to lead them out of the forest, and, twice at the Elidir gate, flatly refusing to leash him even when it cost him entry. "Yes, I've got him, and he's a very good boy."
  • He plays the tank as a caretaker: led the watch rotations, talked Panacea through her positioning all fight, and in the capital deferred every kill decision to the mission ("Anything wrong if I kill this guy?"). The materialism gag carried over lightly ("Finally, we can sell all these") but reads as a quartermaster's habit now, not greed. He's the one keeping the lights on.

Cyrathul · the Profitable Rebel

  • The party's mouth and its scalpel. He delivered the HQ report, summed up a season of war in a sentence, and gave the session its thesis on the way to sell loot: "Rebellion is profitable."
  • His presentation of Viro's corpse was peak Cyrathul — gesturing to the pouch "under my wing" and tapping it with a claw, "He's in here" — theatrical, dry, and faintly monstrous, right down to asking the goblin arsonists to teach him the body-incineration trick.
  • In combat the enemy-reader and the closer: Exploit Vulnerability every fight, a ghoul's head bitten off, and in the capital he methodically took the masked constable apart — Exploit, Demoralize, claws through the chest armor — before threading the needle that mattered: a hero-pointed, MAP-penalized, non-lethal claw that just connected, capped with a spinning tail-strike that crumpled Adiutor "like a rag doll" without killing him.
  • He owns the party's self-awareness, too — eyeing the infiltration plan and admitting "I can keep myself not a dragon for an hour… but I'm not really good at being inconspicuous. I don't think our party in general is" — and surveying Urgothoa's creepy altar with a glowing Shining Symbol pinned to him: "I'm sparkly. Are we going to sacrifice someone now?"

Panacea · the Hand That Has Everybody Covered

  • Ran the whole back line under pressure: Light so the no-darkvision half could even fight, Vitality Lash and Divine Lance into the undead, and a perfectly-placed three-action burst Heal that mended the party and torched every walking corpse in range.
  • Her positioning instincts are sharp and stated aloud ("First I'm going to move out of the way, because if it dies and flats on me, I don't want that"), and she came loaded — buying a Staff of Healing specifically for the dark mission ahead.
  • Facing Moob and Fang — the goblins her crit-failed Battle Medicine killed two sessions back — she didn't flinch: "Don't worry, I've learned a lot more since then. I've got everybody covered."
  • At Isger's Circle she got curious and reckless, trying to channel Urgothoa herself (she speaks Necril) to scare the captives into talking — only for her own patron to recoil through her familiar and warn "Don't do that again." Her reply was the line of the night: "Well, I thought it might be fun." And she's a genuine infiltrator — a hero-pointed crit-success acrobatics tumble slid her into the city with a troupe of thieves.

Zanthar Melodune III · the Apprentice Who Logged On Mid-Fight

  • The funniest entrance of the campaign so far: he joined the session in the middle of the undead ambush, half-asleep, immediately whiffed his first attack (the DM narrating the hammer slipping from his grip) — and loved it. "Oh, I'm going to cry. I love that."
  • From there, exactly what the party needed: he screamed the warning that revealed the second wave ("I'll scream like a little girl"), shrugged off two life-draining wight bites, dazzled a cluster, and closed the fight with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice crit — "the head of the hammer just flies sideways… and takes his head off."
  • The party's problem-solver out of combat: he scribed Grease and Force Barrage into his book, stocked Helpful Step and Heal scrolls with the next mission in mind, and at the Elidir gate ran the cleverest con — reading the Andoran clockmaker's real fear, then talking both of them past the guard.
  • Even his combat read of the enemy is comedy with a point — clocking Adiutor's empty musket and calling it: "I guess he's not very good at his job. He just waves the gun around — he doesn't actually use it." From "rebel without a clue" to the guy quietly carrying the utility belt.

Sammy Shadowfoot · the Song From the Wings

  • fallernon was absent and DM-run all session, but Sammy still earned her keep. She traded her frying pan for a 10-foot-reach halberd and immediately put it to work — crit-swinging into the white wight while keeping Courageous Anthem up over the party.
  • On the road, a hearts-and-minds weapon: a Hellbreaker ditty at a farmstead that landed like real enchantment ("you can almost swear there's magic behind her singing") and won a reputation point.
  • Her best beat was the capital ambush. With the party trying to keep the fight non-lethal, her calm-song lullaby placated one of Adiutor's thugs into swaying on the spot, taking him out of the fight without a scratch. (The masked constable shook it off entirely, which is its own kind of ominous.)
  • Reliable, present, and quietly load-bearing even when her player can't be.
