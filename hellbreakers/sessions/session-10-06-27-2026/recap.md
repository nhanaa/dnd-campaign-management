## Session Summary

The hero's hangover wore off fast. The party woke at Thorngrip the morning after the Stagfall victory, sold and re-geared with Gug (Bai Jian splurging on a brand-new suit of full plate), and set off through the Chitterwood toward Saringallow as the first snow of the year began to fall. Three days of bad survival rolls and quiet, too-quiet forest later, the quiet broke: undead came crunching through the snow at the third night's camp — ghouls, a life-draining "white" wight, and a pair of slow zombie shamblers. Bai Jian, the only one who'd slept in his armor, met them in the dark while Panacea threw up Light; what followed was a one-sided slaughter (the DM "only rolling crits for the players"), with Bai Jian cleaving corpses in half, **Ren** ripping a zombie's leg clean out of the socket, Cyrathul biting a ghoul's head off, and Zanthar — who'd logged on mid-fight, half-asleep, and immediately fumbled his own hammer — closing it out with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice decapitation. **The party hit level 4** (Ren matured right after).

Out of the woods (Ren finally allowed to lead the way by scent), two crit-success diplomacy stops swelled the Hellbreaker ranks, and they reached Saringallow to report in. The HQ scene paid off a season of work: Cyrathul tipped Viro Ahala's week-old corpse out of the spacious pouch at Rhokrag's feet (Marie ran for a barrel), and Rhokrag confirmed the kill — Viro was the apprentice-thief he'd been chasing when he was captured, the man gathering Isger's scattered Hell Knights, undone with a stolen suit of plate. Moob and Fang incinerated the body; the goblins flatly refused to ever leave HQ again. Then a new mission: **Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias are pinned down in the capital, Elidir** — too hot, too identified. Rhokrag is sending the party in undercover for Hedvind's gala in ten days. *Stay incognito. No spouting off about the League.* A long shopping spree (Zanthar scribing scrolls, Panacea buying a Staff of Healing, Bai Jian silvering his falcata) and the brand-new **Uprising / Suppression tracker** later, they marched north.

Elidir's gate was a Disneyland-line of refugees and inspectors, and getting in meant splitting up to blend with the crowd: Bai Jian muscled in as a green northern recruit (after fumbling his own heavy sword and flatly refusing to leash Ren), Panacea crit-tumbled through with a troupe of acrobats who turned out to be working thieves, and Zanthar talked a nervous Andoran clockmaker — and himself — past the guard. Then to the meeting point: **Isger's Circle**, an ancient, shunned monument to Urgathoa, goddess of excess and the first undead. They waited hours in the snow. Their courier finally arrived — *"friends of the rose"* — and so did the trap: *"Halt, criminals!"* A devil-masked constable named **Adiutor** and his thugs had been watching the circle. The fight was fast and deliberately non-lethal-leaning (the new Suppression rules in everyone's heads): Bai Jian one-shot a thug, Sammy's calm-song placated another, and Cyrathul clawed the masked constable bloody before a hero-pointed **tail-strike to the skull** put him down alive. They stuffed Adiutor into the spacious pouch — *no body, no evidence* — and their courier **Sarevial** hustled them through the dark to a safehouse. Session ends inside, undercover, in the capital, with a captive in the bag.

## Key Events

### Act I — Thorngrip Morning & the Road North (00:00–19:00)

- The party woke at **Thorngrip** the morning after Stagfall, hungover on goblin mead (*"Wake up, hangover. It's been a while since I drank this much."*). Everyone banked an extra hero point and took a long rest.
- **Bai Jian shopped Gug** one more time: he spent his own gold on a full suit of **plate armor** (the goblin smith measuring the too-tall human with two notched sticks taped together), and the party picked **Sammy a halberd** out of the stash to replace her frying pan.
- They set off through the **Chitterwood** toward Saringallow — roughly 3½ days, no real paths, very bad survival rolls ("you guys kind of wish you had brought a ranger along"). The **first snow of the year** fell on the second night; winter was arriving (the 13th of Kuthona).
- The forest went **unnervingly quiet** — "a little off-putting." Bai Jian and Ren both clocked it on watch but chalked it up to the snow. On the **third night**, crunching footsteps in the now-crusted snow woke them: visitors, from the east.

### Act II — The Snowbound Undead (19:00–60:00) — *Combat 1*

- **Bai Jian woke the camp.** As the only one who'd slept in his armor, he ran up to meet the threat; **Panacea cast Light on him** so the party's no-darkvision members (her, Bai Jian, Sammy) could see at all. Zanthar and Cyrathul could see in the dark.
- The enemies were **undead**: ghouls with a paralytic venom bite, slow **zombie shamblers** that grab, and a fancy life-draining **"white" wight**. They came in two waves — the second (more shamblers + a wight) revealed when **Zanthar** caught movement to the south (*"I'll scream like a little girl."*).
- **Zanthar arrived mid-fight, half-asleep** — logged on, **whiffed his first swing**, which the DM narrated as the hammer slipping from his sleepy grip ("still a little sleepy-eyed… the hammer drops out of your hand"). His verdict: *"Oh, I'm going to cry. I love that."* (Both Zanthar and **Cyrathul** had been **DM-run** until their players logged in mid-fight — Bai Jian greeting **Snowgeen's** arrival: *"You're right on time for our first combat."*)
- **Bai Jian carved through them**: an opening **40-damage crit destroyed a wight**, then a second **crit (54) split a zombie shambler clean in half** — *"I'm overkilling these guys. Holy."* — the DM narrating it "like an anime." He commanded **Ren** (still a young companion) to **finish a near-dead zombie**, a bite the DM rendered as the hound ripping its leg clean out of the socket, black goo pouring from the hole.
- The undead bit back: the **white wight's life-draining dagger punched through Bai Jian's brand-new plate** (*"I need to get a refund."* — he made every Fortitude save, **spending a hero point** to reroll the last). The white wight's draining bite also caught **Zanthar** twice; he shrugged both off (incapacitation, higher level).
- **Panacea** anchored the back line — **Vitality Lash** (save, but the weakness still bit), **Divine Lance**, and a clutch **three-action burst Heal** that topped the party while searing every undead in range (the white wight **crit-failed** and screeched her aura off itself). **Cyrathul** read the enemies and **bit a ghoul's head off** ("one of the more disgusting things you've ever had to taste"). **Sammy** (DM-run) buffed with **Courageous Anthem**, put **Runic Weapon** on herself, and crit-swung her new halberd into the white wight. **Zanthar** ended it with a **Hand of the Apprentice crit** — *"the head of the hammer just flies sideways… and takes his head off."*
- **Level 4.** Ren leveled to a **Mature companion** (now acts independently, +Str/Dex/Con). Bai Jian retrained his level-1 ancestry feat tankier still (*"How tanky do you want to be?" "Really tanky."*), and the casters agonized over spell picks while the Fighter finished in seconds.

### Act III — Hearts, Minds & the Hero's Report (60:00–98:00)

- The next morning the forest's sounds returned — and **Ren scent-tracked the party out of the woods** by an elk trail (Bai Jian: *"You wonder why you haven't been listening to him this whole time."*), popping out southwest of Fernsby.
- Two **crit-success diplomacy** stops on the road: a farmstead already on-board immediately offered to march to Saringallow (**+2 reputation**), and **Sammy sang a Hellbreaker ditty** at a second — *"you can almost swear there's magic behind her singing"* — winning over the whole stand (**+1 reputation**).
- At **Saringallow HQ**, Marie welcomed them (*"friends of the rose"*) and led them down to **Rhokrag**. Cyrathul delivered his report and, asked where the false Hell Knight was, **gestured to the spacious pouch "under his wing" and tapped it with a claw** — *"He's in here."* Marie recoiled at the week-old smell and ran to vomit into a barrel.
- **Rhokrag identified the body: Viro Ahala.** This was the man he'd been chasing — an **apprentice himself** who'd **stolen Hell Knight plate after the Battle of Breachill** and was trying to gather Isger's scattered, fleeing Hell Knights. Rhokrag looked ashamed: this was the trail that got him captured. **Moob and Fang torched the corpse** in the alchemy lab — and flatly refused to ever go into the field again (*"We die when we leave."*), eyeing Panacea, who reassured them: *"I've learned a lot more since then. I've got everybody covered."*
- **The new mission**: Alceo and Phoebe Demetrias have stirred up too much trouble in **Elidir, the capital** — the authorities have made them, and they can't operate. Rhokrag is sending his most trusted: get into the locked-down city **incognito**, go to **Isger's Circle**, and wait for a courier. **Hedvind is throwing a gala in ~10 days** and the party will be on the guest list. He handed over a goblin-made magic item — a **Lifting Belt** — for Bai Jian.

### Act IV — Spoils & the Long Shop (98:00–123:00)

- **Zanthar scribed two spells into his book** — Scroll of Grease and Scroll of Force Barrage (Arcana checks, hero-pointed).
- The party **liquidated the dungeon**: seven Hellknight breastplates, weapons, rare mushrooms, rubies, ritual supplies, an electrical trinket, 35 crossbow bolts. *"Rebellion is profitable."* / *"We're rich!"*
- **Kept for the mission**: Viro's **Hell Knight plate** (for a future infiltration disguise), the devil journal, a single **climbing bolt**, and a **silver chunk** — which Bai Jian used to **silver his falcata**.
- Gear: **Cyrathul** claimed the **Pendant of the Occult** and the **Shining Symbol** (constant light — *"I'm sparkly"*) and upgraded his **handwraps to striking**. **Panacea** bought a **Staff of Healing** (two extra heals a day). **Zanthar** stocked **Helpful Step** scrolls and second-level **Heal** scrolls for the undercover stretch ahead. **Bai Jian** added a **crushing rune** (the Wand of Tailwind everyone wanted was a level too high).
- On the road north, the DM introduced the **Uprising / Suppression tracker**: Uprising points build Hellbreaker support and unlock perks; Suppression points accrue from noise or killing (a flat check each time). Travel passed the very spot where they'd **sunk the Chelaxian ship in Session 2**.

### Act V — The Gate of Elidir (123:00–157:00) — *Skill Challenge*

- **Elidir** — a walled capital of ~20,000, the Carnecia River running through it — greeted them with a **refugee line "as long as the Matterhorn at Disneyland,"** roped off, exhausted, gouged by water-and-ale vendors. Several groups offered cover to blend in.
- **Bai Jian** sized up the **green northern recruits** and tried to pass as one (Athletics). It went badly first: showing off, he **dropped his own heavy falcata** in the snow, and when the guard demanded he **leash Ren**, he **refused outright** — portcullis down, denied.
- **Panacea** joined the **Menagerie of Fae Fantastico** acrobats — a hero-pointed **crit-success triple-backflip** that won the guard's grin — and realized mid-tumble the troupe were **working thieves** pickpocketing the line. *"I'll blend right in. I'll just keep a low profile, smile and nod."*
- **Zanthar** disguised as a plain human and worked the **nervous Andoran clockmaker dwarf** (Crafting, crit success) — deducing the dwarf feared rejection over the Andor–Cheliax war, then **sidestepping the guard** with a cover story and walking him through. The grateful dwarf gifted him a little hand-carved clock.
- **Bai Jian hero-point-retconned** his attempt and got in clean: vouching for the green recruits (*"They're new, but they have the spirit"*) and **still refusing to leash Ren** — the guard, eyeing his gleaming new plate and huge sword, waved them through. The party drew a little suspicion at the gate (a **Suppression point**), but everyone made it inside.

### Act VI — Isger's Circle & the Trap (157:00–202:00) — *Combat 2*

- Inside the capital at last, the party **immediately forgot why they'd come** — *"Let's go bar hopping"* — until the DM reminded them about the meeting (*"Not a single note-taker in the group."*).
- With a week to kill before the gala, they found **Isger's Circle** by asking around (a snobby half-elf gave directions and a verdict: *"There'll be no one else there. Why would you want to go there? Disgusting."*). They crossed Elidir's great bridge, passing **Moyle** (the former slave market, slavery only just outlawed by the Emancipation Edict — now a debtor's munitions workhouse), the docks, and a derelict menagerie.
- **The Circle** was an ancient, worn standing-stone monument to **Urgathoa** — goddess of excess, disease, and the undead, the first being to refuse death at the River Styx. Centuries of attempts to remove it had failed (workers always fell ill). Everyone avoids it for a block. Cyrathul, unbothered: *"Are we going to sacrifice someone on the altar now?"*
- They **waited hours in the snow**. At last a courier walked up — *"Friends of the rose? Wonderful, we've been expecting you"* — and then: **"HALT, CRIMINALS!"** Out of the snow stepped a devil-masked constable, **Adiutor**, with two thug "Toadies." He'd been watching the circle, waiting for the League to surface.
- **The fight** (deliberately restrained — the Suppression rules looming): **Zanthar** opened with **Blazing Bolt** at all three; **Bai Jian dropped a Toady with a single 42-damage falcata strike**; **Adiutor fumbled his flintlock musket** (no ammo loaded — *"I guess he's not very good at his job"*), then got it firing and shot Bai Jian. **Sammy's calm-song** placated the surviving Toady (it swayed in place), but **Adiutor shrugged it off** (made his save). **Panacea's** improvised attempt to **channel Urgathoa** to terrify the captives was firmly shut down by her own patron (via her familiar). **Cyrathul** Exploited, **Demoralized** (Intimidating Glare), and clawed Adiutor open — then **hero-pointed a non-lethal claw and finished with a kung-fu tail-strike to the head**, dropping the constable alive.
- **No body, no evidence**: they **stuffed Adiutor into the spacious pouch**, the calmed Toady fled, and they made a **clean getaway with zero exposure**. Their courier — **Sarevial** — exhaled (*"That was so intense. Let's keep going."*) and led them through the dark, doubling back, to a small mansion safehouse. **Session ends** inside, undercover, with a captive in the bag.

## Character Spotlight

### Bai Jian · the White Blade in Winter

Pax's session was the quiet competence the party leans on. He's the one who slept in his armor, woke the camp, and ran *toward* the dark to buy everyone else a turn — and once Panacea lit him up, he was a butcher: a falcata crit that **slid an undead in half "like an anime,"** an overkill crit on a shambler (*"I'm overkilling these guys. Holy."*), a ghoul beheaded, and in the capital a **single 42-damage strike that one-shot a thug**. He ate a wight's life-draining dagger through his **brand-new plate** with the session's best deadpan — *"I need to get a refund"* — and made every save it threw at him (hero-pointing the last).

But the spotlight beat was **Ren**. With the boarhound newly matured, Bai Jian stopped babysitting him and started *trusting* him — handing Ren the survival check to lead them out of the forest, and, twice at the Elidir gate, **flatly refusing to leash him** even when it cost him entry. *"Yes, I've got him, and he's a very good boy,"* he told the guard, signaling Ren to roll over and wag like a harmless mutt. He plays the tank as a caretaker: he led the watch rotations, talked Panacea through her positioning all fight, and in the capital deferred every kill decision to the mission (*"Anything wrong if I kill this guy?"*). The materialism gag from last session carried over lightly — *"Finally, we can sell all these and get some money"* — but it reads as a quartermaster's habit now, not greed. He's the one keeping the lights on.

### Cyrathul · the Profitable Rebel

Snowgeen's dragon was the party's **mouth and its scalpel**. He delivered the HQ report, summed up a season of war in a sentence (*"Our popularity in the South, a lot higher than it was"*), and gave the session its thesis as he headed off to sell loot: *"Rebellion is profitable."* His presentation of **Viro's corpse** — **gesturing to the pouch "under my wing" and tapping it with a claw, "He's in here"** — was peak Cyrathul: theatrical, dry, and faintly monstrous, right down to asking the goblin arsonists to **teach him the body-incineration trick**.

In combat he's the enemy-reader and the closer. He **Exploited Vulnerability** every fight, **bit a ghoul's head off** ("one of the more disgusting things you've ever had to taste"), and in the capital methodically took the masked constable apart — Exploit, **Demoralize**, claws **through the chest armor** ("blood gushing down the front") — before threading the needle that mattered: a **hero-pointed, MAP-penalized, non-lethal claw** that *just* connected, capped with a **spinning kung-fu tail-strike** that crumpled Adiutor "like a rag doll" without killing him. He also owns the party's self-awareness — eyeing the infiltration plan and admitting, *"I can keep myself not a dragon for an hour… but I'm not really good at being inconspicuous. I don't think our party in general is,"* and surveying Urgathoa's creepy altar with a glowing Shining Symbol pinned to him: *"I'm sparkly. Are we going to sacrifice someone now?"*

### Panacea · the Hand That Has Everybody Covered

Bog Witch's healer ran the whole back line under pressure and quietly drove some of the session's best scenes. Mechanically she was everywhere — **Light** so the no-darkvision half of the party could even fight, **Vitality Lash** and **Divine Lance** into the undead, and a perfectly-placed **three-action burst Heal** that mended the party and torched every walking corpse in range. Her positioning instincts are sharp and stated out loud (*"First I'm going to move out of the way, because if it dies and flats on me, I don't want that"*), and she came **loaded** — buying a **Staff of Healing** specifically for the dark mission ahead.

Her character beats were the standouts. Facing Moob and Fang — the goblins she'd accidentally killed with a crit-failed Battle Medicine two sessions back — she didn't flinch: *"Don't worry, I've learned a lot more since then. I've got everybody covered."* At Isger's Circle she got curious and a little reckless, trying to **channel Urgathoa herself** (she speaks Necril) to scare the captives into talking — only for her own patron to recoil through her familiar and warn *"Don't do that again."* Her reply was the line of the night: *"Well, I thought it might be fun."* And she's a genuine infiltrator: a hero-pointed **crit-success acrobatics tumble** slid her into the city with a troupe of thieves, no notes, just *"keep a low profile, smile and nod, and keep going."*

### Zanthar Melodune III · the Apprentice Who Logged On Mid-Fight

Scott's gnome had the funniest entrance of the campaign so far: he **joined the session in the middle of the undead ambush, half-asleep**, immediately **whiffed his first attack and dropped his own hammer** — and loved it. *"Oh, I'm going to cry. I love that."* From there he settled into being exactly what the party needed: the artillery and the utility brain. He **screamed the warning** that revealed the second wave (*"I'll scream like a little girl"*), shrugged off **two life-draining wight bites** (incapacitation, higher level), dazzled a cluster with a cone, and **closed the fight with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice crit** — *"the head of the hammer just flies sideways… and takes his head off."*

He's the party's problem-solver out of combat, too. He **scribed Grease and Force Barrage** into his spellbook, stocked **Helpful Step** and **Heal** scrolls with the next mission in mind, and at the Elidir gate ran the cleverest con: reading the **Andoran clockmaker's** real fear (the Andor–Cheliax war), then **talking both of them past the guard** with a cover story. Even his combat read of the enemy is comedy with a point — clocking Adiutor's empty musket and calling it: *"I guess he's not very good at his job. He just waves the gun around — he doesn't actually use it."* From "rebel without a clue" to the guy quietly carrying the utility belt.

### Sammy Shadowfoot · the Song From the Wings

fallernon was absent and **DM-run all session**, but Sammy still earned her keep. She traded her frying pan for a **10-foot-reach halberd** out of the stash and immediately put it to work — the DM "only rolling crits for the players," she **crit-swung her halberd into the white wight** (then chipped it again on a MAP hit) while keeping **Courageous Anthem** up over the party. On the road, she was a hearts-and-minds weapon: **a Hellbreaker ditty at a farmstead** that landed like real enchantment (*"you can almost swear there's magic behind her singing"*) and won another reputation point.

Her best beat was the capital ambush. With the party trying to keep the fight **non-lethal**, the DM cued her turn — *"Sammy, Sammy Skinner. Do the old razzle dazzle."* — and her **calm-song lullaby** placated one of Adiutor's thugs into swaying on the spot, taking him out of the fight without a scratch. (The masked constable shook it off entirely, which is its own kind of ominous.) Reliable, present, and quietly load-bearing even when her player can't be.

### Encounter 1 — The Snowbound Undead (128 XP · ~3 rounds; level-up after)

On paper a hard fight; it played as a rout only because the party crit repeatedly and the undead whiffed nearly every bite (the DM audibly lamenting his rolls). Wave one came from the **east**; **Zanthar's secret Perception** caught wave two crunching in from the **south**.

- **Setup**: Bai Jian (the only one who slept armored) woke the camp and ran to engage; **Panacea cast Light on him** so the no-darkvision half could see. His opening **falcata crit (40) destroyed the first wight** — whose **dying-reaction claw** clipped him for 10 (Fort save, **success**).
- **Bai Jian** — **4 kills**: the 40-crit wight; a second **crit (49 rolled → 54 with weakness) that split a zombie shambler in half** (*"I'm overkilling these guys"* / "like an anime"); a **Sudden Charge (14) that finished the white wight**; and a **MAP strike (13) that beheaded a ghoul**. He commanded **Ren** (still a **Young** companion this fight) to **bite a near-dead zombie for the kill** (narrated as a leg-rip). Made every paralysis/drain Fort save — **spending a hero point to reroll** the white wight's final draining dagger (*"I need to get a refund"*).
- **Cyrathul**: **Exploit Vulnerability** flagged the undead's weaknesses (vitality/slashing), then **chomped a ghoul's head off** (30 → 33). Whiffed both attacks on the last ghoul late.
- **Panacea**: **Vitality Lash** (target saved, weakness still bit two undead for 3/8), **Divine Lance**, and a clutch **three-action burst Heal** — healing the party 7 each while searing all three remaining undead; the **white wight crit-failed** for 14.
- **Zanthar**: arrived mid-fight half-asleep, **whiffed his first hammer Strike** (the DM narrating it as a dropped hammer), chipped a zombie with **Phase Bolt (7)**, **Dizzying Colors dazzled two**, shrugged off **two life-draining wight bites** (incapacitation, higher level), and **ended the fight with a Hand-of-the-Apprentice crit (30) decapitation**.
- **Sammy** (DM-run): **Courageous Anthem** up, **Runic Weapon** on herself; **crit-swung her halberd into the white wight (14), then MAP-hit it again (9)**. Soaked the wave-2/3 bites (~22 dmg) and passed every paralysis save.
- **Ren** (matured to Lv 4 *after* this fight): landed the finishing zombie bite here; next morning, scent-led the party out of the forest.
- **Outcome**: all undead destroyed; no party drops. **Party levels to 4.**

### Encounter 2 — The Trap at Isger's Circle (capture fight)

**Initiative** (per Pax's tracker): Zanthar · Toady · Adiutor · Bai Jian · Panacea · Sammy · Cyrathul · **Sarevial (ally)** · Toady

An ambush sprung the moment the courier made contact: constable **Adiutor** (devil-mask with flaming horns; **flintlock musket** + knife-tipped bayonet-stick; a **divine** caster) and two billy-club **Toadies** had been watching the circle. **Bai Jian delayed** his turn (no darkvision — waiting for Light). The party fought to **subdue, not kill**, mindful of the new Suppression tracker.

- **Zanthar**: opened with **Blazing Bolt** as three rays at all three — **two missed, one landed for 12** on a Toady; later a **non-lethal Hand of the Apprentice** at Adiutor (miss); pegged the empty musket for the table.
- **Bai Jian**: delayed until Panacea lit him, then a **crit Strike for 42 → Toady killed** outright (declared Slam Down, swapped to a plain Strike when he saw the number). Round 2: commanded **Ren to Support**, then a **non-lethal Slam Down on Adiutor** (-2, miss) — after shouting to Sarevial to confirm a kill was off the table. Got a **Reactive Strike** when Adiutor cast (a manipulate action) but **missed**. Tanked the musket (15) and **Crisis of Faith** (saved → 8 mental).
- **Adiutor** (enemy): *"You're all under arrest."* **Fumbled the unloaded musket**, reloaded, and **shot Bai Jian for 15**; cast **Crisis of Faith** at Bai Jian (saved); bayonet-stabbed (miss). Took 23 / 16 / 18 / 20 from Cyrathul across two rounds.
- **Sammy** (DM-run): **Courageous Anthem** + **Calm** — **placated Toady #2** (failed save, swaying in place); **Adiutor made his save** and shrugged it off. Sustained the calm to cover the getaway.
- **Panacea**: improvised a **Necril plea to Urgathoa** to scare the captives — **shut down by her own patron** via her familiar (a free action); **Light on Bai Jian**; **Life Boost** on Bai Jian; familiar-step → **auto-Stupefy on Adiutor**; **Trade Death for Life** on Adiutor (saved).
- **Cyrathul**: **Exploit Vulnerability (Personal Antithesis)** + **Demoralize (Intimidating Glare, success → Frightened 1)** + claws **through Adiutor's chest armor**; finished with a **hero-pointed, MAP- and non-lethal-penalized claw that just connected** (the DM narrating it as a spinning tail-strike to the head) — Adiutor **down alive**.
- **Sarevial** (ally courier): took cover behind the Urgathoa monument once the party had it handled.
- **Outcome**: one Toady dead (Bai Jian), one fled (Calmed), **Adiutor captured** and bagged in the **spacious pouch**. **Clean getaway — no exposure/suppression point gained.** Sarevial leads the party to a safehouse.
