## Session Summary

The Lord in Iron is dead. Gorum, god of war and battle, was slain by the mantis god Achaekek during the Godsrain — and when a god dies, the afterlife he kept dies with him. The Clashing Shore, the endless proving ground where the faithful of Gorum were promised they would test their mettle forever, vanished "like smoke and ash, scattered to the cosmic winds."

Six dead warriors were left standing on the palm of Gorum's severed gauntlet. Every one of them had already died once — Keegru bled out in a bar fight, Jassik was murdered in his sleep for being a greedy pillaging warlord, Graz was a gladiator who challenged his way from the bottom to the top until he could no longer bear the consequences of his own arrogance. What they were offered on that iron palm was not resurrection. It was five fights, ten minutes apart, escalating from trivial to extreme, with Cody's own framing hanging over it: *"The chances that your characters will not survive to the end of the gauntlet is possible."*

They survived all five. Not one PC died. The party killed **eighteen enemies across eleven stat blocks**, dealt roughly **9,500 points of damage**, and burned three party-wide safety nets and most of a healer's spell list. The Sword Master of Gorum had to be reduced to zero **four separate times** before he stayed down — but it was the High Cleric who actually put people on the floor.

The shape of the day was set in the first ninety seconds of the first fight and never really changed: **Graz's Battle Cry Demoralize at initiative, Tam's Dread Striker converting that fear into off-guard, and Perfect Finisher rolling two dice for every swing that mattered.** By the end Graz was saying it out loud about his own build — *"I think the Perfect Finisher is just — I don't know. It's crazy"* — while Cody measured it from the other side of the screen: *"There was a point where you took down like all 600 of the champion's HP in two rounds."*

## Key Events

### Prelude (13:06 – 13:29)
- Character introductions and pre-buff logistics. Cody lays out the format: five encounters, ten minutes between each, increasing difficulty, no guarantee of survival.
- Cody's ruling that shapes everything: **"Hero points don't come back between fights. You've got three for the whole five fights."**
- Graz pre-casts **Foresight** (1 hour), **Fly**, **Heroism**, **See the Unseen**, and drinks **three Greater Resistance potions — fire, cold and electricity.** Keegru pre-casts **Sacred Covenant** on the whole party.

### Act I — The Magma Worms (13:33 – 14:04)
- Three level-18 Magma Worms erupt from drops of blood on the sand. Budgeted at exactly **Trivial**.
- **Graz opens with a critical hit before any enemy acts** and never lets up. Worm B dies without taking a single turn despite rolling 48 initiative.
- **Shuyinlo is swallowed whole in round 1** and spends four PC turns fighting from inside a worm's stomach.
- **Keegru's Roaring Applause hits a natural 20 on the save** — the worm keeps its reactions, and four minutes later uses **Fast Swallow** to eat Chthon'ar'ax too.
- Chthon'ar'ax bites his way out through Rupture 36. Cody: *"As this enormous dragon just bursts its way out."*
- Cody, three separate times, apologises for his own dice. **Graz takes all three kills and zero damage.**

### Act II — Two Dragons and a Rider (14:08 – 14:38)
- A Wyrmwraith, a Delight Dragon, and a **Reveler mounted on the dragon** — a Braggart Swashbuckler built as a mirror of Graz.
- Chthon'ar'ax grapples the Wyrmwraith; it **critically fails its Escape** and stays locked down, off-guard and frightened, until Tam deletes it.
- **Graz deals 238 damage in two actions** on the Reveler, including a nat-20 finisher for 174.
- The Reveler, the fight's Graz-mirror, gets **one attack all encounter and rolls a natural 1.**
- Jassik fails both dragon Will saves on nat 7s, spends a round Confused swinging at his own healer, and finishes the fight having dealt **zero damage.**
- Graz takes **11 damage all fight** — from Shuyinlo's own cold rune.

### Act III — The Battlemage and the Lich Legion (14:42 – 15:09)
- A Battlemage (AC 44) backed by the **Lich Legion** — three **troops**, AC 41, ~350 HP each in four segments.
- **The troops inverted the party.** Weakness 15 to area damage, resistance 15 to single-target physical Strikes, immune to bleed, and — Cody's ruling — **impossible to Demoralize.** Half of Graz's kit became illegal and the strikers got taxed on every hit.
- **Keegru, the healer, was the top damage dealer of the fight at 528** — ahead of Tam and Graz. His 7th-rank Fireball collected the area weakness twice on one roll for **183 damage** and killed Lich Legion B outright.
- **Shuyinlo etches Germantria, Rune of Partnership onto Graz** — a damage-sharing rune that splits everything Graz takes onto himself. *"I look forward to getting the shit kicked out of me."* It rides Graz through this fight and into the next, gets consumed mid-encounter-4, and is re-etched and then moved off him early in the finale.
- Shuyinlo builds his portal network: *"This is pure setup, and I am nothing if not on theme... So I can make fucking portals around."* **He never gets to use it** — the fight ends one initiative slot before his round-3 turn.
- **Graz Tumble Throughs into the Battlemage** (critical success by +16), which grants panache *and* off-guard through Tumble Behind, and immediately lands two critical hits for **162 and 149 — 311 damage in one turn, boss dead.**
- **Graz makes six saving throws in this fight and critically succeeds all six** — three Reflex, three Will, including two natural 20s. He takes **zero damage**, so the new damage-share rune never fires once.
- **Graz activates Foresight** for the first time at 15:03:14.
- Chthon'ar'ax chases the teleporting Battlemage with a Long Jump that crits by +32 — and leaves the party standing in four Falling Stars bursts. Cody: *"who left you all to get nuked. What a jerk."*
- **Tam's Aid ends the encounter**, converting Chthon'ar'ax's Miss-by-1 into the kill on the last troop.
- Keegru's Garden of Healing, on his own assessment: *"It's just a stupid strong spell."*

### Act IV — The Adamant Sentinels and the Norn (15:27 – 16:17)
The longest fight of the day — **50 minutes, four rounds**, and the first one Cody had genuinely tuned up.
- Four **Adamant Sentinels** run at **AC 45 / ~345 HP** (book: 42 / 255), resistant to physical, **immune to mental** — which switched Graz's entire Demoralize engine off. Cody, immediately: *"Unfortunately, they are mindless."*
- The **Norn** runs at **AC 47 / ~650 HP** (book: 46 / 375) with cold-iron-gated regeneration nobody could turn off.
- She opens with rank-10 **Tear the Veil**, DC 47 Fortitude. Jassik, Chthon'ar'ax and Tam all fail; Graz and Keegru succeed; **Shuyinlo critically succeeds.** Then the Norn uses **Shift Fate** to reverse Keegru's success into a failure for 48 damage — so three saves became two.
- Three Sentinels vent 30-foot fire cones back to back for ~323 damage. Cody, narrating the party's afternoon in two words: *"Getting crispy."*
- **Phantasmagoria confuses Chthon'ar'ax and Shuyinlo into friendly fire.** Round 2 is the low point of the session.
- **Jassik's Reactive Strike disrupts the Norn's Snip Thread** — Cody flags the manipulate trait himself, Jassik crits for 102, and her 125-point void death effect never resolves. The highest-leverage single action of the gauntlet.
- **Jassik kills the Norn** on a swing Foundry scored as a miss, after Cody works out the software wasn't applying her prone off-guard.
- **Graz takes three of the five knockdowns**, all three set up by a Tumble Through — crits of 108 and 127, and a persistent-fire tick.
- **Sentinel A silently falls out of the Foundry initiative tracker for rounds 2 and 3.** Graz spots it and tells Cody at 16:07:18. Nobody went down in this fight, and a quarter of the enemy melee going missing for two rounds is part of why.

### Act V — The Champions of Gorum (16:20 – 17:19)
The finale. Three champions: **Sword Master** (AC 47→48, ~600 HP), **High Cleric** (AC 47), **Monk** (AC 46).
- **The first ninety seconds decide the fight.** Graz's Battle Cry Demoralize **crits at initiative** → Frightened 2 → the Sword Master is off-guard to **Tam's Dread Striker**. Tam deals **316** (a 148 crit plus a 168 amped Imaginary Weapon); Graz follows with **301** across two Perfect Finisher crits. **617 damage in two consecutive PC turns, before a single enemy acted.**
- **Gorum's Major Boon**: heal to half and become Doomed 1 whenever reduced to 0 — **three charges shared between all three champions.** The party believed it belonged to the Sword Master alone and focused him accordingly. **He ate all three.** The High Cleric and Monk got none and each died on their first drop. The wrong premise produced the right play: killing one enemy three times was cheaper than killing three enemies twice.
- **Graz's Hide fails against the Monk** — a 49 beat the Sword Master's DC 46 but not the Monk's ~51. The Monk walks over and opens a two-round beating.
- **Graz's Dazzled rider erases a critical Crashing Slam** on a failed DC 5 flat check. Cody: *"All right, because Dazzled. Wow."*
- **Dazzling Display**: three Demoralizes in one action, **all three critical successes** — one via Graz's only hero point of the day, one on a natural 20 — with **Exemplary Finisher** stripping the Sword Master's Demoralize immunity to make the third legal.
- **Shuyinlo moves Germantria off Graz** at 16:31:03. Graz eats 151 and 111 unsplit over the next two turns.
- **The Overwhelming Blow**: Cody calls the shot — *"if it would crit, it's a crit for max damage."* Graz: *"I think I'm going down."* **Shuyinlo's Warping Pull reaction** and a cold damage roll of 118 out of a possible 184 leave him alive at ~14 HP.
- **Graz drops to 0** on a 39-damage **Pierce the Sky** ray. **Sacred Covenant** catches him at 1 HP + 50 temp — he never goes unconscious. The spell's sonic component also leaves him **Deafened for a round** — the condition sat on his sheet far longer only because nobody cleared it.
- **Chthon'ar'ax hits 0 three times**: two consecutive natural 20s from the Sword Master (Greater Fortification rolling a 4 both times, 194 damage), then a silent Orc Ferocity reaction nobody noticed, then genuinely unconscious — dropped by a **Reactive Strike while attempting Battle Medicine on himself.**
- **Keegru revives him** with a 10th-rank Heal through Echoing Channel.
- **Graz kills the Sword Master** at 17:07:26 with a 181-damage Perfect Finisher crit — *"oh my god, it's a crit. Well, they stay down."*
- **Jassik kills the High Cleric.** **Graz kills the Monk** on a nat 20 chained straight into a second crit, 151 then 143.
- **Tam takes zero damage the entire encounter.**

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — 3× Magma Worm · *Trivial* · 3 rounds, 31 minutes

**Enemies**: Magma Worm ×3 — level 18, AC 40, ~476–515 effective HP (roughly +20–25% over book). Fire-immune, cold-weak ~12. Fire Breath 18d6 DC 41, Swallow Whole, Rupture 36.

**Initiative**: Graz 60 · Tam 56 · *Worm B 48* · Jassik 47 · Keegru 45 · Shuyinlo 42 · *Worm C 41* · Chthon'ar'ax 35 · *Worm A 34*

**Round 0 — the free actions.** Graz's Battle Cry Demoralize **crits by +12** on Worm B → Frightened 2, with Antagonize flooring it at 1. Chthon'ar'ax crit-Demoralizes the *same* worm a beat later — Graz: *"Oh, yeah, it's already Demoralized, so... Shit."*

**Round 1.**
- **Graz** — Stride (Mobility), **Illimitable Finisher** into Worm B. AC 40 → 38 (frightened) → **36** (Surprise Attack off-guard). `2d20kh + 38 = 50`, **crit by +14**, **157 applied**. Free second finisher hits for 70. Hides on a critical success. **227 damage, round one.**
- **Tam** — discovers he has **no bolts** at level 20; Cody hand-adds ammunition. Then **Eldritch Shot** crits for 104 + a heightened Gouging Claw for 70.
- **Graz reaction — Opportune Backstab.** Cody warns it reveals his hiding spot. He takes it anyway: **crit by +12, 114 applied.** *"And the first of the worms falls."* **Worm B never took a turn.**
- **Worm C** bites Shuyinlo, **critically succeeds the grab, then Fast Swallows him.** Cody: *"that's a minor inconvenience for level 20."* Shuyinlo: *"Minor inconvenience for someone who's built around teleporting."*
- Two Fire Breaths land on Graz across the round. **Two critical successes. Zero damage.**

**Round 2.**
- **Graz** — Threatening Approach → Frightened 2 → finisher **crit for 171** → Illimitable second finisher **crit for 148**. **319 damage in one turn.**
- **Tam's Fake Out** Aids Jassik's opener and **upgrades a hit into a crit** worth 84. It is his first Aid of the session and he had to be reminded.
- **Graz's Opportune Backstab** kills Worm A. Cody catches a stray bonus on Graz's sheet mid-adjudication and downgrades the crit to a hit; it still lands the kill.
- **Keegru's Roaring Applause, DC 47 Will — the worm rolls a natural 20.** A plain success would have denied it reactions.
- **Shuyinlo, from inside the worm**, lands an **Engraving Strike** and invokes two runes at once for cold and a contained void cone.
- **Worm C crits Chthon'ar'ax** for 93 after his Greater Fortification flat check comes up a **natural 1**, grabs him, and **Fast Swallows him** with the reaction Roaring Applause would have denied.
- Cody: *"Yeah, I did not expect these guys to hit the way they're hitting. Math-wise, they should just not be, but I am rolling hot."* He had rolled 16+ on seven of eleven enemy d20s.

**Round 3.** Chthon'ar'ax bites out through Rupture 36 for 82. Graz finishes Worm C with a **crit for 122** and a follow-up for 66. *"And with that, the worm is down, Shuyinlo is free, and the first victory of the gauntlet goes to you all."*

**Kill credit**: **Graz ×3.**
**Damage**: Graz **907 (56%)** · Tam 380 · Jassik 181 · Chthon'ar'ax 84 · Shuyinlo 63 · Keegru 0.
**Damage taken**: Chthon'ar'ax 145 · Shuyinlo 93 · Jassik 81 · Keegru 64 · **Graz 0** · **Tam 0.**

### Encounter 2 — Wyrmwraith, Delight Dragon, Reveler · 3 rounds, 30 minutes

**Enemies**: Wyrmwraith (AC 42, ~417–488 HP) · Delight Dragon (AC 42, ~506 HP) · Reveler (**AC 43** — highest on the field, ~412 HP), mounted on the dragon but rolling separate initiative.

**Round 1.**
- **Chthon'ar'ax grapples the Wyrmwraith.** It attempts to Escape at −10 MAP and rolls **33 vs DC 48 — a critical failure.** It stays grabbed, immobilized, off-guard and frightened 2 until it dies. It lands one attack all fight and critically misses twice. Cody: *"Ooh, double nat 1s. Makes sense. I had to pay for the worms."*
- **Delight Dragon — Breath of Fresh Air**, 17d8 spirit, DC 42 basic Will. Shuyinlo eats the full **77** (*"That was... quite rude."*). **Graz critically succeeds on a nat 12 — zero damage, no slow.**
- **Graz's double finisher on the Reveler: 64 + 174** — the 174 on a **natural 20** that also applied Clumsy 1, Dazzled and persistent fire. **238 damage in two actions**, ~58% of the Reveler's HP.

**Round 2.**
- **Let's Play!** — 14d6 mental, DC 42 basic Will, Confused on a failure. **Graz critically succeeds on another nat 12.** Jassik fails on a nat 7 and spends his round **swinging at Keegru three times, missing by 1, 1, and 4.**
- Chthon'ar'ax burns **Perfect Clarity** to reroll — which **ends his Rage** — and is Confused anyway, then fails his fortification check against a 108-damage jaws crit. He loses his Rage, his round, and 170 HP in one exchange.
- **Chthon'ar'ax grapples the Reveler.** With its mount already moved, the rider is stranded. Cody: *"They are quite literally locked down."* It never acts again.
- **Shuyinlo kills the Reveler** with an invoked cold rune — converted by an **En- diacritic** into a 15-foot emanation that also catches three of his own party. Graz's only damage all fight is those 11 points.

**Round 3.**
- Cody: *"Are you aiding?"* Tam: **"Always."** The very next roll — **Graz's finisher on the Delight Dragon — is a normal hit that the +4 turns into a crit for 143.** Four Aids landed this fight; exactly one changed an outcome, and it was worth ~79 damage.
- **Graz kills the Delight Dragon with an Opportune Backstab reaction.**
- Keegru closes with Garden of Healing — **100d4 = 253 to each** of Chthon'ar'ax, Jassik and Shuyinlo. 759 HP from one roll.

**Kill credit**: Tam → Wyrmwraith · Shuyinlo → Reveler · **Graz → Delight Dragon.**
**Damage**: Graz **544** · Tam ~365–490 · Shuyinlo 210 · Chthon'ar'ax 14 · Keegru 2 · **Jassik 0.**
**Damage taken**: Chthon'ar'ax 170 · Jassik 112 · Shuyinlo 94 · **Graz 11 (friendly fire)** · Keegru 0 · Tam 0.

### Encounter 3 — Battlemage + Lich Legion ×3 · 3 rounds, 26 minutes

**The fight that inverted the party.**

**Enemies**: **Battlemage** (AC 44, ~452 HP, Falcata + Arrow Salvo) · **Lich Legion A/B/C** — level-18 **troops**, AC 41, ~350 HP each across four segments (~1,050 total). Their defensive profile broke the party's normal hierarchy in half:

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Weakness 15 to area damage** | applied *per instance, per troop* — even successful saves ate it |
| **Resistance 15 to single-target physical Strikes** | Graz's 63-damage finisher landed as 48; Chthon'ar'ax's jaws 58 → 43 |
| **Bleed immunity** | deleted Graz's Bleeding Finisher and every one of Tam's crit-spec bleeds |
| **Troops can't be Demoralized** (Cody's ruling) | switched off Battle Cry, Threatening Approach, Antagonize, Dazzling Display *and* Aura of Despair |
| **Segments** | each HP threshold shrinks the troop's footprint |

**Round 1.**
- **Shuyinlo etches Germantria, Rune of Partnership onto Graz** at 14:49:32 — the damage-share that rides him into the next two fights. *"I look forward to getting the shit kicked out of me."*
- He also builds the portal engine — Dimensional Cleavestone into Tear Rift, plus Define the Canvas. *"This is pure setup, and I am nothing if not on theme... So I can make fucking portals around."*
- **Chthon'ar'ax Long Jumps after the teleporting Battlemage** — critical success **by +32** — and pins it with a critical Grapple. It also pulls the party's only frontliner out of the cluster immediately before four 40-foot Falling Stars bursts. Cody names it: *"Everyone is subject to all of this, save for Chthon'ar'ax, who left you all to get nuked. What a jerk."*
- **Tam forgets to Aid.** Graz's second Strike rolls **51 against an effective AC 42 — one short of the crit line at 52.** The +4 would have been a critical hit. Tam catches it himself a minute later.

**Round 2 — Graz deletes the Battlemage.**
- **14:54:53–14:55:36**: Stride → **Tumble Through, critical success by +16** — which does double duty, generating the panache the finisher needs *and* applying off-guard through **Tumble Behind**. That −2 is exactly what turns a roll of 53 into a critical hit against AC 44 instead of an ordinary one.
- Two Perfect Finisher crits: **162, then 149. 311 damage in one turn, boss dead.** *"Oh my god, nat 20."* ... *"Just destroyed. That's my turn."*
- **14:57:24 — Keegru's 7th-rank Fireball.** One roll, two troops, a failure and a critical failure, area weakness collected on both: **66 + 117 = 183 damage from a single 14d6 roll**, killing Lich Legion B outright. Cody: *"He is gone. Wow."*
- Cody, finally saying it out loud: *"Well, really, I think they're a troop."*

**Round 3.**
- **Graz's kit is mostly illegal here** and he says so — *"Well, here comes the shit part for me."* He adapts, using Demoralize purely as a panache battery: *"I still gain my panache, even if it's immune."*
- He then spends a **Bleeding Finisher on a bleed-immune target** — 6d6 persistent, entirely wasted — and his next finisher **destroys the very segment he was standing next to.** Cody: *"You cannot, because you've eliminated their section. You're no longer adjacent."*
- **Tam kills Lich Legion C** for 132.
- **Tam's Aid ends the fight**: Chthon'ar'ax's jaws roll a **Miss by −1**, the +4 converts it, and Lich Legion A is destroyed at 15:08:45. *"Lich Legion is down. We are out of combat."*
- **Shuyinlo never gets a round-3 turn** — one initiative step below Chthon'ar'ax, and the fight ends on Chthon'ar'ax's action. **Tear Rift was never used once.**

**Graz's six saving throws — all successes, four at the critical tier, two natural 20s:**

| Effect | Save | Result | Cost of failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrow Salvo (6th) | Ref DC 41 → **nat 20 = 60** | crit success | 55 / 110 + Prone |
| Falling Stars, Plasma (9th) | Ref DC 40 → 59 | crit success | 83 / 166 |
| Phantasmagoria (9th) | Will DC 40 → **nat 19 = 55** | crit success | 51 + Confused / 102 + Confused 1 min |
| Falling Stars, Asteroids (9th) | Ref DC 39 → **nat 20 = 60** | crit success | 72 / 144 |
| Phantasmagoria | Will DC 39 → 41 | crit success | 53 + Confused |
| Phantasmagoria | Will DC 40 → 48, **Foresight spent** | crit success — *"Not confused."* | 58 + Confused |

**Kill credit**: **Graz → Battlemage** · Keegru → Lich Legion B · Tam → Lich Legion C · **Chthon'ar'ax → Lich Legion A** (on Tam's Aid).
**Damage**: **Keegru 528** · Tam 487 · **Graz 454** · Chthon'ar'ax 135 · Jassik 52 · Shuyinlo 43.
**Damage taken**: Keegru 133 · Chthon'ar'ax ~114–161 · Tam 107 (Confused for four minutes) · Jassik 88 · Shuyinlo 28 · **Graz 0.**

> **The healer out-damaged both dedicated strikers.** Area weakness 15, collected per instance per troop, meant Keegru's Fireballs and Divine Armageddon harvested free damage the melee could never match — while resistance 15 taxed every single-target Strike in the party. It is the one fight all session where Graz was not the top damage dealer, and the reason is a defensive stat block, not a bad turn.

### Encounter 4 — 4× Adamant Sentinel + the Norn · 4 rounds, 50 minutes

**Enemies**: Adamant Sentinel ×4 — **AC 45, ~345 HP** (book 42/255), resistant to physical, **immune to mental**, *not* immune to fire; Fist +38 for 4d12+22, Vent Flames 30-ft cone on a 1d6 recharge. **The Norn — AC 47, ~650 HP** (book 46/375), cold-iron-gated regeneration, Tear the Veil (rank 10), Phantasmagoria, Snip Thread (125 void + death rider, 3/day).

**Round 1 — eighteen minutes.**
- Graz opens with Battle Cry into a wall. Cody: *"Unfortunately, they are mindless."* His entire fear engine is offline for this fight.
- **Tear the Veil**, DC 47 Fortitude, party-wide. Jassik, Chthon'ar'ax and Tam fail; Graz and Keegru succeed; **Shuyinlo critically succeeds.** The Norn then spends **Shift Fate** to flip Keegru's success into a failure for 48.
- **Three Vent Flames cones back to back** — ~323 fire damage. Cody: *"Getting crispy."* And, helpfully: *"Funnily enough, they're not immune to fire."*
- Chthon'ar'ax's entire round produces **zero damage**: Grapple, Crushing Grab resisted to 0, a critically successful Whirling Throw resisted to 0. A grappler against four physically-resistant constructs is a hard counter.

**Round 2 — the low point.**
- **Phantasmagoria** confuses Chthon'ar'ax and Shuyinlo. Shuyinlo crits Chthon'ar'ax for 10; Chthon'ar'ax's jaws swing back at Shuyinlo repeatedly and only his transposition reaction keeps them off. Cody's read: *"One round instead of a whole fight. I'd say that's a worthy one."*
- Shuyinlo's **Annihilating Compound Invocation** — four runes, four traditions, a full two-action setup — nets **45 damage into a 345 HP construct**, and consumes the traced resistance-debuff rune that was the actual right answer. It also costs Graz his damage-share rune.
- **Sentinel A silently drops out of the initiative tracker** and takes no turns in rounds 2 or 3.

**Round 3.**
- Shuyinlo's contingency invisibility fires at half HP; he is never targeted again.
- **Graz's double-finisher, 16:00:21–16:01:03**: Tumble Through (crit success) → finisher **crit for 108, killing Sentinel B** → panache refreshes → **nat-20 finisher for 136.** *"Wow, that's a shitload of damage."*
- **16:02:20 — Jassik's Reactive Strike disrupts Snip Thread.** Crit for 102. Cody: *"There we go. She does not get to do that. That is her turn."* The Norn's death effect never resolves once all fight.

**Round 4.**
- **Tam's Transform Ammunition crit knocks the Norn prone** and deals 188 in a turn.
- **Jassik kills the Norn** at 16:05:40 — the swing reads as a miss until Cody works out Foundry wasn't applying her prone off-guard. *"That is a kill on her."*
- **Chthon'ar'ax: "Save me, Protector Tree."** Cody: *"Well, first see if you can save yourself."* Then: *"Protector Tree did not save me."* The tree still absorbed 70 of a 131-point crit.
- **Graz kills Sentinel A** with a nat-20 finisher for 127 (*"This one's down"*), and **Sentinel C** falls to his persistent fire.
- **Keegru kills Sentinel D** with a critical Warshard Shot. *"With that, that is the penultimate fight."*

**Kill credit**: **Graz ×3** · Jassik → the Norn · Keegru → Sentinel D.
**Damage**: Graz **~988** (five crits, two natural 20s) · Tam ~472 · Jassik ~438 · Chthon'ar'ax ~220 · Shuyinlo ~45 · Keegru 40.
**Damage taken**: Chthon'ar'ax **~675** · Keegru ~253 · Shuyinlo ~241 · **Graz ~139** (hidden three straight rounds) · Jassik ~129 · Tam ~67.
**Healing**: Keegru 491 · Shuyinlo 137 · Protector Tree 70.
**Nobody dropped.**

### Encounter 5 — The Champions of Gorum · 5 rounds, 56 minutes

**Enemies**: **Sword Master of Gorum** (AC 47→48, **~600 HP**, Crashing Slam, Overwhelming Blow, Reactive Strike) · **High Cleric of Gorum** (AC 47, 449–544 HP, Divine Armageddon, Pierce the Sky, Divine Decree, *Inviolable* retaliation shield) · **Monk of Gorum** (AC 46, 430–572 HP, Perception DC ~51, Flurry, Inner Upheaval, Stand Still).

**Gorum's Major Boon** — *"Whenever you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points, you are instead healed to half your maximum Hit Points and become Doomed 1."* **Three charges, shared between all three champions.**

**Round 0.** Graz's Battle Cry Demoralize on the Sword Master: **critical success by +18 → Frightened 2 + panache.** Chthon'ar'ax's Battle Cry frightens the Monk. Graz **delays** to act just after Tam.

**Round 1 — 617 damage before an enemy acts.**
- **Tam**, with the Sword Master off-guard via **Dread Striker**: arbalest **crit for 148** (One Shot One Kill, Sneak Attack, Backstabber, Slinger's Precision, Crossbow Crack Shot — plus prone and Stupefied 1 riders), then an amped **Imaginary Weapon for 168**. **316 in one turn.**
- Graz, watching: *"Oh my god, what the hell? It's over 300 damage."*

## Pax's Notes — Graz's Journal

*Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.*

### What Graz did this session

- Opened every fight the same way and it worked every time: **Battle Cry Demoralize at initiative**, free action, before anyone moved. Critically succeeded on it in the finale and that one roll produced 617 damage in two turns.
- Killed **ten enemies** — all three Magma Worms, the Delight Dragon, the Battlemage, three Adamant Sentinels, the Sword Master of Gorum, and the Monk of Gorum.
- **Missed one attack all day.** Forty swings, twenty-three crits.
- Used **Tumble Through six times and critically succeeded six times.** It was the fallback whenever fear was illegal — against the mindless Sentinels it was the entire off-guard engine.
- **Passed all thirteen saving throws**, ten of them critically.
- Spent **one hero point**, on a Dazzling Display reroll against the High Cleric. Turned a failure into a critical success.
- Hit **0 hit points once**, in the finale, and was caught by Keegru's Sacred Covenant before going down.

### Things Graz knows that the party does not

- **The Foresight reaction can be pointed at the enemy, not just at himself.** He spent the whole gauntlet using it to reroll his own saving throws. Its other half makes an attacker roll twice and take the *lower* — against a called-shot Overwhelming Blow that would have been the better use, and nobody at the table knew it was on the menu.
- **Moving Germantria off him is what got him killed.** Shuyinlo shifted the damage-share rune to Chthon'ar'ax at a reasonable moment. Graz then ate 151 and 111 unsplit and went to zero on a 39-point ray. The rune was worth more than it looked.
- **His Hide is not universal.** A 49 beat the Sword Master's Perception DC of 46 and lost to the Monk's ~51. Legendary Sneak didn't fail — the Monk was simply out of reach of it, and walked over and opened a two-round beating because of it.
- **The Dazzled rider on his finisher erased a critical Crashing Slam.** Not the frightened, not the AC — a DC 5 flat check. The best defensive moment of his night came from a condition he applies as an afterthought.
- **Exemplary Finisher is doing more work than the damage feats.** Without it, Demoralize is once per enemy per ten minutes and the whole loop dies after one turn. With it, every finisher hit resets the clock.

### What Graz is thinking about

- **Fortitude is still the hole, and it never got properly tested.** Three saves, cleared by +6, +4 and +2 — all successes, none upgraded, because that axis has no clause. Reflex and Will each converted five saves into critical successes on the same nights. The Norn's DC 47 was the only real probe and half the party failed it.
- **Tam is the multiplier.** Frightened 2 from Battle Cry turns straight into Dread Striker off-guard, and Tam's standing Aid turns normal hits into crits. Two of Graz's biggest turns exist because Tam was doing something on someone else's initiative.
- **Fear got switched off twice, by two different mechanisms, and the fallback held both times.** The Adamant Sentinels were mindless; the Lich Legion were troops Cody ruled immune to Demoralize outright. Tumble Through and Legendary Sneak carried both fights. Worth remembering under the worst case — but also worth noting he still dealt 454 and 988 in those two fights.
- **Against troops the whole striker chassis is the wrong tool.** Resistance 15 to single-target physical, immune to bleed, four HP segments, and a footprint that moves when a segment dies — a finisher literally removed the square he was standing next to. Keegru's Fireballs out-damaged him. If troops show up again, the third action is better spent somewhere other than a Bleeding Finisher.
- **Opportune Backstab earned its slot.** It killed three enemies outright — both surviving Magma Worms and the Delight Dragon — including one where giving up the hidden position was the explicit cost and it was still correct.
- **Three actions is rarely three attacks.** The turns that produced the most damage were Tumble Through → finisher → chained finisher, or Demoralize → finisher → chained finisher. The finisher trait shuts off attacks afterwards; the third action was always setup, Hide, or Shield.

- Is there a way to raise Fortitude that isn't blocked by Greater Resilient's item bonus and Heroism's status bonus? Nothing found before this session; nothing tested during it.

- The Monk of Gorum's Perception was roughly 51. What tier of enemy makes Legendary Sneak stop working entirely, and how common is that at this level?
