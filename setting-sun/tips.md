# Tips — Tivael (Bard/Sorcerer)

## Daily Preparation Checklist

1. Cast **Wand of Tailwind** (rank 2): +10 ft speed for 8 hours
2. Cast **Wand of Pocket Library** (rank 1): +1 status to RK checks for 24 hours
3. Invest spell slot into **Pipes of Compulsion** for staff charges
4. Carry **silver material** for Needle Darts
5. Carry **Bottled Sunlight** (shake for bright light OR throw as bomb)
6. Carry **wooden stakes** for finishing unconscious vampires

## Action Economy Framework

### Round 1 (Always)
- **Free**: Lingering Composition
- **1A**: Composition cantrip (Courageous Anthem default, Dirge of Doom if within 30 ft of intelligent enemies)
- **1A**: Recall Knowledge (Bardic Lore, Wand of Pocket Library bonus)
- **1A**: Bon Mot (soften target's Will by -2)

### Rounds 2+ (Lingering active)
Three free actions. Pick based on situation:

**Debuff turns:**
- Infectious Ennui (2A, Will, 60 ft) + Bon Mot (1A)
- Worm's Repast (2A, Fort, 30 ft) + Bon Mot (1A)
- Befuddle (2A, Will, Clumsy + Stupefied) + move (1A)

**Damage turns:**
- Electric Arc (2A, 2 targets, Reflex) + Bon Mot (1A)
- Biting Words R4 initial cast (2A) + Bon Mot (1A), then 1A per round for follow-up attacks
- Silver Needle Darts (2A) + Bon Mot or Shield (1A)

**Support turns:**
- Summon Fey (3A, full turn, setup investment) — then 1A Sustain on following rounds
- Sustain Unicorn (1A) + Electric Arc (2A) or Bon Mot (1A) + something (1A)

**Emergency turns:**
- Sanctuary on self (2A) + Shield (1A) — enemies must save to target you, only use non-hostile actions after
- Cloak of Illusions hood up (2A) + Warp Step escape (2A)... wait, that's 4A. Pick one.
- Hidebound (reaction, no action cost) — use when ally takes physical hit

## When to Use Each Composition

| Composition | Use when... |
|-------------|------------|
| **Courageous Anthem** | Default. Party needs to hit harder and end fights faster. |
| **Rallying Anthem** | Heavy AoE incoming (Lightning Bolt, Divine Wrath). Party needs defense over offense. |
| **Dirge of Doom** | Within 30 ft of intelligent enemies. -1 to everything, no save. Don't use against mindless or fear-immune. |

## Save Targeting Guide

After Recall Knowledge identifies the weakest save:

| Weakest Save | Your Best Spell |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Will** | Bon Mot (-2 Will) → Infectious Ennui (Slowed + Frightened) or Phantom Pain (Sickened + persistent mental) |
| **Fort** | Worm's Repast (Off-Guard + persistent piercing + undead bonus) or Frostbite (cold + bludgeoning weakness on crit fail) |
| **Reflex** | Electric Arc (2 targets, electricity) |

## Damage Type Selection

| Enemy Type | Best Damage |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Vampires (physical res 5)** | Silver Needle Darts, Dragonet Breath (mental/silver), Electric Arc, Biting Words (sonic) |
| **Mindless undead** | Electric Arc, Frostbite, Worm's Repast, silver Needle Darts |
| **Mental weakness enemies** | Phantom Pain, Dragonet Breath, Daze (from Pipes) |
| **Incorporeal undead** | Summon Unicorn (Ghost Touch), Force Barrage... wait, no Force Barrage. Use Flurry of Claws (force component) |
| **Living enemies** | Full toolkit — all spells work |

## Summon Fey — Best Picks by Situation

| Creature | Level | When to summon |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| **Unicorn** | 3 | Default for vampire fights. Rank 3 Heal (37 HP or vitality damage to undead), Ghost Touch Strikes, Cleanse Affliction. |
| **Satyr** | 4 | When you need extra spellcasting. Has Charm, Fear, Suggestion, Courageous Anthem. 80 HP meat shield. |
| **Pixie** | 4 | When you need flying scout, Dispel Magic, or Entangling Flora for area control. AC 23, 45-ft fly. |

## Positioning

- **Safe range (35+ ft from enemies)**: Courageous Anthem reaches (60 ft), Commander banner doesn't, Dirge doesn't. Safe from melee.
- **Aggressive range (25-30 ft)**: Dirge of Doom reaches, most spells in range. Airborne at 25 ft altitude if possible.
- **Danger zone (<20 ft)**: Only go here for Dragonet Breath cones or emergency Sanctuary.
- **Always**: Stay behind Bargrum's Intercept Attack range (10-15 ft from him).
