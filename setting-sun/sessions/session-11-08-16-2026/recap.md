## Session Summary

This was the session Danger Island collected its dues. Seven fights in four hours, and for the first time in the campaign the party lost — twice, to rival teams that were simply better, and a third time to something no team on the island is meant to beat at all.

The morning started deceptively well: a sweep of quiet hexes (an abandoned saurian ranch, a druid-circle base, the eerily vacated **Temoutan toy village**, the first **transport tower**), then a clean, bloody win over a den of five carnivorous **Dromornis** terror-birds — 200 platinum from Ingdani, and the debut of Sovael's new Living Nexus toy, a 60-foot force beam that also introduced the concept of friendly fire to Seva. Then the day turned. **Under the Pale Sun** — two katana masters and two archers whose Rain of Arrows crit the whole backline at once — ground the party down until the DM's gentle off-ramp ("weigh your options") became unavoidable, and the party forfeited its first feather. Reconnoitering the very next cliff face produced **Jaiban, the Lord of the Island** — a titanic croc-saurian with an AC in the 40s whose one-action stomp is a literal Earthquake. The party used the group-escape rule for the first time and ran for their lives, Sovael shouting "I'll take revenge!" as he flew away.

It got worse before it got better. The next team encounter was **the Lightkeepers** — Syu Tak-nwa, Shino Hakusa, Blue Viper, and Ran-to, a squad so far above the party's weight that the DM apologized for "punching down." Ferrok took a dose of Cerulean Scourge, and when Blue Viper held up the antidote and suggested trading it for the feather before Ferrok's organs melted, Sattva spoke for everyone: *"I like my organs. I'll take that deal."* Down to three feathers, the comeback finally started: a convincing kill on a **Caustic Monitor** (300 platinum), a genuine first team win over **the Nightmares** behind back-to-back Divine Wraths, and a grinding, ugly victory over **the Last Breath**, whose monk knocked Sovael out twice — critting his unconscious body in between — before ending the day surrounded and forfeiting. Back to five feathers — exactly where the day started, but with two dens cleared, half the island mapped, a mountain of platinum, and one very personal grudge against a kaiju.

## Key Events

### Act I — Rules Refresher & the Quiet Hexes (0:00 – 0:21)

- Three real-world weeks since S10, so the DM re-briefed the qualifier: 10 feathers by end of Day 3, active hours 6 AM–10 PM, 10-minute clock ticks, and the **group-escape rule** — if the whole party agrees and spends a round, you can flee any non-tournament fight.
- The team-encounter flat check is now **DC 18** — Sovael, checking his notes, caught the discrepancy and got the rule: **it rises by 1 with every tournament fight and partially resets each day.**
- The hex sweep found: another arena, an abandoned **saurian ranch** ("something massive must have been here"), a druid-circle team base, an old **oryx ranch**, an observatory base — and two standouts:
  - **The Temoutan Village** — a toy-scale town, fully inhabited-looking and completely empty, with a polite note: the villagers arranged with the Ruby Phoenix to leave, and request their village be left undisturbed.
  - **The first transport tower** — a rune-engraved teleportation platform. Every discovered tower links to every other; the party found a **second** one later in the session, activating the network.

### Act II — The Dromornis Den (0:21 – 0:49)

- First creature den of the day: a cave nest of **five Dromornis** — carnivorous terror-birds "a bit bigger than dodos... a cross between dodos and ostriches" that hit like trucks and left persistent bleed on nearly every strike.
- Clean win, detailed below. **Ingdani paid 200 platinum** — den rewards scale with how many dens you've cleared, not how dangerous the occupant was.
- **Sovael's rebuild debuted**: Absorb Magic (inhaling a nearby spell to become "overflowing"), then **Aether Beam** — *"I just want to use my new toy. Sorry."* The 60-foot force line clipped Seva for 32 on a failed save. She was gracious about it. Mostly.

### Act III — Under the Pale Sun (0:49 – 1:18) — FIRST LOSS, feathers 5 → 4

- At a lighthouse base, a team of four challenged for one feather: **Under the Pale Sun**, two katana swordsmen (Swift Blow quick-draws; **Dueling Expertise** +2d10 against isolated targets) and two archers whose **Rain of Arrows** applies one attack roll against every AC in a burst.
- The archers' volleys crit repeatedly — 65 damage to three PCs at once, then 63/63/57 — while the swordsmen quick-drew into the melee. Clutch ate an 89-damage katana crit. Sovael was cut down to single digits — *"Oh my god, nine to ten. Are you dead dead?"*
- The DM offered the resource-management off-ramp twice; Ferrok called it — *"It definitely seems like we're going to lose"* — and the party waved the proverbial white flag. **First feather lost.**
- The aftermath was pure character: Sattva asked the victors how they came up with their name (an overcast battlefield — "the Battle of the Pale Sun"), Seva seethed — *"So they beat us, and they have a cool name, and a backstory. Be off with you!"* — and Ferrok noted that naming yourselves after your first battle would make the party **"Sandy Ragequit."**

### Act IV — Jaiban, the Lord of the Island (1:22 – 1:34) — ESCAPED

- The very next hex's "creature den" was a waterfall grotto littered with giant reptile bones — and **Jaiban, legendary beast of Bonmou**, a titanic quickened croc-beast. Sattva's Recall Knowledge crit-failed against a DC in the high 40s: *"I don't know what this is."* His arrows crit-missed against an AC over 40. Seva: *"Is that a fucking kaiju?"*
- Jaiban's **Quaking Stomp** — a one-action miniature Earthquake — hit the entire party (Ferrok and Sovael crit-failed the DC 41 save; the others' failures cost ~48, less for Clutch's resistance). Its jaws caught Clutch on a **natural 3**; its tail crit Ferrok for 90 through a shield block and sent the Minotaur flying — 171 damage into Ferrok in a single enemy turn.
- **The party used the group-escape rule for the first time** — everyone declared, the DM let Sovael throw a heal onto the battered Ferrok as they scrambled, and they ran. Sovael, flying away: **"I'll take revenge!"**
- Ingdani afterward: they won't be the first nor the last to face his wrath — *"the Lord of the Island... mayhap the most dangerous thing on this island."* The DM, out of character, on the party's pathing: *"You literally just made a fucking beeline right for Jaiban. You're like heat-seeking missiles toward the most dangerous place."*
- Post-fight, Sattva's Risky Surgery streak nearly finished what Jaiban started — two failed surgeries in a row that only cut the badly wounded **Ferrok** deeper (down to roughly 1 HP on the operating table) until Sovael gave up waiting, pulled a Scroll of Heal, and did it himself.

### Act V — The Lightkeepers (1:56 – 2:26) — SECOND LOSS, feathers 4 → 3

- A team of four legends: **Syu Tak-nwa** (white-haired leader in violet robes, a giant cicada on her shoulder), **Shino Hakusa** (featureless beige gi, pressure-point striker), **Blue Viper** (Ysoki alchemist draped in grenades), and **Ran-to** — "the most jacked goblin any of you have ever seen," who opened with: *"Ah, fucking finally. All right, don't know who the fuck you are, don't care. Lightkeepers challenge you for two feathers."*
- The party talked the wager down from two feathers to one. Syu: *"If you would prefer one, we will yield to one."* Ran-to: *"Makes no difference to me. More fights in the end. All right, fuckers, let's do this."*
- The fight was one-way traffic, detailed below: DCs in the high 30s, nat-20 saves against Divine Wrath, a reactive-strike *grapple* that caught Clutch by the neck mid-Defensive-Advance, and **Cerulean Scourge** poison ticking in Ferrok's veins.
- **Blue Viper ended it holding up a bright-red vial**: *"Got this for your buddy. I'd suggest giving us that feather before his organs start melting."* Sattva: *"I like my organs. That was like a good deal. I'll take that deal."* The party forfeited — and Blue Viper tossed Ferrok the antidote anyway. Ferrok drank it without a second thought.
- **"That makes nine"** — the Lightkeepers left one feather short of locking in their tournament seat. The party's sore-loser exit interview (Seva: *"We are the Lightkeepers. Your name is uncool."* Blue Viper: *"Now you're just being sore losers."*) is already a classic.
- Ferrok, refusing the doom spiral: *"That's alright. This is setting our comeback arc on."*

### Act VI — The Market, the Mountain, and the Strange Village (2:26 – 2:33)

- A second **stone market** hex — the haul was liquidated and **60 platinum + 140 gold distributed to each PC**. Ferrok had a rules epiphany about **rune-grade materials** (low-grade silver caps at level-8 runes) and is now saving for an upgrade.
- **Mount Haminabu** revealed — the tallest peak on Bonmou. On Day 3, teams holding 10 feathers ascend it to lock in their tournament seat.
- A **Strange Village** of aggressively plain humans with sub-tournament gear stared the party down — "almost like giving you that mad dog look, daring you to approach." The party detoured around it. Unresolved.

### Act VII — The Caustic Monitor (2:33 – 2:46) — WIN, 300 platinum

- Third creature den: an enormous acid-drooling monitor lizard. It opened with a nat-20 bite on Clutch (93 damage) and an acid cone, and then the party took it apart — details below. **Clutch landed the kill.**
- Seva, watching it die efficiently: *"I wish our team fights worked that well."*

### Act VIII — The Nightmares (2:47 – 3:20) — FIRST TEAM WIN, feathers 3 → 4

- A team of four dark-armored skirmishers, paired wakizashis, moving like ninjas: **the Nightmares** challenged for one feather.
- **Sovael spent his first hero point of the day rerolling initiative** — *"I'm going to risk it... Well worth the investment"* — and used the won tempo to fire **back-to-back Divine Wraths**: the first crit-failed two Nightmares (sickened AND slowed), the second landed failures across everything in the burst. *"You know what they say, if it works, try to do it again."*
- The sickened/slowed debuffs visibly broke the Nightmares' Tumbling Attack game; the DM admitted the slows "saved your bacon." One Nightmare **bled out mid-turn** on Seva's wounding rune — a real death in a tournament of knockouts — and the last one standing, burning on her 81-damage Ignition crit, **forfeited**. First feather won back.
- The cost: the party's hero-point pool cratered — Ferrok burned his last on a Spellstrike reroll (it turned a 26 into a **43 crit**), and Sattva's went out with the line of the night: *"Last hero point gone. All of them spent on medicine checks."*
- Mid-fight bonus: after ten-plus sessions of fighting the automation, **Ferrok finally got Arcane Cascade working.** *"I figured it out!"*

### Act IX — Treasure at the Mountain's Base (3:23 – 3:26)

- The hex at Mount Haminabu's base held an old shrine with real loot: a **Candle of Truth**, a **golden spyglass** (~1,000 gp), and **three Greater Healing Potions**.
- Ferrok, inventorying the swan-boat tokens and the spyglass: *"We have a boat and a spyglass. We can be pirates."*

### Act X — The Last Breath (3:26 – 4:02) — WIN, feathers 4 → 5

- A team of three: a katana **swordsman** (same murderous Swift Blow/Dueling Expertise kit as the Pale Sun), an unarmed **monk** with a force Qi Blast cone, and a longsword **"mage"** who turned out to be a magus. The party debated wagering two feathers and settled on one — *"Slow and steady wins the race."*
- The roughest fight of the day, detailed below. The short version: **Ferrok walled the enemy magus off with Wall of Metal and killed him**; the enemy monk **knocked Sovael out twice** with prone-punishing flurries (and crit his unconscious body for good measure); the swordsman crit Sattva for 97 before **Clutch's hero-pointed crit dropped him**; and at the top of the final round, the monk — last one standing, surrounded, with the party actively healing — **forfeited.**
- **Back to five feathers.** Seva: *"Did we make any progress?"* The DM: *"And we met Jaiban."* Sovael: *"That's the highlight of the day."*
- 640 XP. No level-up — but the DM closed with a promise: *"Provided you survive whatever your next encounter might be, you will level up next session."* **Level 13 incoming.**

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Five Dromornis (Den #2) — WIN, 200 pp

**Enemies**: Dromornis A–E — carnivorous terror-birds. AC 27–28, ~175 HP, +24 to hit, beak (reach 10, 2d12+12 + 1d8 persistent bleed) and talon (agile, 2d8+12 + 1d6 bleed), Rushing Attack (stride + strike with bonus speed).

- **Round 1**: Clutch opened with Battle Cry Demoralize + Group Taunt; Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrike hit for 54. The birds rushed in — misses on Seva, a 27-damage beak into Sovael, a 27 into Sattva, and a nat-20 talon crit into Clutch ("That's the only way I could have crit you on that"). Sovael's standard opener: Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition (3 rounds) + Talent Envy Demoralize (nat 1 — the bird was unimpressed). Then Seva's crit Ignition Spellstrike left one bird at 2 HP and cooking on 6d6 persistent fire — *"You fucking bird brain die!"* — and it obliged, burning to death before its next turn.
- **Round 2**: The bleed economy set in — at one point Sovael, Sattva, Clutch, and Ferrok were all bleeding simultaneously. Clutch's Defensive Advance crit a second bird for **70**, Shock arcing 12 into its neighbor; Sovael debuted **Aether Beam** — 32 force into one bird (shoved 10 feet), 16 into another, and 32 into Seva's back, the only damage she took all fight. When that bird went for Seva anyway, Clutch intercepted *both* strikes, his new **Armored Counterattack** (level-12 feat) answering with a hit and a 50-damage crit — and Seva's dagger finished it: her second kill.
- **Rounds 3–4**: A beak crit came for Sovael — Clutch intercepted that too (33) and counterpunched, prompting the DM's Guardian PSA. Sattva's bow found its range — an 87-damage crit and a 55 crit back-to-back deleted his hunted prey — while Ferrok's sword crits (72, then 84) killed the last two birds outright. Clutch triaged himself mid-fight with Doctor's Visitation Battle Medicine (53).
- **Aftermath**: Kill ledger — Seva 2 (one by burn), Ferrok 2 (both crit finishes), Sattva 1; Clutch dealt 216 with zero kills, a third of it on reaction counterattacks. Sattva's Ward Medic mass-Treat Wounds (crit, 56 HP to four PCs at once) reset the party while Clutch self-treated for 60. Ingdani delivered 200 platinum.

### Encounter 2 — Under the Pale Sun — FORFEIT (feathers 5 → 4)

**Enemies**: 2 Pale Sun Swordsmen (AC 33, katana +29, Swift Blow quick-draw + bleed, Dueling Expertise +2d10 vs isolated targets, demoralize), 2 Pale Sun Archers (+28, Rain of Arrows — one roll vs every AC in a burst, Deadly d10).

- **Round 1**: Both swordsmen landed quick-draw crits — Swordsman B into Seva for 68 with stacked bleed, Swordsman A into Ferrok for 67. Sattva hero-pointed a Battle Medicine to stitch Seva back up (61). Archer A's first Rain of Arrows nat-1'd harmlessly — the last good news: Archer B's volley crit the whole backline, 65 each into Sattva, Sovael, and Seva (Ferrok's emergency shield blocked his to 46). Sovael layered Dirge of Doom and burned a **Scroll of Synesthesia (rank 5)** on Swordsman B — saved, one round of Clumsy 3/Dazzled — while Ferrok went Haste + Glass Shield and popped a potion patch.
- **Round 2**: Clumsy 3 didn't slow the katana — Swordsman B demoralized Clutch and crit him anyway for **89 (Dueling Expertise)** — but the AC drop bought Sattva a 60-damage crit and a follow-up hit that would otherwise have missed ("Clumsy came in clutch" — the DM). Archer A's second volley crit for 63/63/57; Sovael's entire turn was triage (rank-5 self-Heal, Prayer Beads on Ferrok) as the DM slid in both resource off-ramps mid-turn. Then Swordsman A went "for the healers" and crit **Sovael for 90 — 9 HP left.** *"Are you dead dead?"*
- **Forfeit at the top of Round 3.** Ferrok called it — the party had put 160 into Swordsman B (Sattva's bow doing 106 of it) and **never touched either archer**. Ingdani called victory for the Pale Sun. The archer's exit: *"May we meet again."* Full XP for the loss.

### Encounter 3 — Jaiban, the Lord of the Island — GROUP ESCAPE

**Enemy**: Jaiban — "CrocaMantis" kaiju. Perception +34, AC low-40s, Recall Knowledge DC ~48, permanently quickened, Jaws +37 (4d12+24, reach 20), Tail +35 (4d10+23, reach 25), **Quaking Stomp** (1 action, 1/minute — Earthquake, 11d6+4 bludgeoning, DC 41 basic Reflex, 100-ft range).

- **The only round**: Sattva's arrows crit-missed by 11 and 17 against its AC; his RK crit-failed. Jaiban — permanently quickened, so a four-action turn — stomped (Ferrok and Sovael crit-failed the DC 41 save: 96 into Ferrok, 84 into Sovael after his False Vitality temp HP; Seva and Sattva failed for 48, Clutch's resistance shaved his to 41), then strode, hit Clutch with its jaws **on a natural 3** (63 rolled, 56 through his resistance), and tail-crit Ferrok for 90 — 75 through the shield block. **171 damage into Ferrok in one enemy turn.**
- **The escape**: before the round even finished, the party unanimously declared the **group escape** — its first use ever. Sovael declared a parting heal on Ferrok as they scrambled (resolved moments later as the Scroll of Heal, once they were clear). Clutch's in-chat commentary on first seeing the thing: **"NANI!?"**
- No feathers at stake — wildlife. No shame either. *"Come back at a higher level."*

### Encounter 4 — The Lightkeepers — FORFEIT (feathers 4 → 3)

**Enemies**: Syu Tak-nwa (occult witch — Synesthesia DC 39, Telekinetic Projectile +31, Shield), Shino Hakusa (monk — Disorienting Tumble, +30 nonlethal cold-iron/silver fists, pressure-point strikes → Enfeebled 2 + flat checks on manipulate), Blue Viper (Ysoki alchemist — Quick Bomber, bombs +31, shortsword coated in **Cerulean Scourge**), Ran-to (goblin grappler — gauntlets +32, reactive-strike *grapple*, Whirlwind Toss, Escape DC 42).

- **Round 1**: Blue Viper feinted a sword charge on Ferrok, then point-blank crit him with bottled lightning (47) and put an acid flask into Sattva (15 + persistent acid; the splash caught Sovael). Ferrok's Spellstrike answer connected only on a hero-point reroll — to hit by exactly 0 — for 47, the mirror of what he'd just taken. Blue Viper, to his allies: *"Why can't I hit the fucking Minotaur?"* Sovael stacked the defenses (Anthem, Benediction, and a Demoralize that left Ran-to Frightened 1 — both his gauntlet swings at Seva whiffed). Shino tumbled clean over Sovael's head; Clutch Intercepted her follow-up (35). Syu landed **Synesthesia on Clutch** (36 vs DC 39) — Clumsy 3, Dazzled, one minute — and when Clutch tried to Defensive-Advance, **Ran-to's reactive-strike grapple caught him by the neck** (against Escape DC 42, there was no getting out).
- **Round 2**: Blue Viper's shortsword put **Cerulean Scourge stage 1** into Ferrok — 31 poison on a failed save. Ferrok weighed it out loud — *"Do I want to burn stuff on an unwinnable fight?"* — and fired Chain Lightning anyway; the first link crit-saved and the chain died. Sovael's Divine Wrath met a nat 20 and a nat 18 ("I have good dice too" — Seva, dryly) — 12 damage through, total. Shino's pressure point stacked Enfeebled 2 onto Clutch's Synesthesia through a rerolled save. Syu crit Seva with a Telekinetic Projectile for **52** — the biggest hit of the fight. Ran-to's **Whirlwind Toss** used Clutch as the weapon (Seva clipped, Clutch thrown five feet), Clutch Intercepted the follow-up gauntlet too — and Seva's Spellstrike finally tagged Ran-to for 30: *"How do you like that?"*
- **The ultimatum**: Round 3 opened with Blue Viper producing the antidote and pricing it at one feather. The ledger said everything: **~111 damage dealt (Syu and Shino untouched) against ~316 taken.** The party took the deal, Blue Viper tossed the vial over anyway, and Ferrok drank it on the spot. The Lightkeepers walked off at **nine feathers** — one from qualification.

### Encounter 5 — Caustic Monitor (Den #3) — WIN, 300 pp

**Enemy**: Huge acid monitor lizard — quickened stride, Jaws with Improved Grab and a Fort-save venom, talon rakes, Caustic Spray (cone, DC 36 basic Reflex).

- **Round 1**: Clutch's Battle Cry Demoralize landed at initiative (Frightened 1), but the monitor opened anyway with a **nat-20 bite on Clutch for 93** and an acid cone that caught Ferrok for 68 (Sattva crit-saved for nothing). Sattva's Recall Knowledge actually *failed* — the DM volunteered the profile anyway: best save Fortitude, worst Will, no weaknesses. Seva ran Doctor's Visitation triage on Clutch mid-fight, and Sovael's opener stacked the fear: Anthem, then a Talent Envy Demoralize upgraded to **Frightened 2**, then rank-3 **Roaring Applause** stripping its reactions (it saved — Roaring Applause denies reactions anyway).
- **Round 2**: With the fear stack and a flank discounting its AC, Ferrok — *"Ferrok is tired of getting beat up"* — landed a Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike **crit for 124**, the biggest single hit of the session. Clutch's Combat Grab locked it down; its Improved Grab grapple-back **crit-failed on a nat 1**, Clutch forced it prone, and all three of its desperate talon rakes missed.
- **Round 3**: Sovael's Fire Ray hit for 51; Seva mimicked the monitor's own slashing with a Gouging Claw Spellstrike; Sattva's arrows chipped it down — and **Clutch's Snagging Strike claw landed the killing blow.**
- 300 platinum from Ingdani — den rewards keep scaling.

### Encounter 6 — The Nightmares — WIN via forfeit (feathers 3 → 4)

**Enemies**: Nightmares A–D — four dark-armored wakizashi skirmishers. Tumbling Attack (stride through a PC's space without provoking; that PC is off-guard to the next strike), Kip Up, feint, sneak attack. Fortitude ~+20 — their soft spot. ~205 HP each.

- **Round 1**: Sovael's hero-pointed initiative (a 32 rerolled into 44) put him ahead of the pack — Anthem + Lingering (a failed Performance rerolled into 3 rounds — the Persona Mask earning its keep), then **Divine Wrath #1**: C and D both rolled nat 2s — **crit fails, 64 damage each, sickened 2 AND slowed**. The debuffs cashed out instantly: both slowed Nightmares missed their round-1 strikes by exactly the sickened −2. Ferrok drank his pre-held Potion of Quickness; Clutch's Battle Cry frightened C at initiative, then he tripped and grabbed it. B answered with a 70-damage sneak-attack crit into Sattva — the start of a fight-long fixation.
- **Round 2**: **Divine Wrath #2** — free, off the Holy Prayer Beads — and all three Nightmares in the burst failed; the whole team fought sickened from there ("the getting them slowed... definitely saved your bacon" — the DM). D's nat-20 Tumbling crit hit Ferrok for 71 (shield-blocked to 52), but **Clutch's Snagging Strike crit (56) dropped Nightmare C**, and when A crit Sattva for 97, **Clutch intercepted it for 90** — then intercepted A's follow-up meant for Sovael too, Armored-Counterattacking after each.
- **Round 3**: Ferrok's last-hero-point reroll turned a 26 into a **43 crit** — 122 damage in one turn — and **Sattva's point-blank "shoot D in the face" arrow finished Nightmare D**. A tumbled through Sovael and landed a nat-20 crit for 69 — and after the session's most sincere apology ("Sorry, Sovael, I thought you were 20 feet away"), **Clutch realized his Taunt let him intercept from 15 feet and ate that one as well** (62 — his third intercept of the fight, all from Nightmare A). Seva "avenged Sovael" with a 40-damage crit that left A bleeding on her wounding rune.
- **Round 4**: Sovael's Sure-Struck Fire Ray (46) set the ground burning under A; Sattva's last hero point saved a failed Battle Medicine ("Last hero point gone. All of them spent on medicine checks."); Clutch's claw left A at **exactly 1 HP**. A tumbled through Sattva for a last spite hit — and **bled out mid-turn on Seva's wounding rune**: "And then we'll pass out. Oh, he died." Seva then crit B with an Ignition Spellstrike — 81 damage and heavy persistent fire ("You might burn to death after that") — and **B forfeited**.
- **First team win of the qualifier.** Feather recovered. Sovael took zero damage; Clutch absorbed 169 for the party; Sattva quietly tanked 205 of Nightmare B's undivided attention.

### Encounter 7 — The Last Breath — WIN via forfeit (feathers 4 → 5)

**Enemies**: Last Breath Swordsman (katana +29, Swift Blow, Dueling Expertise — the Pale Sun kit), Last Breath Monk (Qi Blast — 11d6 force, 30-ft cone at 2 actions; Punch Down +2d10 vs prone; trip; nonlethal cold-iron/silver fists), Last Breath Mage (a magus — Quickened Casting Translocate, Flowing Strike, Zephyr Slip, **Disintegrate**, telekinetic boulder lob).

- **Round 1**: The monk opened with the 2-action Qi Blast cone — Sovael and Seva shrugged off half (21 each), but Sattva failed and was blasted into the rocks for 42. The swordsman's quick-draw whiffed off Seva's parry. Sovael, delaying behind the swordsman, ran the full control suite: **Dirge of Doom crit-lingered to 4 rounds**, Talent Envy crit-upgraded Demoralize (**Frightened 2** on the swordsman), and rank-6 **Roaring Applause** — both frontliners saved and lost their reactions anyway. **Ferrok walled the magus off with a 60-foot Wall of Metal** — so the magus Quickened a 1-action Translocate through it, wound up for a Flowing Strike into Ferrok, got ruled back (it's 3 actions), and spitefully fired **Disintegrate** instead: 66, halved to 33 on Ferrok's save. ("He said, fuck you." — Seva.)
- **Round 2**: The monk tripped Seva but whiffed both Inner Upheaval fists. The swordsman ran the long way to the isolated Sovael — Dueling Expertise online — and **crit him for 103**; Sovael sustained the Applause, walked away untouched through reaction-less enemies, and self-Healed 69. Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrike landed on the magus, who **Zephyr Slipped** away — and the slip provoked **Ferrok's reactive-strike crit (68 + burning)** on top of the full Spellstrike. Then the magus lobbed a **rank-7 telekinetic boulder** into the clumped party: Sovael nat-2'd the save (42, **knocked prone** — the prone that fueled everything after), Clutch went prone with 34 through a damaged shield, Seva saved.
- **Round 3**: The monk found the prone Sovael: **Punch Down flurry, 72 damage — down**, then crit the unconscious body to push him toward elimination. With the Applause sustain dead, the enemies got their reactions back. The swordsman **crit Sattva for 97**; Clutch intercepted the follow-up and Armored-Counterattacked. **Ferrok finished the magus** with a 47-damage swing ("one down"). Sattva's Battle Medicine (36) pulled Sovael back up — Wounded 1 — Clutch **Combat-Grabbed the swordsman**, and Seva's Dimensional Assault + Gossamer Blade left persistent force gnawing on him.
- **Round 4**: Sovael — still prone — spent his whole turn on others anyway: Healing Nexus (40, self) and a **rank-6 Heal for 80 on Sattva**: *"I might be knocked out after this, but at least I heal."* The monk's four strikes ended in a **nat-20 — down again** (tournament nonlethality doing heavy lifting). The swordsman unloaded on Seva and rolled his own nat 20 — an **88 crit that Clutch intercepted for 81**, countering with a 44 crit as the grab slipped.
