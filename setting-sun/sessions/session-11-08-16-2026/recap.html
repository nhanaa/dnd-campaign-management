Session Summary

This was the session Danger Island collected its dues. Seven fights in four hours, and for the first time in the campaign the party lost — twice, to rival teams that were simply better, and a third time to something no team on the island is meant to beat at all.

The morning started deceptively well: a sweep of quiet hexes (an abandoned saurian ranch, a druid-circle base, the eerily vacated Temoutan toy village, the first transport tower), then a clean, bloody win over a den of five carnivorous Dromornis terror-birds — 200 platinum from Ingdani, and the debut of Sovael's new Living Nexus toy, a 60-foot force beam that also introduced the concept of friendly fire to Seva. Then the day turned. Under the Pale Sun — two katana masters and two archers whose Rain of Arrows crit the whole backline at once — ground the party down until the DM's gentle off-ramp ("weigh your options") became unavoidable, and the party forfeited its first feather. Reconnoitering the very next cliff face produced Jaiban, the Lord of the Island — a titanic croc-saurian with an AC in the 40s whose one-action stomp is a literal Earthquake. The party used the group-escape rule for the first time and ran for their lives, Sovael shouting "I'll take revenge!" as he flew away.

It got worse before it got better. The next team encounter was the Lightkeepers — Syu Tak-nwa, Shino Hakusa, Blue Viper, and Ran-to, a squad so far above the party's weight that the DM apologized for "punching down." Ferrok took a dose of Cerulean Scourge, and when Blue Viper held up the antidote and suggested trading it for the feather before Ferrok's organs melted, Sattva spoke for everyone: "I like my organs. I'll take that deal." Down to three feathers, the comeback finally started: a convincing kill on a Caustic Monitor (300 platinum), a genuine first team win over the Nightmares behind back-to-back Divine Wraths, and a grinding, ugly victory over the Last Breath, whose monk knocked Sovael out twice — critting his unconscious body in between — before ending the day surrounded and forfeiting. Back to five feathers — exactly where the day started, but with two dens cleared, half the island mapped, a mountain of platinum, and one very personal grudge against a kaiju.

Timeline

Act I · Exploration · Rules Refresher & the Quiet Hexes (0:00 – 0:21)

Re-briefing, and a very empty stretch of island

  • Three real-world weeks since S10, so the DM re-briefed the qualifier: 10 feathers by end of Day 3, active hours 6 AM–10 PM, 10-minute clock ticks, and the group-escape rule — if the whole party agrees and spends a round, you can flee any non-tournament fight.
  • The team-encounter flat check is now DC 18 — Sovael, checking his notes, caught the discrepancy and got the rule: it rises by 1 with every tournament fight and partially resets each day.
  • The hex sweep found: another arena, an abandoned saurian ranch ("something massive must have been here"), a druid-circle team base, an old oryx ranch, and an observatory team base.
  • The Temoutan Village — a toy-scale town, fully inhabited-looking and completely empty, with a polite note: the villagers arranged with the Ruby Phoenix to leave, and request their village be left undisturbed.
  • The first transport tower — a rune-engraved teleportation platform. Every discovered tower links to every other; the party found a second one later in the session, activating the network.
Act II · Combat · The Dromornis Den (0:21 – 0:49)

Five terror-birds and a new toy

  • First creature den of the day: a cave nest of five Dromornis — carnivorous terror-birds "a bit bigger than dodos... a cross between dodos and ostriches" that hit like trucks and left persistent bleed on nearly every strike.
  • Clean win — full round-by-round below. Ingdani paid 200 platinum: den rewards scale with how many dens you've cleared, not how dangerous the occupant was.
  • Sovael's rebuild debuted: Absorb Magic (inhaling a nearby spell to become "overflowing"), then Aether Beam — "I just want to use my new toy. Sorry." The 60-foot force line clipped Seva for 32 on a failed save. She was gracious about it. Mostly.
Act III · Combat · Under the Pale Sun (0:49 – 1:18) · First loss · Feathers 5 → 4

The archers the party never touched

  • At a lighthouse base, a team of four challenged for one feather: Under the Pale Sun — two katana swordsmen (Swift Blow quick-draws; Dueling Expertise +2d10 against isolated targets) and two archers whose Rain of Arrows applies one attack roll against every AC in a burst.
  • The archers' volleys crit repeatedly — 65 damage to three PCs at once, then 63/63/57 — while the swordsmen quick-drew into the melee. Clutch ate an 89-damage katana crit. Sovael was cut down to single digits — "Oh my god, nine to ten. Are you dead dead?"
  • The DM offered the resource-management off-ramp twice; Ferrok called it — "It definitely seems like we're going to lose" — and the party waved the proverbial white flag. First feather lost.
  • The aftermath was pure character: Sattva asked the victors how they came up with their name (an overcast battlefield — "the Battle of the Pale Sun"), Seva seethed — "So they beat us, and they have a cool name, and a backstory. Be off with you!" — and Ferrok noted that naming yourselves after your first battle would make the party "Sandy Ragequit."
Act IV · The Kaiju · Jaiban, the Lord of the Island (1:22 – 1:34) · Escaped

Heat-seeking missiles toward the most dangerous place

  • The very next hex's "creature den" was a waterfall grotto littered with giant reptile bones — and Jaiban, legendary beast of Bonmou, a titanic quickened croc-beast. Sattva's Recall Knowledge crit-failed against a DC in the high 40s: "I don't know what this is." His arrows crit-missed against an AC over 40. Seva: "Is that a fucking kaiju?"
  • Jaiban's Quaking Stomp — a one-action miniature Earthquake — hit the entire party (Ferrok and Sovael crit-failed the DC 41 save; the others' failures cost ~48). Its jaws caught Clutch on a natural 3; its tail crit Ferrok for 90 through a shield block and sent the Minotaur flying — 171 damage into Ferrok in a single enemy turn.
  • The party used the group-escape rule for the first time — everyone declared, Sovael threw a heal onto the battered Ferrok as they scrambled, and they ran. Sovael, flying away: "I'll take revenge!"
  • Ingdani afterward: they won't be the first nor the last to face his wrath — "the Lord of the Island... mayhap the most dangerous thing on this island." The DM, out of character, on the party's pathing: "You literally just made a fucking beeline right for Jaiban. You're like heat-seeking missiles toward the most dangerous place."
  • Post-fight, Sattva's Risky Surgery streak nearly finished what Jaiban started — two failed surgeries in a row that only cut the badly wounded Ferrok deeper (down to roughly 1 HP on the operating table) until Sovael gave up waiting, pulled a Scroll of Heal, and did it himself.
Act V · Combat · The Lightkeepers (1:56 – 2:26) · Second loss · Feathers 4 → 3

Punching up at legends

  • A team of four legends: Syu Tak-nwa (white-haired leader in violet robes, a giant cicada on her shoulder), Shino Hakusa (featureless beige gi, pressure-point striker), Blue Viper (Ysoki alchemist draped in grenades), and Ran-to — "the most jacked goblin any of you have ever seen," who opened with: "Ah, fucking finally. All right, don't know who the fuck you are, don't care. Lightkeepers challenge you for two feathers."
  • The party talked the wager down from two feathers to one. Syu: "If you would prefer one, we will yield to one." Ran-to: "Makes no difference to me. More fights in the end. All right, fuckers, let's do this."
  • The fight was one-way traffic — DCs in the high 30s, nat-20 saves against Divine Wrath, a reactive-strike grapple that caught Clutch by the neck mid-Defensive-Advance, and Cerulean Scourge poison ticking in Ferrok's veins.
  • Blue Viper ended it holding up a bright-red vial: "Got this for your buddy. I'd suggest giving us that feather before his organs start melting." Sattva: "I like my organs. That was like a good deal. I'll take that deal." The party forfeited — and Blue Viper tossed Ferrok the antidote anyway. Ferrok drank it without a second thought.
  • "That makes nine" — the Lightkeepers left one feather short of locking in their tournament seat. The sore-loser exit interview (Seva: "We are the Lightkeepers. Your name is uncool." Blue Viper: "Now you're just being sore losers.") is already a classic.
  • Ferrok, refusing the doom spiral: "That's alright. This is setting our comeback arc on."
Act VI · Market & Mystery · The Mountain and the Strange Village (2:26 – 2:33)

Shopping, and a village that stares back

  • A second stone market hex — the haul was liquidated and 60 platinum + 140 gold distributed to each PC. Ferrok had a rules epiphany about rune-grade materials (low-grade silver caps at level-8 runes) and is now saving for an upgrade.
  • Mount Haminabu revealed — the tallest peak on Bonmou. On Day 3, teams holding 10 feathers ascend it to lock in their tournament seat.
  • A Strange Village of aggressively plain humans with sub-tournament gear stared the party down — "almost like giving you that mad dog look, daring you to approach." The party detoured around it. Unresolved.
Act VII · Combat · The Caustic Monitor (2:33 – 2:46) · Win · 300 platinum

The comeback starts with a lizard

  • Third creature den: an enormous acid-drooling monitor lizard. It opened with a nat-20 bite on Clutch (93 damage) and an acid cone — and then the party took it apart.
  • The fear stack (Clutch's Battle Cry, Sovael's Talent Envy Demoralize to Frightened 2) and Roaring Applause's reaction-stripping set up Ferrok's 124-damage Spellstrike crit, and Clutch landed the kill after dumping the monitor prone off its own nat-1 grapple.
  • Seva, watching it die efficiently: "I wish our team fights worked that well."
Act VIII · Combat · The Nightmares (2:47 – 3:20) · First team win · Feathers 3 → 4

The light-name curse is broken

  • A team of four dark-armored skirmishers, paired wakizashis, moving like ninjas: the Nightmares challenged for one feather.
  • Sovael spent his first hero point of the day rerolling initiative — "I'm going to risk it... Well worth the investment" — and used the won tempo to fire back-to-back Divine Wraths: the first crit-failed two Nightmares (sickened AND slowed), the second landed failures across everything in the burst. "You know what they say, if it works, try to do it again."
  • The sickened/slowed debuffs visibly broke the Nightmares' Tumbling Attack game; the DM admitted the slows "saved your bacon." One Nightmare bled out mid-turn on Seva's wounding rune — a real death in a tournament of knockouts — and the last one standing, burning on her 81-damage Ignition crit, forfeited. First feather won back.
  • The cost: the party's hero-point pool cratered — Ferrok burned his last on a Spellstrike reroll (it turned a 26 into a 43 crit), and Sattva's went out with the line of the night: "Last hero point gone. All of them spent on medicine checks."
  • Mid-fight bonus: after ten-plus sessions of fighting the automation, Ferrok finally got Arcane Cascade working. "I figured it out!"
Act IX · Treasure · The Mountain's Base (3:23 – 3:26)

An old shrine with real loot

  • The hex at Mount Haminabu's base held an old shrine: a Candle of Truth, a golden spyglass (~1,000 gp), and three Greater Healing Potions.
  • Ferrok, inventorying the swan-boat tokens and the spyglass: "We have a boat and a spyglass. We can be pirates."
Act X · Combat · The Last Breath (3:26 – 4:02) · Win · Feathers 4 → 5

The roughest fight of the day

  • A team of three: a katana swordsman (the same murderous Swift Blow/Dueling Expertise kit as the Pale Sun), an unarmed monk with a force Qi Blast cone, and a longsword "mage" who turned out to be a magus. The party debated wagering two feathers and settled on one — "Slow and steady wins the race."
  • The short version: Ferrok walled the enemy magus off with Wall of Metal and killed him; the enemy monk knocked Sovael out twice with prone-punishing flurries (and crit his unconscious body for good measure); the swordsman crit Sattva for 97 before Clutch's hero-pointed crit dropped him; and at the top of the final round, the monk — last one standing, surrounded, with the party actively healing — forfeited.
  • Back to five feathers. Seva: "Did we make any progress?" The DM: "And we met Jaiban." Sovael: "That's the highlight of the day."
  • 640 XP. No level-up — but the DM closed with a promise: "Provided you survive whatever your next encounter might be, you will level up next session." Level 13 incoming.

  • Clutch opened with Battle Cry Demoralize + Group Taunt; Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrike hit for 54. The birds rushed in — misses on Seva, a 27-damage beak into Sovael, a 27 into Sattva, and a nat-20 talon crit into Clutch ("That's the only way I could have crit you on that").

Round 2 — The bleed economy, and friendly fire

  • At one point Sovael, Sattva, Clutch, and Ferrok were all bleeding simultaneously. Clutch's Defensive Advance crit a second bird for 70, Shock arcing 12 into its neighbor.

Rounds 3–4 — Crit-cleanup

  • A beak crit came for Sovael — Clutch intercepted that too (33) and counterpunched, prompting the DM's Guardian PSA. Sattva's 87-damage and 55-damage crits back-to-back deleted his hunted prey; Ferrok's sword crits (72, then 84) killed the last two birds outright.
  • Aftermath: Clutch dealt 216 with zero kills, a third of it on reaction counterattacks. Sattva's Ward Medic mass-Treat Wounds (crit, 56 HP to four PCs at once) reset the party. Ingdani delivered 200 platinum.

Round 1 — Outclassed immediately

  • Syu landed Synesthesia on Clutch (36 vs DC 39) — Clumsy 3, Dazzled, one minute — and when Clutch tried to Defensive-Advance, Ran-to's reactive-strike grapple caught him by the neck (against Escape DC 42, there was no getting out).

Round 2 — The poison, and the biggest hit

  • Blue Viper's shortsword put Cerulean Scourge stage 1 into Ferrok — 31 poison on a failed save. Ferrok weighed it out loud — "Do I want to burn stuff on an unwinnable fight?" — and fired Chain Lightning anyway; the first link crit-saved and the chain died.
  • Sovael's Divine Wrath met a nat 20 and a nat 18 ("I have good dice too" — Seva, dryly) — 12 damage through, total. Shino's pressure point stacked Enfeebled 2 onto Clutch's Synesthesia through a rerolled save. Syu crit Seva with a Telekinetic Projectile for 52 — the biggest hit of the fight.

The ultimatum

  • Round 3 opened with Blue Viper producing the antidote and pricing it at one feather. The party took the deal, Blue Viper tossed the vial over anyway, and Ferrok drank it on the spot. The Lightkeepers walked off at nine feathers — one from qualification.

Encounter 7 · Tournament Bout · Win via forfeit · Feathers 4 → 5

The Last Breath

EnemiesSwordsman (katana +29) · Monk (Qi Blast, Punch Down) · Magus (Disintegrate)
Sovael's day103 crit taken · KO'd twice · healed everyone anyway
Kills / dropsFerrok → magus · Clutch → swordsman
OutcomeMonk forfeited, surrounded, nearly untouched

Round 1 — The wall and the spite-beam

  • The monk's 2-action Qi Blast cone — Sovael and Seva shrugged off half (21 each), but Sattva failed and was blasted into the rocks for 42. The swordsman's quick-draw whiffed off Seva's parry.
  • Sovael, delaying behind the swordsman, ran the full control suite: Dirge of Doom crit-lingered to 4 rounds, Talent Envy crit-upgraded Demoralize (Frightened 2 on the swordsman), and rank-6 Roaring Applause — both frontliners saved and lost their reactions anyway.
  • Ferrok walled the magus off with a 60-foot Wall of Metal — so the magus Quickened a 1-action Translocate through it, wound up for a Flowing Strike, got ruled back (it's 3 actions), and spitefully fired Disintegrate instead: 66, halved to 33 on Ferrok's save. ("He said, fuck you." — Seva.)

Round 2 — The 103, and the boulder

  • The monk tripped Seva but whiffed both Inner Upheaval fists. The swordsman ran the long way to the isolated Sovael — Dueling Expertise online — and crit him for 103; Sovael sustained the Applause, walked away untouched through reaction-less enemies, and self-Healed 69.
  • Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrike landed on the magus, who Zephyr Slipped away — and the slip provoked Ferrok's reactive-strike crit (68 + burning) on top of the full Spellstrike.
  • Then the magus lobbed a rank-7 telekinetic boulder into the clumped party: Sovael nat-2'd the save (42, knocked prone — the prone that fueled everything after), Clutch went prone with 34 through a damaged shield, Seva saved.

Round 3 — Punch Down, and one down

  • The monk found the prone Sovael: Punch Down flurry, 72 damage — down, then crit the unconscious body to push him toward elimination. With the Applause sustain dead, the enemies got their reactions back.
  • The swordsman crit Sattva for 97; Clutch intercepted the follow-up and Armored-Counterattacked. Ferrok finished the magus with a 47-damage swing ("one down"). Sattva's Battle Medicine (36) pulled Sovael back up; Clutch Combat-Grabbed the swordsman; Seva's Dimensional Assault + Gossamer Blade left persistent force gnawing on him.

Round 4 — "At least I heal"

  • Sovael — still prone — spent his whole turn on others anyway: Healing Nexus (40, self) and a rank-6 Heal for 80 on Sattva: "I might be knocked out after this, but at least I heal." The monk's four strikes ended in a nat-20 — down again (tournament nonlethality doing heavy lifting).
  • The swordsman unloaded on Seva and rolled his own nat 20 — an 88 crit that Clutch intercepted for 81, countering with a 44 crit as the grab slipped. Then Clutch hero-pointed his claw from a nat 2 into a 42-crit, 54 damage — and the swordsman dropped (~300 damage across the fight).

Final round — The forfeit

  • Having saved his Flourish for exactly this, Clutch's Doctor's Visitation strode through the fallen swordsman's square and crit the Battle Medicine that brought Sovael back — Wounded 3, still prone, still alive — while Seva shadow-stepped along behind him and patched herself. The monk — last of the Last Breath standing, nearly untouched but alone, surrounded, with the party actively healing — forfeited.
  • Feather won — back to five. Hero-point ledger at fight's end: Seva out, Sattva out, Ferrok out, and Clutch's last one spent.
