The party patched themselves up after the previous session's clockwork ambush and pushed deeper into the Azlanti prison's medical wing. What looked like a frightened survivor — a cowering woman in tattered robes among the malfunctioning stasis tubes — was a Lamia named Zatrine, hidden among three bandit accomplices (Aguilar, Ularette, Iverness) who had been freed when the pods failed. Ferrok opened with a crit Ignition Spellstrike chain on the bandit Iverness — 85 damage dropped him before he ever got a turn. Round 3, Ferrok unloaded a 108-damage Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike on Zatrine herself, and Bael denied Ferrok the killing blow round 4 with a one-action fire blast that made the DM groan: "And here comes Bael with a steel chair."

Past the cell block, a Clockwork Soldier and three Living Graffiti — peeled straight off the murals — ambushed them in a small chamber. Tivael survived being repeatedly punched by paintings, with the iconic line "Why'd you hit me, you stupid painting?" and a brand-new chosen tactic: insult enemies as part of a turn that already includes Recall Knowledge. With the medical wing cleared, the party climbed the spiral stairwell — and met four lesser Shadows and a Greater Shadow swirling down toward them. Bargrum couldn't intercept void damage. Raylta's heightened Moonlight Ray crit the Greater Shadow for 88 damage, vaporizing it.

After a long rest and the party's promotion to level 8, they ascended the final hundred feet into the Shadow Tower lobby. Three larger Clockwork Sentries — multi-armed, with grab reactions and pneumatic warhammers — proceeded to grapple Bargrum, Raylta, and Bael in succession before Bael's Lightning Dash chain finished them. A few doors deeper, the party heard Azlanti voices shouting "Hit me!" through a closed door — seven of Tokainen's Chosen playing blackjack. Bargrum opened with "Oh, no calls for alarm. What's the buy-in?" and rolled initiative when they refused. The session ended just after the fight cleared, when Ferrok kicked open a door instead of opening it normally and woke up a dormitory full of guards. "That's where we call the session, folks."

Timeline

Act I · Cleanup · The Medical Wing

Patching Up and Pushing Through

  • Session opened post-clockwork-fight. Ferrok drank a potion to skip another Treat Wounds risk; Bael invested an Aeon Stone of Vital Amplification (+1 HP per 10 healed). The DM and Bargrum spent five minutes nailing down its automation in Foundry.
  • The party found a deep pit full of giant frogs — content as long as the party stayed above. A magitek control panel went untouched: "If you push the wrong button, you're scared." A cryo chamber: "It's a cryo chamber like Demolition Man" (Bargrum).
  • Bael's first Lightning Dash deployment: a security gate required 5 badges; the party had 4. Bael declared "What if I turn into lightning and teleport?" and zapped past the trap. DM: "You're not really in physical form enough for it to register you."
  • A long-dead sailor's body in a corner — frost-blasted, not Azlanti, likely a Talmandor's Bounty prisoner who came through here months ago. No loot.
Act II · Combat · The Lamia Ambush

Zatrine the Lamia + Aguilar, Ularette, Iverness

  • A cowering woman in tattered robes called out "Go away! Go away!" and claimed to have woken up with everything broken. Ferrok declined to trust her: "They didn't put nice people in here."
  • Several armed figures sprang from behind the cells. The "prisoner" grimaced: "Impatient fuckers, aren't you all." She transformed into a Lamia (upper-half human, lower-half serpent) — name in-fiction: Zatrine. Surprise round — party initiative.
  • Aguilar's turn: tumbled past Bael, feinted (success → off-guard), struck (Bael Nimble Dodged the crit to a normal hit). Zatrine then cast Agonizing Despair on Ferrok — Will save failed, Frightened 1.
  • Ferrok's round-1 finisher: crit Ignition Spellstrike on the bandit Iverness for 62 damage (38 weapon-crit + 24 fire), followed by a normal Strike for 23 more — 85 damage dropped Iverness before he ever got a turn. Raylta: "Oh, that guy didn't get a turn. — Way to go, Ferrok."
  • Bael's two-action Four Winds repositioned Ferrok, Raylta, and Bargrum. Tivael placed his composition aura, cast Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition (success — 3 rounds), and Demoralized Ularette in Common (success).
  • Round 2: Tivael's silver Needle Darts (19) + Bael's blast (13) + Thermal Nimbus persistent fire (7) put down Aguilar. Raylta crit Needle Darts on Ularette ("low-rate thieves").
  • Round 3 BIG HIT: Zatrine cast Noise Blast (Ferrok failed save, 20 sonic). Ferrok burned Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike — crit on Zatrine for 108 damage: "Whatever glass was left on these containment bays just shatters and showers the floor."
  • Raylta double-tapped Heal scrolls on Ferrok (41 then 35) to keep him standing through Zatrine's Vampiric Feast. Bael's Demoralize on Zatrine — critical failure.
  • Round 4: With Zatrine at ~10 HP, Ferrok's Force Fang missed. Bael moved in and finished her with a one-action blast — kill stolen. DM: "And here comes Bael with a steel chair." Ularette cleared on cleanup.
Act III · Combat · The Living Murals

Clockwork Soldier + 3 Living Graffiti

  • The Soldier activated as the party entered the chamber. Three Living Graffiti peeled off the walls. Tivael was standing right by one when it started crawling out.
  • Tivael Recalled Knowledge: full constructs, best save Reflex / worst Fort, NOT mindless (Demoralize and Bon Mot work), construct immunities (bleed, death, sickened). The Clockwork Soldier in the room specifically had electricity weakness; the Graffiti's weakness was not exposed.
  • Iconic Tivael moment: hit by a painting, Tivael attempted Bon Mot — "Why'd you hit me, you stupid painting?" — and the failure made the joke. The painting is too 2D to be insulted by being called dumb. DM, deadpan as the painting: "Well, thank you, Captain Obvious." The painting then specifically targeted Tivael with paint as petty revenge.
  • Ferrok's pair of crit Ignition Spellstrikes ended Graffiti C (34 + 36 = 70 crit, plus 26 cleanup) and Graffiti A (32 + 32 crit + 17 persistent fire). "Burn up the painting."
  • Raylta's crit Needle Darts on Graffiti B for 38 damage — pinned to the wall but not destroyed; finished it off several rounds later. DM: "If he was on the internet, would he be on Pinterest after that?"
  • Bargrum's tank RP: with Ferrok flanked, "Bargrum will heroically stride up to interpose himself between and look up at Ferrok and say, stay behind me, I'll protect you." Ferrok, in character, walked through his legs.
Act IV · Combat · The Shadow Stairwell

Greater Shadow + Shadow A/B/C/D

  • The party climbed the spiral stairwell upward. Out of the dark, four lesser Shadows and a much larger quadrupedal Greater Shadow descended toward them. Music change.
  • Bargrum's helplessness moment: "I cannot intercept this damage because it is void. So good luck. Have fun, everyone." Raylta: "Shit. I'm shit out of luck. Welcome to my world. No intercepts in sight."
  • Shadow C stole Ferrok's shadow with Steal Shadow (Enfeebled 1). Raylta: "You have a really big shadow." DM, in NPC voice: "Big tasty shadow." Then breaking: "I have to admit, if something came up to me and said, 'you've got a real nice shadow,' I don't know if I'd know how to respond to that."
  • Tivael's shadow also got stolen: "Yeah, take it. I don't care. Take it. Like a little snake. The ugly shadow." Then Tivael RK'd: incorporeal undead, weak only to force, ghost touch, vitality, spirit, best Reflex / worst Fort, not mindless. Tivael landed a Bon Mot on a Shadow afterward — "the ugly shadow" insult stuck (-2 Will).
  • Bael's cross-PC follow-up: "Nice — okay, it has a minus two Will save, so I will follow up on Tivael's insult and try to intimidate it." Demoralize landed; then started Thermal Nimbus + fire blast (incorporeal resistance ate the damage).
  • First Dragonet Breath of the campaign — 20-ft cone (Inhale, Exhale!), mental + silver. Shadow C crit failed, B failed, C succeeded. Resistance ate the damage but the silver-mental debuff held.
  • Bael's Aeon Stone of the Crescent activation: "I'll grab one of the particles flying around my head... And I'll yell, Moonbeam attack!" 100-ft line, 4d12 spirit silver. Shadow B and C took 26 each; D saved for 13. Raylta: "What the fuck? I have Moonbeam. That's my thing!" Ferrok: "Bael is Moon Knight."
  • Raylta's revenge: "I'm not going to let you upstage me. I also cast Moonlight Ray on the one guy. On the one fucking guy at rank four." — crit on the Greater Shadow for 88 damage. Destroyed. Bargrum: "Nice moon laser."
  • Bael's Ash Strider through Shadow B — smoke-form polymorph overflow, 26 fire damage, destroyed. ("I'll turn into smoke and pass through B.") Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit on Shadow A for 14 force damage — destroyed (force bypasses incorporeal). Ferrok's Gouging Claw Spellstrike crit on Shadow D — destroyed.
  • Fight cleared. The stairs continued up another ~100 feet, into the Shadow Tower proper. The party leveled up to 8 and took a long rest before climbing.
Act V · Combat · The Lobby Sentries

3 Clockwork Sentries (Shadow Tower Lobby)

  • Climbing 100 feet up, the party entered a grand lobby: polished black marble, green-and-gold tiled walls, vaulted ceiling 40 feet up, two three-tiered fountains running clear, geometric crystal sculptures, and two life-size Azlanti sentinel statues flanking a garden of swords-and-polearms growing from the floor.
  • Their security badges did not work up here. Three much larger Clockwork Sentries activated. "Surrender your weapons and come quietly." They didn't wait for a response.
  • Sentry abilities: Tackle (stride+fist+trip), Sudden Seize (reaction grapple on manipulate/move), Pneumatic Swing (warhammer reach 10 ft), Knockdown rider. Electricity weakness 10.
  • Ferrok's level-8 power flex: "All right. I'll go big or go home, right?" — Conflux Spellstrike (Force Fang) chained into a Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike. Both hit; HTS exploited electricity weakness.
  • Tivael's first Fortissimo Composition of the campaign: rolled 31 — Success → Courageous Anthem bumped from +1 to +2 status for the party (Crit Success would've been +3).
  • Bael's Lightning Dash for 33 damage hit Sentry B — survived the pass. Bargrum and Raylta both grappled simultaneously by separate sentries with Sudden Seize.
  • Raylta tried to Heal Bargrum while grabbed — flat check DC 5, rolled a 2, burned a hero point, succeeded, healed 29.
  • Bargrum's biggest play: Intercept Attack + Disarming Intercept crit, wrenching a sentry's halberd off its arm.
  • Bael's second Lightning Dash for 50 damage ripped through and finished Sentry C — the kill: "Lightning that arcs across the ground instead of through the air :D". Ferrok's new Reactive Strike caught Sentry C on the move earlier in the fight.
  • Loot: 18 pairs of average manacles — the manacle joke begins.
Act VI · Combat · The Gambling Cult

7 Tokainen's Chosen (Lounge / Poker Game)

  • Bargrum looked for traps. Tivael listened at the door. Through it: Azlanti voices — "Hit me! Motherfucker! Take this one. Double or nothing." — Blackjack.
  • The party walked into a comfortable burgundy lounge. Seven humans of clearly Azlanti descent, all in matching uniforms, all with the same burn mark on their left cheek.
  • Bargrum's RP moment of the session: "Bargrum will just say, oh, no calls for alarm. What's the buy-in?" A Chosen replied after a beat: "Ah, maybe a few fingers of yours." Bargrum: "Seems like a steep price. Is that what everyone else is putting in the pot as well?" Another Chosen: "Just shut up and grab him. Tokainen's gonna want him." Roll initiative.
  • Bael went first (Chosen were on break, weapons sheathed). Narrating: "Nonchalantly raise a finger by his side, and you will see a small orange bead appear above his finger. He flicks his finger forwards, and as it flies, it just gets bigger and bigger and bigger until it explodes in the middle of the room in a 20-foot burst." — Solar Detonation, 6d6 fire + 2d6 vitality, hit all 7 Chosen for 8-34 damage each. Chosen C crit failed (blinded 1 min); others dazzled or saved.
  • The Tokainen Cataclysm Brand — once per hour, 60-ft range, 10-ft burst, 6d6 fire + persistent 1d4 fire on a failed save — turned the lounge into a fire pit. Two Chosen ignited the burst pattern; persistent fire latched onto Raylta and Tivael.
  • Raylta's "I failed again" run: failed three persistent-fire saves in a row, then accepted a hero point from Bael for one. "Let's see you watch me fail. Ah, I failed again. Oh, Bael."
  • Tivael played it safe — moved out of the burst pattern around a corner, cast Rallying Anthem, attempted Fortissimo (just-failed). Spent the rest of the fight as ranged anchor: Demoralize, Needle Darts, then Flurry of Claws.
  • Tivael's Flurry of Claws: non-crit prong on Chosen B for 27 damage finished B (softened by Solar Detonation + Lava Bomb + persistent fire). Crit prong on Chosen E for 60 damage only bloodied E — full HP, couldn't drop him.
  • Ferrok stretched reach + crit Ignition Spellstrike on Chosen A for 86 cumulative damage (kill). Bael's Lava Bomb (Eternal Eruption) hit 5 Chosen for 8-17 fire each. Bargrum used a hero point on a missed Strike; reroll connected.
  • Bargrum's second crit Disarming Intercept on Chosen F's glaive: "Oh, fuck your eyes!" Bael's birdie-Flying Flame closed out the fight.
  • Cards glued to the underside of the table — someone was cheating, classic.

Things Tivael knows that the party does not

  • "Big Tasty Shadow": When the Shadow stole Tivael's, it was too small for anything substantial. "Like a little snake" was literal.
  • Cataclysm Brand uniformity: All 7 Chosen had identical brands and identical 1/hour limits. Same magical signature on each cheek — they're branded as a batch. Either Tokainen herself does this or there's an apparatus.
  • Cards glued to the underside of the table: Bael spotted them; the others may not have shared. Someone in that lounge was cheating.

What Tivael is thinking about

  • Dragonet Breath recharge is 1d4 rounds: Inhale-Exhale! makes it significantly better, but mental-silver damage is wasted on most non-undead. Save for vampires + Nizca's vampires when they appear.
  • Fortissimo + Courageous Anthem stacking is the new default opener for big fights. Critical Success = +3, Success = +2 (today's result). Pair with Lingering and the party gets a multi-round +2 attack/damage/fear-save buff.
