## Session Summary

The party patched themselves up after the clockwork ambush at the end of last session and pushed deeper into the Azlanti prison's medical wing. What looked like a frightened survivor — a cowering woman in tattered robes among the malfunctioning stasis tubes — was a Lamia named **Zatrine**, hidden among three bandit accomplices who had been freed when the pods failed. Ferrok opened with a crit Ignition Spellstrike chain (85 damage) on the bandit Iverness — dropping him before he ever got a turn. Round 3, Ferrok unloaded a **108-damage Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike on Zatrine herself**, and Bael denied Ferrok the killing blow on the Lamia round 4 with a one-action fire blast that made the DM groan: "And here comes Bael with a steel chair."

Past the cell block, a Clockwork Soldier and three Living Graffiti — peeled straight off the murals — ambushed them in a small chamber. Tivael survived the encounter being repeatedly punched by paintings, with the iconic line "Why'd you hit me, you stupid painting?" and a brand-new chosen-tactic for the Shadow Tower: insulting enemies on a turn that's already loaded with Recall Knowledge. With the medical wing cleared, the party climbed the spiral stairwell — and met four lesser Shadows and a Greater Shadow swirling down toward them. Bargrum couldn't intercept void damage. Raylta's heightened Moonlight Ray crit the Greater Shadow for 88 damage, vaporizing it. Bael cribbed her signature spell from the Aeon Stone of the Crescent — "Moonbeam attack!" — and earned an irritated "I have Moonbeam. That's my thing!" in reply.

After a long rest and the party's promotion to level 8, they ascended the final hundred feet into the Shadow Tower lobby. Three larger Clockwork Sentries — multi-armed, with grab reactions and pneumatic warhammers — proceeded to grapple Bargrum, Raylta, and Bael in succession before Bael's first Lightning Dash since gaining the feat tore through Sentry B for the finishing 33. A few doors deeper, the party heard Azlanti voices shouting "Hit me!" and "Motherfucker!" through a closed door — seven of Tokainen's Chosen playing blackjack. Bargrum opened with "Oh, no calls for alarm. What's the buy-in?" and rolled initiative when they refused his offer. The Chosen turned out to be a fire-themed cult with a 1/hour Cataclysm Brand — 60-foot range, 10-foot burst, 6d6 fire plus persistent — and tried to nuke their own lounge twice. The session ended just after the fight cleared, when Ferrok kicked open a door instead of opening it normally and woke up a dormitory full of guards. "That's where we call the session, folks."

## Key Events

### Act I — Cleaning Up the Medical Wing
- Session opened with the party still recovering from the previous session's clockwork fight. Ferrok drank a potion to skip another risky Treat Wounds attempt; Bael invested an Aeon Stone of Vital Amplification (+1 HP per 10 healed). The DM and Bargrum spent five minutes nailing down its automation in Foundry.
- Exploration through the medical wing: a deep pit full of giant **frogs** (gave the party an explicit pass, content not to be disturbed); a magitek control panel left untouched ("if you push the wrong button, you're scared"); a **cryo chamber** prisoner-blast room — "It's a cryo chamber like Demolition Man" (Bargrum).
- The party found a **security badge gate** that required 5 badges but they only had 4. Bael declared "What if I turn into lightning and teleport?" and **used Lightning Dash for the first time** to zap past the trap. "You're not really in physical form enough for it to register you."
- Found a long-dead **sailor's body** in a corner — frost-blasted, not Azlanti, possibly a Talmandor's Bounty prisoner who'd come through here months ago.

### Act II — Zatrine the Lamia (Cell Block)
- The party entered a wrecked cell block — most of the stasis tubes broken or open, the air thick with the foul green soup of failed prisoners. A cowering woman in tattered robes called out **"Go away! Go away!"** and claimed to have woken up with everything already broken.
- Ferrok declined to trust her: "Yeah, they didn't put nice people in here, so I don't trust her." Bael and Raylta were debating whether to give her the benefit of the doubt when several armed figures sprang out from behind the cells — and the "prisoner" grimaced: **"Impatient fuckers, aren't you all."** She transformed into a Lamia (upper-half human, lower-half serpent). The party rolled initiative with surprise.
- **Bael's first move**: a two-action Four Winds, repositioning Ferrok, Raylta, and Bargrum to set up the fight.
- **Aguilar's turn**: tumbled past Bael, feinted (success — off-guard), struck Bael (Nimble Dodge converted a crit to a normal hit). Zatrine then cast **Agonizing Despair** on Ferrok (Will save, mental + Frightened 1). He failed and ate the damage.
- **Ferrok's round-1 finisher**: **crit Ignition Spellstrike on the bandit Iverness for 62 damage** (38 weapon + 24 fire), then a normal Strike for 23 more — **85 damage dropped Iverness before he ever got a turn**. Raylta deadpanned: "Oh, that guy didn't get a turn. — Way to go, Ferrok."
- **Tivael's setup**: lost his aura placement on the first try ("I lost my aura"), corrected, then cast **Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition** for 3 rounds, Demoralized Ularette in Common (success). On round 2 his silver Needle Darts (19) on **Aguilar** combined with Bael's blast (13) + Thermal Nimbus persistent fire (7) to drop him.
- Round 3 BIG HIT: **Zatrine** opened up with Noise Blast on Ferrok (failed save, 20 sonic). Ferrok responded with **Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike — crit on Zatrine for 108 damage** ("This shockwave. Whatever glass was left on these containment bays just shatters and showers the floor."). DM: "I'll take it."
- Raylta burned a **3rd-rank Heal scroll** on Ferrok for 41 to keep him standing while Zatrine retaliated with **Vampiric Feast**. A second Heal cast (35 HP) on Ferrok mid-fight kept him in the green.
- **Bael's steel chair**: Bael's Demoralize attempt on Zatrine was a critical failure ("you'll die" — Foundry rolled a 12 vs DC ~24). Round 4: with Zatrine at ~10 HP, Bael moved in and finished her with a single-action elemental blast just before Ferrok could land the killing Force Fang. DM: "And here comes Bael with a steel chair. I'm like, what the fuck? I didn't pay for you to get the kill." Raylta deadpanned: "Well, that was unsatisfying because Bael just took the kill."
- Loot from Zatrine and her three bandit accomplices: 3× +1 Striking Short Sword, 3× +1 Striking Short Bow, 3× lesser darkvision elixir, Zatrine's **+1 Striking Fearsome Scimitar**, and her **+1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing** (Tivael — needs an hour to transfer runes; Raylta will have to drag the train of the giant Lamia-sized robe behind him).

### Act III — Living Graffiti + Clockwork Soldier
- In a small chamber, a Clockwork Soldier activated and three **Living Graffiti** peeled themselves off the murals — Tivael was conveniently standing right by one when it started crawling out. ("That's a good place you chose to stand right there.")
- **Tivael Recalled Knowledge**: full constructs, best save Reflex, worst Fort, NOT mindless (so Demoralize and Bon Mot both work), construct immunities (bleed, death, sickened). The **Clockwork Soldier** in the room specifically had electricity weakness — Bael and Tivael leaned into Electric Arc / electric blast against it.
- **Iconic Tivael moment**: hit by a painting, Tivael attempted Bon Mot — **"Why'd you hit me, you stupid painting?"** — and the **failure was the joke**. The painting is too 2D to be insulted by being called dumb. DM, voicing the painting: *"Well, thank you, Captain Obvious."* The painting then specifically targeted Tivael with paint as petty revenge.
- **Ferrok's pair of crit Ignition Spellstrikes** ended Graffiti C and most of Graffiti A — "Burn up the painting." / "Ferrok will hold his sword up as flames shimmer all over it."
- **Raylta's crit Needle Darts** pinned Graffiti B against the wall: "He's become pinup art." / DM: "If he was on the internet, would he be on Pinterest after that?"
- **Bargrum's RP highlight**: with Ferrok flanked by the last Graffiti and a Clockwork Soldier, "Bargrum will heroically stride up to interpose himself between and look up at Ferrok and say, stay behind me, I'll protect you." Ferrok, in character, walked through his legs: "He walks up through his legs and he's like, what?"

### Act IV — The Shadow Stairwell
- The party began climbing the spiral stairwell upward. Out of the dark, a swarm of incorporeal undead descended: **Shadow A**, **B**, **C**, **D** (smaller wisps), and a much larger quadrupedal **Greater Shadow**.
- **Bargrum's helplessness moment**: "I cannot intercept this damage because it is void. So good luck. Have fun, everyone." Raylta: "Shit. I'm shit out of luck. Welcome to my world. No intercepts in sight."
- **Best DM bit of the night**: Shadow C stole Ferrok's shadow with Steal Shadow, applying Enfeebled. Raylta deadpanned "You have a really big shadow." DM, in NPC voice: **"Big tasty shadow."** Then, breaking: "I have to admit, if something came up to me and said, 'you've got a real nice shadow,' I don't know if I'd know how to respond to that." Ferrok rolled with it: "I don't know if it's just lighting in here, but your shadow looks great." — Raylta: "Creep."
- **Tivael's shadow also got stolen**: "Yeah, take it. I don't care. Take it. — Like a little snake. — The ugly shadow." Then Tivael Recalled Knowledge again: incorporeal undead, resistant to most damage except **force, ghost touch, vitality, spirit**, best Reflex, worst Fort, not mindless (so Bon Mot lands). Tivael landed a Bon Mot on a Shadow afterward — "the ugly shadow" insult stuck (-2 Will).
- **Bael's cross-PC follow-up**: "Nice — okay, it has a minus two Will save, so **I will follow up on Tivael's insult and try to intimidate it**." First action Demoralize landed; then started Thermal Nimbus; last action fire blast. ("So I'm not useful when it comes to damage this fight" — incorporeal resistance ate the blast.)
- Tivael's **first Dragonet Breath of the session** — 20-foot cone (Inhale, Exhale!), mental + silver, hit three shadows: 1 crit fail, 1 fail, 1 success. Resistance ate most of the damage but the debuff held.
- **Bael's Aeon Stone of the Crescent activation**: "I'll grab one of the particles flying around my head. I'll turn around towards these guys. And I'll yell, Moonbeam attack!" — 100-foot line, 4d12 spirit silver, hit Shadow B and Shadow C for 26 each; Shadow D saved for 13. Raylta: **"What the fuck? I have Moonbeam. That's my thing!"** Ferrok: "Bael is Moon Knight."
- **Raylta's revenge**: "I'm not going to let you upstage me. I also cast Moonlight Ray on the one guy. On the one fucking guy at rank four." — **crit on the Greater Shadow for 88 damage. Destroyed.** Bargrum: "Nice moon laser."
- **Bael's Ash Strider** through Shadow B at the end — smoke-form polymorph overflow, 26 fire damage, destroyed: "I'll turn into smoke and pass through B... you literally just take off the arms that are grabbing Raylta as you plow through them just as this thing sparks and collapses."
- **Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit on Shadow A** — 14 force damage on a clean hit, destroyed (force bypasses incorporeal resistance). Force damage continues to pay its way against incorporeal undead.
- Fight cleared. The party realized the stairs continued up another ~100 feet, into the Shadow Tower proper. **The party leveled up to 8** and took a long rest before climbing.

### Act V — The Shadow Tower Lobby (Clockwork Sentries)
- Climbing 100 feet up, the party entered the Shadow Tower's grand lobby: polished black marble, green-and-gold tiled walls, vaulted ceiling 40 feet up, two three-tiered fountains running clear. Geometric crystal sculptures and two life-size Azlanti sentinel statues flanking a garden of swords-and-polearms growing from the floor.
- Their security badges did **not** work up here. Three much larger **Clockwork Sentries** activated. "Surrender your weapons and come quietly." They didn't wait for a response.
- **Sentry Tackle**: Sentry C strode into Bargrum, fist-struck, and knocked him prone. Sentry A did the same on the next turn — and when Bargrum tried to use Intercept on a hit, A then Sudden Seize'd him as he tried to step, **grappling him while prone**.
- **Ferrok's level-8 power flex**: "All right. I'll go big or go home, right?" — followed up his Conflux Spellstrike with a Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike. Both connected; the sentries had electricity weakness 10 and crumpled.
- **Tivael's first Fortissimo Composition**: rolled 31 on the performance check — Success (+2 instead of the usual +1 from Courageous Anthem). He spent five minutes figuring out how to apply the upgraded effect token. (Crit Success would've been +3.)
- **Bael's signature Lightning Dash** turn: "He transforms into a streak of lightning and goes 30 feet through the sky." Hit Sentry B for 33 damage (electricity weakness 10 applied). B survived this pass and was finished later in the chain.
- Bargrum and Raylta were both grappled simultaneously by separate sentries. Raylta tried to cast a Heal while Grabbed — flat check DC 5, rolled a 2, **burned a hero point**, succeeded, healed Bargrum for 29.
- Bargrum's biggest tank play of the encounter: **Intercept Attack with Disarming Intercept**, **crit success**, wrenching a sentry's halberd off its arm.
- **Bael's second Lightning Dash for 50 damage** ripped through and finished Sentry C — the real kill. ("Lightning that arcs across the ground instead of through the air :D")
- All three sentries down after eight rounds. Loot: 18 pairs of average manacles (manacle joke incoming) — no magical weapons.

### Act VI — The Gambling Cult (Tokainen's Chosen)
- A door deeper. Bargrum looked for traps; Tivael listened. Through the door they heard **Azlanti voices**: "Hit me!" / "Motherfucker!" / "Take this one." / "Double or nothing." / "You're on." — **Blackjack.**
- The party walked in. Comfortable lounge, burgundy walls, lamps, a card-game in progress at a large table with eight chairs. Seven humans of clearly Azlanti descent, all in matching uniforms, all with the **same burn mark on their left cheek**. The DM let them call out their alarm in Azlanti.
- **Bargrum's RP moment, second-best of the night**: "Bargrum will just say, oh, no calls for alarm. What's the buy-in?" One of the Chosen, after a beat, replied: **"Ah, maybe a few fingers of yours."** Bargrum: "Seems like a steep price. Is that what everyone else is putting in the pot as well?" Another Chosen: "Just shut up and grab him. **Tokainen's gonna want him.**" Roll initiative.
- **Bael went first** (caught them mid-break, weapons sheathed). Narrating in third-person: "Nonchalantly raise a finger by his side, and you will see a small orange bead appear above his finger. He flicks his finger forwards, and as it flies, it just gets bigger and bigger and bigger until it explodes in the middle of the room in a 20-foot burst." — **Solar Detonation** (Feat 8), 6d6 fire + 2d6 vitality, hit all 7 Chosen for 8-34 damage each (Chosen C crit failed → blinded 1 min; others dazzled or saved).
- The Tokainen's Chosen **Cataclysm Brand** — once per hour, 60-foot range, 10-foot burst, 6d6 fire damage with persistent 1d4 fire on a fail — turned the lounge into a fire pit. Two of the Chosen ignited the burst pattern from the same spot (one was blind and threw it blind), catching Ferrok, Tivael, Raylta, and Bael in successive blasts. Persistent fire on Raylta and Tivael burned for rounds.
- **Raylta's "I failed again" run**: she failed three persistent-fire saves in a row, eventually accepting a hero point from Bael to reroll one. "Let's see you watch me fail. Ah, I failed again. Oh, Bael."
- **Tivael's safety call**: with persistent fire ticking, Tivael played it safe — "I have a feeling I'm gonna play it safe... Yeah, I'm gonna move out of here" — repositioned around a corner out of the burst pattern, cast Rallying Anthem for AoE defense, then Fortissimo'd it (just-barely-failed). Spent the remaining rounds debuffing and casting Needle Darts.
- **Tivael's Flurry of Claws — non-crit hit on Chosen B for 27 damage killed B** (already softened by Solar Detonation + Lava Bomb + persistent fire). The **crit prong on Chosen E for 60 damage instant-bloodied E** but didn't drop him — E was at higher HP.
- **Bargrum used a hero point on a missed strike** late in the fight; succeeded. Used Group Taunt to keep three Chosen targeting him at once.
- **Best disarm of the session**: Ferrok stood up next to Bargrum, drew reactive strikes from three Chosen (took two hits, intercepted the third). **Bargrum used Intercept + Disarming Intercept on F's glaive, ripping it free**. The Chosen, hurled: "Oh, fuck your eyes!"
- **Bael landed multiple kills late** with his birdie-shape Flying Flame and stepping into Thermal Nimbus emanation to consume the burning Chosen. **Tivael's Flurry of Claws picked up the B kill mid-fight** — the non-crit prong (27) was the finisher on the already-softened B; the crit prong (60) bloodied E without dropping him.
- Fight cleared. The party found 7× +1 Guisarme, 7× +1 Bola, 14 more pairs of manacles (now 32 total), a **Prime Arcidux Board Game** (200gp, 3-player Azlanti strategy), and a stash of **Lava Bombs (Eternal Eruption)** that triggered for 17 fire damage in a save-or-burn pattern (5d6).
- **Cards glued to the underside of the table** — someone was cheating, classic.

### Act VII — Session-End Cliffhanger

The DM: "You open the door to a dormitory full of sleeping guards who all shoot at you." Ferrok: "It wouldn't have woken up if you just open it normally." DM: "Fuck. They wouldn't have woken up, but you know."
