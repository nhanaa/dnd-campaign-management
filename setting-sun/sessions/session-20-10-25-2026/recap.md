## Session Summary

**Book Two of *Fists of the Ruby Phoenix* ended with the music stopping.** Cody opened by promising it — *"we will absolutely be finishing Book 2 of Ruby Phoenix today… prepare to level up mid-session"* — and the party picked up in Goka, cleaning up after the last fight on the main street: Sovael spent both of his Restoration scrolls on Clutch (Drained 4 → 2) and Seva, and Sattva offered Clutch an hour of acupuncture the city did not have time for.

**On the Seven Dragon Bridge**, three Portal Eaters — iridescent, phoenix-shaped things with eighty-foot wingspans and forty feet of reach on their cloaks — were tearing up the bridge between the arena and the city. **Mafika Ayuwari**, the professor they had beaten in the losers' final, flew in to help (*"Hope you don't mind if I lend my aid"*; Ferrok: *"Yes, Mafika. We got a ringer"*). The birds grabbed, pushed and pulled; Clutch spent the first two rounds being shoved twenty feet, restrained, and intercepting Chromatic Rays for Sattva. Nobody knew what hurt them until the party tested it. Sattva guessed after a crit (*"Maybe force?"*), Seva put force on her Arcane Cascade to check (*"Why can't we roll RK? Why are we guessing here?"*), and Sovael's **Aether Beam** and Ferrok's Force Fang confirmed it: **force weakness.** Clutch then caught a rule the party had missed all campaign — Aether Beam pushes 10 feet on a failed save — and the push pulled a Portal Eater off Sattva. Then, at the top of round four, Mogaru turned and looked at the bridge, and a **240-foot cone of fire** washed over everyone on it. Sattva backflipped (*"It's not Cataclysm. This is what Sattva has been training for"*) and crit-saved; Sovael failed and took the persistent fire — *"Thank you, Mogaru."* Mafika killed two of the birds — a Chain Lightning and a punch — and **Ferrok's Force Fang** put the third into the boiling sea. Mafika pointed them across the city: the music was coming from **the opera house.**

**In the Empress Yin Opera House** a tengu in fine performer's robes was stringing an erhu bow. *"Maestro Razu, at your service… this is my stage debut, after all."* He blamed Hao Jin — *"She's simply reaping the seed she's sown so long ago"* — and Sovael would not have it: *"Well, I don't think the people here have to suffer from that."* Razu offered to compose a song in their name, then remembered they'd been *"utterly trounced by the Lightkeepers… Sorry, I don't compose songs about losers."* Sovael: ***"Don't worry, you don't have to compose. I'll compose a song once that we beat the shit out of you."***

Razu was Sovael's mirror again — a bard on Dirge of Doom and Lingering Composition, with **Incredible Volume** stretching every song to three hundred feet — and his opener was a ninth-rank **Phantasmagoria** that left Ferrok, Sattva and Sovael confused (Seva saved with her Aeon Stone; Clutch crit-saved). Ferrok, his hero-point reroll no better, stepped forward *"glaring at Sovael as I try to dismember him"* — and his first hit forced the flat check that **snapped Sovael out of it**. Sattva, *"scared, so his hands are shaking,"* shot Sovael too. Sovael, clear-headed and 90 hit points lighter: *"I should heal myself, but what's the fun in that?"* — and put a **ninth-rank Heroism on Ferrok**, the man who had just hit him. Clutch **long-jumped** clear across the theatre twice (*"the good old Hulk jump"*), Noise Blast and Pierce the Sky deafened most of the party (which, Cody ruled, also switched off the Dirge's frighten), and Sattva found out how to *"fight air with air"*: a crit on a Melodic Squall. **Seva killed both Squalls.** Ferrok's Blink Charge drew Razu's first blood.

When Clutch finally grabbed him, Razu's **Contingency** fired a Translocate a hundred and twenty feet away — straight to Sattva, who had not moved all fight. Sattva grappled him, burned his **Sash of Prowess** for the first time to turn it into a critical success, and held him restrained. Sovael's Whispers of Weakness (weakest save Fortitude, no weakness) and a **Fire Ray (75)** followed, then Foretell Harm; Seva's **Rend Magic** Spellstrike (*"no funny business"*). At the top of his turn Razu took the eighteen, smiled — *"Well, aren't you all just something? I suppose there's some worth to you… But I've played my part, as have you. Adieu, everyone"* — and **dissolved into prismatic slime** on Sattva's fingers. He had taken about 164 of his 520 hit points. Ferrok stomped on the slime.

With the music gone, **Mogaru looked around as if waking from a daze, walked back the way he had come, and sank beneath the bay.** *"And so concludes Book Two of Fists of the Ruby Phoenix. Congratulations, you all reach level 18."*

**The next morning**, with ash still falling on the arena, **Empress Nai Yan Fei** addressed the finalists. Hao Jin has not been seen since; the glow on the southern horizon that night was her, casting. The Lightkeepers and Tino's Toughest still have not reported, and the Empress thinks the disappearances are connected. **Without its Grand Judge the tournament cannot continue** — *"I beg of you all, seek out and bring back the Ruby Phoenix."* Sovael asked what the teams would have to work with; Seva asked whether anyone had found the Lightkeepers, and then whether the Empress knew Razu, *"because Razu seems to be familiar who Hao Jin is. Like, personally."* She did not: *"whoever he serves must be terrifyingly powerful."* The finalists were given **plover chimes** — glass wind chimes Hao Jin made from axiomite dust and phoenix flame, meant as mementos, now modified to point toward her — and a grant. **The Nameless' share was 200,000 gp**, plus the finalists' regalia: the use of a ship and its pilot.

A shopping trip at the Empress's favour followed (uncommon items to level 20, rare to 15), and so did an argument about grappling. Clutch had just bought **Bracers of Strength** for **Bear Hug** when Cody said he thinks grappling is too strong; Clutch argued that martials have nothing else built up to that level; Sovael agreed the feat support is grapple-centric, and pointed out that Freedom of Movement does nothing once you're restrained and can't cast it. Ferrok took the major striking and greater shock runes, Clutch the +3 armour rune, and Clutch handed Seva 7,400 gp so she could afford her major striking rune. When Seva asked how on earth Sovael had afforded an apex item: *"Well, I saved my money. I don't have to spend money on weapon runes."* He upgraded his Ring of Divine Might and his armour to +3 instead, and bought an Aeon Stone against confusion.

**Ysoren**, a middle-aged Minkaian pilot, was waiting at the Ruby Village with his family's flying yacht, the ***Solar Geon II*** — a gift from his great-great-grandmother to his great-great-grandfather, returned to the family by Hao Jin, lent to the tournament's victory parade each decade. He had watched the finals (*"what a damn match against the Speakers to the Wind"*) and he wanted them to know the ship has no cannons. Sovael: *"That's what we're here for. Getting rid of trouble."* Other teams scattered into the sky and the wilderness after the same goal — the Arms of Balance on elemental horses, Winter's Roar on an elven ship, the Steps of the Sun riding a flying rock, the Speakers to the Wind running barefoot at impossible speed.

Three days north, the chime led them to **Iron Mountain** in the Flying Mountains, where **an island floats upside down above the summit**, ringed by its own ocean. Flying at it got them no closer, and the mirage around it turned into a hall of infinite reflections — a Will save that hit Clutch for 50 mental (*"Clutch howls with pain like he's experiencing a very bad brain freeze"*). The chime swung downward. At the summit — a sulfur lake that will not freeze, twin vermilion shrine gates, twelve Tian zodiac statues — **three Island Oni** with twenty-foot electrified spears came out from behind the statues. Sattva's free Recall Knowledge got one fact: *"they are Oni. And you at least know from the previous Oni that Oni do not like beans."* (Clutch, in chat: *"Clutch looks at his toe beans."*) Sovael's Whispers of Weakness got the real answer: **spirit**. Clutch's new Bear Hug and a crit Spine Breaker pinned one; **Ferrok's Goring Charge** rooted another with the horns; **Seva, Clutch and Ferrok** each took one down, Clutch on an Intercept counter-strike. Cody stopped there: *"next session is going to start with a bit of exposition."*
