## The short version

In the winners'-bracket final against the Lightkeepers, **all three surviving Lightkeepers received the Elite template mid-fight, unannounced**: +2 to attacks, damage, AC and spell DCs, and +20 max HP. It happened within about a minute of **Blue Viper dying (14:30:10)**. At that moment the fight was going the party's way: one of four enemies dead, three Slowed, no enemy reactions, and the party at about 79% HP.

Nothing in the log explains it: no spell, aura, ability or effect message. Cody didn't mention it during the fight or in the debrief, even while crediting Sovael's Roaring Applause with "buying time." Afterward he said the match was *"not designed for you to be able to win."*

The issue is the decision, not the result. A GM can only act on what he can see at the time. What Cody could see was a fight that was supposed to be a loss starting to look winnable, and the stat change is what followed. Whether the +2 ended up deciding the fight doesn't change that.

## For the record: what it changed

This is recorded only so nobody has to recompute it later. Pax's position is that this doesn't bear on the integrity question, and that position is correct.

- **It didn't cause Sovael's round-3 knockout.** His Spirit Song save fails either way, and Shino's critical still crits without the +2.
- **Afterward it added roughly 250–300 damage to the party and 60 enemy HP.**
  - Ferrok's Spirit Song #2 critical failure (98, Stunned 2) would have been a failure (49, Stunned 1).
  - Clutch's Spirit Song #1 would have been a success.
  - Five enemy hits would have missed: four from Ran-to, one from Shino.
  - +2 damage on every hit after the change.
- The party would still most likely have lost. The fight priced at **2–2.75× an Extreme encounter as run** (see the session analysis).

## What to watch for

1. **Mid-fight stat changes that aren't announced, especially right after the party gains the upper hand.** This is the new item. The only earlier mid-fight change recorded for Cody's tables went the other way: S15, removing Jaiban's Quickened.
2. **"Scripted loss" fights.** If a match is meant to be lost, the honest options are to say so beforehand, or to let the table forfeit (the party has forfeited before and that was fine). Quietly raising the numbers once the party starts winning isn't one of them.
3. **The buff-then-resent pattern from RotR S7**, now on this table too:
   - After Winter's Roar: *"you guys proved me wrong, so I guess I don't hold back from now on."*
   - *"I buff things to hell and back."*
   - *"I might need to rethink my item policy… or I might just need to give enemies items."*

   None of these lines is wrong by itself. They're logged so the pattern can be judged against a record rather than memory.
