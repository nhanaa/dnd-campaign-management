## Session Summary

**The Nameless are one win from a rematch with the Lightkeepers.** Three matches, three days, one loss — and the loss was the fight Cody said afterward was *"not designed for you to be able to win."*

Cody opened by correcting last week's bracket math: with only eight teams, winning the winners' bracket takes three wins, and the last team knocked out of it is **seeded** into the losers' bracket and needs only two wins to reach the final. Then the semifinal: **Winter's Roar**, Ulfin warriors from the lands of the Linnorm Kings — **Urnak Lostwind** (greataxe, Whirlwind Strike, *"a Linnorm King in the making"*), **Huldrin Skolsdottir** (trident and spiked shield, Iron Maiden Stance, a second reaction for Shield Block) and the halfling caster **Juspix Rammel**, who opened with a **seventh-rank Haste** on all three. With Seva out, their hobgoblin monk sat out too. Sattva bowed, and his robe began to shine.

Winter's Roar got their reactions in round one — a Reactive Strike on Clutch's potion, two Shield Blocks from Huldrin — and then **Sovael took them away**. Round two: Ancestral Memories on Huldrin and a **sixth-rank Roaring Applause**; Huldrin failed and Urnak failed on a natural 2. Ferrok: *"No more Quicken for them."* Sovael: *"I think the big price is no reaction, because they have pretty strong reactions."* For the rest of the match the two warriors were Slowed, had to clap on their own turns, and never reacted again — Cody: *"They have not had reactions this whole fight."* Clutch held Urnak in a grapple he kept renewing, and when Juspix's Eclipse Burst came in, his Shield Block spread 15 resistance to Sovael and Sattva through Improved Reflexive Shield, with a **Mending Lattice** to repair the shield after. Juspix's thrown fork at Ferrok went wide — *"I catch it and break it in half." / "Not how that works, but…"* When Juspix's targeted Desiccate came in, Sovael's **eighth-rank Spell Riposte** counteracted it and turned it back on him; Juspix failed his own save for 56.

Clutch wanted to throw Urnak into Huldrin (*"Can I throw Urnak into Huldrin?"*), and, told he could only throw him for distance, threw him anyway: *"We'll just throw them away and say we're just getting rid of the trash."* Sovael: *"Don't say that. Let's be respectful."* Clutch took the note in character — *"Sovael probably lectured Clutch at some point about his trash talking, and Clutch starts to say something, and then looks over at Sovael and goes, ah, shit."* Ferrok went after the caster (*"Got to geek the mage. Got to do it"*), blinding him with a Briny Bolt and then critting him with Blink Charge. **Sovael finished Urnak with a Quickened Chain Lightning**, Juspix's own Fireball caught Huldrin (*"Huldrin will be accepting of this"*), **Sattva's Hunted Shot dropped Huldrin**, and **Sovael finished Juspix** with a natural-20 Needle Darts. Emi: *"The Nameless win by a landslide."*

Afterward Cody admitted he had **nerfed Winter's Roar** because his mythic Saturday group had nearly been wiped by them the day before — and that, between that and his own cold dice, the Nameless had *"proved me wrong."* No more adjustments: *"You had very good teamwork… I feel like you know what you're doing."*

The **winners'-bracket final** was the **Lightkeepers**, the team that beat the party in S11. Ran-to smirked: *"I didn't think you bastards would be the ones to get here."* Syu Tak-nwa, from the back: *"I'll acknowledge you've gotten a bit stronger, but if you think that means this is going to go any differently than last time, you're sorely mistaken."* Sattva: *"We meet again… Well, let's see about that."*

For two rounds it was a different fight than the first time. Sovael had Ferrok and Sattva delay behind him and **Hasted the whole party from a scroll**. Sattva put **179 into Blue Viper in his first turn** — a natural-20 Hunted Shot and then a second natural 20 at −10. Clutch's Aeon stone **reflected Syu's Phantasmagoria back at her**. Sovael's second **Roaring Applause** hit hard: Blue Viper failed, Shino crit-failed, Syu failed on a natural 2 — three Slowed, all four without reactions. Ferrok, confused, swung at Ran-to three times and rolled three critical misses (Sovael: *"You have to be more specific."*), then used **Take Your Places** to teleport Sovael out of Ran-to's grip and nearly kill Blue Viper; **Sattva finished it**.

Then it turned. **Ran-to kept grabbing the dragon** — four grapple attempts on Sovael all fight, every one a critical success — and Syu's Spirit Song stunned him, and **Shino's critical dropped him and ended the Applause**: *"They have their reactions back."* Clutch's Battle Medicine brought him up, and **Sattva's Moment of Renewal** — a giant palm overhead and the sound of a gong — renewed Clutch and Sovael (Clutch and Sovael both flagged at the table that it heals but doesn't bring spells back). Ran-to took him again: *"Dude, after the shit you've been doing this fight, he's not letting you go."* Sovael: *"I think I made a bit of a miscalculation with my skills."* Clutch Repositioned him free — *"Best I can do is get you un-grabbed and five feet away."* Syu laid down Visions of Danger; Shino critted Ferrok to 23 (*"Hanging on my thread"*); the Visions dropped him at the top of his next turn and he came back with **It's Not Over**. Then Syu: *"That's quite enough of this. You fought well, but I think we're done here"* — a **tenth-rank Tear the Veil**. Clutch saved on a natural 20; Ferrok and Sattva critically failed for 121 each, and Ferrok dropped again. *"You're the first ones who have had to make me use that, so I applaud you."*

Ran-to headlocked Sovael once more. With nothing left to cast, Sovael **Bon Mot'd the goblin holding him** — and answered the goblin's retort with *"Well, I haven't been able to cast because of you."* Ran-to whispered in his ear: *"All your shit makes it harder for me to actually fight. So fuck you."* Sovael: *"Well, you make it harder for me to cast spells as well, so…"* Cody: *"Just a little dragon trash talking while he's headlocked by this goblin."* Then Shino's falling knee took Sattva (*"I do the Street Fighter death sound and drop to the ground"*) and her shuriken took Sovael. Ran-to beckoned Clutch — *"Come on, big guy. I know you got some more in you"* — and Clutch grabbed **Syu** and hurled her 35 feet. Ferrok stabilised (*"I'm going to live forever"*). Sattva got up on his fast healing twice to shoot Shino in the back from the floor — *"having trouble knocking me out?"* … *"right in the groin… Still hurts"* — and was finally put out with a critical. **Clutch was the last one standing**, and gracious about it: *"I don't see any reason to burn consumables because I'm pretty sure we all see the way this is going. You got pretty far."* In round nine Shino dropped him. **The Lightkeepers won.** Cody gave them a round of applause and named what had bought them their time — **Sovael's Roaring Applause** — and why the enemies kept going for him: *"It's just because you do things that shut everybody else down so effectively."*

**Level 17.** After the break, Cody ran the losers' bracket: the Biting Roses beat the Arms of Balance, the Speakers to the Wind beat Winter's Roar, Tino's Toughest beat the Steps of the Sun. The Nameless, seeded, drew **the Biting Roses** — the team that beat them in S14.

Sovael cast a **ninth-rank Sacred Covenant** over the party before the bell. Lantondo opened with Pierce the Sky at everyone but Clutch (*"they know what Clutch is about"*); Yarrika opened with Phantasmagoria, and Clutch's Aeon stone reflected it again (*"It's okay, I came prepared,"* said Ferrok). Ferrok cast Haste. Sovael opened **Dirge of Doom with a critically successful Lingering (four rounds)**, a **critical Talent Envy Demoralize on Grandfather Mantis**, and a **ninth-rank Heroism on Sattva**. In round two Yarrika banished Clutch into a **Quandary** puzzle room and critted Sovael with a Mantis Strike (Wooden Double soaked 25). Ferrok's **Briny Bolt Spellstrike critted her for 210**; Sovael followed with **Wails of the Damned** — Yarrika, the Mantis and Artus all failed, and Lantondo, a Wyrwood, was immune (Sovael caught that the spell hits *"each living enemy,"* which Cody had read as every creature: *"If I had read that right, then Syu would have used Wails of the Damned on all of you way earlier"*) — and a **Quickened Roaring Applause**. **Sattva's Hunted Shot killed Yarrika** — *"That's for taking out Clutch"* — and the Quandary ended with her. Artus critted Sovael, who went invisible off **Dazzling Dragonet Disappearance**; the Mantis had Truesight and clawed him anyway. Round three, striding past the Mantis's announced reactive strike (*"Please don't kill me"*), Sovael **healed himself for 101**, and Sattva cast his second **Moment of Renewal**. Then Lantondo's Corrosive Muck caught Ferrok on a natural 1 (*"Oh, my reflex sucks." / Sovael: "You wore too much armor."*). **Ferrok killed Grandfather Mantis**, and next round **critically Disintegrated Artus**. Sovael chased Lantondo with his first **Canticle of Everlasting Grief** and then a **Fire Ray critical for 142** (*"Oh, that's without a Sure Strike. Well, I'll just take that."*), and **Sattva's Hunted Shot finished Lantondo.** No PC went down.

**Next session: the Speakers to the Wind** (Tino's Toughest are eliminated — Sovael: *"Oh, Tino, no"*), and if the Nameless win, **the Lightkeepers again**. Seva's player is back next week.
