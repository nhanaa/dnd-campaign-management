The session opened on the corpse of last week's nightmare — the surgeon Geff dead, his table cleared, and the woman he'd been cutting on (no anesthetic) finally freed. Her name was Sarah, and once the party calmed her with a blanket, a cup of whiskey-flavored water, and the world's least reassuring introduction ("we're just the good guys, lady"), she pointed them onward: the upper floors are a warzone, ruled by a red-robed vampire woman with long black hair — not Nizca — and stalked by tortured, self-mutilated "things" that fight the vampires on sight.

What they found first was a Dero warren and an eleven-round meat grinder. Three spiked-chain Stranglers screamed for help, three kukri-swinging Sentries poured in, a wall-busting Zombie Hulk lumbered through, and presiding over it all was Vye, a vampire spellcaster who spent the fight stacking Synesthesia, Vision of Death, and a party-wide Synaptic Pulse on Bargrum — and, cornered at single digits, Translocated across the room and critted on the way out. The dwarf soaked literally every natural 20 the dice rolled; Ferrok could not buy a hit. It ended only when Raylta's needle dart finally put Vye in the ground.

After a full rest the floor turned gentle and strange. The party adopted Speckle, a three-eyed winged homunculus janitor, and looted Deg's workshop, whose journals laid the whole plot bare: a retrofitted Shadow Beacon now drawing power from the Plane of Shadow to blot out the sun, so the Dero can walk the surface and Nizca can cross the sea to find a living man named Lograsi. (To stop the Beacon: smash it.) Then they climbed — past a manacle-happy pair of Clockwork Sentries, into a ruined ballroom where two vampires were losing to an Irnakurse fleshwarp, and finally into the fled vampires' ambush, which turned out to be four: Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso. The vampires critted Ferrok into paste, but Raylta's vitality aura made standing near her lethal, and the party closed the night four-for-four. Two vampire-haunted floors and the Shadow Beacon remain — this floor ran long, so the next session is the second-to-last of Shades of Blood, with the finale after it.

Timeline

Act I · RP · The Rescued Captive

Sarah

  • The party stabilized the freed captive — operated on with no anesthetic — as she sat terrified amid the carnage. Bargrum treated her for shock in careful third person; Bael flavored a cup of water to taste like whiskey ("it's a mental thing — you think you're getting drunk, so you relax").
  • Tivael delivered the grand introduction ("We are the liberators. We are the vampire destroyers."); Raylta cut it down to size ("We're just the good guys, lady"). The captive gave her name: Sarah.
  • Sarah's intel: she came from upstairs, where the floors are a battleground. A vampire woman in bright red with long black hair seems to run the upper levels — and is not Nizca. "Things that looked like they'd tortured themselves" fight the vampires up there.
  • The party gave Sarah directions and rations and sent her down, resolving to clear the rest of the floor first.
  • Loot from the previous fight was claimed: Geff's +1 Striking Bonesaw and +1 Striking Scalpel; the bomber Zer's +1 Striking Wounding Sickle, +1 leather (work coat), and 5 lesser elixirs of life; plus a room of sellable surgical instruments.
Act II · Combat · The Dero Warren

Vye + 3 Stranglers + 3 Sentries + a Zombie Hulk (11 rounds)

  • A mold-choked fungal room held three Dero Stranglers (spiked chains, Strangle), who screamed for intruders. The fight snowballed: three Dero Sentries (kukri + bola, Focused Blow), a wall-busting Zombie Hulk (weak to vitality, not fire), and finally Vye, a vampire spellcaster.
  • Bargrum group-Taunted the whole warren onto himself and absorbed an absurd crit streak — Vye's Vision of Death (30), Synesthesia (Clumsy 3 + Dazzled), repeated focus-blow nat-20s. The DM's refrain: "Bargrum gets all my nat 20s this combat."
  • Ferrok had the inverse luck — missing nearly every Spellstrike for ten rounds ("I think I rolled above five, like, twice that fight") and, in frustration, lowering his horns to gore Vye when his sword wouldn't connect.
  • Bael's Solar Detonation crit-failed the Zombie Hulk for ~68 and dazzled the room; a later flying-flame birdie double-killed two Dero Sentries at once.
  • Tivael worked the debuff lane — a Demoralize crit and a Telekinetic Projectile crit (44) on Strangler A, Evangelize to stupefy Vye, and a for-fun Fireball that caught both the Zombie Hulk and Vye (both failed — the Hulk destroyed).
  • Vye stacked control on Bargrum, hit Tivael with Agonizing Despair (which the bard shrugged off — the DM's "Nope — that's our bard"), Vampiric-Feasted Ferrok (who Reactive-Struck him), and at single digits Translocated (rank 4) across the room and critted on arrival. Raylta: "My top five fight right here."
  • The party wore him down; Raylta's needle dart ended the 11-round fight — the DM's longest of the campaign so far.
Act III · RP / Explore · Speckle & Deg's Workshop

A Pet Rock and the Shadow Beacon

  • After a full 8-hour rest, the party swept the Dero bunkrooms — finding a Crater Mage's hat, a wand of Summon Elemental (Tivael), assorted bombs, and a smoke fan.
  • Under a bed they found Speckle, a three-eyed winged homunculus (the floor's cleaner). Bargrum Recall-Knowledge'd it; Bael chatted with it in Azlanti; and Raylta adopted it on the spot ("It's my dumb three-eyed rock"). Ferrok promptly jinxed it ("Speckle's definitely going to tragically die before we finish") — "do not give him a death flag!"
  • Deg's workshop journals cracked the campaign open: Deg discovered N'Gokal ~90 years ago; the Dero have long supplied the vampires with goods and prisoners as food; the N'Gokal vampires worship the Children of the Night pantheon (Kamazotz, Nin, Urgathoa, and others).
  • Nizca only joined Nalushae ~3 years ago (freed from stasis late). Deg and Nizca then retrofitted the Shadow Beacon — once an Azlanti device that siphoned the Plane of Dreams to pacify rioting prisoners — to draw from the Plane of Shadow, blot out the sun, and let Nizca cross the sea to find a living man, Lograsi.
  • How to stop it: the device has failsafes, but smashing it would functionally work — "the percussive engineering I've heard about."
  • Reception-room maps showed three more floors plus the observation deck above. The party torched a room of six empty coffins, then — behind a Nin-warded blood-siphon trap (all five resisted: "Nobody makes me bleed my own blood") — found a cell of prisoners drained near-dry of blood, staged for later rescue with Sarah.
Act IV · Combat · The Clockwork Sentries

"You're under arrest!"

  • Behind a locked door (Bael picked it after a crit-fail and a hero point) stood a pair of giant multi-armed Clockwork Sentries — hammer-wielding and weak to electricity.
  • Their gimmick: Sudden Seize grapples plus an arrest action that claps manacles on grabbed targets. They bound Tivael's legs ("You're under arrest!") and tried to chain the whole party; Tivael hero-pointed a flat check to keep casting while grabbed.
  • Ferrok (Ignition Spellstrikes, shock rune punching the electricity weakness), Bael (electric blasts), Bargrum (Titan-wrestler trip + Taunts), and Raylta (needle darts) dismantled both with no real danger.
  • Loot: two +1 Striking Mauls and 12 sets of manacles — the party's manacle fortune grows.
Act V · Combat · The Irnakurse

The Fleshwarp in the Ballroom

  • Up the stairs to a grand octagonal landing — four doors: west (frantic singing/screaming), north (a glowing magical ward of sigils and equations), east (clawed and battered), and south (shattered open, sounds of fighting).
  • Through the south door, a wrecked moon-themed ballroom: two vampires losing a brawl to a flailing fleshwarp. Raylta RK'd it — a flesh warp, once people, broken down and re-amalgamated, with only faint scraps of human consciousness (not mindless).
  • The party chose to let the duel play out for milestone XP; the vampires faltered, nodded to each other, and misted away — at which point the fleshwarp, an Irnakurse, turned and charged.
  • Its Storm of Tentacles (reach ~22 ft) hit Ferrok, Bargrum, Tivael, and Raylta at once, and Mindlash stunned Bargrum and Raylta.
  • Bael (Nimbus + a nat-20 blast), Ferrok (Ignition Spellstrike + swings), and Tivael (Courageous Anthem + Fortissimo +2, Telekinetic Projectile 25) hacked it silent fast. The party took the kill grimly — "this poor creature."
Act VI · Combat · The Four Vampires

Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso (finale)

  • The two fled vampires barricaded a north door. The party forced it (Bael unlocked — "righty tighty, lefty Lucy" — Ferrok charged it open), and the ambush sprang: not two vampires but four — Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso — with a +2 initiative bonus (the party had been loudly threatening them through the door).
  • The vampires (Fast Healing, feint, Nimble Dodge, flanking; best save Will, worst Fort; resist non-silver physical, cold/frost rapiers) focus-fired Ferrok and critted him into the floor repeatedly (DM: "I've done enough damage to kill you three times over").
  • Bael read the room and flipped Thermal Nimbus to cold for party-wide cold resistance, then flying-flamed multiple vampires per pass. Raylta's Cloak of Light vitality aura was a lethal backstop (the DM noted two would have died just standing beside her), on top of rank-4/5 Heals and a 48-point Lay on Hands keeping Ferrok alive.
  • Tivael front-loaded Flurry of Claws (force), fired Needle Dart and Frostbite combos, and ran Bon Mot at their Wills (which bounced — their best save). Ferrok finished vampires with Ignition Spellstrike and used Echo Jump to teleport past the doorways that plague him.
  • The party went four-for-four: Tivael dropped Kaba (silver Needle Darts), Bargrum crushed Xibin (Boulder Roll), Ferrok ran Chamadi through (Ignition Spellstrike), and Bael's final blast finished Lauso. The dice were brutal but the threats were under-level ("all crits by things lower level than you").
  • DM's wrap: this floor ran long, so the finale slipped — next session is the second-to-last of Shades of Blood, then the finale, then Fists of the Ruby Phoenix.

Combat Encounters

Encounter 1 · Vye + 3 Dero Stranglers + 3 Dero Sentries + Zombie Hulk · 5 PCs · 11 rounds

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Vye → Dero Sentry B (joins later) → Ferrok → Dero Strangler A → Bargrum → Zombie Hulk → Raylta → Tivael → Bael → Dero Strangler B → Dero Sentry C (joins later) → Dero Strangler C → Dero Sentry A. Vye — male vampire spellcaster (Synesthesia, Vision of Death, Chromatic Ray, Agonizing Despair, Synaptic Pulse, Noise Blast, Vampiric Feast, rank-4 Translocate, Shield; +1 Striking Light Mace; resist non-silver physical, Fast Healing). Stranglers ×3 — spiked chains, Strangle (grab + garrote, silences casting). Sentries ×3 — kukri + bola, Focused Blow, Demoralize. Zombie Hulk — huge undead, Wide Swing (two strikes), too big for doors, weak to vitality (not fire).

The fight

  • The Stranglers opened; Ferrok's first Ignition Spellstrike landed, but his dice cratered for the rest of the fight. The Stranglers grappled Ferrok and repeatedly tried Bargrum; the Sentries filed in with Focused-Blow kukri swings and bolas.
  • Bargrum group-Taunted to pull the warren onto himself and tanked a brutal crit streak: Vye's Vision of Death (30; a Hero Point reroll still failed → Frightened 2), Synesthesia (Clumsy 3 + Dazzled), and repeated focus-blow nat-20s. Raylta kept him up with stacked Heals (rank 2/3/4 + scrolls) and Nudge the Scales (sliding into Cursebound/Enfeebled).
  • Bael's Solar Detonation crit-failed the Zombie Hulk (~68) and dazzled multiple Dero; Tivael added a Demoralize crit and a Telekinetic Projectile crit (44) on Strangler A, then Evangelize (stupefied) on Vye.
  • Vye's Synaptic Pulse caught most of the party (Stunned 2 Bargrum, Stunned 1 Bael; Tivael's +18 Will shrugged it off), and his Agonizing Despair on Tivael bounced too — the DM's "Nope, that's our bard." Vye Vampiric-Feasted Ferrok, who Reactive-Struck him (Vye burned a Wooden-Double reaction to eat 25).
  • Tivael's Fireball caught both Vye and the Zombie Hulk — both failed their saves, and the Hulk was destroyed; Bael's flying-flame birdies double-killed two Dero Sentries in one pass.
  • Down to Vye alone at single digits, he Translocated (rank 4) across the room and critted — buying another round. Raylta fatigued him with Interstellar Void; the party pinned him; Raylta's Needle Dart landed the kill.

Outcome

An eleven-round grind — the DM's longest fight of the campaign so far. Loot: 3× +1 Striking Spiked Chain, 3× +1 Hand Crossbow (Stranglers); 3× +1 Striking Kukri, 3× +1 Striking Bola (Sentries); Vye's +1 Striking Light Mace, +1 Resilient Hooded Robe, 2 scrolls of False Vitality, Wand of Raise Skeletons.

Setup

Initiative (no tracker — reconstructed): the party surprised them opening a locked door; order traded between Ferrok, Bael, Bargrum, Tivael, and Raylta vs. the two Sentries. Clockwork Sentries ×2 — giant multi-armed hammer constructs; weak to electricity; Sudden Seize reaction grapples; an arrest action that locks manacles on grabbed targets (six pairs each).

  • Ferrok (Ignition Spellstrikes, shock rune punching the electricity weakness), Bael (electric blasts), Bargrum (Titan-wrestler trip + Taunts; intercepted a trip onto himself), and Raylta (needle darts) brought both down.

Outcome

No party member seriously threatened. Loot: 2× +1 Striking Maul, 12 sets of manacles.

Encounter 3 · The Irnakurse (fleshwarp) · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Bael → Ferrok → Irnakurse → Bargrum → Irnakurse (Tivael and Raylta acted lower in the order; both were Mindlash-stunned mid-fight). Irnakurse — a fleshwarp (once people; faint human consciousness, not mindless); Storm of Tentacles (up to four reach-~22 ft strikes) + Mindlash (Will save or Stunned).

The fight

  • The party watched it overwhelm two vampires, who misted away; the Irnakurse then charged. Its opening Storm of Tentacles hit Ferrok, Bargrum, Tivael, and Raylta, and Mindlash stunned Bargrum and Raylta.
  • Bael (Thermal Nimbus + a nat-20 blast), Ferrok (Ignition Spellstrike + swings), and Tivael (Courageous Anthem + Fortissimo +2, Telekinetic Projectile 25) cut it down quickly. Bargrum Shield/Taunted through the stun.

Outcome

A short, grim fight — the party regretted not helping the tortured creature against the vampires.

Encounter 4 · Four Vampires: Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso · 5 PCs · finale

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Kaba → Tivael → Chamadi → Bael → Xibin → Ferrok → Lauso → Bargrum → Raylta. Four Vampires — rapiers (mix of Shock and Frost runes), Fast Healing, feint, Nimble Dodge, flanking, Demoralize; best save Will, worst Fort; resist non-silver physical. They ambushed with a +2 initiative bonus (the party had been yelling threats through the barricaded door).

The fight

  • The vampires focus-fired Ferrok and critted him relentlessly (feints → off-guard → rapier crits); his glass shield, Emergency Targe, and Raylta's rank-4/5 Heals + 48-point Lay on Hands barely kept pace.
  • Bael flipped Thermal Nimbus to cold to counter the frost rapiers (party-wide cold resistance), then flying-flamed multiple vampires per pass. Bargrum Taunted, intercepted, and shoved with the silver Monkey's Fist.
  • Tivael ran Flurry of Claws (force) and Needle Dart / Frostbite combos, with Bon Mots that bounced off their strong Wills (one crit-fail bounced the penalty back onto him). Ferrok kept finishing vampires with Ignition Spellstrike and used Echo Jump to skip the doorway.
  • Raylta's Cloak of Light vitality aura was a lethal backstop — the DM confirmed two of the downed vampires "would have died as they started their turn next to Raylta" — though the actual killing blows were landed by the front line.

Outcome

Four-for-four: Tivael killed Kaba (silver Needle Darts), Bargrum killed Xibin (Boulder Roll), Ferrok killed Chamadi (Ignition Spellstrike), and Bael's final two-action blast killed Lauso (Tivael's Frostbite had dropped her to single digits first). Loot: 4× +1 Resilient Leather Armor; four rapiers (Shock and Frost runes mixed).

Character Spotlights

Tivael · the Liberator with the Long Game ★

  • Set the tone in the first five minutes — rattling off titles at the rescued Sarah ("We are the liberators. We are the vampire destroyers. The liberators of the Bounty. Your saviors."), then, with a beat, "Hey, let me brag a bit. I had to after all that."
  • Worked every lane in the Vye grinder — a Telekinetic Projectile crit and a Demoralize crit on the Stranglers, Evangelize to stupefy Vye, and a for-fun Fireball that caught both the Zombie Hulk and Vye (both failed — the Hulk destroyed).
  • The squishy-caster self-knowledge — "I'm gonna stay here and not get in the way of anything scary."
  • The table's sharpest plot-reader — to Raylta's theory that Deg is the real puppet-master behind Nizca, the line of the night: "I'm not sure if you're cooking or you're burning the kitchen."
  • In the vampire finale — killed Kaba with silver Needle Darts, chipped Lauso to single digits with Frostbite, and front-loaded Flurry of Claws (force) while his Bon Mots bounced off the vampires' iron Wills. Composed, funny, three moves into the campaign's mystery.

Bargrum · the Wall That Soaks Every Nat 20

  • Mechanically he was the Vye fight — Group and Shielding Taunts pulling the whole warren onto himself, Defensive Advances, Intercepts, and Shield Blocks, while the dice dropped what felt like every enemy crit on his head (the DM kept apologizing).
  • Took it in character — when the DM teased "you get quieter with every sentence, Bargrum," he answered, "I'm getting older and fading away," and even while grappled kept up the smack talk, telling the Dero choking him to go right ahead and let the minotaur go.
  • The party's heart and over-engineer — field-treated Sarah for shock, and pitched an elaborate plan to crack the vampires' coffins open and excavate a sunlight hole so any returning vampire would get a nice sun bath.
  • Pure delight at the manacle-happy Clockwork Sentries — "I love creatures that do weird shit, okay?"
  • The only possible Conan response to the Nin blood-siphon trap — "Nobody makes me bleed my own blood." And he crushed Xibin with a Boulder Roll in the finale.

Ferrok · Cursed Dice, Steady Hands

  • A study in heroic frustration that pays off — for most of the Vye fight he simply could not connect, until he narrated the breaking point: "there is no way this man lives another round — Ferrok, extremely frustrated, lowers his horns and tries to gore him."
  • Then the luck turned — across the back half of the night Ferrok was the kill leader, finishing both Clockwork Sentries, hacking the Irnakurse silent, and running Chamadi through in the finale — all while being the enemies' favorite target.
  • The party's blunt moral compass — to Sarah: "We're here to put an end to all this. That's why we're here."
  • Solved his oldest problem — the doorways that plague him ("I always get stuck on doorways") now simply blinked past, courtesy of a brand-new Echo Jump teleport.
  • Comedy in the margins — "Evil Geff," the Fullmetal-Alchemist homunculus aside, and the death-flag he hung on Speckle the instant the party adopted it.

Bael · the Adaptive Pyromaniac

  • Opened combats the way he always does — "Bael will raise up his finger and send in the solar detonation" — and crit-failed a Zombie Hulk for ~68.
  • The standout was his read in the finale — clocking the vampires' cold/frost rapiers, he flipped Thermal Nimbus to cold so the whole party got cold resistance, then carved them up with flying-flame birdies (and landed the final blow on Lauso).
  • Bedside manner is its own bit — the whiskey-water placebo for Sarah; for the drained prisoners, "I'll write a small note saying: this is food for you. Please eat up."
  • Sound engineering instincts — on disabling the world-ending Shadow Beacon, "Is this the percussive engineering I've heard about?" He also ran the lockpicking sideshow (crit-fail, hero point, "righty tighty, lefty Lucy") and translated for Speckle in Azlanti.
  • When his blasts ran dry against Vye, he "heroically ran up and conjured a small fiery knife" to stab him. Chaos with a working brain.

Raylta · the Conscience and the Quiet Deterrent

  • A lethal backstop by standing still — her Cloak of Light vitality aura had the DM marveling that two of the felled vampires "would have died" just from starting a turn beside her ("just by standing near me") — and she landed the killing needle dart on Vye, the session's 11-round boss.
  • The healing economy — rank-2-through-5 Heals, Nudge the Scales, heal scrolls, and a 48-point Lay on Hands, all while sliding into Cursebound/Enfeebled to keep a crit-battered Ferrok upright.
  • The conscience first — introduced the party as "just the good guys, lady," noted with pride that "we're getting better at talking to people," and grieved the drained prisoners ("these poor people").
  • The session's purest joy — adopted Speckle the three-eyed homunculus ("I'd love a pet rock!… It's my dumb three-eyed rock") and fiercely defended him from Ferrok's jinx ("do not give him a death flag!").
  • The party's theorist — floated that Deg might be the true mastermind pulling Nizca's strings; and she relishes a good villain — of Vye's clutch Translocate, "I love spellcasters, man."
