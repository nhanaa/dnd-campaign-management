## Session Summary

The session opened on the corpse of last week's nightmare — the surgeon Geff dead, his table cleared, and the woman he'd been cutting on (no anesthetic) finally freed. Her name was **Sarah**, and once the party calmed her with a blanket, a cup of whiskey-flavored water, and the world's least reassuring introduction ("we're just the good guys, lady"), she pointed them onward: the upper floors are a warzone, ruled by a red-robed vampire woman with long black hair — *not* Nizca — and stalked by tortured, self-mutilated "things" that fight the vampires on sight. The party sent Sarah down to safety and pressed on to clear the rest of the surgical floor.

What they found instead was a Dero warren and an **eleven-round meat grinder**. Three spiked-chain Stranglers screamed for help, three kukri-swinging Sentries poured in, a wall-busting **Zombie Hulk** lumbered through, and presiding over it all was **Vye**, a vampire spellcaster who spent the fight stacking debuffs on Bargrum — Synesthesia, Vision of Death, Chromatic Ray, a party-wide Synaptic Pulse — and, when finally cornered at single digits, **Translocated across the room and critted someone on the way out** ("it's like the end of a movie, suddenly the villain can fight on par with the protagonist"). The dwarf soaked literally every natural 20 the dice rolled that fight; Ferrok, by contrast, could not buy a hit. It ended only when the party ground Vye down and Raylta's needle dart finally put him in the ground.

After a full rest, the floor turned gentle and strange. The party adopted **Speckle**, a three-eyed winged homunculus janitor hiding under a bed, and looted **Deg's workshop**, whose journals laid the whole plot bare: the Dero engineer Deg discovered N'Gokal ninety years ago, allied the Dero to the vampires as a food-supply chain, and — only after Nizca arrived three years ago — retrofitted an old Azlanti pacification device into the **Shadow Beacon**, now drawing power from the Plane of Shadow to blot out the sun so the Dero can walk the surface and Nizca can cross the sea to find a living man named **Lograsi**. (To stop the Beacon: smash it.) Then they climbed. The next floor's grand octagonal landing held four doors and the sounds of a fight; through the open one, two vampires were losing badly to an **Irnakurse** — a fleshwarp built from broken people. The party let the duel play out, the vampires misted away, and the screaming fleshwarp charged. They put it down fast, then chased the fled vampires — who turned out to be **four** (Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso) — into a rapier ambush. The vampires critted Ferrok into paste round after round, but Raylta's vitality aura was a lethal backstop (the DM noted two would have died just standing beside her), and the party closed the night four-for-four — a kill apiece for Tivael, Bargrum, Ferrok, and Bael. Two vampire-haunted floors and the Shadow Beacon remain.

## Key Events

### Act I — Sarah (RP)
- The party stabilized the freed captive, **Sarah**, who'd been operated on with no anesthetic. **Bargrum** treated her for shock; **Bael** flavored a cup of water to taste like whiskey ("*it's a mental thing — you think you're getting drunk, so you relax*"); **Tivael** delivered the mission statement (*"We are the liberators. We are the vampire destroyers."*).
- Sarah's intel: she came from **upstairs**, where the floors are a battleground. A **vampire woman in bright red with long black hair** seems to run the upper levels — and is *not* Nizca. There are also "things that looked like they'd tortured themselves" fighting the vampires up there (later identified as fleshwarps).
- The party gave Sarah directions and rations and sent her down, resolving to clear the rest of the floor first. Loot from the previous fight was claimed: Geff's **+1 Striking Bonesaw** and **+1 Striking Scalpel**, the bomber Zer's **+1 Striking Wounding Sickle**, **+1 leather (work coat)**, and **5 lesser elixirs of life**, plus a room of sellable surgical instruments.

### Act II — The Dero Warren (Combat — 11 rounds)
- Exploring the southern half of the floor, the party opened into a mold-choked fungal room where **three Dero Stranglers** (spiked chains, Strangle) yelled for intruders. The fight snowballed: **three Dero Sentries** (kukri + bola, Focused Blow), a **Zombie Hulk** that bashed through a wall (too big for the door; weak to vitality, *not* fire), and finally **Vye**, a vampire spellcaster.
- **Bargrum** became the fight's lightning rod — Group/Shielding Taunts pulled everyone onto him, and he absorbed an absurd run of enemy crits (Vye's **Vision of Death** for 30, **Synesthesia** for Clumsy 3 + Dazzled, focus-blow kukri crits). The DM's running joke: "*Bargrum gets all my nat 20s this combat.*"
- **Ferrok** had the inverse luck — he missed nearly every Spellstrike for ten rounds ("*I think I rolled above five, like, twice that fight*") and, in pure frustration, lowered his horns to gore Vye when his sword wouldn't connect.
- **Bael's Solar Detonation** crit-failed the Zombie Hulk for ~68 and dazzled the room; later flying-flame birdies scored a double kill on two Dero Sentries at once.
- **Tivael** worked the debuff lane: a Demoralize crit and a Telekinetic Projectile crit (44) on Strangler A, **Evangelize** to stupefy Vye, a **Fireball** that caught both the Zombie Hulk and Vye (both failed their saves — the Hulk destroyed), Electric Arc, and Dirge.
- **Vye** stacked control on Bargrum, hit Tivael with **Agonizing Despair** (which the bard shrugged off — the DM's *"Nope — that's our bard"*), Vampiric-Feasted Ferrok (who Reactive-Struck him), and at single digits **Translocated (rank 4)** clear across the room and critted on arrival. Raylta: "*My top five fight right here.*"
- The party finally wore him down; **Raylta's needle dart ended the 11-round fight.**

### Act III — Rest, Speckle & Deg's Workshop (RP / Explore)
- The party retreated to the administration wing for a full **8-hour rest**, then swept the Dero bunkrooms. Finds included a **Crater Mage's hat**, a **wand of Summon Elemental** (Tivael), assorted bombs, and a smoke fan.
- Under a bed they found **Speckle**, a three-eyed winged **homunculus** (the floor's cleaner). **Bargrum** Recall-Knowledge'd it (a construct, "the alchemist equivalent of pet rocks"); **Bael** acted as interpreter, talking to it in **Azlanti** once they realized it understood the language; and **Raylta** adopted it on the spot (*"It's my dumb three-eyed rock"*). **Ferrok** promptly jinxed it (*"Speckle's definitely going to tragically die before we finish"*) — *"do not give him a death flag!"*
- **Deg's workshop** journals cracked the campaign open:
  - Deg discovered **N'Gokal ~90 years ago** and met Lady Nalushae in the lower prison; the Dero have long supplied the vampires with goods and **prisoners as food**.
  - The N'Gokal vampires worship the **Children of the Night** pantheon (Kamazotz, **Nin**, Urgathoa, and others).
  - **Nizca only joined Nalushae ~3 years ago**, freed from stasis late; Deg and Nizca have worked together in this tower since.
  - The **Shadow Beacon** at the tower's top was originally an Azlanti device that siphoned the **Plane of Dreams** to pacify rioting prisoners. Deg and Nizca **retrofitted it to draw from the Netherworld / Plane of Shadow** — to blot out the sun, let the Dero walk the surface, and let Nizca cross the sea to find a living man, **Lograsi**.
  - **How to stop it:** the device is complex enough to have a failsafe, but smashing it would functionally work ("*percussive engineering*").
- Reception-room maps showed the tower's layout: **three more floors plus the observation deck** above. The party torched a room of **six empty coffins** and found, behind a **Nin-warded blood-siphon trap** (all five resisted — *"Nobody makes me bleed my own blood"*), a cell of **prisoners drained near-dry of blood**; they staged them with Sarah for later rescue.

### Act IV — The Clockwork Sentries (Combat)
- Behind a locked door (Bael picked it after a crit-fail and a hero point) waited a pair of giant multi-armed **Clockwork Sentries**, hammer-wielding and **weak to electricity**.
- Their gimmick: **Sudden Seize** grapples plus an **arrest** action that claps *manacles* on grabbed targets — they bound Tivael's legs ("*You're under arrest!*") and tried to chain the whole party. Tivael hero-pointed a flat check to keep casting while grabbed.
- **Ferrok** (Ignition Spellstrikes, shock rune doing work), **Bael** (electric blasts), **Bargrum** (Titan-wrestler trip + Taunts), and **Raylta** (needle darts) brought them down. Loot: **two +1 Striking Mauls** and **12 sets of manacles** (the party's growing manacle fortune).

### Act V — The Irnakurse (Combat)
- Up the stairs to a grand **octagonal landing**: four doors — west (frantic singing/screaming), north (a glowing magical ward of sigils and equations), east (clawed and battered), and south (shattered open, sounds of fighting).
- Through the south door, a wrecked moon-themed **ballroom**, where **two vampires** were losing a brawl to a flailing **fleshwarp**. **Raylta** RK'd it: a **flesh warp** — once people, broken down and re-amalgamated, with only faint scraps of human consciousness left (not mindless). The party chose to **let the duel play out** for milestone XP; the vampires faltered, nodded to each other, and **misted away**.
- The fleshwarp — an **Irnakurse** — turned and **charged**. Its **Storm of Tentacles** (reach ~22 ft) hit Ferrok, Bargrum, Tivael, and Raylta at once, and **Mindlash** stunned Bargrum and Raylta. **Bael** (Nimbus + a nat-20 blast), **Ferrok** (Ignition Spellstrike + swings), and **Tivael** (Courageous Anthem + Fortissimo +2, Telekinetic Projectile 25) hacked it apart fast. The party took the kill grimly — "*this poor creature*."

### Act VI — The Four Vampires (Combat — finale)
- The two fled vampires **barricaded** a north door. The party forced it (Bael unlocked — "*righty tighty, lefty Lucy*" — Ferrok charged it open), and the ambush sprang: not two vampires but **four** — **Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso** — rapiers ready, with a **+2 circumstance bonus to initiative** because the party had been loudly threatening them through the door.
- The vampires (Fast Healing, feint, Nimble Dodge, flanking; best save Will, worst Fort; resist non-silver physical, **cold/frost rapiers**) zeroed in on **Ferrok** and critted him into the floor repeatedly (DM, later: "*I've done enough damage to kill you three times over*"). His glass shield, Emergency Targe, and a wall of Raylta heals kept him standing.
- **Bael** read the room and fired up **Thermal Nimbus on cold** to blanket the party in cold resistance against the frost rapiers, then flying-flamed multiple vampires per turn.
- **Raylta's** vitality aura (Cloak of Light field) was a lethal backstop — the DM noted two of the downed vampires "*would have died as they started their turn next to Raylta*" — on top of rank-4/5 Heals, Nudge, and a 48-point Lay on Hands that kept a crit-battered Ferrok alive.
- **Tivael** front-loaded **Flurry of Claws** (force, bypassing resistance), fired Needle Dart and Frostbite combos, and ran **Bon Mot** at their Wills (which bounced — it's their best save; one crit-fail bounced the penalty back onto him). **Bargrum** taunted, intercepted, and shoved with his silver Monkey's Fist. **Ferrok** spent the fight as the vampires' favorite target but kept finishing them with Ignition Spellstrike, and finally used **Echo Jump** to teleport past the doorways that plague him.
- The dice stayed cruel (DM: "*1920 is my mode this session*"), but the party closed it **four-for-four** — **Tivael** dropped **Kaba** (silver Needle Darts), **Bargrum** crushed **Xibin** (Boulder Roll), **Ferrok** ran **Chamadi** through (Ignition Spellstrike), and **Bael's** final two-action blast finished **Lauso**. Loot: **4× +1 Resilient Leather Armor** and **four rapiers** (a mix of Shock and Frost runes).
- DM's wrap: this floor ran long, so the finale slipped — **next session is the second-to-last** of Shades of Blood, then the finale, then Fists of the Ruby Phoenix.

## Character Spotlight

### Tivael · the Liberator with the Long Game
The party's herald and debuff engine, and this session its closer-by-attrition. Tivael set the tone in the first five minutes, rattling off titles at the rescued Sarah (*"We are the liberators. We are the vampire destroyers. The liberators of the Bounty. Your saviors."*) and then, with a beat, *"Hey, let me brag a bit. I had to after all that."* In the eleven-round Vye grinder he worked every lane he has — a Telekinetic Projectile crit and a Demoralize crit on the Stranglers, **Evangelize** to stupefy Vye, a for-fun **Fireball** that double-failed both the Zombie Hulk and Vye (destroying the Hulk), and the dry self-knowledge of a squishy caster: *"I'm gonna stay here and not get in the way of anything scary."* He's also the table's sharpest reader of plot — when Raylta spun a theory that Deg was the real puppet-master behind Nizca, Tivael landed the line of the night: *"I'm not sure if you're cooking or you're burning the kitchen."* In the vampire finale he front-loaded Flurry of Claws on Kaba and kept firing despite his Bon Mots bouncing off their iron Wills. Composed, funny, and always three moves into the campaign's mystery.

### Bargrum · the Wall That Soaks Every Nat 20
The load-bearing dwarf, and the session's quiet emotional spine. Mechanically he *was* the Vye fight — Group and Shielding Taunts pulling the whole warren onto himself, Defensive Advances, Intercepts, and Shield Blocks, while the dice dropped what felt like every enemy critical hit on his head (the DM kept apologizing). He took it in character: when the DM teased "*you get quieter with every sentence, Bargrum,*" he answered, *"I'm getting older and fading away,"* and even while grappled he kept up the smack talk, telling the Dero choking him to go right ahead and let the minotaur go. Out of combat he's the party's heart and its over-engineer: he field-treated Sarah for shock in careful third person, and pitched an elaborate plan to crack the vampires' coffins open and excavate a sunlight hole so any returning vampire would get a nice sun bath. He met the manacle-happy Clockwork Sentries with pure delight — *"I love creatures that do weird shit, okay?"* — and gave the Nin blood-siphon trap the only possible Conan response: *"Nobody makes me bleed my own blood."*

### Ferrok · Cursed Dice, Steady Hands
A study in heroic frustration that pays off. For most of the Vye fight Ferrok simply could not connect — Spellstrike after Spellstrike whiffing, until he flatly narrated the breaking point: *"there is no way this man lives another round — Ferrok, extremely frustrated, lowers his horns and tries to gore him."* (DM and table both felt it: "*every crit that Ferrok usually gets, all the enemies got.*") But the luck turned: across the back half of the night Ferrok was the **kill leader**, finishing both Clockwork Sentries, hacking the Irnakurse silent, and running **Chamadi** through in the vampire finale — all while being the enemies' favorite target. He's also the party's blunt moral compass (to Sarah: *"We're here to put an end to all this. That's why we're here."*) and quietly solved his oldest problem with a brand-new **Echo Jump** teleport — his eternal nemesis the doorway (*"I always get stuck on doorways"*) now simply blinked past. His comedy lives in the margins — *"Evil Geff,"* the Fullmetal-Alchemist homunculus aside, and the death-flag he hung on Speckle the instant the party adopted it.

### Bael · the Adaptive Pyromaniac
The session's tactician-in-clown's-clothing. Bael opened combats the way he always does — *"Bael will raise up his finger and send in the solar detonation"* — and crit-failed a Zombie Hulk for ~68, but the standout was his read in the vampire finale: clocking their **cold/frost rapiers**, he flipped Thermal Nimbus to **cold** so the whole party got cold resistance, then carved the vampires up with flying-flame birdies. His bedside manner is its own bit (the whiskey-water placebo for Sarah; *"I'll write a small note saying, this is food for you. Please eat up"* for the drained prisoners), and his engineering instincts are sound — on disabling the Shadow Beacon, *"Is this the percussive engineering I've heard about?"* He also ran the party's lockpicking sideshow (crit-fail, hero point, *"righty tighty, lefty Lucy"*) and, when his blasts ran dry against Vye, *"heroically ran up and conjured a small fiery knife"* to stab him. Chaos with a working brain.

### Raylta · the Conscience and the Quiet Executioner
The party's healer, heart, and — this session — its quiet deterrent. Her **Cloak of Light vitality aura** was a lethal backstop: the DM marveled that two of the felled vampires "*would have died*" just from starting a turn beside her (her own delighted "*just by standing near me*"), all while she cycled rank-2-through-5 Heals, Nudge the Scales, and a 48-point Lay on Hands to keep a crit-battered Ferrok upright. But Raylta is the conscience first: she introduced the party to Sarah as *"just the good guys, lady,"* noted with real pride that *"we're getting better at talking to people,"* grieved the drained prisoners (*"these poor people"*), and — the session's purest joy — adopted **Speckle** the three-eyed homunculus (*"I'd love a pet rock!… It's my dumb three-eyed rock"*) and fiercely defended him from Ferrok's jinx (*"do not give him a death flag!"*). She's also the party's theorist, floating that Deg might be the true mastermind pulling Nizca's strings. And she relishes a good villain: of Vye's clutch Translocate, *"I love spellcasters, man."*
