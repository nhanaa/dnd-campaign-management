## Session Summary

**The Nameless reached the final of the Ruby Phoenix Tournament, and the final did not happen.** The Lightkeepers never walked out. A kaiju did.

The losers'-bracket final came first: **the Speakers to the Wind**, led by **Mafika Ayuwari**, a professor of the Magaambya, with four of his students. *"I've been eager to face you all since we met on Bonmou. I hope you'll not disappoint."* Clutch: *"I've been looking forward to it."* Sovael: *"You were very formidable the one time I had the chance to observe you."* Before the bell Sovael had told the table what he was casting — a **ninth-rank Sacred Covenant** on everyone — and Ferrok had told him not to draw all the fire. Sovael: *"It's kind of like, damn you do, damn you don't, because I need to do my stuff as well."*

Mafika smiled — *"As I understand it, you're all fans of rather large spells. Then let's open this with a bang, shall we?"* — and dropped a ninth-rank **Falling Stars** on the whole party **and two of his own students**. Cody, out of character: *"I've already declared it. I can't just walk back now that I see the math."* It cost his side 264 hit points; the archer Surjit lost half his health to his own professor, took one turn all fight, and was dead to Clutch's jaws by the end of round one. (*"Man, Surjit's not having a good day, you guys."*) What followed was the longest fight of the tournament: eight rounds, an hour and forty minutes. Three times Mafika chained a big spell straight into a flurry of fists in a single turn — Cody: *"He is Muscle Wizard, very much so… If Bruce Lee was a wizard."* Sovael put a **ninth-rank Heroism on Sattva** (*"I'm betting on you"*) and went in with a ninth-rank Wails of the Damned; **Mafika burned a tenth-rank Nullify to stop it** and took 39 damage for the privilege. In round three a ninth-rank **Pierce the Sky** critted Sovael for 104 and Sattva for 124, deafening both, and the follow-up spells put both at zero inside one enemy round. **Sacred Covenant held them both at 1 HP.** That left four of the five deaf, the Courageous Anthem playing to a front line that couldn't hear it, and the table played it: Seva — *"I'm the only one with hearing right now."* Ferrok — *"Are they cheering for us?"* Sovael — *"Why is the stadium so empty?"* Sattva shot Akila dead on the same turn: *"The lack of sound made me focus."*

Ferrok hit zero **four times** — Sacred Covenant, an actual knockout that Seva's Battle Medicine reversed, It's Not Over, and finally Living for the Applause — while naming every attack like an anime protagonist (*"Ferrok raises his sword and shouts, 1,000-Year Slash"*). Clutch finally got to say the line he has been rehearsing since the Ruby Palace: *"Clutch is going to put all his practice to use and say, I must break you. And hope it sounds good, because he can't hear himself."* When Clutch landed a natural-20 Demoralize on Mafika, the professor's **Brilliant Wordplay** turned it around and landed it on Seva instead. **Seva killed Umbasi and then Mafika** — *"With that strike, he falls to his knees and cannot rise again."* Boundless Hummingbird, last one standing on 2 HP, teleported 120 feet away and turned to face them: *"Come on, then."* Sovael: *"I commend your spirit. Let's end this one."* **Ferrok**, at 1 HP, ran 70 feet and ended it with a **Goring Charge**. *"With that, the Nameless are the victors."*

A day passed. **Finals day**: Tagata Emi in attendance, **Hao Jin herself presiding** — *"Well fought, Nameless. This will be the final battle."* And then nothing. The Lightkeepers were not there. Neither, the party noticed, were **Tino's Toughest**, missing from the competitors' stands. Sattva gave Seva a nervous look — she has suspected the Lightkeepers since the party in the Ruby Village. *"I didn't do anything. Not yet."* Ten minutes; the crowd turning restless; the Lightkeepers about to be disqualified.

Then the pavilion shook. A shape taller than any building in Goka rose out of the bay to the west — *"a nightmare of saurian scales and fiery breath"* — and Halspin the Stung shouted its name from the stands: ***"That's Mogaru, the Final King!"*** From the east came the screech of a bow across strings, a hundred times too loud, and a haunting melody under it. Hao Jin had been watching the chaos without expression. At the music she snapped rigid and muttered a name: ***"Syndara, you fool."*** Then, to every competitor in the arena: *"Mogaru is not here by chance. I will deal with the one who sent him here. Please, warriors all, do what you can to divert Mogaru's attention away from the people. But do not try to fight him. Even I don't dare face the Final King directly."* The prize stands — *"We will settle the matter later."* She gave the Nameless a drake-drawn chariot, told them to start at the docks and *"for the love of Abadar, please find out where that infernal melody is coming from,"* and vanished in crimson flame.

Seva, heading out: *"I think it's looking for this. It was in my notes. We found an egg in Bonmou."* Sovael: *"Wait, what?"* Seva: *"We found an egg in Bonmou and we couldn't figure out what it was."* (The house-sized eggshell from the hex crawl. Sovael did not remember it.) A Recall Knowledge got only this much: a very large egg, from the bottom of the ocean.

**At the docks**, three-eyed musicians ten feet tall were playing Mogaru on — *"Dance to the sweet melody, Final King. Destroy, burn, crush"* — while mule-sized things dropped off the kaiju's hide into the bay. **Halspin** ran up to help (Ferrok: *"He thought we knew what we were doing"*) and offered Sovael a duet: both of them opened with **Spirit Song**. Sovael followed with a ninth-rank **Wails of the Damned** for 264 — and Cody warned him to *"be careful with your budget. This is not the only thing you're doing today."* At the top of round three **Mogaru's footfall sent a wave across the docks**, friend and foe alike, and one of the Shadow Oni was dragged out to sea and under. Sovael: *"A watery grave."* Sattva had just explained that earthquakes can't affect you if you're in the air doing a backflip; he critically succeeded the save. Five rounds, nobody down. *"Good work, Halspin. Thank you for the help."*

**On the main street** it was worse: more Rivka, two enormous floating fish, and two **jiang-shi** in bright robes that never touched the ground — **wearing faces the party recognised from the Fallen Moon**, the mob they fought at the Ruby Palace gates. **Urnak Lostwind** of Winter's Roar rounded a corner, already bloodied: *"Well, at least this tournament's ending with a little excitement, eh?"* The first spell thrown at Sovael was a Polar Ray; **his Spell Riposte counteracted it and sent it back**, and after a rules argument over what "a spell you're aware of" means, Cody ruled for him: *"Rules as written… So, you're good."* Then the jiang-shi went to work. They cannot be tripped, which Seva and Clutch each learned the hard way. They grab, and then they **Drain Qi** — and one of them drained **Clutch to Drained 5, which kills outright**, with Clutch still on 127 hit points and Sacred Covenant no help at all. **Sovael's Breath of Life caught it.** *"Honestly, now it becomes useful. Because now we can actually die."* A round later his **Fire Ray critted for 156** and dropped the jiang-shi holding Clutch. Urnak took three kills, Seva finally landed the Spell Swipe she has been trying to set up for weeks (*"It finally happened"*), and Clutch's jaws ended it.

Cody: *"Math-wise, that one wasn't supposed to be that intense, but I was rolling hot."* Healing happens next session. **Two weeks off — then "the conclusion of Book Two of Ruby Phoenix."**

## Character Spotlight

Breath of Life, which he said himself *"would be useless because we were fighting in tournaments,"* turned out to be the only thing between Clutch and an outright death on the main street: *"Honestly, now it becomes useful. Because now we can actually die."* And the Living Thunderbolt scroll he had been holding for Ran-to got him through two jiang-shi and a fish instead.

Let's end this one."* To Halspin after the docks: *"Good work, Halspin. Thank you for the help."*

So let's fix that"* — into fire resistance. To Sattva, mid-heal: *"This is a fortitude, so I don't think you can backflip this one."* To Sattva's Dragon Ball Z teleport sound: *"I have bad news for you, man.
