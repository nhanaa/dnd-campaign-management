# Encounter Analysis — Session 19 (2026-10-11)

Party: five PCs at level 17 — Sovael 212 HP, Clutch 316, Sattva 263, Seva 229, Ferrok 235. **Party HP pool: 1,255.**

Sources: the Foundry export (`rolls.txt`, single paste, 1,508 messages), the transcript, and the local *Fists of the Ruby Phoenix* Book 2 data files for the book values. Enemy max HP is Foundry's own number from the export header (all marked exact). AC, attack bonuses and DCs are read off the rolls. Enemy attack modifiers are visible in this log, so enemy dice luck is measured, not assumed.

**Headline.** The Speakers to the Wind were the hardest fight of the campaign so far by damage absorbed: the party took **160% of its HP pool** and won. By the book that team prices as a Low encounter. As Cody ran it, it prices past Extreme — and Mafika, who did most of the damage, also rolled hot. The docks were a comfortable Moderate. The main street was a true Extreme that the enemy dice did not explain: their attacks were average.

Sovael was the party's top damage dealer for the session (28%), while enemies saved against his spells well above chance. The buffs and debuffs mattered less often than usual — the whole package flipped 24 rolls all day and none at the docks — but two of his casts did heavy work that the damage column cannot show: Heroism on Sattva, and Sacred Covenant.

### Speakers: where the 2,005 came from

- **Mafika did the damage.** His three Halcyon Surge turns, Pierce the Sky and his fists account for most of it. He rolled 22 attack d20s at a mean of 13.6 (z +2.55, about a 1-in-200 run), with 11 of them at 15 or higher and two natural 20s. So this fight was both tuned high and rolled high.
- **His own team paid for his opener.** Falling Stars did 264 to Akila and Surjit — 14% of the enemy HP the party had to clear. Without it the fight is a round longer against a team that was already winning the damage race.
- **Round 3 was the breaking point.** Pierce the Sky critted Sovael (104) and Sattva (124); Arrow Salvo and Visions of Danger then took both to 0. Both of the party's Heal casters would have been unconscious in one enemy round. Sacred Covenant kept both standing, and each cast a big heal on the following turn.

### Main street: where the danger came from

- **Not from the dice overall.** Enemy attacks and Athletics checks: 98 rolls, mean 10.3.
- **Kun A specifically.** 13 rolls at a mean of 13.6, four Jaws crits, grab rolls of 18, 16, 19 and 20. It did all 223 of Sattva's damage.
- **Drain Qi.** Two actions a turn against a held target, and each step makes the next one easier. Clutch went 1 → 2 → 4 → 5 over four rounds. Five of the Risen Combatants' successful checks on him passed only because of his own Drained penalty.
- **Enemy Reflex saves against Sovael** (section 5). Cody's own read was that the fight ends two rounds sooner if one Chain Lightning save goes the other way.

## 4. What the buffs and debuffs bought

A flip is a roll whose result changed tier because of a party-supplied modifier. All 528 rolls with a DC were recomputed and match the logged result, and the recount matches all 62 of Foundry's own modifier tags.

### The whole package

**24 real flips all session — 10 against the Speakers, 0 at the docks, 14 on the main street.**

| Roll type | Flips |
|---|---|
| Party attacks | 9 miss → hit, 6 hit → crit |
| Enemy attacks | 2 hit → miss |
| Enemy saves vs PC spells | 2 success → failure |
| PC saves | 5 |
| Enemy skill checks | 0 of 27 |

Six more were critical miss ↔ miss, which is the same outcome and counts for nothing. The average flip had 2.4 points of party modifiers on it and needed 1.75. Sovael's effects were part of 13 of the 24 and supplied 37% of the points. The largest single lever, as usual, was off-guard from flanking and grabs (10 of Foundry's essential tags).

### Sovael's effects one at a time

| Effect | Flipped on its own | Part of package flips | Damage it added | What it prevented |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heroism (rank 9) on Sattva** | 3 saves, failure → critical success | 3 | 0 | **About 160 damage to Sattva, a one-minute deafen and a stun** |
| **Courageous Anthem** (three casts) | 2 | 3 | About 53 from flips + about 55 flat = **about 108** | — |
| Dirge of Doom | 1 | 4 | 66 (9 net of later overkill) | — |
| Scare to Death on a Rivka | 1 | 1 | 0 | About 32 — a claw on Sovael that missed by 1 |
| Overflowing (Absorb Magic) | 2 of his own saves | 2 | 0 | About 38 damage, a deafen, and the save that let him disbelieve Visions of Danger |
| Living Thunderbolt's dazzle | 1 flat check failed, of 6 | — | 0 | About 32 — a claw that had hit Ferrok |
| Talent Envy Demoralize on Rivka C | 0 of 9 rolls it touched | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Ancestral Memories | 0 of 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **Heroism was the most valuable buff cast of the session, and all of its value was defensive.** In round 2 against the Speakers, Sattva passed three Fortitude saves by +1, +0 and +0 — Desiccate, Explosive Barrage, Umbasi's Spirit Song. Each is a failure without the +3, and his ranger features turned each success into no effect at all. On his attacks it changed nothing: with it on, he missed Mafika by 1, 2 and 3.
- **The Anthem's value was mostly its flat damage, plus one chain.** Two flips in 46 rolls that carried it; four or five would be the expectation, so this is low dice. The one that mattered made Seva's Reactive Strike on Umbasi a crit by exactly 10. Her rune's clumsy from that crit is what let Ferrok's next two attacks on Umbasi land by exactly 0 — 30, and a 97-point Briny Bolt. That is 127 damage downstream of a +1. The Fortissimo cast at the docks flipped nothing and added 15.
- **Ancestral Memories went 0 for 2.** Mafika failed by 4 and Hummingbird by 6 with the −3 on; both fail without it.
- **At the docks the entire package changed no result.** Every hit would have hit and every miss would have missed. That fight was won on raw damage and Halspin.
- **Taunt's −1 was on 29 enemy attacks and changed none of them.** Its off-guard half did the work.

### What does not show up as a flip

- **Sacred Covenant** triggered three times against the Speakers. In round 3 it is the difference between both Heal casters standing and both unconscious.
- **Breath of Life** prevented a PC death.
- **Spell Riposte** kept 51 damage and Drained 2 off Sovael and put 41 and Drained 2 on a Kun.
- **Spirit Song** cost Mafika an action and his reactions for a round, and cost a Shadow Oni an action.
- **Wails of the Damned into Nullify** cost Mafika his rank-10 slot and 39 HP for one of Sovael's rank-9 slots.

## Uncertainty

- **Hero points.** Six re-rolls are counted from what players said at the table and are not flagged in the log. They are excluded from the dice statistics only where the log shows two dice.
- **Enemy saves are identified by token, not name.** They were matched to creatures by the damage applied afterwards.
- **Save-luck figures are raw spell damage**, not HP removed, except where stated.
- **The as-run levels in the budget are estimates** from the per-axis profile, rounded to a single level per creature.
- **Abilities "never fired" means absent from the log**, which records only what the table's Foundry client received.
- **Shadow Oni regeneration** ticked four times when fire or acid should have stopped it (60 HP). It is included in the HP cleared.
