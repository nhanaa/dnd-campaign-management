## Session Summary

**Ferrok came back, and two magma worms came up out of the floor.** Cody opened on next-campaign votes (*Dead God's Hand* won after a re-vote over the break) and a forecast of three or four sessions left of Ruby Phoenix. Halfway through the tunnels to the Blasted Lands, two enormous red cave worms burst out of the magma pools and breathed fire down the corridor. Two breaths took 91 off Sovael in the first round, and after a third he was at 64. His explanation, to Seva: *"Flame Oracle means I'm bad into flame, actually."* Clutch intercepted every bite that came near Sattva and Seva, however hard Sattva tried to stop him, and when a worm crit-grabbed Clutch, Ferrok's Swift Choreography got him out of it. **Sattva shot Worm A down. Sovael finished Worm B** by pulling a Sure Strike scroll out of his Retrieval Belt and freezing it with a critical Ray of Frost from the staff.

**The Blasted Lands belonged to two kaiju,** a purple manta ray that seeds obsidian forests and a gargantuan ooze of emerald lightning that tramples them. The party was beneath their notice. What stopped them was the **Leydroths**, horned anti-magic beasts circling the Speakers to the Wind, four of them unconscious, while Mafika's spells fizzled against their roar. Seva: *"We interfere."* Ferrok, in chat: *"Ferrok yells and charges."* Clutch's suggestion (*"Wait, you guys, no. I think he's got it. We wouldn't want to interrupt"*) was overruled. The opening **Dispelling Roar** was a 10th-rank counteract that stripped Ferrok's Heroism, and Leydroth B crit him twice (*"Ferrok coughs up blood"*). At the end of the round Sovael gambled a Heal scroll on him anyway, and the counteract rolled a natural 1. Clutch took a 116-point Horn Sweep crit meant for Seva and rooted Leydroth A with a natural-20 counter; Seva's own natural 20 put a 20-a-turn bleed on it, and **Leydroth A bled out on Seva's Gouging Claw**. **Mafika finished Leydroth B** with three Unfolding Wind punches. He ceded the Cypress (*"I think you've every right to claim it after that"*) and promised to be at Iron Mountain.

**The Boundary Cypress** stood alone on a hill, untouched by the kaiju's war. Sovael had *"never touched grass in my life,"* and the table agreed it was Sattva's. With Sovael's Guidance and Ferrok's Aid, he asked the ancient tree for one branch and got it (Nature 45). Around its roots lay what others had left behind: a Dispelling Sliver, a Staff of Nature's Vengeance, an 8th-rank Wand of Force Barrage and a suit of Impenetrable Scale.

**A long rest on the Solar Geon II, and a second interruption.** The Winter's Roar lay unconscious on a beach while Urnak Lostwind fought a fully grown linnorm alone, and a second one was coming out of the water behind him. The party took the second one, a **Cairn Linnorm** with an acid breath that drains and regeneration that only cold iron stops. Its breath critically failed Ferrok for 170, leaving him on 44 until Clutch's critical Battle Medicine brought him back. When its tail tripped Clutch, Ferrok called a Swift Choreography (*"I say do a Barrel Roll"*), and Clutch kipped up and broke its spine. Then Ferrok's natural-20 crit left it **Frightened 2**, which made the difference on the next turn: **Sovael's Debilitating Dichotomy** needed it to fail its save by one, it did, and it died. Killing a cairn linnorm carries the **Curse of the Crooked Cane**, permanent Enfeebled 2 and a year of age every day; Sovael critically succeeded the save. Urnak: *"Couldn't pass up the opportunity."* Sovael: *"Because sometimes passing up opportunities is a good idea."*

**Kaifen Bay.** The **Biting Roses** were already on the black sand. They had stowed away on a boat out of Goka until the captain threw them overboard, a pod of whales carried them inland, and a rogue wave washed them ashore here. Now they were preparing a ritual to glimpse the afterlife at sunset, and Yarrika offered to fold the party into it. While they waited, Sattva cast Augury and opened his eyes buried in sand, and Seva established that only one of them could be the Intercessor, who would also have to dance. Sattva: *"So from what I'm hearing, we got to have a dance battle, right?"* **Sovael won it, 44 to Ferrok's 40.** Ferrok typed *"Ferrok is shamed"* into chat and then argued that a magus with legendary Performance was a perfectly normal build. Sovael announced a post-tournament career as a star, with an album called *Finding Hao Jin*.

**At sunset the sea stopped.** The beach came back covered in old weapons, the Biting Roses were gone, and a ghostly woman with hook swords sat on a rock with her feet in frozen waves: **Jin-hae**, the last Intercessor. Sovael played background music while Sattva called her name and gave theirs; Seva explained what they needed and why. Jin-hae had been cut down on this beach and never moved on, and had always wanted one more real fight. *"Nameless. You want one of your number to be named the intercessor? Face me in battle. Win, and the title's yours."* Ferrok: *"Fair enough. We'll accept that."* She locked her swords into one blade and called up **Cloudsplitter**, a floating, stag-like kirin.

**Sovael gave the title away before the first action.** Sattva wished him luck, and Ferrok typed that he *"hands Sovael his bastard sword so he can duel her."* Sovael: *"I don't want that responsibility. I just want to stay home… Sovael is allergic to responsibility, you see."* Cloudsplitter opened with **Pierce the Sky**: crits on Sovael (102 through his Wooden Double) and Seva, lines of lightning across the beach, and Sovael, Seva and Sattva deafened. Jin-hae swept Seva and Clutch from Cloudsplitter's back. Seva's crit with her Rooting rune pinned the kirin in place, and **Ferrok's Thousand Year Slash** turned it to powder. Jin-hae landed, pulled a spear out of the sand and kept going. Cody first said she *"gains the elite adjustments"*; when she fell below half (*"Not done yet"*), he corrected himself: she had *lost the weak adjustment*. Sovael's **Roaring Applause** failed her Will save, which took away her reactions (a high-level fighter's extra one every turn among them), slowed her, and turned her applause into a manipulate action. Cody, afterwards: *"I fucking hate Roaring Applause as a GM… it was the perfect way to shut her down."* When Sovael's Bon Mot told her she wasn't even stronger than Ferrok, she answered: *"Sometimes you need to know when to shut up and fight."* — *"Well, this is how I fight."* At the start of her last turn she clapped, the applause provoked, and **Ferrok's Reactive Strike killed her.** Sovael: *"Let's go, Ferrok. I knew you were the chosen one."*

**Ferrok, Intercessor.** Before she reappeared, Ferrok typed that he *"presents her spear to Sovael"*, still offering the dance winner the title. Jin-hae chose anyway: *"Only fitting you earned the killing blow."* — *"Very well. I accept the title."* She laid a hand on his brow (*"I relinquish my mantle. May my successor burn bright"*), the sun set, and she was gone in a green flash. Back in the present, Grandfather Mantis turned into an old man who looked like Yarrika and vanished.

**The ritual at Iron Mountain.** Every finalist team was waiting at the peak. Ferrok, *"pending a written apology from Sovael"* about whose dancing was better, danced the Kagura with the Cypress branch while each team kept the ritual alive. The first movement was a critical success, with a +2 for the party having saved the Steps of the Sun, who played and danced alongside; the Biting Roses warded off the spirits it drew. The second was a critical success with the Arms of Balance's help, while the Winter's Roar shattered a boulder blown at the dais. In the third the braziers went out and the Arms of Balance relit them. The fourth was a success, and the Speakers to the Wind shored the wavering gate with a lattice of magic. Then every team lit up and pledged its strength to the Nameless. The **Celestial Dragon**, black and white and big enough to coil around the mountain, noticed it had only been eleven years and came anyway. Ferrok asked in Draconic for Hao Jin. *"I have granted your wish. It now falls to you to see it through."* The pearl it made passed through each of them and struck the Solar Geon II, which now has a dragon-headed lance on each prow. **The way to the island is open.** Level 19, a new team feature called **Affinity Ablaze**, and about 30,000 gp each to shop with.
