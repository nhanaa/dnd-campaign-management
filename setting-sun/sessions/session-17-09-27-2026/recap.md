## Session Summary

**The Nameless are 1–0 in the Ruby Phoenix Tournament.** The session opened on the one exhibition bout still owed from S16 — team versus team, against a troupe of performers called **the Steps of the Sun** — and ended three hours later with a sweeping first-round win over the monks of **the Arms of Balance**, in front of thousands, with an announcer calling the kills.

The Steps of the Sun were two dancers and two musicians, and the musicians went first. **Joon-seo opened with Spirit Song, and Halspin the Stung cast it again straight after** — two top-rank cones before most of the party had acted, stunning Seva and Sattva; Sovael saved both, the second on a natural 20. Sovael answered with his own round-one package — Courageous Anthem, a Lingering Composition that failed, and an **eighth-rank Divine Armageddon across all four** — and all four made the save. Then the dancers went to work. **Amihan**, a swashbuckler with a star knife, Vexing-Tumbled clean through Ferrok and landed a **Confident Finisher on Sovael for 94**. Joon-seo followed with a Telekinetic Bombardment, then cast **Courageous Anthem and Lingering Composition** on his own side — and succeeded at the Lingering check Sovael had just failed. Sovael: *"They're doing my thing."* Seva: *"Except he actually succeeded the trick."* When Halspin's Phantasmal Calamity came in, Sovael critically succeeded and threw **Ferocious Will** back at him for 29 — and then Halspin simply shot him. **Sovael went down in the second round.** Seva: *"Fuck! Sovael! … We'll get you up."*

Clutch got him up first — Taunt on three of the four, a Doctor's Visitation to Sovael's side and a Battle Medicine for 48 — then finished **Maalya** with a Hasted bite he hadn't expected to kill her (*"I wasn't expecting her to die, but I guess let's take advantage"*). Seva followed with a critical Battle Medicine for 62: *"Told you we'll get you up."* From the ground, Sovael kept casting. The musicians kept finding him — *"I took all the AoE spells this fight"* — and his answer was an **Incarnate Ancient Specter** that knocked Amihan and Halspin prone (Halspin failed and couldn't stand for the spell's duration), a **sixth-rank Roaring Applause** that stripped both musicians' reactions, and later an **eighth-rank Chain Lightning** and two Foretell Harms. Ferrok spent the middle rounds unable to hit anything — Seva counted the magi's misses, four in a row, then six; Ferrok spat into his palms *"like a Major League batter,"* re-gripped, and missed again (*"I will continue to look foolish and end my turn"*). **Sattva put Amihan down** — *"I need to kill her first." / Sovael: "Hey, no killing here." / "Incapacitate her first."* — and at the top of round six, **Halspin and Joon-seo forfeited**. *"Well fought."* Sovael, to his fellow performers: *"I enjoy your performances. I didn't do so well today."* When Cody had them react — *"you're a bard? What?"* — he held his ground — *"I haven't warmed up today, okay?"* — for about four seconds: *"To be fair, actually, that was a lie."*

**Four for four.** The other results: Tino's Toughest beat the Biting Roses, the Lightkeepers *"utterly decimated"* the Arms of Balance, and the Speakers to the Wind beat Winter's Roar in a long fight that came down to **Mafika Ayuwari against Ernak Lostwind**, which Mafika won. Hao Jin closed the evening, and the prize purse landed: **60,000 gold, 12,000 each** (Cody quoted tiers of 5,000, 10,000, 15,000 and 20,000 per win, then the 60,000 total, which is what was paid) (Seva: *"Swimming in money"*). Then **level 16**, a shopping break against Goka's level-20 market, and a week of retraining. Sovael took his new feat with a warning: *"You guys are going to see Sovael hurting himself even more."* Ferrok: *"That's why he's the cryptic, because I don't understand."* Clutch swapped Shielding Taunt for **Long-Distance Taunt**; Ferrok cleared a rare spell, *Take Your Places* — *"It's his theme. Most important part of a character. It's all theme."*

**Goka** was the largest city any of them had seen — dragon-drawn carts racing through the sky, thousands of fans on the docks for the teams' ships, an escort of monks and priests of Abadar, fireworks, and the Nameless' names shouted in the street because word of Bonmou had arrived before them. Hao Jin led them to **the Ruby Village**, a walled compound of red villas in the upper city, patrolled from the air by drake-riders. Sattva meditated to steady a mind the bards had rattled. Sovael sat down to **write songs and practise** — *"so that I don't screw it up again on stage."* That night the **Steps of the Sun threw a party** and every team came except most of the Lightkeepers (**Ran-to** showed; *"Of course, Ran-to, my boy… I like him"*).

The party was where the question of the session got asked. Seva asked **Takatorra** what Tino's Toughest wanted after the tournament (they'll decide if they win), then worked the room, team by team: Winter's Roar are in it to compete; **Mafika Ayuwari wants one of the artifacts of Old-Mage Jatembe**, which he believes Hao Jin holds; the Lightkeepers kept quiet; the Biting Roses will see what's there. Then Sattva turned it on the party — *"all this questioning of other teams has made me think, what do you guys want out of the vault?"* **Sattva** wants an item that can take him to any realm. **Clutch**, after visible effort, wants a scroll of the resurrection ritual, or something to make him stronger — and asked the better question of everyone else: *"Is it just to have the thing, or is there a place you want to take it, a thing you want to do?"* **Ferrok** wants *"the same thing I've wanted since I first set foot in [an] arena, which is just to be the champion"* — and after that, to open his own gladiatorial school; Clutch promised him the belt for the mantle. **Sovael** will find his cousin Tivael *"and then rub it in his face,"* and after that, maybe a prestigious post somewhere — or retire early. **Seva** wants nothing from the vault — *"I doubt Hao Jin has anything that I would want"* (Sovael: *"Don't let Hao Jin hear that"*) — and admitted her reasons for being here are *"purely selfish,"* and hers. Ferrok, helpfully: *"Are you going to assassinate Hao Jin?"*

Then Seva voiced something she'd been carrying. **Syu and Shino hadn't come**, and she didn't like it. Ten years ago, she said, there had been cheating at the last tournament — and things now felt *"a little too smoothly."* She turned to Clutch, who was *there* ten years ago. Clutch was honest about his own loss: *"Clutch was just out grappled and squeezed"* — a tougher opponent, not a cheat — but he'll keep his eyes open.

The **final exhibition** was at the **Ruby Phoenix Arena** across the Dragon Bridge, before Hao Jin, Empress Nai Yan Fei and a row of senior priests of Abadar. The Nameless drew **two Thrasfyrs** — red-scaled, twelve-foot bear-reptiles with chained arms and a second mouth at the end of a dragon's tail. They opened with **a double breath weapon**; Clutch's new **Improved Reflexive Shield** spread his block to Seva, and Sovael threw **Scintillating Safeguard** over three allies before the second breath. Sovael's **Whispers of Weakness** found the fight's key — **weak to cold and cold iron, Will their worst save** — and the Thrasfyrs' **regeneration only shut off to cold or acid**, which the party barely carried. Sovael had it in the **Boreal Staff**: he flew the length of Thrasfyr B in a **Frigid Flurry** (ally-safe — *"This doesn't hit Clutch"*), then followed with a new L16 trick, **Debilitating Dichotomy**, which he narrated as *"for some reason Sovael is shivering and saying nonsense"* — 66 mental into a Thrasfyr that failed on a natural 2, while Sovael's own half critically succeeded. Seva: *"Oh, I love this one."* **Clutch grabbed Thrasfyr B *"real hard"* and broke its spine** — Cody: *"That echoes across the arena as something snapped."* **Sattva killed B**; after a round of **Bon Mot, Synesthesia, a critical Demoralize, True Target on four allies and a fresh Anthem** from Sovael, **Clutch finished A** off a Spinebreaker-and-grab turn he'd opened with *"maybe Clutch's post-tournament plans are to become a chiropractor."*

Hao Jin laid out the bracket again — double elimination, and **the second-place team in the winners' bracket is seeded**, needing only three losers'-bracket wins. **+1 hero point for every match finished.** Round one: **Tino's Toughest vs the Lightkeepers; the Nameless vs the Arms of Balance.**

Opening day. Streets of screaming fans, the Dragon Bridge, an arena broadcast across Tian Xia, and the announcer, **Emi**: *"They might be the underdogs of this tournament, but have they ever shown promise? Give a welcome to the Nameless."* Sattva unfurled his wings and backflipped; Ferrok waved his black sword in gleaming dawnsilver; Clutch, a little overwhelmed, asked the crowd *"Who wants to fight after this? You? You?"* Seva: *"Oh my god, why am I nervous?"*

**The Arms of Balance** were four monks from the Houses of Perfection — Usvani (Qi Blast, Inner Upheaval), **Pravan Majinapti** (Breath of the Glacier: a cone that encases and slows), **Ranya Shibhatesh** (Draw of the Aurora: four 60-foot lines of lightning that drag their targets in) and **Jivati Rovat** (Magma's Grasp, which burns a Focus Point off anyone who fails). Cody's advice before the bell: *"Don't hoard spell slots… You're going to get to use them every fight."* **Sovael took him at his word.** Round one, a hero point rescued a natural-1 Lingering Composition, Talent Envy Demoralized Pravan, and **Divine Armageddon at DC 39 landed on three failures and a success — 266 damage** before the monks had moved. The monks answered hard — Qi Blast, Breath of the Glacier encasing Sovael and Sattva, Draw of the Aurora pulling the party together, then Jivati's Magma's Grasp stripping Focus Points from Sovael and Ferrok. In round two Sovael took a Stand Still hit on the way out, kept moving, and cast **Spirit Song into three monks: two failures and a critical failure — Jivati took 102 and was stunned 2** (Seva: *"102 damage. Stunned two. Yes."*). Then a quickened sixth-rank Heal on himself, and a Bounce Away off Usvani's Inner Upheaval.

After that it was, in Cody's words, *"crit, crit, crit, crit, crit, crit."* **Ferrok's Disintegrate Spellstrike and a MAP −10 follow-up flung Ranya twenty feet across the arena** — Emi: *"the first combatant to fall with a devastating series of strikes."* Seva ate a double-critical flurry from Pravan and answered with **Wooden Double** — *"I learned this from Sovael"* — which soaked 25 of the 154 and left her standing on 25 HP. Sovael spent his last Focus Point on Ancestral Memories and **flew the length of the line in a second Frigid Flurry, dropping Pravan**. **Ferrok's Blink Charge crit took Jivati** (Cody: *"with Ferrok, it's either all 20s all the time, or all 1s all the time"*; Sovael: *"The ultimate Magus experience"*), and **Sattva's Hunted Shot finished Usvani**. Emi: *"It is a sweeping victory for the Nameless as they proceed to the next bracket."* Sovael, flying slowly over everyone: *"Guys, did we win? Did we win?"* Group high five.

**Sovael did 646 of the ~1,640 damage the party rolled in that fight — about 39%, and 45% of the monks' actual 1,430 hit points** — with one kill. Tino's Toughest lost to the Lightkeepers and dropped to the losers' bracket with the Arms of Balance. **Next: the Nameless vs the Biting Roses**, the team that beat them in S14. Sovael: *"Guys, take care of the Mantis."*

Out of character: **Seva's player is out next week**; **Cody has closed StartPlaying applications** — *"It's going to be you five all the way through"* — and expects to finish the campaign around November or December.

### Seva · the Shade

### What Sovael is thinking about

- **The round-one AoE gets saved against.** Bout 4's Divine Armageddon went 0-for-4 at DC 37; the tournament's went 3-for-4 at DC 39. Two points of DC (level 16 + the Necklace) moved it from a table-setter to a fight-winner.

Ancestral Memories (−3) is the tool for a target that isn't already Bon Mot'd or frightened; on one that is, it buys at most the difference. Spend the Focus Point where the target is clean. **And it only works on spells** — it did nothing for Debilitating Dichotomy, which is a feat action; the Thrasfyr's save line shows no −3. Pair it with Frigid Flurry, Spirit Song, Divine Armageddon, Canticle or a Fire Ray attack, never with Dichotomy, Aether Beam or Scare to Death.
