## Session Summary

This was the session Danger Island paid the party back, and the session the party finally got a name.

They went **3–0 in team fights, never once forfeiting, and lost no feathers** — the first clean sweep of the qualifier — cleared three creature dens for 1,300 platinum, banked another ~3,900 gold each at the market, collected three separate divine blessings from a chain of mountain shrines, and **knocked a rival team out of the tournament entirely**. They closed on **eight of the ten feathers they need**, with a day and a half still on the clock.

It opened with a grave spinosaurus that swallowed Seva whole, and that turned out to be the session's motif. Ninety minutes later a tyrannosaur swallowed **Sovael** — and it was Seva, calling her shot out loud (*"I want to crit for my best buddy, Sovael"*), who killed the thing and cut him out. In between: Clutch punched a dinosaur in the face and narrated his own kip-up; a hundred-strong troop was routed off the field in a single round and shaken hands with afterward, one at a time; the party ate a meal for one god, swam for another, and sang a love song for a third at sunset — Sovael protesting the whole way (*"I don't love a love song"*), Seva on flute, Ferrok on bass, and Sattva volunteering as backup dancer. They also found a haunted watchtower with an unlit pyre at the top and decided, sensibly, to come back for it later.

The name arrived by accident. Challenged formally with nothing prepared, Seva blurted *"I don't have a team name. Let's do it — **the Nameless accept**,"* and Sovael declared it registered before she could take it back (*"I already registered."*). Ferrok had the shirts printed inside ten seconds. She spent the rest of the day losing the argument and by nightfall had surrendered to it: *"And we gained the name. The most important part on our first day."*

The cost came against the **Glowing Embers**, five arcane artillerists fighting on the day's last dregs of spell slots and still nearly winning. Sattva was dropped and revived. Seva took a critical Disintegrate for 146 and the table learned what the Phoenix Necklaces actually do: in a sanctioned bout you cannot die — a killing blow simply removes you, with no feather cost, and you return when the fight ends. She was *reduced to fine powder*, swept up by her teammates, and reformed from the dust afterward. Their handler announced the Embers had lost their last feather and were **eliminated**. Then the horn sounded over Bonmou, and the party watched lantern-lit boats carry the day's losers off the island for another ten years.

Day 2 opened on the session's centrepiece: **Brarkor and seven of Golarion's Finest** — eight ki adepts built like sculpture, the hardest encounter of the campaign so far. Eight rounds, sixty-eight minutes. Ferrok was critically tripped in round one, stayed down for the whole first half, and was beaten out of the fight entirely before a retroactive hero point and — at Sovael's direction — Seva's critical Battle Medicine put him back on his feet — after which he crit twice for 136 with the adamantine he'd bought that morning. Clutch took 412 damage and intercepted 230 points of it on other people's behalf. Sovael, having eaten a 72-point crit, spent 135 gold on Dust of Disappearance and was never targeted again for the remaining thirty-seven minutes — the adepts around him peeled off to the front line — while he delivered 201 points of healing. Sattva shot the boss dead from prone at a −5 penalty, then — put on his back three separate times — rose, grabbed the last man standing by the throat, and told him: *"You will never break my spirit."*

And Brarkor, going down, made the whole running joke canon: ***"You might have got me, but at least our team has a name."***

## Key Events

### Act I — The Grave Spinosaurus (0:00 – 0:25) — DEN, 300 pp

- Picking up straight from the Last Breath fight, the party healed and did arithmetic on its own advancement. **Clutch: *"We need 40 XP from somewhere. Let's run in!"*** **Sovael's** proposal: *"Let's go back and fight Jaiban."* — **Seva**, one beat later: *"No! Not Jaiban! No."*
- The next hex offered a creature den. Ferrok, waiting for the catch: ***"I'm waiting for the *but*. 'Found a creature den, but…' — oops, all kaijus."*** Sattva, moments before it mattered enormously: *"Who's ready for Swallow Hole?"*
- A **grave spinosaurus** — a level-15 book monster run with roughly two extra tiers of defence (AC 35 against a printed 30, all three saves +6, and **411 damage absorbed** against a printed 280 HP).
- It crit-tripped Clutch on the way in, then turned on **Seva**: 44 damage, a grapple, and a Swallow Whole that **crit-failed on a natural 1**. It ate her on the second attempt. She fought on from inside its stomach — ***"If you guys can hear me, you have to kill this thing quickly"*** — weighed teleporting out against stabbing out, and chose stabbing: *"I'm just going to hit it from the inside. **You fool!**"*
- Clutch's answer to a gargantuan opponent was to abandon the tools that don't scale and simply commit: *"It's gargantuan, so we can't do any of the normal stuff."* Then, narrating his own choreography — ***"Clutch is going to kip up, kind of rolling backwards out of the hit"*** — sizing up which limb to use (*"Looks like it means hands, so I got to throw these hands instead of the jaws"*), and, with undisguised joy, ***"We have punched a dinosaur in the face."***
- Sattva — who narrates a prayer before he buffs, all session long (*"that's Sattva saying a little prayer"*) — put an arrow into the thing's eye and told it to let her go. — then gave the correct, unglamorous advice: *"Spellstrike is casting a spell, so be careful with your air if you want to risk that."*
- **Ferrok's round-2 Spellstrike crit for 90 in a single action**; **Sattva's round-3 arrow crit for 90**; Ferrok's Force Fang finished it. Ryu: *"As this enormous titan of a dinosaur comes crashing to the ground, Seva comes sliding out of its mouth."* Seva: ***"That wasn't on my bingo card."*** — *"I feel very slimy."*
- Ferrok's first team-name pitch, on the spot: ***"Team name: The Tasty Treats."***

### ★ Level 13 (0:25 – 0:34)

- The spinosaurus's 120 XP tipped it. The DM's mid-day ruling: **no HP restore, no ability recharge; new limited-use abilities and *new* spell slots come online immediately, but already-spent slots stay spent.**
- Clutch reasoned his level-13 feat out loud from first principles. He wanted **Bonekeeper's Bane** (*"anything that starts its turn has to make a save… takes a status penalty to attack rolls and skill checks"*), tested it against how he actually plays (*"if something comes close and I grapple it, then as long as it's close, it's taking a penalty for as long as it's grappled"*), hit the only real objection — ***"It's good, but there's no automation for it. So it's like Bane minus automation"*** — and then **solved his own problem**: *"I think I have automation from a previous character… I'm going to go dig that up."* He took the feat, and it fired on a Glowing Ember two fights later.
- Seva, seeing the result: *"Whoa, what did you get? How did you get so big?"* **Clutch: *"Oh, you know, I found a little shop. They're selling a drink… a little kholo growth hormone or whatever it's called."***
- Ferrok on his spell pick: ***"It was a hard choice between picking thematic spells and picking useful spells."*** He took **Beheading Buzzsaw**, which went on to decide an entire fight.
- **Ingdani paid 300 platinum** for the den.

### Act II — Hana's Hundreds (0:38 – 0:48) — TEAM WIN, feathers 5 → 6

- Over twenty people came out of the trees behind a grinning human woman: *"Thought it was nothing but trees and, well, monsters. Well met, everyone. **Hana's Hundreds** challenges you for one of your feathers."*
- Seva's objection was procedural — *"Ugh, we can't even fucking refuse."* → **Ingdani: *"There is technically no team size limit for the Ruby Phoenix."*** → Seva: ***"Okay, I think you're just cheating at this point, but we accept."***
- **Sovael rolled Diplomacy for initiative** — the tournament-crowd mechanic — rerolled a 4 into a 17 for **45**, and put the party's entire alpha strike ahead of a troop that had rolled 41.
- What followed was **376 damage in one round** against a 450-HP troop. Clutch's Battle Cry Demoralize came up a natural 20 and was then **voided on the spot** — the DM ruled single-target mental effects don't work on a troop, so the Frightened it actually carried came from Sovael's **Dirge of Doom**, which allows no save. Sovael cashed his brand-new rank-7 slot on **Eclipse Burst**, which hit the troop's **weakness 20 to area damage** for 72; two reactive strikes punished the trample on its way in; Clutch's Defensive Advance jaws **crit for 54**; and **Ferrok's Spellstrike crit for 140 in a single action.** Three natural 20s inside a minute.
- Sattva's targeting advice was pure comedy: *"You see those two guys over there? They're bumping into each other. Strike them."* Seva: *"Which one? I squint. There's like a hundred of them."*
- Clutch intercepted Seva's failed trample save and converted it into a free Armored Counterattack. **The troop forfeited at the top of round 2.**
- Ryu, genuinely startled: ***"What was that? You guys were out for blood that fight."*** Seva: *"We're trying to survive, Cody."* Sattva, flat: ***"After Jaiban, this is nothing."***
- Then the grace note: Seva went down the line shaking hands — *"Well fought, I guess. To all hundred of you"* — one at a time, like a baseball team, while **Sattva blessed each of the hundred individually.**

### Act V — The Hex Crawl, Part 2 (1:44 – 2:00) — Ingdani, the Watchtower, and the love song

Something nasty haunts that tower."* Ten minutes, pass or fail, "similar to an encounter." **The party deferred it.** Sovael: ***"I think that's a comeback for later."*** Sattva plotted the route around it. **The single biggest unclaimed thread on the board.**

Yes, I play my flute."*** Ferrok came in on bass, Sovael called for a duet, and **Sattva volunteered: *"I'm the backup dancer for all your songs."*** Seva's verdict: ***"This is no longer a love song. ### Act VI — The Black Scorpion (2:00 – 2:17) — DEN, 600 pp

- Dusk on the dunes: *"you come upon what seems like this gleaming field of obsidian… these sleek, black, glass-like rocks that look gorgeous against the setting sun. But then they begin to move and rearrange themselves. And it's with that realization you come to the understanding — oh, those aren't rocks."*
- A **Black Scorpion** run at about level 16 — AC 39, attack +32, venom DC 38, and roughly 45% more HP than even an Elite template would give it. **Sattva's Hunt Prey plus a free Recall Knowledge exposed Greater Constrict before it could ever be used** — and because the scorpion never established a grab, **the ability never fired.** That is the entire reason this fight had no casualties.
- **It was a Clutch showcase.** He intercepted essentially every Rapid Stinging attack aimed at the party, took the venom saves himself, and by the end Ryu was narrating him as *"practically glowing green."* His own explanation: ***"These I don't mind too much, because I resist a bunch of it and then I get to hit."***
- Sattva took one of the stings himself rather than let Clutch eat all three — *"I'm good to take that. You can do the last one"* — and afterwards: ***"We're all patting your back after taking the hits for us."*** Then: *"Clutch, do you feel like you have scorpion powers?"* — Clutch: *"I was trying to remember what the DC Scorpion superhero was."*
- Sattva's hero point turned an 11 into a **natural 20** for a 62-damage crit; Clutch landed the kill.
- **600 platinum** — *"a satchel of platinum, and ever-growing."* Sovael's thesis for the whole Bonmou run: ***"That's what we're here for — making money. Even if we lose the tournament, we're going to be set for life."***

### Act VII — The Glowing Embers (2:19 – 3:05) — TEAM WIN, feathers 6 → 7 · **opponents eliminated** · **THE NAME**

- Ten minutes left in Day 1, one more hex. *"Stumbling out of the forest towards you all you will see a group of people, all of whom look just as exhausted as you, but they all lock eyes with you as one calls out: 'You there. **The Glowing Embers** challenges you for one of your feathers.'"*
- **★ Seva named the team by accident.** With nothing prepared: ***"I guess he fucking accepts. I don't have a team name. Let's do it. The Nameless accept."*** Sovael pounced instantly — ***"You know what? I like the Nameless"*** — and when she tried to retract it (*"No — pending!"*), ***"I already registered."*** Seva, conceding: *"Too late."* Ferrok: ***"Hands out t-shirts with the team name on. It's already printed."*** Sattva: *"Sorry, Seva."*
- **★ Sattva set an image for himself before the fight and held it for forty minutes.** *"I have something for this fight as well, since it's the last one for the day… as everybody starts to get ready to fight, you will see Sattva's sash that he has over his chest start glowing white."* He cashed it at the dramatic moment — immediately after being revived himself: ***"My sash glows really brightly, and I cast a level 7 heal on Clutch."*** No other player authored a piece of standing fiction this session.
- A nice DM touch: the Embers ran **with fatigue debuffs and randomized remaining spell slots** — *"they are tired too… for a few spell levels, what spell they have at that level is randomized, because others have been burned through, because it is late today."* The party had predicted exactly that. Their leader's only line: *"Do or die, everyone! Light them up!"*
- Even depleted, five level-13 arcane artillerists was **exactly an Extreme encounter**. Telekinetic Bombardment, Chain Lightning, Vampiric Exsanguination, Howling Blizzard, and three Disintegrates.
- **Ferrok's round-one Beheading Buzzsaw caught two Embers on double natural 1s** — 70 each plus doubled bleed. He announced the ability with relish beforehand (*"on a critical failure, they get to roll a DC 30 Fortitude or be beheaded"* — Sattva: *"You're like Krillin."* Seva: *"Destructo Disk."*), both Embers survived the decapitation save, **and the bleed alone finished one of them rounds later without him touching it again** — the other he had to return to three more times.
- Sattva's natural 20 on a Fortitude save erased a Disintegrate that had already hit for 78; he critically succeeded against Chain Lightning twice, ending the chain both times. Then a natural 1 against a *rank-3 Fireball* cost him 66, a Chromatic Ray took 50, and a Blink Charge crit dropped him. **Sovael's rank-7 Heal for 91 brought him back** — and Sattva woke straight into another spell: ***"Sattva wakes up and immediately rolls away from a lightning bolt."*** Seva, twice: *"That's so badass. That is so badass."*
- **★ Seva was disintegrated.** A Sure-Struck Disintegrate crit her for 146, and the DM stopped to explain the rule the table had never tested: ***"It would instantly kill you. However, because it is the Ruby Phoenix tournament and you've got your Phoenix Necklaces in effect, you can't die. But any event that would kill you outright instead puts you out of the fight, period."*** Asked whether a feather was forfeit: *"No — that is if you would actually die outside of a tournament fight. In this case you're back after the combat, no matter what… there's no reviving you in battle."* Seva, before the number landed: ***"Well, don't count me out yet!"*** And after: ***"Reduced to fine powder. Damn!"***
- Ferrok killed the last Ember. Their handler called out: *"**The Glowing Embers have lost their last feather. They are eliminated.**"* The party swept her up with a dustpan; **Seva: *"I reform myself from the dust."*** Sattva: *"Like a phoenix?"* Seva: ***"Sattva, not now."***
- **★ The name was ratified.** Ferrok, unprompted: *"The Nameless… one again. The Nameless."* Seva: *"Wait — is that our name?"* Sattva: *"I mean, it has a ring to it."* **Sovael: *"I like it. And we won after we declared it."***
- **Seventh feather. Day 1 over.** *"A horn is blown that resonates over the setting sun of Bonmou… as you begin walking back inland you turn south toward the coast and see several shining boats, their lanterns glowing amid the black sea, carrying the losers of the qualifying rounds away from Bonmou for 10 more years. **You've survived day one.**"*
- Seva, closing the day: *"I had a bad session today, guys."* → **Sovael: *"I believe in you. The nat 20 shall roll into your pockets."*** → and then, conceding the name at last: ***"And we gained the name. The most important part on our first day."***

- **Ferrok finally bought his high-grade adamantine** — a purchase that paid for itself within two hours — and **Sattva bought five bottles of fine plum wine**: ***"You gotta celebrate for the first day."*** *"We are filthy rich right now, so might as well."* He then corrected Ferrok on precious-material rune gating, working shown.
- **Full rest. Day 2 begins.** Sovael, on his own rebuild, unprompted: ***"Man, the Living Nexus is not worth it, guys."***

### Act IX — Brarkor & Golarion's Finest (3:19 – 4:30) — TEAM WIN, feathers 7 → 8

- Stepping out of their own base on the morning of Day 2, the Nameless met a team of **eight**: *"a variety of ancestries — orcs, elves, humans — but all of them, regardless of gender, are just these perfect, muscular, chiselled sculpture versions of each of their respective ancestries. Pro-bodybuilder type builds."* Leading them, **Brarkor**, whose artwork the DM simply put on screen: *"He's jacked. He's super strong."*
- *"Alright, hey, good morning to ya! **Golarion's Finest** — we challenge you for one of your feathers. What say ya?"*
- **★ Clutch made the name official, to their faces: *"You don't gotta glaze us like that, though. We're Nameless, not Golarion's Finest."*** The DM ran a bit of pretending not to hear it — *"So you haven't picked a team name yet, got it"* / *"You don't have a team name yet, I getcha"* — which only made it stick harder.
- Ryu, setting expectations honestly: ***"I make no promises that we can get through this fight by the end of the game."*** Seva: *"There's so many! They're so buff! Holy shit!"* The party actually coordinated for once — **Ferrok: *"I'll hit the right flank. You hit the left flank."***
- **Eight rounds, sixty-eight minutes.** Full breakdown below. The shape of it:
  - **Round 1 decided the next twenty minutes.** Ferrok took the very first turn off a Performance initiative; Sovael's Eclipse Burst caught six enemies for 169 applied damage off one roll. Then **a critically-successful Trip put Ferrok on the ground, and he stayed there until long after he'd been knocked out** — every punch aimed at him afterward carried Punch Down's +2d10.
  - **Brarkor opened by bear-hugging Clutch from behind** while Clutch had one of the adepts locked up in turn, and the resulting tangle became a running bit: *"It's just like one guy full-Nelson and a guy doing a full-Nelson. There's a magazine cover somewhere about this."* Seva: ***"I don't know what's going on here, but stop it at once."***
  - **Ferrok went out of the fight entirely**, then asked the question that saved him — *"Could I have burned my last hero point to not be dead and out of the fight?"* — and the DM allowed it **retroactively**. **Sovael called the play** — Seva had reached her turn unsure what to do (*"Would this work?"*) and he answered *"Can you all go over there and battle medicine for him?"*; her movement was exactly tight (*"Yeah, it's like just enough"*), and **her critical Battle Medicine got him upright** (*"I'm not really the best medic in this group, but here we go"*); Sovael welcomed him back (*"Welcome back, friend. Come help us over here"*); Seva supplied the reason: ***"Because of the power of friendship. Go get him, Ferrok!"*** He answered with **142 damage in two adamantine criticals.** His own accounting of going down: *"I took three of them occupied for a while."*
  - **Sovael spent 135 gold to stop being a target.** After a 72-point crit: ***"I'm going to do this, because I'm tired of getting hit. That's why I'm over here."*** In the fight where five artillerists dropped three 10-foot bursts placed to catch as many PCs as possible, he was **never once in one** — 68 damage while Sattva took 362.

### Honest ledger on the rest

Right decisions, smaller returns than the highlight reel suggests — worth separating the two:

- **The hero-pointed Diplomacy initiative bought one turn, not the party's alpha strike.** Only he and Sattva beat the troop's 41; Ferrok and Seva both acted after *Run Them Over!*. That one turn was still the best in the fight (Dirge + a 72-damage burst before it moved).
- **The Diplomacy initiative and the Eclipse Burst were one plan, and it worked.** He rerolled to get ahead of the troop specifically so the burst would land *before* it moved into the party — and he already knew **every troop carries a weakness to area and splash damage**. The +20 wasn't a discovery, it was the reason.
- **Divine Wrath's Sickened 1 flipped exactly one roll out of eighteen.** The decision was right and cheap; adept A's futility for the rest of the fight was dice.
- **Bounce Away prevented exactly 13 and did not reposition him** — he was prone and said so himself: *"I have a reaction that reduces the damage, and then I can go away, but I'm prone, so I probably can't."*
- **Fire Ray at AC rather than a save was a wash**, not a read — the adepts' AC was 31 and their save DC was also 31.
- **Talent Envy's critical Demoralize flipped nothing** on the target's next turn. It costs nothing, so keep taking the free lottery ticket.

### The one real habit to fix

**He spent about twelve minutes of the finale on the floor.** Tripped at 16:44, and the bill came due at 16:56 — the adept's crit landed against **AC 33 instead of 35**, which turned a **36-point hit into a 72-point critical**. The lesson isn't that he refuses to get up; it's that **standing earlier is cheaper than the crit**, and the crit arrived first.
