## Session Summary

After the bloody climb of the previous two sessions, the party took a breath. Back at Talmandor's Bounty they sold a literal thousand gold of looted manacles, split ~1,300 gp apiece, transferred runes, and bantered their way through a shopping montage before riding the long way back down to the Ebon Tower — through the cave, Underheaven, the Bleeding Palace, the clockwork elevator, and up the spiral stairs into the tower's administration wing. They expected a sea of guards. Instead, three sessions of slaughtered security had left the floor nearly empty, and waiting in the gap was the **Warden Tokainen** herself — a meteor-hammer Dampyr flanked by an ethereal Mistbear and a fifteen-foot animated statue of the Azlanti goddess Sigva.

Tokainen went down hard but fast; the real menace was her statue, a construct-armored juggernaut that grabbed Bargrum and tried to feed him into its own brazier. With the Warden dead, the party freed the tower's terrified kitchen staff, mourned the now-vanished engineer Aruneth, looted a secret reliquary behind the Nin shrine, and climbed — invisible — into a grand underwater lobby, where a guard-pack of clockwork soldiers and then a coiling **Medusa named Firitula** with three vampire servitors stood between them and the floors above. Firitula's petrifying gaze nearly turned Tivael to stone, but Ferrok ran her down the stairs. Level nine arrived on her corpse.

Above lay a rotting, dilapidated cellblock. A Dero strangler-pack and a lumbering flesh-golem "Charnel Creation" came first; a room of shambling filth-things — **Ofalths** — came second, and the party gave the corpses they were made from a funeral pyre on the way out. Then, behind a padlocked door, they found a woman strapped to a table and a grinning Dero surgeon — **Geff** — poised over her with a scalpel. What should have been a rescue became the hardest fight of the campaign so far: Geff's "1,000 Cuts" carved the whole party round after round, his bomb-throwing partner **Zer** rained elemental grenades, and a pair of Giant Crawling Hands grappled the frontline. Tivael — fully buffed, with Geff stacked in debuffs — was cut down by the scalpel-storm, hauled back up by Raylta, and rose to land a 72-damage Flurry of Claws crit that finished the surgeon. The party put Zer down — Tivael gallows-humor jinxing the killing blow, which connected anyway — and freed the captive. Two sessions of Shades of Blood remain.

## Key Events

### Act I — Downtime at Talmandor's Bounty
- The party rested, restocked, and liquidated the spoils of "like 20 guards" — including ~30 sets of manacles that sold for a thousand gold. Ferrok: *"Crash the market for manacles locally."*
- Each PC walked away with ~1,300 gp. Runes were shuffled: Ferrok took a **shock rune** for his bastard sword; Bargrum, Ferrok, Bael, and Tivael split three **resilient runes**; Raylta claimed the **Charm of Cold Resistance**.
- The settlement now sells up to 8th-level items (the party is level 8). Shopping ran long and loose, with retroactive purchases allowed up to next session.

### Act II — The Warden Tokainen (Combat)
- Returning to the administration wing, the party met **Tokainen**, a Dampyr Warden with a meteor hammer, her ethereal **Mistbear Eskis**, and a **Giant Animated Statue of Sigva** (the "Mistress of All").
- Bargrum opened with a defensive advance and a group Taunt; the statue immediately fixated on him, grabbing him and using **Burn Alive** to grind him into its red-hot brazier — repeatedly, with no save.
- In a comedy beat, Tokainen and Raylta both reached for **Divine Wrath** on the same round — Tokainen got it off first ("*That was my move!*"), then Raylta answered with her own — and the Warden crit-failed the save into Sickened 2 and Slowed.
- Tivael Recall Knowledge'd the statue (construct armor, Hardness 10, weak Reflex/Will) and dropped Phantom Pain on Tokainen; Bael's Solar Detonation and Battle-Medicine crit kept tempo.
- **Ferrok killed Tokainen**, running her through the breastplate with a flaming Ignition Spellstrike. After Ferrok softened the incorporeal **Eskis**, **Raylta's Divine Lance crit put the Mistbear down** (spirit damage bypasses incorporeal). **Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit and Bael's blasts cracked the statue's construct armor**, and **Ferrok crit-finished the statue**, which burst into flames.
- Loot: **+1 Resilient Breastplate**, **+2 Striking Thundering Meteor Hammer**.

### Act III — The Kitchen and the Reliquary (RP / Explore)
- The tower's **kitchen staff** (a chef and two servants, non-hostile) were thrilled the party had killed "Tokainen" — *"no longer have to prepare for these bastards"* — and were given safe directions out, plus an all-you-can-eat invitation to the abandoned feast.
- The party discovered **Aruneth the engineer had absconded** with his familiar — no note, no goodbye. Raylta took it personally: *"I loved him and now he's gone."*
- Behind the giant **Nin statue** they found a secret reliquary: a skeleton with **+1 leather armor**, a **candle of truth**, and an **emergency eye** (Tivael claimed the eye against future blindness).

### Act IV — The Clockwork Lobby (Combat)
- Ferrok cast **Shared Invisibility** and the party crept up into a vast lobby of deconstructed stained-glass sculptures — actually **30 feet underwater**, with a domed solarium looking into the ocean.
- A scout pass revealed hidden **rune-traps on the stairs** (later disabled via an Arcana check, with an Aid from Bargrum and a Guidance boost). Then **three Clockwork Soldiers** (weak to electricity, 10-ft reach).
- Tivael's opening Electric Arc broke the invisibility; the party traded with the soldiers. **Bael phased through one as a bolt of lightning** (Lightning Dash) for heavy damage, but the kills landed with **Bargrum** and **Raylta** (adamantine Needle Darts).
- Exploration filled in lore: a statue of **High Governor Avres of Vespera**, the prison's pre-Earthfall founder; a **grudge stone** (to Ferrok); and a psychometric read from Raylta that turned up a recent visitor — someone lounging here, reading, just days ago.

### Act V — Firitula the Medusa (Combat)
- Down a side door: **Firitula**, a snake-haired **Medusa**, with **three Vampire Servitors**. She promised to display their corpses "in the grand entry."
- Raylta RK'd her: **living** (not undead), best Reflex / worst Will, with a **petrifying gaze** (Focus Gaze) and a petrification **aura**, plus a Biting Snakes reaction and Serpent Venom.
- Tivael's weak Fortitude made him the gaze's first target — Slowed and one save from petrification; the gaze then turned on Raylta, who passed via Avert Gaze. Tivael **tried to Command Firitula prone (she saved — no effect)** and answered with **Flurry of Claws**.
- Bael's Solar Detonation dazzled her (forcing flat checks on her gaze); **Ferrok chased her down and killed her as she fled down the stairs.**
- Loot: **+1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing**, **+1 Striking Flaming Shortsword**, **+1 Striking Shock Composite Shortbow**, **Fortune's Coin** (Raylta), four explosive arrows. **The party hit level 9.**

### Act VI — Level Up & the Dilapidated Floor (RP / Explore)
- Long level-up beat: Tivael can now **fly** and picked up rank-5 spells ("*You know what's coming, guys*"); Ferrok got **Friendly Fling**; **Bael upgraded his Thermal aura junction** so allies in his aura get a free Step ("*I gave you ADHD*").
- Up a long dark stair into a rotting, mold-choked level. The party explored a **laundry/clothing room** of the dead and found an animal-companion-only **tree** (Raylta nearly ate it).

### Act VII — The Dero Stranglers & the Charnel Creation (Combat)
- Behind a door: a pack of **Dero** with spiked chains (**Strangle**: grapple + garrote) and a lumbering **Charnel Creation** — a mindless flesh-golem, resistant to fire and spell damage (save adamantine), weak Reflex, prone to going Berserk, and rigged to grab anyone who shocks it.
- The Dero strangled Ferrok and Bargrum; **Ferrok, warned off using his shocking sword, lowered his horns and gored the golem** instead. Tivael landed a Telekinetic Projectile crit; **Bael finished the Charnel Creation** with a flying flame.
- Afterward Bargrum proposed turning the corpse-room into a crematorium.

### Act VIII — The Ofalths (Combat)
- A cell filled with corpses and severed limbs; the refuse rose up as two **Ofalths** — aberrations born of filth, best Fort / worst Reflex, not undead, not mindless.
- Tivael RK'd them and hit both with rank-3 Fear; **Bael's Solar Detonation** lit the room (the bodies screamed). A quick, grim fight.
- The party gave the bodies a **funeral pyre** — Bael lit it, fanned it with air, and they offered a prayer.

### Act IX — Geff the Surgeon (Combat — the finale)
- Behind a padlocked door, a woman lay strapped to a table, marked for cutting, as a grinning Dero **surgeon, Geff**, raised a scalpel. His pet **Chomper** blocked the doorway.
- **Ferrok killed Chomper fast**, but Geff fled deeper, calling for help — and the fight exploded: **Zer**, a Dero **bomb-thrower** (fire/cold/acid grenades), and two **Giant Crawling Hands** (not mindless — grab + claw) joined.
- Geff's **1,000 Cuts** (a no-MAP scalpel-storm cone) tore the whole party apart round after round, repeatedly critting near-max. Zer's grenades chipped the backline; the Hands grappled Ferrok and Bael.
- Tivael unloaded his new level-9 toolkit — **Synesthesia** on Zer, **Inner Radiance Torrent**, an **Interposing Earth** reaction. Raylta hauled him back from a 68-damage scalpel-storm, and he rose to **land the 72-damage Flurry of Claws crit that finished Geff** (the surgeon Sickened and Off-Guard — the screenshot swing).
- It still went sideways: Raylta nearly died repeatedly, and the party limped to the finish — **Tivael openly jinxing the very last blow** (Bael's flame-bird going out to finish Zer), which landed anyway. Heavy healing (Raylta's rank-5 Heals, Bael's Battle Medicine and potion patch) held the line.
- With Geff dead, **Zer fell to Bael's flying flame**, and the party freed the captive woman. The DM called it the strongest fight yet — *"the dice just weren't in your favor."* Two sessions of Shades of Blood remain.

## Character Spotlight

### Tivael · Knowledge First, the Killing Blow Last
The party's knowledge anchor and, this session, its closer. Tivael **Recall Knowledge'd every single fight** — the statue, the Medusa, the Charnel Creation, the Ofalths — handing the party each enemy's weak save before a die hit the table, and he's the one barking the focus-fire call (*"Jump her, guys. Jump her."*). His debuff lane stayed busy even when the dice didn't cooperate: rank-3 **Fear** scattered the Ofalths and the finale's Crawling Hands, while a **Command** on the Medusa and a **Phantom Pain** on the Warden both ran into saves. Level nine landed mid-session (*"You know what's coming, guys"*) — flight and a fistful of rank-5 spells, debuted in the finale alongside the run's signature swing: cut down by Geff's scalpel-storm, hauled back up by Raylta, he rose and **critted the surgeon for 72 to end him** (*"You crit me, I'll crit you."*). And he's refreshingly honest about it — he'd written Geff off out loud (*"I thought he was gonna be just a bit stronger, but not that strong"*), and he owns his glass-jaw Fortitude and his habit of rolling last. Knowledge first; the killing blow last.

### Bargrum Stonefist · the Retired Wall
The load-bearing wall, and quietly the session's most human voice. Bargrum opened the Warden fight with flat dwarven defiance (*"Only half. We'll have to do better." … "I don't think we were asking."*), then spent the next four hours doing the unglamorous work: group Taunts, Defensive Advances, Intercept Attacks eaten on everyone's behalf, and a long stretch grabbed and shoved into a statue's brazier without complaint. Between fights he drifts into wistful, very-old-man tangents — staring out the underwater dome (*"I didn't expect retirement to be quite like this"*), a rambling fond story about his "nice but not good" cat, a deadpan claim to have read a magic book once *"back in college… over 200 years ago."* His moral streak shows, too: it's Bargrum who proposes giving the Ofalths' corpse-room a proper crematorium. Dryly funny, durable, and decent.

### Ferrok the Unyielding · the Closing Argument
The party's finisher and its driest wit. Ferrok personally **ended three of the session's marquee threats** — running Tokainen through with a flaming Ignition Spellstrike, crit-shattering the Sigva statue, and chasing Firitula down the stairs to cut her dead — and improvised cleverly when his shocking sword would have triggered the Charnel Creation's grab, **lowering his horns to gore it instead**. His comedy is deadpan and constant: *"Fashion is always the true endgame"* during shopping, *"I like the image of Bargrum taunting them while being burned alive,"* and a flat *"You're not my supervisor"* fired back at Geff mid-vivisection. He also cast the Shared Invisibility that got the party into the lobby clean — and, beneath the deadpan, let one note of warmth slip for the woman strapped to Geff's table: *"I think the lady would look happier now that we're here."* Methodical, lethal, and quietly decent.

### Bael · the Walking Detonation
The session's AoE artillery and its most quotable pyromaniac. Bael opened three separate fights with **Solar Detonation** and narrated each like a ritual — *"The power of the sun in the palm of my hands… oh god, it's gonna smell so bad"* over the Ofalths — then closed them out with flying-flame birdies and, in the lobby, **a Lightning Dash kill where he phased through a clockwork soldier as a bolt of electricity**. He's also the party's secret support spine: Battle Medicine crits, Thermal Nimbus fire/cold resistance bubbles, and a level-9 aura upgrade he gifted the whole party (*"I gave you ADHD"* — free Steps for everyone in range). When the DM pegged his theme as *"I'm a bomb and I'm about to blow up,"* Bael just leaned in: *"I explode better for myself."* And it was Bael who actually lit the Ofalths' funeral pyre and fanned it with air. Chaos with a conscience.

### Raylta Cailleach · the Heart and the Hype
The party's healer, its loudest cheerleader, and its conscience. Mechanically she ran the whole back line — Nudge the Scales every round, rank-5 Heals, a potion patch and Healer's Gloves in the finale to keep a dying party upright — while contributing real offense (the **Divine Wrath** that broke the Warden open — a crit-failed save into Sickened 2 — plus Holy Light and silver/adamantine Needle Darts, and the Divine Lance crit that put down the incorporeal Mistbear). But Raylta's signature is her voice: pure delight at her teammates (*"My teammates are so fucking cool"*), genuine grief for the dead (*"these poor people"* over the Ofalths, mourning Aruneth), a running love of gambling and cats (she snatched the Fortune's Coin: *"Raylta's gonna grab it… oh look at him, okay, I want a cat"*), a sweetly insecure streak about being left out of the post-fight heal-huddles (*"you don't have to say I'm not part of the group"*), and a beautifully exasperated *"Put the scalpel down!"* barked at Geff with her Security Badge. When the finale ground everyone down, hers was the honest line: *"That usually goes better in our usual fights than this. What the fuck is happening?"*

## Combat Encounters

> Round-by-round reconstruction for all six fights was verified by per-encounter review (one reviewer each). Initiative orders for Encounters 1, 4, 5, and 6 are from Pax's tracker; Encounters 2 and 3 are reconstructed from the rolls log.

### Encounter 1 — Warden Tokainen + Eskis the Mistbear + Giant Animated Statue of Sigva
**Initiative**: Bargrum → Giant Animated Statue → Tivael → Bael → Tokainen → Eskis → Ferrok → Raylta

- **Tokainen** — Dampyr Warden, meteor hammer, caster (Divine Wrath, Divine Immolation). **Eskis** — ethereal Mistbear (incorporeal; ignored Bael's fire). **Statue** — animated object of Sigva, construct armor (Hardness 10 until bloodied or crit), Burn Alive brazier grab; best Fort, worst Reflex/Will.
- **Bargrum** opened: Defensive Advance (Raise Shield/Stride/Strike) + group Shielding Taunt. The statue grabbed him and used **Burn Alive twice** (no save), then sustained the grab across multiple rounds.
- **Bael** opened with **Solar Detonation** (dazzle). **Raylta** answered Tokainen's Divine Wrath with her own — failed saves left Tokainen Sickened 2 / Slowed.
- **Tivael** RK'd the statue, then cast **Phantom Pain** on Tokainen (she saved — initial damage only, no persistent). **Bael** Battle-Medicine crit-healed Bargrum (37).
- **Ferrok killed Tokainen** with an Ignition Spellstrike, "running her through the breastplate."
- **Eskis the Mistbear** (incorporeal — ignored fire) was softened by Ferrok's Ignition crit + Force Fang and **killed by Raylta's Divine Lance crit** (spirit bypasses incorporeal).
- The statue was the real fight: **Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit and Bael's one-action blasts cracked its construct armor**, then **Ferrok crit-finished it** — it burst into flames.
- *Loot*: +1 Resilient Breastplate, +2 Striking Thundering Meteor Hammer.

### Encounter 2 — Three Clockwork Soldiers (the lobby)
**Initiative** (reconstructed): Tivael → Soldier A → Bael → Bargrum → Raylta → Ferrok → Soldier B → Soldier C

- Constructs, **weak to electricity**, 10-ft reach, Reactive Strike. The party started invisible (Shared Invisibility).
- **Tivael's** opening Electric Arc broke invisibility. **Bargrum** tripped and tied up soldiers with Taunts; **Raylta** used adamantine Needle Darts and got Reactive-Struck for it but landed the hit.
- **Bael phased through a soldier as a bolt of lightning** (Lightning Dash) for heavy damage — but it survived at single digits. The kills went to **Bargrum** (finishing that soldier next turn, *"that was Bargrum's kill"*) and **Raylta** (adamantine Needle Darts on another).
- A clean, mid-length fight — no party member seriously threatened.

### Encounter 3 — Firitula the Medusa + Three Vampire Servitors
**Initiative** (reconstructed): Firitula → Vampire Servitors → Tivael → Ferrok → Raylta → Bael → Bargrum

- **Firitula** — Medusa, **living**, best Reflex / worst Will. **Focus Gaze** (petrify a Slowed target), petrification **aura**, **Biting Snakes** reaction, Serpent Venom. **Vampire Servitors** — Fort weakest, rapier/bow, Nimble Dodge.
- **Raylta** RK'd Firitula and led the anti-petrification play (Avert Gaze). **Tivael** was Slowed by the first gaze and one save from stone (his weak Fort); the gaze then turned on **Raylta, who passed via Avert Gaze**. Tivael answered with **Flurry of Claws**.
- **Bael's Solar Detonation** dazzled Firitula (forcing flat checks on her petrifying gaze). **Tivael's Flurry of Claws killed one servitor; Raylta's Needle Darts (silver, then adamantine) dropped the other two** — Ferrok's Force Fang/Ignition chipped but didn't land the kills.
- **Tivael tried to Command Firitula prone — she saved.** **Ferrok ran her down and killed her** as she fled down the stairs.
- *Loot*: +1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing, +1 Striking Flaming Shortsword, +1 Striking Shock Composite Shortbow, Fortune's Coin, 4 explosive arrows. **Level 9.**

### Encounter 4 — Dero Stranglers (A/B/C) + Charnel Creation
**Initiative**: Bael → Dero Strangler C → Tivael → Dero Strangler A → Charnel Creation → Bargrum → Ferrok → Raylta → Dero Strangler B

- **Dero Stranglers** — spiked chains, **Strangle** (grab + garrote bludgeoning, silences spellcasting). **Charnel Creation** — mindless flesh-golem; resist fire + spell damage (save adamantine), weak Reflex, **Berserk at half HP**, **Electric Reflexes** (grabs whoever shocks it).
- **Bael's Solar Detonation** opened (dazzle/blind). The Dero strangled Ferrok and Bargrum; Bargrum tanked the golem's haymakers and Intercepted.
- **Ferrok**, warned that shocking it would trigger the grab, **gored the Charnel Creation with his horns** instead of his sword — and elsewhere killed **two of the three Dero Stranglers** (a Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike crit one-shot one). **Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit killed the third Strangler.**
- **Bael finished the Charnel Creation** with a flying flame as it tried to roar (it never went Berserk — it passed the check).
- *Loot*: +1 Hand Crossbows and +1 Striking Spiked Chains (three between them).

### Encounter 5 — Two Ofalths
**Initiative**: Ofalth A → Tivael → Bael → Bargrum → Ferrok → Ofalth B → Raylta

- **Ofalths** — aberrations of filth/detritus, best Fort / worst Reflex, immune disease/poison, **not** undead, **not** mindless.
- **Tivael** RK'd them, hit both with rank-3 **Fear**, then added a **Flurry of Claws** (43). **Bael's Solar Detonation** lit the room (the component bodies screamed). **Tivael's Flurry plus Bael's Eternal Eruption bead dropped one Ofalth; Raylta's Needle Darts finished the other.**
- A fast (2-round), grim fight; afterward the party cremated the corpse-room as a funeral pyre.

### Encounter 6 — Geff the Surgeon + Chomper + Zer + Two Giant Crawling Hands (finale)
**Initiative**: Ferrok → Zer → Geff → Giant Crawling Hand B → Giant Crawling Hand A → Tivael → Raylta → Bargrum → Chomper → Bael

- **Geff** — Dero surgeon boss; scalpel Strikes, **Daze**, and **1,000 Cuts** (7d6+2 piercing cone, no MAP restriction — fires every turn, hits the whole front). **Zer** — Dero bomber (fire/cold/acid grenades + splash). **Chomper** — Geff's pet beast. **Giant Crawling Hands** — grab + claw, **not** mindless (frightened by Fear).
- **Ferrok killed Chomper fast** at the doorway. Geff fled and called for help, bringing in Zer and the Hands.
- **1,000 Cuts** was the story of the fight — repeated near-max crits chewing through the whole party. The Hands grappled Ferrok and Bael (Bargrum **Interpose**'d Bael free); Zer's grenades forced Bargrum's Energy Interceptor.
- **Tivael** debuted his level-9 kit — **Synesthesia** on Zer (she saved — one round), **Inner Radiance Torrent**, an **Interposing Earth** reaction. Cut down by a 68-damage scalpel-storm and hauled back by Raylta's rank-5 Heal, he rose to **crit Geff for 72 with Flurry of Claws — the killing blow** (Geff Sickened/Off-Guard; the screenshot swing).
- It still nearly broke them: Raylta cycled rank-5 Heals/potion patch/Healer's Gloves to keep a dying party up. The win came with **Tivael jinxing the very last blow** — Bael's flame-bird on Zer — which connected anyway.
- With Geff dead, **Zer fell to Bael's flying flame**, and the party freed the captive. Hardest fight of the campaign so far.
