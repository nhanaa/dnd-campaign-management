Heath

After the bloody climb of the previous two sessions, the party took a breath. Back at Talmandor's Bounty they sold a literal thousand gold of looted manacles, split ~1,300 gp apiece, transferred runes, and bantered their way through a shopping montage before riding the long way back down to the Ebon Tower. They expected a sea of guards. Instead, three sessions of slaughtered security had left the floor nearly empty, and waiting in the gap was the Warden Tokainen herself — a meteor-hammer Dampyr flanked by an ethereal Mistbear and a fifteen-foot animated statue of the Azlanti goddess Sigva.

Tokainen went down hard but fast; the real menace was her statue, a construct-armored juggernaut that grabbed Bargrum and tried to feed him into its own brazier. With the Warden dead, the party freed the tower's terrified kitchen staff, mourned the now-vanished engineer Aruneth, looted a secret reliquary behind the Nin shrine, and climbed — invisible — into a grand underwater lobby, where a guard-pack of clockwork soldiers and then a coiling Medusa named Firitula with three vampire servitors stood between them and the floors above. Firitula's petrifying gaze nearly turned Tivael to stone, but Ferrok ran her down the stairs. Level nine arrived on her corpse.

Above lay a rotting cellblock. A Dero strangler-pack and a lumbering flesh-golem came first; a room of shambling filth-things — Ofalths — came second, and the party gave the corpses they were made from a funeral pyre on the way out. Then, behind a padlocked door, they found a woman strapped to a table and a grinning Dero surgeon — Geff — poised over her with a scalpel. What should have been a rescue became the hardest fight of the campaign so far: Geff's "1,000 Cuts" carved the whole party round after round, his bomb-throwing partner Zer rained grenades, and Giant Crawling Hands grappled the frontline. Tivael — fully buffed, with Geff stacked in debuffs — was cut down, hauled back up by Raylta, and rose to land a 72-damage Flurry of Claws crit that finished the surgeon. The party put Zer down and freed the captive. Two sessions of Shades of Blood remain.

Timeline

Act I · Downtime · Talmandor's Bounty

The Shopping Montage

  • The party rested, restocked, and liquidated the spoils of "like 20 guards" — including ~30 sets of manacles that sold for a thousand gold. Ferrok: "Crash the market for manacles locally."
  • Each PC walked away with ~1,300 gp. Runes were shuffled: Ferrok took a shock rune for his bastard sword; Bargrum, Ferrok, Bael, and Tivael split three resilient runes; Raylta claimed the Charm of Cold Resistance.
  • The settlement sells up to 8th-level items (uncommon counts as +5 levels, rare as +10). Shopping ran long and loose, with retroactive purchases allowed up to next session.
  • Pure character voice all the way through — Ferrok on the Skittering Mask ("Fashion is always the true endgame"), Ferrok remembering his forgotten Pyrefeather cloak, a tangent about The Boys, and the party briefly fantasizing about retirement.
  • Then back to Vil-Azmar — through the cave, Underheaven, the Bleeding Palace, the clockwork elevator, and up the spiral stairs into the Ebon Tower's administration wing.
Act II · Combat · The Warden Tokainen

Tokainen + Eskis the Mistbear + the Statue of Sigva

  • Waiting in the administration wing: Tokainen, a Dampyr Warden with a meteor hammer; her ethereal Mistbear Eskis; and a fifteen-foot Giant Animated Statue of Sigva ("the Mistress of All"). "I wondered when the bastards who took half my security down would be back." — Bargrum: "Only half. We'll have to do better."
  • Bargrum opened with a Defensive Advance and a group Taunt; the statue immediately fixated on him, grabbing him and using Burn Alive to grind him into its red-hot brazier — repeatedly, with no save.
  • A comedy beat: Tokainen and Raylta both reached for Divine Wrath the same round. Tokainen got it off first ("That was my move!"); Raylta answered with her own, and the Warden crit-failed the save into Sickened 2 + Slowed.
  • Tivael RK'd the statue (construct armor, Hardness 10, weak Reflex/Will) and cast Phantom Pain on Tokainen (she saved — initial damage only). Bael opened with Solar Detonation and crit-healed Bargrum with Battle Medicine (37).
  • Ferrok killed Tokainen, running her through the breastplate with a flaming Ignition Spellstrike. Raylta's Divine Lance crit put the incorporeal Eskis down (spirit bypasses incorporeal) after Ferrok softened it.
  • The statue was the real fight: Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit and Bael's blasts cracked the construct armor, then Ferrok crit-finished it — it burst into flames.
  • Loot: +1 Resilient Breastplate, +2 Striking Thundering Meteor Hammer.
Act III · RP / Explore · The Kitchen & the Reliquary

Freed Cooks, a Vanished Engineer, and a Candle of Truth

  • The tower's kitchen staff (a chef and two servants, non-hostile) were thrilled the party had killed Tokainen — "no longer have to prepare for these bastards" — and were given safe directions out, plus an all-you-can-eat invitation to the abandoned feast.
  • The party discovered Aruneth the engineer had absconded with his familiar — no note, no goodbye. Raylta took it personally: "I loved him and now he's gone."
  • Behind the giant Nin statue, a secret reliquary: a skeleton with +1 leather armor, a candle of truth, and an emergency eye (Tivael claimed the eye against future blindness).
  • Bargrum lingered at the underwater dome between rooms — staring into the ocean, drifting into a fond ramble about his cat and his long-ago "college."
Act IV · Combat · The Clockwork Lobby

Three Clockwork Soldiers (30 feet underwater)

  • Ferrok cast Shared Invisibility and the party crept up into a vast lobby of deconstructed stained-glass sculptures — actually 30 feet underwater, with a domed solarium looking out into the ocean.
  • A scout pass revealed hidden rune-traps on the stairs (later disabled via an Arcana check, with an Aid from Bargrum and a Guidance boost). Then three Clockwork Soldiers (weak to electricity, 10-ft reach) closed in.
  • Tivael's opening Electric Arc broke the invisibility. Bael phased through one soldier as a bolt of lightning (Lightning Dash) for heavy damage — but the kills landed with Bargrum and Raylta (adamantine Needle Darts).
  • Exploration filled in lore: a statue of High Governor Avres of Vespera, the prison's pre-Earthfall founder; a grudge stone (to Ferrok); and a psychometric read from Raylta that turned up a recent visitor — someone lounging here, reading, just days ago.
Act V · Combat · Firitula the Medusa

Firitula + Three Vampire Servitors

  • Down a side door: Firitula, a snake-haired Medusa, with three Vampire Servitors. "Would you like your corpses displayed where you die, or in the grand entry?"
  • Raylta RK'd her: living (not undead), best Reflex / worst Will, with a petrifying gaze (Focus Gaze), a petrification aura, a Biting Snakes reaction, and Serpent Venom.
  • Tivael's weak Fortitude made him the gaze's first target — Slowed and one save from stone; the gaze then turned on Raylta, who passed via Avert Gaze. Tivael tried to Command Firitula prone — she saved — and answered with Flurry of Claws.
  • Bael's Solar Detonation dazzled Firitula (forcing flat checks on her gaze). Tivael's Flurry killed one servitor; Raylta's Needle Darts (silver, then adamantine) dropped the other two.
  • Ferrok ran Firitula down and killed her as she fled down the stairs.
  • Loot: +1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing, +1 Striking Flaming Shortsword, +1 Striking Shock Composite Shortbow, Fortune's Coin (Raylta), 4 explosive arrows. The party hit level 9.
Act VI · Explore · Level Up & the Rotting Floor

New Tricks and a Room of the Dead's Laundry

  • A long level-up beat: Tivael can now fly and picked up rank-5 spells ("You know what's coming, guys"); Ferrok got Friendly Fling; Bael upgraded his Thermal aura junction so allies in his aura get a free Step ("I gave you ADHD").
  • A long, warm digression about cats — the DM's, Bargrum's "nice but not good" cat — between rounds.
  • Up a long dark stair into a rotting, mold-choked level. The party explored a laundry/clothing room of the dead and found an animal-companion-only tree (Raylta nearly ate it).
Act VII · Combat · The Dero Stranglers & the Charnel Creation

Spiked Chains and a Flesh Golem

  • Behind a door: a pack of Dero with spiked chains (Strangle: grab + garrote) and a lumbering Charnel Creation — a mindless flesh-golem, resistant to fire and spell damage (save adamantine), weak Reflex, and rigged to grab anyone who shocks it.
  • The Dero strangled Ferrok and then Bargrum; Bargrum tanked the golem's haymakers and Intercepted.
  • Ferrok, warned that his shock sword would trigger Electric Reflexes, lowered his horns and gored the golem instead — and elsewhere killed two of the three Dero (a Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike crit one-shot one). Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit killed the third Strangler.
  • Bael finished the Charnel Creation with a flying flame as it tried to roar (it never went Berserk — it passed the check).
  • Afterward Bargrum proposed turning the corpse-room into a crematorium.
Act VIII · Combat · The Ofalths

Two Things Made of Filth

  • A cell filled with corpses and severed limbs; the refuse rose up as two Ofalths — aberrations born of filth, best Fort / worst Reflex, not undead, not mindless.
  • Tivael RK'd them and hit both with rank-3 Fear, then a Flurry of Claws (43). Bael's Solar Detonation lit the room and the component bodies screamed.
  • Kills: Tivael's Flurry plus Bael's Eternal Eruption bead dropped one; Raylta's Needle Darts finished the other. A fast, grim two rounds.
  • The party gave the bodies a funeral pyre — Bael lit it, fanned it with air, and they offered a prayer.
Act IX · Combat · Geff the Surgeon (Finale)

The Rescue That Became the Hardest Fight Yet

  • Behind a padlocked door, a woman lay strapped to a table, marked for cutting, as a grinning Dero surgeon, Geff, raised a scalpel. His pet Chomper blocked the doorway.
  • Ferrok killed Chomper fast, but Geff fled deeper, calling for help — and the fight exploded: Zer, a Dero bomb-thrower (fire/cold/acid grenades), and two Giant Crawling Hands (not mindless — grab + claw) joined.
  • Geff's 1,000 Cuts (a no-MAP scalpel-storm cone) tore the whole party apart round after round, repeatedly critting near-max. Zer's grenades chipped the backline; the Hands grappled Ferrok and Bael (Bargrum Interpose'd Bael free).
  • Tivael debuted his level-9 toolkit — Synesthesia on Zer (she saved — one round), Inner Radiance Torrent, an Interposing Earth reaction. Raylta's "Put the scalpel down!" (her Security Badge) caught Geff slowed.
  • Cut down by a 68-damage scalpel-storm and hauled back by Raylta's rank-5 Heal, Tivael rose to land the 72-damage Flurry of Claws crit that finished Geff — fully buffed, the surgeon Sickened and Off-Guard (the screenshot swing). "You crit me, I'll crit you."
  • It still nearly broke them: Raylta cycled rank-5 Heals, a potion patch, and Healer's Gloves to keep a dying party up. The win came with Tivael jinxing the very last blow — Bael's flame-bird on Zer — which connected anyway.
  • With Geff dead, Zer fell to Bael's flying flame, and the party freed the captive. The DM called it the strongest fight yet — "the dice just weren't in your favor."

Combat Encounters

Encounter 1 · Tokainen + Eskis + Statue of Sigva · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Bargrum → Giant Animated Statue → Tivael → Bael → Tokainen → Eskis → Ferrok → Raylta. Tokainen — Dampyr Warden, meteor hammer, Divine Wrath / Divine Immolation caster. Eskis — ethereal Mistbear (incorporeal; ignored fire). Statue — animated object of Sigva, construct armor (Hardness 10 until bloodied or crit), Burn Alive brazier grab; best Fort, worst Reflex/Will.

The fight

  • Bargrum opened with Defensive Advance + group Shielding Taunt. The statue grabbed him and used Burn Alive twice (no save), sustaining the grab across rounds.
  • Bael opened with Solar Detonation (dazzle). Raylta answered Tokainen's Divine Wrath with her own — failed saves left Tokainen Sickened 2 / Slowed.
  • Tivael RK'd the statue, then cast Phantom Pain on Tokainen (she saved — initial damage only, no persistent). Bael Battle-Medicine crit-healed Bargrum (37).
  • Ferrok killed Tokainen with an Ignition Spellstrike, "running her through the breastplate."
  • Eskis the Mistbear (incorporeal — ignored fire) was softened by Ferrok's Ignition crit + Force Fang and killed by Raylta's Divine Lance crit (spirit bypasses incorporeal).
  • The statue was the real fight: Tivael's Telekinetic Projectile crit and Bael's one-action blasts cracked its construct armor, then Ferrok crit-finished it — it burst into flames.

Outcome

All three down; Bargrum badly singed but standing. Loot: +1 Resilient Breastplate, +2 Striking Thundering Meteor Hammer.

Encounter 2 · Three Clockwork Soldiers · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (reconstructed): Tivael → Soldier A → Bael → Bargrum → Raylta → Ferrok → Soldier B → Soldier C. Constructs, weak to electricity, 10-ft reach, Reactive Strike. The party started invisible (Shared Invisibility).

The fight

  • Tivael's opening Electric Arc broke invisibility. Bargrum tripped and tied up soldiers with Taunts; Raylta used adamantine Needle Darts and got Reactive-Struck for it but landed the hit.
  • Bael phased through a soldier as a bolt of lightning (Lightning Dash) for heavy damage — but it survived at single digits. The kills went to Bargrum (finishing that soldier next turn, "that was Bargrum's kill") and Raylta (adamantine Needle Darts on another).
  • A clean, mid-length fight — no party member seriously threatened.
Encounter 3 · Firitula the Medusa + 3 Vampire Servitors · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (reconstructed): Firitula → Vampire Servitors → Tivael → Ferrok → Raylta → Bael → Bargrum. Firitula — Medusa, living, best Reflex / worst Will; Focus Gaze (petrify a Slowed target), petrification aura, Biting Snakes reaction, Serpent Venom. Vampire Servitors — Fort weakest, rapier/bow, Nimble Dodge.

The fight

  • Raylta RK'd Firitula and led the anti-petrification play (Avert Gaze). Tivael was Slowed by the first gaze and one save from stone (his weak Fort); the gaze then turned on Raylta, who passed via Avert Gaze. Tivael answered with Flurry of Claws.
  • Bael's Solar Detonation dazzled Firitula (forcing flat checks on her petrifying gaze). Tivael's Flurry of Claws killed one servitor; Raylta's Needle Darts (silver, then adamantine) dropped the other two — Ferrok's Force Fang/Ignition chipped but didn't land the kills.
  • Tivael tried to Command Firitula prone — she saved. Ferrok ran her down and killed her as she fled down the stairs.

Outcome

Firitula and all servitors dead. Loot: +1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing, +1 Striking Flaming Shortsword, +1 Striking Shock Composite Shortbow, Fortune's Coin (Raylta), 4 explosive arrows. The party leveled to 9.

Charnel Creation — mindless flesh-golem; resist fire + spell damage (save adamantine), weak Reflex, Berserk at half HP, Electric Reflexes (grabs whoever shocks it).

Encounter 6 · Geff + Chomper + Zer + 2 Giant Crawling Hands · 5 PCs · the finale

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Ferrok → Zer → Geff → Crawling Hand B → Crawling Hand A → Tivael → Raylta → Bargrum → Chomper → Bael. Geff — Dero surgeon: scalpel, Daze, and 1,000 Cuts (7d6+2 piercing cone, no MAP restriction). Zer — Dero bomber (fire/cold/acid grenades + splash). Chomper — Geff's pet (died fast). Giant Crawling Hands — grab + claw, not mindless.

The fight

  • Ferrok killed Chomper fast at the doorway. Geff fled and called for help, bringing in Zer and the Hands.
  • 1,000 Cuts was the story of the fight — repeated near-max crits chewing through the whole party. The Hands grappled Ferrok and Bael (Bargrum Interpose'd Bael free); Zer's grenades forced Bargrum's Energy Interceptor.
  • Tivael ran his level-9 kit — Synesthesia on Zer (she saved — one round), Inner Radiance Torrent, an Interposing Earth reaction. Cut down by a 68-damage scalpel-storm, he was hauled back by Raylta's rank-5 Heal (63).
  • Risen, Tivael crit Geff for 72 with Flurry of Claws — the killing blow (Geff Sickened/Off-Guard; the screenshot swing). Zer then fell to Bael's flying flame.
  • Raylta cycled rank-5 Heals, a potion patch, and Healer's Gloves to keep a dying party upright; Tivael dropped unconscious one final time as the fight closed.

Outcome

Geff and Zer dead, the captive woman freed. The hardest fight of the campaign so far. Two sessions of Shades of Blood remain.

Character Spotlights

Tivael · Knowledge First, the Killing Blow Last ★

  • The party's knowledge anchor — Recall Knowledge on every single fight (the statue, the Medusa, the Charnel Creation, the Ofalths), handing the party each enemy's weak save before a die hit the table.
  • The focus-fire caller — the one barking "Jump her, guys. Jump her." to sic the party onto the Medusa, and again onto Zer in the finale.
  • Debuff lane stayed busy even when the dice didn't — rank-3 Fear scattered the Ofalths and the finale's Crawling Hands; a Command on the Medusa and a Phantom Pain on the Warden both ran into saves.
  • Level nine and the killing blow — flight and rank-5 spells ("You know what's coming, guys"); in the finale, cut down by Geff's scalpel-storm and revived by Raylta, he rose to crit the surgeon for 72 to end him ("You crit me, I'll crit you.").
  • Refreshingly honest — wrote Geff off out loud ("I thought he was gonna be just a bit stronger, but not that strong") and owns his glass-jaw Fortitude and his habit of rolling last. Knowledge first; the killing blow last.

Bargrum Stonefist · the Retired Wall

  • The load-bearing wall — group Taunts, Defensive Advances, Intercept Attacks eaten on everyone's behalf, and a long stretch grabbed and shoved into a statue's brazier without complaint.
  • Flat dwarven defiance to open the Warden fight: "Only half. We'll have to do better." … "I don't think we were asking."
  • Quietly the session's most human voice — staring out the underwater dome ("I didn't expect retirement to be quite like this"), a fond ramble about his "nice but not good" cat, a deadpan claim to have read a magic book once "back in college… over 200 years ago."
  • A moral streak: it's Bargrum who proposes giving the Ofalths' corpse-room a proper crematorium.
  • Dryly funny, durable, and decent.

Ferrok the Unyielding · the Closing Argument

  • The party's finisher — personally ended three of the session's marquee threats: running Tokainen through with a flaming Ignition Spellstrike, crit-shattering the Sigva statue, and chasing Firitula down the stairs to cut her dead. Plus two Dero and Chomper.
  • Clever improvisation: when his shocking sword would have triggered the Charnel Creation's grab, he lowered his horns and gored it instead.
  • Deadpan and constant: "Fashion is always the true endgame" during shopping, "I like the image of Bargrum taunting them while being burned alive," and a flat "You're not my supervisor" at Geff mid-vivisection.
  • Cast the Shared Invisibility that got the party into the lobby clean — and, beneath the deadpan, let one note of warmth slip for the woman on Geff's table: "I think the lady would look happier now that we're here."
  • Methodical, lethal, and quietly decent.

Bael · the Walking Detonation

  • The session's AoE artillery — opened three separate fights with Solar Detonation and narrated each like a ritual: "The power of the sun in the palm of my hands… oh god, it's gonna smell so bad" over the Ofalths.
  • A Lightning Dash where he phased through a clockwork soldier as a bolt of electricity, and the flying-flame birdie that put down Zer to end the night.
  • The party's secret support spine: Battle Medicine crits, Thermal Nimbus fire/cold bubbles, and a level-9 aura upgrade he gifted the whole party ("I gave you ADHD" — free Steps for everyone in range).
  • When the DM pegged his theme as a walking bomb, Bael leaned in: "I explode better for myself." And it was Bael who lit the Ofalths' funeral pyre and fanned it with air.
  • Chaos with a conscience.

Raylta Cailleach · the Heart and the Hype

  • Ran the whole back line — Nudge the Scales every round, rank-5 Heals, a potion patch and Healer's Gloves in the finale to keep a dying party upright.
  • Real offense, too: the Divine Wrath that broke the Warden open (a crit-failed save into Sickened 2), Holy Light, silver/adamantine Needle Darts, and the Divine Lance crit that put down the incorporeal Mistbear.
  • The party's loudest cheerleader and its conscience — pure delight at her teammates ("My teammates are so fucking cool"), genuine grief for the dead ("these poor people"; mourning Aruneth).
  • A running love of gambling and cats (she snatched the Fortune's Coin), a sweetly insecure streak about the heal-huddles ("you don't have to say I'm not part of the group"), and a beautifully exasperated "Put the scalpel down!" barked at Geff.
  • When the finale ground everyone down, hers was the honest line: "That usually goes better in our usual fights than this. What the fuck is happening?"
