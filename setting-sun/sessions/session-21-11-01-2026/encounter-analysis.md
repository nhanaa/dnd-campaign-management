# Encounter Analysis — Session 21 (2026-11-01)

Party: four PCs at level 18 — Clutch 334 HP, Sattva 278, Seva 242, Sovael 224. Ferrok out. **Party HP pool: 1,078.**

Sources: the Foundry export (`rolls.txt`, single paste, 692 messages), the transcript, and the book entries in the local *Fists of the Ruby Phoenix* Book 3 data for the four creature types fought. Max HP is Foundry's own number from the export header. Base AC is the printed value corrected for active conditions.

**Headline.** The sparring match and the Abbot were one fight with a single round of healing in between, and that is the session's real difficulty. Together they took **1,628 damage, 151% of the HP pool**, and left three of the four PCs at or under 37% HP while the Abbot still had 60% of hers. She stopped the fight; the party didn't finish it. The Kaminari were Moderate on paper and a hit harder in practice: 58% of the pool, on full rest, spread evenly, never close.

**Enemies rolled hot.** Their 45 attacks averaged a natural 12.47 (z +2.29, about a 1-in-90 result), so some of the 151% is dice.

**Sovael's biggest contribution was Synesthesia's Clumsy 3**, which took part in all 11 flipped party attack rolls, worth about 527 HP of damage that otherwise wouldn't have landed. In damage he was third of four by a sliver (28.1%), or first (32.5%) if the 152 HP that Drained 4 took off the monks counts. Sattva had the session's best numbers: four kills and the best damage per action.

## Uncertainty

- **Enemy modifiers are visible** in this log, so enemy luck is measured, not assumed. Saves are identified by token and matched to creatures by the damage that followed.
- **Reroll counts are low.** Five reroll lines; Sattva spoke of two more hero points on Treat Wounds that the log doesn't show, and a Reflex reroll called at the third Thunder Drums shows only one die.
- **Flip HP values are estimates**: the "normal hit" half of a crit is half the rolled amount, and the prevented-damage figures use averages.
- **Seva's 138 and Sattva's end-of-fight HP are derived**, not logged, from the heals that followed.
- **The as-run budget** rounds each creature to one level.
- **Abilities "never fired" means absent from the log.** Foundry records only what Pax's client received.
- **Level benchmarks** are medians from the local packs, which include legacy data.
