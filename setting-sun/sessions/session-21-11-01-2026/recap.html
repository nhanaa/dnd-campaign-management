Session Summary

The party could not read the statue. With the oni dead, the summit of Iron Mountain offered a sulfur lake, twelve zodiac statues, one of them (the Blossom Guard) snapped off at the base, and scripture in Tortoise script, the language Tian descended from. Sovael, Sattva and Seva tried Society to decipher it and got nowhere. Sattva sounded it out phonetically to no effect, a rock went into the lake, a Recall Knowledge established 10d6 fire per round for anyone following it, and Cody finally said it outright: "Guys, you just need to find a way to decipher the language." Sovael: "I think we're a bunch of bums." Clutch proposed the long way: "Let's get a rubbing of the language. We'll go on a 10-year journey. We'll go and learn it ourselves." Sattva: "Now that's the way an elf thinks." A scroll was three days away each way, so they settled in to wait a day for a spell swap, and a few hours later help arrived on a fiery horse.

Usvani of the Arms of Balance read the statues for them ("Tortoise script is a rather niche field of study") and explained the problem. This is a ritual site. Once every twelve years a ritual calls the Celestial Dragon, who grants a wish, and whatever is keeping everyone off the inverted island is too strong to break without Hao Jin, so they need that wish. It needs three things: the Dragon's Breath, a dance; the Dragon's Spirit, a named Intercessor; and the Dragon's Bones, a Boundary Cypress, a plant that is extinct. And it has not been twelve years. The lead was Rai Sho Monastery at the foot of the mountain, whose abbot could only be woken for allies of the monastery. "Basically, they want us to train with them."

Leeyan, a nervous samsaran monk in his early thirties, walked them through it. The tree: 150 damage in a single turn to a regenerating trunk, one attacker, buffs allowed. Sovael's critical Fortissimo (+3) set up Clutch, Seva told him to shout his attack, and the "Limit break" took three swings: 39, 73, 48, for 160. Sovael's +3 turned the second into a crit by exactly the margin it needed. The bell: "No way, performance? Hold on, I might know something or two about that." Sovael hit it on the first try by one point. The yak: Sattva hunted a dri for most of an hour (46 against DC 40), flying above it on new wings. The sand mandala: Seva, the best modifier at the table, needed a 38 and got 34, 37 and 36 across a hero point and an Aid from Sattva. She narrated kicking the sand ("You're just too impatient. I'm sorry.") and then Aided Sattva, who got 39. Sattva's version: Seva blending with his shadow so they synchronized. Somewhere out on the slopes, Usvani was screaming at a yak.

The sparring match came after the oxtails: two Rai Sho Disciples and two yeti Postulants. Sovael's Reach Spell and ninth-rank Synesthesia went at all four; three were caught, and Postulant B rolled a natural 1, stunned 2. When Disciple B's Inner Upheaval hit him, he debuted Dazzling Dragonet Disappearance and went invisible, which lasted through the next fight. Unable to be healed by anyone who couldn't find him, he burned quietly: "You don't even see me burning because I'm invisible… Why is it so hot right here?" He also cast Debilitating Dichotomy on Disciple B and failed his own half, 101 to the monk and 50 to himself ("I'll be fine"). A critical Demoralize sent Disciple A running out of reach (Terrified Retreat), which Cody said can backfire on a party, though this one had ranged attacks. Clutch's Intercepts took 186 in the opening round. Sattva dropped Postulant A and Disciple B; Seva dropped Postulant B. Disciple A jogged back, looked at his allies and yielded.

Then the actual exam. An elderly woman with a shaved head and a mischievous smile came around the corner, Leeyan stepped up beside her, and both opened their mouths and spoke in the same creaking voice: "I would test you myself. Gather yourselves and come at me." One round to heal. Abbot Tsujon (500 HP) and Leeyan (450) went straight for Clutch, and on round two the Abbot crit him twice in one Inner Upheaval flurry for 212. The block would have destroyed his shield, and a Mending Lattice saved it. Then Sovael's Whispers of Weakness paid off: Fortitude. Wails of the Damned came down on both, Leeyan rolled a natural 1 and the Abbot a 4, and Cody rolled the maximum Drain on her: Drained 4 on both, 76 hit points each on top of the damage. Quickened Casting put a seventh-rank Divine Wrath behind it, the Abbot rolled another natural 1, and she was Sickened 2 and Slowed 1. Once he vanished in the sparring match, no enemy targeted him again through the whole Abbot fight.

The monks had one lesson left. When Clutch dropped his grab to intercept an 80-point flurry on Sattva, the Abbot slipped his counterattack with the back of her hand: "Your protectiveness over your allies is admirable, young one. But do not disregard their attempts to protect you, either." Leeyan then crit Sattva twice for 197, and on the last round crit Seva twice for 250 (nonlethal; these were sparring fists). Seva answered with Wooden Double; Sovael told her she wasn't down because of the Sacred Covenant, and showed her the Step that took her out of reach. Sattva's arrow finished it. Leeyan vanished into pale blue mist, which drifted across the courtyard and into the Abbot's head and chest, and she smiled. "I think that's enough for now. You all pass."

She apologized for the deception ("a way for me to observe you from a distance") and, over tea, gave them the rest. The Kagura of Invocation is the dance, and the exercises were its basic steps. The last living Boundary Cypress grows in the Blasted Lands, under the feet of two kaiju who have been fighting for several thousand years. The Intercessor's mantle is still held by Jin-hae, dead at Kaifen Bay, because the bell has not rung. Her spirit is presumably still looking for a fight on that beach. And the missing years of energy? If all the finalist teams come together, she believes that will be enough. Sattva: "So we're giving Goku energy."

She also opened the vault (see Loot), and they chose the Cypress first: Seva thought it might impress Jin-hae to already have the last component.

On the way, the sky got weird. A roc carrying the Steps of the Sun came up out of a storm with two 20-foot Flying Mountain Kaminari behind it. One lightning bolt later the roc was falling through the clouds, and the brothers rode their thunderheads up either side of the ship: "Tell us, which of the two is superior, lightning or thunder?" Sovael, reasonably: "Lightning strikes first, right?" Sattva thought it was a trick question, Sovael predicted that fence-sitting would get them attacked by both, Sattva figured they'd get mad regardless, and Seva shoved him forward to say it anyway. "They're both two sides of the same coin." Both brothers, in unison: "No." Seva tried once more ("It is a real answer… No one has to be superior"), and they attacked. Thunder picked out the one who had answered and hit Sattva with a critical Storm Blast. He waved off Clutch's Intercept: "Honestly, I can take it."

The party fought it from the deck and from the air on potions and wings, with Sovael's reactive Scintillating Safeguards taking the edge off the opening drums and the Hurricane Bag, and a Synesthesia landing on each brother. When Lightning used thunder's drums, his brother caught him at it: "Ha! You admit it, brother! Thunder is superior!" — "Drop off, you idiot! I'm trying to help you!" Sattva, point blank in the air, shot both of them out of the sky: a crit for 109 on B, who fell yelling, and a crit for 104 on A. Seva: "We're sorry about your brother." Then: "I hope they work it out amongst themselves. Not our problem, really." Sattva: "They even used each other's abilities." Seva: "They were one and the same."

The rescued Steps of the Sun turned back for the peak. Halspin the Stung had answered thunder ("I'm a volume man") and been attacked by both as well, so nothing they could have said would have helped. At the edge of the Blasted Lands, a twenty-foot boulder papered in talismans sealed the only tunnel: Sovael's natural-20 Dispel Magic took down what he and Clutch had identified as a ninth-rank dimensional lock, and Clutch, with Sattva's Aid, lifted several tons out of the way (46).

Cody stopped at the tunnel mouth, with a warning that kaiju work like hazards rather than opponents, and a forecast: he is confident they will finish Ruby Phoenix in November.
