## Session Summary

**The Nameless hit ten feathers.** Not on Day 3, not on the mountain — in the middle of Day 2, in a nothing hex on the southern shore, off a feather somebody else had dropped while running for their life. Seva said *"By the gods, we did it."* Clutch said *"All right, let's go retire."* Then the same hex turned out to be a creature den, and the session went on for another two and a half hours.

The number is the headline; holding it is now the whole game. Ten feathers means every remaining encounter is downside-only — and the island noticed. Forty minutes after they hit the mark, the **enforcers** stepped out of a manor house and wagered *three of them* on a fight the party was not allowed to refuse.

It opened with a rematch. **Tino's Toughest** — the team that fought them to a draw on the beach at level 11, in the session that cost the party its monk — came back for an official bout, and this time it was not close. Four rounds, one feather, a handshake. It cost Sovael more than anyone: he opened round two with a **rank-6 Roaring Applause** across all four opponents, was told to his face by the DM *"I warn you, this will make you a target,"* answered ***"It is what it is,"*** and then spent the fight being the target — a Disintegrate, two Takatorra criticals, and finally the floor at Dying 2. He took **237 damage** — nearly twice what anyone else in that fight took — and for two rounds he was right: with the enemy team's reactions switched off, Ferrok crossed to the archer untouched and Clutch moved freely. The irony is exact — **the table ran the spell as sustained, and it died the moment he did.** Sattva ran into Takatorra's reach to revive him thirty seconds later, was puzzled that Takatorra had a Reactive Strike at all, and Sovael had to explain from the floor what had been keeping it switched off for two rounds — pointing out, with some heat, that this was not the first time he'd cast it in this campaign. **The reactions were back because he was down.** Takatorra swung and missed on a natural 2. Afterward: *"I think I did quite a bit. I provided values."* Ferrok, immediately: ***"You were essential to the battle."***

Then the island reminded them it does not care about their record. **Tamikan** — a kongamato, level 16, and the first wild thing on Bonmou that *talks* — hovered overhead and opened a conversation. Sattva got the first word in (*"You can speak?"*); Sovael pressed her for a body count and then, when she declined, tried to arrest her (*"You got a license for that? Gotta see your credential."*). She was not amused — *"No, you're trying to ease your own fear by making quips. I've seen it before"* — and opened with **18d6 of sonic screech** that parted the water and left a rut in the ground. The party got exactly two actions all fight: Clutch's Battle Cry Demoralize critically failed, and **Sattva's Recall Knowledge rolled a natural 20 and still failed a DC 45.** They ran on resource cost. Ferrok tipped his hat. Sovael promised a rematch when he's bigger. Sattva covered their tracks. Zero XP, and the DM's flat assessment: *"She's one of the nastier things you can find out here."*

The middle of the day was the best of it. A shrine to **Baba Nabi**, mountain god of scripture, storytellers and travelers, asked for ten minutes of reading aloud — so Ferrok read vampire poetry (*"They're so sad because they live forever and they're sad about it"*), Seva cried behind her mask, and Clutch talked the table through a kholo picture-book history. Then the tenth feather, on a branch, at the end of a trail of long running strides. Then three creature dens: a **Dread Roc** that carried Clutch off in its beak and Ferrok in its talons, an **enforcer camp** that cost them nothing and paid 500 platinum, and a **mammoth turtle** that Ferrok tried to befriend and that ate him for it.

And it ended in a farce. Two gargantuan **cave worms** swallowed, at various points, Sattva, Ferrok, and Clutch three separate times — Sattva got lost inside one across five failed escapes, Clutch cut himself out of one with a 68-damage critical and then stood back inside its reach and narrated being eaten again as a dive (*"what do you mean, that I can't miss?"*), and Ferrok missed with his horns from inside a stomach and observed ***"it's softer from the inside."*** Sattva finally surfaced with a blank expression, refused to speak, and drank a potion. Then he grew a cave worm's jaw with **Animal Feature**, bit the worm down to two hit points, and ate through it to find Clutch. Seva called the finish — *"This has been a fucking circus. I'm ending it here"* — and killed it. Sovael, surveying the wreckage: ***"Everyone's a little part of the club now. I guess we are in this together."***

Level 14. **800 platinum** still in the pot. Ten feathers, a day and a half to hold them, and an enforcer of higher rank than Gomwai somewhere out there whose entire job is taking feathers out of circulation. *"You may run into them. **May.**"*

## Key Events

### Act I — Rematch: Tino's Toughest (0:08 – 0:53) — TEAM WIN, feathers 8 → 9

- Ryu opened the day with the arithmetic: **8 of 10 feathers**, and the reminder that qualifying means *holding* them to the end of Day 3, not just touching the number. The team-encounter DC had risen to **19** (17 inside a team base).
- Ten minutes in, **Clutch stated the party's whole doctrine** and nobody argued: ***"I mean, I think this is great. If we just fight every team before the tournament and knock them out, there's nobody left to fight."*** Everything he does for the rest of the session follows from that sentence.
- The first Traverse produced a familiar silhouette. **Takatorra** called the wager. **Tino**, plainly: *"Just know we've gotten a bit tougher ourselves these past few days. This island puts you through it."* **Sovael**: *"Well, let's see then. Because we also got tougher ourselves."* **Clutch**, opening the bout: ***"It's time to see how much you've grown."***
- Seva, who had not been at the beach spar in S9, spent the pre-fight staring at the enemy formation: *"We fought them at level 11. Look at the formation! Why can't we do that?"*
- **Ji-yook rolled highest at 43 and immediately Delayed** to after Tino — Ryu, out loud: *"Running in there alone is not what she's built for."* Played order all four rounds: **Sovael → Sattva → Ferrok → Tino → Ji-yook → Clutch → Yabin → Takatorra → Seva.**
- Sovael's opener misfired: **Lingering Composition came up a natural 1**, and he chose to eat it rather than spend a hero point (*"for a round I'll just let it fail"*). **Divine Wrath** caught three — Tino failed for 21 and Sickened 1, Yabin succeeded for 10, Takatorra rolled a natural 20.
- **Tino answered with a three-action Qi Blast** — a 60-foot cone that caught the entire party for 39. **Yabin the Just** followed with **Arrow Salvo**, an 8d10 burst at DC 35 Reflex; Ferrok Shield Blocked, Sattva critically saved (*"I dodge all the arrows"*).
- **Clutch's Limit Break bit was born here.** He held out a paw, yelled ***"LIMIT BREAK"***, and — after a beat of table silence — added: ***"The screen shatters."***
- Seva clocked Yabin from across the field: ***"Is that man corn? Oh no, sorry. He's a Nagaji. He's a Nagaji with a mohawk."*** Ryu: *"I thought he was a goron."* The bit paid off seven minutes later when **Ferrok** dropped him and reported it as ***"The corn is down, I repeat."***
- **★ Sovael's round-2 gamble.** Courageous Anthem, Lingering on a Performance of 45, a free Talent Envy Demoralize on Takatorra — and then a **rank-6 Roaring Applause** placed to catch all four opponents inside 60 feet. **Takatorra failed** (no reactions, Slowed 1); **the other three succeeded** (no reactions). Ryu, before the dice: *"I warn you, this will make you a target."* Sovael: ***"It is what it is."*** *(Foundry ran the spell as **sustained**, not the printed ten minutes — a distinction that decided the fight's best sequence two rounds later.)*
- **Ferrok crossed the field with Time Jump and put 128 into Yabin in a single action-pair** — one critical Ignition Spellstrike, 76 weapon plus 52 fire. Yabin burned **Wooden Double** to claw 35 back. Ferrok's reason for walking at the archer was pure grievance: *"I am tired of becoming a pincushion."*
- **Sovael became the enemy's whole plan, exactly as promised.** Yabin spent Sure Strike into a **rank-6 Disintegrate** on him — and it should have been a critical hit, except that **Clutch's Bonekeeper's Bane aura shaved a point off the attack roll and downgraded it.** 57 rolled, **28 through a successful basic Fortitude save**. **Ji-yook rolled a natural 20 on Sattva for 92**, the fight's biggest single blow after Sovael's. Takatorra crossed the field and **critically hit Sovael for 60**, then came back a round later and **critically hit him for 62** — Dying 2, Unconscious, Blinded, Prone.
- **★ And that ended Roaring Applause.** Sovael was sustaining it; when he went down, it went with him. Thirty seconds later **Sattva ran into Takatorra's reach to revive Sovael with greater Healer's Gloves for 31** — and then asked why Takatorra had a Reactive Strike to make at all, since he hadn't provoked one. Sovael answered from the floor, and the frustration in it was not about the ruling: ***"It's because of Roaring Applause. Come on, guys. Come on. I casted it like three times already."*** Two full rounds of total reaction denial across four opponents, and the people it was protecting had not registered that it was there — or that it had just stopped, because he had. **Takatorra swung and missed on a natural 2.**
- **Yabin went down twice, but not to the same person.** Ferrok dropped him first. **Tino spent his last focus point on Lay on Hands** to bring him back at 22 HP — and **Clutch's Hasted bite dropped him again.** Ryu: *"Yabin is down again."*
- **Sattva was the fight's number-two damage dealer at 260**, almost all of it Thundering-rune criticals: a hero-pointed **85 on Takatorra** in round 2, then a **69-damage crit plus 32** at point-blank in round 4. After one of them: ***"That's what you get for hitting my friend."*** Told he was the back line, he agreed: ***"I am the back line."***
- Seva spent round 3 on triage rather than damage, reading the board out loud first — *"I think, honestly, our backline's a bit gone"* — then Doctor's Visitationed to Ferrok. She also earned a **Reactive Strike** by clocking Tino's action type (*"Oh, is this a move action? Yes! I react and strike"*), and when Takatorra put Sovael on the floor: ***"You fucking birds are dead."***
- **Clutch Intercepted 70 of Tino's 77-damage flurry** off Seva, and Taunted three opponents at a time all fight.
- **Tino's Toughest forfeited mid-round 4**, after Clutch's turn — Takatorra and Seva never got round-4 turns. Clutch's bite had already missed a beat earlier (*"Someday Clutch will bite"*), but the table spent a minute joking that he'd swung past the whistle anyway; Ryu asked the real question — *"Does Clutch actually do that? If they were surrendering, no"* — and **Ferrok** shut the bit down: *"Don't get us disqualified."*
- The handshake was the best NPC beat of the session. **Ji-yook** sighed: ***"You guys are weird. You realize that?"*** Seva: *"You mean we're winners."* Ji-yook: ***"But will you be eight hours from now when you're exhausted? We might have gotten you — but then we'd be out of steam for the next eight hours."*** Sovael conceded the point out loud: *"Perhaps. You're right. Resources are important in this tournament."*
- **Ferrok**: ***"We the Nameless, thank you for the match."*** **Clutch** demanded they hand back *the original feather* — *"the same markings that we handed over"* — and Ryu had to correct him: *"They never took a feather from you."* The beach spar in S9 had never been for stakes. Ryu, as Tino's team: *"I'm predicting we'll see you guys in the main tournament."* **Ferrok**: ***"Look forward to the final."***
- **160 XP. Feathers 8 → 9.**

### Act II — Tamikan (0:55 – 1:03) — FLED, no XP

- Reconnoitering a marshy hex produced a screech and a wingspan. Ryu: *"a flying pterodactyl-like creature… it seems to fly faster than anything you've seen in the past."* It hovered, and then it **spoke**: ***"Ah, more of these tournament fighters. Ample prey they've brought to us."***
- **Sattva got the first word in** — *"You can speak?"* — and she turned to him for it. **Clutch**, less reverently: *"I can't really hear you. Could you come closer?"* Seva: *"Could you fly low?"*
- Ryu: ***"And what dragon is incapable of speech, hmm? You didn't think everything on this island was some mindless brute, did you?"***
- **★ Sovael pressed her for intelligence.** *"How many people have you eaten? … Sounds like you have encountered other teams before."* She declined — ***"surely you all knew the risks. This is our island, and you all trespass"*** — and he went straight at the claim of ownership: ***"You got a license for that? Gotta see your credential."*** Seva, joining in: *"Got a license for this island?"* Sovael: *"I'm just making sure."* Tamikan, to him specifically: ***"Don't deign to get smart with me, runt. … No, you're trying to ease your own fear by making quips. I've seen it before."***
- **Ferrok tried the honest exit**: *"We have no quarrel with you. If this is your area, we can move on."* Tamikan: ***"You've given up your lives just by stepping into my domain, and now you'll pay dearly for it, like those before you."***
- **The party got exactly two actions in the entire encounter, and both were bad news.** Clutch's **Battle Cry Demoralize critically failed** against a Will DC of 38. **Sattva's free Recall Knowledge rolled a natural 20 and still failed a DC 45** — Ryu handed over the essentials as a courtesy: a **kongamato**, dragon trait, **fly 130**, immune to paralyzed and sleep, **Boat Breaker** (ignores half a shield's Hardness), and a breath weapon. *(Behind the screen: level 16, AC 39, 450 HP.)*
- She flew 20 feet up and opened with **Sonic Screech — 18d6 in a 100-foot line, DC 37 basic Reflex.** Sattva and Ferrok both rolled to exactly 36 and failed by one, taking 63; Sovael made it at 39 and halved it. She then flew a further **75 feet on her Quickened action** — 130 feet of movement and a 63-point line, in one turn, before anyone else acted. Ryu: *"She lets out this screech of sonic energy that parts the water and just leaves this rut in the ground."*
- **The party voted to run, on resources, before round 2.** **Sovael** had called it before initiative was even finished: ***"I think we might want to run, guys. I'm not gonna lie."*** Clutch named the real problem — it flies, and the party has nothing that answers a flier. Ryu agreed: *"you might win, but you might totally drain yourselves in the process."*
- **The second group escape in campaign history** — though unlike the Jaiban escape in S11, this one was **narrated rather than run as a declared round**: Ryu simply waved them out. **Ferrok**: *"We'll tip his hat and then leave."* **Sovael**, over his shoulder: ***"I'll see you again, big dragon. Maybe one day I'll grow bigger and then I'll come by for revenge."*** Seva: *"Aww. Little dragon."* **Sattva**: *"And I cover her tracks."*
- Ryu, after: ***"She's one of the nastier things you can find out here."*** **Zero XP** — his stated principle: *"I don't ascribe to the philosophy of, oh well, you didn't kill it so you don't get XP — but in that case, literally the first thing you guys did was run away."*

### Act III — The Hex Crawl (1:04 – 1:11) — a shrine and the tenth feather

- Four hexes swept along the southern shore: another **docks**, a **Shrine of Gozreh** team base (unoccupied), and a **gold priest's estate** — which prompted **Ferrok**: ***"Is it against the rules to put graffiti on their base walls or anything?"*** *(He got his chance forty-eight minutes later, typing into Foundry chat mid-session: **"Ferrok carves 'Ferrok was here' into the wall."**)*
- **★ The shrine of Baba Nabi** — a Temoutan shrine to the **mountain god of scripture, storytellers, and travelers**. Honoring her costs ten minutes of reading aloud. Seva set it up as an in-fiction prompt: ***"Oh, Ferrok, tell us about your previous journey before coming here."***
  - **Ferrok** read a book of **vampire poetry**. *"They're so sad because they live forever and they're sad about it."*
  - **Seva**: ***"That's so sad. Seva cried. Seva started crying behind her mask."***
  - **Clutch** read *"a picture book of the kholo's history"* — and talked the table through it.
  - Reward: **Baba Nabi's blessing — the Gift of Sight.** A **+2 circumstance bonus to saves against blinded and dazzled** for the rest of their stay on Bonmou. Sovael, checking the type: *"You said circumstance bonus? Nice. Huge."*
- **★ The tenth feather.** The next hex was flagged as treasure, and Ryu apologised in advance — *"this is kind of anticlimactic."* It wasn't. ***"You will find a bunch of footsteps, long striding footsteps, as though someone were running for their lives… snagged on a branch here is a single silver feather."***
  - **Seva**: ***"By the gods, we did it."*** **Clutch**: ***"All right, let's go retire."*** **Sovael**, already on the next problem: *"Now we gotta dodge the teams."* **Sattva**: *"Okay, so we're gonna be sneaking through the rest of the map."*
  - **Feathers 9 → 10.** They had reached the qualifying number **on the morning of Day 2**, with a day and a half left to hold it.
- Then Ryu finished the sentence: ***"But you soon find out what precisely they were running from, because this is also a creature den."*** **Clutch**: ***"Grab the feather and dip!"*** — and, when the bird landed, the sensible follow-up nobody else asked: *"Are we just running? Do we already have the feather?"*

### Act IV — The Dread Roc (1:11 – 1:38) — DEN, 600 pp

- *"You hear a screech and watch as an absolutely enormous bird descends upon all of you."* The party seriously considered leaving — they already had the feather — until Ryu offered the calibration: ***"not everything is threat level Tamikan. It's just a giant chicken."*** **Sovael**: *"We can try one round. And then we'll see."*
- **Sattva**, sizing it up: ***"It's a bird. How magic can it be?"*** He was wrong within thirty seconds.
- **★ A level-1 general feat bought the party the alpha strike.** Ryu called the 38–38 initiative tie for the enemy; Sovael remembered his **Pilgrim's Token** and went first. He spent the turn on Courageous Anthem, Lingering (Performance 38, three rounds), and a **Recall Knowledge** Ryu answered generously despite a low roll: **no resistances, no weaknesses, best save Fortitude.** Clutch had already stuck **Frightened 1** on it with his Battle Cry.
- Then the roc opened with **Frozen Feathers**, a 60-foot cone on a **non-basic save** that only Sattva failed — and **Clutch immediately Intercepted the worse damage roll off him and ate 49 instead.** *"It's energy and physical, so I'll intercept."* **Sattva took zero damage in the entire fight.**
- **The bird's whole gimmick was leverage.** A 30-foot wing reach, a **Wing Rebuff** reaction that punished anyone closing (a 66-point crit on Seva in round 1, a 50-point crit on Ferrok in round 3), **Improved Grab**, and **Snatch** — the ability to fly at half speed while carrying grabbed creatures. In round 2 it grabbed **Clutch in its beak and Ferrok in its talons and flew 30 feet backwards** with both. Seva: ***"Oh god, we are so fucked."***
- **★ Seva walked into the roc's reaction range twice on purpose.** In round 2, with both frontliners airborne and unreachable, she stepped in to stabilise herself under a Wing Rebuff. In round 3 she came back for Clutch and **hero-pointed a natural 2 into a natural 19 for a critical Battle Medicine — 74 hit points, from the floor of a fight she was losing.**
- **Ferrok broke a grab by exactly zero and then ordered dinner.** Escape check: 41 against a DC of 41. Then, weighing whether the action was worth it — *"Do I really want to Spellstrike that?"* — he answered himself: ***"You know what? I want fried chicken, so Ignition Spellstrike."*** It landed for 60, and he finished as the roc's top damage dealer at **128**.
- **Clutch is the reason the fight was winnable.** His Shielding Taunt held the roc **off-guard at AC 34 from round 2 onward**; the roll log flags that as *essential* on three of the fight's four decisive rolls, including his own **58-point Armored Counterattack crit — a reaction, in a round he never got a turn in.** He absorbed 172 damage, **152 of it redirected off other people.**
- Sovael spent most of it **out of position**, which he diagnosed live and correctly: with **Living Nexus** taken at 11 he'd dropped Fleet and his Arcane casting, which meant no Wand of Tailwind and a 25-foot fly speed. *"I need to take weapon proficiency. Yeah, that's biting me in the ass right now."* His Needle Darts **missed by exactly 1** even with Whispers of Weakness's +2 behind it; a Demoralize was rejected by the game as "too far"; two full rounds of Stride-Stride ended with him still 40 feet short. He shielded and held his slots.
- **Sattva killed it.** Round 4, a Hunted Shot that **critted for 92 into 29 remaining hit points**: ***"You put an arrow right in front of its eyes as the bird lets out a screech and drops."*** **Ferrok**: *"I call a drumstick."*
- The party had spent almost nothing to do it. **Ferrok**: ***"All we have to do is get really beaten and we don't have to use any spells."***
- **120 XP. Sixth creature den — 600 platinum.**

### Act V — The Enforcer Camp (1:54 – 2:35) — WIN, 500 pp, three feathers defended

- Table banter first, and it landed a real piece of worldbuilding: Ryu had to check his notes because *"there's y'all and then there's the Saturday Ruby Phoenix group."* **Ferrok**: *"We're better than them."* Ryu: ***"They've killed Jaiban."*** **Seva**, refusing to accept it: *"You are lying. There's no way they killed Jaiban."* The party's own timeline for Jaiban: level 15, Day 3, maybe.
- **Clutch's hex-crawl procedure, executing the doctrine he'd stated at minute six:** ***"Clutch will yell out — who here is not a coward?"*** Seva: *"Don't, Clutch. Don't."* **Ferrok**: *"We're doing the vacuuming method."* **Sattva**: ***"The Roomba method."***
- The hex held an old manor, and it was occupied — not by a team, but by *"several people dressed similarly to your guide, Ingdani."* A middle-aged orc stepped out. ***"Well met. As I understand, you're a team that's yet to find a name. I am Gomwai, head enforcer of Bonmou."***
- **Ferrok**: ***"We have a name. It's the Nameless."*** *(Seva coined it by accident in S12; this was the first time anyone said it to an official.)* Gomwai, grudgingly: ***"I cannot say it's what I would have chosen, but well met, Nameless."***
- **The terms were brutal for a team sitting on exactly ten feathers.** *"In coming here, you have volunteered yourselves for a challenge. **You will wager three feathers against us.** If you fail, we will confiscate them… This is not a challenge you can refuse without forfeiting feathers."* **Ferrok**: *"Well, we're all in."* **Sovael**: ***"Time to protect our feathers."***
- Sovael tried to pre-buff with Heroism and was refused: *"It's similar to other tournament matches. You still can't buff beforehand. It all has to happen in the moment."*
- **Ferrok's Gladiator archetype surfaced in fiction for the only time this session**: *"Can I still consider this one with spectators for my gladiator stuff?"* — and then, to a table that immediately went for it, ***"What? I'm known for good times."*** **Seva**: ***"You're known for* a *good time?"***
- **★ And then Ingdani planted the season's next hook**, unprompted: ***"Gomwai is the head enforcer for this base, though there is technically one of higher rank than he. One who is also responsible in weeding out the weak and taking feathers out of circulation. You may run into them. May."***
- Four enforcers, all built on real class chassis: a **Warrior** (katana, dueling expertise), a **Mage** (Vampiric Exsanguination), a **Rogue** (wakizashi, Tumbling Attack), and a **Monk** — a dedicated **trip build** that made the next forty minutes miserable for anyone with a poor Reflex.
- **Clutch got the first debuff of the fight in before initiative finished** — a Battle Cry Demoralize that stuck Frightened 1 on the Mage — and **Sattva picked his prey off it**: *"since the mage is frightened, hunt the mage."* Two shots, 104 damage, before the enforcers had moved. The Warrior answered with a clean 49 crit on Sattva for 62.
- **Sovael opened with Dirge of Doom rather than Courageous Anthem** — no save, Frightened 1 on everything in the emanation, and a floor the enemies could not recover past. He lingered it on a **critical Performance (45)** for four rounds, and spent the free Talent Envy Demoralize on the Warrior for a crit and **Frightened 2**. That −1 held the whole fight and **downgraded exactly three enemy criticals to normal hits** — Ryu called two of them out from behind the screen.
- **Ferrok and Clutch focused the Mage down**: 70 from an Ignition Spellstrike, then Clutch Snagging Struck it off-guard and **Combat Grabbed it — grabbed and immobilised**, unable to reposition. **Sattva's Hunted Shot finished it** at the top of round 2. *"Mage is down."* Seva: *"Off the board."*
- **★ Ferrok's best mechanical play of the session, and it turned on a rules argument.** When the Warrior crit him in round 3, he used **Emergency Targe** to retroactively raise his AC to 36 — turning the crit into a normal hit — then spent Bastion's *extra* reaction to **Shield Block 20** of what got through. Twelve damage from what should have been a critical. He and Ryu negotiated the two-reactions-on-one-activity question live, and Ryu allowed it.
- **The Monk's trip build was the story.** Seva went down first — *"Fuck me without Kip Up"* — and stayed down most of the fight, narrating every second: ***"Don't trip me. Don't trip me. They're gonna trip ya. I'm never getting up!"*** and later, weighing whether to stand: ***"I could just sit here, but I'll stand up. It's kind of nice down here."*** It also dropped a **91-damage combined Punch Down on a prone Sattva**, the largest single hit of the encounter. Ryu, from behind the screen, with feeling: ***"Look, just share my pain for a brief moment, because when players have trip builds, this is the reality."***
- **★ Sattva answered a trip build by announcing one he had no intention of making.** ***"You know what would be really funny? If I tripped you."*** — and then: ***"I fake a trip and shoot the Monk in the face."*** Natural 20 for 61, then 23. Seva: ***"What a feint!"***
- **Clutch tanked the entire camp.** **Six Intercept Attacks**, an **Armored Counterattack on all but the first** (including a natural-20 crit for 54), Shielding Taunt on three enemies at a time — **264 damage absorbed, 245 dealt**, and he killed the **Rogue** in round 3 with a 64-damage crit. He also failed his own Battle Medicine and deliberately declined to fix it: *"one hero point left in an hour and a half — I guess we'll keep it."* When Ryu warned him *"Clutch is about to take a lot of damage,"* the answer was ***"What makes you say that?"***
- **Sovael was never targeted once** — 24 damage all fight, nearly all incidental. He spent it entirely on Dirge uptime and **97 points of healing into Clutch** off two Scrolls of Heal, then ran **Whispers of Weakness** on the Monk and got the least useful possible answer: *"No weakness, and all their saves are equal."* He respected it out loud — ***"Nice build. Very nice build."*** — and hero-pointed a natural 1 into a **44-damage Fire Ray** anyway.
- **Ferrok forgot to refocus and spent a round as a normal person.** *"Fun fact, I forgot to refocus last round, so I'm out of focus points. Yeah, so I'm stepping forward and swinging my sword like a normal melee guy."* He burned his only hero point on that turn and **still missed**. First blood on the untouched Warrior came a round later — 30 damage, leaving it at 240/270 when the fight was called.
- **★ And then Seva killed the Monk from the floor.** A **Reactive Strike** she earned by watching the Monk stride past had put her **Deathseeker's wounding rune** on it in round 3; her Ignition Spellstrike in round 4 refreshed the bleed and left it on exactly 6 hit points. The bleed rolled a **6**. Ryu: *"they had exactly six hit points, so because it was max bleed…"* With one enforcer standing, **Gomwai called it**: ***"That is enough. ### Honest ledger

- **He was out of the roc fight entirely.** Zero damage, four rounds, two of them spent Striding. That is a build problem, not a dice problem — see below. His Anthem still shows up in the log as *essential* on Seva's round-4 hit, which is the honest counterweight.

### The build problem, stated plainly

**Living Nexus cost him movement, and the roc fight is the bill.** Taking the archetype at 11 meant dropping Fleet *and* his Arcane casting, which meant no Wand of Tailwind, which meant a **25-foot fly speed against a creature with 30 feet of reach that repositioned 30–60 feet a round**. He spent two full rounds Striding and was still 40 feet short. He diagnosed it correctly and live — *"that's biting me in the ass right now"* — and **level 14 restored the Arcane casting**, and with it Tailwind and 35 feet.

That is the right pick for a boss fight and the wrong pick for Tamikan. **He was standing in a stone market an hour later stocking up on Scrolls of Heal, and did not buy this one.** He has 360 platinum and a den's worth still in the pot.

- **Tamikan spoke in the plural.** *"Ample prey they've brought to **us**"* — and *"this is **our** island."* She was not speaking only for herself, and nobody at the table picked it up.
- **Ingdani volunteered the higher enforcer without being asked**, in the same conversational slot where she volunteered the coin warning in S12. When she offers information nobody requested, it is a telegraph, not colour.
- **Ten feathers means the party's incentives have inverted.** Everything past this point is downside. The Jade Phoenix Idol is worth more than any item in the stash, and the correct play for the rest of Day 2 and Day 3 is to avoid rival teams entirely — which is exactly what Sattva said out loud (*"we're gonna be sneaking through the rest of the map"*) and the exact opposite of Clutch's stated doctrine, which the party has never actually argued about.

### What Sovael is thinking about

- **Buy the Falling Sky scroll.** The party has met two fliers it could not answer and has explicitly identified the fix. He has the money, he was at a market, and he did not.
- **Roaring Applause is proven and should be the default opener against any team of 3+** — ten targets at rank 6, not an incapacitation spell, strips reactions *even on a success*. **But this table runs it sustained**, which changes the calculus: it is worth most in a fight where he is *not* also the focus target, and it is worth confirming the duration with Cody before relying on it again.
- **Positioning against reach creatures is the recurring failure.** The roc has 30 feet of reach and he could not get inside it; the worms have 15 and he ended a turn inside a burning square he had created himself. Same problem, different clothes.
- **The Hall of Swallow is now the whole party.** He and Seva were charter members from S12; Ferrok joined at the turtle, then Sattva and Clutch at the worms. Worth noticing that **every swallowing this session happened to someone standing in melee** — and that the same distance problem that cost him the roc fight is the reason he wasn't there for any of them.
