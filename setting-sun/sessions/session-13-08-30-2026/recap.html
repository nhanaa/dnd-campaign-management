Session Summary

The Nameless hit ten feathers. Not on Day 3, not on the mountain — in the middle of Day 2, in a nothing hex on the southern shore, off a feather somebody else had dropped while running for their life. Seva said "By the gods, we did it." Clutch said "All right, let's go retire." Then the same hex turned out to be a creature den, and the session went on for another two and a half hours.

The number is the headline; holding it is now the whole game. Ten feathers means every remaining encounter is downside-only — and the island noticed. Forty minutes after they hit the mark, the enforcers stepped out of a manor house and wagered three of them on a fight the party was not allowed to refuse.

It opened with a rematch. Tino's Toughest — the team that fought them to a draw on the beach at level 11, in the session that cost the party its monk — came back for an official bout, and this time it was not close. Four rounds, one feather, a handshake. It cost Sovael more than anyone: he opened round two with a rank-6 Roaring Applause across all four opponents, was told to his face by the DM "I warn you, this will make you a target," answered "It is what it is," and then spent the fight being the target — a Disintegrate, two Takatorra criticals, and finally the floor at Dying 2. He took 237 damage — nearly twice what anyone else in that fight took — and for two rounds he was right: with the enemy team's reactions switched off, Ferrok crossed to the archer untouched and Clutch moved freely. The irony is exact — the table ran the spell as sustained, and it died the moment he did. Sattva ran into Takatorra's reach to revive him thirty seconds later, was puzzled that Takatorra had a Reactive Strike at all, and Sovael had to explain from the floor what had been keeping it switched off for two rounds — pointing out, with some heat, that this was not the first time he'd cast it in this campaign. The reactions were back because he was down. Takatorra swung and missed on a natural 2. Afterward: "I think I did quite a bit. I provided values." Ferrok, immediately: "You were essential to the battle."

Then the island reminded them it does not care about their record. Tamikan — a kongamato, level 16, and the first wild thing on Bonmou that talks — hovered overhead and opened a conversation. Sattva got the first word in ("You can speak?"); Sovael pressed her for a body count and then, when she declined, tried to arrest her ("You got a license for that? Gotta see your credential."). She was not amused — "No, you're trying to ease your own fear by making quips. I've seen it before" — and opened with 18d6 of sonic screech that parted the water and left a rut in the ground. The party got exactly two actions all fight: Clutch's Battle Cry Demoralize critically failed, and Sattva's Recall Knowledge rolled a natural 20 and still failed a DC 45. They ran on resource cost. Ferrok tipped his hat. Sovael promised a rematch when he's bigger. Sattva covered their tracks. Zero XP, and the DM's flat assessment: "She's one of the nastier things you can find out here."

The middle of the day was the best of it. A shrine to Baba Nabi, mountain god of scripture, storytellers and travelers, asked for ten minutes of reading aloud — so Ferrok read vampire poetry ("They're so sad because they live forever and they're sad about it"), Seva cried behind her mask, and Clutch talked the table through a kholo picture-book history. Then the tenth feather, on a branch, at the end of a trail of long running strides. Then three creature dens: a Dread Roc that carried Clutch off in its beak and Ferrok in its talons, an enforcer camp that cost them nothing and paid 500 platinum, and a mammoth turtle that Ferrok tried to befriend and that ate him for it.

And it ended in a farce. Two gargantuan cave worms swallowed, at various points, Sattva, Ferrok, and Clutch three separate times — Sattva got lost inside one across five failed escapes, Clutch cut himself out of one with a 68-damage critical and then stood back inside its reach and narrated being eaten again as a dive ("what do you mean, that I can't miss?"), and Ferrok critically missed goring his way out of a stomach and later reviewed the accommodation: "it's softer from the inside." Sattva finally surfaced with a blank expression, refused to speak, and drank a potion. Then he grew a cave worm's jaw with Animal Feature, bit the worm down to two hit points, and ate through it to find Clutch. Seva called the finish — "This has been a fucking circus. I'm ending it here" — and killed it. Sovael, surveying the wreckage: "Everyone's a little part of the club now. I guess we are in this together."

Level 14. 800 platinum still in the pot. Ten feathers, a day and a half to hold them, and an enforcer of higher rank than Gomwai somewhere out there whose entire job is taking feathers out of circulation. "You may run into them. May."
