The floor the party had bloodied last session turned out to belong to something far worse than vampires. Pressing past an electrified gate, they walked into a halberd-line of Living Guards — bite-marked human thralls in ancient Azlanti armor — and the red-clad vampire Freileth, who drifted in through the gate as mist and very nearly drained Raylta to death with a void beam. It was a grinding, reach-choked brawl in a tiny room, but the guards were weak to fire (Bael's whole thesis) and Freileth folded to silver: Tivael's silver Needle Dart put her down. Battered, over-encumbered, and absurdly rich, the party took a full day-trip back to Talmandor's Bounty to offload loot and shop ("we're suffering from successes"), then climbed back up.

What waited above was a Zon-Kuthon torture-house. The hacked-up corpses of the vampires they'd killed last session now hung from the ceiling on hooks — claimed by a pack of Evangelists, chain-and-hook velstracs who were genuinely offended the party had "stolen their kill" ("I hear you called dibs" / "No dibs was called!"). The velstracs grappled half the party with impaling chains — Bargrum crit-grabbed first, Raylta hooked through both palms mid-cast, Tivael through the wings — but silver and fire ground them down. Only a retroactive Recall Knowledge named them: velstracs, fanatics of the god of pain. "Basically the aliens from Hellraiser."

Then the session's heart: behind a reinforced door, a brave little poppet — a toy guard named Anvaca, animated by the spirits of long-dead N'Gokal guardsmen and built in the likeness of the moon-goddess Asavna — squared up alone against the whole party in defense of a kingdom a thousand years dead. Raylta refused to kill it; the party closed the doors on its bolt-emitter, beat it unconscious with the flats of their weapons, left it a note ("sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys"), and locked it safely back in its room. A hallway of mirror-strapped torture victims followed (freed and staged for evacuation), and then the floor's master: Jubilant, a Conductor velstrac — a maestro with a conductor's baton, a throne of flesh, and a hunger to find a dragon's pain receptors. She and her two Ostiarius and a Sacristan nearly screamed the party apart, but Tivael's control engine (Evangelize, a spreading Infectious Ennui, Fortissimo Anthems) locked the choir down while Ferrok focus-fired the conductor. Bael's blast burned Jubilant to a crisp; Ferrok's blade swept the rest. The party hit level 10 — one session from the top of the tower.

Timeline

Act I · Explore · The Electrified Gate

Shutting Off the Fence

  • After refocusing and healing off the previous fight (Bael: "Getting better at exploding myself"), the party found a bare room with three exits — two doors barricaded with furniture, the north one behind a very clearly electrified gate (purely mechanical, no magic to dispel).
  • Bael volunteered (shock-resistant), borrowed a charm of resistance from Bargrum as a hedge, and disabled the power source with a thievery check — a nat-20 critical success after a hero point.
  • Beyond lay an alchemy workroom, and through the doorways, the rush of multiple humanoids — living, breathing humans in ancient Azlanti arms and armor. Bargrum tried to head it off ("Can't we talk this out?"); they only shook their heads and attacked.
Act II · Combat · The Living Guards & Freileth

Seven Halberds and a Vampire in Red

  • Seven Living Guards (halberds, 10-ft reach, reactive strikes) swarmed the cramped room. Raylta's Recall Knowledge read them as martially-trained humans with bite marks on their necks — vampire thralls, not undead — and weak to fire.
  • For most of three rounds the reach-and-reactive-strike wall meant nobody landed a hit. Bargrum anchored it (Group/Shielding Taunts, Intercepts, trips); Bael's fire and Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes chewed guards down one a round.
  • Tivael worked control — Phantom Pain, a rank-3 Fear frightening most of the pack (sparing Raylta), Courageous Anthem, Electric Arc — and summoned a unicorn (Summon Fey) that ate a reactive strike and then healed Raylta.
  • At the top of a round, mist poured through the gate and became Freileth — a vampire in a well-tailored red outfit, hair in old Azlanti fashion, glowing green eyes — who snarled "You wretches!"
  • Freileth's Enervation (void line) drained Bael, then a rank-4 line caught Raylta for 48 persistent void + Drained (doomed-adjacent — "I would die"). She also missed a Chromatic Ray and Shielded.
  • The party answered with a combo: Ferrok placed Incendiary Fog and Bael's incendiary aura instantly ignited it into "a gigantic flaming sphere"; Raylta's Divine Wrath left Freileth Sickened 2 / Slowed; Moonbeam and a 46-point Lay on Hands held the line.
  • Near death, Tivael failed an Evangelize on Freileth, then finished her with a silver Needle Dart — "that was actually a lot of damage."
Act III · Social · Suffering from Successes

The Manacle Monopoly

  • Badly drained and over-bulk, the party took the full day-trip back to Talmandor's Bounty to sell and shop. Bargrum ran the auction-house bit — quartermaster, appraiser, and "the number one manacle dealer in the region — real monopoly," closing each lot with "going once, going twice, sold."
  • They were drowning in money. Raylta: "Scrooge McDuck swims in his coinage — there's just too much." Bael upgraded to greater healer's gloves ("Damn, we're so rich"); Ferrok put a +2 potency rune and a flaming rune on his bastard sword; Tivael bought a new instrument and scrolls.
  • Hard cap: level-10 items are still out of reach ("nine is your cap"). Then the long climb back up the Ebon Tower to where they'd left off.
Act IV · Combat · Dibs

The Evangelists

  • Back in the southern ballroom, the hacked-up corpses of the vampires the party had killed now hung from the ceiling on spiked hooks. Among them stood chain-impaled figures, grinning: "Are you the ones that finally beat those wretches?"
  • The mood curdled when the party answered wrong: "What matters is that they were ours, not yours… I hear you called dibs." — Raylta: "Right? You didn't call dibs!" / Bael: "Yeah, it's very important. No dibs was called."
  • Bael opened with Solar Detonation, dazzling two of the five Evangelists (A, C) and blinding a third (D). The velstracs closed with Impaling Chain (a crit auto-grabs and impales for persistent bleed): Bargrum was crit-grabbed first, then Raylta got hooked through both palms mid-cast ("No! You motherfucker… you're on my shit list") and Tivael through the wings.
  • Bargrum held the grapple-line, his crit-taunt landing the session's hardest line ("Yeah, we killed him. So what? What are you gonna do about it?"). Bael cracked back at a hook-threat ("Are you a dentist?"); Tivael carved with Flurry of Claws, Electric Arc, and a Telekinetic Projectile crit.
  • Ferrok was the kill engine — his stretching reach killed two at once and freed a grabbed Raylta, with Force Fang and Ignition finishes, and a Demoralize on a frightened survivor ("You're next!").
  • The party identified them only afterward, by retroactive Recall Knowledge: velstracs, fanatics of Zon-Kuthon, god of pain. Ferrok: "So they're basically the aliens from Hellraiser."
Act V · RP / Combat · The Brave Little Guard

Anvaca, and the Mercy of the Murdery

  • The party examined a defaced statue of Asavna (Azlanti goddess of companionship, defense, and the moon — destroyed at Starfall), decapitated atop a pile of viscera, and cleared a coffin room and a coat room, picking up a Fireball scroll, Shape Stone, and a Silver Slough (Ferrok, to silver his blade).
  • Bargrum face-checked a reinforced door and a little toy guard stabbed at him — Anvaca, a poppet animated by the spirits of old N'Gokal guardsmen and built in the likeness of Asavna: "Back! Back, fiends! In defense of N'Gokal, I shall slay thee!" It worked a remote Bolt Emitter (32 electricity) and twice unleashed Dead, But Not Forgotten (a spirit-guardian AoE).
  • Recall Knowledge confirmed it: a construct, but a humanoid one, not mindless. Bael's RK on the bolt-emitter reframed the whole fight — take the controller, and the machine dies. The party closed the door on its firing line and decided to take it alive.
  • Raylta refused to kill it ("It's just a little guard"), tried her Security Badge (it shrugged off the command) and outright pleading ("we don't want to hurt you, just drop the remote!"). The party switched to non-lethal — Bael dunked his fist (with Raylta's Guidance) to soften it, and Ferrok's flat-of-the-sword blow knocked it unconscious, not dead.
  • They took the remote, left it a note ("sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys"), dragged it back into its room, and locked it in. Ferrok: "I don't know. We're pretty murdery. One good deed of the day." (The spared list: the poppet and Speckle.)
Act VI · Explore · The Mirror Hall

What the Velstracs Left

  • A long hall of countless mirrors (odd, given vampires cast no reflection) gave way to a rotten stench and a string of torture cells: men with their eyelids cut off, heads strapped, forced to stare at their own reflections, hooks shoved beneath their flesh, fingers and nails removed. "The velstracs have been to work on this man."
  • The victims were in deep shock (no quick recovery). It was Bargrum's plan to unstrap each prisoner and stage them by the stairs with blankets and food for evacuation — and his idea to improvise saline/eye-drops for the lidless eyes.
  • Grim, methodical care between fights — the cost of clearing a torture-house one room at a time.
Act VII · Combat · The Conductor's Kingdom

Jubilant, and the Choir of Screams (finale)

  • The north door opened on a grisly shrine: bone pillars, a statue of organs and limbs, and four velstracs — two Ostiarius with bleeding palms, a blindfolded Sacristan, and on a throne of flesh, a sewn-together woman with pitch-black eyes and a conductor's baton: Jubilant, a Conductor velstrac.
  • Jubilant mused about a new canvas: "I've never worked on a dragon before. I wonder where the pain receptors in your wings are found." Tivael answered cold: "Too bad you won't ever find out in this life… maybe you'll be condemned so deep in hell that you won't ever be reincarnated." Jubilant: "Oh, sweet summer child. I come from a far worse place."
  • Raylta led on Oracular Warning (+2 initiative party-wide, at the cost of self-Cursebound — "Yes, Bargrum's cursed!"). Bargrum opened with a Defensive Advance and called the room's tech: the velstracs are weak to silver, and silver stops their fast healing.
  • Jubilant was the engine — huge, so her Scream for Me and Unholy Exaltation reached the whole clustered party every round; the Sacristan added Chilling Darkness and Fear; the Ostiarius chained the front line and buffed the choir with Compel Courage.
  • Tivael ran control all fight: Evangelize stupefied the Sacristan, a sustained Infectious Ennui spread Slowed + Frightened velstrac to velstrac round by round, and Fortissimo +2 Anthems propped the party up. Raylta's Divine Wrath caught both Ostiarius.
  • Ferrok focus-fired the conductor: self-Haste + Arcane Cascade, an Echo Jump onto her, Sure Strike + Ignition Spellstrike runs dropping her to 2 HP; Bael's one-action blast finished Jubilant ("she burns to a crisp").
  • The party swept the choir — Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes (a 50 on an Ostiarius) and the final blade on the last velstrac (Raylta: "That'll do it. Bye, you freak."). The DM closed the night with level 10.

Combat Encounters

Encounter 1 · Seven Living Guards + Freileth · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Living Guard C → Freileth (joins later, via mist) → Living Guard E → LG F → Raylta → Ferrok → LG B → Bargrum → Bael → Tivael → LG D → LG A → LG G. Living Guards ×7 — halberd thralls in Azlanti armor, 10-ft reach + reactive strike, bite-marked (vampire servants, not undead), weak to fire, Demoralize. Freileth — vampire arcane caster (Enervation, Chromatic Ray, Shield; resist non-silver physical, Fast Healing), red Azlanti outfit, green eyes.

The fight

  • A cramped room of reach attackers made the opening rounds a whiff-fest in the party's favor — Bargrum Taunted the line onto himself while Bael's fire (their weakness) and Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes picked guards off one a round.
  • Tivael controlled: Phantom Pain, a rank-3 Fear (sparing Raylta), Courageous Anthem, Electric Arc, and a Summon Fey unicorn that healed Raylta after eating a reactive strike.
  • Freileth misted in and hit hard with Enervation — Bael drained on the first line, then a rank-4 line caught Raylta for 48 persistent void + Drained (near-death). She also missed a Chromatic Ray on Raylta and Shielded.
  • The combo answered: Ferrok placed Incendiary Fog, Bael's aura ignited it into a flaming sphere on Freileth + a guard; Raylta's Divine Wrath left Freileth Sickened 2 / Slowed; Moonbeam and Lay on Hands stabilized the line.
  • Tivael failed an Evangelize on Freileth, then killed her with a silver Needle Dart. The guard kills split between Ferrok (Ignition Spellstrikes — including a 56-damage crit that "took a guard's head off") and Bael (Flying Flame finishes + blasts); Tivael's Enc-1 work was pure control.

Outcome

The party cleared the room but was badly drained — Raylta doomed-adjacent, the whole party over-bulk and low — and decided to rest and resupply in town. Loot: 7× +1 Striking Halberd; from Freileth, a +1 Striking Wounding Silver Corset Knife, a Wand of Shardstorm, and a Wand of Paralytic Shock.

Encounter 2 · Five Evangelists (velstracs) · 5 PCs

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Tivael → Bael → Evangelist B → Evangelist D → Ferrok → Evangelist C → Evangelist A → Bargrum → Evangelist E → Raylta. Evangelists ×5 — chain-and-hook velstracs; Impaling Chain (a crit auto-grabs/impales for persistent bleed and immobilizes), Demoralize, Fast Healing (only stopped by silver). Best save Reflex.

The fight

  • Bael opened with Solar Detonation — A and C Dazzled, D Blinded (B and E were just outside the burst) — producing a string of flat-check misses all fight.
  • The velstracs chained the party: Bargrum took the first crit-grab, then Raylta (hooked through both palms, interrupting her Needle Darts → persistent bleed) and Tivael (through the wings) were impaled; Ferrok was chained later. Casting while grabbed forced flat checks; Raylta lost a turn to it.
  • Bargrum held the grapple line (Shielding Taunts, the "we killed him, so what?" crit-taunt). Tivael carved with Flurry of Claws (a hero-pointed second-prong crit), Electric Arc, and a Telekinetic Projectile crit, then chipped a survivor with a silver Needle Dart + Bon Mot ("Oh, you stupid bitch").
  • Bael ran Flying Flame birdies + blasts. Ferrok was the kill engine — stretching reach to kill two Evangelists at once and free a grabbed Raylta, plus Force Fang / Ignition finishes — and Demoralized a frightened survivor ("You're next!").
  • Silver wasn't found as the Fast-Healing lever until round 4 (Bargrum's fist); the last Evangelist survived to the break and was finished after.

Outcome

The party only identified the foe by a retroactive Recall Knowledge: velstracs, fanatics of Zon-Kuthon. Loot: none (velstracs carry no gear).

Encounter 3 · Anvaca, the Poppet Guard · 5 PCs · spared

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Anvaca → Bael → Tivael → Raylta → Ferrok → Bargrum. Anvaca — a poppet (construct, humanoid, not mindless) animated by the spirits of old N'Gokal guardsmen, built in the likeness of the goddess Asavna. Weakness silver. Wielded a remote-controlled Bolt Emitter (32 electricity/strike, ignores MAP) and Dead, But Not Forgotten (a recharging spirit-guardian AoE) twice.

The fight

  • The poppet fought alone in defense of a dead kingdom ("I am the shield that never breaks"). The party closed the doors between it and the emitter's firing slits to neutralize the AoE, then chose to take it alive.
  • Raylta tried a Security Badge command (it resisted — incapacitation) and pleaded with it. The party switched to non-lethal (−2) attacks: Bael's fist-dunk (with Raylta's Guidance) softened it, and Ferrok's flat-of-the-sword blow knocked it unconscious.
  • They took the remote, left a note, dragged the poppet back to its room, and locked it in alive.

Outcome

No PC was seriously threatened. The poppet was spared, not killed — one of only two things (with Speckle) the party has chosen to leave alive. No loot beyond the bolt-emitter remote.

Encounter 4 · Jubilant (Conductor) + Sacristan + Ostiarius A/B · 5 PCs · finale

Setup

Initiative (Pax's tracker): Bargrum → Jubilant → Sacristan → Tivael → Ferrok → Ostiarius B → Raylta → Bael → Ostiarius A. Jubilant — a Conductor velstrac; auditory powerhouse (Scream for Me single + secondary-AoE scream once/round, Unholy Exaltation sonic cone, conductor's-baton Strike, Sonic Redirection reaction); best save Reflex, worst Fort/Will, unholy, weak to silver + holy, Fast Healing — and not a spellcaster (her screams are actions). Sacristan — blindfolded velstrac caster (Chilling Darkness, rank-3 Fear). Ostiarius ×2 — bleeding-palm velstracs (Compel Courage buff, barbed-chain Strikes + bleed). All silver-weak, Fast Healing.

The fight

  • Raylta opened with Oracular Warning (+2 initiative, self-Cursebound). Bargrum Defensive-Advanced in and called the silver weakness (which also shuts off the choir's Fast Healing).
  • Jubilant's screams reached the whole clustered party every round (she's Large): Scream for Me and Unholy Exaltation hammered Bargrum, Ferrok, and Bael; the Sacristan added Chilling Darkness and Fear; the Ostiarius chained the front line and buffed with Compel Courage. Raylta's Divine Wrath caught both Ostiarius.
  • Tivael ran control all fight: Evangelize (Sacristan Stupefied 2), a sustained Infectious Ennui spreading Slowed + Frightened velstrac to velstrac, Fortissimo +2 Anthems, Demoralize, Biting Words, Electric Arc — and once let Raylta use him as a body-shield against a chain.
  • Ferrok focus-fired Jubilant: self-Haste + Arcane Cascade, Echo Jump onto her, Sure Strike + Ignition Spellstrike runs dropping her to 2 HP; Bael's one-action blast killed Jubilant ("she burns to a crisp").
  • The party finished the choir: Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes (a 50 on an Ostiarius) and the final blade on the last velstrac, Bael's Flying Flame, Raylta's Holy Light + heals, Bargrum's taunts and intercepts.

Outcome

A near-thing — Tivael bled to death's door, the screams reached everyone — but the choir fell, and the party leveled to 10. Tivael's read on Jubilant: "She was like a bard, basically." Loot: none (velstracs carry no gear).

Character Spotlights

Tivael · the Conductor's Better Half ★

  • His control session, by design — against Jubilant's choir he ran the whole board at once: Evangelize to stupefy the Sacristan, a sustained Infectious Ennui nursed every single round so it kept hopping Slowed-and-Frightened from velstrac to velstrac, and Fortissimo +2 Anthems holding the party's numbers up as the screams came in.
  • Still closed threads with violence when it counted — the silver Needle Dart that killed Freileth ("that was actually a lot of damage"), a hero-pointed Flurry of Claws crit and a Telekinetic Projectile crit among the Evangelists — but the fingerprints this week are on control, not the kill log.
  • Voice stayed sharp — the deadpan "oh, you stupid bitch" as he Bon-Mot'd a survivor, and the post-fight read that named Jubilant for what she was ("She was like a bard, basically" — the maestro clocking his own dark reflection).
  • The chilling sentence he handed Jubilant before initiative — a bard condemning a bard to a hell past reincarnation.
  • Honest about the cost afterward — "I think I need to play more defensively" — after a fight that nearly bled him out.

Bargrum · the Wall and the Auctioneer

  • The same immovable object — Group/Shielding Taunts pulling reach-guards, velstrac chains, and an entire screaming choir onto himself; Intercepts, Defensive Advances, trips, and the silver Monkey's Fist.
  • The party's reader of mechanics — called the Living Guards' positioning problem and announced the velstracs' silver weakness ("they will not heal because they've been damaged by silver").
  • The quartermaster-in-chief — ran a full auction house out of the loot pile back in town: "the number one manacle dealer in the region, real monopoly," "going once, going twice, sold."
  • The session's hardest line — his crit-taunt against the Evangelists: "Yeah, we killed him. So what? What are you gonna do about it?"
  • The dry philosopher in the poppet debate — "Well, let's get philosophical — is it a good thing that you have a soul?" and, to Raylta, "You're the one who didn't want to kill it. So this is your child now, right?" Also drove the mirror-hall prisoner evacuation.

Ferrok · the Murdery Conscience

  • The kill leader and priority-target eliminator — Ignition Spellstrikes all night, a stretching-reach swing that killed two Evangelists and freed a grabbed Raylta, the Echo Jump teleport onto Jubilant, a 50-damage Ignition on an Ostiarius, and the final blade on the last velstrac.
  • Gallows deadpan — "We don't negotiate," "basically the aliens from Hellraiser," "still traumatized by evil Greg."
  • The poppet cracked something honest open — he flagged the party's own hypocrisy ("murder a defenseless little poppet?") and supplied the merciful logistics (the flat-of-the-sword KO, "write a note and say sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys").
  • The self-aware thesis of the night — "I don't know. We're pretty murdery. One good deed of the day."
  • Echo Jump finally fixed his oldest problem — teleporting straight onto a priority target instead of fighting a doorway.

Bael · the Adaptive Pyromaniac (and Reluctant Diplomat)

  • The opener and the closer — disabled the electrified gate (clutch nat-20 thievery), opened the Evangelist fight with Solar Detonation, ran the Incendiary-Fog ignition combo with Ferrok, and landed the killing blast on Jubilant ("as she burns to a crisp").
  • His RK reframed the poppet fight — clocking that the bolt-emitter was a remote-controlled construct that died if you took the controller, which turned a slugfest into a capture.
  • The self-immolation gag stayed strong — "Getting better at exploding myself," the doomed Brace-for-Impact recovery rolls, Treat-Wounds whiffs ("cross your fingers I don't fuck it up again") — and his greater healer's gloves made him a real second healer.
  • The reluctant diplomat — he kept trying to defuse the poppet (a web scroll — "Just stop fighting!" — then the non-lethal fist-dunk).
  • The kindest possible aftercare — leaving the freed poppet "a brief recap of the thousand years of history he missed."

Raylta · the Heart That Wouldn't Let Go

  • The heal economy under brutal pressure — Benediction, rank-2-through-5 Heals, Nudge the Scales (sliding into Cursebound/Enfeebled), Divine Wrath, Moonbeam, Holy Light, a 46-point Lay on Hands.
  • Nearly died twice — Freileth's 48-point void beam left her doomed-adjacent; the Evangelists hooked her through both palms mid-cast. She took it with gallows humor ("this ain't my fight," "you're on my shit list") and a flash of healer frustration when the chains ate two of her turns ("Don't pity me!").
  • She IS the poppet arc — saw a brave little guard instead of a boss ("It's just a little guard"), refused to kill it, tried to talk it down ("we don't want to hurt you, just drop the remote!"), and pulled the whole table into sparing it.
  • Called out the party's bloodlust — "That's what you guys are doing! I was trying to defuse!" The note in that locked room is hers as much as anyone's.
  • Opened the finale by Cursing herself (Oracular Warning) to give everyone a head start against the choir — heart and sacrifice in one action.
