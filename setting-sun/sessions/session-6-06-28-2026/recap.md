## Session Summary

The floor the party had bloodied last session turned out to belong to something far worse than vampires. Pressing past an electrified gate, they walked into a halberd-line of **Living Guards** — bite-marked human thralls in ancient Azlanti armor — and the red-clad vampire **Freileth**, who drifted in through the gate as mist and very nearly drained Raylta to death with a void beam. It was a grinding, reach-choked brawl in a tiny room, but the guards were weak to fire (Bael's whole thesis) and Freileth folded to silver: Tivael's silver Needle Dart put her down. Battered, over-encumbered, and absurdly rich, the party took a full day-trip back to Talmandor's Bounty to offload loot and shop ("we're suffering from successes"), then climbed back up.

What waited above was a Zon-Kuthon torture-house. The hacked-up corpses of the vampires they'd killed last session now hung from the ceiling on hooks — claimed by a pack of **Evangelists**, chain-and-hook velstracs who were genuinely offended the party had "stolen their kill" ("I hear you called dibs" / "No dibs was called!"). The velstracs grappled half the party with impaling chains — Bargrum was the first crit-grabbed, then Raylta got hooked through both palms mid-cast and Tivael through the wings — but silver and fire ground them down. Only afterward did a Recall Knowledge name them: velstracs, fanatics of the god of pain. "Basically the aliens from Hellraiser."

Then the session's heart: behind a reinforced door, a **brave little poppet** — a toy guard named **Anvaca**, animated by the spirits of long-dead N'Gokal guardsmen and built in the likeness of the moon-goddess Asavna — squared up alone against the whole party in defense of a kingdom a thousand years dead. Raylta refused to kill it; the party closed the doors on its bolt-emitter, beat it unconscious with the flats of their weapons, left it a note ("sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys"), and locked it safely back in its room. A hallway of mirror-strapped torture victims followed (freed and staged for evacuation), and then the floor's master: **Jubilant**, a Conductor velstrac — a maestro with a conductor's baton, a throne of flesh, and a hunger to find a dragon's pain receptors. She and her two Ostiarius and a Sacristan nearly screamed the party apart, but Tivael's control engine (Evangelize, a spreading Infectious Ennui, Fortissimo Anthems) locked the choir down while Ferrok focus-fired the conductor. Bael's blast burned Jubilant "to a crisp"; Ferrok's blade swept the rest. The party hit **level 10**, one session from the top of the tower.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Electrified Gate (Explore)
- After refocusing and healing off the previous fight (Bael: *"Getting better at exploding myself"*), the party found a bare room with three exits: two doors barricaded with furniture, the north door behind a **very clearly electrified gate** — purely mechanical, no magic to dispel.
- **Bael** volunteered (shock-resistant), borrowed a charm of resistance from **Bargrum** as a hedge, and disabled the power source with a **thievery check** — a nat-20 critical success after a hero point.
- Beyond lay an alchemy workroom (writing desk, alchemical tools) and, through the doorways, the rush of **multiple humanoids** — living, breathing humans in ancient Azlanti arms and armor. **Bargrum** tried to head it off (*"Can't we talk this out?"*); they only shook their heads and attacked on sight.

### Act II — The Living Guards & Freileth (Combat)
- **Seven Living Guards** (halberds, 10-ft reach, reactive strikes) swarmed the cramped room. Recall Knowledge (Raylta) read them as martially-trained human guards with **bite marks on their necks** — vampire thralls, *not* undead — and **weak to fire**.
- For most of the first three rounds, the reach-and-reactive-strike wall meant *nobody landed a hit* (the DM, repeatedly: "with none of them landing an attack"). **Bargrum** anchored it — Group/Shielding Taunts pulling the line onto himself, Intercepts, and trips — while **Bael's fire** (their weakness) and **Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes** chewed guards down one a round.
- **Tivael** worked the control lane: Lingering Composition, a Phantom Pain on a guard, a rank-3 **Fear** that frightened most of the pack (carefully excluding Raylta), Courageous Anthem, and Electric Arc. When the party was bleeding out, he summoned a **unicorn (Summon Fey)** that ate a reactive strike on arrival and then healed Raylta.
- At the top of a round, a **mist** poured through the electrified gate and coalesced into **Freileth** — a vampire in a well-tailored red outfit, hair in old Azlanti fashion, glowing green eyes — who took one look at her dead kin and snarled, *"You wretches!"*
- Freileth cast **Enervation** (a 30-ft void line): Bael failed and took heavy persistent void + Drained, and a later rank-4 iteration **caught Raylta for 48 persistent void + Drained**, leaving her doomed-adjacent ("I'm doomed one? I would die"). She also threw a **Chromatic Ray** (missed Raylta — "my Fort is so bad") and Shielded up.
- The party answered with a clean combo: **Ferrok placed Incendiary Fog** and **Bael's incendiary aura instantly ignited it** into "a gigantic flaming sphere," roasting Freileth and a guard. **Raylta's Divine Wrath** caught the back line and left Freileth **Sickened 2 / Slowed**; her **Moonbeam** and a 46-point Lay on Hands kept the party upright.
- Near death, **Tivael** tried to **Evangelize** Freileth into submission (she refused — "she said no"), then finished her with a **silver Needle Dart** — *"that was actually a lot of damage."* The seventh coffin's tenant was dead.
- Loot from the floor and the rooms beyond closed the encounter; the party, badly drained and over-bulk, decided to rest and resupply.

### Act III — Suffering from Successes (Downtime / RP)
- The party took the **full day-trip back to Talmandor's Bounty** to sell and shop. **Bargrum** ran the auction-house bit — quartermaster, appraiser, and "**the number one manacle dealer in the region — real monopoly**," closing each lot with *"going once, going twice, sold."*
- They were drowning in money. **Raylta**: *"Scrooge McDuck swims in his coinage — there's just too much."* **Bael** upgraded to **greater healer's gloves** (*"Damn, we're so rich"*); **Ferrok** put a **+2 potency rune** and a flaming rune on his bastard sword; **Tivael** bought a new instrument and a stack of scrolls. (Hard cap: level-10 items are still out of reach — "nine is your cap.")
- A long OOC tangent about dollar stores aside, the party then made the **return climb** all the way back up the Ebon Tower to where they'd left off.

### Act IV — Dibs (the Evangelists) (Combat)
- Back in the southern ballroom (last session's Irnakurse fight), the **hacked-up corpses of the vampires** the party had killed now hung from the ceiling on spiked hooks "like twisted ornaments." Among them stood humanoid figures impaled with chains, hooks, and whips, grinning: *"Are you the ones that finally beat those wretches?"*
- The mood curdled fast when the party answered wrong: *"What matters is that they were ours, not yours… I hear you called dibs."* / **Raylta**: "Right? You didn't call dibs!" / **Bael**: "Yeah, it's very important. No dibs was called."
- **Bael opened with Solar Detonation**, dazzling two of the **five Evangelists** (A, C) and blinding a third (D) — B and E were just outside the burst. The velstracs closed with **Impaling Chain** — a crit auto-grabs and impales for persistent bleed: **Bargrum** was crit-grabbed first, then **Raylta** got **hooked through both palms** mid-cast (her Needle Darts interrupted: *"No! You motherfucker… you're on my shit list."*) and **Tivael** through the wings; Ferrok was chained later too.
- **Bargrum** held the grapple-line; his crit-taunt earned the session's best smack-talk (*"Yeah, we killed him. So what? What are you gonna do about it?"*). **Bael** cracked back at a hook-threat (*"Are you a dentist?"*); **Tivael** carved with Flurry of Claws (a hero-pointed second prong crit), Electric Arc, and a Telekinetic Projectile crit — chipping a survivor with a silver Needle Dart and a Bon Mot (*"Oh, you stupid bitch — there you go"*). **Ferrok** was the kill engine: his stretching reach **killed two at once and freed a grabbed Raylta**, with Force Fang and Ignition Spellstrike finishes; one frightened survivor ate a Demoralize (*"You're next!"*). The last Evangelist lived to the break and was cleaned up after.
- The party didn't know what they'd fought until a retroactive **Recall Knowledge**: **velstracs**, Netherworld natives and fanatics of **Zon-Kuthon**, the god of pain — who make no distinction between pain and pleasure and "lead others to that same state" through torture. **Ferrok**: "So they're basically the aliens from Hellraiser." (DM: "That's pretty much what they're inspired by.")

### Act V — The Brave Little Guard (Combat → Mercy)
- After a break, the party examined a defaced **statue of Asavna** — Azlanti goddess of companionship, defensive tactics, and the moon (destroyed at Starfall) — decapitated, its head set atop a pile of viscera. They cleared a coffin room (the slain vampires' empty coffins, stakes left in) and a coat room, and picked up gear: a **Fireball** scroll, **Shape Stone**, and a **Silver Slough** (Ferrok, to silver his blade).
- Bargrum face-checked a reinforced door and a **little toy guard** stabbed at him — **Anvaca**, a **poppet** animated by the spirits of old **N'Gokal guardsmen** and built in the likeness of the goddess Asavna. In a voice far too big for it: *"Back! Back, fiends! In defense of N'Gokal, I shall slay thee!"* It worked a wall-mounted **bolt emitter** (32 electricity) by remote and twice unleashed **Dead, But Not Forgotten** (a spirit-guardian AoE).
- Recall Knowledge confirmed it: a construct, but a *humanoid* one, **not mindless** — channeling multiple guard-spirits. The party closed the door between it and its bolt-emitter sightline and decided to take it **alive**.
- **Raylta refused to kill it** — *"It's just a little guard"* — and tried her **Security Badge** (an incapacitation command; it shrugged it off) and outright pleading: *"Come on, come on — we don't want to hurt you, just drop the remote!"* The party switched to **non-lethal** strikes (−2): **Bael dunked his fist on its head** (with Raylta's Guidance) to soften it, and **Ferrok's flat-of-the-sword blow knocked it unconscious** — not dead.
- They took the remote, **left it a note** — *"sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys"* (plus, per Bael, "a brief recap of the thousand years of history he missed") — dragged it back into its room, and locked it in. **Ferrok**: *"I don't know. We're pretty murdery. One good deed of the day."* (DM's tally of the spared: the poppet and Speckle. **Bael**: "We have a penchant for cute things.")

### Act VI — The Mirror Hall (Explore / RP)
- A long hall of countless mirrors (odd, given vampires cast no reflection) gave way to a rotten stench and a string of torture cells: men with their **eyelids cut off**, heads strapped in place, **forced to stare at their own reflections**, hooks and chains shoved beneath their flesh, fingers and nails removed. "The velstracs have been to work on this man."
- The party gave first aid where they could, but the victims were in **deep shock** (no quick recovery). It was **Bargrum's plan** to **unstrap each prisoner and stage them by the stairs** with blankets and food for evacuation — and his idea to improvise saline/eye-drops for the lidless eyes. Grim, methodical care between fights.

### Act VII — The Conductor's Kingdom (Combat — Finale)
- The north door opened on a **grisly shrine**: bone pillars, a statue built from organs and severed limbs, and **four velstracs**. Flanking the central throne, two **Ostiarius** with gouged, bleeding palms; guarding the center, a blindfolded **Sacristan** draped in chains; and on the throne, ecstatic, a sewn-together woman with pitch-black eyes and a **maestro's conductor baton** at her sash — **Jubilant**, a Conductor velstrac.
- Jubilant welcomed them as guests, then mused about a new canvas: *"I've never worked on a dragon before. I wonder where the pain receptors in your wings are found."* **Tivael** answered cold: *"Too bad you won't ever find out in this life… maybe you'll be condemned so deep in hell that you won't ever be reincarnated."* Jubilant: *"Oh, sweet summer child. I come from a far worse place."*
- **Raylta led on Oracular Warning** (+2 initiative for the party, at the cost of going Cursebound — "Yes, Bargrum's cursed!"). **Bargrum** opened with a Defensive Advance and immediately read the room's tech: **the velstracs are weak to silver, and silver damage stops their fast healing.**
- **Jubilant was the engine of the fight** — huge, so her **Scream for Me** (single-target, then a secondary AoE scream) and **Unholy Exaltation** (sonic) reached the whole clustered party every round. The **Sacristan** added Chilling Darkness and a rank-3 Fear; the **Ostiarius** buffed the choir with **Compel Courage** and chained the front line. (Raylta's own **Divine Wrath** answered, catching both Ostiarius.)
- **Tivael ran the control board all fight** (he leaned into control/buff over kills this session): **Evangelize** stupefied the Sacristan, a sustained **Infectious Ennui** spread Slowed + Frightened across velstrac after velstrac round by round, and **Fortissimo Courageous/Rallying Anthems (+2)** propped the party's accuracy and saves. He also fed **Biting Words** (sonic) and Electric Arc into the pile — and, when a Sacristan chain came for him, **Raylta grabbed him and used him as a shield** ("he's luckier than me").
- **Ferrok focus-fired the conductor** as the priority kill: self-Haste + Arcane Cascade, an **Echo Jump** to teleport up beside her, then Sure Strike + Ignition Spellstrike runs that dropped Jubilant to **2 HP**. **Bael's one-action blast finished her** — *"that hit on Jubilant, that was enough, as she burns to a crisp."*
- With the conductor dead, the party mopped the choir: Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrikes (a 50 on an Ostiarius), Bael's Flying Flame, Raylta's Holy Light and heals, Bargrum's taunts and intercepts, and Tivael's spreading Ennui. **Ferrok's blade ended the last velstrac** — Raylta: *"That'll do it. Bye, you freak."*
- Out of combat, the table exhaled. *"What a huge tonal shift from the poppet."* Tivael's read on Jubilant: *"She was like a bard, basically."* The DM closed the night with **level 10**, and a promise: next week, the top of the tower and Nizca.

## Character Spotlight

### Tivael · the Conductor's Better Half
This was Tivael's control session, by his own design — fewer kills, far more orchestration, and it showed in the finale against a literal dark-mirror maestro. Against Jubilant's choir he ran the whole board at once: **Evangelize** to stupefy the Sacristan, a **sustained Infectious Ennui** that he nursed every single round so it kept hopping Slowed-and-Frightened from one velstrac to the next, and **Fortissimo +2 Anthems** holding the party's numbers up while the screams came in. He still closed threads with violence when it counted — the **silver Needle Dart that killed Freileth** ("that was actually a lot of damage"), a hero-pointed Flurry of Claws crit and a Telekinetic Projectile crit among the Evangelists, Biting Words into the surviving choir — but his fingerprints this week are on *control*, not the kill log. His voice stayed sharp throughout: the deadpan "oh, you stupid bitch" as he Bon-Mot'd a survivor, the post-fight read that named Jubilant for what she was ("She was like a bard, basically" — the maestro clocking his own dark reflection), and the genuinely chilling sentence he handed her before initiative — a bard condemning a bard to a hell past reincarnation. He ended the night honest about the cost — *"I think I need to play more defensively"* — after a fight that nearly bled him out.

### Bargrum · the Wall and the Auctioneer
The load-bearing dwarf, and this session the party's quartermaster-in-chief. In combat he was the same immovable object — Group/Shielding Taunts pulling reach-guards, velstrac chains, and an entire screaming choir onto himself, Intercepts, Defensive Advances, trips, and the silver Monkey's Fist — and he's the party's *reader of mechanics*, the one who called the Living Guards' positioning problem and announced the velstracs' silver weakness ("they will not heal because they've been damaged by silver"). But his best material was the town trip, where he ran a full auction house out of the party's loot pile — *"the number one manacle dealer in the region, real monopoly,"* "last call for weapons or runes, going once, going twice, sold." His crit-taunt against the Evangelists produced the session's hardest line (*"Yeah, we killed him. So what? What are you gonna do about it?"*), and in the poppet debate he played the dry philosopher: *"Well, let's get philosophical — is it a good thing that you have a soul?"* and, to Raylta, *"You're the one who didn't want to kill it. So this is your child now, right?"*

### Ferrok · the Murdery Conscience
The kill leader, the priority-target eliminator, and — somehow — the session's bluntest moral voice. Mechanically Ferrok was the closer: Ignition Spellstrikes all night, a stretching-reach swing that **killed two Evangelists and freed a grabbed Raylta** in one motion, the **Echo Jump teleport** onto Jubilant that set up her death, a 50-damage Ignition on an Ostiarius, and the **final blade** on the last velstrac. His comedy is pure gallows deadpan — "We don't negotiate," "basically the aliens from Hellraiser," "still traumatized by evil Greg" — but the poppet arc cracked something honest open. He flagged the party's own hypocrisy on it (*"murder a defenseless little poppet?"*), supplied the merciful logistics (the flat-of-the-sword KO, "write a note and say sorry we knocked you out, we're not bad guys"), and landed the self-aware thesis of the whole night: *"I don't know. We're pretty murdery. One good deed of the day."*

### Bael · the Adaptive Pyromaniac (and Reluctant Diplomat)
The opener, the closer, and the party's quiet problem-solver. Bael **disabled the electrified gate** with a clutch nat-20 thievery to start the session, **opened the Evangelist fight with Solar Detonation** (dazzle + blind), ran his Incendiary-Fog ignition combo with Ferrok against Freileth, and **landed the killing blast on Jubilant** ("as she burns to a crisp"). It was also Bael's **Recall Knowledge on the bolt-emitter** that reframed the whole poppet fight — clocking that the machine was a remote-controlled construct that died if you took the controller, which is what turned a slugfest into a capture. His running self-immolation gag stayed strong — *"Getting better at exploding myself,"* the doomed Brace-for-Impact recovery rolls ("this hero point is not working"), Treat-Wounds whiffs ("cross your fingers I don't fuck it up again") — and his upgraded greater healer's gloves made him a real second healer, quietly topping off whoever Raylta couldn't reach. His softest beat was the poppet: he was the one who kept trying to *defuse* (a web scroll — "Just stop fighting!" — then the non-lethal fist-dunk) and proposed the kindest possible aftercare, leaving the freed poppet "a brief recap of the thousand years of history he missed."

### Raylta · the Heart That Wouldn't Let Go
The conscience of the party, and this session its center of gravity. She ran the entire heal economy under brutal pressure — Benediction, rank-2-through-5 Heals, Nudge the Scales (sliding into Cursebound/Enfeebled), Divine Wrath, Moonbeam, Holy Light, a 46-point Lay on Hands — and nearly *died* twice (Freileth's 48-point void beam left her doomed-adjacent; the Evangelists hooked her through both palms mid-cast). She took it with gallows humor — *"this ain't my fight,"* *"You motherfucker… you're on my shit list"* — and with a real flash of healer frustration when the chains ate two of her turns (*"I did not help in this fight"* / *"Don't pity me!"*). But Raylta **is** the poppet arc: she's the one who saw a brave little guard instead of a boss (*"It's just a little guard"*), refused to kill it, tried to talk it down (*"we don't want to hurt you, just drop the remote!"*), called out the party's bloodlust (*"That's what you guys are doing! I was trying to defuse!"*), and pulled the whole table into sparing it. The note left in that locked room is hers as much as anyone's. Also: she opened the finale by **Cursing herself on purpose** (Oracular Warning) to give everyone a head start against the choir — heart and sacrifice in one action.
