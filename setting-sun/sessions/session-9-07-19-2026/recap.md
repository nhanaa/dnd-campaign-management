## Session Summary

A new adventure path, a new party, and a new island. Six months after the Ebon Tower, phoenix-sealed invitations gathered five strangers onto a magically propelled ship bound for the Isle of Bonmou — the once-a-decade Ruby Phoenix tournament, hosted this year by Hao Jin herself, returned from a death everyone had assumed was permanent. Thirty-two hand-picked teams. Only eight advance. And this particular team is the wildcard: not a veteran adventuring company but a set of individual fighters Hao Jin assembled because they were each worthy and each teamless.

The day began with a handshake and ended with a temple full of dead things. A rival team — Tino's Toughest — flagged the party down on the beach and asked for a friendly spar, and it turned into a genuine brawl: three rounds, both sides trading knockdowns, Alastor and Clutch on the sand for the visitors and **Takatorra flattened by Ferrok** at the bell. Partway through, Alastor's player took a bad run of dice, went down, and left the call, Foundry, and the DM's Discord permanently. The remaining four shrugged, agreed the crabs got him, and carried on as a party of four.

Their assigned base turned out to be a derelict **Temple of Irori** — which meant something particular to Savatt, an Irorian pilgrim who spent the session treating the dungeon as a desecrated holy site rather than a loot pile. "Make it safe for habitation" meant clearing it room by room: two Goliath Spiders whose venom put Ferrok on the floor and nearly killed him, four incorporeal Irorian ghost monks, a catoblepas rotting in the atrium pond, six sand-elemental monks that sheathed themselves in electricity-resistant glass, a one-shot sutra trap, and finally **Kannitri** — the grief-stricken ghost of an Irorian nun who mistook the party for the raiders who massacred her temple centuries ago.

Six encounters in one session, three silver feathers recovered, the Phoenix Necklace still missing, and a level promised for next week.

## Key Events

### Act I — Session Zero and the Voyage (0:00–46:00)

- **DM (Cody) framed the campaign**: ~20 sessions, running through the end of 2026. Book 1 extended, Book 2 shortened ("*Book 2 of Ruby Phoenix is the way it is due to Paizo needing to hit a page count*"), Book 3 vanilla. Described as "**the Shonen Anime Tournament campaign**" — expect combat to dominate every session.
- **Content warnings**: giant insects (heavily, Book 1), some gore. Tournament fights are explicitly *not* to the death, but fights against wildlife and monsters are. Torture and sexual violence are off the table entirely.
- **Homebrew rules established** — see the Table Rules section below. The big one: **three hero points per session, rationed as you like.**
- **The premise**: Hao Jin returned to Goka, resumed control of her own tournament, cut the field to 32 hand-selected teams, and replaced the opening bracket with a **three-day free-for-all on the Isle of Bonmou** — nicknamed **Danger Island**. Top eight advance.
- **Character introductions** aboard the ship:
  - **Alastor** — a finalist from last decade's tournament, now middle-aged, estranged from his old team and given one more shot with strangers.
  - **Ferrok** — of Absalom, a fixture of the **Irorium** arena, who was *en route* to Tian Xia to train early for this tournament when the Ebon Tower sidetracked him.
  - **Savatt** — a pilgrim and wanderer from Janja. Unfamous, long-lived, and reading his invitation as a sign pointing toward enlightenment. **A follower of Irori** — which becomes the throughline of his entire session.
  - **Sovael** — of Tenshou. **Tivael's cousin**, a devotee of Hao Jin who came to meet her, and to find the cousin who told him he'd be here and never showed.
  - **Clutch** — a Kholo whose cousin **Clinch** competed ten years ago on a *separate* team. Both lost. Clutch has spent the decade since tearing through legal and underground fight circuits, and he is here to compete *and* to find Clinch.
- **Sovael and Ferrok connected over Tivael** — Ferrok had adventured with him and told the table the old party "*quit adventuring to open a manacle store.*" Sovael's verdict on his cousin: "*Not as skilled as me, of course.*"
- **Ferrok recognized Clutch's face** and couldn't place it — then landed on it: "*the only Kholo I've seen was a dead one in Ebon Tower.*" Clutch, who is on this island hunting his cousin, let it pass without comment.
- **Savatt practiced archery on the deck with no bow**, repeating the draw over and over. Sovael, watching: "*Good old shadow archery*" — and went back to practising his singing.

### Act II — Landfall, the Ceremony, and the Spar (36:00–89:00)

- **Shifu Shou Noh**, emissary of the tournament and guardian of Hao Jin's legacy, welcomed the 32 teams from the docks.
- **The task**: each team is assigned a site on the island, occupied by something dangerous. Clear it, claim the **Phoenix Necklace** and the **three silver feathers** hidden there, then acquire **seven more feathers** over three days.
- **Ingdani**, a Tian human woman in her mid-thirties, introduced herself as the team's handler. She enforces rules, witnesses outcomes, and — she was very clear — **will not intervene to save them**, even from death. The only exception is a verbal forfeit, which ends their run permanently.
- **Tino's Toughest approached on the beach**: **Tino Tong** (a Tian monk in an orange gi, **Irori's symbol tattooed on his chest**), **Takatorra** (a near-seven-foot Tengu, they/them, wielding a Tengu Gale Blade), **Ji-yook** (a Kitsune fighting bare-clawed), and **Yabin the Just** (a Nagaji caster with a staff). Savatt greeted Tino as a fellow Irorian with a bow — "*I see you're a follower as well*" — the first real connection anyone on either team made. Yabin singled out Ferrok as an Ebon Tower survivor and asked to discuss the Netherworld later. Tino asked for a **sparring match** — no stakes, three rounds.
- **Clutch opened negotiations** by tilting his muzzle to the sky and growling "**I must break you**" at a man who had just offered a friendly warm-up. Sovael, immediately: "*Maybe not that.*" Takatorra, delighted: "*That a threat or a promise, big guy?*"
- **The spar was a real fight** — detailed below. Both sides put people on the sand.
- **Alastor's player left mid-fight** after Tino's second Inner Upheaval hit him for **105**, disconnecting from Discord and Foundry and blocking the DM. After the break the DM confirmed he would not return or be invited to future games. Ferrok's take was gracious — "*low rolls are part of the game, it happens*" — and then his eulogy: "*We can assume the crabs ate him on the beach then, right?*"

### Act III — The Temple of Irori (104:00–178:00)

- Ingdani led the party fifteen minutes inland to a temple wrapped in enormous webs, a **fully grown triceratops** cocooned and writhing against the northern wall. She smiled, leapt several dozen feet backward into the treeline, and called: "*Welcome to your base. Your first task is to make it safe for habitation.*"
- **Two Goliath Spiders** descended — a brutal, poison-driven fight that put Ferrok on the floor.
- Inside, **paintings of a faceless man** undergoing trials — wading a river, balancing atop a tree, meditating in a storm. The party identified the figure as **Irori**. They had been quartered in a ruined temple to Savatt's own god.
- The training courtyard held a **30-foot pagoda sculpture covered in hundreds of carved names** — a memorial for those who lived their lives in service to the temple, not only its battle dead — and **four Irorian ghost monks** rose from the sand.
- **Dormitory loot**: a holy rune, a Ring of Climbing, a Wand of Cleanse Affliction (5th rank), and a pouch of opals worth ~400 gp. The Ring of Climbing prompted the session's best running bit — Clutch offering it to his dead cousin, because "*he's got to climb out of his grave, you know, so he needs the climb speed.*"
- **Mess hall loot** (found because Clutch insisted people always drop things while eating): a moderate potion of fire resistance, a **+2 Striking Astral Hook Sword**, a vial of endless water, and **Greater Holy Prayer Beads** — which went to Sovael, who accepted with a simple "*I am holy.*"
- **Savatt asked to clean the temple** rather than strip it, and pressed the DM on why an Irori temple would be haunted — establishing that there had been violence here in the past, and that the scattered weapons were the mark of a battle rather than a ritual.
- **The atrium pond** — dark, fouled water around a stone platform — opened its eyes. A **catoblepas**. Killing it began to purify the water on its own.
- **A sand room** held the first silver feather and **six sand-elemental monks**.

### Act IV — Feathers and the Broken Rebus (198:00–231:00)

- **First silver feather** recovered from the sand room.
- **A prayer chamber** of candles, bells, and singing bowls held the second feather — and a **one-shot sutra hazard**: a spectral monk appeared, recited a sutra, filled the room with kinetic energy, and vanished. **Savatt recited the sutra back at it**: "*One must know the teachings of Irori.*" The DM confirmed it was a complex hazard with a single trigger. "*Nothing happens. That was the threat.*"
- **A chamber with a eucalyptus tree** growing up through a ceiling window, an old skeleton slumped at its base, and the third feather among the bark scraps. Beside the skeleton sat **Kannitri**, the ghost of an Irorian monk woman, dazed and staring past them.
- She stood, focused on the party, and said: "*I am sorry. We could not stop them.*" Asked what happened, she answered: "**The broken Rebus. They came, they showed no mercy.**" Then her eyes cleared into rage — "*They've come back!*" — and she attacked.
- **Savatt tried to defuse it**, delaying his turn and spending a Recall Knowledge specifically to check whether she would respond to his **Irorian holy symbol** — "*maybe we can resolve this without destroying her.*" The DM ruled it changed nothing. There was no peaceful exit from killing another of his own god's dead.
- She was destroyed, fading "*with this horribly sad expression on her face, this guilt-ridden look of anguish*" — and the DM noted **her presence is still here. She's gone, but not completely.**
- **Third silver feather** claimed. Session end.

## Table Rules — Cody's Homebrew (established this session)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hero points** | "Epic hero points" — **3 per session**, granted up front, rationed however you like (not 1 + hourly) |
| **Loot tracker** | Whoever tracks party loot earns **+1 hero point per session** |
| **8-hour rest** | Automatically restores **full HP** |
| **Persistent damage / poison** | Ends when combat ends, unless it's lethal enough to matter |
| **Dungeon rests** | One free 10-minute rest between encounters; 2nd and 3rd consecutive rests require flat checks |
| **Falling unconscious** | You do **not** drop held items |
| **Caster DC items** | Homebrew "Rings of Magic Might" — +1 (level 5, 180 gp) or +2 (level 17, 13,000 gp) to spell attack and DC |
| **Shopping** | Uncommon items count as **5 levels higher**, rare as **10 levels higher**; party has access to a level-20 settlement |
| **Rune upgrades** | Specific magic items' runes can be upgraded so they don't fall off |
| **Attendance** | 8 hours' notice to skip (vs. Start Playing's 24) |
| **Restricted options** | Exemplar dedication, Giant Instinct barbarian, Large ancestries (the last not enforced this AP) |

### Encounter 1 — The Spar vs. Tino's Toughest (3 rounds, both sides bloodied)

**Initiative**: Sovael → Yabin the Just → Ji-yook → Tino → Savatt → Takatorra → Alastor → Ferrok → Clutch
*(As rolled, Ji-yook was ahead of Yabin; she **Delayed** on her first turn to act after him, and that order held for the rest of the fight.)*
**Terms**: fought only to the end of Round 3; Ingdani and the attendants on standby. Both sides agreed not to hold back.

**Opponents**: **Tino Tong** (Tian monk, Irori worshipper, barehanded — Flurry of Blows, **Inner Upheaval**, **Retributive Strike**), **Takatorra** (Tengu, they/them — Gale Blade with 5-ft reach, **Blade Barrage**), **Ji-yook** (Kitsune, claws — Skirmish Strike, feint, **Foxfire Blast**, Nimble Dodge), **Yabin the Just** (Nagaji caster — rank-6 **Arrow Salvo**, **Fireball**, **Phantasmal Killer**, Shield).

**Round 1**
- **Alastor** triggered **Battle Cry** on his initiative roll, Demoralizing Tino with "a very accurate wolf howl." It landed — the DM later narrated Tino as "*scared back a little bit by your sudden intimidation*" before he grinned and took it head on.
- **Sovael** led with **Courageous Anthem** + **Lingering Composition**, extending it **three rounds — the entire spar**. Then **Divine Wrath**: Tino succeeded (13); **Takatorra critically failed for 54 → sickened 2, slowed 1**, the single biggest hit of the fight.
- **Yabin** retreated and answered with a **rank-6 Arrow Salvo** across the party — Sovael 27, Clutch 21, Ferrok 27. **Clutch** shield-blocked.
- **Ji-yook** used **Skirmish Strike** to step-and-strike, clawing Clutch and then Ferrok. **Clutch Intercepted** the blow meant for Ferrok, offering him a free 10-ft reposition; Ferrok declined it to stay on the offensive.
- **Tino** charged **Alastor** with a **Flurry of Blows** using **Inner Upheaval**, choosing force damage — both strikes hit and were combined, **34 and 38**. A follow-up MAP strike was blocked.
- **Savatt** ran to Alastor, laid a hand on his back to "*align your chi*" and **healed him 36**, then **Hunt Prey**'d Takatorra.
- **Takatorra** closed and landed a Gale Blade strike on Clutch for 24.
- **Alastor** entered **Clinging Shadows Stance**, then spent **a hero point** on a **Combat Grab** — which hit and grabbed Tino, dealing **Shadow Grasp** damage (19). *This was the only hero point spent by anyone in the entire encounter.*
- **Ferrok** opened with **Sure Strike** into an **Ignition Spellstrike** on Ji-yook — she **Nimble Dodged** and it missed by a hair. ("*Now Ferrok's sad.*")
- **Clutch** ran **Defensive Advance** (raise shield, stride, strike) for 28 into Ji-yook. **Tino answered with Retributive Strike** — the **Irori tattoo flaring on his own chest** — granting Ji-yook **resistance 11** and cutting the hit to **16**. Clutch followed with a MAP claw strike and **grabbed Ji-yook**, with 6 shock damage splashing onto Takatorra.

**Round 2**
- **Sovael** cast a **three-action rank-6 Blazing Bolt**, hitting **Tino (36)** and **Takatorra (40)**; the third ray at Ji-yook went wide. He triggered **Foretell Harm** as a free action — accepting **Cursebound** and its persistent fire (1/round from here on) — and Tino took **12** more at the start of his turn from the rider.
- **Yabin** dropped a **Fireball** catching four of the party. **Clutch Intercepted for Ferrok**; Ferrok used his **cloak reaction** for +1 to the save and fire resistance 5, taking **23**. Sovael and Savatt each took 23 as well.
- **Ji-yook**, still grabbed, **feinted Ferrok successfully** and hit him with a **Foxfire Blast** — a claw strike that would have critted him off-guard for **43**. **Ferrok used Emergency Targe** to cast Shield and downgrade it to a normal hit.
- **Tino** hit Alastor with a second **Inner Upheaval** for **105**, dropping him unconscious. The DM apologized in the moment: "*I didn't mean to pick on you that hard, I'm sorry.*"
- **Alastor's player left the call here.** Savatt covered the gap, resuming his archery — two arrows on a **Hunted Shot** at Takatorra (**27**, **13**) — then moving to lay hands on Ferrok for **45**.
- **Takatorra** moved on Savatt "*because healers*" and missed.
- **Ferrok** answered with a **Force Fang** and an **Ignition Spellstrike** on Takatorra for **76** — the biggest single hit of the fight — plus persistent fire. "*Takatorra gets rocked by that one.*"
- **Clutch** used **Group Taunt** via **Shielding Taunt** on Ji-yook, Tino, and Takatorra, then a MAP Combat Grab to maintain his hold.

**Round 3**
- **Sovael** used **Elf Step** to disengage (prompting Ferrok's "*You don't look like an elf*"), weighed a rank-6 breath weapon, and stood down when the DM warned him this was only the first fight of the day — settling for **Electric Arc** on two of them, one shrugging it off entirely and one taking it full.
- **Yabin cast Phantasmal Killer on Clutch.** Clutch **failed** — taking the damage and **frightened 2** — but his **Black Pearl Aeon Stone** reflected a *copy* back on a **+30 counteract**, and **Yabin failed his own spell**, taking **27** and frightened 2 in turn. The stone survived its flat check (rolled 9). Clutch noted it's a **2,000 gp consumable**; the table spent a minute simply disbelieving the +30 before confirming it was RAW.
- **Ji-yook** feinted Clutch and landed another Foxfire Blast (37).
- **Tino** flanked and dropped **Clutch** with **64** — "*a liver punch that sends you to your knees.*"
- **Savatt** put his bow away and punched Takatorra "*right in the beak*" (**42**), then kept punching once he learned Clutch would be revived at the bell (**26**).
- **Takatorra** used **Blade Barrage** on Savatt — three strikes, two landing, inflicting **persistent bleed** and off-guard.
- **Ferrok closed the spar by putting Takatorra on the sand** with a sword strike, then landed a second swing on Ji-yook (18).
- **Ingdani raised her hands**: "*That is quite enough. Well fought, everyone.*"

**Aftermath**: Handshakes all round — **Ferrok helped Takatorra up** ("*Takatorra will take your hand*"), Savatt helped Clutch. Ingdani dryly noted this had been suggested as a *sparring* match "*and yet, it seems martial artists just can't help themselves,*" leaving Tino's Toughest visibly sheepish. Tino hoped for a rematch as an official bout. Everyone healed to full over half an hour on Ward Medic and Continual Recovery.

> **The read**: unprompted, the DM told Ferrok — the only Ebon Tower veteran present — that each member of Tino's Toughest struck him as **as powerful as Nizca**. Ferrok's response: "*Yeah, I figured they were a higher level than us, honestly.*"

### Encounter 2 — Goliath Spiders ×2 (6 rounds)

**Initiative**: Savatt → Goliath Spider B → Goliath Spider A → Sovael → Clutch → Ferrok

Two gargantuan spiders — big enough that a fully grown triceratops and a pegasus were both cocooned in their webbing. **Goliath Spider Venom** drove the fight: a repeating Fortitude save on every bite *and* at the end of the spider's turn, escalating through stages with **paralysis** at the top. Ferrok made five of these saves across the fight, yo-yoing between stages.

- **Savatt** opened by **Hunt Prey**ing Spider A and casting **Gravity Weapon** — the campaign's first focus spell — then landing both arrows of a **Hunted Shot** (31, 27). Sovael, baffled: "*Wait, how do you do two hits?*" Savatt: "*It's Legolas. Just Ranger things.*"
- **Ferrok became the venom sponge immediately**, taking bite after bite (15, 20, 24, 22, 34) and stacking **slowed 1 → slowed 2**. He burned **the fight's only hero point** on a save at [115:16], chugged a moderate healing potion off the floor, and at one point simply drank while prone: "*That's all I can do.*"
- **Clutch Intercepted** the first bite meant for Ferrok and made the venom save himself, then cycled **Taunt** and **Shielding Taunt** to hold both spiders' attention while landing **Snagging Strikes** to stack off-guard. He waved off Savatt's offer to block for him with the session's most quietly telling line: "*Cody never goes for me. He goes for you all to hit.*" The DM agreed — "*that is the best way to hit a guardian*" — and Clutch confirmed he'd taken the feat specifically so allies could disengage.
- **Sovael ran the entire support engine solo**: **Benediction**; **Whispers of Weakness** on Goliath A, revealing **no weaknesses and Will as its worst save**; then **Fire Ray**, his rank-6 revelation spell, which set the spider's square alight — *"the ground in the target space catches fire."* **The Shadow Signet was no help here** — it converts spell attacks to target Fort or Reflex, and the weak save was Will. What actually paid was **Whispers' own +2 status bonus to his next attack**. He followed with **Courageous Anthem**, **Ancestral Memories** for a +2 accuracy swing, and **Fear** — all while taking 1 persistent fire per round from Cursebound.
- **Ferrok went down** mid-fight after a **76-damage** bite — unconscious, dying 3, and still forced to save against the venom. He raised the idea of his **delaying Aeon Stone**; the DM talked him out of it ("*the poison would bring him down to zero anyway*") and **he never used it**. What actually saved him was **Sovael's rank-3 Heal** at [122:34] — Ferrok: "*I'm alive.*"
- **Clutch took a 72-damage nat-20 bite** and chose to eat it rather than break his shield.
- **Savatt doubled as second healer** throughout — **healer's gloves** on Ferrok (27, 30, 45), **Guidance** on Sovael, tactical advice ("*Hit it in the head*"), and **Elf Step** to pull out of reach. He also made the fight's best rules play alongside Clutch, who caught that **Shielding Taunt and Doctor's Visitation are both flourishes** and can't be combined in one turn.
- **Goliath Spider A died** between Clutch's **Snagging Strike crit** at [123:43] (DM: "*Single digits*") and Savatt's follow-up — the DM never announced the kill on tape, so **credit between Clutch and Savatt is unresolved.**
- **Ferrok killed Goliath Spider B from the floor** — two actions, a **Battle Medicine** on Clutch and then a swing from prone that **critted**: "*As you just spear this thing from underneath, with this spray of flames and ichor, it dies.*"

**Aftermath**: Ferrok stood up and vomited poison. Savatt offered to align his chi; Ferrok asked if he could try exploding instead — "*I knew a guy that did this once, and it really worked well*" — a Bael reference that landed. Savatt, deadpan: "*I could try blocking your meridian so your chi explodes, but I don't think that's healthy.*"

### Encounter 3 — Irorian Ghost Monks ×4 (3 rounds)

**Initiative**: Sovael → Savatt → Ghost Monk B → Ferrok → Ghost Monk C → Clutch → Ghost Monk A → Ghost Monk D

**Incorporeal undead**, risen from the sand of the training yard beneath the memorial pagoda. Kit: **Flurry of Blows**, **Frightful Moan** (30-ft mental AoE), and **Telekinetic Assault** (an emanation of hurled objects — the DM ruled non-magical gear took no damage from it).

- **Sovael opened with Benediction and a plain Recall Knowledge** — not Whispers of Weakness, so no Cursebound cost — establishing **incorporeal undead, best save Will, worst Fortitude**. That single check set up the whole fight.
- **He then asked the sharp question**: could RK tell him whether they had reactions? The DM ruled they'd had chances and used none, so he could assume not — and **Sovael repositioned freely on that information** and dropped a **Divine Wrath**. The chain is the tidiest piece of play in the session: worst save Fortitude → Divine Wrath is a **Fortitude-save, spirit-damage, enemies-only burst**, a triple match against incorporeal undead needing no ally saves. It **destroyed Ghost Monk C** outright. (Sovael, checking mid-cast: "*Can undead be sickened?*" DM: "*Amazingly, yes.*" At least one survivor was.)
- **Savatt** switched his conjured bow to **spirit damage** to bypass incorporeality — the bow "*glowed and shimmered, a white coloration this time*" — then **Hunt Prey**'d **Monk C** and later crit **Monk D**.
- **Ferrok** applied **Ghost Oil** to his weapon — a level-4 consumable he'd been carrying unused — and went to work.
- **Clutch's Black Pearl couldn't reflect Frightful Moan.** He checked the wording carefully — "*It says you're **targeted** by its mental effect*" — and the DM confirmed "*It's everything*," an emanation rather than a targeted effect. Correct read, no trigger.
- **Clutch** cycled taunts and Defensive Advances, landing a **jaw strike crit** (noting his jaws have the bigger die but his claws have deadly). **Monk D disappears from the initiative immediately afterward** — the kill is almost certainly his, though the DM never called it.
- **Ferrok killed Monk B outright** ("*B is down*"), then **finished Monk A** with an **Ignition Spellstrike** boosted by Savatt's Guidance: "*This thing evaporates into red mist and is gone.*"

**Aftermath**: Savatt healed Ferrok and asked why an Irori temple would be haunted. The DM confirmed **signs of past violence** — scattered weapons as the mark of a battle, not a ritual. Sovael: "*Were there monks massacred here?*" Savatt read the pagoda's carved names, recognized none, and learned from the DM that it was a memorial for all who served the temple, not only its battle dead.

### Encounter 4 — Catoblepas (3 rounds)

**Initiative**: Ferrok → Catoblepas → Clutch → Savatt → Sovael

A large skeletal-headed beast festering in the pond at the atrium's center, its corruption fouling the water. The DM ruled the pond **difficult terrain**, deep enough to require Swimming more than one square in — which is why Clutch had to **Leap** to engage. **No weaknesses, lowest save Reflex** (Sovael, Whispers of Weakness — his second Cursebound stack of the session).

- **Ferrok** strode in and opened with a flaming **Spellstrike** for **45**.
- The catoblepas answered with an antler strike (40 into Ferrok), then a **60-ft poison breath cone** that caught the party — **Sovael 42, Savatt 42, Ferrok 21**, with at least two of them sickened.
- **Clutch** ran **Shielding Taunt**, leapt down, and hit it with a **Snagging Strike** to make it off-guard. He later attempted an agile **Combat Grab** at full MAP; the outcome was never adjudicated on tape.
- **Savatt** Hunt Preyed, missed both **Hunted Shot** arrows, and used **Battle Medicine**.
- **Sovael** kept **Courageous Anthem** running and threw a **Fire Ray** that missed — his only whiffed offensive turn of the night. He still took his own Cursebound persistent fire for it. **Ferrok was healed for 55** mid-fight.
- **Ferrok's Round 2** was a **Force Fang** into a **Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike** — the DM's only quantitative remark all fight was "*not great damage.*" The catoblepas then **failed its poison-breath recharge**.
- **The catoblepas critted Ferrok** with an antler strike and kept hammering. **Clutch Intercepted the jaws strike**; Ferrok **Emergency Targe'd** the follow-up antler.
- **Ferrok's normal sword strike would have killed it** — but it used its reaction for **Ferocity**, dropping to **1 HP** and wounded. An intervening swing went unresolved, and he finished it with a **Force Fang**, which automatically deals its damage: "*Down into the water.*"

**Aftermath**: The water began to self-purify, though the DM noted it will take days to fully clear. Savatt patched the party up with Battle Medicine — "*I strike you in the wounds that the monster made and stop the bleeding*" — and asked the room: "*How many times can you tangle up your meridians in one day, people?*" Ferrok refocused.

### Encounter 5 — Sand Monks ×6

**Initiative**: Savatt → Sand Monk D → SM F → SM E → Sovael → SM C → SM A → Clutch → Ferrok → SM B

Six monk-shaped **elementals** rising from piles of coloured sand, guarding the first silver feather. **Best save Fortitude, worst Reflex** (Savatt's Recall Knowledge). Their tricks: **burrowing** to reposition beneath the party, **blinding sand** on a critical hit (blinded 1 round), and — the fight's real problem — **Glass Armor**: the monks sheathe themselves in glass, gaining **AC +2 and resistance to acid, cold, electricity, fire, force, piercing, and slashing**. Crucially, **electricity is what set it off.**

- **Savatt** opened with a **crit** on Sand Monk A (64), self-correcting his damage type from spirit to piercing mid-roll.
- **Sand Monk D burrowed twice** to surface behind **Sovael** and crit him for **38** — **blinding him for a round.**

### Encounter 6 — Kannitri, the Irorian Ghost

**Initiative**: Savatt → Sovael → Ferrok → Clutch → Kannitri

The ghost of an Irorian monk woman, seated beside a skeleton under a eucalyptus tree. She spoke before she fought — apologizing for a failure centuries old, naming "**the broken Rebus**" as the ones who came and showed no mercy — then mistook the party for those raiders. Kit: **Flurry of Blows**, a three-action **One Inch Punch** (with a **4d6 void** rider), **Corrupting Gaze** (Will save → **stupefied 1 minute**), **Draining Touch** (considered and discarded), and Demoralize. **No weaknesses, weakest save Fortitude.**

- **Savatt attempted a peaceful resolution first.** He **delayed his turn** so Sovael could act first, then spent a Recall Knowledge checking whether she'd respond to his **Irorian holy symbol** — "*maybe we can resolve this without destroying her.*" The DM ruled it changed nothing. He was trying to spare one of his own faith's dead, and the game did not offer him the option.
- **Sovael led with Lingering Composition**, then pulled a **rank-5 Scroll of Synesthesia** from his Retrieval Belt and cast it. **She succeeded on the save** — which, importantly, grants the *same three effects* as a failure and differs only in **duration: one round instead of one minute.** The log confirms all of it fired: **Dazzled**, **Clumsy 3**, and **every party member Concealed to her at Flat Check DC 5.**

> **The window, turn by turn.** Four turns fell inside it. **Ferrok spent his entire turn on Haste and Arcane Cascade — "that's all my actions" — and made no attack at all.** **Clutch** taunted, ran Defensive Advance and swung, and missed. **Kannitri's own three-action One Inch Punch missed** — the DM said outright it was *because she was clumsy*. **Savatt** wasted one action on a redundant Recall Knowledge the DM shut down ("*you don't have a lot more to learn here*") but then **landed both Hunted Shot arrows for 13 and 17.** So three of the four turns used the window; Ferrok's was the one that didn't. By [214:09] it had expired.

- **Sovael's Whispers of Weakness** identified **Fortitude** as her weakest save, and he immediately followed with **Spirit Blast** — 16d6 spirit, basic Fortitude, DC 31. The sequencing was his sharpest play of the fight; the dice were not. **She succeeded on the save *and* he rolled 34 on 16d6** (average 56), so it landed for only **17**.
- **Ferrok** opened his real offense with a **Blink Charge** teleport-strike (22), then cycled Spellstrikes — including a **Disintegrate Spellstrike** that missed. He finished the night **5 attacks missed** and out of hero points: "*I'm not having luck. Unfortunate rolls today.*"
- **Clutch** tried repeatedly to **trip** her and failed, then went back for more on principle: "**Must go for Redemption.**" His **Astral rune** let him **Combat Grab a ghost**. He correctly *declined* one Intercept after working out that moving would break his grab — **but he did make another, eating 32 for Savatt**, and took 60 more before the fight ended.
- **Kannitri's Corrupting Gaze stupefied Savatt for a full minute**, breaking his spellcasting for the rest of the fight — he failed flat check after flat check trying to cast Shield. He stayed on his bow and kept hitting, including a **nat 20 for 28**. His in-character read on it is the best line of the session: "*I keep trying to pray, but I fumble my words every time.*"
- **Sovael kept the party standing** — **Ferrok took 101 through a 7-point shield block**, and Sovael answered with a **rank-5 Heal for 67**. His **Demoralize (Intimidating Glare) failed.** Late on he burned **Cursebound once more** for another Whispers of Weakness, then landed a **Telekinetic Projectile for 16** — "*Hey, finally.*"
- **Ferrok stepped forward to flank and swung** for the last **20**: "*They are destroyed.*" She faded with "*this horribly sad expression on her face, this guilt-ridden look of anguish.*"

**Aftermath**: The DM noted **her presence is still in the room. "She's gone, but not completely."** The skeleton at the tree's base remains unexamined. The party claimed the **third silver feather** and the session ended.

## Character Spotlight

### Sovael · the Burning Pearl
The team's only healer, only buffer, only debuffer, only counteract, and its Recall Knowledge lane — and he ran all of it at once, all session, while on fire. **Every Whispers of Weakness costs him a Cursebound stack and a point of persistent fire per round**, and he took that trade **five times** across six fights, plus a sixth stack from Foretell Harm in the spar. That's the shape of his session: a Tiny dragonet hovering over the scrum, setting himself alight to tell everyone else where to hit.

- **He is the reason the ghost monk fight was clean.** A plain Recall Knowledge established worst-save Fortitude; he then asked whether RK could reveal *reactions*, got a ruling that they had none, repositioned freely on that information, and dropped a **Divine Wrath** — Fortitude-targeting, spirit damage, enemies-only — that destroyed Monk C outright. Identify, match the save, pick the ally-safe burst. Textbook.
- **His best mechanical read was recognising a dead end.** Against the Goliath Spider, Whispers revealed *Will* as the weak save — which meant his Shadow Signet, which converts to Fort or Reflex, was useless. He took the **+2 status bonus** from Whispers itself and landed **Fire Ray** anyway.
- **He converted a self-inflicted burn straight into a kill** against the sand monks — taking Cursebound purely for the attack bonus, not the information ("*I just need that bonus*"), then critting: "*Double that. Shatters in your glass.*"
- **He kept Ferrok alive twice** — a rank-3 Heal off the floor mid-spider-fight, and a rank-5 Heal for **67** after Ferrok ate 101 through a shield block.
- **In character he is a delight and slightly a menace** — a devotee who came to meet Hao Jin, a cousin who introduced Tivael to the table purely to rank himself above him ("*Not as skilled as me, of course*" / "*Such a disappointment*"), a tiny dragon explaining his Elf Step with "*I had a lot of elf best friends growing up*," and the party's designated straight man to Clutch: "*Maybe not that.*"

### Ferrok the Unyielding · the Load-Bearing Minotaur
The only survivor of the old campaign, and the party's lightning rod exactly as he always was. **Everything in the temple went for him**: the spiders put him unconscious and dying, the catoblepas critted him, the sand monks blinded him, and Kannitri hit him for 101 through a shield block. He is also the session's kill leader — **Takatorra in the spar, Goliath Spider B, Ghost Monks A and B, the catoblepas, at least one sand monk while blind, and Kannitri.**

- **He kept fighting from the floor.** Unconscious and dying 3 from spider venom, he came back up and killed Spider B with a **crit from prone** — "*you just spear this thing from underneath.*"
- **He fought an entire stretch blind**, asking the rules, eating flat checks on every swing, connecting anyway, and then casting Glass Shield because "*I have no idea what's going on around me.*"
- **His most knowingly reckless call paid off anyway.** Chain Lightning into a room of sand monks — an *electricity* spell that triggers their electricity-resistant Glass Armor. He flagged the risk out loud first ("*this seems like a bad idea now, but I really want to use this spell*"), then hit all six for **247 total** and handed the survivors resistance in the same breath. Verdict: "*I was just happy to use it.*"
- **He carries the campaign's memory** — the only one who can speak to the Ebon Tower, the only one Yabin the Just wanted to interrogate about it, and the one who told Clutch, without quite realising what he was saying, that the only Kholo he'd ever seen was a dead one in that tower.
- **He was the most gracious person at the table** about Alastor's exit — "*low rolls are part of the game, it happens*" — and then wrote the eulogy anyway: "*We can assume the crabs ate him on the beach then, right?*"

### Savatt · the Pilgrim Come Home
A wanderer from Janja who is not famous, has never competed, and reads his invitation as a sign pointing toward enlightenment — and then the tournament quartered him in **a ruined temple to his own god**. That coincidence is the session's quietest and best arc, and Savatt played every beat of it.

- **He greeted Tino as a fellow Irorian** before a punch was thrown — a bow and "*I see you're a follower as well*" — the first real connection anyone on either team made.
- **He wanted to clean the temple, not loot it.** Asked why an Irori temple would be haunted, read the hundreds of names on the memorial pagoda looking for ones he knew, and asked whether monks had been massacred here.
- **He recited the sutra back at the trap.** A spectral monk filled the prayer room with kinetic energy and vanished; Savatt's answer was "*One must know the teachings of Irori.*"
- **He tried to spare Kannitri.** He delayed his turn and spent an action checking whether she would respond to his **holy symbol** — "*maybe we can resolve this without destroying her*" — and the game gave him nothing. He then had to help kill one of his own faith's dead.
- **Mechanically he was the party's second healer and its most reliable gun** — Gravity Weapon, Hunted Shot doubles, healer's gloves, Battle Medicine, Guidance, and a bow he re-tuned to spirit damage on the spot to hurt incorporeal monks. When Kannitri stupefied him for a full minute and broke his spellcasting, he shrugged and kept shooting, landing a nat 20 for 28.
- **His register is entirely his own** — chi, meridians, and acupuncture as combat medicine. "*Do not worry, I will align your chi.*" "*I could try blocking your meridian so your chi explodes, but I don't think that's healthy.*" "*How many times can you tangle up your meridians in one day, people?*"

### Clutch · Ten Years Later
Came to this island for two reasons: to win, and to find **Clinch** — his cousin, who competed on a *different* team ten years ago, and who both of them lost. Everything funny about Clutch this session is sitting directly on top of that.

- **He is the reason the party had a front line.** Group Taunt and Shielding Taunt on three enemies at a time, Snagging Strikes generating off-guard for everyone else, Combat Grabs on a spider, a ghost, and a Kitsune — and Intercept after Intercept, including a 72-damage nat-20 bite he chose to eat rather than break his shield, and a 32-point hit he took for Savatt.
- **His best mechanical moment was rules mastery, not damage** — reading Black Pearl's wording closely enough to know it wouldn't reflect Frightful Moan (an emanation, not a targeted effect), and catching that Shielding Taunt and Doctor's Visitation are both flourishes.
- **He reflected Phantasmal Killer back at Yabin the Just.** He failed the save himself — damage and frightened 2 — but the Black Pearl's +30 counteract sent a copy back, and *Yabin failed his own spell*. A 2,000 gp consumable, spent to make an enemy caster share his condition.
- **The Ring of Climbing bit is the session's best sustained joke and its saddest.** Offered the ring, he turned it down on behalf of a dead man — "*Clinch can't use it just yet, but maybe someday in the future*" — and when the table pushed back, doubled down: "*Not with that attitude. He's pretty determined, you never know.*" Then: "*He's got to climb out of his grave, you know, so he needs the climb speed.*"
- **And after losing the spar, the mask came off entirely**: "*I thought after 10 years I'd gotten stronger, but it seems I still need to keep growing.*" That's the whole character in one sentence.

### Alastor · One More Shot
Present for a little over an hour. A finalist from last decade's tournament, past his peak and estranged from his old team, given a place with strangers on the strength of an old result.

- **He got the session's first good beat** — Battle Cry on the initiative roll, a Demoralize on Tino delivered as "*a very accurate wolf howl*," and the DM narrating Tino as genuinely rattled before he shook it off.
- **He spent the encounter's only hero point**, on a Combat Grab into Clinging Shadows Stance that landed and held Tino.
- **Tino's second Inner Upheaval hit him for 105** and dropped him. He left the call, Foundry, and the DM's Discord shortly after. The DM confirmed after the break that he will not be returning.

### What Sovael is thinking about

- **Synesthesia's success/failure split is about duration, not effects.** This is the correction worth internalising: a *success* still applies **Dazzled, Clumsy 3, and the concealment rider** (everything Concealed to her, flat check DC 5) — it just lasts **one round instead of one minute**. So the scroll wasn't wasted on a weak outcome; it bought a full-strength round. The lesson is about the *clock*, not the effect.
- **The window was mostly used, and the one miss was Ferrok's.** Three of the four turns inside it did something: Clutch swung (missed), Kannitri's One Inch Punch **missed because of the Clumsy**, and Savatt landed both arrows for 13 and 17. **Ferrok spent the entire round on Haste and Arcane Cascade and never attacked.** That's the actual complaint — and it's a communication problem, not a Ferrok problem. **When a consumable buys a window, say out loud what it bought and how long it lasts before the next person acts.** This party has never fought together before and cannot yet read each other's setups.
- **Whispers of Weakness cost six Cursebound stacks this session** (five Whispers + one Foretell Harm), each one a point of persistent fire per round. It does **not** compete for the focus pool — it's a Cursebound action, not a focus spell — so the real constraint is HP and the fire-resistance suppression, not actions. At 151 HP that's still affordable, but six is a lot more than it felt like in the moment. **Refocusing between rooms is for Lingering Composition and Fire Ray; the Cursebound bill is separate and paid in blood.**
- **Whispers is worth taking for the +2 alone.** The sand monk kill proves it: no useful information, just the attack bonus, straight into a crit. That reframes it from an RK action to an attack buff that happens to also scout.
- **Divine Wrath is the best spell in the book for this party.** Enemies-only means no friendly-fire discipline with four melee bodies in the room. It killed a ghost monk outright and needed no ally saves. Lead with it over Fireball once the party has closed.
- **Match the spell to the revealed save, and check the item actually helps.** The spider taught this the hard way: Whispers said *Will*, and the Shadow Signet only converts to Fort or Reflex — so the Signet did nothing and the **+2 status bonus** was the real payload. Against Kannitri it went right: Fortitude revealed → Spirit Blast (Fortitude) one action later.
- **The sole-healer problem is real but survivable.** Savatt is a genuine second healer (healer's gloves, Battle Medicine, Guidance) and Ferrok has Battle Medicine too. Better coverage than the dynamics predicted. **Ask whether Seva brings any.**
- **Ferrok is the aggro magnet exactly as before** — down once, 101 through a shield block, blinded, critted, poisoned. Beseech the Sphinx and Vital Beacon should default to him.
- **Being blinded by a sand monk crit was a warning.** A Tiny caster hovering near the scrum still eats crits. Permanent flight and Elf Step exist; use them earlier.
