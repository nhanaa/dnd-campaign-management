The "tri-ultimate" session of Shades of Blood picked up exactly where the last cliffhung: Ferrok's kicked-in door had woken a dormitory of Tokainen's Chosen, and the party — minus Raylta, whose player hadn't arrived yet — fought them prone, unarmored, and weaponless. Bael opened with a Solar Detonation that blinded the room, Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit for 64, and four PCs cleared the dorm before the fifth even logged in. Raylta arrived to a pile of corpses: "Oh awesome, I came right on time."

Deeper into the floor, the party crashed a vampire's salon — two preening lords, Ilnaphrae and Xarbaene, lounging with a piano, a painting, and a court of enslaved human thralls. Bargrum traded insults ("the only garbage I see here is you"), and the fight turned on Tivael's rank-1 Fear landing a natural 1 on Xarbaene — Frightened 3 and fleeing. Ferrok cut down Ilnaphrae; Raylta softened the fleeing Xarbaene with a silver Moonbeam; and Bael finished him in the hallway with a flaming bow conjured "from the remaining fire." Mid-fight the party realized the thralls were enslaved humans, not undead — Raylta's mortified "Oh, I thought they were undead. Oh, my bad" — and they spared the survivors.

The middle was exploration: a clockwork prisoner-records archive (they looked up Nizca Iricold, the non-binary Strigoi mastermind, and confirmed via Augury that her at-large human lover Lagrassi is still alive), a scripture book of the vampire god Nin, a captive engineer Aruneth (and his fox familiar Kikiri), and a put-upon kitchen staff the party kept accidentally menacing. Aruneth named the floor boss — the Warden Tokainen, a meteor-hammer fighter-caster with a ghostly bear guardian. The back half was two grinding fights: four crit-immune globsters plus two ogres (Hurkum and Ogenni), then — as the party tried to evacuate freed prisoners — a second wave of seven Tokainen's Chosen that nearly broke them. Ferrok went down repeatedly, Raylta hit Cursebound 2 and danced on death's edge, and Tivael summoned a unicorn as an emergency heal-bot. They won, armed the freed prisoners with looted guisarmes, and sprinted for safety. The Warden still waits.

Timeline

Act I · Combat · The Dormitory

Tokainen's Chosen, Asleep (4 PCs)

  • Direct continuation of the S2 cliffhanger. The DM ruled the woken Chosen start prone, without armor, without weapons, at −4 initiative — the party got a strong jump.
  • Bargrum tried to defuse it, "ruling diplomacy for initiative" — "don't worry about it, just go back to sleep," then "Oops, sorry about that, clumsy me." It didn't take.
  • Bael opened with Solar Detonation — the "small bead above the finger" that swelled into a room-filling blast. One Chosen crit-failed (blinded a minute); the rest were dazzled or blinded (treating the floor as difficult terrain).
  • Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit for 64 on Chosen F and 50 on Chosen B (force), on top of Courageous Anthem + Lingering, a Telekinetic Projectile, Electric Arc, and silver Needle Darts. On a kill: "Now they're going to sleep forever. Never wake up again."
  • The Chosen still popped Cataclysm Brand repeatedly (one threw it blind), but Bargrum's Energy Interceptor ate the fire for Bael, and Ferrok's Gouging Claw Spellstrikes mopped up.
  • Bargrum dropped the final Chosen: "Down he goes… that was probably all my luck for the session, so it's up to you guys now."
  • Raylta's player arrived right after — "Hello. Oh, welcome." / Raylta: "Oh awesome, I came right on time… sorry guys." / Tivael, reassuring: "No disaster has happened."
Act II · Combat · The Vampires' Court

Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene + Favored Thralls

  • A furnished salon: grand piano, harp, fine art. Two vampires — one playing piano while a human "danced," one drinking blood, one painting — plus human Favored Thralls. "Look what delightful morsels visit our court, Ilnaphrae…"
  • Bargrum's banter: "First of all, I am more of a sour, less sweet. Second, we're here to clear," and "you're speaking as if our lives are garbage, but the only garbage I see here is you." Ferrok: "the dead should stay dead. That's why we're here."
  • Ilnaphrae produced a repeating crossbow (5 shots before reload, 120-ft range) and targeted the squishy Tivael; Bargrum intercepted.
  • Tivael's setup crit: Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition — crit success at DC 24 → 4-round party buff. Ferrok self-Hasted into Arcane Cascade.
  • Xarbaene turned to mist and floated to the backline; thralls buffed by Lord's Blessing swarmed Bargrum and Tivael.
  • THE moment: after a Biting Words round (hero-pointed the reroll — "I'll [use a] hero point. I hate this."), Tivael cast a rank-1 Fear on Xarbaene → natural 1, critical failure: Frightened 3 + fleeing. "It's a crit fail?! It's fleeing!" — Raylta, incredulous: "At rank 1?"
  • Ferrok killed Ilnaphrae — Force Fang to recharge, then an Ignition Spellstrike (rerolled the miss): "She is down."
  • The chase on Xarbaene: Raylta had already tagged him with a silver Moonlight Ray (56) in the salon; now she shot the fleeing vampire with a Moonbeam (18) over Bargrum's head — "Flee, coward. Oh, the provoke is so good" / "I do a finger gun and I just shoot right above Bargrum to get to him." Bael finished him: "He will raise up his arms, conjure a flaming bow, and using the remaining fire in the hallway, shoot him down."
  • The thrall reckoning: the DM flagged the thralls as enslaved, not undead. Raylta, horrified — "Oh, I thought they were undead. Oh, my bad" — and the party stopped, Bargrum administering first aid to spare the survivors.
Act III · Explore · The Archive & the God Nin

Prisoner Records, Augury, and a Vampire God's Doctrine

  • Clockwork records archive: a wall of clockwork filing cabinets. Tivael (Crafting) and Ferrok (Society) both succeeded (Ferrok: "I'll do society so I can feel like I'm helping" — "Hey, there we go, you ARE helping!") and realized it's a prisoner-records system keyed to per-prisoner ciphers.
  • Nizca → Nizca Iricold, non-binary Strigoi vampire. Methodical, obsessive, aloof. Known accomplice: their lover Lagrassi, a male human — still at large. Zatrine and Nalushae were also looked up; the current vampires were not in the records (followers, not former prisoners).
  • An exploding door (Furious Explosion trap, 5d10 fire) caught the whole party when Bargrum tried a key; Bargrum intercepted for Raylta. Ferrok, deadpan: "We're used to getting exploded on at this point." (The key worked fine — "it's just a prank.")
  • The book of Nin ("The Immaculate Solution"): scripture for the vampire god Nin, whose symbol is made of silver. The doctrine — overcoming vampire weaknesses through "logic, experimentation, and determination." Bael: "if you want to be resistant to fire, you gotta burn yourself every day. That's how I am."
  • Augury (new Aeon Stone): "Is Lagrassi still alive?" → Yes. Nizca's obsession isn't delusional — the lover lives, somewhere outside the tower.
  • The garbage room: a tarp-covered chest triggered a Stinkface Surprise trap; Tivael saved. Inside: a haul of Aeon Stones (a Sprouting Aeon Stone, +8 temp HP on vitality heal → Bargrum), a Glittering Mask, and a mundane bracelet.
Act IV · Social · The Captive Engineer & the Kitchen

Kikiri, Aruneth, and the Cooks They Kept Menacing

  • Kikiri: a little fox/snake familiar found tidying a captive's room, who begged the party to save its master and pointed them to the northern workshop.
  • Aruneth, the captive engineer: forced to maintain the tower's clockwork soldiers, guarded by a multi-armed construct. After the party's clumsy "we are your saviors" routine, he agreed to be freed once the Warden is dead.
  • The intel: the floor boss is the Warden, Tokainen — a fighter-caster wielding a meteor hammer, with a guardian: an ethereal, ghostly bear. Currently praying at the giant Nin statue in the north room. Clockwork soldiers are weak to electricity (or bypassed by adamantine). Degg is on the top floor with Nizca, never leaves.
  • The kitchen: a human head chef berating two sous-chefs — not enemies, just prison staff. The party's running gag of menacing the people they're saving peaked here: demanding "a slow-cooked brisket" and "beef bourguignon," warning of "loud noises." DM: "I'm loving the pattern of you all just being antagonistic toward the people you're trying to save."
Act V · Combat · The Globster Slog

4 Globsters + Hurkum & Ogenni

  • Passing a glass-less magical aquarium, four globster oozes slithered out — immune to critical hits (but taking crit riders like persistent fire), weak to electricity (discovered late), with grabbing tendrils and Nauseating Slap (stacking sickened), and enormous HP.
  • Round-2 reinforcements: two ogres, Hurkum and Ogenni, the prisoners' guards. "How'd they get out? We was watching them the whole time." Ferrok: "I'd rather fight them now than behind us when we fight the boss." Tivael RK'd the ogres: best Fortitude, worst Reflex.
  • Tivael's first Wooden Double: Hurkum's crit triggered the reaction — the wooden block ate the ~57-damage hit (+25 HP absorbed) and Tivael stepped clear. "Never used that spell."
  • A 6-round grind — "bags of hit points that live to go forward and attack." Bargrum tanked and Boulder-Rolled, Bael chained Solar Detonations + Flying Flame birdies, Ferrok burned them with Ignition Spellstrikes (still igniting despite crit-immunity), Raylta nudged everyone topped up.
  • Tivael's Electric Arc exposed the electricity weakness ("big damage!"); Bael pivoted to lightning. Ogenni fell to Bargrum's strikes; Hurkum to Bael's blasts; the last globster finally dropped to Ferrok — Tivael: "Bring us home, Ferrok" / "It's down. Oh, finally. Finally. Oh my god."
  • Afterward the party freed 7–8 abducted prisoners (sailors, not Aslanti), sending them out single-file. Call-center jokes: "has everyone been meeting their KPIs?"
Act VI · Combat · The Second Wave

7 Tokainen's Chosen (the near-TPK)

  • As the party tried to evacuate the prisoners and retreat to rest, a fresh squad of seven Chosen intercepted. "Stop right there!" — brands heating up — DM (Chosen): "Kill the dwarf first." Bargrum: "from where we're standing, it seems more like you're the problem."
  • The hardest fight of the session. Seven Cataclysm Brands meant relentless fire AoE (DM: "We're a month out from 4th of July. Let's get some fireworks going."), plus Take 'Em Down trip-strikes that kept PCs prone.
  • Ferrok's collapse-and-rise loop: tripped → stabbed → dropped, repeatedly. Kept alive by Raylta's Heals, Bael's Healer's Gloves, and his own grit. Clutch: Sure Strike + Briny Bolt Spellstrike = "one shot, one kill" on Chosen F.
  • Raylta on the edge: Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2, repeatedly one hit from death, she stepped three times out of reach into Bargrum's intercept umbrella. "I trust my team. Three times. I've never done that before."
  • Tivael summoned a unicorn (Summon Fey) as a heal-bot — it cast a rank-3 Heal on Bael, then died tanking. Raylta: "You know it's bad when we summon the unicorn." Tivael also dropped Biting Words for 62 on Chosen A, a Flurry of Claws on B and C, Hidebound on himself, and a string of Demoralizes.
  • Bael's clutch kill: a Flying Flame birdie "through his skull" finished Chosen G. Bargrum doesn't die — the immovable anchor — DM's words exactly.
  • All seven ground out (Tivael: "We're suffering, guys"). Loot: 21× +1 Bola + 21× +1 Guisarme — handed to the freed prisoners (the "we build an army" bit). Party sprinted to safety to rest. Session called.

Combat Encounters

Encounter 1 · Tokainen's Chosen Dormitory · 4 PCs · ~5 rounds

Setup

Direct continuation of the S2 cliffhanger. Six Chosen woke prone, unarmored, weaponless, at −4 initiative. Initiative: Chosen F, Bargrum, Tivael, Bael, Chosen B/D, Ferrok, Chosen C/A/E. Raylta's player absent — joined after.

Round 1

  • Chosen F stood, grabbed a guisarme, strode in. Bargrum Shielding-Taunted the three nearest. Tivael cast Courageous Anthem + Lingering (3 rounds) + Telekinetic Projectile (15).
  • Bael's Solar Detonation filled the room — one crit-fail (blinded 1 min), the rest dazzled/blinded. Blinded enemies treated the floor as difficult terrain. Ferrok stepped through the doorway (a reactive strike missed due to dazzle).

Rounds 2–5

  • Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit — 64 on Chosen F (32 × 2) and 50 on Chosen B (force). Then Electric Arc on A+B, Demoralize, and Needle Darts.
  • The Chosen popped Cataclysm Brand repeatedly (one blind, splitting the burst). Bargrum's Energy Interceptor ate the fire for Bael; Defensive Advance strikes ground the line.
  • Ferrok Gouging Claw Spellstrikes finished the cluster. Bael Thermal Nimbus + blasts + a flaming birdie. Bargrum dropped the last Chosen.

Outcome

Six Chosen dead, no PC seriously hurt. Loot: 7× +1 Guisarme, 7× +1 Bola, locker supplies (Potion of Leaping, Salve of Antiparalysis, Moderate Healing Potions, an ancient golden necklace). Raylta arrived immediately after.

Encounter 2 · Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene + Favored Thralls · 5 PCs · 4 rounds

Setup

Two vampire lords + several human Favored Thralls in the salon. Initiative: Ilnaphrae, Tivael, Ferrok, Bargrum, Raylta, Bael, Xarbaene, Thrall B/A/C.

Round 1

  • Ilnaphrae fired her repeating crossbow at squishy Tivael; Bargrum intercepted (and again on the second shot's target). Tivael Courageous Anthem + Lingering — crit success (DC 24), 4 rounds — then Shield. Ferrok self-Hasted + Arcane Cascade. Raylta held back and cast Benediction.

Round 2

  • Bael Solar Detonation on the thrall cluster (48 to Thrall A, 12 to B, splash to Ilnaphrae). Xarbaene feinted Bargrum, struck, then Turned to Mist and floated to the backline. Thralls (Lord's Blessing) swarmed Bargrum and Tivael.

Round 3 — the swing

  • Tivael Biting Words (hero-point reroll), then a rank-1 Fear on Xarbaene → natural 1, crit fail → Frightened 3 + fleeing. Raylta had already tagged Xarbaene with a silver Moonlight Ray (56) in the salon. Ferrok Force Fang → Ignition Spellstrike (rerolled) killed Ilnaphrae. Bael demoralized and blasted thralls down.

Round 4 — the chase

  • Xarbaene fled, provoking. Raylta chased with a silver Moonbeam (18) over Bargrum (the finger-gun shot); Tivael Telekinetic Projectile killed a thrall; Ferrok beheaded another ("Off with his head"). Bael killed the fleeing Xarbaene with a conjured flaming bow.

Outcome

Both vampires destroyed (silver + burned). The party stopped killing thralls on realizing they were enslaved, and first-aided the survivors. Loot: two Resilient Leather Armors, +1 Striking Frost Rapier, +1 Striking Shock Rapier, two +1 Repeating Crossbows, an Agate Ellipsoid Aeon Stone (Augury) and a Preserving Aeon Stone (Raylta), a Potion of Quickness, gold, and art objects.

Encounter 3 · 4 Globsters + Hurkum & Ogenni · 5 PCs · 6 rounds

Setup

By the magical aquarium. Four globsters round 1; the ogres Hurkum and Ogenni joined round 2. Initiative: Hurkum (R2), Ogenni (R2), Globster C/A, Bael, Bargrum, Globster D/B (Tivael, Ferrok, Raylta also in order).

Globster profile

Immune to crits (still take crit riders — persistent fire/bleed), grab tendrils, Nauseating Slap (stacking sickened), very high HP, weak to electricity (discovered round ~5). Mindless — mental debuffs are wasted on them.

The fight

  • A globster grabbed/sickened Raylta. Bael Solar Detonations + Flying Flame birdies. Bargrum Shielding-Taunted and Boulder-Roll tripped. Tivael RK'd the ogres (best Fort / worst Reflex), then leaned into Electric Arc once the globsters' electricity weakness surfaced ("That's why you recall knowledge, kids").
  • Tivael's first Wooden Double absorbed Hurkum's crit (+25 HP block, step away). Both ogres had reach + reactive strike, and Ogenni chained Sweeping Hook + Punting Kick.
  • Ferrok Ignition Spellstrikes (igniting despite crit-immunity); Bael pivoted to lightning; Raylta nudged everyone topped up.

Outcome

Ogenni dropped to Bargrum's strikes after heavy debuffs; Hurkum to Bael's blasts; the final globster fell to Ferrok ("It's down. Oh, finally.") at round 6. Party badly attrited; freed 7–8 abducted prisoners afterward.

Encounter 4 · 7 Tokainen's Chosen, Second Wave · 5 PCs · the near-TPK

Setup

The party tried to evacuate prisoners and retreat; a second squad of seven Chosen intercepted. "Kill the dwarf first." Initiative: Chosen B/E, Raylta, Ferrok, Chosen G, Tivael, Bael, Chosen C/A/D/F.

The threat pattern

Seven Cataclysm Brands = relentless fire AoE bunched on whoever clustered ("healing provokes aggro"), plus Take 'Em Down trip-strikes that kept PCs prone. The session's near-death fight.

The fight

  • Ferrok tripped → stabbed → dropped, repeatedly; stayed up via Raylta's Heals, Bael's Healer's Gloves, and Vital Beacon supplication. Clutch: Sure Strike + Briny Bolt Spellstrike = "one shot, one kill" on Chosen F (42 weapon + 60 Briny Bolt).
  • Raylta, Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2, one hit from death repeatedly, stepped three times out of reach into Bargrum's intercept range ("I trust my team"); burned Heals, Nudges, Healer's Gloves, a Potion Patch.
  • Tivael: Biting Words (62 on Chosen A), Flurry of Claws on B + C (Demoralize first), Summon Fey unicorn as a heal-bot (rank-3 Heal on Bael, then tanked a fatal hit), Hidebound on self, repeated Demoralizes.
  • Bael: Solar Detonations (the last mostly whiffed the saves — the "Pass. Pass. Pass…" wall), Flying Flame birdies (killed Chosen G "through his skull"), Demoralize, Healer's Gloves, Treat Wounds, Thermal Nimbus.
  • Bargrum: the immovable anchor — Group/Shielding Taunt to pull the squad, Intercept after Intercept for Raylta, trips, Defensive Advance. "Bargrum doesn't die. We've established this."

Outcome

All seven Chosen killed after a long, brutal slog (Tivael: "We're suffering, guys"). Loot: 21× +1 Bola + 21× +1 Guisarme — handed to the freed prisoners. Party sprinted to safety to rest. Session called.
