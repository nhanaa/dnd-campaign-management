## Session Summary

The "tri-ultimate" session of Shades of Blood picked up exactly where the last one cliffhung: Ferrok's kicked-in door had woken a dormitory of **Tokainen's Chosen**, and the party — minus Raylta, whose player hadn't arrived yet — fought them prone, unarmored, and weaponless. Bael opened with a Solar Detonation that blinded the whole room, Bargrum tried (and failed) to talk them back to sleep, and four PCs cleared the dorm before the fifth even logged in. Raylta arrived to a pile of corpses: "Oh awesome, I came right on time."

Deeper into the floor, the party crashed a vampire's salon — two preening vampire lords, **Ilnaphrae** and **Xarbaene**, lounging with a piano, a painting, and a court of enslaved human thralls. Bargrum traded insults ("the only garbage I see here is you"), and the fight turned on **Tivael's rank-1 Fear landing a natural 1 on Xarbaene — Frightened 3 and fleeing**. Ferrok cut down Ilnaphrae; Raylta softened the fleeing Xarbaene with a silver Moonbeam; and Bael finished him in the hallway with a flaming bow conjured "from the remaining fire." Mid-fight the party realized the thralls were enslaved humans, not undead — Raylta's mortified "Oh, I thought they were undead. Oh, my bad" — and they spared the survivors afterward.

The middle of the session was exploration and worldbuilding: a clockwork prisoner-records archive (they looked up **Nizca Iricold**, the non-binary Strigoi mastermind, and confirmed via Augury that her at-large human lover **Lagrassi** is still alive), a scripture book of the vampire god **Nin**, an engineer named **Aruneth** held captive (and his fox familiar **Kikiri**), and a put-upon human kitchen staff the party kept accidentally menacing. Aruneth named the floor's boss — the Warden **Tokainen**, a meteor-hammer fighter-caster with a ghostly bear guardian. The back half was two grinding fights: a brutal slog against four crit-immune **globsters** plus two ogres (**Hurkum** and **Ogenni**), and then, as the party tried to evacuate freed prisoners and retreat to rest, a second wave of **seven Tokainen's Chosen** that nearly broke them — Ferrok went down repeatedly, Raylta hit Cursebound 2 and danced on the edge of death, and Tivael summoned a unicorn as an emergency heal-bot ("You know it's bad when we summon the unicorn"). They won, armed the freed prisoners with looted guisarmes, and sprinted for safety. The Warden still waits. The DM is now calling **three sessions left** of Shades of Blood before the Ruby Phoenix link goes out.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Dormitory (Tokainen's Chosen, S2 cliffhanger payoff)
- The session resumed on the kicked-open door from last session. The DM ruled the awakened Chosen start **prone, without armor, without weapons, and at −4 initiative** (they'd been asleep), so the party got a strong jump.
- **Bargrum tried to defuse it** — "ruling diplomacy for initiative," he muttered *"don't worry about it, just go back to sleep,"* then *"Oops, sorry about that, clumsy me."* It didn't take.
- **Bael opened with Solar Detonation** — the now-signature "small bead above the finger" that swelled into a room-filling blast. One Chosen crit-failed (blinded a minute); the rest were dazzled or blinded, and blinded enemies treated the floor as difficult terrain.
- **Tivael** led with Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition (three rounds) and a Telekinetic Projectile, then ground the dorm down with Electric Arc, Demoralize, and Needle Darts. On the kill: *"Now they're going to sleep forever. Never wake up again."*
- The Chosen still managed to pop **Cataclysm Brand** repeatedly (one threw it blind), but **Bargrum's Energy Interceptor** ate the fire for Bael, and Ferrok's Gouging Claw Spellstrikes mopped up.
- This was a **4-PC fight** — Raylta's player arrived right after it ended (*"Hello. Oh, welcome."* / Raylta: *"Oh awesome, I came right on time… sorry guys."* / Tivael, reassuring: *"No disaster has happened."*).
- **Loot**: 7× +1 Guisarme, 7× +1 Bola, plus locker supplies — a Potion of Leaping (Raylta took it), a Salve of Antiparalysis (Bael carries it, hands usually free), Moderate Healing Potions (Ferrok and Tivael each took one), and an ancient golden necklace.

### Act II — The Vampires' Court (Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene + Favored Thralls)
- Behind the next door: a furnished salon with a grand piano, a harp, and fine art. **Two vampires** — one playing piano while a human "danced," one drinking from a human's neck, one painting at a canvas — plus human **Favored Thralls** and servants. The vampires monologued: *"Look what delightful morsels visit our court, Ilnaphrae… what desires have drawn you into our illustrious presence?"*
- **Bargrum's banter**: *"First of all, I am more of a sour, less sweet. Second, we're here to clear,"* and *"you're speaking as if our lives are garbage, but the only garbage I see here is you."* Ferrok: *"the dead should stay dead. That's why we're here."* DM (vampire): *"you look like you taste like rocks."*
- **Ilnaphrae** produced a **repeating crossbow** (120-ft range, 5 shots before reload) and targeted the squishy Tivael; **Bargrum intercepted**.
- **Tivael's setup crit**: Courageous Anthem + Lingering Composition landed a **crit success at DC 24 for a 4-round buff.** Then Shield.
- **Ferrok** self-cast Haste and entered Arcane Cascade, then went to work with Force Fang + Ignition Spellstrike.
- **Xarbaene turned to mist** and floated away to harass the backline; the thralls, buffed by **Lord's Blessing** (within 25 ft of a master), swarmed Bargrum and Tivael.
- **THE moment — Tivael's Fear**: after a round of Biting Words (hero-pointed the reroll: *"I'll [use a] hero point. I hate this."*), Tivael cast a **rank-1 Fear on Xarbaene — natural 1, critical failure: Frightened 3 and fleeing.** *"It's a crit fail?! It's fleeing!"* (Raylta, incredulous: *"At rank 1?"*)
- **Ferrok killed Ilnaphrae** — Force Fang to recharge, then an Ignition Spellstrike (rerolled the miss) dropped her: *"She is down."*
- **The chase on Xarbaene**: earlier in the salon, Raylta had already tagged him with a silver **Moonlight Ray (56)**; now, betting on his silver weakness, she shot the fleeing vampire with **Moonbeam (18)** over Bargrum's head — *"Flee, coward. Oh, the provoke is so good"* / *"I do a finger gun and I just shoot right above Bargrum to get to him."* Bael finished him in the hallway: *"He will raise up his arms, conjure a flaming bow, and using the remaining fire in the hallway, shoot him down."*
- **The moral beat**: mid-fight the DM reminded them the thralls weren't willing servants. Raylta, horrified: *"Oh, I thought they were undead. Oh, my bad."* They stopped, and after the fight **Bargrum administered first aid to spare the surviving thralls** ("you can spare them… but they're not going to be in any condition to speak or move").
- The party confirmed the vampires were destroyed for good (silver used; bodies burned to ash).
- **Loot**: from **Xarbaene** — +1 Striking Frost Rapier, +1 Striking Repeating Crossbow, Resilient Leather Armor, an **Aeon Stone (Agate Ellipsoid)** (Augury 1/day), gold. From **Ilnaphrae** — +1 Striking Shock Rapier, +1 Repeating Crossbow, Resilient Leather Armor, a **Preserving Aeon Stone** (Raylta took it for innate Stabilize), gold. Plus art objects and a **Potion of Quickness** (free Haste).

### Act III — The Archive, the God Nin, and the Captive Engineer
- **Clockwork records archive**: behind the servants' quarters, a wall of clockwork filing cabinets. **Tivael (Crafting) and Ferrok (Society) both succeeded** ("I'll do society so I can feel like I'm helping" — *"Hey, there we go, you ARE helping!"*) and realized it's a **prisoner-records system keyed to per-prisoner ciphers**. With a name, they can look anyone up.
  - **Nizca** → *Nizca Iricold, non-binary Strigoi vampire.* Crimes: sedition, insurrection, cult formation, occult rituals, vampirism, murder. Methodical, obsessive, aloof. **Known accomplice: their lover Lagrassi, a male human — still at large.**
  - **Zatrine** (last session's Lamia) → false-cult formation, manipulation, theft.
  - **Nalushae** → *Nalushae Meronis, female vampire* (the Book-2 antagonist). Arrogant, luxurious tastes.
  - The current vampires (Ilnaphrae, Xarbaene) were **not** in the records — likely followers who came with Nizca, not former prisoners.
- **An exploding door** (Furious Explosion trap, 5d10 fire, 15-ft radius) caught the whole party when Bargrum tried a key; **Bargrum intercepted for Raylta**. Ferrok, deadpan: *"We're used to getting exploded on at this point."* (The key worked fine afterward — "it's just a prank.")
- **The book of Nin** ("The Immaculate Solution"): scripture for the vampire god **Nin**, whose symbol is **made of silver**. Raylta's theory, supported by the text: Nin is about **overcoming vampire weaknesses through "logic, experimentation, and determination"** — perfecting vampires past silver/sunlight. Bael: *"if you want to be resistant to fire, you gotta burn yourself every day. That's how I am."*
- **Augury** (from the new Aeon Stone): *"Is Lagrassi still alive?"* → **Yes.** Confirmed Nizca's obsession isn't delusional — the lover lives, somewhere outside the tower.
- **The garbage room**: a chest under a tarp triggered a **Stinkface Surprise** trap; Tivael saved. Inside: a haul of **Aeon Stones**, including a **Sprouting Aeon Stone** (8 temp HP when healed by vitality — given to **Bargrum**, now orbiting up to three stones), a Glittering Mask, and a mundane homemade bracelet.
- **Kikiri**: a little fox/snake **familiar** found tidying a captive's room. It begged the party to save its master and pointed them to the northern workshop.
- **Aruneth**, the captive **engineer**: forced to maintain the tower's clockwork soldiers for the Warden, guarded by a multi-armed construct. After the party's clumsy "we are your saviors" routine landed, Aruneth agreed to be freed once the Warden is dead, and gave intel:
  - The floor's boss is the **Warden, Tokainen** — *"capable both as a fighter and as a caster,"* wields a **meteor hammer**, and keeps a **guardian: an ethereal, ghostly bear.** Currently praying at the giant shaved-head **Nin statue** in the big north room.
  - **Clockwork soldiers**: weak to **electricity**, bypass-able with **adamantine** (none available — Nizca and Degg hoarded it).
  - **Degg** is on the **top floor** with Nizca and never leaves.

### Act IV — The Kitchen (the people they kept menacing)
- After the break, the party cleared side rooms. A dining hall, then an **active kitchen** with a human head chef berating two sous-chefs — **not enemies**, just prison staff.
- The party's running gag of being **accidentally hostile to the people they're trying to save**: demanding food, requesting *"a slow-cooked brisket"* and *"beef bourguignon,"* and warning the chef that *"there's going to be some loud noises — just ignore them."* DM: *"I'm loving the pattern of you all just being antagonistic toward the people you're trying to save."*

### Act V — The Globster Slog (4 Globsters + Hurkum & Ogenni)
- Passing a glass-less **magical aquarium**, **four globster oozes** slithered out. They proved **immune to critical hits** (oozes) but still took **persistent fire/bleed** riders — and, crucially, were **weak to electricity** (discovered late, to general groaning: *"That's why you recall knowledge, kids"*). They had enormous HP and **Nauseating Slap** (stacking sickened) plus grabbing tendrils.
- **Round 2 reinforcements**: two ogres, **Hurkum** (male) and **Ogenni** (female), rounded the corner — the prisoners' guards. *"How'd they get out? We was watching them the whole time."* Ferrok: *"I'd rather fight them now than behind us when we fight the boss."* Tivael RK'd the ogres: **best Fortitude, worst Reflex.**
- **Tivael's first Wooden Double**: Hurkum's crit triggered the reaction — the wooden block ate the blow (a 57-damage hit reduced, +25 HP block) and Tivael stepped away. *"Never used that spell."*
- The fight was a **6-round grind** — the globsters were "bags of hit points that live to go forward and attack." Bargrum tanked and tripped (Boulder Roll), Bael chained Solar Detonations and Flying Flame birdies, Ferrok burned them with Ignition Spellstrikes (crit-immune but still igniting), and Raylta nudged everyone topped up.
- **Ogenni** went down to Bargrum's strikes after stacking debuffs; **Hurkum** fell to Bael's blasts. The last globster finally dropped — Tivael: *"Bring us home, Ferrok"* / *"It's down. Oh, finally. Finally. Oh my god."*
- Afterward the party freed **7–8 abducted prisoners** (sailors, not Aslanti) from the Prisoner Pits, sending them out single-file (call-center jokes: *"has everyone been meeting their KPIs?"*).

### Act VI — The Second Wave (7 Tokainen's Chosen) & Session End
- As the party tried to **evacuate the prisoners and retreat to rest**, a fresh squad of **seven Tokainen's Chosen** intercepted them. *"Stop right there!"* — brands heating up — DM (Chosen): *"Kill the dwarf first."* Bargrum: *"from where we're standing, it seems more like you're the problem."*
- This was the **hardest fight of the session.** Seven brands meant relentless **Cataclysm Brand** AoE (DM: *"We're a month out from 4th of July. Let's get some fireworks going."*) plus **Take 'Em Down** (trip-then-strike, no MAP) that kept knocking PCs prone.
- **Ferrok went down repeatedly** — tripped, stabbed, dropped, stood, dropped again — and was kept alive by Raylta's Heals, Bael's Healer's Gloves, and his own grit.
- **Raylta hit Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2** and spent the fight one hit from death, eventually **stepping three times** to escape reach into Bargrum's intercept range: *"I trust my team. Three times. I've never done that before."*
- **Tivael summoned a unicorn** (Summon Fey) as an emergency heal-bot — it cast a rank-3 Heal on Bael, then died tanking a hit. Raylta: *"You know it's bad when we summon the unicorn."* Tivael also dropped **Biting Words for 62 on Chosen A**, a Flurry of Claws on B and C, Hidebound on himself, and a string of Demoralizes.
- **Ferrok's clutch kill**: Sure Strike + **Briny Bolt Spellstrike on Chosen F — one shot, one kill.** **Bael's clutch kill**: a Flying Flame birdie "through his skull" finished Chosen G.
- The party ground out all seven (Tivael: *"We're suffering, guys"*; DM: *"Bargrum doesn't die. We've established this"*), looted **21× +1 Bola and 21× +1 Guisarme** (the "we build an army" bit — they handed the spares to the freed prisoners), and **sprinted back to safety to finally rest.**
- **Session called.** The Warden Tokainen, Aruneth's rescue, and the climb to Nizca all remain. DM: leaning toward **three sessions left** of Shades of Blood; Ruby Phoenix StartPlaying link goes out next week.

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Tokainen's Chosen, Dormitory (4 PCs)
- **Setup**: Direct continuation of the S2 cliffhanger. Six Chosen woke prone, unarmored, weaponless, at −4 initiative. Initiative: Chosen F, **Bargrum, Tivael, Bael**, Chosen B, Chosen D, **Ferrok**, Chosen C, A, E. (Raylta's player absent — joined after.)
- **Round 1**: Chosen F stood, grabbed a guisarme, strode in. **Bargrum** Shielding-Taunted the three nearest. **Tivael** cast Courageous Anthem + Lingering (3 rounds), then Telekinetic Projectile (15). **Bael** opened **Solar Detonation** — full room, one crit-fail (blinded 1 min), rest dazzled/blinded. The Chosen could only stumble in (blinded → difficult terrain). **Ferrok** stepped through the doorway (eating a reactive strike that missed due to dazzle).
- **Rounds 2–6**: The Chosen popped **Cataclysm Brand** repeatedly (one blind, splitting the burst between PCs). **Bargrum** used **Energy Interceptor** to eat the fire for Bael, plus Defensive Advance strikes. **Tivael** dropped a **Flurry of Claws — crit for 64 on Chosen F and 50 on Chosen B** (force), then Electric Arc on A+B, Demoralize, and on a kill turn *"Now they're going to sleep forever."* **Ferrok** Gouging Claw Spellstrikes finished the cluster. **Bael** Thermal Nimbus + blasts + a flaming birdie. Bargrum took the last two desperation swings and dropped the final Chosen: *"Down he goes… that was probably all my luck for the session."*
- **Outcome**: Six Chosen dead, no PC seriously hurt. Loot as above. Raylta arrived immediately after.

### Encounter 2 — Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene + Favored Thralls (5 PCs)
- **Setup**: The vampires' salon. Two vampire lords + several human Favored Thralls. Initiative: **Ilnaphrae, Tivael, Ferrok, Bargrum, Raylta, Bael, Xarbaene**, Thrall B, A, C.
- **Round 1**: **Ilnaphrae** fired her repeating crossbow at squishy Tivael; **Bargrum intercepted** (and again on the second shot's target). **Tivael** cast Courageous Anthem + Lingering — **crit success (DC 24), 4 rounds** — then Shield. **Ferrok** self-Hasted and entered Arcane Cascade. **Bael** warned he'd blast the corner trio of thralls. **Raylta** held back (*"somebody just shot that arrow"*) and cast Benediction.
- **Round 2**: **Bael Solar Detonation** on the thrall cluster (48 to Thrall A, 12 to B, splash to Ilnaphrae). **Xarbaene** feinted Bargrum, struck, then **Turned to Mist** and floated to the backline. Thralls (Lord's Blessing) swarmed Bargrum and Tivael. **Tivael** went invisible-adjacent and worked the line.
- **Round 3 — the swing**: **Tivael** Biting Words (hero-point reroll), then a **rank-1 Fear on Xarbaene → natural 1, crit fail → Frightened 3 + fleeing.** **Raylta** had already tagged Xarbaene with a silver **Moonlight Ray (56)** while he was still in the salon. **Ferrok** Force Fang → Ignition Spellstrike (rerolled) **killed Ilnaphrae.** **Bael** demoralized and blasted thralls down.
- **Round 4 — the chase**: **Xarbaene fled**, provoking. **Raylta** chased with a silver **Moonbeam (18)** over Bargrum (the finger-gun shot); **Tivael** Telekinetic Projectile killed a thrall; **Ferrok** beheaded another (*"Off with his head"*). **Bael** chased into the hallway and **killed the fleeing Xarbaene with a conjured flaming bow.**
- **The thrall reckoning**: the DM flagged the thralls as enslaved, not undead; the party stopped, and **Bargrum first-aided the survivors** to spare them.
- **Outcome**: Both vampires destroyed (silver + burned). Loot: two Resilient Leather Armors, +1 Striking Frost Rapier, +1 Striking Shock Rapier, two +1 Repeating Crossbows, **Agate Ellipsoid Aeon Stone** (Augury) and **Preserving Aeon Stone** (Raylta), Potion of Quickness, gold, art objects.

### Encounter 3 — 4 Globsters + Hurkum & Ogenni (5 PCs)
- **Setup**: By the magical aquarium. Four globsters round 1; two ogres (Hurkum, Ogenni) joined round 2. Initiative: **Hurkum (R2), Ogenni (R2)**, Globster C, A, **Bael, Bargrum**, Globster D, B. (Tivael, Ferrok, Raylta also in order.)
- **Globster profile**: **immune to crits** (still take crit *riders* — persistent fire/bleed), grab tendrils, **Nauseating Slap** (stacking sickened), very high HP, **weak to electricity** (discovered round ~5).
- **Round 1–2**: A globster grabbed/sickened Raylta. **Bael** Solar Detonation (blinded several). **Bargrum** Shielding-Taunted + Boulder-Roll tripped. **Tivael** moved up, cast Dirge of Doom (then realized globsters are **mindless** — *"Are they mindless? Am I cooked?"* — so mental debuffs were wasted on them). The ogres arrived: *"How'd they get out?"* **Tivael RK'd** the ogres (best Fort / worst Reflex).
- **Mid-fight**: **Hurkum critted Tivael → Tivael's first Wooden Double** absorbed it (+25 HP block, step away). **Ogenni** used Sweeping Hook + Punting Kick chains on the frontline; both ogres had **reach + reactive strike** (which repeatedly punished movement).
- **The grind**: globsters were "bags of hit points." **Ferrok** Ignition Spellstrikes (igniting despite crit-immunity). **Tivael** Electric Arc revealed the electricity weakness (*"big damage!"*). **Bael** pivoted to **lightning** late. **Raylta** Nudged everyone topped up.
- **Kills**: **Ogenni** dropped to Bargrum's strikes after heavy debuffs; **Hurkum** to Bael's blasts; the final globster finally fell to Ferrok — *"It's down. Oh, finally."* (6 rounds.)
- **Outcome**: All six enemies dead; party badly attrited and in need of rest. Freed 7–8 abducted prisoners afterward.

### Encounter 4 — 7 Tokainen's Chosen, Second Wave (5 PCs)
- **Setup**: The party tried to evacuate prisoners and retreat; a second squad of **seven Chosen** intercepted. *"Kill the dwarf first."* Initiative: Chosen B, E, **Raylta, Ferrok**, Chosen G, **Tivael, Bael**, Chosen C, A, D, F.
- **The threat pattern**: seven **Cataclysm Brands** = relentless fire AoE bunched on whoever clustered (DM: *"healing provokes aggro"*), plus **Take 'Em Down** trip-strikes that kept PCs prone. This was the session's near-death fight.
- **Ferrok's collapse-and-rise loop**: tripped → stabbed → dropped, repeatedly. Stayed up only via Raylta's Heals, Bael's Healer's Gloves, and Vital Beacon supplication. Clutch: **Sure Strike + Briny Bolt Spellstrike = "one shot, one kill" on Chosen F.**
- **Raylta's edge-of-death**: Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2, repeatedly one hit from dying; **stepped three times** out of reach into Bargrum's intercept range (*"I trust my team"*); spent Heals, Nudges, Healer's Gloves, and a Potion Patch.
- **Tivael's contribution**: Biting Words (62 on Chosen A), Flurry of Claws on B + C (Demoralize first), **Summon Fey unicorn** as a heal-bot (rank-3 Heal on Bael, then tanked a fatal hit), Hidebound on self, repeated Demoralizes.
