## Session Summary

**The Nameless qualified.** The session opened on the cliffhanger from S15 — a mob of eliminated fighters hammering on the Ruby Palace gates, screaming for Hao Jin to answer for the island she'd sent them to — and it ended a day later with the party three-for-three in the Ruby Phoenix exhibition, in front of the Empress of Goka. In between: ten silver feathers turned in, an infinite buffet, the final eight teams named, and Hao Jin raising every beast on Bonmou from the dead with a flick of her hand.

The gate mob called themselves **the Fallen Moon** — not a team but a collection of them, ten fighters deep, who turned on the Nameless the moment someone pointed out that *"the gate opens for the feathers, nothing more"* and *"bet you they've got some on them."* Clutch answered the ultimatum in kind — *"I would say the same to you. If you value your life, turn away"* — and then three more voices came out of the wind behind the party. **Ran-to** of the Lightkeepers (*"Bunch of sore fucking losers. Didn't we beat half of you once already?"*), **Mafika Ayuwari** of the Speakers to the Wind, and **Tino** of Tino's Toughest all fell in beside them. It was the first fight of the campaign the party did not have to win alone, and it showed: of the ten Fallen Moon, **the allied NPCs put down six.** Tino alone opened with a 130-damage Inner Upheaval flurry and killed three, which prompted Seva's dry *"Wait, we're kind of getting shown up, team."*

Sovael's opener was the biggest single action of the fight. Courageous Anthem, Lingering Composition, a Talent Envy Demoralize off the same Performance check, then an **eighth-rank Divine Armageddon** dropped across nine of the ten — **six failures, two successes, and a critical failure that took the Sneak for 132.** Ferrok answered with a Sure Strike **Beheading Buzz Saw Spellstrike** that critically hit and made the swordsman roll for his own head (Sovael caught that it was two saves, not one, and the man survived the second). The cost fell on Ferrok, who ate a critical Rain of Arrows in round one and spent a chunk of the fight prone — *"Well, I've fought on the ground before"* — and dropped the swordsman from the dirt anyway.

Afterward came the part of the session that will be remembered longer than the fight. **Sattva's Risky Surgery went catastrophically, repeatedly wrong** outside the gates — ninety seconds of stabbing Ferrok and Sovael while trying to heal them. Ferrok: *"Why did you just stab me?"* Sattva: *"Stop moving so much."* The joke has a real mechanical spine: he **failed three straight DC-40 Treat Wounds**, drawing blood each time and healing nothing, then gave up and **dropped to DC 30** — *"Okay, fine. I'll stop using my experimental methods. I'll just massage you. Here, a deep tissue"* — and immediately **critically succeeded twice, for 56 and 51 across the party.** Clutch delivered the verdict: *"I don't think we needed the deep tissue massages."* Ferrok's summary of the whole forty minutes: *"After making a fool of ourselves for 40 minutes outside the gates, we're ready to walk in."*

At the gate stood a Tian woman in violet robes with vermilion hair, who apologised for the ambush — *"I was about to step in and deal with the Fallen Moon myself, but at a point it seemed you were all enjoying yourselves"* — collected ten silver feathers, and opened the Ruby Palace. **Sovael did not play it cool.** *"I'm a big fan. Can I get an autograph?"* — then realised he had nothing to sign, pulled out a spell scroll, and got **Hao Jin's signature on it**: *"I'll treasure this scroll and I'll never cast it."* Ferrok offered his chest. Seva, a beat late: *"Oh, it **is** her! Oh my gosh, I bow."*

Inside: **Ingdani's farewell** after three days of guiding them, a suite with its own hot spring, and a few hours to decompress. Sattva took a sauna and stepped straight out into the snow to meditate. Ferrok worked on his forms and drafted a speech. Sovael worked on the song he owed Sattva from S13. Clutch produced a threadbare doll, sat facing a corner, and practised saying *"I must break you."* Then a feast that Cody described as *"a scene out of Dragon Ball Z"* — an infinite, self-replenishing buffet where the roast duck respawned the instant someone carried it off. Sovael, watching it: *"This could have fed my whole village. Not that I had one, anyway."*

**Hao Jin named the final eight** — the Arms of Balance, the Biting Roses (Grandfather Mantis included; Sovael: *"Yep, that Mantis"*), the Lightkeepers, the Speakers to the Wind, the Steps of the Sun, Tino's Toughest, Winter's Roar, and the Nameless last. The room turned and openly sized them up, because word had spread that these were **the ones who killed Jaiban**. Then she walked to a balcony, let vermilion flame run down her arm, and threw a phoenix out over the island — and every beast the teams had killed on Bonmou **roared back to life**. *"Well then, dessert anyone?"* Sovael called the dessert first; Clutch wanted both — *"This means all the monsters have revived. And Clutch will turn to the party and say, let's go again."* Sovael: *"Let's leave it for tomorrow. I just want some ice cream right now. I've been burning myself all day."*

The tournament proper is a **double-elimination bracket** — winners' and losers' — so a team must lose twice to go out. Before that, one more evening: **an exhibition in front of the sponsors**, including **Nai Yan Fei, Empress of Goka**, personal guest of the Ruby Phoenix. Four bouts per team, three against opponents of Hao Jin's choosing and one against another team. No bearing on seeding; victories earn prizes. Clutch asked when they'd get to fight the dignitaries. Sattva had to explain: *"They fight in a different way. They fight with words."*

The Nameless were called up first, three times.

**Bout one was the Empress's own personal guard — five Butterfly Blades**, identically armed twin-blade duellists with Parry, feints, mobility strides and Reactive Strikes. It ran thirty-eight minutes and cost the party a body: the Blades swarmed Clutch and put him down. He had opened by **howling into Animal Form for the crowd**, which dropped his AC from 41 to 33 and which he took anyway — *"It looks cool, and it increases size and reach."* Sattva landed a clean 100-damage crit; Seva sank her dagger into Clutch's enormous shadow, drew it out as a greatsword (**Inscribe Shadow Pamor**) and asked the dice *"Please hit. I really want to impress people"*; Sovael's rank-6 Divine Wrath finished one outright. Clutch got up on a Kip Up, killed another, then grappled a third and **Whirling Threw him out of the ring** purely for style. Cody: *"Just so you know, it's not a ring out kind of thing."* Seva: *"We're here for flair."* Afterwards Sattva, having stabbed Seva with a concealed arrow mid-high-five: *"I hope the Empress didn't see me stabbing you."* Sovael: *"I think she saw everything."*

**Bout two was a pair of Rune Giants**, level 16, 330 HP each, AC 38, with 20-foot-reach greatswords and Invoke Rune — a 30-foot electricity cone that also charges the sword with 3d6 fire for a minute. Ferrok marked the moment with **the speech of the session**, bellowed to the crowd before initiative: *"We are the Nameless, and though we don't have names yet, I am Ferrok the Unyielding. We have Seva the Shade, Clutch the Rampart, Sattva the Unerring, and Sovael the Cryptic. We will win this tournament and earn true names."* Sovael, flatly: *"I was never the cryptic, though. I'm always very transparent."* Ferrok, unbothered: *"He didn't get any feedback from you guys on those. He doesn't say."*

The giants were **immune to fire**, which blanked Ferrok's Ignition Spellstrike — his signature — and cost him the Flaming rune on every swing that connected. Sovael started to send a Fire Ray into one before Cody stopped him at the declaration, and pivoted to Telekinetic Projectile in the same turn. What decided it was **a sixth-rank Roaring Applause that both giants failed on a natural 2 and a natural 3.** The half that mattered most was the quiet half: rune giants carry a bonus reaction each turn usable only for Reactive Strike, and those reactions had already taken **85 HP off the party in round one alone**. After the applause landed, **they got none for the rest of the fight** — across at least five repositions through 20-foot reach. The loud half was the *manipulate* rider: the giants had to clap on their own turns, which Sovael flagged for the table (*"he will trigger reaction from manipulate"*) and then narrated — *"Clap, clap, clap for the audience."* That bought four provoked Strikes for 72, **one of which was Seva's natural-20 crit that killed Rune Giant B on its own turn-start, before it ever acted.** Cody, on the spell: *"No one's ever failed it until now is the thing."* **Sovael corrected him — enemies had failed it before in this campaign** (*"Twice already, actually"*; the Duneshaker Solifugid ate 141 damage off the same combo in S14). The more telling line was the one just before it, after four casts across the campaign and a fight this spell had just decided: ***"Guys, I casted, like, four times this campaign."*** The party still did not know what it did. Clutch tripped and grappled *both* giants off Legendary Athletics, and **Ferrok killed the other** with a Live Wire Spellstrike. Sovael finished the fight on **16 of 188 hit points**, having lost 114 in a single enemy turn — his Cursebound flames suppress his own fire resistance, so every point of the giants' Rune of Flames landed: *"I'm still alive. I'm still standing. And I'm burning as well."*

**Bout three was a Clockwork Dragon and two Clockwork Choppers**, and it was the hardest fight of the night by a distance — **roughly 1,530 damage into the party across six rounds.** The dragon had AC 42, 400 HP, a 120-foot fly speed, Draconic Frenzy, **physical resistance 15** that only adamantine bypassed, and a **20d6 Breathe Oil** cone it got back on a fixed two-round timer rather than a recharge roll. It fired that breath **three times** — 67, 79 and 76. The choppers had resistance that blunted Sattva's and Seva's damage. The lever was **electricity**, weakness **15**: Sovael's opening Chain Lightning arced Chopper A → Dragon → Chopper B for 33 / 51 / 33 and exposed it on the first action of the fight. **Sattva then led the damage with 328 of the party's 1,000** — a third of it — with Ferrok on 228.

The fight was carried on the defensive end, and the single best number in the session is Clutch's: **he absorbed 776 damage, and 552 of it was aimed at somebody else.** Eight Intercept Attacks, three party-wide Taunts, four Battle Medicines, Paragon's Guard the whole way, and one of those Battle Medicines rerolled with **his last hero point** to stay upright. Only 224 points were ever pointed at him. Sovael, by contrast, finished on **131 taken** — because Clutch ate a 158-damage crit-failed breath and a 48 on his behalf, both of which the log reverts off Sovael's sheet entirely. Sovael still burned **Wooden Double** on a Draconic Frenzy critical, and before accepting one of those Intercepts he stopped to ask Clutch *"Do you have HP, brother?"*

Ferrok was reduced to 0 by the third oil breath and **sprang back up on It's Not Over** at exactly 25 hit points, having spent the previous five minutes insisting to a sceptical table that he could come back to life (*"No, seriously, I can. I have the feat for it"*). Sattva went scroll-first — sixth, sixth and seventh-rank Heals out of his retrieval belt (*"These are cheap at this point. We're living rich"*) — **shot Chopper B's head off** with a critical Hunted Shot, and closed by putting two arrows in the dragon's eyes for what Cody could only call *"insane."* Seva killed Chopper A and hero-pointed a **Comet Charge Spellstrike for 57**. Ferrok put the dragon down with a **Live Wire Spellstrike for 82** into a construct sitting on 74.

A small, good moment near the end: Sovael cast **Incarnate Skeletal Giant** and Sattva asked whether it was Jaiban's skeleton. Seva: *"Jaiban's not dead!"* Sovael: *"Oh yeah, it can't be Jaiban."* The phoenix had put him back that morning.

Three bouts, three wins. Cody: *"Exhibition number three, you are victorious."* Seva: *"Hopefully the last."* Cody: *"Well, no, that's four."* **Seva: *"There's more?"*** — the fourth bout, **team versus team**, is still owed. Cody closed by noting the campaign is at roughly the halfway point of the AP, and that the tournament proper could begin next session.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Gate (Day 3, last light)
- A mob from several eliminated teams, calling themselves **the Fallen Moon**, besieges the Ruby Palace doors demanding Hao Jin answer for Bonmou. One of them notices the Nameless and their feathers.
- **Clutch** answers the ultimatum: *"If you value your life, turn away."*
- **Ran-to** (Lightkeepers), **Mafika Ayuwari** (Speakers to the Wind) and **Tino** (Tino's Toughest) step up beside the party unprompted. Ten Fallen Moon against eight.
- **Fight 1** — see Combat Encounters. Won; the NPC allies take six of the ten kills.
- Mafika introduces himself and his students, all from **the Magaambya**. Ferrok: *"We are the Nameless. I am Ferrok."* Sovael: *"I am Sovael."*
- **The Risky Surgery disaster** — three straight DC-40 failures with Risky Surgery on, cutting Ferrok and Sovael for nothing, until Sattva drops the DC to 30 ("I'll just massage you") and crit-succeeds twice for 56 and 51. Sovael meanwhile baits a cantrip out of Seva purely to trigger **Absorb Magic**, then spends the overflow on **Healing Nexus** to top himself off for free.
- **Mafika hears where they came from.** Ferrok: *"All the way from Talmandor's Bounty."* Mafika: *"I've heard something of that incident. Of the tower that arose from the sea and blocked out the sun."* — the first outside confirmation that Shades of Blood is known beyond the isles.

### Act II — Into the Ruby Palace
- **Hao Jin** meets them at the gate in person, apologises for the ambush, and **takes the ten silver feathers**. The doors open. **The qualifying round is over.**
- Sovael gets her autograph on a spell scroll. Ferrok offers his chest. Seva bows a beat late.
- **Ingdani departs** — three days of guiding the party done. *"It's been an honour to travel with you all these past few days."*
- Guest quarters with a private hot spring. Sattva saunas then meditates in the snow; Ferrok drills forms and writes a speech; Sovael works on Sattva's song; **Clutch rehearses "I must break you" at a threadbare doll.**

### Act III — The Feast
- An enormous, **infinitely self-replenishing buffet**. Sattva is the only one who asks whether it's illusory. It isn't.
- **The final eight teams are named**: Arms of Balance, Biting Roses, Lightkeepers, Speakers to the Wind, Steps of the Sun, Tino's Toughest, Winter's Roar, and the Nameless.
- The room sizes the Nameless up — word has spread that they **killed Jaiban**.
- **Hao Jin throws a vermilion phoenix over Bonmou and revives every beast on the island.** *"Well then, dessert anyone?"*
- **The tournament structure is explained**: double elimination, winners' and losers' brackets. A team must lose twice to be out. Boats leave the day after the exhibition, **a week at sea**, several more days to **Goka**, then **one week** of tournament.

### Act IV — The Exhibition (the following evening)
- Sponsors and delegates arrive by magic, including **Nai Yan Fei, Empress of Goka**, guest of the Ruby Phoenix.
- Format: **four bouts per team** — three opponents of Hao Jin's choosing, one team-vs-team. Prizes for victories; no bearing on seeding.
- Downtime: stash sold, **1,070 gp each**. On the way in, **Seva and Sattva have their fortunes read** and Seva casts **Lucky Number** (her number: 1); Clutch casts his own and rolls a 9. Seva later spends hers *instead of* a hero point.
- Sovael claims the **Boreal Staff (Major)**; Clutch takes the **charm of electricity resistance**; Ferrok turns up to the exhibition in **gleaming Dawnsilver armour**. Seva's Drained is removed free by the palace physicians.
- **Bout 1 — five Butterfly Blades**, the Empress's personal guard. Won.
- **Bout 2 — two Rune Giants**, through a 30-foot crimson portal. Won. Preceded by **Ferrok's team-introduction speech**.
- **Bout 3 — a Clockwork Dragon and two Clockwork Choppers**. Won in six rounds, at the cost of ~1,530 damage taken — 776 of it by Clutch.
- **Bout 4, team versus team, is still owed.**

### What Sovael is thinking about

- **Roaring Applause is the single best spell he owns against a reaction-heavy opponent — but for the boring half, not the flashy one.** The manipulate rider is the fun part and it bought 72 damage; **the reaction denial bought more.** The giants' bonus Reactive Strikes took 85 HP off the party in round one and then nothing at all for three rounds. Both halves are real and both are repeatable — this is the third time the combo has paid (the Duneshaker Solifugid ate 141 damage the same way in S14). Against the tournament teams, stripping reactions from up to ten enemies is worth more than any damage spell in the repertoire. Cody: nobody had ever failed the save before — the DC finally caught up at Master Spellcaster.
- **Whispers of Weakness cannot solve the fire problem.** It returns weaknesses and the lowest save — **never immunities** — and he *did* cast it first against the Rune Giants; it could not have surfaced the immunity. The real lesson is narrower: against an unknown opponent, **spend the cheap probe before the focus point.** Chain Lightning found the constructs' electricity weakness on action one in bout 3. A cantrip or a small AoE is a better opening question than a 16d6 Fire Ray, because an Oracle of Flames whose entire signature is fire will reach for it by reflex — and two of the three exhibition opponents punished exactly that.
- **Chain Lightning found the constructs' weakness on action one.** Opening with a multi-target elemental spell doubles as a weakness probe when Recall Knowledge is not on the table. Worth making that the default opener against unknown non-humanoids.
- **Divine Armageddon is the right opener against a crowd and the wrong one against a party.** It caught nine bodies at the gate because the allied NPCs hadn't closed yet. In a four-on-five team bout there will be no such window — **Divine Wrath stays the only enemies-only burst.**
- **The exhibition is an audition, not a bracket.** Hao Jin said outright that the people watching are potential patrons and that the bouts have no bearing on seeding. The value of a Whirling Throw out of the ring or a howl into Animal Form is real here in a way it will not be next week.
- **Energy Aegis paid for itself — on him alone.** The spell is *touch, 1 creature*, so the break-time cast (rank 7, 14:30) covered Sovael and nobody else. Rune Giant A's Invoke Rune rolled 47 electricity: Seva took the full 47, Sattva 23, and **Sovael 42**. Keep casting it before every bout — and remember that covering anyone else costs a separate minute-long casting each.
- **The party is running hot on consumables.** Sattva burned three healing scrolls in the last fight alone and called them cheap.
