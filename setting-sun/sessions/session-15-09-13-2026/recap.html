Session Summary

The Nameless finished Danger Island completely — every hex walked, every team base found, all four haunted Watchtowers put to rest, and both of the island's apex beasts brought down: Jaiban, the Lord of the Island, who had sent them running in S11, and Tamikan, the talking kongamato they had fled in S13. Cody closed the last fight with "Bonmou has been 100%ed," and Ferrok claimed the platinum trophy.

The morning was the map's last loop — a team base at a shrine of Tsukiyo with docks and a stone market, a cemetery, a temple of Hei Feng, a lighthouse, and a Taumatan shrine to the god of games, competition and boasting. Sattva called rock, paper, scissors; Clutch proposed a roll-off to settle the winner and took it on a natural 20. Then the Watchtowers: narrow haunted towers, each guarded by a pair of incorporeal undead that re-form in twelve hours unless the tower's unlit signal brazier is finally lit. Cody's Recall Knowledge said the garrison had died before they could light it; Ferrok read that as unfinished business — "So we'd be finishing their task then" — and Clutch took it one step further: "So maybe lighting it sets their souls at peace or something?" Purple fire laid the Poltergeists to rest and left a returning chakram in the ashes. The Shadows came next, with Seva arriving just in time to learn the ghost-oil rule the hard way, and then a break and level 15.

Freshly levelled, the party voted to go back for Jaiban. Sovael opened with Benediction and threw a scroll Synesthesia and a True Target for all four allies — which went 3-for-4 with two crits: a natural-20 Jaws from Clutch and a stunned, one-action sword critical from Ferrok. Jaiban crit Clutch for 112, grabbed and restrained him, and Clutch kept Taunting and counter-biting from inside its jaws. Ferrok's critical Ignition Spellstrike left the kaiju on 1 hit point. Then Sovael pulled a Scroll of Sure Strike, routed Fire Ray through the Shadow Signet against Jaiban's Reflex DC, and rolled a natural 20 — 100 damage. Jaiban answered with Earth-Shaking Ferocity: it roared back up at 100 hit points with a free Quaking Stomp. The Foretell Harm tick, the burning ground and persistent fire ground it back down, and Seva's Gouging Claw Spellstrike finished it. "I told you I will come back for you, Jaiban." When Ferrok suggested Sattva skin the corpse, Cody reminded the table that the beasts brought down in the qualifier are resurrected by the Ruby Phoenix afterward, and Sovael answered for the party: "Let's be respectful. At least we proved our might."

The afternoon was the hardest stretch, and Seva carried the cost of it. The Banshees' opening Wails of the Damned left her Drained 4 for the rest of the day; she spent the fight Frightened 2, took a natural-20 touch for 92, and still landed the killing Spellstrike on the banshee that drained her, right after Sovael's Spiritual Torrent critically failed it down to 5 hit points. "I'm taking my soul back. Am I still drained?" She was. The Wraiths — the tower the party had flagged as the most dangerous — opened with a seventh-rank Howling Blizzard and an Eclipse Burst; Seva's Rooting rune pinned both of them on natural-20 criticals, one mid-flight, and the pinned wraith dropped its own Eclipse Burst on itself. Ferrok killed both. The four braziers paid out a chakram, two major staves and a set of scalding gauntlets.

Tamikan was left. Sovael had wanted her since Jaiban fell; Seva thought it was pushing it and the table did the last tower first. At four o'clock the question came back — Clutch: "Tamikan, everyone"; Ferrok: "We could"; Sovael: "The completion is in me. I just cannot leave it alone" — and Seva, who had wanted to head straight for the mountain, talked herself into it: "We do get experience, so might as well." This time the party had an answer to a flier. Ferrok had named it at the S13 level-up and Sovael had priced it as a scroll; now he'd bought two scrolls of Earthbind. The first dropped Tamikan 120 feet out of the sky on his first action. He took her reactions with Roaring Applause, suggested Seva ready a strike, and grounded her a second time. Clutch intercepted four attacks meant for his friends and took 317 damage doing it. After Ferrok's Goring Charge left Tamikan on 32, Sovael took Whispers of Weakness for the +2 and ended it with a Fire Ray that hit by exactly zero: "You shouldn't have said you're gonna eat us. This is for all the people you've eaten."

They healed, teleported, and started up Mount Haminabu in the last light — Ferrok: "It'll surely be just to walk up the mountain." It was not. The gates of the Ruby Palace were surrounded by about ten armed fighters from several teams, hammering on the doors and shouting for Hao Jin. "The doors only open for the feathers," one of them said, turning. "But I'm betting you they've got some."

Character Spotlight

Sovael · the one who came back for Jaiban

  • He made good on a vow from S11, and he said so. Sovael swore revenge when the party group-escaped Jaiban at level 12. Four sessions later he asked the table "You think we can take Jaiban now?", opened the fight, and landed the hit that dropped it to 0 — then stood over it once Seva had finished it: "I told you I will come back for you, Jaiban."
  • ★ He turned a known answer into a bought one. In S13 Ferrok named Earthbind as the party's counter to fliers and Sovael priced it as a scroll. This session, when Seva asked whether they could even deal with Tamikan, he'd already bought two — "I bought a bunch of Earthbind scrolls" — and used both as planned: one on his first action, the second the moment she climbed back up, with Clutch delayed behind him and a readied strike suggested to Seva.
  • He dropped Jaiban and ended Tamikan. A natural-20 Sure Strike Fire Ray through the Shadow Signet hit Jaiban for 100 and forced Earth-Shaking Ferocity; Seva finished it. Against Tamikan, Whispers of Weakness for the +2 and a Fire Ray that hit by exactly zero, on the Off-Guard Clutch's Taunt had created, ended the island: "You shouldn't have said you're gonna eat us. This is for all the people you've eaten."
  • He set the tone over the corpse. When Ferrok wanted Sattva to skin Jaiban and Cody reminded them the Ruby Phoenix brings the beasts back: "Let's be respectful. At least we proved our might." — the same instinct as S14's "Hey, let's be graceful."
  • He kept asking for the last thing on the map. He pushed for Tamikan right after Jaiban and the table chose the towers; when it came back up at four o'clock he was one of three voices for it — "The completion is in me. I just cannot leave it alone" — with the fallback ready: "We can always just run."
  • ★ His support was measurable. True Target on Jaiban for all four allies went 3-for-4 with two crits, and his Synesthesia's Clumsy 3 turned two of Ferrok's hits into crits worth about 145 damage. His Demoralize flipped Shadow A into a critically failed Spiritual Torrent for 70, and his Torrent crit-failed Banshee A for 66 to set up Seva's kill. He carried Seva through the Banshees with a sixth-rank Heal scroll and a rank-5 Heal after her natural-20 hit.
  • He looks after people between fights. Checking Clutch was topped up before Jaiban; reminding Sattva he had a free Recall Knowledge he'd stopped using (which then crit); and, right after the Banshees, working through every fix he could find for Seva's Drained 4 before accepting there wasn't a cheap one.
  • He plays the table as much as the dice — the in-character ghost-advice scene with Ferrok ("But I can't strike them." / "Well, good luck." / "Goddammit."), volunteering himself as scout ("Let's send Sovael to scout"), and his quick self-deprecation: fifteen minutes muted ("I've been talking to a void") and, after learning a rule about his own composition, "I don't know my character, guys."

Clutch · the one who called it

  • He called the ending before it arrived. "What if we get there and it's like a King of the Hill style battle where you got to fight all the other teams?" Three hours later the party reached the Ruby Palace to find ten fighters from several teams at the gates, turning on them for their feathers.
  • He asked the question that solved the Watchtowers. He called for the Recall Knowledge on the brazier, and when Cody said the garrison had died before lighting it, he took Ferrok's "finishing their task" one step further: "So maybe lighting it sets their souls at peace or something?"
  • He looks after the details other people miss. He planned the route to 100% the island, wanted the full party present to decide on the dangerous tower, checked Seva's ghost oil the moment she arrived, and reminded Sattva that legendary Medicine treats eight at once.
  • ★ He did real damage to Jaiban from inside its jaws. A natural-20 True Target Jaws crit (48) and, grabbed and restrained, a natural-20 Armored Counterattack crit (56) — "Clutch will just say, don't take your eyes off me! You're wide open!" Cody: "And there's Clutch with the RKO." About 130 damage in all, while eating a 112-point crit.
  • ★ He took the Tamikan fight on his body. Four intercepts — Sonic Screech for Seva, a wing for Sattva, Screech and a beak for Sovael — each answered with Armored Counterattack, 317 damage taken, 167 of it for other people. His Taunt created the Off-Guard that Sovael's killing Fire Ray needed.
  • He grabbed a ghost. With ghost touch, Combat Grab works on incorporeal creatures: "Only ever chase shadows." He killed Poltergeist B and Shadow A, and against the Wraiths landed a natural-20 Battle Cry and a natural-20 Rooting Jaws crit.
  • Dry, constant comedy — and he means the kholo stuff. "Alright, everybody hit save so we can save scum." The puppy eyes over his shoulder when Sattva asked to shoot him in the back. "Just give him a big hug." And in chat, after the first tower: "I'm going to start eating more, I need to be bigger / So I can protect all of the pack."

Ferrok the Unyielding · the finisher who wanted a mount

  • He did the heaviest lifting in the two hardest Watchtowers and on Jaiban. Killed Banshee B (237 of its 255, including a critical Spellstrike for 132) and both Wraiths. Against Jaiban, a stunned one-action True Target sword crit — "Ferrok looks a little dizzy, and he swings his sword" — then the critical Ignition Spellstrike that left it on 1 hit point.

  • He has the session's best answers to other people's questions. Sovael asks for ghost-fighting tips: "Strike them before they strike you." → "Well, good luck." Seva, Drained and Frightened, asks what anyone knows about Banshees: "They're blue." Sovael volunteers to scout: "They have to challenge a team, not an individual team member. A loophole."
  • He wanted to tame the kaiju. "Let's use non-lethal so we can tame him afterwards. My new mount." When Cody ruled out skinning it: "Alright, just selfies with his corpse and then we move on."
  • He called both of the day's big turns. "So we'd be finishing their task then" at the first brazier; "I also think the later we go, the more desperate the teams left will be" hours before the gate.

"You need to save your gold for your medical bills, Seva." And the last line before the ambush: "It'll surely be just to walk up the mountain."

  • He carried the first fight. Precision damage useless against incorporeal targets, he still put 156 of Poltergeist A's 190 into it and killed it.

Shared precision on Jaiban doubled on both True Target crits (Ferrok's own count: "182 damage with Sattva's extra precision"); Benediction for the Banshees; he read the scalding gauntlets and pitched them to Clutch. "How can they get so many spoons in the middle of a forest?" / "Sattva starts ice skating." / "There's just 20 arrows in the trees right here." And he'll pile on a friend: "Yeah, do you know how much you racked up throughout this journey with me?"
