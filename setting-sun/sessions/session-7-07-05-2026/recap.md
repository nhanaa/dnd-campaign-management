*The party fought this entire session at **level 10**. It was billed as the finale — but the tower kept going. The DM called it at the end: next session **concludes Shades of Blood**, "less than one full floor to go." Nizca, Degg, and the Shadow Beacon are at the top.*

## Session Summary

This was the long climb — six fights up the last habitable floors of the Ebon Tower, the architecture itself curdling into the Netherworld as they rose (the honest Azlanti stone giving way to black-mist glass, every cast shadow deepening, every lamp dimming). It opened quietly: cleanup from the velstrac massacre below, one more room of half-broken captives staged for rescue, a reading nook where they could look *out* through the tower wall and see the world beneath them swallowed in supernatural dark. Then the fights started and mostly didn't stop.

The floor's guardians were a rogue's gallery of "not again" enemies. First, two winged **vampire-Strix** — Okoa and Rukh — dropped screeching into a sea-battered reception hall; Ferrok cracked Okoa in half and Bael's fire ran down the fleeing Rukh. Then a hall of paintings breathed out **five Greater Shadows** that spent the fight literally *stealing the party's shadows* (Ferrok's, Raylta's, Bael's, and Bargrum's all went — only Tivael's "evasive dragon" kept his), until Tivael answered with a spell he'd been itching to cast — a three-action **Spiritual Torrent** cone, "Kaboom" — that vaporized one shadow and softened two more, and Raylta punched the one that stole *her* shadow to death with Holy Light. A statue of the vampire god **Nin** split in two and lunged as a pair of **Stone Bulwarks**; a wall-mural of gems tore itself down as **three Aeon Stone Swarms** and turned into the worst grind of the night — every swarm exploded when it died, so the party "kept losing when they won."

Between the gem swarm and the finish, the session's one real conversation: the party found **Anareth Zorlan**, the captured engineer they'd crossed paths with before — dragged back here by Degg to build spare parts, and now willing to hand them **blueprints for safely dismantling the Shadow Beacon** (and a key past a paralysis trap). The reveal that landed with him: the beacon is a *rushed prototype*. Degg and Nizca skipped the tests, so only the surrounding seas are shrouded so far — but given time it will tie all of Golarion to the Netherworld, permanently. Two more fights closed the floor: a pair of masochist velstrac **Sacristans** who *begged* to be hurt (and whom Bargrum met with the session's best bars), and finally a **river of blood** that congealed into four self-healing **Bulbous Blood Oozes**. The party ended battered, rich, and one short floor from the top of the world.

## Key Events

### Act I — Cleanup and the Climb (Explore / RP)
- The session picked up right after the velstrac boss floor (Jubilant, last session). A rough start on hero-point-fueled healing rolls (Bael: *"failed again — good start"*) gave way to methodical cleanup: one more room of captives, **not** as broken as the mirror-hall victims, staged with food, water, and directions for later evacuation.
- Exploring the last rooms of the floor: a small **torture chamber** (Tivael, deadpan: *"This is a torture chamber."*), coffin rooms already emptied (the vampires the party killed), and a **Nin shrine/office** full of pre-Earthfall prison records. Loot: a **Spiritual Warhorn** (Bael took it — needs a free hand), and a legend-boosting relic that needed a *sanctified* wielder (nobody qualified — *"you need to be sanctified. And I think none of us are."*).
- A **reading nook** with windows let them look out the tower wall: below, the world was twisted and obscured by supernatural darkness; above, everything vanished into shadow. The higher they climbed, the more the Netherworld bled through — the Azlanti stone literally replaced by clear glass full of dense inky mist.
- Bargrum, ever the quartermaster, appraised a rug (*"It really does tie the room together, you know?"*) and floated the post-campaign dream: *"If we build an adventuring team house after all this, we're going to be set."* Ferrok: *"Manacles hanging from everything."*

### Act II — Okoa & Rukh, the Vampire-Strix (Combat)
- In a **salt-encrusted reception hall** slick with sea spray near the ocean surface, a screech echoed and two shadow-clad figures dropped in on enormous wings — shortbows, claws, burning red glares, no words. Bargrum tried anyway (*"Let's talk about this."*); they answered with weapons.
- **Tivael's Recall Knowledge** named them: **Strix** — winged humanoids native to Golarion — but infused with Netherworld energy and turned into **undead vampires**. High physical resistance; even Raylta's adamantine barely bit.
- **Okoa** was the melee flyer: claws, a **Vicious Flurry**, and a **Strafing Strike** that turned her into a winged shade and carved the whole party line. **Rukh** was the caster — **Synesthesia** on Bargrum (Clumsy 3 / Dazzled), a rank-4 **Grim Tendrils** void-line that bled most of the party, and self-healing. Both Strix ticked ~10 HP back a turn.
- **Bargrum** anchored: a Juggernaut Charge that wrapped the silver monkey's fist around a Strix's neck to *drag* it, Group/Shielding Taunts, and — the discovery of the night — **two Intercepts in one round** (*"you learn to block things with your face as well as your chest"*). His clutch play was a **Commitment to Protection** call-out — *"Watch out for her claws, Ferrok"* — reading the danger and handing Ferrok a defensive edge (+1 AC) against the Strix (Raylta: *"That call-out was clutch."*).
- **Ferrok killed Okoa** with an Ignition Spellstrike run — *"that guy's dead. Dead, dead."* / Raylta: *"Why are you so strong?"* **Bael's Flying Flame finished Rukh** — *"that is enough to break her down."* Tivael controlled with a rank-3 Fear (both frightened), Befuddle (Stupefied), and Frostbite; Raylta chipped with Vitality Lash (vitality vs. undead) and kept Bael topped with Nudge.
- Afterward, a callback to last session's spared poppet (*"We did a good thing, you guys."* — *"That puppet hurt you guys good."*). Loot: **+2 handwraps of mighty blows**, a **+1 striking astral shortbow**, +1 resilient leather, +1 resilient scroll robes, and a scroll of Grim Tendrils.

### Act III — The Painting Shadows (Combat)
- A U-shaped hall lined with paintings and propped canvases; a staircase ascended at the center. As the party entered, **five Greater Shadows** peeled out of the paintings. Ferrok: *"Painting monsters. I pull out my eraser."* (DM: *"I think that would just make the eraser very dirty."*)
- Incorporeal — resistant to everything but force, ghost touch, and vitality — and armed with **Steal Shadow**: every solid hit enfeebled a PC by ripping their shadow off. Over the fight they stole **Ferrok's** (*"I'm already weak, you fool. I look so weird walking around with no shadows."*), **Raylta's** (*"How dare you? Shadow wants it. It's mine!"*), **Bael's**, and **Bargrum's** — only Tivael, the self-styled *"evasive dragon,"* kept his.
- **Tivael ran the fight.** First a spell he'd wanted to cast for ages — **Wall of Mirrors** — which both punished anything that struck it and became difficult terrain that bogged the incorporeal shadows down. Then, once the party clocked that **vitality** was the lever (Bael called it first — *"I think vitality might hurt them"* — with Raylta seconding it once the numbers proved out), Tivael dropped a **three-action Spiritual Torrent** — a 60-foot vitality cone. *"Kaboom."* It **destroyed Greater Shadow B** (84 damage) and gutted two others. Ferrok: *"That is a clutch spell."*
- **Bael** opened with his **Crescent Aeon Stone's Moonbeam** — a 100-foot silver line (*"Big Beam Attack! His Crescent Stone"* / Raylta, dry: *"It's so lame."*) — then chewed shadows down with Flying Flame birdies, blasts, and, with his new **handwraps**, a literal fire-fist punch: *"I cover my hand with fire and I punch it in the face."* (Raylta: *"Oh my god, he's so good at punching."*)
- **Raylta** took her revenge personally: **Holy Light killed the shadow that stole her shadow** — *"I just punch him with a holy light. Where's your holy light?"* Ferrok's Force Fang and Ignition Spellstrikes chipped through the incorporeal resistance; Bargrum reactive-struck and taunted, his silver armor sparking a weird rules interaction.
- Out of combat, Bargrum found the perfect reference: *"Has anybody seen the old, old Peter Pan? Bargrum's gonna take his shadow and nail it back on."*

### Act IV — The Scrying Circle and the Golden Goose (Explore / RP)
- A room held a **heavily-warded scrying circle**. Ferrok RK'd it (a scrying tool, but trapped — a botched attempt to use it deals *"pretty intense mental damage"*). Tempting to spy on the floors above, but nobody was skilled enough to risk it. Left alone.
- A lounge with a display cabinet held a **cursed golden goose** — feed it the bloody heart of a *non-evil* creature and wait, and it lays a gold egg. The catch (petrification? something worse) spooked them. Raylta, gleefully: *"I kind of want to see if it's the 12th egg or not. I love gambling."* Ferrok: *"I feel like we should probably destroy the golden goose. It's freaking cursed."* They left it.
- A **magically locked door** (detect magic flagged it) got noted for later — it would turn out to be the Warden's old chambers.

### Act V — The Stone Bulwarks (Combat)
- A bare lounge held a single large statue of the vampiric god **Nin** (Bael: *"He just looks like a dude."* / Ferrok: *"We are all set to start a vampire cult after this."* / Raylta: *"We're **trying to destroy** the cult."*). As they stepped in, **the statue split in two** and both halves lunged — a pair of **Stone Bulwarks**.
- Mindless, with heavy physical resistance bypassed only by **adamantine**. Ferrok ran the party's signature combo — **Incendiary Fog** placed over the enemies, then **Bael's Thermal Nimbus ignited it** into a fireball — though one Bulwark **crit-saved** the blast (a soul-crushing nat-20 that shrugged off the fire; only its twin took the brunt). **Bargrum** anchored: double Shielding Taunts pinning both statues (which forced a string of taunt-misses) and a 38-damage Intercept eaten for Raylta. **Raylta's adamantine Needle Darts crit shattered one Bulwark** (*"Off the board!"*); **Tivael's Electric Arc finished the other**, with Ferrok crowing *"No mercy — all crits!"* over the lucky streak.
- The statue left behind a copy of **The Immaculate Solution** (Nin's scripture — the party had sold their first copy). Raylta: *"Are we spreading the religion accidentally?"*
- The northern **library** held a dozen glowing hovering notes; **Raylta's Occultism** cracked them: schematics for the **Shadow Beacon**. Confirmed — the device is still a **prototype**, which is why only this region is shadowed rather than all of Golarion; continued work could blot the sun entirely. Bael, unbothered: *"Hitting it really hard is my plan. If there isn't a flaw, we'll make one."*

### Act VI — The Aeon Stone Swarms (Combat)
- An office's east wall was a mural of tiny polished stones. As they searched, the mural **tore itself off the wall** and became **three Aeon Stone Swarms** — *"we're not rocks, we're gems"* (Ferrok, theorizing why they'd attack: *"they crave our blood. That's all I can think."*).
- This was the ugliest grind of the session. The swarms flew into the party's squares and hammered them with **Battering Orbit** AoE and launched-stone strikes — and each swarm, when killed, detonated with **Stone Shatter** (a 30-foot burst that caught allies too). Raylta ate a 56 (DM: *"a headshot and a nutshot simultaneously"*) and summed it up: *"Why are we still losing when we win?"*
- The three swarms fell one apiece: **Bargrum** cracked the first with a Silver Monkey's Fist strike, **Bael** popped the second with a crit blast, and **Tivael** finished the last with a **Frostbite** after deliberately switching to hit their weaker Fortitude save (*"these guys keep saving my spells, so I'll do a 40 instead"*). Around the kills, Bael's **Four Winds** scattered the party out of the swarms and he delivered his signature line as he stopped dodging and started catching — *"Instead of dodging, I just **Brace**, and everything that flies into my face I catch in my hand and burn."* **Tivael** ran the reliable AoE all fight (Flurry of Claws on two swarms, Electric Arc every round); **Ferrok** ground away with Ignition Spellstrikes through a genuinely cursed streak — he was **Enfeebled 2** the whole fight, a leftover from the Greater Shadows; and **Bargrum** interposed his own body over Raylta to eat AoE (*"He just dives and rolls and blocks both hits"*) until his Intercepts ran dry.
- The room's Aeon Stones became loot — a **black pearl** (+4 vs. mental effects *and* reflects them back; Bargrum took it for his low Will save), a **delaying stone** (Ferrok), and a stasis/enfeebled-reducer stone. The party's absurd wealth became a running bit — Raylta refusing Bael's expensive potions (*"I only have moderates. Look at all the money I'm carrying around."* / Raylta: *"I didn't know we were made out of money."*) and the whole haul feeling *"like a dragon's hoard."*

### Act VII — Anareth Zorlan and the Beacon Blueprints (RP — the pivot)
- In a converted **workshop**, the party found **Anareth Zorlan** — the same engineer they'd tried to free two floors down (the one who seemingly *absconded* from the Warden's floor back in S4). The reframe landed here: he hadn't run off at all — Degg's people **captured** him after the party left and put him to work building spare parts for the beacon. Ferrok, deadpan: *"There — you listen to us this time and actually run."* (Anareth had tried; Azlanti fireball-guards caught him.)
- Asked to help, he refused to fight (*"I'm no warrior. Or mage. I'm an inventor."*) — but offered the campaign's most important handoff. The engine, he explained, is **unstable**: Degg and Nizca **rushed the activation and skipped tests**, which is the only reason the shadow stopped at the surrounding seas instead of the whole planet. Given time, *"Galarian will become tied to the Netherworld. Permanently."* Degg is *"mad, insane — but he's a mind for this kind of thing."*
- He gave them **blueprints to safely dismantle the beacon** (ten minutes of study → mechanical circumstance bonuses to next session's disable checks) with a blunt warning: fail the disable and *"big boom"* — a mass release of Netherworld energy. He also slid them the **enchanted key to the Warden's old chambers** (it disables a paralysis trap), then took the now-cleared stairs out. Raylta: *"See? I knew you weren't such a bad guy."*
- The **Strix journal** (Okoa and Rukh's room) filled in the rest: the two were **exiled Strix** who joined Nizca hoping their own Netherworld ties would buy them belonging. It also confirmed the backstory of the whole upper tower — when the beacon first darkened the sky, it drew the **velstracs** in from the Netherworld; Nizca struck an *uneasy alliance* with them, but once he and Degg got consumed with repairs up top, the velstracs turned on everyone below — captives *and* vampires alike (which is why half the vampire floor was already dead by the time the party arrived last session).

### Act VIII — The Sacristans (Combat)
- Behind the Warden's key: a chamber preserved since Earthfall, full of luxury and red embroidery — occupied by **two velstrac Sacristans** still grieving Jubilant. *"You brought an end to her. Please show us how."* They were **masochists** — they *wanted* to be hit — which flipped the fight's whole emotional register.
- **Tivael controlled hard**: Dirge of Doom lingered to four rounds, then a **Laughing Fit** that stuck a Sacristan unable to use reactions (*"No reaction strike for you, bitch."*), plus a second Laughing Fit sustained, Evangelize (Stupefied), Demoralize, Telekinetic Projectile, and Electric Arc. He paid for standing too close — a 56-damage hit and stacked persistent bleed he never once saved against, which became his running gag (*"I'm just going to bleed to death."*).
- **Bargrum** got the session's sharpest exchange. As he deliberately Intercepted hit after hit — and **Shield-Blocked a Sacristan's nat-20 crit** chain-strike — a Sacristan admired him: *"I was unfamiliar with your game. You've my respect."* Bargrum, spitting blood: **"We're nothing alike."** — later, on the same theme: **"You're taking hits for the love of the pain. I'm shielding crits for the love of the game."** Raylta got it exactly right: *"He's right — he's not one of you! ... I'm a fucking freak."*
- **Tivael's Electric Arc killed Sacristan B** (the DM: *"B had exactly nine hit points — that is enough"*). The last Sacristan came down to **Raylta**, who **did not want the kill** — *"I hope I miss. I hope I miss."* — precisely because giving a masochist his death felt like giving him what he wanted. Her Needle Darts connected anyway, and with cruel irony **critted**: *"the darts just shatter the glass behind him as he tumbles out the window into the ocean."*

### Act IX — The Bulbous Blood Oozes (Combat — Finale)
- Clearing the last rooms (an extravagant bath, a fine bedroom — *"this is where the rich people who own the prison operate"*), the party turned back toward the stairs and a **massive river of blood** poured down and congealed into **four Bulbous Blood Oozes**.
- Mindless, **immune to crits**, high HP, and armed with **Siphon Vitality** — a 15-foot pulse that damaged everyone nearby *and healed the ooze* (Raylta, exasperated: *"Man, they get healed."*). Another grind, and another one where the party defaulted to damage before **Recall Knowledge** (their reflex saves turned out to be the weak point — learned late).
- **Bargrum** was the immovable object — Group/Shielding Taunts holding three oozes at once, Shield Blocks, and Intercepts soaking Siphon Vitality until his allies begged him to stop (*"Bargrum, no, you can't!"* / Raylta: *"I'm actually starting to feel bad."* / Bargrum: *"I can take it."*). The DM's verdict: *"If you ever GM with a Guardian in the party, the best way to damage the Guardian is to attack literally everybody else."*
- **Tivael** did the quiet closing work — his **Electric Arc** finished two of the four oozes on their weak reflex, alongside Flurry of Claws. **Ferrok** re-ran the **Incendiary Fog / Nimbus** combo with Bael, ground the oozes down with Force Fang and Ignition Spellstrikes, and put the **final blade on the last one** while bleeding profusely. **Bael** flung Raylta clear on a conjured gust, healed Bargrum with a crit **Battle Medicine** (40), and sent phoenixes through the pack. **Raylta** reached for the AoE that finally fit — a rank-5 **Divine Immolation** (*"That's a divine fireball"*) aimed at the oozes' weak reflex — only for both targets to shrug it off on a **nat-19 and a nat-20** (*"very unfortunate"*), the exact luck that defined the whole grind.
- The last ooze fell around the 3h50m mark, and the DM called it there. Next week: the top of the tower, and the end of Shades of Blood.

## Character Spotlight

### Tivael · the Spell He'd Been Saving
Pax played the ascent as a control-and-artillery act, and it produced the single most satisfying moment of the night. Against the incorporeal Greater Shadows he finally uncorked **Wall of Mirrors** — a spell he flagged out loud as one he'd *"been itching to do"* — using it both as a damage-reflecting barrier and as terrain to bog the shadows down, and then followed it with a three-action **Spiritual Torrent** cone (*"Kaboom"*) that erased one shadow and cracked two more open. Across the grinding fights he was the party's most reliable engine: the **Recall Knowledge** anchor (he's the one who ID'd the Strix as Netherworld-infused vampires) and the dependable AoE (Flurry of Claws and Electric Arc, every single grind) — and, quietly, the *finisher* more often than a glance suggests, dropping the last **Aeon Stone Swarm** with a **Frostbite** (after deliberately switching to its weaker save) and closing two of the four **Blood Oozes** with Electric Arc. Against the Sacristans he ran a full control board — a four-round Dirge, a **Laughing Fit** that locked a velstrac out of its reactions (*"No reaction strike for you, bitch"*), Evangelize, and Demoralize — capped by the **Electric Arc that killed Sacristan B** on the nose (exactly 9 HP left). His one running cost was self-inflicted geometry: he clustered too close to the Sacristans, ate a 56 and a stack of persistent bleed, and never once passed the save — which he turned into the session's best self-deprecating bit (*"I never succeeded a save for the persistent bleed. I literally bled to death every time."*). Also the table's loot pragmatist: *"Money is money."*

### Bargrum · "I Can Do This All Day"
The load-bearing wall, and this session the emotional counterweight to a floor full of pain-worshippers. Mechanically he was everywhere at once — the double-Intercept discovery in the Strix fight (*"you learn to block things with your face as well as your chest"*), the monkey's-fist-around-the-neck drag, the body-block **Interpose** that ate Aeon-swarm AoE meant for Raylta, and enough taunt-and-soak that the DM openly conceded *"the best way to damage the Guardian is to attack literally everybody else."* But his best material was voice. Against the masochist Sacristan who offered him *"my respect"* for taking hits on purpose, Bargrum drew the exact line between a tank and a fanatic: **"We're nothing alike"** and, better, **"You're taking hits for the love of the pain. I'm shielding crits for the love of the game."** Out of combat he was the party's dry heart — the rug that *"really ties the room together,"* the dream of a fully-decorated adventuring-team house, and the Peter Pan fix for everyone's stolen shadows (*"take his shadow and nail it back on"*). And when it counted, his **Commitment to Protection** call-out in the Strix fight was, by Raylta's own word, *"clutch."*

### Ferrok · the Hammer
The kill leader, and the party's blunt comic engine. His combat thesis got stated outright — *"when you have a hammer, everything is a nail. So Ignition Spellstrike"* — and he backed it: **he cracked Okoa the vampire-Strix in half** (*"Dead, dead."*), ran the **Incendiary Fog** setups that Bael detonated in both the Bulwark and ooze fights, and put the **final swing on the last Blood Ooze** while bleeding profusely. Even through a genuinely cursed dice streak in the Aeon-swarm grind (*"you're killing me"*), he stayed the finisher of record. His humor carried the dungeon crawl: theorizing that denied gems *"crave our blood,"* proposing the party *"start a vampire cult after this"* in front of a Nin idol, cheerfully mocking the beacon apocalypse (*"won't be able to tan anymore"*), and — best of all — reacting to losing his shadow to a Greater Shadow with pure minotaur exasperation: *"I'm already weak, you fool. I look so weird walking around with no shadows."* Watching the masochist Sacristans revel in their own beatings, he deadpanned their whole ethos right back at them (*"It hurts so good."*), and against the oozes he cut to the action-movie bone: *"If it bleeds, we can kill it."*

### Bael · Big Beam, Bigger Wallet
The AoE opener, the closer, and — increasingly — a real second healer, all wrapped in the campaign's most relaxed showman. He announced his Crescent Aeon Stone like a JRPG super (*"Big Beam Attack! His Crescent Stone"*), covered his new handwraps in fire to punch a shadow in the face, and — his defining image this session — planted himself in the Aeon-swarm storm and stopped dodging: *"Instead of dodging, I just Brace and everything that flies into my face I catch in my hand and burn."* He **finished Rukh** with a Flying Flame, cleared most of the Greater Shadows, ran the Nimbus-ignition combo with Ferrok twice, Four-Winds-flung allies out of danger, and crit a **Battle Medicine** for 40 on Bargrum mid-ooze-fight. The other half of Bael this week was the wealth gag — the guy who *only* carries expensive potions (*"I only have moderates. Look at all the money I'm carrying around"*), reframed by the DM as *"the guy who only buys expensive wine."* When the party learned the beacon might be flawed, Bael's whole engineering philosophy fit in one line: *"If there isn't a flaw, we'll make one."*

### Raylta · The Heart That Kept Score
The conscience, the divine artillery, and — under a full session of grinding damage — the one keeping everyone standing. She ran the heal economy through six fights (rank-2-through-5 Heals, Nudge into the Cursebound spiral, Lay on Hands, Vital Beacon), read the **beacon blueprints** that reframed the whole endgame, and picked up divine offense that finally clicked: the **Holy Light that killed the Greater Shadow that stole her shadow** (*"How dare you? It's mine!"* → *"I just punch him with a holy light"*), and a rank-5 **Divine Immolation** *"divine fireball"* she was delighted to finally have a target for (even if the oozes shrugged it off on a nat-19/20). But Raylta's throughline this session was her *reluctance to enjoy the violence*: she balked at soaking Bargrum's hits (*"I'm actually starting to feel bad"*), and against the last Sacristan — a creature that would *savor* its own death — she openly hoped to whiff the killing blow (*"I hope I miss. I hope I miss."*). When a velstrac tried to claim kinship with the party, she snapped the line shut on their behalf: *"He's right — he's not one of you! ... I'm a fucking freak."* Her comedy was pure exasperated-heroine — the whole muted-turn disaster (*"I did a whole speech and I was muted. What the fuck?"*), the potion-snob standoff with rich Bael, the golden-goose gambling itch (*"I love gambling"*), and the despairing thesis of every grind fight: *"Why are we still losing when we win?"*

## Combat Encounters

> Round-by-round detail below is reconstructed from the cleaned transcript and the Foundry roll log (`rolls.txt`), and verified by the per-encounter combat-review pass. Initiative for all six encounters is from Pax's tracker.

### Encounter 1 — Okoa & Rukh (vampire-Strix)
**Initiative**: Bargrum → Bael → Raylta → Ferrok → Tivael → Rukh → Okoa

- **Enemies**: two winged **Strix** infused with Netherworld energy and turned to **undead vampires**. High physical resistance (adamantine only partly bypassed; silver/force/vitality are the levers); both self-heal ~10 HP/turn. **Okoa** — melee flyer: claws, **Vicious Flurry**, **Strafing Strike** (semi-incorporeal line dash). **Rukh** — caster: **Synesthesia** (Clumsy 3 / Dazzled on Bargrum), rank-4 **Grim Tendrils** (void line + persistent bleed).
- The party won initiative decisively (the Strix rolled a 1 and a 3). **Bargrum** opened Defensive Advance, discovered the deep physical resistance, and set up Group/Shielding Taunts, a Juggernaut Charge drag, and **two Intercepts in one round**.
- **Tivael** RK'd them (Strix / undead vampires), stuck a Stupefied on one, then a rank-3 **Fear** (both frightened), Befuddle, and Frostbite. **Bael** used Flinging Updraft (wind-jump on Ferrok), blasts, and Flying Flame. **Raylta** worked Vitality Lash (vitality vs. undead), Divine Lance, and a Nudge for Bael.
- **Ferrok killed Okoa** — an Ignition Spellstrike run that near-maxed (*"Dead, dead."*). **Bael's Flying Flame finished Rukh** (*"that is enough to break her down"*). Bargrum's Commitment-to-Protection call-out fed Ferrok a +1 AC circumstance bonus vs. the Strix (Raylta: *"clutch"*).
- *Loot*: **+2 handwraps of mighty blows**, **+1 striking astral shortbow**, +1 resilient leather armor, +1 resilient scroll robes, scroll of Grim Tendrils.

### Encounter 2 — Five Greater Shadows
**Initiative**: Bargrum → Ferrok → Tivael → Raylta → Greater Shadow B → Greater Shadow A → Bael → Greater Shadow D → Greater Shadow C → Greater Shadow E
