## Session Summary

The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty with rescued prisoners from the Bleeding Palace, only to find the settlement under attack by shadow-tainted dinosaurs driven mad by the pall of darkness overhead. Tivael, a tiny House Drake Dragonet scholar drawn to the isles by reports of Azlanti ruins and the shadow crisis, joined the fight and proved his worth immediately with debuffs and arcane force damage.

After dealing with the surface threat and a brief resupply, the party descended back through Underheaven, through the Bleeding Palace, and into the Azlanti elevator — this time riding it deeper toward the Shadow Tower. They emerged into an underground rail junction controlled by Dero, shadow-dwelling humanoids who had been subjugated by Nizca's forces. A tense negotiation with a Dero leader named Zovi yielded critical intelligence about the tower's layout and its defenders: Degg (a traitorous Dero inventor) and Nizca herself at the top.

The party pressed deeper through a haunted medical wing (poltergeists of trapped Azlanti doctors), ancient clockwork defenses, and cursed artifacts. Tivael read Azlanti medical papers revealing the tragic fate of doctors trapped during some ancient calamity. The session ended with the party battered but pressing forward, the Ebon Tower looming above.

## Key Events

### Act I — Talmandor's Bounty (Surface)
- The party arrived with rescued prisoners; the crowd was searching for the faces of the original (dead) adventuring party
- Shadow-tainted Pachycephalosaurus attacked the settlement — three of them, driven mad by the shadow pall
- **Tivael's introduction**: A tiny blue-purple scaled dragonet with a curious, haughty look, carrying books and pipes. Joined the fight without extended introductions.
- First combat established party dynamics — Bargrum intercepted for allies, Ferrok opened with Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike for 56 damage, Tivael opened with Dirge of Doom + Recall Knowledge + Bon Mot
- Shadow Expulsion (void AoE cones) hammered the party — 15-40 damage per blast to multiple members
- Tivael used Flurry of Claws for the first time — crit on first target for 34 damage (slashing + force)

### Act II — The Descent
- Party resupplied and descended through Underheaven, the Bleeding Palace, and into the Azlanti elevator
- Emerged at an underground rail junction with tracks running in multiple directions
- Encountered Dero Sentries — first fight where Tivael used Lingered Courageous Anthem
- The Dero Magister cast Noise Blast (sonic AoE, Fort save), Deafening most of the party. Tivael's compositions have the auditory trait — Deafened required flat checks to cast them
- Agonizing Despair (Will save, mental damage + Frightened 2) hit Bargrum hard
- Tivael attempted to Demoralize a Dero in Common — Foundry bugged and showed failure, but DM confirmed success
- Raylta exclaimed "This is too scary. I feel too powerful. Is it because of the ring?" after critting with Needle Darts

### Act III — Negotiation with Zovi
- The party encountered a Dero leader named **Zovi**, who had been deposed by Degg and Nizca
- Zovi offered information in exchange for being left alone
- The party negotiated terms: Zovi's people stay underground, no undead allowed, information provided about the tower
- **Tivael took point on diplomacy**: "Well, from my good friend here, I want to propose a term before we move forward with any further deal."
- Key intel gained: **Degg** is a traitorous Dero inventor, Nizca's favorite lackey. The **Shadow Beacon** is their device to blot out the sun. The tower layout: medical wing → cell block → administrative wing → Ebon Tower heights → Nizca and Degg at the top.
- Ferrok established the ultimatum: "Just to be clear, if we see you behind us, we'll assume the agreement is null."
- Zovi's parting words: "When you gut Degg, tell him Zovi says hi."

### Act IV — The Haunted Medical Wing
- The party rode the south track capsule to the medical wing
- Three **Poltergeists** — incorporeal undead, the ghosts of Azlanti doctors trapped during an ancient calamity
- Poltergeists used Telekinetic Storm (multi-target physical attack, 3d12 per target) devastating the party — Raylta took 32 + 28 = 60 damage from focused strikes
- Ferrok used Ghost Oil on his weapon to bypass incorporeal resistance
- Tivael used Flurry of Claws (force damage bypasses incorporeal!) — crit for 37 damage on one poltergeist
- Worm's Repast landed on Poltergeist C — Off-Guard condition applied
- Raylta's Divine Lance (spirit damage) was effective against the poltergeists
- Bargrum intercepted a 40-damage Telekinetic Storm hit meant for Ferrok
- Raylta: "I love the bromance up there. We're fighting for our lives down here."

### Act V — Traps and Treasures
- A statue in an alcove triggered "Let Me Out!" — a mental trap that forced Bargrum to save or deal 4d10+18 damage to himself. He passed.
- Tivael attempted Dispel Magic (rank 2) on enchanted doors — narrowly failed (needed 26, hero pointed but still missed)
- Bargrum touched the door and took a Telepathic Jolt — 40 mental damage and Stupefied 1 for 24 hours. "24 hours?! Are you okay? All my spells — I can't cast them now! Wait, what spells? Honestly..."
- The key worked on the door, revealing a pristine Azlanti sitting room preserved through time
- Tivael read the **Medical Papers** (in Azlanti): written by Dr. Elimaria, a priest of Issachar, documenting being trapped in the medical wing with two colleagues (Beliana and Fernius) after a calamity collapsed the tunnels. The three poltergeists were likely these three trapped doctors.
- Found: Romance Novel (100gp), Framed Painting (50gp), Aeon Stones, healing equipment

### Act VI — Clockwork Soldiers
- Three **Clockwork Soldiers** — Azlanti constructs guarding the facility
- One was instantly destroyed by a trap/environmental effect (took 100 damage)
- These constructs had physical resistance — Needle Darts and Strikes were reduced by 5
- Tivael's Electric Arc was devastating (electricity vs constructs, 33 damage to one in a single cast)
- Raylta used Security Badge's Authoritative Command (1/day) — attempting to force a construct to stop
- The fight was grindy — constructs had high HP and Activate Defenses (+2 AC to self or ally)
- Bargrum continued absorbing massive hits via Intercept Attack
- Session ended mid-exploration, party battered but alive, pressing deeper toward the Ebon Tower

### Things Tivael knows that the party does not
- The medical papers detail Dr. Elimaria's desperation — a priest of Issachar who just wanted to see the sun again. The poltergeists were these trapped doctors. There's a melancholy to their destruction that the party may not fully appreciate.
- The Security Badge grants a daily Authoritative Command (1A, incapacitation, forces target to stop and drop items on failed Will save). Haven't used it yet.

### What Tivael is thinking about
- **Void damage is a problem.** Shadow Expulsion hit everyone for 15-40 void damage. The Vital Amplification Aeon Stone (resistance 5 void) would directly mitigate this. Bargrum claimed it — probably the right call since he takes the most damage, but Tivael is also vulnerable.
- **Deafened shuts down compositions.** Noise Blast Deafened the party. Compositions are auditory — flat check required to cast while Deafened. Need a plan for this. Sound Body can remove Deafened, but it costs a rank 2 slot.
- **The tower layout is known now.** Medical wing (cleared) → cell block (prisoners in stasis) → administrative wing (Nizca's allies live there) → Ebon Tower heights (Dero, then vampires, then Nizca and Degg at the top). Long road ahead.
- **Clockwork constructs have physical resistance.** Electric Arc and force damage bypass it. Needle Darts and regular Strikes are reduced by 5. If more constructs appear, prioritize electricity and force.
