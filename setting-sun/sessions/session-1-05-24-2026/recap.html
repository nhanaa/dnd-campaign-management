Tivael joined the party at Talmandor's Bounty as shadow-tainted dinosaurs attacked the settlement. After a violent welcome, the party descended through the Azlanti elevator into a Dero-controlled rail junction, where a shrewd negotiation with the deposed leader Zovi yielded critical intelligence about the Ebon Tower, its master Nizca, and the traitorous inventor Degg. The path deeper led through a haunted medical wing — poltergeists of ancient Azlanti doctors trapped during a long-forgotten calamity — and past clockwork defenders still guarding ruins that no longer need protecting.

The tiny dragon scholar played his pipes, scattered force-claws through incorporeal ghosts, read Azlanti medical papers the rest of the party couldn't decipher, and proved that the smallest member of the party might be the most impactful.

Timeline

Act I · Combat · Talmandor's Bounty

Shadow-Tainted Pachycephalosaurus × 3

  • Tivael joined the fight mid-attack — "Protect me" — and opened with Dirge of Doom + Recall Knowledge + Bon Mot in a single turn. The textbook Bard opener.
  • RK revealed: no weaknesses/resistances, best save Fort, worst save Will.
  • Ferrok opened with Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike (3-action) for 56 damage — the biggest single hit of the session.
  • Shadow Expulsion (30-ft cone, void AoE) hit the party twice — 30 and 40 raw damage. Tivael took 55 total.
  • Tivael's Flurry of Claws crit on the first target for 34 damage (slashing + force). First blood for the tiny dragon.
  • Raylta crit with Needle Darts for 34 damage — "Is it because of the ring? Oh my god, the power is getting to my head."
  • Bargrum intercepted a hit meant for Ferrok, absorbing 16 damage. Business as usual.
Act II · Exploration · The Descent

Through Underheaven to the Elevator

  • Party resupplied at Talmandor's Bounty and distributed loot from previous sessions.
  • Descended through Underheaven, the Bleeding Palace, and loaded into the Azlanti elevator.
  • Emerged at an underground rail junction with tracks running in multiple directions.
  • Bael used Four Winds to reposition the party into formation before exploration.
Act III · Combat · Dero Sentries + Magister

The Rail Junction Defenders

  • Tivael used Lingered Courageous Anthem for the first time, then switched to Rallying Anthem when AoE pressure mounted.
  • Dero Magister cast Noise Blast — sonic AoE, Fort save — Deafening most of the party.
  • Deafened compositions require a flat check to cast (auditory trait). Tivael managed to keep Rallying Anthem running.
  • Agonizing Despair hit Bargrum for 17 mental damage + Frightened 2.
  • Tivael used Electric Arc against two targets and Daze (from Pipes of Compulsion) for mental damage.
  • Bael's Flying Flame hit the Magister + Sentry B for 20 damage each — "It's finally happening! The year of the spellcasters!"
  • Ferrok crit for 40 damage on a Dero Sentry. The table went "O.O"
Act IV · Social · Negotiation with Zovi

The Deposed Dero Leader

  • Met Zovi, a Dero leader deposed by Degg (traitorous inventor) and stuck underground.
  • Bargrum insisted on formal terms before accepting information — "I think it would be distasteful for Bargrum to not come to terms."
  • Tivael took point on diplomacy: "From my good friend here, I want to propose a term before we move forward with any further deal."
  • Terms agreed: Zovi's people stay underground, no undead, information provided. Mutual non-interference.
  • Intel gained: Degg is Nizca's lackey, invented the Shadow Beacon. Tower layout revealed — medical wing → cell block → admin wing → Ebon Tower → Nizca at the top.
  • Ferrok delivered the threat: "If we see you behind us, we'll assume the agreement is null."
  • Zovi's parting words: "When you gut Degg, tell him Zovi says hi."
Act V · Combat · The Haunted Medical Wing

Poltergeists × 3

  • Three incorporeal undead — the ghosts of Azlanti doctors (Elimaria, Beliana, Fernius) trapped during an ancient calamity.
  • Telekinetic Storm devastated the backline — Raylta took 60 damage (32 + 28) from focused strikes.
  • Ferrok applied Ghost Oil to his weapon — smart tactical adaptation for incorporeal enemies.
  • Tivael's Flurry of Claws (force damage) crit for 37 damage against a poltergeist. Force bypasses incorporeal resistance.
  • Worm's Repast landed on Poltergeist C — Off-Guard condition applied, Fort save targeting.
  • Bargrum intercepted a 40-damage Telekinetic Storm hit meant for Ferrok.
  • Raylta: "I love the bromance up there. We're fighting for our lives down here."
  • Raylta's rank 4 AoE Heal for 59 HP saved the party from collapse — "Here's my Hail Mary."
Act VI · Exploration · Traps and Treasures

The Preserved Azlanti Wing

  • A cursed statue triggered "Let Me Out!" — Bargrum saved against 34 mental damage.
  • Tivael attempted Dispel Magic (rank 2) on enchanted doors — needed 26, failed even with hero point.
  • Bargrum touched the door: Telepathic Jolt — 40 mental damage + Stupefied 1 for 24 hours. "Wait, what spells?"
  • The key opened the door to a pristine Azlanti sitting room preserved through time.
  • Tivael read the Medical Papers (Azlanti) — Dr. Elimaria's account of being trapped with two colleagues after a calamity. The three poltergeists were these doctors.
  • Found: Aeon Stones, healing equipment, a 100gp romance novel, and a duplicate Cloak of Illusions (Tivael already owns one).
Act VII · Combat · Clockwork Soldiers × 3

Ancient Azlanti Constructs

  • Three constructs with physical resistance 5 — Needle Darts and Strikes reduced.
  • One destroyed instantly by environmental trap (100 damage).
  • Tivael's Electric Arc dealt 33 damage to one — electricity bypasses physical resistance.
  • Constructs used Activate Defenses (+2 AC to self or adjacent ally) — tactical shielding.
  • Grindy multi-round fight. Bargrum's shield took 11 damage from blocking.
  • Session ended with the last construct falling. Party battered, pressing deeper.
