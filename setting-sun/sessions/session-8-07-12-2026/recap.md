## Session Summary

This was the end of the climb. After a quick resupply stop at Talmandor's Bounty — level-10 gear finally within reach, everyone loading up on moderate and greater potions — the party took the last stairs and walked into a floor that was one enormous **treasure vault**. It belonged to **Viseldron the Fortune Dragon**, a gem-bedecked young-adult emerald dragon whom Nizca and Deg had *hired* to guard the final ascent. There was a genuine parley first — Bael addressing the "distinguished gentleman," Bargrum offering to reinvest the hoard into renovations, Tivael reminding a sun-hating dragon about the merits of vitamin D — but Viseldron had been paid, and gold glimmers even under a blotted sky. What followed was a full-length dragon fight: a rank-5 Fireball opener that scorched the clustered party, four **Animated Treasure Swarms** lashing everyone with sharp art objects, Disruptive Breath, Draconic Frenzy, and a treasure-shedding Share the Wealth. Tivael spent the whole fight running a bit — the tiny Dragonet challenging the "real" dragon to prove who was superior — while Ferrok, Bael, and Bargrum ground down the hoard. **Ferrok ran Viseldron through** as **Bael's Thermal Nimbus fire** and Ferrok's blade cooked it down to single digits (Raylta's cold Interstellar Void kept it Fatigued the whole time), and the party went swimming in the coins.

Then the last stairs, and the pinnacle. The **Shadow Beacon** — an irregular spire of brass, wire, and black crystal — was already lit and screaming shadows into the sky, with **Deg** cavorting at its base and **Nizca**, the vampire lord of the Ebon Tower, waiting with a smile. There was a real confrontation: Nizca's tragic villain monologue (thousands of years in stasis, an ocean between them and their living lover, the whole sun-blotting plan a love letter), and the party's flat refusal — Bargrum's answer the sharpest of the campaign. Then the gut-punch: **Speckle**, the three-eyed homunculus the party had adopted as a pet, was revealed to be **Deg's spy the whole time**, and shattered apart into the **Aeon Stone Swarm** they now had to fight. Raylta spent the battle grieving her pet even as it attacked her. Nizca opened with a **Vampiric Exsanguination that hit Tivael for 98** — he survived only because **Bael had pre-bought and pre-cast party-wide Death Ward from scrolls** (it upgraded the crit-fail to a fail and cut the hit to 39). Deg activated the beacon into a set of expanding **Shadow Discharge hazards**, and the finale became two problems at once: kill the bosses *and* disable the device before it finished. **Ferrok killed Deg**; **Bargrum dropped Nizca to zero** — but Nizca used **Spiteful Escape** to fuse their soul into the beacon rather than die, and the machine got worse. The last several rounds were a pure disable-the-device gauntlet under escalating void and cold, everyone rolling skill checks, Raylta swearing she was too stupid to crack it — until she rolled the final counteract (a **31**). The beacon combusted in black flame, Nizca's skull-faced soul was rent apart with it, and the shadows lifted like fog. For the first time in months, the sun hit their skin.

The epilogue: the party looted the two villains, found a lovingly-painted portrait of Nizca's lover **Lograsi** (who turns out to be a doomed figure from Raylta's *other* campaign), gave their after-the-war send-offs, and — six months later — received letters sealed with the image of a **phoenix**. That is the end of Shades of Blood.

## Key Events

### Act I — Shopping and the Last Stairs (RP / Downtime)
- A quick hop back to **Talmandor's Bounty** to resupply before the top. The party is absurdly rich and finally shopping in the right bracket — Bael pushing everyone to stop carrying lesser potions (*"Moderates are only 50g, and greaters are only 400, so we're fine"*), rune transfers and swaps traded around, Bael eyeing a level-5 summon and Raylta talking level-10 items.
- Bargrum, in-character deadpan, floats getting a tattoo (Raylta: *"Anybody want to get a tattoo?"*), and the group divvies leftover armor, scroll robes, and dragon-scale trophies.
- They take the elevator/stairs up. The floor is quiet, the eastern wall crumbled into darkness, 40-foot ceilings — and then coins. A *gargantuan* hoard of gold, gems, statuettes, and art objects, deliberately arranged. Bargrum, sensing the scene: *"Stand by for exposition."*

### Act II — Viseldron, the Fortune Dragon (Combat + Parley)
- A booming voice: *"Have you come to bask in the majesty of Viseldron?"* An emerald dragon, its scales adorned with gems, coins, and rings worn like jewelry, looks the party over.
- **The parley.** Bael, courtly: *"This is your hoard, I presume? Distinguished gentleman?"* Viseldron confirms Nizca ("Master Niska") and Deg are above — and that it has been *paid* to keep the party from reaching them. Bargrum needles the pittance ("this fortune is all gonna go into renovations"; Viseldron: *"My dear dwarf, that is what taxes are for"*), Ferrok challenges the logic of ruling a sunless world ("No sun on your scales?"), Tivael pitches vitamin D. Viseldron: *"What does it matter, the absence of the sun, when the glimmer of gold holds so much more sway."* Then it animates its own hoard.
- **Initiative** (per tracker): Tivael → Viseldron → Ferrok → Treasure Swarm → Bael → Bargrum → Treasure Swarm → Raylta → Treasure Swarm → Treasure Swarm. Four **Animated Treasure Swarms** plus the dragon; the party was bunched in the middle at the start.
- **Viseldron opens with a rank-5 Fireball** that catches the whole clustered party (Tivael 20, Raylta 35, Bargrum 40, Ferrok 40, Bael 30). Ferrok, deadpan: *"Just a dragon, guys. We're fine."*
- **Tivael runs the "superior dragon" bit all fight.** He challenges Viseldron in melee, crits his Lingering Composition (a 4-round anthem), drops his instruments, quaffs a Potion of Quickness, and later Evangelizes and Synesthesias the dragon while narrating: *"Well, hello, dragon. I am the superior dragon… I shall give you my present as a fellow dragon."*
- The **Treasure Swarms** carve the party with **Blade Storm** (piercing/slashing to everyone in their space) and **Trash the Place** (bludgeoning bursts). Bargrum group-Shielding-Taunts them, Intercepts for Raylta and Bael, and — improbably — **lands a trip on the dragon itself** (his Titan Wrestler makes a Tiny dwarf tripping a dragon possible), knocking Viseldron Prone and forcing it to burn an action standing. He aids the frontline with **Commitment to Protection** (Warfare Lore).
- **Bael** sets his **Thermal Nimbus** (*"So everybody stay close if you don't want to be hurt by fire"*), sends flaming-birdie Flying Flames at the swarms, and finishes with a signature combo: *"I use the bird to round them up all in one place, then send a blast to kill them all at the same time."*
- **Raylta** runs triage — heals (perpetually targeting Ferrok, *"because you get hit so much!"*), and casts her cold **Interstellar Void** on Viseldron, sustaining it to keep the dragon **Fatigued** while its Fort save shrugs the actual damage. (The fire aura that grinds the dragon down is Bael's **Thermal Nimbus** + persistent fire, not the Void.)
- **Viseldron** throws two **Disruptive Breaths** (force cone), **Draconic Frenzy** (two claws + tail, a claw landing on Bael), and — the scariest beat — a reaction **Capture Spell** that crit-succeeds against one of Tivael's spells to **steal back a spent 5th-rank slot** (a nasty surprise it died before it could spend). Mid-fight it also uses **Share the Wealth**, shaking its treasure off its body in a 30-foot spray of coins and gems.
- **Ferrok kills the dragon** with a straight sword-strike (*"you run the dragon through as it falls"*). **Tivael's payoff:** *"Take that, stupid dragon. Just because you're bigger than me doesn't mean you're better."* The party then dives into the hoard — Ferrok narrating Bargrum *"swimming through it like Scrooge McDuck,"* Bael calling dibs-jumpers late, Raylta *"showering in coins."*

### Act III — The Pinnacle, Nizca and Deg (RP / Confrontation)
- The top of the tower, spires reaching into the Netherworld. The **Shadow Beacon** — brass, wire, black crystal, motes of raw magic — is *already active*, shrieking shadow into the sky. **Deg** cavorts at its base; **Nizca** turns, a rare smile on their somber face.
- **Nizca's monologue:** *"Well, if it isn't the ones who have been ransacking my tower… We've already completed the Shadow Beacon, and this time it's perfect… The shadows of the Netherworld will pour into the sky, blotting out the sun for an eternity… Surrender and I'll allow you to leave with your lives."*
- **The refusal.** Tivael: *"We will stop you here today."* Ferrok: *"We didn't come all this way just to give up now."* **Bargrum lands the party's answer:** *"There are people in this world whose lives burn too brightly to be overshadowed by your love. We'll put a stop to you."* Nizca, stung — *"What would you know of my love, dwarf?"* — and gives the tragedy plainly: thousands of years alone in stasis, an ocean now between them and the lover who first showed them the Netherworld's magic, whom they can still *sense* waiting across the sea. Tivael: *"Do you think they'd be happy with what you're doing here?"* Bael: *"Then after we take care of you, maybe we'll find them."* Nizca: *"All the more reason I shall gut you here and now."*
- **The Speckle betrayal.** Deg cackles — he's had eyes on the party the whole way up: *"Had eyes on the back of my head, I do. Or in the back of yours, I should say."* **Speckle**, the party's adopted three-eyed homunculus pet (originally Deg's creation), glows and **shatters into the Aeon Stone Swarm.** Raylta, immediately: *"See — I told you we should have killed it. No, it's not true!"*
- Ferrok dedicates the fight to the fallen: *"Remember, everybody, we're doing this for Clinch and Nihkos"* — the original party that TPK'd in the Bleeding Palace.

### Act IV — The Shadow Beacon (Combat + Skill Gauntlet)
- **Initiative** (per tracker): Tivael → Bargrum → Raylta → Bael → Nizca → Ferrok → Deg → Aeon Stone Swarm. Raylta pops **Oracular Warning** for the party's initiative and temp HP. Two **Shadow Discharge** hazards later roll in (Deg activates the beacon mid-fight) and join a round later.
- **Nizca opens brutal:** a **Vampiric Exsanguination** cone that would have one-shot Tivael — **98 damage** (a crit-fail) — but **Bael's pre-cast party-wide Death Ward** upgraded it to a fail and its void resistance cut the hit to a survivable 39. The scare set the tone. (Bael had bought five Death Ward scrolls for 900 gp and dosed the whole party before the fight; stacked with Thermal Nimbus, Tivael sat at "resistance 20 from death" — Bargrum: *"money well spent."*)
- **Tivael runs a two-boss lockdown all fight:** **Slow on both Nizca and Deg** (halving each boss's actions), **Synesthesia on both** (Clumsy 3, −10 ft Speed, and — the decisive rider — *everything Concealed from them*), **Dirge of Doom** (Frightened), and **Evangelize** (Stupefied on Nizca). Only Bon Mot bounced (Nizca's high Will). Bael opens on the swarm with **Solar Detonation** — *"Since you want to blot out the sun so bad, I guess you want a taste of it. Here you go."* Ferrok self-Hastes and pours **Ignition Spellstrikes** into Deg.
- **Raylta grieves Speckle in real time** while the swarm attacks her: *"But they were my friends! We bonded… I learned from the best… He would have wanted this."* Bargrum, less sentimental, kills swarm fragments with *"Sending off speckles in a spectacular fashion."*
- **Deg activates the Shadow Beacon** as a free action — magical darkness floods the arena and two **Shadow Discharge** hazards spawn (Fort-save void/cold AoE that *intensifies each time a boss falls*). The DM reframes the win condition: the device must be **disabled** (Crafting, Thievery, Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion — a 2-action Disable Device, doable from anywhere on the map). Raylta reads it with Recall Knowledge and relays the plan.
- **Ferrok kills Deg** — his Sure Strike + Ignition Spellstrike crit chain (56/64/50) *softened* a Deg already Slowed, Synesthesia'd (Clumsy 3), and Off-Guard from Tivael (*"he's not out yet"*), and a plain follow-up strike finished him a round later. *"With Deg dead, the energies of the beacon start to go haywire"* — the discharge damage climbs.
- The party grinds Nizca down under focus fire and Bargrum's taunts. **Bargrum's Shielding-Taunt strike drops Nizca to 0** — but Nizca **evaporates into black smoke and uses Spiteful Escape**, flying *into* the beacon to fuse their soul with it rather than die. The discharges intensify again (more damage, higher DC).
- **The disable gauntlet.** With both bosses gone, it's a pure skill challenge under escalating void/cold: each of the two discharges needs three successful disables. **Charge A**: Bargrum (1/3) → Bael (2/3) → **Ferrok completes it**. **Charge B**: Tivael (1/3, self-Guidance + Arcana) → Bael (2/3, aiding on both charges) → Raylta's finish. Raylta rolls and rolls and misses along the way (*"Maybe I'm too stupid to disable it. Maybe I'll stop trying."* Bael: *"We're just gonna let you keep working on it."*).
- **Raylta lands the finish.** With a Guidance bonus stacked on, she rolls a **31** — success. *"I now know what to do."* The device sputters, showers black sparks, and combusts in black flame. *"The last thing you see is the skull-like face of Nizca screaming as their soul is rent apart with the destruction of the Shadow Beacon."*

### Act V — Sunrise, Send-offs, and a Phoenix (Epilogue)
- The shadows lift like dissipating fog. Sunlight hits their skin for the first time in months. Bael: *"I miss this."* Ferrok: *"This is what we were fighting for."* Bael, seconds later: *"nothing is like the sun… I'm going back inside. This is terrible."* — Raylta drags Bargrum out into it (*"You grumpy old man, come over here"*).
- **Loot from the villains** (below). Among Nizca's belongings: a **painting of their lover, Lograsi**, rendered with obsessive care. The reveal — Lograsi is a figure from **Raylta's player's other campaign (Revenge of the Runelords)**, where that party *killed* him. His last words there: *"I'll be with you soon, Nizca."* The tragedy completes itself; the table sits with the bittersweet.
- **The DM asks each PC what they do in the months after** (see the Character Spotlights for each send-off). Raylta confirms she is **staying on Talmandor's Bounty and not continuing to the next AP** — a new character is coming.
- **The hook:** legend spreads of the heroes who kept the sun from being extinguished. Six months later, letters arrive at each of their lodgings, sealed in wax stamped with the image of a **phoenix.** *"And that is the end of Shades of Blood, everyone. Thank you for playing."* (Next: Fists of the Ruby Phoenix, starting at level 11.)

## Character Spotlight

### Tivael · the Superior Dragon
The finale gave Pax the thematic payoff the whole campaign had been building toward, and he leaned all the way in. Faced with an actual dragon many times his size, the tiny Dragonet spent the entire Viseldron fight running a bit — **challenging the "real" dragon to prove which of them was superior**, opening with a melee challenge (*"I'm going to show him who's the superior dragon"*), narrating his casts as a smaller dragon's condescension (*"Well, hello, dragon. I am the superior dragon… I shall give you my present as a fellow dragon"* — Synesthesia), and closing over its corpse with *"Take that, stupid dragon. Just because you're bigger than me doesn't mean you're better."* It's the single most **Tivael** sequence of the campaign.

And mechanically he was **the control engine that decided the finale** — not flavor, the actual load-bearing work. In the dragon fight his **Synesthesia** hit Viseldron with Clumsy/Off-Guard that set up Ferrok's 57-damage Ignition Spellstrike (Ferrok, out loud: *"Because of clumsy"*) — the single biggest swing of that encounter. At the beacon he **locked down both bosses at once**: **Slow on *both* Deg and Nizca** (halving their actions), **Synesthesia on *both*** (Clumsy 3 — and, crucially, *everything Concealed from them*), Dirge for Frightened, Evangelize for Stupefied. That concealment is why **Nizca kept whiffing its Channel Smites** on DC-5 flat checks — which is what made the DM openly *"salty,"* and why Tivael's cheeky *"you failed all my big spells"* was a victory lap, not an apology (the *boss* was failing the saves against *his* spells). Only Bon Mot bounced, off Nizca's high Will. He also **ate the two scariest hits of the night** — the 98-damage Vampiric Exsanguination and a later 68-damage discharge crit — surviving the first only because **Bael's pre-cast party-wide Death Ward** turned his crit-fail into a fail. (He still adores Fireball even when it's the *dragon's* rank-5 landing on him: *"Man, Fireball is so OP."*) His epilogue is pure Tivael: bard *and* researcher, he **writes a song about the journey** and travels to spread the story — *"he saw a dragon and beat a dragon, as a smaller dragon who aspired to be a real dragon someday."* His finale reflection was honest and warm: *"My first finished AP, I guess."* He carries on into Ruby Phoenix.

### Bargrum · the Load-Bearing Heart
Bargrum was, as ever, the wall — group/Shielding Taunt on the treasure swarms and the beacon fight, Intercept and **Energy Interceptor** eating cold and fire for Raylta and Bael, Defensive Advance, Juggernaut Charge, and — improbably — **actually tripping the dragon** (his Titan Wrestler made it stick, knocking Viseldron Prone and costing it an action). His self-aware guardian comedy landed all night: *"Sorry if I'm taking everybody else's hits."* But the reason he's the party's heart is the RP. He carried the **dragon parley** (the renovations bit, the sunbathing-dragon logic — *"gold will not glimmer"* under a blotted sky) and, more importantly, he delivered **the party's answer to the villain**: *"There are people in this world whose lives burn too brightly to be overshadowed by your love. We'll put a stop to you."* That single line reframed the whole confrontation — meeting Nizca's tragedy with a principled refusal rather than a taunt. His **Shielding-Taunt strike is the blow that dropped Nizca to zero** (triggering Spiteful Escape), and he rolled one of the three beacon disables. His send-off fits the Eagle Knight exactly: he **sends a copy of the Lograsi sketch to the Pathfinder Society**, reports back to the Eagle Knights, *visits some graves*, and heads back out.

### Ferrok · the Finisher of Record
Ferrok closed the campaign the way he ran it: the deadpan comic who quietly kills everything. His *"Just a dragon, guys. We're fine"* opened the Viseldron fight, and his **straight sword-strike put the dragon down** at the end. In the beacon fight he was the damage that mattered on the clock — self-Haste, **Ignition Spellstrikes into a Slowed, Synesthesia'd Deg** (his crit chain softened Deg; a plain strike finished him), Force Fang, Emergency Targe, Fire Shield, a Reactive-Strike crit, and repeated Ignition Spellstrikes into Nizca, holding through a genuinely cursed stretch of misses with grace — and even a flicker of sympathy for the doomed villains (*"I feel kind of bad for them now"*). He supplied the session's best physical-comedy image (Bargrum *"swimming through it like Scrooge McDuck"*), the war-dead dedication (*"we're doing this for Clinch and Nihkos"*), and the quiet emotional beat when the sun returned: *"This is what we were fighting for."* His epilogue is on-brand for the gladiator: **drinks to celebrate the victory, then takes up whatever fights he can** to keep training for whatever comes next. He continues into Ruby Phoenix (excited to use the new Magus rules).

### Bael · the Showman with the Sun in His Hand
Bael opened the campaign's last fights and closed a lot of them. He ran the **dragon parley's opening line** (*"This is your hoard, I presume? Distinguished gentleman?"*), set his **Thermal Nimbus** to protect and chip, and executed his signature Flying-Flame roundup on the treasure swarms (*"round them up all in one place, then send a blast to kill them all at the same time"*). Against Nizca and the Speckle-swarm he opened with **Solar Detonation** and the perfect line for it: *"Since you want to blot out the sun so bad, I guess you want a taste of it. Here you go."* But his quiet MVP play was **foresight**: he spent **900 gold on five Death Ward scrolls and dosed the entire party before the beacon fight** — the single reason Tivael and the front line survived Nizca's void alpha strikes (Bargrum, later: *"money well spent"*). He was also the party's genuine second healer — Treat Wounds on Tivael, greater healer's gloves on Bargrum, potions passed around (*"I'll extend my hand and ask if you want a drink"*) — and he **disabled on both beacon charges**, completing one and aiding Raylta's finish. His finale voice was the best comic register at the table: the moment the sun returned he basked, then flipped instantly to *"nothing is like the sun… I'm going back inside. This is terrible,"* needling Bargrum as a *"grumpy old man."* His send-off: **learning what his new powers can do, hunting down his favorite potion seller, and enjoying being rich** — he's staying, taking it easy, and (probably) continuing into Ruby Phoenix.

### Raylta · the Heart Who Buried Her Pet
Raylta ran the heal economy through both grueling fights — rank 1-4 Heals, Nudge, Quickened-Casting a rank-3 Heal, **Cloak of Light** as an anti-vampire aura against Nizca, potion patches, Vital Beacon high-fives — and she carried the session's emotional through-line *and* landed its final blow. The emotional line was **Speckle**: the pet she adopted turned out to be Deg's spy and became the Aeon Stone Swarm, and she spent the fight openly grieving it while it tried to kill her (*"But they were my friends! We bonded… I learned from the best… He would have wanted this"*) — a tragicomic beat only she could have carried. Her tactical reads stayed sharp under it all (*"Wait, Nizca's a vampire, right? So I can use Cloak of Light so they can't get near me"*), and her healer comedy was pure Raylta (perpetually pre-targeting Ferrok *"because you get hit so much!"*; cutting a potion-happy Bargrum off, *"you're so addicted, we're cutting you off"*). Then, at the very end, after rolling miss after miss on the beacon and declaring herself *"too stupid to disable it,"* **she rolled the 31 that destroyed the Shadow Beacon and ended Nizca** — the literal last act of Shades of Blood, hers. Fittingly, her epilogue is the campaign's quiet goodbye: she **reports to the Bountiful Venture, object-reads the whole Ebon Tower's story before the loot is confiscated, says her farewells, and stays on Talmandor's Bounty.** She's the one player stepping away — a new character joins for Ruby Phoenix. A hell of a note to leave on.

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Viseldron the Fortune Dragon + 4 Animated Treasure Swarms
**Enemies:** Viseldron (young-adult emerald "Fortune" dragon, spellcaster) + Animated Treasure Swarms A/B/C/D.
**Initiative:** Tivael → Viseldron → Ferrok → Treasure Swarm → Bael → Bargrum → Treasure Swarm → Raylta → Treasure Swarm → Treasure Swarm.

- **Round 1.** Tivael crit-lands Lingering Composition (4 rounds), Rallying Anthem, drops instruments, drinks a **Potion of Quickness** (Bargrum quaffs one too). A treasure swarm delays to act after the dragon; **Viseldron opens with a rank-5 Fireball** into the clustered party (Tivael 20, Raylta 35, Bargrum 40, Ferrok 40, Bael 30 — two 40s turn one) and Shields itself. The swarms Stride in and start **Blade Storm**-ing everyone in their spaces. Ferrok answers with **Ignition Spellstrike**; Bael sets **Thermal Nimbus** and sends flaming birdies at the swarms; Bargrum group-Shielding-Taunts and Intercepts.
- **Mid rounds.** The party focuses the swarms down one at a time (Ferrok's Ignition/Shielding Strikes and Bael's Flying-Flame roundup do the bulk), while Tivael works the dragon — his **Synesthesia lands Clumsy/Off-Guard that teed up Ferrok's 57-damage Ignition Spellstrike** (*"Because of clumsy"*), the fight's biggest single hit. **Bargrum trips the dragon** (Titan Wrestler — Viseldron goes Prone and must Stand), Group-Taunts, and aids with **Commitment to Protection**; **Bael Demoralizes it**; Raylta lands and sustains her cold **Interstellar Void** to keep Viseldron **Fatigued** (its Fort shrugs the damage). The dragon casts a **second rank-5 Fireball** and **Disruptive Breath** (used twice across the fight), and — the scariest beat — reacts with **Capture Spell**, crit-succeeding once to steal back a spent 5th-rank slot (which it died before spending). The swarms rack up chip damage with Blade Storm and **Trash the Place**; Bargrum and Raylta Intercept the worst of it.
- **Late.** Viseldron uses **Share the Wealth** (30-ft treasure spray) and closes to melee — **Draconic Frenzy** (two claws + tail; a claw catches Bael). Ground to single digits under **Bael's Thermal Nimbus fire** + persistent fire and Ferrok's blade, it falls to **Ferrok's plain finishing strike** — "you run the dragon through as it falls." Bael had already rounded up and blasted the last treasure swarms.
- **Result:** Dragon and all four swarms destroyed (swarm kills: Bargrum ×1, Ferrok ×1, Bael ×2; **dragon: Ferrok**). The party takes the hoard. Heavy chip damage all around, but **no PC dropped** — the two 40-damage Fireballs and relentless Blade Storm were the real strain.

### Encounter 2 — Nizca & Deg at the Shadow Beacon (+ Aeon Stone Swarm / Shadow Discharges)
**Enemies:** Nizca (Strigoi vampire, they/them — void caster), Deg (Dero inventor — energy-expulsor gauntlet + cannon), the Aeon Stone Swarm (formerly "Speckle"), and two **Shadow Discharge** magical hazards spawned by the beacon.
**Initiative:** Tivael → Bargrum → Raylta → Bael → Nizca → Ferrok → Deg → Aeon Stone Swarm. (Shadow Discharge hazards roll in mid-fight and act a round later.)

- **Round 1.** Raylta pre-empts with **Oracular Warning** (party init + temp HP). Tivael lands **Slow** on Nizca and sets Lingering. The party pre-buffs and spreads (Bargrum drinks, taunts all three, raises shield; Raylta Benediction/Shield; Ferrok/Bael prep) — all standing on **Bael's pre-cast party-wide Death Ward** (five scrolls, cast before combat). Bael opens the swarm with **Solar Detonation**. **Nizca, though Slowed, casts Vampiric Exsanguination** — a void cone that crit-fails Tivael for **98**, downgraded to a fail and cut to **39** by the Death Ward, and chunks Bargrum (39), Ferrok (39), Raylta (14, saved).
- **Mid rounds.** Ferrok self-Hastes and focuses **Deg** with Ignition Spellstrikes; Bael flying-flames Deg and the swarm; **Tivael Slows and Synesthesias *both* bosses** (Clumsy 3 + concealment), Dirges them Frightened, and Stupefies Nizca with Evangelize; Raylta works **Cloak of Light** (anti-vampire aura), heals, and Recall-Knowledges the hazards. **Deg activates the Shadow Beacon** as a free action → magical darkness + two **Shadow Discharge** hazards (Fort-save void/cold AoE that scales up **each time a boss dies**). The DM reframes the objective: the device must be **disabled** (Crafting/Thievery/Arcana/Nature/Occultism/Religion, 2-action, from anywhere on the map).
- **Bosses fall.** **Ferrok kills Deg** — a Sure Strike + Ignition crit chain (56/64/50) softened the Slowed, Off-Guard Deg (*"he's not out yet"*), and a plain ~24 strike finished him a round later; the beacon's energies go haywire (+1d6). The party grinds Nizca under focus fire and Bargrum's taunts; Nizca self-heals ~15/round (Fast Healing) and lands Channel Smite / Shadow Blast, but keeps **whiffing Channel Smites on the concealment flat checks Tivael's Synesthesia forces** (the DM: *"this fight's making me salty"*). The discharges intensify again (+1d6, +2 DC).
- **The disable gauntlet.** With both villains gone, the last several rounds are a pure skill challenge under escalating void/cold. Each of two discharges needs three successful disables — **Charge A**: Bargrum (1/3) → Bael (2/3) → **Ferrok completes it**; **Charge B**: Tivael (1/3, self-Guidance + Arcana) → Bael (2/3) → **Raylta's 31 (Guidance-boosted)** finishes it. After a long miss streak (*"maybe I'm too stupid to disable it"*), Raylta lands the last roll of the campaign.
- **Result:** Shadow Beacon destroyed; Nizca's fused soul rent apart with it; Deg dead; Speckle-swarm cleared. The shadow pall over the sky dissolves. Never a true near-TPK — the two-healer economy + Death Ward + Bargrum's Intercept kept every PC off the floor; the real threat was the **clock**, and Tivael's control is what beat it. **End of Shades of Blood.**

### Things Tivael knows that the party does not

### What Tivael is thinking about
- **Bael's Death Ward scrolls were the difference.** The 98-damage Exsanguination was a crit-fail that would have dropped me; the pre-cast Death Ward upgraded it to a fail and cut it to 39. When a boss telegraphs a void/death alpha strike, buy the scrolls in advance — it's the whole reason I'm standing.
- **Clustering nearly cost us again** — Viseldron's Fireball caught the whole party bunched up (same lesson as Jubilant's screams and the Sacristan bleed). Spread out against AoE bosses.
- **My control lane won the beacon fight.** Synesthesia is the MVP against a single boss: Clumsy 3 is nice, but the *concealment* rider is what made Nizca miss — everything is Concealed from the target, so it flat-checks to hit anyone, and Nizca kept failing. Slow landed on both bosses (action denial). Only Bon Mot bounced (Nizca's high Will) — skip it vs high-Will enemies and lead Synesthesia/Slow.
- The win condition wasn't damage — it was the **disable check**. When the objective is an object, pivot the whole party to it early rather than tunnel-visioning the boss (the DM literally warned us about boss tunnel vision).
