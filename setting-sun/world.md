# World — Setting Sun, Rising Phoenix

## Campaign Overview

- **AP**: Shades of Blood (Books 1-3) → Fists of the Ruby Phoenix (Books 4+)
- **System**: Pathfinder 2e Remastered
- **Setting**: Talmandor's Bounty (Andoran colony), isles of ancient Azlant, Golarion
- **Variant Rules**: Free Archetype, Ancestry Paragon, Epic Hero Points (3/session)
- **Theme**: Combat-heavy dungeon crawl against vampires and undead. Roleplay present but secondary.
- **Availability**: All Common & Uncommon official material. Rare requires GM approval.
- **GM**: Cody McGraw (Ryu K.) — StartPlaying
- **Schedule**: Weekly, Sunday 1:00 PM CDT, 3.5-4.5 hours
- **Platform**: Foundry VTT (The Forge) + Discord
- **Party Size**: 5 (Tivael joined Session 1, 2026-05-24)

## Homebrew Rules

- **Ring of Magic Might**: Homebrew level 5 item, +1 item bonus to spellcaster/kineticist spell/blast attack rolls and DCs
- **8-Hour Rests**: Full HP restoration
- **Epic Hero Points**: 3 per session, replenish on chapter completion or level-up
- **Falling Unconscious**: Don't drop held items
- **Persistent Damage After Combat**: Ends unless life-threatening or requiring specific rest
- **Resting in Dungeons**: 1 free 10-min rest between encounters. 2nd rest DC 11 flat check, 3rd rest DC 16. Failed check = random encounter interrupts rest.
- **Uncommon/Rare Magic Items**: Uncommon items treated as 5 levels higher for settlement purchase. Rare items treated as 10 levels higher.
- **Loot Tracker**: Volunteer gets +1 hero point per session

## Campaign Summary

### Book 1 — The Prison
The party arrived at Talmandor's Bounty guarding Inizkar (Iruxi astrologist) on an Azlanti research expedition. The sky was covered in shadow-clouds, dangerous monsters spawned from black rain. Investigation led to cave systems beneath the isle of Vil-Azmar, revealing an ancient Azlanti prison run by a Camazotz cult. The cult's leader Romi Bracken was defeated. Tunnels deeper underground revealed connections to Azlanti vampires.

### Book 2 — The Bleeding Palace
The party descended further, was trapped by Azlanti security systems, and discovered Underheaven — an enormous cavern with a simulated sun and a civilization bred as vampire food. The original party (Clinch, Suma, Nihkos, Seri) was killed (TPK) by Morlock Thralls in the vampire palace. A second party of prisoners broke free and fought through the Bleeding Palace, defeating the ruling vampires and freeing prisoners. The connection between the Bleeding Palace and the Shadow Tower was discovered.

### Book 3 — The Shadow Tower (Current)
The Shadow Tower is controlled by **Nizca**, a vampire seeking to expand the shadow clouds to cover all of Golarion's sky, permanently blocking sunlight. The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty to resupply before using an Azlanti elevator to reach the Shadow Tower.

**Tivael joined here** (Session 1, 2026-05-24) — a scholar drawn to the isles by reports of Azlanti ruins and the shadow crisis.

### Book 3, Session 1 — Into the Ebon Tower
The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty with prisoners, fought shadow-tainted dinosaurs on the surface (Tivael's first fight), then descended via the Azlanti elevator deeper into the underground. Emerged at a Dero-controlled rail junction. Negotiated with **Zovi**, a deposed Dero leader, gaining intelligence about the Ebon Tower layout and its defenders. Pressed through a haunted medical wing (poltergeists of trapped Azlanti doctors), past traps and cursed artifacts, and through clockwork soldier defenses. Currently in the Azlanti prison complex, heading toward the cell block and administrative wing above.

### Book 3, Session 2 — The Long Climb
The party cleared the medical wing dungeon level: defeated **Zatrine** the Lamia (disguised as a fleeing prisoner) and three bandit accomplices (Aguilar, Ularette, Iverness) in the cell block, destroyed three Living Graffiti + a Clockwork Soldier in a side chamber, and wiped a stairwell of voracious Shadows (Greater Shadow + 4 lesser). **Leveled to 8** and took a long rest. Climbed 100 feet up to the **Shadow Tower lobby**; destroyed three larger Clockwork Sentries. Surprised seven **Tokainen's Chosen** mid-blackjack in a side lounge and wiped them. Session ended on a cliffhanger — Ferrok kicked open a door and woke up a dormitory of guards. DM telegraphed three sessions left of Shades of Blood before transition to Ruby Phoenix.

### Book 3, Session 3 — The Court, the Cooks, and the Globster Slog
Picked up on the cliffhanger: the party (4 PCs; Raylta's player joined right after) wiped the woken **dormitory of Tokainen's Chosen** — prone, unarmored, weaponless, at −4 init. Pressing on, they crashed a vampire salon ruled by two lords, **Ilnaphrae** and **Xarbaene**, attended by enslaved human **Favored Thralls**. Tivael's **rank-1 Fear crit-fail (nat 1) sent Xarbaene fleeing (Frightened 3)**; Ferrok killed Ilnaphrae, and Bael ran down the fleeing Xarbaene in the hallway. Mid-fight the party realized the thralls were enslaved humans, not undead, and spared the survivors. Exploration filled in the lore: a **clockwork prisoner-records archive** (cipher-keyed; they pulled Nizca's full file and confirmed via **Augury** that Nizca's lover **Lograsi** is still alive and at large), the **scripture of the vampire god Nin**, and the captive engineer **Aruneth** (+ familiar **Kikiri**), who named the floor's boss: the Warden **Tokainen** — a meteor-hammer fighter-caster with a ghostly bear guardian, praying at the Nin statue in the north room. After a comedic run-in with the (non-hostile) human kitchen staff, the party slogged through four crit-immune **globsters** and two ogres (**Hurkum & Ogenni**) by an aquarium, freed ~8 abducted prisoners, then survived a near-TPK second wave of **seven Tokainen's Chosen** while trying to evacuate. They retreated to rest. The Warden, Aruneth's rescue, and the climb to Nizca all remain. DM: ~3 sessions left of Shades of Blood.

### Book 3, Session 4 — The Warden, the Medusa, and the Surgeon
After a downtime/shopping stop at Talmandor's Bounty (sold ~1,000 gp of looted manacles; ~1,300 gp each; runes/charms distributed), the party rode back down and re-entered the administration wing — and finally fought the **Warden Tokainen**, a Dampyr meteor-hammer fighter-caster flanked by her ethereal **Mistbear Eskis** and a 15-ft **Giant Animated Statue of the goddess Sigva**. Ferrok killed Tokainen; Raylta's Divine Lance dropped the incorporeal bear; the statue's construct armor was cracked (Tivael/Bael) and burned down (Ferrok). They freed the tower's non-hostile **kitchen staff**, found that the engineer **Aruneth had absconded**, and looted a secret **Nin reliquary** (candle of truth, emergency eye). Climbing (invisible) into a grand **underwater lobby/solarium**, they destroyed **three Clockwork Soldiers**, then **Firitula** (a Medusa) and three **Vampire Servitors** — Ferrok ran Firitula down the stairs. **Leveled to 9.** A rotting cellblock floor above held a **Dero Strangler** pack + a **Charnel Creation** flesh-golem, then two **Ofalths** (filth-aberrations, given a funeral pyre). The finale: a Dero **surgeon, Geff**, caught mid-vivisection of a captive woman, with his pet **Chomper**, the Dero bomber **Zer**, and two **Giant Crawling Hands**. Geff's "1,000 Cuts" near-TPK'd the party — the hardest fight of the campaign so far — but Tivael (downed, revived) rose to land the killing 72-damage Flurry crit. **Geff and Zer dead, the captive freed.** DM: **two sessions left** of Shades of Blood.

### Book 3, Session 5 — The Warren, the Beacon, and Speckle
Opened right after Geff's death. The rescued captive — now named **Sarah** — was healed (Bael flavored water "like whiskey"; Bargrum treated her for shock) and sent to safety; she reported the upper floors are a **vampire-vs-fleshwarp warzone** ruled by a **red-robed, black-haired vampire woman who is *not* Nizca**. Clearing the surgical floor's southern half triggered an **11-round grind**: 3 Dero Stranglers, 3 Dero Sentries, a wall-busting **Zombie Hulk** (weak vitality, not fire), and the vampire spellcaster **Vye** (Synesthesia / Vision of Death / Synaptic Pulse / Vampiric Feast / a clutch rank-4 Translocate). Bargrum soaked every enemy crit; Ferrok missed almost everything; **Raylta's needle dart finally killed Vye**. After a full rest the party adopted **Speckle** (a three-eyed homunculus janitor) and looted **Degg's workshop**, whose journals laid the plot bare: Degg discovered N'Gokal ~90 years ago and allied the Dero to the vampires as a food chain; **Nizca arrived only ~3 years ago**; together they retrofitted the **Shadow Beacon** (originally an Azlanti device that siphoned the Plane of Dreams to pacify prisoners) to draw from the **Plane of Shadow** — to blot out the sun, let the Dero walk the surface, and let Nizca cross the sea to find the living **Lograsi** (spelling confirmed here). Disable plan: **smash it**. Climbing, they destroyed two manacle-arresting **Clockwork Sentries**, then an **Irnakurse** fleshwarp (after letting it overwhelm two vampires who fled), then ambushed those four vampires — **Kaba, Chamadi, Xibin, Lauso** — and wiped them four-for-four. **No level-up.** This floor ran long, so the finale slipped: **next session is the second-to-last** of Shades of Blood. Three floors + the observation deck remain above.

## Key Locations

- **Talmandor's Bounty**: Andoran colony, resupply point
- **Vil-Azmar**: Island with cave systems leading underground
- **Azlanti Prison**: Ancient prison beneath Vil-Azmar, formerly run by Camazotz cult
- **Underheaven**: Underground cavern with simulated sun, civilization bred as vampire food
- **The Bleeding Palace**: Vampire rulers' palace in Underheaven (cleared by current party)
- **The Ebon Tower / Shadow Tower**: Nizca's base, source of the shadow clouds. Connected to Bleeding Palace via Azlanti elevator. The party has entered the lower levels. Layout (per Zovi): rail junction → medical wing (cleared) → cell block → administrative wing (Nizca's allies) → Ebon Tower heights (Dero) → vampires/shadows → Nizca and Degg at the top.
- **The Shadow Beacon**: Device at the top of the Ebon Tower generating the shadow pall over the sky. **Originally an Azlanti device that siphoned energy from the Plane of Dreams to pacify rioting prisoners**; Degg and Nizca **retrofitted it to draw from the Netherworld / Plane of Shadow** instead (S5 journals). Must be destroyed to restore sunlight — per Degg's notes it has failsafe protocols, but **smashing it would functionally work** ("percussive engineering"), with likely unstable results.
- **The Octagonal Landing (Book 3, S5)**: the grand landing one floor above the surgical wing — decorative pillars, four massive curtained portraits, and **four doors**: west (frantic singing/screaming beyond), north (a glowing magical ward of sigils, circles, and equations), east (clawed and battered), and south (shattered open — the ruined moon-themed **ballroom** where the Irnakurse fight happened). Only the south door has been entered; west, north, and east remain unexplored.
- **Rail Junction**: Underground hub with multiple tracks. Western track = elevator from Bleeding Palace. Southern track = medical wing. Other tracks unexplored.
- **Shadow Tower Lobby**: Grand entrance hall (100 ft up the spiral stairwell from the medical wing). Polished black marble, walls tiled in green and gold, vaulted 40-ft ceiling, two three-tiered fountains running clean, geometric crystal sculptures. Two life-size armored Azlanti sentinel statues flank a sword-and-polearm garden growing from the floor. Multiple halls and doors branch off.
- **The Warden's Floor (Book 3)**: The level above the lobby, run by Warden **Tokainen**. Explored S3–S4: the guards' **dormitory** (cleared), a **vampire salon** (Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene, cleared), a **clockwork prisoner-records archive** (cipher-keyed; pull any named prisoner's file), an **office** with the Nin scripture, a **garbage den** (chest of Aeon Stones), **Aruneth's workshop** (now empty — engineer absconded), a **kitchen** (non-hostile human staff, freed), a **magical aquarium** (glassless water; globsters), the **Prisoner Pits** (sunken cubicle living-quarters; ~8 abducted sailors freed), and the **northern Nin-statue room** — **cleared S4** (Tokainen, Eskis, and the Sigva statue killed). Behind the Nin statue: a secret reliquary (skeleton, +1 leather armor, candle of truth, emergency eye).
- **The Underwater Lobby / Solarium (Book 3, cleared S4)**: ~100 ft up from the Warden's floor and **~30 ft below the surface of the sea** — a grand hall of deconstructed-stained-glass sculptures with a domed solarium looking out into the ocean (the tower is built into and below the seafloor). Hidden acid-rune traps on the stairs (disabled). Held three **Clockwork Soldiers** and the **Medusa Firitula** + Vampire Servitors. Contains a statue of the prison's founder, **High Governor Avres of Vespera**.
- **The Cellblock Floor (Book 3, cleared S4)**: A rotting, mold-choked level above the lobby — a dank U-shaped landing, a **laundry/clothing room of the dead**, empty cells, an **Ofalth-infested corpse cell** (cremated by the party), and a **surgery** where the Dero surgeon **Geff** was vivisecting a captive. Cleared of Dero Stranglers, a Charnel Creation, Ofalths, Geff, Zer, and Giant Crawling Hands.

## Enemy Intelligence

### Vampire Common Traits
- Physical resistance 5 (bypassed by silver, force, energy damage)
- Fast Healing (varies: 5-10 per round, cannot be shut off — it's Fast Healing, not Regeneration)
- Coffin Restoration: At 0 HP, vampires go unconscious + Mist Escape to coffin. Reform in 1 hour. Must destroy coffin or stake through heart while unconscious.
- Vampire Vulnerabilities: Garlic (can't approach within 10 ft), religious symbols (Will save or Frightened), sunlight (severe penalties), running water (can't cross), stakes (destroy while unconscious)
- Void Healing: Void damage heals them. Never use void damage against vampires.
- Drink Blood: Athletics vs Fort DC, inflicts Drained. Entire party carries Drained 1 persistently.

### Save Profiles (from combat data)
- **Nalushae**: Fort +13 (lowest!), Ref +18 (highest), Will +16
- **Vampire Servitors**: Fort +9 (lowest!), Ref +13 (highest), Will +11
- **Glormungost**: Fort +12, Ref +14, Will +10 (lowest, fear-immune)
- **Morlock Thralls**: Fort +13 (highest), Ref +8 (lowest), Will +10. Weakness: Mental 10. Light Blindness.
- **Vampire Nettles**: Fort +10, Ref +7 (lowest), Will +8. Weakness: Fire 5, Slashing 5.

### Key Patterns
- Enemies target party Fort saves most often (Divine Wrath, Harm, Enervation, Drink Blood, Shadow Expulsion, Noise Blast)
- Physical damage is the most common incoming damage type, followed by electricity and void
- Enemies frequently have Reflex as their BEST save — Flying Flame targets the wrong save
- Tivael now provides Recall Knowledge round 1 of every fight — identified worst save (Will) on the pachycephalosaurs immediately
- Tivael provides debuff infrastructure (Dirge of Doom, Bon Mot, Rallying Anthem, Worm's Repast)
- The party still does not flank consistently
- **New threat: Deafened** — Noise Blast can shut down Tivael's auditory compositions. Need counterplay.
