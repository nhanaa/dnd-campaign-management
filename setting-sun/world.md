# World — Setting Sun, Rising Phoenix

## Campaign Overview

- **AP**: Shades of Blood (Books 1-3) → Fists of the Ruby Phoenix (Books 4+)
- **System**: Pathfinder 2e Remastered
- **Setting**: Talmandor's Bounty (Andoran colony), isles of ancient Azlant, Golarion
- **Variant Rules**: Free Archetype, Ancestry Paragon, Epic Hero Points (3/session)
- **Theme**: Combat-heavy dungeon crawl against vampires and undead. Roleplay present but secondary.
- **Availability**: All Common & Uncommon official material. Rare requires GM approval.
- **GM**: Cody McGraw (Ryu K.) — StartPlaying
- **Schedule**: Weekly, Sunday 1:00 PM CDT, 3.5-4.5 hours
- **Platform**: Foundry VTT (The Forge) + Discord
- **Party Size**: 5 (Tivael joined Session 1, 2026-05-24)

## Homebrew Rules

- **Ring of Magic Might**: Homebrew level 5 item, +1 item bonus to spellcaster/kineticist spell/blast attack rolls and DCs
- **8-Hour Rests**: Full HP restoration
- **Epic Hero Points**: 3 per session, replenish on chapter completion or level-up
- **Falling Unconscious**: Don't drop held items
- **Persistent Damage After Combat**: Ends unless life-threatening or requiring specific rest
- **Resting in Dungeons**: 1 free 10-min rest between encounters. 2nd rest DC 11 flat check, 3rd rest DC 16. Failed check = random encounter interrupts rest.
- **Uncommon/Rare Magic Items**: Uncommon items treated as 5 levels higher for settlement purchase. Rare items treated as 10 levels higher.
- **Loot Tracker**: Volunteer gets +1 hero point per session

## Campaign Summary

### Book 1 — The Prison
The party arrived at Talmandor's Bounty guarding Inizkar (Iruxi astrologist) on an Azlanti research expedition. The sky was covered in shadow-clouds, dangerous monsters spawned from black rain. Investigation led to cave systems beneath the isle of Vil-Azmar, revealing an ancient Azlanti prison run by a Camazotz cult. The cult's leader Romi Bracken was defeated. Tunnels deeper underground revealed connections to Azlanti vampires.

### Book 2 — The Bleeding Palace
The party descended further, was trapped by Azlanti security systems, and discovered Underheaven — an enormous cavern with a simulated sun and a civilization bred as vampire food. The original party (Clinch, Suma, Nihkos, Seri) was killed (TPK) by Morlock Thralls in the vampire palace. A second party of prisoners broke free and fought through the Bleeding Palace, defeating the ruling vampires and freeing prisoners. The connection between the Bleeding Palace and the Shadow Tower was discovered.

### Book 3 — The Shadow Tower (Current)
The Shadow Tower is controlled by **Nizca**, a vampire seeking to expand the shadow clouds to cover all of Golarion's sky, permanently blocking sunlight. The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty to resupply before using an Azlanti elevator to reach the Shadow Tower.

**Tivael joined here** (Session 1, 2026-05-24) — a scholar drawn to the isles by reports of Azlanti ruins and the shadow crisis.

### Book 3, Session 1 — Into the Ebon Tower
The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty with prisoners, fought shadow-tainted dinosaurs on the surface (Tivael's first fight), then descended via the Azlanti elevator deeper into the underground. Emerged at a Dero-controlled rail junction. Negotiated with **Zovi**, a deposed Dero leader, gaining intelligence about the Ebon Tower layout and its defenders. Pressed through a haunted medical wing (poltergeists of trapped Azlanti doctors), past traps and cursed artifacts, and through clockwork soldier defenses. Currently in the Azlanti prison complex, heading toward the cell block and administrative wing above.

### Book 3, Session 2 — The Long Climb
The party cleared the medical wing dungeon level: defeated **Zatrine** the Lamia (disguised as a fleeing prisoner) and three bandit accomplices (Aguilar, Ularette, Iverness) in the cell block, destroyed three Living Graffiti + a Clockwork Soldier in a side chamber, and wiped a stairwell of voracious Shadows (Greater Shadow + 4 lesser). **Leveled to 8** and took a long rest. Climbed 100 feet up to the **Shadow Tower lobby**; destroyed three larger Clockwork Sentries. Surprised seven **Tokainen's Chosen** mid-blackjack in a side lounge and wiped them. Session ended on a cliffhanger — Ferrok kicked open a door and woke up a dormitory of guards. DM telegraphed three sessions left of Shades of Blood before transition to Ruby Phoenix.

### Book 3, Session 3 — The Court, the Cooks, and the Globster Slog
Picked up on the cliffhanger: the party (4 PCs; Raylta's player joined right after) wiped the woken **dormitory of Tokainen's Chosen** — prone, unarmored, weaponless, at −4 init. Pressing on, they crashed a vampire salon ruled by two lords, **Ilnaphrae** and **Xarbaene**, attended by enslaved human **Favored Thralls**. Tivael's **rank-1 Fear crit-fail (nat 1) sent Xarbaene fleeing (Frightened 3)**; Ferrok killed Ilnaphrae, and Bael ran down the fleeing Xarbaene in the hallway. Mid-fight the party realized the thralls were enslaved humans, not undead, and spared the survivors. Exploration filled in the lore: a **clockwork prisoner-records archive** (cipher-keyed; they pulled Nizca's full file and confirmed via **Augury** that Nizca's lover **Lagrassi** is still alive and at large), the **scripture of the vampire god Nin**, and the captive engineer **Aruneth** (+ familiar **Kikiri**), who named the floor's boss: the Warden **Tokainen** — a meteor-hammer fighter-caster with a ghostly bear guardian, praying at the Nin statue in the north room. After a comedic run-in with the (non-hostile) human kitchen staff, the party slogged through four crit-immune **globsters** and two ogres (**Hurkum & Ogenni**) by an aquarium, freed ~8 abducted prisoners, then survived a near-TPK second wave of **seven Tokainen's Chosen** while trying to evacuate. They retreated to rest. The Warden, Aruneth's rescue, and the climb to Nizca all remain. DM: ~3 sessions left of Shades of Blood.

## Key NPCs

- **Nizca**: Big bad. Vampire controlling the Shadow Tower / Ebon Tower. Goal: block all sunlight on Golarion. Located at the top of the tower with Degg.
- **Degg**: Traitorous Dero inventor. Nizca's lackey. Built the **Shadow Beacon** that creates the shadow pall. Led the Dero from the Darklands into N'Gokal, negotiated the original truce with Nalushae. Betrayed his own people. Zovi wants him dead.
- **Zovi**: Deposed Dero leader. Stuck underground after Degg took her influence. Provided the party with intelligence about the tower layout in exchange for mutual non-interference. Parting words: "When you gut Degg, tell him Zovi says hi."
- **Inizkar**: Female Iruxi astrologist. Original expedition leader. Research shifted to shadow investigation.
- **Romi Bracken**: Camazotz cult leader (defeated, Book 1). Fed prisoners to the vampires.
- **Nalushae Meronis**: Level 8 vampire arcane caster (defeated, Book 2). Lightning Bolt specialist. Had a journal in Azlanti. Special vulnerability to her vampire nettles.
- **Glormungost**: Level 6 vampire (recurring). Low Will, immune to fear. Physical resistance 5, bludgeoning resistance 5. Fast Healing 7.
- **Dr. Elimaria**: Ancient Azlanti doctor and priest of Issachar. Trapped in the medical wing with colleagues Beliana and Fernius during a calamity. All three became poltergeists. Their story was found in medical papers (read by Tivael in Azlanti).
- **Zatrine** (defeated, Session 2): Lamia who ambushed the party disguised as a freed prisoner in the Azlanti cell block. Cast Agonizing Despair, Noise Blast, and Vampiric Feast. Killed by Bael's one-action fire blast after Ferrok's 108-damage Sure Strike + Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike crit softened her. Had a +1 Striking Fearsome Scimitar and +1 Resilient Explorer's Clothing. *"Impatient fuckers, aren't you all."*
- **Tokainen**: The **Warden of this floor** of the Ebon Tower (per Aruneth, S3) — a fighter-caster wielding a **meteor hammer**, with a guardian: **an ethereal, ghostly bear**. Currently praying at the giant shaved-head **Nin statue** in the large northern room. The Chosen carry his name ("Tokainen's gonna want him") and identical **Cataclysm Brand** marks — likely batch-branded by him or an apparatus. **Not yet fought** (next session's likely boss). *Open question: is the Warden the same Tokainen the Chosen serve, or a namesake?*
- **Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene** (defeated, S3): Two vampire lords of a salon court — piano, painting, blood-drinking, attended by enslaved Favored Thralls. Used repeating crossbows, Turn to Mist, and buffed thralls via **Lord's Blessing** (+2 attack / +1d6 damage within 25 ft of a master). Ilnaphrae killed by Ferrok; the fleeing Xarbaene run down by Bael. Both confirmed silver-killed and burned. Neither appeared in the prison records — likely outside followers who came with Nizca, not former prisoners.
- **Aruneth** (ally-in-waiting): Captive **engineer** forced to maintain the tower's clockwork soldiers for Warden Tokainen, guarded by a multi-armed construct. Will leave with the party once the Warden is dead. Can read/operate the clockwork systems. Familiar: **Kikiri**.
- **Kikiri**: Aruneth's little fox/snake **familiar**, found tidying his room; begged the party to save its master. Under orders to stay put.
- **Nin**: The **vampire god** whose scripture ("The Immaculate Solution") the party found. Doctrine: **overcoming vampire weaknesses through "logic, experimentation, and determination"** — perfecting vampires past silver/sunlight. His holy symbol is **made of silver** (the tell). *Implication to watch: elite/Nin-enhanced vampires may resist silver.*
- **Nizca Iricold**: Full name confirmed (S3 records). **Non-binary Strigoi vampire.** Crimes: sedition, insurrection, cult formation, occult rituals, vampirism, murder. Profile: methodical, obsessive, aloof.
- **Lagrassi**: Nizca's lover, **a male human, at large** — **confirmed still alive via Augury** (S3). Never a prisoner here. Nizca's plan may hinge on reaching him. How a "human" survives a thousand-year-old record is unknown (immortal? turned?).
- **Hurkum & Ogenni** (defeated, S3): A pair of ogres (male Hurkum, female Ogenni) guarding the prisoners by the aquarium. Ogre hooks, Sweeping Hook, Punting Kick, reach + reactive strike. Best Fort / worst Reflex.

## Key Locations

- **Talmandor's Bounty**: Andoran colony, resupply point
- **Vil-Azmar**: Island with cave systems leading underground
- **Azlanti Prison**: Ancient prison beneath Vil-Azmar, formerly run by Camazotz cult
- **Underheaven**: Underground cavern with simulated sun, civilization bred as vampire food
- **The Bleeding Palace**: Vampire rulers' palace in Underheaven (cleared by current party)
- **The Ebon Tower / Shadow Tower**: Nizca's base, source of the shadow clouds. Connected to Bleeding Palace via Azlanti elevator. The party has entered the lower levels. Layout (per Zovi): rail junction → medical wing (cleared) → cell block → administrative wing (Nizca's allies) → Ebon Tower heights (Dero) → vampires/shadows → Nizca and Degg at the top.
- **The Shadow Beacon**: Device at the top of the Ebon Tower generating the shadow pall over the sky. Built by Degg. Must be destroyed to restore sunlight.
- **Rail Junction**: Underground hub with multiple tracks. Western track = elevator from Bleeding Palace. Southern track = medical wing. Other tracks unexplored.
- **Shadow Tower Lobby**: Grand entrance hall (100 ft up the spiral stairwell from the medical wing). Polished black marble, walls tiled in green and gold, vaulted 40-ft ceiling, two three-tiered fountains running clean, geometric crystal sculptures. Two life-size armored Azlanti sentinel statues flank a sword-and-polearm garden growing from the floor. Multiple halls and doors branch off.
- **The Warden's Floor (Book 3, current)**: The level above the lobby, run by Warden **Tokainen**. Explored S3: the guards' **dormitory** (cleared), a **vampire salon** (Ilnaphrae & Xarbaene, cleared), a **clockwork prisoner-records archive** (cipher-keyed; pull any named prisoner's file), an **office** with the Nin scripture, a **garbage den** (chest of Aeon Stones), **Aruneth's workshop** (captive engineer), a **kitchen** (non-hostile human staff), a **magical aquarium** (glassless water; globsters), the **Prisoner Pits** (sunken cubicle living-quarters; ~8 abducted sailors freed), and the **northern Nin-statue room** — Tokainen's location, **not yet entered**.

## Enemy Intelligence

### Vampire Common Traits
- Physical resistance 5 (bypassed by silver, force, energy damage)
- Fast Healing (varies: 5-10 per round, cannot be shut off — it's Fast Healing, not Regeneration)
- Coffin Restoration: At 0 HP, vampires go unconscious + Mist Escape to coffin. Reform in 1 hour. Must destroy coffin or stake through heart while unconscious.
- Vampire Vulnerabilities: Garlic (can't approach within 10 ft), religious symbols (Will save or Frightened), sunlight (severe penalties), running water (can't cross), stakes (destroy while unconscious)
- Void Healing: Void damage heals them. Never use void damage against vampires.
- Drink Blood: Athletics vs Fort DC, inflicts Drained. Entire party carries Drained 1 persistently.

### Save Profiles (from combat data)
- **Nalushae**: Fort +13 (lowest!), Ref +18 (highest), Will +16
- **Vampire Servitors**: Fort +9 (lowest!), Ref +13 (highest), Will +11
- **Glormungost**: Fort +12, Ref +14, Will +10 (lowest, fear-immune)
- **Morlock Thralls**: Fort +13 (highest), Ref +8 (lowest), Will +10. Weakness: Mental 10. Light Blindness.
- **Vampire Nettles**: Fort +10, Ref +7 (lowest), Will +8. Weakness: Fire 5, Slashing 5.

### Session 1 New Enemies
- **Shadow-Tainted Pachycephalosaurus**: No weaknesses/resistances. Best save Fort, worst save Will. Shadow Expulsion (30-ft cone void AoE). Clobbering Charge.
- **Dero Sentries**: Focused Blow (extra damage on next Strike). Language: Undercommon (not Common — Demoralize in Common may fail depending on specific Dero).
- **Dero Magister**: Noise Blast (sonic AoE, Fort save, Deafened on failure). Agonizing Despair (mental damage + Frightened, Will save). Deafened shuts down auditory compositions.
- **Poltergeists**: Incorporeal. Telekinetic Storm (multi-target physical strikes, up to 3d12 per target focused). Site Bound (can't leave 120 ft from origin). Immune to: paralyzed, precision, unconscious. Force damage and spirit damage bypass incorporeal.
- **Clockwork Soldiers**: Constructs. Physical resistance 5. Activate Defenses (+2 circumstance AC to self or adjacent ally). Stupefied 1 on hit. Electricity weakness — electricity bypasses physical resistance.

### Session 2 New Enemies
- **Lamia (Zatrine)**: Upper-half human, lower-half serpent. Spell list: Agonizing Despair (occult, Will save, mental + Frightened), Noise Blast (sonic AoE, Fort), Vampiric Feast (touch, void, heals caster for half damage dealt). High HP — survived a 108-damage Spellstrike crit + a 35-damage Heal-scroll-equivalent of damage before falling.
- **Bandit Rogues (Aguilar, Ularette, Iverness)**: Rogue thugs. Feint + strike combos. Nimble Dodge (reaction +2 AC, can downgrade crit to hit). +1 Striking Short Sword + Short Bow + Lesser Darkvision Elixir each.
- **Living Graffiti**: Constructs that peel off mural walls. Best save Reflex, worst Fort. NOT mindless (Demoralize and Bon Mot work). Standard construct immunities. No known weakness exposed (the Clockwork Soldier paired with them was the electricity-weak one, not the Graffiti themselves).
- **Shadow / Greater Shadow**: Incorporeal undead. Resistant to all damage except **force, ghost touch, vitality, spirit**. Best Reflex / worst Fort. NOT mindless. **Steal Shadow** (Enfeebled stacking up to 3 — at 3+, shadow is separated; spawns a shadow spawn). Decreases by 1 per hour.
- **Clockwork Sentry**: Larger, multi-armed clockwork construct. **Tackle** (stride + fist + trip), **Sudden Seize** (reaction grapple on manipulate/move actions), **Pneumatic Swing** (warhammer reach 10 ft, knockdown trip-rider). Electricity weakness 10.
- **Tokainen's Chosen**: Azlanti-descent humans in matching uniforms, identical **Cataclysm Brand** burn on left cheek. **Cataclysm Brand** 1/hour, 60-ft range, 10-ft burst, 6d6 fire + persistent 1d4 fire on failed save (+ for 1 min their Strikes radiate +1d10 fire). **Take 'Em Down** (guisarme: Trip + Strike, same MAP, reach). Bola side-arm. Confirmed S3: a 7-strong squad with synchronized brand bursts is a near-TPK threat (the trip-prone-then-stab loop kept Ferrok on the floor all fight). Loot per Chosen: +1 Guisarme + +1 Bola.

### Session 3 New Enemies
- **Globsters** (oozes): **Immune to critical hits** (but still take crit *riders* — persistent fire/bleed apply normally). **Weak to electricity** (learned mid-fight). **Mindless** — Fear/Dirge/Bon Mot do nothing. Very high HP ("bags of hit points"). **Nauseating Slap** on a tendril hit (Fort save or stacking sickened, max 4). Grab tendrils. Slow movement. Electric Arc / lightning is the lever.
- **Favored Thralls**: Enslaved humans (NOT undead, NOT willing). Daggers. Buffed by **Lord's Blessing** within 25 ft of their vampire master (+2 attack, +1d6 Strike damage); the buff drops when the master flees/dies. Treat as rescuable, not kill-on-sight.
- **Hurkum & Ogenni** (ogres): Ogre hooks, **Sweeping Hook** (free Strike on a successful Trip), **Punting Kick** (Strike + shove a prone creature). Reach 10 ft + reactive strike. Best Fort / worst Reflex.

### Key Patterns
- Enemies target party Fort saves most often (Divine Wrath, Harm, Enervation, Drink Blood, Shadow Expulsion, Noise Blast)
- Physical damage is the most common incoming damage type, followed by electricity and void
- Enemies frequently have Reflex as their BEST save — Flying Flame targets the wrong save
- Tivael now provides Recall Knowledge round 1 of every fight — identified worst save (Will) on the pachycephalosaurs immediately
- Tivael provides debuff infrastructure (Dirge of Doom, Bon Mot, Rallying Anthem, Worm's Repast)
- The party still does not flank consistently
- **New threat: Deafened** — Noise Blast can shut down Tivael's auditory compositions. Need counterplay.
