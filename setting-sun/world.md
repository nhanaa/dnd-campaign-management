## Homebrew Rules

- **Ring of Magic Might**: Homebrew level 5 item, +1 item bonus to spellcaster/kineticist spell/blast attack rolls and DCs
- **8-Hour Rests**: Full HP restoration
- **Epic Hero Points**: 3 per session, replenish on chapter completion or level-up
- **Falling Unconscious**: Don't drop held items
- **Persistent Damage After Combat**: Ends unless life-threatening or requiring specific rest
- **Resting in Dungeons**: 1 free 10-min rest between encounters. 2nd rest DC 11 flat check, 3rd rest DC 16. Failed check = random encounter interrupts rest.
- **Uncommon/Rare Magic Items**: Uncommon items treated as 5 levels higher for settlement purchase. Rare items treated as 10 levels higher.
- **Loot Tracker**: Volunteer gets +1 hero point per session

## Campaign Summary

### Book 1 — The Prison
The party arrived at Talmandor's Bounty guarding Inizkar (Iruxi astrologist) on an Azlanti research expedition. The sky was covered in shadow-clouds, dangerous monsters spawned from black rain. Investigation led to cave systems beneath the isle of Vil-Azmar, revealing an ancient Azlanti prison run by a Camazotz cult. The cult's leader Romi Bracken was defeated. Tunnels deeper underground revealed connections to Azlanti vampires.

### Book 2 — The Bleeding Palace
The party descended further, was trapped by Azlanti security systems, and discovered Underheaven — an enormous cavern with a simulated sun and a civilization bred as vampire food. The original party (Clinch, Suma, Nihkos, Seri) was killed (TPK) by Morlock Thralls in the vampire palace. A second party of prisoners broke free and fought through the Bleeding Palace, defeating the ruling vampires and freeing prisoners. The connection between the Bleeding Palace and the Shadow Tower was discovered.

### Book 3 — The Shadow Tower (Current)
The Shadow Tower is controlled by **Nizca**, a vampire seeking to expand the shadow clouds to cover all of Golarion's sky, permanently blocking sunlight. The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty to resupply before using an Azlanti elevator to reach the Shadow Tower.

**Tivael joined here** (Session 1, 2026-05-24) — a scholar drawn to the isles by reports of Azlanti ruins and the shadow crisis.

### Book 3, Session 1 — Into the Ebon Tower
The party returned to Talmandor's Bounty with prisoners, fought shadow-tainted dinosaurs on the surface (Tivael's first fight), then descended via the Azlanti elevator deeper into the underground. Emerged at a Dero-controlled rail junction. Negotiated with **Zovi**, a deposed Dero leader, gaining intelligence about the Ebon Tower layout and its defenders. Pressed through a haunted medical wing (poltergeists of trapped Azlanti doctors), past traps and cursed artifacts, and through clockwork soldier defenses. Currently in the Azlanti prison complex, heading toward the cell block and administrative wing above.

## Key NPCs

- **Nizca**: Big bad. Vampire controlling the Shadow Tower / Ebon Tower. Goal: block all sunlight on Golarion. Located at the top of the tower with Degg.
- **Degg**: Traitorous Dero inventor. Nizca's lackey. Built the **Shadow Beacon** that creates the shadow pall. Led the Dero from the Darklands into N'Gokal, negotiated the original truce with Nalushae. Betrayed his own people. Zovi wants him dead.
- **Zovi**: Deposed Dero leader. Stuck underground after Degg took her influence. Provided the party with intelligence about the tower layout in exchange for mutual non-interference. Parting words: "When you gut Degg, tell him Zovi says hi."
- **Inizkar**: Female Iruxi astrologist. Original expedition leader. Research shifted to shadow investigation.
- **Romi Bracken**: Camazotz cult leader (defeated, Book 1). Fed prisoners to the vampires.
- **Nalushae Meronis**: Level 8 vampire arcane caster (defeated, Book 2). Lightning Bolt specialist. Had a journal in Azlanti. Special vulnerability to her vampire nettles.
- **Glormungost**: Level 6 vampire (recurring). Low Will, immune to fear. Physical resistance 5, bludgeoning resistance 5. Fast Healing 7.
- **Dr. Elimaria**: Ancient Azlanti doctor and priest of Issachar. Trapped in the medical wing with colleagues Beliana and Fernius during a calamity. All three became poltergeists. Their story was found in medical papers (read by Tivael in Azlanti).

## Key Locations

- **Talmandor's Bounty**: Andoran colony, resupply point
- **Vil-Azmar**: Island with cave systems leading underground
- **Azlanti Prison**: Ancient prison beneath Vil-Azmar, formerly run by Camazotz cult
- **Underheaven**: Underground cavern with simulated sun, civilization bred as vampire food
- **The Bleeding Palace**: Vampire rulers' palace in Underheaven (cleared by current party)
- **The Ebon Tower / Shadow Tower**: Nizca's base, source of the shadow clouds. Connected to Bleeding Palace via Azlanti elevator. The party has entered the lower levels. Layout (per Zovi): rail junction → medical wing (cleared) → cell block → administrative wing (Nizca's allies) → Ebon Tower heights (Dero) → vampires/shadows → Nizca and Degg at the top.
- **The Shadow Beacon**: Device at the top of the Ebon Tower generating the shadow pall over the sky. Built by Degg. Must be destroyed to restore sunlight.
- **Rail Junction**: Underground hub with multiple tracks. Western track = elevator from Bleeding Palace. Southern track = medical wing. Other tracks unexplored.

## Enemy Intelligence

### Vampire Common Traits
- Physical resistance 5 (bypassed by silver, force, energy damage)
- Fast Healing (varies: 5-10 per round, cannot be shut off — it's Fast Healing, not Regeneration)
- Coffin Restoration: At 0 HP, vampires go unconscious + Mist Escape to coffin. Reform in 1 hour. Must destroy coffin or stake through heart while unconscious.
- Vampire Vulnerabilities: Garlic (can't approach within 10 ft), religious symbols (Will save or Frightened), sunlight (severe penalties), running water (can't cross), stakes (destroy while unconscious)
- Void Healing: Void damage heals them. Never use void damage against vampires.
- Drink Blood: Athletics vs Fort DC, inflicts Drained. Entire party carries Drained 1 persistently.

### Save Profiles (from combat data)
- **Nalushae**: Fort +13 (lowest!), Ref +18 (highest), Will +16
- **Vampire Servitors**: Fort +9 (lowest!), Ref +13 (highest), Will +11
- **Glormungost**: Fort +12, Ref +14, Will +10 (lowest, fear-immune)
- **Morlock Thralls**: Fort +13 (highest), Ref +8 (lowest), Will +10. Weakness: Mental 10. Light Blindness.
- **Vampire Nettles**: Fort +10, Ref +7 (lowest), Will +8. Weakness: Fire 5, Slashing 5.

### Session 1 New Enemies
- **Shadow-Tainted Pachycephalosaurus**: No weaknesses/resistances. Best save Fort, worst save Will. Shadow Expulsion (30-ft cone void AoE). Clobbering Charge.
- **Dero Sentries**: Focused Blow (extra damage on next Strike). Language: Undercommon (not Common — Demoralize in Common may fail depending on specific Dero).
- **Dero Magister**: Noise Blast (sonic AoE, Fort save, Deafened on failure). Agonizing Despair (mental damage + Frightened, Will save). Deafened shuts down auditory compositions.
- **Poltergeists**: Incorporeal. Telekinetic Storm (multi-target physical strikes, up to 3d12 per target focused). Site Bound (can't leave 120 ft from origin). Immune to: paralyzed, precision, unconscious. Force damage and spirit damage bypass incorporeal.
- **Clockwork Soldiers**: Constructs. Physical resistance 5. Activate Defenses (+2 circumstance AC to self or adjacent ally). Stupefied 1 on hit. Electricity bypasses physical resistance.

### Key Patterns
- Enemies target party Fort saves most often (Divine Wrath, Harm, Enervation, Drink Blood, Shadow Expulsion, Noise Blast)
- Physical damage is the most common incoming damage type, followed by electricity and void
- Enemies frequently have Reflex as their BEST save — Flying Flame targets the wrong save
- Tivael now provides Recall Knowledge round 1 of every fight — identified worst save (Will) on the pachycephalosaurs immediately
- Tivael provides debuff infrastructure (Dirge of Doom, Bon Mot, Rallying Anthem, Worm's Repast)
- The party still does not flank consistently
- **New threat: Deafened** — Noise Blast can shut down Tivael's auditory compositions. Need counterplay.
