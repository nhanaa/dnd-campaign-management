## How Tivael Changes the Party

### Raylta (Oracle) — Unlocked
- Courageous Anthem replaces Bless (no sustain needed) → frees 1 action/round + 1 prepared spell slot
- Rallying Anthem replaces Benediction → frees another spell slot
- Less healing needed (buffs/debuffs reduce incoming damage) → fewer Cursebound triggers → less Enfeebled/Sickened self-penalty
- Can finally use her offensive divine spells: Holy Light, Behold the Weave, Moonbeam, Fear the Sun

### Bargrum (Guardian) — Sustained
- Courageous Anthem +1 makes his Strikes more accurate
- Reduced incoming damage means less HP burned through Intercept Attack
- Rallying Anthem +1 AC/saves when party needs defense

### Ferrok (Magus) — Amplified
- +1 attack from Anthem increases Spellstrike hit/crit rate (crits are where Magus damage explodes)
- Bon Mot/Dirge softening enemies makes his attacks land more often
- Recall Knowledge identifying lowest saves helps him choose which spell to Spellstrike with

### Bael (Kineticist) — Complemented
- +1 attack/damage from Anthem on every action
- Bon Mot -2 Will helps his rare Demoralizes land
- Tivael covers Fort and Will targeting that Bael can't
- Electric Arc covers Reflex alongside Flying Flame

## Character Relationships (Session 1 Observations)

- **Bargrum**: Immediately started intercepting for Tivael. Volunteered to test every trap. Led the negotiation structure with Zovi while Tivael handled the diplomacy. Complementary dynamic — the dwarf provides the moral framework, the dragon provides the silver tongue.

- **Bael**: Repositioned the party with Four Winds at the start of exploration. Flying Flame remains his primary damage tool. Thermal Nimbus provides consistent chip + fire resistance. Treat Wounds between fights. Crit-failed a Treat Wounds at session end — "I explode before he can help me."

Her rank 4 AoE Heal for 59 HP was the session's biggest save. Already pushing into Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2 from healing pressure. Tivael's Rallying Anthem visibly helped — "Oh, still up" after hits that would have downed someone without the +1 AC/saves.
- **Tivael + Party**: The party welcomed Tivael naturally. No awkward integration. Tivael immediately contributed with RK, debuffs, and damage. "Protect me" set the tone — tiny dragon, big impact. The Zovi negotiation showed the party deferring to Tivael's Diplomacy naturally.

## DM Profile — Cody McGraw (Ryu K.)

- **Style**: Fair but unforgiving. Plays enemies intelligently. Targets the healer. Positions AoE optimally.
- **Difficulty**: High. Let the original party TPK. Tracks regeneration, persistent damage, and enemy abilities precisely.
- **Safety nets**: 3 hero points/session, persistent damage clears after combat, full HP on long rest, don't drop items when unconscious.
- **RK policy**: Doesn't give false info on poor rolls. Generous with information when discovered through play. Dubious Knowledge not recommended.
- **Death trait ruling**: Spells with death trait still function against death-immune creatures, just the death effect portion is negated.
- **Encounter design**: Mix of single bosses and 4-6 enemy hordes. ~5 encounters per long rest. Back-to-back fights with limited resting.
- **Session 1 observations**: 4 combat encounters + 1 negotiation + exploration/traps in ~3h50m. High combat density. DM uses AoE frequently (Shadow Expulsion, Noise Blast, Telekinetic Storm). Traps deal significant damage (40 mental from Telepathic Jolt). Welcomes new player smoothly — Tivael integrated within 10 minutes.
- **Session 2 observations**: 5 combat encounters + minimal exploration in ~3h35m — extremely high combat density. DM mixed AoE-heavy fights (Cataclysm Brand spam from Tokainen's Chosen, Solar Detonation from Bael) with positioning puzzles (Sentry grapples). New threat type — **persistent fire** stacked on Raylta + Tivael for the whole Tokainen fight; multiple failed saves in a row. RP highlights came from in-character DM voicing (Lamia "Impatient fuckers", Tokainen "what's the buy-in" exchange, painting "thank you Captain Obvious" deadpan). DM telegraphed three sessions left of Shades of Blood + transition to Ruby Phoenix; new StartPlaying link out next session.

## Session 2 — Key Patterns

- **Persistent fire is the new AoE threat**: Cataclysm Brand stacks persistent damage on save failures. 7 Chosen each carrying a 1/hour Brand turns the lounge into a soak. Raylta failed three saves in a row. Need a Cleanse Affliction or persistent-damage counter for the next encounter against a fire-themed group.
- **Force damage is bypass tech**: Flurry of Claws killed an incorporeal Shadow (force vs resistance) and a Tokainen's Chosen (force against high-HP humanoid). Tivael should keep front-loading Flurry of Claws against priority targets.
- **Lightning is the new electricity-weakness lever**: Bael's Lightning Dash × 4 in two encounters (3 against electricity-weak constructs). Tivael's Electric Arc covered the Reflex-save AoE lane. Ferrok's Horizon Thunder Sphere brought the single-target crits. The party now has three viable electricity damage sources — plays well against constructs especially.
- **Sudden Seize is a problem**: Clockwork Sentries grappled Bargrum (multiple times prone), Raylta (mid-Heal cast), and Tivael (snatched out of the air). Manipulate actions trigger it; flat-check on grabbed spell casting becomes routine. Raylta hero-pointed a flat check this session.
- **Prong assignment on Flurry of Claws**: target the *softened* enemy with the second prong (high MAP makes crit less likely there); reserve the first prong for the highest-HP target where a crit might one-shot. This session's lesson — the crit prong bounced off Chosen E (full HP), and the non-crit prong finished Chosen B (softened).

## Session 3 — Key Patterns

- **Electricity is now a confirmed lever against oozes too** (not just constructs). Tivael's Electric Arc / Bael's lightning cracked the globsters after fire stalled. RK oozes *before* committing to a damage type — the table guessed fire and wasted rounds.
- **Mental debuffs are dead weight vs mindless enemies** (globsters ate Tivael's Dirge/Fear). Reserve Fear/Dirge/Bon Mot for the Chosen and the Warden; use Worm's Repast / Electric Arc / Needle Darts on mindless/construct foes.
- **Persistent fire (Cataclysm Brand) remains the party's worst unanswered threat**: a 7-Chosen wave with synchronized bursts is a near-TPK. Spreading out + Thermal Nimbus + the defensive Rallying Anthem mitigate but don't solve it — a Cleanse Affliction / persistent-damage counter would change these fights.
- **Single-healer fragility is now load-bearing**: Raylta at Cursebound 2 nearly died while being the only healer. Tivael's Summon Fey unicorn (a second vitality-heal source) is the emerging answer; keep a rank-4 slot for it on hard fights.
- **The ghost-bear problem ahead**: the Warden's incorporeal guardian needs **force / ghost touch / vitality / silver**. The party has only **one ghost oil** for a melee-heavy roster — Tivael's Flurry of Claws (force) and Raylta's vitality/silver will carry that fight unless they stock more oils.
