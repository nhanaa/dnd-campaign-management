## How Tivael Changes the Party

### Raylta (Oracle) — Unlocked
- Courageous Anthem replaces Bless (no sustain needed) → frees 1 action/round + 1 prepared spell slot
- Rallying Anthem replaces Benediction → frees another spell slot
- Less healing needed (buffs/debuffs reduce incoming damage) → fewer Cursebound triggers → less Enfeebled/Sickened self-penalty
- Can finally use her offensive divine spells: Holy Light, Behold the Weave, Moonbeam, Fear the Sun

### Bargrum (Guardian) — Sustained
- Courageous Anthem +1 makes his Strikes more accurate
- Reduced incoming damage means less HP burned through Intercept Attack
- Rallying Anthem +1 AC/saves when party needs defense

### Ferrok (Magus) — Amplified
- +1 attack from Anthem increases Spellstrike hit/crit rate (crits are where Magus damage explodes)
- Bon Mot/Dirge softening enemies makes his attacks land more often
- Recall Knowledge identifying lowest saves helps him choose which spell to Spellstrike with

### Bael (Kineticist) — Complemented
- +1 attack/damage from Anthem on every action
- Bon Mot -2 Will helps his rare Demoralizes land
- Tivael covers Fort and Will targeting that Bael can't
- Electric Arc covers Reflex alongside Flying Flame

## Character Relationships (Session 1 Observations)

- **Bargrum**: Immediately started intercepting for Tivael. Volunteered to test every trap. Led the negotiation structure with Zovi while Tivael handled the diplomacy. Complementary dynamic — the dwarf provides the moral framework, the dragon provides the silver tongue.
- **Ferrok**: Had mic trouble for the first 20 minutes. Once online, delivered the biggest hit (56 damage Spellstrike) and the firmest ultimatum to Zovi. Plays bad cop naturally. Adapted to incorporeal enemies with Ghost Oil — tactically flexible.
- **Bael**: Repositioned the party with Four Winds at the start of exploration. Flying Flame remains his primary damage tool. Thermal Nimbus provides consistent chip + fire resistance. Treat Wounds between fights. Crit-failed a Treat Wounds at session end — "I explode before he can help me."
- **Raylta**: "I wanted to heal the new guy" — immediately looked out for Tivael. Took massive damage from Telekinetic Storm (60 in one fight). Her rank 4 AoE Heal for 59 HP was the session's biggest save. Already pushing into Cursebound 2 / Enfeebled 2 from healing pressure. Tivael's Rallying Anthem visibly helped — "Oh, still up" after hits that would have downed someone without the +1 AC/saves.
- **Tivael + Party**: The party welcomed Tivael naturally. No awkward integration. Tivael immediately contributed with RK, debuffs, and damage. "Protect me" set the tone — tiny dragon, big impact. The Zovi negotiation showed the party deferring to Tivael's Diplomacy naturally.

## DM Profile — Cody McGraw (Ryu K.)

- **Style**: Fair but unforgiving. Plays enemies intelligently. Targets the healer. Positions AoE optimally.
- **Difficulty**: High. Let the original party TPK. Tracks regeneration, persistent damage, and enemy abilities precisely.
- **Safety nets**: 3 hero points/session, persistent damage clears after combat, full HP on long rest, don't drop items when unconscious.
- **RK policy**: Doesn't give false info on poor rolls. Generous with information when discovered through play. Dubious Knowledge not recommended.
- **Death trait ruling**: Spells with death trait still function against death-immune creatures, just the death effect portion is negated.
- **Encounter design**: Mix of single bosses and 4-6 enemy hordes. ~5 encounters per long rest. Back-to-back fights with limited resting.
- **Session 1 observations**: 4 combat encounters + 1 negotiation + exploration/traps in ~3h50m. High combat density. DM uses AoE frequently (Shadow Expulsion, Noise Blast, Telekinetic Storm). Traps deal significant damage (40 mental from Telepathic Jolt). Welcomes new player smoothly — Tivael integrated within 10 minutes.
