# Progression — Sovael

## Current Level: 11 (Fists of the Ruby Phoenix, entering 2026-07)

Built at level 11 as Tivael's replacement. Wealth matched to Tivael's at retirement.

## Design Thesis

The party lost its dedicated healer (Raylta retired) and re-formed around three martials. Sovael covers **healing + AoE + force-multiplication** in one slot, keeping Tivael's anthem engine while adding real burst damage and the only counteract magic in the party.

Two structural instincts drive the build:

1. **Precast economy.** Ruby Phoenix is discrete, scheduled bouts. Mind of Menace (24 hr), Beseech the Sphinx (10 min), and Vital Beacon all spend slots *before* the bell, costing zero combat actions — the exact pressure point for an action-starved support caster.
2. **Attack-roll spells behave like save spells.** Shadow Signet retargets spell attacks at Fort/Reflex DC, and Whispers of Weakness reveals the lowest save *before* committing. That's what makes Fire Ray and Blazing Bolt reliable rather than swingy.

## Key Build Decisions (settled)

### Blazing Bolt signature at rank 5 (replaced Thunderstrike)
Signature at R5 makes it castable at **ranks 2–6**. The 2A/3A versions hit 2–3 targets at full damage each, all rolls resolved before MAP applies — a crit-fishing spell in a kit built to fish (Shadow Signet, Sure Strike, anthem +1). Thunderstrike's loss is covered by Chain Lightning; it remains reclaimable via Divine Access.

### Dispel Magic as the rank-2 signature
Non-signature Dispel is stuck at counteract rank 2 — auto-fail against anything cast at your level. As a signature it counteracts up to **rank 6** from a top slot at +20. Sovael is the party's only counteract source in a bracket full of buffed champions and mystics.

### Domain Acumen (Fire Ray) over Advanced Revelation (Whirling Flames)
Whirling Flames is base rank 3 with a **+2** heightening interval, so it only improves at ranks 5/7/9 — and focus spells auto-heighten to half level, meaning it **stalls at every even rank**:

| Level | Focus rank | Whirling Flames | Fire Ray |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11–12 | 6 | 8d6 (28) ×2 bursts | **12d6 (42)** |
| 13–14 | 7 | 11d6 (38.5) ×3 | **14d6 (49)** |
| 15–16 | 8 | 11d6 — **no gain** | **16d6 (56)** |
| 17–18 | 9 | 14d6 (49) ×4 | **18d6 (63)** |
| 19–20 | 10 | 14d6 — **no gain** | **20d6 (70)** |

Fire Ray gains 2d6/rank; Whirling Flames 1.5d6/rank with three dead level-pairs. In a tournament of elite single opponents, per-target scaling beats area. **Domain Acumen is also a level-2 feat versus Advanced Revelation's level 6** — cheaper feat, better spell.

**Confirmed: Greater Revelation requires only "initial revelation spell," NOT Advanced Revelation.** Flaming Fusillade at 12 is unblocked.

### Aiuvarin + Ascended Dragonet Heritage
Costs one ancestry feat (L7) to regain all Pearl Dragonet benefits (Bounce Away) while gaining the elf trait, low-light vision, and elf feat access — cashed in for **Elf Step** at 11. All dragonet feats key off the ancestry, not the heritage, so nothing else moved. Elf Step exists specifically to disengage from reactive strike, which the Incendiary Aura game plan requires.

### Roaring Applause known at both R3 and R6
At rank 6 it hits **10 creatures**, and even on a *success* targets lose all reactions. With three melee allies moving through threatened squares every round, mass reaction denial outperforms any damage spell of the same rank.

## Rank 6 Shortlist (level 12+)

- **Radiant Beam** — 120-ft line, spirit damage, **dazzled on failure** (party-wide 20% miss chance vs those enemies). Already known at R5; a R6 copy upgrades the die count. *Legacy good/holy traits — confirm sanctification handling with the DM.*
- **Vampiric Feast** — ~35 at R5 plus temp HP that soaks curse damage before real HP. Touch range is the tax.
- **Spiritual Guardian** — soaks the first 10 damage per hit on a chosen ally; converts idle third actions into ~20–30 HP/round of mitigation. **Peaks at 11–14 and ages out by 15** (the soak never scales).
- **Breath of Life** — reaction death-veto. Near-zero value most bouts, decisive once a book. Never ages out. Note in-bracket bouts may be non-lethal, lowering trigger frequency.

## Divine Access — Unclaimed Picks

Anras grants **three** spells, added free to the repertoire without consuming normal picks. Only **Chain Lightning** is claimed.

- **Thunderstrike (R1)** — free, no cost to any pick
- **Wall of Wind (R3)** — blocks physical ammunition entirely, −2 to big ranged weapons, difficult terrain, Fortitude save for flyers to cross. A hard counter to archers and flying skirmishers, cast from 120 ft.

**Deity sweep result (79 qualifying deities with dust/fire/star/sun domains):** only **Cixyron** also grants Chain Lightning, and its other two spells are worse — Conductive Weapon would *downgrade* a martial already carrying +2 striking runes, and Toxic Cloud fogs your own melee. **Anras is already optimal if keeping Chain Lightning.**

The only genuinely tempting alternative is **Ragathiel** (Sure Strike / **Haste** / Fire Shield). Haste from a rank-3 slot gives a martial +1 Strike per round for a minute (~80 damage over four rounds) versus Chain Lightning's ~39 EV first target from a rank-6 slot. Better per slot in a martial-heavy party; costs the 500-ft multi-target nuke and a rank-6 signature. **Not taken — logged as a live alternative.**

> RAW note: Divine Access says "choose one deity," with no requirement it be your own — the flavor text explicitly contrasts with "conventional worshippers." Sovael can keep Anras as his faith (needed for Evangelize and Pilgrim's Token) while pointing Divine Access elsewhere. **Worth a one-line DM confirmation.**
