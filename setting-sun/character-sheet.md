### Curse Management
Cursebound stacks deal persistent fire equal to their value, now up to **Cursebound 4** (Extreme Curse). Fire Ray, Incendiary Aura **and Debilitating Dichotomy** all advance it. With the Ash Gown gone there is no fire resistance to lose — and none to fall back on.

### Known Weak Points
- **Most of the top-rank AoE friendly-fires.** Spirit Song (cone), Eclipse Burst and Divine Armageddon (60-ft bursts) all hit allies, and Aether Beam hits anything in its line. **Divine Wrath and Frigid Flurry** are the big area spells that need no positioning.
- **Whispers of Weakness** — take it for the +2 status bonus with a Fire Ray queued behind it, not as a scouting action. It does not reveal immunities.
- **Nearly all control is mental/emotion.** Mindless or construct opponents blank the whole wing; fall back to the Incarnate spells' non-mental riders, Fire Ray, Frigid Flurry, Pierce the Sky and AoE damage.
- **Scare to Death is incapacitation** — against creatures above his level its degree of success drops by one.
- **The clustering problem is still live.** Enemy teams route their skirmisher straight at him. Fly first, stay within 15 ft of Clutch (Taunt-extended Intercept), Elf Step, Spiritual Transport.
