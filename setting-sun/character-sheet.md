## Combat Priorities

### Opening (before the bell — tournament bouts are scheduled)
1. **Mind of Menace** (10 min, 24 hr) — banks the Fight with Fear reaction
2. **Vital Beacon** — converts healing into allies' spare Interact actions
3. Wand of Tailwind for the day
> ⚠️ **Pre-buffing is banned during the Ruby Phoenix qualifier** — exploration actions are fine, but you cannot Haste yourself before a bout. Plan for round-one setup instead.

### Round 1 (the standard opener — do this every fight)
1. **Free**: Lingering Composition (Performance +23 vs standard DC → very likely 3–4 rounds)
2. **1A**: Courageous Anthem
3. **Free**: **Talent Envy** — on a successful Performance check, Demoralize an onlooker within 60 ft as a **free action**, and a *critical* Performance success upgrades the Demoralize by one degree. **This is free value on a check you were already making. Use it every single fight**, aimed at whatever the martials are about to hit.

### Rounds 2+ (composition lingering — actions freed up)
- **Boss**: **Fire Ray** via Shadow Signet at their revealed weak save (~42 EV) → **Foretell Harm** free action
- **Sustained fire pressure**: **Flaming Fusillade** — one minute of **1-action Ignitions**. Best in a long fight where you want a cheap repeatable attack every round rather than one big hit.
- **Crowd control**: **Dirge of Doom** (free, 30-ft emanation, **Frightened 1 with no save and no roll**). The real value is the rider: **enemies can't drop below Frightened 1 while they stay in the area**, so it stops the usual end-of-turn tick-down and holds a −1 on everything they do. Pairs directly with Talent Envy's Demoralize — one lands the stack, the other stops it decaying.
- **Crowd**: **Roaring Applause** at rank 6 — **10 creatures lose reactions**; failures also Slowed 1
- **Ally-safe AoE**: Divine Wrath (enemies-only burst) or Chain Lightning (you pick the arc)
- **Pre-contact AoE**: Fireball before the martials close
- **Caster shutdown**: Manifestation of Spirits (Stupefied 3) · Dispel Magic at rank 6
- **Party defense**: **Scintillating Safeguard** (reaction — resistance 10 vs one damage type, for you *and* allies) against a telegraphed big hit
- **Healing**: signature Heal at whatever rank fits; Staff of Healing to save slots
- **Emergency**: Martyr's Intervention · Bounce Away (bludgeoning) · Elf Step · fly straight up

### Curse Management
Cursebound stacks deal persistent fire equal to their value **and suppress your own fire resistance**. On 164 HP that's affordable chip damage — but **on Bonmou, Refocusing costs 10 minutes of the qualifier clock**, so clearing Cursebound is no longer free between fights. The Prognostic Veil's daily Focus Point partly covers this.

### Known Weak Points
- **Whispers of Weakness has stopped paying for itself in this AP.** Three uses in S10 returned no actionable information (an unusable area/splash weakness, then "no weakness" twice). **Take it for the +2 status bonus to your next attack, with a Fire Ray already queued behind it** — not as a scouting action.
- **Fortitude AoE lane is thin** — Divine Wrath carries it alone
- Nearly all control is **mental/emotion** — mindless or construct opponents blank the debuff wing entirely; fall back to Fireball / Chain Lightning / Fire Ray and healing
- **Incendiary Aura requires you within 10 ft** of enemies — that's what Elf Step and permanent flight are for
- **The inherited clustering problem is still live.** In S10 he parked inside a tick swarm's space and needed Clutch to Intercept for him three separate times, then ate 90 in one turn from an archer with a clean line. **He has permanent flight and used it exactly once all session — and it worked perfectly.** Distance first, debuff second.
