### Defensive Layers
- **Absorb Magic** (Living Nexus reaction) — on an enemy's magical action within 30 ft, become **overflowing** for 1 minute: **+2 status to saves vs magical effects** (up from +1 at 15). Overflowing is then spent on Healing Nexus, Aether Beam or Overflowing Blade.
- **Ferocious Will** ⭐ **NEW L15** (reaction, 1/hour) — when you succeed at a save against a magical mental effect, **8d6 mental** back at its source (basic Will vs spell DC 37; Slowed 1 on a critical failure). Mysterious Resolve turns Will successes into crits, so this triggers on most mental effects.
- **Form a Flock** ⭐ (L13, 2 actions) — **25 temporary HP**, **enemies within 10 ft are Dazzled**, grants **Acrid Barrage**; costs **weakness 5 to area/splash** while it lasts.
- **Bounce Away** (Pearl Dragonet reaction) — resistance 15 to bludgeoning from the triggering Strike, then Stride/Fly half Speed. No counter-strike.
- **Spell Riposte** (R7 reaction) — counteract a spell targeting you; on a counteract, it hits its caster instead.
- **Drop Dead** (R5 reaction) — an ally hit by an attack turns Invisible and leaves an illusory corpse.
- **Wooden Double** (occult reaction) — when critically hit, Step away and a wooden block takes the blow.
- **Scintillating Safeguard** (R6 reaction) — resistance to one damage type of a triggering effect, for you *and* allies in range.
- **Eat Fire** (arcane cantrip reaction) — resistance 5 fire, then Belch Smoke.
- **Elf Step** (1 action, Step 5 ft twice) · permanent flight · Shield cantrip · Sanctuary (R2) · Mirror Image (R2)
- **Robust Health** ⭐ **NEW L15** — +15 HP to every Treat Wounds / Battle Medicine received, and Battle Medicine immunity lasts 1 hour instead of 1 day. **With three party medics this is a lot of extra healing.**

## Combat Priorities

### Opening
> ⚠️ **Pre-buffing is banned during the Ruby Phoenix qualifier** — exploration actions are fine, casting buffs before a bout is not.

### Round 1 (the standard opener)
1. **Free**: Lingering Composition (**Performance +28/+30** — crit success far more often now)
2. **1A**: Courageous Anthem (or Dirge of Doom)

### Rounds 2+
- **Action denial (S14's proven lane)**: **Incarnate Ancient Specter** (Prone even on a successful save) · **Roaring Applause** (Slowed cancels Quickened; the clapping provokes) · **Wall of Wind** vs archers · **True Target** before the martials swing
- **Boss single-target**: **Fire Ray** (16d6) through the Shadow Signet at the revealed weak save → **Foretell Harm**
- **Big AoE**: **Spirit Song** (Fort, 60-ft cone) · **Eclipse Burst** (Reflex, 60-ft burst) · **Divine Armageddon** (60-ft burst) — **all three hit allies**; Divine Wrath remains the only enemies-only burst
- **Ally-safe single-arc**: Chain Lightning (you pick the arc)
- **Caster shutdown**: Manifestation of Spirits · Dispel Magic · **Spell Riposte** on a targeted spell
- **Party defense**: Scintillating Safeguard · **Drop Dead** on an ally about to be focused · Wand of Energy Aegis
- **Healing**: signature Heal · Lively Flight (heal + free reaction-proof fly) · Staff of Healing · Healing Nexus on yourself when overflowing
- **Emergency**: Form a Flock (25 temp HP, Dazzled aura) · Bounce Away · Elf Step · fly straight up · Dust of Disappearance

### Curse Management
Cursebound stacks deal persistent fire equal to their value **and suppress your own fire resistance — including the new Ash Gown's 10.** On Bonmou, Refocusing costs 10 minutes of the clock. The Prognostic Veil's daily Focus Point partly covers this.

### Known Weak Points
- **Most of the new top-rank AoE friendly-fires.** Spirit Song (cone), Eclipse Burst and Divine Armageddon (60-ft bursts) all hit allies, and Aether Beam hits anything in its line. With four melee allies, Divine Wrath is still the only big area spell that needs no positioning.
- **Whispers of Weakness** — take it for the +2 status bonus with a Fire Ray queued behind it, not as a scouting action.
- **Nearly all control is mental/emotion** — mindless or construct opponents blank the debuff wing; fall back to the Incarnate spells' non-mental riders, Fire Ray, and AoE damage.
- **The clustering problem is still live.** Enemy teams route their skirmisher straight at him every fight. Fly first, stay within 15 ft of Clutch (Taunt-extended Intercept), Elf Step.
