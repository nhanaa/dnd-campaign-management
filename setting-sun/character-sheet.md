### The L16 additions, in brief
- **Fortissimo Composition** (free-action spellshape, 1 Focus Point) — if your next action is Courageous Anthem, roll Performance vs the highest Will DC among the targets: **success → +2 status, critical success → +3**; on a failure the Anthem stays +1 and **the Focus Point is refunded**. This directly answers S14's finding that a +1 Anthem flipped almost nothing. It competes with Lingering Composition for the free spellshape slot before the Anthem.
- **Ancestral Memories** (1 action, Imperial focus spell, rank 8 when heightened) — **+3 status** to your next spell attack this turn **or −3 status** to an enemy's next save against your spell this turn. Stacks in front of Fire Ray (attack) or any save spell. Status penalty — does **not** stack with Frightened.
- **Scare to Death** (1 action, **incapacitation**) — Intimidation vs Will DC within 30 ft: crit success → Fort save vs your Intimidation DC or die; otherwise Frightened 2 + Fleeing 1 round. Success → **Frightened 2**; failure → Frightened 1. Target immune for 1 minute. At +32 it is effectively a one-action Frightened 2 against level-16 opponents (it crit Thrasfyr B by 12 on its debut).
- **Debilitating Dichotomy** — see the class-feat table. A single-target, no-attack-roll 21d6 mental hit that costs Sovael almost nothing because of Mysterious Resolve.

### Curse Management
Cursebound stacks deal persistent fire equal to their value **and suppress your own fire resistance — including the Ash Gown's 10.** Fire Ray, Incendiary Aura **and Debilitating Dichotomy** all advance it. The Prognostic Veil's daily Focus Point partly covers the focus-point side.

### Known Weak Points
- **Most of the top-rank AoE friendly-fires.** Spirit Song (cone), Eclipse Burst and Divine Armageddon (60-ft bursts) all hit allies, and Aether Beam hits anything in its line. **Divine Wrath and Frigid Flurry** are the big area spells that need no positioning.
- **Whispers of Weakness** — take it for the +2 status bonus with a Fire Ray queued behind it, not as a scouting action. It does not reveal immunities.
- **Nearly all control is mental/emotion** — and the new L16 picks (Canticle, Dichotomy, Scare to Death) deepen that. Mindless or construct opponents blank the whole wing; fall back to the Incarnate spells' non-mental riders, Fire Ray, Frigid Flurry and AoE damage.
- **Scare to Death is incapacitation** — against creatures above level 16 its degree of success drops by one.
- **The clustering problem is still live.** Enemy teams route their skirmisher straight at him every fight. Fly first, stay within 15 ft of Clutch (Taunt-extended Intercept), Elf Step, Spiritual Transport.
