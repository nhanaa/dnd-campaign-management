### 1. Hero point house rule

**Rule**: spend a hero point, reroll the d20; if the new die is 10 or lower, add +10.

- Earliest sighting — **S6** (kitchen fight): *"Rama: Stance up. Missed first attack — 'I'm going to hero point that. I'm tired of this shit.' (House rule 10+ kicked in: rerolled, hit.)"*
- **S15**: *"Spent his first-ever hero point on a Dashing Pounce leap and got the table's house rule demonstrated live — low reroll, +10, crit success"* (Zarok). Same session, Bai Jian: *"hero-pointed Trip reroll (nat 1 → reroll 5, +10 house rule → 29, success)."* Zanthar: *"nat 1 → 6, +10 → 27, hit."*
- **S16**: *"Hero point — under the table's +10 rule an automatic success — 'I'm going to keep our reputation clean.'"* (a DC 10 flat check).
- **S18**: *"Bai Jian spent a hero point on the reroll (10 → +10 house rule → 32)"*; *"Cyrathul hero-pointed a natural 1 into a critical success (reroll 10, +10 house rule → 32)"*; *"critically failed his DC 11 persistent-damage flat check on a natural 1 and spent a hero point, which the house rule makes an automatic success against that DC."*
- **S19** (world.md, "Table rules confirmed S19"): *"the hero-point reroll +10 on a die ≤10 house rule is in use (logged as `+ 10`)."*
- **S20**: *"Two carry a `+ 10` — the table's hero-point house rule firing on a reroll of 10 or lower."* The S20 recap distinguishes this from the **Elidir city-bonus rerolls** (a fortune reroll that must be kept, and a roll-twice-take-better) — *"Four of the five Elidir rerolls came up 10 or lower and none took the +10, which is the tell."*
