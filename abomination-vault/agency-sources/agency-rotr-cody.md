## Section C — campaign summary

### 1. Hero point house rule

**None — hero points are not in play.** A grep for "hero point" returns zero hits across all six recaps, `dynamics.md`, `world.md`, `character-sheet.md`, `progression.md`, and `rolls.txt` (S9 export header: `rerolls=1`, and that one reroll is Sevek's mythic-proficiency initiative reroll at 14:23:07). Every reroll and "stay up" beat in this campaign is a **Mythic Point** spend. (Per CLAUDE.md, Cody's other table `setting-sun/` uses a 3-per-session hero-point ration with no +10 rule; nothing of the kind appears at this table.)

The operative economy, quoted from `world.md` → House Rules & Table Facts:

> "**Mythic Point economy**: killing blow on a mythic enemy = +2 MP to the killer, +1 to everyone else; completed hazards refund similarly (best contributor +2). Mythic-proficiency rerolls can rescue *untrained* skill checks — the party's emergency key."
> "**Mythic hazards refund all mythic points on completion** (S7, Zutha's Legacy) — not just +2/+1."

And from `dynamics.md` → Mythic Economy:

> "Cody rules generously on player-side mythic creativity (S4's daily Summon Mythic Power reset; S5's Draconic Salvation on a plain fail) but enemies get the same toys: Mythic Ferocity on all Risen, Mythic Resilience on the natbakhs, Remove Condition/Recharge on Gavergash."

**Changes over time**
- **S4**: Aureys discovers Summon Mythic Power resets daily; Cody allows it ("designers clearly never considered it"). Kill/refresh refunds observed ("mythic points refresh" on Krune's death).
- **S6**: Cody tightens the calling refill — Sevek's pre-bed Long Jump denied ("it's fulfilling your mythic calling, not just jumping up a wall"); the same jump in the finale, made for movement, is allowed. Cody **refuses** Aureys' Thief's Calling refund on a nat 20 (she nets −1 on the Mirror).
- **S7**: **Mythic hazards refund everyone to full** (Zutha's Legacy, six MP spent, all returned).
- **S9**: **Liralarue's Glimpse** (once-weekly free reroll, non-MP) surfaces via Paya. Cody, pre-fight: *"Be prepared to burn mythic points, guys. You get them back if they die."* Paradigm Cube's trade value doubled "because it's mythic."
