## Section C — campaign summary

### 1. Hero point house rule

- **Core rule (S4 recap, "Hero point ledger"):** *"house rule: reroll OR bump one degree"*. `dynamics.md`: *"**Hero-point house rule (confirmed S4):** spend = **reroll OR bump one degree of success**. Degree bumps are invisible in the Foundry export (no second d20)."* Examples of bumps: Argon's F1 miss→hit (S4), Ruben's Vampiric Touch save (S4), Snagg's staff crit-fail → fail (S6), Ruben's Dispel Magic counteract fail → success (S8).
- **Refresh cadence — changed:** S1 recap: *"camp overnight; safe patrolled road; hero points topped to max"* (per-session/rest refill at the start). `dynamics.md`: *"**CHANGED — as of S7 hero points refresh on a LONG REST, not per session** ('that was the start and then we changed to long rest' — Xurl); the overnight rest granted one each, and both were spent in round 1 of the next fight."* S7 recap: *"The night's rest restored everyone to full, gave the party **a hero point each**."* S8: *"one each from the overnight long rest."*
- **Mid-session awards by the DM:** S2 (+1 to Xurl for honestly flagging void healing); S4 (+1 each after the mirror vision — *"The DM awarded everyone a hero point"*); S8 (+1 each mid-fight — *"thank you for not trying to look this up… I don't want to kill you here"*).
- **Not in use:** S8 recap: *"this table does **not** use the +10-on-a-low-reroll house rule — the rerolls came up 3, 8, 9 and 20 with no bonus applied."* `dynamics.md`: *"This table has **no blanket nat-1 auto-reroll**."*
- **Pax-PC hero point totals across the campaign:** Argon ≥6 (S2 ≥2, S3 1, S4 3); Snagg 3 (S5 1, S6 1 out-of-combat, S7 1, S8 0). Snagg went into the S7 chain room with none available and spent none in S8 despite holding two.

### 4. Least-agency fights (3)

1. **S4 C3 — The Last Door (Argon).** Paralyzed at initiative 24 before his own 17 came up, disarmed by the same crit, and left at 10 HP among four undead; he never took a turn. The only "agency" was the RP call to hold the door.
2. **S3 — The Weeping Scribe (Argon).** PL+3 elite incorporeal solo with Rejuvenation: Argon dealt 0 of the party's 6 damage, missed his one Strike, ate a 50-point crit, and the only lever that worked (the pendant) was RP the party couldn't complete in the room.
3. **S7 E1 — The Chain Room (Snagg).** Zero Strikes in two rounds: every action went into Escape attempts and self-heals after Impaling Chain anchored him; chains attacked from anywhere so there was nothing to stand in front of; regen only silver/holy stopped and he had neither. Full → 2 HP, 0 damage dealt.

*(Runner-up: S8 Volluk — Snagg dealt 30 of ~175, went down three times, and his Combat Grab was immune-locked; but Snagging Strike, the Clumsy 3 crit card, the dispel call and the run-back-in were real levers, so it doesn't make the bottom three.)*

Also called the shove-Ruben-clear play and delayed behind the healer.
2. **S5 — The Decoy Run (Snagg).** 124 damage (60% of PC damage), **three kills** on five jaws connections, and the Reactive Strike one-shot that broke the enemy captain's morale and ended the fight by surrender.
3. **S4 C1 — The Flesh Reliquary (Argon).** 123 of 260 (47%), both weapon kill-blows including the called-shot nat-20 50-damage crit on the priestess; the fight's two biggest hits were his.

*(Runners-up: S7 E3 Dining Room — 99 dmg, 2 kills incl. a Reactive Strike kill and the finisher; S7 E2 Feeding Ground — Snagging Strike's off-guard was the difference between Alder's fight-ending crit and a plain hit.)*
