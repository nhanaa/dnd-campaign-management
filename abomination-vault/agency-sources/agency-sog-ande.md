## Section C — campaign summary

### 1. Hero point house rule

**Core rule** (`sessions/session-0-06-23-2026/session-0-notes.md`, S0, 2026-06-23):
> "Hero points (generous — variant 'Heroic Rerolls' from Starfinder 2e) — **Heroic Reroll:** on a hero-point reroll, any result **under 10 becomes a 10.** Start of session: **1** (RAW). Sources of extra: +1 for everyone's first session of the AP. +1 if you're 10+ min early and active in VC. → First session can start at 3 hero points. +1 for assisting in the prior-session recap (replaces the first-session bonus going forward). +1 if Ande is ever late / returning from break (rare). +1 every real-time hour of play. Hero points **can** be spent on initiative rolls."

Restated in play:
- S1 recap: "hero points + the table's 'under-10-becomes-10' heroic-reroll rule make it a clean sweep — no one is affected."
- S8 recap (Kaelen's Flurry): "under AndeGM's Heroic Reroll house rule (*'any result under 10 becomes a 10'*) the reroll floored to exactly 10, converting the miss into a hit."
- `dynamics.md` (Open threads): "AndeGM's house-ruled hero-point economy (floor-the-d20-to-10 reroll, ~5–6 held per PC) plus Mu-Jin's per-round Nudge Fate means the party's whiffs get erased and the alpha strike always lands."

**Changes / precedents over time:**
- S1 → S2: the first-session +1 was replaced by the recap-assist +1 (per S0 notes; S7 and S8 recaps confirm "Mu-Jin's session recap earned the table its opening hero points").
- S4, S5: "hero points reset to 1" at session end (no carry-over).
- S5 E3, S6 E2, S7 E2/break: the hourly +1 arrives mid-fight; S6 E3 the GM "Forgot. Final hero point of the night" and handed it out on the spot.
- **S7 (new precedent):** the GM allowed a hero point on **Weaver's** (a familiar's) Fortitude save — "By the way, you are able to use hero points, yeah" — not RAW (`dynamics.md` flags it).
