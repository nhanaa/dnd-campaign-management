# Argon Rath — Progression Plan (L4 → L11)

Orc (Hold-Scarred) Barbarian, Dragon Instinct (**Barrage / Force**), Free Archetype (Mauler). Joined at L4.

## Class-feature milestones (automatic)
- **L5:** attribute boosts (Gradual); aim for Striking rune by now.
- **L7:** **Draconic Rage bonus 4 → 8 force.** Big damage jump. Weapon Expertise (expert).
- **L9:** **Raging Resistance** — resist **piercing + force**. More HP scaling.
- **L13:** master weapons (later).

## Rune / gold priority
1. **+1 potency → Striking** (2 dice). Biggest DPR jump — prioritize over all.
2. **Ghost Touch** (first property slot) — weapon dice must reach incorporeal undead & Belcorra.
3. **Holy** — +2d4 spirit vs unholy (undead + devils).
4. **Armor:** +1 potency → resilient.
5. Greater Striking (3 dice) by ~L10–12.

## Attribute boosts (Gradual)
- **STR** to max first (accuracy + damage), then **CON** (HP/Fort — you're the frontline). WIS for Perception/Will; DEX only to breastplate cap.
