**Source:** extracted from the local `abomination-vaults-bestiary` Foundry pack (95 AP creatures across 3 books) + Monster Core generics. "—" = no special damage interaction.

**Headline:** across ~125 creatures, *nothing* resists sonic. Every other type hits a wall.

## Incorporeal undead (need Ghost Touch / force / vitality to touch them)
All resist **all damage except force, ghost-touch, vitality** (a few also except spirit):
Jarelle Kaldrian (5), Chandriu Invisar (6), Otari Ilvashti (9), Siora Fallowglade (7), Witchfire Warden (9, also IMM fire), Poisoning Room Specter (7), Bright Walker (9), **Belcorra Haruvex (12, FINAL BOSS)**; generics: Shadow & Poltergeist (also except spirit), Specter, Wraith.

## Standout matchups
- **Zombies → WEAK slashing** (your Guisarme shines).
- **Skeletons → resist slashing/piercing + all energy** (lean on force rage bonus; bludgeoning also works).
- **Oozes → immune/resist slashing & piercing + acid** (bludgeoning or force bonus only).
- **Book 2 devils → IMMUNE fire, WEAK holy** (Holy rune gold; never fire).
- **Gibtas & Seugathi (Book 2) → resist bludgeoning.**
- **Urdefhan & Void-Healing living creatures → WEAK vitality** (Sorc's job).

## Argon Rath's takeaways (Barrage / Force instinct)
1. Instinct: **Barrage (Force)** — never resisted, and the force **rage bonus pierces incorporeal** (lands full on ghosts + Belcorra). The strongest pick for the AP's back half.
2. **Ghost Touch rune still mandatory** — the force *bonus* pierces ghosts, but the **Guisarme's 1d10 slashing does not**. Ghost Touch lets the weapon dice land too.
3. **Holy rune** = +2d4 spirit vs the unholy majority (undead + devils) + triggers the 9 fiends' weakness.
4. Guisarme (slashing) matchups: great vs zombies (weak), soft vs skeletons/oozes (resist) — force bonus + Ghost Touch carry those.
5. Vitality is the **Sorcerer's** lane — don't try to cover it.
