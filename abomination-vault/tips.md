# Argon Rath — Tactics & Playstyle

## Turn cadence
- **Quick-Tempered** = Rage as a free action at initiative → turn 1 is full offense.
- Typical turn: **Sudden Charge** (double-Stride + Strike) to engage, or **Strike → Strike**, or **Vicious Swing** (2-action big hit). Rage adds flat **force** damage to every Strike.
- **Guisarme has native 10-ft reach** — no stance toggle to manage. You threaten a big zone by default.

## Reactive Strike (take at L6) + reach
- Your 10-ft reach roughly **triples** the squares you threaten → far more Reactive Strikes, and enemies **can't run past you to the Sorc/Thaum** without eating one. Guisarme makes this automatic — no setup action.

## Flanking
- Flank with the **Monk** (opposite sides) → target is **off-guard (–2 AC) to both**. You feed his flurries; he feeds your Strikes. Reach lets you flank from 10 ft / behind the line.

## Damage-type discipline (see bestiary-reference.md)
- **Force rage bonus is never resisted AND pierces incorporeal** — it's the only dragon type whose bonus lands full on ghosts *and* Belcorra. Big edge in the back half of the AP.
- **Ghost Touch rune still mandatory:** the bonus pierces ghosts, but your **weapon's 1d10 slashing doesn't** — Ghost Touch lets the dice land too.
- **Holy rune** = +2d4 spirit vs the unholy majority (undead + devils).
- **Never** rely on fire (Book 2 devils are fire-immune); **never** bring void (heals undead).
- **Vitality is the Sorc's job** (undead + Urdefhan) — don't try to cover it.

## Fear niche
- **Raging Intimidation** + **Intimidating Glare** = Demoralize while raging, no shared language needed (works on undead). Frightened stacks with off-guard. *Your* debuff — nobody else brings it.

## Survivability (the table's tank-by-HP)
- 72 HP + Rage temp HP (level + CON each fight) — biggest body at the table.
- **Orc Ferocity** = drop to 1 HP instead of 0 (1/day) — your panic button. **Diehard** = die at dying 5, not 4.
- Lower AC than a shield-Champion → you eat more crits; reach helps by keeping fewer enemies adjacent.

## Party (who covers what)
- **You:** frontline, damage, fear.
- **Monk (wrestler):** control (Grapple/Trip), Thievery, mobility.
- **Thaumaturge:** versatile damage, Recall Knowledge (Esoteric Lore).
- **Divine Sorcerer:** healing (Heal + Lay on Hands + Medicine), vitality vs undead, buffs.
