## Weapon & gear
- **Guisarme** (+1 striking · **2d10 slashing**, **reach 10 ft, trip**) — native reach, no stance toggle. Feeds Slam Down's trip and (at L6) Reactive Strike.
  - **Property slot is EMPTY — no Ghost Touch yet.** Against incorporeal undead your slashing eats resistance ~5; only your **force rage bonus** ignores it. Ghost Touch removes the ‑5 on the dice (it doesn't unlock damage from zero — the dice already land, just resisted).
- **Breastplate** (no runes yet — resilient is the priority buy).
- **One Hundred Victories** (L3) — **+1 item bonus to Intimidation**; the first time each day you use **Ferocity**, your HP is *set to your ancestry HP (12)* instead of staying at 1. Turns Orc Ferocity from a 1-HP panic button into a real second wind.
- **Boots of the Dead** (L2) — +1 item bonus to saves/DCs vs **forced movement**; **Activate "One of You" (1/hr):** one undead treats you as undead → it's **Off-Guard** to your next melee Strike this turn. Strong opener vs AV undead.
- Mundane kit: rope, waterskin, flint & steel, torch, bedroll, soap, chalk, rations.
- **Purse: 1 gp 1 sp.** (You are broke. A Ghost Touch rune is 75 gp — nowhere close.)
- **Rune priority when money exists:**
  1. **Resilient** (armor) — save cushion; you're the crit-sponge frontliner.

  3. **Holy** (weapon) — +1d4 spirit vs unholy (undead + Book-2 devils) + triggers fiend weaknesses.

## Identity & niche
The party's **frontline + primary damage** (alongside a controller-Monk, striker-Thaumaturge, and Divine-Sorc/Champion healer).

**Secondary niche pivoted this build: from *fear* → *Athletics maneuvers*.** You are now the **battlefield-control bruiser** — **Slam Down** (Shove/Trip + Strike), guisarme **Trip** at reach, **Titan Wrestler** grapples on big things, **Raging Athlete** mobility. The old Demoralize/Intimidating-Glare fear lane is **gone**; the only fear you keep is the **Intimidating Strike** on-hit rider and the +1 Intimidation item.

**Anti-undead tech (AV-relevant):** **Boots of the Dead** opener (off-guard vs an undead), force rage that pierces incorporeal, and Religion +9 for exorcism-style checks.

> ⚠️ **Build-pivot note for analysis:** because the fear feats are gone, do **not** assume Argon is the go-to for Intimidation-based problems (e.g. cowing a haunt's spirits at DC 22). He *can* attempt it if trained, with a +1 item — but it is no longer a specialty. His designated lanes now are **damage, frontline tanking, and Athletics control.**
