## Weapon & gear
- **Guisarme** (1d10 slashing, **reach 10 ft, trip**) — native reach, no stance toggle. Ideal for Reactive Strike (L6).
- **Breastplate** (or better) — buy armor.
- **Rune priority:**
  1. **Striking** (extra weapon dice) — biggest damage spike.
  2. **Ghost Touch** — still mandatory: your force *rage bonus* pierces incorporeal, but the **weapon's 1d10 slashing does not** — Ghost Touch lets the dice land on ghosts + Belcorra.
  3. **Holy** — +2d4 spirit vs all unholy (undead + devils) + triggers the 9 fiends' weakness.
  4. *(Optional)* **Vitalizing** — anti-undead/ghost swap.

## Instinct mechanics (Barrage / Force)
- **Draconic Rage:** rage bonus 2 → **4 force** (→ **8 at L7**, →16 at L15 w/ greater weapon spec). Bonus is force = **pierces incorporeal undead** (the only dragon type that does).
- **Raging Resistance (L9):** resist **piercing + force**.
- **Draconic Arrogance:** +2 vs emotion while raging.
