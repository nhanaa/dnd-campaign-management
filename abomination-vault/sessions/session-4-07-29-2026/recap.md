## Session Summary

The party returned to the Gauntlight's third floor to finish clearing it before tomorrow's twin deadlines — the pirate shipment and the midnight sacrifice of the captured dwarf. Behind the heavy stone doors of the floor's central chamber they found a reliquary desecrated into a shrine: smashed display cases, and a 15-foot statue of a woman stitched together from squares of preserved flesh. Kneeling before it was the cult's spellcasting ghoul priestess, who turned, smiled, and made the same offer as before — *"Drop your weapon and join us."* Ruben answered for the party: *"Pitiful creatures. You will receive your judgment from Pharasma."* The fight that followed was an 80-minute war of attrition against a caster who healed herself by eating pieces of the flesh statue, dropped Alder outright with an upcast three-action Harm (a hero-point reroll that came up nat 1 twice — "one in 400," said the DM), and finally died to Argon's guisarme: a called vertical cut past Alder's shoulder, straight down through her skull, for a 50-damage critical.

The middle of the night belonged to exploration — and to two mysteries walking the halls. An armored knight, visibly not human, twice crossed the party's path and ignored them; it left carrying a huge book taken from a hidden wall-library the party then found for themselves. Inside were the **Gauntlight Blueprints** and a **Folio of Teleportation Research** — Ruben's nat-20 find — which by night's end had the third floor's teleportation circle reactivated (one of nine in the dungeon). The Cracked Magic Mirror showed the room's past: twenty or thirty robed librarians kneeling before empty cases, and then, when the image cleared, devouring one another — the birth of the flesh-eating cult, centuries ago. A second fight against two Canker Cultists mid-ritual in a bone-figurine circle went the party's way (Ruben's Vitality Lash zapped one dead; Argon's 38-damage crit sent the other flying into the flesh mound), though it cost Argon a round paralyzed and Xurl a faceful of Phantom Pain.

Then, with the session winding down and the party healing up to leave, one last unopened door. Xurl said what everyone was thinking — *"Surely that won't go wrong."* Argon stepped forward: *"Let me do it."* Behind the door: a small corpse on a table, three ghouls feeding on it, and the ghast leader the veterans had once fled from, who looked up in annoyance — *"You guys again? Why don't you leave me alone?"* The party ran. Argon held the doorway so they could — took a 34-damage crit that dropped him, until Xurl called across the map, *"You have Ferocity, right?"*, and the One Hundred Victories tattoo stood him back up at 10 HP — then failed the paralysis save. Pax made the call above the table: *"No, just leave. Leave him. He's dead. Don't even try."* The last the party heard, Argon was still fighting. The DM's ruling on ghoul custom: they won't eat him — they'll turn him. **Argon Rath is gone.** The party leveled to 5 on the walk out. Everyone at the table is now on their second character.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Reliquary of Flesh
- The corpses the party left behind yesterday were gone — *"They are aware of our presence, probably"* (Argon).
- Behind the double stone doors: smashed, empty display cases under spotlights, and a **15-ft statue of a woman made of stitched flesh squares** — the source of the room's stench.
- The **ghoul priestess** (Foundry name *Nhakazarin* — never spoken at the table) and two ghoul attendants knelt before it. She repeated the cult's offer: *"Drop your weapon and join us."* / *"Either way, you will be mine."*
- Argon clocked that Xurl's bioluminescent stripes trace the **One Hundred Victories** pattern — the same tattoo Argon wears in scar. Both carry it.

### Act II — Killing the Priestess (Combat 1)
- See Combat Encounters. Key beats: Alder dropped at 46 damage on a double-nat-1 hero point reroll; the priestess healed by **eating a piece of the flesh statue**; Argon ended her with a 50-damage skull-splitting crit; Ruben spat on the corpse; Xurl took her head off with Argon's axe afterward — *"I'm not leaving her mostly intact in one room."*
- Mid-fight, a **figure appeared in the doorway, laughed, and vanished** — Ruben later identified it as the "jokester" that pranked the *previous* party during their fights. First contact for this group.

### Act III — The Knight
- On the way to the safe room, footsteps: a **knight in full plate — visibly not human** — walked past the battered party, ignoring them entirely, and went into the reliquary. They shut the secret door and holed up — *"encountering an enemy we don't know anything about, while we're injured, would be suicide"* (Ruben). Argon: *"I wonder if we're doing something that we would regret later, though."*
- After the healing round it appeared a second time, now **carrying a huge book**. Xurl called out — *"Where'd you find that book?"* — no answer. The party debated jumping it and stood down: *"The last time we picked a fight with a creature who was ignoring us, it went poorly"* (Xurl). It descended the stairs toward the floor below and disappeared.
- Perception checks connected it: **both the knight and the book appear in the recovered tapestries.** Argon, who joined after the tapestries were found, couldn't place it.

### Act IV — The Hidden Library and the Mirror
- Ruben found a **hidden wall-niche library** (nat-20 Perception) — the room the knight took its book from; a conspicuous gap remained in the shelf.
- Recovered: **Folio of Teleportation Research** and the **Gauntlight Blueprints** (a full-day downtime project; about the lighthouse itself, not the dungeon). *"It seems Pharasma has smiled upon me very well today."*
- The **Cracked Magic Mirror** (cracking further with use) showed the room's past: 20–30 robed librarians kneeling before intact-but-empty cases — then devouring each other. The cult's origin. The DM awarded everyone a hero point.
- End of night: Alder performed the activation ritual — **a nat-20 Arcana** (*"That's funny. You activate this."*) — and **brought the third-floor teleportation circle online**. The nat 20 bought extra insight: **nine circles total**, roughly one per floor, each needing on-site reactivation — and the ground-floor circle's connection is severed.

### Act V — The Ritual Chamber (Combat 2)
- A room with a **dead flesh-mound** (like the gibbering mouther, but dark red and dead) ringed by **dozens of tiny bone figurines wrapped in hair**, with two **Canker Cultists** mid-ritual. Corpse-stench: DC 20 Fort at the door (fail = sickened 1; success = immune 24h).
- See Combat Encounters. Ruben's Vitality Lash killed one (*"No, not slash. Zap."*); Argon crit the other for 38 and sent it flying over Xurl into the flesh pile.

### Act VI — The Mushroom Door
- One more door: a soil-floored room with **two Violet Fungi**. One tentacle-slashed **Xurl** for 16 + 5 poison (Enfeebled 1) before the party slammed the door on it. *"Who puts hostile mushrooms in their basement?"* No one went back in.

### Act VII — The Last Door (Combat 3 — Argon's death)
- Heading out, the party passed one unexplored door. Xurl: *"Surely that won't go wrong."* Argon: *"Let me do it"* — deliberately taking point because, as he said after, *"at least I can tank if needed."*
- Inside: a small corpse on a table, **three ghouls feeding**, and the **ghast leader** (Foundry name *Aller Rosk*) — the same creature the three veterans once fled from. It recognized them: *"You guys again? Why don't you leave me alone?"* Argon, who'd never met it: *"I don't know what you're talking about. I don't know you."*
- The party fled; Argon held the doorway. Two ghoul hits (5, 4), then the ghast leader's claw **crit for 34** — Argon dropped, and **One Hundred Victories + Orc Ferocity** set him back to 10 HP on his feet — but the crit tore the guisarme from his hands, and he **failed the Paralysis save (DC 24) by 8.**
- Xurl, above table: *"Pax, are we trying?"* Pax: *"No, just leave. Leave him. He's dead. Don't even try."* Xurl popped Boots of Elvenkind — *"And we're fuckin' runnin'!"* — as Argon shouted *"I'll hold him back!"*
- The DM, as the three escaped: *"You hear that Argon keeps fighting, holding the ghouls as best as he can."*
- Recall Knowledge on the run: the party has killed so many ghouls that the cult will almost certainly **turn Argon rather than eat him** — *"you might have a couple days to save him."* Xurl said the quiet part: *"He's going to come back as an undead ghoul. We're going to have to put him down next time we come down here."*
- Above table: Pax retired Argon on the spot (*"I'd rather just play other characters so we can fill the party hole better"*). The DM noted every player has now lost a character in this campaign. **Party leveled to 5**; Pax builds a new level-5 PC before next session. Next week: the pirate raid — though the party floated calling it off and *"sending the navy"* to buy time for the rescue clock(s).

## Character Spotlight

### Argon Rath · the Wall That Held — *final session*
- The session bookended itself on him. At the door-duty question in the first five minutes he deferred — *"You have more AC than me. You should be first, actually"* (to Xurl). By the last door he'd stopped deferring: **taking point so the squishier three didn't have to.** He volunteered for the fatal door in plain words — *"If you're going to open that door, let me open it. At least I can tank if needed"* — and when it went wrong, his first and only instinct was *"I'll hold him back! Run!"*
- Before the end, he was the session's **executioner**: a 42-damage crit that dropped a ghoul in fight 1, the **50-damage called-shot crit** that killed the priestess (*"curve my blade, just slightly past Alder, and slash straight down on the top of her head"*), and the **38-damage crit** that ended fight 2 by launching a cultist into the flesh mound. Every boss-tier enemy that died to a weapon died to his.
- Sharp above the table too: talked the table through the counteract rules, argued the priestess's Grim Tendrils line down to a single target with a geometry catch, and litigated the incapacitation trait on his own paralysis before accepting the DM's *"trust the system, please."* Read the room at the last door before anyone rolled: *"It's not too late to run away. Or maybe it is too late."*
- Fought the whole first fight **Stupefied 1** — the Furnace's memory-burn from last session only lifted mid-session, giving him two clear-headed hours before the end.
- His body — weaponless, paralyzed, at 10 HP — was left among four undead. The cult will raise him as one of their own.

### Xurl · the Doctrine of Closed Doors
- The party's conscience and its Cassandra. He spent the whole session warning about doors — *"Don't open that door. Just keep walking. There's some bullshit behind all these doors, man"* — and the night's standing wisdom, deployed to talk the party out of jumping the knight: *"The last time we picked a fight with a creature who was ignoring us, it went poorly."*
- **Corpse-disposal doctrine**: borrowed Argon's axe to behead the dead priestess and carried the head away — *"I'm not leaving her mostly intact in one room."* In a dungeon where a pillar of void energy heals the undead, this is not paranoia, it's protocol.
- In combat, the grapple curse continued (two hero-pointed grapples, both still failed — he blamed Ruben for one) but the fists never stopped working: killed a ghoul with Flurry of Blows in fight 1, landed a 20-damage crit on the priestess, stunned a Canker Cultist with Stunning Blows, and delivered *"shockingly, we're going to punch him a bunch"* as an actual mission statement.
- At the end he was the one still watching Argon's sheet: when the 34 crit landed, it was Xurl who called *"Is he done? You have Ferocity, right?"* — Argon had forgotten the feat that stood him back up. Then, when Pax released them, he made the exit cleanly: Boots of Elvenkind, *"and we're fuckin' runnin'!"* — and, first of anyone, named what comes next: putting ghoul-Argon down.

### Ruben Falmoor · Pharasma's Weary Instrument
- Opened the session in full Pharasmin voice — *"Pitiful creatures. You will receive your judgment from Pharasma"* — and spent the boss fight as her favorite target: Chilling Darkness (21), Daze (9), a staff to the head (12), Vampiric Touch (hero-pointed down to 12), and two Harms. He healed through all of it and still brought Alder back from dying with a 30-point Heal.
- The night's best luck ran through him: the **nat-20 Perception** that found the hidden library and the blueprints — *"It seems Pharasma has smiled upon me very well today"* — and the mission-defining line as he shelved the loot: *"Come. We have a sacrifice to find."*
- Kill credit with style: his Vitality Lash dropped a Canker Cultist, and he corrected the DM's narration in real time — *"No, not slash. Zap."*
- Parented his kid Skylar off-mic through the entire boss fight — *"Okay, Skylar, I'm sorry. It just tickles me, okay?"* — without dropping a single heal.
- Carried the retreat's grief: *"Dammit, I hate this"* — then, bitterly, the session's epitaph: *"Every door we open is a freaking encounter. Why do we keep opening doors?"*

### Alder Ungram · the Scholar Who Already Knows How This Goes
- The quiet one had the night's darkest arc. Dropped to dying by a double-nat-1 hero-point reroll against the priestess's Harm (46 damage) — his whole reaction: *"Yeah, yeah. What the fuck?"* When she wound up the second Harm, he deadpanned: *"Yeah, I guess I'll die. That's what happened to me"* — this is the player already on his second character.
- The RK engine ran all night: Exploit Vulnerability on every enemy, and the party's standing orders delivered in his broken deadpan — *"Don't use will save on them... They have much mind power."* His Implement's Interruption punished the priestess's spellcasting; his nat-20 chakram crit helped bring her down (offered bleed-or-double, he took double — 27).
- **He's the one holding the clock**: *"Tomorrow we need to save the person from the ritual."* And he performed the ritual that brought the third-floor teleportation circle online — the party's first real infrastructure in the megadungeon.
- One of the two minds (with Xurl) that placed the armored knight and its stolen book **in the recovered tapestries** — the night's biggest lore connection, made on a post-fight Perception check.

## Combat Encounters

### Combat 1 — The Flesh Reliquary: Ghoul Priestess + 2 Ghouls (~80 min)
**Initiative**: Ghoul B 26 · Xurl 24 · Ruben 24 · Priestess 19 · Ghoul A 15 · Argon 15 · Alder 14 *(Xurl delayed to after the priestess; Argon delayed to after Alder)*
**Enemy stats observed**: Priestess AC 23 base (21–22 under Clumsy/Frightened), spell DC 23–24, Fort +17, Perception +12. Ghouls AC 18 (17 frightened).

- **Round 1** — Ruben opened with **Benediction** + Healing Plaster; the priestess's opening Demoralize was retconned (out of 30-ft range), and her **Chilling Darkness** hit Ruben for **21 cold** — its counteract, after a long table-rules debate, **snuffed every magical light in the party** (counteract 19 vs DC 18; even Ruben's level-3 lamp went out; Xurl burned an action re-lighting his stripes). Both ghouls converged on Xurl — four attacks, one claw connecting for 7; the paralysis save squeaked through *(the die was actually 16 vs DC 17 — the card was quietly adjusted to a success and the table moved on)*. Alder read a **Bless scroll** and tagged Ghoul A with **Exploit Vulnerability**. Then Argon's delayed turn (still **Stupefied 1** from the Furnace): Intimidating Strike missed — **hero point, bumped to a hit** (13, Frightened 1) — he realized Rage never auto-fired, switched it on (7 temp HP, +4 force applied retroactively), and his second swing **crit Ghoul A for 42 — dead on the spot.**
- **Round 2** — Ruben's **Spiritual Armament** hit Ghoul B for a max-roll 16 and he put Guidance on Xurl; the priestess **Dazed** Ruben (9, failed by 1). Xurl's **Flurry of Blows** finished Ghoul B (8+15) — *"quick work of these two,"* Argon noted — and Xurl slid behind her. Alder's Exploit Vulnerability on the priestess succeeded but learned only that she's *not a regular ghoul* — no weaknesses to find. Both minions dead inside two rounds; then the long war began.
- **Rounds 3–7 (attrition vs the priestess)** — Her kit: a **three-action Harm (upcast rank 3)** — Alder **hero-pointed the Fort save and rolled nat 1 twice** ("one in 400"), took **46**, dropped dying, and lost his grip on chakram *and* buckler; Argon failed (23), Ruben and Xurl saved (11 each); she healed herself 23 off the same pulse. **Ruben's two-action Heal (30) brought Alder back** (Wounded 1); Alder retrieved the chakram (the buckler stayed down) and went back to work. She ate a **piece of the flesh statue** to heal 16 — which also scrubbed off the **Clumsy 2 / −5 speed** that Xurl's nat-20 crit card had pinned on her ("until healed," taken literally). Her **Grim Tendrils** got argued down to a single target by Argon's line-geometry catch (Ruben saved, took 3); her **Vampiric Touch** forced Ruben to **hero-point the save to a success** (*"that would have put me out"*) on a **Quickened 4-action turn** that also put a staff into his head (12). Her **second 3-action Harm** went out at rank 2 — the DM misclicked and the table invoked *no take-backs* — and still crit-caught Argon (22). Meanwhile: Xurl went 0-for-3 on grapples (two hero-pointed rerolls, both still crit fails, **prone twice** — he blamed Ruben for one) but **Demoralized her twice** (one success by exactly the tattoo's +1) and crit for 20; Alder's **Implement's Interruption** punished her casting once (18 with the exploited weakness) and backfired once — a crit-miss fumble card left *him* Clumsy 2; his **nat-20 chakram crit** offered bleed-or-double and he took **double (27)**. A figure appeared at the doorway mid-fight; the party glimpsed it on Argon's Perception 28, and later it laughed and vanished — costing Argon only a Will save he passed (Draconic Arrogance +2). A separate rules summit established she's a **unique creature, RK DC 32** — the earlier "weakest save is Fort" slip was minion info — and even Alder's Scholarly Recollection double-roll (28) couldn't crack her.
- **The kill** — After a full whiff cycle (Xurl's readied flank-Flurry missed twice — one nat 1 saved by Halfling Luck, still a miss by 1 — and Argon's nat-1 fumble card shoved him 10 feet), Argon stepped back into Xurl's flank and called the shot: blade curved past Alder, straight down through the top of her skull — **nat 20, 50 damage, dead** (21:47, on his second strike). Ruben spat on her; Argon stabbed the chest *"to make sure she's dead for good"*; Xurl took the head.

**Headline numbers**: Argon landed the fight's two biggest hits (42 and 50) and both weapon kill-blows; damage dealt — Argon 123, Alder 58, Xurl 53, Ruben 26. The priestess self-healed exactly 50 (the statue bite + her own Harm pulses) and put more damage into Ruben (79 taken) than anyone else; Alder was the only PC to drop. Six hero points spent in this fight — only the two degree bumps actually changed an outcome.

### Combat 2 — The Ritual Chamber: 2 Canker Cultists (~37 min)
**Initiative**: Cultist A 31 · **Argon 23** · Ruben 17 · Cultist B 16 · Alder 12 · Xurl 11
**Enemy stats observed**: AC 20–21, attacks +14, spell DC 21–22, occult casters (Grim Tendrils, Phantom Pain, Harm). Level 4 — same level as the party, per the DM.

- **Round 1** — Cultist A opened with **Grim Tendrils**: Xurl failed (10 damage + 5 bleed), Argon and Alder saved (5 each). Argon's Intimidating Strike came up **nat 1 — hero point, rerolled to a 22, hit** (17 damage, Frightened 1), then a crit-fumble on the follow-up left him with 1d8 bleed (*"how do you even clip your hand with a reach weapon?"*). Ruben's Vitality Lash chipped 6 and he put Guidance on Argon. Cultist B slapped **Phantom Pain** on Xurl (15 + persistent 1d4+4 mental **+ Sickened 1** — the spell's failure rider, a fight-long −1). Alder's chakram hit for 18 (Exploit Vulnerability success; his RK verdicts: *"Don't use will save on them"* — strong minds, weak bodies), and Xurl's Crane Wing hit for 15 — **Stunning Blows stuck**: Cultist A **Stunned 1 + Frightened 1**.
- **Round 2** — Cultist A's **3-action Harm** (15) healed itself 15 and caught Alder (7) and Ruben (7 — **hero-pointed a nat 1 into a success**). Mid-round the DM collected the forgotten corpse-stench saves: Xurl and Alder immune, **Ruben Sickened 1** — which he later **retched off with a nat 20** (*"Nat 20, baby"*). Argon hit for 16 (Guidance helping). **Ruben's Vitality Lash killed Cultist A** (8 — *"No, not slash. Zap."*). Cultist B leapt its dead partner and **bit Argon** (14): the first Fort save (22 vs DC 22) beat the Ghoul Fever — *"you're not catching it again"* — but the second (21) **failed the paralysis**, and a claw (10) hit him while he stood frozen. Pax litigated the incapacitation trait; the DM: *"Trust the system, please. They're level 4, same as you."* Xurl asked to jump over the cultist — the DM ruled a high jump can't clear a creature taller than 5 feet — so he **Tumbled Through** instead (success by 5) and flurried 13+14.
- **Round 3** — Argon, paralyzed, **lost his entire turn**, then broke free with a **30 Fort** at its end. Ruben poured a **24-point Heal into Xurl** — then wasted his last action on Bleed Out against a bleed-immune ghoul (Alder warned him mid-roll: *"I think they're immune to bleed." "I didn't hear him."*). Cultist B's second Harm caught the room (Ruben 13, others 6–13; healed itself 13) — but casting it triggered **Alder's Implement's Interruption**: a reaction chakram for 17. Alder's own turn was three straight misses (*"he don't like you either"*); Xurl flurried 10 more off the flank with the freed Argon.
- **Round 4** — Argon's Intimidating Strike came up **nat 20: crit, 38** — the cultist flew over Xurl into the dead flesh-mound. Dead (23:34).

### Hazard — Violet Fungi (out of initiative)
Opening the soil-room door, **Xurl** took a tentacle slash for **16** plus **5 poison (Violet Rot, Enfeebled 1)**. Nobody identified the fungi — the whole table failed the Recall Knowledge; the DM narrated what they'd met on pre-campaign travels (*"this is not a fun mushroom"*). The party slammed the door and did not go back.

### Combat 3 — The Last Door: Ghast Leader + 3 Ghouls + Ghast (unresolved — Argon lost)
**Initiative**: Alder 30 · Ruben 30 (Ruben resolved first — Argon told him "Ruben, you go first") · Ghoul 29 · Ghoul 27 · Ghast Leader 24 · **Argon 17** (Quick-Tempered auto-Rage, 7 temp HP; paralyzed at initiative 24, before his 17 ever came up) · Ghast 16 · Xurl 13 *(three ghouls narrated feeding; two rolled initiative)*
**Enemy stats observed**: Ghast leader claw +17, 2d6+9, Paralysis DC 24 — well above the floor's other undead. Ghast Stench DC 16.

- **The setup** — Argon opened the door on three ghouls feeding on a small corpse, with the ghast leader working at a table. The party chose flight immediately; this was never a fight they meant to have.
- **Ruben** readied a Guidance and ran. **Alder** failed the Stench save (Sickened 1), used Ruben's called play — step, close the door, run — and fled (the door bought seconds, not safety).
- **The lead ghoul** reached Argon in the doorway: jaws (5 — Ghoul Fever save passed, 19 vs 17), then claw (4). Rage's temp HP ate most of it — Argon met what came next at 27 real HP.
- **The ghast leader** recognized the three veterans — *"You guys again? Why don't you leave me alone?"* — Argon, who'd never met it: *"I don't know what you're talking about. I don't know you."* Then the claw: **critical, 34 damage.** Argon dropped — and it was **Xurl who caught it from across the map**: *"Is he done? You have Ferocity, right?"* Argon had forgotten his own feat. **One Hundred Victories + Orc Ferocity** stood him back up at **10 HP** *(table-stated; the tattoo's RAW value for Hold-Scarred is 12)* — but the crit had torn the guisarme from his grip, and with the DC 24 Paralysis save coming — *"I don't think it matters that much to me"* — he **failed it by 8.**
- **Xurl** activated Boots of Elvenkind and ran with the others. The encounter ended with Argon paralyzed, weaponless, at 10 HP, surrounded by four undead — still fighting, the DM narrated, as long as the party could hear.
- **Aftermath** — RK on the run: the cult will **turn him, not eat him**; a couple of days before it's done. The party will likely find a ghoul wearing Argon's scars the next time they come down. The DM, above table: *"I don't want you to feel bad because I killed your character... you guys didn't heal it up back to full"* — and the log agrees: Argon met that door at 29 of 72 HP.
