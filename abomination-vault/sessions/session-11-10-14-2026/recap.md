## Session Summary

The party finished clearing the fourth floor. Xurl walked a tunnel past Otari's island for ten minutes until it was obviously never going to end. Then the party worked through the dining room's remaining doors: a storeroom, a hatch that turned out to be a waste chute running straight into the green pond, the floor's **teleport circle** (Alder reactivated it), and a wine cellar Xurl looted for ten bottles. The hatch room was haunted. A **poltergeist** frightened half the party before initiative, flung broken knives and plates at them, and turned invisible. Ruben's third Vitality Lash ended it. Back in the lounge, Xurl took down the haunted painting Ruben had told him to leave alone (*"Brother, I'm already cursed"*), and there was a door behind it. Behind a second hidden door was a round chamber with **the Gauntlight's pale blue beam** rising through it from about four floors below. Beside it stood a stone slab with iron rings in the corners, and burned into it the silhouette of **a dwarf**. Xurl said what everyone was thinking: this is where the cult sacrificed the dwarf the party failed to save.

Then they went back for **the chain woman**, the S7 fight that cost them 132 HP and dealt her zero. Alder walked in first wearing his Masquerade Scarf disguised as Volluk, the floor boss they beat in S8: *"I need you for something. Come here."* Lap: *"It doesn't matter. She attacks you."* It was four rounds of chains from anywhere in the room. Her gaze kept the party Frightened, she put Alder down in round 2 and pinned him bleeding to the floor, and her **regeneration** undid most of what the martials dealt. Xurl and Snagg kept her grabbed, tripped and off-guard. Then the party found her off switch as a relay. **Snagg** kept pushing for it: spend Alder's Recall Knowledge on the regeneration, because *"there must be something to turn it off."* Lap wouldn't take it as first put; Xurl tightened it to *"How do I disable it?"*, and Alder's answer was fire or holy (Lap: one true, one a lie; her stat block says holy or silver). Snagg: *"Do you have any holy damage?"* After the break, Ruben did. **Holy Light**, the light-trait spell the party had been missing since S9, hit, he spent a hero point to make it a critical, and **85 damage** burned her to ash. *"Pharasma may save your soul, but I will not."*

Alder's mirror showed the dining room in its prime: **Belcorra hosting four giants**, leaving at a whisper, and her servants murdering the guests and then each other, until the head chef killed two more and himself. Everyone got a hero point for it. Back in Otari the next morning, the mayor wasn't home. He was at the Dawnflower Library, standing beside an empty case. **Vol Rajani's sword was stolen last night**, during the one night a week its alarm is down for maintenance. An acolyte blurted out that it has to be **Carman Rajani**. Vandy shut him down for lack of evidence, then admitted she and the mayor think so too. Xurl caught the mayor's face changing when the party said the Roseguard lived happily ever after, and asked. In private, the mayor told them **the Roseguard were cursed**. Zarmavdian left to find a cure and never came back. Ten or fifteen years later the captain lost herself and **Aesephna had to kill her with her own hand**, then sealed herself into a secret tomb rather than risk what she'd become after death. That tomb is the crypt the previous adventurers found under the fishery. **The cloak is in it**, and so is a negative energy that raises anything that dies there. The spellbook's lead is a bookstore owner. Lap: *"It is a module, so it's got to happen like this."*

## Key Events

### Act I — Loose Ends on Floor Four (0:00 – 0:19)
- Lap's opener re-set the board: four Roseguard icons. The **key** is in hand, the **sword** is known (the library), the **cloak** is probably with the mayor's family, and the **spellbook** is probably in Absalom.
- Ruben tied the cloak to the mayor's family, and Xurl brought up **the huntress's crypt**. The previous party found **Aesephna Menhemes's crypt** under the Otari fishery (while dealing with kobolds and a dragon), reported something strange inside it to the mayor, and no one ever followed up.
- The party chose to push on. Xurl (*"I just need a Battle Medicine or a Lay on Hands and I'm good to keep going"*) got Lay on Hands from Ruben.
- **The endless tunnel.** Xurl jumped across the water (Cat Fall made the 15-foot drop moot; Snagg: *"Okay, he's fine. See? Monk bullshit."*) and followed a passage upward for ten minutes. It never ended, so he came back: *"Long-ass fucking tunnel. Ain't shit back there. Except more tunnel."* It keeps climbing and might reach the surface.
- **The dining room's other doors.** Xurl rolled a die to pick doors, which visibly upset Ruben (*"You're causing my OCD to flare up"*). Room one was a storeroom with two skeletons by the barrels and nothing useful. Snagg: *"Nothing useful. Story of my life."* Room two had an open metal hatch in the floor and bins of broken tools, stones and bones. Ruben made sure the doors behind them wouldn't lock.

### Act II — The Poltergeist (0:19 – 0:53)
- While Xurl leaned over the hatch, a ghostly figure appeared beside him and everyone rolled Will against fear. **Snagg crit-succeeded. Xurl and Ruben were Frightened 2.** Snagg: *"I hate ghosts. I hate ghosts."*
- Three rounds. Broken knives rose out of the bins at Xurl (21), then a storm of plates and utensils. It turned invisible partway through. Alder's read that a bodiless thing's **lowest save is Fortitude** kept Ruben on Vitality Lash, which ignores its resistance. Snagg's ghost-touch jaws grabbed it on a natural 19, Alder crit with the chakram, and **Ruben's third Lash** finished it. See Combat Encounters.
- Afterwards, Ruben lit the hatch. **The pipe below is a waste chute** running west, and it drops out into the **green pond** with the dead tentacled thing from S10. Xurl climbed down, looked, and shut the hatch: *"because it makes me nervous."*

### Act III — The Teleporter, the Wine, and the Beam (0:53 – 1:22)
- A room with a dusty washtub and a skeleton half inside it. Then **the floor-4 teleport circle**. Alder reactivated it later, during Snagg's healing break.
- **The wine cellar.** Ten old sealed bottles. Xurl wanted to load up until he was nearly encumbered, and Lap capped him at ten: *"Xurl doesn't really know shit about alcohol."* Snagg wanted to check whether it was poisonous; Xurl: *"Definitionally alcohol is a poison… it's just a poison that people like the effects of."*
- The last door opened onto a staircase that comes out on **floor 3 by the library**, which is already explored.
- **The haunted painting.** Xurl wanted the graveyard painting from the lounge that Ruben had told him to leave (*"I'm the designated thing toucher. I see a thing, I touch the thing."* / *"Brother, I'm already cursed. Let's see how many curses we can stack in this baby"*). He touched it, something happened and then nothing did, and **there was a door behind it**. *"At this point, I've stolen every other painting I've touched."*
- **The beam chamber.** Xurl found a second hidden door at the corridor's dead end. Behind it was a round grey room with **the Gauntlight's pale blue energy** running floor to ceiling. Alder's **Scholarly Recollection** Recall Knowledge: undead that touch it heal, and it rises **from about four floors below** to the lighthouse. On a stone slab with **metal rings in the corners** is a black silhouette of a body, **a dwarf** by the wide shoulders.
- Xurl: *"This is probably where they sacrificed the dwarf we failed to save."* Ruben felt pure evil magic, which Lap confirmed: *"Let us leave this place. It sickens me."* Alder read the rings for what they were (*"I think that's how they chained him to the center"*): *"Well, don't stay in the light, guys."*
- Snagg slipped while searching a wall, banged his head on a washtub, and took 4.

### Act IV — Going Back for the Chain Woman (1:22 – 1:32)
- Alder: *"Well, let's deal with the chain lady."* Snagg had already been game (*"Why not? We can try again"*), but **not hurt**: *"Hold on. We can spend time healing first… Let's spend 20 minutes so I can heal."* Alder: *"Are you about to stab me as I walk away?"* Snagg's Risky Surgery cycles (critical successes on Alder and on himself) topped them off, and Alder reactivated the circle in the same ten minutes.
- The plan. Snagg pointed out that she can attack from anywhere in the room, so sneaking buys little. Xurl had to turn off his bioluminescent stripes (*"it is mechanically impossible for me to be hidden while using stripes"*). Everyone agreed there was no surprise round to win.
- **Alder's idea:** the **Masquerade Scarf**, disguised as *"that thing that we killed that was the boss of this place"* (Volluk, Discorporated in S8 and due to re-form). *"Oh, God, I'm not good at lying."* Ruben read the full Illusory Disguise text aloud and put the effect on him in Foundry (+4 status, plus his level while untrained). Alder walked in. She paused: *"What are you doing here?"* Alder: **"I need you for something. Come here."** His Impersonate checks came to 24 and 23. Lap: *"It doesn't matter. She attacks you."* 15 damage and bleed, then initiative.

### Act V — Vaulgrist (1:32 – 2:48, break 2:23 – 2:38)
- Four rounds. Her chain reaches anywhere in the room, her gaze (**Unnerving Gaze**, DC 23 Will) re-Frightened someone every turn, and **regeneration 10** healed her each round. In round 2, when her gaze landed on Snagg, **her face became his father's**. Snagg: *"Hey, yo. The hell?"* While he stayed Frightened she was Concealed to him.
- **Alder went down in round 2.** A hit and a crit, and the chain bound him to the floor: grabbed, prone, and unable to recover from the bleed while anchored. Snagg's **Battle Medicine** critical (27) stood him up, and Ruben's two-action Heal (41) refilled him.
- Xurl and Snagg kept her locked down: **Dirty Trick** (Clumsy 1), **Combat Grab** three times, Xurl's reaction punch each time she escaped (*"I punch her in the face for the audacity"*). In round 3 Snagg's **Slam Down** hit for 20 (max damage), and the trip failed until he spent a **hero point** to bump it: prone *and* grabbed.
- **The question.** Alder was chained and still had his Recall Knowledge. Snagg pushed him to spend it on the regeneration: *"Ask about the fast healing… there must be something to turn it off."* Lap said they already knew it regenerates and wanted a different question; Xurl reworded it (*"How do I disable it? No numbers required?"*), and Lap took that. The answer: an underworld creature whose regeneration **fire or holy damage** shuts down. Lap framed it as *"one true and one lie"*; her block says **holy or silver**, so fire was the lie, and silver, the other true answer, was on Snagg's handwraps all along. Alder: *"I'm pretty sure it's either fire or it's holy."* Snagg: **"Do you have any holy damage?"**
- In round 4 she crit Xurl on a natural 20 (Critical Hit Deck: **Clumsy 1 until healed**). **Ruben's Protector's Sacrifice** took 9 of it. Lap called the break as Ruben's turn came up.
- **The kill.** After the break Ruben played his **Class Might** hero card, Lay on Hands'd Xurl, and cast **Holy Light**: 31, a hit. *"I'm popping a hero point to make that into a critical."* **85 damage** into her last 66 HP. Ruben's narration: *"He summons the power of Pharasma deep within his soul as a beam of light shoots down through the ceiling… similar to how the evil from the bottom up of the lighthouse."* She burned to ash. **"Pharasma may save your soul, but I will not."** Lap: *"Why didn't you do that sooner?"* Xurl: *"That shit was rad."*
- Lap afterwards: she isn't unique. **She's one of a type, a people from the underworld**, and the party will meet more of them, regeneration and all. Ruben: *"Then I will grow stronger to make sure they all suffer to the same fate."*

### Act VI — The Chain Room and the Mirror (2:48 – 3:13)
- Three cages, all empty. The north one holds **an inactive magic circle**, the same kind as the teleporters. The Scalathrax she let out in S7 are **not here**. Chains, manacles, dried blood. Xurl: *"When would we ever need to teleport into the prison?"* Snagg: *"The safest place is the one where you least anticipate it."* The party left it off.
- On the table, **a whip**. Xurl took it (*"I touched the probably cursed whip"*).
- Snagg's post-fight surgery. Xurl crit for 41 (*"41? I mean, thanks, but ow"*). Alder got two natural 1s in a row and then a 19. Xurl: *"We need to give this man medical training."* Snagg: **"I have medical training. How do you think I'm keeping you alive?"**
- **The lantern debate.** Snagg argued for selling the Lantern of Empty Light. The incorporeal-undead mode is 4d8 against a fixed DC 20 basic Will, once a day (about 16 after saves, by his math), and *"it's like a cantrip from Ruben."* Xurl suggested holding it for an Absalom collector. Alder's **natural-20 Esoteric Lore**: an Absalom buyer would pay more, but **it's probably cursed**, and a wealthy buyer will have it examined and cut the price. Xurl: *"We now have five quests leading to Absalom."*
- **The mirror.** Alder used it in the dining room. In the vision it was in perfect condition: **Belcorra at the head of the table with four nine- or ten-foot guests**, servants bringing dishes. Someone whispered in her ear and she left. Then about ten servants came out of every door and killed the four giants, then chased and killed each other, and **the head chef killed two more and then himself**. *"Well, that's concerning."* Alder: *"Which way did Belcorra walk?"* and, looking at the room: *"Pretty sure these bodies have just been lying here ever since."* **Everyone got a hero point.**
- The new circle took them to floor 2, and they reached the surface near midnight and took a long rest.

### Act VII — The Empty Case (3:13 – 3:52)
- In the morning Xurl raised the **+1 armour potency rune** from the studded leather. Snagg, saving up for his own, handed it over: *"You can take it since you're broke."*
- **The mayor's house.** The butler (Lap: **Ichabod**) said the mayor had urgent business at the library. Xurl stayed behind to sell him the wine. Ichabod held each bottle to the light, called them older than he is, **took two, and said the rest had gone bad. 40 gp.** Xurl: *"Xurl is not what you might call trade savvy. He'll take the first offer and be happy about it."*
- **The library.** Two acolytes guarding a door that is always open. Lap put it to Snagg: as a local, he has never seen that, so something is wrong. Ruben pushed (*"This cannot wait"*), Vandy waved them in, and **the sword's case was empty**. Ruben: *"I sense foul malevolence."*
- **Vandy's account.** An alarm spell protects the case. **Once a week it is disabled for maintenance, and few people know when.** Last night was that night. The acolyte on duty stepped away for a moment, and the sword was gone when he came back. Ruben: *"That is incredibly unfortunate timing."*
- Ruben told them everything: Otari, the four icons, the plan. Xurl showed the key (*"I probably won't fully hand it over"*). Alder asked to read their reactions as Ruben talked, and the others joined in. **Xurl's was the only Perception roll that succeeded.** Both were genuinely surprised that Otari didn't die in the battle, and **both clearly had a suspect**. Then an acolyte blurted: *"It's got to be Carman, the blacksmith."* Vandy: *"Calm yourself. We don't have any evidence."*
- **Snagg's local memory.** Four years ago, the last election, Carman lost and there was a rumour about him and the sword. Vandy filled in the rest. The sword was his ancestor **Vol Rajani's**, it is the Menhemes family's now, and few people know the maintenance timing, so the thief learned it somewhere or is close to someone who knows.
- Xurl to Ruben, after divination came up empty: *"Magic boy. You got any magic?"*
- **The mayor's secret.** Xurl caught the look when the party said the Roseguard lived happily ever after, and asked. The mayor sent the acolytes away and lowered his voice. **The happily-ever-after is a lie.** After the battle the Roseguard believed they were cursed, and it got worse in body and mind. **Zarmavdian** left to find a cure and never returned. Ten or fifteen years on, **the captain lost control, and Aesephna had to kill her with her own hand.** (S10 has Vol Rajani as the captain, which would make it Carman's ancestor; Lap didn't name her this session.) Aesephna held out against the curse with her faith (in a deity Lap named, unclear on the recording), but feared what would happen after she died, so **she sealed herself into a burial place no one knew**. The family lost its location until the previous adventurers found it. *"Please just keep it to yourself."* Ruben: *"Only to be done in by curses. It is a horrible tragedy."*
- The mayor added the sword's history. The Rajanis lost it many years ago, and **his great-grandfather recovered it**, so it belongs to the Menhemes now. Ruben pressed: *"And what makes you think that he is the one who stole it?"* The mayor: Carman, drunk after losing the last election, said that if he got his family's sword back it would win him the mayoralty.
- **The cloak is buried with her.** Vandy and the mayor went to look after the last party's report. **A strong negative energy fills the crypt, and anything that dies there comes back.** During their fight with skeletons, a gecko killed by accident got up as a skeleton. Vandy: *"I don't think I can deal with it… but you guys might have a chance."* Ruben: *"I believe we can handle it."* Alder: *"We'll take it on."*
- **The spellbook:** the mayor suggests one of the bookstore owners may know.
- Ruben, half out of character: *"I don't think Paizo would make it that easy… it could be somebody framing him."* Xurl: *"First answer, crime."* Snagg: *"I don't know about the first answer."* Xurl: *"First step, get a preemptive pardon from the mayor. Second answer, crime."*

Never named at the table. Foundry: **Poltergeist**, level 6 elite (75 HP, AC 24, Fort +11, **resist all damage 5 except force, ghost touch, spirit and vitality**, immune to bleed, naturally invisible). The roll log flags **rejuvenation** (it reforms in 2d4 days unless whatever keeps it from resting is set right), **but this was never said at the table**.
