## Session Summary

The session opened in the cellar where S13 ended: three bandits and a **basilisk** wearing a chain collar and a flip-up eyepatch. Alder's Recall Knowledge gave the rule for the gaze: it slows you first and petrifies you the second time. *"Close your fucking eyes if you don't want to become a statue like that one."* Ruben was slowed for the fight and cast part of it with his eyes shut. **Snagg** bit the brute (the **Legbreaker**) down, and **Xurl** finished the basilisk. Snagg had the **Gang Leader** in his grab when **Alder** threw a natural-20 chakram at it. Its Escape failed while the table was still arguing the crit's damage, and Lap called it dead. The rogue (the **Burglar**) ran, and Xurl and Ruben overheard the plan first: get out and *"report to the boss."* One of the bodies carried a **viper tattoo**, which ties the gang to the gun-carrying bandits who have been hitting the Otari road. Lap told Xurl something odd: the bandits didn't seem affected by their own basilisk's gaze. Its blood failed to restore the screaming woman, because she had been stone too long. Under the ruin were body bags and the young woman, tortured before she died.

Back in Otari the mayor had news. The Absalom Navy raided **Black Eel**'s lair, a **dragon** turned up mid-raid, and Black Eel escaped with a large red "dog". Xurl's memory and Recall Knowledge placed it: something big and red that **walked on two legs, had arms, and tackled him** before he woke in the cave. Lap added that it was more jackal than dog, a child of **Lamashtu** like the Kholo, with **a third eye**. That's when the table recognised it as the creature from the fight that killed the first party (Ruben: *"Oh my god, it's that thing!"*). The Gauntlight is getting brighter faster, so the party took the **four Roseguard icons** and went down that night. Ruben carried the spellbook, Xurl the key, Alder the Cloak of the Huntress and Snagg the Rose Blade.

On floor four the icons pulled north, toward the **altar of Nhimbaloth**. First, Xurl paid off Otari's ghost. The first dagger burned him, because it carries the rune from the torture whip. On a natural-20 Diplomacy the ghost took the second. Xurl then held him to the deal, and he grudgingly described where his old stash is: a cave roughly five days east of Absalom. He looked a long time at the cloak, then gave them his theory: put the icons on the evil altar and the seal should break. With one icon-holder at each corner of the altar, the dungeon shook, and **thirteen ghosts** came up the stairs: a **Ghostly Mob** troop and a **Specter**. The party had to hold the icons, next to the altar, while it burned down. When Alder's two natural 20s left the troop three points from breaking, the altar **healed both ghosts to full** and burned itself lower doing it. Alder went down three times; a natural 1 on the Specter's *"killing roll"* is why he lived. Snagg shoved the Specter off him and stopped its move with a natural-20 Reactive Strike. Xurl threw himself in front of its last attack on a hero-pointed Acrobatics crit. Ruben's three-action Heals carried the party, two of them cast through Stupefied 2. Then the altar's light went out. The ghosts screamed and vanished, and the barrier began to fail. In the last seconds **Otari's ghost came out through the wall and was dragged screaming down the stairs**.

Lap closed by calling it **the halfway point of the campaign** and asking the table for feedback.

## Key Events

### Lap's New Exploration Rule (0:00 – 0:13)
- Recap: the spellbook in hand, the Doriana news, the bloody campsite, the house, the trapdoor, the cellar.
- **New house rule.** Exploration activities carry into combat:
  - A raised shield stays up, a spell kept running while exploring is active when combat starts, and Avoid Notice and Scout apply.
  - If you were **Searching or Investigating**, you may spend your **round-1 reaction**, at the start of your first turn, on a **Seek or Recall Knowledge**. A successful Seek reveals the **enemy initiative order**.
  - None of it applies if a fight breaks out mid-conversation.
  - Lap's reasoning: a free Recall Knowledge lasts the whole fight, where a free Raise a Shield lasts one turn, so it should cost something.
- Twan explained that a GM can let you roll initiative with the skill that matches what you were doing (Intimidation if your threat starts the fight). Lap: *"Most of the time it's the perception."*
- Xurl, on first sight of the cellar: *"I'm sure they're just misunderstood and definitely not cultists of some sort."* / *"Ignore the butchered travelers and the demon dog."*

### The Altar of Nhimbaloth — Ghostly Mob + Specter, 6 rounds (ritual)

It started at level 8: 105 HP, AC 25, **resist all 10** except ghost touch, force and vitality, **weak to area and splash 8**, and Clutching Hands, a basic Reflex DC 23 in a 5-ft emanation. Its thresholds are at 70 and 35. **It was silently made Elite between rounds 1 and 2**: 125 HP, AC 27, DC 25.
- **Specter** (elite, level 8): 115 HP, AC 27, **resist all 5**, Vile Touch 2d8+10 void. **Spectral Corruption**, DC 26 Will: on a success, Stupefied 2 for an hour; on a failure, controlled.

**Objective**: hold the four icons next to the altar until it burns out.
