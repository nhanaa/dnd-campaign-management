## Session Summary

The party spent the day chasing two of the four Roseguard icons, and got both. At Wrin's tent, the haunted painting Xurl had carried out of the dungeon turned out to be worthless (*"It is trash"*), but Wrin knew exactly where the third icon is. **Zarmavdian's spellbook** belongs to the private collection of **Nacklewick Tallowpot**, a gnome professor at the **Arcanamirium** in Absalom. Wrin knows him from before she came to Otari, says they never got along, and wrote the party a letter of recommendation anyway. Then **Ruben** had the idea of the night: point Alder's mirror at the library and watch the theft. It worked. An acolyte cleaned the case at night and walked away from it, and a hooded figure took the sword. When she turned, it was not Carman Rajani. It was his daughter, **Amicia**.

Her running footprints led behind the library, up the abandoned graveyard path and into the forest. **Xurl** ran the trail ahead with chalk and a dagger and found her in a small cave, clutching the sword. Ruben talked her out of it. He made a long speech about what the Rose Blade means to the town, rolled a natural 20 on Diplomacy, and she dropped the sword and cried. He hugged her. Back at the library the mayor ordered her taken to Captain Longsaddle without hearing a word, so Ruben stepped between them and cast **Calm**. The mayor rolled a natural 1, and then listened to Ruben argue for leniency: *"A law is a law… But I can assure you that we will give her a chance."* The sword went back to the library. **Snagg** carried it home: +1 striking, made entirely of silver, with more to it than he could read.

The mayor and Vandy took the party straight to **Aesephna's crypt**, through a hidden cliff cave on the beach, the old kobold den, and a throne hall built two or three centuries after the Roseguard. The mayor's family ring opened the sarcophagus, and a **wraith** flew out. A **dread wraith** was already behind Alder, and it opened with a 42-damage critical. It took five rounds. The wraith's aura stripped everyone's reactions, the dread wraith absorbed the wraith when Xurl killed it and hit harder for a round, and then it went after Vandy and put her down. Xurl took **114 damage**, including a 44-point critical he provoked by reaching for a potion for Vandy. It dropped him to 1 HP and knocked the potion away, so he pulled another and fed it to her. Snagg dealt the most damage (82) and took none at all. When Ruben's Holy Light finally pulled it off Vandy, three reactions caught it on the way to him, and **Xurl's Stand Still crit** finished it. The huntress inside was a perfectly preserved old woman in a white gown, who crumbled to ash when the lid came off. The **Cloak of the Huntress** went to the mayor, who will lend it but will not give it up.
