## Session Summary

One fight. Ten rounds. Two hours and fifty minutes of table time. The party opened the wrong door on the fourth floor and found a pale man standing in a lounge who turned around wrong — no humanoid stands up that way — and whose face, up close, was moving. It was a human-shaped body packed with worms and maggots, and it opened its mouth and asked, in Common, in a very deep voice, *"Who are you?"*

Ruben answered honestly, because it had asked honestly: the place posed a danger to the nearby city, and they were hired to establish how much. The door behind them swung shut on its own. The thing started laughing. **"Of course. That's the reason we built this thing."** Then it was initiative, and the party spent the next three hours discovering, one dead lever at a time, that they had walked into a creature built specifically to no-sell them. Xurl opened by grabbing it and came away with a handful of maggots. Alder's Recall Knowledge came up a four — *"this is not something you ever recall studying in a book."* Ruben tried to gauge how dangerous it was and got the only answer that mattered: *"This will be a hard fight."*

It was worse than that. Ten minutes in, a Recall Knowledge finally placed the clothes — **they matched a portrait of Volluk**, the name Argon took off a dead scribe in S3 — and the fight was going so badly by then that nobody stopped to say it out loud. Xurl couldn't grapple it, couldn't trip it, couldn't stun it and couldn't Demoralize it — six Stunning Blows saves, six crit successes — and said so out loud inside twelve minutes: *"So I can't punch it or grapple it, so I'm completely fucking useless."* Alder burned a Recall Knowledge and a Scholarly Recollection reroll — both failed — before a second Exploit Vulnerability finally coughed up the weakness: area damage. The DM's response was immediate and deflating: *"I don't think anyone here has that."* Ruben did, as it turned out, and it made no difference at all. Snagg, who is the reason this party retreats at all now, called for the exit inside twenty minutes — *"we're at a massive disadvantage here, do we even want to fight"*, *"I don't think it's winnable as long as we're in this room"*, *"we should try our best to open the damn door and get out of here"* — and organised a fighting withdrawal around a door that would not open, handing off the crowbar, calling delays, trying to get Alder clear.

They never got the door open. And around the ninety-minute mark the retreat stopped being possible, because Alder went down paralyzed with doubled persistent damage chewing forty hit points a round out of a forty-three-point health bar, and leaving meant leaving him. Xurl asked the real question — *"I guess the question is, Ricardo, how much do you like Alder?"* — and Ricardo, to his enormous credit, refused to make it easy: *"Hey, man, do what your character would do."*

Snagg ran back in. *"Don't touch my friend. I was hired for this job. I won't abandon you."* He hit, hit hard, and turned around to the room: *"It might be winnable, guys. Come back here."* Xurl: *"Fuck it, we ball. Fucking heroes."* And a few minutes later, with every resource spent, Snagg put the whole thing in one sentence: **"Either we survive this or we TPK. I don't accept any other thing. I mean, that's me. You guys can run, but that's me."**

Nobody ran. It took three natural 20s and the same triple-damage crit card pulled twice to finish it, with Snagg unconscious and dying on the floor and Alder one failed roll from gone. Xurl kicked through its chest and splattered bugs across the room. The DM: *"This is by far the hardest fight that you guys have been in your whole life."* And then, in the quiet after, the dungeon went completely silent, a heartbeat-deep hum came up through the floor, the walls shook — and the lights went out.

What followed was worse than the fight. Thirteen voices in a lightless void, crying and cursing and praying and laughing in languages nobody knew, going out one by one while the cold and the pressure climbed. Then thirteen presences, watching. Then nothing, and they were back in the cave. And when Ruben cast a three-action Heal on his dying goblin friend, **Snagg's eyes opened with no pupils in them, he began to hover, and Belcorra Haruvex spoke out of his mouth** — a beautiful, cold, female voice, using Snagg's torn-open grin to deliver an ultimatum. Leave, and keep what little remains of your lives. Stay, and lose your friends, your home, your little victories, everything you fought to protect — *"and you'll know it is your own foolishness that brought your hand upon it."*

Then he dropped, and woke up, and said: *"What the hell happened? Did we win?"*

## Key Events

### Act I — The Lounge (0:00 – 15:39)
- Picked over the dining-room remains from S7: a **bracelet**, an **arrow**, and **48 gp** off the guests' corpses. Snagg's Seek turned up a nat 19; Ruben and Alder swept the room.
- Worked the ring of unopened doors. Alder wiggled his and found it open — *"hey, this one's open. Guess we need to find another way out."*
- Snagg flagged the pattern from last session: *"the last time we found a door that leads to another door, we run away. Let's be careful this time."*
- Found a **pale man standing in a lounge**, back turned, who stood up in a way no humanoid should. Up close: **a human-shaped body filled with worms and maggots**, speaking Common in a deep voice. *"Who are you?"*
- **Alder's Recall Knowledge failed (nat 4)** — the DM: *"this is not something that you ever recall studying in a book, or anyone that talked about it."* Ruben's danger read: *"This will be a hard fight."*
- Snagg deferred the talking: *"I sense the danger, and Snagg will let the other who's better at talking talk this time."* Alder: *"We're investigating here."* Ruben laid out the mission honestly.
- **The door behind them closed on its own.** The thing laughed. **"Of course. That's the reason we built this thing."**
- A later Recall Knowledge caught a detail that paid off ten minutes later: **its clothing looked familiar** — *"you can't quite tell where you saw this, but you think you've seen it before."*

### Act II — The Long Fight (15:39 – 185:00)
- **Round 1**: **Xurl** went first on a 29; Ruben's **Pilgrim's Token** broke his 27–27 tie *with the worm*, putting him ahead of it. Xurl opened by grappling and got *"a handful of worms and maggots."* Ruben's Spectral Blade hit for 15 — and it healed 7 back immediately.
- The lockout revealed itself piece by piece: **immune to grabbed, prone and restrained**; **immune to precision**; **resistant to all physical damage**; **fast healing 7**; and a Tendril that hit at +20 for **2d8+10 persistent piercing** — a wound that keeps opening, needing a DC 15 flat check to close.
- **Mid-fight, the party got its name.** As the green mist (Stinking Cloud) filled the room, the earlier clothing detail resolved: *"at this point you finally remember — this thing wears the same clothes as one of the portraits, which belongs to **Volluk**."* Nobody stopped to say what that meant. The fight was already going badly.
- **Alder found the weakness the hard way.** His Recall Knowledge failed, his **Scholarly Recollection** reroll *also* failed (*"much better — but you still fail"*), and Dubious Knowledge gave him a fire reading he correctly identified as half a lie. He went back in with a second **Exploit Vulnerability** and got the real answer: **broad-area damage.** The DM, immediately: *"I don't think anyone here has that."* — but Alder himself could: his successful Exploit Vulnerability took **Mortal Weakness**, which made his own chakram trigger the creature's `weak[area 7]`. **His hits were the only physical damage all night that didn't lose 7 to resistance.**
- **Snagg called for the retreat early** and ran it as an operation: pass the crowbar, delay after Ruben, push Alder clear, force the door. The door never opened.
- **Snagg called the dispel; Ruben landed it.** With the whole party Sickened and Slowed and rolling a save every turn's end, Snagg asked the question nobody else had — *"Can you dispel magic? Does it seem magical?"* Ruben: *"I can certainly try."* His first-ever Foundry counteract failed the check, he spent a hero point to bump it to a success, and **the mist came down.**
- The creature cycled a full caster's kit: **Phantom Pain**, **Worm's Repast**, **Gasping Marsh**, **Vomit Swarm**, **Stinking Cloud**, **Suggestion**, **Dimension Door**, and Demoralize at +19. Alder ate a **critical Tendril** that doubled his persistent damage to **2×(2d8+10) — 40 a round, against a 43-point health bar.**
- **Snagg went down three times** (Dying 1 → 2 → 3), failing flat checks at 11, 11, nat 1 and 5. **Xurl dropped his backpack to get under the encumbrance limit so he could physically pick Snagg up and run with him.**
- **The DM handed out a hero point to everyone** mid-fight, unprompted, for not looking the stat block up online: *"thank you for not trying to look this up... I don't want to kill you here."*
- **The door finally got explained, by the only PC trained in Arcana.** The DM asked who had Arcana and got one hand — *"Alder's the only one."* Alder's Recall Knowledge diagnosed the whole floor: **there is magic here that automatically closes and re-locks any door left open**, and it isn't a trap — *"it's not a defense mechanism. It's more like just make sure everything is closed and locked."* Snagg: *"It's like a smart house, basically."* The DM: *"That is totally correct."*
- **The DM told them what they were in, mid-fight**: *"just to be open with you guys — **he is the main boss for this floor**."* Then, rolling: *"if he succeeds, you guys make TPK here."*
- **Snagg plants himself first (126:45), before the famous line.** With the party debating whether to run and Alder stuck, he narrated it in third person the way he does: *"Snagg here, I'm gonna play Snagg. And Snagg, he will try to stand his ground. Because he had enough."*
- **The turn (139:55).** Alder paralyzed, bleeding 40 a round, about to be left. Xurl: *"how much do you like Alder?"* Ricardo: *"do what your character would do."* **Snagg: "I'll run back in."** — *"Don't touch my friend. I was hired for this job. I won't abandon you."* He connected, hard, and pulled the party back: *"It might be winnable, guys. Come back here."*
- **Snagg's commitment (142:09)**: *"Either we survive this or we TPK. I don't accept any other thing... You guys can run, but that's me."*
- Alder, prone and bleeding out, **pulled a scroll and cast Bless** for the party's attack rolls, then kept swinging and lost his grip on his chakram.
- **Round 10 — Xurl's natural 20**, off-guard, Bless up: a critical hit and the **triple-damage crit card, drawn for the second time that night**. *"Earl just fucking kicks through his chest... just splatters bugs in every fucking direction."*
- The DM: *"There's no internal organ. There's no bones. There's nothing in there."* The energy holding the shape let go and the maggots fell. **Some of them were dead. Some of them were still moving.**
- **No one died.** The DM: *"This is by far the hardest fight that you guys have been in your whole life."* Later: *"I was expecting to kill at least one or two."*

### Act III — The Void and the Voice (197:00 – 207:00)
- The dungeon went **completely silent** — no water, no insects — then a hum felt rather than heard, a vibration like a heartbeat, dust from the ceiling, stone flaking off the walls. Then **total darkness**, so complete that Ruben couldn't see his own hand.
- **Thirteen voices**, all around, unlocatable. Crying, cursing, laughing; one whispering a prayer; several in unknown languages. Each distinct, none intelligible.
- Then **twelve. Eleven. Ten.** One by one they went out. With every silence the air got colder and the pressure got heavier until nobody could move. Fear and despair.
- The last voice went out. Then **thirteen presences, surrounding them, watching.** Then everything lifted and they were back in the cave — *"your instinct tells you that something went wrong."*
- Xurl: *"Am I the only one who just had a wild fucking hallucination?"* Ruben pushed it down and cast a three-action Heal on the dying Snagg.
- **Belcorra Haruvex took Snagg's body.** His eyes opened white and pupil-less, he began to hover, and a cold, beautiful female voice came out of him: *"Ah, I know you. So you have wandered so far into my home. You have torn apart my servants. You have defiled my will."*
- She studied each of them, then softened, *like talking to a friend*: *"Leave. Walk away from this place. And perhaps I will allow you to keep what little remains of your lives."*
- Snagg's head turned to a non-humanoid angle; a cruel laugh came out of his throat; the skin and muscle of his face tore into a wide grin. *"But if you continue... I will take everything from you. Your friends, your home, your little victories. Everything you have fought so hard to protect. You will watch it all crumble. And you'll know this is your own foolishness that brought your hand upon it."*
- *"Go now. Or stay. And let me show you what it meant to be an enemy of Belcorra Haruvex."* Then Snagg's body dropped to the floor, and the party could act again.

### Act IV — Triage, Loot, and the Sealed Way Down (207:00 – 230:00)
- **Snagg diagnosed the Foundry bug live**: hitting Heal on a downed character was *advancing* the dying condition instead of removing it. Ruben's fix for the table: *"whenever you're down, remove the dying condition first, then push the heal button."* The DM confirmed it as a bug, probably from a recent update.
- Snagg treated himself with **Risky Surgery at 6 HP** — stabbed himself for 8, healed 13. Ruben's verdict: *"Never use Risky Surgery when you have eight hit points left."* Xurl: *"Is he one surgical mishap away from a tragedy?"* Snagg: *"My gamble has paid off."*
- **All that was left of the creature was its clothes on the floor** — the familiar clothing — and nothing in the pockets. Xurl, calling it in advance: *"I swear if I succeed and you just say a worm, I'm going to be mad."*
- **The worms scattered.** Some dead, some crawling away. Nobody had area damage to finish them.
- **Xurl took the door off its hinges** so it could never shut them in again: *"This door is not closing again."* He kept doing it to other doors out of spite. Snagg: *"He just wants to break stuff. Xurl has had enough."*
- Searched the lounge: walls covered in **paintings of haunted places** — a graveyard, a swamp, an abandoned church — that **made ghost noises every minute or two**. Ruben: *"There is much evil in this room. Let us grab what we need and hurry up."*
- Xurl cut up a couch cushion into **makeshift gloves** to handle a painting without touching it — and as he cut it free, discovered the ghost noises **were not coming from the painting. They were coming from behind it.** He left it.
- Recovered **Detailed Blueprints of the Abomination Vaults**, **three scrolls**, and a **staff with a skull on top** (Item 6, 230 gp — still unidentified; Ruben insists it's cursed, Xurl doesn't remember that).
- On the way out: **the magical barrier over the stairs down has solidified and gone active.** Inert last session, now a wall. Ruben: *"It seems that the ones who noticed us have blocked off our route down further."* It cannot be broken by normal means.
- **The party leveled to 6.** The DM normally awards milestones on descending a floor, but gave it early: *"hopefully you guys have more tools in your belt so that I don't need to hold back."* Snagg: *"I actually don't need more tools. I just need a fucking nat 20."*

## Character Spotlight

### Snagg · the one who turned around
- **He tried to leave. That's what makes the rest of it land.** Inside twenty minutes he had read the fight correctly and said so — *"we're at a massive disadvantage here, do we even want to fight"*, *"I don't think it's winnable as long as we're in this room"* — and then ran the withdrawal like a job: hand off the crowbar, delay after Ruben, push Alder out of the way, force the door. The S7 doctrine, applied on time.
- **And then he threw the doctrine away, on purpose, for one reason.** When the only remaining exit ran through abandoning Alder, he stopped arguing and went back in: *"Don't touch my friend. I was hired for this job. I won't abandon you."* Argon held a doorway in S4 so the others could get out; Snagg has now refused to let that trade happen twice.
- **He pulled the party back with him.** One good hit and an immediate read of the room — *"It might be winnable, guys. Come back here."* — flipped a retreat into a stand. Then he made the stakes explicit and gave everyone else an out he wasn't taking: *"Either we survive this or we TPK... You guys can run, but that's me."*
- **The medic worked the whole night, mostly while dying.** Three knockdowns, four failed flat checks against a wound that would not close, and he still ran Treat Wounds cycles after — including Risky Surgery on himself at **6 hit points**, which is exactly the kind of decision he keeps making. *"My gamble has paid off."*
- **The single best mechanical decision of the night was his, and it cost him damage.** Round 2, natural 20 on Jaws, Crit Deck #24. The DM: *"take the normal damage and the critical effect, or just do double damage."* Snagg halved his own crit to 10 and took the effect — **Clumsy 3 and Enfeebled 3** — because *"Clumsy 3 for a round until his next round is really good anyway."* The worm's AC fell from 27 to 22 while off-guard, and Xurl immediately hit a number that would otherwise have missed by three. He also worked out that **Snagging Strike still functioned** against something immune to grabs, which is why a fifth of the party's swings resolved against the lower AC.
- **He was also the table's rules engine**: caught the Foundry heal-advances-dying bug from his own death spiral and handed the table the fix, worked out that Snagging Strike still applies off-guard against a creature immune to grabs, and diagnosed the fight's actual shape out loud — *"it's just a math problem... when you hit it for 13 damage, it takes 5, and then heals for 7."*
- **His closing note was the best thing anyone said all night**, and it wasn't about tactics: *"When I changed my perspective, it was much more fun. It's like, how absurd can we get to survive this fight?"*

### Xurl · the halfling who dropped his pack
- **His entire kit was disabled and he stayed useful anyway.** Grapple, trip, stun, Demoralize — all dead on arrival (six Stunning Blows saves, six crit successes). He named it immediately and with total accuracy: *"So I can't punch it or grapple it, so I'm completely fucking useless."* Then he punched it for **55% of the party's total damage.**
- **The best summary of the fight came from him**, and it's a genuinely sharp piece of analysis delivered as a complaint: *"entirely resistant to your damage type, resistant to your free archetype, and immune to your gimmick from other items you've acquired."*
- **He dropped his backpack to carry Snagg.** Literally shed encumbrance mid-combat so he could pick up the goblin and run — the second session running he's physically hauled Snagg out of something. He also asked the hard question about Alder rather than quietly leaving, which is what made the moment a decision instead of a default.
- **He called the stand thirteen minutes before Snagg did.** With the exit closing he ran the numbers out loud and picked violence: *"We've already established that thing's intelligent. So if we don't escape now, it's just going to be chaos on that retreat. So I think fuck it. We ball and pray for some crits."* When Snagg finally turned around, Xurl's answer was instant — *"Fuck it, we ball. Fucking heroes."* — and he burned his hero point trying to force a save he had no real odds on.
- **He tried to taunt it with no taunt.** With allies bleeding out on the floor and nothing left in his kit, he shouted at it anyway and said so plainly: *"Oi, fucker! Look at me!... I have no mechanical way to taunt it. I just want to taunt it."* Earlier, hauling Snagg clear, he'd described the image himself — *"just a three-foot-four halfling deadlifting and carrying a fully armored goblin."*
- **He landed the kill** — round 10, natural 20, off-guard, and the triple-damage crit card for the second time that night. *"Earl just fucking kicks through his chest... splatters bugs in every fucking direction. If there's some kind of core in the middle, just shatters it."*
- **His comedy is destruction with a reason.** He took the lounge door off its hinges so it could never seal them in again — *"This door is not closing again"* — then kept going to other doors purely out of grudge. And he cut up a couch cushion into gloves to steal a haunted painting, which is both funny and the correct call.

### Alder Ungram · the one who could only use his mind
- **He is the only person in this party trained in Arcana, and that is what finally explained the room.** The DM asked who had it and got exactly one hand — *"Alder's the only one."* His Recall Knowledge diagnosed the whole floor: **there is magic here that automatically closes and re-locks any door left open**, and crucially it isn't a trap — *"it's not a defense mechanism. It's more like just make sure everything is closed and locked."* Snagg: *"It's like a smart house, basically."* The DM: *"That is totally correct."* That answer is why the party never got out, and why Xurl eventually started taking doors off their hinges.
- **He found the weakness through two failed rolls — and he was the only one who could actually use it.** Recall Knowledge failed; the **Scholarly Recollection** reroll failed too (*"much better — but you still fail"*); Dubious Knowledge handed him a fire reading he identified on the spot as half a lie. A second **Exploit Vulnerability** got the real answer — area damage — and the **Mortal Weakness** he took off it made his own chakram trigger that weakness. **His were the only physical hits all night that landed full instead of shedding 7 to resistance.**
- **He chose to stay.** Told that his escape needed an 18 with no hero point left, and offered the chance to do something else instead, he picked the fight: *"Yeah, I'll just fucking attack it."*

He reported it to the table in the flattest possible terms: *"So just an FYI, that was actually my full health bar in damage?"*

- **Ricardo made the session's most generous out-of-character call.** Asked point-blank whether the party should cut Alder loose, he refused to let anyone off the hook — *"Hey, man, do what your character would do."* That line is why Snagg's stand is a character moment and not a tactical one.
- **Paralyzed, freshly crit, and told his turn was over, he went hunting for anything he could still contribute** — and found it: *"I guess I can Guidance Xurl."* Then, brightening: *"I can Guidance everyone, actually."*
- **He cheered the kill from the floor**, flat on his back and bleeding: *"Ayo! Ayo! Or die! Or is it save-or-die? Or die!"* — and had the night's most honest reaction to a critical failure on a 16: *"This is bullshit."*

### Ruben Falmoor · the one who answered honestly
- **He set the tone of the encounter by taking it seriously.** The thing asked who they were, and Ruben's instinct was *"well, he asked us a question, so let's answer it earnestly"* — and then he actually did, explaining the danger to Otari and the party's commission, in good faith, to a creature made of worms. The parley failed, but it failed because of what the creature was, not because Ruben mishandled it.
- **He called the fight correctly before anyone else did**: *"if his AC is that high, then this is another entity here that we can't fight right now."* That's the second time in two sessions his first read has been the right one.
- **He took the mist off the board.** Stinking Cloud had the whole party Sickened, Slowed, and saving at the end of every turn. Snagg asked whether it could be dispelled; Ruben said *"I can certainly try"* and did — a rank-2 **Dispel Magic**, his first counteract ever in Foundry, failed on the roll and bumped to a success with a hero point. **It cleared.** In a fight where almost nothing the party did stuck, that one landed.
- **He carried the healing against a wound that kept reopening**, which is a thankless job — Spectral Blade for the party's opening damage, Lay on Hands, and the three-action Heal emanation.
- **His own stated virtue was used as the weapon against him.** The DM asked, out of nowhere, *"Are you a team player, or more the selfish one?"* Ruben answered without hesitating — *"Ruben's a team player."* Two minutes later **Suggestion** landed on him and compelled exactly that: heal only, nothing else, for a full minute, in the one fight where healing could never keep up. *"That's fucked. He can only heal. I'm under Suggestion. I cannot attack."*
- **He broke it himself on a natural 20**, and the table heard about it: *"Yay!"*
- **His Heal is what Belcorra hijacked.** He pushed a hallucination of thirteen dying voices out of his mind to go save his friend — *"Ruben is going to push that at the back of his mind as he casts a three-action heal"* — and got a five-hundred-year-dead tyrant talking out of Snagg's mouth for his trouble.
- **He's the party's caution and its conscience in the lounge**: *"There is much evil in this room. Let us grab what we need and hurry up"*, and, when Xurl went for the paintings, *"I suggest not actually touching the paintings. There is something foul about them."* He was right; there was something behind them.
- **He closed the night on the party's obligations**, which is very him: *"We have much to report to both the mayor and lady."*

## Combat Encounters

### Volluk, the Worm That Walks — 10 rounds, ~2h 50m

**Enemy**: **Volluk** — a worm-that-walks, level 8 elite. *(Named in-fiction at [26:05], when a Recall Knowledge matched its clothing to a portrait. The surname "Azrinae" is Foundry-only and was never spoken at the table.)* **105 HP · AC 27 (25 off-guard) · Fort +15 / Ref +19 / Will +17 · Tendril +20.**

**The lockout** — why this played the way it did:

| Trait | What it turned off |
|---|---|
| `immune[grabbed, prone, restrained]` | Xurl's Grapple, Snagg's **Combat Grab**, all trips and shoves — but **not Snagging Strike**, which was the one lever that kept working |
| `immune[precision]` | All precision damage |
| `resist[physical 7]` | Ate **119 of the 293 damage the party rolled** — 15 resisted applications |
| `fast healing 7` | **70 HP regenerated over 10 procs** — effective HP ~175 |
| `weak[area 7, splash 7]` | The party's one area spell **hit it and did zero** (see below) |
| Reflex +19 (its best save) | Guards the only weakness — and **crit-succeeded even at Clumsy 3** |
| Tendril **2d8+10 *persistent*** | Hit Snagg's AC 23 on a 3; **doubled to ~40/round on a crit** |
| Discorporate at 0 HP | Doesn't actually die without area/splash damage |

**Damage dealt**: **~175 HP removed** across the fight — exactly its effective HP — from **293 rolled**, with **119 eaten by resistance**. Contribution: **Xurl 96 · Snagg 30 · Alder 26 · Ruben 23**. **21 connections out of 48 swings.**

**Two things the party did land, which the lockout table alone would hide:**

- **Snagg turned his own critical hit into a debuff.** Round 2, natural 20 on Jaws, Crit Deck #24. The DM offered the standard choice: *"take the normal damage and the critical effect, or just do double damage."* Snagg took the effect — **halving his own crit to 10** — to put **Clumsy 3 and Enfeebled 3** on it. *"Clumsy 3 for a round until his next round is really good anyway."* That dropped its AC from 27 to 24, and to **22 while off-guard**, for the rest of the round. Xurl immediately landed a hit at 22 that would have missed by three.
- **Snagging Strike never stopped working.** Immunity to *grabbed* killed Combat Grab, but Snagging Strike only imposes **off-guard**, and Snagg spotted that at the table: *"it's not Grapple, it's Snagging Strike, so he's off-guard. So even if we can't flank, we can still impose off-guard."* **21 of 48 attack rolls resolved against AC 25 instead of 27** because of it, turning four outright misses into hits — including the one Xurl hero-pointed into a 19-damage crit.

**And the one that stings**: the party *did* have an area option. Ruben cast **Inner Radiance Torrent** (rank 2, 60-foot line, basic Reflex) — straight into the creature's advertised weakness, while it was sitting at **Clumsy 3** from Snagg's crit card, i.e. Reflex reduced to +16. It rolled a 15. **Critical success. Zero damage. The weakness never triggered.** Snagg bought a −3 to exactly the right save and it changed nothing.

**Round-by-round shape** *(round boundaries taken from the roll log's own markers)*:
- **R1** — **Xurl acts first** (initiative 29); his Grapple succeeds mechanically and accomplishes nothing (*"a handful of worms and maggots"*). **Ruben's Pilgrim's Token wins him the tie against the boss at 27**, so he slots in ahead of it: Spectral Blade hits for 15, and it heals 7 straight back.
