## Session Summary

The party woke up on the floor of Belcorra's private suite with nothing left. The will-o'-wisp from S9 was gone, but not provably dead: Lap's description this time had its eyes and hands pulled back through **a hole torn into another dimension**, which then closed. Ruben came to and called it *"definitely one of our most deadliest encounters,"* then put the party back on its feet with two three-action Heals and still had half his spell slots left. Snagg spent the next half hour on Treat Wounds, clearing the Wounded conditions. Xurl carved a **+1 striking composite longbow** and his lost crowbar out of the slime wall, and Ruben dated the trapped bodies to **one or two hundred years ago**, not Belcorra's era. On the way out, Ruben laid his hand on the stairway barrier and read it more clearly than before. His Recall Knowledge says it blocks **the enemies of whoever cast it**, one way only, so whatever is below can come up. His own read is that it was made against the party specifically, out of souls: *"I can feel the anguish, the hunger, the sadness radiating from this barrier... It's only meant to keep us out, not made to keep anything in."*

A day in Otari turned up a new thread. Wrin bought all seven tapestries for 100 gp, admitted she doesn't recognise the wisp, and said she wants to go down there herself and can't. Snagg, the local, knew why that line didn't add up: Wrin isn't from Otari, she's a very good sorceress, and whatever keeps her out of the Gauntlight isn't fear of monsters. Then **Mayor Oseph** summoned the party. His eldest daughter **Doriana**, already mid-way through a psychic awakening, has started sitting at her window ignoring everyone, and the colourful patchwork man who saves her in her nightmares is now *"my savior."* Snagg's examination found her in perfect health apart from lack of sleep. Xurl's read was that she was telling the truth and something was still wrong. Ruben's question about the colourful man made her pause before she answered. Snagg's local knowledge filled in the politics: the blacksmith **Carman Rajani**, a Roseguard descendant with a bad reputation, is running for mayor a third time on a working-class platform, and his Founders' Day speech *"doesn't sound like him."* Ruben's research at the library with Vandy came up empty, and the trail points to the bigger library in Absalom.

Back underground, the party found that **the dead in the Nhimbaloth shrine had been cleared away too**, the third place the dungeon has been "cleaned" behind them. They poked at a dead, mushroom-covered tentacled thing in a green pond, decided a half-flooded crawlspace was *"a great way to die,"* and followed a strange light. Snagg boosted Xurl's first jump with a critical success, Xurl wall-jumped onto a small island in an underground stream, and a green-skinned human ghost asked him how he'd got in. His name was **Otari**: the Roseguard rogue the town is named after, dead down here for 500 years of filth fever and a collapsed ceiling, and **he had no idea there was a town**. *"There's no way there's a town called Otari. Otari is me."*

A pack of flaming skulls interrupted. Their dives and their drain did nothing to the ghost, and their hits barely bothered him (*"I've been fighting this for hundreds of years. They never learn"*). Otari killed nearly all of them himself. Snagg bit one in half on a critical. Afterwards Snagg filled in the Roseguard's history from what every Otari kid is told, and Otari added two things: a warning that **Belcorra is back, below them, still weak and getting stronger every day**, and a plan for the barrier. Bring together **the four Roseguard icons**: Vol Rajani's sword (in the Dawnflower Library's display case), Zarmavdian's spellbook (Absalom, Alder thinks), Aesephna Menhemes's cloak (the mayor is her descendant) and Otari's own key, which he handed over from his stash with the rest of his belongings. Xurl tried to carry his bones out to free him. It didn't work, because a ghost is bound to the place it died. Xurl then offered to make him a ghost-touch beer. Otari wants a good ghost-touch dagger, and if he gets one he'll share his secrets.

## Key Events

### Act I — Lap Rewrites the Hero Point Rules (0:00 – 0:08)
- Lap opened by **withdrawing the S9 compromise** (one hero point at session start if you have none), *"for now,"* and replacing it with two new rules:
  - **Hero Actions** (Paizo's optional Hero Point Deck): whenever a PC gains a hero point, they draw a card from a ~52–54-card deck. The card is an extra way to spend that hero point. Cards can't be traded, and an irrelevant card can be ignored and the hero point spent normally.
  - **Heroic Buff**: every PC automatically gets one at the **start of every combat**, and it lasts only that combat. Spend it **before** a roll for advantage (roll twice, take the better) or **after** to reroll (take the second result, even if worse). It **cannot** bump a degree of success.
- Regular hero points now **also** allow the advantage option, and **keep** Lap's degree-bump house rule. Long-rest hero points continue.
- Lap's stated goal: *"I want you guys have at least one reroll when you get into combat."* The table took it. *"OK, I think that's fair... I'll take that."*
- Ruben made Lap restate the details before play started. Lap clarified that the buff arrives every combat **no matter how many hero points you hold**, that it is reroll/fortune only, and that degree bumps stay with real hero points.

### Act II — Waking Up in Belcorra's Suite (0:08 – 0:55)

- **The wisp's fate, partly answered.** Lap described the round-12 kill again: a void hole ripped into another dimension, the eyes and hands sucked back into it, and the hole closed. *"You're not sure, did you actually destroy it or did it escape?"* **Alder's** Recall Knowledge later classed it as **an apparition, not a creature from this plane**.
- Ruben, back on his feet: *"We shouldn't stick around for too long... This was definitely one of our most deadliest encounters."* Two three-action rank-3 Heals healed everyone for about 21. *(The accidental heroic-buff and hero-action draws from Lap's demo were reverted.)* Even after both, he still had half his slots: *"because I was pretty much useless that fight."*
- **The slime wall.** Ruben tried his tinderbox on the webbing (*"I got this item. Just want to use it at least once"*). It isn't flammable. Xurl spent ten minutes carving out the bow and his crowbar: a **+1 striking composite longbow**. A body freed with it **decayed to bone the moment it touched air**. Ruben's examination: the bodies are **at least a hundred years old, but not from Belcorra's time**, so somewhere in the last one or two centuries.
- **Snagg's Treat Wounds cycles** cleared the Wounded conditions. He failed the expert DC, re-ran it (*"I just need a five"*), healed Xurl on a 30, and then insisted on treating Alder's Wounded 1 as well (*"I don't know, man. In this campaign..."*), via Risky Surgery: 2 damage, 24 healed.
- **The barrier, read properly this time.** A thrown rock passed straight through it. Ruben's hand met a wall, and with it a wave of fear, despair, hunger and revenge. His Guidance-assisted Recall Knowledge: the barrier is **designed to block the enemies of whoever cast it, it blocks one way only, and whatever is below can come up**. Ruben's in-character read: *"Whoever erected this barrier made it specifically for us... it's possibly created with souls... It's only meant to keep us out, not made to keep anything in."* His two ways through: *"either defeat the one who cast the spell itself, or send someone that whoever cast this spell does not know."*
- **Jaul Mezmin's body is gone.** No body and no blood on the pavilion, and nothing to show where it went. The druid job came up, and Xurl spelled it out: *"there was a secondary job of recovering the body so the druids could bury him."* Later he listed it plainly among the day's results: *"We failed to find the corpse."*
- Xurl's curse: the full moon has passed. He's clear for roughly 20–25 days.
- Back at the base around 8:30 pm the kobolds were partying, but **Fiverr and Eero weren't among them**. Long rest; everyone drew their first Hero Action. Lap also gave **Ruben an extra hero point** and wouldn't say why.

### Act III — Wrin, and What Snagg Knows About Her (0:59 – 1:12)
- **All seven tapestries sold to Wrin for 100 gp.** Alder judged a specialist buyer would pay more. Nobody wanted to spend time hunting one. Ruben: *"I believe it would be best in your hands."*
- Wrin didn't recognise the wisp, but agreed it is the figure on tapestry 7. On the barrier: *"very powerful,"* built to block intruders. Her advice was to search the rest of the fourth floor, and to bring back anything, however small.
- *"I really want to go see myself, but I just could not."* Ruben: **"No, it is much too dangerous."**
- **Snagg's local knowledge:** Wrin isn't from Otari. She arrived five or six years ago, she is known as a very, very good sorceress, and **it isn't the monsters she's afraid of. Something else keeps her out of the Gauntlight.** Snagg didn't say it in front of her. He **pulled Xurl aside to gossip** (Xurl: *"Got all kinds of tea to spill"*).

### Act IV — The Mayor's Daughter (1:12 – 1:47)
- A messenger brought the party straight to **Mayor Oseph**, who skipped the pleasantries: *"My daughter's condition is getting worse."*
- **The background**, from Xurl's memory and the DM: **Doriana**, the eldest of three, is going through a psychic awakening (migraines, objects moving around her) and has nightmares she can't remember, except that **a man dressed in colourful, mismatched fabric** saves her in them. The party first saw her before Snagg joined; it isn't in the campaign files. For the past few days she has sat and ignored everyone, still eating and walking, *"in her own world."*
- **Snagg, as the town local**, got the mayor's file: mayor for the last 20–30 years, the richest man in Otari, owner of the lumber company, trusted because he keeps the business and the governing strictly separate (mornings for one, afternoons for the other), and no rumour has ever stuck to him.
- **Snagg's Medicine check** (the party's new guy in the room): *she is in perfect health, apart from lack of sleep.* She didn't react to being examined at all, then came round with *"oh, sorry, I didn't see all of you guys."*
- Ruben: **"My dear, your father is worried about you. He says that you're not yourself."** She: *"I'm fine, father. I'm just thinking about something."*
- **Xurl's read: she's telling the truth, and something still isn't right.** So he asked: *"So what are you thinking about? Usually when you're thinking that hard about something, it's something important."* Her answer: her future with her power, the town's future, her friends, *"especially Amicia."*
- **Snagg recognised the name.** Amicia is the blacksmith's daughter. **Carman Rajani** is a descendant of Roseguard **Vol Rajani**. He drinks and gambles, works only when he's broke, and has been hard to deal with since his wife and younger son died in an accident; Amicia keeps the smithy running. He has **lost two mayoral elections to Oseph** and is running a third time. His Founders' Day speech two weeks ago *"doesn't sound like him"*: the richest man in Otari versus a man of the working class. Election day is very soon.
- Ruben asked whether she still sees the colourful man. **She paused**, as if she had to place the question. Then: *"Oh, right, right. Yes, I still see him... He's my savior."* **Ruben is suspicious and kept it to himself.** To the mayor: *"This is definitely something not normal. But I need to do research."*
- **Ruben and Vandy at the library** (+2 for her help) confirmed only what they already knew. It is a psychic awakening, the colourful man appears in no book, and **it looks like a one-off case**. Lap: *"There is a bigger library in Absalom. Another reason to go there."*
- The others **earned income for half a day**. Snagg, as a local, could find level-4 work without a roll. Xurl and Alder only got level-2 work (*"people don't know well about you"*). Alder earned a few silver on Esoteric Lore.

### Act V — The Cleaned Shrine and the Green Pond (1:47 – 2:28)
- Back through the Gauntlight. Floor 3 was quiet and **the ghouls in the library hallway are gone**. The bound demon (who promised to kill the party unless they find its contract) and the weeping scribe-ghost were both left alone.
- *(~13-minute break.)*

Someone at the table: *"Something cleaned up... it must have been brought somewhere, and someone must have done that."* Xurl: *"Definitely magically cleaned up. There should have been chunks of metal and bones scattered around."* Lap: *"That is the theme of this game. Annoying and weird."*

- **The green pond.** Under a dry metal pipe in the ceiling sat a swampy pool, and in it a heap that looked like meat or plant, shaped like a tentacled monster and covered in mushrooms. Xurl: *"Someone shoot it."* Nobody did. Snagg wouldn't eat these: *"This looks dirty. This doesn't look tasty."* **Alder**'s Recall Knowledge, on the way back: **a creature, dead, type unknown.** Ruben warned against touching the water; Xurl: *"That makes me want to touch it more now, honestly."*

A stream cave narrowing until even Snagg and Xurl would have to crawl half-submerged, with rumbling water beyond. Xurl: *"That seems like a great way to die."* Unanimous no. Xurl's guess is that it comes down from the floor-3 teleporter.
- **Snagg got the lantern.** He's the only PC without a light source. Xurl handed over the Lantern of Empty Light, and once Lap fixed it in Foundry it burns **pale blue**.

### Act VI — Otari (2:28 – 2:44)
- A strange light around a bend. To reach it Xurl argued that an initiative-style moment let him use **Crane Stance** to jump; Lap allowed it for a jump, but not as a pre-emptive stance before opening a door. **Snagg gave him a boost and rolled a critical success on the Aid** (*"Snagg will help give him a little boost"*). Xurl made the first jump, onto a ledge, easily.
- **Xurl rolled a 20 on Perception**: the light around the corner **looks like the light of the messages** the party keeps finding.
- Xurl long-jumped and wall-jumped onto a small island in the stream with a human-sized skeleton on it, and a **green-skinned human ghost** appeared: *"How did you get here?"*
- **Xurl: "Oh, hi. Sorry. My name's Xurl. I jumped."** Why not swim? *"I didn't want my boots wet."*
- *"What's my name? ... My name is Otari."* Xurl asked if he meant *the* Otari, the Roseguard, the local hero. The ghost had no idea what that meant. **"There's no way there's a town called Otari. Otari is me."** Xurl: *"That's why it's crazy, because they really like you up there and they named their whole town after you to honor you."*
- He's been stuck in the cave for many years and has tried everything to leave. Xurl's idea: *"often ghosts are attached to their body, so if we take your body outside the barrier, you might be able to kind of hitch a ride with it."* Otari: *"Hey, why I never think about it?"*

### Act VII — The Flaming Skulls (2:44 – 3:08)
- *"Oh yeah, those guys again. I hate those."* Flaming skulls burst out of the chamber and went straight for Xurl. See Combat Encounters.
- Otari fought for the party. The skulls dove through him and tried to drain him, and nothing happened; when they actually hit him he only complained (*"ouch, Jesus, why?"*). *"I've been fighting this for hundreds of years. They never learn."* **He killed almost all of them himself.** Xurl: *"Shockingly, the guy who can solo this fight and has been doing it for years."*
- **Snagg bit one in half on a critical.** Lap: *"there's no taste, and there's no blood... The mushroom back then tastes way better."* Snagg: *"Yeah, I'd rather have some more mushroom."*

### Act VIII — What Otari Knows (3:08 – 3:51)
- **Snagg's Medicine diagnosis: Otari died of filth fever.** He explained it carefully, as something he'd come across in his work and heard from other medics. Otari heard him out, nodding, then: *"Do you think I don't know what happened to myself, huh? I know I'm passing away because of the fever."*
- **The Roseguard history**, as Snagg's local knowledge (what every Otari kid is told). They met defending Absalom from a monster attack. **Vol Rajani** stepped up first, the plate-armoured fighter whose rose-hilted sword gave the group its name, and she became the captain. **Zarmavdian** was a dwarf wizard of unknown origin. **Aesephna** was the healer, good with a bow. **Otari was the brains and the rogue.** After Belcorra, three founded the town and named it for him. Zarmavdian later left and was never heard from again; the other two lived to old age.
- What actually happened to Otari: the legend says he fell in the battle. He planned the surprise attack on Belcorra (*"I'm the one that planning all of this. It works. I just didn't know that the fucking ceiling would collapse on me"*). The collapse is why the others thought he was dead. He was then chased deeper by monsters and died down here, of the fever. He was **glad Aesephna had a good life**, and hurt that nobody came for him: *"Maybe they're too busy? Maybe they can't afford my corpse? Or, oh, maybe they forgot about me?"*
- 500 years. Snagg knew the number. *"Only 500 years old."*
- ⭐ **Otari, serious for the only time: "I can feel that Belcorra is back. She is below us right now... still weak. She's not able to go far away from where she's at right now. But I assure you, I can feel that she's getting stronger and stronger every day."** When she regains her full power she can leave. Xurl: *"Great, a second timeline on our timeline."*
- **The bones.** Xurl packed the skeleton into his backpack (*"is that a bone in my backpack? Are you happy to see me?"*). Otari got no further than the cave mouth. Ruben's hypothesis was that the cave has a barrier like the stairway one. His high Religion roll gave the real answer: **a ghost is bound to the place it died, not to its bones.** Otari wasn't bothered; his home is 500 years gone anyway.
- **He felt the stairway barrier go up two or three days ago**, and asked whether the party had triggered it. Its power impressed him, and he has an idea: **gather the four Roseguard icons and break the seal with them.**
  - **Vol Rajani's sword.** Snagg knew at once: it's on display in the **Dawnflower Library**.
  - **Zarmavdian's spellbook.** **Alder** has heard of it, probably somewhere in **Absalom**.
  - **Aesephna's cloak.** Nobody had heard of it, but **the mayor is her descendant**: Aesephna Menhemes is the older woman in the portrait over his meeting-room fireplace.
  - **Otari's key.** Hidden behind a rock on his island with the rest of his stash. He gave it all to the party: *"that's all my belongings, I don't need that."* He explained each item himself, so no identification was needed. He also thinks **the key can become more powerful** but doesn't know how to unlock it. Xurl is the party's Thievery user and can't use it until he's an expert.
- *"I told you I'm street smart. I don't like books. Books are for nerds."* Lap: that offends Ruben and Alder a little. Xurl: **"Everyone here is a vibes caster if they cast at all."**
- **The ghost-touch negotiation.** Xurl offered Otari a beer, then offered to put **ghost touch on the mug** so he could at least hold it. Otari: *"I can now hold a mug, but I cannot drink from it. That is even worse."* What Otari actually wants is **a ghost-touch dagger**, and a good one: *"Just don't fucking give me any shitty one, okay? I don't use cheap daggers."* In exchange: *"maybe I can share your secret to you."* He also had a dig at shields in general: *"Shield is for people that get scared... You never need a shield."* Xurl: *"Sometimes it's nice to let the guy with the shield get hit instead of you getting hit."*
- Leaving, Xurl offered to bury the bones properly, in a quiet spot in the woods or a graveyard. Otari, after some thought, wanted them left where they were: *"I've been with my bones for many years."* Xurl put the bones back and mostly got it wrong (*"Why do you have so many ribs?"*), with Otari directing from the side and giving an anatomy lesson.
- Xurl, leaving: *"Somehow I feel like we're going to have to go to Otari to find a good dagger. Or not Otari, Absalom."* / *"Somebody tells me we have like four different quest lines pointing us that way. Very not subtle."* The library, the spellbook, the dagger and the daughter's colourful man all point to the capital. **Road trip.**

## Character Spotlight

### Snagg · the Local Who Knows Where Everything Is
- **He's the party's memory of Otari, and this session leaned on it.** Three times the DM handed the table something only a lifelong local would know, and each time it went through Snagg: **Wrin isn't from here and is afraid of something that isn't monsters**; the mayor's reputation and **Carman Rajani's run for office**; and the moment Otari named the sword, *where it is*: on display at the Dawnflower Library. He also knew how long the town's namesake has been dead: *"About 500. Only 500 years old."*
- **He handled the Wrin read with judgement.** He didn't announce in her tent that the town's best sorceress is scared of something. He **pulled Xurl aside to gossip** instead.
- **He's still the party's doctor.** He woke Ruben, then ran the Treat Wounds cycles that cleared the party's Wounded conditions after Ruben's Heals, re-running one after missing the expert DC, and insisted on treating Alder's Wounded 1 as well: *"I don't know, man. In this campaign..."* He was also the medic who examined **Doriana** (perfect health, no sleep) and who diagnosed **Otari's filth fever**. Otari's reply was the funniest exchange of the night: *"Do you think I don't know what happened to myself, huh?"*

- **Combat: one bite, one kill.** He waded in at a declared 3 ft 6 in (*"I'm small"*), **crit a flaming skull with his jaws and split it in two**, then raised his shield and let the 500-year-old rogue handle the rest. The fight ended before his second turn.
- **He's the one who won't let the party walk around hurt.** With two PCs still at half health and the others ready to explore: *"I'm like, why are we running around with two of us still at half health? I was planning on just leaving."* The party stopped and he healed them.

So he asked the one question that got an answer: *"Usually when you're thinking that hard about something, it's something important."* Her answer put Amicia and the Rajani family on the board. He also remembered the daughter's symptoms when nobody else did, and graded himself fairly: *"So other than the fact I was entirely wrong, kind of right."*
- **Rules and table-craft.** He argued that time pressure justified Crane Stance outside combat and won it for jumps, worked out the Aid DC, and showed Lap how to make the new heroic buff expire at the end of combat in Foundry.
- **Funniest person at the table again, and the bones bit was all his**: *"is that a bone in my backpack? Are you happy to see me?"* / *"Why do you have so many ribs?"* / *"Everyone here is a vibes caster if they cast at all."* / *"Great, a second timeline on our timeline."* / *"That makes me want to touch it more now, honestly."*

### Ruben Falmoor · the One Who Reads the Walls

- **He ran the Doriana scene and the mayor.** *"My dear, your father is worried about you. He says that you're not yourself."* *"And what of your nightmares?"* *"Do you continue to see him?"* That last question is the one that made her pause. He saw it, **kept his suspicion to himself**, and told the mayor only what he could stand behind: *"This is definitely something not normal. But I need to do research."* Then he spent his afternoon in the library with Vandy doing it.
- **He refuses to make it about money.** On the tapestries: *"Ruben doesn't really care... He's not a very materialistic guy."* To the mayor, after the daughter: he does this not for money but so a young girl gets her chance. And about the colourful man: **"I have my suspicions, but I do not have enough information to express them yet."**

- His hypothesis about Otari's cave was that it had a barrier like the stairs. His **Religion** roll was high enough to give the party the real rule: a ghost is bound to where it died.

### Alder Ungram · the Nerd, Proudly
