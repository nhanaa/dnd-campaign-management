# Session 2 Recap — Into the Dark

**Date**: 2026-07-15
**System**: Pathfinder 2e Remaster — Abomination Vaults (Book 1: *Ruins of Gauntlight*)
**Duration**: ~4.7 hours

> *Argon's first real dive.* Session 1 was all intros and RP — not a blade drawn. Session 2 was the opposite: three fights against undead and aberrations in the Gauntlight, all of them tuned above the party's level. Nobody died. Nobody rolled well, either.

## Session Summary

After a whole session of talking, the party finally went underground. They spent the morning resting and outfitting — most importantly, Argon finally bought the **striking rune** for his guisarme (he was four gold short; Xurl quietly covered it) — then made the half-hour march out to the Gauntlight at dusk and picked up where the veterans left off, deep on the third floor. For Argon, everything below the entry hall was new: a collapsed bridge crossed on a rope, a dead giant frog, stained-glass saints, teleport circles no one can switch on, and a slow drip of five-hundred-year-old history about Belcorra, the Roseguard, and the town named for a hero who didn't actually die.

Then the dungeon showed its teeth. A **Gibbering Mouther** — a level-6 aberration two levels above the party, which the DM cheerfully admitted was a modded fight — crawled out of a cavern pond and nearly ended Argon's first combat before it started, opening with a critical hit for 34 (half his health) and dragging him into a restrain. What followed was a forty-minute war of attrition against a creature that can't be flanked and engulfs whoever it grabs, fought by four people who could not buy a good die roll to save their lives. Argon tanked and hero-pointed hits into existence (his trips whiffing the whole time); Alder read out its weakness (bludgeoning — which nobody had) and got swallowed whole; Ruben burned focus keeping the party upright; and Xurl finally elbow-dropped the thing into paste with a hero-pointed Flurry of Blows.

The dice — and the momentum — turned after that. Exploring on, the party found a live-but-dormant teleport circle Alder thinks he can reactivate with the right ritual book, and overheard two flesh-eating cultists behind a secret door planning to **sacrifice a captured red-haired dwarf at midnight, two days out, on the floor below.** They kicked in the door and cut down the two ghouls behind it — Argon opening the account with a two-handed dragon crit that took a ghoul's head clean off. A long rest later, the last door on the floor opened onto the real fight: a **ghast, a spellcasting ghoul, and its packmates**, in a library guarding a carved stone door. The caster led with Grim Tendrils, healed its undead with a Harm burst, and offered the party immortality if they'd drop their weapons and join. They declined, ground the room down over another chaotic forty minutes (Twan's internet dying mid-fight, the DM rolling his saves by proxy), and Argon smashed the last head off. On the way out, Argon's reading of the room's books confirmed what the carved door meant: the boss room. Belcorra's tomb. That's where they stopped.

## Key Events

**Act I — Kit Up, Head Out (town → the Gauntlight)**
- The party opts to rest the night, revisit the mayor's pirate plan in the morning, and dive the Gauntlight after dark. **Argon** finally affords the **striking rune** for his guisarme — he was 4 gp short, and **Xurl** spotted him the gold ("Luckily for you, I had six gold standing around").
- Half-hour march NW through thinning forest into swamp to the Gauntlight; the party descends to the **third floor**, where the veterans had stopped.
- **The broken bridge:** a 20-ft gap, no bridge (the old party's kineticist used to Stepping-Stone across). Argon long-jumps a **grappling hook and rope** across; Alder anchors it; **Xurl** walks the taut rope on top "the exact wrong fucking way" while the other two hang beneath and haul across. DM: *"Stepping Stone, so OP."*
- Lore for the newcomer: the dead giant frog, the stained-glass chapel (ghost rising from a grave under a four-point star), the **teleport circles** (one seen earlier, none working), the **River Drake** and the **purple-worm corpse** with a pillar of negative energy that *heals* undead, and the story of **Otari** — the Roseguard hero the town is named for, who didn't die in the battle but went *deeper*, leaving messages the party keeps finding.
- **Xurl's glow, explained (sort of):** asked where his light comes from — *"Skin grafts, baby. I'm bioluminescent."*

**Act II — The Blob of Meat (Gibbering Mouther)**
- In a cavern by a dripping pond, a **Gibbering Mouther** oozes into view. Xurl: *"Kill it with fire. Gross."* Roll initiative.
- **Turn one disaster:** the Mouther's first bite is a **critical hit on Argon for 34** — half his HP — then it grabs and restrains him. Argon: *"Well, good thing I'm a D12 HP class."* Alder: *"What the fuck, bro?"*
- Argon can't act while restrained; he burns a **hero point to Escape** the grab (*"Get off of me!"*).
- **Alder's Exploit Vulnerability + Recall Knowledge** cracks the thing: too many eyes to be flanked, **weak to bludgeoning** — *"Hit him with some blunt objects"* — which nobody is carrying. Its lowest save is Fortitude-or-Will, and Alder can't tell which.
- The whole table rolls catastrophically low for the entire fight — the session's running gag is born. Argon: *"I roll like shit. I roll like actually like shit."*
- The Mouther **engulfs**, moving through the party — it swallows **Alder** (16 damage; grabbed, slowed, holding his breath), while **Xurl leaps clean over the creature on a crit-success wall-run**, clearing everyone's heads. **Argon's next slash hits the rupture threshold and cuts Alder free**, and **Ruben's clutch 22 Heal** keeps the near-dead thaumaturge on his feet.
- Argon works **Slam Down** and force-charged swings, hero-pointing hits when he has to, and **repositions Ruben** out of the Mouther's reach — but his **trips never land** (one crit-fails on a nat 1 and drops Argon himself prone), and every slash just triggers the Mouther's **Reactive Gnaw**, opening a fresh mouth. Ruben works Lay on Hands, Enfeeble, Benediction and Guidance to hold the line.
- **Xurl finishes it:** a hero-pointed Flurry of Blows — *"Xurl just elbow-drops whichever tentacle and head is grabbing him, and it sinks into the ground. Meat pieces falling apart everywhere."* Exit initiative.

**Act III — The Circle and the Overheard Sacrifice (exploration)**
- Party patches up (Lay on Hands / Treat Wounds). Xurl pokes the Mouther's remains — nothing but small bones.
- They find a **silvery teleport circle** matching the big dormant array. Alder can't switch it on by bluster (*"chanting gibberish… everyone here is a vibes-caster"*), but his lore tells him it's **reactivatable with the right ritual instructions** — a book or scroll — as long as the circle isn't defaced.
- Through a **secret door**, the party overhears **two cultists**: a **red-haired dwarf** they've captured will be **sacrificed at midnight, two days from now, on the floor below.** The two then bicker over whether flesh tastes better raw or poisoned with fungus.
- The party weighs it, decides to hit these two now, and pushes the door.

**Act IV — Two Ghouls at the Door**
- The secret door opens onto **two ghouls**. Initiative.
- The lead ghoul opens by clawing **Argon for 11** — he makes his save, no paralysis.
- **Xurl** Crane-Stances and **jumps clean over both**, Flurrying the rear one for a combined ~21 and **stunning** it.
- **Alder** Exploits (no notable weakness), throws his chakram with Instructive Strike, and reads their saves.
- **Argon opens his account:** a **critical hit for 44** with the two-handed guisarme drops the **stunned rear ghoul — Xurl's flank target — before it ever acts.** *"Two-handed dragon barbarian crits. I just slice his head off."* (Alder, deadpan: *"Let's just say that would one-hit me."*)
- A **fumble-deck draw** then leaves Argon **sickened**; he spends actions retching it off while Ruben chips with Vitality Lash and Demoralize, and **Xurl's Stand Still** stops the surviving ghoul's flight — eating a **22-damage crit claw** for it. **Argon finishes that ghoul** with a normal hit (the DM narrates the decapitation).
- Loot: dry jerky and a necklace off the corpses. The party drags the bodies aside, retreats to the empty secret room, and **rests to full**.

**Act V — The Library, the Ghast, and the Caster**
- The floor's last door opens on a **library** ("restricted collection"): bookshelves, a **carved double stone door** (a ghost-lady rising from a grave), and a **ghast** in front — plus a smarter, robed ghoul and a **spellcasting ghoul**.
- The caster opens with **Grim Tendrils** (Ruben: *"Finger of death?!"*); everyone saves but **Xurl**, who **rerolls the failed save with Halfling Luck** and lands a **crit success** (takes none).
- The **ghast's stench** (sicken + slow) and the ghouls' paralytic bites hammer at Argon, who **holds the whole hallway with his reach guisarme** — the DM literally names him "the guardian" — soaking saves so nothing reaches the squishies.
- The caster **Harms** the room (14 to everyone, half to Ruben) to heal its undead — after which **Xurl honestly flags** that the enemy has void healing, and the DM rewards the honesty with a **hero point**. It also **Commands** Alder to hit the deck, and drops **Phantom Pain** on Argon (who saves but still takes 12). **Ruben answers with his own 3-action Heal** (11 to each ally) — cast by the DM as proxy, because Twan's internet had dropped and he spent the back half of the fight "playing by post."
- The caster makes its pitch: *"Drop your weapon and join us. You will gain immortal life."* The party passes — Xurl: *"I'm good, thanks."* Argon: *"Don't want to either."*
- Through repeated Flurries, Stunning Blows, Alder's Implement's Interruption on the caster, Ruben's heals and a Vitality Lash, and Argon's Intimidating Strikes and trips — including one that **drops the ghast outright** (the DM: *"scared to death — literally to death"*) — the room is ground down. **Argon smashes the last head off** — *"This time you're not cutting his head off; you just smash him. The whole head is gone."* Xurl: *"God, I love barbarians when they get to hit."*
- **Cliffhanger:** the DM calls for Perception; Argon's read of the library's books tells the party the **carved stone door is the boss room.** *"I think we found the boss room."* Session ends.

## Character Spotlight

### Argon Rath · the Wall That Wouldn't Fall
Argon's first real fight was a trial by fire, and the trial cheated. The Gibbering Mouther opened combat by critting him for 34 — half his health, turn one — and dragging him into a restrain, and from there the dice simply refused to cooperate for the entire first fight. What matters is that none of it moved him off his job. He shrugged off the opening crit with a soldier's dark humor (*"good thing I'm a D12 HP class"*), hero-pointed his Escape, and spent the rest of the night being exactly the wall the party needed — Slam Down swings that whiffed every trip, force-charged guisarme blows, hero points spent to *make* misses into hits (*"I'll use all your equipment, I'll die later"*), and, in a genuinely selfless beat, spending an action to **reposition Ruben out of the Mouther's reach** instead of swinging. When the dice finally turned, he became the party's execution engine: a two-handed dragon crit that took a ghoul's head off *before it could act* (*"I just slice his head off"*), the surviving ghoul finished a beat later, an **Intimidating Strike that dropped the ghast outright** — the DM: *"scared to death, literally to death"* — and, in the climax, a stand in the hallway mouth as literal guardian (the DM's word), eating stench, paralysis, and Phantom Pain so the casters behind him stayed clean, before he smashed the last head off to end the night. Away from the dice, the code he arrived with was still there — a quiet *"A moment of silence"* over a sacrificial victim's corpse, and a plain *"I will defend my friends"* as he set his feet in the doorway. He waved off the caster's offer of immortality flat (*"Don't want to either"*), and closed the session honest about the experience: he *sounded* frustrated during the low-roll grind, but said plainly he'd had fun and wants the harder combat. New build on debut — Slam Down, Intimidating Strike, Demoralize — and a clear identity: he is the one who stands in the doorway.

### Alder Ungram · the Man with the Answers (and the Chakram)
Alder is the party's brain, and combat didn't change that — it just gave him targets. Every fight, his **Exploit Vulnerability + Instructive Strike + Recall Knowledge** produced the intel that made the difference: the Mouther can't be flanked and folds to bludgeoning; the ghouls' saves; the ghast's stench mechanics. He fights from range with a returning chakram and no illusions about his own durability — told to bodyguard the healer, he shot back *"You got a barbarian there? I'm as squishy as you,"* and watched Argon's ghoul-crit with a flat *"let's just say that would one-hit me."* His best solo bit was the teleport circle: he first tried to bluff it awake by "chanting gibberish in a random language, because everyone here is a vibes-caster," then — correctly — deduced he could genuinely reactivate it later with the right ritual book. He kept forgetting cover rules and paying for it (his chakram foiled twice; the DM ribbing him, *"I thought you said you don't care"* / Alder: *"I don't"*), landed a clutch **Implement's Interruption** on the enemy caster, and got the night's driest line when the DM learned a new dirty trick mid-fight: *"Don't give them ideas."*

### Ruben Falmoor · the Shepherd of Pharasma
Ruben kept the party breathing and never let anyone forget *why* he hates the things they're fighting. Mechanically he was the engine room — Lay on Hands, two-action Heals, Enfeeble, Benediction, Guidance, and Vitality Lash aimed at the undead — all run on a first-ever sorcerer he's clearly still learning (*"I've played, what, 60% of the other classes"*). But the character underneath is a **devout of Pharasma with a psychopomp bloodline**, and it drives his play: he treats the undead as an offense to the natural order, blessed swamp water into holy water on a whim, and, when Xurl called him *"a pizza cutter… no point,"* leaned all the way in — *"He is a devout follower of Pharasma. Even his bloodline is psychopomp."* He's also the table's warmest presence: he welcomed Pax to the DM's lethal reputation with a cheerful body count (*"one character died, and one was permanently crippled"*), refused to heal the DM's own monster (*"you keep that minus two to attack like a man"*), and stayed gracious through a genuinely cursed night of dice — and a genuinely cursed internet that had the DM rolling his saves for him through the whole climax.

### Xurl · the Halfling Who Ignores Physics
Xurl was, quietly, the session's MVP — and its funniest player. He finished the Gibbering Mouther with a hero-pointed elbow-drop, and across three fights he **leapt over enemies again and again on crit after crit** until it stopped being a tactic and became a bit (*"Xurl seems to be not limited by physics at this point"*), wall-running clean over the Gibbering Mouther to reposition and rerolling a Grim Tendrils death-spell into a crit-save with Halfling Luck. He's the party's tactical brain too — he flagged the dungeon's dormant teleport arrays before anyone, explained the Lay-on-Hands-vs-Treat-Wounds math, worked out Ruben's psychopomp auto-refocus, and called shots for the whole group — and its conscience, honestly flagging that the enemy had void healing even though it hurt the party's odds (and getting a hero point for it). The one thing he *couldn't* do was grapple — every attempt whiffed, until *"I'm never going to use my fucking class feature"* became its own running joke — but he papered over it with Stunning Blows, Stand Still disrupts, and an auto-16 Assurance Demoralize. His voice carried the table: *"Welcome to the meat tide,"* *"He has approximate knowledge of many things"* (of Alder), and, over Argon's kills, *"God, I love barbarians when they get to hit."*

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — Gibbering Mouther ("the blob of meat")
**A level-6 aberration vs. the level-4 party — a deliberately over-tuned fight (DM: "this is a modded encounter"). ~40 minutes, many rounds. No deaths.**

- **Opening:** the Mouther crits **Argon for 34** on its first bite (persistent bleed), then **grabs and restrains** him. Argon burns a hero point to **Escape**.
- **Key traits (via Alder's Exploit Vulnerability / RK):** cannot be flanked; **weak to bludgeoning**; can **engulf** creatures it moves through (Reflex save; hold breath; damage each turn); **Reactive Gnaw** opens a new mouth when it takes slashing damage.
- **Alder** engulfed for 16 (3d6+2 piercing); freed when **Argon's slash hits the rupture threshold** and by **Ruben's 22-point Heal**.
- **Xurl** leaps over the Mouther on a **crit-success wall-run** to reposition, and later **finishes the creature** with a hero-pointed **Flurry of Blows**.
- **Argon:** Slam Down and force-charged swings; hero points to convert misses to hits; repositions Ruben to safety. **No trip lands** — one crit-fails on a nat 1 and drops Argon prone — and his slashing repeatedly triggers the Mouther's **Reactive Gnaw** (a new mouth each time).
- **Alder:** Instructive Strikes + Recall Knowledge (reads the no-flank / bludgeoning weakness); chakram throws; taken by the engulf.
- **Ruben:** Lay on Hands (12) on Argon; a clutch **22-point Heal** on a near-dead Alder; Enfeeble; Benediction; Guidance; Divine Lance.
- **Result:** grueling win, everyone battered and bleeding; no casualties. XP not announced on-mic (party remains level 4).

### Encounter 2 — Two Ghouls (behind the secret door)
**A short, sharp fight — the dice finally cooperate.**

- The lead ghoul's opening claw hits **Argon for 11** (he saves — no paralysis).
- **Xurl** Crane-Stances and **jumps over both ghouls**, Flurrying the rear one for ~21 and **stunning** it.
- **Argon** lands a **critical guisarme hit (44) — decapitates the stunned rear ghoul before it acts** (would one-shot a squishier PC). A fumble-deck draw leaves him **sickened**; he retches it off (single actions + Fort saves), then **finishes the surviving ghoul** with a normal hit.
- **Xurl's Stand Still** disrupts the surviving ghoul's flight — taking a **22-damage crit claw** in return; **Assurance Intimidation** for a flat 16 Demoralize.
- **Alder** throws chakram + Instructive Strike (learns cover rules the hard way).
- **Ruben** chips with Vitality Lash (anti-undead) and Intimidate.
- **Result:** clean win. Loot: jerky, a necklace. Party rests to full before the last door.

### Encounter 3 — The Library: Ghast + Ghoul Spellcaster + packmates
**The climax — another over-level, ~40-minute grind guarding the carved boss door. Complicated by Twan's internet dropping (DM rolled Ruben's saves by proxy). No deaths.**

- **Enemy caster opening:** **Grim Tendrils** (party makes saves; only Xurl fails — he **rerolls with Halfling Luck** and turns it into a **crit success**, taking none).
- **The ghast:** **stench emanation** (Fortitude or sickened; slowed on a crit-fail) — its only rider. **The ghouls** carry the paralytic claw/bite (incapacitation). **Argon holds the hallway as "the guardian"** with his 10-ft reach, soaking saves and blocking access to the casters.
- **Enemy caster:** **Harm** (3-action void emanation, 14 to all / half to Ruben, heals its undead); **Command** (forces Alder prone/crawl); **Phantom Pain** on Argon (he saves, still takes 12); **Consume Flesh** on its own necklace to self-heal 8; and the recruitment pitch (*"drop your weapon and join us"*), refused.
- **Xurl:** repeated leap-ins (his 3rd crit long-jump of the night), **Stunning Blow** (28), multi-target Flurries splitting the cultist and non-cultist; honestly flags the void healing for a **hero point**.
- **Ruben:** a 3-action **Heal** (11 to each ally — cast by the DM as proxy while Twan's internet was down); a 2-action **Heal on Argon** (~32, near max); a **Vitality Lash** (whiffed — the enemy rolled a **nat 20** Fort save); **Bleed Out** (missed).
- **Alder:** **Implement's Interruption** on the caster (hit for 9, not a crit — so the Command wasn't disrupted; Alder was forced prone anyway); Exploit Vulnerability; chakram (foiled by cover again).
- **Argon:** an **Intimidating Strike that drops the ghast outright** ("scared to death"), trips, and the **final smashing kill** — *"the whole head is gone."*
- **Result:** room cleared; the carved stone door confirmed as the **boss room**. Session ends on the threshold.
