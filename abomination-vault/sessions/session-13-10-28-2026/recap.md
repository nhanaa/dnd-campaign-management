## Session Summary

No combat. The party rode two and a half days to **Absalom** with Wrin's letter, went to the **Arcanamirium** in the Wise Quarter, and came out with **Zarmavdian's spellbook**, the third of the four Roseguard icons. It cost them two magic items and a promise. **Professor Nacklewick Tallowpot** cared about the Gauntlight only as an ancient artifact, and not at all about what it would do to Absalom. He made every member of the party speak before he would deal. Xurl showed him one page of the Gauntlight blueprints and refused to hand over the rest. Snagg's offer of the blue-light **Lantern of Empty Light** got a *"now we're talking."* The deal closed when **Alder** gave up his **cracked mirror** implement after showing what it could do. The price was the lantern, the mirror, and full public credit for whatever the party does about the Gauntlight. His goodbye: *"Remember what you promised. I know where to find you."* The book is white leather that looks unused, written in **Dwarven**, and full of everything Zarmavdian learned. The spells run to rank 5. Its last section is unreadable, animated script, locked like the other icons.

The second half was the city. The party sold the spare gear, bought runes, and moved the whip's ghost-touch rune onto a pair of daggers for Otari's ghost. Snagg's Crafting check found out what the whip was for: torturing a ghost after you've killed it. Then **Snagg raised the mayor's daughter**. **Doriana**'s psychic awakening was the one thread they could chase in Absalom. A psychic in the Ivy District confirmed the pain and moving objects are a normal awakening. The patchwork man in her dreams is not. *"Something is talking or communicating with her in her dream… I don't think this creature have good intent… your time may be short."* Then Xurl's Diplomacy came up a 2 and the two sides fell out over payment. **Alder** paid him two gold to smooth it over; he took one and handed one back.

Ruben and Alder's research landed a natural 20. The dreams started *after* the awakening, so something sensed her power. It rescues her in the dreams to win her trust. It has to look like the patchwork figure, or she won't know it when they meet. It is probably not undead or unholy, and the rumours point to a dream-controlling creature **from another plane**. Nobody could afford a Banishment scroll. On the road home Snagg smelled blood. The trail led from a bloody campsite to an abandoned house, down a trapdoor, and through a hole behind a closet. In the room beyond: a dead woman beside a half-dug hole, a lifelike statue of an old woman frozen mid-scream, and three armed humans and a black dog with yellow eyes, all watching them. **Next session opens in combat.**

## Key Events

### Act I — The Road to Absalom (0:00 – 0:28)
- Horse or ship: the next boat leaves tomorrow (Lap's d4), so the party **rented horses** to go now. Xurl: *"I'm going to just rent you a horse."* The first-edition price list gave the table a scare: *"They try to screw over the players as much as possible."*
- Snagg, on the first night's long rest: *"No more dream."* Lap: *"That is very important."*
- **Day two, a storm.** Everyone rolled Athletics and a Fortitude save. Ruben used Follow the Expert on the Athletics and everyone pressed on. The storm cleared before sunset, but the rain cost them that night's long-rest hero point. Snagg: *"Rude."*
- **Day three, Absalom.** On a Recall Knowledge check Alder went with Esoteric Lore over Absalom Lore (*"instead of 7, it's fucking 14"*), and Lap gave the city's history through him:
  - Five thousand years of sieges, two attacks in the last ten alone.
  - A ring of settlement outside the walls (the **Cairnlands**) that always suffers first.
  - **If the Gauntlight reaches full power, millions of corpses could rise outside Absalom's walls under Belcorra's command.**
  - It ties back to the five dioramas from S9, one of which shows the dead rising inside the city.
  - The city's graveyards, plus the **Flotsam Graveyard** of wrecks visible under the harbour. Xurl: *"So basically the turbo fuck if that happens."*

### Act II — The City at the Center of the World (0:28 – 0:43)
- At the gate Xurl asked who'd been here before. **Snagg** had, for work: *"Snagg probably been here before… Big city. Too many things going on."* Ruben had too, once or twice. Xurl: *"First time for me."*
- The walls: about **200 feet tall and 20 feet thick**, scarred and patched from old sieges. Two bored guards waved Alder through on *"just here looking for some work."*
- Xurl had the school's name (*"Arcanamirium"*) but nobody knew the district. Snagg: *"Very long name. Snagg can't remember."* Xurl: *"The magic place."* The first person they asked pointed them north to the **Wise Quarter**.
- **Xurl's first time in a real city.** Mismatched architecture, a ten-foot giant walking beside a two-foot gnome, a carriage pulled by a giant spider that nobody gave a second look. Xurl: *"Xurl is having a bad time right now. Like, no check required. This man is uncomfortable."* / *"Way too many things way too close together."* **Snagg** put a hand on his shoulder: *"You'll be fine. You'll get used to it."*
- **The Arcanamirium**: towers tipped with purple-pink flame, students arguing theory in the courtyard and practising spells on each other, one flying in circles and laughing. Alder knew the school is **practical, not theoretical**. It teaches you to *use* spells, so its graduates become city guards and adventurers.

### Act III — The Reception Desk (0:43 – 0:53)
- A voice from behind a three-foot desk: *"Who are you?"* Snagg, at goblin height: *"Hello, anyone here? Come closer. I can't see you."* The receptionist was a round gnome who decided on sight that none of them were students. Xurl, eyebrows up: *"Really, what gave it away?"*
- *"Do you have an appointment?"* Alder said yes, then: *"No, I was kidding. We don't actually have one."* She did not like the joke.
- Ruben produced Wrin's letter, and Xurl tried to soften the joke: *"I get that he's a bit of a dumbass, but it is actually important."* Ruben made the case: *"There is life hanging on the balance, and we need to speak with him."* After a private Diplomacy roll she said *"fine"* and took the letter in.
- Xurl and Snagg nosed through the papers in the waiting room. Lap's verdict, both times: *"real fucking sciencey."* Alder apologised for the joke on the way in and got a side-eye.

### Act IV — Professor Tallowpot (0:53 – 1:30)
- The office: a wizard's office lined with books, small curiosities in display cases (a wand, a gold disc on a chain). Behind the desk sat an older gnome in gold spectacles and a blue robe, with Wrin's letter already open in front of him.
- **Ruben told the whole story**: the Gauntlight, what they'd found, and what it could do. **Xurl alone** caught, on a secret Perception check, what was going on underneath. The professor's eyes lit up at *"an ancient artifact built 500 years ago."* He tuned out the party's adventures. And when Ruben explained what the Gauntlight would do to Absalom, he *"don't give a shit."*
- Ruben: *"We require the tome of Zarmavdian."* (The table's aside before it: nobody, Lap included, could pronounce the name. Xurl: *"One of our most dangerous characters we've ever played was fucking Jerry."*)
- **Xurl's turn.** *"Might I instead suggest a trade?"* (*"Xurl speaks up probably for the second time this entire conversation."*) He handed over **one page** of the Gauntlight blueprints, enough to prove they were real. The professor asked about the rest, then waved it off: *"this blueprint is nothing to me unless I have the actual thing."* Xurl went flat: *"That will never be released into the world… That is non-negotiable."*
- **He made each of them speak**, one at a time (Lap afterwards: *"he's going to pick every fucking student to stand up and answer a question"*). He turned to **Snagg**: *"I can give you a cool lantern."* The **Lantern of Empty Light** got *"now we're talking"* and a spell cast over it. It could be *part* of a deal, but it wasn't enough for the book. Alder's **slide pistol** got one look and was tossed back.
- **The huddle.** Ruben suggested putting up **Otari's key** as collateral. Xurl stopped it: *"We need all of them at the same time."* Xurl worked out the professor's angle: *"Perhaps it is the mirror he is interested in… it is not one of the four keys which we need."* Ruben left it to the mirror's owner: *"It is entirely your decision, Alder… it is your implement."* Alder: *"I can find another mirror."*
- **Alder's pitch.** *"Nacklewick. Mind if I call you Nick?"* — *"Excuse me. It's Professor."* He demonstrated on the spot. The mirror clouded, then showed **this office in the past: the professor talking to a middle-aged human woman whose back was to the glass.** The professor stood up to watch. He examined it, and his complaint was that it's broken. Ruben finished the pitch: *"if you're in the right location, well, you can see incredibly important events, even uncover some mysteries that have been lost to time"* — and with the professor's resources, maybe it could be restored.
- **The price.** The lantern and the mirror, plus one more thing to make it even: *"I'm going to need full credits of what you guys do to this Gauntlight thing… everyone needs to know that it is me giving you the spell book."* Ruben agreed: *"we will praise your name as the one who aided us in our mission to save Otari, as well as possibly the rest of the world."*
- **The spellbook** flew out of a display case into the professor's hand, and he handed it to Ruben.
  - Heavy, bound in **white leather** with no dust and no fingerprints, as if it had never been used.
  - Written in **Dwarven**, and holding everything Zarmavdian mastered: crafting, alchemical formulas, architecture, mining, farming, art.
  - The spell section is thin by a wizard's standards. Cantrips through **rank 3** are readable, and an Arcana check put the book's reach at **rank 5**.
  - The last section is **shifting, animated script** that no Comprehend Language will crack. It's locked like the other icons.
  - Ruben, on the party's rotation: *"Killed off our alchemist and got rid of our alchemist and our wizard. Like the two perfect characters that would like this book."*
- The professor: *"Remember what you promised. I know where to find you."*

### Lap's Aside (1:30 – 1:34)
- Lap said he'd built the professor with set needs and a set personality. *"If you guys spend a little time to ask around, you might be able to find out more about him before you go in blind."*
- He hadn't expected them to give up the mirror: *"that is the one source of hero points that you guys can get."*
- Ruben said he had deliberately held back this session after being *"kind of the main character"* last time.
- Lap said that if he ever runs a second campaign, it will be *"something totally different"*: *"This is way too much combat."* Alder: *"So many encounters per floor."*

### Act V — Selling, Runes, and the Whip (1:50 – 2:36)
- **The whip.** Alder's first attempt to identify it failed; Ruben's succeeded: a **ghost touch** rune in an unfamiliar script that works like a normal one. Then **Snagg's Crafting check** worked out what it was for: *"This ghost touch whip is specifically there so that once they torture someone to death… they become ghost and they can continue torture the ghost for eternity."* Alder: *"What the fuck? This is from the whip lady, right?"* Snagg, practical: *"We can still transfer the rune."*
- **Otari's venom dagger** was tested before the sale. Equipping it put its holder **prone at Dying 1** in Foundry, a coding problem with the item. Snagg: *"Why are you prone, sir? … Are you cursed, sir? … Don't spread the curse."* Ruben: *"If that's the case, then Otari is a demon because it's his dagger."*
- **Sold**:
  - The longbow, the venom dagger, the **emerald grasshopper** talisman and the **viper arrow**.
  - Xurl tried to find an orc tattooist to buy the tattoo books. Gather Information found a group, but they weren't interested.
  - The final sale was a DC 22 Diplomacy. **Alder** crit the Aid (Cooperative Nature) for +2, and **Ruben** hero-pointed his roll to a **28** for a 5% premium.
- **Bought**:
  - **Snagg**: a **+1 armour potency rune** (*"I'm broke. I'm broke. I'm broke."*).
  - **Alder**: +1 armour potency plus a property rune.
  - **Xurl**: moved the stash armour's +1 rune onto his explorer's clothing. He also moved the whip's +1 and ghost touch onto one of **two fine daggers**, and bought ghost touch for the second, so **Otari's pair of ghost-touch daggers is ready**. He borrowed gold from the party to cover it (*"I'm not even borrowing it because the fucking ghost touch daggers are for the entire adventuring party"*), and gave Alder his 15 back once the whip's +1 came across.
  - **Ruben** debated a Staff of Healing and held off. Lap said he can pick something before next session.
- Snagg talked Ruben out of the Staff of Healing: *"Honestly, staff of healing is kind of whatever, because you already have heal."* Xurl looked into doubling rings for the ghost-touch pair and found it would take the 1,300 gp greater version: *"Motherfucker, it doesn't."*
- Rooms cost 1 gp a night. Nobody bothered with the Gather Information roll that would have found 5 sp ones. Everyone left the city broke. Alder ended with **five silver**.

### Act VI — The Psychic of the Ivy District (2:36 – 2:58)
- With a day to kill while the runes were set, **Snagg brought up the mayor's daughter**: *"What about the daughter of the mayor, right? … They wanted us to try and find an actual doctor to try and help her."* Then: *"Gather information. Like, what are we doing? … We're looking for a doctor that's specialized in curing mind disease."* He also asked what they actually wanted from one: *"How to stabilize?"*
- Alder's Gather Information was a **critical success**. It turned up a famous psychic in the **Ivy District**: a crystal-ball sign, a man in a wizard robe and pointy hat, cards, and a loose bird with a fan of envelopes in front of it. Ruben: *"This seems sketchy as fuck."* Snagg: *"It's a little scammy. Why not try it?"*
- **Xurl described the symptoms.** *"Shit's flying around the room. She's seeing a man in her dreams… Migraines. I don't know. She's like 12."* — Lap: *"She's 16."* — *"She's like 12. Fucking humans, who knows?"* Ruben added the long stretches staring into space.
- **The reading.**
  - The headaches, the pain and the moving objects are **a normal awakening**: *"the pure energy inside your head is too much. The body is not ready yet."*
  - After ten silent minutes with a second book: *"I think the man that you see in the dream is a problem… Something is talking or communicating with her in her dream… I don't think this creature have good intent."*
  - Its range depends on how powerful it is.
  - It is not a common case, and there are no other sources to try.
- Xurl asked him to come to Otari and offered to have her father pay. His Diplomacy came up **a 2**. *"I think the only advice I can give you is find this creature."*
- **The bill.** The psychic put out his hand: *"Payments."* Xurl: *"You never said anything about payment… Notably, this isn't Xurl being a dick. This is just how Xurl thinks."* The wind started circling the room. **Alder**: *"Sorry about his manners. He's not accustomed to treating city folk,"* and put down two gold. The psychic took one and handed one back: *"I do fair business."*
- Snagg, the local businessman: *"All the people I work with, I always speak money first."* Ruben: *"Let us not insult the man in his own business."* Ruben thanked him properly, and got the warning in return: *"you better find this man… your time may be short."*
- Xurl: *"Well, we haven't got the guard captain or the mayor mad at us yet, so we've still got some targets."*

### Act VII — Research (2:58 – 3:41)
- Snagg went looking for guild work (*"There should be a job somewhere in Absalom we can take"*). Alder brought them back: *"Shouldn't we be finding out about what's happening to her?"*
- **Information vs. hunting.** Ruben wanted to look for sources on the creature. Xurl argued that the psychic had told them plainly he didn't know: *"I think we'd also probably be better off searching for the creature."* — *"Through the power of dice, all things are possible."* Ruben: *"Yeah, and including getting your character killed."* Alder suggested keeping a closer eye on her, or moving her away, even to Absalom. **Snagg's call on the mayor**: *"Seeing he's her father, he should know at least."*
- Recall Knowledge (Ben, a natural 1: *"Xurl doesn't know shit about fuck"*). Alder's result:
  - Some creatures enter dreams, either to feed on fear or just to play.
  - Whatever this is, **its reach has a limit**.
  - Moving her out of range is one option. At least warn the mayor.
- **Ruben and Alder researched** the rest of the day. Alder used **Scholarly Recollection** to roll twice and rolled a **natural 20**; Ruben rolled 19. They built a timeline:
  - **Awakening first, nightmares after**, so something sensed her power and wants it.
  - It's intelligent and has a purpose, not a beast feeding on dreams.
  - It **rescues her inside the nightmares to win her trust**.
  - So it has to **look the way it looks in the dream** when they meet, or she won't recognise it. That look: a **wizard-style robe of sewn-together squares in many colours**, easy to remember.
  - Lap tied it to the Gauntlight.
- **One more question.** Snagg proposed asking for traits rather than the creature's identity: *"Is it unholy or something? … Because if we know things like that, we can have some more preparation."* The answer:
  - **Probably not undead or unholy.** Those would want to consume her: the fear, the power, or the girl herself.
  - **Rumours of a creature from another plane that can control dreams.** Lap reminded them the chain woman also came *from somewhere else*. Belcorra commands undead, underworld creatures, extraplanar things and demons.
- **Banishment.** Alder proposed a Banishment scroll. Snagg flagged that it's an **incapacitation** spell (it is a rank-5 spell, so it won't work on anything above level 10). Then Lap and Snagg argued over the price. Lap read the 150 gp as the cost to *craft* it, plus 80 to hire a caster. **Snagg** walked him through it: *"That's the price of the scroll. It's not the price of crafting… if available, you can buy a 150 gold."* Lap: *"I see, I see, I see."* Snagg: *"The problem is, do we have that money?"* Alder: *"We're still broke."*
- Xurl, on bringing a 9th-level answer to an early adventure path: *"So we either need to go and find an Adventurer's Guild quest to earn some money, or we say, fuck it, we ball."* Snagg had a cheaper idea: *"What if we just make her… the daughter never sleep again?"* Alder: *"Sounds like a perfect thing to do to a psychic to go insane."* Xurl: *"crack cocaine and five-hour energy for the rest of their life."*

### Act VIII — Blood on the Road (3:41 – 4:00)
- They picked up the rune work and headed home. Xurl, on the Perception roll: *"I welcome the robbing attempt. I want to stab something."*
- **Day two, Snagg smelled blood.** A campsite in the trees off the road:
  - Two dead men dressed like hired guards, one with his throat cut and one with half his face gone.
  - **Four bedrolls.**
  - **Two sets of heavy boot prints** walking away, as if carrying the other two.
- Ruben: *"Ruben is definitely in the corner of catch these bastards who dare spill the blood of the innocent."* Alder: *"Help whoever was taken."* Alder also bought his new implement: a cosmetic mirror. *"Bright pink. As it should be."*
- **The house.** The footprints led thirty minutes into the woods to an abandoned house and walked straight into the fireplace. Snagg's Perception failed and Ruben's came up a 2 (*"Snagg is just clueless"*, said by Ruben and agreed by Snagg). **Alder found the trapdoor.** A ten-foot ladder led down to a storage room of crates and barrels, much newer than the ruin above. The prints led into a **closet with a hole where its back wall should be**. Xurl posted up beside it, with Snagg on the other side. Xurl: *"This is not, in fact, a stealth mission."*
- **The room.** A short hall opened into a stone-tiled room with a square of bare dirt in the middle. Beside it:
  - A **dead, half-naked woman lying face down** next to a shovel and a half-dug hole.
  - At the far end, **a very realistic statue of an older woman, mouth open, screaming**.
  - **Three armed humans with weapons ready, and a black dog with yellow eyes**, looking at the party.
- Lap: *"next time when we come back, we'll go straight into combat… Like I say, remember, I don't like easy combats."*
