The morning after the pirate raid, the Gauntlight business became official. At the mayor's manor, Wrin restated the stakes of the blueprints (a restored Gauntlight dooms Absalom and the whole region), the mayor closed the pirate thread himself — the dock messenger was glimpsed (a human) but not caught, the two captives go to Absalom for trial, and Black Eel's island is the Absalom Navy's problem now — and then turned to the goblin: "You are the best guy that we have right now... I would like you to accompany them and see the threat of the Gauntlight is dealt with." Snagg, who had gone home to his retired goblin parents the night before and reported for work like any other day, shrugged it into his job description: "I guess a nice break from all these boring jobs." Klorte walked him out, put a hand on his shoulder — "I know this is very dangerous. Don't die. Come back in one piece" — and got "No promise, though" for his trouble, plus 50 gold from the mayor personally. The party spent the day identifying loot (the elven-runed chest is a dead lock-glyph; the skull staff reached for Snagg's life force the moment he touched it — a hero point turned the critical failure into a plain one, no drain — and went straight into the locked box; Alder claimed the slide pistol), retraining feats, and buying a scroll and a bow, then rode the newly reactivated floor-2 teleport circle down to a third floor that was quieter than it had ever been.

Below the floor-3 stairs — the route the armored knight took — the party opened a door onto a stone pavilion ten feet above an underground lake, where a pale, dry-voiced man and a wolf sat at a campfire. He wanted them gone; Xurl wanted to know who he was; Ruben pitched the Gauntlight mission and blanked on the name of the town he was protecting ("Otari," Snagg supplied). When "I don't fucking care about Otari" came back, Xurl looked over his shoulder — "Punch in time?" — and the fight was on. The wolf mauled Xurl, three reactions cut it down as it tried to break away, and then the man dropped to all fours, turned into a wolf, and crit Xurl with a bite that carried the curse of the werewolf. Snagg's local knowledge named him: Jaul Mezmin, 300 gold bounty, the werewolf who killed the wife of Kaledo, owner of Otari Market — "Hey, I know that guy." Alder's turn was the stuff of table legend — a natural 20 on Exploit Vulnerability, then a natural 20 on the chakram, then a Critical Hit Deck card that read triple damage and a Fortitude save — and a level-7 elite werewolf with 109 of his 140 hit points still in him failed the save by one. "I don't think he dies. Fucking save or die effects." Jaul's locket ("Isla, my love") is the bounty proof. Ruben's examination confirmed the worst: Xurl is cursed, the full moon is tonight or tomorrow, and — the DM swears he didn't plan it — the Gauntlight's graveyard night was a new moon exactly two weeks ago.

They pressed on into a domed chamber whose walls were carved as a graveyard, ghosts rising toward a giant eye at the apex, and a seven-fingered stone hand of an altar clutching a flicker of blue light: a shrine to Nhimbaloth, outer god of emptiness, despair, and ghosts — "the empty death," as Ruben named her. The room itself frightened anyone who looked too long; Snagg's verdict, seconds before the altar disgorged five crawling hands and five will-o'-wisps, was "It seems suspiciously minor." It wasn't. Two and a half hours and nine rounds later — magic-immune wisps confusing the party into swinging at each other, a hand clamped on Snagg's throat, and then two Cairn Wights bursting from a sarcophagus (tripped by Alder's mirror-image stepping fifteen feet too close) to chain seven critical hits in eighteen swings behind a fear-chant that kept everyone Frightened — Ruben had gone down once, Alder twice, Xurl had been saved by a Halfling Luck reroll of a natural 1 and then knocked to zero by a dying wight's last swing, and Snagg had ripped the second wight's head off and thrown it across the room. Its last words, in gasping Common: "Leave this place." The DM called it an Extreme encounter, admitted his damage-carry-over house rule was the only thing between the party and a TPK, and reminded everyone on the way out: "Xurl is cursed."

"Don't die. Come back in one piece."

  • Snagg's morning: the DM established that Snagg lives at home in Otari with his parents — both goblins, both Otari Lumber lifers, recently retired. He reported to Klorte as usual, and Klorte walked him to the mayor's house where the party had slept.
  • Wrin's summary of the blueprints: fully restored, the Gauntlight's reach isn't just Absalom but the whole region.
  • The mayor closes the pirate thread: his men only glimpsed the dock messenger — a human — and he assumes that person knows the plan is blown. The two prisoners go to Absalom for trial; the island hideout gets reported to the Absalom Navy. "Thank you for what you did for us. I will take care of that from now on."
  • Gold: the second 50 gp of the pirate job, split three ways among the veterans (17 gp each). Snagg, pointedly not included (Xurl: "Just taking psychic damage because you don't get the gold").
  • Snagg is assigned to the Gauntlight: "You are the best guy that we have right now — I mean, except Klorte." Snagg: "Wasn't it for me? ... I guess a nice break from all these boring jobs."

Act II · Downtime · Loot, Identification, Retraining

The staff that bites back

  • Xurl dumped the party's mystery loot on "whoever looks the most confident": the runed chest, a vial of oil, and the skull staff. Ruben, as an Aiuvarin who reads Elven, translated the chest: a lock glyph, no longer powered — a well-made lockbox. Kept for the base.
  • The staff: Alder's Occultism failed (14 vs DC 22). Snagg tried — natural 1 — and the DM ran the critical failure as the staff itself biting back: "the negative energy consume your mind... Snagg, you are Drained 1." Ruben: "I knew that stuff was nothing but trouble." Snagg spent his one hero point — the table's degree-bump rule turned the critical failure into a plain failure — and walked away undrained. The staff went into the locked chest at the hideout, unidentified; nobody wants it ("this staff doesn't deserve to be used").
  • The slide pistol went to Alder: five shots, no ammunition for sale in Otari. Snagg suggested making it his weapon implement; Alder: "Just five shots is not worth making my weapon implement."
  • Retraining (the DM opened it up): Alder swapped Instructive Strike → Breached Defenses; Xurl swapped Suplex → Combat Grab ("I've never once had the ability to trigger it"); Ruben swapped Positive Attunement → Blessing of Vigor. Alder bought a composite bow and a rank-2 Tailwind scroll (cast on himself before the descent). Ghost Touch (75 gp) discussed and not bought.
Act III · Explore · The Descent

"Look at us taking the main path for once"

  • Left town before sunset. Reactivated the floor-2 teleport circle (10 minutes) and rode it to floor 3 — the party's first teleport: dizzy, shaking, and "on the third floor without any incidents." Snagg beforehand: "I don't trust these magical things. I guess if we all go in, I'll go."
  • Floor 3 is quieter than ever (Ruben, Perception 28). The party skipped the unfinished ghoul room — "we have bigger things to worry about now than some loose ghouls" (Ruben) — and took the stairs the armored knight used (Xurl: "The man who definitely didn't retrieve a grimoire directly from in front of our faces").
  • Snagg: "Remind me what we're here to do again." Xurl: "find a way to turn off the light. Which is almost certainly the ninth layer."
  • Wrin's answer to Ruben's map request: the blueprints are a how-it-works document, not a floor plan.

"Punch in time?"

Snagg: "Otari." Diplomacy 18 at −2: "I don't fucking care about Otari." Xurl, over his shoulder: "Punch in time?" Alder: "I don't want to fight you, but we're gonna get through."

  • Alder's execution: two natural-20 Exploit Vulnerabilities and a natural-20 chakram in one fight; the crit-deck card killed a werewolf with 109 HP left outright. "Well, I kind of just blew his head off." (Full round-by-round below.)
  • Loot: Jaul's katar and a locket — a woman's portrait, "Isla, my love." Bounty proof: "That's easier than lugging his whole head back" (Xurl). Snagg: "Can't pass on bounty."

Act V · Mystery · The Shrine of Nhimbaloth

"It seems suspiciously minor"

  • A domed chamber, ceiling 30 ft up: the walls carved as a graveyard with ghosts rising toward a giant eye at the dome's center; a 15-ft circular platform three feet high; on it an altar assembled from stone slabs into a seven-fingered hand clutching a flicker of blue light, with a small object on top.
  • Recall Knowledge: the DM routed the identification through Ruben's prior-party knowledge (Religion 27) — Snagg's Religion 28 was the table's high roll — a shrine to Nhimbaloth, outer god of emptiness, despair, and ghosts; her shrines have nothing in them but the altar. Ruben: "The empty death."
  • The room effect: DC 22 Will at the start of every turn — fail Frightened 1, crit fail Frightened 2, crit success immune; the fear lasts until you leave. Snagg failed and shrugged it off with Bravery: "It seems suspiciously minor."
  • Then the light inside the hand skipped, and small hands came crawling out of the altar.
Act VI · Combat · The Altar Fight

Nine rounds, two wights, seven crits

  • Five crawling hands and five will-o'-wisps, then two Cairn Wights bursting from a sarcophagus at round 3 — nine rounds, ~2h30m of table time, an Extreme encounter by the DM's own accounting.
  • Ruben down (round 4), Alder down (round 7) and again (round 8), Xurl at 3 HP then 0 HP. Both of Xurl's potions and the "Reaction Heal" went into the downed casters; Xurl stayed upright on Snagg's Battle Medicine (23), Ruben's Lay on Hands (18), a Halfling Luck reroll of a natural 1, and finally Orc Ferocity. Snagg ended it by pulling the second wight's head off. (Full round-by-round below.)
  • Post-mortem (DM): the encounter triggered because Alder's mirror reflection stepped within 15 ft of the sarcophagi ("It's an illusion, but it still counts"); the hands were level 1; "those wisps immune to magic is the problem"; and "if I'm not doing that carry damage over it might be a TPK."
  • Going home: Alder Drained 3, Ruben Drained 1, Xurl at 8 HP with persistent bleed, and a werewolf in the party. Xurl's plan for the moon: "go up to the first floor, past the barrier, inside the secret room in Belcorra's office. With the door shut, there is a solid stone wall on all sides" — wait until noon, then go home.

Character Spotlight

Snagg · the Company Man, Now Under Contract

  • The session opened on his life: a home in Otari, two retired goblin parents who gave Otari Lumber their whole working lives, and a security lead who reports to Klorte the morning after a pirate battle like it's any other Tuesday. The mayor's assignment made the temporary escort permanent — "a nice break from all these boring jobs" — and the Klorte hallway scene was the best two minutes of RP he's had: "Don't die. Come back in one piece" / "No promise, though" / "Think of your parents" / "You know how good I am. It's going like a piece of cake." He pocketed the mayor's private 50 gp — "That's a very good motivation. Now count on me."
  • Otari-local lane, delivered: his Society check named Jaul Mezmin and unlocked the bounty, Kaledo, and Isla — the fight's only piece of story context, and the reason the locket means anything. "Hey, I know that guy." He supplied "Otari" when Ruben blanked mid-speech, offered the werewolf the only deal on the table ("we can just leave your territory... after we just get past it"), and rolled the room's best Religion (28) on the shrine.
  • The natural 1 on the staff ("Wow. What the fuck?" / "Hell no") — and the hero point he spent turning it into a plain failure, no drain. Comedy elsewhere: his standing distrust of teleporters ("I don't trust these magical things"), "Remind me what we're here to do again," "Why are there so many doors down here?" and "I can't see shit."
  • The jaws did the heavy lifting again — session-high 187 damage and seven kills (four wisps, two hands, a wight) on a night when he never rolled a natural 20 ("I don't think I had a nat 20 tonight at all"). A Reactive Strike for 18 on a wisp flying past to hunt Ruben set up his crit-kill on it next round; a hand crawled up and grabbed him by the throat, so — hand still on his neck — he crit-bit a wisp dead, then bit the hand off and chewed it: "Disgusting." He called the wight's random target onto himself — "Hit me. Hit me, me, me" / "I can take it" — and ate 15 for it. He ended the fight with Snagging Strike into Combat Grab ("I love being a fighter"), then the finisher: "Just fucking bite his head off. Just yank it off" — the DM had him leap, seize the wight's head, tear it free, and throw it across the room.
  • Medic and triage lead: Doctor's Visitation + Battle Medicine on Ruben (20) once the hand was chewed off, a crit-success Battle Medicine on Xurl (23) mid-fight, Risky Surgery on Xurl after both fights (21, then 7). He also made the calls: the shove-Ruben-clear play Xurl executed, the kill priority ("stop the body so that Ruben can heal us"), and delaying behind Ruben so the 3-action Heal could land first. The one blank: a Slam Down trip that rolled a 2. His mood in the trough: "I roll fucking seven damage... This game's a stupid joke, man."

Xurl · the Cursed Sailor Who Gives No Fucks

  • He ran the pavilion parley with exactly the patience the man deserved — "That's great, but I actually do care who you are" / "We're not asking you to trust us. We just want to walk past you" — and when diplomacy died, he ended it with two words: "Punch in time?" ("Xurl gives no fucks.")
  • The curse is his now, and he took it like a sailor: "Fucking furries" on the bite, "Is it a cool curse?" on the diagnosis, "I just like that we end up fighting an unshifted werewolf on the night of the full moon," and the threat: "if I manage to turn into a werewolf I will be an absolute fucking nightmare if I retain any of my class feats." He immediately started staying out of the light and, by session's end, had the full plan: the sealed stone room in Belcorra's office, door shut, until noon.
  • Killed the wolf with a Stand Still as it fled, and landed a natural-20 Crane Wing on Jaul that Slowed him — the reason Jaul only had two actions when he turned. Later, Combat Grab finally on his sheet and whiffing all night: "Just for you, Lap, that would have been a Combat Grab." — "Never."
  • The fight's field medic and rules-brain: two healing potions, both poured into downed allies ("you're fucking lucky I carry two healing potions" / "Wake up, samurai!" / "And why are you dead again, motherfucker?"), the shove that got Ruben out of a wight's reach, and the judgement calls — flanking off a prone-but-conscious Alder, "now is the time to blow any spells you have." He crit-succeeded the shrine's fear and then the wisps' Flicker: "My mind is a fortress now. Unfortunately, my body is frail and bleeding."
  • His Dirty Trick debut crit-failed him prone — the DM: "The dirty trick in your mind is go for the balls. You reach down. There's no fucking balls" — and he spent a round pondering ("That's a girl zombie"). Then a natural 20 for 34 to the skull killed the first wight ("Goodbye, sweet dream" — Snagg), and the second wight's dying swing took him to zero — Orc Ferocity and the tattoo stood him back up at ancestry HP, bleeding, and "he just fucking throws his arm up and flings that shit back." He walked out at 8.

Ruben Falmoor · Full Support Mode

  • The Aiuvarin who reads Elven opened the session in character ("Oh, wait. Wrong voice. I can speak the Elven language"), translated the lock-glyph chest, and declined the cursed staff on Ruben's behalf twice over. Undead lore lane: he carried the identification of the shrine of Nhimbaloth and its empty-room signature, went and read up on her mid-fight — "The empty death" — and ran the lycanthropy exam on Xurl with the bedside manner of a Pharasmin: "I am sorry, my friend, but it seems that you have caught the... Jesus Christ, what the hell? ... It seems that you have contracted the curse."
  • The face, as ever, and for once the face fumbled: "our only mission here is to prevent the Gauntlight activating... this is our mission to protect... God damn it, I forgot the name of the town." The Diplomacy check landed on a man who doesn't care about Otari.
  • The 3-action Heal that wiped three crawling hands at once — "I wonder if I should just nuke the whole room" — and the discovery, one wasted Divine Lance later, that the wisps are immune to magic: "Looks like I'm on full support mode." Which he then ran flawlessly: Protector's Sacrifice on the wights' opening crit against Alder, Benediction (which turned a wight hit into a miss and was still up while he lay unconscious — "It's a good thing my spell's still active, even though I'm unconscious"), Shield blocking 10 off a crit, Lay on Hands on himself and Xurl, and the 3-action Heal that stood Alder up from Dying 3 — the table's "Reaction Heal."
  • Down once: two wight crits in one turn ("Luckily, will saves are my best save" had aged badly minutes earlier — "You speak too soon"). His diagnosis: "You ran away from your healer. And now your healer is exposed. Number one in the party. Always protect your..." His verdict on the DM: "Throwing double-digit monsters at us."
  • 100 HP of in-combat healing — the party's #1 healer again — and two natural 20s all night, both on non-attack rolls (a Recall Knowledge, and the Will save that shrugged off the first Funereal Dirge). His own count: "Neither did I. Oh no, I had one. And it was a Will save."

Alder Ungram · the Man in the Mirror

  • The kill of the campaign so far. "You guys probably weren't paying attention to Alder this fight, but he was holding a mirror in his left hand" — then Mirror's Reflection, a natural-20 Exploit Vulnerability that read the werewolf's whole sheet ("Poison ain't going to work on him too well. But Silver is"), and a natural-20 chakram whose Critical Hit Deck card asked a boss with 109 HP left to make a Fortitude save. Jaul failed it by one. "Well, I kind of just blew his head off." The DM's tally: one critical Strike and two critical Exploit Vulnerabilities in one fight. He'd already hit the fleeing wolf with an Implement's Interruption throw a minute earlier.
  • The mirror implement is new and he leaned on it all night — flanking with himself (the table's standing joke, per Xurl: "It is the flanking line. It happens in every game with flanking"), repositioning the reflection round after round, and, in the end, being the reason the wights woke up: "Fucking Alder's illusion" (Xurl). "But I was here, right? This is at 20 feet?" It wasn't.
  • He was also the first to say the word: "Oh, well, yeah, like lycanthropy. Careful, guys" — before Ruben's exam confirmed it — and the party's light source in the dark ("Yep, I am giving you light"). Character in the small moments: refusing the pistol as an implement, "That looks terrifying" when the sarcophagus opened, "Contemplating the meaning of life" for a prone, ball-less Xurl, and — standing up from Dying 3 with a chakram in hand — "Personal Antithesis!" and "Bring your no ball having ass."
  • The wights hunted him: Drained 1, 2, then 3 across two knockdowns — the first a natural-20 crit for 34 that stripped chakram, crystal, and mirror from his hands; the second a natural-20 crit for 28 with a hero point burned on a natural-1 Drain save. Before either, his confused turns were the best thing that happened all fight — three attacks on the same wight to a chorus of "Let's go!", including a natural 20 at −8 MAP whose card left the wight Clumsy 1 and one-handed. "I think that's the fun part of Confuse."
  • Still the party's practical voice: "By the way, we can't go back because we have the Lycanthropy," the Breached Defenses retrain, the composite bow, the Tailwind scroll — and a hero point held in reserve (he declined one on a crit-failed room save — "It's fine") until the worst possible moment.

Pax's Notes — Snagg's Journal

Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.

What Snagg did this session

  • Slept at home. Reported to Klorte in the morning like normal. Ended up in the mayor's parlor being told I'm the best they've got — except Klorte — and that I'm on the Gauntlight job now. Fine. Beats guarding lumber.
  • Klorte walked me out. Hand on the shoulder, "come back in one piece," "think of your parents." Told him no promises and that it'd be a piece of cake. He gave me 50 gold from the mayor's own purse. Just for me. The other three split their fifty.
  • Touched the skull staff. Rolled the worst roll a person can roll. It reached into my head for something — and I got it back out before it took. Cost me my luck for the night. Staff's in the lockbox now. Nobody touch it.
  • Rode the teleporter. Don't trust the thing. Came out fine.
  • Told the wolf-man we'd leave his territory once we were past it. He didn't want a deal. Put a name to him once he changed: Jaul Mezmin. Three hundred gold on his head, posted by Kaledo at Otari Market for his wife Isla. Alder killed him in one throw. We have the locket. Cut Xurl open after and put 21 back in him.
  • The shrine: killed seven — two hands, four of the lights, one of the sword-things. Bit a light out of the air with a hand still on my throat, then bit the hand off. Told the sword-thing to hit me instead of the others, and it did. Grabbed it so it couldn't get away. Pulled its head off at the end and threw it.
  • Patched Ruben (20) once the hand was off my neck, patched Xurl (23) mid-fight, and cut Xurl open again on the way out for 7 more. Called the shove that got Ruben clear, called the kill order, and held my turn so Ruben's heal could land first. No nat 20 all night. Didn't need one.

Things Snagg knows that the party does not

  • Kaledo. I know the man; I know the market. The bounty gets paid faster if a local walks it in.
  • My parents worked the lumber company their whole lives. Klorte knows that. That's why "think of your parents" — he wasn't guessing.
  • I can see in the dark down here better than the rest of them. The wall had more sarcophagi — some closed. I saw them. I don't think anyone else registered it.

What Snagg is thinking about

  • Xurl is a werewolf now. Bit on the night before a full moon. If he turns down here, he turns next to us. His plan is the sealed room upstairs until noon. Good plan. I'm sleeping across the door.
  • Two of these things nearly killed all four of us, and they came out of the wall. There are more coffins. Alder's mirror woke these. Nobody stands within fifteen feet of a sarcophagus again.
  • The lights were immune to magic and the healer was the one they kept knocking down. Whatever the plan is next time, it starts with where Ruben stands.
  • The mayor's washed his hands of the pirates. Navy's problem. The watcher on the docks is still in town, though, and he's human. Locals talk to locals.
  • Alder's Drained 3. Ruben's at one. We're not going back down until that's cleared.
