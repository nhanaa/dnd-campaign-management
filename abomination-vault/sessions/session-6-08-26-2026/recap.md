## Session Summary

The morning after the pirate raid, the Gauntlight business became official. At the mayor's manor, Wrin restated the stakes of the blueprints (a restored Gauntlight dooms Absalom and the whole region), the mayor closed the pirate thread himself — the dock messenger was glimpsed (a human) but not caught, the two captives go to Absalom for trial, and Black Eel's island is the Absalom Navy's problem now — and then turned to the goblin: *"You are the best guy that we have right now... I would like you to accompany them and see the threat of the Gauntlight is dealt with."* Snagg, who had gone home to his retired goblin parents the night before and reported for work like any other day, shrugged it into his job description: *"I guess a nice break from all these boring jobs."* Klorte walked him out, put a hand on his shoulder — *"I know this is very dangerous. Don't die. Come back in one piece"* — and got *"No promise, though"* for his trouble, plus 50 gold from the mayor personally. The party spent the day identifying loot (the elven-runed chest is a dead lock-glyph; the skull staff reached for Snagg's life force the moment he touched it — a hero point turned the critical failure into a plain one, no drain — and went straight into the locked box; Alder claimed the slide pistol), retraining feats, and buying a scroll and a bow, then rode the newly reactivated floor-2 teleport circle down to a third floor that was quieter than it had ever been.

Below the floor-3 stairs — the route the armored knight took — the party opened a door onto a stone pavilion ten feet above an underground lake, where a pale, dry-voiced man and a wolf sat at a campfire. He wanted them gone; Xurl wanted to know who he was; Ruben pitched the Gauntlight mission and blanked on the name of the town he was protecting (*"Otari,"* Snagg supplied). When *"I don't fucking care about Otari"* came back, Xurl looked over his shoulder — *"Punch in time?"* — and the fight was on. The wolf mauled Xurl, three reactions cut it down as it tried to break away, and then the man dropped to all fours, turned into a wolf, and crit Xurl with a bite that carried the **curse of the werewolf**. Snagg's local knowledge named him: **Jaul Mezmin**, 300 gold bounty, the werewolf who killed the wife of Kaledo, owner of Otari Market — *"Hey, I know that guy."* Alder's turn was the stuff of table legend — a natural 20 on Exploit Vulnerability, then a natural 20 on the chakram, then a Critical Hit Deck card that read *triple damage and a Fortitude save* — and a level-7 elite werewolf with 109 of his 140 hit points still in him failed the save by one. *"I don't think he dies. Fucking save or die effects."* Jaul's locket (*"Isla, my love"*) is the bounty proof. Ruben's examination confirmed the worst: Xurl is cursed, the full moon is tonight or tomorrow, and — the DM swears he didn't plan it — the Gauntlight's graveyard night was a new moon exactly two weeks ago.

They pressed on into a domed chamber whose walls were carved as a graveyard, ghosts rising toward a giant eye at the apex, and a seven-fingered stone hand of an altar clutching a flicker of blue light: a **shrine to Nhimbaloth**, outer god of emptiness, despair, and ghosts — *"the empty death,"* as Ruben named her. The room itself frightened anyone who looked too long; Snagg's verdict, seconds before the altar disgorged five crawling hands and five will-o'-wisps, was *"It seems suspiciously minor."* It wasn't. Two and a half hours and nine rounds later — magic-immune wisps confusing the party into swinging at each other, a hand clamped on Snagg's throat, and then two **Cairn Wights** bursting from a sarcophagus (tripped by Alder's mirror-image stepping fifteen feet too close) to chain **seven critical hits in eighteen swings** behind a fear-chant that kept everyone Frightened — Ruben had gone down once, Alder twice, Xurl had been saved by a Halfling Luck reroll of a natural 1 and then knocked to zero by a dying wight's last swing, and Snagg had ripped the second wight's head off and thrown it across the room. Its last words, in gasping Common: *"Leave this place."* The DM called it an Extreme encounter, admitted his damage-carry-over house rule was the only thing between the party and a TPK, and reminded everyone on the way out: *"Xurl is cursed."*

## Key Events

### Act I — The Mayor's Manor
- **Snagg's morning**: the DM established that Snagg lives at home in Otari with his **parents — both goblins, both Otari Lumber lifers, recently retired**. He reported to Klorte as usual, and Klorte walked him to the mayor's house where the party had slept.
- **Wrin's summary** of the blueprints: fully restored, the Gauntlight's reach isn't just Absalom but the whole region.
- **The mayor closes the pirate thread**: his men only *glimpsed* the dock messenger — **a human** — and the mayor assumes that person knows the plan is blown. The two prisoners go to **Absalom for trial**; the island hideout gets reported to the **Absalom Navy**. *"Thank you for what you did for us. I will take care of that from now on."*
- **Gold**: a pouch on the table — the second 50 gp of the pirate job, split three ways among the veterans (17 gp each in Foundry). Snagg, pointedly not included, took his psychic damage in silence (*"Just taking psychic damage because you don't get the gold"* — Xurl).
- **Snagg is assigned to the Gauntlight**: *"You are the best guy that we have right now — I mean, except Klorte."* Snagg: *"Wasn't it for me? ... I guess a nice break from all these boring jobs."*
- **Klorte's send-off** in the hallway, hand on shoulder: *"I know this is very dangerous. Don't die. Come back in one piece, okay?"* — *"No promise, though."* — *"God damn it. Think of the town. Think of your parents."* — (Pax, above the table: *"Does Snagg have parents?"* — the DM had just given him two) — *"Don't worry about it. You know how good I am. It's going like a piece of cake."* Then a bag of **50 gp from the mayor personally**, for Snagg alone: *"That's a very good motivation. Now count on me."*

### Act II — Loot, Identification, Retraining
- Xurl dumped the party's mystery loot on *"whoever looks the most confident"*: the runed chest, a vial of oil, and the skull staff. **Ruben, as an Aiuvarin who reads Elven**, translated the chest: a **lock glyph, no longer powered** — a well-made lockbox worth selling or keeping. Kept for the base.
- **The staff**: Alder's Occultism failed (14 vs DC 22). Snagg tried — **natural 1** — and the DM ran the critical failure as the staff itself biting back: *"the negative energy consume your mind... Snagg, you are Drained 1."* Ruben: *"I knew that stuff was nothing but trouble."* **Snagg spent his one hero point** — the table's degree-bump rule turned the critical failure into a plain failure (*"if it's a fail, then you don't have a Drained 1"*) — and walked away undrained. The staff went into the locked chest at the hideout, unidentified; nobody in the party wants it (*"this staff doesn't deserve to be used"*).
- **The slide pistol** went to **Alder** (martial proficiency): five shots, no ammunition for sale in Otari. Snagg suggested making it his weapon implement; Alder: *"Just five shots is not worth making my weapon implement."*
- **Retraining** (the DM opened it up): Alder swapped Instructive Strike → **Breached Defenses**; Xurl swapped **Suplex → Combat Grab** (*"I've never once had the ability to trigger it"*); Ruben swapped Positive Attunement → **Blessing of Vigor**. Alder bought a **composite bow** and a rank-2 scroll (**Tailwind**, eight hours of +10 ft Speed, cast on himself before the descent). Ghost Touch (75 gp) discussed and not bought.

### Act III — The Descent
- Left town before sunset. **Reactivated the floor-2 teleport circle** (10 minutes) and rode it to floor 3 — the party's first teleport: *"the image around you starts to shake and you feel a little dizzy... you find yourself on the third floor without any incidents."* Snagg beforehand: *"I don't trust these magical things. I guess if we all go in, I'll go."*
- **Floor 3 is quieter than ever** (Ruben, Perception 28). The party skipped the unfinished ghoul room — *"we have bigger things to worry about now than some loose ghouls"* (Ruben) — and took the stairs **the armored knight used** (*"The man who definitely didn't retrieve a grimoire directly from in front of our faces"* — Xurl). Snagg: *"Remind me what we're here to do again."* Xurl: *"find a way to turn off the light. Which is almost certainly the ninth layer."*
- Wrin's answer to Ruben's map request: the blueprints are a how-it-works document, **not a floor plan**.

### Act IV — The Pavilion and the Werewolf *(see Combat Encounters)*
- The door at the bottom opened onto a **stone pavilion 10 ft above an underground lake**, cold and wet, brown fungus on the far shore, a **man and a wolf at a campfire**. Pale, skinny, two knives, a **cracked, dry voice** — he hasn't been talking to anyone in a long time. *"I don't care who you are, leave."*
- **The parley**: Xurl — *"That's great, but I actually do care who you are. We're on a job"* (the word *job* visibly worried him) — *"We're not asking you to trust us. We just want to walk past you."* Snagg: *"Yeah, we can just leave your territory. Like, after we just get past it."* Ruben — *"our only mission here is to prevent the Gauntlight activating... this is our mission to protect... God damn it, I forgot the name of the town."* Snagg: *"Otari."* Diplomacy 18 at −2: *"I don't fucking care about Otari."* Xurl, over his shoulder to the party: *"Punch in time?"* Alder: *"I don't want to fight you, but we're gonna get through."*
- **Jaul Mezmin** — named by Snagg's second Society check, once the man had shifted into a wolf: **300 gp bounty**, killed the wife of **Kaledo, owner of Otari Market** *(spelling tentative)*, about a year ago; Kaledo recently raised the bounty. Snagg: *"Hey, I know that guy."*
- **Alder's execution**: two natural-20 Exploit Vulnerabilities and a natural-20 chakram in one fight; the crit-deck card killed a werewolf with 109 hit points left outright. *"Well, I kind of just blew his head off."*
- **Loot**: Jaul's katar and a **locket** — a woman's portrait, *"Isla, my love"* — Kaledo's wife. Bounty proof: *"That's easier than lugging his whole head back"* (Xurl). Snagg: *"Can't pass on bounty."*
- **Xurl is cursed.** Alder flagged it first (*"Careful, guys... I don't want to get too close to him"* — Xurl: *"You already bit me"*); Ruben checked him over (*"Come. Let me check you over"*) and the DM was explicit: it's a curse, not a disease — no early stage, he's cursed. Werewolves turn under **direct moonlight on the full moon**, lose control, and rampage; the full moon is **tonight or tomorrow** (the Gauntlight's graveyard night was a new moon two weeks ago — *"I'm not making this up"*). Underground is safe; the plan is to stay out of the sky until the moon sets. Xurl: *"Is it a cool curse?"* / *"if I manage to turn into a werewolf I will be an absolute fucking nightmare if I retain any of my class feats."*
- **Snagg's surgery on the spot**: Risky Surgery on Xurl — 4 slashing, critical success, **21 healed** (64/66). When the heal went through in Foundry without the cut, Pax in chat: *"you need to take the damage as well."*
- **The cave beyond**: water flowing in from a natural cave to feed the lake, a **metal pipe near the ceiling** (Alder), passages to the northeast, northwest, and southwest — unexplored. A north door led to a tight **spiral stair** going both up and down; the party went **up** and took the door on the landing. Snagg, in the dark: *"I can't see shit."* Alder: *"Yep, I am giving you light."*

### Act V — The Shrine of Nhimbaloth
- A **domed chamber**, ceiling 30 ft up: the walls carved as a graveyard with **ghosts rising toward a giant eye** at the dome's center; a 15-ft circular platform three feet high; on it an **altar assembled from stone slabs into a seven-fingered hand** clutching a flicker of **blue light**, with a small object on top.
- **Recall Knowledge**: the DM routed the identification through Ruben's prior-party knowledge (Religion 27) — Snagg's **Religion 28** was the table's high roll — a **shrine to Nhimbaloth**, outer god of **emptiness, despair, and ghosts**; the emptiness is the tell — her shrines have nothing in them but the altar. Ruben: *"The empty death."* The DM later: this place is dedicated to her alone.
- **The room effect**: DC 22 Will at the start of every turn while inside — fail Frightened 1, crit fail Frightened 2, crit success immune; the fear lasts until you leave. Snagg failed and shrugged it off with Bravery. *"It seems suspiciously minor."*
- Then the light inside the hand skipped, and **small hands came crawling out of the altar**.

### Act VI — The Altar Fight *(see Combat Encounters)*
- Five crawling hands and five will-o'-wisps, then **two Cairn Wights** bursting from a sarcophagus at round 3 — nine rounds, ~2h30m of table time, an **Extreme** encounter by the DM's own accounting.
- **Ruben down** (round 4), **Alder down** (round 7) and **down again** (round 8), Xurl at **3 HP** then **0 HP**. Both of Xurl's potions and the "Reaction Heal" went into the downed casters; Xurl himself stayed upright on Snagg's Battle Medicine (23), Ruben's Lay on Hands (18), a Halfling Luck reroll of a natural 1, and finally Orc Ferocity. Snagg ended it by pulling the second wight's head off.
- **Post-mortem** (DM): the encounter triggered because **Alder's mirror reflection** stepped within 15 ft of the sarcophagi (*"It's an illusion, but it still counts"*); the hands were level 1; *"those wisps immune to magic is the problem"*; and *"if I'm not doing that carry damage over it might be a TPK."*
- **Going home**: the party is spent — Alder Drained 3, Ruben Drained 1, Xurl at 8 HP with persistent bleed still ticking, and a werewolf in the party. Xurl's plan for the moon: *"go up to the first floor, past the barrier, inside the secret room in Belcorra's office. With the door shut, there is a solid stone wall on all sides"* — wait until noon, then go home. The DM will roll for moonlight if they leave.

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Pavilion: Jaul Mezmin and his Wolf (~26 min, 2 rounds)
**Field**: a stone pavilion over an underground lake, 10 ft above the water; a campfire; the fight was on the platform. **Enemies**: **Jaul Mezmin** (werewolf — the book's level 6 run Elite: level 7, AC 26, 140 HP, Fort +14, weakness silver 7 — nobody had any) and **Jaul's wolf** (Elite, level 5, AC 23, 75 HP). **Initiative**: wolf 33 · Xurl 32 · Jaul 31 · Alder 31 · Ruben 27 · Snagg 17 — *"We rolled great. They rolled better."* On paper **~120 XP — a Severe encounter** for four level-5s (Foundry's own tag agrees).

- **Before the dice**: the man's Perception (26) caught Xurl's aside — *"I'm trying not to start a fight right now"* — and out came the two knives: *"last time, leave."* Ruben's Diplomacy at −2 landed an 18 on a man who doesn't care about Otari. Snagg's first Society check (13): *familiar, can't place him.*
- **Round 1.** The **wolf** opened on Xurl — jaws for **16**, Knockdown trip failed. Xurl entered **Crane Stance** and tumbled past the wolf (failed, then succeeded — *"Step around"*). **Jaul**, two knives, rushed Xurl — *"Hey, nice to meet you"* — and missed twice, by 2 and 5. Alder delayed to after Snagg. **Ruben** cast **Spiritual Armament**: a natural 2 at the wolf (*"we know he's at the very least got an AC of 24"* — it was 23), then **Guidance** on Snagg. **Snagg**: Snagging Strike missed by 1, Guidance and all; **Combat Grab** connected (fist, 8) — wolf **Grabbed**. **Alder**: **Exploit Vulnerability — natural 20**. The DM answered about the wrong creature first — *"his body's really skinny, so you can tell that Fort save is the lowest"* — then caught it: *"the wolf is a wolf. It's a regular wolf. But the man is not human."* The chakram into the grabbed, off-guard wolf: **natural 18, critical, 33** (*"Wowzers. That was fantastic"* / DM: *"Choke that wolf"*).
- **Round 2.** The **wolf** escaped Snagg's grab (24 vs DC 23), bit at Snagg — **natural 1**, fumble card: **it bit itself for 13** (*"He bit his lip"*) — and broke away. That provoked, and all three neighbours swung before its turn was over: **Snagg's Reactive Strike** missed (natural 2), **Alder's Implement's Interruption** throw hit (14), and **Xurl's Stand Still** Crane Wing landed for one more than it had left — *"you guys just pile on him and you see that the wolf fall down."* *"I feel kind of bad. That was fucking brutal."* **Xurl's** own turn went entirely into Jaul: a Step, then a Flurry — **natural 20, critical, 22** (he drew a crit card and took plain double damage instead) and a natural 2. Two Fortitude saves: **Stunning Blows** failed by 2 but the incapacitation trait bumped it to a success (Jaul outlevels Xurl); the **brawling crit-spec** failed — **Slowed 1**. His new **Combat Grab** at full MAP whiffed. **Jaul**, down to two actions, **dropped to all fours and became a wolf**, and bit Xurl: **natural 18, crit for 26, plus Curse of the Werewolf (DC 25)** — Xurl's Fortitude save was a **20. Failed.** Xurl at 24 of 66: *"Fucking furries."* **Snagg's second Society check (14, with +2 now that the shape was showing)** named him: **Jaul Mezmin**, 300 gold, Kaledo's wife. *"Hey, I know that guy."* **Ruben** sustained the Armament (miss, 20 vs AC 26) and put a **rank-2 Heal into Xurl (23)**. **Snagg** walked in, **Snagging Strike hit Jaul (9)** — off-guard to everyone now — and raised his shield. **Alder**: *"You guys probably weren't paying attention to Alder this fight, but he was holding a mirror in his left hand"* — **Mirror's Reflection**, then **Exploit Vulnerability — natural 20 again** (a werewolf; a curse, not a disease; *"Poison ain't going to work on him too well. But Silver is"*), then the chakram: **natural 20 — Critical Hit Deck #32: triple damage and a Fortitude save.** The DM set the DC by level: 20. Jaul, at +14, needed a 6 and rolled a 5 — **19**. *"Zero of five."* *"I don't think he dies. Fucking save or die effects."* No damage roll: the DM zeroed him — **109, his exact remaining hit points** — and Jaul Mezmin was human again on the floor. Alder: *"Well, I kind of just blew his head off."* Xurl: *"What the fuck was that fight?"*
