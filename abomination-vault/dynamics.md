## Combat notes (validated through S4)
- **The DM ("Lap") runs deliberately over-level, lethal combat** — most fights are 1–2 levels above the party. Expect grinding attrition fights, not curbstomps. **S4 proved the lethality is real: Argon is dead.** Party is now **level 5**.
- **Hero-point house rule (confirmed S4):** spend = **reroll OR bump one degree of success**. Degree bumps are invisible in the Foundry export (no second d20). The DM also hands out mid-session hero points for discoveries (the mirror vision).
- **Snagg = the wall + primary damage (S5-validated).** Five jaws connections, four crits, 124 damage, three kills — including a Reactive Strike one-shot — in his debut fight. The stickiness package (Combat Grab → grabbed; Snagging Strike → off-guard; brawling crit-spec → Slowed 1) feeds Xurl's flanks. **Incorporeal warning carried over from Argon**: unarmed piercing dice bounce off ghosts — the Ghost Touch shopping note now lives on the handwraps. Also note: **failed Risky Surgery = stabbing your ally** (Xurl took 8).
- **S5 counter-lesson (low-level swarms don't work on this party)**: 8 boarders + officers at ~PL−3 got two-rounded; four one-shots. The DM's stated adjustment: *"I guess next time I can just put more out."* Expect volume or levels next time — the "over-level, lethal" doctrine will reassert.
- **DM-improvised morale checks (NEW, S5)**: unlabeled enemy Will rolls after gruesome kills — one pirate fled ("Fuck this!"), the raid commander surrendered mid-fight, and even allied Klorte rolled one to stop killing. Spectacle ends fights early at this table.
- **Player-piloted crew NPCs (S5 pattern)**: in big fights the DM hands allied NPCs to players (number lottery). Roll-log caution for future exports: crew actions ride players' voices, and **proxy rolls off PC sheets happen** (Clive's shove was rolled off Xurl's sheet; the rally check came off Alder's).
- **Xurl** — the grapple lane **whiffed all session** (running joke), but his **jumps / Stunning Blows / Stand Still / Assurance Demoralize** carried; also the party's rules-brain and conscience.
- **Alder** — RK/Exploit Vulnerability lane confirmed (reads weaknesses every fight); squishy chakram thrower; keeps forgetting cover.
- **Ruben** — healer/face confirmed (Lay on Hands, Heal, Enfeeble, Vitality Lash vs undead); first-time sorcerer; **psychopomp bloodline auto-refocuses** (can Treat Wounds + refocus together).
- **S6 — the over-level doctrine reasserted (two fights, Severe then Extreme)**: a level-7 elite werewolf boss (deleted in 2 rounds by Alder's crit-deck save-or-die — luck, not tuning), then a 9-round shrine fight at ~230 XP for four PCs. **The new enemy signature is fear-lock + attrition**: Funereal Dirge every 1d4 rounds kept the whole party Frightened 1–2 for most of the fight, Drain Life stacked Alder to 3, and the wisps' Confusion had PCs swinging at each other. Two knockdowns on Alder, one on Ruben, Xurl to 0.
- **Magic-immune enemies exist (the wisps)**: Ruben's damage kit is dead against them and his AoE Heal only cleared the hands. Plan on melee-only rooms.
- **The enemies go around the wall.** Snagg took 31 all fight behind AC 25–26; the wights and wisps targeted Ruben (down), Alder (down twice), and Xurl (107 damage, 162% of his max). Nothing forces them to engage the tank — positioning the healer is the party's problem to solve, and Snagg is starting to call it (shove-clear, kill order, delaying behind Ruben).
- **Carry-over house rule**: excess damage from a kill rolls onto the adjacent enemy — fired four times in S6 and the DM credits it with preventing a TPK. Kill chaining into the next target is now a real tactical resource.
- **Luck note**: the wights rolled five natural 20s in 18 swings (~1 in 650) while Snagg rolled zero in 50 dice all night and still topped damage (187) — the +16 vs AC 20–22 math carries him even when the d20 doesn't.
