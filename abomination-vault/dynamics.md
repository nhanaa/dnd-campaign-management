## Combat notes (validated through S4)
- **The DM ("Lap") runs deliberately over-level, lethal combat** — most fights are 1–2 levels above the party. Expect grinding attrition fights, not curbstomps. **S4 proved the lethality is real: Argon is dead.** Party is now **level 5**.
- **Hero-point house rule (confirmed S4):** spend = **reroll OR bump one degree of success**. Degree bumps are invisible in the Foundry export (no second d20). The DM also hands out mid-session hero points for discoveries (the mirror vision). **CHANGED — as of S7 hero points refresh on a LONG REST, not per session** (*"that was the start and then we changed to long rest"* — Xurl); the overnight rest granted one each, and both were spent in round 1 of the next fight.
- **Reading hero points out of the roll log — do NOT assume Foundry automation.** A hero-point reroll renders as **two d20 blocks on one line**. This table has **no blanket nat-1 auto-reroll**: S7 had **seven natural 1s and only two rerolls**, both PC hero-point spends confirmed in the transcript. **Verification method: count natural 1s against rerolls in the same log.** If most nat-1s stand un-rerolled, every two-d20 line is a hero point.
- **Snagg = the wall + primary damage (S5-validated).** Five jaws connections, four crits, 124 damage, three kills — including a Reactive Strike one-shot — in his debut fight. The stickiness package (Combat Grab → grabbed; Snagging Strike → off-guard; brawling crit-spec → Slowed 1) feeds Xurl's flanks. **Incorporeal warning carried over from Argon**: unarmed piercing dice bounce off ghosts — the Ghost Touch shopping note now lives on the handwraps. Also note: **failed Risky Surgery = stabbing your ally** (Xurl took 8).
- **S5 counter-lesson (low-level swarms don't work on this party)**: 8 boarders + officers at ~PL−3 got two-rounded; four one-shots. The DM's stated adjustment: *"I guess next time I can just put more out."* Expect volume or levels next time — the "over-level, lethal" doctrine will reassert.
- **DM-improvised morale checks (NEW, S5)**: unlabeled enemy Will rolls after gruesome kills — one pirate fled ("Fuck this!"), the raid commander surrendered mid-fight, and even allied Klorte rolled one to stop killing. Spectacle ends fights early at this table.
- **Player-piloted crew NPCs (S5 pattern)**: in big fights the DM hands allied NPCs to players (number lottery). Roll-log caution for future exports: crew actions ride players' voices, and **proxy rolls off PC sheets happen** (Clive's shove was rolled off Xurl's sheet; the rally check came off Alder's).
- **Xurl** — the grapple lane **whiffed all session** (running joke), but his **jumps / Stunning Blows / Stand Still / Assurance Demoralize** carried; also the party's rules-brain and conscience.
- **Alder** — RK/Exploit Vulnerability lane confirmed (reads weaknesses every fight); squishy chakram thrower; keeps forgetting cover.
- **Ruben** — healer/face confirmed (Lay on Hands, Heal, Enfeeble, Vitality Lash vs undead); first-time sorcerer; **psychopomp bloodline auto-refocuses** (can Treat Wounds + refocus together).
- **S6 — the over-level doctrine reasserted (two fights, Severe then Extreme)**: a level-7 elite werewolf boss (deleted in 2 rounds by Alder's crit-deck save-or-die — luck, not tuning), then a 9-round shrine fight at ~230 XP for four PCs. **The new enemy signature is fear-lock + attrition**: Funereal Dirge every 1d4 rounds kept the whole party Frightened 1–2 for most of the fight, Drain Life stacked Alder to 3, and the wisps' Confusion had PCs swinging at each other. Two knockdowns on Alder, one on Ruben, Xurl to 0.
- **Magic-immune enemies exist (the wisps)**: Ruben's damage kit is dead against them and his AoE Heal only cleared the hands. Plan on melee-only rooms.
- **The enemies go around the wall.** Snagg took 31 all fight behind AC 25–26; the wights and wisps targeted Ruben (down), Alder (down twice), and Xurl (107 damage, 162% of his max). Nothing forces them to engage the tank — positioning the healer is the party's problem to solve, and Snagg is starting to call it (shove-clear, kill order, delaying behind Ruben).
- **Carry-over house rule**: excess damage from a kill rolls onto the adjacent enemy — fired four times in S6 and the DM credits it with preventing a TPK. Kill chaining into the next target is now a real tactical resource.
- **S7 — the party learned to disengage, and it worked.** Three encounters: one on the enemy's terms (**the chain woman** — 2 rounds, 132 HP lost, **13 damage dealt, zero to her**, fighting withdrawal) and two on the party's own (both clean wins, 3 rounds each). The **nine-minute retreat argument** that produced the withdrawal is now the template: Xurl argues from spent resources, Ruben argues from remaining slots and defers, Snagg mediates and rules. Xurl's diagnostic is the one to reuse — *"we go from 100 to 0 in one or two turns, and we can't kill anything in those one or two turns"* — **count the party's attacks, not its hit points.**
- **"Nobody has silver" has now cost two boss fights running.** Jaul Mezmin (weak silver 7) in S6, the chain woman (**regeneration 10 shut off only by holy or silver**) in S7. **Fixed for one PC**: Snagg bought silver handwraps; Alder took cold iron for fiends. Watch the inverse — **Xurl is now permanently weak to silver.**
- **Knowing a weakness ≠ being able to use it.** Alder's Exploit Vulnerability rider applies to **his own Strikes only**. His S7 fire read on the Scalathrax was broadcast to the party and turned out worthless — *"has anyone got any fire for these guys?"* / nobody did. Read the party's actual damage types before treating a weakness as actionable.
- **Reactions are the answer to fleeing enemies.** Three of four Corpselight kills landed on wisps trying to fly clear — **Xurl's Stand Still, Snagg's Reactive Strike**, and Ruben's standing Sepulchral Mask aura. The wall works when the enemy has to leave.
- **Luck note**: the wights rolled five natural 20s in 18 swings (~1 in 650) while Snagg rolled zero in 50 dice all night and still topped damage (187) — the +16 vs AC 20–22 math carries him even when the d20 doesn't.

- **S8 — the lockout, and the lesson that replaces "nobody has silver."** Volluk (level 8 elite, PL+3, a **Severe** encounter by the book) nearly TPK'd a party that has cleared Severe and Extreme fights before, because it wasn't a difficulty spike — it was a **capability lockout**. `immune[grabbed, prone, restrained]` deleted both control PCs' primary lanes; `immune[precision]` and `resist[physical 7]` taxed a nearly all-physical party twice (119 of 293 rolled damage eaten); `fast healing 7` added 70 HP over 10 procs. **The new rule: knowing and even owning a weakness is not enough — you have to beat the save.** Ruben *had* an area spell, cast **Inner Radiance Torrent** straight into `weak[area 7]` while the target sat at **Clumsy 3**, and it **critically succeeded its Reflex +19 for zero.** Before buying bombs, check what save the weakness is gated behind.
- **Persistent damage is the real killer at this table.** A 2d8+10 persistent Tendril (doubling to ~40/round on a crit) put Snagg down three times and Alder to Dying 3 — healing cannot outpace a wound that reopens, and DC 15 flat checks fail. Compare the chain woman's Impaling Chain in S7. **Expect the pattern to continue.**
- **Hero points, S8 ledger**: seven spent (Xurl 3, Ruben 3, Alder 1, Snagg 0) on a long-rest allocation of one each — because the **DM handed the whole table an extra one mid-fight** for not looking the monster up online. Remember that **degree-bump spends leave no second d20 in the export**, so the roll log's reroll count undercounts. Confirmed again: **this table does not use the +10-on-a-low-reroll house rule.**
- **The Critical Hit Deck is a tactical resource, not a bonus.** Snagg traded double damage for Clumsy 3 + Enfeebled 3 (AC 27 → 22 off-guard) and it directly bought Xurl a hit; the same triple-damage card came up **twice in one night** and the second one ended the fight. Xurl made the same trade in S7 (Stunned 2 over 10 damage).
- **The AP itself is the problem, not the DM's scaling.** Lap's post-mortem: *"the only thing I did is add a spell to his list — Stinking Cloud. Everything else is from the AP."* The elite template scaled the numbers (+20 HP, +2 AC, +2 attack, **+2 DCs**); the immunity/resistance stack is Paizo's.
