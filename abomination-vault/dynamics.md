# Abomination Vault — Party Dynamics

## Party composition & roles

| Character | Player | Class | Lane |
|-----------|--------|-------|------|
| **Argon Rath** | Pax | Orc (Hold-Scarred) Barbarian — Dragon/Barrage-Force | **Frontline · damage · fear** (Demoralize/Intimidating Glare). The party's only real muscle. |
| **Alder Ungram** | Ricardo | Human Thaumaturge (returning chakram) | **Recall Knowledge / occult investigation · versatile striker.** Esoteric + Diverse Lore, Dubious/Unmistakable Lore; uses scrolls (e.g. object reading). The party's "answers guy." |
| **Ruben Falmoor** | Twan | Half-Elf Divine Sorcerer of Pharasma | **Healer · party face · undead lore.** Battle Medicine + Heal/Lay on Hands. Zero INT (dumped for CHA/WIS) — does the talking, not the deducing. |
| **Xurl** | Ben | Halfling Monk (wrestler) | **Control (Grapple) · scout · mobility.** Athletics expert, former sailor (navigation/dead reckoning). Bioluminescent. Also the party's sharpest tactical reasoner. |

**Combat engine (once fights start):** Xurl grabs / sets off-guard → Argon hits (flank partner). Ruben heals + Battle Medicine. Alder strikes + supplies Recall Knowledge. Argon is the frontline wall (72 HP + Rage temp HP); the others are squishier.

**Skill/knowledge lanes:**
- **Recall Knowledge / lore** → **Alder** (Esoteric Lore covers most things at −2 via Diverse Lore). Ruben owns **undead** knowledge specifically.
- **Healing** → **Ruben** (divine) + Battle Medicine. Alder can pick up second-hand.
- **Social / negotiation** → **Ruben** (face). Alder does legwork/recon; Xurl deadpans; Argon speaks plainly and with a code.
- **Navigation / sailing / dead reckoning** → **Xurl** (ex-sailor).
- **The party is universally low-INT** — a running joke ("we are a group of zero intelligence"). Nobody currently reads exotic languages; **Kholo** (the gnoll tongue on the pirate notes) is unreadable to them since the polyglot prior members left.

## Newcomer dynamic (as of S1)
- **Argon owes the party a life-debt.** They cured his ghoul fever and gave him a room; he signed onto their contract to repay it. As the newcomer he defers heavily — at the session's one real fork (chase the flying note vs. do the Gauntlight job they were hired for), Argon stayed passive and let the three veterans decide. His stated wants are simple: a fight, and to repay what he owes.
- **Argon came in blind** — the DM gave him zero AP background above-table, so in-fiction and out he's learning Otari / the Gauntlight / Belcorra alongside no prior knowledge. The other three are the veterans.
- Easy rapport so far: Ruben swore a Pharasma oath to protect him; Xurl slots him straight into the "I grab, you hit" plan.

## NPC relationships
- **Vandy** (Dawnflower priestess) — friendly; cured Argon free as goodwill. Recognizes Xurl (met the party's first day at the mayor's office); charges normally going forward.
- **Wrin Sivinxi** (fortune-teller, Wrin's Wonders) — cordial business relationship; grateful the party took over the Gauntlight job; had a friend in the previous party who died. A knowledge/magic-item resource. Offered to read the party's fortune.
- **Mayor Osif Mahimis** — employer (Gauntlight contract + now the pirate job). Values the party's intel highly. Owns Otari Lumber.
- **Iqbal** — the mayor's aide/gatekeeper; reads magic on the notes.
- **The kobolds** at the base — shared living space, non-hostile, "kind of weird." Argon is wary of having them at his back.

7. **Unexplored 3rd-floor water** — the **River Drake**, the **purple-worm corpse**, and the **negative-energy pillar** (heals undead) still down there; plus the ghost-haunted frog room Ruben wants to cleanse.

## Combat notes (validated in S2)
- **The DM ("Ab") runs deliberately over-level, lethal combat** — most fights are 1–2 levels above the party. Expect grinding attrition fights, not curbstomps. Debuffs and positioning matter a lot at level 4.
- **Argon = the wall + primary damage.** Debuted his S2 build: **Slam Down** (Strike+Trip, via Mauler), **Intimidating Strike**, **Demoralize/Intimidating Glare**. Holds doorways with 10-ft reach so nothing reaches the squishies; execution engine once the dice cooperate (multiple decapitation crits). Reactive Strike at L6 will make the wall much stronger.
- **Xurl** — the grapple lane **whiffed all session** (running joke), but his **jumps / Stunning Blows / Stand Still / Assurance Demoralize** carried; also the party's rules-brain and conscience.
- **Alder** — RK/Exploit Vulnerability lane confirmed (reads weaknesses every fight); squishy chakram thrower; keeps forgetting cover.
- **Ruben** — healer/face confirmed (Lay on Hands, Heal, Enfeeble, Vitality Lash vs undead); first-time sorcerer; **psychopomp bloodline auto-refocuses** (can Treat Wounds + refocus together).
