# Pax's PF2e Character Tier Lists

*Ranking every Pathfinder 2e character Pax has ever played. Four characters, zero objectivity.*

---

---

## Most Likely to Solo a Boss

1. **Tivael** — Lingering Composition + Dirge of Doom + Infectious Ennui + Biting Words means the boss is debuffed into oblivion before the party even swings. He doesn't kill it himself, but the boss wishes it was dead.
2. **Mo** — Surprise Attack round 1, Overwhelming Combination for two Sneak Attacks, crit procs Unbalancing Blow for free off-guard next turn. The burst is real. Falls apart if the boss survives round 2 and looks at him.
3. **Marwen** — Ice Ball go boom. Then die immediately because 48 HP and explorer's clothing.
4. **Bai Jian** — Will outlast the boss. Won't outdamage it. Dueling Parry and pray.

---

## Survivability

1. **Bai Jian** — Breastplate + Dueling Parry = AC 21 at level 2. Fortitude Expert. Dragonblood fear immunity. He doesn't dodge, he just stands there and takes it. Got hit by four nat 20s in S4 and lived.
2. **Tivael** — Seven defensive layers deep: Shield cantrip, Wooden Double, Hidebound, Bounce Away, Cloak of Illusions, Sanctuary, and flight. The drake is an onion of "please don't hit me."
3. **Mo** — Nimble Dodge, Deny Advantage, Dance of the Mousedeer for cover, smokescreens, 30 ft speed. Can't hit what you can't see. Can't survive what does hit him.
4. **Marwen** — Explorer's clothing. 48 HP. Stands behind everyone and hopes for the best. Blood Magic +1 AC is the saddest defensive feature in the roster.

---

## Drip (Character Concept)

1. **Tivael** — A Tiny house drake who plays magical pipes, sings compositions that buff the entire party, breathes silver-laced mental damage, and multiclassed into Sorcerer because one spellcasting tradition wasn't enough. Worships Desna. Speaks seven languages. He's a 6-inch dragon bard. Unbeatable concept.
2. **Mo** — Shadow-folk street urchin who punches ghosts with Spirit Warrior fists and dances the Mousedeer to literally disappear. Heritage shadow is described as "thick and liquid, like it could quench the finest ore." Come on.
3. **Bai Jian** — Tian swordsman hunting his missing brother across continents, carrying Sovereign Dragon blood he never asked for. The jian on his back, the flute in his pack, the dog at his side. Clean, classic, earned.
4. **Marwen** — Kitsune street urchin ice sorcerer with a star orb familiar and a demon mask from the queen's treasury. Strong! But "fox person shoots ice" is a well-trodden path.

## "The DM Remembers My Backstory" Index

1. **Mo** — Season of Ghosts is literally set in Mo's hometown. The AP is his backstory. Every NPC knows him. Unfair advantage.
2. **Bai Jian** — Kaazz seeded the Bai Feng nightmare through the Drude at Wolf Point. The follower on the road might be connected to Hellknight Alaya, who might be connected to the infernal creature that took his brother. The threads are pulling.
3. **Tivael** — Student of the Ancients background feeding into the Fists of the Ruby Phoenix tournament arc. The Azlanti lore is load-bearing.
4. **Marwen** — Campaign abandoned before the backstory could fire. We'll never know what Fioren's real name meant. F.

---

## Party Carry Potential

1. **Tivael** — Courageous Anthem is +1 attack/damage to the ENTIRE PARTY for 3-4 rounds off one focus point. Dirge of Doom is free Frightened 1 no save. He makes everyone else better. The definition of force multiplier.
2. **Bai Jian** — Snagging Strike off-guard for Cyrathul's Exploit Vulnerability. Ren's flank. Reactive Strike zone. Medicine backup. Diplomacy face. He's not flashy, but pull him out and the party collapses.
3. **Mo** — Smokescreens give concealment to allies. Dance of the Mousedeer is self-only though. Demoralize via Intimidating Glare helps. Mostly he carries by killing things before they act.
4. **Marwen** — Heal (signature, upcastable), Fear, Haste, No Cause for Alarm. Actually solid support... if she lives long enough to cast them.

---

## Action Economy Efficiency

1. **Tivael** — Free action Lingering Composition + 1 action composition cantrip = party-wide buff sustained for 3-4 rounds using 1 action and 0 spell slots. Then has 2 actions left for Bon Mot + spell. Obscene.
2. **Mo** — Overwhelming Combination: 1 action, 2 Strikes. That's two Sneak Attacks for the cost of one action. Spirit Warrior was a cheat code pickup.
3. **Marwen** — Signature Heal means flexible slot usage. Whispers of Weakness is 1 action for weakness info + attack bonus. Efficient but nothing broken.
4. **Bai Jian** — Command Animal costs 1 action for 2 companion actions (net +1). Snagging Strike is 1 action for a Strike + debuff. Dueling Parry is 1 action for +2 AC. Individually fine, but he needs all three every round and only has three actions. Tight.

---

## "Oh No" Moments Per Session

1. **Bai Jian** — Four nat 20s in S4. Crit-downed. Ren crit-downed. Voice stolen by the Attic Whisperer in S5. Enfeebled AND stupefied simultaneously. This man is a damage sponge and the DM knows it.
2. **Marwen** — Greater Demon Mask from the queen's treasury at level 5. That item is level 10. The "oh no" is what happens when the DM asks where you got it.
3. **Mo** — 38 HP at level 3, Small size. One crit from anything and he's making recovery checks.
4. **Tivael** — Tiny size, flies, seven defensive layers. The "oh no" is when he forgets to fly and something has reach.

---

## Dragon Energy Index

1. **Tivael** — IS a literal dragon. House Drake. Breathes mental damage. Draconic Sorcerer archetype. Scales of Steel. He IS the dragon energy.
2. **Bai Jian** — Sovereign Dragon blood. Dragonblood heritage. Dragon Lore feat. Four dragon-adjacent party members. The blood is internal, only surfacing in moments of intensity. Restrained dragon energy.
3. **Marwen** — Draconic Bloodline (Rime). Ice-reflavored Flurry of Claws. She channels dragon power but isn't one. Borrowed dragon energy.
4. **Mo** — Shadow of the Smith heritage. Zero dragon anything. His shadow is thick and liquid. That's cool but it's not dragon energy. Sorry, Mo.

---

## Overall Power Ranking (Current Level)

1. **Tivael (Lv 7)** — Master Diplomacy, Expert spellcasting, 7 defensive layers, Lingering Composition, Dirge of Doom, 3 focus points, arcane archetype for extra slots. The most complete character in the roster by a mile. Level advantage is real but even at equal levels he'd be top 2.
2. **Marwen (Lv 5)** — Rank 3 spells (Ice Ball, Haste, Slow), Expert Deception/Diplomacy/Intimidation, Oracle Dedication for Whispers of Weakness. Raw power is high. Survivability is paper. Glass cannon with great social skills.
3. **Mo (Lv 3)** — Sneak Attack + Thief DEX-to-damage + Overwhelming Combination + Surprise Attack. Burst damage king at his level. Spirit Warrior and Smokescreens give him tools most rogues don't have at 3. Fragile but lethal.
4. **Bai Jian (Lv 2)** — The build hasn't come online yet. Dueling Parry and Snagging Strike are solid foundations. Ren is growing. Level 4 (Striking rune + Dual-Handed Assault) is where this character transforms. Right now he's a sturdy body with a 1d6 sword and a dream.
