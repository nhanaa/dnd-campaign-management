# Progression — Avor (Hobgoblin Thief Rogue)

> Current level: **1** (joining ~Session 4, 2026-07-07). The path below is **tentative** — Pax wants to see how the group plays before locking the archetype and mid-level feats. Treat everything past L1 as a plan, not a commitment.

## Build Identity

DEX-based Thief Rogue, dual-wield (Aldori Dueling Sword + Shortsword). Damage = **Sneak Attack**, which rewards *every* Strike vs an off-guard target — so two-weapon fighting is a natural fit (more Strikes = more sneak dice). The distinguishing engine is a **fear-lockdown loop** that makes off-guard self-sufficient without needing a flank. Team stance: with three-plus damage martials already, Avor leans **support/utility** (Scout) over adding redundant damage.

## The Off-Guard Timeline (play it right)

- **L1–L3:** Demoralize does **not** grant off-guard yet. Sneak Attack comes from:
  - **Flanking** — Soft Willow (Swashbuckler) is the reliable melee flank partner; the Commander adds a third body.
  - **Surprise Attack** — open fights by rolling **Stealth/Deception** for initiative → round-1 off-guard alpha.
  - Frightened at these levels is still worth applying as a raw −status debuff (helps the whole party).
- **L4 — the keystone (Dread Striker):** frightened enemies become off-guard to you. Now the loop closes:
  - **Intimidating Glare** frightens (visual, no language penalty) → **Dread Striker** makes them off-guard → **Remorseless Lash** keeps them frightened ≥1 as you keep hitting → they stay off-guard round after round, **no re-Demoralize needed, no flank needed.**
