## Class Features

- **Rogue's Racket: Thief** — add DEX mod to damage with finesse/agile weapons.
- **Sneak Attack** — +1d6 precision vs off-guard targets (scales: 2d6 @5, 3d6 @11, 4d6 @17). Applies to *every* qualifying Strike, not just the first.
- **Surprise Attack** — round 1, if you roll **Stealth or Deception** for initiative, creatures that haven't acted are off-guard to you.

## Build Identity

Hobgoblin Thief Rogue dual-wielding an **Aldori Dueling Sword + Shortsword** — an Aldori-styled duelist walking into a campaign that opens at Lady Aldori's manor. Team lean toward **utility/support** (Scout) rather than more damage, since the party is martial-rich. See `tips.md` for the turn structure and `progression.md` for the level path.
