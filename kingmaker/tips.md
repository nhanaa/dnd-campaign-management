# Tips & Tactics — Avor (Hobgoblin Thief Rogue)

## Core Engine: Sneak Attack on every Strike

- Sneak Attack (+1d6 → scales) hits **every** qualifying Strike against an **off-guard** target — not just the first. That's why dual-wield is good: two Strikes = two sneak-attack riders. Thief racket puts DEX on both weapons' damage.
- **Weapon sequencing:** lead with the **Aldori Dueling Sword** (d8) at MAP 0, follow with the **agile Shortsword** (its agile trait softens the second Strike's MAP). Third action → Demoralize, reposition, or Nimble Dodge held in reserve.

## Getting Off-Guard (the whole game)

Priority order by level:

1. **Flanking (L1+)** — your bread-and-butter now. Get to the far side of a target **Soft Willow** (Swashbuckler) is on. The Commander can be the third body, but he drifts mid-line — build the flank around the Swashbuckler.
2. **Surprise Attack (L1+)** — round 1, roll **Stealth or Deception** for initiative → everyone who hasn't acted is off-guard. Open fights this way for an alpha strike.
3. **Fear-lockdown (L4+, the payoff):**
   - **Intimidating Glare** → Demoralize is *visual*, no language penalty → reliable **frightened**.
   - **Dread Striker (L4)** → frightened = off-guard to you → Sneak Attack **without a flank**.
   - **Remorseless Lash** → hitting the frightened target in melee keeps it frightened ≥1 → it stays off-guard turn after turn. Frighten once, then just keep swinging.

> Until Dread Striker (L4), Demoralize does **not** grant off-guard — it's still a useful −status debuff, but your Sneak Attack comes from flanking/Surprise Attack. Don't expect the fear loop to power your damage before L4.

## Survivability (d8 melee rogue)

- **Nimble Dodge** — hold the reaction for the scariest incoming hit.
- Position for flanks **without** overextending past the front line; you're squishy and the Stolen Lands hit back. The forums' universal rogue warning: don't be the one who gets focus-fired.
- Cleric heals are finite — chip damage is fine, don't beg for tops.

## Answering flyers / kiters

- You're melee with no reach. Carry a **shortbow**: Demoralize the floater → (with Dread Striker) it's off-guard → your **ranged** Strike still gets Sneak Attack. Keeps you relevant against a will-o'-wisp over Candlemere.

## Kingmaker encounter approach

- **Single tough monsters** (Tuskgutter, owlbear, Stag Lord, trolls) — your best content: one target, fear-lock it, dismantle it over several rounds. No re-setup churn.
- **Packs** (bandits, kobolds/mites) — flank the priority target, drop it, move on. Your fear/off-guard is single-target, so pick the scariest enemy rather than trying to cover the room.
- **Hexcrawl scouting** — you're the eyes. With **Scout** (planned), you Scout while Avoiding Notice and hand the whole party an initiative edge into ambush-prone fights — which then feeds your own Surprise Attack.

## Team stance

The party has plenty of damage (Swashbuckler, Exemplar, Commander-buffed martials). Your marginal damage is cheap; your **utility is not**. Favor picks that help the team (Scout initiative/recon, fear debuffs the party cashes in) over raw personal DPR.
