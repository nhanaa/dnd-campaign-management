The send-off dinner was hours ago; now the manor is bleeding. The party wakes in Lady Amandi Aldori's guest rooms to the smell of iron and salt — the Black Tear Cutthroats, a company of assassins out of Port Ice, have infiltrated the estate under cover of night with a pack of giants, butchering the charter-guests in their beds. Avor and Caleb come up swinging in one room; Soft Willow, Zouse, and Drek in another. Both fights are short and lethal, and when the two groups punch through their doors into the same corridor, they stop being two knots of survivors and become a unit.

What follows is a room-by-room push through a house turned abattoir: an indoor water-garden held by four more assassins, a ruined library guarded by the ogre Gromog, and a servants' wing where the blue giant Jurgrindor sits in a pile of the dead. Between fights the party interrogates (Soft Willow by threat of drowning, Avor by pretending to be a Black Tear "sky-spy"), loots the assassins, and drags the wounded out — rescuing the bard Linzi, the barbarian Amiri, the swordswoman Valerie, and a dwarf named Olgrim. Avor rolls absurdly hot all night, racking up killing blow after killing blow; Caleb keeps everyone standing; Soft Willow leads from the front with genuine menace; Zouse conducts from the banner; and Drek finds their departed friend Buggy dead in the ogre's trophy-pile and carries the remains for the rest of the night.

By session's end the party reaches the dining hall, links up with Aldori's chief of security Evenzi, and learns Lady Amandi is making a stand in the Dueling Hall in the smoke-choked West Wing. They start in — and a Foundry lighting bug in the burning wing forced an early stop, so the West Wing rescue opens next session.

Timeline

Act I · RP · Recaps & a Rude Awakening

Two Recaps, Then the Knives

  • Recaps were done for hero points: Soft Willow's player delivered a gleeful kobold-POV recap in the voice of "Soup" — the departed kobold PC they replaced ("soup is bestest… that is why soup is in your room") — while Zouse gave the straight version, name-dropping the young noble Maegar Varn he'd befriended at the feast (whom the DM pointedly tagged "a traitor").
  • Avor and Caleb wake — foggy and hungover from the dinner — to the smell of blood and a black shape looming over the bed. Caleb rolls off just ahead of a dagger stab; Avor comes up to find three assassins in the room.
  • Across the manor, Soft Willow, Zouse, and Drek stir to the same intrusion: Zouse had slept in his armor ("armored regiment"), Drek was curled on a wall-shelf (their empty bed threw the killers off), and Soft Willow had their armor off.
  • The Black Tears say nothing. They came to kill everyone in the house — and they very nearly had, before the party woke up.
Act II · Combat · The Two Bedrooms

Short, Lethal, and Loud

  • Avor opens the account and never really slows down: a Surprise-Attack thrust straight through the first assassin's heart (7 + 2 sneak = 9) drops it outright.
  • Caleb calls on Erastil, blasts an assassin with Void Warp, and lands an uppercut — dropping a second that same round — careful, at first, not to zap the "dinner companion" (Avor) he'd only just met.
  • The last assassin in the room makes an "audacious play" — bolts through a door for cover, can't reload, and hurls its dagger at Avor. It misses. Avor slips out and kills it with a single maximum-damage crit ("well over double its HP").
  • In the other room: Soft Willow tumbles into an assassin's space and opens its throat ("You have made a mistake coming here"); Zouse unfurls his bedside banner and calls Strike Hard; Drek bites one, loads the arbalest, and — after Willow trips a target off-guard — puts a bolt through it.
  • A fleeing assassin blunders into Avor and Caleb in the corridor; Soft Willow chases it down and buries a throwing knife through its spine. The two groups meet over the bodies.
Act III · Explore · The House of the Dead

Triage and the Black Tear Trail

  • The parties merge. Caleb drops an AoE Heal, patching everyone to full, and the group catches its breath before pushing on.
  • They find a guest bed with five murdered charter-guests (two women, a man, a dwarf, an elf) — people who'd been at the dinner.
  • Avor's Underworld Lore and Drek's Recall Knowledge name the killers: the Black Tears — a company of assassins out of Port Ice, "fanatically loyal and exceptionally ruthless," known for kidnapping, torture, and assassination.
  • Avor scouts doors and rooms ahead; Soft Willow loots the fallen assassins as the group organizes the push deeper into the manor toward the sounds of fighting.
Act IV · Combat · The Water-Garden

Four Assassins, and a Prisoner Taken

  • Soft Willow shoulders the door — "For the Bernie!" — and Avor bursts into an indoor garden of river and bridges to slit a throat so hard he takes the head off, killing on the first exchange (crit, 32).
  • Caleb feathers one with a longbow arrow; Soft Willow crits another and then grapples one alive for questioning.
  • The grappled assassin fights it — failing repeated Escapes but stabbing Willow before it goes down; Drek plinks it from the safe rear.
  • Avor Demoralizes the prisoner (Intimidating Glare, crit) and then knocks it out nonlethally — a live captive for the interrogation to come.
Act V · Combat · The Ruined Library

Gromog the Ogre — and Buggy

  • A damaged door falls flat, revealing a room piled with severed heads and limbs, an ogre mid-rampage ("Come back here, you little rats. I'm gonna gut you like a fish"), and a terrified halfling bard, Linzi, hiding behind a chair and a big book.
  • Linzi had already jabbed her rapier into the ogre's eyes before hiding — so Gromog fights blind the whole encounter, and the party is effectively off-guard to it.
  • Avor — "promoted to the front line" (his AC beats the Swashbuckler's) — carves the blind ogre up twice while it flails (a fatal-hook hit on Soft Willow for 11, then a crit-miss that jams its weapon in a shelf); Caleb's Void Warp adds to the pile-on.
  • Zouse yells Avor into a Strike Hard bonus attack, and Avor leaps, opens the eye, and drives a blade through the ogre's skull.
  • As the ogre falls, the party sees one body in its trophy-pile was Buggy — Drek's friend (a former party member). Drek is gutted, and gathers the remains to carry.
Act VI · RP · Interrogation & Rescue

One Prisoner, Worked Two Ways

  • Soft Willow revives the captured assassin and offers a choice in chillingly specific terms — talk, or be held underwater "until the bubbles stop." When Drek tries to menace the prisoner and gets laughed at ("you're just a flying snake"), Willow pulls them off: "Darling, darling, darling. Let the adults speak."
  • Avor then runs a Deception con on the same man — posing as an undercover Black Tear "sky-spy" — and milks him for the giant's name (Jurgrindor) and intent before ending him.
  • Pushing through servants' quarters (14 dead in their beds) and a storage room, the party finds and heals survivors: the barbarian Amiri (itching to go finish the giant herself), the swordswoman Valerie ("my knight!" — Drek), and the dwarf Olgrim.
  • Avor finds a hidden chamber behind a wall-catch in the library — a padlocked trunk inside. The party declines to rob their hostess (Caleb: "…but if our hostess is dead, I suppose it's up for grabs") and leaves it. Linzi tags along, offering spellcasting help.
Act VII · Combat · Jurgrindor the Giant

The Runt of the Litter

  • A blue giant sits in a nest of corpses, already bleeding from one arm and small by giant standards — the table needles it as "the runt of the litter" ("You should see my siblings." / "You won't."). Soft Willow tries to parley; it refuses.
  • Caleb leads with Bless and arrows (which miss to his longbow's close-range Volley penalty); the giant answers with a 15-ft cone of cold breath — Soft Willow and Zouse take the full 7.
  • Soft Willow Parries in without provoking and cuts the back of its knee to drop it prone — setting up everything that follows.
  • Zouse commands "Strike harder!", and Avor — Bless-buffed and off-guard against the prone giant — lands a 30-damage sneak crit, then the killing blow, as the giant reached for Soft Willow.
Act VIII · Social · Toward the West Wing

Evenzi, and the Fire Ahead

  • In the dining hall — the very one they'd feasted in hours before — the party meets Evenzi, Lady Amandi Aldori's chief of security, and the surviving Aldori guards.
  • Evenzi confirms the scale: the Black Tears struck with several giants, killed everything they could reach, and were driven off only after a hard fight; most attackers fled to the West Wing.
  • Lady Amandi is holding the Dueling Hall at the far end of the West Wing — now full of smoke and fire. Soft Willow demands the guards fetch buckets; the party heads in.
  • The burning wing is choked with smoke — and a Foundry lighting bug made it unplayable, so the DM called it there. The West Wing rescue opens next session.

Combat Encounters

Foundry note: "Gnasher" in the roll log = Soft Willow's token, and "Volk" = Drek's token — neither is a separate creature. Initiative orders are from Pax's trackers (authoritative). Assassins ≈ 9 HP.

Encounter 1 · Avor & Caleb's Bedroom · 3 assassins

Setup

Initiative: Avor → Cutthroat → Caleb → Cutthroat → Cutthroat. Avor wakes off-guard via Avoid Notice → Surprise Attack (Stealth for initiative), so every enemy that hasn't acted is off-guard to him round 1.

The fight

  • Round 1 — Avor: dueling-sword strike 7 + 2 sneak = 9, killing the first assassin outright ("perishes under your blade").
  • Caleb: calls on Erastil — Void Warp (DC 17 basic Fort) for 7 — then an uppercut Strike for 2, dropping the second assassin (7 + 2 = 9) the same round. A melee dagger displayed as "ranged" (token misconfig) chipped Caleb across the fight (4, 2, 5).
  • Round 2 — Avor: a successful Feint sets up the last assassin, but the follow-up Strike misses. He spends Nimble Dodge on a crossbow bolt aimed at him (it misses anyway).
  • Finish: the last assassin bolts for cover and throws its dagger (miss). Avor steps out and crits — 2×(1d8+4) = 24, "massive damage immediately kills."

Outcome

Three assassins down. Kill credit: Avor ×2, Caleb ×1.

Encounter 2 · Willow / Zouse / Drek's Bedroom · 3 assassins

Setup

Initiative: Cutthroat → Cutthroat → Drek ("Volk") → Soft Willow → Zouse. Zouse slept in armor (deflects the opening stab); Drek slept on a wall-shelf (empty bed fools the killers); Soft Willow's armor is off.

The fight

  • Cutthroats: stab at Zouse (deflected), tag the unarmored Soft Willow, and jam a dagger into a bed frame hard enough to lose it.
  • Drek: a Dragonet bite connects for 2, a second misses; loads the arbalest.
  • Soft Willow: a first tumble is stopped and re-rolled, then they slide into an assassin's space and open a throat with the kukri — 1d6+3 +2d6 = 14 — killing one.
  • Zouse: raises the banner and uses Strike Hard to command Gnasher (Soft Willow) to Strike again — the 50/50 tactic whiffs ("weak sauce") — then draws his scimitar. Later tries to command a shove and fails ("just a day of disappointment for me").
  • Drek (again): after Soft Willow trips a target off-guard, Drek's arbalest bolt kills it "by zero" — 1d10+1 = 10 (Willow: "you killed it because I set it up for you, right?").
  • Finish: a fleeing assassin stumbles into Avor & Caleb in the hall; Soft Willow's held throwing knife (1d4+3 +2d6 = 11) drops it, punching clean through the spine.

Outcome

Three assassins down. Kill credit: Willow ×2, Drek ×1.

Encounter 3 · The Water-Garden · 4 assassins (1 captured)

Setup

Initiative: Avor → Caleb → Zouse → Cutthroat → Soft Willow → Cutthroat → Drek → Cutthroat → Cutthroat. An indoor garden of river and two bridges; four Black Tears waiting.

The fight

  • Avor: bursts the door and crits — 2×(1d8+4+1d6) = 32, a decapitating kill on the opener.
  • Caleb: first longbow arrow misses; the second lands for 8 ("right on their hearts").
  • Soft Willow: crit strike 2×(1d6+3) = 14; then a failed long jump (an exploding-hero-point reroll still fails — no panache) before grappling an assassin alive.
  • Drek: arbalest from the rear → 5 on the grappled target.
  • The prisoner fights it: restrained, it fails repeated Escapes and stabs Soft Willow for 4 before going down.
  • Avor (capture): Intimidating Glare Demoralize (crit success) → then a nonlethal strike (−2), crit for 24, knocking the assassin out for interrogation.

Outcome

Four assassins neutralized, one taken alive. Kills: Avor and Willow, with the mop-up; the captive is worked over in Act VI.

Encounter 4 · Gromog the Ogre · ruined library

Setup

Initiative: Avor → Soft Willow → Drek → Caleb → Gromog → Zouse. Before the party arrived, Linzi had jabbed her rapier into the ogre's eyes — so Gromog fights blind the whole encounter, and the party is effectively hidden/off-guard to it.

The fight

  • Avor (front line — his AC 20 > Willow's): two sneak strikes on the off-guard ogre — 1d8+4+1d6 = 12 and 1d6+4+1d6 = 12.
  • Soft Willow: fails a style/parkour flourish (no panache), moves in and stabs. Drek: a bite misses from the edge of the fray.
  • Caleb: Void Warp — the ogre saves for half (no rider), but it adds to the pile-on.
  • Gromog: swings blind — a fatal-d10 hook hits Soft Willow for 11 ("a lot for level one"); crit-misses its last swing and jams the hook in a shelf.
  • Zouse → Avor: Strike Hard returns Avor's reaction; Avor's bonus sneak Strike hits for 14 and kills — a leap, the eye, a blade through the skull.

Outcome

Gromog dead (kill: Avor). Buggy's body is revealed in the trophy-pile as the ogre falls.

Encounter 5 · Jurgrindor the Giant · servants' wing

Setup

Initiative: Caleb → Jurgrindor → Soft Willow → Zouse → Avor → Drek. A blue giant already bleeding from one arm — small for its kind, "the runt of the litter." Soft Willow's parley is refused ("no chance in hell").

The fight

  • Caleb: Bless (+1 status to attacks), then arrows that miss — his longbow's Volley trait (−2 within 30 ft) bites at close range.
  • Jurgrindor: 15-ft cone of cold breath, basic Reflex, 2d6 = 7 — Caleb saves for half; Soft Willow and Zouse take the full 7.
  • Soft Willow: Parry, runs in without provoking (the giant has no reactive strike), and cuts the back of its knee to drop it prone — a legal trip (Large vs. Medium is one size). This sets up the crit.
  • Zouse: Strike Hard → "Strike harder!" at Avor.
  • Avor: Bless-buffed and off-guard against the prone giant — a sneak crit, 2×(1d8+4+1d6) = 30; an offhand swing misses; then the Strike-Hard bonus attack lands for 12 — the killing blow, as the giant reached for Soft Willow.
  • Drek: repositions for a shot but the giant dies before they can fire.

Outcome

Jurgrindor dead (kill: Avor). The party moves on to rescue Amiri, Valerie, and Olgrim.

Character Spotlights

Avor · the Duelist Who Talks His Way In ★

  • Pax's debut, and Avor made his mark twice over — with the blade and with his mouth. The clean Thief Rogue engine was on display all night: Avoid Notice → Surprise Attack off-guard, Feint for openings, and a textbook Demoralize → nonlethal capture to take a prisoner alive instead of dead.
  • The kill leader — opened nearly every fight and landed the killing blow on both Gromog the ogre and Jurgrindor the giant, plus the decapitating crit that opened the water-garden.
  • His sharpest moment wasn't a strike at all — the con on the captured assassin: posing as an undercover Black Tear "sky-spy" and turning a dying man's loyalty against him to pull the giant's name and the shape of the threat out of his own mouth.
  • Scout and accidental tank — ran point clearing rooms and checking doors, and stepped to the front line when it turned out his AC beat the Swashbuckler's.
  • The dice ran hot — Avor cheerfully called his own rolls "bullshit" — but the positioning and the patter underneath were the real story. Third-person self-narration throughout ("Avor will step here… then slash at his eye") is a nice tell that Pax is playing Avor, not just running him.

Soft Willow · the Blade Who Leads

  • The de-facto captain of the night — tumbling through assassins to open throats, throwing a knife clean through a fleeing man's spine, and organizing every triage, every door, every "watch the back line."
  • Menace with precision — the two-part interrogation of the captured assassin (the drowning speech is genuinely unsettling) shows a person who's made peace with cruelty and uses it deliberately.
  • The gentle flip side — real tenderness with the shell-shocked Linzi ("You did fine. You're still alive") and hard-won grace with Amiri ("We do not live in a world of what could have been").
  • The session's comedy engine — the Soup kobold recap, the exasperated "I left that kobold alone here for three days… assassins?!", and the immortal "what the hell kind of 7-Eleven bullshit is this?"
  • Willow's player is the ex-Soup player, and the continuity from goblin-brained kobold to razor-competent Kholo is played for all it's worth.

Drek · the Tiny Dragon With a Big Grief

  • Sensible in combat, scene-stealing in RP — Drek plays the arbalest from the safe rear (reload, shoot, don't get stabbed) and lets the roleplay do the heavy lifting.
  • The paranoid dragonet bit reads every room the worst possible way — "These are bandits! …They're here to steal my heart!" — and deepens into a genuine mid-combat existential crisis about whether their own family sent the assassins.
  • The session's gut-punch — Buggy, Drek's "one true friend on this God's-cursed adventure," found dead in the ogre's pile. Drek carries the remains the rest of the night and refuses to let go ("whatever I have left of Bugsy, I'm not letting him go").
  • The closing beat lands because of it — accepting Soft Willow's offer of friendship with a watery "I suppose you're better than the kobold."

Zouse Von Kusby · the Banner

  • He isn't in the scrum, he's conducting it — his whole night is the banner and Strike Hard, handing allies extra attacks, played as a pompous-noble bit.
  • Both commands whiffed at least once (the Strike Hard tactic, then a failed shove-order — "if this doesn't work, I'm gonna cry"), which he wore with theatrical despair.
  • The self-image is the joke and the character — "As a future noble, it's our duty to protect the innocent and the well-to-do… and my money."
  • Real work under the bit — the Warfare/Society Recall Knowledge on the giant, and sleeping in his armor so he wasn't caught defenseless.
  • His one live social thread — the friendship struck at the feast with the young noble Maegar Varn (whom last session's recap tagged a "traitor"). Signs off promising to be "more pompous and arrogant next time, as a noble."

Caleb · the Faithful (and Fiscally Motivated) Shepherd

  • The party's only real caster, and its MVP by attrition — the AoE Heal that reset the party to full mid-crawl, Bless into the giant, Void Warp contributions, and steady Battle Medicine/first-aid on downed prisoners and rescues.
  • A warm, slightly mercenary Erastil evangelist — won't blast a stranger who "seems not to be attacking me" ("we'll give him the benefit of the doubt") and invites survivors into the faith mid-cleanup ("Erastil will bless us all — it's an excellent faith").
  • A cleric's flexible view of property — "we probably don't want to be robbing our hostess… but if our hostess is dead, well, I suppose it's up for grabs."
  • The reason the party never bled out — every time the group would have dropped, Caleb was the answer. Also plinks in with the longbow between spells.

Pax's Notes — Avor's Journal

Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.

What Avor did this session

  • Woke to assassins in the room; killed the first with a clean thrust and the last with a single crit after it tried to flee.
  • Ran point clearing the manor — scouting doors, checking rooms, taking the front line when it turned out my AC (20) is higher than Soft Willow's.
  • Opened the water-garden fight with a decapitating crit; took a prisoner alive (Demoralize → nonlethal knockout).
  • Conned the prisoner by pretending to be an undercover Black Tear "sky-spy" — got the giant's name (Jurgrindor) and confirmation more enemies were ahead, then finished him.
  • Landed the killing blows on both Gromog (ogre) and Jurgrindor (giant).
  • Found a hidden chamber in the library with a padlocked trunk. Left it — didn't want to be caught robbing the host. Noted the catch.

Things Avor knows that the party does not

  • The exact spot of the secret wall-catch and the locked trunk in the ruined library. Nobody else clocked it.
  • The dying assassin believed I was one of them right up to the end — the Black Tears apparently don't all know each other by face. Their loyalty is real but their internal security is thin.
  • The prisoner spoke of Jurgrindor as the enforcer — "turn them into smush." There may be more than one giant.

What Avor is thinking about

  • The dice will regress. The positioning won't, if I keep leading with Avoid Notice → Surprise Attack and keeping a flank live. Don't get cocky about the crits.
  • I'm accidentally the tank. Fine for now, but I'm a d8 Rogue — Nimble Dodge is precious; don't spend the reaction early against something with a fatal hook.
  • Soft Willow is genuinely dangerous and genuinely in charge. Good person to flank with, better person not to cross. (Told Drek as much.)
  • The Black Tears came in force with giants to wipe a room full of charter-holders. This wasn't a robbery. Someone wanted these specific people dead.
