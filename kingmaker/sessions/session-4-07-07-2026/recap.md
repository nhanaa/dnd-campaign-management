## Session Summary

The send-off dinner was hours ago; now the manor is bleeding. The party wakes in Lady Amandi Aldori's guest rooms to the smell of iron and salt — the **Black Tear Cutthroats**, a company of assassins out of Port Ice, have infiltrated the estate under cover of night with a pack of **giants**, butchering the charter-guests in their beds. Avor and Caleb come up swinging in one room; Soft Willow, Zouse, and Drek in another. Both fights are short and lethal, and when the two groups punch through their doors into the same corridor, they stop being two knots of survivors and become a unit.

What follows is a room-by-room push through a house turned abattoir: an indoor water-garden held by four more assassins, a ruined library guarded by the ogre **Gromog**, and a servants' wing where the blue giant **Jurgrindor** sits in a pile of the dead. Between fights the party interrogates (Soft Willow by threat of drowning, Avor by pretending to be a Black Tear "sky-spy"), loots the assassins, and drags the wounded out — rescuing the bard **Linzi**, the barbarian **Amiri**, the swordswoman **Valerie**, and a dwarf named Olgrim. Avor rolls absurdly hot all night, racking up killing crit after killing crit; Caleb keeps everyone standing; Soft Willow leads from the front with genuine menace; Zouse conducts from the banner; and Drek finds their departed friend **Buggy** dead in the ogre's trophy pile and carries the remains for the rest of the night.

By session's end the party reaches the dining hall, links up with Aldori's chief of security **Evenzi**, and learns Lady Amandi is making a stand in the **Dueling Hall** in the smoke-choked West Wing. They start in — and a Foundry lighting bug in the burning wing forced an early stop, so the West Wing rescue opens next session.

## Key Events

### Act I — A Rude Awakening (two bedrooms)
- **Recaps for hero points:** Soft Willow's player delivered a gleeful **kobold-POV recap** in the voice of "Soup" (the departed kobold PC they replaced — *"soup is bestest… that is why soup is in your room"*), and Zouse gave the straight version, name-dropping the young noble **Maegar Varn** he'd befriended at the feast.
- **Avor & Caleb's room:** Avor wakes a beat behind Caleb, who has already rolled off his bed dodging a dagger. Three assassins. Avor opens the account and never really slows down — a dueling-sword thrust straight through the first assassin's heart drops it instantly.
- **Caleb** calls on **Erastil**, blasts an assassin with **Void Warp**, and throws an uppercut for good measure — careful, at first, not to zap the "dinner companion" (Avor) he'd only just met.
- The last assassin in the room makes an "audacious play" — bolts through a door for cover, can't reload, and hurls its dagger at Avor. It misses. Avor slips out from the doorway and kills it with a single **maximum-damage** sneak strike ("well over double its HP").
- **Willow / Zouse / Drek's room:** Zouse slept in his armor ("armored regiment"), Drek slept on a wall shelf (their empty bed fooled the killers), Soft Willow had armor off. Soft Willow tumbles into an assassin's space and opens its throat with a kukri — *"You have made a mistake coming here."* Zouse unfurls his **bedside banner** and calls **Strike Hard**; Drek loads the **arbalest** and, after Willow trips a target, puts a bolt through it. A fleeing assassin blunders into Avor and Caleb in the corridor — Soft Willow chases it down and buries a **throwing knife** through its spine.

### Act II — Clearing the House
- **The merge & triage:** The two groups meet over the bodies. Caleb drops an **AoE heal**, patching the party to full, and everyone catches their breath. They find a guest bed with five murdered charter-guests, and Underworld/Recall Knowledge checks (Avor, then Drek) ID the killers: the **Black Tears** — assassins, kidnappers, torturers, "fanatically loyal and exceptionally ruthless," out of Port Ice.
- **The water-garden (4 assassins):** Soft Willow shoulders the door — *"For the Bernie!"* — and Avor bursts in to **slit a throat so hard he takes the head off**, killing on the first exchange. Caleb feathers one with an arrow; Soft Willow crits another; Drek plinks from the safe rear. Soft Willow **grapples** one alive, and Avor **Demoralizes** it (crit) then knocks it out **nonlethally** for questioning.
- **The ruined library — Gromog the ogre:** A damaged door falls flat, revealing a room piled with severed heads and limbs, an ogre mid-rampage (*"Come back here, you little rats. I'm gonna gut you like a fish"*), and a terrified halfling bard, **Linzi**, hiding behind a chair and a big book — who, before hiding, had already **jabbed her rapier into the ogre's eyes**, leaving it **blinded**. Avor — "promoted to the front line" — carves the ogre up twice while it flails blind (a fatal-hook hit on Soft Willow for 11, then a crit-miss that jams its weapon in a shelf); Caleb's Void Warp adds to the pile-on. Zouse yells Avor into a **Strike Hard** bonus attack, and Avor leaps, opens the eye, and drives a blade through its skull.
- **Buggy:** As the ogre falls, the party sees one of the bodies in its trophy-pile was **Buggy** — Drek's friend (a former party member). Drek is gutted, and gathers the remains to carry.

### Act III — Interrogations & the Giant
- **One prisoner, worked two ways.** Soft Willow revives the captured assassin and offers a choice in chillingly specific terms — talk, or be held underwater "until the bubbles stop." When Drek tries to menace the prisoner and gets laughed at (*"you're just a flying snake"*), Willow pulls them off: *"Darling, darling, darling. Let the adults speak."* Avor then runs a **Deception** con on the same man — posing as an undercover Black Tear "sky-spy" — and milks him for the giant's name (**Jurgrindor**) and intent before ending him.
- **The rescues:** Pushing through servants' quarters (14 dead in their beds) and a storage room, the party finds and heals survivors — the barbarian **Amiri** (itching to go finish the giant herself), the swordswoman **Valerie** ("my knight!" — Drek), and the dwarf **Olgrim**. Linzi tags along, offering spellcasting help.
- **Jurgrindor the giant:** A blue giant in a nest of corpses. Caleb leads with **Bless** and arrows (dodged); the giant answers with a **15-ft cone of cold breath** (Soft Willow and Zouse eat the full 7). Soft Willow **Parries** in, cuts the back of its knee to drop it; Zouse commands *"Strike harder!"*; and Avor — riding Bless and off-guard — lands a **30-damage sneak crit**, then a follow-up, taking the light from its eyes as it reached for Soft Willow.
- **To the West Wing:** In the dining hall the party meets **Evenzi**, Aldori's chief of security, and learns Lady Amandi is holding the **Dueling Hall** in the West Wing — now full of smoke and fire. They grab buckets and head in. *(Session paused here on a VTT lighting bug.)*

## Character Spotlight

### Avor · the Duelist Who Talks His Way In
Pax's debut, and Avor made his mark twice over — with the blade and with his mouth. The clean Thief Rogue engine was on display all night: **Avoid Notice** into a round-one **Surprise Attack** off-guard, **Feint** when he needed an opening, and a textbook **Demoralize→nonlethal** capture to take a prisoner *alive* instead of dead. He opened nearly every fight and landed the killing blow on both **Gromog** the ogre and **Jurgrindor** the giant. But his sharpest moment wasn't a strike at all — it was the **con on the captured assassin**: posing as an undercover Black Tear "sky-spy" and turning a dying man's loyalty against him to pull the giant's name and the shape of the threat out of his own mouth. He also quietly became the party's scout *and*, to his own surprise, its front-line tank (his AC beat the Swashbuckler's). The dice ran hot — Avor cheerfully called his own rolls "bullshit" — but the positioning and the patter underneath were the real story. Third-person self-narration throughout ("Avor will step here… then slash at his eye") is a nice tell that Pax is *playing* Avor, not just running him.

### Soft Willow · the Blade Who Leads
The de-facto captain of the night. Soft Willow was everywhere — tumbling through assassins to open throats, throwing a knife clean through a fleeing man's spine, and organizing every triage, every door, every "watch the back line." But the character is far more than a knife: the two interrogations show a person who has made peace with menace and uses it precisely (the drowning speech is genuinely unsettling), and the rescue scenes show the flip side — real gentleness with the shell-shocked Linzi (*"You did fine. You're still alive"*) and a hard-won grace with Amiri (*"We do not live in a world of what could have been"*). They also carry the session's comedy: the Soup recap, the exasperated *"I left that kobold alone here for three days at most… assassins?!"*, and the immortal *"what the hell kind of 7-Eleven bullshit is this?"* Willow's player is the ex-Soup player, and the continuity from goblin-brained kobold to razor-competent Kholo is played for all it's worth.

### Drek · the Tiny Dragon With a Big Grief
Drek spends the combats sensibly in the back with the arbalest — reload, shoot, don't get stabbed — and the roleplay stealing every scene they're in. The bit is a paranoid dragonet who reads the room in the worst possible way (*"These are bandits! …They're here to steal my heart!"*), and it deepens into a genuine mid-combat **existential crisis** about whether their own family sent the assassins ("they'd say shit around me because they didn't know I understood them… would my family work with these lowlives?"). Then the session lands its emotional gut-punch: **Buggy**, Drek's *"one true friend on this God's-cursed adventure,"* dead in the ogre's pile. Drek carries the remains the rest of the night and refuses to let go (*"whatever I have left of Bugsy, I'm not letting him go"*). The grief is real, and it's what makes the closing beat land — accepting Soft Willow's offer of friendship with a watery *"I suppose you're better than the kobold."*

### Zouse Von Kusby · the Banner
The Commander plays it exactly right: he isn't in the scrum, he's *conducting* it. His whole night is the **banner** and **Strike Hard** — handing allies extra attacks — and it's played as a pompous-noble bit that mostly works and occasionally, gloriously, doesn't ("This better work, I swear. If this doesn't work, I'm gonna cry. …How do I word this command?"). The self-image is the joke and the character: *"As a future noble, it's our duty to protect the innocent and the well-to-do… and my money."* Both his commands whiffed at least once (the Strike Hard tactic, then a failed shove-order — "if this doesn't work, I'm gonna cry"), which he wore with theatrical despair. But Zouse also does real work — the Warfare/Society Recall Knowledge on the giant, and sleeping in his armor so he wasn't caught defenseless. He also carries the session's one live social thread: the friendship he struck up at the feast with the young noble **Maegar Varn** (whom last session's recap pointedly tagged a "traitor"). He signs off promising to be "more pompous and arrogant next time, as a noble," which is somehow both a threat and a love letter to the table.

### Caleb · the Faithful (and Fiscally Motivated) Shepherd
The party's only real caster, and its MVP by attrition — the **AoE Heal** that reset the party to full mid-crawl, **Bless** into the giant, **Void Warp** contributions, and steady **Battle Medicine**/first-aid on downed prisoners and rescues. Caleb's voice is a warm, slightly mercenary Erastil evangelist: he won't blast a stranger who "seems not to be attacking me" (*"we'll give him the benefit of the doubt"*), he invites survivors into the faith mid-cleanup (*"Join us here, friend. Erastil will bless us all — it's an excellent faith"*), and he has a cleric's flexible relationship with other people's property — *"we probably don't want to be robbing our hostess… but if our hostess is dead, well, I suppose it's up for grabs."* Every time the party would have bled out, Caleb is the reason they didn't.

## Combat Encounters

> Foundry note: **"Gnasher"** in the log = Soft Willow's token, and **"Volk"** = Drek's token — neither is a separate creature. Init orders are from Pax's trackers (authoritative).

### Encounter 1 — Avor & Caleb's Bedroom (3 assassins)
*Init: Avor · Cutthroat · Caleb · Cutthroat · Cutthroat*
- **Round 1 — Avor** (off-guard via Avoid Notice → Surprise Attack): dueling-sword strike **7 + 2 sneak = 9**, killing the first assassin outright ("perishes under your blade").
- **Cutthroats**: close on Caleb (a melee dagger displayed as "ranged" due to a token misconfig); Caleb takes chip damage across the fight (4, 2, 5).
- **Caleb**: calls on Erastil — **Void Warp** (DC 17 basic Fort) for **7** — then an **uppercut** Strike for **2**, dropping the second assassin (**7 + 2 = 9**) that same round.
- **Round 2 — Avor**: a successful **Feint** sets up the last assassin, but the follow-up Strike **misses**. He also spends **Nimble Dodge** on a crossbow bolt aimed at him (it misses anyway).
- **Finish** — the last assassin bolts through a door for cover and throws its dagger (miss). Avor steps out to line it up and **crits — 2×(1d8+4) = 24**, "massive damage immediately kills" (an earlier Void Warp tick of 2 had already chipped it). Three down. Kill credit: Avor ×2, Caleb ×1.

### Encounter 2 — Willow / Zouse / Drek's Bedroom (3 assassins)
*Init: Cutthroat · Cutthroat · Drek ("Volk") · Soft Willow · Zouse*
- **Cutthroats**: stab at Zouse (deflected by his armor), tag the unarmored **Soft Willow**, and jam a dagger into a bed frame hard enough to lose it.
- **Drek**: a Dragonet **bite** connects for **2**, a second bite misses; loads the **arbalest**.
- **Soft Willow**: a first tumble is stopped and re-rolled, then they slide into the assassins' space and open a throat with the kukri — **1d6+3 +2d6 precise = 14** — killing one.
- **Zouse**: raises the **banner** and uses **Strike Hard** to command Gnasher (Soft Willow) to Strike again — the 50/50 tactic whiffs ("weak sauce") — then draws his scimitar.
- **Drek** (again): after Soft Willow **trips** a target off-guard, Drek's arbalest bolt kills it "by zero" — **1d10+1 = 10** (Willow: "you killed it because I set it up for you, right?").
- **Zouse**: tries to command a **shove** to line up a free hit — and fails ("just a day of disappointment for me").
- **Finish:** a fleeing assassin stumbles into Avor & Caleb in the hall; Soft Willow's held **throwing knife** (**1d4+3 +2d6 = 11**) drops it, punching clean through the spine. Kill credit: Willow ×2, Drek ×1.

### Encounter 3 — The Water-Garden (4 assassins)
*Init: Avor · Caleb · Zouse · Cutthroat · Soft Willow · Cutthroat · Drek · Cutthroat · Cutthroat*
- **Avor**: bursts the door and crits — **2×(1d8+4+1d6) = 32**, a decapitating kill on the opener.
- **Caleb**: first longbow arrow misses; the second lands for **8** ("right on their hearts").
- **Soft Willow**: crit strike **2×(1d6+3) = 14**; then a failed **long jump** (an exploding-hero-point reroll still failed — no panache) before **grappling** an assassin alive.
- **Drek**: arbalest from the rear → **5** on the grappled target.
- **The prisoner fights it:** restrained, the grappled assassin fails repeated Escapes and manages to stab Soft Willow for **4** before going down.
- **Avor** (capture): **Intimidating Glare Demoralize (crit success)** → then a **nonlethal** strike (−2), crit for **24**, knocking the assassin out for interrogation.

### Encounter 4 — The Ruined Library: Gromog the Ogre
*Init: Avor · Soft Willow · Drek · Caleb · Gromog · Zouse*
- **Already blinded:** before the party arrived, **Linzi** had jabbed her rapier into the ogre's eyes — so **Gromog fights blind** the whole encounter, and the party is effectively hidden/off-guard to it.
- **Avor** (front line): two sneak strikes on the off-guard ogre — **1d8+4+1d6 = 12** and **1d6+4+1d6 = 12**.
- **Soft Willow**: fails a style/parkour flourish (no panache), moves in and stabs.
- **Drek**: a bite misses from the edge of the fray.
- **Caleb**: **Void Warp** — the ogre *saves* for half (no rider), but it adds to the pile-on.
- **Gromog**: swings blind — a fatal-d10 hook hits Soft Willow for **11** ("a lot for level one"); crit-misses its last swing and jams the hook in a shelf.
- **Zouse → Avor**: **Strike Hard** returns Avor's reaction; Avor's bonus sneak Strike **hits for 14** and kills — a leap, the eye, a blade through the skull. **Buggy's** body is revealed in the trophy-pile as the ogre falls.

### Encounter 5 — Jurgrindor the Giant
*Init: Caleb · Jurgrindor · Soft Willow · Zouse · Avor · Drek*
- **A wounded runt:** Jurgrindor is already bleeding from one arm and, by giant standards, small — the table needles it as "the runt of the litter" (*"You should see my siblings." / "You won't."*). Soft Willow tries to **parley**; the giant refuses — "no chance in hell you're getting out of this alive."
- **Caleb**: **Bless** (+1 status to attacks), then arrows that miss — his longbow's **Volley** trait (−2 within 30 ft) bites at this close range.
- **Jurgrindor**: **15-ft cone of cold breath**, basic Reflex, **2d6 = 7** — Caleb saves for half; **Soft Willow and Zouse take the full 7**.
- **Soft Willow**: **Parry**, runs in without provoking (the giant has no reactive strike), and cuts the back of its knee to **drop it prone** — a legal trip (Large vs. Medium is one size). This sets up everything that follows.
- **Zouse**: **Strike Hard** → *"Strike harder!"* at Avor.
- **Avor**: Bless-buffed and off-guard against the prone giant — a sneak **crit, 2×(1d8+4+1d6) = 30**; an offhand swing misses; then the Strike-Hard bonus attack lands for **12** — the killing blow, as the giant reached for Soft Willow.
- **Drek**: repositions for a shot but the giant dies before they can fire.

- Woke to assassins in the room; killed the first with a clean thrust and the last with a single crit after it tried to flee.

- The dice will regress. The *positioning* won't if I keep leading with Avoid Notice → Surprise Attack and keeping a flank live. Don't get cocky about the crits.

- Soft Willow is genuinely dangerous and genuinely in charge. Good person to flank with, better person not to cross. (Told Drek as much.)
- The Black Tears came in force with giants to wipe a room full of charter-holders. This wasn't a robbery. Someone wanted these specific people dead.
