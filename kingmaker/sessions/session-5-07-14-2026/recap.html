The manor is still burning. Picking up exactly where Session 4 left off, the party ropes themselves together and pushes into the smoke-choked West Wing, holding their breath through corridors of charred beds and charred sleepers, looking for Lady Amandi Aldori. Halfway in they stumble into a pocket of calm — a small shrine to Milani, the little goddess of hope and defiance, whose statue is somehow holding the smoke at bay. They stop, pray, leave a coin, and are quietly rewarded: a full night's rest folded into a single held breath. Then it's back into the fire.

Two fights close out the massacre. First, a knot of Black Tear looters stripping a game room — led by a white-haired boss with two curved knives (Nishkiv the Knife) and holding a gagged banquet-guest for ransom. Soft Willow talks her way to arm's reach and then stops talking; Avor, Caleb, and Drek carry the rest, and the captive is freed. She points the way: the Lady of the Manor is cornered in the dueling pit by a boss and two giants. The party bursts in on a bloodbath — Lady Aldori trading axe-blows with two blue giants while Volodmyra, a greataxe-swinging Black Tear leader, and five more cutthroats close in. Drek opens with a starshot that detonates two of the cutthroats; then the fight nearly turns tragic. Volodmyra buries an Overhand Smash crit into Avor's skull for 20 and drops him — the hot-dice hero of Session 4, down and dying. Caleb hauls him back from the light, and Avor stands up in the same fight, kills a cutthroat, and Demoralizes the woman who'd just killed him: "You were not able to kill me." Soft Willow disarms Volodmyra and finally puts her offhand claws through the boss's throat while Lady Aldori finishes the giants herself.

The manor arc ends there. In the aftermath the party loots an armory (and clocks that the Black Tears left too much treasure behind to have been mere robbers), buries the dead — Zouse keeps a hand of the fallen bard Buggy, which turns into an unexpectedly tender bit of Kholo bone-keeper lore from Soft Willow — and rests one uneventful night. At dawn Lady Aldori issues the charters: the party is sent southwest into the Greenbelt to break the bandits terrorizing the Rostland Hinterlands. They pick up new companions, trade insults with a rival crew (the Iron Wraiths), toast with a morning beer, and — at last — turn to face the open sandbox they came here for.

Timeline

Act I · Explore · Into the Smoke

The Burning West Wing

  • Roping up: the West Wing is a wall of smoke — thick enough that breathing it in "might not come out." Soft Willow organizes the crossing: rope everyone together ("just stay connected. If you pass out, we need to be able to find you"), hand a line to Avor and to Zouse, and hold breath (5 + CON rounds).
  • Drek, with "negative CON," peels off to scavenge textile from the dead assassins' armor for improvised masks before rejoining.
  • Rooms of ash: they move past entire rooms reduced to embers, the only distinguishing features "the charred remains of those who fell asleep in these rooms." Nothing to loot, nothing to save — just push forward.
  • The Milani shrine: one room is inexplicably clear of smoke. Inside, a marble statue of an armored half-elf, fist clenched over her heart. Avor recognizes the goddess — Milani, a minor deity of hope and uprising; Caleb doesn't.
  • Caleb, Avor, and Drek stop to pray; Soft Willow lays a silver piece on the altar ("I am not one to look a gift god in the mouth"). The shrine radiates tranquility and holds the smoke out — and their prayers are rewarded with the mechanical effect of a full night's rest.
  • Drek's own read on the goddess ("a low deity… most likely one below you") becomes a running dragon-superiority bit.
Act II · Combat · The Game Room

Nishkiv's Looters

  • Talking their way in: the party hears furniture being dragged in a room to the north — looters, not survivors. Soft Willow opens the door without stealth and finds Black Tears picking the room clean, plus a gagged, hog-tied woman on a back couch. A looter sneers, "Huh, I thought there were no survivors."
  • Willow runs a negotiation feint — "Why don't we make a deal? …if we just talk like reasonable individuals, we can come to an agreement right now" — then rushes the room, switching to Scout to hand the whole party the round-one initiative bonus.
  • Avor spots the captive and recognizes her from the banquet (a Diplomacy recall) — a person, not a threat. He resolves to free her.
  • The boss stands apart: different attire, two long curved knives, long white hair, half-covered face — Nishkiv the Knife. Avor softens her with a Demoralize + a crushing nonlethal hit; Caleb's second arrow drives into her forehead and finishes the boss ("I wanted you to finish her").
  • Zouse steps onto Nishkiv's body and whiffs twice at a cutthroat (a crit-fail on the MAP swing, "Is that all you got?") — then that cutthroat grabs him ("I'm being touched by the plebeian!") before he shrugs it off.
  • Soft Willow tumbles through and opens the other cutthroat's spine with a kukri Precise Strike ("Now is not the time for talking. He learned that the hard way"). Drek flies through the doorway and puts a bolt through the last thug's eye — "Dragon?" — dead.
  • Freeing the guest: Avor picks the sock-gag from her mouth. She reveals the Lady of the Manor is cornered in the dueling pit by "the boss and two giants." The party sends her to safety and hurries on.
Act III · Combat · The Dueling Hall (Extreme)

Volodmyra — and Avor Down

  • A bloodbath in progress: the trophy hall opens onto the dueling pit, where Lady Aldori is single-handedly holding off two blue giants. Flooding in behind: five Black Tear Cutthroats and Volodmyra, a greataxe-swinging leader ("they just don't stop coming, do they"). Aldori: "Help me with them, I'll hold off the giants."
  • Drek levels the room: after a cutthroat shoots Zouse then crit-misses and snaps its own crossbow string, Drek fires the Giant-Felling Comet — a starshot that detonates in a 5-ft burst (DC 17 basic Reflex, −2 to bigger targets). Two of the five cutthroats fail and die in the blast; the rest are mopped up over the following rounds.
  • Avor goes down: Avor delays, slips in on Volodmyra for 8, and Zouse's Strike Hard whiffs. Volodmyra — who also delayed — returns to find Avor exposed and brings an Overhand Smash crit down for 20 (29 vs his off-guard AC 18, no Nimble Dodge banked) → Avor drops, dying. "Well, goodbye," the table murmurs.
  • Back from the dead: Volodmyra turns for Zouse ("I'm coming for you, froggy"). Caleb heals the downed Avor for 14 — "Do not go into the light!" — and Avor keeps his weapons on a clutch Reflex save. Then, on his turn, Avor stands up, crits the adjacent cutthroat for 18 as he rises, and Demoralizes Volodmyra: "No mercy… You were not able to kill me." The boss is left frightened.
  • Soft Willow closes it: Willow vaults the guardrail into the pit, Disarms Volodmyra for a −2, and rides that debuff through her flailing sweeps ("I love seeing that minus two — that means the debuffs made the difference").
  • Caleb Blesses the party (the reason Avor now swings at +8); Avor flanks with Caleb for an 11-damage Sneak Attack; Volodmyra's Sweep hits Avor again for 13, leaving him on 1 HP.
  • The kill: Caleb's Sure Strike arrow grazes for 1, and Soft Willow drives their offhand claws through Volodmyra's throat — the killing blow, the light fading from her blue eyes. Meanwhile Lady Aldori has killed both blue giants herself and walks away virtually untouched.
Act IV · RP · The Dead & the Bone-Keeper

Aftermath

  • Not a robbery: the hall holds a full armory — swords, glaives, halberds, shields, half-plate, chain — all left untouched by the Black Tears. The takeaway: they weren't here to steal; they were here to stop the party from reaching the Stolen Lands. Aldori admits she overheard bandit chatter hinting at another motive she's still trying to make sense of.
  • Lady Aldori mourns: walking the pit among her fallen guards — "I knew the names of each and every one of them. I met their families. I employed them. And now they died here in my service… I shall compensate their families." She insists the party rest.
  • The bone-keeper: Zouse keeps the hand of the fallen bard Buggy — "when we claim this land and find a proper lake, I will sink his hand into the lake as a burial. I can't keep all of him." This prompts Soft Willow to reveal Kholo bone-keeper culture ("My people keep the bones of the dead so that they're remembered always") and offer to clean the bones properly; Drek offers to preserve the hand in salt so it won't rot.
  • Drek claims a halberd from the armory (Aldori's blessing) and Zouse re-mounts his banner on it.
  • The rest: the party sleeps in the dining hall — Caleb wedges the door and rigs a jangly alarm; Soft Willow takes first watch; Avor sleeps in the Milani shrine for the blessing. The night passes without incident.
Act V · Social · The Charters

Dawn, and the Open Sandbox

  • The charters: at dawn Lady Aldori convenes the surviving adventuring bands and issues the exploration charters for the Stolen Lands. The party is assigned the Greenbelt — southwest into the Narlmarches forest and the Kamelands hills — to break the bandits raiding the Rostland Hinterlands, starting at a hard-hit trading post.
  • Other charters go to a swaggering crew called the Iron Wraiths, to Maegar Varn (Zouse's noble friend), and to others. The Iron Wraiths mock the party on the way out ("bring your stuffed animals so you don't get scared… I bet 30 gold these guys won't last a fortnight").
  • Building the roster: the party recruits Valerie (a noble knight, offering to serve as Zouse's protective knight now that his squire is dead) and Amiri (the loud barbarian, who bonded with the departed kobold "Soup" and now attaches to Soft Willow).
  • Soft Willow reveals Soup was a 12-year-old kobold child she'd been minding, now sent home before his coming-of-age — then challenges Amiri to a beer-chug.
  • Jaethal (the freed captive — a Cleric of Swords / ex-Inquisitor who acknowledged Avor's fearsomeness) asks in; Avor interviews and welcomes her. A lead on Linzi the halfling bard is on the table (Drek notes grabbing her would free Caleb from babysitting Bless).
  • They kick around a party name — the Green Arrows ("arrows held together are much more difficult to break") — and toast with a morning beer.

Encounter 1 · The Game Room · Nishkiv the Knife + 2 Cutthroats

Setup

Initiative: Avor → Caleb → Zouse → Soft Willow → Cutthroat → Drek → Cutthroat → Nishkiv the Knife. Three enemies; the party opens off-guard via Willow's Scout hand-off. A gagged banquet-guest (Jaethal) is held on a back couch.

The fight (one round)

  • Avor: moves in, Demoralizes Nishkiv (Success → frightened), and lands a crushing nonlethal Strike, 1d8+4+1d6 = 15, softening the boss.
  • Caleb: fires the longbow twice — the first arrow chips a cutthroat (2), the second drives into Nishkiv's forehead (8) and finishes the boss Avor had softened. Kill: Caleb, on Nishkiv.
  • Zouse: steps onto Nishkiv's body and swings twice at a cutthroat — whiffs both (a crit-fail on the MAP swing); the cutthroat dodges and taunts, "Is that all you got?"
  • Soft Willow: Tumble Through, then a kukri Precise Strike, 1d6+3+2d6 = 15, opening the other cutthroat's spine — dead.
  • Cutthroat (enemy turn): Grabs Zouse ("I'm being touched by the plebeian!") — Zouse shrugs it off on a Fortitude save — then misses two desperate stabs.
  • Drek: fires up A Moment Unending (+1d6 precision), flies through the doorway, and crits (nat 20) — 2×(1d10+1d6) = 14 — a bolt through the last cutthroat's eye ("Dragon?"). Dead.

Outcome

All three looters down in a single round. Nishkiv the Knife killed in the opening exchange (Avor's nonlethal softener + Caleb's finishing arrow). Kills: Caleb ×1, Willow ×1, Drek ×1. The captive (Jaethal) is freed.

Encounter 2 · The Dueling Hall · Volodmyra + 5 Cutthroats (Extreme, 128 XP)

Setup

Initiative: Cutthroat → Drek → Avor (delay) → Cutthroat → Zouse → Volodmyra (delay) → Caleb → Cutthroat → Soft Willow → Cutthroat. Lady Aldori fights two blue giants alone nearby. Tuned Extreme — Volodmyra soaked ~55–60 damage: a real level-3/4 boss body.

The fight

  • Opening: a cutthroat can't see Avor and shoots Zouse — a hit for 4, then a second shot crit-misses and snaps its own crossbow string.
  • Drek — the Comet: a starshot into a cutthroat detonates in a 5-ft burst (DC 17 basic Reflex, 8 on a fail / 4 on a success, −2 to bigger targets). Five saves come up 23 / 8 / 12 / 22 / 21: two cutthroats fail and die in the blast; three (and Volodmyra) succeed for 4. "Two dead."
  • Avor (from delay): Strikes Volodmyra for 8 ("she's actually kind of strong"); a cutthroat's answering stab misses (Nimble Dodge); Zouse's Strike Hard whiffs.
  • Volodmyra (from delay) — the down: finds Avor exposed and lands an Overhand Smash crit for 20 — 29 beats Avor's off-guard AC 18 by 11 (no Nimble Dodge held) → Avor (17 HP) drops to dying. She advances on Zouse.
  • Caleb: 2-action Heal on Avor for 14, back from dying; Avor makes Reflex DC 15 to keep his weapons in hand.
  • Soft Willow: Acrobatics vault over the guardrail into the pit, then Disarm Volodmyra (−2 to her attacks).
  • Avor — the comeback: stands up and crits the adjacent cutthroat for 18 (split open, dead), then Demoralizes Volodmyra → frightened. "You were not able to kill me."
  • Grind: Caleb Blesses the party (Avor now +8 to hit); Avor flanks with Caleb for an 11-damage Sneak Attack; Zouse's second Strike Hard whiffs ("damned by a minus one"); Volodmyra's Sweep hits Avor for 13, leaving him on 1 HP, and misses Willow (the −2 biting).
  • The kill: Caleb's Sure Strike arrow grazes for 1; then Soft Willow drives their offhand claws through Volodmyra's throat (12) — the killing blow. Meanwhile Lady Aldori kills both blue giants herself (+29 to hit, hits on a 1).

Outcome

Volodmyra dead (kill: Soft Willow); all cutthroats cleared. Avor downed once (the Overhand Smash) and left on 1 HP — survived by a single point. No party deaths. The manor arc is over.

Character Spotlights

Avor · Back From the Dead ★

  • A full arc — the caretaker, the corpse, and the comeback. Before the swords came out, Avor was the party's conscience toward the helpless: he recognized the gagged captive from the banquet, insisted she was a guest to be saved not fought, and personally freed her and pointed her to safety.
  • The down: Volodmyra's Overhand Smash crit dropped Avor to dying — a 20-damage one-shot a banked Nimble Dodge (+2 AC) would have downgraded to a mere hit — and for a round he was a body on the floor, deadpanning through the heal ("still alive, but I'm still down, so I'll pretend I'm dead").
  • The comeback that makes him Avor: the moment Caleb pulled him up, he stood, crit the cutthroat next to him on the way to his feet, and spent his last action to Demoralize the boss who'd just killed him — "You were not able to kill me." Fragile, lucky, and absolutely unwilling to be intimidated.
  • The welcoming committee — closed the night interviewing Jaethal for a roster slot and folding a doomsday dwarf and a lapsed-religion backstory into the banquet flashbacks.
  • And, of course — "I am literally green, by the way" — the Green Arrows have their mascot.

Soft Willow · The One Who Stops Talking

  • The party's field captain, and the closer. Every practical decision in the smoke was Willow's — the rope discipline, the watch order, the reminder to hug the wall and shut the doors behind you.
  • The cold-blooded feint in the game room — all reasonable-individuals patter right up until they were in charging range.
  • Author of the boss's death: a cinematic guardrail vault into the pit, a Disarm that hung a −2 on Volodmyra, the tactician's glee as that −2 made her sweeps miss ("I love seeing that minus two"), and finally the killing blow, offhand claws through the throat, dismissing her name as unimportant.
  • The soul of the night — Kholo bone-keeper culture delivered with real reverence, an offer to clean Buggy's bones for Zouse, and the quietly moving reveal that "Soup" was a 12-year-old kobold child Willow had been minding and sent home.
  • Menace, mercy, and a druid-circle backstory, all in one night.

Drek · The Tiny Dragon With the Big Bang

  • Opened the session with a rambling, funny in-character recap that banked the party's hero points — then stole the middle of the biggest fight.
  • The Giant-Felling Comet is the combat highlight: one starshot, a five-foot detonation, and two of the five mooks dead in the blast (with the rest softened for the mop-up) — narrated with the perfect Drek shrug about the −2 "because almost everybody's fucking larger than me."
  • The voice was on all night — "when you are tiny, everybody's a giant to you," the dragon condescending to a goddess ("that's what you can expect from a minor deity"), the anxious "I feel very scared" at Lady Aldori's +29 to hit.
  • The grief thread stayed live — pushed back on Zouse pocketing Buggy's hand ("you could keep his fucking instrument, not his fucking hand"), then offered to salt-cure it so it wouldn't rot: care and gallows-humor in the same breath.
  • Plays sharp — it was Drek who pointed out that grabbing Linzi would free Caleb from babysitting Bless every round.

Zouse Von Kusby · The Noble Who Kept the Hand

  • The Commander brain, even when the follow-through failed — he drew up the party's opening plan for the Dueling Hall ("Avor, you go in, stab the woman, I tell you to stab her again"), the exact Strike-Hard combo the team runs.
  • Then the dice betrayed him — grabbed by a cutthroat ("I'm being touched by the plebeian!"), whiffing his own attacks and both Strike Hard commands, riding a wave of theatrical despair ("we're not gonna hit her at all… what world is this?").
  • He carried the grief thread more than anyone — keeping Buggy's hand to one day "sink into a proper lake as a burial… I can't keep all of him, that would be unrealistic," macabre and completely sincere.
  • The pomp keeps cracking open — he worried about Maegar Varn, then admitted the truth under the title: "I'm here without my family's backing… they help me so much I forget."
  • Tied his banner to a looted halberd, welcomed Valerie into his service, and took the Iron Wraiths' mockery on the chin. Comic relief with a family, a dead squire, and a friend he's afraid to lose.

Caleb · Do Not Go Into the Light

  • The reason the party still has five members. Caleb's night pivoted on one action: the heal that pulled Avor back from dying ("Do not go into the light!"), thrown the instant the axe came down instead of the arrow he'd been lining up.
  • The full field-medic kit — Bless for his "newfound family," Sure Strike arrows into Volodmyra ("there's times like these when Erastil's magic is necessary"), and Treat Wounds on everyone afterward.
  • A devout, practical veteran — stops to pray at every good-god shrine, wedges a chair under the door and rigs a jangly alarm before anyone sleeps, and (it turns out) learned his trade as a field medic in the army of Rostland.
  • Opinions about other people's gods — sizing up Harrim the doomsday dwarf: "that looks like a grotesque worshiper to me if I ever seen one."

  • Freed the gagged banquet-guest (Jaethal) — recognized her, cut her loose, sent her to safety.
  • Fought Nishkiv the Knife's looters: Demoralized and softened the boss with a nonlethal hit — Caleb's arrow finished her. All three down in one round.
  • Went down to Volodmyra's Overhand Smash crit — 20 damage (29 vs my AC 18; I hadn't banked Nimble Dodge, so no +2 to bump it out of crit range). Got healed for 14, stood up, crit the cutthroat next to me for 18, and Demoralized the boss on the way up. Took another 13 later — ended the fight on 1 HP.
  • Flanked with Caleb to land an 11-damage Sneak Attack on Volodmyra; Soft Willow finished her.
  • Slept in the Milani shrine for the blessing. Talked to Jaethal and Harrim (doomsday dwarf) in the banquet flashback; welcomed Jaethal toward the party.

Things Avor knows that the party does not

  • Milani's shrine is a genuine safe spot in the manor — smoke won't enter, and there's a real tranquility effect. Worth remembering the location.
  • Jaethal is a Cleric of Swords / ex-Inquisitor. She clocked my Demoralize game and gave me pointers — she's read as fearsome herself. Useful, and slightly unsettling, to have around.

What Avor is thinking about

  • I got impatient and it nearly killed me. 29 to hit vs my AC 18 = a crit for 20 on a 17-HP body. The real gut-punch: had I banked Nimble Dodge, +2 AC makes that a normal hit, not a crit — I probably don't go down. I walked into a delayed heavy-hitter's reach with my reaction already spent. The dice evened out from S4; the positioning has to carry me now.
  • Note the lever, not the loss: don't stand adjacent to a delayed heavy-hitter without a reaction held. Overhand Smash needs adjacency and telegraphs the swing. Bait it, stay out of reach, or keep Nimble Dodge ready.
  • We're building a real roster now (Valerie, Amiri, Jaethal, maybe Linzi). More bodies means I can go back to skirmisher and let someone else eat the front line — I am not built to tank a greataxe.
  • The Iron Wraiths think we're a joke. Fine. Let them bet against us.
