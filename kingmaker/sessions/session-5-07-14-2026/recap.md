## Session Summary

The manor is still burning. Picking up exactly where Session 4 left off, the party ropes themselves together and pushes into the smoke-choked **West Wing**, holding their breath through corridors of charred beds and charred sleepers, looking for Lady Amandi Aldori. Halfway in they stumble into a pocket of calm — a small **shrine to Milani**, the little goddess of hope and defiance, whose statue is somehow holding the smoke at bay. They stop, pray, leave a coin, and are quietly rewarded: a full night's rest folded into a single held breath. Then it's back into the fire.

Two fights close out the massacre. First, a knot of **Black Tear looters** stripping a game room — led by a white-haired boss with two curved knives (**Nishkiv the Knife**) and holding a gagged banquet-guest for ransom. Soft Willow talks her way to arm's reach and then stops talking; Avor, Caleb, and Drek carry the rest, and the captive is freed. She points the way: the Lady of the Manor is cornered in the **dueling pit** by a boss and two giants. The party bursts in on a bloodbath — **Lady Aldori trading axe-blows with two blue giants** while **Volodmyra**, a greataxe-swinging Black Tear leader, and five more cutthroats close in. Drek opens with a starshot that detonates two of the cutthroats in a single blast; then the fight nearly turns tragic. Volodmyra buries an **Overhand Smash crit into Avor's skull for 20 and drops him** — the hot-dice hero of Session 4, down and dying. Caleb hauls him back from the light with a heal, and Avor *stands up in the same fight*, kills a cutthroat, and Demoralizes the woman who'd just killed him: *"You were not able to kill me."* Soft Willow disarms Volodmyra, rides her debuffs, and finally puts her offhand claws through the boss's throat while Lady Aldori finishes the giants herself.

The manor arc ends there. In the aftermath the party loots an armory (and clocks that the Black Tears left too much treasure behind to have been mere robbers), buries the dead — Zouse keeps a hand of the fallen bard **Buggy** as a keepsake, which turns into an unexpectedly tender bit of Kholo bone-keeper lore from Soft Willow — and rests one uneventful night. At dawn **Lady Aldori issues the charters**: the party is sent southwest into the **Greenbelt** to break the bandits terrorizing the Rostland Hinterlands. They pick up new companions (Valerie, Amiri, Jaethal, and a lead on Linzi), trade insults with a rival crew of arrogant adventurers (the **Iron Wraiths**), toast with a morning beer, and — at last — turn to face the open sandbox they came here for.

## Key Events

### Act I — Into the Smoke (the burning West Wing)
- **Roping up:** The West Wing is a wall of smoke — thick enough that breathing it in "might not come out." Soft Willow organizes the crossing: rope everyone together (*"just stay connected. If you pass out, we need to be able to find you"*), hand a line to Avor and to Zouse, and hold breath (5 + CON rounds). Drek, with "negative CON," peels off to scavenge textile from the dead assassins' armor for improvised masks before rejoining.
- **Rooms of ash:** They move past entire rooms reduced to embers, the only distinguishing features "the charred remains of those who fell asleep in these rooms." Nothing to loot, nothing to save — just push forward.
- **The Milani shrine:** One room is inexplicably clear of smoke. Inside, a marble statue of an armored half-elf holding a shield, fist clenched over her heart. **Avor recognizes the goddess — Milani**, a minor deity of hope and uprising; Caleb doesn't. Caleb, Avor, and Drek stop to pray; **Soft Willow lays a silver piece on the altar** (*"I am not one to look a gift god in the mouth"*). The shrine radiates tranquility and holds the smoke out. Their prayers are rewarded — the GM grants everyone the mechanical effect of a **full night's rest** (and delivers the session's hero points). Drek's own read on the goddess ("a low deity… most likely one below you") becomes a running dragon-superiority bit.

### Act II — The Game Room (Nishkiv's looters)
- **Talking their way in:** Past the shrine, the party hears furniture being dragged in a room to the north — looters, not survivors. Soft Willow opens the door without stealth (letting the others set up) and finds Black Tears picking the room clean, plus a **gagged, hog-tied woman** on a back couch. A looter sneers, *"Huh, I thought there were no survivors."* Willow runs a **negotiation feint** — *"Why don't we make a deal? …if we just talk like reasonable individuals, we can come to an agreement right now"* — then **rushes the room, switching to Scout** to hand the whole party the round-one initiative bonus.
- **The captive:** Avor spots the tied-up guest and **recognizes her from the banquet** (a Diplomacy recall of his drunken socializing) — this is a person, not a threat. He resolves to free her.
- **The boss:** One assassin stands apart — different attire, two long curved knives, long white hair, half-covered face: **Nishkiv the Knife**. Avor opens on Nishkiv with a **Demoralize and a crushing nonlethal strike** to soften her; **Caleb's second arrow then drives into her forehead and finishes the boss** (*"I wanted you to finish her"*). Zouse steps onto Nishkiv's body and swings twice at a cutthroat, **whiffing both** (a crit-fail on the MAP swing), and gets a taunt for it (*"Is that all you got?"*) — then that same cutthroat **grabs him** (*"I'm being touched by the plebeian!"*) before he shrugs it off. Soft Willow **tumbles through and opens the other cutthroat's spine with a kukri Precise Strike** (*"Now is not the time for talking. He learned that the hard way."*). Drek **flies through the doorway and puts a bolt through the last thug's eye** — the man looks up, marvels, manages a single word, *"Dragon?"*, and dies.
- **Freeing the guest:** Avor picks the sock-gag out of her mouth. She thanks them, explains the Black Tears were holding her for ransom, and — crucially — reveals that **the Lady of the Manor has been cornered in the dueling pit by "the boss and two giants."** The party sends her to safety and hurries on.

### Act III — The Dueling Hall (the Extreme fight)
- **A bloodbath in progress:** The trophy hall opens onto the **dueling pit**, where **Lady Aldori is single-handedly holding off two blue giants** with battle-axes. Flooding in behind them: five **Black Tear Cutthroats** and **Volodmyra**, a Black Tear leader with a three-tears greataxe and a very mean look (*"they just don't stop coming, do they"*). Aldori: *"Guests, I'm sorry for putting you in this position — help me with them, I'll hold off the giants."*
- **Drek levels the room:** After the opening exchange (one cutthroat shoots Zouse, then crit-misses and snaps its own crossbow string), **Drek fires the Giant-Felling Comet** — a starshot into a cutthroat that **detonates in a five-foot burst**, forcing a DC-17 basic Reflex on everyone nearby (at −2, since almost everyone is bigger than him). **Two of the five cutthroats fail and die in the blast;** the rest are mopped up over the following rounds.
- **Avor goes down:** Avor delays, slips in on Volodmyra for 8, then Zouse's Strike Hard whiffs. Volodmyra — who had also delayed — returns to find *"a very attackable Avor right in front of her,"* raises the greataxe overhead, and **brings an Overhand Smash crit down onto Avor's head for 20 — 29 vs his off-guard AC 18, no Nimble Dodge banked. He drops, dying.** *"Well, goodbye,"* the table murmurs.
- **Back from the dead:** Volodmyra turns for Zouse (*"I'm coming for you, froggy"*). **Caleb heals the downed Avor for 14** — *"Do not go into the light!"* — pulling him back; Avor keeps his weapons in hand (a clutch Reflex save) and plays possum a beat. Then, on his own turn, **Avor stands up, splits the nearest cutthroat open as he rises, and Demoralizes Volodmyra** with his last action: *"No mercy… I'm back from the death. You were not able to kill me."* The boss is left **frightened** by the man she'd just killed.
- **Soft Willow closes it:** Willow **vaults the guardrail** into the pit (a cinematic Acrobatics move — foot on the railing, drop in behind her, ready to Parry), **Disarms Volodmyra** to slap a −2 on her, then rides that debuff through the boss's flailing sweeps (*"Look me in the eyes while you do it. I love seeing that minus two"*). Caleb Blesses the party and adds a Sure Strike arrow that only grazes (1); Avor flanks in for an 11-damage Sneak Attack; Zouse's Strike-Hards whiff again. Finally, off that grazing arrow, **Soft Willow drives their offhand claws through Volodmyra's throat** — the killing blow, the light fading from her blue eyes. Meanwhile **Lady Aldori has killed both blue giants herself** and walks away virtually untouched.

### Act IV — Aftermath, the Dead, and the Charters
- **Not a robbery:** Searching the hall, the party finds a full armory — swords, glaives, halberds, shields, half-plate, chain — all **left untouched by the Black Tears**. The takeaway: they weren't here to steal. They were here to *stop the party* (and the other charter-holders) from ever reaching the Stolen Lands. Aldori, pressed by Soft Willow, admits she overheard bandit chatter hinting at **another motive she's still trying to make sense of.** Drek claims a halberd (Aldori's blessing); Zouse ties his banner to it.
- **Lady Aldori mourns:** She walks the pit avoiding the corpses of her own guards. *"I knew the names of each and every one of them. I met their families. I employed them. And now they died here in my service… I shall compensate their families."* She insists the party rest.
- **The dead and the bone-keeper:** Bodies are gathered for cremation outside. **Zouse keeps the hand of the fallen bard Buggy** as a keepsake — *"when we claim this land and find a proper lake, I will sink his hand into the lake as a burial. I can't keep all of him. That would be unrealistic."* This prompts a genuine beat: **Soft Willow reveals Kholo bone-keeper culture** (*"My people keep the bones of the dead so that they're remembered always"*) and offers to clean the bones properly; Drek offers to preserve the hand in salt so it won't rot. The party rests one uneventful night (Caleb wedges the door and rigs a jangly alarm; Soft Willow takes first watch; Avor sleeps in the Milani shrine for the blessing).
- **The charters (dawn):** Lady Aldori convenes the surviving adventuring bands and issues the **exploration charters** for the Stolen Lands. The party is assigned the **Greenbelt** — southwest into the Narlmarches forest and the Kamelands hills — to break the bandits raiding the Rostland Hinterlands, starting at a hard-hit trading post. Other charters go to a swaggering crew called the **Iron Wraiths**, to Maegar Varn (Zouse's noble friend), and to others. The Iron Wraiths mock the party on the way out (*"bring your stuffed animals so you don't get scared… I bet 30 gold these guys won't last a fortnight"*).
- **Building the roster:** Over breakfast the party recruits: **Valerie** (a noble knight, offering to serve as Zouse's protective knight now that his squire is dead); **Amiri** (the loud barbarian, who bonded with the departed kobold "Soup" and now attaches to Soft Willow); **Jaethal** (the freed captive — a Cleric of Swords / ex-Inquisitor who acknowledged Avor's fearsomeness and wants in); and a lead on **Linzi** the halfling bard. They kick around a party name — **the Green Arrows** ("arrows held together are much more difficult to break") — and toast with a morning beer.

## Character Spotlight

### Avor · Back From the Dead
Pax's session was a full arc — the caretaker, the corpse, and the comeback. Before the swords came out, Avor was the party's conscience toward the helpless: he **recognized the gagged captive from the banquet**, insisted she was a guest to be *saved* not fought, and personally **freed her and pointed her to safety** (*"if you want to stay safe, you should leave here… there are people on the other side who can help you"*). Then the Dueling Hall nearly ended him. Volodmyra's **Overhand Smash crit dropped Avor to dying** — a 20-damage one-shot on a 17-HP body that a banked Nimble Dodge (+2 AC) would have downgraded to a mere hit, the exact d8-Rogue nightmare his own S4 journal warned about — and for a round he was a body on the floor, deadpanning through the heal (*"still alive, but I'm still down, so I'll pretend I'm dead"*). What makes it *Avor*, though, is what he did next: the moment Caleb pulled him up, he **stood, killed the cutthroat next to him on the way to his feet, and spent his last action to Demoralize the boss who'd just killed him** — *"You were not able to kill me."* That's the whole character in one turn: fragile, lucky, and absolutely unwilling to be intimidated. He closed the night as the party's welcoming committee too, interviewing Jaethal for a roster slot and folding a doomsday dwarf and a lapsed-religion backstory into the banquet flashbacks. (And yes — *"I am literally green, by the way"* — the Green Arrows have their mascot.)

### Soft Willow · The One Who Stops Talking
The party's field captain again, and the closer. Every practical decision in the smoke was Willow's — the rope discipline, the watch order, the reminder to hug the wall and shut the doors behind you. In the game room they ran a **cold-blooded negotiation feint** — all reasonable-individuals patter right up until they were in charging range — and in the Dueling Hall they authored the boss's death: a **cinematic guardrail vault** into the pit, a **Disarm** that hung a −2 on Volodmyra, the tactician's glee as that −2 made her sweeps miss (*"I love seeing that minus two — that means the debuffs made the difference"*), and finally **the killing blow, offhand claws through the throat**, dismissing her name as unimportant. But Willow was more than a knife this session. The keepsake scene pulled out the character's soul: **Kholo bone-keeper culture** delivered with real reverence (*"My people keep the bones of the dead so that they're remembered always. Their spirits are continued through the bones"*), an offer to clean Buggy's bones for Zouse, and — recruiting Amiri — the quietly moving reveal that **"Soup" was a 12-year-old kobold child** Willow had been minding and sent home. Menace, mercy, and a druid-circle backstory, all in one night.

### Drek · The Tiny Dragon With the Big Bang
Drek opened the session (a rambling, funny in-character recap that banked the party's hero points) and stole the middle of the biggest fight. The **Giant-Felling Comet** is the highlight of the night's combat: one starshot into a cutthroat, a five-foot detonation, and **two of the five mooks dead in the blast** (with the rest softened for the mop-up) — narrated with the perfect Drek shrug about the −2 "because almost everybody's fucking larger than me." The voice was on all night: *"when you are tiny, everybody's a giant to you,"* the dragon condescending to a goddess ("that's what you can expect from a minor deity"), the anxious little "I feel very scared" at Lady Aldori's +29 to hit. And underneath the bit, the grief thread stayed live — Drek pushed back on Zouse pocketing Buggy's hand (*"you could keep his fucking instrument, not his fucking hand"*) and then, characteristically, offered to **salt-cure the hand so it wouldn't rot**, care and gallows-humor in the same breath. They also play sharp: it was Drek who pointed out that grabbing Linzi would free Caleb from babysitting Bless every round.

### Zouse Von Kusby · The Noble Who Kept the Hand
Zouse had the worst dice and some of the best character work. The Commander brain was on display even when the follow-through wasn't — he **drew up the party's opening plan** for the Dueling Hall (*"Avor, you go in, stab the woman, I tell you to stab her again, and we'll go from there"*), the exact Strike-Hard/off-guard combo the team runs. Then the dice betrayed him: grabbed by a cutthroat (*"I'm being touched by the plebeian! Someone help!"*), **whiffing his own attacks and both of his Strike Hard commands on Avor**, riding a rolling wave of theatrical despair (*"It's Zouse's time to shine… we're not gonna hit her at all… what world is this?"*). But the pomp keeps cracking open to show something real. He carried the **grief thread** more than anyone: keeping Buggy's hand to one day *"sink into a proper lake as a burial… I can't keep all of him, that would be unrealistic,"* macabre and completely sincere. He worried aloud about **Maegar Varn** and then, unprompted, admitted the truth under the title — *"I'm here without my family's backing… they help me so much I forget."* He tied his banner to a looted halberd, welcomed Valerie into his service, and took the Iron Wraiths' mockery on the chin. Comic relief, sure — but comic relief with a family, a dead squire, and a friend he's afraid to lose.

### Caleb · Do Not Go Into the Light
The party's only caster and, this session, the reason it still has five members. Caleb's night pivoted on one action: **the heal that pulled Avor back from dying** (*"Do not go into the light!"*), thrown the instant the axe came down instead of the arrow he'd been lining up. Around it, the full field-medic kit — **Bless** for his "newfound family," **Sure Strike** arrows into Volodmyra (*"there's times like these when Erastil's magic is necessary"*), and Treat Wounds on everyone afterward. The character is a devout, practical veteran: he stops to pray at every good-god shrine, wedges a chair under the door and rigs a jangly alarm before anyone sleeps, and — it turns out — **learned his trade as a field medic in the army of Rostland** ("he's seen a bit of war"). Erastil's shepherd also has opinions about other people's gods: sizing up Harrim the doomsday dwarf, *"that looks like a grotesque worshiper to me if I ever seen one."*

*(Three enemies — the boss and two cutthroats — all dropped in a single round.)*

- **Caleb**: fires the longbow twice — the first arrow chips a **cutthroat** (2), the second drives **into Nishkiv's forehead (8) and finishes the boss** Avor had softened (*"I wanted you to finish her"*). **Kill credit: Caleb, on Nishkiv.**
- **Zouse**: steps onto **Nishkiv's downed body** and swings twice at a **cutthroat** — whiffs both (a crit-fail on the MAP swing); the cutthroat dodges and taunts, *"Is that all you got?"*

- **Outcome:** all three looters down in one round; **Nishkiv killed** in the opening exchange; the gagged guest (**Jaethal**) freed.

### Encounter 2 — The Dueling Hall: Volodmyra + 5 Cutthroats (+ Lady Aldori vs. 2 blue giants)
*Init: Cutthroat · Drek · Avor (delay) · Cutthroat · Zouse · Volodmyra (delay) · Caleb · Cutthroat · Soft Willow · Cutthroat*
*Pax's note: this was tuned at **Extreme (128 XP)** — likely Extreme for a smaller party, at least Severe for six. Volodmyra soaked ~55–60 damage: a real level-3/4 boss body.*
- **Opening:** A cutthroat can't see Avor and **shoots Zouse** — a hit for 4, then a second shot **crit-misses and snaps its own crossbow string**.
- **Drek — the Comet:** loads the starshot, fires into a cutthroat, and **Giant-Felling Comet detonates in a 5-ft burst** — DC 17 basic Reflex, spirit damage (8 on a fail / 4 on a success), targets at −2 for being larger. Five saves come up **23 / 8 / 12 / 22 / 21**: **two cutthroats fail and die in the blast**; three (and Volodmyra) succeed for 4. *"Two dead."*
- **Avor** (from delay): Strikes Volodmyra for **8** ("she's actually kind of strong"); a cutthroat's answering stab **misses (Nimble Dodge)**; Zouse's **Strike Hard whiffs**.
- **Volodmyra (from delay) — the down:** finds Avor exposed and lands an **Overhand Smash crit for 20** — **29 beats Avor's off-guard AC 18 by 11** (no Nimble Dodge held) → Avor (17 HP) **drops to dying.** She then advances on Zouse (*"I'm coming for you, froggy"*).
- **Caleb**: 2-action **Heal on Avor for 14**, back from dying; Avor makes a **Reflex DC 15** to keep his weapons in hand.
- **Soft Willow**: **Acrobatics vault** over the guardrail into the pit, then **Disarm** on Volodmyra — success (−2 to her attacks).
- **Avor — the comeback:** **stands up and crits the adjacent cutthroat for 18** (split open, dead) on the way to his feet, then **Demoralizes Volodmyra** — she ends the turn **frightened**.
- **Grind:** Caleb **Blesses** the party (+1 status, the reason Avor now swings at +8) and moves up; Avor **flanks with Caleb** for an **11-damage Sneak Attack**; Zouse's second **Strike Hard whiffs** ("damned by a minus one"); Volodmyra's **Sweep hits Avor again for 13**, leaving him on **1 HP**, and misses Willow (the −2 biting).
- **The kill:** Caleb's **Sure Strike** arrow grazes Volodmyra for 1; then **Soft Willow drives their offhand claws through her throat (12) — the killing blow.** Meanwhile **Lady Aldori kills both blue giants** herself (she hits on a 1; +29 to attack).
- **Casualties:** Avor **downed once** (the Overhand Smash) and left on **1 HP** by the end; the rest took chip damage. No party deaths. **No hero points spent** (the log's "streak" lines are Foundry low-roll automation, not hero-point rerolls).
