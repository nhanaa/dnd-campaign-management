## NPCs & Locations

- **Lady Amandi Aldori** — Rostland swordlord; issued the charters; hosted the send-off dinner. **Survived the assault (S5)** — personally **killed both blue giants** in the Dueling Hall (+29 to hit, "hits on a 1"), virtually untouched. Mourned her dead guards by name and pledged to compensate their families. At dawn she granted the exploration charters. - **Nishkiv the Knife †** — a Black Tear leader (white hair, two curved knives, half-covered face); ran the game-room looting crew. **Killed in S5** (Avor's nonlethal softener + Caleb's finishing arrow).
- **Volodmyra †** — a Black Tear leader; greataxe-wielder with **Overhand Smash** and sweeping attacks (she/her). Cornered Lady Aldori in the dueling pit with two blue giants; **dropped Avor to dying**. **Killed in S5** — Soft Willow's offhand claws through the throat.

- **The Iron Wraiths** — a rival band of five arrogant adventurers who also got a charter (the Glenebon Uplands / Tiger Lords / Pitax). Mocked the party on the way out ("bring your stuffed animals… I bet 30 gold these guys won't last a fortnight"). Rivalry seeded. *(Homebrew; GM also rendered it "Iron Raiders" — confirm.)*
- **Black Tear Cutthroats** — a company of assassins out of **Port Ice** (kidnapping, torture, assassination; "fanatically loyal"). Struck the manor with **giant-kin muscle**: the ogre **Gromog**, the giant **Jurgrindor**, and (S5) two **blue giants**, all slain. **Motive (S5 revelation):** they left a full armory of loot untouched — this was **sabotage, not robbery**, meant to stop the charter-holders from ever reaching the Stolen Lands. The specific paymaster and full objective remain unknown; both named leaders died without talking.
- **Milani's shrine** — a small chapel to **Milani** (minor goddess of hope, uprising, and defiance) in the West Wing: a marble statue of an armored half-elf, fist over her heart. Radiates tranquility and **keeps the smoke out**; prayer there (S5) granted the party the effect of a full night's rest. A genuine safe spot in the manor.
- **The Aldori manor** — locations seen: the guest bedrooms, an indoor **water-garden**, a **ruined library** (hidden chamber, **padlocked trunk** still unlooted), the **servants' wing**, the **dining hall**, the **Milani shrine**, and the **West Wing / Dueling Hall** (now cleared; a trophy hall + sunken dueling pit + armory). The fires were put out by a guard bucket brigade; the manor is stone, so damage stayed contained.
- **The charters (dawn, S5)** — Lady Aldori issued exploration charters to several bands: **the party → the Greenbelt** (Narlmarches forest + Kamelands hills; break the bandits raiding the **Rostland Hinterlands**, starting at a hard-hit trading post); the **Iron Wraiths → Glenebon Uplands**; **Maegar Varn → Nomen Heights**; a **Baron (Drelev?) → Hooktongue** trade routes. **Tartuccio** (a gnome in purple robes) and others were also present.
