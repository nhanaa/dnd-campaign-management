Session Summary

Session 6 closes Book One. The party — now five PCs strong, with two newcomers arriving via Shinzo's late-night recruiting run — completes the noppera-bo disguise infiltration of the lumber camp and walks straight into the BBEG of the arc: Zoudou the Zealous, the faceless cult leader the rebels had warned about from the start. The "her." Mo (in the noppera-bo hood from S5) and Ming Bao (bound as Mo's "captive") sell the disguise long enough to be marched into the inner ritual site, where Mugirou — the noppera-bo rebel, named for the first time this session — drops the pretense and joins them openly. Riddle (Skyborn Tengu Animist) and Zhao Lishen (Mage Automaton Commander on a terracotta Riding Drake named Qian Siam) arrive from the south side of the camp escorted by Shinzo, who hands them off at the treeline with a wordless tip of the hat and the promise "a great trial lies ahead of you."

The fight is uglier than S5's clean ambush. Zoudou is mid-ritual on Kageyuri, who is bound at the shrine, with a darkening silhouette behind her — something cosmically wrong, banging against a wall of fog like glass at an aquarium with three-headed necks and many-fisted arms. Zhao opens with a textbook Commander turn (Gather to Me! → flanking → Set-Up Strike for 10 damage and off-guard); the team converges; the small-fry occultists and grunts go down quickly under Mo's Crushing-rune chains (a 44-damage Overwhelming Combination crit on the Occultist; Ming Bao's flurry-and-shield-raise dance; Jun-Yeong's two-action Heal-vs-Living to keep Mo upright). Then Zoudou casts Darkness, drops the Trickster into stealth-bow harassment mode, and conjures a hazard — a wickerwork demigod-effigy that uses her spell slots to cast Noise Blast (24 damage to three party members) and Harm (a 30-foot emanation that, after Jun-Yeong rerolls his failed save with a hero point, kills one of her own Occultists outright and ticks down everyone in range).

The Hail Mary that kills Zoudou's own ally also signals she has run out of cards. Riddle communes with the agitated spirits trapped inside the hazard-effigy on a critical Occultism success, singing them quiet for a round (and catching a flash of golden light behind the silhouette — a separate figure trapped in there with Kugaptee, fighting it). The spell-pump goes offline. Mo demoralizes Zoudou to Frightened 1. The party drops her into single digits. Rather than be captured, Zoudou stabs herself in the heart, screams "Kugaptee!" into the void, and the trees beyond the fog wall begin to shake. The thing on the other side gets through. It comes out as a 15-foot eyeless wicker-helmed fiend, ribcage bulging with red butterflies, and one-shots the fleeing Trickster mid-sentence (76 damage from a Ruined Great Katana, Reach 15, Unholy) as a demonstration. The party runs.

They are saved by ghosts they didn't know they had. Ming Bao, fleeing the map, feels a hand on his shoulder and a familiar voice — "I'll take care of this. I always have." It's Mingxi. Erased from everyone's memory but his own, alive enough to wield the Tengu form he died in, alive enough to 1v1 the demigod-aspect long enough for the party to clear the treeline. The downtime is heavy: Yami the temple cat finally drops a name — "That Kugaptee finally showed his face... he is a demigod" — everyone at the table shares the same dream of Tan Sugi Monastery and a five-pronged claw of red butterflies in the sky; Riddle's apparitions panic the first time he tries to scry on Kugaptee and beg him not to speak the name aloud; and Eliza translates the Chthonian half of Zoudou's Tattered Ledger — the cult was hunting reincarnated souls specifically, and the ledger names Kageyuri, Ming Bao, and Jun-Yeong as targets. Sixty days of in-fiction downtime later, Book Two opens on Toilday, Rova 1st, and "a lot of spiders" on the landing page.

Act VI · Mystery · The Tattered Ledger

The Cult Has Been Hunting Reincarnated Souls

  • The party returns to the lumber camp the next day. Every noppera-bo is dead — cleaved in half by the demigod-aspect during its rampage. The cage of farmers is intact; all bleed red. "This demon from the woods, they slaughtered all the faceless men in this place."
  • Loot from Zoudou: Fiend's Hunger (unique +1 silver dagger), the Tattered Ledger, and a Twisted Knot of Wood religious symbol of Kugaptee. Jun-Yeong checks Fiend's Hunger for curses, then formally hands it off to Mo.
  • Mugirou's status: alive but separated. Was at the camp briefly, then moved on, being hunted himself.
  • Eliza translates the Chthonian half. Jun-Yeong reads the Aklo half. The Chthonian half is the load-bearing reveal: Zoudou had been hunting reincarnated souls specifically — "a creature whose soul has been reincarnated several times, a discovery that I think I've made while examining this Kageyuri woman." The ledger names targets: Kageyuri, Ming Bao, Jun-Yeong. Mo is not named.
  • Sanpachi lore (Jun-Yeong's deep cut): With his Sanpachi-specific lore training, Jun-Yeong recognizes the philosophy — "essentially Buddhism. You are judged based on your actions in life. Depending on those actions, you have positive or negative karma, which will influence your next life. This will repeat until you reach Samsara, your perfected form. Then you move on to Pharasma's Court."
  • Kugaptee's profile. Eliza summarizes from the Chthonian sections. Anathema: perform a burial ritual meant to provide peace to the dead, remove a curse from anyone other than yourself. Edicts: curse living creatures, defile graveyards and pastoral natural regions. Areas of concern: curses, defiled graves, haunted wildlands, seasonal disasters. The implication: Kugaptee forcibly pulls spirits out of the cycle, and what gets pulled becomes something else entirely — ghosts, thatchlings, phantoms, wraiths, shadows, or spectres.
