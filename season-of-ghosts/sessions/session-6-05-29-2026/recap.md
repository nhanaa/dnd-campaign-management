## Session Summary

Session 6 closes Book One. The party — now five PCs strong, with two newcomers arriving via Shinzo's late-night recruiting run — completes the noppera-bo disguise infiltration of the lumber camp and walks straight into the BBEG of the arc: **Zoudou the Zealous**, the faceless cult leader the rebels had warned about from the start. The "her." Mo (in the noppera-bo hood from S5) and Ming Bao (bound as Mo's "captives") sell the disguise long enough to be marched into the inner ritual site, where Mugirou — the noppera-bo rebel, named for the first time in this session — drops the pretense and joins them openly. Riddle and Zhao Lishen arrive from the south side of the camp escorted by Shinzo, who hands them off at the treeline with a wordless tip of the hat and the promise *"a great trial lies ahead of you."*

The fight is uglier than S5's clean ambush. Zoudou is mid-ritual on Kageyuri, who is bound at the shrine, with a darkening silhouette behind her — something cosmically wrong, banging against a wall of fog like glass at an aquarium with three-headed necks and many-fisted arms. Zhao opens with a textbook Commander turn (Gather to Me! → flanking → Set-Up Strike for 10 damage and off-guard), the team converges, and the small-fry occultists and grunts go down quickly under Mo's Crushing-rune chains (44-damage Overwhelming Combination crit on the Grunt; Ming Bao's flurry-and-shield-raise dance; Jun-Yeong's two-action Heal-vs-Living to keep Zhao upright). Then Zoudou casts Darkness, drops the Trickster into stealth-bow harassment mode, and conjures a hazard — a wickerwork demigod-effigy that uses her spell slots to cast Noise Blast (24 damage to three party members) and Harm (a 30-foot emanation that, after Jun-Yeong rerolls his fail with a hero point, kills her own Occultist outright and ticks down everyone in the area).

The Hail Mary that kills Zoudou's own ally also signals that she has run out of cards. Riddle communes with the agitated spirits trapped inside the hazard-effigy, singing them quiet for one round (a clean Occultism crit), and the spell-pump goes offline. Mo demoralizes Zoudou to Frightened 1. Ming Bao drops her into single digits. Rather than be captured, Zoudou stabs herself in the heart, screams *"Kugaptee!"* into the void, and the trees behind the fog wall begin to shake. The thing on the other side gets through. It comes out as a 15-foot eyeless wicker-helmed *fiend*, ribcage bulging with red butterflies, and one-shots the Trickster mid-sentence as a demonstration. The party runs.

They are saved by ghosts they didn't know they had. Ming Bao, fleeing the map, feels a hand on his shoulder and a familiar voice — *"I'll take care of this. I always have."* It's **Mingxi**. Erased from everyone's memory but his own, alive enough to wield the Tengu form he died in, alive enough to 1v1 the demigod-aspect long enough for the party to clear the treeline. The party runs without looking back. Ming Bao sits down in the road later and refuses to talk for a while.

The downtime is heavy. Yami the temple cat finally drops a name — *"That Kugaptee finally showed his face... he is a demigod"* — and confirms that what they fought at the shrine was only one *aspect* of Kugaptee. Jun-Yeong reports a new nightmare he didn't used to have: Mingxi at Tan Sugi monastery, the sky a five-fingered claw, the world flickering between intact and ruined. **Everyone at the table dreams the same dream that night**, even though they don't realize it until morning. Riddle's apparitions panic the first time he tries to scry on Kugaptee and beg him not to speak the name aloud. The party loops back to the lumber camp the next day to recover Zoudou's body and find a tattered ledger half-written in Aklo and half in Chthonian. Eliza translates the Chthonian half — Zoudou had been hunting *reincarnated souls* specifically, and the ledger names **Kageyuri, Ming Bao, and Jun-Yeong** as targets her spies were watching. The party then takes 60 days of in-fiction downtime to keep Willowshore alive through the harvest. Book Two opens with Toilday, Rova 1st, and "a lot of spiders" on the landing page.
