# World — Season of Ghosts (Willowshore, Shenmen)

> Spoiler-safe. Built from the Player's Guide + published Tian Xia setting lore only. AndeGM asked everyone NOT to look up the AP itself.

**Setting**: Willowshore, a remote lumber town of ~225 people in the rural nation of **Shenmen**, on the continent of **Tian Xia**.
**Year**: **7108 IC** (Imperial Calendar). The Age of Lost Omens began two years prior, in **7106 IC** (= 4606 AR — Aroden's death and the global storms). This is set ~116 years before the standard Inner Sea AP timeline.
**Calendar note**: SoG uses the Imperial Calendar (IC). Offset to Absalom Reckoning is −2500 (7106 IC = 4606 AR).

---

## The premise

A **horror campaign** where the PCs are **hometown heroes** defending Willowshore from a curse and supernatural threats, motivated by compassion for their neighbors (not gold), while bracing for a hard winter. Runs **levels 1–11** across four seasonal parts (summer → fall → winter → spring).

**"Season of Ghosts"** is an in-world thing: a stretch in **early summer** when Shenmen's woodlands see a surge of hauntings, spirits, and undead — ghosts that lure people to their deaths.

**The opening — the Reenactment Festival**: each year the town ceremonially "abducts" a handful of townsfolk to spend a night in a forest clearing on the edge of town; at the first dawn of summer they're "ransomed" with breakfast by that year's festival leader, **Choe Chun-hu** (the millers' leader). The PCs are this year's abductees. The campaign begins when they wake on the first morning of summer and something is wrong. *(Details past this point = spoilers.)*

## History

- **7020 IC** — the **Tan Sugi Monastery** is founded by the Sangpotshi monk **Zhi Hui**, built around an ancient sugi tree said to hold mystic properties, deep in unsettled Specterwood. **Willowshore is founded to support it**; the trail between them becomes the **Pilgrim's Path**.
- **7054 IC** — Zhi Hui dies; monks drift away; Willowshore shifts to lumber.
- **~7060–7062 IC** — the monastery is abandoned. When woodcutters try to harvest its ancient sugi trees, they return as **undead** ("the **Night of Broken Blades**"); dozens of townsfolk die. The ruined monastery is shunned as cursed/haunted ever since.
- Later — Shenmen enlists Willowshore for **Lung Wa's lumber ambitions**: bridges, a dam, exploitative lumber bosses, and the town used as a "getaway" for Lung Wa aristocrats. Imperial soldiers stationed there.
- **7106 IC** — Lung Wa collapses (Aroden's death). The lumber lords flee with their wealth; most soldiers leave or desert. Willowshore's remoteness spares it the banditry and chaos that grip the rest of Shenmen.

### Imperial Lung Wa (background — for Zhen's father)
The third great empire of the Tian-Shu, founded **6642 IC** by emperor Wuo Wu Zheng, fallen **7106 IC** (~464 years). By its late period it was a mighty-looking colossus **rotting from within**: a bloated, arbitrary aristocracy, deep corruption, open contempt for non-humans (tengu especially), and in its final decades it created the **Jinyiwei** secret police to hunt internal corruption. **Shenmen** under the empire was a neglected backwater — "a dumping ground for politicians and bureaucrats who had broken the law or shamed themselves," barely governed. *(After the fall it became monster-country under the jorogumo — but that's the present, post-collapse state.)*

## Willowshore

- **Geography** (Player's Guide): Willowshore sits on the banks of the **Ceiba River**, near its confluence with its distributary the **Duyue River** to the east (rendered "Duyu" in the S4 notes), with **Dragonfly Creek** to the south.
- **Government**: appointed governor **Heh Shan-Bao**.
- **Two factions** (reputation tracked in play):
  - **Southbank** — old settlers, traditionalists, self-sufficient, anti-jorogumo. *(Zhen's lean.)*
  - **Northridge** — newer, pragmatic, growth-minded, some open to jorogumo rule.
- **The Eight Practices** — local anti-ghost superstitions for the season: (1) don't call a ghost a ghost; (2) don't pat people on the head/shoulders; (3) avoid water when a ghost is near; (4) don't eat food with two stick-like objects in it; (5) don't lean on walls by day; (6) don't whistle at night; (7) don't leave laundry out at night; (8) if someone calls your name from behind at night, don't turn around.

### Faiths (shrines in town)
**Daikitsu** (nine-tailed fox; agriculture/fortune) and **Pharasma** (souls/death) have temples; **Abadar**, **Lady Nanbyo**, and **Sangpotshi** (the reincarnation philosophy of the old monastery) are also honored; many small/forgotten shrines besides. *(Zhen privately reveres **Tsukiyo**, Prince of the Moon and patron of samsarans, keeps Pharasma's death-rites, and walks the Sangpotshi path.)*

### Key NPCs (Player's Guide)
- **"Granny" Hu Ban-niang** — retired imperial guard captain (LN).
- **"Old" Matsuki Shou** — retired carpenter, the town's living history, Southbank spokesman (NG).
- **Heh Shan-Bao** — appointed governor (LN).
- **You So-Jin** — elf herbalist, keeper of **Graveside Manners** (the cemetery); former Tan Sugi pilgrim. *(Zhen's NPC tie.)*
- **Choe Chung-hu** — millers' leader (Milling Houses), beloved; this year's festival ransomer. *(Zhen's friend.)* *(Player's Guide gazetteer spelling; the intro text also renders it "Chun-hu".)* **Survived the occupation trapped in his own house (S4) — he never abandoned the abductees.**
- **Ba-Ming Ouh** — priest of Daikitsu, Nine Ear Shrine.
- **Elizeth Candora** — cleric of Pharasma, the Lady of Souls cathedral.
- **Yong Wu-Xiu** — kitsune blacksmith, Eternal Blaze Ironworks.
- **Mother Otis** — half-elf innkeeper, the Thrice-Blessed Inn.
- **Igawa Jubei** — resident wizard/scholar, Mother's Coil.

### Notable locations
Eternal Blaze Ironworks · Willowshore Stables · Abadar Shrine · **Graveside Manners** (cemetery + herbalist) · Silvermist Lodges (ranger halls) · Matsuki Estate · Thrice-Blessed Inn · Milling Houses · **Nine Ear Shrine** (Daikitsu, the giant stone nine-tailed fox) · **Lady of Souls** (Pharasma cathedral) · the **Eternal Lantern** · the ruined **Tan Sugi Monastery** (cursed, up the Pilgrim's Path).

- **Refuge:** the lime-warded **Matsuki Estate** (Old Matsuki Shou) now shelters much of South Willowshore; others have gathered at the **Thrice-Blessed Inn** (Mother Otis).
