# Tips & Tactics — Zhen Stillness

Caster-first divine gish-healer. Reach Nodachi, two apparition modes, Druid-Storm utility. **Not a tank** — the party has two (Kineticist, Monk). Your job is **glue**: heal, buff, contribute melee, and be the eyes.

---

## The core discipline: a caster who closes, not a martial who casts

The gish trap is playing a half-martial like a full martial — swinging at lower-than-martial proficiency while your spells rot. **Avoid it.** Your weapon is *an option on the menu*, not your identity.

- **Swing** when: single target in reach, you've set up off-guard, or your Reactive Strike is live. The Nodachi is the *closer*.
- **Cast** when: you can hit multiple foes, control the field, **heal**, or **buff**. That's where you do what the martials can't.
- Healing is frequently your **highest-value action** — a round saving Mu-Jin (HP 14) or the Magus is *the* round, not an "off" one.

## Your two modes (toggle by apparition)

- **Battle mode** (Witness to Ancient Battles primary): cast **Embodiment of Battle** → +status atk/dmg, **Reactive Strike**, martial-weapon prof. Then swing + Sustain. Use **Ghostly Weapon** to hit incorporeal ghosts.
- **Healer/caster mode** (Custodian of Groves and Gardens, or just slots): **Garden of Healing** out of combat, **Heal** in combat, control/save spells at full DC.
- You don't have to live in Embodiment — flip it on for grindy/boss fights; skip it on protect-and-heal rounds (frees the Sustain action).

## The one DC rule that matters

Your spell DC is **expert from L7** and applies to *everything*, divine and primal (Electric Arc, Tempest Surge included). The **only** thing that drops it is **Embodiment of Battle's −2**, and it's a toggle:

| Mode | Cast these | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Embodiment ON** (−2 DC) | Buffs & heals — Bless, **Heroism**, Sure Strike, **Heal**, Garden of Healing (no DC needed) | Save-or-suck (DC gimped) |
| **Embodiment OFF** (full DC) | **Save/control spells at full strength** — Tempest Surge, fear, debuffs, blasts | — |

So: in battle mode, off-rounds are buffs/heals; in caster mode, off-rounds are full-DC control.

## Embodiment of Battle — the Sustain truth

It's **Sustained** (1 action/round). That action would otherwise be a junk −10 third Strike, and Embodiment converts it into a full-bonus **Reactive Strike** + persistent buff — so with your **10-ft reach** maximizing reaction triggers, it's usually action-*positive*. At **L9 (Liturgist Dancing Invocation)** the Sustain folds into a Step/Leap for free.

## Crit-fishing engine

- **Reach (10 ft)** → flank from 10 ft (off-guard while staying safe) + many Reactive Strikes.
- **Flank partners**: Monk + melee Kineticist (the Magus is a darting Spellstriker, opportunistic flanker).
- **Deadly d12** Nodachi → big crits; **Sure Strike** (roll twice) on a flanked target to force one.
- **Grudge Strike (L6)**: +2 *circumstance* to hit (stacks with Embodiment's status) + **+2d6 vitality vs undead** — and vitality is *energy*, so it lands full on incorporeal ghosts. Your anti-ghost nova.

## The Oil of Potency button

One-shot, +1 striking (2d8, +1 to hit) for 1 minute. **Apply round 1 of a boss fight** (1 action) and it covers the whole fight — don't waste a whole turn cramming apply + Sure Strike + Strike. Save it for "this fight matters."

## Healing economy (table house rules help you)

- **Refocus is unrestricted** (10 min/point between fights) — re-arm Embodiment / swap to Garden of Healing freely.
- **Long rests heal to full** — so out-of-combat healing is gravy, not load-bearing.
- **Battle Medicine** allowed even two-handing the Nodachi (DM ruling) — your in-combat backup when out of Heal slots. Buy a healer's toolkit.

## Table-awareness duties

- **You are the Perception anchor** (+7 vs the party's +3–4). Your initiative and Seeks carry the horror-genre awareness everyone else lacks. Lead the watch.
- **You own Religion + Nature RK** — but spirit/undead RK is shared with Mu-Jin (Occultism). Coordinate so nobody crit-fails (DM lies on a crit-fail RK).
- **Don't crit-fail Recall Knowledge** — the DM seeds false info into the bestiary tracker on a crit-fail.
- **Heroes' tempo**: once you have **Haste (L8)**, dropping it on the party each big fight is your single highest-impact play — it makes the un-optimized builds shine.

## Social posture (you, the player)

New 5th in a tight, mid-experience group. Be the helpful elder, not the "well-actually" guy. Buff their builds, don't critique them. Offer rules help when asked. Mirrors Zhen in the fiction — the patient old hand.
