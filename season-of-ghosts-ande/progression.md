# Progression — Zhen Stillness (Animist / Druid)

**Concept**: Samsaran (Hungerseed) Animist (Liturgist) — caster-first gish-healer with a reach Nodachi. Free Archetype = **Druid (Storm order)**, taken for the **primal spells divine can't give** (Haste above all). Campaign runs **L1–11**.

**Attributes** (Gradual Boosts): STR 18 · DEX 12 · CON 16 · INT 10 · WIS 18→**19 (L5)** · CHA 10.

---

## Free Archetype — the Druid (Storm) plan

The whole FA is built to reach **Haste** (3rd-rank, primal/occult/arcane — NOT divine, so only obtainable this way). Self-Haste = +1 action/round, which pays for the Embodiment-of-Battle Sustain; or buff the Monk/Magus for party tempo.

| FA level | Feat | Gets |
|---|---|---|
| **L2** | Druid Dedication (**Storm** order) | 2 primal cantrips — **Electric Arc** + one more |
| **L4** | Order Spell | **Tempest Surge** focus spell |
| **L6** | Basic Druid Spellcasting | 1st + 2nd-rank primal slots |
| **L8** | Druid Spellcasting Breadth | **3rd-rank slot → Haste online** |
| **L10** | Breadth / Animal Companion / etc. | more slots, or a flank-pet |

> DC note: spell DC is **one shared statistic** (use highest proficiency). Zhen is **expert at L7**, and that applies to the Druid primal spells too (same WIS attribute) — so Electric Arc / Tempest Surge cast at full expert DC. The *only* DC penalty is **Embodiment of Battle's −2**, a toggle (see `tips.md`).

## Gear priorities
1. **Handwraps of Mighty Blows** — not needed (no unarmed-strike build); skip.
2. **Nodachi runes** — **potency then striking** are the #1 money sink. A +1 striking Nodachi (2d8) is your accuracy/damage backbone. (The L1 **Oil of Potency** heirloom is a one-shot stand-in for a striking rune — save it for a boss.)
3. **Armor potency / resilient** on the breastplate as gold allows (AC + saves scale with worn armor — don't go unarmored).
4. **Healer's toolkit** — cheap, enables Battle Medicine.

## Apparition strategy
- **Witness to Ancient Battles** (war) = primary on fight-heavy days → Embodiment of Battle + Ghostly Weapon (hits incorporeal ghosts — your anti-undead answer).
- **Custodian of Groves and Gardens** (life) = primary on healing-heavy days, or swap to it on Refocus → Garden of Healing for between-fight top-offs.
- Liturgist lets you Refocus to change primary mid-day; pick per encounter.
