## Session Summary

The five abductees of this year's Reenactment Festival wake at dawn in a forest clearing — but on the *east* side of Willowshore, not the west where they were carried, and no one has come to ransom them with breakfast. Uncle Choe Chung-hu, the miller who leads the festival, never arrived. As they free themselves from the straw mats and start home down a game trail, two giant centipedes attack — and the insects *speak*, hissing "meat, flesh, bones" as they bite, before Iryoku burns one in half and Kaelen flattens the other. Recall Knowledge only deepens the wrong: these are ordinary giant centipedes, yet they have a human's intelligence and were speaking plain Tian. Something is influencing the animals.

The walk home turns the morning into a horror story. Fog rolls in unnaturally fast; an old nursery rhyme surfaces in Kaoru's memory about a lazy lamplighter, a blood moon, and the ghosts that come when the lamp goes dark. At the Spider Gate, the town's beloved stone guardian "Ugly Cute" is simply *gone* — its lantern broken on the ground, deep furrows showing the horse-sized statue walked off east on its own. At the Eastern Watchtower they find four corpses: two town guards dead of viper venom, and two jinkin (blue-grey gremlin-fey) cut down by the guards' blades. A surviving guard, frightened in her tower, recounts that the jinkin came from the south after midnight with trained serpents. Then night falls early, the moon turns blood-red, and rain of real blood coats everything before reverting to water — the party only shrugs it off thanks to a generous hand of hero points.

After gathering cold iron, ghost charges, and gear from their homes, the party reaches Dawnstep Bridge and finds the worst confirmed: the **Eternal Lantern is dark**, and a flesh-eating **Buso** lounges on a throne of stolen furniture, swilling stolen liquor while jinkin caper around it. The party opens with buffs — Mu-Jin's Forbidding Ward and familiar trick, Zhen finally drawing the wrapped nodachi he has carried unsheathed for fifty years — and the fight is over almost before it begins: the jinkin flee, and a crit from Kaelen's Inner Upheaval drops the drunken Buso in the first round. The loot includes the three ancient copper coins whose blessing kept the Lantern lit — blessing now stripped away. The party shelters the night at the lime-warded Matsuki Estate and, by the next foggy dawn, has a plan: tour three shrines, re-bless the coins, and relight the Lantern before whatever has taken downtown comes for the rest of Willowshore.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Clearing (East of Willowshore)
- The party wakes blindfolded in straw mats in a clearing **east** of town — the wrong side; they were carried west the night before. The sun is up and **Choe Chung-hu has not come with the ransom breakfast**, which is deeply abnormal.
- They free themselves easily (no check needed). **Mu-Jin casts Know the Way** to find the game trail south toward Willowshore — silver thread spooling from his fingers as he stitches the spell into the air.
- Old Zhen, who has lived in Willowshore his whole life, frets that the festival organizers have never botched this before.

### Act II — The Talking Centipedes (Combat)
- Two **giant centipedes** burst from the trees and attack. As they bite, their hissing forms *words*: "Meat. Flesh. Bones. Fingers. Hair. Skin. Eyes." They demand a Fortitude save from those they reach.
- Quick, decisive fight (see Combat). **Iryoku crit-burned one in half; Kaelen's Flurry of Blows finished the other.** Iryoku took 3 damage, Kaoru 1; no one was poisoned.
- **Recall Knowledge (Mu-Jin, Occultism):** ordinary giant centipedes — but with **+0 INT (average human intelligence)** and able to **speak Common/Tian**, both wildly abnormal for vermin that normally flee daylight. Working theory (Mu-Jin, with Zhen and Kaoru): the animals are **possessed or influenced by evil spirits**, perhaps beguiled by fey.
- **Zhen casts Garden of Healing** out of combat to top everyone off (apologizing that he forgot his healer's toolkit — "old age").
- The party does above-table **character introductions** on the walk back (see Spotlights).

### Act III — The Road Home Turns Wrong
- Ten minutes out of the clearing, an unnatural **fog** floods the trees. Mu-Jin re-casts Know the Way to keep their bearing as they cross the **Douyu River** onto the Riverside Road into town.
- A childhood **nursery rhyme surfaces in Kaoru's memory**: *"Lazy Ado took a break and did not light the lamp that day… Hazy mist and moon of blood, all because he just gave up."* — implying the **Eternal Lantern may have gone out.**
- **Detect Magic** (Mu-Jin, as exploration activity) lights up the whole area — "lit up like a Christmas tree" (Iryoku). Magic is everywhere.

### Act IV — The Spider Gate & the Watchtower (Investigation)
- At the **Spider Gate**, the horse-sized stone guardian **"Ugly Cute" is missing.** Its lantern lies broken on the ground (no oil). No chisel marks, but **large furrows** show the statue **animated and walked east**. Mu-Jin is despondent; a Survival check reads the tracks.
- At the **Eastern Watchtower** (~1 mile west): the smell of blood and **four corpses** — two town guards (legs swollen, dead of poison) and **two jinkin** (tiny blue-grey gremlin-fey, sharp teeth, big ears) slain by sword. Two dead **vipers** nearby.
  - **RK (Zhen):** the humanoids are **jinkin** — fey/gremlin, **weak to cold iron**. A second RK on the party yields nothing more about them.
  - **Medicine/RK (Zhen):** the guards **died of viper venom**; the vipers are ordinary snakes. Reconstruction: the two guards killed all four attackers but succumbed to the snakebites.
  - Theory firms up: **Iryoku** seeds it — *"something is influencing the animals to attack"* — and **Mu-Jin** develops the mechanism: **jinkin are fey, known for enchantment**, so they likely **beguiled the animals** to strike at Willowshore. Possibly the front of a larger invasion.
- A surviving guard (the table nicknames her **"Ha-hire"** — unnamed in canon) hails them, frightened, from the tower.
  - **Eight Practices challenge:** each PC names one of the town's anti-ghost tenets; collectively they pass.
  - Her account: Choe Chung-hu's crew carried the actors **west**; the crew returned **without them**; after midnight, **jinkin came from the south with trained serpents**; the other two guards killed them but died to the venom. Screams and roars echoed from town all day, then silence at dawn. She's staying in the tower; she worries for her **daughter** (with Granny Hu) and **husband** (at the Matsuki Estate).
  - **Comedy beat:** Zhen moves to comfort her with a pat on the shoulder — directly violating Eight Practice #2 (*don't pat people*). The table catches it and rolls it back (the old elder would *never*). She gives the party supplies (flares/oil, a ladder, a utility item).

### Act V — Blood Moon
- Night falls early (the DM runs time at 1 real-second = 1 in-game-minute). The fog clears into a **blood-red moon**, and **rain of actual blood** coats everything for about a minute before reverting to plain water.
- Everyone makes the Will save (hero points + the table's "under-10-becomes-10" heroic-reroll rule make it a clean sweep — no one is affected). Zhen confirms by touch that the blood was **real, not illusion.**
- The DM hands out a hero point and officially welcomes the table to *Season of Ghosts* over the official soundtrack. **(Break.)**

### Act VI — The Eternal Lantern & the Buso (Combat)
- After looting cold iron (Mu-Jin's heirloom, for the jinkin weakness), **ghost charges** (handed to Kaoru, who has the proficiency), an alarm snare and silver (Iryoku), and healing potions + a holy symbol (Kaelen), the party reaches **Dawnstep Bridge.**
- The **Eternal Lantern** — an immobile 5-foot stone lamp — is **dark.** A large **Buso** (a flesh-eating humanoid) sits on a **throne of stolen furniture**, drinking from a brewing pot, while jinkin entertain and taunt it. Blood smears mark a recent fight.
  - **RK (party):** Buso — humanoid, "a simmering desire to consume the flesh of others," **AC 18, no weaknesses.**
- Buffs, then the fight (see Combat). The **jinkin flee** the instant combat starts; only the (drunk, sickened) Buso fights, and it falls in the first round. **Kaelen's Inner Upheaval crit lands the kill.**
- **Loot from the throne:** a kukri + studded leather (Iryoku), a silver scepter w/ stones (15 gp), an elegant silver jewelry case (5 gp), 3 copper, and — crucially — **three ancient copper coins.** Detect Magic confirms the coins' blessing is **gone**; these are the very coins that kept the Eternal Lantern lit. Iryoku's Ignition can't relight the Lantern — the block is magical, not fuel.
- **The quest:** carry each blessed copper coin to a **shrine** and use the **Invoke Shrine Blessing** exploration activity (Religion / deity-lore / associated skill check, easy DC) to re-bless it. Success heals 1d8+8 and grants Guidance 1/24h; **crit-fail destroys the coin and stupefies you.**

### Act VII — Refuge & the Plan
- The party hustles to the **Matsuki Estate** (lime-warded walls, the town's safest building). **Old Matsuki Shou** admits them after another Eight Practices challenge. South Willowshore's townsfolk are sheltering here; others are at the Thrice-Blessed Inn with Mother Otis. Ha-hire's husband is confirmed safe here.
- They rest the night. **Reward: +1 reputation with both Northridge and South Bank** for clearing Dawnstep Bridge.
- Next foggy dawn, they recount everything to Old Matsuki and set the day's plan: bless the three coins at three shrines (Abadar next door → a shrine at Mother's Coil → the Kofusachi shrine at the Trade Office, where Granny Hu is), then relight the Lantern. Matsuki warns: **light the Lantern before touching downtown** (north of the river), which is in shambles and "particularly infested with monsters."

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Blade Still Wrapped
The session's quiet emotional weight belongs to Old Zhen. For most of it he is exactly who the town thinks he is — the gentle, unhurried elder-healer who calls grown adventurers "you children," frets that the festival organizers were careless, and works the party's investigation with patience: it's Zhen who identifies the **jinkin** and their cold-iron weakness, reads the guards' **viper-venom** deaths off a Medicine check, and quietly **Garden-of-Healings** everyone back to full while apologizing that he forgot his toolkit. His Animist self-image lands perfectly in one line to the dead-eyed guard — *"I do have a lot of spirits around me, but none of them is evil, so I wouldn't know"* — kind, a little uncanny, exactly the man on the character sheet. His one near-misstep (going to pat a frightened woman on the shoulder, a direct Eight-Practices taboo) is corrected in-fiction precisely *because* Zhen of all people would know better. And then, at the Eternal Lantern, the beat the whole character is built around: facing the Buso, **Zhen draws the wrapped nodachi for the first time** — fifty years bound in cloth and prayer paper — strides in on his reach, and lands the blade. He closed the night, OOC, asking the DM to make combat *harder.*

### Iryoku-Yasha · the Town That Walks
Iryoku is the deepest root in Willowshore and plays it that way: an ancient **Yaksha nature spirit** who has *"been in the town since its founding,"* who manifested to protect the people who came to trade with the monks, who quietly resents the town's slide toward something "larger and more civilized," and who wanders the streets helping whoever he finds. He's also the party's reliable damage and its driest voice — opening the Buso fight with a flat *"Well, I say we fight them, guys,"* calling the detect-magic chaos *"lit up like a Christmas tree,"* and announcing **looting** as *"my favorite part of this game"* a half-second after the boss hits the dirt. Mechanically he carried the centipede fight: **Flying Flame** across both bugs, then a **weapon-infusion fire blast crit** that cut one *"like a fire halberd, straight through the body."* He's the party Face by build, and the one who quietly handles the bookkeeping (reputation tracking, who can use which martial gear).

### Mu-Jin · the One Who Reads the Threads
If anyone "ran" this session it was Mu-Jin — the lifelong Willowshorer who actually knows the town, its NPCs, its shrines, and its monsters, and who drove nearly every investigative beat. His **Spinner of Threads** witchcraft gets the session's most striking image: *"silver thread comes from his fingers, making a symbol, as if he's stitching the very magic into the air."* He's the knowledge engine — the **Occultism RK** that cracks the talking-centipede mystery (*"even from 50 feet away, I very clearly heard them speaking Tian"*), the **Detect Magic** that exposes the saturated battlefield, the theory-builder who lands on **possession/enchantment by fey**, and the planner who turns a pile of clues into an actual to-do list (*"a tour of the village — a blessing for each coin"*). His RP is rich, too: a whole backstory (elven adventurer father **Mubong**, late herbalist mother **Xion**, his spider familiar **Weaver**, mentors **You So-Jin** and the doctor **Dami**) and small grace notes like stroking Weaver's head as he reasons aloud.

### Kaoru · the Boy with the Note
Kaoru is the quiet one and almost vanished into the recording — but he's clearly the party's strange, soft-hearted heart. His introduction is the session's saddest thread: a kitsune of about 21, grown up in Willowshore making mischief, whose **mother disappeared when he was ~15 and father when he was ~17**, and who now spends his days in libraries **trying to decipher the note his mother left behind.** That bookish, town-rooted childhood pays off when the fog rolls in and it's *Kaoru* who dredges up the old local **nursery rhyme** — *"Lazy Ado took a break and did not light the lamp that day…"* — the clue that first tells the party the Eternal Lantern may have gone dark. His tenderness shows when he greets the (now-missing) stone guardian like an old friend — *"Hey there, little guy!"* — a half-beat before the horror of its absence lands. He's got a genre-savvy streak too (calling the blindfolded-abductee opening *"that one intro to Skyrim,"* floating that the centipedes are *"genetically modified… or brought back from the dead"*), and the party's cleanest mechanical signature: stepping up to the Buso and calling *"Actually, I'm going to Spellstrike."*

### Kaelen Ashford · the Open Door
Kaelen is the outsider who chose to belong. A clean-shaven, bald **Human Monk**, he came to Willowshore on **pilgrimage** to honor the Sangpotshi faith at the temple up the Pilgrim's Path — and then, with no real stake in the place, volunteered for the town's festival and threw in with strangers to defend it. That open-handedness is his throughline: when the exhausted party needs somewhere to weather the night, it's Kaelen who offers up his own (tiny) shack without a second thought — *"No, it'll be fine."* His comic timing is sneaky-good, the dry newcomer's-eye view of a town's weird rituals: deadpanning *"I didn't realize we were being kidnapped"* mid-combat, and answering "whose house do we crash at?" with a wordless *"I look at the magus."* He also carried real weight in both fights despite battling the worst laptop in the room (the good-natured "this laptop is smoking some crack" saga) — landing two of the night's biggest hits: the **Flurry of Blows that finishes the second centipede**, and the **Inner Upheaval Ki-spell crit that kills the Buso** — *"First crit of the first one!"*

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Talking Centipedes (game trail, east of town)
**Enemies:** 2× giant centipede (AC 15, ~11 HP each, animal trait; abnormally INT +0 and speaking Common/Tian)
**Setup:** Ambush on the trail. Initiative run with the party's scout bonus (Iryoku's *Unwavering Guide*).

**Round 1:**
- **Zhen:** casts **Needle Darts** at a centipede — **misses** (a 15 into AC 15, foiled by the target's lesser cover) — then **casts Shield.**
- **Iryoku:** makes a melee **Strike** (hit, minimum damage), then **casts Shield.**
- The party maneuvers; a **centipede** moves to **Iryoku**, bites, and demands a **Fortitude save** (Iryoku saves but takes **3 damage**, Barely Injured). Its hisses form words.

**Round 2:**
- **Iryoku:** **Flying Flame** (catching one centipede; 1d6 fire, basic Reflex), then **weapon-infusion → fire Elemental Blast → crit** — *"like a fire halberd, straight through the body, cut in half."* **Kill #1.**
- The **surviving centipede** bites again; **Kaoru takes 1 damage** (Barely Injured). No one is poisoned.
- **Kaelen:** strides into reach and uses **Flurry of Blows** (fist, +7 / MAP −4); both connect thanks to **off-guard**. **Kill #2.**

**Result:** Clean win over ~2 rounds, modest chip damage (Iryoku 3, Kaoru 1). Mystery > danger — the real payload was the RK reveal that these "animals" think and talk.

### Encounter 2 — The Buso at the Eternal Lantern (Dawnstep Bridge)
**Enemies:** 1× **Buso** (flesh-eating humanoid; **AC 18, no weaknesses**; entered combat **sickened/drunk**) + several **jinkin** (who immediately fled)
**Setup (pre-combat buffs):** Mu-Jin casts **Nudge Fate** (on Kaelen) and uses his familiar **Weaver** to raise **Iryoku's AC +1**; **Zhen casts Embodiment of Battle** and **draws the nodachi for the first time.**

- **Combat opens — the jinkin flee** the moment initiative starts; only the Buso fights.
- **Zhen:** strides in (reach), **strikes the Buso with the nodachi**, then **casts Shield.** With Embodiment of Battle's reach + Reactive Strike online, he also gets a **reach reactive strike (11 damage)** as the enemy repositions (the table was unsure whether the target was the Buso or a fleeing jinkin; the DM waved it through).
- **Buso:** sickened/drunk — spends an action **retching** (reducing its actions), then tries to **Demoralize Iryoku** in Fey (Iryoku's Will is low, but it doesn't matter — the fight ends fast).
- **Iryoku:** strides, **weapon-infusion → fire Elemental Blast** (hero point), big damage; drops his whip to keep a light up.
- **Mu-Jin:** moves beside Kaoru and casts **Forbidding Ward** (ally **Kaoru** / enemy Buso) — +1 AC & saves vs the Buso.
- **Kaoru:** *"Actually, I'm going to Spellstrike."* — channels a save-spell through the blade; the Buso makes the save (sparking a friendly rules debate about Spellstrike + save spells).
- **Kaelen:** moves in and uses his Ki spell **Inner Upheaval** — **crit** — *"First crit of the first one!"* — for big spirit damage. **Kill — the Buso drops in round 1.**

**Result:** A one-round rout. **No party damage taken.** The fight's significance was the loot (the three blessing-stripped copper coins) and the confirmation that the Eternal Lantern is out.

**XP:** 32 XP each for the session.
