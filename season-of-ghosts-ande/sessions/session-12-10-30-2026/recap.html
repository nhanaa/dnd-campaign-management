Session Summary

The session picked up the instant the last one ended. The burning caretakers were dead, night had fallen at the Garden Shrine, and Yeri, the girl the party had found weeping in a tree, had just convulsed and let her head tear free of her body. She was an elite penanggalan: a floating head trailing its own lungs and entrails. Mu-Jin, giving the recap, admitted he’d thought the lungs were “a flower thing at first.” Iryoku knew what she was: “a famous, like, vampire equivalent.” She went straight for Kaoru. He couldn’t fully see the near-invisible tongue. Two critical tongue-lashes for 58 dropped him before anyone else had acted, though he hero-pointed the bile save so the drain didn’t take. Zhen’s two-action Heal brought him back. Iryoku and Zhen cut into her slashing weakness, and Zhen’s nodachi wound sprayed bile on Iryoku and Kaelen beside her. Her entrails and tongue missed Kaelen three times (“Ooh, sorry, it’s just a flesh wound”), and Kaelen’s Flurry of Blows finished her in round two. She was wearing a bracelet stamped with the wheel of Sangpotshi, a charm of mental resistance, and the GM offered it to the party’s monk of that faith.

The party stayed at the Garden Shrine that night. The blue orchids under the burned oak turned out to be healing vapors. Mu-Jin wouldn’t light the stone lanterns until he knew what they were for, and Zhen’s Religion said they burn incense for protection, so Mu-Jin set the found incense in them and Iryoku lit them. Kaoru patched the roof with Engineering Lore so they could sleep. The GM then said the shrine blessings are not temporary: the Bridge Shrine’s +1 to Reflex and this shrine’s +1 to Will last for all of Season of Ghosts.

The next day, in fog on a steep climb, a woman’s voice cried that she had fallen and broken her leg. Zhen had already glimpsed a lantern through the trees, and he had a sense of déjà vu. The voice was a lie. It was a will-o’-wisp, immune to spells, and it ate fifty-six minutes of real time. Zhen found it with a hero-pointed Seek and pointed it out. Mu-Jin’s Revealing Light made it a target. Then the wisp flew thirty feet up a cliff face and Kaelen, Iryoku and Kaoru spent rounds climbing, swapping weapons and missing. Iryoku’s blast did nothing (the GM ruled kinetic impulses blocked too), and Mu-Jin’s “Thanks to Abomination Vaults, I, as the player, know exactly how Will-o’-Wisps work to the point where I’m fucking sick of them” spoke for most of the table. Mu-Jin deliberately drew its attacks with Force Barrage and took 29 for it, and Zhen healed him back to exactly full. In round five Zhen, unable to hit it, rolled a critical Aid instead (“Why don’t I just hit instead?”). Kaelen took that bonus into a natural-20 Flurry, which killed it.

After the break the road ran past dozens of human heads hanging by their hair from the branches, whispering. The heads whispered “We welcome you to our family,” and a floating monk’s skull came at Zhen. Zhen: “I would like to reject your offer. I don’t think I want to be in your family.” The skull answered with an emotion effect in Kugaptee’s name (Foundry: Behold the Promises of Kugaptee). Zhen hero-pointed through it, and when it bit at him: “Is this how you treat your potential family member?” The skull was weak to vitality. Iryoku’s wood blasts, Kaelen’s vitality Inner Upheaval and Zhen’s Vitality Lash took it apart in two rounds, and Iryoku finished it. Afterward the heads chanted “join our voices and become one with Kugaptee,” and Kaoru asked whether he recognized any of them. This was personal for him: his mother vanished on this road. They were too decayed to tell.

At dusk, in a rising thunderstorm, the party reached the third of Zhi Hui’s pilgrim shrines, at a fifty-foot waterfall. They climbed the escarpment rather than trust the rickety bridge. Zhen threw a rope down, Mu-Jin climbed it by Follow the Expert, and Kaoru critically failed the climb anyway. At the top, the Storm Mother came for them: Iogaka, the hag who had tormented the kappa Enko last session, with two spark moths. She opened with a Lightning Bolt for 20 into each of Zhen and Iryoku. Zhen burned both moths with Earth’s Bile, critted one apart with the nodachi (“I’m about to destroy this thing’s whole career”), and finished the other next round. Mu-Jin quickened four of the party with Loose Time’s Arrow. Iryoku critted Iogaka on a natural 20, and Kaelen flurried her for 29. She hit Kaelen with a Hydraulic Torrent and pulled back; Kaelen: “I yell coward.” Mu-Jin killed her with a cold-iron Needle Darts behind Sure Strike, a spell he had never cast before. In her room the party found a thurible of revelation and invigorating soap, then bathed in the shrine’s lake and rested for its blessing. Then the party reached level 6.
