## Session Summary

The thing Session 7 set up paid off in the first fifteen minutes, and it paid off by *not* being a fight. The last unopened corner of the lumber camp held a tent, a cage of captured farmers, and the second name the beaten kappa had coughed up: **Mugirou the Rebel**. They did not attack. They greeted the party telepathically, and the first thing they said was that they were not the one who ordered the abductions — *"Your real vengeance should be towards Zoudou."* The caged farmers had been handed to them as a peace offering, which they understood perfectly well for what it was: *"This is just a way for Zoudou to pass off undesirable trash. I've kept this trash alive long enough."* They offered a deal. Kill every red-robed cultist in the camp and at the ritual site, and they would release the prisoners and let the party walk out. Zhen, who had spent Session 7 doing exactly that, answered them with the driest line of the night — *"I believe the job is already half done"* — and then asked the only question that mattered: *"But how do we guarantee that you don't go back on your words?"* Mu-Jin ran a Sense Motive, got sincerity, and closed it with a promise and a threat in the same breath. Mugirou handed over a **Battle Medic's Baton** as a token of friendship. The GM awarded experience without anyone rolling initiative. The party had turned the camp's civil war into an alliance.

Northwest, at the end of the trail, they found the rest of it. **Zoudou the Zealous** stood on the second step of a three-tiered shrine with **Kum Meng-Sung** bound hand and foot on the top tier, gagged and thrashing, mid-**Consecrate ritual**, in front of a fifty-foot wall of screaming mist. She would not stop for shouting. She offered a trade instead, and Zhen saw it coming and said it out loud before she could — *"What? Replacing him? Is that what you're going to say?"* — *"That is correct. Only one of you."* Zhen's refusal was flat and immediate: ***"No way. No one's going to fall today for your ritual."*** Kaelen drew his bo staff, and Zoudou answered the drawn weapon with the session's best villain line: *"I'm not surprised by your lack of conviction and spiritual cowardice."* Kaelen was unimpressed (*"So you can go at yourself"*), and Zhen told her exactly where to put the offer: *"Why don't you make yourself the target? Go use your own life."* The fight that followed lasted **one round**, and was over four turns into it. Kaoru leapt in on a conflux spell and then Spellstruck, Mu-Jin's Noise Blast found the sonic weakness and Elemental Betrayal opened a fire weakness on Zoudou, and Zhen dropped Earth's Bile across all three and finished with a two-target Electric Arc that **killed Zoudou and one of her occultists in the same action.** Zoudou had rolled a natural 7 for initiative. **She never took a turn in her own boss fight** — and neither did Iryoku, or any enemy at all.

Killing them did not end it. Stopping the ritual did something worse. A shadow reared up beyond the mist and **lunged at the wall from the far side**, bulging it like a solid thing being pushed against — and failed to come through. Then every noppera-bo in the clearing, *living or dead*, burst apart, and three **Kugaptee's Blessings** climbed out of the wreckage: mindless unholy zombie brutes with moldy wicker cages for heads, filled with blood-coloured butterflies. *"It's like every single boss fight in a game,"* said Kaelen. *"It has a second phase."* Seventy hit points each, healed by void, weak to vitality and slashing, and slowed — and they hit like trucks. Over two rounds they shoved Kaelen, shoved Iryoku, shoved Kaoru off the altar, and dropped a 22-point critical into Kaelen that left him at **10 of 47**. Zhen took nothing at all; the one Strike aimed at him was a critical miss. Mu-Jin's free Recall Knowledge named them, then his Needle Darts **critically hit** for the first kill. Zhen took the second — and said why out loud first: *"Since I'm flanking Kaelen on this one and he is in danger, I'm going to try to finish this game."* The nodachi came down for a **critical hit, 44 damage**. Then he turned to Kaelen, at 10 HP with one enemy left, and talked him into the focus spell he'd been sitting on. Kaelen's Inner Upheaval Flurry critted for **57**. Mu-Jin narrated it as Fist of the North Star. Kaelen stepped back and said *"Ew, I got zombie all over me,"* and Kaoru — who is covered in fur — told him to count his blessings.

Mu-Jin walked up past the gore and untied the boy himself. *"Your father was looking for you."* Mugirou kept their word and released the farmers. And then the session did something this campaign has not done before: it stopped being an adventure and became a **town**. Roughly **eighty days of downtime across three months**, and the party spent all of it. Mu-Jin, Kaoru and Kaelen took over the derelict **Cerulean Tea House** and **fully restored it** — a base of operations and a reopened business, finished by Kaoru on an Athletics check he cheerfully admitted he was bad at. Kaelen befriended **Yami**, the grey feral cat still living in it; Mu-Jin announced he intends to be the main tea guy with Weaver as the mascot. Iryoku and Kaelen worked the milling houses. Zhen earned income at the **Mother's Coil**, then with **Religion at the Lady of Souls** — the town's healer paid to tend its dead. They talked the Imperial Guard's **Zhang Peng** out of demanding free arms and armour from the blacksmith and into an IOU instead, and spent five days harvesting fallen peachwood for the Woodcarver's Guild's fulu paper. Both jobs bought reputation, and the party ended the season **liked by Northridge and Southbank both** — which turned out to matter enormously.

Because the last thing summer produced was a succession crisis. With no one governing Willowshore, **Old Man Matsuki publicly challenged Granny Hu** for the leadership, and the two of them agreed to settle it by a champions' duel in seven days. The town read that correctly as a coming bloodbath. The party was invited by both sides — the people who relit the Eternal Lantern are worth a great deal to whoever can claim them — and they refused to pick. They put themselves forward as a collective third candidate, and then went further: not one of them as governor, but **all five, plus both elders, as a council.** *"I think we're the guardians of this village,"* Mu-Jin said, *"but are any of us leadership material?"* Zhen: *"I like the sound of that."* It required seven influence points with each elder. They got wine into Matsuki and folk tales at him — Iryoku, an ancient yaksha, cheerfully noted he was exempt from the old man's contempt for the younger generation because *"I'm older than him"* — and worked Granny Hu's fear of a famine. **They finished in two of the seven days.** No duel. A governing council, two reputation points with each faction, and eighty experience.

Then the milestone landed. **Level 4. Book One is over.**

## Key Events

**Act I — Mugirou's Truce (day 11, the lumber camp's north end)**

- Mu-Jin opened with the session recap, earning the table its first hero points: the interrogation that gave nothing, the kappa on the island, the stable master, and *"some friction among the noppera-bo, between Zoudou and Mugirou."*
- Zhen investigated the camp's outhouses and **critically failed a DC 18 Fortitude save on a natural 1** against the stench, taking **enfeebled 1 for ten minutes** off it. His public-service announcement afterward: *"This is a reminder, kids, don't go to any weird toilet."*
- The last unopened corner held a tent, **four captured farmers in a cage**, and a noppera-bo who did not attack. The farmers called out for help on seeing the party, which alerted the guard.
- They opened telepathically — *"You must be here for the farmers"* — and immediately distanced themselves: *"Keep in mind that I was not the one who orchestrated the abductions."* Mu-Jin asked them straight out: *"Would you perhaps be Zoudou or Mugirou?"* — **"Mugirou."** *"Your real vengeance should be towards Zoudou."*
- **The farmers were never a peace offering.** *"I had meant them to be a peace offering. But… there's no peace offering. This is just a way for Zoudou to pass off undesirable trash."*
- They pointed the party northwest to the ritual site, and confirmed the sacrifice was not their department: *"It's not my place. That's the prayers' job."*
- **The deal**: eliminate all the cultists in the camp and at the ritual site → *"I will release the prisoners. And you may leave the camp unharmed."* Zhen: *"I believe the job is already half done… and we will finish it either way. But how do we guarantee that you don't go back on your words?"* Mugirou: *"I've kept this trash alive long enough."*
- **Mu-Jin ran a Sense Motive** — offering a hero point if he needed one, which he didn't — and the GM's answer was *"Yeah, he seems to be sincere."* Mu-Jin turned and relayed it to the party (*"He seems to be earnest in his words. Would you all agree?"*), then sealed the deal with the threat: *"If you play false with us, if any harm comes to the villagers, we will find you."*
- Mugirou gave a **token of friendship**: a **Battle Medic's Baton**. When somebody offered to approach the tent alone, Zhen shut it down: *"We'll follow you. We don't want you to be alone."*
- **The GM awarded XP and explicitly did not roll initiative** — *"I said no need to roll initiative or anything."* A named enemy leader resolved entirely through negotiation.

**Act II — The Ritual Site (northwest, at the end of the trail)**

- *"The forest trail suddenly reaches a clearing… the clearing's northwestern side is obscured by a seething wall of mist that reaches fifty feet into the sky. Now and then, what appear to be silently screaming ghosts thrash and writhe through this mist."* A three-tiered stone shrine stood near the wall, and a dome-shaped cage of bent branches to the east.
- **Zoudou the Zealous** (she/her) stood on the second step with **Kum Meng-Sung** bound on the top tier, *"squirming and thrashing and shrieking through gagged wares."* Two noppera-bo occultists flanked the shrine's base.
- Four of the five PCs rolled Recall Knowledge (Kaoru, Zhen, Mu-Jin and Iryoku — Kaelen was still fighting his client); the party learned they were **in the middle of a Consecrate ritual**.
- The party rushed in shouting — *"Stop what you're doing!"* — and Zhen: *"Stop the ritual, release him. Stop whatever madness you're up to."* Zoudou ignored them and kept casting, then answered telepathically: *"I'm not interested in halting the ritual. However, if you truly wish to save him, then I agree to release him under one condition."*
- **Zhen called the offer before she made it.** *"What? Replacing him? Is that what you're going to say?"* — *"That is correct. Only one of you."* — ***"Typical. No way. No one's going to fall today for your ritual."***
- **Kaelen answered by drawing** — *"I pull out my bo staff"* — and **Zoudou answered that**: *"I'm not surprised by your lack of conviction and spiritual cowardice."* Kaelen, unmoved: *"I don't want to get eaten, or I don't want to be used for rituals. So you can go at yourself."* Zhen: *"Why don't you make yourself the target? Like the sacrifice. Go use your own life."*
- Kaoru drew and charged his blade — *"you can actually see the electric sparks flying down it"* — and Kaelen sent him in: *"Show him what you're made of."*
- **Kaelen's laptop had desynced to the old lumber camp map at 6,000 ms ping**; the GM rolled his initiative for him. (Same lag problem as S2 and S7.)
- **Combat** (below). **One round**, decided four turns in — Zoudou and one occultist dead, the third left standing at 21 of 30. *"Oh, we killed them real fast,"* said Mu-Jin. Iryoku, at initiative 16: *"Oh, we got two out of the three dead."* **Zoudou never got a turn.**

**Act III — The Second Phase**

- *"Having stopped the ritual, there is a sudden rumble, as if an enormous tree suddenly toppled over, rolls out from beyond the wall of mist."* The surviving cultist **dropped to its knees**, saying something the party could not hear.
- ***"But as you look up, there is a vaguely humanoid shadow rearing up from beyond this wall. The shadow lunges at the wall from the far side, causing the misty barrier to suddenly flex and bulge as if it were a solid wall being pushed against from beyond."*** The shadow vanished. **The wall held.**
- *"But the noppera-bo, living or dead, suddenly burst apart as immense mockeries of their previous forms climb out of their bodies, shedding the smaller frames to emerge as misshapen undead versions of themselves. Their arms hang low, nearly to their ankles, and appear to be broken in several places. **Instead of heads, they have moldy wicker cages filled with blood-colored butterflies.**"*
- Kaelen: *"It's like every single boss fight in a game. It has a second phase."* Mu-Jin: *"I was thinking, this battle is going really easy. Ah, there we go, second phase."*
- Mu-Jin's Elemental Betrayal did **not** carry over to the new forms, and the GM ruled the transformation too sudden for Kaoru to recharge his Spellstrike.
- **Combat** (below). Two rounds, three kills, one PC nearly dropped.

**Act IV — The Rescue and the Walk Home**

- **Mu-Jin untied the boy himself**: *"Mu-Jin walks up past the gore and unbinds Meng-Sung, saying, 'Your father was looking for you.'"* — the direct payoff of Kum Soon-Chong's plea in Session 7.
- Loot from the shrine: a **tattered leather-bound ledger** and an **unusual dagger**. Kaoru identified the dagger on a **natural 20** — it is used *"to send souls to empower Kugaptee,"* and *"uses the fury of those slain to potentially slow fiends on a crit."* Iryoku's verdict: *"That's a bad unique weapon."*
- **The ledger is written in a combination of Aklo and Chthonian**, and needed both languages to decipher — which only **Mu-Jin** has (*"I picked those things on random. Glad to hear that they worked."*). It teaches the **Consecrate ritual**. Mu-Jin took it as a long-term project.
- The cage held nothing. *"You can only assume that's where they held him."*
- **Mugirou kept their word** and released the farmers as the party left the camp, with a parting gift. The party returned to Willowshore with every abductee alive.
- Mu-Jin's summary, and the session's honest limit: *"We stopped quite possibly an evil ritual, but we still didn't quite learn if they had anything to do with the curse."*

**Act V — Roughly Eighty Days (the rest of Sola, and the two months after)**

- **The Cerulean Tea House.** *"The tea house still stands, but it was in rough shape after months of neglect… Water leaks in through the doors and holes in the walls and roof when it rains, and a cold draft chills the bones in the evenings. Still, the building's core is sound — and once repaired could actually serve well as a base of operations in addition to bringing back a beloved local business."* Mu-Jin, Kaoru and Kaelen worked it up through five restoration points across three rounds of repairs — Mu-Jin twice on Crafting (one a **natural 20**, spending 10 gold of party funds each time), Kaoru on **Engineering Lore** when his Architecture Lore didn't apply — and **Kaoru finished it** with a borrowed 10 gold and an Athletics check he had no confidence in at all. *"Are you even good at athletics?"* asked Zhen. *"Not great. I'm trained in it, but still."* It was a success. **The Cerulean Tea House is restored**, and the party gained **+1 reputation with each faction** for it. Mu-Jin later bought a standard artisan's toolkit so he could Earn Income crafting tea there.
- **Yami.** A grey feral house cat had been hiding in the tea house since the previous summer. **Kaelen befriended her** — *"Yami does take a liking to you and allows you to pet and hold her"* — though the GM noted she stays randomly standoffish, *"you know, cats."*
- **Iryoku and Kaelen** worked the **milling houses** (a level 4 business) on Athletics — Iryoku taking the level-3 task rather than pushing his luck. **Zhen** earned income at the **Mother's Coil** (a level 3 business), then closed the season with **Religion at the Lady of Souls** — a **level 5** task at DC 20, the highest anyone attempted, and the one job on the board that actually matched his character: the man who hears the dead, paid to tend them.
- **The blacksmith's problem.** The **Eternal Blaze Ironworks** was being pressured by the Imperial Guards — allied with Northridge — to make weapons and armour free of charge now that Willowshore looks cut off. Iryoku, Kaelen and Kaoru went to the guard office to argue with **Zhang Peng**, needing six victory points across two-hour blocks. Their line held all the way through: *"I can understand the need for it, but she can't do it for free. We need to work this out."* They **won it in a single day**, and Zhang Peng agreed to find funds or issue an IOU rather than demand free labour. **+1 reputation with Southbank.**
- **The peachwood.** The Woodcarver's Guild in Northridge needed fallen peachwood branches for rare **fulu** paper, normally imported and now unobtainable. The guild asked them not to cut the trees — *"no one in Willowshore has the advanced carpentry skill or tools required to properly harvest peach wood"* — only to gather what had fallen. Zhen and Mu-Jin went first on Nature/Survival, Kaelen and Iryoku joined; a critical failure would have locked a grove for a year. **Five days** got them there. **+1 reputation with Northridge**, **20 XP**, two free fulus, and the offer of a **fulu formula** — which nobody can use yet, since all fulus require **magical crafting** and no one is even expert in Crafting.
- The party ended the summer **liked by both Northridge and Southbank.**

**Act VI — The Succession, and the Council**

- *"The friendliness between Granny Hu and Old Man Matsuki has effectively broken down."* With no one in charge of Willowshore, **Matsuki very publicly challenged Granny Hu for the leadership**, and the two resolved to settle it *"via a duel between chosen champions, drawing upon a Willowshore tradition for solving public disputes."* **Seven days hence.**
- *"As word spreads through the town, dread grows as folks realize that a duel will likely lead to a bloodbath."* The milling houses' leader asked the party to de-escalate. **Both elders had already invited the party in**, *"because the support of those who relit the Eternal Lantern will definitely go a long way towards establishing their faction's claims of leadership."*
- Mu-Jin laid out the factions for the table from memory — Matsuki the old carpenter from the **Tan Sugi Monastery**, whose mansion was a safe house because he'd coated its walls in ghost-repelling lime; Granny Hu the retired guard captain. *"Southbank is more traditional. They just want to be this quiet, chill village. Whereas Northridge wants to become more developed, more progressive."*
- The options were: de-escalate, back a faction, stand as a collective third candidate, or walk away. Mu-Jin killed the last one immediately — *"That's just going to lead to bloodshed"* — and asked the real question: *"I think we're the guardians of this village, but are any of us leadership material?"*
- **The council idea landed when someone reframed it**: *"Collective is not just one of us being the leader. It's all five of us being the leaders."* Zhen: *"I like the sound of that."* Mu-Jin: *"So basically it'd just be what we're doing now, but in an official setting. We'll just be calling it the Governing Council."* And, from somewhere in the party: *"The ultimate plan is the Jedi Council with all seven of us."*
- Cost: **seven influence points with each elder** — thirteen more than the one Mu-Jin already held with Matsuki.
- **Discoveries** (Perception / Society / Underworld Lore / Willowshore Lore / Farming Lore) found the levers. **Granny Hu** *"is more focused on shoring up Willowshore's defenses, she's aware that the town will struggle to subsist"* — any appeal invoking **food shortages** dropped her DC by 2. **Matsuki** disdains appeals asking him to **trust the younger generation**, and is a **drunkard** (Alcohol Lore is a valid influence skill on him).
- **Matsuki, at night, over wine.** The party bought wine and started drinking as the meeting began. The GM also offered a **+1 item bonus** (+2 on a critical success) to anyone who **prepared him dinner** — but cooking needed advance preparation, so it never landed that night; Mu-Jin asked to roll Crafting for food and was told he could only do it before the *next* meeting. Kaelen volunteered anyway, entirely unqualified: *"I'm going to make him a bowl of spaghetti."* (He does not have Cooking Lore. The GM had just said so.) Mu-Jin *"started jabbering to him about some of his favourite folk tales."* Iryoku spotted the loophole in the generational penalty: ***"Probably none of us should suffer from that, because I'm older than him."***
- **Granny Hu, in the mornings.** The first day was all discoveries and no progress — **Zhen's** turned up the cheapest lever on the board, **DC 18 Diplomacy** (*"oh, we got that one in the bag"*), and a later one added Willowshore Lore. Then Iryoku ran agriculture and farmland — *"concerned that putting up a wall or significant defences would cut us off from our farmland"* — and Mu-Jin *"talked about how difficult it was to survey and find good farmland."* **Zhen's own influence attempt failed even after he spent a hero point on it.** Day one ended at **3 of 7**.
- **Mu-Jin critically succeeded** on the second day with the story that broke her: *"a very chilling story of this one time a village was destroyed during a famine"* — straight into the food-shortage weakness. That put her at **5**. Zhen stood down rather than risk a critical failure (*"I'm not going to roll because I might crit fail, so you guys go ahead"*), and **Kaelen took the last roll of the whole arc with no hero points left to protect it.** The GM warned him: *"Oh, dang. You have no hero points left."* He rolled Diplomacy anyway. ***"And that's two."*** **Seven of seven. Kaelen closed Granny Hu.**
- **Mu-Jin's closing pitch to Matsuki** was pure Southbank: *"As according to the old traditions, just like in the tale of so-and-so, we need to come together and form a group, a strong united front."* He agreed to join.
- **Resolved in two of the seven days.** *"He will be having a governing council with both — and Old Man Matsuki."* **+2 reputation points with both factions. 80 XP.** No duel.
- The GM's closing summary of the season: *"With everything done for the summer — the making of money, the ending of the noppera-bo menace, the bringing together of the council of Willowshore to take over temporarily **until the missing governor is found**. And with a new season comes a new season of problems."*
- **Milestone: level 4. Book One complete.**

## Character Spotlight
