## Table & House Rules (AndeGM)

### Progression & difficulty
- **Leveling = "Mixed":** XP awarded normally, *but* milestone catch-up — if you hit a story milestone under the expected level, he tops you up to it.
- **Combat difficulty = three tiers:** Easy (enemies play dumber than they should), **Standard (by the book — current setting)**, Hard (enemies play optimally). May shift to Hard later; Ande thinks the group would find it interesting.
- **Variant rules in use:** Free Archetype, Gradual Attribute Boosts, Ancestry Paragon. Remaster supersedes Legacy (legacy options updated to fit remaster).

### Death & damage
- **Character death = "roll of the dice"** (Pax's choice): RAW dying/crit-fail = dead, no narrative cushion. (The alternative he offered was collaborative "story death.")
- **Lethal vs non-lethal = your call**, no standard penalty — you decide the damage type. Optional flavor: describe killing/knockout blows (not required).

### Hero points (generous — variant "Heroic Rerolls" from Starfinder 2e)
- **Heroic Reroll:** on a hero-point reroll, any result **under 10 becomes a 10.**
- Start of session: **1** (RAW). Sources of extra:
  - **+1** for everyone's **first session** of the AP.
  - **+1** if you're **10+ min early and active in VC.**
  - → **First session (this Friday) can start at 3 hero points.**
  - **+1** for **assisting in the prior-session recap** (replaces the first-session bonus going forward).
  - **+1** if Ande is ever late / returning from break (rare).
  - **+1** every **real-time hour** of play.
- Hero points **can** be spent on initiative rolls.

### Recall Knowledge (Bestiary Tracker house rule)
- **Success:** name, rarity, size, traits, elite/weak template, **+1 mechanical fact** of your choosing — can be specific ("their Fortitude") or general ("their weakest save"). HP/AC/a save/skills/immunities/resistances/weaknesses/an action all valid.
- **⚠️ Critical failure on an *already-identified* creature:** he gives one fact and **lies, 100%, no remorse** — and seeds the misinformation into the tracker. (A party member once "learned" a thing was *both resistant and weak to holy.*) → Be careful crit-failing RK.

### Initiative
- **Nat 20 on initiative → Quickened** round 1. **Nat 1 → Slowed 1** round 1.

### Spellcasting / rest / Foundry
- **Refocus: unrestricted** — 10 min per focus point between encounters, no daily cap. Only blocked by time pressure (ambush, enemy territory, villain monologue). Full rest restores all focus.
- **Long rest:** everyone heals **to full** before sleeping (HP topped up).
- **PF2E Dailies module:** rest → click the cup by the bed for daily prep; Animist also picks the day's **apparitions** there.
- **Foundry lore quirk:** lore skills must end in "Lore" (e.g., "Art Lore") or Foundry won't parse chat references — Ande appended "Lore" to all of Zhen's apparition lore skills.

### Logistics
- **Breaks:** ~10–15 min after the 2-hour mark. **Stars & Wishes** at end of each session.
- **Starting gear can't be sold** (free clothing + adventurer's pack — wear and tear).
- **Common language is Tien** (not Taldane) — edited in Foundry.
- **No Exemplar** (class postdates the setting's 7108 era). **No content from other adventures/APs** (spoiler safety) — *except* the SoG Player's Guide.
- **SoG Player's Guide backgrounds treated as Common** (no need to ask). Uncommon Tian Xia options freely available; Rare = DM discretion.
- **Alignments → edicts & anathema** (do's/don'ts). Recommended (optional): ~2 personal edicts + 2 personal anathema, to define the character.
- **Attendance (paid game):** miss without prior notice = still charged; two consecutive no-comms = removed. But notice the day before = free skip, and "everybody gets one" emergency pass. ("Missed my alarm" ≠ acceptable; genuine life events are fine.) Evening slot, so low alarm risk for Pax.
- **AI art:** Ande uses only paid/official art on *his* end (customer-facing reasons) but won't police players' art. Has a **Midjourney** sub as a player resource — happy to run Pax's prompts. Pax: pro-AI, will make his own art, will send art + a token-border color/hex.
