## Session Summary

Session 2 is the cleanup shift after the invasion — a long, methodical relief sweep of Willowshore that trades S1's chaos for a to-do list, three shrines, and a slow-tightening dread. Picking up where they'd stopped (lantern dark, its three blessed copper coins gone dull, Old Matsuki asking them to hunt down the vanished stone guardian Ugly Cute), the four able-bodied heroes push out into fog thick enough to blunt Perception and swallow the road. The plan is simple: re-bless the three coins at whatever shrines are closest and relight the **Eternal Lantern** before crossing the river into the monster-held downtown.

They knock out the first blessing at the Abadar shrine (Zhen's Religion, aided into a success), then run into the night's first fight on the way to find Old Matsuki's hunter: a pair of jinkin and their two animals at a fog-bound farm, cackling a nonsense rhyme and calling themselves *the abacus sisters* (four, by their own count). The party talks them into a bickering retreat — but when the fae start slinking off, Iryoku, bound by a Yaksha's edict to confront cruel fae, opens fire, and the abacus sisters die in the mud. Kaoru asks, pointedly, whether that was necessary. The barricaded hunter turns out fine, hands over a Spirit Trap snare, and points them at a second shrine (to an odd, half-remembered deity the table heard as *Catephis*). A third blessing at the Lady of Souls (Pharasma) — a clean crit — completes the set, and the lantern flares to life "as if magic." The fog thins. The town exhales.

Across the river, the bill comes due. Granny Hu, holding the north together from the trade office, is unimpressed that they relit the lantern with the *wrong* gods — legend says Master Zhi Hui blessed the original coins with Calistria, Kofusachi, and Shelyn, none of which the party used — and she's especially disappointed in Zhen, a north-sider who should have known better. (Zhen elects to keep that quiet.) She trades information for labor: retake the downtown barracks, check on the stubborn Dr. Dami, and find her missing grandchildren. She also drops the night's real lead — the "brain" behind the invasion is rumored to be someone called **Modoku**, maybe a human occultist, maybe a human-shaped yokai. The party disarms a lethal trap-web around Granny Hu's grandkids (Kaoru's Thievery, Iryoku's wood-kinesis auto-aiding), clears a nest of centipedes out of Dr. Dami's clinic — sparing the incorporeal guardian-gecko that had been fighting the vermin the whole time — and escorts the doctor and a handful of unaffiliated villagers to safety.

Downtown is where it goes cold. The governor's manor is simply *gone* from its cliffside lot. Jinkin in stolen costumes jeer from a rooftop stage and demand a play; the party burns them off the roof instead (and accidentally sets the stage on fire, then very nearly sets Foundry on fire — a deleted spell-region crashes the whole scene for the better part of half an hour). In the barracks they find Granny Hu's son-in-law and his guards caged, fatigued, and at 1 HP each, and get the first real description of the enemy: a bald, sharp-toothed, hooded man whose **neck rotates further than a neck should.** They loot cold iron and silver from the armory, pick up the blood trail out the back, and follow it to the Happy Kappa bathhouse — where a halfling owner is stuck in a locker and a knot of repentant, *bribed* jinkin confess they never wanted to hurt anyone, name **Modoku** as the one who bought them, and point to the **tea house** as where he went. The party, wisely, does not pick that fight. They call it a night on the doorstep of the lead.

## Key Events

### Act I — Fog, and the First Coin (South Willowshore)

- **Cold open / S1 recap** (Iryoku): woke east of town, killed talking centipedes, found Ugly Cute's pedestal empty, cleared a guard tower (2 dead guards, 2 dead jinkin) and met the lone survivor **Ha Haier**, killed a **Buso** and scattered jinkin at Dawnstep Bridge, found the Eternal Lantern dark with its three copper coins no longer blessed, and learned the **Invoke Shrine Blessing** activity from Old Matsuki.
- **Two standing asks**: Old Matsuki also wants a **hunter found to track Ugly Cute**; Ha Haier asked them to check on her daughter (at Granny Hu's) and husband (at the Matsuki Estate).
- **The fog is a mechanic now**: thick enough to hinder Perception *and* Survival to sense direction. Cross-town travel eats real time (an hour-plus per leg) and demands checks.
- **Abadar shrine (first blessing)**: Zhen takes the Invoke Shrine Blessing on Religion; Iryoku aids. A joke about drafting the absent Mu-Jin to aid gets shut down — *"he's actually currently staying with Matsuki."* Success (with a hero point in the mix). **Coin #1 blessed.** Zhen: *"So, light it up. Let's go, younglings."*

### Act II — The Abacus Sisters (jinkin, a fog-bound farm)

- **The commotion**: through the fog, a small farm — **two jinkin** plus a pecking bird and a small weasel-ish critter (four "sisters" by their own count), all harassing farmers barricaded inside. One jinkin coos *"here, kitty kitty, come out and get a treat"* at the door.
- **The rhyme**: the fae announce themselves — *"swift like a fawn… together we are the abacus sisters"* — then dissolve into cackling. Kaoru, dry: *"Apparently they can't count."*
- **The talk-down**: Iryoku declares the party is here to protect the farmers; Kaoru offers the alternative reading — *"I just thought we were teaching them math, since they call themselves an abacus."* A Recall Knowledge round goes around (Zhen bows out: *"Today I feel very knowledgeable about farming, so I shall rest"*). Learning OOC that the real cattle-thief word is *abacter*, Zhen decides to tell the jinkin they've had their own name wrong — and the four fall into a genuine four-way argument and start wandering off, leaving.
- **The turn**: Iryoku won't let cruel fae simply leave. *"My heritage gives me an edict to confront cruel fae."* Opens fire on the retreating jinkin → initiative.
- **The fight** (one-sided): Iryoku's fire and one-action blasts, Zhen dropping into **Embodiment of Battle** and cutting down the bird and then the weasel with the nodachi, Kaoru's slashing gust + Shield, and **Kaelen's Bo-staff Flurry of Blows** finishing the last jinkin. Jinkin AC 15, armed with short swords; they *"all fall dead."*
- **The aftermath beat**: Zhen out-of-combat heals the party. Kaoru: *"I have to ask — was that necessary?"* Iryoku: *"Yes. They are evil."* Zhen splits the difference — *"they were terrorizing this place… but in their own silly way."* The moral-grey note lands and lingers.
- **The hunter**: the barricaded farmer (the very hunter Old Matsuki sent them to find) is unharmed, gives them a **Spirit Trap** snare as thanks, and points them south to a shrine to **Catephis**. The party escorts her back toward the Matsuki Estate.

### Act III — Two More Coins, and the Lantern Relit

- **Catephis shrine (second blessing)**: Zhen on Religion again; Kaoru burns a hero point on an aid. Success. **Coin #2 blessed.** Zhen: *"Teamwork is dreamwork."*
- **Lady of Souls / Pharasma (third blessing)**: at the cathedral above the graveyard, Zhen rolls a **critical success** — full HP restore plus Guidance once in the next 24h. *"Good thing I don't have Guidance."* **Coin #3 blessed.** Zhen: *"I feel very blessed today. Not by my deity, but blessed nonetheless."*
- **Relight**: at the bridge, the Eternal Lantern catches *"as if magic."* The fog doesn't fully lift, but thins hard, and *"some sort of weight has been lifted."* The party gains a **+2 item bonus to saves vs the blood-moonlight** and a **+1 item bonus** each to Medicine (Pharasma), Society (Abadar), and Survival (Catephis).

### Act IV — Crossing North: Zhen's House, Granny Hu, the Wrong Gods

- **Zhen's house**: the only PC who hadn't stopped home. He collects his heirlooms — an **Oil of Potency** (turns the nodachi +1 striking for a minute — his "this fight matters" button) and the **Predictable Silver Piece**.
- **Granny Hu, at the trade office**: gruff, suspicious. *"You didn't bargain with them, did you?"* — Kaoru: *"Define bargain."* Iryoku: *"No deals with ghosts."* The party reports the Buso, the jinkin, the survivors at Matsuki, and Ha Haier.
- **The wrong gods**: Granny Hu (via Kaoru's read of the lantern legend) notes the *original* three blessings came from **Calistria, Kofusachi, and Shelyn** — none of which the party used. She's specifically let down in Zhen: *"I'm mostly surprised by you, Zhen. You live up here. You should know better."* Zhen chooses to **hide** that the coins were re-blessed by the wrong deities.
- **The Santa Claus problem**: Granny Hu wants credit for the doctor's rescue as much as the rescue itself. Kaoru, unmoved: *"I trust Granny Hu as far as I can throw her… I believe that like I believe in Santa Claus."*
- **Three downtown tasks**: (1) retake the **downtown barracks** / armory — her son-in-law is the captain; (2) check on **Dr. Dami**, who won't abandon his clinic; (3) find her **missing grandchildren** near the northern drying yards.
- **The lead**: rumor names the invasion's "brain" as **Modoku** — possibly a human occultist, possibly a human-shaped yokai.

### Act V — The Mushroom House (grandchildren, a killing trap)

- **The trap-web**: four grandchildren huddled at the center of a lumber-drying yard inside a lattice of trip lines, tension dowels, heavy timbers, and sharpened stakes — a genuine hazard.
- **The disarm**: three legal approaches (Thievery / Crafting / Engineering Lore). **Kaoru** (highest Thievery) takes the roll; **Iryoku** uses base wood-Kinesis, which the DM allows as an **auto-success aid**. Clean disarm → **1 XP + Northridge reputation** (Iryoku's Northridge Scholar background adds a bonus point).
- **The kids come home** safe.

### Act VI — Dr. Dami's Clinic (centipedes and a guardian ghost)

- **The room**: several centipedes (some *flying*), a frog, and a strange darting lizard the party can't place — Kaelen: *"Centipedes are flying."*
- **The fight** (one-sided again): **Iryoku's Flying Flame hits all three centipedes for 56 damage, no crit fails**, then a blast finishes one off. Zhen enters Embodiment of Battle and drops another with the nodachi. **Kaelen's Flurry of Blows (hero-point reroll) obliterates a centipede outright** — "massive damage," near four times its max HP. Kaoru comes in with **Foxfire** for max damage, then Shield.
- **The gecko is not an enemy**: the lizard is a manifestation of a **guardian spirit** that has long protected the clinic — it had been attacking the *centipedes.* The party pivots to mercy. Zhen: *"Maybe we'll just knock it out. We don't have to kill it."* Kaoru wants to keep it: *"…it might be giving us the death glare, I'm still going to take it home."* Kaelen argues to leave it be. It turns out **incorporeal** (all-damage-resistant, doubled vs non-magical), takes Zhen's non-lethal swing and Iryoku's non-lethal blast, then simply **dissipates back to its plane** (it will reform).
- **Dr. Dami**: grateful, gives the party **antidotes and darkvision elixirs**, but flatly refuses to part with his one healer's kit — *"I need it for whoever comes here."* He and a few unaffiliated villagers (including folks from Second Best and Mother's Coil) agree to be escorted out; the party takes them to **Granny Hu's** (shortest, promised, avoids downtown).

### — Mid-session break —

- Kaelen steps away for a drink; the table falls into a long, warm build-and-rules argument — the Animist archetype, going Oracle for fire, and the eternal **prepared-vs-spontaneous caster** debate, capped by Iryoku's war story about a Sandpoint boss who crit-failed a Slow and got walked down. Kaelen, back and engaged: *"Andy just loves people casting slow."* Zhen, gleefully: *"Slow! Slow!"*

### Act VII — Downtown Willowshore (the missing manor, the rooftop stage, the barracks)

- **The manor is gone**: crossing into downtown, the party looks over the cliffside and finds the **governor's manor simply missing** from its lot. No governor's been active in a while — but Kaoru notes someone could have been sheltering there. Filed as a large, cold mystery.
- **A locked shrine/strongbox** downtown yields nothing; the DM gently notes that breaking-and-entering isn't obviously "protecting Willowshore."
- **The public stage**: jinkin in stolen costumes roughhouse on the roof of the open-air stage (its mural defaced) and demand *"a play… we want to see the cowardness and foolishness of the people here."* Kaoru flips it: *"No. You do a play for us. I think you have the wrong people."* Iryoku (trained in Performance) offers a real demonstration, but the roof blocks the sightline; the jinkin lob a shingle, Kaoru throws it back, and Iryoku is done: *"I'm no longer willing to entertain this course. I'm getting got."*
- **The rooftop fight**: the jinkin have greater cover (high AC) up top. Zhen answers with **Earth's Bile** — the vessel spell of a lava/magma apparition he'd swapped in for the downtown day — a molten-stone burst across the rooftop that catches all three jinkin and sets the stage alight. The DM, dryly: *"Setting buildings on fire. Why not?"* Iryoku Flying-Flames in support and later **Suppresses** the blaze. The jinkin break and flee rather than fight.
- **The Foundry crash**: cleaning up Zhen's lingering **Earth's Bile** template — the spell is sustained, so it leaves a persistent region; the DM deletes it — **crashes the entire scene** for ~25–30 minutes of refreshes and reloads (later logged by the DM as "don't use region unless you want to break the game").
- **The barracks**: the office is ransacked *"like a hurricane went through it."* The cells hold Granny Hu's son-in-law (the captain) and his guards — all **fatigued, enfeebled 3, unarmed, and at 1 HP.** He gives the first clear description of the enemy: *"a bald man with sharp teeth who wore a hooded cloak, and his neck seemed capable of rotating more than it should"* — **Modoku** — leading a jinkin warband. *"We didn't stand a chance."* Keys under a loose brick free them; **Zhen Garden-of-Healings the whole cellblock.**
- **The armory**: the party stocks **cold iron and silver** (jinkin are weak to cold iron). Kaelen takes a **+1 spear** (a monk weapon); Zhen takes a **silver short sword** and a **cold iron dagger** to feed his Needle Darts the right materials, plus an **oni tetsubo** he can wield. The captain and guards head for the trade office.

### Act VIII — The Happy Kappa (Modoku's trail goes to the tea house)

- **Blood trail**: bloody prints run from the stables through the northern gate. **Zhen tracks it** (Survival) — Mu-Jin the only other trained tracker, and he's absent — north to the **Happy Kappa bathhouse.**
- **The locker**: a sound leads to a **halfling** wedged inside a locker — the bathhouse's owner, who tracked a bloody trail here, panicked, and hid. He glimpsed only a **long-necked** creature.
- **The repentant jinkin**: in the bath area, a group of jinkin — the *bribed*, not-enthralled kind — admit **Modoku** paid them, and say they regret ever taking the coin now that they've seen what the invasion did. They give up his name freely and point the party to the **tea house.** They mean to stay at the bathhouse peacefully; the party lets them, and does not pick a fight it would likely lose.
- **End of night**: with the lead pointing at the tea house and the scene (literally) in pieces, the party opts to regroup rather than push on. Zhen: *"I'd rather not, to be honest."*

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Town's Quiet Keystone
The session's connective tissue and its conscience. Zhen is the one who actually *does* the ritual work — all three shrine blessings run on his Religion, and he closes the set with a crit at the Lady of Souls. He's also the party's memory and manners: he corrects the table's Tian Xia geography without breaking stride (*"This is technically not Japan"*), knows the town well enough that Granny Hu singles him out for using the wrong gods, and quietly decides to *let her keep believing it went by the book.* In combat he's exactly what his sheet promises — drop into **Embodiment of Battle**, swing the nodachi, put things down — and out of it he's the healer who tops everyone off and the tracker who reads the blood trail to the bathhouse. His mercy instinct is real, too: he's first to argue the guardian-gecko doesn't need to die. A grounded, competent, faintly weary elder who holds the party's center without ever raising his voice.

### Iryoku-Yasha · the Zealous Face
The party's mouth and its moral engine — and this session, the source of its one genuinely uncomfortable choice. Iryoku carries every conversation (Granny Hu, the hunter, Dr. Dami) and every bit of bookkeeping (the recap, the reputation math, the loot ledger), all on a nature-spirit's easy authority. But the Yaksha's edict to *confront cruel fae* isn't a decoration: when the abacus sisters are literally walking away mid-argument, Iryoku opens fire anyway — *"My heritage gives me an edict to confront cruel fae"* — and later doubles down (*"Yes. They are evil"*) when Kaoru questions it. That same conviction reads as principle elsewhere (*"No deals with ghosts"* to Granny Hu) and as comedy at the rooftop stage (*"I'm getting got"*). Mechanically dominant when the fights are on — the 56-damage Flying Flame in the clinic is the single biggest number of the night — and the wood-Kinesis that auto-solves the grandkids' death-trap is a quietly clever use of the kit.

### Kaoru · the Deadpan South-Banker
Quiet at the table, but every line lands. Kaoru is the party's skeptic and its dry wit — the one who calls the abacus sisters' math (*"Apparently they can't count"*), refuses to launder Granny Hu's politics (*"I trust Granny Hu as far as I can throw her… I believe that like I believe in Santa Claus"*), and flips the rooftop jinkin's demand straight back at them (*"No. You do a play for us. I think you have the wrong people"*). He's also the party's hands: highest Thievery, so *he's* the one threading the disarm on the Mushroom House trap-web, and the committed South-Banker whose distrust of the Northridge matriarch is a running thread he caps at session's end (*"I still can't stand Granny Hu"*). Softest under all the deadpan is the bit where he decides the death-glaring guardian ghost should come home with him — Kaoru wants a pet, and won't be talked out of it.

### Kaelen Ashford · the Quiet Wall
Fought a laptop the whole night and still showed up where it mattered. Tech lag kept Kaelen sparse and pulled him AFK across the break, but the monk was present in the moments that count: his **Bo-staff Flurry of Blows** cleaned up the last of the abacus sisters — and in the clinic his Flurry obliterated a centipede outright — he's the one who first clocks the centipedes are *flying*, and he plants a firm flag for mercy on the guardian-gecko (*"Because honestly, the lizard ain't attacking anyone"*) — the same instinct Zhen voices, from the party's quietest seat. When he *is* mic'd in, the enthusiasm is unmistakable, both in the build-debate stretch and in the simplest line of the night: *"I thoroughly enjoy this."* He closes the session a little better-armed, having claimed the armory's +1 spear.

## Combat Encounters

*No Foundry roll log was available this session; the reconstructions below are from the transcript. All three fights ran heavily in the party's favor — a continuation of the group's standing note that the AP's combats have been very one-sided so far.*

### Encounter 1 — The Abacus Sisters (fog-bound farm)

**Enemies**: 2× jinkin (AC 15, short swords — "the abacus sisters"), plus their two animals, a pecking bird and a small weasel-like critter (four "sisters" by their own count).
**Trigger**: after being talked into a bickering retreat, the jinkin start to leave; **Iryoku opens fire** under the "confront cruel fae" edict → initiative.

- **Iryoku**: fire attacks / Elemental Blasts with Weapon Infusion (reach), one-action blasts with Sweep for the MAP bonus. Primary damage dealer.
- **Zhen**: **Embodiment of Battle** (war-apparition vessel), then nodachi Strikes — dropped the bird, then struck the weasel/critter. Reactive Strike available in the war stance.
- **Kaelen**: **Flurry of Blows with the Bo staff** — finished the last standing jinkin. *(Note: the transcript's diarization briefly labels this turn as Kaoru, but the Bo-staff + Flurry is Kaelen's kit — the monk landed it.)*
- **Kaoru**: **Slashing Gust** cantrip, then **Shield** — support/cleanup.
- **Outcome**: both jinkin and their animals dead; the party barely scratched (Iryoku took ~5 from the fight, the bird's hidden peck the only enemy hit of note), healed out of combat by Zhen. The one lingering cost is a moral one — the fae were mid-retreat when the fight started.

### Encounter 2 — Dr. Dami's Clinic (vermin + a guardian spirit)

**Enemies**: a swarm-nest of **centipedes** (some flying, poison mandibles) and a frog; plus one **guardian-gecko** — *not hostile to the party*, a clinic-warding spirit that was fighting the centipedes.
**Setup**: the party breaches the clinic; the gecko is initially marked as opposition but reads as a "three-way" — it's on the centipedes, not the PCs.

- **Iryoku**: **Flying Flame across all three centipedes — 56 damage, no crit fails** (the night's biggest hit), then a two-action blast to finish the last one.
- **Zhen**: Embodiment of Battle + nodachi to drop a centipede (~8 HP or less to finish).
- **Kaelen**: **Flurry of Blows with a hero-point reroll obliterated a centipede** — "massive damage," roughly four times its max HP. *(His DM-declared turn at ~92:03; the lines scatter onto Zhen/Kaoru labels because Zhen was proxy-driving his lagged tokens — but Flurry + Parry is the Monk's kit.)*
- **Kaoru**: **Foxfire** (kitsune ancestry) for max damage, then **Shield**; an unarmed strike on the gecko bounced off (incorporeal).
- **The gecko**: revealed as an **incorporeal guardian spirit** (all-damage resistance, doubled vs non-magical). The party deliberately goes non-lethal — Zhen's knock-out swing and Iryoku's non-lethal blast — and it **dissipates back to its plane** (will reform). No one wanted it dead; Kaoru wanted it as a pet.
- **Outcome**: clinic cleared, guardian spared, Dr. Dami rescued.

### Encounter 3 — The Public Stage Jinkin (rooftop)

**Enemies**: 3× jinkin in stolen costumes, positioned on the stage **roof** with **greater cover** (inflated AC).
**Trigger**: the jinkin demand a mocking "play," lob a shingle; **Iryoku** calls it — *"I'm getting got"* → initiative.

- **Zhen**: opened by missing a **Needle Darts** cast (hero-point reroll still missed on a 17), then landed **Earth's Bile** — the vessel spell of a lava/magma apparition swapped in for the downtown day (rank-1 animist, 10-ft burst, fire + bludgeoning + persistent fire, sustained) — catching all three rooftop jinkin and setting the stage ablaze.
- **Iryoku**: Flying Flame in support; later **Suppress Element** to put the stage fire out.
- **Kaoru**: targeted a jinkin through the fire's visual effect (still a hit).
- **Outcome**: the jinkin **broke and fled** rather than trade blows. The encounter ended in the party's favor — and immediately preceded the ~30-minute Foundry crash when the DM deleted the fire spell-region.
