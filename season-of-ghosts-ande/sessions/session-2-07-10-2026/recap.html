Session Summary

Session 2 is the cleanup shift after the invasion — a long, methodical relief sweep of Willowshore that trades Session 1's chaos for a to-do list, three shrines, and a slow-tightening dread. Picking up where they'd stopped (lantern dark, its three blessed copper coins gone dull, Old Matsuki asking them to hunt down the vanished stone guardian Ugly Cute), the four able-bodied heroes push out into fog thick enough to blunt Perception and swallow the road. The plan is simple: re-bless the three coins at whatever shrines are closest and relight the Eternal Lantern before crossing the river into the monster-held downtown.

They knock out the first blessing at the Abadar shrine (Zhen's Religion, aided into a success), then run into the night's first fight on the way to find Old Matsuki's hunter: a pair of jinkin and their two animals at a fog-bound farm, cackling a nonsense rhyme and calling themselves the abacus sisters. The party talks them into a bickering retreat — but when the fae start slinking off, Iryoku, bound by a Yaksha's edict to confront cruel fae, opens fire, and the abacus sisters die in the mud. Kaoru asks, pointedly, whether that was necessary. The barricaded hunter turns out fine, hands over a Spirit Trap snare, and points them at a second shrine. A third blessing at the Lady of Souls (Pharasma) — a clean crit — completes the set, and the lantern flares to life "as if magic." The fog thins. The town exhales.

Across the river, the bill comes due. Granny Hu, holding the north together from the trade office, is unimpressed that they relit the lantern with the wrong gods — legend says Master Zhi Hui blessed the original coins with Calistria, Kofusachi, and Shelyn, none of which the party used — and she's especially disappointed in Zhen, a north-sider who should have known better. (Zhen elects to keep that quiet.) She trades information for labor: retake the downtown barracks, check on the stubborn Dr. Dami, and find her missing grandchildren. She also drops the night's real lead — the "brain" behind the invasion is rumored to be someone called Modoku, maybe a human occultist, maybe a human-shaped yokai.

Downtown is where it goes cold. The governor's manor is simply gone from its cliffside lot. Jinkin in stolen costumes jeer from a rooftop stage and demand a play; the party burns them off the roof instead (and accidentally sets the stage on fire, then very nearly sets Foundry on fire — a deleted spell-region crashes the whole scene for the better part of half an hour). In the barracks they find Granny Hu's son-in-law and his guards caged, fatigued, and at 1 HP each, and get the first real description of the enemy: a bald, sharp-toothed, hooded man whose neck rotates further than a neck should. They loot cold iron and silver from the armory, pick up the blood trail, and follow it to the Happy Kappa bathhouse — where a knot of repentant, bribed jinkin give up Modoku's name and point to the tea house as where he went. The party, wisely, does not pick that fight. They call it a night on the doorstep of the lead.

Timeline

Act I · Exploration · Fog and the First Coin

South Willowshore, in the Fog

  • Cold open / S1 recap (Iryoku): woke east of town, killed talking centipedes, found Ugly Cute's pedestal empty, cleared a guard tower (2 dead guards, 2 dead jinkin), met the lone survivor Ha Haier, killed a Buso at Dawnstep Bridge, found the Eternal Lantern dark with its three copper coins no longer blessed, and learned the Invoke Shrine Blessing activity from Old Matsuki.
  • Two standing asks: Old Matsuki wants a hunter found to track Ugly Cute; Ha Haier asked them to check on her daughter (at Granny Hu's) and husband (at the Matsuki Estate).
  • The fog is a mechanic now — thick enough to hinder Perception and Survival to sense direction. Every cross-town leg eats an hour-plus and demands a check.
  • Abadar shrine (first blessing): Zhen invokes on Religion; Iryoku aids. A joke about drafting the absent Mu-Jin to aid gets shut down — "he's actually currently staying with Matsuki." Success (a hero point in the mix). Coin #1 blessed.
  • Zhen: "So, light it up. Let's go, younglings."
Act II · Combat · The Abacus Sisters

Cruel Fae, Walking Away

  • The commotion: through the fog, a small farm — two jinkin plus a pecking bird and a small weasel-ish critter (four "sisters" by their own count) harassing farmers barricaded inside. One coos "here, kitty kitty, come out and get a treat" at the door.
  • The rhyme: the fae announce themselves — "swift like a fawn… together we are the abacus sisters" — then dissolve into cackling. Kaoru, dry: "Apparently they can't count."
  • The talk-down: Iryoku says they're here to protect the farmers; Kaoru offers the alternative read — "I just thought we were teaching them math, since they call themselves an abacus." Learning that the real cattle-thief word is abacter, Zhen tells the jinkin they've had their own name wrong — and the four fall into a genuine argument and start wandering off.
  • The turn: Iryoku won't let cruel fae simply leave. "My heritage gives me an edict to confront cruel fae." Opens fire on the retreating jinkin → initiative.
  • The fight (one-sided): Iryoku's fire, Zhen in Embodiment of Battle cutting down the bird then the weasel, Kaoru's Slashing Gust + Shield, and Kaelen's Bo-staff Flurry of Blows finishing the last jinkin. They "all fall dead."
  • The aftermath beat: Kaoru — "I have to ask, was that necessary?" Iryoku — "Yes. They are evil." Zhen splits the difference — "they were terrorizing this place… but in their own silly way."
  • The hunter: the barricaded farmer (the very hunter Old Matsuki sent them to find) is unharmed, gives them a Spirit Trap snare, and points them south to a shrine to Catephis. The party escorts her toward the Matsuki Estate.
Act III · Exploration · Two More Coins, the Lantern Relit

Teamwork is Dreamwork

  • Catephis shrine (second blessing): Zhen on Religion; Kaoru burns a hero point on an aid. Success. Coin #2 blessed. Zhen: "Teamwork is dreamwork."
  • Lady of Souls / Pharasma (third blessing): at the cathedral above the graveyard, Zhen rolls a critical success — full HP restore plus Guidance once in the next 24h. "Good thing I don't have Guidance." Coin #3 blessed.
  • Zhen: "I feel very blessed today. Not by my deity, but blessed nonetheless."
  • Relight: at the bridge, the Eternal Lantern catches "as if magic." The fog doesn't fully lift but thins hard, and "some sort of weight has been lifted."
  • Blessings gained: +2 item bonus to saves vs the blood-moonlight, and +1 item bonus each to Medicine (Pharasma), Society (Abadar), and Survival (Catephis).
Act IV · Social · Granny Hu and the Wrong Gods

Crossing to Northridge

  • Zhen's house: the only PC who hadn't stopped home. He collects his heirlooms — an Oil of Potency (turns the nodachi +1 striking for a minute) and the Predictable Silver Piece.
  • Granny Hu, at the trade office: gruff, suspicious. "You didn't bargain with them, did you?" — Kaoru: "Define bargain." Iryoku: "No deals with ghosts."
  • The wrong gods: the original three blessings came from Calistria, Kofusachi, and Shelyn — none of which the party used. Granny Hu is specifically let down in Zhen: "I'm mostly surprised by you, Zhen. You should know better." Zhen chooses to hide it.
  • The Santa Claus problem: Granny Hu wants credit for the doctor's rescue as much as the rescue. Kaoru, unmoved: "I trust Granny Hu as far as I can throw her… I believe that like I believe in Santa Claus."
  • Three downtown tasks: retake the barracks (her son-in-law is the captain); check on Dr. Dami; find her missing grandchildren.
  • The lead: rumor names the invasion's "brain" as Modoku — possibly a human occultist, possibly a human-shaped yokai.
Act V · Exploration · The Mushroom House

A Killing Trap Around the Kids

  • The trap-web: four grandchildren huddled at the center of a lumber-drying yard inside a lattice of trip lines, tension dowels, heavy timbers, and sharpened stakes — a genuine hazard.
  • The disarm: three legal approaches (Thievery / Crafting / Engineering Lore). Kaoru (highest Thievery) takes the roll; Iryoku uses base wood-Kinesis, which the DM allows as an auto-success aid.
  • Clean disarm → 1 XP + Northridge reputation (Iryoku's Northridge Scholar background adds a bonus point).
  • The kids come home safe.
Act VI · Combat · Dr. Dami's Clinic

Centipedes, and a Guardian Ghost

  • The room: several centipedes (some flying), a frog, and a strange darting lizard the party can't place — Kaelen: "Centipedes are flying."
  • The fight (one-sided again): Iryoku's Flying Flame hits all three centipedes for 56 damage, no crit fails. Zhen enters Embodiment of Battle and drops another with the nodachi. Kaelen's Flurry of Blows obliterates a centipede outright ("massive damage"). Kaoru comes in with Foxfire for max damage.
  • The gecko is not an enemy: the lizard is a manifestation of a guardian spirit long protecting the clinic — it was fighting the centipedes. The party pivots to mercy. Zhen: "Maybe we'll just knock it out." Kaoru wants it: "…it might be giving us the death glare, I'm still going to take it home." Kaelen argues to leave it be.
  • It turns out incorporeal (all-damage-resistant, doubled vs non-magical), takes Zhen's non-lethal swing and Iryoku's non-lethal blast, then simply dissipates back to its plane (it will reform).
  • Dr. Dami: grateful, gives the party antidotes and darkvision elixirs, but flatly refuses to part with his one healer's kit. He and a few unaffiliated villagers agree to be escorted to Granny Hu's (shortest, promised, avoids downtown).
Act VII · Roleplay · The Break

Prepared vs. Spontaneous

  • Kaelen steps away for a drink; the table falls into a long, warm build-and-rules argument — the Animist archetype, going Oracle for fire, and the eternal prepared-vs-spontaneous caster debate.
  • Capped by Iryoku's war story about a Sandpoint boss who crit-failed a Slow and got walked down. Kaelen, back and engaged: "Andy just loves people casting slow." Zhen, gleefully: "Slow! Slow!"
Act VIII · Mystery · Downtown Willowshore

The Missing Manor, the Stage, the Barracks

  • The manor is gone: crossing into downtown, the party looks over the cliffside and finds the governor's manor simply missing from its lot. No governor's been active in a while — but Kaoru notes someone could have been sheltering there. A large, cold mystery.
  • The public stage: jinkin in stolen costumes roughhouse on the roof and demand "a play… we want to see the cowardness and foolishness of the people here." Kaoru flips it: "No. You do a play for us. I think you have the wrong people." The roof blocks Iryoku's Performance sightline; the jinkin lob a shingle, Kaoru throws it back, and Iryoku is done: "I'm getting got."
  • The rooftop fight: the jinkin have greater cover up top. Zhen answers with Earth's Bile — the vessel spell of a lava/magma apparition swapped in for the downtown day — a molten-stone burst that catches all three jinkin and sets the stage alight. The DM, dryly: "Setting buildings on fire. Why not?" Iryoku Flying-Flames in support and later Suppresses the blaze. The jinkin break and flee.
  • The Foundry crash: Earth's Bile is sustained, so it leaves a persistent template; cleaning up that spell-region (the DM deletes it) crashes the entire scene for ~25–30 minutes — later logged as "don't use region unless you want to break the game."
  • The barracks: the office is ransacked. The cells hold Granny Hu's son-in-law (the captain) and his guards — all fatigued, enfeebled 3, unarmed, at 1 HP. His description of the enemy: "a bald man with sharp teeth… his neck seemed capable of rotating more than it should" — Modoku — leading a jinkin warband. Keys under a loose brick free them; Zhen Garden-of-Healings the whole cellblock.
  • The armory: the party stocks cold iron and silver. Kaelen takes a +1 spear; Zhen takes a silver short sword, a cold iron dagger (to feed his Needle Darts), and an oni tetsubo.
Act IX · Mystery · The Happy Kappa

Modoku's Trail Goes to the Tea House

  • Blood trail: bloody prints run from the stables through the northern gate. Zhen tracks it (Survival) — Mu-Jin the only other trained tracker, and he's absent — north to the Happy Kappa bathhouse.
  • The locker: a sound leads to a halfling wedged inside a locker — the bathhouse's owner, who tracked a bloody trail here, panicked, and hid. He glimpsed only a long-necked creature.
  • The repentant jinkin: in the bath area, a group of jinkin — the bribed, not-enthralled kind — admit Modoku paid them and regret ever taking the coin. They give up his name freely and point the party to the tea house. They mean to stay put; the party lets them.
  • End of night: with the lead pointing at the tea house and the scene (literally) in pieces, the party regroups. Zhen: "I'd rather not, to be honest."

We used Abadar, Catephis, Pharasma. It lit "as if magic" regardless. Either the lantern doesn't care which gods, or it lit for a reason that isn't the blessing at all. Watch this.

What Zhen is thinking about

  • The neck that turns too far. Bald, sharp teeth, hooded, and a neck that rotates past what a neck should — Modoku isn't human. Human-shaped yokai. He broke the barracks captain's men down to 1 HP each and left. He is at the tea house. That is a hunt, not an errand.
  • The jinkin aren't a monolith. The abacus sisters were cruel and died for it. The Happy Kappa jinkin were bribed, regret it, and gave up Modoku freely. Bribed, not enthralled — Modoku is buying this invasion, not compelling it. Coin, not curse. Different problem.

  • The blade stays wrapped. Nothing this session asked for it. Embodiment of Battle and the nodachi did the work without it becoming a thing I chose. Good.
