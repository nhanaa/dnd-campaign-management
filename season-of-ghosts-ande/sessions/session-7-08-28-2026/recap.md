## Session Summary

Four PCs, no Kineticist, and a stockade full of faceless things. The party picked up exactly where Session 6 left off — standing over three dead noppera-bo and one live one — and the first thing they did was try to make the prisoner talk. It gave them nothing. *"I'm only a lonely guard. What would I know why we attacked Willowshore?"* Mu-Jin pressed, threatened, and finally rolled Sense Motive: the thing genuinely did not care whether it lived. *"I have nothing to say to people from Willowshore."* Mu-Jin turned to the one person at the table carrying a sword — *"Old Zhen, can you take care of it?"* Zhen, whose entire life is an argument for restraint, agreed on a condition (*"I mean, I can, if you don't want to"*), then pushed it straight back (*"you can also do it yourself"*), and when Mu-Jin asked for a kneeling, sword-to-the-neck execution because *"this is kind of a medieval Japan period,"* Zhen refused the theatre: *"We're not in medieval Japan."* Mu-Jin closed it himself — *"We kill the guy"* — and the GM's entire narration was four words: **"Essentially, he's dead."** Who actually swung was never established. This was the same prisoner Zhen had knocked out nonlethally in Session 6 specifically so the party could question him.

Then the night became a methodical, building-by-building sack of the camp. Cackling from a western island drew them across the water, where four "heroic adventurers" were beating a **kappa** half to death and cheerfully invited the party to join in. Zhen tried to talk them off it (*"You probably have other more heroic things to do instead of this minor threat"*); Kaoru, who had called it before anyone, watched the disguises drop and said **"Told you. Disguise."** The party killed all four, and the battered kappa paid them back with the single most useful thing they learned all night: there is **friction between two of the noppera-bo leaders**, and their names are **Zoudou** and **Mugirou**. The camp is not one enemy. It's two.

The southern building held two **noppera-bo occultists** — red-robed, masked, one of them wearing a human face it was still trying to press into place. One of them reached for Kaoru's face and nearly took it; he burned a hero point on the Fortitude save and made it **by exactly one**. The other spent the fight slamming the door in the party's face until Zhen dropped Earth's Bile through the gap for exactly enough damage to kill it, then ran down the survivor and finished it with a hero-pointed **natural 20** through the nodachi. Behind a curtain at the back of that room was a human being at death's door: **Kum Soon-Chong**, the man who runs the Willowshore stables — the same stables this party rebuilt for him. Zhen healed him, and he asked for the only thing he wanted. *"I know I asked for your help with the stables — but they took my son. Please, can you save them?"* The party nearly walked out before hearing the rest, and the rest was the session: the son, **Meng-Soon**, was taken **northwest, to the end of a trail**; he is *"some sort of **old soul**"*; and the cultists were eager to use him as **an offering to something they called the Great Kugapti**. Mu-Jin hero-pointed a **natural 20** on Willowshore Lore and got back almost nothing — *an ancient and long-dead monster*, and no more than that.

The rest of the camp came apart in pieces. Three hunting spiders in the storage room went straight for **Weaver** instead of the party (*"Territorial,"* said the GM) and put venom in him. Two more occultists were found **praying** in a room lit by dozens of blood-red candles carved into the shapes of different ancestries, burning without smoke; one tried to take Zhen's face and failed; Mu-Jin dropped the first with a natural-20 Needle Darts before it ever took a turn, and Zhen closed the second with an Earth's Bile that also caught Kaelen in the blast (*"Small price to pay. I'll heal you up, don't worry."*). Six more grunts were staging a rat fight in the courtyard over roasting meat and didn't even look up until the party opened on them. And in an office at the west end, Mu-Jin disturbed something on the floor and woke three **elite air wisps**, the only creatures all night that fought back properly: they knocked **Mu-Jin unconscious** on the first enemy turn of the fight — one round after he'd spent his own turn learning they had no weaknesses — then battered Zhen for 17. Kaelen answered with an Inner Upheaval crit for **36** — enough that a *second* wisp detonated itself trying to shield the first, and still not enough to save it. Mu-Jin, back on his feet with a potion in him and Weaver shaving a point off the thing's AC, put the last one down himself.

They ended the night looking at a second courtyard to the north with **five people in a cage, spaced out** — and the GM called it three minutes early, on purpose, *"just so Iryoku can take part in it when he comes back next week."* Six fights, one execution, two rescues, and a name nobody can look up.

## Key Events

**Act I — The Prisoner (dawn, day 11, inside the camp)**

- Mu-Jin's session recap earned the table its opening hero points and immediately relitigated Session 6: *"Kaelen was mind-controlled by the fungi, considered it its friend, and decided to just stand there and watch it be murdered. So that's what Kaelen's like towards his friends. Keep that in mind, everyone."* Kaelen: *"You're not going to let that go, aren't you?"* The GM, cheerfully: *"I'll bring it up every session for you."*
- The GM confirmed **Iryoku is simply absent** — no DMPC, four PCs. Mu-Jin's contingency: *"Kaelen, you're going to be the tank for this party right now."* Zhen: *"As long as he doesn't turn back on us, that's fine."*
- **Zhen opened the day with a full Garden of Healing cycle** (20d4 = 53) and noted his healing had grown with level 3 — *"my healing is even more OP now."* Mu-Jin: *"Animists are fucking broken."* Zhen: *"Can also be useless, depending on how you play."*
- The three dead noppera-bo **bled purple** — Zhen flagged it as confirmation of the S6 lore. Loot: an **Oil of Potency** (Mu-Jin identified it, Occultism 25). Kaelen offered it round — *"Okay, does Kaoru need it?"* — and when it turned out both Kaoru and Zhen already had one, settled it with *"just put it in the stash until someone needs it."*
- **The interrogation.** The prisoner was bound and woken. It refused to speak aloud, only *"hissing — imagine something without a face hissing angrily at you. And also hissing in your mind, because they have telepathy."* Zhen answered it telepathically: ***"Shut the hell up. Can we talk?"*** Kaelen offered to cut the hand off a corpse and slap the prisoner with it — which prompted the **GM** to post Kaelen's own deity into Foundry chat without comment, and Mu-Jin's verdict: *"I don't think this is a character problem, I think this is a **player** problem. He keeps forgetting he's a light-side Jedi and keeps acting like a Sith."*
- Mu-Jin's **Demoralize** (Intimidation 14, exact success) shifted it from hostile to unfriendly. It still gave nothing: *"Everyone has a leader. Don't you listen to yours? Isn't that why you're here? Why you're all here?"* Zhen, dryly: *"Yes, we do have a reputation."*
- Mu-Jin's **Sense Motive** (Perception 14, exact success) confirmed it was neither lying nor magically compelled — it genuinely did not care if it died. *"I will speak plainly. Either you come up with a reason, or you give us something worth our while, or we're just going to kill you here."* — *"Kill me? I have nothing to say to people from Willowshore."*
- **Mu-Jin: *"Old Zhen, can you take care of it?"*** Zhen agreed conditionally — *"I mean, I can, if you don't want to"* — and when Mu-Jin pressed (*"You have a sword"*) he handed it back: *"to a degree, you can also do it yourself."* Mu-Jin wanted a ceremony — *"I was trying to go for an execution thing because you have the sword. This is kind of a medieval Japan period. That's usually what they do is they chop their head off."* Zhen declined it: ***"We're not in medieval Japan."*** Mu-Jin: *"Never mind. Forget it. We move on. **We kill the guy.**"* GM: ***"Essentially, he's dead."*** The party killed the prisoner Zhen had taken alive in Session 6; the recording never establishes whose hand did it.

**Act II — The Island and the Kappa (log pond, west)**

- Cackling and taunting voices carried from a small island across a sagging bridge, where logs are floated for storage. The party debated bypassing it entirely for the secret stockade gap. **Mu-Jin pushed to investigate** (*"We should investigate that first"*); Zhen was ready to skip the whole thing — *"Do we even want to encounter them?"* — right up until he saw what they were doing to the kappa. ***"That thing, you gotta save the turtle."*** Then he argued the party in, and they swam across.
- Four figures who read as **human guards** were beating a **kappa**. They greeted the party in perfect good cheer: ***"We're heroic adventurers, taking out this monstrous threat here. Would you like to join us?"***
- **Kaoru saw through it immediately** — *"It looks like that thing's suffering from shell shock"* … *"I question their heroism. By beating on a poor defenseless kappa."* Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge on kappa returned no weaknesses; Zhen tried diplomacy (*"How about you guys leave the turtle alone?"*), Mu-Jin asked to roll Persuasion — and the GM never called for it, going straight to the reveal instead. **Kaoru: *"Told you. Disguise."*** Mu-Jin: *"Well, they're faceless monsters. That's what they're for."*
- **Combat** (below). All four grunts dead in two rounds.
- **The kappa's testimony** — the party's first hard intel on the camp's internal politics:
  - There is **treasure nearby**, near some floating logs.
  - *"The only thing I've heard is that there's some **friction between two of the individuals**."* Their names: ***Zoudou*** and ***Mugirou***.
  - Beyond that it knew nothing. It had been bullied and beaten and wasn't sure it would survive.
- **Mu-Jin ran the folklore courtesy that has now worked on kappa twice** (cf. S4's elder kappa): *"Greetings, kappa. We are friends with some cousins of yours to the southeast of Willowshore."* — and afterwards directed it to those settlements as somewhere safer. Kaelen sent it off with *"Peace be with you, Brother Kappa."*
- Party decision: **do not** fight the front-gate guards (who could raise the alarm); go back around through the secret gap and work the buildings.

**Act III — The Southern Building: Occultists, and the Man Behind the Curtain**

- Zhen picked the southern buildings as the more self-contained target and handed Kaelen the door: *"You want to take the honor of opening the door, Mr. Kaelen?"*
- Inside: **red-robed noppera-bo with masks**, one of them with ***"a human face trying to attach to itself."*** They opened with recognition, not surprise: ***"Heroes. Here to rescue your prisoner, I see."*** Zhen: *"Yes, we are."* Straight to initiative. Kaoru: *"Ain't nobody got time for that, apparently."*
- **Steal Face on Kaoru.** He rolled a 6, spent a hero point, and cleared DC 18 **by exactly one** — walking away merely Dazzled. Kaoru afterwards: ***"Since they like trying to grab my face — I know I'm cute and all, but damn."***
- The second occultist spent the fight **closing the door** on the party over and over, which triggered a table-wide grievance (Mu-Jin: *"In our past campaign it got to the point where my character just broke down and tore off all the doors"*). **Zhen solved it by opening the door himself and casting Earth's Bile through the gap** — the GM: *"Because it failed, that is literally just enough damage to kill it."*
- The survivor **tried to escape out a back window**. Kaelen chased it down; Zhen finished it with a **hero-pointed natural 20 crit** through the nodachi.
- **Mu-Jin's free Recall Knowledge (crit success)** on the occultists — a rider off his exploration activity — bought two facts: they are **noppera-bo occultists**, *"essentially the same as the grunts, except this one harnesses their natural magic further to frighten people,"* and they have **sensitive hearing — a weakness to sonic / anything with the auditory trait.**
- **Behind the curtain: a human, near death.** The GM: *"That's actually the guy in charge of the stables."* — **Kum Soon-Chong**, a South Bank man, and the beneficiary of the party's own downtime work rebuilding the Willowshore Stables. (Kaoru, to Mu-Jin: *"I think that was the day you missed."*) Mu-Jin: *"God, I just suck in medicine"* — **Zhen healed him with focus healing** and rolled it out for the whole party.
- He asked for one thing: ***"I know I asked for your help with the stables — but they took my son. Please, can you save them?"*** The party agreed to send him home and go after the boy. Kaoru: *"Just get out of here. We'll deal with it. We will see what we can do about his son."* Mu-Jin, in character: *"Just go back to Willowshore. We'll find your son for you."*
- **They almost left before the payload.** The GM: *"I'm guessing you didn't want to hear what else he had to say."* Zhen re-opened it, and got the session's real intel:
  - The son — **Meng-Soon** — was **taken to the northwest, at the end of a trail.**
  - The son is ***"some sort of old soul."***
  - **The cultists were eager to use him as an offering to something, or someone, they called a "Great Kugapti."**
- **Mu-Jin hero-pointed Willowshore Lore into a natural 20** — and the GM gated it hard: *"Even with the nat 20, you **barely** recognize the name… it's that of an **ancient and long-dead monster** of some sort, but other than that you don't."*

**Act IV — Spiders, Candles, and the Courtyard**

- **The storage room.** Skittering behind the door; Kaelen opened it onto **three giant hunting spiders**, one **quickened**. Kaelen, still nursing the door grievance: ***"At least they won't shut doors on you."***
- The spiders **ignored the PCs and went for Weaver** — the GM's justification: ***"Territorial."*** Weaver ate a crit and **Hunting Spider Venom**, and failed the DC 16 Fortitude save even with a hero-point reroll. Zhen: *"Weaver, no."* Mu-Jin: *"Stop attacking Weaver."*
- Kaelen's tech collapsed mid-fight (mic died, then a full Discord restart) and the **GM rolled his turn for him** — which still produced two bo staff hits for 16 and the first spider dead.
- A spider lunged at Zhen and missed twice. **Zhen: *"Not today, Spider. You need ten or a hundred more years if you want to do that."***
- Kaoru crit for 12 and left the last one on **1 hit point**; Kaelen's Flurry of Blows landed for **exactly 1 damage** and destroyed it. Kaelen: *"I couldn't have killed it. I could have killed it."*
- **The candle room.** *"An elevated floor takes up the majority of this room. Dozens of blood-red candles carved into the shapes of various ancestries are lit here, their wicks burning without smoke."* At the centre of the irregular ring of lights hunkered **a misshapen effigy carved from a badly deformed burl.** Two more **noppera-bo occultists**, **praying** to it. They ignored the party entirely until the party opened the doors on them. Kaelen: *"They seem to be praying. Should we take them down?"*
- **Steal Face on Zhen** — Fortitude 25 vs DC 18, clean save, no hero point needed. Zhen was Dazzled for a round and shrugged it off (*"You know what doesn't matter when you're dazzled?"*).
- Kaelen took Parry over a third attack, on principle: ***"Because I don't want to get my face taken off. Not today."*** (The GM: *"Thankfully, this one has already done that, so it can't do it."*) It cast **Phantom Pain** at him instead; Kaelen made the Will save and took 5.
- **Zhen killed both occultists** — a crit for 17 on the first, and an Earth's Bile that finished the second and caught Kaelen in the burst. Kaelen made the DC 19 Reflex for half. Zhen: ***"Small price to pay. I'll heal you up, don't worry."*** Kaelen: *"Hey, I'm still alive, aren't I?"*
- **Break**, and with it the GM's top-of-the-hour hero points plus a **2nd-rank Guidance innate spell** installed on Kaelen's sheet.
- On return the party spotted **a second courtyard to the north with a cage — and people in it** — plus a western group. Mu-Jin's call: *"Let's try to take out the noppera-bo to the left first, and then we'll go and rescue the guys to the north."*
- **The courtyard revel.** *"Sounds of revelry and the scent of cooking meat come from here."* **Six noppera-bo grunts** around a fire, **staging a fight between two caged giant rats**. Kaelen set his own threshold out loud — ***"If they're just tormenting rats, then cool. If they're actually harming humans, then we're going to have to do it"*** — and asked for the Recall Knowledge; Mu-Jin rolled it. Giant rats, no weaknesses, *"enormous versions of common vermin."* No humans in that cage. They attacked anyway. **72 XP.**
- Kaoru drew fire in nearly every fight; Zhen noticed out loud — *"Why does it always end up Kaoru being the target?"* Mu-Jin: ***"That's only because Iryoku's not here."***

**Act V — The Haunted Office, and the Cage They Didn't Open**

- Two buildings at the west end. The first was **an office**. Mu-Jin disturbed something on the floor, and it woke up. Kaelen: *"Oh shit — or even worse. That's a ghost. Fantastic."* **Zhen: *"A haunted office."***
- **Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge**: **air wisps**, air and elemental traits. He asked for weaknesses. *"No weaknesses."* Mu-Jin: *"Goddammit."* (Run **elite**: level 2, AC 18, HP 22, with **Resonance** giving every air-trait attack in 30 feet a +1 status bonus — including their own.)
- Mu-Jin used his turn on the Recall Knowledge above. Then the wisp he'd woken came straight back at him — the GM: *"you're the one that stepped on it"* — put **three tendril strikes** in, and **knocked Mu-Jin unconscious.** *"Well, I'm down."* Kaelen: *"We got you, buddy."* Zhen: *"I'm also in trouble"* — and the other two wisps converged on him for **17**.
- **Combat** (below), including the fight's defining mechanic: an air wisp's **Accord Essence** reaction lets it **detonate itself** to give its allies temporary hit points. Kaelen's Inner Upheaval crit hit hard enough that one wisp blew itself up trying to save another, and the other died anyway.
- **Mu-Jin — dropped in round one — landed the killing blow on the last wisp** in round three, with Weaver's **Familiar of Balanced Luck** shaving the wisp's AC to 17 to make the Needle Darts connect.
- Cleanup: **Kaelen force-opened a locked door** (Athletics DC 23) on a hero-pointed **natural 20** after critically failing the first attempt. Inside: a looted storeroom of empty shelves and open chests. All four Search checks failed DC 18 — and the GM pointed out **a hidden trap door between two of the northern shelves** regardless. *(Unexplored — the session ended here.)*
- Final loot sweep, then the north courtyard: **five captives in a cage, each one spaced apart.** The party was mid-plan on how to pull the fight away from them when the GM called it — deliberately, three minutes early, ***"just so Iryoku can take part in it when he comes back next week."***
- Closing table talk. Zhen, holding the line on what the party actually knows: ***"Taking their face seems like just one of the occultist's abilities. We haven't seen any of these others do it. I don't know if it's a special thing the occultist can do, or something they all do."*** Mu-Jin, from the lore: *"The noppera-bo run to two extremes — some are just pranksters who like being jackasses, but some are evil bastards who like inflicting harm and causing terror in mortals."* Zhen: *"These are definitely on the latter side. Because they've been capturing people — and remember the house? It was like blood stuff."*

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Hand That Was Asked

- **He was asked to kill the prisoner, and he didn't refuse.** Zhen took this one alive in Session 6 for exactly this conversation, and when the conversation produced nothing, Mu-Jin turned to him. He said yes on a condition (*"if you don't want to"*), pushed it back once (*"you can also do it yourself"*), and refused to make a ritual of it (*"We're not in medieval Japan"*). Mu-Jin narrated the kill for the party. Whose hand it was never came up. His own sheet called this exactly: *"It would not be a thing he chose. It would be a thing that was asked of him."*
- **He carried the leadership tier.** Three of the four noppera-bo occultists died to him: both in the southern building (an Earth's Bile through a door gap for *exactly* lethal damage, then a hero-pointed **natural 20** crit on the one that ran out a window) and the second in the candle room. The fourth he crit down to 11 and Mu-Jin took. **Six kills across the night — tied with Kaelen for the most.**
- **He kept talking first.** He tried to defuse the island before it was a fight (*"You probably have other more heroic things to do instead of this minor threat"*), answered the prisoner's telepathic hissing with *"Shut the hell up. Can we talk?"*, and re-opened the conversation with the stable master when the rest of the party was already walking out the door — which is the only reason anyone knows the words "Great Kugapti."
- **He ran the party's healing economy end to end**: opening Garden of Healing cycle, focus healing for Kum Soon-Chong, the in-combat **Heal** that brought Mu-Jin off the floor, and a full 20d4 pulse between every fight. Level 3 made it noticeably better and he noticed: *"my healing is even more OP now"* — followed immediately by *"Can also be useless, depending on how you play."*
- **He caught Kaelen in his own AoE and owned it in one line** — *"Small price to pay. I'll heal you up, don't worry."*
- Still the rules anchor, and not always right: he tried to rule Kaelen's Inner Upheaval out as a second Flourish and the GM overruled him (though he was correct about the multiple attack penalty). He worked the cover math with the GM, coached positioning all night, and closed the session by refusing to over-generalize the party's own intel about Steal Face.

- **He took point all night, and he kept volunteering for it.** Zhen handed him the honor formally (*"You want to take the honor of opening the door, Mr. Kaelen?"*), and he took it every single time — onto three giant spiders, onto a locked storeroom he critically failed to force and then broke open on a hero-pointed natural 20. After a night in which his microphone died, his Discord had to be restarted and his laptop rebooted itself out of the session, Mu-Jin said *"this time you get in front of me"* and Kaelen's answer was *"All right, are you guys ready?"* — and he went first again.
- **He is the most generous player at the table about loot, and it never gets noticed.** Offered the Oil of Potency, his first question was *"Okay, does Kaoru need it?"*, and he settled it with *"Or just put it in the stash until someone needs it."* When Mu-Jin wanted the item off the occultist's arm: *"If it's useful to you, take it."* When Mu-Jin beat himself up over a Medicine roll (*"God, I just suck in medicine"*), Kaelen caught it: *"That's cool. Hey, don't we all?"*
- **He owns the Session 6 joke before anyone else can make it.** Asked to distract an enemy: ***"Are you sure you want me to dance for him? We know how well that goes."*** Three other people made that joke at him this session; this is the one time he made it himself, first, unprompted.
- **Six kills across the night — tied with Zhen for the most at the table** — two spiders, two grunts, an air wisp and a grunt on the island, and **his best turn effectively deleted two elite creatures at once.** Inner Upheaval into Flurry of Blows: a **critical hit by +15** plus a second hit, **36 damage** — enough that a neighboring air wisp detonated itself to grant temporary hit points, and enough to kill through them anyway.
- **He set his own moral threshold out loud** before the courtyard fight — *"If they're just tormenting rats, then cool. If they're actually harming humans, then we're going to have to do it"* — had it checked with a Recall Knowledge, and held to the answer.
- **He chose defense over greed on principle**, taking Parry instead of a third swing: *"Because I don't want to get my face taken off. Not today."*
- **The wit is his, not just aimed at him**: the corpse-hand slapping proposal that got the GM to quietly post his own deity into chat; *"At least they won't shut doors on you"* on opening a door onto giant spiders; and finishing a spider that had one hit point left, then being audibly cheated of the moment — *"I couldn't have killed it. I could have killed it."*
- He also ran the break: minded the room, counted people down, pinged everyone back. When the GM asked the table how the campaign was going, he was the only player who answered — *"It's really fun."*

### Kaoru · the One Who Called It

By beating on a poor defenseless kappa"* → and the instant the masks dropped: ***"Told you. Disguise."*** Two sessions running, his instinct about what's actually in front of the party has been the sharpest at the table.

A plain failure would have cost him his face only until the end of the occultist's next turn; a *critical* failure is the one that sticks. His entire comment on the save: ***"Since they like trying to grab my face — I know I'm cute and all, but damn."***

- **He made the call on the stable master.** With the party circling the question of whether to hold the dying man or send him home, Kaoru cut it: ***"Just get out of here. We'll deal with it. We will see what we can do about his son."*** That decision is what the back half of the session was about. He is also the one who **spotted the captive cage first** — *"I think he's outside, because I just saw a cage out that way"* — 95 minutes before the party went to it.
- **He scouted before anyone committed**, reading the front gate off the map (*"I see we have two guards at the gate and one dog"*) and asking the question nobody else did — *"Or are we spotted?"* The committed South-Banker of the party also clocked that the rescued man was **South Bank** before deciding what to do with him.
- **He was the enemy's favorite target all night** — spear crits on the island, the Steal Face, Phantom Pain, and spear hits in the courtyard — and Mu-Jin named the reason without being asked: *"That's only because Iryoku's not here."* Kaoru absorbed the aggro the missing tank would have taken and kept swinging.
- **He advocated for other players' turns**, backing Mu-Jin's ruling that got Kaelen moving during the spider fight (*"Just let him do the Flurry of Blows and punch Spider"* — GM: *"With the bo staff."* — *"Yeah, because he's not going to touch him by hand"*), and pushing the party to check the courtyard cage because he'd spotted it first.
- The S6 callback is his to run now: *"Alright, Kaelen. **Go punch Ghost.**"* … *"Dude, you're never going to live that down."*

### Mu-Jin · the One Who Asks the Next Question

- **He ran the interrogation from open to close** — Demoralize to shift it off hostile, three rounds of pressing it on motive and leadership, then Sense Motive to establish it truly didn't care whether it died. When the well was dry he said so plainly and made the call: *"I will speak plainly. Either you come up with a reason, or you give us something worth our while, or we're just going to kill you here."*
- **His folklore-correct courtesy worked on a kappa for the second campaign running** — *"Greetings, kappa.
