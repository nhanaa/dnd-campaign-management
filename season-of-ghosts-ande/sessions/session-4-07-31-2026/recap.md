## Session Summary

The victory lap lasted exactly one scene. A day after the party reclaimed Willowshore, the GM counted the cost — **nearly three dozen dead, the town at 225 souls, the Governor's Manor simply *gone*** — and Old Matsuki asked the heroes to carry the news to **Karahai**, the fortified settlement on the Sea of Ghosts that is Willowshore's closest neighbor and most important trade partner. The party geared up (runes, a Gate Attenuator, a Ring of Discretion for Zhen's wrapped nodachi), met a too-smooth traveling merchant named **Sahina Shinzo** on the way out of town, and rode southeast along the Duyu River.

They never arrived. A few hours short of the coast — with **Karahai's fortress actually visible on the bluffs** — an unnaturally thick fog descended, and moments after entering it the party found themselves back where they started. They camped at Gord Lake in a thunderstorm and tried again at dawn; the fog bounced them a second time, and worse: what felt like moments inside cost a full day outside. Two days gone, message undelivered. **Willowshore is cut off, and the curse now has a shape: a wall.**

So the heroes went home and did what hometown heroes do — they worked. From Matsuki's list of townsfolk needing help they rebuilt the **Willowshore Stables** (Zhen's blessed-coin crit leading the way), talked a troop of prank-happy **kappa** out of two stolen boats with a basket of cucumbers and elaborate politeness (Mu-Jin's finest scene), found **Choe Chung-hu alive** at the Milling Houses, and twice failed to sweet-talk the stone **kami** guarding a languishing Desna shrine — the one job this party of five off-Face casters is genuinely bad at. The session closed at the **long-abandoned village** north of town, Matsuki's hope for emergency winter crops, which sits right at the fog's edge — where an **elite slime mold** nearly killed Zhen (a crit for 20 and a dose of **Slime Rot**) before he cut it down himself.

## Key Events

**Act I — The Cost, and the Commission (day 2 after reclamation)**
- The GM tallied the occupation: **~3 dozen citizens dead** (population 225), funerals underway, the Governor's Manor vanished — "it looks like it was never there to begin with" — **Granny Hu and Old Matsuki** de facto running the town.
- Matsuki's ask: ride southeast along the **Duyu River** to **Karahai** — a fortress-settlement on the coast of the **Sea of Ghosts**, Willowshore's closest neighbor — and deliver news of the invasion to **Commander Wen**. Horses provided; one day's ride.
- Gear-up: the party returned the stolen jewelry to its families, sold loot (~106 gold), and spent it — **Iryoku** a Gate Attenuator, **Zhen** a +1 potency rune for the nodachi, **Kaelen** transferring his spear's +1 rune to the bo staff (plus a potency crystal), **Kaoru** moving the silver dagger's +1 to his ninjato. The **Ring of Discretion** went to Zhen — his "big-ass sword" now walks through town invisible.
- Along the way the table settled exploration activities (Mu-Jin Investigating — the GM rules it grants a free Recall Knowledge at initiative; Kaoru swapping Slashing Gust for Detect Magic; Kaelen Avoiding Notice), and Kaoru quietly noted the party may have seen more of him than he's said: *"Depends on how observant you were in the battle — I did change."*

**Act II — Sahina Shinzo (the merchant who shouldn't be here)**
- On the way out of town, a **man with a raven in a horse-drawn cart**: traveling merchant **Sahina Shinzo** and his raven **Yix**, looking to add Willowshore to his route. Mu-Jin's hero-pointed Sense Motive read him clean: *solely here to set up shop.*
- He takes orders for next visit (a walking cauldron for Mu-Jin; journey bread and iron wine for Zhen — his stock is level-locked to the party's level, per the GM), stays "in no place longer than 24 hours," and — when Zhen asked directly where he came from — answered, *"I came from out there somewhere… where I come from, it's not important."*
- Mu-Jin, courteous and pointed: *"Be careful with your words. It was very recent — literally a day ago — that we lost over three dozen people."* Zhen's first answer had been grimmer — asked what happened to the town: *"Nothing good. That's for sure."*
- The question nobody could answer by session's end: **the fog bounces the party — so how did Shinzo get in?**

**Act III — The Fog Wall (days 2–3)**
- Hours from the coast, first glimpse of the **Sea of Ghosts** and Karahai's fortress on the bluffs — then fog, unnaturally thick, horses skittish, and moments later the party was **back where they started** in the hinterlands. Every direction, every attempt, same result.
- Recall Knowledge (three PCs, hero points burned) topped out at: *whatever causes this is beyond your current knowledge* — some strange **curse settled on the land**, no doubt tied to the invasion and the vanished governor.
- The party camped at **Gord Lake** through a thunderstorm — Iryoku raised lean-tos with wood kinesis, watches were set, nothing came — and tried again at dawn. Bounced again. And the second bounce revealed the trap: **moments inside the fog cost a day outside.** They left in the morning; they came out at sunset. Two days total, gone.
- The party rode home through the storm and reported to an exhausted Matsuki at 1 AM. His answer: *give me a few days to figure out where to go from here — for now, people need help.*

**Act IV — The Help List (day 4)**
- Matsuki's list of townsfolk needing aid, worked as a quest board:
- **Matsuki himself**: if the curse lasts, **Willowshore won't have enough food to last the winter.** His hope — the **long-abandoned village to the north** (no name anyone remembers) as ground for emergency crops. Kaelen's immediate theory: *"Couldn't that be where the source of the fog could be at?"*
- **The docks**: a dockworker (name garbled on the recording — rendered "Samudra"/"Rajul"; a Nagaji per the GM) reported kappa stole two boats and fled downriver. Mu-Jin's kappa lore unlocked the plan — *bring cucumbers*.
- **The Willowshore Stables**: wrecked in the invasion; a 10-Victory-Point repair (Survival or Crafting, 8 hours, once each per day).
- **Choe Chung-hu — alive** at the Milling Houses. (The table's reaction ran from "He's still alive?!" straight to Kaelen's *"can I please punch him in the face for leaving us there?"* — Kaoru: "No punches." Mu-Jin: "Don't punch him yet.") He'd been trapped in his house the whole occupation — he never abandoned them. His ask: relocate the **Desna shrine** languishing in the abandoned estates to the Milling Houses — once its guardian **kami** agree.
- **Xi Yang** (spelling unconfirmed), the town's pointedly unaffiliated lawyer, offered help parsing Qing Mai-Lai's will; the **Eyes of Fumeiyoshi** turn out to lie **northwest** of town. The party kept the will.

**Act V — Working the List (days 4–5)**
- **The kami said no. Twice.** The party's Diplomacy ceiling is Iryoku's +6 and Mu-Jin's +5 (Kaelen sits at +0; the GM: "I would not recommend it"), and two days of persuasion attempts — hero points included — failed. (The job's full shape: Diplomacy to win the kami over, *then* Athletics or Religion checks — four hours each — to 5 Victory Points for the actual move.) The kami *can* be retried daily, or bypassed by force at a penalty; Zhen closed that door: *"We're a pretty diplomatic group. We don't want to upset people."*
- **The kappa job, by the book**: tracked to Gord Lake, where the boats floated offshore while kappa played. Mu-Jin ran the scene as written in the folklore — polite greeting, no accusations, gesturing to the cucumber basket: *"Let's just call it a good show"* — and an **elder kappa** apologized for the youngsters and returned the boats. Kaoru engineered the tow home (one horse per bank, boats strung between). **+1 Northridge reputation, 40 XP, zero dice thrown in anger.**
- **The stables rebuilt in two days**: day one banked 6 Victory Points — **Zhen's blessed-coin crit** ("That's two victory points, Zhen — the coins have brought you a blessing") leading the way — and day two finished the job. **Willowshore Stables open for business; +1 South Bank reputation, 10 XP.**

**Act VI — The Abandoned Village (day 5)**
- An hour's ride north: the nameless, long-abandoned village — with **the same fog hanging at its far edge**. The village itself is reachable; whatever is past it is not. Kaoru's settlement instinct: use the land, but *"tell them not to go past the village."*
- Something was moving among the ruins — a **heap of living moss and fungus**. Recall Knowledge: a **slime mold** (fungus/mindless/ooze)… and, as the fight revealed, one wearing the **elite template** the GM's bestiary view didn't show (the AC 12 / Reflex +3 he read out to Iryoku was two points low, and the thing was *durable*).
- The fight nearly took Zhen off the board (details below); he answered by killing it himself. The session closed on the cleared village, **Garden of Healing** ticking the party back up — and Zhen carrying **Slime Rot, Stage 1** home with him.
- Stars & wishes: Zhen asked for tighter pacing on downtime activities — *"I know I like the activities, but I think they can be sped up a bit… I enjoyed the plot so far"* — and the GM took it graciously. And the table's closing consensus, via Zhen: *"I still kind of think that Shinzo guy is kind of sus."*

## Character Spotlight

The town's healer had a workman's session that turned into a survival story. In fiction, Zhen was quietly everywhere: the one who asked Shinzo the question the merchant wouldn't answer (*"Where did you come from, Mr. Shinzo?"* — and, at session's end, the one still gnawing on the dodge: *"I still kind of think that Shinzo guy is kind of sus"*), the samsaran who met the merchant's "no fixed route" with *"So I guess it's just going to be fate that we can meet again,"* and the elder who kept the party honest with the kami — *"We're a pretty diplomatic group. We don't want to upset people."* The night's quietest comedy was his gourmand-elder run: no food orders from a merchant he might not see for months (*"they might go bad before we meet again"*), and — after Kaoru threatened a centipede-flavored tonic — *"I wonder what that tastes like. ## Combat Encounters

**E1 — The Elite Slime Mold** *(the abandoned village · 1 slime mold, elite template — fungus/mindless/ooze; GM's RK readout said AC 12 / Reflex +3, actual AC 14 / +5 with the template the bestiary view hid)*

*Initiative: Ooze → Zhen → Iryoku → Kaelen → Mu-Jin → Kaoru. Kaelen hero-pointed away a nat-1 initiative; Mu-Jin had already hero-pointed the Recall Knowledge that named the thing.*

- **Round 1** — The ooze lumbered forward (speed 10, no reach yet). **Zhen** opened with a strike, the one-action **Earth's Bile** (failed save — full damage plus persistent fire), and **Shield** up. **Iryoku** moved up beside him and fired a **weapon-infusion two-action reach blast with fire**. **Kaelen** spent two actions closing and answered with **Flurry of Blows**. **Mu-Jin** ran his signature setup: dismount **Weaver**, hex the ooze with **Elemental Betrayal** (fire weakness 2), **Familiar of Balanced Luck** for −1 AC, Shield up. **Kaoru** hero-pointed **Ignition** into a hit (6 damage with the fire weakness) and raised Shield.
- **Round 2** — The ooze went for **Kaelen**: a hit for 7 with a Fortitude save against its spores (the GM rolled for the absent Kaelen — **it failed; Kaelen is infected too**, confirmed post-session), then a **nat-20 crit** on its last attack — Kaelen stayed up, and the persistent fire ticked 3 into the weakness. **Zhen** rolled a would-be crit (oozes are crit-immune, but the table let deadly d12 ride — 11 damage), **sustained Earth's Bile — and the ooze rolled a nat-20 Reflex, total 25**, the number that outed the elite template ("I misread it as 12"). Then he **deliberately stepped toward it to pull its attention** off the backline. **Iryoku's** two-action blast also crit — flattened to normal by the immunity — for 12 +2 weakness, and he hero-pointed the follow-up MAP blast. **Kaelen's delayed turn never came** (BRB the rest of the fight). **Mu-Jin** learned two witch lessons in one turn — Elemental Betrayal had lapsed unsustained, and **Biting Words can't target a mindless ooze** (linguistic trait; spell refunded) — then moved in and **Soothed Kaelen for 6**. **Kaoru** hero-pointed his attack and recast Shield.
- **Round 3** *(the rolls.txt round)* — The step-up worked: *"You garnered his attention by moving up."* The ooze **crit Zhen's AC 19 by +10: 20 damage** and a dose of **Slime Rot** — the hero-pointed Fortitude reroll turned a crit fail into a plain failure by 4: **Stage 1, Enfeebled 1 + Sickened 1**. Its two follow-up Pseudopods missed (MAP), and it **crit-failed its own flat check** to shake the persistent fire (down to 1/round with the hex lapsed). Zhen, at ~10 HP: **two-action Heal on himself for 16**, then the +1 nodachi — first die a nat 1, **rerolled into a 22 hit — 11 slashing, dead** before anyone else's Round 3 came up. *"Alright, take it down. Jesus Christ."*
- **Threat:** real for once — the elite template plus a crit put **Zhen within a hit of dropping** (20 damage in a single attack, ~10 HP left before his Heal) and left him **diseased**; Kaelen ate a hit, a nat-20 crit, and an infection of his own (Soothe 6 mid-fight, Garden 21 after). The GM's mis-read RK numbers meant the party spent two rounds swinging at phantom AC 12. **XP 48.**
- **Aftermath:** Zhen swapped vessels (Earth's Bile → Garden of Healing) and **Garden of Healing** (10d4 = 21) topped up Zhen and Kaelen; hero points reset to 1. **Both Zhen and Kaelen carry Slime Rot Stage 1** — DC 20 Fortitude, and its stage 6 reads: *dead, and the body erupts to release a new slime mold.*

### Things Zhen knows that the party does not

### What Zhen is thinking about
- **The fog is a wall, not weather.** It sees you, it moves you, and it steals time — moments inside cost the better part of a day outside; two days went to it. If it wanted us dead, we'd be dead; it wants us *contained*. Why contain a town you've already invaded once?
- **Shinzo crossed it.** Either the wall lets some people through, or he was already inside. Both answers matter. Ask the other villagers whether he actually helped anyone while we were gone.
- **Food math.** If the wall stands through autumn, Willowshore starves. The abandoned village is the only arable answer — and it sits at the fog's edge, and an elite-strength ooze was already picking through it. Clearing it for farmers means *keeping* it clear.
- **We have no Face.** Two days of failed kami diplomacy off a +6 ceiling. Either someone invests in Charisma at level 3, or we find another way to be persuasive. Do not let the party force the shrine — guardians remember.

- Can the **kami** be won over with something other than a raw Diplomacy roll — an offering, Desna's own rites, an actual worshipper's voice? (The moving itself is Athletics/Religion once they agree; the bottleneck is only the persuasion. Mu-Jin floated adopting Desna — worth encouraging.)

- What are the **Eyes of Fumeiyoshi** (northwest) — and what's waiting in a death-god's ponds?
