Session Summary

The victory lap lasted exactly one scene. A day after the party reclaimed Willowshore, the GM counted the cost — nearly three dozen dead, the town at 225 souls, the Governor's Manor simply gone — and Old Matsuki asked the heroes to carry the news to Karahai, the fortified settlement on the Sea of Ghosts that is Willowshore's closest neighbor. The party geared up (runes, a Gate Attenuator, a Ring of Discretion for Zhen's wrapped nodachi), met a too-smooth traveling merchant named Sahina Shinzo on the way out of town, and rode southeast along the Duyu River.

They never arrived. A few hours short of the coast — with Karahai's fortress actually visible on the bluffs — an unnaturally thick fog descended, and moments after entering it the party found themselves back where they started. They camped at Gord Lake in a thunderstorm and tried again at dawn; the fog bounced them a second time, and worse: what felt like moments inside cost a full day outside. Two days gone, message undelivered. Willowshore is cut off, and the curse now has a shape: a wall.

So the heroes went home and did what hometown heroes do — they worked. They rebuilt the Willowshore Stables, talked a troop of prank-happy kappa out of two stolen boats with cucumbers and elaborate politeness (Mu-Jin's finest scene), found Choe Chung-hu alive, and twice failed to sweet-talk the stone kami guarding a languishing Desna shrine. The session closed at the long-abandoned village north of town — Matsuki's hope for winter crops, sitting right at the fog's edge — where an elite slime mold nearly killed Zhen before he cut it down himself, and both he and Kaelen walked away carrying Slime Rot.

Nearly Three Dozen Dead

  • The GM tallied the occupation: ~3 dozen citizens dead (population 225), funerals underway, the Governor's Manor vanished — "it looks like it was never there to begin with" — and Granny Hu and Old Matsuki de facto running the town.
  • Matsuki's ask: ride southeast along the Duyu River to Karahai — a fortress-settlement on the coast of the Sea of Ghosts — and deliver news of the invasion to Commander Wen. Horses provided; one day's ride.
  • Gear-up: stolen jewelry returned to its families, loot sold (~106 gold) and spent — Iryoku a Gate Attenuator, Zhen a +1 potency rune for the nodachi, Kaelen transferring his spear's +1 to the bo staff, Kaoru moving the silver dagger's +1 to his ninjato.   • And Kaoru, on whether the party has seen his kitsune form: "Depends on how observant you were in the battle — I did change."
Act II · Roleplay · Sahina Shinzo

The Merchant Who Shouldn't Be Here

  • On the way out of town: a man with a raven in a horse-drawn cart — traveling merchant Sahina Shinzo and his raven Yix, looking to add Willowshore to his route.
  • Mu-Jin's hero-pointed Sense Motive read him clean: solely here to set up shop. Zhen's first answer was grimmer — asked what happened to the town: "Nothing good. That's for sure."
  • He takes orders for next visit — a walking cauldron for Mu-Jin, journey bread and iron wine for Zhen (his stock is level-locked to the party's level) — and stays "in no place longer than 24 hours."
  • Zhen asked directly where he came from: "I came from out there somewhere… where I come from, it's not important."
  • Mu-Jin, courteous and pointed: "Be careful with your words. It was very recent — literally a day ago — that we lost over three dozen people."
  • The question nobody could answer by session's end: the fog bounces the party — so how did Shinzo get in?
Act III · Mystery · The Fog Wall

Karahai, Visible and Unreachable

  • Hours from the coast: first glimpse of the Sea of Ghosts and Karahai's fortress on the bluffs — then fog, unnaturally thick, horses skittish, and moments later the party was back where they started. Every direction, same result.
  • Recall Knowledge (three PCs, hero points burned) topped out at: whatever causes this is beyond your current knowledge — some strange curse settled on the land, tied to the invasion and the vanished governor.
  • Camp at Gord Lake through a thunderstorm — Iryoku raised lean-tos with wood kinesis, watches were set, nothing came.
  • Second attempt at dawn: bounced again — and the trap revealed. Moments inside the fog cost a day outside. They left in the morning; they came out at sunset. Two days total, gone.
  • Home through the storm to an exhausted Matsuki at 1 AM: give me a few days to figure out where to go from here — for now, people need help.
Act IV · Town · The Help List

Matsuki's Quest Board

  • Matsuki himself: if the curse lasts, Willowshore won't have enough food to last the winter. His hope — the long-abandoned village to the north as ground for emergency crops. Kaelen, immediately: "Couldn't that be where the source of the fog could be at?"
  • The docks: kappa stole two boats and fled downriver. Mu-Jin's kappa lore unlocked the plan — bring cucumbers.
  • The Willowshore Stables: wrecked in the invasion; a 10-Victory-Point repair (Survival or Crafting, 8 hours, once each per day).
  • Choe Chung-hu — alive at the Milling Houses. The table's reaction ran from "He's still alive?!" straight to Kaelen's "can I please punch him in the face for leaving us there?" (Kaoru: "No punches." Mu-Jin: "Don't punch him yet.") He'd been trapped in his house the whole occupation — he never abandoned them. His ask: relocate the Desna shrine to the Milling Houses — once its guardian kami agree.
  • Xi Yang, the town's pointedly unaffiliated lawyer, offered help parsing Qing Mai-Lai's will; the Eyes of Fumeiyoshi turn out to lie northwest of town.
Act V · Town · Working the List

Kami, Kappa, and Carpentry

  • The kami said no. Twice. The party's Diplomacy ceiling is Iryoku's +6 and Mu-Jin's +5 (Kaelen sits at +0; the GM: "I would not recommend it") — two days of persuasion, hero points included, failed. Retry daily; force is possible at a penalty, but Zhen closed that door: "We're a pretty diplomatic group. We don't want to upset people."
  • The kappa job, by the book: tracked to Gord Lake. Mu-Jin ran the scene folklore-correct — polite greeting, no accusations, the cucumber basket offered with "let's just call it a good show" — and an elder kappa apologized for the youngsters and returned the boats. Kaoru engineered the tow home (one horse per bank). +1 Northridge, 40 XP, zero dice thrown in anger.
  • The stables rebuilt in two days: day one banked 6 Victory Points — Zhen's blessed-coin crit ("That's two victory points, Zhen — the coins have brought you a blessing") leading the way — and day two finished it. Open for business; +1 South Bank, 10 XP.
  • A character thread opened at the shrine: Mu-Jin — "This is a cool goddess. I don't want her shrine to be just left to dust" — realized he's never had a deity, and asked about adopting Desna.
Act VI · Combat · The Abandoned Village

The Ooze at the Edge of the Fog

  • An hour's ride north: the nameless, long-abandoned village — with the same fog hanging at its far edge. The village is reachable; whatever lies past it is not. Kaoru's settlement instinct: use the land, but "tell them not to go past the village."
  • Something moved among the ruins — a heap of living moss and fungus. Recall Knowledge: a slime mold (fungus / mindless / ooze)… wearing an elite template the GM's bestiary view didn't show.
  • The fight nearly took Zhen off the board (below); he answered by killing it himself. Both Zhen and Kaelen left carrying Slime Rot, Stage 1.
  • Stars & wishes: Zhen asked for tighter pacing on downtime activities — "I know I like the activities, but I think they can be sped up a bit… I enjoyed the plot so far" — and the GM took it graciously.
  • The table's closing consensus, via Zhen: "I still kind of think that Shinzo guy is kind of sus."

E1 · The Elite Slime Mold

Enemy
1× Slime Mold, elite template (fungus / mindless / ooze) — actual AC 14 / Reflex +5; the GM's RK readout said AC 12 / +3
Danger
Crit Pseudopod on Zhen for 20 + Slime Rot · nat-20 crit on Kaelen + a failed save

Zhen — +1 nodachi, 11 slashing (nat 1 rerolled into a 22)

48 each
  • Initiative: Ooze → Zhen → Iryoku → Kaelen → Mu-Jin → Kaoru. Kaelen hero-pointed away a nat-1 initiative; Mu-Jin had already hero-pointed the Recall Knowledge that named the thing.
  • Round 1: The ooze lumbered forward (speed 10). Zhen opened with a strike, the one-action Earth's Bile (failed save — full damage plus persistent fire), and Shield up. Iryoku moved up beside him — weapon-infusion two-action reach blast with fire. Kaelen closed and answered with Flurry of Blows. Mu-Jin ran his signature setup: dismount Weaver, Elemental Betrayal (fire weakness 2), Familiar of Balanced Luck for −1 AC, Shield. Kaoru hero-pointed Ignition into a hit (6 with the weakness) and raised Shield.
  • Round 2: The ooze went for Kaelen — a hit for 7 with a Fortitude save vs its spores (the GM rolled for the absent Kaelen — it failed; Kaelen is infected too), then a nat-20 crit on its last attack. Kaelen stayed up; the persistent fire ticked 3 into the weakness. Zhen rolled a would-be crit (crit-immune, but deadly d12 rode — 11 damage), sustained Earth's Bile — and the ooze rolled a nat-20 Reflex, total 25, the number that outed the elite template ("I misread it as 12"). Then he deliberately stepped toward it to pull its attention off the backline. Iryoku's blast also crit — flattened to normal by the immunity — 12 +2 weakness, and he hero-pointed the MAP follow-up. Kaelen's delayed turn never came (BRB the rest of the fight). Mu-Jin learned two witch lessons in one turn — Elemental Betrayal had lapsed unsustained, and Biting Words can't target a mindless ooze (linguistic trait; refunded) — then moved in and Soothed Kaelen for 6. Kaoru hero-pointed his attack and recast Shield.
  • Round 3 (the roll-log round): The step-up worked — "You garnered his attention by moving up." The ooze crit Zhen's AC 19 by +10: 20 damage and a dose of Slime Rot — the hero-pointed Fortitude reroll turned a crit fail into a plain failure by 4: Stage 1, Enfeebled 1 + Sickened 1. Its two follow-up Pseudopods missed; it crit-failed its own flat check to shake the persistent fire (down to 1/round with the hex lapsed). Zhen, at ~10 HP: two-action Heal on himself for 16, then the +1 nodachi — first die a nat 1, rerolled into a 22 hit — 11 slashing, dead before anyone else's Round 3 came up. "Alright, take it down. Jesus Christ."
  • Threat: real for once — the elite template plus a crit put Zhen within a hit of dropping (20 damage in a single attack) and left him diseased; Kaelen ate a hit, a nat-20 crit, and an infection of his own. The mis-read RK numbers meant two rounds swinging at phantom AC 12.
  • Aftermath: Zhen swapped vessels (Earth's Bile → Garden of Healing) — Garden of Healing (10d4 = 21) topped up Zhen and Kaelen; hero points reset to 1. Both Zhen and Kaelen carry Slime Rot Stage 1 — DC 20 Fortitude, and stage 6 reads: dead, and the body erupts to release a new slime mold.

Things Zhen knows that the party does not

  • The wrong-gods secret (S2) is still his alone — and it just got more complicated. The blessing that carried his stables crit came from the re-blessed lantern coins — the wrong-gods coins. They are answering anyway. Are they owed something for that?
  • The Slime Rot in his blood has a stage 6: the body dies and erupts into a new slime mold. He read the affliction card. He has not shared that detail with the others. The hunger inside him has never needed help; now something else is growing in him too. Shake it fast.

What Zhen is thinking about

  • The fog is a wall, not weather. It sees you, it moves you, and it steals time — moments inside cost the better part of a day outside; two days went to it. If it wanted us dead, we'd be dead; it wants us contained. Why contain a town you've already invaded once?
  • Shinzo crossed it. Either the wall lets some people through, or he was already inside. Both answers matter. Ask the other villagers whether he actually helped anyone while we were gone.
  • Food math. If the wall stands through autumn, Willowshore starves. The abandoned village is the only arable answer — and it sits at the fog's edge, and an elite-strength ooze was already picking through it. Clearing it for farmers means keeping it clear.
  • We have no Face. Two days of failed kami diplomacy off a +6 ceiling. Either someone invests in Charisma at level 3, or we find another way to be persuasive. Do not let the party force the shrine — guardians remember.
