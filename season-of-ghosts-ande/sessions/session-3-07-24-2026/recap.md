## Session Summary

The party finished what S2 started: clearing monster-held downtown Willowshore and running down the "long-necked man" — **Modoku**, the invasion's rumored brain — all the way to his den in the **Cerulean Teahouse**. The morning ran long and comic before it turned grim. A house full of *drunk* jinkin (the GM rolled six nat-20s and narrated the fey petting and cursing at their own clubs) nearly cost Kaoru his life at the very first fight, then the party rolled through a slaughterhouse-stable cockroach swarm and a chorus of **animated cookware** that demanded they join a parade for Willowshore's "new rulers." A fiat level-up to **2** landed mid-session, and with it a round of build shopping.

The teahouse itself was a horror set-piece: a giant frog that disarmed and tongue-grabbed Zhen, a hidden variant scorpion, a will-and-testament promising the deed to whoever recovers two pearls from **Fumeiyoshi's eyes**, and finally the rokurokubi himself — **Mo Douqiu the Hedonist** — lounging in a room of flayed and half-digested bodies with a pet weasel. He pitched the party on how much *fun* his horror story had been. They declined, at length and with profanity. The bleed he handed out was the only thing all session that actually threatened them, and even that was cleaned up. Zhen cut Mo Douqiu down with the wrapped nodachi; Iryoku vaporized the weasel.

With the yokai dead and the caged villagers freed, **Willowshore is reclaimed from the monsters** — and the session closed on a cutscene: a mysterious figure watching from the outskirts of town.

## Key Events

**Act I — The Drunken House (downtown, ~7 AM)**
- Following the S2 blood-trail, the party opened a mud-walled house to find **two captive villagers (Yuli Sanmi, Hu Lelong)** guarded by a mob of **jinkin**.
- The GM went on an absurd hot streak of **nat-20s** and leaned into it in fiction: the jinkin were *drunk*, praising their clubs, petting them, and yelling at them in Common for missing.
- The jinkin's attack bonus was effectively +0, so the only hits that could land were auto-crits — and **three nat-20 club crits all landed on Kaoru**, dropping him to **3 HP (Near Death)**. The scariest moment of the night came at the weakest fight of the night, entirely on crit luck.
- Kills came fast once the party found its feet: **Kaelen's bo-staff dropped one for 29** (a crit plus a second hit — massive-damage instakill), **Zhen's Needle Darts** (his divine cantrip) dropped another, Earth's Bile chipped the cluster. Mu-Jin's Soothe pulled Kaoru back.

**Act II — The Stables & the Swarm**
- The stable behind the guardhouse held a dozen **slaughtered horses** — the party's first real look at the invaders' cruelty. ("What barbaric madness are they doing?")
- A **cockroach swarm** boiled out of the carnage. **Kaelen one-shot it** — Flurry of Blows, second strike crit, 21 after resistance. The entire encounter was one turn.
- Escorts and cleanup: the party freed **caged villagers** ("thrown in to repent, or sent to the teahouse") and walked rescued Southbankers back across **Dawnstep Bridge**, banking reputation.

**Act III — The Cookware Parade**
- A racket "like hundreds of pieces of cookware clattering, combined with off-key humming" resolved into **three animated objects** — cleavers, kettles, chopsticks, and a **bloody butcher's apron**.
- One declared itself among Willowshore's **new rulers** and ordered the party to **join the parade**. **Iryoku's** Make an Impression actually *landed* (hero-pointed to a success) — but the party had no interest in celebrating, and **Kaelen** torpedoed the whole thing: *"No, you ain't. You're too small and short."*
- The engulfing cauldron-thing **swallowed Kaelen** (failed Reflex + two Fortitude saves, losing air) before the party cracked it open. Kills: **Iryoku** solo-killed one with a crit blast, **Kaoru** dropped the engulfer with a Phase Bolt (spilling Kaelen back out), and **Kaelen** finished the third after **Zhen** cracked its construct armor.

**Act IV — Level 2 & Build Shopping**
- The GM applied the mixed-XP catch-up he'd forgotten: everyone hit **level 2**.
- Live retooling, heavy on table advice: **Kaelen** dropped the long-planned Spirit Warrior (Zhen argued it's redundant with Monk/Flurry) for **Staff Acrobat** + Elemental Fist + Slippery Prey ("I like bonking people to death"); **Mu-Jin** leaned into **Familiar Master** (Enhanced Familiar → Cantrip Connection, Light, Willowshore Lore) to make Weaver tougher; **Kaoru** kept **Shocking Grasp** for the spellstrike; **Zhen** continues on Druid Dedication (Storm).

**Act V — The Cerulean Teahouse**
- **The frog.** A giant frog slept in the wreck of the teahouse floor; waking it, the party ate a **tongue disarm** (Zhen's nodachi knocked from his hand) and a **tongue-grab** on Zhen, who failed both Escapes and only came loose when the frog died. **Mu-Jin's Needle Darts crit for 22** killed it.
- **The will.** Teahouse paperwork yielded the **Last Will & Testament of Qing Mai-Lai**: with no heirs left, the deed passes to whoever recovers **the two pearls she threw into Fumeiyoshi's eyes** — two ponds north of town locally called the **Eyes of Fumeiyoshi** (a Tian deity). A standing quest.
- **The scorpion.** A hidden **variant forest giant scorpion** clambered down; **Zhen's Seek (25)** caught it before it could drop on the party. **Zhen's "burst combo"** softened it and **Kaoru finished it with a Shocking Grasp Spellstrike** (1d6 + 2d12 electricity, 23). The party later learned it was a variant, lower-level than a standard scorpion.
- **The body room.** Flayed and partially-digested corpses hung from the beams — the yokai's larder.

**Act VI — Mo Douqiu the Hedonist (the rokurokubi)**
- The **long-necked man** — Modoku, now named **Mo Douqiu the Hedonist** — lounged among the bodies with a **weasel**. He argued the town had been "the most boring place ever" and that he'd done everyone a favor by dropping them into a horror story: *"Admit it — haven't you been having fun?"*
- The party refused across the board — **Mu-Jin** ("an unwanted guest… we ask you to leave"), **Iryoku** ("Violence."), **Zhen** ("Get the fuck out"), **Kaoru** ("you need a new definition of fun"), **Kaelen** ("you bring chaos… get the hell out") — and rolled initiative.
- Mo Douqiu fought dirty: a **20-ft elongating neck**, Jaws with **1d6 persistent bleed**, Demoralize, and **"Drop Corpse"** — biting the ropes to drop hanging bodies onto the party as improvised weapons. He **demoralized Iryoku and Kaoru** and bit Iryoku, Zhen, and Kaoru; the **bleed** stuck on all three and refused to clear.
- **Mu-Jin's signature play:** dismount Weaver, send it in, land **Elemental Betrayal** (fire weakness) on the rokurokubi and drop its AC via Familiar of Balanced Luck.
- **Zhen cut the boss down** — stepping to an off-guard angle and running him through with the wrapped nodachi. **Iryoku vaporized the weasel** (a blast well past its remaining HP) after Kaoru's Foxfire crit softened it.
- The party freed a back room of **bound, fatigued villagers**, then took stock: **Willowshore reclaimed.**

**Cliffhanger** — as the party settled, the GM cut to a **mysterious figure at the outskirts of Willowshore**, watched "from far away." (*"Yeah, so we just jump to the Death Star." — Kaoru*)

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Steady Hand
Pax's animist ran the session as glue and closer both. In fiction he was the party's conscience — the one who told Mo Douqiu flatly, *"We don't want any of this here. Get the fuck out. You have caused enough havoc,"* and who insisted on **letting the dead villagers' families know** rather than presuming to burn the bodies. He's also the table's peacemaker: when Kaoru wanted Granny Hu *"bitch-slapped,"* Zhen just shrugged the youngest off. His clearest character note is quiet and mechanical at once: after the boss dropped, he begged off healing for a moment — *"I do need to refocus… I'll just sit here and refocus. Talk to my spirits a bit — they're asking me how I'm doing."* That's the Liturgist in one line. Mechanically he was everywhere: **Earth's Bile** persistent-fire chip in every fight, the **Garden of Healing** cleanup that undid the boss's bleed, and the **killing blow on Mo Douqiu himself**. He also spent a good chunk of the level-up steering Kaelen off a redundant archetype and pointing the GM at a lighter Foundry client. He ate a rough one physically — tongue-disarmed and grabbed by the frog, bled by the rokurokubi — and closed it standing.

### Iryoku-Yasha · the Oathbound
The party's Face, bookkeeper, and — increasingly — its edge. Iryoku kicked off the whole session with a clean, thorough recap of S2, then spent the night tracking the party stash (the mystery **+1 silver dagger**, Kaelen's **+1 spear**), the reputation math, and everyone's scout bonus. His paladin-flavored **oath** keeps surfacing: he framed even the S2 jinkin as something *"my oath demanded we confront."* When Mo Douqiu asked what entertainment the party had brought him, Iryoku answered for all of them in one word — *"Violence."* And when he unlocked **Timber Sentinel**, the delight was pure: *"I make trees now. Probably the best class feat in the game — just cast a spell at will."* He put the boss fight to bed by **overkilling the weasel** at double its max HP. *"Goodbye."*

### Kaelen Ashford · Have Bonk, Will Travel
Kaelen played half the night around job applications and laptop lag, near-silent through stretches — and still posted **two one-shot kills**: the 29-damage jinkin crit and the swarm-ending Flurry crit (*"Have Bonk will travel"*). His comedy is the load-bearing kind — and he frames it as deliberate, not distraction (*"monks are supposed to have some sense of humor"*): he's the one who deflates every monologue. He told the cookware claiming to rule the town, *"No, you ain't. You're too small and short,"* and stuck to the bit an hour later (*"I told you that guy was too short to be their leader"*). He met the weasel with *"So we're fighting a damn weasel? … Let the weasel talk. Fuck it — what's its health?"* But he's not only the punchline: when Mo Douqiu tried the "aren't we the same" gambit, Kaelen drew the line cleanly — *"We bring what we bring to the table. And you bring chaos with you. Get the hell out."* He settled his build this session too, committing to **Staff Acrobat** because bonking is the plan.

### Kaoru · the Deadpan Blade
Quiet at the table, sharp every time he speaks. Kaoru carries the party's dry wit and its **South-Bank grudge**: on Granny Hu, *"She still needs to be bitch-slapped… watch out — bitch-slapper, with my tail."* He reads every room a half-beat ahead of the horror — *"It's gonna be hopping mad"* at the frog, *"It looks like we're late to the party — it's wrecked in here,"* and *"we just jump to the Death Star"* at the cliffhanger. He's also the party's quiet lore hand, walking Mu-Jin through **external digestion** to explain the corpses. Mechanically he's the **Spellstrike** specialist — he channels **Shocking Grasp (2d12) through the ninjato** and used exactly that to **end the scorpion** in one burst. To Mo Douqiu's "haven't you been having fun?" he had the cleanest reply in the room: *"No. I think you need a new definition of fun."*

### Mu-Jin · the Support Mage Who Came Back
Back after missing S2, and glad to be running his own game plan. Mu-Jin is unapologetically the **support mage** — Nudge Fate on Kaoru nearly every round, Familiar of Balanced Luck shaving enemy AC, Soothe when Kaoru cratered. His proudest moment was tactical and he said so at the table: dismount **Weaver**, send it in, land a Hex, drop the boss's AC — *"That's why I have familiars to do this sort of shit. Pet fight."* (He picked **Familiar Master** precisely so Weaver can survive being sent out.) But his best beat was human: faced with the flayed bodies he recoiled in genuine horror — asking whether something had *eaten and digested them, then spat them back out* — and then insisted on doing it right: cover them, don't presume to burn them, **notify the families** and Granny Hu, since he didn't know the village's customs for the dead and wouldn't presume. He also opened the boss scene trying diplomacy first: *"No entertainment, Yokai. You're an unwanted guest in our village, and we ask you to leave."*

## Combat Encounters

**E1 — The Drunken Jinkin** *(the mud-walled house · ~6 jinkin, AC 12, clubs 1d6−2)*
- Round 1: Mu-Jin opened with Nudge Fate + AC support on Kaoru. Kaoru struck (ninjato). Zhen opened two doors and dropped **Earth's Bile** on a cluster (fail/crit/fail on the saves; 6/6 + persistent fire). The **GM's nat-20 barrage** began — with the jinkin at effectively +0 to hit, the only strikes that could connect were auto-crits, and **three club crits all landed on Kaoru** (4, 8, 1 = 13), taking him to **3 HP (Near Death)**. Iryoku's thrown fire blast tagged one.
- **Kaelen bo-staff (crit + a second hit → 29) → massive-damage instant kill.**
- Round 2: **Mu-Jin's Soothe** healed Kaoru (7). Kaoru flew in (Sky Laughs at Waves) and struck. **Zhen's Needle Darts (9, his divine cantrip) killed a second jinkin**; Earth's Bile mopped up the survivors (all half-damage saves).
- **Threat:** all incoming damage landed on **Kaoru** (~14, to **3 HP**), all of it from nat-20 auto-crits. Party HP pool at L1 is tiny — this "trivial" fight was the second-most dangerous of the night. **XP 96.**

**E2 — Cockroach Swarm** *(the slaughtered stables · 1 swarm)*
- **Kaelen Flurry:** first strike missed (GM spent a hero point → hit), second strike **crit → 23 rolled, 21 after resistance → dead.** One round, no damage taken. **XP 48.**

**E3 — Animated Cookware** *(3 animated objects: Slice-and-Pound, Engulf/Constrict, Construct Armor)*
- Iryoku's Make an Impression *succeeded* (hero-pointed 9→18 vs DC 15) — but the party refused the "parade" demand and rolled initiative anyway. Combat opened with a **Slice and Pound** AoE (everyone saved, ~1 each).
- **Engulf object swallowed Kaelen** — failed Reflex, then two Fortitude saves (Awful Engulf + Constrict), to **Badly Injured** (~10 damage, losing air) — until it died and dumped him out ("I landed on my face… thumbs up, I'm okay").
- Kills: **Iryoku** solo-killed one with a **crit Elemental Blast (10)**; **Kaoru's Phase Bolt (9)** dropped the engulfer (freeing Kaelen); **Kaelen** finished the third after **Zhen** cracked its construct armor with a nodachi hit.
- ~12 total damage taken (~10 of it on the engulfed Kaelen). **XP 112.** Loot: +1 silver dagger, oil of potency ×2, potency crystal ×2, **Mesmerizing Opal**.

**E4 — The Giant Frog** *(Cerulean Teahouse, ground floor · 1 frog, AC 19)*
- The frog **tongue-disarmed Zhen** (crit; nodachi knocked away) and **tongue-grabbed** him; Zhen **failed both Escapes** and only came free when the frog died.
- Chip from the whole party (Needle Darts, Ray of Frost, Elemental Blast, bo-staff, Earth's Bile) softened it; **Mu-Jin's Needle Darts crit for 22 → dead.**
- **The frog dealt no HP damage** — its whole turn was disarm + grab. **XP 48.**

**E5 — Variant Forest Giant Scorpion** *(teahouse upstairs · 1 scorpion, AC 18)* — *(missing from the shared init list)*
- Hidden; **Zhen's Seek (25)** beat its Stealth and denied the ambush. **Zhen's burst combo** (Nodachi 10 + Harm + Earth's Bile) and **Kaoru's Shocking Grasp Spellstrike (1d6 + 2d12 = 23) → dead.**
- All Recall Knowledge rolls whiffed in the moment ("none of you know… a coward"), but the GM revealed it anyway: a **variant forest scorpion**, lower-level than standard, with forest scorpion venom. No damage taken. **XP 32.**

**E6 — Mo Douqiu the Hedonist + the Weasel** *(the boss · rokurokubi AC 19 + weasel AC 17)*
- **Round 1:** Mo Douqiu **Demoralized Iryoku (Frightened 1)** and bit him (10 + bleed → Injured), then his Reactive Strike bit Zhen (10 + bleed). **Drop Corpse** rained a hanging body on Iryoku. The weasel bit **Weaver** (6) and failed its grab. The party answered: Kaoru ninjato + Breathe Fire, Iryoku's big blast (15 → Badly Injured), Kaelen bo-staff (9 → Near Death on the boss), Mu-Jin's **Elemental Betrayal (fire weakness)** + AC drop via Weaver.
- **Round 2:** the **persistent bleed** ground on — **Zhen, Iryoku, and Kaoru all failed recovery** (four failed DC-15 flat checks across the fight), the one threat the party couldn't shrug off. Mo Douqiu demoralized and bit **Kaoru**; the weasel bit **Iryoku (8 → Near Death)** and crit-failed its grab. **Zhen killed Mo Douqiu** — stepping to an off-guard angle and landing the nodachi (9 → dead). **Iryoku killed the weasel** with a two-action melee blast (14, well past its remaining HP); Kaoru's Foxfire crit (4) had set it up.
- **Threat:** ~64 damage across the party (Iryoku 25, Zhen 18, Kaoru 15, Weaver 6) — nearly half the L2 HP pool, plus stacked bleed. **Iryoku hit Near Death; Zhen and Kaoru were driven to Badly Injured.** The only fight all session that behaved like a fight. Cleaned up afterward by **Zhen's Garden of Healing** (10+ ticks). **XP 72.**

## Pax's Notes — Zhen Stillness's Journal

*Private notes, observations, and things to think about. Pax's notes only — not shared with the rest of the party.*

### What Zhen did this session
- Ran **Earth's Bile** as the workhorse in every fight (persistent-fire chip, sustained), swapping to **Garden of Healing** to clean up between and after.
- Took a beating in the teahouse: **tongue-disarmed and grabbed** by the frog (failed two Escapes), **bitten and bled** by Mo Douqiu. Ended the session standing.
- **Killed Mo Douqiu the Hedonist** — stepped to an off-guard angle to shave his AC, then cut him down with the wrapped nodachi. First boss kill of the campaign.
- Steered **Kaelen** off Spirit Warrior (redundant with Monk/Flurry) toward Staff Acrobat; pointed the GM at a lightweight Foundry client.
- Handled the dead with respect — pushed to **notify the families** and Granny Hu/Old Matsuki rather than burn the bodies.

### Things Zhen knows that the party does not
- Still carrying the **wrong-gods secret** from S2: the Eternal Lantern was relit with Abadar/Catephis/Pharasma, not the legendary Calistria/Kofusachi/Shelyn. Granny Hu suspects; Zhen has not confirmed it. Whether the ward holds is unknown.
- His **nodachi was his mother's** — an oni's blade, still wrapped, drawn now to kill a yokai in a room full of the yokai's victims. He does not know its history. He does know it cut the boss down cleanly.
- The **persistent bleed** was the only thing that hurt the party. Note it: our answer to ongoing damage is thin — flat DC-15 recovery, and we kept failing it. Hero points don't fix a flat check.

### What Zhen is thinking about
- The fights are still easy — even the boss only bit because of bleed, not because we were outmatched. Consider **not burning Shield/hero points** on one-round fights and holding them for whatever the stranger at the edge of town turns out to be.
- **Fumeiyoshi.** A deity of envy and undeath, and now his name is on a dead aristocrat's will and on two ponds outside town. The teahouse deed is a lure; the deity is the thread. Watch it.
- The **stranger at the outskirts.** Mo Douqiu called this a horror story he authored. If he was only a chapter, someone else is holding the pen.

### Open questions for next session
- Where are the **two pearls in Fumeiyoshi's eyes**, and what happens — to us and to the ward — when we pull them out of a death-god's ponds?
- Who is the **figure watching Willowshore**, and how far away is "far away"?
- With downtown reclaimed, does the town start to recover — or does clearing the last yokai just move the story to its next act?
