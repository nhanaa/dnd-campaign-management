## Session Summary

Iryoku gave the recap, and the GM walked the party over the last crest of the Pilgrim's Path into the caldera of a long-dormant volcano. At a small shrine by the final wooden arch they found **seven magic items** (among them a monk's **Spirit Fan**, which Kaelen took, a **Ghost Scarf** for Mu-Jin, **Root Boots** for Iryoku, and a **Resolute Mind Wrap** for Zhen). Then they came to the **Tan Sugi Monastery**, the temple of Kaelen's own faith, Sangpotshi. It is in ruins under the giant sugi tree. The protective fulus on the gate had been deliberately shredded, and Mu-Jin was the one who asked.

At the first mold-covered shrine a ghost rose, lamented her death, and loosed ghostly tigers on the party (16 to each of Zhen, Mu-Jin and Iryoku). Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge named her: **Yuni, Yeri's sister.** Zhen critted her with the nodachi on a natural 20, and Kaelen's Inner Upheaval Flurry destroyed her in one round. As she faded she reached for **Kaelen**, who was carrying the Sangpotshi charm taken off Yeri. To lay her to rest, the party has to give her the charm and tell her what became of her sister, but only after she re-forms in **six days**.

Mu-Jin scraped the black mold off a **Kodama Kami** statue, and the ghost of the monastery's founder, **Zhi Hui**, appeared and told them the truth of the Willowshore curse: *"It is no mere curse that traps you in Willowshore. Time itself has been stolen from you."* Willowshore is a **cycling mindscape** that repeats every year from the first day of summer to the last day of spring, with everyone's memory reset. The governor, **Heh Shan-Bao**, tried to bolster the Tan Sugi, cracked **Kugaptee's** grave instead, and was whisked out of the loop himself. *Something is different this cycle.* Mu-Jin: *"Okay, so just to summarize, we're in a fucking time loop."*

In the refectory, three **monks of Kugaptee** (Mu-Jin: *"Kaelen's future if he keeps messing up his vows"*) asked only for tea. Zhen's Sense Motive said they meant no harm, and Zhen alone found the hidden cache in the pantry. Mu-Jin weighed poisoning the tea with ghost charges, then chose to do it honestly. His tea ceremony, aided by Iryoku and boosted by a Tin of Rare Tea, was a **critical success**. The monks crumbled to dust after telling the party where they had hidden the **head of Pharasma's statue: in the upper boughs of the sugi tree.** **96 XP** for no combat.

The dormitory held a toad-spider with a woman's head in its cage-mouth (**Guhdggi**) and five fawning underlings (**Meokdan**). Nobody could identify them. Mu-Jin told them the party's story, *"a tale for a tale,"* while Iryoku and Kaelen saw them licking their lips. Iryoku's Blazing Wave opened. **Zhen's Earth's Bile and Fireball killed four of the five underlings**, Kaoru's Fireball the fifth, and Kaelen teleported in with his new **Shrink the Span** and finished the big one. Mu-Jin never got a turn. Zhen: *"We literally just burned down this place."* A **Spiritsight Ring** from the room went to Zhen.

The greenhouse was the long fight: two **slime molds** and six **fungus leshies** that heal each other when they die, in difficult terrain everywhere. It took four rounds, the leshies punched the monk (*"How's it going to beat me in my own damn game?"*), and the kills were spread across all five PCs. The stag statue there brought Zhi Hui back once more. Asked how to escape, she said: **find Heh Shan-Bao** once Kugaptee's influence is weakened, and *"Take the hidden library. My friend Yen Rui can help."*

Behind a secret door, the disembodied **Yen Rui**, the last loyal monk, who died of thirst after traitors sealed her in, told the rest. **Kugaptee is a Nindoru fiend** opposed to everything Sangpotshi teaches (the GM: *"Kaelen's effective mortal enemy"*). He was defeated by **Tan Sui Jing**, whose soul became the sugi tree that seals him. The tree is sickening as he strives for rebirth. Zhi Hui's student **Xin Yue** was corrupted and *"toils below in his grave to see the fiend's soul reborn,"* and the fiends here are desecrating the monastery's statues to amplify him. The library is safe to rest in. It also held a haul of rare rank-4 scrolls. The session ended with the plan set: get Pharasma's head out of the sugi tree, then the remaining statues. Afterwards the table talked about raising the difficulty, and the GM agreed to start adjusting encounters.
