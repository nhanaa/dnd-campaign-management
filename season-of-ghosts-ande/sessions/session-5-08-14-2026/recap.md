## Session Summary

The chore list finally ran dry — and the campaign's real shape came into focus. The party opened by finishing what S4 started: they secured the **abandoned village as Willowshore's emergency farmland**, dove the **Eyes of Fumeiyoshi** (fighting off the hunting spiders nesting in the right eye) and surfaced with **both pearls** — making them **eligible to claim the Cerulean Tea House as their own business**. On the road home Zhen's hero-pointed Fortitude save landed on exactly DC 20 and **cleared his Slime Rot**. Then the white whale: after S4's two failed persuasion days, **the Desna shrine finally moved** — Mu-Jin, the party's would-be Desna convert, talked the kami around at last (Diplomacy 20 with Weaver's Bolstering Aid), and Zhen, Iryoku, and Kaelen hauled the shrine down to the Milling Houses by nightfall. A day and a half of curing the blight at the **Leshy's Salon** earned the party a future tea wholesaler, courtesy of the agender leshy **Mountain Summit Grass**.

Then Granny Hu and Old Matsuki — cooperating, of all things — summoned the party to the Lady of Souls and laid out **the three standing mysteries**: the vanished governor and his estate, the mists walling in the hinterlands, and the disappearance of Ugly Cute. With them came the campaign's darkest backstory beat yet: late last year, the governor had **a hunted fugitive and her allies** cornered at the **Canary Inn**, a roadside shelter northeast of town — and the standoff ended in a fire that some whisper *the governor's own guards started*. The fugitive's dying song: *"All those who serve Lung Wa will soon know how it feels to lose it all."*

The party worked the leads in order: the Silvermist hunters (where **Sumika**, the one-eyed elven ranger — most likely the unnamed Hunter from the S2 relief sweep, finally named — pointed them toward the **Gorge of Fangs and Teeth**, where Ugly Cute's stone was quarried), then the **Great Willow** — the town's guardian kami, found **infested with gray fungus** and fighting it back, who begged the party to save her friend in an infested grove to the south from a "foul-tempered mushroom." Finally, at night, the burned-out Canary Inn — where a young girl in a red hood sang to three seated gray-hooded figures, and only Mu-Jin's Perception pierced the illusion: **thatchlings**, undead effigies of dried grass and ghostly ectoplasm, straight out of the folktales he collects. A performance from Iryoku bought one question; the answer ("your governor *chose* to leave you") bought a fight. The party destroyed all four, gave the three unburied skeletons proper rites — Zhen's idea, true to Pharasma — and the grateful dead yielded **Hongrui's Gratitude**, a red-hooded cloak whose inscription names the ghost at the heart of it all: **Fan Hongrui**, a performer — one of four travelers who met their tragic end at that inn. The governor lead is a dead end; the Canary Inn rests a little easier; and the infested grove and the Gorge are next.

## Key Events

**Act I — Farmland Secured (day 6)**
- Two four-hour work blocks at the abandoned village banked the **8 Victory Points** needed to certify it for emergency winter crops — Zhen's critical success led the first block, and Kaoru's final check sealed it. **20 XP, +1 South Bank reputation.**
- GM ruling of note: the **blessed lantern coins are permanent** ("these blessed coins are permanent"), not seasonal — Zhen's crit engine keeps running.
- The party camped on-site rather than ride home — "eerily quiet, especially near the thick fog" — and cut through the forest toward the Eyes of Fumeiyoshi at dawn.

**Act II — The Eyes of Fumeiyoshi (day 7)**
- The GM's lore drop: two brackish pools in bowl-shaped depressions where nothing grows, said to be where **Fumeiyoshi — god of envy, graves, infamy, and undead, patron deity of evil oni** — once looked out at the world. His symbol: a red demonic face.
- **Hunting spiders** had taken up residence in the right eye (combat below). After clearing them, all five PCs passed the DC 10 swim checks (three crits), and the underwater **search** (Perception DC 15 — Iryoku and Mu-Jin both at 22, Kaoru hero-pointing in a third success) turned up **both pearls** — worth 10 gp each, but worth far more as the keys to **Qing Mai-Lai's will**: the party is now **eligible to claim the Cerulean Tea House as their business**.
- On the ride home, Zhen's last **Slime Rot** save came due: a hero-pointed reroll landed on **exactly 20 vs DC 20** — cured. *"I'm too old for this."* (Kaelen's S4 off-mic infection never came up on-screen — status unknown.)

**Act III — The Shrine Moves (day 7, evening)**
- New day, new kami attempt — and **it finally worked.** Iryoku went first with Weaver's Bolstering Aid (+2) and a hero point; even with the house-rule floor-to-10 his 18 *barely* failed. Then **Mu-Jin** — the man who realized last session he's never had a deity and has his eye on Desna — took over with Weaver bolstering *him*, and rolled a **20: success.** "That is finally, after days of trying to persuade this thing… a success."
- The physical move (5 VP, Athletics/Religion, four hours per check) went in one pass: Zhen and Iryoku banked the first points and **Kaelen's Athletics finished it**. The **Desna shrine now stands at the Milling Houses** on the south bank — Choe Chung-hu's ask, delivered.
- Mu-Jin went home to the Silvermist Lodges; Zhen went home feeling every year of his back. *"Zhen can feel his back hurting from all the work he had to do for this."*

**Act IV — The Leshy's Salon (days 8–9)**
- Word of the pearls spread overnight; the **Leshy's Salon**'s keeper — the agender leshy **Mountain Summit Grass** — asked for help with a **blight** spreading through their plants. 10 VP, Nature or Farming Lore, eight hours per check.
- The party ground it out across a day and a half (hero points burning on the margins — Kaoru blew two on back-to-back failures and a success-by-zero), finishing the morning of day 9. **20 XP** — and when the Cerulean Tea House opens, **the Leshy's Salon will supply the tea.**

**Act V — The Elders' Briefing (day 9, midday)**
- A missive summoned the party to the **Lady of Souls**, where **Granny Hu and Old Matsuki were cooperating** — a sight in itself. Their charge: investigate the town's **three standing mysteries** — (1) the **vanished governor and his estate** (most pressing), (2) the **mists** (map their extent; consult the **Great Willow**), (3) **Ugly Cute** (see the Silvermist hunters).
- The Canary Inn backstory, told at last: near the end of last year the governor had **a dangerous fugitive and her allies** tracked to the Canary Inn. In the standoff, a **fire erupted** — officially the fugitive's doing, but more than a few claim **the governor and his guards started it** to force them into the open. As the building burned, the fugitive **sang out a promise**: *all those who serve Lung Wa will soon know how it feels to lose it all.*
- At the **Silvermist Lodges**, **Sumika** — the one-eyed elven ranger, evidently the unnamed Hunter Old Matsuki sent the party to find back in S2, now named — reported the hunters' tracking of Ugly Cute has gone nowhere, even the old trick of returning to the quarry's home. Recall Knowledge reframed it: the Spider Gate was a **posting, not a home** — and Ugly Cute's stone was originally **quarried from the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth**, south of town. A lead.

**Act VI — The Great Willow (day 9, afternoon)**
- The party bought sake (the elders' recommended offering) and climbed the trail-less hill north of town. The Great Willow — 90 feet of the only willow in the forest — showed **gray fungal growth infesting its lower roots**, and its spirit **emerged fists-raised, defensive**, until the offering and the party's respect calmed it (Iryoku's hero-pointed check; Mu-Jin's sake laid down reverently).
- The kami — tiny, childlike voice, slow and careful words — explained: the fungus is *"an infection I brought back from a visit to check on a friend to the south. I am fighting it back, but I fear for my friend."* Posted in full via Foundry: **the curse is a cage.** On the first day of summer the Willow was suddenly alone — couldn't hear its friends' voices, jumped to its closest friend's grove and found **"a foul-tempered mushroom"** in her place, and had to flee home *on foot*, tracking invasive spores with it. It dare not leave the hill again, but it can search for the curse's edges from home — **after** the party saves its friend from the infested grove.
- Mu-Jin's Nature success earned techniques to fight the fungal infection — and the party the Willow's gratitude. **20 XP.** The infested grove sits **right next to the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth** — two leads, one trip.

**Act VII — The Canary Inn (day 9, night)**
- The party took the main road northeast (skirting the mist wall on the way — it still holds), arriving after dark to **a young girl's song about butterflies and dreams** drifting over the burned bungalows — sung to **three gray-hooded figures seated facing away from her**.
- Perception DC 22: only **Mu-Jin** (exactly 22) pierced the **magical disguise** — the "girl" is a **blood-soaked mobile effigy of dried grass held together by wisps of ghostly ectoplasm**. He widened his eyes and cautioned the party to hold back: *"That is not a young woman."* Recall Knowledge named it: a **red-hooded thatchling**, an undead, unholy variant of the **thatchlings** the party had destroyed on the road hours earlier (below) — creatures Mu-Jin knew only from old folktales.
- The thatchling stopped singing, pointed at Iryoku — the only trained performer — and asked: **"You here to entertain us? It's been so long since we've had others to perform for us."** Iryoku's expert Performance (22 vs DC 17 — clean, no aid needed; the GM confirmed Weaver's late Bolstering Aid wouldn't have changed it) satisfied her, and she offered the party **one question**.
- Iryoku asked about the governor. Her expression darkened (Zhen and Mu-Jin's Sense Motive: **anger, intensifying**): *"He was a lapdog of Lung Wa and an enemy."* Mu-Jin tried a follow-up — why such hostility to Lung Wa? — and was refused: the party had spent its one question. Her parting words: *"Remember, those in power will always abuse or abandon their responsibility, given time. Be grateful your governor chose to leave you rather than inflict upon your town something worse. **Now leave.**"* Zhen pressed — "Wait, so you do know about his whereabouts?" — and argued for answers over execution ("press for more first before you decide to off with the head"; was she a *victim*?) against Mu-Jin's "I think we should still kill her." The party stood its ground. Initiative.
- After the fight (below): Mu-Jin's follow-up Recall Knowledge on the thatchlings' origin failed twice (hero point spent; repeat-RK DCs climb), but the party found **shallow graves and three unburied skeletons**. **Zhen insisted on proper burial** — and as the rites finished, a sudden sense of **gratitude** washed over the party. With one skeleton lay a red cloak: **Hongrui's Gratitude** (item 3) — its description finally naming the ghost: **Fan Hongrui**, an elaborately dressed performer whose two companions held her lantern and umbrella, one of *"four unfortunate travelers who met a tragic end in the Willowshore Hinterlands."* **40 XP.**
- The GM's closing: this line of investigation is **a dead end** for finding the governor — but the Canary Inn now feels *"a little less haunted."* Mu-Jin's working theory, seconded by Zhen: the four thatchlings **were** the four fugitives — their ghosts riding straw effigies since the fire. And the red-hood's words leave a splinter: did the governor *choose* to leave?

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Keeper of Rites
The session's quiet through-line was Zhen doing exactly what Willowshore keeps him around for. His defining beat came last: standing in a burned-out inn over three unburied skeletons, the town's tender-of-the-dead said simply, *"I think we should at least keep them all properly buried then"* — and that small Pharasman kindness, not a die roll, is what turned four hostile ghosts into a gift and a name. Before that he'd carried the workman's session on his back — literally, by his own telling: *"Zhen can feel his back hurting from all the work he had to do for this"* — leading the farmland certification with a blessed-coin crit, banking shrine-moving points, grinding the salon blight (*"Nothing is too hard for this old man"* — Kaelen: "Are you sure your back is still 80?"), and finally shaking the **Slime Rot** with a hero-pointed save that landed on exactly 20 (*"I'm too old for this"*). In combat he had his best night of the campaign: he opened the thatchling ambush by dropping **Earth's Bile on all four** without hesitation (*"Yeah, all four, actually… I'm not gonna play stupid"*), which single-handedly revealed their fire weakness and bludgeoning resistance; he pivoted to **Vitality Lash** the moment Mu-Jin's RK came back "undead" and finished the last roadside thatchling with it; he debuted **Electric Arc** (his new Storm-druid cantrip — he'd gone spell-shopping at daily prep for "a more fun spell"); and at the Canary Inn he landed **his first-ever nodachi crit**, killing a gray thatchling outright (*"Oh my god, my first crit with my Nodachi"*). He was also the table's positioning conscience — talking Kaelen out of blocking his burst (*"Give me space, or I'm going to blast you as well"*) — and its skeptical ear: his Will save shrugged off the thatchlings' curse (*"No more giggling in my head. Get out of my head"*), and his was the question that kept the scene alive: *"Wait, so you do know about his whereabouts?"* Notably, in the last breath before initiative, Zhen was the one arguing **mercy-first** — press her about *why* she hates Lung Wa, ask whether she was a victim, before deciding to "off with the head" — the same instinct that later buried her bones properly.

### Mu-Jin · the Folklorist Vindicated
If S4 was Mu-Jin's social-engine session, S5 was the one where his whole character concept paid off at once. **He solved the Desna shrine** — the Face failure that ate two days of S4 — rolling the Diplomacy 20 himself with Weaver bolstering, a beat made sweeter by the S4 thread that he's considering Desna as his first deity: the would-be convert is the one her kami finally listened to. At the Great Willow he was reverence itself: *"Mu-Jin will reach into his bag, pull out some sake, and reverently place it down as an offering."* And at the Canary Inn his two lanes converged — his Perception (exactly 22) was the **only** one to pierce the red-hood's disguise, and his Recall Knowledge named the thatchlings from the folktales he collects, an arc he narrated himself: *"I made it a hobby of mine to learn old folk tales, mostly because I just enjoy them. But now, for better or worse… maybe that's actually going to come in handy."* His horror-genre metagame ran all night (*"I play too many Resident Evil games. Why is a young woman singing the scariest fucking thing in the world?"*), his pessimism stayed on-brand (*"Alright, brand new day, guys. Let's see what new horrors visit upon the tiny village of Willowshore"*), and his hero-point wealth became a running bit — "as always, I'll use a hero point if I fail." In combat he ran the full witch toolkit: the nat-20 initiative that triggered the GM's four-action house rule, the Weaver dismount + Elemental Betrayal + Balanced Luck setup, Phase Familiar negating a spider's fangs on Weaver entirely, the Needle Darts that finished the last spider — and the three-action **Force Barrage** that dropped Fan Hongrui herself (*"Man, that is shit damage. Oh, I killed it."*). He closed the night theorizing the fugitive-ghost connection and, chillingly, that Willowshore might be the *safe* spot: "maybe everyone else is fucked with ghosts, and we're just getting the leftovers."

### Kaelen Ashford · the Unbeliever
Kaelen's session was the monk id, distilled. Handed a supernatural fear aura mid-charge, he produced the line of the night: *"I'm sorry, I don't believe in these giggles. So I'm going to attack immediately"* — and backed it up, Flurrying the gray-hoods flat. He was the party's execution arm all session: the hero-pointed crit on the hunting spider, a Flurry kill in the roadside ambush, **two more kills at the Canary Inn** — a flanking Flurry takedown in round 1 ("Holy shit") and the crit-plus-hit that deleted the last thatchling in round 2 — and the Athletics check that **physically finished the shrine move** the party had been chasing since S4. His comedy stayed load-bearing: petting Kaoru on the head mid-combat (*"Man, I'm sorry, but before I move, I pet Kaoru on the head"* — the kitsune reveal continues to go acknowledged and unexplained), the XP audit (*"We're exactly at 666 experience, guys. Oh my God, we're about to summon the devil"*), "Someone call Ghostbusters," the Preston Garvey groan at the salon quest, and a real-life mid-session boss fight against his roommate's cat ("Alright, cat has been evicted" — Mu-Jin). Even his tech gremlins arc improved — he switched Foundry to performance mode and finished the night lag-free. And give him the diplomatic credit too: seconds before initiative he was still trying — *"Ma'am, we're just trying to get answers to something."*

### Kaoru · the Deadpan Vanguard
A quiet-volume, high-hit-rate session. His pre-fight speech at the ghost picnic was peak Kaoru: *"Draw your sword, change your mask and change your underwear."* His skepticism stayed a half-step ahead of the table — *"I don't think it's alone"* about the red-hood (it wasn't), "There's nobody to ask. It's an abandoned village" when the farmland planning got ahead of itself, and the gentle close-out on Mu-Jin's loose threads: "I'm sure it'll present itself." In the roadside ambush he drew first blood among the killers — a fire cantrip into the thatchlings' freshly-revealed weakness took the first one off the board ("Just blast it to kingdom come," Zhen urged, and he did). He spent the downtime grind as the party's good-humored fall guy — burning two hero points at the salon and coining his own epitaph, *"First you don't succeed, keep on sucking until you do succeed"* — then paid one more at the Canary Inn trying to Ignition the red-hood (a reroll of exactly 10, one short: "I tried"). His table-coach mode ran quietly all night: narrating Kaelen's monk for him ("He's going to walk up and say, I punched a spider"), steering his positioning ("You don't have enough to step up and punch the next one"), calling the whack-a-mole. And his instinct for the dead was the session's best unused idea: *"If I got Speak with Dead, maybe we can find out what happened"* — offered right as Zhen's burial rites were answering the same question a different way.

### Iryoku-Yasha · the Performer
A low-volume session with the single most consequential skill check in it. When the red-hooded thatchling stopped singing, she pointed at exactly one member of the party: the expert performer. Iryoku took the stage — *"that juicy expert proficiency performance check,"* per the GM — rolled a clean 22, and turned an ambush into an audience: the party's one question, and everything the ghost said after, flowed from his performance. He asked the governor question. He also opened the session (recap duty, as ever), lawyered the quickened-condition rules correctly ("That's haste. Quicken is not naturally limited"), and fought a tidy artillery war: Timber Sentinel + weapon-infusion reach blasts in the spider fight (one kill), the propulsive blast that dropped a roadside thatchling into its fire weakness, and a **max-damage** two-action melee blast that left Fan Hongrui "barely" standing for Mu-Jin to finish. His hero-point philosophy stayed communal: *"I'll use a hero point if everyone fails."* And the cloak that names the session went to him — Hongrui's Gratitude, +1 Diplomacy under the lantern: the Face gets a little brighter.

## Combat Encounters

**E1 — Hunting Spiders at the Right Eye** *(Eyes of Fumeiyoshi · 3 hunting spiders, AC 17, Ref +9, venom on hit · 2 rounds · the fight's opening rolls predate the surviving log)*

*Initiative: Mu-Jin (nat 20 → 4 actions via house rule) → Zhen → spiders (two of them also rolled nat 20s) → Kaoru → Iryoku → Kaelen. New GM house rule revealed: nat-20 initiative grants 4 actions in round 1; nat-1 starts you slowed 1.*

- **Round 1** — **Mu-Jin** (quickened): dismounted **Weaver**, hexed a spider with **Elemental Betrayal** (fire weakness 2), **Familiar of Balanced Luck** for −1 AC, and hit **Needle Darts** for 10 ("badly injured, but still up"). **Zhen** debuted **Electric Arc** (his new Druid-dedication cantrip — the GM noting his pilgrim's token "is helping you here") on two spiders, then dropped **Earth's Bile** over both — the GM noted Foundry failed to apply the fire weakness on the hexed one. The **spiders** turned on Weaver: one fangs hit landed and forced the **venom Fortitude save** (passed), a second hit for 4 was **fully negated by Mu-Jin's Phase Familiar** (resistance 5) — "Weaver takes no damage" — and the rest missed (Weaver AC 18, off-guard 16). One spider dropped dead mid-phase — **Zhen's Earth's Bile catching up with it** (his kill, whichever tick did it). **Kaoru** cast his own **Electric Arc** — both spiders saved (one by +9) for 2 and 2 — and raised **Shield**. **Iryoku** planted a **Timber Sentinel** protector tree, then a **weapon-infusion reach fire blast** hit for 8 — enough to finish that spider. **Kaelen** hero-pointed his opening bo-staff strike into a **crit** (2 → 18 on the reroll) for 10, then missed at MAP −5 and whiffed a third swing at −10 for a **total of 1** after the table talked him into trying anyway — on a die of 2, so Kaoru's "Hey, at least it wasn't a one — then I'd say you'd be hunting a new bo" held. Barely.
- **Round 2** — **Mu-Jin** cast **Nudge Fate** on himself, then killed the last spider with **Needle Darts** for 9. Fight over before anyone else's turn came up.
- **Aftermath**: no PC took a scratch — Weaver absorbed the fight's only real hit. Zhen swapped vessels to **Garden of Healing** ("I guess we need to heal the familiar") and pulsed the group back up (10d4 = 19), then swapped back to Earth's Bile. **72 XP.**

**E2 — Thatchling Ambush on the North Road** *(road to the Canary Inn · 4 thatchlings — undead, unholy, weak fire 5, resistant bludgeoning; a giggling herald and a targeted "Thatchling's Curse" (Will) · 2 rounds)*

- **Round 1** — **Zhen** won initiative, stepped up into the giggling ("Totally not creepy at all"), and committed immediately: **Earth's Bile centered on all four** (*"I'm not gonna play stupid"*) — three failed, one saved, and the spell's damage readout handed the party both defensive facts at once: **weak to fire, resistant to bludgeoning**. His follow-up nodachi swing scratched one. The thatchlings' aura answered: Zhen saved against the muttering nonsense (−5 speed for a round on the success). **Kaoru** — after a rules sidebar on Arcane Cascade and fire damage ("Fire can trigger the weakness," Zhen advised) — **killed the first thatchling** with a fire cantrip into the weakness (*"Just blast it to kingdom come"* — "That one is dead"; his exact rolls fell in a lost stretch of the log). **Kaelen** refused the fear outright — *"I don't believe in these giggles. So I'm going to attack immediately"* — and his **Flurry of Blows killed another** (21 rolled, 19 through the bludgeoning resistance), though a third reach swing at MAP −10 went nowhere. **Mu-Jin**'s free investigate-RK named them — **thatchlings**, undead and unholy, folklore made real — then his Needle Darts missed (15 vs AC 16) and he shielded up. **Iryoku**'s two-action **propulsive blast** (11 + fire weakness 5 = 16) **destroyed a third**; the last thatchling put its curse on **Kaelen**, who passed the Will save (with a −1 "Distracting Giggle" penalty riding the roll) at the cost of 5 feet of speed.
- **Round 2** — **Zhen**, now knowing they're undead, swapped ammunition: **Vitality Lash**. The last thatchling saved (19 vs DC 18) but the halved 7 → 3 was still enough — **destroyed**. Four thatchlings, four different killers — and the only physical strike the enemy managed (at Zhen) missed: **the party took zero damage.**
- **Aftermath**: Mu-Jin — folklore-brained and cautious — had the effigies piled up and asked Iryoku to **burn the bodies** before moving on. **64 XP.**

**E3 — Fan Hongrui at the Canary Inn** *(3 gray-hooded thatchlings + the Red-Hooded Thatchling — variant, undead/unholy, magical disguise, fire weakness 5 · 2 rounds · the fight's first minutes predate the surviving log; Pax stepped away briefly after his own turn — "im back" mid-round-1 — and missed only Iryoku's and Kaoru's turns)*

- **Round 1** — A thatchling's curse targeted **Zhen** first: he **saved**, earning 24-hour immunity (*"No more giggling in my head"* — GM: "Oh no, the giggling's still there"). A gray-hood put the same muttering on **Kaelen** (−5 speed), who answered by Flurrying it down anyway with flanking — *"Apparently they're weak. Nice."* ("It's only because of flanking.") "Holy shit." — and spent his last action on a **bo-staff parry**. **Zhen** slashed another gray-hood with the nodachi and rolled **his first nodachi crit — dead on the spot.** **Iryoku** rode up and put a **max-damage two-action melee fire blast** into the red-hood. **Kaoru** hero-pointed **Ignition** at her and rerolled to exactly 10 → 16, **one short of AC 17** ("I tried"). **Mu-Jin** sized her up — *"Oh shit, it's still alive."* "Barely." — and committed the full turn: **three-action Force Barrage**, three shards auto-hitting for 2 + 3 + 2 — the third shard **destroyed Fan Hongrui**. The last gray-hood slashed **Iryoku** with a frond (hit for 4; its MAP attacks missed).
- **Round 2** — **Kaelen**, after formally petting Kaoru on the head, moved into flanking and rolled a **crit** (23 vs flanked AC 13) plus a MAP hit on the last thatchling — 18 rolled, 16 through the resistance, **destroyed**. ("It's another critical?" — "Yes, it is.")
- **Aftermath**: hero points had refreshed at the top of the hour mid-fight; Mu-Jin burned one anyway failing the thatchling-origin RK twice. Search of the grounds (Kaoru's 22) found the **shallow graves and three unburied skeletons** → burial rites → **Hongrui's Gratitude**. **80 XP** (fight) + **40 XP** (laying the site to rest). Hero points reset to 1 at session end.
