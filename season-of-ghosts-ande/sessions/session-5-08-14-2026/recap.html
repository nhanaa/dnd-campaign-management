Session Summary

The chore list finally ran dry — and the campaign's real shape came into focus. The party finished what S4 started: they secured the abandoned village as Willowshore's emergency farmland, dove the Eyes of Fumeiyoshi (clearing out the hunting spiders nesting in the right eye) and surfaced with both pearls — making them eligible to claim the Cerulean Tea House as their own business. On the road home Zhen's hero-pointed Fortitude save landed on exactly DC 20 and cleared his Slime Rot. Then the white whale: after S4's two failed persuasion days, the Desna shrine finally moved — Mu-Jin, the party's would-be Desna convert, talked the kami around at last (Diplomacy 20 with Weaver's Bolstering Aid), and Zhen, Iryoku, and Kaelen hauled the shrine to the Milling Houses by nightfall. A day and a half curing the blight at the Leshy's Salon earned the party a future tea wholesaler, courtesy of the agender leshy Mountain Summit Grass.

Then Granny Hu and Old Matsuki — cooperating, of all things — summoned the party to the Lady of Souls and laid out the three standing mysteries: the vanished governor and his estate, the mists walling in the hinterlands, and the disappearance of Ugly Cute. With them came the campaign's darkest backstory beat yet: late last year, the governor had a hunted fugitive and her allies cornered at the Canary Inn — and the standoff ended in a fire some whisper the governor's own guards started. The fugitive's dying song: "All those who serve Lung Wa will soon know how it feels to lose it all."

The party worked the leads: the Silvermist hunters (where Sumika — the one-eyed elven ranger, most likely S2's unnamed Hunter finally named — pointed them toward the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth, where Ugly Cute's stone was quarried), then the Great Willow — the town's guardian kami, found infested with gray fungus and begging the party to save her friend from a "foul-tempered mushroom" in a grove to the south. Finally, at night, the burned-out Canary Inn — where a young girl in a red hood sang to three seated gray-hooded figures, and only Mu-Jin's Perception pierced the illusion: thatchlings, undead effigies of dried grass and ghostly ectoplasm, straight out of the folktales he collects. A performance from Iryoku bought one question; the answer ("your governor chose to leave you") bought a fight. The party destroyed all four, gave the three unburied skeletons proper rites — Zhen's idea, true to Pharasma — and the grateful dead yielded Hongrui's Gratitude, the red-hooded cloak that names the ghost at the heart of it all: Fan Hongrui, a performer — one of four travelers who met their tragic end at that inn. The governor lead is a dead end; the Canary Inn rests a little easier; the infested grove and the Gorge are next.

Timeline

Act I · Town · Farmland Secured (Day 6)

Eight Victory Points Before Nightfall

  • Two four-hour work blocks at the abandoned village banked the 8 Victory Points to certify it for emergency winter crops — Zhen's critical success led the first block, and Kaoru's final check sealed it. 20 XP, +1 South Bank reputation.
  • GM ruling of note: the blessed lantern coins are permanent — "these blessed coins are permanent" — not seasonal. Zhen's crit engine keeps running.
  • Kaelen nearly burned a hero point on the margin before the table talked him down; the party's insurance culture is now a running bit ("as always, I'll use a hero point if I fail" — Mu-Jin, all night).
  • The party camped on-site — "eerily quiet, especially near the thick fog" — and cut through the forest toward the Eyes of Fumeiyoshi at dawn.
Act II · Exploration · The Eyes of Fumeiyoshi (Day 7)

Swimming in a Death God's Eyes

  • The GM's lore drop: two brackish pools in bowl-shaped depressions where nothing grows — where Fumeiyoshi, god of envy, graves, infamy, and undead, patron deity of evil oni, once looked out at the world. His symbol: a red demonic face.
  • Hunting spiders had taken up residence in the right eye — cleared in two rounds (see Combat). Weaver took the fight's only hit; no PC was scratched.
  • All five PCs passed the DC 10 swim (three crits), and the underwater search (Perception DC 15 — Iryoku and Mu-Jin both at 22, Kaoru hero-pointing a third success) turned up both pearls: 10 gp each, but really the keys to Qing Mai-Lai's will. The party is now eligible to claim the Cerulean Tea House. 40 XP.
  • On the ride home, Zhen's last Slime Rot save came due: a hero-pointed reroll landed on exactly 20 vs DC 20 — cured. "I'm too old for this."
  • Unaddressed: Kaelen's S4 off-mic infection never came up. Status unknown.
Act III · Town · The Shrine Moves (Day 7, Evening)

The Kami Say Yes

  • New day, new attempt — and it finally worked. Iryoku went first with Weaver's Bolstering Aid (+2) and a hero point; even floored to a 10 by the house rule, his 18 barely failed.
  • Then Mu-Jin — the man who realized last session he's never had a deity, with his eye on Desna — took over with Weaver bolstering him, and rolled a 20: success. "That is finally, after days of trying to persuade this thing… a success."
  • The physical move (5 VP, Athletics or Religion) went in one pass: Zhen and Iryoku banked the points and Kaelen's Athletics finished it.
  • The Desna shrine now stands at the Milling Houses on the south bank — Choe Chung-hu's ask, delivered. Zhen went home feeling every year of his back: "Zhen can feel his back hurting from all the work he had to do for this."
Act IV · Town · The Leshy's Salon (Days 8–9)

A Blight and a Business Partner

  • Word of the pearls spread overnight; the Leshy's Salon's keeper — the agender leshy Mountain Summit Grass — asked for help with a blight spreading through their plants. 10 VP, Nature or Farming Lore, eight hours per check.
  • The party ground it out across a day and a half — hero points burning on the margins. Kaoru blew two on back-to-back attempts and a success-by-zero: "First you don't succeed, keep on sucking until you do succeed."
  • Morning of day 9: blight scoured. 20 XP — and when the Cerulean Tea House opens, the Leshy's Salon will supply the tea. (Kaelen: "She's going to be our wholesaler. Nice.")
  • Mu-Jin's morning mood-setter for the whole stretch: "Alright, brand new day, guys. Let's see what new horrors visit upon the tiny village of Willowshore."
Act V · Mystery · The Elders' Briefing (Day 9, Midday)

Three Mysteries and a Burned Inn

  • A missive summoned the party to the Lady of Souls, where Granny Hu and Old Matsuki were cooperating — a sight in itself. ("For now," said the GM.)
  • Their charge — the town's three standing mysteries: (1) the vanished governor and his estate (most pressing), (2) the mists (map their extent; consult the Great Willow), (3) Ugly Cute (see the Silvermist hunters).
  • The Canary Inn backstory, told at last: near the end of last year the governor had a dangerous fugitive and her allies tracked to the roadside inn northeast of town. In the standoff a fire erupted — officially the fugitive's doing, but more than a few claim the governor and his guards started it to force them into the open.
  • As the building burned, the fugitive sang out a promise: all those who serve Lung Wa will soon know how it feels to lose it all.
  • At the Silvermist Lodges, Sumika reported the hunters' tracking of Ugly Cute has gone nowhere — even the old trick of returning to the quarry's home. Recall Knowledge reframed it: the Spider Gate was a posting, not a home — and Ugly Cute's stone was originally quarried from the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth, south of town. A lead.
Act VI · Roleplay · The Great Willow (Day 9, Afternoon)

The Guardian Is Sick

  • The party bought sake (the elders' recommended offering) and climbed the trail-less hill north of town. The Great Willow — 90 feet of the only willow in the forest — showed gray fungal growth infesting its lower roots, and its spirit emerged fists raised, defensive.
  • The offering and the party's respect calmed it — Iryoku's hero-pointed check, and Mu-Jin's sake "reverently placed down as an offering."
  • The kami — tiny, childlike voice, slow careful words — explained: "An infection I brought back from a visit to check on a friend to the south. I am fighting it back, but I fear for my friend."
  • Posted in full via Foundry: the curse is a cage. On the first day of summer the Willow was suddenly alone — couldn't hear its friends' voices, jumped to its closest friend's grove and found "a foul-tempered mushroom" in her place, and had to flee home on foot, tracking invasive spores with it.
  • It dare not leave the hill again — but it can search for the curse's edges from home, after the party saves its friend. Mu-Jin's Nature success earned techniques against the fungal infection. 20 XP.
  • The infested grove sits right next to the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth. Two leads, one trip.
Act VII · Mystery · The Canary Inn (Day 9, Night)

The Red-Hooded Singer

  • The party took the main road northeast — skirting the mist wall on the way; it still holds — and arrived after dark to a young girl's song about butterflies and dreams drifting over the burned bungalows, sung to three gray-hooded figures seated facing away from her.
  • Perception DC 22: only Mu-Jin (exactly 22) pierced the magical disguise — the "girl" is a blood-soaked mobile effigy of dried grass held together by wisps of ghostly ectoplasm. "That is not a young woman."
  • Recall Knowledge named it: a red-hooded thatchling — an undead, unholy variant of the thatchlings destroyed on the road hours earlier, creatures Mu-Jin knew only from old folktales.
  • She stopped singing, pointed at Iryoku — the only trained performer — and asked: "You here to entertain us? It's been so long since we've had others to perform for us." Iryoku's expert Performance (22 vs DC 17, clean) satisfied her: the party earned one question.
  • Iryoku asked about the governor. Her expression darkened (Zhen and Mu-Jin's Sense Motive: anger, intensifying): "He was a lapdog of Lung Wa and an enemy." Mu-Jin's follow-up was refused — the question was spent — and her parting words carried the session's biggest clue: "Be grateful your governor chose to leave you rather than inflict upon your town something worse. Now leave."
  • Zhen argued for answers over execution — press her about why, ask whether she was a victim — against Mu-Jin's "I think we should still kill her." The party stood its ground. Initiative. (See Combat E3.)
  • After the fight: shallow graves and three unburied skeletons. Zhen insisted on proper burial — and as the rites finished, a sudden sense of gratitude, and a red cloak with one of the skeletons: Hongrui's Gratitude, naming Fan Hongrui — an elaborately dressed performer whose two companions held her lantern and umbrella, one of "four unfortunate travelers who met a tragic end in the Willowshore Hinterlands." 40 XP.
  • The GM's closing: the governor line of investigation is a dead end — but the Canary Inn now feels "a little less haunted."

Character Spotlight

Zhen Stillness · the Keeper of Rites

  • The defining beat came last: standing over three unburied skeletons in a burned-out inn, the town's tender-of-the-dead said simply — "I think we should at least keep them all properly buried then" — and that small Pharasman kindness, not a die roll, turned four hostile ghosts into a gift and a name.
  • And in the last breath before initiative, he was the one arguing mercy-first — press her about why she hates Lung Wa, ask whether she was a victim, before deciding to "off with the head" — the same instinct that later buried her bones.
  • Carried the workman's session on his back — literally, by his own telling ("Zhen can feel his back hurting from all the work he had to do for this"): the farmland crit, shrine-moving points, the salon grind ("Nothing is too hard for this old man" — Kaelen: "Are you sure your back is still 80?"), and the hero-pointed exactly-20 save that cured his Slime Rot ("I'm too old for this").
  • His best combat night of the campaign: opened the ambush with Earth's Bile on all four thatchlings ("I'm not gonna play stupid") — single-handedly revealing their fire weakness and bludgeoning resistance — pivoted to Vitality Lash the moment Mu-Jin called them undead, and finished the last one with it.
  • Debuted Electric Arc (the new Storm-druid cantrip he'd picked at daily prep, shopping for "a more fun spell") and landed his first-ever nodachi crit, deleting a gray-hood in one stroke.
  • The table's positioning conscience — "Give me space, or I'm going to blast you as well" — and its skeptical ear: his Will save shrugged off the thatchlings' curse, and his was the question that kept the ghost scene alive: "Wait, so you do know about his whereabouts?"

Mu-Jin · the Folklorist Vindicated

  • He solved the Desna shrine — the Face failure that ate two days of S4 — rolling the winning Diplomacy 20 himself with Weaver bolstering. The would-be Desna convert is the one her kami finally listened to.
  • At the Great Willow he was reverence itself: "Mu-Jin will reach into his bag, pull out some sake, and reverently place it down as an offering."
  • At the Canary Inn his two lanes converged: his Perception (exactly 22) was the only one to pierce the red-hood's disguise, and his Recall Knowledge named the thatchlings from the folktales he collects — an arc he narrated himself: "I made it a hobby of mine to learn old folk tales… maybe that's actually going to come in handy."
  • Horror-genre metagame all night ("I play too many Resident Evil games. Why is a young woman singing the scariest fucking thing in the world?"), pessimism on-brand ("Brand new day… new horrors" — "I'm a realist"), and the hero-point runner: "as always, I'll use a hero point if I fail."
  • Full witch toolkit: the nat-20 initiative that debuted the four-action house rule, Weaver dismount + Elemental Betrayal + Balanced Luck, Phase Familiar negating a spider's fangs entirely, the Needle Darts that finished the last spider — and the three-action Force Barrage that dropped Fan Hongrui ("Man, that is shit damage. Oh, I killed it.").
  • Closed the night theorizing the fugitive-ghost connection — and, chillingly: "Maybe Willowshore is the only safe spot right now and everyone else is fucked with ghosts. And we're just getting the leftovers."

Kaelen Ashford · the Unbeliever

  • Line of the night, handed a supernatural fear aura mid-charge: "I'm sorry, I don't believe in these giggles. So I'm going to attack immediately." And he backed it up.
  • The party's execution arm: the hero-pointed crit on the hunting spider, a Flurry kill in the roadside ambush, and two more at the Canary Inn — a flanking Flurry takedown in round 1 ("Holy shit") and the crit-plus-hit that deleted the last thatchling in round 2.

  • Tidy artillery war: Timber Sentinel + weapon-infusion reach blasts in the spider fight (one kill), the propulsive blast that dropped a roadside thatchling into its fire weakness, and a max-damage two-action melee blast that left Fan Hongrui "barely" standing for Mu-Jin to finish.
