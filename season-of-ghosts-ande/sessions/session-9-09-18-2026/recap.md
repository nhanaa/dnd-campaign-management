## Session Summary

Book Two opened on a change of season rather than a fight. Iryoku recapped the lumber camp for the table's first hero point, and the GM moved the story into fall: the summer rains gone, the monsters that menaced the region dead, a governing council that includes all five PCs, and a town that takes little comfort from any of it. The governor is still missing. The curse still keeps anyone from leaving or arriving, except **Shinzo**, who comes and goes and *"is unsure of why."* Winter is coming, and Willowshore cannot stockpile enough to survive it alone. *"Fortunately for Willowshore, the townsfolk aren't alone in this plague. Because they have all of you… to look up to."*

What followed was the AP's fall downtime system, run week by week for six weeks. Each week the party took up to four **preparation activities** (aid the harvest, aid the hunt, aid the townsfolk, reinforce the buildings, host a tea ceremony) to build three town tracks: **Food**, **Security** and **Hope**. Each PC also took a standard downtime activity, and a new option was **researching the curse** itself. The party settled into lanes fast. **Kaoru** reinforced the town's buildings with Engineering Lore every week without exception. **Iryoku** and **Zhen** worked the fields and fences on Athletics. The fifth seat (the aider) rotated: **Kaelen** in weeks one and three, **Zhen** in weeks two, four and six, and Zhen's aids turned two of those rolls into crits. **Mu-Jin** did the research that mattered. Summer's work paid off too: because the party had secured the northern farmland, the first harvest roll each week was upgraded a degree, which Iryoku worked out means *"guaranteeing two food points a week."* **Security reached its twelve-point minimum in week three, Hope during the festival, Food in week four.**

Every week also brought an event, and those carried the roleplay. Zhen named **inari sushi** as the right offering for a boy who believes a white fox was Daikitsu's messenger. He relocated a nest of vipers from a man's outhouse with Mu-Jin weaving him a basket, and thanked Mu-Jin for the help like the elder he is. The **First Long Night** festival was three contests and the party won all three: Mu-Jin's moon poetry on the Art Lore he'd *"finally"* found a use for, Kaoru's wire-to-wire win at **bundle cutting**, and a sky lantern Mu-Jin spent his last hero point on and shaped like Ugly Cute. A haunting at **Mama Bao's** steamed-bun shop turned out to be **Hinode Akari**, an herbalist whose corpse was once found with every hair plucked from her head. Mu-Jin called her by name and failed to reach her. Zhen prayed, and between the prayer and Mu-Jin's calming words **the haunting ended**. The story behind it came out afterward: Mama Bao's grandfather built the shop on money that arrived a few nights before Akari's body was found.

Then the dead started walking. **Elizeth Candora**, head priestess of Pharasma, privately asked the party to find a corpse that had gotten up and left, and she suspects **You So-Jin**. Zhen tracked the shambling prints through the river and across town to the one place none of them expected: **their own tea house**. The corpse was crouched in the bushes out back, and beside it sat **Yami**, the tea house cat, who looked up and spoke. *"I see you found her. This lady was the one who fed me during her life. So I wish to keep her. She also dances well in the moonlight."* Yami is a **bakeneko**. Kaelen befriended it and Iryoku talked it into letting her go, and Kaelen asked for the burial the party gave her. Behind all of this, Mu-Jin's research kept turning up breakpoints, and in week six it turned up the one he stopped the table for: **a specialized ritual that might allow passage through the fog wall.** The session ended on a faceless thing in a stone mask lurking in a bachelor's yard. It is a **Dalgyal Gwishin**, jealous of charismatic people, and it is not friendly. **Combat next session.**

## Key Events

The party chose relocation over killing. **Mu-Jin wove a basket to trap them** (Crafting aid, 18) and **Zhen's Survival came up a critical success** with it. *"I've done this in the past… And thank you for the help, young man."* **80 XP, +1 hope, 2 gp**, and treasure the GM teased as *"might be very useful."* By the festival the party was saying *"we picked up Ghost Touch"*, so this was evidently the **ghost touch rune**.

**The First Long Night (1:16–1:43)**. Three contests, four checks each. Four VP by anyone earns +1 hope, and six VP earns a party prize.
- **Admiring the Moon** (poetry to the harvest moon, *"as the night goes on, tea is invariably replaced with wine and spirits"*). **Mu-Jin used Art Lore** (*"Holy shit, I can finally use that damn thing"*), **Iryoku Performance as oratory**, **Zhen Religion**, and the GM invited **Kaelen** to try Religion as well. **Mu-Jin critted his final check to reach 6 VP.** Prize: **a Cognitive mutagen** for Mu-Jin.
- **Bundle Cutting.** Contestants start bound hand and foot, escape (DC 17), run for a stone dagger, then cut straw bundles with Thievery, Athletics or Survival. The GM gave the lore: it's disguised training for escaping bandits and **jorogumo webbing**, with ropes knotted to look as unlike webs as possible and farming songs loud enough to offend city collaborators. Mu-Jin first wanted to sit it out (*"these seem very physical things"*), then joined. The escape dialog made it look like his +10 was **the party's best**: *"I'm the best in the party? What the fuck? I'm a witch."* It isn't, since Iryoku has +12 on Athletics (*"I guess that dialogue box is misleading"*). He needed two escapes, and **refused to spend a hero point and lost a VP**. **Zhen** went Athletics. **Kaoru chose Thievery (*"Don't break anything"*), rolled a natural 20 and a run of crits, and led from start to finish.** **Iryoku and Kaoru both reached 6 VP.** Prize: **a striking rune**, which Iryoku gave away on the spot (*"Who needs striking? Because I can't use either of these."*).
- **Lantern Making.** Sky lanterns in every shape but crows or ravens, because of *"the famed jorogumo's intense dislike of tengu."* Crafting only, so it was all Mu-Jin, with his contest bonus. He rolled fail, crit, crit, then **spent his last hero point on the final check**: *"I hope you guys are happy I used all my hero points on this shit."* **Hope reached 12 → 40 XP.** His lantern looks like **a miniature Ugly Cute**.
- **Festival close: +1 hope and 120 XP for taking part; all three contests won → +1 reputation with both Northridge and Southbank.**

Every morning there were clumps of hair hanging from her ceiling, and today her customers bit into buns and *"spit out clumps of hair mixed in with the bun fillings."* The party's Perception found the strands were all from the same head, well kept, and **plucked at the root, not cut.**
- **The party stayed up.** Zhen admitted *"I'm not very good at waiting,"* and Kaelen told him he'd be fine. An hour after midnight **a ghostly woman drifted in and began pulling out her own hair.** Kaelen: *"Guys, do we need to call Ghostbusters? … We are the Ghostbusters."*

He argued for giving the striking rune to **Kaelen** (*"A striking gives you another dice… He has the biggest dice, basically"*), and Mu-Jin agreed (*"he's our main DPS"*). Iryoku argued first for putting ghost touch on **Kaoru**, so his Spellstrikes would carry it, then reversed himself: the weakness only triggers once either way, and *"Kaelen's damage potential is lower. It's a **haunt**. Mu-Jin asked the GM for the Eight Practices, which came back as never call a ghost a ghost, and avoid water when one is near. *"Her name is Akari. Call her Akari."* Kaelen: *"Ew, I stepped in a puddle."* (*"He was making a joke,"* the GM clarified.)

- **The party told Mama Bao the story**, and she filled in the rest. **A few nights before Akari's body was found, her grandfather came into a sudden windfall** and used it to build the bun shop. He claimed it came from *"selling an old collection of wigs to a puppet maker"* and was evasive whenever it came up. Someone at the table had already floated that the bun lady herself might have done something to Akari, and now followed up with *"Told you. Told you the grandfather killed…"* Mu-Jin put it in folklore terms: *"the ghosts will haunt you even if you didn't directly do it, but because… your ancestors did something wrong to them. So even though Mama Bao is herself completely innocent, she suffered the collateral damage."*

The party picked the trail up where the prints came out of the mud heading northwest, and Zhen tracked them through town **to the Cerulean Tea House.**

So I wish to keep her. She also dances well in the moonlight."* Zhen: *"Make sure this is really a cat."*

Iryoku: *"It's an egg ghost, because it looks like an egg."* **Mu-Jin took its lore. Zhen took two answers:** its reaction is emotion/mental, because **it is easily jealous of more charismatic characters**, so the GM said it *"might"* go after **Iryoku** first; and **its lowest save is Fortitude, +9.**
