Session Summary

Book Two opened on a change of season rather than a fight. Iryoku recapped the lumber camp for the table’s first hero point, and the GM moved the story into fall: the summer rains gone, the monsters that menaced the region dead, a governing council that includes all five PCs, and a town that takes little comfort from any of it. The governor is still missing. The curse still keeps anyone from leaving or arriving, except Shinzo, who comes and goes and “is unsure of why.” Winter is coming, and Willowshore cannot stockpile enough to survive it alone. “Fortunately for Willowshore, the townsfolk aren’t alone in this plague. Because they have all of you… to look up to.”

What followed was the AP’s fall downtime system, run week by week for six weeks. Each week the party took up to four preparation activities (aid the harvest, aid the hunt, aid the townsfolk, reinforce the buildings, host a tea ceremony) to build three town tracks: Food, Security and Hope. Each PC also took a standard downtime activity, and a new option was researching the curse itself. The party settled into lanes fast. Kaoru reinforced the town’s buildings with Engineering Lore every week without exception. Iryoku and Zhen worked the fields and fences on Athletics. The fifth seat (the aider) rotated: Kaelen in weeks one and three, Zhen in weeks two, four and six, and Zhen’s aids turned two of those rolls into crits. Mu-Jin did the research that mattered. Summer’s work paid off too: because the party had secured the northern farmland, the first harvest roll each week was upgraded a degree, which Iryoku worked out means “guaranteeing two food points a week.” Security reached its twelve-point minimum in week three, Hope during the festival, Food in week four.

Every week also brought an event, and those carried the roleplay. Zhen named inari sushi as the right offering for a boy who believes a white fox was Daikitsu’s messenger. He relocated a nest of vipers from a man’s outhouse with Mu-Jin weaving him a basket, and thanked Mu-Jin for the help like the elder he is. The First Long Night festival was three contests and the party won all three: Mu-Jin’s moon poetry on the Art Lore he’d “finally” found a use for, Kaoru’s wire-to-wire win at bundle cutting, and a sky lantern Mu-Jin spent his last hero point on and shaped like Ugly Cute. A haunting at Mama Bao’s steamed-bun shop turned out to be Hinode Akari, an herbalist whose corpse was once found with every hair plucked from her head. Mu-Jin called her by name and failed to reach her. Zhen prayed, and between the prayer and Mu-Jin’s calming words the haunting ended. The story behind it came out afterward: Mama Bao’s grandfather built the shop on money that arrived a few nights before Akari’s body was found.

Then the dead started walking. Elizeth Candora, head priestess of Pharasma, privately asked the party to find a corpse that had gotten up and left, and she suspects You So-Jin. Zhen tracked the shambling prints through the river and across town to the one place none of them expected: their own tea house. The corpse was crouched in the bushes out back, and beside it sat Yami, the tea house cat, who looked up and spoke. “I see you found her. This lady was the one who fed me during her life. So I wish to keep her. She also dances well in the moonlight.” Yami is a bakeneko. Kaelen befriended it and Iryoku talked it into letting her go, and Kaelen asked for the burial the party gave her. Behind all of this, Mu-Jin’s research kept turning up breakpoints, and in week six it turned up the one he stopped the table for: a specialized ritual that might allow passage through the fog wall. The session ended on a faceless thing in a stone mask lurking in a bachelor’s yard. It is a Dalgyal Gwishin, jealous of charismatic people, and it is not friendly. Combat next session.

A new season, and a town that has to survive the winter

  • Iryoku recapped the lumber camp (the Mugirou deal, the ritual, Kugaptee’s failed push through the fog) for the table’s opening hero point.
  • The GM closed summer at roughly day 14. The rest went to retraining, Earn Income, crafting, restoring the tea house, and making friends with the grey cat that lives there.
  • Fall narration: new leaders in place (“all of you as well as the other two”); the governor and his manor still missing; the curse still sealing the town; Shinzo alone can come and go, and “has no insight into the curse’s nature.”
  • The subsystem. Three victory-point tracks: Hope, Food (12 recommended), Security (12 recommended). Up to four preparation activities a week, with the fifth PC able to aid one. Each PC also gets a standard downtime activity, and Research the Curse takes the full week. Every week brings an event.
  • Iryoku asked for the activity list in Discord, and the GM posted it.
  • The farmland perk: for securing the northern farm in summer, +1 circumstance to Aid Harvest, and the first Aid Harvest roll each week is improved one degree.

The First Long Night · Festival · Three Contests, Three Wins

Moon poetry, bundle cutting, and a lantern shaped like Ugly Cute

  • Admiring the Moon: poetry to the harvest moon, “as the night goes on, tea is invariably replaced with wine and spirits.” Mu-Jin used Art Lore (“Holy shit, I can finally use that damn thing”), Iryoku Performance as oratory, Zhen Religion. Mu-Jin critted the last check for 6 VP: a Cognitive mutagen.
  • Bundle Cutting: escape your bonds, grab a stone dagger, cut straw. It is disguised training for escaping jorogumo webbing, with ropes knotted to look as unlike webs as possible. Mu-Jin tried to sit it out, joined anyway, and saw the escape dialog call his +10 the party’s best: “I’m the best in the party? What the fuck? I’m a witch.” (Iryoku has +12.) He refused to spend a hero point and lost a VP.
  • Kaoru chose Thievery (“Don’t break anything”), rolled a natural 20 and a run of crits, and led from start to finish. Kaelen: “Did you get a nat 20?… Good job.”
  • Kaoru and Iryoku both reached 6 VP: a striking rune, which Iryoku gave away on the spot (“Who needs striking? Because I can’t use either of these.”).
  • Lantern Making: never crows or ravens, “due to the famed jorogumo’s intense dislike of tengu.” Crafting only, so all Mu-Jin: fail, crit, crit, then his last hero point on the final check. “I hope you guys are happy I used all my hero points on this shit.” His lantern is a little Ugly Cute.
  • Hope reached 12 → 40 XP. Festival close: +1 hope and 120 XP; all three contests won → +1 reputation with both factions.
Week 4 · Mystery · The Hair in the Buns

Hinode Akari, and a grandfather’s windfall

  • Mu-Jin (Aid Hunt) and Kaelen (Aid Harvest) brought Food to 12 → 40 XP. Zhen aided Iryoku to a crit, taken as 1 security + 1 hope: “Gotta get the hope up.” / “Hope seems harder to build.” / “Always gotta make this place more hopeful.”
  • Nobody who worships a deity has that deity’s Lore. “They’re not devout enough, I’m sorry.” Zhen: “I’m just a casual.”
  • Mama Bao’s bun shop had hair hanging from the ceiling each morning, and today customers “spit out clumps of hair mixed in with the bun fillings.” Every strand was from one head, plucked at the root, not cut.
  • The party stayed up. An hour after midnight a ghostly woman drifted in and began pulling out her own hair. Kaelen: “Guys, do we need to call Ghostbusters? … We are the Ghostbusters.”
  • Rune break: Zhen argued striking onto Kaelen’s bigger dice; Iryoku argued ghost touch for Kaoru’s Spellstrikes, then reversed (“Kaelen’s damage potential is lower”). Both runes went on Kaelen’s bo staff. Zhen bought striking; Kaoru is 40 gp short of ghost touch.
  • Willowshore Lore named her: Hinode Akari, an herbalist found near Gord Lake with every hair plucked. Mu-Jin: “Her name is Akari. Call her Akari.” Kaelen, on the no-water rule: “Ew, I stepped in a puddle.”
  • Mu-Jin’s named Diplomacy failed. Zhen’s prayer succeeded, and the GM ended the haunt on both: “convince the spirit to abandon the haunting with prayer, and Mu-Jin being able to calm her down.” +1 hope.
  • Mama Bao’s grandfather came into sudden money a few nights before Akari’s body was found, built the shop with it, and blamed “an old collection of wigs” sold to a puppet maker. Mu-Jin: “Mama Bao is herself completely innocent, she suffered the collateral damage.” She will honor Akari’s memory: +1 hope, +1 Northridge.
