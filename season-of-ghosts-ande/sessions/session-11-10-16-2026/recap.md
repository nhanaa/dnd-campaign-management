## Session Summary

The party came out of the Wall of Ghosts at midnight, still on the Pilgrim's Path. They had opened the wall, but they had never reached the Tan Sugi Monastery, so they made camp and went on west in the morning. The first thing on the road was a kappa lying face-down with an empty head-bowl. Iryoku topped it up from a waterskin, and Mu-Jin read him with a critical-success Sense Motive. His name was **Enko**, and something was tormenting him: a voice only Mu-Jin heard, a woman's anguished cry promising that when Enko got home she would pick him up and drop him off the edge of the world. Enko named her **Iogaka, the Storm Mother**, a creature of lightning and flesh-stripping wind who hates the smell of burning incense. Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge turned up the ability she was using, a hag's **Voice of the Storm**, which carries a whisper or a scream fifty miles. The party sent Iryoku back to Willowshore on horseback for 10 gp of incense and walked Enko home. At the river, two **amphisbaenas** came out to meet them. Kaelen, whose laptop was still on a black loading screen, opened the fight on a natural 20 the GM rolled for him; he and Zhen killed one snake each in two rounds. Enko dove into the water and came back with thanks: a silver shortsword the GM re-flavored as a ninjato for Kaoru, a wand and a sturdy shield.

Further on, a rope bridge led to a hexagonal shrine standing in the middle of the river. It was one of **Master Zhi Hui's pilgrim stations**, the GM said, built so that pilgrims could see who they really were reflected in the water, and Kaelen knew its story without a check: it belongs to his faith. Mu-Jin's Willowshore Lore turned up the strange part. This shrine was washed away in a flood ten years ago, and it was standing there in perfect repair. When the party stepped onto it, ghostly arms rose from the river and two leech swarms poured onto the floor, spelling **Kugaptee** over and over as they moved. **Kaelen disabled the haunt with a prayer**, a Religion check in a shrine to his own faith, and the casters burned the swarms out inside a round and a half. The party slept above the river. In the morning the dippers had filled themselves, and Mu-Jin, moved to fill six clay vials instead of drinking, came away with six **ghost charges** and a shrine blessing on everyone's Reflex saves.

The next day brought two twisted burls that unfolded into **mandragoras** whispering *Kugaptee*. They landed the biggest single hit of the night: one shriek left Kaelen sickened 2, and a bite crit him for 28 and grabbed him. Kaoru and Iryoku killed them. Then, in the hollow of a dead tree, the party found a sobbing young woman calling for her big sister. She said her name was **Yeri** and her sister was **Yuni**. Mu-Jin's Willowshore Lore said Yeri was from Willowshore and had gone missing **ten years ago**, the same span as the vanished shrine. Mu-Jin kept that to himself and relayed it to the party one by one with a subtle Message cantrip. They let Yeri come along. On the walk, Iryoku connected the two: *"We've had time stuff happen so far. The shrine and with the monk."* Then Kaoru took Mu-Jin aside: *"Keep watchful. My mother disappeared on the same route."*

At the Garden Shrine, a burned cottage at a second Zhi Hui station, four **Shrine Caretakers** rose out of the ashes, undead burning alive and immune to fire. Iryoku's **Extract Element** stripped that immunity off three of them, and the party took all four down over three rounds, with one kill each for Mu-Jin, Iryoku, Zhen and Kaelen. Then night fell. Yeri began to writhe and convulse, and her head came clean off her body. Iryoku: *"Hey, I know that thing."* That was where the GM ended the session.

## Key Events

**Prologue: recap and camp (0:00–13:45)**
- **Mu-Jin gave the recap** (the hero points went out at the start of play). He corrected himself once in the telling: *"Note to self, the Wall of Ghosts is not a part of the Mist Wall. It is a separate thing."*
- It was midnight in the Willowshore Hinterlands on the Pilgrim's Path. **Zhen** asked whether the party had healed after the hazard (assumed yes) and weighed the six-to-seven-hour ride home against resting where they stood. **Mu-Jin** and **Iryoku** settled it: they never actually reached the monastery, so the party camped and pushed on west in the morning.

**Enko the kappa (13:46–32:48)** — scene B1, "Tormented Kappa"
- Half an hour along the path: a kappa face-down on the trail, crawling out of the way to let them pass, his **head-bowl empty**. **Iryoku** offered his waterskin (*"Maybe his bowl is why he's prone in the first place"*) and the party lifted him up and refilled it.
- **Mu-Jin's Sense Motive** on the kappa: a critical success.
- **Only Mu-Jin heard the scream**: a woman's voice promising that when the kappa got home she would *"pick him up and drop him off at the edge of the world."* Mu-Jin asked the kappa whether he was cursed. He introduced himself as **Enko**.
- Enko's tormentor: **Iogaka**, *"known as the Storm Mother… a terrible and frightening monster who can hurl lightning and blasts of wind that can strip flesh from bone."* He doesn't know why she has fixed on him. She has *"a peculiar and strange distaste for the scent of burning incense."*
- **Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge** (*"I'll use a hero point if it's bad"*; it succeeded) brought up the ability, **Voice of the Storm**: a hag can send a whisper or a scream on the wind to any place she has seen within 50 miles, and use it to Demoralize. The GM held back the full entry *"because it'll show who she is."* Mu-Jin and Iryoku worked out that the ability targets *places*, not people, and Iryoku wondered whether she'd put a **Sigil** on Enko to track him.
- **The incense.** Iryoku raised it (*"We have any incense? Because he said she doesn't like incense"*), and **Zhen** suggested picking some up in Willowshore. Willowshore was the wrong direction, so Zhen proposed splitting up (*"We don't have to all come. Just some of us."*). Iryoku did the horse math (an hour and a half each way) and volunteered. **10 gp of plain incense** came out of the stash.
- Mu-Jin, on whether Iogaka can actually hurt Enko from afar: people threaten to kill you all the time and don't mean it. Zhen: *"It also doesn't mean they won't."*

### Encounter 3 — The Mandragoras (scene C1)
