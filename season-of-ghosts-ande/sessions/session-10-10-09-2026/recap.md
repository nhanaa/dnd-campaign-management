## Session Summary

The session opened on the fight S9 left hanging. The **Dalgyal Gwishin** in the bachelor's yard went straight for **Iryoku**, as the research had said it would, and spent two rounds headbutting him while the rest of the party took it apart. Kaoru's Spellstrike left it *"barely still in existence"* and **Kaelen** finished it with the first strike of a Flurry of Blows. Zhen's verdict on Iryoku's evening: *"I guess it's not always good to be charismatic."* The town gained a point of Hope and a point of Northridge reputation.

One more week of downtime closed the fall research. Mu-Jin ran his own investigations and **Zhen took the Great Willow on Nature and critically succeeded**, and the GM announced the final research threshold. With it came a ritual, **Open the Wall of Ghosts**, and the news that everything left to learn about the curse lies beyond that wall, at the ruins of the Tan Sugi Monastery. Then everyone **leveled to 5**. Zhen and Kaoru both came out of it with **Fireball**, Mu-Jin learned **Fear**, and Iryoku's fire junction now gives enemies near him a weakness to his fire. The ritual can only be cast under a crescent or new moon (weeks 7, 8, 11 or 12), and it was week 7. Iryoku wanted to wait and keep preparing the town. Mu-Jin, Kaoru, Zhen and Kaelen wanted to go, and the table went four to one. **Zhen led the ritual as primary caster** on Religion, with Mu-Jin, Kaoru and Iryoku as his secondaries, and it worked.

The Wall of Ghosts let them in and closed behind them. Inside was a fog-bound forest with no wind and no animal sound. Iryoku recognized the ground as the monastery's site, and Mu-Jin read the trees: this woodland is **nearly a thousand years younger than it should be**, from before the monastery existed. At the end of the trail, on a slab of stone, lay a woman with her chest torn open and fog pouring out of the wound. Mu-Jin named her on a natural 20: **Tan Sui Jing**, the heroine who defeated a fiend here a thousand years ago. The word carved into the stone under her read **Kugaptee**. Her arm pointed north, to a mound of fallen trees and a **talking giant vulture** that told them to leave or be added to its meals.

That fight is the one Pax asked to have remembered, and the log bears it out. Zhen rolled a natural 20 on initiative and opened with the spell he had learned an hour earlier. **One Fireball: the vulture critically failed for 50, two of the three Cacodaemons died outright, and the third was left on 10 hit points.** Kaoru rolled a natural 20 too, walked up and cast his own. The vulture got exactly one turn. Kaelen killed it, Iryoku picked off the last Cacodaemon, and the whole thing was over inside a single round. Then the vulture's corpse burst into razor-winged red butterflies, and in its talon was a **human heart, whispering *"save me."***

The party already meant to carry the heart back to the body. Mu-Jin picked it up, and the forest went dark. Something enormous began roaring and walking toward them through the trees, and the trees themselves started clawing at the party. Nobody could see it and nobody could identify it. Mu-Jin stopped trying and made the call: everyone back to the slab, now. It became a relay. Kaoru took the heart, Mu-Jin's Loose Time's Arrow gave him the extra stride, and **Kaoru ran a hundred feet and put the heart back in Tan Sui Jing's chest.** The unseen thing howled and fled. Her body sank into the stone, a **sugi tree** grew a hundred feet out of it and drove the fog away, and the party was standing on the Pilgrim's Path again. The GM's reveal afterward: it was a hazard, and nobody could realistically have spotted it.
