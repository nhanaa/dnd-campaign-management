Session Summary

The party rode home from the Canary Inn, slept, and went south at dawn to keep the promise they'd made to the Great Willow — and walked into the hardest fight of the campaign. The infested grove on the western bank of Dragonfly Creek was a carpet of gray and yellow fungus with one resident: a "mushroom man" who answered "Who goes there?" with a sob story about vanished kodama and a colony that cast it out. Kaelen answered it with "Your mom." Mu-Jin's hero-pointed Recall Knowledge named it — a myceloid, carrier of purple pox, the exact infection the Great Willow brought home — and the story collapsed. What followed was the campaign's first fight with real teeth: the myceloid's spores put purple pox in Kaelen and then dominated him outright (a failed Will save with no hero points left — helpful to the myceloid for a full minute, unable to raise a hand against it), it two-punched Zhen into the dirt, and two consecutive critical fists dropped Iryoku from the fight. Mu-Jin ran dry of healing. Kaoru ended it: Sky Laughs at Waves to recharge his Spellstrike, then Ignition through the ninjato — a natural 19 — into the weaknesses the party had pried open. The grove began to heal the moment it died. Kaelen's turns of standing by while his friends were beaten — "Forgive me, my friend, I just got to do this" — became the table's new running joke before the fight was even over.

Healed and humbled, they tracked the sound of grinding stone into the Gorge of Fangs and Teeth and found Ugly Cute — the Spider Gate's stone guardian, missing since the first dawn of summer — being eaten by three living boulders. The party readied actions and ambushed the ambushers as they surfaced: Kaoru's readied strike, Kaelen's readied crit, and Iryoku's hero-pointed crit blast deleted the first; the second crit Kaoru for 24 and then died to Zhen's readied Brace strike (a natural 20 — 34 damage plus precision, "maximum damage"); Zhen's second crit finished the third. Then Ugly Cute spoke, and knew every one of them by name, Weaver included: it had been waylaid by faceless monsters tormenting folk on Willowshore's outskirts, pursued them, and crawled here wounded. Its testimony reframed the whole mystery — the curse struck at the height of the Reenactment ceremony (not because of it), the mists are "the beginning of the town's doom," the faceless things have claimed an old lumber camp to the west near the Ceiba River's source, and farther west it senses a stronger malevolence beyond its reach. Zhen and Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge put a name to the faceless: noppera-bo, humanoids warped by curses into yokai. Ugly Cute broke off a shard of its own carapace as a gift — a spiked gauntlet, Ugly Cute's Gift — which Iryoku carries, though nobody at the table expects to use it much.

Back in town the elders heard the report (and sniped at each other through it), and the party hit level 3: Dispel Magic for Zhen, Fleet and two new skills for Kaelen (Diplomacy — "Kaelen is no longer allowed to talk to people"), rank-2 spells, Deception, and a scroll-fed extra spell for Mu-Jin. Iryoku's nat-20 Society check mapped the lumber camp, secret entrance and all. Shinzo the merchant returned with the party's orders and asked how they meant to approach the faceless things — fight, or fool them? When Zhen asked if he knew a method, he produced a Noppera-Bo Hood, and when the party moved to pay, waved it off as free. Zhen trusted that exactly as far as you'd expect ("Nothing is free in this world"); Kaoru's Sense Motive on the merchant's "I came from the south" got a flat he seems to be telling the truth. Later that day the party was at the lumber camp's secret stockade gap, listening to a rowdy celebration and the sound of crossbows, and standing in a dormitory whose walls were painted with blood silhouettes. Four noppera-bo grunts ignored them until Mu-Jin announced the party was from Willowshore. Two rounds later three were dead and one was face-down and breathing, knocked out nonlethally by Zhen for interrogation. The door closed on a cliffhanger, and the GM's parting note: this adventure runs on a four-act structure, and you are still in act one.

Act II · Mystery · The Infested Grove (Day 10)

The Mushroom Man Lies

  • Off-road to the western bank of Dragonfly Creek south of town: the once-idyllic willow grove overgrown with sheets of gray and pale-yellow fungi, spotted mushrooms and warty toadstools where the underbrush used to be. Recall Knowledge (Mu-Jin, Zhen): the fungus itself is largely harmless — "Tell that to the giant centipede," said Kaoru.
  • One fungus was not harmless. A mushroom man challenged the party ("Who goes there?" — Kaelen: "Your mom." GM: "Says the monk of Sangpotshi."). It claimed it had been chased out of its underground colony, that the kodama who dwelled here welcomed it, and that they all vanished — "decades ago."
  • Kaelen asked the one sharp question ("When did they vanish?"); Mu-Jin asked what happened to the Willow's friend.
  • Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge (hero point pre-committed) landed: a myceloid, and — because he asked specifically — it carries purple pox, exactly what was afflicting the Great Willow. The party concluded it had been "lying through its teeth" (Kaoru). Kaelen: "You know what always happens to liars? They always get their pants on fire."
  • Combat E1 (below) — the hardest fight of the campaign so far.
  • With the myceloid dead, "the grove is immediately beginning to recede with the mushrooms and mold shrinking, melting, and evaporating." An hour's wait and the grove was fully restored. 120 XP.
Act III · Mystery · The Gorge of Fangs and Teeth (Day 10, Afternoon)

Ugly Cute Speaks

  • Zhen's Survival (16 vs DC 15) picked up tracks leading down into the Gorge, and the sound of rock grinding. Three squat stony creatures were gnawing on a much larger rock — and the rock was Ugly Cute, the stone spider.
  • Mu-Jin's RK: living boulders — earth elementals, no weaknesses, and they burrow. All three went underground after his Force Barrage; the party readied actions and ambushed them as they surfaced (Combat E2). 96 XP.
  • After Zhen's Garden of Healing and a search for a repair kit nobody owned (it intends to stay and mend for a few days), the guardian addressed the party by name — all of them, including Weaver. It was "confronted by a group of faceless monsters who were tormenting people on Willowshore's outskirts," pursued them, "ended up lost and wounded and had to return here to recover."
  • It had visions of an abandoned Willowshore crumbling into ruins, reclaimed by the forest after a monstrous invasion — "It was that vision that allowed me to awaken and come to your aid."
  • "While I don't think the Reenactment festival was the source of the curse, whatever caused the curse did take place during the height of the ceremony itself."
  • "The mists surrounding Willowshore are the beginning of the town's doom, a curse brought upon the region by an unknown force — one that I sense is tied to the faceless monsters that I confronted. They have claimed an old lumber camp to the west of town near the Ceiba River's source. Perhaps clues about the curse can be found there. Farther west, I can sense an even stronger source of malevolence, but I haven't been able to observe the source myself, as it lies beyond the range I can travel from Willowshore."
  • Recall Knowledge (very hard; Zhen and Mu-Jin succeeded, Weaver's Bolstering Aid on Mu-Jin): wild tales of noppera-bo — humanoids warped by curses into naturally spawning yokai. Zhen's follow-up — "So, faceless creature. Does that mean they cannot see?" — went unanswered for now.
  • Ugly Cute's gift: a shard of its own carapace — a +1 spiked gauntlet that, once a day, gains Ghost Touch and +2 spirit damage for a minute. Not a monk weapon, not a sword, STR-based. Iryoku hesitated on principle ("I already got one unique item that's been given to me"), Zhen offered it to Kaelen, then floated selling it; Mu-Jin: "We're not selling Ugly Cute's gift." Iryoku took it in the end — for the once-a-day Ghost Touch, if nothing else. Kaelen asked whether they could carry Ugly Cute home; it needs to rest where it is.

Act V · Roleplay · Shinzo Returns (Day 11)

Nothing Is Free in This World

  • Asahina Shinzo was back with the orders: iron wine, journey bread, the walking cauldron. Mu-Jin had forgotten his name and guessed it blind ("Oh, shit. I was right.").
  • The party told him about the noppera-bo and the lumber camp. His question back: "Do you intend to bring furious ruin to them in battle? Or do you think you can fool them into believing that you are one of them?" Zhen: "Do you know a method that you can tell us?" — and he produced a Noppera-Bo Hood ("a completely smooth, round sack of skin that feels uncannily warm to the touch"; 1/day Illusory Disguise as a morph effect, counts as a disguise kit for your own ancestry). Zhen moved to buy it; Shinzo: "No need. You can have it for free… No strings attached."
  • Zhen: "Nothing is free in this world." … "Conveniently has an item that conveniently helps us against a mysterious creature." Kaelen: "Are you sure? Because nothing is ever free."
  • Kaoru's Sense Motive was the pointed one: "He keeps saying he's coming from the south, but we know the fog stops everyone from moving through it." GM: "I don't think he comes in and out. He seems to be telling the truth." (Zhen's own Sense Motive went unresolved on mic.)
  • Iryoku's verdict: "If this guy's lying, he's been beating all of our perception checks we've ever made. So we should maybe not piss him off." Zhen: "We'll pretend that we really believe him." Kaoru's practical note: the hood disguises you as your own ancestry — "I would never come off as a human."
  • Bought: iron wine + journey bread (6 gp from the stash — "It adds up, man"). Orders for next time: Dancing Scarf (Iryoku). Zhen is saving for a striking rune. The hood went into the party stash.
Act VI · Combat · The Lumber Camp (Day 11)

Blood on the Dormitory Walls

  • Eight miles west on a poorly maintained road along the north bank of the Ceiba River — an hour at double time. From outside: a rowdy celebration, smoke and roasting meat, a 15-foot log stockade sharpened to stakes.
  • Infiltrate or storm? Only Kaoru and Kaelen are trained in Stealth; Zhen: "If one of us is going to fail a stealth check, we're just going to be cooked." Consensus: the secret gap — even if they storm the place, storm it from the less-guarded side. Everyone found the hidden door except Iryoku, the man who drew it.
  • Outbuildings: toilets ("Paizo loves their toilets"), conspicuously empty stables, and the sound of crossbows going off from a larger building. Inside: "Blood splatters and weapon marks cover this dormitory's walls. Pools of dried blood stain the floor and bedding of the bunk beds. On the southeastern wall, several humanoid silhouettes have been painted using blood."
  • Four noppera-bo grunts inside — and they ignored the party. Mu-Jin's RK (Iryoku read the entry aloud): aberrations; "a typical noppera-bo is cruel and revels in the act of frightening or tormenting humanoid creatures."
  • Information first, the table agreed — Zhen wanted one left alive; Iryoku: "We could try and hold one of them and kill the rest." Mu-Jin called out: "You there, the ugly one. Have you visited Willowshore recently? We had a festival. Had some uninvited guests." — "You from Willowshore?" — Mu-Jin: "Indeed." Kaelen: "Correct." Initiative. Mu-Jin: "So we have learned something. If you announce you're from Willowshore, they'll try to kill you."
  • Combat E3: two rounds, three dead, one knocked out alive by Zhen for questioning. 60 XP. Zhen noticed mid-fight: their blood is purple. The door was closed. Session.
  • Debrief: Zhen — "I feel like we're about to enter the climax." GM: the AP follows a four-act Japanese storytelling structure rather than three — "You're still on the first act."

Character Spotlight

Zhen Stillness · the Old Man Who Got Back Up

  • The campaign's first real brush with his limits, played straight. Caster first, Fear then Earth's Bile; when the Fear bounced he closed with the nodachi and found the weakness the hard way ("Very good damage" — GM: "because this thing is weak to slashing"). The myceloid answered by turning on him: "Half my HP gone. More than half." Then the second fist. "This guy just two-punched me."
  • Down, sword in the dirt, until Mu-Jin's Soothe brought him back — and his next turn was the unglamorous, correct one: stand, pick up the blade ("How do you rule that — I have to pick up my weapon?"), swing.
  • The table's rules anchor even flat on the ground: "Maybe Dispel Magic?" for Kaelen (rank 2, not yet), and the catch that stopped Kaoru from Spellstriking twice without a recharge ("You don't have it. You have to recharge it") — which led straight to the Sky Laughs at Waves turn that won the fight.
  • The Gorge was his reward: a readied Brace strike on a natural 20 that crit the boulder chewing on Kaoru for 34 plus precision — "maximum damage" — and a second hero-pointed crit (2 → 18) that finished the third.
  • Quietly the party's Survival anchor again (the tracks into the Gorge), the one who asked Ugly Cute the follow-up nobody else did ("Faceless creature — does that mean they cannot see?"), the deductive voice on the governor ("His house is gone"), and the voice at the elders' table promising more than reconnaissance ("chase them away").
  • At level 3 he took Dispel Magic with Kaelen's domination in mind ("the anti-unfun spell") and spent the rest of the level-up advising everyone else's picks. His read on Shinzo's free gift was the old man's whole worldview in five words: "Nothing is free in this world."
  • Tried to find Ugly Cute's Gift a home — Kaelen first, then a sale Mu-Jin wouldn't hear of (it ended up with Iryoku); kept his stash-treasurer's hat on over three gold of journey bread ("It adds up, man").
  • The session's last act was his: he walked up to the lone surviving grunt and knocked it out nonlethally — a nat 1, a hero point, a crit — so the party would have someone to question.

Kaelen Ashford · the Kind of Friend Kaelen Is

  • His session, for better and for worse, and he owned every second of it. He set the tone with the myceloid before a die was rolled — "Who goes there?" "Your mom." … "You asked who was there, and I gave you an answer" — and had the sharpest interrogation question in the scene ("When did they vanish?").
  • Then the spores: a hero-pointed Fortitude save that still gave him purple pox, and a Will save with nothing left to spend. "Guys, I might be screwed." For the rest of the fight he was helpful to the myceloid — no hostile actions, no flanking, not even a Demoralize — and spent two turns hunting for a loophole ("Can I betray him?" "Is there any way I can make him hit me?") while his friends were beaten unconscious in front of him.
  • He played the condition in character rather than around it: "Forgive me, my friend. I just got to do this" — and, to Zhen, the line that gave it a moral shape: "I won't do this to these people, but to the monster, there's a double standard."
  • The verdicts — GM: "Just think, guys, this is the kind of friend Kaelen is"; Mu-Jin: "technically the best thing for us, but narratively you kind of seem like a backstabbing cult bastard"; Iryoku: "Maybe the rock would be a better friend" — were still being delivered two fights later. "Is this going to be a running joke now, for sure, this campaign?" — "Yes."
