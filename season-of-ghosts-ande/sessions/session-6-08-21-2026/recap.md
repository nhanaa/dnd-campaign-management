## Session Summary

The party rode home from the Canary Inn, slept, and went south at dawn to keep the promise they'd made to the Great Willow — and walked into the hardest fight of the campaign. The **infested grove** on the western bank of Dragonfly Creek was a carpet of gray and yellow fungus with one resident: a **"mushroom man"** who answered *"Who goes there?"* with a sob story about vanished kodama and a colony that cast it out. Kaelen answered it with *"Your mom."* Mu-Jin's hero-pointed Recall Knowledge named it — a **myceloid**, carrier of **purple pox**, the exact infection the Great Willow brought home — and the story collapsed. What followed was the campaign's first fight with real teeth: the myceloid's spores put purple pox in Kaelen and then **dominated him outright** (a failed Will save with no hero points left — *helpful* to the myceloid for a full minute, unable to raise a hand against it), it **two-punched Zhen into the dirt**, and two consecutive critical fists **dropped Iryoku** from the fight. Mu-Jin ran dry of healing. Kaoru ended it: **Sky Laughs at Waves** to recharge his Spellstrike, then **Ignition through the ninjato** — a natural 19 — into the weaknesses the party had pried open. The grove began to heal the moment it died. Kaelen's turns of standing by while his friends were beaten — *"Forgive me, my friend, I just got to do this"* — became the table's new running joke before the fight was even over.

Healed and humbled, they tracked the sound of grinding stone into the **Gorge of Fangs and Teeth** and found **Ugly Cute** — the Spider Gate's stone guardian, missing since the first dawn of summer — being *eaten* by three **living boulders**. The party readied actions and ambushed the ambushers as they surfaced: Kaoru's readied strike, Kaelen's readied crit, and Iryoku's hero-pointed crit blast deleted the first; the second crit Kaoru for 24 and then died to **Zhen's readied Brace strike** (a natural 20 — 34 damage plus precision, *"maximum damage"*); Zhen's second crit finished the third. Then Ugly Cute spoke, and knew every one of them by name, Weaver included: it had been waylaid by **faceless monsters** tormenting folk on Willowshore's outskirts, pursued them, and crawled here wounded. Its testimony reframed the whole mystery — the curse struck **at the height of the Reenactment ceremony** (not because of it), the mists are *"the beginning of the town's doom,"* the faceless things have **claimed an old lumber camp to the west near the Ceiba River's source**, and farther west it senses **a stronger malevolence** beyond its reach. Zhen and Mu-Jin's Recall Knowledge put a name to the faceless: **noppera-bo**, humanoids warped by curses into yokai. Ugly Cute broke off a shard of its own carapace as a gift — a spiked gauntlet, **Ugly Cute's Gift** — which Iryoku carries, though nobody at the table expects to use it much.

Back in town the elders heard the report (and sniped at each other through it), and the party **hit level 3**: Dispel Magic for Zhen, Fleet and two new skills for Kaelen (Diplomacy — *"Kaelen is no longer allowed to talk to people"*), rank-2 spells, Deception, and a scroll-fed extra spell for Mu-Jin. Iryoku's nat-20 Society check mapped the lumber camp, secret entrance and all. **Shinzo** the merchant returned with the party's orders and asked how they meant to approach the faceless things — fight, or fool them? When Zhen asked if he knew a method, he produced a **Noppera-Bo Hood**, and when the party moved to pay, waved it off as free. Zhen trusted that exactly as far as you'd expect (*"Nothing is free in this world"*); Kaoru's Sense Motive on the merchant's "I came from the south" got a flat *he seems to be telling the truth.* Later that day the party was at the **lumber camp's** secret stockade gap, listening to a rowdy celebration and the sound of crossbows, and standing in a dormitory whose walls were painted with blood silhouettes. Four **noppera-bo grunts** ignored them until Mu-Jin announced the party was from Willowshore. Two rounds later three were dead and one was face-down and breathing, knocked out nonlethally by Zhen for interrogation. The door closed on a cliffhanger, and the GM's parting note: this adventure runs on a four-act structure, and **you are still in act one.**

## Key Events

**Act I — Home and Out Again (night of day 9 → morning of day 10)**
- Nightfall at the Canary Inn; the party chose the river road home over camping ("Why do I have a bad feeling about this?" — Kaoru). Nothing happened. Zhen: *"This old man needs some sleep."*
- Morning plan: the grove first, the Gorge second (neighbouring hexes), report to the elders after. Governor theories that morning — Mu-Jin's teleporting-governor hypothesis met Zhen's *"In this backwater town? I don't know about that, kid"* and his flat deduction: *"His house is gone."* Iryoku's read: the thatchlings may not even have known he was gone.

**Act II — The Infested Grove (day 10)**
- Off-road to the western bank of **Dragonfly Creek** south of town: the once-idyllic willow grove overgrown with sheets of gray and pale-yellow fungi, spotted mushrooms and warty toadstools where the underbrush used to be. Recall Knowledge (Mu-Jin, Zhen): the fungus itself is largely harmless — *"Tell that to the giant centipede,"* said Kaoru.
- One fungus was not harmless. A **mushroom man** challenged the party (*"Who goes there?"* — Kaelen: *"Your mom."* GM: *"Says the monk of Sangpotshi."*). It claimed it had been **chased out of its underground colony**, that **the kodama who dwelled here welcomed it**, and that they all vanished — *"decades ago."* Kaelen asked the one sharp question (*"When did they vanish?"*); Mu-Jin asked what happened to the Willow's friend.
- Mu-Jin's **Recall Knowledge** (hero point pre-committed) landed: a **myceloid**, and — because he asked specifically — it carries **purple pox, exactly what was afflicting the Great Willow**. The party concluded it had been *"lying through its teeth"* (Kaoru). Kaelen: *"You know what always happens to liars? They always get their pants on fire."* **Combat** (below).
- Aftermath: with the myceloid dead, *"the grove is immediately beginning to recede with the mushrooms and mold shrinking, melting, and evaporating."* An hour's wait and the grove was fully restored. **120 XP.**

**Act III — The Gorge of Fangs and Teeth (day 10, afternoon)**
- Zhen's Survival (**16 vs DC 15**) picked up tracks leading down into the Gorge, and the sound of **rock grinding**. Three squat stony creatures were gnawing on a much larger rock — and the rock was **Ugly Cute**, the stone spider.
- Mu-Jin's RK: **living boulders** — earth elementals, **no weaknesses**, and they burrow. All three went underground after his Force Barrage; the party **readied actions** and ambushed them as they surfaced (combat below). **96 XP.**
- **Ugly Cute speaks.** After Zhen's Garden of Healing and a search for a repair kit nobody owned (*"We can come back later? We can drag it back to town?"* — it intends to stay and mend for a few days), the guardian addressed the party **by name — all of them, including Weaver**:
  - It was *"confronted by a group of faceless monsters who were tormenting people on Willowshore's outskirts,"* pursued them, *"ended up lost and wounded and had to return here to recover."* It's glad they're alive and helping the town; it declined Mu-Jin's offer to fetch a repair kit — *"you might have more important things to do."*
  - It had **visions of an abandoned Willowshore crumbling into ruins, reclaimed by the forest after a monstrous invasion** — *"It was that vision that allowed me to awaken and come to your aid."*
  - *"While I don't think the Reenactment festival was the source of the curse, whatever caused the curse did take place during the height of the ceremony itself."*
  - *"The mists surrounding Willowshore are the beginning of the town's doom, a curse brought upon the region by an unknown force — one that I sense is tied to the faceless monsters that I confronted. They have claimed an old lumber camp to the west of town near the Ceiba River's source. Perhaps clues about the curse can be found there. Farther west, I can sense an even stronger source of malevolence, but I haven't been able to observe the source myself, as it lies beyond the range I can travel from Willowshore."*
- **Recall Knowledge (very hard; Zhen and Mu-Jin succeeded, Weaver's Bolstering Aid on Mu-Jin)**: wild tales of **noppera-bo** — humanoids warped by curses into naturally spawning yokai. Zhen's follow-up — *"So, faceless creature. Does that mean they cannot see?"* — went unanswered for now.
- **Ugly Cute's gift**: a shard of its own stony carapace that fits over the hand — a **+1 spiked gauntlet** that, once a day, gains Ghost Touch and +2 spirit damage for a minute. The table lawyered it hard: not a monk weapon (no Flurry), not a sword (no Aloof Firmament), STR-based (not Kaoru's). Iryoku hesitated on principle (*"I already got one unique item that's been given to me"*), Zhen offered it to Kaelen, then floated selling it; Mu-Jin: *"We're not selling Ugly Cute's gift."* **Iryoku took it in the end** — for the once-a-day Ghost Touch, if nothing else. Kaelen asked whether they could carry Ugly Cute home; it needs to rest where it is.

**Act IV — The Elders, and Level 3 (night of day 10 → morning of day 11)**
- Home after dark; the elders were busy and would meet at sunrise. Kaelen and Kaoru pushed for one meeting rather than two (*"Let's not run our mouth twice"*). At the **Lady of Souls**, Mu-Jin delivered the full report — Canary Inn, grove, Gorge, Ugly Cute wounded not vanished, the lumber camp west and *"an even greater evil further west."* Kaelen added the faceless detail; Mu-Jin named them noppera-bo and called the lumber camp the party's next target.
- Granny Hu, to Matsuki: *"This is a greater fear than, say, accidentally hammering your finger doing carpentry."* Matsuki sneered. GM: *"Needless to say, these two people do not get along."* Zhen's promise: *"We would need more than just finding out — but be able to chase them away. Because it doesn't sound like they intend to leave us alone easily."*
- **LEVEL 3.** Rank-2 spells for every caster, a skill increase, an attribute boost, plus the house rule that **background Lore rises to expert**.
  - **Zhen**: **Dispel Magic** — *"the anti-unfun spell"* — picked with the Kaelen situation in mind; when he asked whether it would have helped, the GM confirmed the myceloid's control *was* magical. (Noise Blast was the selfish alternative.) Zhen also ran advisor for the table: WIS/CON for Kaelen's boost, *"use the ki spell more often,"* Thunderstrike over Noise Blast for Kaoru.
  - **Kaelen**: Acrobatics to expert; **Fleet** (40-ft speed — *"I gotta go fast"*); **Natural Skill → Survival + Diplomacy**: *"Because who knows? We might need a monk to talk to someone…"* Kaoru: *"You can't talk to anybody anyway."* Iryoku: *"Kaelen is no longer allowed to talk to people. Kaelen has caused several fights by attempting to talk to people."* The GM's own friend-group lore followed (the corrupt-mayor incident).
  - **Mu-Jin**: **Noise Blast** and **Loose Time's Arrow**; **Deception** trained; then the witch's scroll trick — **Learn a Spell** (Occultism 21 vs DC 18, 6 gp) to feed a spell to Weaver (Zhen lobbied for Dispel Magic — *"We can never have enough Dispel Magic"*; confirm the pick on his sheet).
  - **Kaoru**: spell shopping — Noise Blast through a Spellstrike? (single target only, and they get a save); the table's consensus was **Thunderstrike** over the swingy 2d10.
  - **Iryoku**: *"I have some healing now."*
- **Lumber camp intel** (Society RK): **Iryoku's nat 20** with a +0 modifier was the only success — and a crit. The camp, abandoned two years, has a **front gate, a back gate, and a secret gap in the stockade wall** the woodworkers used to sneak out to the nicer guardhouse. He drew the party a map.

**Act V — Shinzo Returns (day 11)**
- **Asahina Shinzo** was back with the orders: **iron wine, journey bread, the walking cauldron**. Mu-Jin had forgotten his name and guessed it blind (*"Oh, shit. I was right."*).
- The party told him about the noppera-bo and the lumber camp. His question back: *"Do you intend to bring furious ruin to them in battle? Or do you think you can fool them into believing that you are one of them?"* Zhen: *"Do you know a method that you can tell us?"* — and he produced a **Noppera-Bo Hood** (*"a completely smooth, round sack of skin that feels uncannily warm to the touch"*; 1/day Illusory Disguise as a **morph** effect, counts as a disguise kit for your own ancestry). Zhen moved to buy it; Shinzo: *"No need. You can have it for free… No strings attached."*
- Zhen: *"Nothing is free in this world."* … *"Conveniently has an item that conveniently helps us against a mysterious creature."* Kaelen: *"Are you sure? Because nothing is ever free."* Zhen called a Sense Motive (unresolved on mic); Kaoru's was the pointed one: *"He keeps saying he's coming from the south, but we know the fog stops everyone from moving through it."* GM: *"I don't think he comes in and out. He seems to be telling the truth."* Iryoku's verdict: *"If this guy's lying, he's been beating all of our perception checks we've ever made. So we should maybe not piss him off."* Zhen: *"We'll pretend that we really believe him."* Kaoru's practical note: the hood disguises you as your own ancestry — *"I would never come off as a human."*
- Bought: iron wine + journey bread (**6 gp** from the stash — *"It adds up, man"*). Orders for next time: **Dancing Scarf** (Iryoku). Zhen is saving for a **striking rune**. The hood went into the **party stash**.

**Act VI — The Lumber Camp (day 11)**
- Eight miles west on a poorly maintained road along the north bank of the **Ceiba River** — an hour at double time. From outside: **a rowdy celebration, smoke and roasting meat**, a 15-foot log stockade sharpened to stakes.
- Infiltrate or storm? Only Kaoru and Kaelen are trained in Stealth; Zhen: *"If one of us is going to fail a stealth check, we're just going to be cooked."* Consensus: the **secret gap** — even if they storm the place, storm it from the less-guarded side. Everyone found the hidden door except Iryoku, the man who drew it.
- Outbuildings: **toilets** (*"Paizo loves their toilets"*), **conspicuously empty stables** with old hay, and the sound of **crossbows going off** from a larger building. Inside: *"Blood splatters and weapon marks cover this dormitory's walls. Pools of dried blood stain the floor and bedding of the bunk beds. On the southeastern wall, several humanoid silhouettes have been painted using blood."*
- **Four noppera-bo grunts** inside — and they **ignored the party**. Mu-Jin's RK (Iryoku read the entry aloud): aberrations; *"a typical noppera-bo is cruel and revels in the act of frightening or tormenting humanoid creatures."* Information first, the table agreed — Zhen wanted one left alive; Iryoku: *"We could try and hold one of them and kill the rest."* Mu-Jin called out: *"You there, the ugly one. Have you visited Willowshore recently? We had a festival. Had some uninvited guests."* — *"You from Willowshore?"* — Mu-Jin: *"Indeed."* Kaelen: *"Correct."* Initiative. Kaelen: *"Thank God I didn't say anything."* Mu-Jin: *"So we have learned something. If you announce you're from Willowshore, they'll try to kill you."*
- Two rounds (below): three dead, **one knocked out alive** by Zhen for questioning. **60 XP.** Zhen noticed mid-fight: **their blood is purple.** The door was closed. Session.
- Debrief: Zhen — *"I feel like we're about to enter the climax."* GM: the AP follows a **four-act Japanese storytelling structure** rather than three — *"You're still on the first act."*

## Character Spotlight

### Zhen Stillness · the Old Man Who Got Back Up
Zhen's session was the campaign's first real brush with his own limits, and he played it straight. He opened the myceloid fight the way his sheet says to — caster first, Fear then Earth's Bile — and when the thing shrugged off the Fear, he closed in with the nodachi and found the weakness the hard way (*"Very good damage"* — GM: *"because this thing is weak to slashing"*). The myceloid answered by turning on him: *"Half my HP gone. More than half."* Then the second fist. *"This guy just two-punched me."* He was down, sword in the dirt, until Mu-Jin's Soothe brought him back — and his next turn was the unglamorous, correct one: stand, pick up the blade (*"How do you rule that — I have to pick up my weapon?"*), swing. Even flat on the ground he was the table's rules anchor: *"Maybe Dispel Magic?"* for Kaelen (no — rank 2, not yet), and later the catch that stopped Kaoru from Spellstriking twice without a recharge (*"You don't have it. You have to recharge it"*) — which led straight to the Sky Laughs at Waves turn that won the fight. In the Gorge he got his reward: a **readied Brace strike** on a natural 20 that crit the boulder chewing on Kaoru for **34 plus precision — "maximum damage"** — and a second hero-pointed crit (2 → 18) that finished the third. He was also, quietly, the party's **Survival anchor again** (the tracks into the Gorge), the one who asked Ugly Cute the follow-up nobody else did (*"Faceless creature — does that mean they cannot see?"*), the deductive voice on the governor (*"His house is gone"*), and the voice at the elders' table promising more than reconnaissance (*"chase them away"*). At level 3 he took **Dispel Magic** with Kaelen's domination in mind (*"the anti-unfun spell"*) and spent the rest of the level-up advising everyone else's picks. His read on Shinzo's free gift was the old man's whole worldview in five words: *"Nothing is free in this world."* Two smaller notes: he tried to find Ugly Cute's Gift a home — Kaelen first, then a sale Mu-Jin wouldn't hear of (it ended up with Iryoku) — and he kept his stash-treasurer's hat on over three gold of journey bread (*"It adds up, man"*). The session's last act was his: he walked up to the lone surviving grunt and **knocked it out nonlethally** — a nat 1, a hero point, a crit — so the party would have someone to question.

### Kaelen Ashford · the Kind of Friend Kaelen Is
This was Kaelen's session, for better and for worse, and he owned every second of it. He set the tone with the myceloid before a die was rolled — *"Who goes there?" "Your mom."* … *"You asked who was there, and I gave you an answer"* — and had the sharpest interrogation question in the scene (*"When did they vanish?"*). Then the spores hit: a hero-pointed Fortitude save that still gave him purple pox, and a Will save with nothing left to spend. *"Guys, I might be screwed."* For the rest of the fight Kaelen was **helpful to the myceloid** and barred from any hostile action — no flanking, not even a Demoralize — and he spent two turns trying to find a loophole (*"Can I betray him?" "Is there any way I can make him hit me?"*) while his friends were beaten unconscious in front of him. He played the condition in character rather than around it: *"Forgive me, my friend. I just got to do this"* — and, to Zhen, the line that gave it a moral shape: *"I won't do this to these people, but to the monster, there's a double standard."* The GM's verdict (*"Just think, guys, this is the kind of friend Kaelen is"*), Mu-Jin's (*"technically the best thing for us, but narratively you kind of seem like a backstabbing cult bastard"*), and Iryoku's (*"Maybe the rock would be a better friend"*) were still being delivered two fights later; Kaelen's *"Is this going to be a running joke now, for sure, this campaign?"* — *"Yes."* Once freed he played the Gorge exactly right — Parry plus a **readied strike** that crit the first boulder as it surfaced — and was the one who asked if they could carry Ugly Cute home. He pushed for a single meeting with both elders rather than two. At level 3 he made the most Kaelen choices available: **Fleet** (*"I gotta go fast"*) and **Natural Skill into Diplomacy** (*"We might need a monk to talk to someone…"*), which the table ratified by banning him from talking to people — and then, when the grunt asked if they were from Willowshore, he said *"Correct."* a beat after Mu-Jin had already said "Indeed" (*"Thank God I didn't say anything"*). In the dormitory he set up the flank on the grunt that had just stabbed Zhen (*"I'm going to give you a flanking for your next turn"*) and landed both Flurry hits, one of them on a hero-point floor; and when the party agreed to take a prisoner, his was the call on *which* one — *"Keep the near-death one alive so we can interrogate it."*

### Kaoru · the Closer
Quiet, precise, and decisive at exactly the right moments. Kaoru's skepticism was a half-step ahead of the table twice: *"Tell that to the giant centipede — things we think are harmless usually end up being a pain"* at the grove's edge, and *"We also know it was lying through its teeth"* the moment the RK came back. He caught the AC-22-or-23 error in the myceloid fight (*"If the base AC was 22 and it had a −1 penalty on it, the 21 should have hit"* — it was Kaelen's square giving cover). And he **killed the myceloid**: after a missed Spellstrike and an illegal second attempt that Zhen caught, the GM reminded him that **Sky Laughs at Waves** recharges it — so he flew, struck (5, doubled into the slashing weakness), and followed with **Spellstrike + Ignition at −4 MAP**: a natural 19, 13 damage into fire and slashing weakness, dead. (*"Oh my god, he got Nero's sword from Devil May Cry"* — Kaelen.) In the Gorge his readied Sky Laughs was the plan the whole ambush formed around (*"That way, when they show up, I can automatically be behind, flanking"*) — it fired first and hit — and he ate that fight's only enemy hit, a 24-damage jaws crit, for it. His **Sense Motive on Shinzo** was the session's sharpest social move: *"He keeps saying he's coming from the south, but we know the fog stops everyone from moving through it."* The GM's answer (*he doesn't come in and out; he's telling the truth*) is the first hard data anyone has on the merchant — and his kitsune-specific read on the hood (*"same ancestry as you, so I would never come off as a human"*) was the first practical question about using it. He was also the one who told Zhen to just buy the bread, and the one who, when Kaelen brought up his myceloid friend one more time, told him to quit talking about it. At the lumber camp: a **20-damage Spellstrike Ignition** that deleted the injured grunt.

### Mu-Jin · the One Who Asked the Right Question
Mu-Jin's folklorist-detective engine ran the whole session. He was the one who connected the mushroom man to last session's sick Willow — *"Can I Recall Knowledge to check if this is the same one who caused that?"* — and asked specifically about the infection, which is why the RK came back with **purple pox** and the myceloid's story fell apart. In the fight he ran support until he couldn't: Elemental Betrayal + Familiar of Balanced Luck every round, a **Soothe on Zhen** that he justified in character (*"narratively, him and Zhen are pretty close, so it makes more sense if I heal them"*), and then the honest broadcast — *"Guys, I'm out of healing spells, so you're on your own."* He also wrote Kaelen's epitaph: *"As a player, I appreciate what you did. But in-game, Mu-Jin's starting to beware of you."* At the Gorge his RK on the boulders (*"Weakness?" "No weaknesses." "Oh, shit."*) set the readied-action plan, and he was the first to Ugly Cute's side — *"Mu-Jin actually looks worried about it"* — offering to ride back for a repair kit, and shutting down Zhen's idea of selling the gauntlet: *"We're not selling Ugly Cute's gift."* He gave the elders' report in full (*"We will keep going until this evil is fully cleansed from our home"*), guessed Shinzo's name cold, trained **Deception** at level 3 (which is why the hood question landed on him and Kaoru), and at the lumber camp rolled the RK on the grunts and made the session's big judgment call — **no big spells, don't alert the camp**, then *"You there, the ugly one"* and *"Indeed"* — which started the fight but also taught the party the rule (*"If you announce you're from Willowshore, they'll try to kill you"*). Nat-20 initiative, four actions, cold-iron Needle Darts for 9 on a grunt with an AC *"that sucks"*; and when asked to take the last one alive: *"I'm a mage, there is no non-lethal."* (He punched it anyway. Missed.)

### Iryoku-Yasha · the Mapmaker
Iryoku's night was bracketed by two rolls that could not have been more different. The first: the myceloid's **two consecutive critical fists** — 28, then 26 — that took him from a hero-pointed Fortitude save (*"Fort saves are what I'm good at"*) to 4 HP to the floor, whip and Hardwood Shield clattering out of his hands. He laughed it off (*"I don't need to hold anything, so I have an easier time getting back up"*) and called the fight what it was: *"That was a severe, severe encounter on its own."* The second: a **nat 20 on a +0 Society check** that was the only success on the lumber camp — and the reason the party knew about the **secret gap in the stockade** before they arrived (even if he was then the one person who couldn't find it). In between he was the party's steady artillery: the hero-pointed **crit blast (18)** that finished the first living boulder, and in the dormitory **two kills** — a **Flying Flame through the entire grunt line** (after asking Mu-Jin's permission to singe him and Weaver) that burned down the hexed one, then a melee blast that dropped the quickened one. He was the party's Face at the grove (*"We're from Willowshore investigating the odd happenings"*), its rules-lawyer on the AC and Learn-a-Spell questions, the one who framed the capture plan (*"We could try and hold one of them and kill the rest"*), and its pragmatist on Shinzo: *"If this guy's lying, he's been beating all of our perception checks we've ever made. So we should maybe not piss him off."* His loot ethics showed — he tried to pass on Ugly Cute's gauntlet because he already carries Hongrui's cloak, and took it only when nobody else could use it — and his Kaelen commentary was the driest at the table: *"Didn't do as good as the rock. Maybe the rock would be a better friend."*

## Combat Encounters

**E1 — The Myceloid in the Grove** *(infested grove · 1 myceloid, run elite — AC 22 (23 behind Kaelen's cover), fist +16, Purple Pox DC 22, Spore Domination; weak to slashing 5 · 3 rounds (Foundry's counter had advanced to "Round 4" when the GM ended it) · the fight's first half predates the surviving log)*

*Initiative: Kaelen → Zhen → myceloid → Mu-Jin → Iryoku → Kaoru.*

- **Round 1** — **Kaelen** moved in and missed (21), hero-pointed the reroll and missed again (*"This guy's dangerous"*), then **Parried**. **Zhen** cast **Fear** — it saved (Will +13: *"That's so sad, man"*) — then **Earth's Bile**, which it **failed**: the burst plus 1 persistent fire (it recovered from that on its own turn). The **myceloid** **Emitted Spores**: Kaelen's Fortitude save (hero point spent — his last) still left him with **purple pox**; the follow-up **Spore Domination** Will save failed with nothing left to reroll — *"Good news: you no longer have purple pox.
