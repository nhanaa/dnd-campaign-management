Session Summary

The party finally settles into Nowhere proper — a meal at the Eye of the Storm feasting hall, a private-club errand at the Raid Leaders' Hall, a midnight expedition across the river to the refugee camp, a long rest, and a busy festival-eve morning of dock-side intelligence work. The arc of the night is pivot after pivot: away from killing Toki the halfling refugee leader (who turns out to be an unpaid activist, not a criminal), toward a free crew through mediation; from “Alwel the Dealer wants Toki gone” to “Alwel's map is the prize, and gone might just mean gone with us.”

Glug carries the Raid Leaders' Hall — gets the door password (“Mother's Milk”) from the innkeeper Zelan, identifies their target Varang (Asvard) by playing a wordplay drink-refill game with the half-elf intermediary Rufus, and later proposes the mediation-with-Toki idea that earns DM inspiration on the spot. Gaynor runs telepathic negotiation with Alwel the Dealer, a female “half”-orc who reads the room like a casino floor. Marzena drags the party across the river, then talks them off the assassination ledge once she sees the kids. Aesgor spends the session as the dry chorus — Guidance-cantripping with full RP theatre, voicing Sven's challenge, heckling the duel, and reframing Glug's overshare at the docks. Tree-Hammer wins a 1v1 duel against a self-promoting raider over six rounds, the flunky failing every shield-push before Tree-Hammer puts an axe to his neck.

The session ends just past midnight on festival-eve. Three captain dossiers are now in hand — Skuti, Hadd, Lurx — all three with material weaknesses. The path forward: festival-day at the Feast of Serpents.

Timeline

Act I · Roleplay · The Eye of the Storm (Feasting Hall #4)

Settling In at Nowhere

  • Forgotten campfire vision (retconned in). DM rolls back to a campfire from two sessions ago. The fire turns unnatural; the iron bust face (lips sewn shut, in Marzena's pack) tries to speak from the flames, fails, sees Siddhe and smiles. The fire explodes into kaleidoscopic visions: a great ship beneath a starless sky; an upside-down tower rising from a field of lava; a mass of serpents.
  • The innkeeper Zelan. Sabertooth-from-X-Men build, matronly graying hair, yellow teeth. Wears an amulet of two crossed wooden hammers — the symbol of the twin gods Nark and Idin. Husband died in a raid years back. The fish stew smells horrendous and tastes phenomenal.
  • Two ship dossiers from Zelan. The Grey — Captain Skuti (S-K-U-T-I), middle-aged, missing half his hand, crew loves him. The Raven Ship — Captain Hadd Thornbeard (H-A-D-D), six months in command, crew say he's quite courageous.
  • The shadow-puppet myth. A thin man in green casts shadow puppets of giants, serpents, horses, and warriors clashing — a re-enactment of A Wild World, the creation myth of Mirgal murdered, his body made into the world Grimnir.
  • Asvard, the unemployable Varang. Beat a man to death for no reason, takes any job, renting warehouse #3 in the dock district.
  • Oryx the half-orc carpenter. Marzena slips him a hip-dagger business card with her name scrawled in someone else's blood. Builder-class crew specialist, on soft retainer.
  • The password — Mother's Milk. Aesgor casts Guidance with full RP wand-and-vein theatre: “Mr. Gaynor. Please stand still. This is my stick. Injected into his vein.” Gaynor assists with Help action. Glug rolls 14 with advantage. They're in.
Act II · Roleplay · The Raid Leaders' Hall (Private Club #5)

The Dealer and the Map

  • The captains' room. Eight shield-tables, braziers of colored smoke, a wicker bartender kin to Kinickan serving drinks with extending wooden fingers. Hadd Thornbeard at one table; Rufus the half-elf alone at a corner table.
  • Rufus and the wordplay game. Glug fishes for Asvard's location. Rufus picks up his drink, repeats “Asvar, Asvar,” finishes it, sets the glass down empty, stares at it. Glug refills it. Rufus warms up: the Dockmaster knows everybody.
  • The Hadd / Lurx leak. Aesgor overhears Hadd say: “Lurks is looking to retire. That's funny what love will do for you.” First mention of Captain Lurx and her retirement leverage.
  • Alwel arrives. Female “half”-orc — tall, blonde, elongated jaw, oversized teeth. Trails her fingers down Gaynor's back as she passes. Reads the entire room. Gaynor opens telepathically: “I heard from a little bird with horns that you're in possession of a map.” Best offer on the map so far: 70 gold. Her stock changes frequently — currently has a Potion of Healing for 30 gp.
  • The Toki ask. Alwel would rather trade than sell: if Toki is no longer a bother, she'll hand over the Map of the Known World outright. As she leaves she winks at Tree-Hammer.
Act III · Social · The Pikey Camp, by Night (#6)

The Pivot

  • Marzena launches the night raid. “It's the perfect time to commit crime, baby.” No PC has Stealth proficiency. They go anyway. One copper per crossing.
  • The camp. Multi-colored canvas tents, fire pits, skewered rabbits. Half-orcs, dragonborn, tieflings — about thirty people. An angry halfling ranting about the Jarl stomps off when she sees the party.
  • The kids who change the plan. Half-orc and halfling children run up selling foraged berries. The half-orc child sprints back yelling “we have money for food now!”
  • Confronting Toki. De facto leader of the refugee camp. She's been agitating to get families accepted into town. Celorfin refuses to hire dragonborn or half-orcs — the major employer. The Jarl has forbidden new homes due to “scarcity of wood.” “We're just asking for basic human rights.”
  • The moral pivot. Marzena flips first. Glug: “There were kids around there. I'm not really into stabbing kids.” Tree-Hammer names the vote: “Three of the five of us have decided that killing this person is not the option.” Gaynor holds out: “I'll quite happily fry a fucking halfling.”
  • Glug's mediation pitch — DM inspiration. “Perhaps we could be the mediators.” DM grants inspiration on the spot.
  • Toki's counter-offer. If the party brokers homes and jobs, Toki can assemble a crew at no cost. Two levers: employment (Celorfin, the Jarl, the shipbuilders) and housing (Jarl's wood ban).
Act IV · Combat · Long Rest, Funeral, and the Sven Duel

The Morning of Two Pyres

  • Long-rest tells. Aesgor's Tuss-Afflicted die: 15 — safe. But for the second long rest in a row, Gaynor catches him grabbing his head in pain and trying to hide it. Aesgor cover-stories it as a hangover.
  • Zelan's slip. Lurx has been overheard disparaging Hadd: “Hadd is a terrible leader and not very smart.”
  • Morning funeral. A warrior who died in a brawl burns on a raft. Aesgor: “He will go to Valhalla.”
  • Sven's challenge. A young raider challenges Tree-Hammer to a 1v1 duel. Aesgor voices the threat for the table: “I challenge you to a duel. I'm gonna eat your fucking liver!”
  • The duel. See Combat section below. Tree-Hammer wins over six rounds. Sven fails every shield-push attempt. Final blow: “I will one-hit knock him, cut out his legs from underneath him, and then I'll put the second axe to his neck.”
  • Second pyre. Sven's body burns. Two funerals in one morning.
Act V · Investigation · The Three Captain Dossiers

Intelligence Gathering at the Docks

  • Celorfin greets them. “You guys are really putting coins in my pocket for all these faerings.” Aesgor sends Glug to ask about Asvard; Glug's confessional (“I kill people, and he's on the list”) sours the conversation. Aesgor takes over the interviews.

Captain Lurx — Ship of Vipers

  • Female gnome. Inherited the ship from her parents, as they inherited it from theirs.
  • Ship is older but seaworthy; snake-head prow recently rebuilt.
  • Crew is content but weary — little loot in recent raids.
  • Lost a duel to Skuti and was spared — he felt it would stain his honor to kill a drunken woman.
  • Overheard saying Hadd is a terrible leader and not very smart.
  • In love with a woman at a captured outpost. Wants to retire to live with her lover.

Captain Hadd — The Ravenship

  • Blond-haired human male. Raven-headed prow, older but seaworthy.
  • Raid leader for six months after winning the ship from the previous captain.
  • Served on several Knattle raids (led by Horar Longspear, a dwarf). Fought the Ravenship's former crew.
  • Brother died in that fight. Hadd then determined to claim the longship as his prize.
  • Still dislikes several crew members for their role in his brother's death — gives them unpleasant tasks and throws them into danger first.
  • Left the Knattle clan because he could not tolerate a dwarf being the jarl.
  • Known for taking risks; will do anything to win a challenge.

Captain Skuti — The Grey

  • Middle-aged human, sun-brown skin, missing half his left hand.
  • The Grey is the youngest longship in the docks — fine-milled silver-white pine, figurehead of a one-eyed goat.
  • Crew is proud of him, but past few raids have gone poorly.
  • Has been demanding loan repayments from many people around Nowhere.
  • Some worry he has too much gold — suspicion he's stealing from his own crew.
  • Confirmed same as Sculpty the Grey from session 3.
  • The festival plan crystallizes. Borrow Lurx's ship (love-story persuasion), provoke Hadd at the captains' table, race for pink slips. Backup: the Thinker's Game. Aesgor: “Challenge a man to the Thinker's Game. He's a dumbass.”

Combat

Tree-Hammer vs Sven

1v1 Duel · Day 14, mid-morning · Nowhere docks · 6 rounds

A young raider challenges Tree-Hammer to prove himself and earn a ship berth. Tree-Hammer rages. Sven attempts his shield-push ability every single round — fails every time against Tree-Hammer's rage-advantage STR saves.

  • Rounds 1–3: Sven cannot hit or push. Tree-Hammer chips away. Gaynor catches Tree-Hammer's missing +2 rage damage modifier. “Everyone starts laughing at him because the little guy is just not letting him move.”
  • Round 4: Sven lands a crit plus a follow-up. Tree-Hammer's HP drops into real trouble.
  • Rounds 5–6: Sven's dice go cold again. Tree-Hammer lands the kill blow.

Finishing move: “I will one-hit knock him, cut out his legs from underneath him, and then I'll put the second axe to his neck.”

No spell slots burned. No other PCs in the fight. Tree-Hammer ate one crit. Sven's body burns on a raft — second pyre of the morning.
