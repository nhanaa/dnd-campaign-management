## Session Summary

A divine vision from the Stone Court pulled Aesgor into an alleyway rescue that introduced the party to **Vanth** — a druid from another world, transported to Grimnir by a flash of light and carrying a broken lightning-struck staff that serves as his planar anchor. **Kinickan the Rune Thrower** confirmed that Vanth's arrival was foretold, and that reuniting the staff's fragments — scattered across the Serpent Sea — is the only way home. Vanth joined the party, bound by necessity and a fresh debt of gratitude.

With the new ally in tow, the party turned to unfinished business: **GluG's hunt for Asvard**, the Varang mercenary who murdered her step-family. A pitched warehouse battle across two floors left Asvard's hired thugs dead and Asvard himself cornered. Under a binding oath of safe passage, Asvard revealed the name behind the contract: **King Cenric of the Baendur**. Two Baendur coins bearing the king's likeness confirmed the connection. GluG now knows who ordered her family's death — a king, not a common thug.

## Key Events

### Act I — The White Raven's Call
- A striking **white raven** lands on a building and locks eyes with Aesgor, triggering a divine vision: a well-dressed stranger surrounded by five ruffians in a Nowhere alleyway.
- Aesgor tells the party *"I can't ignore that"* and rushes toward the vision's location. The party follows.
- The stranger is **Vanth** — a human in spring clothes, carrying a lightning-struck staff, clearly not from Grimnir.

### Act II — Street Fight (Rescuing Vanth)
- Five clanless raiders attempt to rob Vanth. The party intervenes.
- Vanth proves capable immediately, casting **Faerie Fire** from his staff and outlining three enemies in crimson light.
- Tree-Hammer **rages** in squirrel form, hurling javelins and dual-wielding axes with "violent chittering of success."
- Aesgor drops one raider with a crossbow bolt. Marzena drops another with a 13-damage crossbow shot. Gaynor finishes a third with Hex + Eldritch Blast.
- Tree-Hammer kills the fourth. The fifth surrenders and flees — Aesgor lets him go: *"I hope this is a good lesson for you. Now, go away."*
- City guards arrive, recognize the ruffians as known troublemakers, and escort Vanth to Kinickan.

### Act III — Vanth Meets the Rune Thrower
- Kinickan confirms Vanth's arrival was foretold by the bones.
- Vanth's **lightning-struck staff** is identified as a **planar anchor** — broken, with fragments scattered across the Serpent Sea. Reassembling it could open a portal home.
- Kinickan warns: *"You must keep this guarded with your life."*
- Vanth gives Kinickan an acorn. Kinickan cradles it like a child.
- The party takes a **short rest**, during which Vanth shares his backstory: a traveling caravan doing "the Lord's work," transported by a flash of light. Tree-Hammer heals during the rest (Hit Die: 8 HP).

### Act IV — The Warehouse Raid
- GluG leads the party to the warehouse district to find **Asvard**, the Varang who murdered her step-family.
- Marzena's **Perception 21** identifies the inactive warehouse. GluG retrieves the key from Celorfin.
- GluG kicks things off: eyes go black, **Thaumaturgy**-amplified voice booms through the warehouse, and she declares her presence.
- **Ground floor battle**: The party fights Gerald, Billy, Terry, Gordy, and Sweetcheeks across stacked crates and narrow aisles.
  - GluG's **Command (Flee)** sends Terry running to the back corner (Wisdom save 4 vs DC 12).
  - Vanth tries **Poison Spray** ("pocket sand") on a thug — resisted.
  - Tree-Hammer scrambles over crates using his Beastborn **climb speed**, dual-axes in hand — but has **no rages left** from the street fight and an earlier use.
  - Aesgor unleashes a **Guiding Bolt** on Billy for **21 radiant damage**, leaving him staggering — *"I'm gonna cast a real spell this time."*
  - Gaynor's **Dissonant Whispers** racks Gerald with 12 psychic damage, forcing him to flee — Gaynor triggers an Eldritch Blast OA via **Warcaster**.
  - Vanth heals Tree-Hammer with **Cure Wounds** (10 HP) when the barbarian drops low.
  - Aesgor uses the party's **healing potion on GluG** (8 HP) — *"Maybe it's time to be a healer."*
  - GluG returns the favor with **Lay on Hands** on Tree-Hammer (10 HP).
- **Second floor push**: Gordy flees upstairs screaming *"Boss, we're being attacked!"* The party pursues — Gaynor up the stairs, Vanth via Thorn Whip range, Aesgor listening at the door.
  - Vanth lands a **Thorn Whip critical hit** on Gordy, pulling him through stacked crates for bonus d6 damage.
  - Tree-Hammer climbs a timber beam to pop out next to Gordy: *"Hey, I'm back."*
  - Gordy **shoves Tree-Hammer off the crates** (contested Athletics), but Tree-Hammer's Beastborn **fall damage reduction** (30 ft) means he lands unharmed: *"I'll be back!"*
  - Vanth **Wild Shapes into a giant badger** and squeezes into Asvard's office.
  - Asvard closes and locks the door on the badger. Marzena, Tree-Hammer, Aesgor, GluG, and Gaynor all take turns smashing it — Gaynor's Eldritch Blast finally splinters it open.
  - Inside, **Asvard** fires his heavy crossbow at Aesgor (14 vs AC 18 — miss) and attacks badger-Vanth with a mace.
  - Vanth's **Primal Protection: Protruding Spines** triggers — 11 piercing damage + 5-foot push on Asvard when he lands a hit.
  - Marzena wades in with dual axes: *"I'm shielding you, Aesgor"* — lands both hits on Sweetcheeks for 13 damage.
  - Sweetcheeks goes down screaming *"I have always loved you, Asvard!"* He replies: *"I don't even know your name."*

### Act V — The Oath and the King's Name
- Asvard drops his weapons. GluG confronts him: *"My name is GluG. You killed my step-family. Prepare to die."*
- Asvard offers a deal: he'll tell the truth under a **binding oath** if the party swears to let him leave unharmed.
- The party swears. Vanth stays cautious: *"I ain't got no pony in this room. That's between y'all."*
- Asvard reveals: **King Cenric of the Baendur** commissioned the murder of GluG's family. The reason is unknown — *"I'm not sure how they upset the king, but that Baendur king really wanted them out of the way."*
- GluG: *"You've kept your oath. We will keep ours. But I will see you again."*
- Asvard leaves with his weapons. The oath holds.

### Post-Combat — The King's Coin
- Gaynor searches Asvard's office (Investigation 15) and finds **25 silver pieces** — two of which are **Baendur coins** bearing the face of **King Cenric**.
- Gaynor's History check (17) confirms the likeness. Physical proof connecting the king to Asvard.
- Vanth acquires winter clothing from the warehouse stores.
