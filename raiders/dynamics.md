## Aesgor's Role in the Party
- **Primary Healer/Support** — Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Bless
- **Secondary Tank** — AC 18, medium armor + shield, decent HP
- **Explorer/Navigator** — Eye of the Navigator, Survival/Perception advantage in wilderness, swim speed
- **Scout (with Aware)** — Once/long rest burst of Perception/Investigation power

## Key NPCs

- **Hrolf** — Veteran Hallharn warrior. Same tribe as Aesgor (and Marzena). Damaged internally — no proficiency bonus on attacks, gains exhaustion when pushing himself. Authority has eroded as the raiding party has been whittled down; after "double nowhere" he effectively defers to the group. Prayed to Hel personally after the zombie attack. **In Session 3 he drove the Oath of Fellowship at Nowhere's docks** once Celorfin confirmed the Whar colors — bled his own palm and demanded the party swear with him to kill **Gulli**, the Whar raid leader, before the snows melt. Now also focused on getting Siddhe to the volv (aligned with Kinickan).
- **Siddhe** — Displaced child from another world. Traveling with the party. Aesgor has promised to find her way home. Innately able to stabilize downed allies; revived Glug in the beach fight by literally jumping on her chest and bit-slapping her awake.

### NPCs at Nowhere (Sessions 3–4)
- **Egill** — Female tiefling, former ship's quartermaster. Has the Naglfar dread-vision. Will assemble a crew for 8 gp; *"knows a thing or two"* and will share for the right price. Sold her **map of the world** to Alwel the Dealer at the Raid Leader's Hall.
- **Celorfin** — Male elf, dock steward. **Missing one arm.** Refuses to hire dragonborn or half-orcs — the major employer in town. Despises the refugees. Confirmed the Whar were in Nowhere a week before the party. Witnessed the Oath of Fellowship.
- **Beowyln** — Female dwarf, Jarl of Nowhere. Silver braid and beard. Trains by knocking guards down. Wants paid dirt on Toki. Has **forbidden new housing** due to "scarcity of wood." Found at the Great Hall (#8) — also jailhouse and **Stone Court house of worship** with alcoves per god.
- **Toki** — Female halfling, de facto leader of the refugee tents (#6). Agitating to get families accepted into town. Can assemble a **free crew** if the party brokers employment and housing. Two levers: Celorfin's hiring discrimination and the Jarl's wood ban. Beowyln wants legal cover to arrest her. Alwel wants her gone.
- **Peron & Garsh** — Married couple at the shipyard (#11). Peron human male, Garsh half-orc male. Sitting on a half-built Explorer-class longship the party cannot afford (1,000 gp to finish, 6,500 gp new). Can also do **minor ship upgrades**.
- **Kinickan the Rune Thrower** — Male wicker, nine-foot wooden seer. Kin to the Kekel wicker sentries (DM said so). Reads runes and tattoos them; refuses to speak directly about more. **Insists Hrolf is right about Siddhe — protect her until she reaches the volv.** Trust the visions, not him.
- **Zelan** — Female Tuss innkeeper of the Eye of the Storm (#4). Amulet of the **twin gods Nark and Idin** (not on Stone Court roster). Source of the Raid Leaders' Hall password and captain intel. Has overheard Lurx disparaging Hadd.
- **Alwel, the Dealer** — Female "half"-orc. Operates from the Raid Leaders' Hall (#5). Stock changes frequently (currently: Potion of Healing, 30 gp). Holds Egill's **Map of the Known World** — willing to trade it if Toki is no longer a bother. Best cash offer so far: 70 gp.
- **Rufus** — Male half-elf intermediary at the Raid Leaders' Hall. Handles Alwel's dealings. Responsive to being bought drinks.
- **Captain Hadd** (Ravenship) — Blond-haired human male. Six months as raid leader. Won the ship after his brother died fighting its former crew. Left the **Knattle** clan (led by dwarf **Horar Longspear**) because he couldn't tolerate a dwarf jarl. Dislikes some crew for their role in his brother's death. Known for taking risks; will do anything to win a challenge. Lurx says he's **not very smart** — the Thinker's Game angle.
- **Captain Lurx** (Ship of Vipers) — Female gnome. Inherited the ship from her parents. Lost a duel to Skuti and was spared. Crew content but weary (little loot). **In love** with a woman from a captured outpost; wants to **retire**. Loaner-ship lever for the festival pink-slip race.
- **Captain Skuti** (The Grey) — Middle-aged human, sun-brown skin, missing half his left hand. Youngest longship (silver-white pine, one-eyed goat figurehead). Crew proud but raids poor. Demanding loan repayments around town; suspicion of **skimming from his own crew**. Confirmed same as Sculpty the Grey from session 3.
- **Oryx** — Male half-orc carpenter. Builder-class crew specialist. On soft retainer with Marzena — will join when the party has a ship.
- **Asvard** — Male Varang. Beat a man to death for no reason. Takes any job. Renting warehouse #3. Glug's second oath has him targeted.

## Faction Relations

### Raider Clans
- **Hallharn** — Aesgor's clan. Known for Mother's Brew mead (sweet fruit honey mead).
- **Knattle** — Hadd's former clan. Led by **Horar Longspear**, a dwarf. Hadd left because he couldn't tolerate a dwarf being the jarl. Relationship to Hallharn unknown.
- Other clans TBD as encountered.

### The Baendur
- Forest/hill-dwelling peoples. Historically subjugated by the Witchking, weakened after raiders overthrew him.

### Ironwood Witches
- Agents of the Witchking. Seeking to reassemble the Rainbow Spear.

### The Whar (formerly "Unknown Enemy Clan" / "War Clan")
- Named in Session 3 by Celorfin the dock steward of Nowhere.
- Attacked the Hallharn on the beach outside Kekel (session 2). Clan sigil keeps changing — predator (beach) → dancing horse → **wolf** (current, session 4). Pattern of identity-laundering.
- Were in Nowhere a week before the party arrived, bartering weapons for wheat. They left.
- **Their raid leader is named Gulli** — he led the beach attack. **Hrolf has sworn to hunt him down and kill him** before the snows melt. The Oath of Fellowship binds Aesgor, Gaynor, and Marzena to the same; failure = Curse of the Exile. Tree-Hammer refused; Glug ambiguous.

### The Stone Court (Gods)
- Petrified Vanir, now divine. Aesgor draws power from the entire court.
- **Aldyhn** — Leader of the Vanir, Aesgor carries a yarn effigy to Aldyhn.

## Plot Threads

- **The Oath of Fellowship clock (active).** Aesgor, Gaynor, and Marzena sworn to kill **Gulli**, the Whar raid leader from the beach attack — *before the snows melt* — or suffer the Curse of the Exile. Tree-Hammer refused. Glug ambiguous. **The campaign's main hard deadline.**
- **The Feast of Serpents — TOMORROW.** Annual festival honoring the Stone Court. Three captains will attend: Lurx, Hadd, Skuti. Festival challenges: tug-of-war, drinking, the Thinker's Game, ship-challenge for pink slips. **Plan:** borrow Lurx's ship (love-story persuasion), challenge Hadd via the Thinker's Game (he's not smart) or pink-slip race. Backup: Skuti's gold scandal as Jarl-side leverage.
- **Naglfar (Egill's vision).** Massive ship of nails, the dead crewing every inch, half-ruined woman at the prow. Egill destroys nails to stop it being built. Not recognized in-world (DC 30 History). End-of-the-world material; Hel-adjacent.
- **The fingernail link.** Egill's nails-in-the-fire ritual + the zombie raider's torn-off fingernails = the same thing, almost certainly. Aesgor has the players' connection but no character knows it yet (no check passed).
- **Falgore.** White marble. Where Gaynor's curse-caster *"yet lives… everlasting."* Specific named destination from Kinickan.
- **The Volva of the Well of Wisdom.** Tree-Hammer's destination via the gray feather, per Kinickan. Where Siddhe must be brought (Hrolf and Kinickan agree).
- **Twice you must walk those halls.** Aesgor's Kinickan vision. Return trip to **Kekel** required.
- **The Varang, the king, and drawn swords.** Glug's vision. A king pays a clanless raider; Glug's family blood drawn. Personal-arc seed.
- **Mediating Toki ↔ Jarl (active).** Toki offers a free crew if party brokers employment and housing. Two sales needed: get the Jarl to authorize tree-cutting/housing, and push Celorfin on hiring dragonborn and half-orcs. Additional paths: town guards for the Jarl, shipbuilder jobs. Beowyln still wants dirt on Toki — but the mediation path is better for everyone.
- **Alwel's Toki-out clause (exploitable).** Alwel wants Toki *no longer a bother* in exchange for the Map of the Known World. If Toki leaves with the party as crew, that still satisfies the wording. Worth pressure-testing.
- **Asvard (warehouse #3).** Confronted in Session 5. Confessed under a binding oath that **King Cenric of the Baendur** ordered GluG's family murdered. Released alive per the oath. Thugs (Gerald, Billy, Terry, Gordy, Sweetcheeks) killed. Asvard knows the party's faces and capabilities. *"I'll remember you all."*
- **King Cenric of the Baendur (GluG's vendetta).** Asvard confessed under oath that King Cenric commissioned GluG's family's murder. The reason is unknown. Two Baendur coins with Cenric's likeness are physical evidence. GluG's Kinickan vision — *"the Varang, the king, and drawn swords"* — is now half-decoded: the Varang is Asvard, the king is Cenric. The drawn swords remain.
- **Vanth's planar anchor.** Vanth's lightning-struck staff is broken, fragments scattered across the Serpent Sea. Kinickan confirmed his arrival was foretold. Reassembling the staff could open a portal home. Vanth needs a ship as badly as the rest of the party.
- **Tree-Hammer's private vengeance-quest.** Reason he refused the Oath. Now explicit at the table.
- **Tree-Hammer's gray feather.** Still hidden from the party. Kinickan reacted to it harder than any other rune. The table noticed; the characters have not confronted it.
- **Kinickan's nature.** Kin to the wicker sentries from Kekel ("a much more refined version"). Trust the visions, not the seer.
- **Who raises the dead?** Zombies are not supposed to exist in Grimnir. The zombie raider at "double nowhere" was missing all its fingernails — tortured before death. Hrolf prayed directly to Hel. Likely connected to Naglfar.
- **Ragnarok's harvest.** Valkyries are actively collecting the slain from pyres to reinforce the armies of the final battle. Aesgor is the only one with clear sight of them; Gaynor understood the implication instantly.
- **The iron bust (lips sewn shut).** Carried from Kekel. Still unexplained.
- **Siddhe's home.** Endless green farmland — not Grimnir. Aesgor has sworn to return her. The seer-path now points at Kinickan and the Volva of the Well of Wisdom (Hrolf and Kinickan agree she must reach the volv).
- **The retconned fire-vision.** The iron bust face tried to speak from the flames, saw Siddhe and *smiled*. Visions: starless-sky ship (Naglfar), upside-down tower from lava, mass of serpents rising. Whoever the bust is, he now knows Siddhe is with the party.
- **Aesgor's hidden morning pain.** Two long rests in a row Gaynor has caught him flinching on wake. The Tuss-Afflicted die didn't fire either time (15 both). Not the racial trait — something else, only since the rune-throwing.
- **Glug's "Lucha."** Named her goddess for the first time on the public record. Not on the Stone Court roster. Heretical for a Grimnir paladin?
- **The twin gods Nark and Idin.** On Zelan's amulet (two crossed wooden hammers). Not previously surfaced; not on the Stone Court roster. Worth a Religion check.
- **Glug's dual oath.** First public admission: *"two oaths are better."* Shows a second scarred palm. The second oath appears to target Asvard.
