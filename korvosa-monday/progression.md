## Forward Plan

### Level 7
- **General**: *Incredible Initiative* (Spellstrike alpha-strike value) is the front-runner; or shore up saves.
- **Skill increase**: push **Society to Master** (skill paragon payoff) or Arcana to Expert for Recognize Spell scaling.

### Level 8 — the payoff
- **Archetype feat: the Champion's reaction** (via Champion Dedication line — Basic Devotion → the reaction). This is the stated goal: durability + ally-protection reaction on top of the Spellstrike chassis. *("Then I'll be goated.")*
- **Class feat**: stronger Spellstrike riders (*Expansive Spellstrike* AoE-into-Strike, *Fortifying Strike*).

### Levels 9–10 (rough)
