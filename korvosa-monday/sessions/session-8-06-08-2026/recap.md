## Session Summary

With Jolistina dead and the Carowyn gala behind them, the party turned to the one thread still open: the missing head physician, **Dr. Davaulus** — the "Nicolas Cage doctor." The lead ran through **Blackjack** — and Izka finally caught up to what the rest of the party already knew: the vigilante is **Vencarlo Orsini**, the fencing master, one and the same. His tracking sigil had gone quiet at a refurbished warehouse "hospice" on the West Dock — people walked in, nobody walked out. He couldn't go in himself, so he sent the party. They strong-armed the temple's **Ishani Dhatri** into coming as a healer (over a relentless tide of "beat off / aftercare" jokes that visibly aged the poor cleric), donned plague masks, and walked in the front door as a fake **hospice inspection** — a bit Meeg improvised on the spot and rode all the way to a body count.

The inspection cracked the place open. A "Sarenrae" priest who knew the secret handshake but not a single farmer's aphorism; masked "Queen's Physicians" who were really the doctor's private muscle; and **Reiner** himself, all plastered smile and twitching eye, who led them upstairs and then refused a handshake one beat too long. The fight that followed was a grind — Kukulan soaked crit after crit (keeping up his bit about always dying), Reiner revealed an inexplicable bard's **Courageous Anthem**, threw **Fear** and a **Fireball** that caught his own physician, and tried to turn invisible and flee. It ended on a stupid, perfect note: **Meeg cracked open a scroll of Acidic Burst on himself**, Reiner **crit-failed the save twice**, and the doctor — whom Kukulan had been carefully trying to keep alive — simply died on the floor.

With Reiner dead, the last surviving Queen's Physician — prone and surrendering — pointed them to an elevator and "the bottom floors." Down it went, into a lightless **Urgathoan temple** of scythe murals and cultists playing blackjack around a crate where they were "making a blade to kill people." The party bluffed, gathered that the operation was commissioned by **nobility — someone royal**, reports addressed to "**A**" — then masked up and cut them down. A dying cultist crawled toward **Rolth** before bleeding out, finally pinning the necromancer's location to this temple. One more room — two explosive **sulfur zombies** over a **glass floor** above a pit of dozens of bodies — ended with Kukulan smashing the glass to drop one zombie into the pit and pinning the other for the party to kill safely. They leveled to **6** somewhere in the middle of all of it, and walked out having killed the doctor, traced the plague up toward the crown, and found where Rolth has been hiding the whole time.

## Key Events

### Act I — Loose Ends at the Manor (RP / Downtime)
- The party is squatting in the dead noble **Roran Carowyn's** manor (the S7 gala house). Roran fled, traumatized and never coming back; he offered to sell the deed for **400 gp**, but the party just decided to live there for free.
- **Delights** tried to raise a thrall of the dead **Jolistina** to interrogate her — no languages, no answers, just a corpse staring. **Kukulan** "helped" by crushing her head.
- The only live lead on the plague is the **missing doctor**, and the way to him is **Blackjack**. They head to the Orsini Academy to summon him.

### Act II — The Man Behind Blackjack (RP)
- After dark, the (quarantine-closed) **Orsini fencing Academy**. Kukulan presses his face to the glass; **Trinia** answers; inside is **Vencarlo Orsini**.
- Kukulan flexes his **Blackjack chest tattoo** and produces a deck of cards (and, when Vencarlo arrives with takeout, hurls it at him yelling "**hit me!**" — Vencarlo catches it). Vencarlo confirms it: **"Me and Blackjack are one and the same."** **Izka**, the newcomer, genuinely didn't know — the rest of the party already did ("Oh, does he not know? I assumed he knew").
- The **literacy scene**: Kukulan vouches that Izka "knows how to read and everything," prompting Vencarlo to note Kukulan *doesn't* — and **Delights** to clarify he's a literate arcane caster who only plays dumb "in solidarity with my brother Kukulan, who is so stupid." Kukulan, wounded ("I'm street smart — it's a different kind of intelligence"), fake-sobs off to "borrow a room."
- Backstory beats land: **Trinia** (here, at his house) is a recent **Acadamae expellee** — framed for the king's death ("regicide") two weeks ago — while Izka's a ten-year alum; the knighthood "that means nothing"; the execution where "Kukulan killed so many people" (the one guard who survived).
- **The hook:** Blackjack's tracking sigil (from "Morris") stopped at a **warehouse-hospice at the West Dock (grid G9)**. Windows boarded, entrance guarded, and *no one ever comes out* — not the doctor, not his masked physicians. He can't go in as a wanted anti-royalist. The party can.
- They crash at Vencarlo's empty house (he has no kitchen — buys them pot pie and **Cooperative Waffles Greater**). Kukulan tries to "inherit the Blackjack title" in a card game but **loses the Games Lore roll**; instead the party — several of them Queen's knights — formally make **Izka their squire** to fit the newcomer in.

### Act III — Recruiting Ishani & the Inspection (RP)
- They strong-arm **Ishani Dhatri** out of the plague temple to serve as a healer ("there's about to be a lot of casualties at this hospice"). Comedy runs hot: Ishani is fragile (41 HP, 14 AC unarmored), the "stop wearing armor, that's what a *real* healer does" bit, and the entire party's unending "beat off / aftercare" innuendo.
- **Ishani's mechanics**: one action/round (+2 if a PC Commands him), **Battle Medicine** + four rank-3 Heals. He's handed a **Wand of Mystic Armor** and **Dust of Disappearance**. **Meeg** preps **Root Magic** on Kukulan (+1 save vs the first spell).
- The warehouse is ~50 ft tall and sealed. They go in the front as a **hospice inspection**, wearing Ishani's plague masks. **Meeg** runs the bit as **"Meeg, the Cromulent Knight."**

### Act IV — Phonies in the Plague Ward (Investigation → Combat)
- A muscular masked **receptionist** (obviously a thug) lets them wait and fetches the doctor. Inside: a vast triage hall of **blood veil** victims, masked **Queen's Physicians**, and a **"Sarenrae" priest**.
- **Meeg works the phonies.** The fake Sarenite dodges a handshake ("I've been touching sick people") — Meeg: "Very good. **You passed your first test.**" Kukulan, meanwhile, plays germ-theory expert ("Germ theory is something I'm very knowledgeable in" — *steps six feet back*). The Sarenite knows the secret dap but **fails the real aphorism** ("the sun and *what* are needed to grow?"); Meeg's Sense Motive + **Esoteric Lore** confirm the robes are fake ("I don't like people who pretend to be something they're not"), and he leaves his familiar, **"Cousin Lush," as a spy** on the impostor.
- **Reiner** (the "Nicolas Cage" doctor) appears, half-recognizes them from "the Citadel," and leads them upstairs to his office — two Queen's Physicians and two Grey Maidens flanking. He claims no royal funding; Sense Motive says he's lying. He refuses Kukulan's handshake, and **initiative drops.**

### Act V — "He Hates All of You" (Combat 1)
- See **Combat Encounters → Encounter 1.** The doctor's whole operation throws down: Reiner, 2 Grey Maiden foot soldiers, 2 Queen's Physicians. Reiner reveals a bard's **Courageous Anthem**, lands **Fear** on the whole party, and **Fireballs** his own side trying to escape — before **Meeg's Acidic Burst scroll** crit-fails him to death.
- With Reiner dead, the **last surviving Queen's Physician** — prone and surrendering — points to an **elevator** down to "the temple" and "the bottom floors," warns them off the southern room (~a dozen **Varisian** plague victims strapped to beds), and admits "what you seek is in the bottom floors."
- Loot pulled: **Plaguebringer's masks**, a note addressed to "**A**" ("send all reports to A down below"), and an **elevator button-key**.
- **The party levels to 6.**

### Act VI — The Temple Below (Investigation → Combat)
- The elevator descends a minute into a dark **Urgathoan temple** — scythe murals, obvious iconography (Izka's Religion check). The party is blind in the dark; Izka glows his horn, Ishani/Delights light a torch.
- Four cultists are **playing blackjack** around a crate where they're "**making a blade to kill people**." Bluffing as new recruits, the party learns the job came from **nobility — someone royal** ("not the king"), reports going to "**A**." Kukulan — who can't read — "deduces" the handler must be **Sabina** because "A and S are almost next to each other" (a bit, born of his illiteracy). The players half-seriously float **Queen Ileosa**; nothing's proven.
- Kukulan **disables the door lock** to seal the gamblers, then the party masks up (now four **Plaguebringer's masks** + a **Masquerade Scarf**) and bluffs the next chamber — until "Blackjack" sets the cultists off and the fight starts.

### Act VII — Where Rolth Is (Combat 2 → Combat 3)
- See **Combat Encounters → Encounters 2 & 3.** Cultists of Urgathoa, a Harpy Skeleton, and Queen's Physicians fall fast to Izka's Spellstrikes, Kukulan's Snagging-Strike/Stunning-Blows, Delights' thralls, and Meeg's Exploit Vulnerability. (An **Abaddon priest** was present but sat the fight out in the back chamber.)
- A dying cultist crawls toward **Rolth** and bleeds out — **Rolth's location is finally confirmed: down here, in this temple.**
- The last room: a **glass floor over a pit of dozens of bodies**, guarded by two explosive **sulfur zombies** (weak to vitality; **Death Throes** ~27 dmg, 30-ft burst). Kukulan smashes the "bulletproof" glass and **drops one zombie into the pit**, then grapples and pins the second so the party can kill it safely — its death-explosion deals a trivial **13 damage**. Won "in record time."
- Aftermath: Ishani de-cloaks; Kukulan admits he wrecked the elevator (Delights' **Helpful Steps** can climb them out, 120 ft); the DM credits the whole run to Meeg's improvised health-inspector bit.

## Character Spotlight

### Izka — The Acadamae Blade
- The party's actual **face and brain.** Held his own with the prickly **Trinia** as a gracious ten-year **Acadamae** alum — taking her shade with a smile (*"I will pretend I didn't notice that"*) and flipping it into sympathy (*"you don't seem like a person that would get caught in such a thing… who would try to blame you on that?"*).
- The only one who actually studied magic — and he let the illiterate party know it. When Kukulan figured the cultists could "just teleport out," Izka set him straight: *"As someone who is well-versed in the art of arcane, I can assure you that it's not that simple… the spell is the whole thing, you know."*
- The measured counterweight to the party's brutality: caught **Reiner's** verbal slip mid-interview (*"don't think I didn't catch that"*), and when Kukulan moved to break the surrendering physician's legs, **talked the man down instead** (*"This guy is not too used to this violence method… so why do you not want to talk about it?"*). As the lone literate PC, he read the note to **"A"** and IDed the **Urgathoan** iconography below.
- A devastating Spellstrike chassis on a dragon: **Combat 1** brought a **Gouging Claw crit-kill** on a Grey Maiden (36), a **Cavalier's Charge crit** (40), a **Force Fang → Gouging Claw** into a Physician (35), and 29 into Reiner — all while shielding the healer (*"Hey, don't hit the healer! Ishani, get down!"*).
- His Acadamae schooling as a weapon: clocked that the **Harpy Skeleton** shrugged off steel — *"I learned about it in school; we need magic against it"* — and **switched to an Ignition Spellstrike to destroy it.**
- So reliable the DM admitted he'd "never seen a single Spellstrike miss all night" — which Izka, ever the self-aware one-tracker, shrugged off as "lame" even while deleting everything he touched (a Telekinetic-Projectile Spellstrike + three guisarme Strikes set up the last **sulfur zombie** in **Combat 3**).
- Closed the Rolth thread with a corpse-side one-liner: *"That's very good information, Mr. Already Dead."*

### Kukulan — Down Once a Fight, Up Twice
- Wolf Stance, Flurry, and a relentless grapple/trip campaign on Reiner and the Grey Maidens; **Stunning Blows** kept the doctor staggered.
- Soaked a streak of crits in **Combat 1** (kept up his "it's not real combat unless I'm dying" bit) — a 24 from a Grey Maiden's longsword and a 28 from a Physician's scalpel, patched by **Meeg's scroll (25)** and **Ishani's Battle Medicine (25)**.
- **Combat 2's heaviest hitter:** a **48-damage Wolf Jaws crit** (two crit fails on the save) dropped a Queen's Physician outright — the single biggest hit of the night — and a **Stand Still** reaction clipped the Harpy Skeleton mid-move.
- In **Combat 3**, smashed the bulletproof glass floor to **drop one sulfur zombie into the pit**, then grappled and pinned the second so the party could detonate it safely.
- The merciful grappler: ran the **fake-Sarenite trap** ("the sun and what are needed to grow?"), tried to take **Reiner alive** before the Acidic Burst made it moot, and momentarily played earnest doctor ("We need to get these patients out of here, stat!" — Delights: "you sound more like a doctor than he does").
- The table's comedy engine: the recurring **feminism bit** ("I had to lecture this man about feminism," using Roran's misogyny as moral cover for squatting his house), **germ theory** ("Germ theory is something I'm very knowledgeable in" — *walks away* six feet), the "stop wearing armor, that's what a *real* healer does" coaching of Ishani, threw his deck at Blackjack yelling "**hit me!**", and named his mount after the Queen he idolizes.
- The "deduction engine": as the illiterate one, gravely concluded the "A" handler must be **Sabina** ("A and S are almost next to each other"), then spiraled into suspecting Blackjack — all "fueled entirely by **Blue's Clues**." Also bluffed the cultists cold ("It's called gathering information, Ishani").

### Delights Qlomaind — The Opportunologist
- Tried (and failed) to interrogate Jolistina's corpse; reframed a plague ward as "an opportunity."
- Combat engine all night: **Hypnotic Lure** + **Taunt** to pull and pin foes, **Thralls**, **Muscle Barrier** feeding Kukulan temp HP and +1 Athletics, and the clutch **Wall of Shadow** to box in Reiner's escape. In Combat 2, his **Grim Tendrils + a Thrall (27)** softened the Harpy Skeleton down for the kill, and his thralls finished the last sulfur zombie.
- The session's driest deadpan — and almost none of it made the plot summary: the **Blackjack bat-signal bit** ("light a large lantern… a symbol of a large tube like what a blackjack is… and 21 on a card"), **"Welcome to my life, babe"** at Vencarlo's empty bachelor house, the sharpest delivery of the aftercare gag ("if you don't play your cards right, we're going to beat you off too"), and his signature **"Got one word: Helpful. Two words: Helpful Step."**
- The necromancer who hates necromancers ("Necromancers, yuck") and the only PC besides Izka who can read — a fact he clarified mainly to dunk on Kukulan ("in solidarity with my brother Kukulan, who is so stupid").
- Connected the plot dots out loud — "someone royal… and those guards were playing **blackjack**" — then deadpanned his Blue's Clues correction: *"I watched Indigo Inquiries."*
- Quietly leveled into **Champion** and kept the running commentary going all night.

### Meeg — the Cromulent Knight *(player-run again)*
- **Improvised the entire hospice-inspection con** — "Meeg, the Cromulent Knight" — and rode it the whole way, flashing his **knightly crest** as his all-purpose prop ("if there's one thing about knights, they always speak some truth").
- The throughline under the con: **an inspector who hates phonies.** He clocked the fake Sarenrae priest by content and character — "Very good, **you passed your first test**" (when the priest dodged a handshake), *"I don't like people who pretend to be something they're not,"* and the capstone once the fight started: *"Fake priests, fake doctors… they're not even sick."* Even left his familiar, **"Cousin Lush," as a spy** on the impostor.
- Did the actual detective work: **Sense Motive (Perception)** and **Esoteric Lore** to expose the fake robes, **Exploit Vulnerability + RK** all session feeding the party weaknesses and lowest saves.
- Combat: crit his initiative off **Twitchy** (+4 vs the doctor's Deception); opening **Chakram (23)** then a **Liar's Hidden Blade Chakram that chunked Reiner for 30**; the **rank-2 Heal scroll** that revived Kukulan; and the fight-ender — a **scroll of Acidic Burst** that crit-fail-deleted Reiner.
- Read the **sulfur zombies' Death Throes** in Combat 3 and called Kukulan's grapple: *"German suplex into the pit!"* The DM's verdict: the inspector bit "annoyed the receptionist enough that she made it the doctor's problem, which got him killed."

### Ishani Dhatri — Best Boy *(DM-run NPC)*
- Recruited under duress and mortified by the party's vocabulary — but gamely played along with the inspection bit ("It's not up to my standards, and that's saying something").
- Actually earned his keep: a mid-Combat-1 **Battle Medicine (25)** that kept Kukulan standing, a Combat-2 **rank-3 group Heal (12 each)**, and **Invisibility** to slip the party past the lower cultists. Stuck between two melee men all of the first fight, he took a blade across the chest (10), refused to fall back ("I need to stand tall still for all of you"), and once just **clubbed a Grey Maiden with a sack of coins** ("I panicked — I hit him with my coins").
- The conscience nobody asked for: objected to the *killing*, not just the jokes ("I don't know why we're killing these people"), and — after the party tried to apologize and dragged him into a bit — flatly declined ("…You're no fun"). Got left holding the bag when Izka funneled cultists his way ("It was a tactical maneuver, Ishani").

## Combat Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Doctor's Office (Hospice, upstairs)
**Enemies**: **Reiner** (Davaulus — the boss), 2 Grey Maiden Foot Soldiers, 2 Queen's Physicians (surgeons).
**Initiative**: Gray Maiden Foot Soldier → Queen's Physician → **Meeg** → **Kukulan** → Reiner → Gray Maiden Foot Soldier → Queen's Physician → **Izka** → **Delights** → **Ishani** *(Ishani always acts last)*.
*Reiner rolled **Deception** for initiative; Meeg's **Twitchy** gave +4 against it, crit-starting him at 32.*

- **Opening:** Grey Maidens draw and **reposition Kukulan** off the doctor (dragged "over the table"). A **Queen's Physician** uses **Medical Malpractice** to mark a target, then **crits Izka for 15**, chewing through his 10 False Vitality temp HP.
- **Meeg:** Exploit Vulnerability + Esoteric-Lore RK on the foot soldiers (lowest save = Reflex), Intimidate, **Chakram for 23** — then his biggest hit of the fight, a **Liar's Hidden Blade Chakram that chunks Reiner for 30** ("half his health").
- **Kukulan:** Wolf Stance + Flurry; grapples/trips Reiner and a Grey Maiden; **Stunning Blows**. Soaks a streak of crits (a **24 from a Grey Maiden's longsword**, a **28 from a Queen's Physician's scalpel**) — **Meeg's rank-2 Heal scroll (25)** early and **Ishani's Battle Medicine (25)** later keep topping him off. The Grey Maiden spends turns repeatedly standing up after his trips ("local woman hater").
- **Izka:** mounted on Po — a **Gouging Claw Spellstrike crit-kills a Grey Maiden Foot Soldier** (36 + bleed, "she's fucking dead, bro") and he enters **Arcane Cascade**; **Force Fang → Gouging Claw Spellstrike chunks a Queen's Physician for 35**; a **Cavalier's Charge crits another Grey Maiden for 40**; a mounted Spellstrike puts **29 into Reiner**. (He ate a 15 Medical-Malpractice crit and the 21 Fireball along the way.)
- **Delights:** **Hypnotic Lure** pulls a physician in, **Taunt**, thralls, **Muscle Barrier** feeding Kukulan temp HP and +1 Athletics, and a fight-defining **Wall of Shadow** to cut off Reiner's escape.
- **Ishani** (NPC, acting last): keeps Kukulan up with a mid-fight **Battle Medicine (25)**, takes a Grey Maiden's blade across the chest (10), refuses to fall back ("I need to stand tall still for all of you"), and at one point — out of options — just **clubs a Grey Maiden over the head with a sack of coins** ("I panicked… I hit him with my coins").
- **Reiner's escalation:** reveals **Courageous Anthem**; casts **Fear** (rank 3 — frightened 2 to the whole party, Ishani included); **punches**; throws a **Fireball** that catches Izka, Kukulan, Po, and *his own Queen's Physician* (Meeg blunts it with **Amulet's Abeyance**); tries **Battle Medicine** (can't see — auto-fails); casts **Invisibility** and scrambles.
- **Resolution:** **Meeg pops a scroll of Acidic Burst on himself** (5-ft emanation). **Reiner crit-fails the Reflex save — two crit fails — and dies**; the last Queen's Physician is left dying, a Grey Maiden foot soldier dies, and Izka takes only 3. **Kill credit: Meeg** (Acidic Burst). Kukulan had gone non-lethal to keep Reiner alive for questioning — too late.

### Encounter 2 — The Urgathoan Temple (bottom floor)
**Enemies**: 2 Cultists of Urgathoa, a **Harpy Skeleton**, 2 Queen's Physicians. (An **Abaddon priest** was in the chamber but sat the fight out.)
**Initiative**: **Izka** → **Meeg** → **Kukulan** → Cultist of Urgathoa → Harpy Skeleton → Cultist of Urgathoa → Queen's Physician → Queen's Physician → **Delights**.

- A cultist summons a skeleton to reinforce the line.
- **Izka:** a **Gouging Claw Spellstrike chunks a cultist (25)** — *"Holy shit, I'm so efficient"* — into **Arcane Cascade**; **Force Fang finishes another cultist**; and a **Reactive Strike** (new L6 reaction) **crits a mover for 22** (+ persistent fire).
- **Kukulan:** Flurry → **Snagging Strike** (off-guard) → a **48-damage Wolf Jaws crit** that crit-fails its save and **drops a Queen's Physician** (the night's biggest hit); later uses **Stand Still** to clip the Harpy Skeleton mid-move (7). **Meeg:** Exploit Vulnerability + **Intimidating Glare** + Chakram (26).
- **Delights** runs the back line: Thralls, **Bone Spear**, and — crucially — **Grim Tendrils (12) + a Thrall (15)** that soften the **Harpy Skeleton** down for the finish.
- **The Harpy Skeleton** screams (**Shriek** — 4d10 mental in a cone, hitting Po, the party, *and both its own cultists for 10 each*) when its master dies, and maneuvers over the beds, **shrugging off piercing** ("we need magic against it"). With Delights' 27 already on it, **Izka's Ignition Spellstrike** — fire, the magic he'd called for — **destroys it for 26**.
- **Ishani** closes the fight with a **rank-3 group Heal** (12 each to Izka, Delights, and Meeg).
- **Rolth reveal:** a dying **Cultist of Urgathoa** crawls toward **Rolth** ("I need to get to Rolth!") and bleeds out at the door — confirming Rolth is down here. *"Well, now we know where Rolth is."*

### Encounter 3 — The Glass Floor (sulfur zombies)
**Enemies**: 2 **Sulfur Zombies** (explosive — **Death Throes** ~27 damage, 30-ft burst; **weak to vitality**). They shamble forward, drawn by the dead Urgathoan's corpse.
*No Foundry roll data was captured for this fight — reconstructed from the audio.*

**Setup:** the room is a **glass floor over a pit of dozens of bodies** — like a glass bridge, it cracks under too much weight. **Izka**, full on spell slots, calls his shot: *"Don't worry, I have my full spell slots, so I'll be quick."*

**Round 1**
- **Kukulan** goes for the shortcut — smash the floor and drop them in. The glass is "basically bulletproof" and only cracks at first, but his second Athletics swing **caves in half the floor and one sulfur zombie drops into the pit** — gone, no explosion ("goodbye corpse… that part's no longer a problem").
- **Izka** directs **Ishani** to get the far door open, then — riffing on his one-trick (*"I'm so lame, I just do the same thing all the time"*) — does the same thing anyway: a **Spellstrike with Telekinetic Projectile** as the payload (the guisarme delivering the cantrip), and raises **Shield**.
- **Delights** tumbles across the glass (Acrobatics DC 15), flanks, and **Taunts** the zombie.
- **Meeg** climbs onto a bed and **Exploits Vulnerability** — "it fucking hates vitality" — with a free Recall Knowledge flagging its **Death Throes** (27 damage, 30-ft burst on death).

**Round 2**
- **Kukulan** walks up and **grapples** the last zombie to drag it pit-ward (Meeg: *"German suplex into the pit!"*). The zombie **tries to Escape twice and fails both.**
- **Izka**, acting right after it, unloads on the pinned, flanked target — **three guisarme Strikes land, the fourth misses** — then deliberately **leaves the kill to Delights** (*"the light will finish it"*) so the death-explosion goes off on the party's terms, not in his face.
- **Delights** steps back and summons **two thralls** flanking; their slash drops the zombie. Its **Death Throes** detonate for only **13 damage** to the party (Kukulan had it restrained and shrugged the blast). Won "in record time."

### Things Izka knows that the party does not
- **The conspiracy reaches the crown.** The cultists said the job came from *royalty* — nobility, high up. The note to "**A**," the Grey Maidens, the "Queen's Physicians" that aren't really the Queen's: my read is **Ileosa**, though it's unproven. Kukulan can't read and keeps joking it's "Sabina" (A≈S) — don't let that become the party's working theory.
- **The temple cultists were making "a blade to kill people"** for **Rolth** — and **Rolth's lair is down there.** Reiner's hospice fed the same operation. The doctor and the necromancer were one machine.
- **Ishani is fragile but loyal.** 41 HP, 14 AC under the armor. He's worth protecting and worth not telling everything to — he's a temple cleric, not a co-conspirator.
- *(Player meta — cross-table.)* On the old Sunday Korvosa table (same DM), the "Nicolas Cage doctor" / **Davaulus** beat ran differently. Here the group found and **killed** him outright, and the trail to Ileosa is opening earlier than where Pax left the Sunday story.

### What Izka is thinking about
- **The one-trick is the win condition — own it.** Spellstrike → Cascade → recharge, every turn, and it hasn't missed. The DM's "Gunslinger Magus" jab is right and it's fine. The build now has **Cavalier's Charge** (swapped in for Devastating Spellstrike) for reach-and-crit on the mount, the **Clay Sphere spellheart** affixing Gouging Claw to the guisarme, and at L6, **Reactive Strike** to punish movers and **Champion Dedication** for durability. The plan: L8 grabs the **Champion reaction**, and then I'm goated.
