Session Summary

With Jolistina dead and the Carowyn gala behind them, the party turned to the one thread still open: the missing head physician, Dr. Davaulus — the "Nicolas Cage doctor." The lead ran through Blackjack — and Izka finally caught up to what the rest of the party already knew: the vigilante is Vencarlo Orsini, the fencing master, one and the same. His tracking sigil had gone quiet at a refurbished warehouse "hospice" on the West Dock — people walked in, nobody walked out. He couldn't go in himself, so he sent the party. They strong-armed the temple's Ishani Dhatri into coming as a healer (over a relentless tide of "beat off / aftercare" jokes that visibly aged the poor cleric), donned plague masks, and walked in the front door as a fake hospice inspection — a bit Meeg improvised on the spot and rode all the way to a body count.

The inspection cracked the place open. A "Sarenrae" priest who knew the secret handshake but not a single farmer's aphorism; masked "Queen's Physicians" who were really the doctor's private muscle; and Reiner himself, all plastered smile and twitching eye, who led them upstairs and then refused a handshake one beat too long. The fight that followed was a grind — Kukulan soaked crit after crit (keeping up his bit about always dying), Reiner revealed an inexplicable bard's Courageous Anthem, threw Fear and a Fireball that caught his own physician, and tried to turn invisible and flee. It ended on a stupid, perfect note: Meeg cracked open a scroll of Acidic Burst on himself, Reiner crit-failed the save twice, and the doctor — whom Kukulan had been carefully trying to keep alive — simply died on the floor.

With Reiner dead, the last surviving Queen's Physician — prone and surrendering — pointed them to an elevator and "the bottom floors." Down it went, into a lightless Urgathoan temple of scythe murals and cultists playing blackjack around a crate where they were "making a blade to kill people." The party bluffed, gathered that the operation was commissioned by nobility — someone royal, reports addressed to "A," then masked up and cut them down. A dying cultist crawled toward Rolth before bleeding out, finally pinning the necromancer's location to this temple. One more room — two explosive sulfur zombies over a glass floor above a pit of dozens of bodies — ended with Kukulan smashing the glass to drop one zombie into the pit and pinning the other for the party to kill safely. They leveled to 6 somewhere in the middle of all of it, and walked out having killed the doctor, traced the plague up toward the crown, and found where Rolth has been hiding the whole time.

The Story

Act I · Downtime · Loose Ends at the Manor

Loose Ends at the Manor

  • The party is squatting in the dead noble Roran Carowyn's manor — the S7 gala house. Roran fled, traumatized and never coming back; he offered to sell the deed for 400 gp, but the party just decided to live there for free.
  • Delights raised a thrall of the dead Jolistina to interrogate her — no languages, no answers, just a corpse staring. Kukulan "helped" by crushing her head.
  • The only live lead on the plague is the missing doctor, and the road to him runs through Blackjack. They set out for the Orsini Academy to summon him.
Act II · Roleplay · The Man Behind Blackjack

The Man Behind Blackjack

  • After dark, the (quarantine-closed) Orsini fencing Academy. Kukulan presses his face to the glass; Trinia answers; inside is Vencarlo Orsini.
  • Kukulan flexes his Blackjack chest tattoo and produces a deck of cards (and, when Vencarlo arrives with takeout, hurls it at him yelling "hit me!" — Vencarlo catches it). Vencarlo confirms it: "Me and Blackjack are one and the same." Izka, the newcomer, genuinely didn't know — the rest of the party already did ("Oh, does he not know? I assumed he knew").
  • The literacy scene: Kukulan vouches that Izka "knows how to read and everything," prompting Vencarlo to note Kukulan doesn't — and Delights to clarify he's a literate arcane caster who only plays dumb "in solidarity with my brother Kukulan, who is so stupid." Kukulan, wounded ("I'm street smart — it's a different kind of intelligence"), fake-sobs off to "borrow a room."
  • Backstory beats land: Trinia (here, at his house) is a recent Acadamae expellee — framed for the king's death ("regicide") two weeks ago — while Izka's a ten-year alum; the knighthood "that means nothing"; the execution where "Kukulan killed so many people" (the one guard who survived).
  • The hook: the tracking sigil (from "Morris") stopped at a warehouse-hospice at the West Dock (grid G9). Windows boarded, entrance guarded, and no one ever comes out — not the doctor, not his masked physicians. Blackjack can't go in as a wanted anti-royalist. The party can.
  • They crash at Vencarlo's empty house (no kitchen — he buys them pot pie and Cooperative Waffles Greater). Kukulan tries to "inherit the Blackjack title" in a card game but loses the Games Lore roll; instead the party — several of them Queen's knights — formally make Izka their squire to fit the newcomer in.
Act III · Roleplay · Recruiting Ishani

Recruiting Ishani & the Inspection Plan

  • They strong-arm Ishani Dhatri out of the plague temple to serve as a healer ("there's about to be a lot of casualties at this hospice"). The comedy runs hot: Ishani is fragile (41 HP, 14 AC unarmored), the "stop wearing armor, that's what a real healer does" bit, and the party's unending "beat off / aftercare" innuendo.
  • Ishani's mechanics: one action/round (+2 if Commanded), Battle Medicine + four rank-3 Heals. Handed a Wand of Mystic Armor and Dust of Disappearance. Meeg preps Root Magic on Kukulan (+1 vs the first spell save).
  • The warehouse is ~50 ft tall and sealed. They go in the front as a hospice inspection, wearing Ishani's plague masks. Meeg runs the bit as "Meeg, the Cromulent Knight."
Act IV · Investigation · Phonies in the Plague Ward

Phonies in the Plague Ward

  • A muscular masked receptionist (obviously a thug) lets them wait and fetches the doctor. Inside: a vast triage hall of blood veil victims, masked Queen's Physicians, and a "Sarenrae" priest.
  • Meeg works the phonies. The fake Sarenite dodges a handshake ("I've been touching sick people") — Meeg: "Very good. You passed your first test." Kukulan, meanwhile, plays germ-theory expert ("Germ theory is something I'm very knowledgeable in" — steps six feet back). The Sarenite knows the secret dap but fails the real aphorism ("the sun and what are needed to grow?"); Meeg's Sense Motive + Esoteric Lore confirm the robes are fake ("I don't like people who pretend to be something they're not"), and he leaves his familiar, "Cousin Lush," as a spy on the impostor.
  • Reiner (the "Nicolas Cage" doctor) appears, half-recognizes them from "the Citadel," and leads them upstairs to his office — two Queen's Physicians and two Grey Maidens flanking.
  • He claims no royal funding; Sense Motive says he's lying. He refuses Kukulan's handshake, and initiative drops.
Act V · Combat · "He Hates All of You"

The Doctor's Office

  • The whole operation throws down: Reiner, 2 Grey Maiden foot soldiers, 2 Queen's Physicians. (Full round-by-round in Combat Encounters below.)
  • Reiner reveals a bard's Courageous Anthem, lands Fear on the entire party, and throws a Fireball that catches Izka, Kukulan, Po — and his own Queen's Physician.
  • It ends when Meeg pops a scroll of Acidic Burst on himself: Reiner crit-fails the Reflex twice and dies, taking a Grey Maiden with him. Kukulan had gone non-lethal to keep the doctor alive — too late.
  • With Reiner dead, the last surviving Queen's Physician — prone and surrendering — points to an elevator down to "the temple" and "the bottom floors," warns them off the southern room (~a dozen Varisian plague victims strapped to beds), and admits "what you seek is in the bottom floors."
  • Loot: Plaguebringer's masks, a note addressed to "A" ("send all reports to A down below"), an elevator button-key. The party levels to 6.
Act VI · Investigation · The Temple Below

The Temple Below

  • The elevator descends a full minute into a dark Urgathoan temple — scythe murals, obvious iconography (Izka's Religion check). The party is blind in the dark; Izka glows his horn, Ishani and Delights light a torch.
  • Four cultists are playing blackjack around a crate where they're "making a blade to kill people." Bluffing as new recruits, the party learns the job came from nobility — someone royal ("not the king"), reports going to "A."
  • Kukulan — who can't read — "deduces" the handler must be Sabina because "A and S are almost next to each other" (a bit, born of his illiteracy). The players half-seriously float Queen Ileosa; nothing's proven.
  • Kukulan disables the door lock to seal the gamblers, then the party masks up (four Plaguebringer's masks + a Masquerade Scarf) and bluffs the next chamber — until "Blackjack" sets the cultists off and the fight starts.
Act VII · Combat · Where Rolth Is

Where Rolth Is

  • Cultists of Urgathoa, a Harpy Skeleton, and Queen's Physicians fall fast to Izka's Spellstrikes, Kukulan's Snagging-Strike/Stunning-Blows, Delights' thralls, and Meeg's Exploit Vulnerability. (Round-by-round below.)
  • An Abaddon priest was present but sat the fight out in the back chamber.
  • The Rolth reveal: a dying Cultist of Urgathoa crawls toward Rolth and bleeds out at the door — confirming Rolth is down here. "Well, now we know where Rolth is."
  • The last room: a glass floor over a pit of dozens of bodies, guarded by two explosive sulfur zombies (weak to vitality; Death Throes ~27 dmg, 30-ft burst). Kukulan smashes the "bulletproof" glass and drops one zombie into the pit, then grapples and pins the second so the party can kill it safely — its death-explosion deals a trivial 13 damage.
  • Aftermath: Ishani de-cloaks; Kukulan admits he wrecked the elevator (Delights' Helpful Steps can climb them out, 120 ft); the DM credits the whole run to Meeg's improvised health-inspector bit.

  • Resolution: Meeg pops a scroll of Acidic Burst on himself. Reiner crit-fails the Reflex — two crit fails — and dies, taking a Grey Maiden foot soldier with him; the last Queen's Physician is left dying, and Izka takes only 3. Kukulan had gone non-lethal to keep Reiner alive for questioning — just too late.

Setup

  • The room is a glass floor over a pit of dozens of bodies — like a glass bridge, it cracks under too much weight. Izka, full on spell slots, calls his shot: "Don't worry, I have my full spell slots, so I'll be quick."

Round 1

  • Kukulan goes for the shortcut — smash the floor and drop them in. The glass is "basically bulletproof" and only cracks at first, but his second Athletics swing caves in half the floor and one sulfur zombie drops into the pit — gone, no explosion ("goodbye corpse… that part's no longer a problem").
  • Izka directs Ishani to get the far door open, then — riffing on his one-trick ("I'm so lame, I just do the same thing all the time") — does the same thing anyway: a Spellstrike with Telekinetic Projectile as the payload (the guisarme delivering the cantrip), and raises Shield.
  • Delights tumbles across the glass (Acrobatics DC 15), flanks, and Taunts the zombie.
  • Meeg climbs onto a bed and Exploits Vulnerability — "it fucking hates vitality" — with a free Recall Knowledge flagging its Death Throes (27 damage, 30-ft burst on death).

Round 2

  • Kukulan walks up and grapples the last zombie to drag it pit-ward (Meeg: "German suplex into the pit!"). The zombie tries to Escape twice and fails both.
  • Izka, acting right after it, unloads on the pinned, flanked target — three guisarme Strikes land, the fourth misses — then deliberately leaves the kill to Delights ("the light will finish it") so the death-explosion goes off on the party's terms, not in his face.
  • Delights steps back and summons two thralls flanking; their slash drops the zombie. Its Death Throes detonate for only 13 damage to the party (Kukulan had it restrained and shrugged the blast). Won "in record time."
