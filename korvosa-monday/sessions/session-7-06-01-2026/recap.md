## Session Summary

The group's investigation into Korvosa's spreading **blood veil** plague led them down into the sewers, where Urgothoan cultists and a warren of were-rats were helping push contaminated coin into the city. The fight that followed was a meat-grinder — and the night's first surprise was that **Meeg, run by the DM, became an unkillable wall**, holding the chokepoint and dropping enemies with his chakrams while nobody could land a hit on him. Kukulan grappled, tripped, and tried to *recruit* the were-rats, taking crit after crit from their leader **Grig** and going down twice before the party patched him back up. Into the chaos rode a stranger on a small dragon — **Izka**, a self-described "rhinoceros man" and graduate of the Acadamae — who picked off cultists, then chased down and killed the fleeing Grig before the party even knew his name.

What followed was a comedy of introductions. The party is functionally illiterate; Izka can read; that, more than anything, earned him a permanent spot. Delights pegged him as a "horned freak, kill stealer, and a nerd," then decided the group was "absolutely fucked without Izka" and welcomed him aboard. After looting the warren and a market run for their level-5 gear, they visited **Ishani Dhatri's** church, now an overwhelmed plague ward, traded an encrypted Urgothoan text for 300 gold, and learned how dire the outbreak has become — only 20% of the afflicted recover, and the head physician everyone calls the "Nicolas Cage doctor" has gone missing.

A rumor of a noble **gala** at the **Carowyn Manor** — held in defiance of the plague — sent them over the wall of a gated community and through a locked front door, behind which the partygoers had already turned to undead. On the balcony waited the necromancer **Jolistina Susperi**, who had crashed the party to build "the perfect zombie" for someone named **Rolth**. It was meant to be an extreme, multi-round fight. It lasted about as long as it took Izka to roll his crits: a Sure-Struck Horizon Thunder Sphere that vaporized the entire Shambler Troop, a crit guisarme Strike that finished Jolistina, and a Spellstrike crit on the wall-busting Zombie Hulk. A lone survivor, the noble **Dante Carowyn**, crawled out of a bathtub to give them the name that ties it all together: **Rolth**.

## Key Events

### Act I — The Rat Warren (Combat)
- The party tracked the plague into the **sewers**, where they'd cornered **Grig** — "the biggest rat who makes all the rules" — leader of a were-rat warren working with **Urgothoan cultists** to spread blood veil through tainted coin. Grig stalled for reinforcements; the fight kicked off.
- The DM dropped **Izka** into the encounter as a passing stranger — "you just happen to be here and hear all this fighting." He rode in on **Po**, his dragonet mount, and opened with a Spellstrike.
- **Meeg** (DM-run) anchored the chokepoint and became the runaway MVP — chakram strikes, Exploit Vulnerability, and an unbroken streak of enemies whiffing on him. "Meeg's the Wonderwall."
- **Kukulan** repeatedly tried to *spare* the were-rats — "Stand back, rats, this isn't your fight" — even promising to pay Ishani to cure their disease, but they wouldn't break from Grig.
- Grig and the rats crit Kukulan over and over, and he **went down twice**: first focused by the rats — **Meeg's rank-2 heal scroll** revived him — then again when Grig **grappled and restrained him to Dying 3**, after which **Delights' healing potion (18)** patched him up.
- Most of the warren died; the party managed to spare one or two rats. Grig, badly beaten, broke and ran.
- **Izka chased the fleeing Grig**, dismounted Po, and cut him down with a melee Strike — the killing blow on the boss the party had spent the whole fight agonizing over.

### Act II — "I Am a Rhinoceros Man" (RP)
- With Grig dead, the party turned on the stranger. Delights shook him: "Who are you?"
- Izka introduced himself as a **graduate of the Acadamae**, Korvosa's College of Wizards — and immediately ran aground on the fact that **the rest of the party can't read.**
- Delights' verdict: "On top of being a horned freak and a kill stealer, you're also a nerd? Get him!" — but after a huddle, he reversed: "Izka is attentive, and Izka can read. I think we need Izka in this group more than I thought." Izka was made a **permanent member.**
- Comedy beats: Izka explaining what a rhinoceros is for five minutes; the **Kashrishi telepathy** discussion; Delights elbowing Izka in the horn.

### Act III — The Plague Ward (Downtime / RP)
- Level-up to **5**, a market run (the dead, plague-emptied market gave generous buy prices), and gear shopping. Izka's potency crystal put him at **AC 23**.
- Visit to **Ishani Dhatri's church** — now an expansive medical wing packed with cots, masked clerics of Abadar, Sarenrae, and others treating **blood veil** victims.
- The party handed over the **encrypted Urgothoan text** looted from the sewer; Ishani bought it for **300 gp** for the temple library and flagged that it's **encrypted** — he offered to research it.
- Worldbuilding learned: only ~**20% of the afflicted recover**; it hits lower-class **Shoanti and Varisians** hardest, while **Kelids** (who quarantined by cutting off Highbridge) are largely untouched. The missing "Nicolas Cage" head physician (**Dr. Davaulus**) is rumored to be in a secret Midland laboratory or near the university in North Point; **Blackjack** is trying to track him.

### Act IV — The Carowyn Gala (Combat)
- A noble **gala** at the **Carowyn Manor** in **South Shore** — thrown despite the plague, with the popular performer **Ruan** invited — became the next lead.
- Turned away at the gate by **Grey Maidens**, the party went over the wall: Delights' "Helpful Steps" stairs and Kukulan flying up on his new **dragon wings.**
- Behind the locked front door, the partygoers had **mutated into undead.** On a balcony 15 ft up waited the necromancer **Jolistina Susperi**, who taunted the party, loosed a **Shambler Troop**, and retreated behind her minions.
- **Kukulan flew up to the balcony** to engage Jolistina directly — grappling and flurrying her as she Feinted to stay slippery.
- **Izka cast Sure Strike, then Spellstruck Horizon Thunder Sphere — and crit**, the explosion wiping out the entire **Shambler Troop** at once.
- Izka recharged with **Force Fang** and Spellstruck Jolistina with Gouging Claw for **35**, softening her; the next turn, with his Spellstrike spent, a **plain guisarme Strike crit — the killing blow on Jolistina.**
- A **Zombie Hulk** with a 15-ft reach burst through a wall (a late addition to the fight). Izka **crit it with a Spellstrike** ("Double it and give it to the next person"), and Delights' thralls finished it off. What was billed as an extreme encounter ended almost instantly.
- The survivor, **Dante Carowyn** (a minor noble of the manor's family, hiding in a bathtub), revealed Jolistina's obsession: she was building "the perfect zombie" to make **Rolth** fall in love with her.

## Combat Encounters

> Initiative orders below are from Pax's tracker. Round-by-round detail is reconstructed from the session audio; damage numbers are included only where the DM stated them.

### Encounter 1 — The Rat Warren (Sewers)
**Enemies**: Grig (were-rat leader), ~4 were-rats, 2 Urgothoan cultists, a Rat Swarm, a Giant Rat.
**Initiative**: Cultist of Urgathoa → Izka → Were-rat → Delights Qlomaind → Meeg → Were-rat → Cultist of Urgathoa → Were-rat → Were-rat → Rat Swarm → Grig → Giant Rat. *(Kukulan fought too; his slot isn't on the tracker list — best read is adjacent to Grig.)*

The fight was already underway when the DM dropped Izka in. It ran long and chaotic; beats below are in chronological order rather than clean rounds.

**Izka (on Po):**
- Opens by riding up on Po and **Spellstriking a cultist with Gouging Claw for 23**, then casts **Shield**.
- A cultist later turns on him — Izka eats **9 damage and a Daze**, then another **6**.
- Mid-fight he **Spellstrikes a Gouging Claw for 26** — "a giant-ass claw forming out of his entire body, pierced through a rat" — then **enters Arcane Cascade.**
- **Commands Po for two strikes**, recharges his Spellstrike, and **burns a hero point** to Spellstrike an unaware cultist (electricity).
- Recharges and **Spellstrikes for 13 + 3 Arcane Cascade splash**, blowing up a cultist outright.
- **Crit-trips Grig** (using **Community Knowledge** for the +2), then — with two actions to spare and the kill left to the others — declares he's just going to sit back and **grab popcorn.**

**Kukulan:**
- **Wolf Stance + Flurry**; first grapple on Grig fails ("Grig is made of sterner stuff") but the Flurry lands **19**.
- Repeatedly **trips** rats and tries to **talk the warren down** — even (failed) Diplomacy offering to pay Ishani to cure them. Most rats won't break from Grig.
- **Stunning Fist (Wolf Jaws) for 34** puts Grig at death's door (Grig resists the stun).
- **Goes down twice**: first focused by the rats — **Meeg's rank-2 heal scroll** revives him — then, after **Grig grapples and restrains him to Dying 3** with no heal handy, **Delights' healing potion (18)** brings him back. Spends his last hero point on a Tumble Through.

**Delights Qlomaind:**
- **Mirror Image** on self (a clone soaks a hit); a **Bane** he immediately regrets ("I baned myself").
- A stream of **thralls** and **Bone Spear** AoEs — **13** to the rat swarm, later **15** that kills the swarm plus **12** to a rat; **hero-point rerolls** a Bone Spear.
- **Guidance** on himself to get back in the game; the clutch **healing potion** on Kukulan.

**Meeg (DM-run):**
- The **"Wonderwall"** — holds the chokepoint essentially untouched all fight, **Chakram** strikes + **Exploit Vulnerability**, and personally **guts a were-rat dead**. Drops his weapon to **heal Kukulan** off the floor at the critical moment.

**Resolution:** the warren is ground down; one or two rats are spared. Grig, beaten and tripped, **flees**. **Izka chases on Po, dismounts, and finishes him with a Strike** — then drags the body back to the party. **Kill credit: Izka.** (He considered Needle Darts first, then chose to dismount and Strike.)

### Encounter 2 — The Carowyn Manor Gala (Zombie Outbreak)
**Enemies**: Jolistina Susperi (necromancer boss), a Shambler Troop, and a Zombie Hulk (late arrival).
**Initiative**: Shambler Troop → Jolistina Susperi → Kukulan → Izka → Delights Qlomaind. **Zombie Hulk joined later (burst through a wall).**

- The party climbs the manor wall (**Delights' "Helpful Steps"** + **Kukulan's dragon-wing flight**); Kukulan picks the lock, and the doors open on **mutated undead partygoers.**
- The **Shambler Troop** opens with a **shambling onslaught** — a 5-ft emanation hitting everyone in reach (no save). **Jolistina** appears on a balcony ~15 ft up, taunts the party ("I had my own fun"), looses her minions, and **withdraws** behind them.
- **Kukulan**: Wolf Stance + Flurry into the Shambler Troop (mostly misses), then uses **dragon-wing flight to fly 20 ft up to the balcony** and engage Jolistina directly — the move that, per the DM, short-circuited a fight meant to last rounds.
- **Izka**: casts **Sure Strike**, then **Spellstrikes Horizon Thunder Sphere — and crits**, the explosion **wiping the entire Shambler Troop** in one action ("Izka saying kamehameha, and then all of them are dead").
- **Zombie Hulk** smashes through a wall (15-ft reach) and swings at Kukulan on entry — **misses (nat 1)**; on a later swing Kukulan **Shield Blocks** (10 reduced).
- **Delights**: runs inside; creates a **thrall opposite Jolistina** (off-guard); a **muscle-barrier thrall** feeds Kukulan temp HP and +1 Athletics; a "make it dream of home" effect **fizzles** on a creature "born a second ago."
- **Kukulan** grapples Jolistina and hammers her for **22** (with a stun), bringing her low.
- **Izka**: hits Jolistina with **Force Fang** (a conflux cast that **recharges his Spellstrike**), then **Spellstrikes Gouging Claw for 35** ("You shouldn't have created such an abomination"), softening her. **The next turn, his Spellstrike spent, a plain guisarme Strike crits — the killing blow on Jolistina.**
- **Izka** then **crits the Zombie Hulk with a Spellstrike** ("This man rolls all the 19s… Double it and give it to the next person"); **Delights' thralls finish the Hulk.** What was billed as an extreme encounter collapses on the Magus's crit streak.

### What Izka is thinking about

Keep a reliable attack-roll spell loaded and bank Sure Strike for the spike turns.
- **Po can't fly.** The wings are decorative for now. Kukulan's flight and Delights' stair/rope tricks cover vertical movement — plan around Po being a ground mount in these vertical urban fights.
