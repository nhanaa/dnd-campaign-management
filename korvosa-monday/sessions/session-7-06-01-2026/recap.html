Session Summary

The group’s hunt for the source of Korvosa’s spreading blood veil plague led them into the sewers, where Urgothoan cultists and a warren of were-rats were pushing contaminated coin into the city. The fight was a meat-grinder — and the night’s first surprise was that Meeg, run by the DM, became an unkillable wall, holding the chokepoint and dropping enemies with his chakrams while nobody could land a hit on him. Kukulan grappled, tripped, and tried to recruit the were-rats, going down twice under the crits of their leader Grig. Into the chaos rode a stranger on a small dragon — Izka, a self-described “rhinoceros man” and graduate of the Acadamae — who picked off cultists, then chased down and killed the fleeing Grig before the party even learned his name.

What followed was a comedy of introductions. The party is functionally illiterate; Izka can read; that, more than anything, earned him a permanent spot. Delights pegged him as a “horned freak, kill stealer, and a nerd,” then decided the group was “absolutely fucked without Izka” and welcomed him aboard. After looting the warren and a market run for their level-5 gear, they visited Ishani Dhatri’s church, now an overwhelmed plague ward, traded an encrypted Urgothoan text for 300 gold, and learned how dire the outbreak has become — only 20% of the afflicted recover, and the head physician everyone calls the “Nicolas Cage doctor” has gone missing.

A rumor of a noble gala at the Carowyn Manor — held in defiance of the plague — sent them over the wall of a gated community and through a locked front door, behind which the partygoers had already turned to undead. On the balcony waited the necromancer Jolistina Susperi, who had crashed the party to build “the perfect zombie” for someone named Rolth. It was meant to be an extreme, multi-round fight. It lasted about as long as it took Izka to roll his crits: a Sure-Struck Horizon Thunder Sphere that vaporized the entire Shambler Troop, a crit guisarme Strike that finished Jolistina, and a Spellstrike crit on the wall-busting Zombie Hulk. A lone survivor, the noble Dante Carowyn, crawled out of a bathtub to give them the name that ties it all together.

Key Events

Act I · Combat · The Rat Warren

Grig, Who Makes All the Rules

  • The party tracked the plague into the sewers, cornering Grig — “the biggest rat who makes all the rules” — leader of a were-rat warren working with Urgothoan cultists to spread blood veil through tainted coin. Grig stalled for reinforcements; the fight kicked off.
  • The DM dropped Izka into the encounter as a passing stranger — “you just happen to be here and hear all this fighting.” He rode in on Po, his dragonet mount, and opened with a Spellstrike for 23.
  • Meeg (DM-run) anchored the chokepoint and became the runaway MVP — chakram strikes, Exploit Vulnerability, and an unbroken streak of enemies whiffing on him. “Meeg’s the Wonderwall.”
  • Kukulan repeatedly tried to spare the were-rats — “Stand back, rats, this isn’t your fight” — even promising to pay Ishani to cure their disease, but they wouldn’t break from Grig.
  • Kukulan went down twice: first focused by the rats (Meeg’s rank-2 heal scroll revived him), then grappled and restrained by Grig to Dying 3 (Delights’ healing potion patched him afterward).
  • Izka chased the fleeing Grig, dismounted Po, and cut him down with a melee Strike — the killing blow on the boss the party had spent the whole fight agonizing over.
Act II · Roleplay · First Impressions

“I Am a Rhinoceros Man”

  • With Grig dead, the party turned on the stranger. Delights shook him: “Who are you?”
  • Izka introduced himself as a graduate of the Acadamae, Korvosa’s College of Wizards — and immediately ran aground on the fact that the rest of the party can’t read.
  • Delights’ verdict: “On top of being a horned freak and a kill stealer, you’re also a nerd? Get him!” — but after a huddle, he reversed: “Izka is attentive, and Izka can read. I think we need Izka in this group more than I thought.” Izka was made a permanent member.
  • Comedy beats: Izka explaining what a rhinoceros is for five straight minutes; the Kashrishi telepathy discussion; Delights elbowing Izka in the horn.
Act III · Investigation · The Plague Ward

Ishani’s Overwhelmed Temple

  • Level-up to 5, a market run (the plague-emptied market gave generous buy prices), and gear shopping. Izka’s potency crystal put him at AC 23.
  • Visit to Ishani Dhatri’s church — now an expansive medical wing packed with cots, masked clerics of Abadar, Sarenrae, and others treating blood veil victims.
  • The party handed over the encrypted Urgothoan text looted from the sewer; Ishani bought it for 300 gp for the temple library and flagged that it’s encrypted — he offered to research it.
  • Worldbuilding learned: only ~20% of the afflicted recover; it hits lower-class Shoanti and Varisians hardest, while Kelids (who quarantined by cutting off Highbridge) are largely untouched.
  • The missing “Nicolas Cage” head physician (Dr. Davaulus) is rumored to be in a secret Midland laboratory or near the university in North Point; Blackjack is trying to track him.
Act IV · Combat · The Carowyn Gala

A Party of the Dead

  • A noble gala at the Carowyn Manor in South Shore — thrown despite the plague, with the popular performer Ruan invited — became the next lead.
  • Turned away at the gate by Grey Maidens, the party went over the wall: Delights’ “Helpful Steps” stairs and Kukulan flying up on his new dragon wings.
  • Behind the locked front door, the partygoers had mutated into undead. The necromancer Jolistina Susperi appeared on a balcony 15 ft up, taunted the party, loosed a Shambler Troop, and retreated behind her minions.
  • Kukulan flew up to the balcony to engage Jolistina directly — grappling and flurrying her as she Feinted to stay slippery.
  • Izka cast Sure Strike, then Spellstruck Horizon Thunder Sphere — and crit, the explosion wiping out the entire Shambler Troop at once. (“Izka saying kamehameha, and then all of them are dead.”)
  • A Zombie Hulk with a 15-ft reach burst through a wall (late to the fight). Izka hit Jolistina with Force Fang to recharge, then Spellstruck Gouging Claw for 35 to soften her, and finished her the next turn with a plain guisarme crit Strike; he then crit the Hulk with a Spellstrike, which Delights’ thralls put down. Two Magus crits collapsed an “extreme” encounter almost instantly.
  • The survivor, Dante Carowyn (a minor noble of the manor’s family, hiding in a bathtub), revealed Jolistina’s obsession: she was building “the perfect zombie” to make Rolth fall in love with her.

Pax’s Notes — Izka’s Journal

What Izka did this session

  • Arrived in Korvosa “after some jobs outside the city,” stumbled into the sewer fight, and pitched in against the Urgothoan cultists.
  • Killed the fleeing were-rat boss Grig (dismounted Po, ran him down with a Strike). Earned the party’s “kill stealer” label immediately.
  • Got himself adopted into the party — mostly because he’s the only literate one. Presented as a graduate of the Acadamae, the College of Wizards.
  • Traded the Urgothoan text to Ishani Dhatri for 300 gp; backed Ishani’s price honestly.
  • At the gala: Sure Strike → Horizon Thunder Sphere Spellstrike crit wiped the Shambler Troop; Force Fang (recharge) → Gouging Claw Spellstrike for 35 softened Jolistina, then a plain crit Strike finished her; a Spellstrike crit on the Zombie Hulk. The crit streak ended the fight.

Things Izka knows that the party does not

  • The party is illiterate. This is leverage and liability both — Izka is now the de facto reader, researcher, and face for anything written.
  • Meeg (the goblin) is dangerous. He held the entire sewer chokepoint untouched. Do not underestimate him when his player returns.
  • (Player meta — cross-table.) Pax played this exact AP on the old Sunday Korvosa table (same DM, Orion). Things that line up and differ:
    • Blackjack: on the Sunday table his identity was never officially revealed — Pax’s character only guessed it (Vencarlo), confirmed out of game. Here this group already knows him as “the guy who runs the fencing academy,” actively tracking the missing plague doctor.
    • Trinia / the scapegoat: both tables saved the real Trinia and the execution scapegoat, with Blackjack spiriting them to a safe house. Same beat.
    • The plague: the Sunday group stopped right before blood veil broke out — Izka is picking the story back up one beat later than where Pax left it.
    • The Acadamae: the Sunday group “never went to the Acadamae at all.” This table hasn’t yet either — but Izka is a graduate of it, which may be a thread worth pulling.
    • Rolth: same antagonist thread in both — Gaedren Lamm’s necromancer son, still at large, with cultists building horrors in his name.

What Izka is thinking about

  • Crit fishing is the win condition. Sure Strike into a big-rank Spellstrike crit deleted the Shambler Troop; the kills came on crits all night — including a plain guisarme Strike crit to finish Jolistina once the Spellstrike was spent. Keep a reliable attack-roll spell loaded and bank Sure Strike for the spike turns.
  • Po can’t fly. The wings are decorative for now. Kukulan’s flight and Delights’ stair/rope tricks cover vertical movement — plan around Po being a ground mount in vertical urban fights.
  • This party kills first and asks questions never. Kukulan wants to spare everyone; Delights wants to rob everyone. Izka can be the one who actually reads the room (and the documents).
