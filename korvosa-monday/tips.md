# Tips & Tactics — Izka (Inexorable Iron Magus / Cavalier)

## Core Engine: Spellstrike on a Reach Polearm

- **Spellstrike (2A)** loads a 1-/2-action spell into a melee Strike. With the **Guisarme** you deliver it at **10-ft reach** — Frostbite/Gouging Claw etc. hit from outside most enemies' reach.
- A **crit doubles both** the weapon damage and the spell. Maximize crit chance: attack **Off-Guard** targets (see party synergy) and lead with Spellstrike *before* spending your multiple-attack penalty.
- **Recharge discipline**: after Spellstrike you can't do it again until you recharge. Options each turn: (a) 1-action Recharge, or (b) cast a **conflux spell** (Thunderous Strike / Force Fang) — conflux casting recharges Spellstrike *and* fuels Arcane Cascade. Build turns so a conflux cast does double duty.

## Inexorable Iron: Stay in the Stance

- **Arcane Cascade** (1A) requires your previous action to be a Cast a Spell / Spellstrike to *enter*, but not to *remain*. Enter it Round 1 and don't drop it.
- While in Cascade **wielding a two-handed melee weapon**, you gain **temp HP = ½ level (min 1)** on entry and at the **start of each turn**. At L5 that's **2 temp HP/turn** — small but it means Caliban shouldn't waste heals on chip damage. Don't switch to a one-handed grip or you lose this.
- Cascade also adds bonus damage (force, or the last spell's damage type) to melee Strikes and makes them magical.

## Mounted Combat (Cavalier)

- **Impressive Mount** = mature companion: during an encounter it can take **1 action on its own** even if you don't **Command an Animal**. Command it (1A) for a full turn of its actions when it matters.
- Use the mount for **mobility + reach threat**: ride up, Spellstrike at 10 ft, and let the mount reposition. You're a hard-to-pin frontliner.
- **Caution — Korvosa is urban**: lots of interiors, doors, stairs, rooftops. A Large mount can't follow everywhere. Have a plan for fights where the mount is stuck outside; you're still a full Magus on foot.

## Action-Economy Templates

**Opening turn (foot or saddle):**
1. Spellstrike (2A) — best attack-roll spell into a reach Strike.
2. Arcane Cascade (1A) — enter stance (you just cast, so it's legal), bank temp HP next turn.

**Sustained turn:**
1. Conflux spell (Thunderous Strike / Force Fang) — recharges Spellstrike, keeps Cascade fed.
2. Spellstrike (2A) again.
   *(Or: 1A Recharge → 2A Spellstrike if you want to save the focus point.)*

**When a caster shows up:**
- **Recognize Spell** (reaction, free via background) to ID and gain +1 AC/save vs it. Izka is trained in Arcana/Occultism/Religion → covers most traditions at low rank.

## Saves & Survival

- **Wind Tempered**: +1 vs air/electricity; *air* successes upgrade to crits. Lean in vs gust/lightning effects.
- **Reflex Expertise** (L5) shores up your worst save line; you're Expert across all three saves now.
- **Wand of False Vitality** + Inexorable Iron temp HP stack as a chunky pre-fight buffer. Pre-cast False Vitality before expected fights.

## Party Synergy Reminders

- **Off-Guard setters**: Kukulan (grapple/trip) and Meeg (Thaumaturge weakness exploit) → time your Spellstrike on a target they've just downed-guard. Your crits are where the damage spikes.
- **Don't double-anchor**: you and Kukulan are both melee. Use your **reach + mount** to take a flank or a second front rather than stacking the same square.
- **Let Caliban heal the squishies** — your temp HP self-sustain means you're a low priority for the cleric font.

## Skill Lane (Society Paragon)

- **Society + Streetwise** is your intrigue toolkit — Korvosa runs on guild/noble/underworld politics. Be the party's read-the-city PC: Gather Information, Recall Knowledge (local power players), navigate factions.
