## AP Background (reference)

Curse of the Crimson Throne is a city-centered campaign of intrigue, plague, and revolution in **Korvosa**, Varisia's largest city. Classic beats: the death of **King Eodred II**, the rise of the cunning young **Queen Ileosa**, the petty crime lord **Gaedren Lamm**, city-wide unrest, a devastating **plague (Blood Veil)**, the queen's elite **Grey Maidens**, the vigilante **Blackjack**, and shadow players like the **Red Mantis assassins** and Harrow-reading allies. The party typically rises from street-level troubleshooters to the city's would-be saviors.

## Story So Far

### Before Session 7 (from the DM's recap)
- The party works for **Cressida Kroft**, captain of the guard, through a string of missions for the city.
- After the **king's assassination**, they were sent to capture the painter **Trinia**, found her innocent, and allied with **Blackjack** — whose identity this group knows: he runs the **fencing academy**. They hid Trinia.
- Two PCs were **knighted as personal guards of Queen Ileosa**.
- When the Queen publicly executed a **scapegoat**, Blackjack rescued her and spirited her to a safe house.
- A plague — **blood veil** — began spreading, first at an **orphanage Kukulan funds**, reported by the Abadar priest **Ishani Dhatri**. The Queen's decree handed sweeping authority to her **physicians** and the **Grey Maidens** (search/arrest any suspected home; brutal punishments).
- Chasing the source, the party went into the **sewers**.

### Session 7 (2026-06-01)
- **The rat warren**: defeated the were-rat boss **Grig** and **Urgothoan cultists** who were spreading blood veil via tainted **coin**. **Izka** joined here, killing the fleeing Grig.
- **Izka introduced**: a graduate of the **Acadamae** (College of Wizards), drafted as a permanent member — mainly because he's the only one in the party who can read.
- **Ishani Dhatri's plague ward**: the temple is overwhelmed (~20% recovery, heading toward triage). The party sold an **encrypted Urgothoan text** to Ishani for 300 gp; he's researching the encryption. Learned the missing head physician **Dr. Davaulus** (the "Nicolas Cage" doctor) has vanished — rumored Midland lab or North Point near the university; **Blackjack** is tracking him.
- **The Carowyn Manor gala** (South Shore): partygoers turned undead. The necromancer **Jolistina Susperi** had crashed it to build "the perfect zombie" for **Rolth**. The party killed her, a Shambler Troop, and a Zombie Hulk. Sole survivor **Dante Carowyn** gave them the Rolth lead. (The party now squats in the dead noble **Roran Carowyn's** manor.)

### Session 8 (2026-06-08)
- **Blackjack = Vencarlo, confirmed (for Izka)**: chasing the missing doctor, the party summoned Blackjack at the **Orsini fencing Academy**. The group already knew his identity (they run with him); **Izka**, the newcomer, only now learned that Blackjack is **Vencarlo Orsini** himself. His tracking sigil had stalled at a refurbished **warehouse-hospice at the West Dock (grid G9)** — people went in, none came out. He sent the party in (he's a wanted anti-royalist). They recruited the Abadar priest **Ishani Dhatri** as a healer-follower for the run. *(Derek returned, so Meeg was player-run; **Caliban/Hawke left the table** for good.)*
- **The hospice was a front.** Posing as a fake "hospice inspection" (Meeg's improvised "Cromulent Knight" bit), they exposed a fake **Sarenrae** priest and the masked **"Queen's Physicians"** as the doctor's private muscle, then fought **Reiner** (= the missing **Dr. Davaulus**), 2 Grey Maidens, and 2 Queen's Physicians. **Reiner died** (Meeg's Acidic Burst scroll crit-failed him). The **last surviving Queen's Physician**, prone and surrendering, pointed them to an elevator down to "the bottom floors."
- **The Urgathoan temple below**: a dark shrine of **Urgathoa** where cultists were "making a blade to kill people." The party learned the operation was commissioned by **nobility — someone royal** (reports sent to "**A**"). *Kukulan's "Sabina" guess is just an illiterate joke (A≈S); the players suspect **Ileosa**, unproven.* They cut down 2 Cultists of Urgathoa, a Harpy Skeleton, and Queen's Physicians (an **Abaddon priest** was present but sat the fight out). A dying cultist crawled toward **Rolth** before bleeding out — **confirming Rolth's lair is here.** A final room of explosive **sulfur zombies** over a **glass-floored pit** of corpses was won by smashing the glass to drop one zombie into the pit and pinning the other for the kill.
- **Level up to 6.** Loot: 4 Plaguebringer's masks, a note to "A," the elevator keys.
