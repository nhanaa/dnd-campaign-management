## Quick Combat Loop

1. **Round 1**: Enter **Arcane Cascade** (gain Inexorable Iron temp HP; need two-handed melee weapon = Guisarme) — usually after a Spellstrike or conflux cast.
2. **Spellstrike** (2A): load a hard-hitting spell (Gouging Claw, Ignition, or a slot spell) into a Guisarme Strike at 10-ft reach. Crit doubles spell + weapon.
3. **Recharge** Spellstrike with a 1A action or by casting **Thunderous Strike**/Force Fang (conflux also keeps Spellstrike online).
4. **Mounted**: command/ride the mature mount for extra mobility and a second body on the line; use reach to threaten from the saddle.
5. **Temp HP each turn** while in Cascade + two-handed weapon — stay in the stance.
