## Synergies to Watch (hypotheses, confirm in play)

- **Off-Guard economy**: Kukulan (grapple/trip) and Meeg (Mortal Weakness / Exploit Vulnerability) can set targets Off-Guard → Izka's Spellstrike crits harder, and Caliban's swashbuckler finishers land.
- **Frontline split**: Izka (mounted reach) + Kukulan (grapple) means two melee anchors; coordinate so they don't crowd the same chokepoint — Izka's reach + mount mobility should flank, Kukulan locks.
- **Action economy on the mount**: Cavalier mount gives Izka extra board presence; pair Trip/Grapple from Kukulan with Izka's reach threats.
- **Healing**: Caliban (Medicine paragon + cleric font) is primary patch. Izka self-sustains temp HP via Inexorable Iron — don't burn heals on him while in Cascade.
